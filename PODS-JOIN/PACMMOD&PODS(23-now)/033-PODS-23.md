## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

We present a new approach for independently computing compact sketches that can be used to approximate the inner product between pairs of high-dimensional vectors. Based on the Weighted MinHash algorithm, our approach admits strong accuracy guarantees that improve on the guarantees of popular linear sketching approaches for inner product estimation, such as CountSketch and Johnson-Lindenstrauss projection. Specifically, while our method exactly matches linear sketching for dense vectors, it yields significantly lower error for sparse vectors with limited overlap between non-zero entries. Such vectors arise in many applications involving sparse data, as well as in increasingly popular dataset search applications, where inner products are used to estimate data covariance, conditional means, and other quantities involving columns in unjoined tables. We complement our theoretical results by showing that our approach empirically outperforms existing linear sketches and unweighted hashing-based sketches for sparse vectors.

本文提出一种独立计算紧凑草图的新方法，用于近似高维向量对之间的内积。基于加权最小哈希算法（Weighted MinHash），该方法在准确度保证上显著优于内积估计的流行线性草图方法（如CountSketch和Johnson-Lindenstrauss投影）。具体而言，虽然本方法对稠密向量的处理与线性草图完全一致，但对于非零项重叠有限的稀疏向量，其误差显著降低。此类向量常见于稀疏数据应用场景，以及日益流行的数据集搜索应用——其中内积被用于估计数据协方差、条件均值及未连接表中列间相关量。实验结果表明，本方法在稀疏向量处理上优于现有线性草图及未加权哈希草图。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

The inner product of two vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b},\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{k = 1}}^{n}\mathbf{a}\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  \mathbf{b}\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ ,is a ubiquitous operation. Among many other applications, inner products can be used to compute document similarities [46], to evaluate learned classification models,and to estimate join sizes [1, 4, 45]. However, in modern applications involving very high-dimensional vectors, computing exact inner products can be intractable. The computational cost is $O\left( n\right)$ and computing $\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$ requires loading $O\left( n\right)$ numbers from memory,or communicating $O\left( n\right)$ numbers if a and $\mathbf{b}$ are stored on different machines.

向量$\mathbf{a}$与$\mathbf{b},\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{k = 1}}^{n}\mathbf{a}\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  \mathbf{b}\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$的内积是普遍存在的运算。除众多应用场景外，内积可用于计算文档相似度[46]、评估分类模型性能，以及估计连接大小[1,4,45]。然而在现代高维向量应用中，精确计算内积可能不可行——其计算复杂度为$O\left( n\right)$，且计算$\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$需要从内存加载$O\left( n\right)$个数字，若向量存储于不同机器还需传输$O\left( n\right)$个数字。

A common approach for resolving this issue is to pre-compute a small space compression (a sketch) of each vector, which we will denote by $\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ and $\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right)$ ,respectively. An estimation function $\mathcal{F}$ is then used to approximate the inner product as $\mathcal{F}\left( {\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) }\right)  \approx$ $\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$ . The beauty of sketching is that it simultaneously reduces storage, communication, and runtime complexity. Moreover, once computed, sketches can be reused again and again to estimate inner products with other vectors. For example,given another vector $\mathbf{c}$ we can estimate $\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{c}\rangle  \approx  \mathcal{F}\left( {\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{c}\right) }\right)$ .

常见解决方案是预计算每个向量的小空间压缩表示（即草图），分别记为$\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$和$\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right)$。通过估计函数$\mathcal{F}$将内积近似为$\mathcal{F}\left( {\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) }\right)  \approx$$\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$。草图技术的优势在于同时降低存储、通信和时间复杂度，且一旦生成可重复用于其他向量的内积估计。例如给定向量$\mathbf{c}$，可估计$\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{c}\rangle  \approx  \mathcal{F}\left( {\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{c}\right) }\right)$。

Sketching methods for approximating inner products are already widely used throughout computer science. In machine learning, they can be used to accelerate the training of large-scale linear models like support vector machines or logistic regression [5, 40]. In relational databases, inner product sketches are used in query optimizers to choose optimal query plans without having to execute expensive queries that involve large joins [19]. More recently, inner product sketches have found applications in dataset search and discovery, where they are used to discover joinable tables [22] and to estimate other column statistics, such as correlation [47], without explicitly performing a join operation between two tables. We discuss these applications and others in Section 1.2.

近似内积的素描方法已在计算机科学领域广泛应用。在机器学习中，它们可用于加速支持向量机或逻辑回归等大规模线性模型的训练[5,40]。在关系型数据库中，内积素描被查询优化器用于选择最优查询计划，从而避免执行涉及大规模连接的高成本查询[19]。最近，内积素描在数据集搜索与发现中获得了新应用，可用于发现可连接表[22]，以及在不显式执行两表连接操作的情况下估算其他列统计量（如相关性[47]）。我们将在第1.2节详细讨论这些应用场景。

What was Previously Known? In all of the applications above, a primary concern is optimizing the trade-off between the sketch size (which governs storage, communication, and runtime efficiency) and how accurately $\mathcal{F}\left( {\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) }\right)$ approximates $\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$ . A large sketch size will in general lead to better approximation, but the question is by exactly how much. Currently, the only methods with strong theoretical guarantees on this tradeoff for general vectors (i.e., vectors without any assumed value distribution or magnitude) are based on linear sketching algorithms. Such algorithms include the famous "tug-of-war" sketch, a.k.a. the AMS sketch [2, 4], the CountS-ketch algorithm [12], and methods based on Johnson-Lindenstrauss (JL) random projection [1, 20].

既有研究成果如何？上述所有应用场景中，核心问题在于权衡草图大小（决定存储、通信和运行时效率）与$\mathcal{F}\left( {\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) }\right)$近似$\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$的精度。较大的草图尺寸通常能带来更好的近似效果，但关键是要量化其提升幅度。目前，对于通用向量（即无任何预设值分布或量级的向量），唯一具有严格理论保证的方法是基于线性素描算法，包括著名的"拔河"草图（又称AMS草图[2,4]）、CountSketch算法[12]，以及基于Johnson-Lindenstrauss(JL)随机投影的方法[1,20]。

All of these approaches have a similar form. We choose a random matrix $\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$ ( $\Pi$ might have i.i.d. random entries or more complex structure) and sets $\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  = \Pi \mathbf{a}$ and $\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right)  = \Pi \mathbf{b}$ . Each sketch is a length $m$ vector and is considered a linear sketch since $\mathcal{S}$ is a linear function. To estimate the inner product, the typical approach is to simply return the sketch inner product $\langle \mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) \rangle {}^{1}$

这些方法都具有相似形式：选择一个随机矩阵$\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$（$\Pi$可能包含独立同分布随机元素或更复杂结构）以及集合$\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  = \Pi \mathbf{a}$和$\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right)  = \Pi \mathbf{b}$。每个草图都是长度为$m$的向量，由于$\mathcal{S}$是线性函数，故被视为线性草图。估算内积时，通常直接返回草图内积$\langle \mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) \rangle {}^{1}$

A textbook theoretical accuracy guarantee for inner product estimation based on linear sketching is:

基于线性素描的内积估算在教科书中的理论精度保证为：

FACT 1 (LINEAR SKETCHING FOR INNER PRODUCTS [5]). Let $\epsilon ,\delta  \in$ (0,1)be accuracy and failure probability parameters respectively and let $m = O\left( {\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) /{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ . Let $\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$ be a random matrix with each entry set independently to $+ \sqrt{1/m}$ or $- \sqrt{1/m}$ with equal probability. For length $n$ vectors $\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ ,let $\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  = \Pi \mathbf{a}$ and $\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right)  =$ $\Pi \mathbf{b}$ . With probability at least $1 - \delta$ ,

事实1（内积线性素描法[5]）。设$\epsilon ,\delta  \in$(0,1)分别为精度参数与失败概率参数，$m = O\left( {\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) /{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$。令$\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$为随机矩阵，其每个元素独立等概率取$+ \sqrt{1/m}$或$- \sqrt{1/m}$。对于长度为$n$的向量$\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$，定义$\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  = \Pi \mathbf{a}$和$\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right)  =$$\Pi \mathbf{b}$。在至少$1 - \delta$的概率下满足：

$$
\left| {\langle \mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) \rangle -\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle }\right|  \leq  \epsilon \parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel 
$$

where $\parallel \mathbf{x}\parallel$ denotes the standard Euclidean norm.

其中$\parallel \mathbf{x}\parallel$表示标准欧几里得范数。

In addition to dense random matrices, analogous results to Fact 1 can be proven for sparse JL matrices, CountSketch matrices, and other linear sketches [19]. The fact provides a powerful accuracy guarantee that improves with the sketch size $m$ and depends naturally on the norms of a and b. To the best of our knowledge, linear sketching methods were previously the only known algorithms to obtain such a strong theoretical guarantee.

除稠密随机矩阵外，事实1的结论可推广至稀疏JL矩阵、CountSketch矩阵等线性素描方法[19]。该事实提供了强有力的精度保证：其效果随草图尺寸$m$提升而改善，并自然依赖于向量a与b的范数。据我们所知，线性素描法是此前唯一能获得如此强大理论保证的算法。

### 1.1 Our Contributions

### 1.1 我们的贡献

In this paper we introduce a novel method for inner product sketching based on the Weighted MinHash sketch [25, 28, 41], which is a variant of the classic MinHash method [8, 9]. We prove that our method obtains a refined guarantee than Fact 1. In particular, it matches the result for linear sketches in the worst case when a and $\mathbf{b}$ are dense ${}^{2}$ ,but always obtains a better bound when $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ are sparse vectors with limited overlap between non-zero entries. As discussed further in Section 1.2, such pairs of vectors are the norm in many applications of inner product sketching to database problems and modern dataset search applications.

本文提出了一种基于加权最小哈希(Weighted MinHash)草图[25,28,41]的内积草图新方法，该方法是对经典最小哈希(MinHash)[8,9]的改进。我们证明该方法能获得比事实1更精确的保证。具体而言，当向量a和$\mathbf{b}$为稠密向量${}^{2}$时，其最坏情况下的性能与线性草图相当；而当$\mathbf{a}$与$\mathbf{b}$是非零项重叠有限的稀疏向量时，总能获得更优的界。如第1.2节所述，这类向量对内积草图在数据库问题及现代数据集搜索应用中的多数场景具有普遍性。

THEOREM 2 (MAIN RESULT). Let $\epsilon ,\delta  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ be accuracy and failure probability parameters and let $m = O\left( {\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) /{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ . There is an algorithm $\mathcal{S}$ that produces size-m sketches (Algorithm 3),along with an estimation procedure $\mathcal{F}$ (Algorithm 5),such that for any $\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ ,with probability at least $1 - \delta$ ,

定理2(主要结果). 设$\epsilon ,\delta  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$为精度参数与失败概率参数，$m = O\left( {\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) /{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$为维度参数。存在算法$\mathcal{S}$可生成尺寸为m的草图(算法3)，配合估计算法$\mathcal{F}$(算法5)，使得对于任意$\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$，至少有$1 - \delta$的概率满足：

$$
\left| {\mathcal{F}\left( {\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\mathcal{S}\left( \mathbf{b}\right) }\right) -\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle }\right|  \leq  \epsilon \max \left( {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}\parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel ,\parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel {\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\parallel }\right) 
$$

Above, $I = \{ i : \mathbf{a}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   \neq  0$ and $\mathbf{b}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   \neq  0\}$ is the intersection of $\mathbf{a}$ ’s and $\mathbf{b}$ ’s supports. ${\mathbf{a}}_{I}$ and ${\mathbf{b}}_{I}$ denote a and $\mathbf{b}$ restricted to indices in $I$ .

其中$I = \{ i : \mathbf{a}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   \neq  0$表示向量维度，$\mathbf{b}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   \neq  0\}$为$\mathbf{a}$与$\mathbf{b}$支撑集的交集。${\mathbf{a}}_{I}$和${\mathbf{b}}_{I}$分别代表向量a和$\mathbf{b}$在$I$索引上的限制。

We always have $\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix} \leq  \parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel$ and $\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix} \leq  \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel$ ,so we can bound $\max \left( {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}\parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel ,\parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel {\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\parallel }\right)  \leq  \parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel$ . That is,the guarantee of Theorem 2 matches that of Fact 1 in the worse-case, but can be significantly better. For example,consider $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ that have roughly the same number of non-zero entries,but only a $\gamma  < 1$ fraction of those entries are non-zero in both a and b. In this case, it is reasonable to expect that ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} \approx  \gamma \parallel \mathbf{a}{\parallel }^{2}$ and ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} \approx  \gamma \parallel \mathbf{b}{\parallel }^{2}$ since ${\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}$ and ${\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}$ contain just a $\gamma$ fraction of entries from the original vectors. Our course, the actually improvement is data dependent; for example, we might have that ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2}$ is significantly smaller than $\gamma \parallel \mathbf{a}{\parallel }^{2}$ ,or that it is not much smaller than $\parallel \mathbf{a}{\parallel }^{2}$ .

恒有$\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix} \leq  \parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel$和$\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix} \leq  \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel$成立，故可界定$\max \left( {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}\parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel ,\parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel {\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\parallel }\right)  \leq  \parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel$。这意味着定理2的保证在最坏情况下与事实1相当，但可能显著更优。例如考虑$\mathbf{a}$和$\mathbf{b}$具有相近非零项数量，但仅有$\gamma  < 1$比例的非零项在a与b中同时存在。此时可合理推测${\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} \approx  \gamma \parallel \mathbf{a}{\parallel }^{2}$且${\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} \approx  \gamma \parallel \mathbf{b}{\parallel }^{2}$，因为${\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}$和${\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}$仅含原向量$\gamma$比例的条目。实际改进程度取决于数据特性：可能${\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2}$远小于$\gamma \parallel \mathbf{a}{\parallel }^{2}$，亦或与$\parallel \mathbf{a}{\parallel }^{2}$相差无几。

Nevertheless,considering the "typical case" when a $\gamma$ fraction of non-zeros overlap, we might expect the bound from Theorem 2 to be better than Fact 1 by a factor of $\sqrt{\gamma }$ . So,to obtain the same error as a linear sketch,our method could set $m$ smaller by a factor of $\gamma$ . In many applications, $\gamma$ is very small. E.g.,in Section 5 we consider a document similarity problem where $\gamma  \leq  {.05}$ for ${95}\%$ of vector pairs sketched. This could equate to roughly a ${20x}$ improvement in sketch size required to achieve a specified level of error.

然而，考虑到非零元素重叠比例为$\gamma$的"典型情况"，我们预计定理2给出的界会比事实1优$\sqrt{\gamma }$倍。因此，为达到与线性草图相同的误差，我们的方法可将$m$设为比$\gamma$更小的值。在许多应用中，$\gamma$非常小。例如第5节讨论的文档相似性问题中，对于${95}\%$的向量对草图，$\gamma  \leq  {.05}$。这意味着在达到指定误差水平时，草图尺寸可能获得约${20x}$倍的改进。

Thanks to their strong theoretical guarantees, linear sketching algorithms have become the go-to approach for generic inner product estimation [19]. Our results show for the first time that an alternative method can provide stronger bounds. We hope that this paper will serve as a starting point for further investigation into hashing-based algorithms for inner product sketching.

凭借其坚实的理论保证，线性草图算法已成为通用内积估计的首选方案[19]。我们的研究首次证明存在替代方法能提供更强的界。希望本文能为基于哈希的内积草图算法研究开辟新路径。

### 1.2 Motivating Application: Dataset Search

### 1.2 应用背景：数据集搜索

Before presenting the technical details of our results and discussing related work, we detail one application that could benefit from our proposed sketches, and helps illustrate the importance of obtaining bounds for inner product estimation that are sensitive to the number of overlapping non-zero entries in a and b. Specifically, we consider the problem of dataset search which has received increasing attention in recent years $\left\lbrack  {{22},{34},{47},{48},{54} - {56}}\right\rbrack$ .

在阐述技术细节和讨论相关工作前，我们详述一个能受益于所提草图的应用场景，该场景有助于说明获取对向量a、b非零元素重叠数敏感的内积估计界的重要性。具体而言，我们探讨近年来日益受到关注的数据集搜索问题$\left\lbrack  {{22},{34},{47},{48},{54} - {56}}\right\rbrack$。

Suppose that a data scientist wants to understand the reasons for fluctuations in taxi ridership in New York City in 2022. The analyst only has a table containing two columns: a date column and the number of taxi rides taken on that day. In order to carry out the analysis, she needs to find other tables, either in her organization's data lake or in public repositories like NYC Open Data (which contain thousands of datasets [15]), that would bring in other relevant variables when joined with the original table. For example, the analyst might hope to find weather data, which can impact taxi ridership. Moreover, she would like to find relevant factors that she might not think of on her own, in an automatic way.

假设数据科学家想探究2022年纽约市出租车载客量波动的成因。分析师仅有一张包含两列的表格：日期列和当日出租车载客量。为开展分析，她需要在其机构数据湖或NYC Open Data等公共库（含数千数据集[15]）中查找能与原表关联并引入相关变量的其他表格。例如，分析师可能希望找到影响载客量的天气数据，更希望能自动发现其未曾考虑的相关因素。

To solve this problem, we would like methods to automatically discover tables that are both 1) joinable with the target table (i.e., also contain columns with dates from 2022) and 2) meaningfully related with the analyst's data. For example, a table containing precipitation data should be returned if taxi ridership is significantly higher or lower on days with high precipitation. To find such tables, brute force search is not infeasible - we typically cannot afford to join the analyst's table with all tables in the search set to look for good candidates. Instead, we need to efficiently estimate statistics between disparate tables without materializing their join [47].

为解决此问题，我们需要能自动发现满足两个条件的表格：1)可与目标表连接（即包含2022年日期列）；2)与分析数据存在有意义关联。例如，若强降水日出租车载客量显著变化，则应返回降水数据表。暴力搜索不可行——我们无法承受将分析表与搜索集中所有表格连接以寻找候选。相反，需在不物化连接的情况下高效估计异构表间统计量[47]。

Sketching has become the most popular approach for performing this sort of estimation between unjoined data tables [22, 47, 48, 54, 56]. Specifically, a small-space sketch is precomputed for all data tables in the search set. When the analyst issues a query to find relevant data, a sketch of her table is compared against these preexisting sketches using a fraction of the computational resources in comparison to explicitly materializing table joins [47].

草图技术已成为未连接数据表间此类估计的主流方法[22,47,48,54,56]。具体而言，预计算搜索集中所有数据表的小空间草图。当分析师发起相关数据查询时，其表格草图将与这些预存草图比对，消耗的计算资源远少于显式物化表连接[47]。

In search applications,we note that the vector length $n$ can be very large. To compare methods, Fact 1 and Theorem 2 suggest that any asymptotic differences in performance between our WMH method and linear sketching will depend on the overlap in non-zero entries between the vectors being sketched. In dataset search, this exactly corresponds to the $\mathcal{J}$ accard similarity of the key sets ${K}_{A}$ and ${K}_{B}$ . Our method will perform better when the Jaccard similarity is small. For example, in Figure 1, only 4 out of 14 unique keys are shared in both tables,so the similarity is $\approx  {.29}$ . In the scenario discussed above, we could imagine a much smaller ratio: for example, our data analyst might only have a table containing taxi data from 2022, but compare it to a weather data table with dates from 1960 through the present day. The Jaccard similarity would be $1/{63} \approx  {.016}$ . ## 2 相关工作

Inner Product Estimation for Binary Vectors. Beyond linear sketching methods for estimating the inner product between general real-valued vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ ,there has been a lot of prior work on the special case of binary vectors with $\{ 0,1\}$ entries. For such vectors, approximating the inner product amounts to approximating the size of the intersection of two sets. Concretely,any $\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n}$ can be associated with sets $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{B}$ that contain integers from $\{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$ . We define $\mathcal{A}$ to contain all $i$ for which $\mathbf{a}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   = 1$ ,and similarly $\mathcal{B}$ to contain all $i$ for which $\mathbf{b}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   = 1$ . Note that $\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle  = \left| {\mathcal{A} \cap  \mathcal{B}}\right|$ .

二进制向量内积估计。相较于针对一般实值向量$\mathbf{a}$与$\mathbf{b}$之间内积估计的线性素描方法，对于具有$\{ 0,1\}$元素的二进制向量这一特例已有大量前人研究。对此类向量而言，近似内积等同于近似两个集合的交集大小。具体而言，任何$\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n}$可关联到包含$\{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$范围内整数的集合$\mathcal{A}$和$\mathcal{B}$。我们定义$\mathcal{A}$包含所有满足$\mathbf{a}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   = 1$的$i$，同理$\mathcal{B}$包含所有满足$\mathbf{b}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   = 1$的$i$。注意$\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle  = \left| {\mathcal{A} \cap  \mathcal{B}}\right|$。

Applying Fact 1,we know that a linear sketch of size $m =$ $O\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ can estimate $\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$ up to additive error $\epsilon \parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel  = \epsilon \sqrt{\left| \mathcal{A}\right| \left| \mathcal{B}\right| }$ . However, a better bound can be obtained using non-linear sketching methods based on the classic MinHash sketch [8, 9, 27, 42], the $k$ -minimum value (KMV) sketch [6],or related techniques $\left\lbrack  {{37},{39}}\right\rbrack$ . With $m = O\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ space,such methods are achieve error $\epsilon \sqrt{\max \left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| ,\left| \mathcal{B}\right| }\right)  \cdot  \left| {\mathcal{A} \cap  \mathcal{B}}\right| }$ ,which is always smaller than $\epsilon \sqrt{\left| \mathcal{A}\right| \left| \mathcal{B}\right| }$ $\left\lbrack  {6,{44}}\right\rbrack$ . For binary vectors,this bound was proven optimal in [44].

应用事实1可知，尺寸为$m =$$O\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$的线性素描能以$\epsilon \parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel  = \epsilon \sqrt{\left| \mathcal{A}\right| \left| \mathcal{B}\right| }$加性误差估计$\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$。但基于经典MinHash素描[8,9,27,42]、$k$最小值(KMV)素描[6]或相关技术$\left\lbrack  {{37},{39}}\right\rbrack$的非线性素描方法可获得更优边界。在$m = O\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$存储空间下，此类方法能达到$\epsilon \sqrt{\max \left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| ,\left| \mathcal{B}\right| }\right)  \cdot  \left| {\mathcal{A} \cap  \mathcal{B}}\right| }$误差，该值恒小于$\epsilon \sqrt{\left| \mathcal{A}\right| \left| \mathcal{B}\right| }$$\left\lbrack  {6,{44}}\right\rbrack$。对于二进制向量，文献[44]证明此边界已达最优。

Our work was motivated by this pre-existing result for binary vectors. In fact, our Theorem 2, is a strict generalization of the bound to all real-valued vectors. When $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ are binary,we have that ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} = {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} = \left| {\mathcal{A} \cap  \mathcal{B}}\right|$ . So it is not hard to see that $\epsilon \sqrt{\max \left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| ,\left| \mathcal{B}\right| }\right)  \cdot  \left| {\mathcal{A} \cap  \mathcal{B}}\right| } = \epsilon  \cdot  \max \left( {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}\parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel ,\parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel {\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\parallel }\right)$ ,which is exactly our bound from Theorem 2. There has been less work on obtaining better results for estimating inner products of vectors with non-binary entries. One recent paper [33] proves refined bounds for the CountSketch method that depend on the ${\ell }_{1}$ norm of a and $\mathbf{b}$ (instead of the Euclidean norm). These bounds can be tighter than Fact 1 for some vectors,especially when the sketch size $m$ is large. However, the results are not directly comparable to ours.

我们的工作受到二元向量这一已有成果的启发。事实上，我们的定理2是对该界限向所有实值向量的严格推广。当$\mathbf{a}$和$\mathbf{b}$为二元向量时，可得${\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} = {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} = \left| {\mathcal{A} \cap  \mathcal{B}}\right|$。因此不难看出$\epsilon \sqrt{\max \left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| ,\left| \mathcal{B}\right| }\right)  \cdot  \left| {\mathcal{A} \cap  \mathcal{B}}\right| } = \epsilon  \cdot  \max \left( {\begin{Vmatrix}{\mathbf{a}}_{\mathcal{I}}\end{Vmatrix}\parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel ,\parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel {\mathbf{b}}_{\mathcal{I}}\parallel }\right)$，这正是我们定理2中的边界值。我们在表1中总结了所有现有内积草图方法与本文结果的对比。超越二元向量。针对非二元向量内积估计的改进成果较少。近期论文[33]证明了CountSketch方法的改进边界取决于a和$\mathbf{b}$的${\ell }_{1}$范数（而非欧几里得范数）。对于某些向量，特别是当草图尺寸$m$较大时，这些边界可能比事实1更紧致。然而其结果与我们的研究并不直接可比。

We take a different approach, moving beyond linear sketching entirely. Our main result is based on a class of sketches that we collectively refer to as "Weighted MinHash" methods [13, 49]. These methods include weighted versions of coordinated random sampling $\left\lbrack  {{17},{18}}\right\rbrack$ ,as well as the "Consistent Weighted Sampling" algorithm $\left\lbrack  {{25},{41}}\right\rbrack$ and its descendants,which are essentially equivalent,but computationally cheaper to apply $\left\lbrack  {{26},{28},{53}}\right\rbrack$ . As shown in Section 4, Weighted MinHash sketches allows us to handle vectors whose entries have highly varying magnitude (in contrast to binary vectors, where all non-zero entries have the same magnitude of 1).

我们采用截然不同的方法，完全跳出了线性草图的范畴。主要成果基于一类被统称为"加权最小哈希"的草图方法[13,49]。这类方法包括协调随机抽样的加权版本$\left\lbrack  {{17},{18}}\right\rbrack$，以及"一致性加权抽样"算法$\left\lbrack  {{25},{41}}\right\rbrack$与其衍生方法——它们在本质上等价，但计算成本更低$\left\lbrack  {{26},{28},{53}}\right\rbrack$。如第4节所示，加权最小哈希草图能处理元素幅值差异显著的向量（与所有非零元素均为1的二元向量形成对比）。

Weighted MinHash sketches have been used in a number of applications, including for approximating weighted Jaccard similarity [53], for near-duplicate detection with weighted features [41], for approximating the distance between two vectors [28], and for sketching image histograms [49]. In many of these applications, the weighted sketches empirically outperform unweighted sketches. Weighted MinHash sketches have also been used to compute general "sum aggregate" queries, for which the inner product is a special case [18]. However, we are not aware of strong worst-case error guarantees for the above applications, let alone for the problem of general inner product estimation. Consistent Weighted Sampling has also been used to approximate inner products in [35], albeit using a different estimator than in our work. However, non-asymptotic worst-case guarantees are not provided.

加权最小哈希草图已应用于诸多场景：近似加权杰卡德相似度[53]、加权特征近重复检测[41]、向量间距近似[28]以及图像直方图草图[49]。在这些应用中，加权草图实证表现普遍优于未加权版本。该方法还被用于计算广义"和聚合"查询（内积是其特例）[18]。然而我们尚未发现上述应用具有强最坏情况误差保证，更不用说广义内积估计问题。文献[35]虽使用不同估计量，但也采用一致性加权抽样来近似内积，但未提供非渐近的最坏情况保证。

Locality Sensitive Hashing. Finally, our problem of estimating inner products from sketches is closely related to cosine similarity and maximum inner product search (MIPS), where the goal is to retrieve vectors from a database with the highest cosine similarity (respectively, inner product) with a given query vector. One approach for solving these problems is locality sensitive hashing [24], and there are methods based on both MinHash and random projections, like SimHash [11]. It has been observed that MinHash often outperforms SimHash for binary data, which parallels what was previously known for binary inner product estimation [50].

局部敏感哈希。最后，基于草图估计内积的问题与余弦相似度及最大内积搜索(MIPS)密切相关——后者的目标是从数据库中检索出与查询向量具有最高余弦相似度（或内积）的向量。局部敏感哈希[24]是解决这类问题的方法之一，其中既包含基于最小哈希的方法，也包含类似SimHash[11]的随机投影方法。已有研究表明，对于二元数据，最小哈希通常优于SimHash，这与二元内积估计领域的既有认知[50]相呼应。

An unweighted method. Before introducing our Weighted Min-Hash sketching method, we review the unweighted MinHash algorithm and prove a inner product estimation bound that can be obtained from this method. The bound closely follows prior work on binary vectors $\left\lbrack  {6,{44}}\right\rbrack$ and only holds under strong assumptions on the sketched vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ - specifically that their entries are uniformly bounded in magnitude. Johnson-Lindenstrauss Projection (JL): equivalent to the AMS sketch $\left\lbrack  {1,4}\right\rbrack$ . Uses a random matrix $\Pi$ with scaled $\pm  1$ entries (Fact 1). CountSketch (CS): classic linear sketch introduced in [12], and corresponds to multiplication with a $\Pi$ that has sparse random entries. We follow the implementation in [33], using 5 repetitions of the sketch and taking the median to improve performance.

Johnson-Lindenstrauss投影（JL）：等同于AMS草图$\left\lbrack  {1,4}\right\rbrack$，使用具有缩放$\pm  1$项随机矩阵$\Pi$（见事实1）。CountSketch（CS）：文献[12]提出的经典线性草图，对应稀疏随机项矩阵$\Pi$的乘法运算。我们采用文献[33]的实现方式，通过5次草图重复取中值来提升性能。

MinHash Sampling (MH): method described in Algorithm 1; we use a single sketch without any median estimate.

MinHash采样（MH）：算法1描述的方法，使用单一草图且不进行中值估计。

$k$ -Minimum Values Sampling (KMV): sampling-based sketch closely related to MinHash, but it draws samples from the vector being sketched without replacement. It can also be used to estimate union size. We follow the implementations from [6] and [47].

$k$-最小值采样（KMV）：与MinHash密切相关的基于采样的草图方法，但采用无放回方式从被草图向量中抽取样本。该方法也可用于估计并集大小。我们遵循文献[6]和[47]的实现方案。

Weighted MinHash Sampling (WMH): our method described in Algorithm 3; we use a single sketch without any median estimate.

加权MinHash采样（WMH）：算法3描述的我们的方法，使用单一草图且不进行中值估计。

Storage Size. For linear sketches, we store the output of the matrix multiplication $\Pi$ a as 64-bit doubles. We also store ${W}_{\mathbf{a}}^{\text{val }}$ and ${H}_{\mathbf{a}}^{\text{val }}$ as 64-bit doubles. Since sampling-based sketches need to store hash values (which in our case are 32-bit ints), a sampling-based sketch with $m$ samples takes ${1.5x}$ as much space as a JL sketch with $m$ rows. In our experiments, we plot storage size which denotes the total number of bits in the sketch divided by 64, i.e., the total number of 64-bit doubles (or equivalent) used in the sketch. Standard quantization tricks could likely be used to reduce the size of numbers in all sketches (linear and sampling), but we leave the development of such methods to future work. As a starting point, we note that there has already been interesting work on quantized JL projections $\left\lbrack  {{29},{38}}\right\rbrack$ ,and the SimHash method for estimating cosine similarity can be viewed as a "1-bit" quantization of a JL sketch [11].

存储空间：对于线性草图，我们将矩阵乘法$\Pi$a的输出存储为64位双精度浮点数，${W}_{\mathbf{a}}^{\text{val }}$和${H}_{\mathbf{a}}^{\text{val }}$同样存储为64位双精度数。基于采样的草图需要存储哈希值（本文采用32位整数），因此具有$m$个样本的采样草图所需空间相当于具有$m$行的JL草图。实验中，我们绘制了存储空间指标——草图总比特数除以64（即草图使用的64位双精度数或等效存储单元总数）。标准量化技术可能适用于所有草图（线性和采样）的数值压缩，但这类方法的开发留待未来工作。值得关注的是，量化JL投影$\left\lbrack  {{29},{38}}\right\rbrack$已有相关研究，而用于估计余弦相似度的SimHash方法可视为JL草图的"1比特"量化版本[11]。

Estimation Error. For all plots, we report the absolute difference between $\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$ and the estimate,divided by $\parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel$ . This is the term appearing on the right-hand side of the accuracy guarantee for linear sketches Fact 1, so this scaling roughly ensures that errors are between 0 and 1 , making it easier to compare across different datasets. We always report average error over 10 independent trials. Choice of $L$ . Note that the choice of $L$ in Algorithm 3 does not impact the size of our final sketch, so in general, it should be set as large as possible. Our bounds from Lemma 3 that suggest $L$ should be set $\geq  {n}^{6}$ are likely loose (we did not attempt to optimize polynomial factors), but we did find that it is necessary to at least ensure that $L > n$ . Ideally it should be larger by a multiplicative factor 100 or 1000 . The reason for this is that, if a is dense and is normalized to have unit norm, as in Algorithm 1, most of its entries could have squared value $< 1/n$ (as the average value of a squared entry in a unit norm vector is always $1/n$ ). If we set $L < 1/n$ ,then any entries with value $< 1/n$ would get rounded to 0,which could negatively impact the accuracy of an inner product estimate.

估计误差。所有图表中，我们报告的是$\langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\rangle$与估计值之间的绝对差除以$\parallel \mathbf{a}\parallel \parallel \mathbf{b}\parallel$的结果。该数值对应于线性草图精度保证（事实1）右侧的项，因此这种缩放方式可使误差大致介于0到1之间，便于跨数据集比较。我们始终报告10次独立试验的平均误差。$L$的选择。注意算法3中$L$的选择不影响最终草图大小，通常应尽可能设大。引理3给出的边界建议<b2></b3>设置可能偏保守（未优化多项式因子），但必须确保至少满足$L > n$。理想情况应乘以100或1000倍系数。原因在于：若如算法1所述，稠密向量a经单位范数归一化后，其多数分量的平方值可能为$< 1/n$（单位范数向量中各分量平方均值恒为$1/n$）。若设$L < 1/n$，则所有值为$< 1/n$的分量将被舍入至0，可能影响内积估计精度。

Efficient Weighted Hashing. When $L$ is large,a naive implementation of Algorithm 3 would be prohibitively slow. The "extended" vector a has length $n \cdot  L$ and we must apply a hash function to every non-zero entry in that vector. Let $\mathcal{A} = \{ i : \mathbf{a}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   \neq  0\}$ as before,so $\left| \mathcal{A}\right|$ is equal to the number of non-zero values in a. If each hash computation is considered unit cost, this amounts to a runtime of $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| m \cdot  L}\right)$ ,which is too large,since $L$ is chosen larger than $n$ .

高效加权哈希。当$L$较大时，算法3的原始实现会极其缓慢。"扩展"向量a的长度为$n \cdot  L$，需对其每个非零分量应用哈希函数。设$\mathcal{A} = \{ i : \mathbf{a}\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   \neq  0\}$如前所述，则$\left| \mathcal{A}\right|$等于a中非零值数量。若每次哈希计算视为单位成本，总运行时间将达$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| m \cdot  L}\right)$，这显然过高，因$L$通常选择大于$n$。

Fortunately,it is possible to improve this cost to $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| m \cdot  \log L}\right)  =$ $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| m \cdot  \log n}\right)$ using techniques for speeding up weighted Min-Hash sketches. Such techniques have been heavily studied in recent years $\left\lbrack  {{26},{28},{49},{53}}\right\rbrack$ . The savings are significant,reducing the computation cost of sketching to nearly-linear in the size of the input for each of our $m$ samples. Among faster methods,we specifically employ the simple "active index" technique, which was first introduced in [25]. The rough idea is that, when hashing non-zero entries in a particular length $L$ block of $\overline{\mathbf{a}}$ ,there is no need to hash all non-zero indices in that block. We can skip over large sections of indices by observing that if $z$ is the minimum hash value generated so far, the next index where a lower hash value will be seen is a distributed as a geometric random variable with parameter $z$ . We can sample from the geometric distribution efficiently (e.g. using a built-in Python routine) and skip ahead to that index. It is possible to prove that the expected cost of this approach is just $O\left( {\log L}\right)$ per block. See the exposition in [41] for further details.

幸运的是，利用加速加权最小哈希草图的技术，可将成本优化至$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| m \cdot  \log L}\right)  =$$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{A}\right| m \cdot  \log n}\right)$。此类技术近年得到深入研究$\left\lbrack  {{26},{28},{49},{53}}\right\rbrack$。这种优化使草图计算成本显著降低至接近输入规模的线性复杂度（针对每个$m$样本）。在快速方法中，我们特别采用文献[25]提出的"活跃索引"技术：当对$\overline{\mathbf{a}}$的特定$L$长度块中非零项进行哈希时，无需处理该块所有非零索引。通过观察当前最小哈希值$z$，可证明下一个更小哈希值出现的索引服从参数为$z$的几何分布，从而高效跳过大量索引（例如使用Python内置几何分布采样）。该方法每块预期成本仅为$O\left( {\log L}\right)$，详见文献[41]论述。

In practice we cannot obtain a truly uniform random hash function from $\{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$ to the reals,so we must use an approximation. In our experiments, we employ a standard 2-wise independent hash function (linear function with random coefficients) that maps from $\{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$ to $\{ 1,\ldots ,p\}$ for a 31 -bit prime $p\left\lbrack  {10}\right\rbrack  .{}^{5}$ We then use as our hash value $h\left( i\right) /p$ ,which is a number between 0 and 1 . $$
