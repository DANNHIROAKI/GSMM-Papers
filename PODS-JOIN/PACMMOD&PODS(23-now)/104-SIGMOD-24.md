# Convolution and Cross-Correlation of Count Sketches Enables Fast Cardinality Estimation of Multi-Join Queries

计数草图的卷积与互相关实现多连接查询基数快速估计

With the increasing rate of data generated by critical systems, estimating functions on streaming data has become essential. This demand has driven numerous advancements in algorithms designed to efficiently query and analyze one or more data streams while operating under memory constraints. The primary challenge arises from the rapid influx of new items, requiring algorithms that enable efficient incremental processing of streams in order to keep up. A prominent algorithm in this domain is the AMS sketch. Originally developed to estimate the second frequency moment of a data stream, it can also estimate the cardinality of the equi-join between two relations. Since then, two important advancements are the Count sketch, a method which significantly improves upon the sketch update time, and secondly, an extension of the AMS sketch to accommodate multi-join queries. However, combining the strengths of these methods to maintain sketches for multi-join queries while ensuring fast update times is a non-trivial task, and has remained an open problem for decades as highlighted in the existing literature. In this work, we successfully address this problem by introducing a novel sketching method which has fast updates, even for sketches capable of accurately estimating the cardinality of complex multi-join queries. We prove that our estimator is unbiased and has the same error guarantees as the AMS-based method. Our experimental results confirm the significant improvement in update time complexity, resulting in orders of magnitude faster estimates, with equal or better estimation accuracy.

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

The analysis of streaming data has amassed considerable attention, driven by the increasing demand for real-time data processing, and the remarkable advancements in algorithms that enable efficiently querying and analyzing data streams under memory constraints. Streaming data refers to data that is received sequentially and is often too large to be stored in its entirety, hence requiring algorithms that can process the data on-the-fly [5, 24]. Efficiently providing answers to queries over streaming data is vital in numerous application environments, including recommendation systems [12, 13, 28], smart cities [6, 22], network traffic monitoring [18, 20], natural language processing [25], and This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial International 4.0 analysis of market data in financial systems $\left\lbrack  {9,{40},{44}}\right\rbrack$ . Our focus on the streaming data setting stems from its generality. Streaming algorithms are not only effective in streaming settings but also seamlessly extend their applicability to non-streaming scenarios. In this work, we present a novel approach to the problem of estimating a crucial collection of complex queries within the general streaming data framework depicted in Figure 1 and elaborated upon below.

实时数据处理需求的增长与内存受限环境下流数据查询分析算法的显著进步，共同推动流数据分析研究获得广泛关注。流数据指按序到达且规模过大难以完整存储的数据，需要支持即时处理的算法[5,24]。高效应答流数据查询对推荐系统[12,13,28]、智慧城市[6,22]、网络流量监控[18,20]、自然语言处理[25]及金融系统市场数据分析等众多应用场景至关重要。我们聚焦流数据环境源于其普适性——流算法不仅适用于流式场景，还能无缝扩展到非流式场景。本研究提出创新方法，针对图1所示通用流数据框架中的复杂查询集合估计问题提供解决方案。

The rise of data-intensive applications has created a need for data structures that can handle massive volumes of data efficiently. This context motivated the emergence of synopsis data structures [23], a family of data structures designed to represent large quantities of data with sublinear space complexity, which is imperative in the given context. Examples include random samples, histograms, wavelets and sketches [17], all of which are actively being researched as a means of analyzing and querying streaming data $\left\lbrack  {1,{15},{17},{32}}\right\rbrack$ . These algorithms operate by generating a compressed representation of the original data, which can then be utilized to estimate a specific property or a set thereof. For example, the popular Bloom Filter [7] is widely used for membership testing, while the Count-Min sketch [19] is commonly used for frequency estimation. Both of these methods are examples of sketches. Besides their supported queries, various factors differentiate sketching methods, including sketch size, sketch initialization time, update time, and inference time [14] (see Section 2 for details). These characteristics serve as catalysts for diverse research avenues and are crucial to consider when utilizing or developing a sketching method that is tailored to a specific use case.

数据密集型应用的兴起催生了对高效处理海量数据的数据结构需求。这一背景促使概要数据结构(synopsis data structures)[23]应运而生，该系列数据结构能以次线性空间复杂度表示大规模数据，在当前场景中至关重要。典型实例包括随机样本、直方图、小波变换和草图算法(sketches)[17]，这些技术作为流数据分析与查询手段正被广泛研究$\left\lbrack  {1,{15},{17},{32}}\right\rbrack$。这类算法通过生成原始数据的压缩表征来估算特定属性或属性集合，例如布隆过滤器(Bloom Filter)[7]广泛用于成员测试，Count-Min草图[19]常用于频率估计，二者皆属草图算法。除支持查询类型外，不同草图方法的差异还体现在草图大小、初始化时间、更新时间和推理时间等维度[14]（详见第2节）。这些特性既推动了多样化研究路径，也是选用或开发特定场景草图方法时需重点考量的核心要素。

Aside from basic statistical properties such as count, sum, and mean, much useful information from a data stream is derived from its frequency distribution, or histogram. This becomes particularly relevant when we need to compare or estimate functions across multiple data sets, such as the number of shared items. Frequency-based sketches are a class of sketching methods specifically designed for estimating functions of the frequency vector. Among these, the AMS (Alon-Matias-Szegedy) sketch [4], also known as Tug-of-War sketch, stands out as a prime example, renowned for its established reputation of being both simple and remarkably effective in a wide array of applications. The AMS sketch was initially introduced to estimate the second frequency moment of a data stream, but it was later demonstrated to also estimate the cardinality of any equi-join between two relations [3].

除计数、求和、均值等基础统计属性外，数据流中更有价值的信息往往来自其频率分布（即直方图）。当需要跨数据集比较或估算函数（如共有项数量）时，这点尤为重要。基于频率的草图算法是专门用于估算频率向量函数的类别，其中AMS(Alon-Matias-Szegedy)草图[4]（又称Tug-of-War草图）因其简洁性和广泛适用性成为典范。该草图最初用于估算数据流二阶频率矩，后经证明还可估算两关系间任意等值连接(equi-join)的基数[3]。

Interestingly, it turns out that many important functions on the frequency vector can be expressed as the cardinality of an equi-join. This equivalence is an important driver behind the development of sketches, often seen as an approximate query processing (AQP) technique [32]. One particularly relevant use case is estimating the join cardinality, which is crucial for query optimizers to efficiently assess the cost of candidate physical join plans. The challenge of determining an appropriate join order is a highly researched problem in the field of databases $\left\lbrack  {{11},{30}}\right\rbrack$ ,and the methods employed typically rely on cardinality estimates as the primary input [31].

值得注意的是，频率向量上的许多重要函数都可表示为等值连接的基数。这种等价关系是推动草图算法发展的重要动力，该技术常被视为近似查询处理(AQP)方法[32]。其中最具代表性的应用是连接基数估算，这对查询优化器评估物理连接计划成本至关重要。确定最佳连接顺序是数据库领域的热点难题$\left\lbrack  {{11},{30}}\right\rbrack$，现有方法通常以基数估算作为主要输入依据[31]。

Two significant breakthroughs emerged a few years after the introduction of the AMS sketch. First, Charikar et al. [10] proposed the Count sketch, which divides estimates into "buckets" instead of computing the mean of multiple independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) estimates. This approach makes the sketch more accurate for skewed data and dramatically speeds up its updates [42, 45]. Second, Dobra et al. [20] proposed a generalization of the AMS sketch that enables the cardinality estimation of multi-join queries, thus considerably expanding the algorithm's applicability.

AMS草图问世数年后迎来两项重大突破：其一，Charikar等[10]提出的Count草图将估算值分入"桶"(buckets)而非计算多个独立同分布(i.i.d.)估算值的均值，该方法显著提升偏态数据估算精度并大幅加速更新[42,45]；其二，Dobra等[20]提出的AMS草图泛化方案实现了多连接查询基数估算，极大拓展了算法适用范围。

Although both methods have gained popularity for their respective advantages, the existing literature has highlighted the task of integrating all these benefits into a unified approach as a challenging and unresolved problem $\left\lbrack  {{14},{29}}\right\rbrack$ . The specific challenge lies in effectively handling multi-join queries with fast updates. This challenge becomes even more significant when considering the prevalence of such multi-joins, as they constitute the majority of queries (see Section 4.1). The difficulty of combining the Count sketch with the AMS-based multi-join query estimation method arises from the use of binning, as we will discuss in Section 3, yet binning is essential for achieving the benefits of the Count sketch.

尽管两种方法各具优势广受青睐，但现有研究指出，将这些优势整合为统一方案仍是亟待解决的挑战$\left\lbrack  {{14},{29}}\right\rbrack$。核心难点在于如何高效处理需要快速更新的多连接查询——考虑到此类查询占总体的大多数（见4.1节），该挑战更具现实意义。如第3节所述，将Count草图与基于AMS的多连接查询估算结合存在固有困难，根源在于分桶操作(binning)虽对Count草图优势实现至关重要，却与后者存在本质冲突。

To address this, we propose a new sketch that combines insights from both Charikar et al. [10] and Dobra et al. [20]. The proposed method relies on the intuitive observation that the operation used to merge single-item AMS sketches to form sketches of tuples, the Hadamard product, is incongruous with the sparse nature of the Count sketch. In essence, when two Count sketches undergo the Hadamard product, the resulting sketch will likely lose information due to the sparsity of the Count sketches.

针对此问题，我们提出融合Charikar等[10]与Dobra等[20]研究思想的新草图方案。该方法基于一个直观发现：用于合并单项AMS草图以构建元组草图的哈达玛积(Hadamard product)运算，与Count草图的稀疏特性存在本质矛盾。当两个Count草图进行哈达玛积运算时，其稀疏性将导致结果草图信息丢失。

The core innovation of our approach lies in employing circular convolution instead of the Hadamard product for counting tuples in a data stream. We show that, unlike the Hadamard product, this operation ensures the preservation of information from the operands in the resulting Count sketch. This is complemented by incorporating circular cross-correlation in the estimation procedure. Our method not only exhibits superior estimation accuracy when applied to real data and queries, but also operates within the same memory constraints. Moreover, we have significantly improved the time complexity of the sketch update process, enabling estimates to be computed orders of magnitude faster. We prove that our estimator is unbiased and offers error guarantees equivalent to Dobra et al. [20]. Importantly, our method does not require prior knowledge of the data distribution. Our empirical findings support the practical applicability of the proposed method, underscoring its significant advancement in addressing the aforementioned open problem.

本方法的核心创新在于采用循环卷积而非Hadamard积来统计数据流中的元组数量。我们证明，与Hadamard积不同，该运算能确保操作数信息在结果计数草图(Count sketch)中的完整保留，并通过在估计过程中引入循环互相关运算进一步增强。该方法不仅在真实数据和查询场景下展现出更优的估计精度，且保持相同内存占用。此外，我们显著优化了草图更新的时间复杂度，使估计速度提升数个数量级。理论证明该估计量具有无偏性，其误差保证与Dobra等[20]的研究等效。值得注意的是，本方法无需预先获知数据分布特征。实验结果表明所提方法具备实际应用价值，为解决该开放性问题提供了重要突破。

### 2.1 Streaming data

### 2.1 流式数据

The focus of this paper is on streaming data analysis, a prominent application area for synopsis data structures [23], which involves real-time processing of data that arrives at a high frequency. Streaming data naturally arises in many big data applications, including network traffic monitoring [18, 20], recommendation systems [12, 13, 28], natural language processing [25], smart cities [6, 22], and analysis of market data in financial systems [9, 40, 44]. Algorithms for streaming data are designed to handle data that can only be observed once, in arbitrary order, as it continuously arrives [20]. Consequently, these algorithms must be highly efficient in processing each input, while utilizing limited memory resources, to keep up with the rapid influx of new data.

本文聚焦流式数据分析——概要数据结构[23]的重要应用领域，涉及对高频到达数据的实时处理。流式数据自然存在于诸多大数据应用场景，包括网络流量监测[18,20]、推荐系统[12,13,28]、自然语言处理[25]、智慧城市[6,22]以及金融系统市场数据分析[9,40,44]。流式数据算法专为处理只能单次观测、无序到达的连续数据而设计[20]，因此必须保证每次输入处理的高效性，在有限内存资源下应对持续涌入的新数据。

By presenting our method within the streaming data setting, we establish its applicability to a broad range of scenarios. This is because streaming algorithms are also applicable when multiple data accesses or a specific access order are allowed. Inversely, algorithms that require multiple data accesses or a specific access order, like many learning-based methods, clearly do not apply in a streaming data setting. Even when a streaming algorithm is not strictly necessary, optimizing for fewer data accesses remains advantageous because it minimizes potentially costly I/O operations.

通过在流数据场景中展示我们的方法，我们确立了其广泛适用性。这是因为流算法同样适用于允许多次数据访问或特定访问顺序的场景。反之，需要多次数据访问或特定顺序的算法（如许多基于学习的方法）显然不适用于流数据环境。即使流算法并非绝对必要，优化减少数据访问仍具优势，因其能最小化可能代价高昂的I/O操作。

We formulate the problem as follows, based on Cormode and Muthukrishnan [19]: consider a vector $f\left( t\right)  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ ,which is assumed too large to be stored explicitly and is therefore presented implicitly in an incremental fashion. Starting as a zero vector, $f\left( t\right)$ is updated by a stream of pairs $\left( {{i}_{t},{\Delta }_{t}}\right)$ which increments the ${i}_{t}$ -th element by ${\Delta }_{t}$ ,meaning that ${f}_{{i}_{t}}\left( t\right)  = {f}_{{i}_{t}}\left( {t - 1}\right)  + {\Delta }_{t}$ ,while the other dimensions remain unchanged. The items ${i}_{t}$ are members of the domain ${}^{1}\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   = \{ 0,1,\ldots ,n - 1\}$ ; ${\Delta }_{t} \in  \mathbb{R}$ are the changes in frequency and $f\left( t\right)$ is called the frequency vector. At any time $t$ ,a query may request the computation of a function on $f\left( t\right)$ . Specific streaming settings are further classified by their type of updates, as follows:

基于Cormode和Muthukrishnan[19]的研究，我们将问题表述如下：考虑一个向量$f\left( t\right)  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$，其规模过大无法显式存储，故以增量方式隐式呈现。该向量初始为零向量$f\left( t\right)$，通过流式数据对$\left( {{i}_{t},{\Delta }_{t}}\right)$进行更新，每次将第${i}_{t}$个元素增加${\Delta }_{t}$，即${f}_{{i}_{t}}\left( t\right)  = {f}_{{i}_{t}}\left( {t - 1}\right)  + {\Delta }_{t}$，其他维度保持不变。其中${i}_{t}$属于域${}^{1}\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   = \{ 0,1,\ldots ,n - 1\}$；${\Delta }_{t} \in  \mathbb{R}$表示频率变化，$f\left( t\right)$称为频率向量。任意时刻$t$都可能收到对$f\left( t\right)$执行函数计算的查询请求。具体流处理场景根据更新类型进一步分类如下：

- cash-register: ${\Delta }_{t} > 0$ on every update;

- 收银机模型：每次更新时${\Delta }_{t} > 0$均为正值；

- strict turnstile: for some updates ${\Delta }_{t}$ can be negative,but ${f}_{i}\left( t\right)  \geq  0$ for all $i$ and $t$ ;

- 严格转门模型：允许部分更新中${\Delta }_{t}$为负值，但要求所有$i$和$t$时刻${f}_{i}\left( t\right)  \geq  0$保持非负；

- general turnstile: both updates and entries of the vector $f\left( t\right)$ can assume negative values at any time $t > 0$ .

- 通用转门模型：向量$f\left( t\right)$的更新项和元素值在任意时刻$t > 0$均可为负。

The algorithms we discuss utilize synopsis data structures to efficiently handle data streams, eliminating the need to explicitly store and compute over $f\left( t\right)$ to answer a set of supported queries. In the following section, we will introduce a group of sketching techniques known as linear sketches. This family of methods, which includes the approach proposed in this paper, is designed to support the most general streaming setting, i.e., the general turnstile.

我们讨论的算法采用概要数据结构高效处理数据流，无需显式存储和计算$f\left( t\right)$即可回答支持的查询集。下文将介绍一组称为线性素描的技术，包括本文提出的方法在内，这类技术专为支持最通用的流处理场景（即通用转门模型）而设计。

### 2.2 Linear sketching

### 2.2 线性素描

Sketching techniques are a popular set of methods for dealing with streaming data and approximate query processing [14]. Both the baselines and the method proposed in this paper are linear sketches, meaning that the summaries they generate can be represented as a linear transformation of the input. In contrast, the Bloom filter [7] serves as a classic example of a non-linear sketch.

素描技术是处理流数据和近似查询的常用方法[14]。基线方法和本文提出的方法均属线性素描，其生成的摘要可表示为输入数据的线性变换。相比之下，布隆过滤器[7]是非线性素描的典型代表。

Formally,for a given vector $\mathbf{x} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ ,we define a linear sketch as a vector obtained by $\Pi \mathbf{x}$ ,where $\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$ is some random matrix,and $m \ll  n$ . The linearity of the transformation offers notable advantages [14]: it allows for processing items in any order and combining different sketches through addition. This enables efficient handling of data and supports map-reduce style processing of large data streams. In every sketching method, the random matrix is thoughtfully designed to enable the estimation of one or multiple functions over $\mathbf{x}$ ,utilizing only its "summary" captured by ${\Pi x}$ ,thereby eliminating the necessity for accessing $x$ itself. When sketching techniques are used in a streaming scenario they are often referred to as frequency-based sketches, where the input vector $x$ is the frequency vector $f\left( t\right)$ defined in Section 2.1. Hereafter,we will omit the time argument from the frequency vector for brevity.

形式上，对于给定向量$\mathbf{x} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$，我们将其线性素描定义为通过$\Pi \mathbf{x}$运算得到的向量，其中$\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$是某个随机矩阵，且$m \ll  n$。该线性变换具有显著优势[14]：允许以任意顺序处理数据项，并支持通过加法合并不同素描。这使得数据高效处理成为可能，并支持大数据流的Map-Reduce式处理。在所有素描方法中，随机矩阵都经过精心设计，使得仅利用${\Pi x}$捕获的"摘要"就能估算$\mathbf{x}$上的一个或多个函数，从而无需访问$x$本身。当素描技术应用于流式计算场景时，通常称为基于频率的素描，此时输入向量$x$即为第2.1节定义的频率向量$f\left( t\right)$。为简洁起见，下文将省略频率向量中的时间参数。

At this point,one naturally wonders: how can we transform the vector $f$ of size $n$ ,which is already considered too large,using a matrix $\Pi$ that is even larger with size ${mn}$ ? Streaming algorithms cleverly represent the matrix $\Pi$ succinctly using hash functions,enabling them to generate just the column of $\Pi$ that is needed to add a given item. Further details on this process will be provided later. Furthermore,it is crucial to ensure that the sketch is efficiently updated as $f$ changes,i.e,as new items stream in. This can be achieved by making $\Pi$ sparse,as we will explore shortly. The effectiveness and versatility of a sketching method primarily relies on the following key properties [14]:

此时人们自然会产生疑问：如何用尺寸更大（${mn}$）的矩阵$\Pi$来处理已被认为过大的$n$维向量$f$？流式算法巧妙地通过哈希函数简洁表示矩阵$\Pi$，使其仅生成添加特定数据项所需的列。该过程的更多细节将在后文阐述。此外，必须确保素描能随$f$变化（即新数据项流入时）高效更新，这可通过使$\Pi$稀疏化实现（后文将探讨）。素描方法的有效性和多功能性主要依赖以下关键特性[14]：

- Sketch size: The total number of counters and random seeds required by the sketch, determined by the parameter $m$ .

- 素描尺寸：由参数$m$决定的计数器总数与随机种子数量

- Initialization time: The time it takes to initialize the sketches. Typically, this involves simply setting a block of memory to zeros, and sampling the random seeds for the hash functions.

- 初始化时间：包括将内存块置零和为哈希函数采样随机种子所需时间

- Update time: In streaming settings, algorithms must keep pace with the high influx of items. The update time determines the highest item throughput rate that can be sustained.

- 更新时效：流式场景中算法必须匹配高速数据流入率，更新时效决定可维持的最高项吞吐率

- Inference time: The time it takes to compute an estimate from the generated sketches.

- 推理时效：从生成素描中计算估计值所需时间

- Accuracy: It is crucial to understand the accuracy of an estimator for a given memory budget (limiting the sketch size) and throughput requirement (constraining the update time).

- 准确度：在给定内存预算（限制素描尺寸）和吞吐要求（约束更新时效）下，理解估算器的准确度至关重要

- Supported queries: Each sketch is designed to enable estimation of a specific set of functions on the input vector. Typically, a query-specific procedure needs to be performed on the sketch to approximate the value of a particular function.

- 支持查询：每种素描设计用于支持输入向量特定函数集的估算，通常需对素描执行查询专用流程来近似特定函数值

In the following, we will describe some important sketching methods from the existing literature that have particularly space- and time-efficient ways of representing and computing ${\Pi f}$ .

下文将介绍现有文献中一些重要的素描方法，它们以特别高效的空间和时间方式表示和计算${\Pi f}$

### 2.3 AMS sketch

### 2.3 AMS素描

The AMS sketch, also referred to as the Tug-of-War or AGMS sketch, is a pioneering technique for frequency-based sketching that was first introduced by Alon, Matias, and Szegedy (AMS) [4]. The method was originally proposed as a way to estimate the second frequency moment ${F}_{2}$ of a data stream,where ${F}_{2} = \parallel f{\parallel }_{2}^{2} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0}}^{{n - 1}}{f}_{i}^{2}$ and $f$ is the frequency vector of the stream as defined in Section 2.1. The AMS sketch is represented by a vector $\mathbf{c}$ ,containing $m = O\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ counters ${\mathbf{c}}_{j}$ , for $j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ ,where $0 < \epsilon  < 1$ is the relative error bound. The counters are i.i.d. random variables obtained by ${\mathrm{c}}_{j} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0}}^{{n - 1}}{f}_{i}{s}_{j}\left( i\right)$ ,where each ${s}_{j} : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \{  - 1, + 1\}$ is drawn from a family of 4-wise independent hash functions (see Definition 2.1). These hash functions are used to compute the random projection ${\Pi f}$ without representing $\Pi$ explicitly,since ${\Pi }_{j,i} = {s}_{j}\left( i\right)$ . To establish a confidence level $\delta$ ,one can take the median of $O\left( {\log 1/\delta }\right)$ independent estimates. The overall method then requires only $O\left( {\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right) \log 1/\delta }\right)$ counters. Taking the median of i.i.d. estimates,sometimes called the "median trick", is universal among sketching methods because it is an effective way to rapidly improve the confidence level of an estimate by the Chernoff Bound [4]. We will, therefore, revisit this concept in the subsequent discussions of other methods.

AMS草图（又称Tug-of-War或AGMS草图）是由Alon、Matias和Szegedy（AMS）[4]首次提出的一种开创性的基于频率的草图技术。该方法最初旨在估计数据流中第二频率矩${F}_{2}$，其中${F}_{2} = \parallel f{\parallel }_{2}^{2} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0}}^{{n - 1}}{f}_{i}^{2}$和$f$如第2.1节所定义为流的频率向量。AMS草图由一个包含$m = O\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$个计数器${\mathbf{c}}_{j}$的向量$\mathbf{c}$表示，其中$j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$且$0 < \epsilon  < 1$为相对误差界。这些计数器是通过${\mathrm{c}}_{j} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0}}^{{n - 1}}{f}_{i}{s}_{j}\left( i\right)$获得的独立同分布随机变量，其中每个${s}_{j} : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \{  - 1, + 1\}$来自一个4-wise独立哈希函数族（见定义2.1）。这些哈希函数用于计算随机投影${\Pi f}$而无需显式表示$\Pi$，因为${\Pi }_{j,i} = {s}_{j}\left( i\right)$。为建立置信水平$\delta$，可取$O\left( {\log 1/\delta }\right)$个独立估计的中位数。该方法整体仅需$O\left( {\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right) \log 1/\delta }\right)$个计数器。取独立同分布估计的中位数（有时称为“中位数技巧”）是草图方法中的通用策略，因其能通过切尔诺夫界[4]快速提升估计的置信水平。因此我们将在后续讨论其他方法时重提此概念。

Definition 2.1 ( $k$ -WISE INDEPENDENCE [37,48]). A family of hash functions $H = \{ h : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow$ $\left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  \}$ is said to be $k$ -wise independent if for any $k$ distinct items ${x}_{0},\ldots ,{x}_{k - 1}$ the hashed values $h\left( {x}_{0}\right) ,\ldots ,h\left( {x}_{k - 1}\right)$ are independent and uniformly distributed in $\left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ .

定义2.1（$k$-WISE独立性[37,48]）。若哈希函数族$H = \{ h : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow$$\left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  \}$对任意$k$个不同项${x}_{0},\ldots ,{x}_{k - 1}$的哈希值$h\left( {x}_{0}\right) ,\ldots ,h\left( {x}_{k - 1}\right)$在$\left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$中独立且均匀分布，则称该族具有$k$-wise独立性。

In their original work,Alon et al. [4] showed that $\frac{1}{m}\langle {\Pi f},{\Pi f}\rangle$ is an unbiased estimator of ${F}_{2}$ . In fact,it can be demonstrated more generally that for any two vectors $\mathbf{f}$ and $\mathbf{g}$ ,the normalized inner product of their AMS sketches $\frac{1}{m}\langle {\Pi f},{\Pi g}\rangle$ is an unbiased estimator for their inner product $\langle \mathbf{f},\mathbf{g}\rangle \left\lbrack  3\right\rbrack$ . Notably,when $\mathbf{f}$ and $\mathbf{g}$ correspond to the frequency vectors of a given attribute of two database relations, this estimated value corresponds to the equi-join size of these relations over that attribute. Theorem 2.1 formally states the expectation, and bounds the variance of the AMS sketch.

Alon等人在其原创研究[4]中证明$\frac{1}{m}\langle {\Pi f},{\Pi f}\rangle$是${F}_{2}$的无偏估计量。更广义而言，对于任意两个向量$\mathbf{f}$和$\mathbf{g}$，其AMS草图归一化内积$\frac{1}{m}\langle {\Pi f},{\Pi g}\rangle$可作为它们内积$\langle \mathbf{f},\mathbf{g}\rangle \left\lbrack  3\right\rbrack$的无偏估计。值得注意的是，当$\mathbf{f}$和$\mathbf{g}$对应两个数据库关系某属性的频率向量时，该估计值即表示这些关系在该属性上的等值连接规模。定理2.1正式阐述了AMS草图的期望值并对方差进行了界定。

THEOREM 2.1 (AMS SKETCH). For any vectors $f,g \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ and a random matrix $\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$ constructed by 4-wise independent hash functions ${s}_{j} : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \{  - 1, + 1\}$ for $j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ and ${\Pi }_{j,i} = {s}_{j}\left( i\right)$ ,we have:

定理2.1（AMS草图）。对于任意向量$f,g \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$及由四向独立哈希函数${s}_{j} : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \{  - 1, + 1\}$构建的随机矩阵$\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$（其中$j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$和${\Pi }_{j,i} = {s}_{j}\left( i\right)$），我们有：

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  \frac{\langle {\Pi f},{\Pi g}\rangle }{m}\right\rbrack   = \langle f,g\rangle ,\;\text{and}\;\operatorname{Var}\left( \frac{\langle {\Pi f},{\Pi g}\rangle }{m}\right)  \leq  \frac{2}{m}\parallel f{\parallel }_{2}^{2}\parallel g{\parallel }_{2}^{2}
$$

Proof. See Lemma 4.4 of Alon et al. [3].

证明。参见Alon等人[3]的引理4.4。

### 2.4 Count sketch

### 2.4 计数草图

In order to ensure fast sketch updates,i.e.,sublinear with respect to its size $m$ ,it is desirable for sketching methods to use a sparse linear transformation $\Pi$ when processing input vectors. However, note that the AMS sketch requires changes in all $m$ counters for each update. Consequently,the accuracy of the estimator is constrained by the need to maintain a throughput that corresponds to the rate of incoming items as well as the available memory budget.

为确保草图快速更新（即相对于其尺寸$m$呈次线性），草图方法在处理输入向量时需采用稀疏线性变换$\Pi$。但需注意，AMS草图每次更新需改动所有$m$个计数器。因此，估计器的精度受限于维持与输入项速率匹配的吞吐量及可用内存预算。

The Count sketch is another linear sketching method that emerged after AMS and overcomes this limitation by allowing the update time to be independent of the sketch size. It achieves this by employing a technique called the "hashing trick," [14, 49] which ensures that only one counter per estimate is modified during each update. As a result, the Count sketch improves the update time complexity from $O\left( {\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right) \log 1/\delta }\right)$ to just $O\left( {\log 1/\delta }\right)$ ,while maintaining not only the same error bounds, but also the same space and inference time complexities as the AMS sketch. The AMS sketch and Count sketch update procedures are compared in Figure 2. Although the Count sketch was originally introduced for the heavy hitters problem [10], the same hashing trick can be applied to speed up the AMS sketch, which is commonly known as the Fast-AMS sketch [16].

计数草图是继AMS之后出现的另一种线性草图方法，通过使更新时间与草图尺寸无关来突破此限制。其采用称为"哈希技巧"[14,49]的技术，确保每次更新仅修改每个估计的一个计数器。因此，计数草图将更新时间复杂度从$O\left( {\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right) \log 1/\delta }\right)$降至$O\left( {\log 1/\delta }\right)$，同时不仅保持相同误差界，还维持与AMS草图相同的空间及推断时间复杂度。图2对比了AMS草图与计数草图的更新流程。虽然计数草图最初是为频繁项问题[10]提出，但相同哈希技巧可加速AMS草图，即广为人知的快速AMS草图[16]。

The value of each counter in the Count sketch is given by ${\mathrm{c}}_{j} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0 : h\left( i\right)  = j}}^{{n - 1}}{f}_{i}s\left( i\right)$ ,where $h : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow$ $\left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ is a random bin function drawn from a family of 2-wise independent hash functions,and $s : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \{  - 1, + 1\}$ is again a random sign function drawn from a family of 4 -wise independent hash functions. The corresponding random matrix $\Pi$ is specified by ${\Pi }_{j,i} = s\left( i\right) \mathbf{1}\left( {h\left( i\right)  = j}\right)$ . Notice that $\Pi$ has only one non-zero value per column,making it highly sparse. Also,the Count sketch requires only one sign and bin hash function per estimate, regardless of the required precision, resulting in a further reduction in memory usage. An estimate is obtained by taking the inner product between sketches. Theorem 2.2 formally states the expectation and bounds the variance of the Count sketch.

计数草图中每个计数器的值由${\mathrm{c}}_{j} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0 : h\left( i\right)  = j}}^{{n - 1}}{f}_{i}s\left( i\right)$给出，其中$h : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow$$\left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$是从二向独立哈希函数族抽取的随机分桶函数，$s : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \{  - 1, + 1\}$是从四向独立哈希函数族抽取的随机符号函数。对应随机矩阵$\Pi$由${\Pi }_{j,i} = s\left( i\right) \mathbf{1}\left( {h\left( i\right)  = j}\right)$定义。注意$\Pi$每列仅含一个非零值，具有高度稀疏性。此外，计数草图每个估计仅需一个符号函数和分桶函数，与所需精度无关，从而进一步降低内存使用。通过草图间内积运算获得估计值。定理2.2正式给出计数草图的期望及方差边界。

THEOREM 2.2 (COUNT SKETCH). For any vectors $f,g \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ and a random matrix $\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$ constructed by 4-wise independent hash function $s : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \{  - 1, + 1\}$ and 2-wise independent hash function $h : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ with ${\Pi }_{j,i} = s\left( i\right) \mathbf{1}\left( {h\left( i\right)  = j}\right)$ ,we have:

定理2.2（计数草图）。对于任意向量$f,g \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$及由四向独立哈希函数$s : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \{  - 1, + 1\}$和二向独立哈希函数$h : \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$构建的随机矩阵$\Pi  \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{m \times  n}$（其中${\Pi }_{j,i} = s\left( i\right) \mathbf{1}\left( {h\left( i\right)  = j}\right)$），我们有：

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {\langle \Pi \mathbf{f},\Pi \mathbf{g}\rangle }\right\rbrack   = \langle \mathbf{f},\mathbf{g}\rangle ,\;\text{ and }\;\operatorname{Var}\left( {\langle \Pi \mathbf{f},\Pi \mathbf{g}\rangle }\right)  \leq  \frac{2}{m}\parallel \mathbf{f}{\parallel }_{2}^{2}\parallel \mathbf{g}{\parallel }_{2}^{2}
$$

Proof. See Appendix 22 of Weinberger et al. [49] and Lemma 4.4 of Alon et al. [3].

证明。参见Weinberger等人[49]的附录22及Alon等人[3]的引理4.4。

The Count sketch has also been shown to outperform the AMS sketch in estimation precision for skewed data distributions [42]. This is because the Count sketch is able to separate out the few high frequency components with high probability. This is an important trait, as it has been widely acknowledged in the literature that the majority of real-world data distributions exhibit a skewed nature $\left\lbrack  {{20},{31},{34},{41},{51}}\right\rbrack$ . We further discuss this topic in Section 4.1.

实验证明，在偏态数据分布场景下，Count sketch的估计精度优于AMS sketch[42]。这得益于Count sketch能以高概率分离出少数高频成分。该特性至关重要，因为学术界普遍认同现实世界的数据分布大多呈现偏态特征$\left\lbrack  {{20},{31},{34},{41},{51}}\right\rbrack$。我们将在4.1节进一步探讨此话题。

### 2.5 Extensions to the Count sketch

### 2.5 Count sketch的扩展形式

The Count sketch was originally introduced for single-dimensional data which is represented by the frequency vector $f$ . More recently,however,several extensions to the Count sketch have been proposed which enable its usage for higher-dimensional data represented by a frequency tensor $\mathcal{F}$ instead [33]. The most notable extensions are the Tensor sketch [39] and the Higher-Order Count (HOC) sketch [43]. Both methods set out to reduce the computational complexity of machine learning applications. The Tensor sketch was used to approximate the polynomial kernel, but finds its origin in estimating matrix multiplication [37]. The HOC sketch was introduced to compress the training data or neural network parameters, in order to speed up training and inference processes.

Count sketch最初是为单维数据（由频率向量$f$表示）设计的。但近年提出的若干扩展方案使其能处理更高维的张量数据（由频率张量$\mathcal{F}$表示）[33]。最显著的扩展是Tensor sketch[39]和高阶计数（HOC）sketch[43]。这两种方法都致力于降低机器学习应用的计算复杂度：Tensor sketch用于逼近多项式核函数，但其原理源于矩阵乘法估计[37]；而HOC sketch通过压缩训练数据或神经网络参数来加速训练和推理过程。

For each incoming item of the stream, both methods start by encoding all axes separately using independent instances of the Count sketch. They differ in the way these individually sketched axes are combined: the Tensor sketch employs circular convolution (see Definition 2.2), generating a sketch vector, whereas the HOC sketch utilizes the tensor product to produce a sketch tensor of the same order but with reduced dimensions compared to $\mathcal{F}$ . The use of the tensor product ensures that axis information about the sketched data is preserved, at the cost of an exponential increase in sketch size with the order of the tensor. The circular convolution, in contrast, preserves the dimensionality of the sketch vector for any tensor order, i.e., it maps tensors to vectors.

对于数据流的每个输入项，两种方法都先使用独立的Count sketch实例对各维度分别编码。其差异在于维度组合方式：Tensor sketch采用循环卷积（见定义2.2）生成草图向量，而HOC sketch通过张量积生成维度降低但阶数不变的草图张量$\mathcal{F}$。张量积虽能保留维度信息，但会导致草图尺寸随张量阶数指数增长；循环卷积则始终保持向量维度，即将任意阶张量映射为向量。

### 2.6 Multi-join with AMS sketches

### 2.6 基于AMS素描的多连接查询

Another significant advancement in linear sketching techniques emerged around the same period as the Count sketch. Dobra et al. [20] proposed a generalization of the AMS sketch that enables the estimation of complex multi-join aggregate queries, such as count and sum queries. These estimates are useful for big data analytics and query optimization [31]. The proposed method addresses the scenario where a query $Q\left( {{R}_{0},{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{r - 1}}\right)$ involves multiple relations ${R}_{k}$ for $k \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ . An example is illustrated in Figure 3, which provides an intuitive visualization of this type of complex query as a disconnected, undirected graph. In this abstraction, each vertex corresponds to an attribute, edges represent the joins between them, and attributes are grouped to form relations.

线性素描技术的另一重大进展与Count素描同期涌现。Dobra等人[20]提出AMS素描的泛化形式，可估算复杂多连接聚合查询（如计数与求和查询）。这些估算结果对大数据分析与查询优化具有实用价值[31]。该方法针对查询$Q\left( {{R}_{0},{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{r - 1}}\right)$涉及$k \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$个关系${R}_{k}$的场景，图3通过非连通无向图直观展示了此类复杂查询的抽象模型：顶点对应属性，边表示连接关系，属性分组构成关系。

The technique generates sketches for each relation by iterating over all the tuples $i$ in each relation once. This iterative traversal of the tuples aligns with the streaming data scenario described in Section 2.1, enabling the sketches to be created on-the-fly as the relations are updated. $$
{\mathrm{c}}_{k,j} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  {I}_{k}}}{\mathcal{F}}_{k}\left( i\right) \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{u \in  \Omega \left( {R}_{k}\right) }}\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{v \in  \Gamma \left( u\right) }}{s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}\right)  \tag{1}

where we use the following notation: $i$ represents a tuple that belongs to the domain ${I}_{k}$ of relation ${R}_{k};{I}_{k}$ is the cross product of item domains $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \times  \cdots  \times  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ for each joined attribute of relation ${R}_{k}$ ; ${\mathcal{F}}_{k}\left( i\right)$ gives the frequency of tuple $i$ in relation ${R}_{k};{i}_{u}$ denotes the value in tuple $i$ for attribute $u;u$ is an attribute from the set of joined attributes of ${R}_{k}$ in the query,denoted as $\Omega \left( {R}_{k}\right)$ (for example, $\Omega \left( {R}_{1}\right)  = \{ 1,2\}$ in Figure 3); $v$ is an attribute from the set of attributes joined with $u$ ,denoted as $\Gamma \left( u\right)$ (for example, $\Gamma \left( 1\right)  = \{ 0,3\}$ in Figure 3). ### 2.7 Other related work

### 2.7 其他相关工作

In this section, we discuss other recent work in cardinality estimation. The Pessimistic Estimator [8] is an interesting sketching technique which provides an upper bound of the cardinality. They show that it improves upon the cardinality estimator within PostgreSQL. However, the practical use of the method is limited due to its lengthy estimation time [26], at times exceeding the query execution time, as also mentioned by the authors.

本节讨论基数估计领域的最新研究。悲观估计器[8]是一种能提供基数上界的创新草图技术，其研究表明该技术优于PostgreSQL内置的基数估计器。但如作者所述，由于估计耗时较长[26]（有时甚至超过查询执行时间），该方法实际应用受限。

Since the inception of sketching, a number of techniques have been proposed that complement the aforementioned sketches. Among these techniques, the Augmented Sketch [41] and the JoinS-ketch [47] aim to improve the accuracy of sketches for skewed data by separating the high- from the low-frequency items in the sketch. The counters of the high-frequency items are explicitly represented in an additional data structure, thereby preventing them from causing high estimation error due to hash collisions. Another notable technique is the Pyramid sketch [51], which employs a specialized data structure that dynamically adjusts the number of allocated bits for each counter, preventing overflows in the case of high-frequency items. It is important to note that these techniques are proposed as complementary tools, compatible with a variety of sketching methods, including the one proposed in this work.

自素描技术诞生以来，已有多种方法被提出以补充上述素描技术。其中，增强素描[41]和JoinS-ketch[47]通过分离素描中的高频与低频项，旨在提升偏态数据下的估算精度。高频项的计数器被显式存储在独立数据结构中，从而避免因哈希冲突导致的高估误差。另一项重要技术是金字塔素描[51]，其采用动态调整计数器位数的专用数据结构，有效防止高频项引发的溢出问题。需特别说明的是，这些技术均作为兼容性补充工具提出，可与包括本研究方案在内的多种素描方法协同使用。

Recently, there has been a parallel effort aimed at harnessing the power of machine learning for cardinality estimation. Among the various approaches, the most promising ones are data-driven methods that build query-independent models to estimate the joint probability of tuples [26]. Notable examples of such techniques include DeepDB [27], BayesCard [50], NeuroCard [52], and FLAT [53]. While machine learning-based cardinality estimation techniques have been receiving increasing attention, they still face important limitations: these methods are presently viable only in scenarios where supervised training is feasible, their accuracy has not consistently lived up to expectations [36], and they prove impractical in situations with frequent data updates, such as streaming settings, due to their high cost of model updates [26]. In Section 4.4, we demonstrate that our proposed method not only avoids these performance limitations but also achieves significantly higher accuracy compared to the machine learning techniques.

近期，学界正并行探索如何利用机器学习进行基数估计。在众多方法中，最具前景的是通过构建查询无关模型来估计元组联合概率的数据驱动方法[26]，其典型代表包括DeepDB[27]、BayesCard[50]、NeuroCard[52]和FLAT[53]。尽管机器学习基数估计技术日益受到关注，仍存在显著局限：当前仅适用于可监督训练场景，其准确性尚未稳定达到预期水平[36]，且在数据频繁更新的流式场景中，由于模型更新成本过高而难以实用化[26]。第4.4节将证明，本方法不仅规避了这些性能限制，相较机器学习技术还能实现显著更高的估计精度。

## 3 METHOD

## 3 方法

In this section, we present our method to solve the longstanding challenge of integrating the key advantages of the Count sketch, such as its efficient update mechanism and superior accuracy when handling skewed data, into a method that effectively estimates the cardinality of multi-join queries. Devising such a method is recognized as a challenging task, as underscored not only by the author of the Count-Min sketch [14] but also by other recent work in the field [29]. This acknowledgment highlights the importance of our contribution. The importance of solving this problem is further underscored when considering the prevalence of multi-join queries. In fact, an analysis of two sets of widely recognized benchmarking queries, as discussed in Section 4.1, reveals that multi-joins constitute approximately 97% of all their queries [26, 31].

本节提出一种创新方法，旨在将Count sketch的高效更新机制和偏态数据处理精度等核心优势，融入多连接查询基数估计这一长期挑战。如Count-Min sketch作者[14]及近期研究[29]所述，该问题公认具有较高难度，这凸显了本研究的贡献价值。多连接查询在实际中的普遍性进一步强化了该问题的重要性——第4.1节对两类基准查询集的分析表明，多连接查询占比高达97%[26,31]。

### 3.1 Key insight for preserving information

### 3.1 信息保留的核心思路

We start with an intuitive discussion on the main insight behind the proposed method, using the example illustrated in Figure 4. The main challenge of combining the Count sketch with the method proposed by Dobra et al. [20] lies in the inability to effectively merge Count sketches using the Hadamard product. Dobra et al. [20] create the sketch for a tuple as the Hadamard product of the AMS sketches for each value in the tuple. As discussed earlier, the advantages of the Count sketch stem from its sparsity. However, as illustrated in the left part of the figure, it is precisely this characteristic that results in a loss of information, with high probability, when combining sketches with the Hadamard product. Essentially, due to the sparsity, the non-zero entry in each sketch is highly likely to appear at a different position, causing the result of the element-wise multiplication to yield a zero vector, devoid of information.

我们通过图4示例直观阐述方法的核心思想。将Count sketch与Dobra等[20]方法结合的主要障碍在于无法通过Hadamard积有效合并素描。Dobra等[20]将元组的素描构造为各属性值AMS素描的Hadamard积。如前所述，Count sketch的优势源于其稀疏性，但如图所示（左半部分），这种特性恰恰导致Hadamard积运算时极大概率造成信息丢失——由于稀疏性，各素描非零项位置大概率不一致，导致逐元素相乘结果为零向量。

To address this issue, the core concept behind our method, as depicted in the right part of Figure 4, involves the utilization of circular convolution paired with circular cross-correlation (see Definitions 2.2 and 3.1) during inference. With circular convolution, the resulting sketch has the product of the non-zero entries in the bin that corresponds to the sum of the non-zero indices,modulo $m$ .

针对该问题，如图4右半部分所示，本方法核心在于推理阶段采用循环卷积配合循环互相关（见定义2.2和3.1）。循环卷积运算可确保非零项乘积保存在"非零索引和模$m$"对应的桶中。

As we will show empirically in Section 4.3, this translates to sketch updates that are orders of magnitude faster.

与Hadamard积不同，该操作能确保证信息完整性。我们将在后续章节形式化该理论。关键的是，单项目Count sketch的循环卷积计算时间复杂度为$O\left( 1\right)$（相对于素描大小$m$），这意味着流数据中每个到达元组的素描更新可在常数时间内完成，而采用Hadamard积的AMS素描需要$O\left( m\right)$时间。第4.3节的实验将证明，本方法能实现数量级更快的素描更新。

DEFINITION 3.1 (CIRCULAR CROSS-CORRELATION). The circular cross-correlation $x \star  \mathbf{y}$ of any two vectors $\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y} \in  {\mathbb{C}}^{m}$ is a vector with elements given by ${\left( \mathbf{x} \star  \mathbf{y}\right) }_{j} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0}}^{{m - 1}}{\bar{x}}_{i}{y}_{\left( {j + i}\right) {\;\operatorname{mod}\;m}}$ for all $j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ , where ${\bar{x}}_{i}$ denotes the complex conjugate of ${x}_{i}$ .

### 3.4 Integration with query optimizers

### 3.4 与查询优化器的集成

The quintessential application of our proposed method is the cardinality estimator within query optimizers. Query optimizers use a plan enumeration algorithm to find a good join order. The cost of a join order dependents upon the sizes of the intermediate results. The cardinality estimator's role is to provide the estimates for these intermediate sizes [30]. Each intermediate cardinality can be expressed as a sub-query which our method can estimate. The sketches for all the evaluated sub-queries can be created in a single pass over the data. Typically, each sub-query requires its own sketches; however, in cases where an attribute is joined multiple times, the sketches can be reused for each join involving that attribute. For example,to decide the join order of joins $\{ 0,1\}$ and $\{ 1,3\}$ in Figure 3,the sketch for attribute 1 can be reused for attributes 0 and 3 . In Section 4.5,we demonstrate the improvement in query execution time after integrating our proposed cardinality estimator into the query optimizer of PostgreSQL.

本方法的典型应用是作为查询优化器中的基数估计器。查询优化器通过计划枚举算法寻找最优连接顺序，其成本取决于中间结果集大小。基数估计器的作用正是提供这些中间规模的估计值[30]。每个中间基数均可表示为子查询，本方法可对其进行估计。所有评估子查询的草图可在单次数据遍历中创建。通常每个子查询需要独立草图，但当某属性被多次连接时，其草图可重复使用。例如为确定图3中连接$\{ 0,1\}$与$\{ 1,3\}$的顺序，属性1的草图可复用于属性0和3。4.5节将展示将本基数估计器集成到PostgreSQL查询优化器后，查询执行时间的改进。

Following Izenov et al. [29], in the experiments the filter predicates of each query are processed during ingestion of the tuples from their respective relation streams. In many streaming algorithms, the query is assumed to be known in advance of the stream. Consequently, filtering the tuples at the time of ingestion offers advantages in terms of performance and accuracy. This approach eliminates the need to update the sketch for tuples that do not satisfy the filters. In addition, the estimation accuracy is significantly improved as some data is already filtered out from the sketches [29]. However, it is worth mentioning that sketching methods, including the one presented, are also capable of handling filter predicates during inference. This can be achieved by treating the filters as joins with imaginary tables, a technique employed, for example, by Cormode [14] and Vengerov et al. [46]. Lastly,in our experiments,we report the median of $l = 5$ i.i.d. estimates as the cardinality estimate for all sketching methods.

## 5 CONCLUSION

## 5 结论

We have introduced a new sketching method that significantly enhances the cardinality estimation for multi-join queries. The proposed approach provides fast update times, which remain constant irrespective of the required estimation accuracy. This crucial feature allows for efficient processing of high-throughput streams, all the while delivering superior estimation accuracy compared to state-of-the-art baseline approaches. This is substantiated by our bound on the estimation error, as well as our empirical findings. Our results underscore the practical suitability of the proposed method for applications such as query optimization and approximate query processing, surpassing the capabilities of previous methods. The presented method successfully addresses the longstanding challenge of integrating the key advantages of the Count sketch with the AMS-based method for multi-join queries.

我们提出新型草图方法，显著提升多连接查询基数估计性能。该方法更新耗时恒定且与估计精度无关，既能高效处理高吞吐流数据，又较现有基线方法具有更优估计精度——这通过误差边界理论和实证研究得到验证。研究结果证实本方法在查询优化、近似查询处理等应用中的实用价值，成功融合Count草图与AMS基方法的核心优势，攻克了多连接查询的长期难题。

Proof for Theorem 2.3. By the definitions of $X$ and ${\mathbf{c}}_{k,j}$ ,with $I = {I}_{0} \times  \cdots  \times  {I}_{r - 1}$ ,and using the linearity of expectation, we get:

定理2.3的证明。根据$X$和${\mathbf{c}}_{k,j}$的定义，结合$I = {I}_{0} \times  \cdots  \times  {I}_{r - 1}$，利用期望的线性性质可得：

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack   = \frac{1}{m}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 0}}^{{m - 1}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}{\mathcal{F}}_{k}\left( i\right) \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{u \in  \Omega \left( {R}_{k}\right) }}\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{v \in  \Gamma \left( u\right) }}{s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

By construction of the sketches, the sign functions are independent across different joins and there are exactly two occurrences of each sign function, one for each end of a join edge. We can thus write:

根据草图构建原理，不同连接间的符号函数相互独立，且每个符号函数恰好出现两次（连接边的两端各一次）。因此可表示为：

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack   = \frac{1}{m}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 0}}^{{m - 1}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}\left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}{\mathcal{F}}_{k}\left( i\right) }\right) \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{\{ u,v\}  \in  E}}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {{s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}\right) {s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{v}\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

Since $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {s\left( a\right) s\left( b\right) }\right\rbrack   = \mathbf{1}\left( {a = b}\right)$ ,we get the desired expectation.

由于$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {s\left( a\right) s\left( b\right) }\right\rbrack   = \mathbf{1}\left( {a = b}\right)$，我们得到期望值。

For the variance,all the dimensions of the sketches are i.i.d.,and since $\operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack   - \mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}$ , for any $j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ ,we have:

对于方差，草图的所有维度都是独立同分布的，且当$\operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack   - \mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}$时，对于任意$j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$有：

$$
\operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  = \frac{1}{m}\operatorname{Var}\left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}{c}_{k,j}}\right)  \leq  \frac{1}{m}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {\left( \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}{c}_{k,j}\right) }^{2}\right\rbrack  
$$

By the definition of ${\mathbf{c}}_{k,j}$ and the linearity of expectation,we have:

根据${\mathbf{c}}_{k,j}$的定义及期望线性性质，可得：

$$
\operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  \leq  \frac{1}{m}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{i}^{\prime } \in  I}}{\mathcal{F}}_{0}\left( i\right) \cdots {\mathcal{F}}_{r - 1}\left( i\right) {\mathcal{F}}_{0}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right) \cdots {\mathcal{F}}_{r - 1}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right) \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{u \in  \Omega \left( {R}_{k}\right) }}\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{v \in  \Gamma \left( u\right) }}{s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}\right) {s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}^{\prime }\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

Taking into account that each sign function occurs twice (now four times since the value is squared), we obtain:

考虑到每个符号函数出现两次（平方后实际出现四次），我们得到：

$$
\operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  \leq  \frac{1}{m}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{i}^{\prime } \in  I}}{\mathcal{F}}_{0}\left( i\right) \cdots {\mathcal{F}}_{r - 1}\left( i\right) {\mathcal{F}}_{0}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right) \cdots {\mathcal{F}}_{r - 1}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right) 
$$

$$
\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{\{ u,v\}  \in  E}}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {{s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}\right) {s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}^{\prime }\right) {s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{v}\right) {s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{v}^{\prime }\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

By the four-wise independence of the sign functions, the expected value is one if there are two equal pairs or if all values are equal, and zero otherwise. Therefore, we have that:

基于符号函数的四向独立性，当存在两对相同值或全部值相等时期望值为1，否则为0。因此可得：

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {{s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}\right) {s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}^{\prime }\right) {s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{v}\right) {s}_{j,\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{v}^{\prime }\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

$$
 = \mathbf{1}\left( {\left( {{i}_{u} = {i}_{v} \land  {i}_{u}^{\prime } = {i}_{v}^{\prime }}\right)  \vee  \left( {{i}_{u} = {i}_{u}^{\prime } \neq  {i}_{v} = {i}_{v}^{\prime }}\right)  \vee  \left( {{i}_{u} = {i}_{v}^{\prime } \neq  {i}_{v} = {i}_{u}^{\prime }}\right) }\right) 
$$

For each join, there are three disjunctions which are conjoined over all the joins. By the distributivity property of conjunction over disjunction,there are thus ${3}^{\left| E\right| }$ disjunctions in total. Since the queries are acyclic, $\left| E\right|  = r - 1$ . The variance is bound by the sum over all disjunctions,where intersections are counted double. Using the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, each disjunction itself is bound by the product of squared frequency norms. This gives the desired upper bound on the variance.

每个连接包含三个析取项，这些析取项通过合取连接。根据合取对析取的分配律，总共有${3}^{\left| E\right| }$个析取项。由于查询是无环的，$\left| E\right|  = r - 1$。方差受所有析取项之和的约束，其中交集被重复计算。运用柯西-施瓦茨不等式，每个析取项本身受平方频率范数乘积的约束，由此得到方差的上界。

Proof for Theorem 3.1. By the definitions of $X$ and ${S}_{k}$ ,with $I = {I}_{0} \times  \cdots  \times  {I}_{r - 1}$ ,and the linearity of expectation, we have:

定理3.1的证明。根据$X$和${S}_{k}$的定义，结合$I = {I}_{0} \times  \cdots  \times  {I}_{r - 1}$及期望线性性质，有：

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack   = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j \in  J}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}{\mathcal{F}}_{0}\left( i\right) \cdots {\mathcal{F}}_{r - 1}\left( i\right) \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}\mathbf{1}\left( {{H}_{k}\left( i\right)  = {G}_{k}\left( j\right) }\right) \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{u \in  \Omega \left( {R}_{k}\right) }}\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{v \in  \Gamma \left( u\right) }}{s}_{\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

By the definitions of ${H}_{k}$ and ${G}_{k}$ ,since the sign and bin functions are independent,and using again the observation that each sign function occurs twice, we can write:

由${H}_{k}$和${G}_{k}$的定义，因符号函数与分桶函数独立，且每个符号函数出现两次，可表示为：

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack   = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}{\mathcal{F}}_{0}\left( i\right) \cdots {\mathcal{F}}_{r - 1}\left( i\right) \left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{\{ u,v\}  \in  E}}\mathbf{1}\left( {{i}_{u} = {i}_{v}}\right) }\right) 
$$

$$
\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j \in  J}}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}\mathbb{1}\left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{u \in  \Omega \left( {R}_{k}\right) }}{h}_{\Psi \left( u\right) }\left( {i}_{u}\right)  - {j}_{\Psi \left( u\right) } \equiv  0\;\left( {\;\operatorname{mod}\;m}\right) }\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

By isolating the case where all ${i}_{u} = {i}_{v}$ ,all the attribute values in the same graph component must be equal. Since each independent bin function is thus called with only one distinct value, the expected value of the bin functions is ${m}^{-\left( {w - r + 1}\right) }$ ,which is exactly the reciprocal of $\left| J\right|$ ,giving the desired expectation.

当所有${i}_{u} = {i}_{v}$时，同一图分量中的所有属性值必须相等。此时每个独立分桶函数仅处理单一取值，其期望值为${m}^{-\left( {w - r + 1}\right) }$，恰好是$\left| J\right|$的倒数，由此得证期望值。

For the variance,we have that $\operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack   - \mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}$ ,where:

对于方差，有$\operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack   - \mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}$，其中：

$$
\mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{i}^{\prime } \in  I}}\left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}{\mathcal{F}}_{k}\left( i\right) {\mathcal{F}}_{k}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right) }\right) \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{\{ u,v\}  \in  E}}1\left( {{i}_{u} = {i}_{v} \land  {i}_{u}^{\prime } = {i}_{v}^{\prime }}\right) 
$$

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack   = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{i}^{\prime } \in  I}}\left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}{\mathcal{F}}_{k}\left( i\right) {\mathcal{F}}_{k}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right) }\right) \left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{\{ u,v\}  \in  E}}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {{s}_{\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}\right) {s}_{\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{u}^{\prime }\right) {s}_{\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{v}\right) {s}_{\{ u,v\} }\left( {i}_{v}^{\prime }\right) }\right\rbrack  }\right) 
$$

$$
\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j \in  J}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{j}^{\prime } \in  J}}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}\mathbf{1}\left( {{H}_{k}\left( i\right)  = {G}_{k}\left( j\right) }\right) \mathbf{1}\left( {{H}_{k}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right)  = {G}_{k}\left( {j}^{\prime }\right) }\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

The expected value of the sign functions is stated in the proof for Theorem 2.3. If we consider only the first disjunction, then we get:

符号函数的期望值如定理2.3证明所述。若仅考虑第一个析取项，则得到：

$$
\mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j \in  J}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{j}^{\prime } \in  J}}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 0}}^{{r - 1}}\mathbf{1}\left( {{H}_{k}\left( i\right)  = {G}_{k}\left( j\right) }\right) \mathbf{1}\left( {{H}_{k}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right)  = {G}_{k}\left( {j}^{\prime }\right) }\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

which is equal to $\mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}$ ,because when ${i}_{u} = {i}_{v} \land  {i}_{u}^{\prime } = {i}_{v}^{\prime }$ ,all graph components must have one or two distinct values each. In the case of one distinct value, ${j}_{q} = {j}_{q}^{\prime }$ for that graph component $q$ . Thus,the expected value per graph component is either $1/{m}^{2}$ or $1\left( {{j}_{q} = {j}_{q}^{\prime }}\right) /m$ ,making the sums over $J$ equal to 1. Now,let us consider everything but $\mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}$ in $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack$ . There must be at least one occurrence of either ${i}_{u} = {i}_{u}^{\prime } \neq  {i}_{v} = {i}_{v}^{\prime }$ or ${i}_{u} = {i}_{v}^{\prime } \neq  {i}_{v} = {i}_{u}^{\prime }$ . Either way, ${j}_{q} = {j}_{q}^{\prime }$ for that graph component $q$ while there are still at least two distinct values. The sums over $J$ is thus $\leq  1/m$ . We then get that:

其值等于$\mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}$，因为当${i}_{u} = {i}_{v} \land  {i}_{u}^{\prime } = {i}_{v}^{\prime }$时，所有图组件必须各自含有一或两个不同的值。若仅有一个不同值，则该图组件的${j}_{q} = {j}_{q}^{\prime }$$q$。因此，每个图组件的期望值为$1/{m}^{2}$或$1\left( {{j}_{q} = {j}_{q}^{\prime }}\right) /m$，使得$J$的总和等于1。现在考虑$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack$中除$\mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2}$外的所有情况：至少会出现一次${i}_{u} = {i}_{u}^{\prime } \neq  {i}_{v} = {i}_{v}^{\prime }$或${i}_{u} = {i}_{v}^{\prime } \neq  {i}_{v} = {i}_{u}^{\prime }$。无论哪种情况，当仍存在至少两个不同值时，该图组件的${j}_{q} = {j}_{q}^{\prime }$$q$。因此$J$的总和为$\leq  1/m$，由此可得：

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {X}^{2}\right\rbrack   = \mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2} + Y \Rightarrow  \operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  \leq  \frac{1}{m}\mathbb{E}{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }^{2} + Y
$$

$$
\operatorname{Var}\left( X\right)  \leq  \frac{1}{m}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  I}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{i}^{\prime } \in  I}}{\mathcal{F}}_{0}\left( i\right) \cdots {\mathcal{F}}_{r - 1}\left( i\right) {\mathcal{F}}_{0}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right) \cdots {\mathcal{F}}_{r - 1}\left( {i}^{\prime }\right) 
$$

$$
\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{\{ u,v\}  \in  E}}\mathbf{1}\left( {{i}_{u} = {i}_{v} \land  {i}_{u}^{\prime } = {i}_{v}^{\prime }}\right)  + \mathbf{1}\left( {{i}_{u} = {i}_{u}^{\prime } \neq  {i}_{v} = {i}_{v}^{\prime }}\right)  + \mathbf{1}\left( {{i}_{u} = {i}_{v}^{\prime } \neq  {i}_{v} = {i}_{u}^{\prime }}\right) 
$$

which is the same expression of the variance bound as the one for Theorem 2.3. The final steps of the variance bound are thus equivalent, resulting in the desired upper bound.
