## Abstract

## 摘要

We present efficient algorithms for Quantile Join Queries, abbreviated as $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ . A $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ asks for the answer at a specified relative position (e.g., 50% for the median) under some ordering over the answers to a Join Query (JQ). Our goal is to avoid materializing the set of all join answers, and to achieve quasilinear time in the size of the database, regardless of the total number of answers. A recent dichotomy result rules out the existence of such an algorithm for a general family of queries and orders. Specifically, for acyclic JQs without self-joins, the problem becomes intractable for ordering by sum whenever we join more than two relations (and these joins are not trivial intersections). Moreover, even for basic ranking functions beyond sum, such as min or max over different attributes, so far it is not known whether there is any nontrivial tractable $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ .

我们提出针对分位数连接查询（Quantile Join Queries，简称$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$）的高效算法。$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$要求在连接查询（JQ）结果集中，根据特定排序规则获取指定相对位置（如中位数对应50%）的答案。我们的目标是避免物化所有连接结果，实现在数据库规模上的拟线性时间复杂度，且不受答案总数影响。最新二分法结果表明，对于通用查询族和排序规则，此类算法不存在。具体而言，对于无自连接的无环JQ，当连接超过两个关系（且非平凡交集）时，按求和排序的问题将变得难解。此外，即便是求和之外的基础排序函数（如对不同属性取最小值或最大值），目前尚未发现任何非平凡的可解$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$。

In this work, we develop a new approach to solving %JQ and show how this approach allows not just to recover known results, but also generalize them and resolve open cases. Our solution uses two subroutines: The first one needs to select what we call a "pivot answer". The second subroutine partitions the space of query answers according to this pivot, and continues searching in one partition that is represented as new %JQ over a new database. For pivot selection, we develop an algorithm that works for a large class of ranking functions that are appropriately monotone. The second subroutine requires a customized construction for the specific ranking function at hand.

本研究提出解决%JQ的新方法，该方法不仅能复现已知成果，还可推广并解决开放性问题。我们的方案采用两个子程序：首先需选择"枢轴答案"，其次根据该枢轴划分查询答案空间，并在表示为新数据库上%JQ的某个分区中继续搜索。针对枢轴选择，我们开发了适用于一大类适当单调排序函数的算法；第二个子程序则需针对具体排序函数定制构建方案。

We show the benefit and generality of our approach by using it to establish several new complexity results. First, we prove the tractability of min and max for all acyclic JQs, thereby resolving the above question. Second,we extend the previous $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ dichotomy for sum to all partial sums (over all subsets of the attributes). Third, we handle the intractable cases of sum by devising a deterministic approximation scheme that applies to every acyclic $\mathcal{J}Q$ .

通过建立多项新复杂性结果，我们验证了本方法的优势与普适性：首先证明所有无环JQ的最小值和最大值问题均可解，从而解答了前述疑问；其次将求和排序的$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$二分法推广至所有属性子集的部分和；第三针对难解的求和情况，设计了适用于任意无环$\mathcal{J}Q$的确定性近似方案。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Quantile queries ask for the element at a given relative position $\phi  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ in a given list $L$ of items [21]. For example,the lower quartile, median, and upper quartile are the elements at positions $\phi  = {0.25},\phi  = {0.5}$ ,and $\phi  = {0.75}$ ,respectively. We investigate quantile queries where $L$ is the result of a Join Query (JQ) $Q$ over a database, with a ranking function that determines the order between the answers. Importantly,the list $L$ can be much larger than the input database $D$ (specifically, $L$ can be $\Omega \left( {\left| D\right| }^{k}\right)$ for some degree $k$ determined by $Q$ ),and so, $Q$ and $D$ form a compact representation for $L$ . Our main research question is when we can find the quantile in quasilinear time. In other words,the time suffices for reading $D$ , but we are generally prevented from materializing $Q\left( D\right)$ .

分位数查询要求从给定项目列表$L$中获取指定相对位置$\phi  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$的元素[21]。例如下四分位数、中位数和上四分位数分别对应$\phi  = {0.25},\phi  = {0.5}$、$\phi  = {0.75}$位置。我们研究当$L$是数据库连接查询（JQ）$Q$的结果，且排序函数决定答案顺序时的分位数查询。关键的是，列表$L$可能远大于输入数据库$D$（具体而言，$L$可能是由$Q$决定的$k$次方的$\Omega \left( {\left| D\right| }^{k}\right)$），因此$Q$和$D$构成了$L$的紧凑表示。核心研究问题是：何时能在拟线性时间内找到分位数？即时间复杂度仅需读取$D$，但通常无法物化$Q\left( D\right)$。

For illustration, consider a social network where users organize events, share event announcements, and declare their plans to attend events. It has the three relations Admin(user, event), Share(user, event, #likes), and Attend(user, event, #likes). We wish to extract statistics about triples of users involved in events, beginning by joining the three relations using the JQ

示例：设社交网络中用户组织活动、分享活动公告并声明参与计划。包含三个关系：Admin(用户,活动)、Share(用户,活动,#点赞数)、Attend(用户,活动,#点赞数)。我们希望通过JQ连接这三个关系，提取涉及活动的用户三元组统计信息。

$$
\operatorname{Admin}\left( {{u}_{1},e}\right) ,\operatorname{Share}\left( {{u}_{2},e,{l}_{2}}\right) ,\operatorname{Attend}\left( {{u}_{3},e,{l}_{3}}\right) \text{.}
$$

Now, suppose that all we do is apply a quantile query to the result of $Q$ ,say the 0.1-quantile ordered by ${l}_{2} + {l}_{3}$ (the sum of likes of the share and the participation). The direct way of finding the quantile is to materialize the join, sort the resulting tuples, and take the element at position $\phi  = {0.1}$ . Yet,this result might be considerably larger than the database, and prohibitively expensive to compute, even though in the end we care only about one value. Can we do better? This is the research question that we study in this paper. In general, the answer depends on the JQ and order, and in this specific example we can, actually, do considerably better.

现在，假设我们仅对$Q$的结果应用分位数查询，例如按${l}_{2} + {l}_{3}$（分享与参与点赞数之和）排序的0.1分位数。直接计算分位数的方法是实现连接操作、对结果元组排序并提取第$\phi  = {0.1}$个元素。然而，即使我们最终只关心单个值，该结果集可能远超数据库容量，计算代价极高。能否优化？这正是本文研究的核心问题。总体而言，答案取决于连接查询(JQ)和排序方式，而在这个具体案例中，我们确实能实现显著优化。

To be more precise, we study the fine-grained data complexity of query evaluation, where the query seeks a quantile over a JQ. We refer to such a query as a Quantile Join Query and abbreviate it as %JQ. So,the JQ $Q$ is fixed,and so is the ranking function (e.g.,weighted sum over a fixed subset of the variables). The input consists of $D$ and $\phi$ . In terms of the execution cost,we allow for poly-logarithmic factors, therefore our goal is to devise evaluation algorithms that run in time quasilinear in $D$ ,that is, $O\left( {\left| D\right| \operatorname{polylog}\left( \left| D\right| \right) }\right)$ .

更准确地说，我们研究查询评估的细粒度数据复杂度，该查询在JQ结果上寻找分位数。我们将此类查询称为分位数连接查询(Quantile Join Query)，简写为%JQ。因此，JQ$Q$是固定的，排序函数（如对固定变量子集的加权求和）也是固定的。输入包含$D$和$\phi$。在执行成本方面，我们允许存在多对数因子，因此目标是设计在$D$上拟线性时间（即$O\left( {\left| D\right| \operatorname{polylog}\left( \left| D\right| \right) }\right)$）运行的评估算法。

To the best of our knowledge, little is known about the fine-grained complexity of %JQ. We have previously studied this problem [7] for Conjunctive Queries (which are more general than Join Queries since they also allow for projection) under the name "selection problem". ${}^{1}$ Two types of orders were covered: sum of all attributes and lexicographic orders. On the face of it, the conclusion from our previous results is that we are extremely limited in what we can do: The problem is intractable for every $\mathcal{J}Q$ with more than two atoms (each having a set of variables that is not contained in that of another atom, and assuming no self-joins), under conventional conjectures in fine-grained complexity. Nevertheless, we argue that our previous results tell only part of the story and miss quite general opportunities for tractability:

据我们所知，关于%JQ细粒度复杂度的研究甚少。我们先前以"选择问题"为名研究过更通用的连接查询（允许投影操作）[7]。${}^{1}$研究涵盖两种排序方式：全属性求和与字典序。表面上看，先前结论显示我们的操作空间极其有限：在细粒度复杂度的常规假设下，任何超过两个原子（且各原子变量集互不包含，无自连接）的$\mathcal{J}Q$问题都难以处理。然而我们认为，这些结论仅揭示了部分真相，忽略了广泛的可行性机会：

- What if the sum involves just a subset of the variables, like in the above social-network example? Then the lower bounds for full sum do not apply. As it turns out, in this case we often can achieve tractability for JQs of more than 2 atoms.

- 若求和仅涉及变量子集（如前述社交网络示例）？此时全求和的复杂度下界不再适用。事实证明，这种情况常可实现对超过2原子JQ的可行处理。

- What if we allow for some small error and not insist on the precise $\phi$ -quantile? As we argue later,this relaxation makes the picture dramatically more positive.

- 若允许微小误差而不强求精确的$\phi$分位数？如后文所述，这一松弛将使问题解决前景显著改善。

In addition, there are ranking functions that have not been considered at all, notably minimum and maximum over attributes, such as MIN(rate1, rate2) and MAX(width, height, depth). We do not see any conclusion from past results on these, so the state of affairs (prior to this paper) is that their complexity is an open problem.

此外，某些排序函数尚未被研究，特别是属性最小值/最大值，如MIN(rate1, rate2)和MAX(width, height, depth)。过往研究未给出任何结论，因此（本文之前）这些函数的复杂度尚属开放性问题。

In this work, we devise a new framework for evaluating %JQ queries. We view the problem as a search problem in the space of query answers, and the framework adopts a divide-and-conquer "pivoting" approach. For a JQ Q,we reduce the problem to two subroutines,given $D$ and $\phi$ :

本研究提出评估%JQ查询的新框架。我们将问题视为答案空间中的搜索问题，框架采用分治式"枢轴"方法。对于JQ Q，在给定$D$和$\phi$条件下，将问题分解为两个子程序：

- PIVOT: Find a pivot answer $p$ such that the set of answers that precede $p$ and the set of answers that follow $p$ both contain at least a constant fraction of the answers.

- 枢轴选择(PIVOT)：寻找枢轴答案$p$，使得在$p$之前和之后的答案集均至少包含恒定比例的答案。

- TRIM: Partition the answers into three splits: less than, equal to,and greater than $p$ . Determine which one contains the sought answer and,if it is not $p$ ,produce a new $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ within the relevant split using new ${Q}^{\prime },{D}^{\prime }$ and ${\phi }^{\prime }$ . We view this operation as trimming the split condition by updating the database so that the remaining answers satisfy the condition.

- TRIM（修剪操作）：将答案划分为三个分区：小于、等于和大于$p$的分区。确定哪个分区包含目标答案，若非$p$所在分区，则使用新的${Q}^{\prime },{D}^{\prime }$和${\phi }^{\prime }$在相关分区内生成新的$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$。该操作通过更新数据库来修剪分区条件，使剩余答案满足条件。

We begin by showing that we can select a pivot in linear time for every acyclic join query and every ranking function that satisfies a monotonicity assumption (also used in the problem of ranked enumeration [15, 23]), which all functions considered here satisfy. Note that the assumption of acyclicity is required since, otherwise, it is impossible to even determine whether the join query has any answer in quasilinear time, under conventional conjectures in fine-grained complexity [6]. Hence, the challenge really lies in trimming.

我们首先证明，对于每个无环连接查询和满足单调性假设的排名函数（该假设也用于排序枚举问题[15,23]），可以在线性时间内选择枢轴点。需注意无环假设是必要的，因为在精细复杂度理论的传统猜想下[6]，非无环情况下甚至无法在拟线性时间内判断连接查询是否有答案。因此，核心挑战在于修剪操作。

Contributions. Using our approach, we establish efficient algorithms for several classes of queries and ranking functions, where we show how to solve the trimming problem.

研究贡献。通过本方法，我们为多类查询和排名函数建立了高效算法，展示了如何解决修剪问题。

(1) We establish tractability for all acyclic JQs under the ranking functions MIN and MAX.

(1) 我们证明了在MIN和MAX排名函数下，所有无环连接查询都具有可处理性。

(2) We recover (up to logarithmic factors) all past tractable cases [7] for lexicographic orders and SUM.

(2) 我们以对数因子为界，重现了词典序和SUM函数下所有已知的可处理案例[7]。

(3) For self-join-free JQs and SUM, we complete the picture by extending the previous dichotomy [7] (restricted to JQs) to all partial sums.

(3) 对于无自连接的连接查询和SUM函数，通过将先前二分法[7]（限于连接查询）扩展到所有部分和，我们完善了理论框架。

We then turn our attention to approximate answers. Precisely, we find an answer at a position within $\left( {1 \pm  \epsilon }\right) \phi$ for an allowed error $\epsilon$ . (This is a standard notion of approximation for quantiles [9,17].) To obtain an efficient randomized approximation, it suffices to be able to construct in quasilinear time a direct-access structure for the underlying JQ, regardless of the answer ordering; if so, then one can use a standard median-of-samples approach (with Hoeffd-ing's inequality to guarantee the error bounds). Such algorithms for direct-access structures have been established in the past for arbitrary acyclic JQs [6, 8]. Instead, we take on the challenge of deterministic approximation. Our final contribution is that:

接着我们研究近似答案。具体而言，在允许误差$\epsilon$范围内，找到位于<b0></b1>位置的答案（这是分位数的标准近似概念[9,17]）。要实现高效随机近似，只需能在拟线性时间内为底层连接查询构建直接访问结构，与答案排序无关；若可行，则可使用标准样本中位数法（借助霍夫丁不等式保证误差界）。先前研究已为任意无环连接查询建立了此类直接访问结构算法[6,8]。而我们挑战确定性近似，最终贡献是：

(4) We show that with an adjustment of our pivoting framework, we can establish a deterministic approximation scheme in time quadratic in $1/\epsilon$ and quasilinear in database size.

(4) 我们证明通过调整枢轴框架，可在$1/\epsilon$的平方时间与数据库大小的拟线性时间内建立确定性近似方案。

In contrast to the randomized case, we found the task of deterministic approximation challenging, and our algorithm is indeed quite involved. Again, the main challenge is in the trimming phase.

与随机方法不同，确定性近似极具挑战性，我们的算法确实相当复杂。核心难点仍在于修剪阶段。

The remainder of the paper is organized as follows: We give preliminary definitions in Section 2. We describe the general pivoting framework in Section 3. In Section 4, we describe the pivot-selection algorithm. The main results are in Sections 5 and 6 where we devise exact and approximate trimmings, respectively, and establish the corresponding tractability results. We conclude in Section 7.

论文结构如下：第2节给出基础定义，第3节描述通用枢轴框架，第4节阐述枢轴选择算法。主要成果见于第5、6节，分别开发精确修剪和近似修剪方法，并建立相应可处理性结论。第7节总结全文。

## 2 PRELIMINARIES

## 2 基础知识

### 2.1 Basic Notions

### 2.1 基本概念

Sets. We use $\left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ to denote the set of integers $\{ 1,\ldots ,r\}$ . A multiset $L$ is described by a 2-tuple $\left( {Z,\beta }\right)$ ,where $Z$ is the set of its distinct elements and $\beta  : Z \rightarrow  \mathbb{N}$ is a multiplicity function. The set of all possible multisets with elements $Z$ is denoted by ${\mathbb{N}}^{Z}$ .

集合。用$\left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$表示整数集$\{ 1,\ldots ,r\}$。多重集$L$由二元组$\left( {Z,\beta }\right)$描述，其中$Z$是相异元素集，$\beta  : Z \rightarrow  \mathbb{N}$为多重度函数。元素为$Z$的所有可能多重集记作${\mathbb{N}}^{Z}$。

Relational databases. A schema $\mathcal{S}$ is a set of relational symbols $\left\{  {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}}\right\}$ . A database $D$ contains a finite relation ${R}^{D} \subseteq  {\operatorname{dom}}^{{a}_{R}}$ for each $R \in  \mathcal{S}$ ,where dom is a set of constants called the domain, and ${a}_{R}$ is the arity of symbol $R$ . If $D$ is clear,we simply use $R$ instead of ${R}^{D}$ . The size of $D$ is the total number of tuples,denoted by $n$ .

关系数据库。模式$\mathcal{S}$是关系符号集$\left\{  {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}}\right\}$。数据库$D$为每个$R \in  \mathcal{S}$包含有限关系${R}^{D} \subseteq  {\operatorname{dom}}^{{a}_{R}}$，其中dom是称为域的常量集，${a}_{R}$是符号$R$的元数。若$D$明确，则简记$R$代替${R}^{D}$。数据库大小用总元组数$n$表示。

Join Queries. A Join Query (JQ) $Q$ over schema $\mathcal{S}$ is an expression of the form ${R}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,{R}_{\ell }\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{\ell }\right)$ ,where $\left\{  {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{\ell }}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathcal{S}$ and the variables of $Q$ are $\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)  = { \cup  }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  \ell \right\rbrack  }{\mathbf{X}}_{i}$ ,sometimes interpreted as a tuple instead of a set. Each ${R}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{i}\right) ,i \in  \left\lbrack  \ell \right\rbrack$ is called an atom of $Q$ . A repeated occurrence of a relational symbol is a self-join and a JQ without self-joins is self-join-free. A query answer is a homomorphism from $Q$ to the database $D$ ,i.e. a mapping from $\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ to dom constants,such that every atom ${R}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{i}\right) ,i \in  \left\lbrack  \ell \right\rbrack$ maps to a tuple of ${R}_{i}^{D}$ . The set of query answers to $Q$ over $D$ is denoted by $Q\left( D\right)$ and we often represent a query answer $q \in  Q\left( D\right)$ as a tuple of values assigned to $\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ . For an atom ${R}_{i}\left( {\mathrm{X}}_{i}\right)$ of a $\mathrm{{JQ}}$ and database $D$ ,we say that tuple $t \in  {R}_{i}^{D}$ assigns value $a$ to variable $x$ ,and write it as $t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack   = a$ ,if for every index $j$ such that ${\mathrm{X}}_{i}\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack   = x$ we have $t\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack   = a$ . For a predicate $P\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{P}\right)$ over variables ${\mathbf{X}}_{P} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ ,we denote by $\left( {Q \land  P}\right) \left( D\right)$ the subset of query answers $Q\left( D\right)$ that satisfy $P\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{P}\right)$ .

连接查询。连接查询（Join Query，简称JQ）$Q$在模式$\mathcal{S}$上是一个形如${R}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,{R}_{\ell }\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{\ell }\right)$的表达式，其中$\left\{  {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{\ell }}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathcal{S}$且$Q$的变量为$\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)  = { \cup  }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  \ell \right\rbrack  }{\mathbf{X}}_{i}$，有时被解释为元组而非集合。每个${R}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{i}\right) ,i \in  \left\lbrack  \ell \right\rbrack$称为$Q$的一个原子。关系符号的重复出现称为自连接，不含自连接的JQ称为无自连接。查询答案是从$Q$到数据库$D$的同态映射，即从$\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$到域常量的映射，使得每个原子${R}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{i}\right) ,i \in  \left\lbrack  \ell \right\rbrack$映射到${R}_{i}^{D}$的一个元组。$Q$在$D$上的查询答案集合记作$Q\left( D\right)$，我们常将查询答案$q \in  Q\left( D\right)$表示为分配给$\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$的值元组。对于$\mathrm{{JQ}}$的原子${R}_{i}\left( {\mathrm{X}}_{i}\right)$和数据库$D$，若对每个满足${\mathrm{X}}_{i}\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack   = x$的索引$j$都有$t\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack   = a$，则称元组$t \in  {R}_{i}^{D}$将值$a$赋给变量$x$，记作$t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack   = a$。对于变量${\mathbf{X}}_{P} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$上的谓词$P\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{P}\right)$，用$\left( {Q \land  P}\right) \left( D\right)$表示满足$P\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{P}\right)$的查询答案子集$Q\left( D\right)$。

Hypergraphs. A hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {V,E}\right)$ is a set $V$ of vertices and a set $E \subseteq  {2}^{V}$ of hyperedges. A path in $\mathcal{H}$ is a sequence of vertices such that every two consecutive vertices appear together in a hyperedge. A chordless path is a path in which no two non-consecutive ones appear in the same hyperedge (in particular, no vertex appears twice). The number of maximal hyperedges is $\operatorname{mh}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \left| \left\{  {e \in  E \mid  \nexists {e}^{\prime } \in  E : e \subset  {e}^{\prime }}\right\}  \right|$ . A set of vertices $U \subseteq  V$ is independent if no pair appears in a hyperedge,i.e., $\left| {U \cap  e}\right|  \leq  1,\forall e \in  E$ .

超图。超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {V,E}\right)$是由顶点集$V$和超边集$E \subseteq  {2}^{V}$构成的集合。路径$\mathcal{H}$是指顶点序列中每两个连续顶点都出现在同一条超边的结构。无弦路径是指路径中不存在两个非连续顶点出现在同一条超边的路径（特别地，不允许顶点重复出现）。最大超边数量为$\operatorname{mh}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \left| \left\{  {e \in  E \mid  \nexists {e}^{\prime } \in  E : e \subset  {e}^{\prime }}\right\}  \right|$。若顶点集$U \subseteq  V$中不存在任何顶点对出现在同一条超边，则该集合是独立的，即$\left| {U \cap  e}\right|  \leq  1,\forall e \in  E$。

Join trees. A join tree of a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {V,E}\right)$ is a tree $T$ where its nodes ${}^{2}$ are the hyperedges of $\mathcal{H}$ and the running intersection property holds,namely: for all $u \in  V$ the set $\{ e \in  E \mid  u \in  e\}$ forms a (connected) subtree in $T$ . We associate a hypergraph $\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)  = \left( {V,E}\right)$ to a JQ $Q$ where the vertices are the variables of $Q$ ,and every atom of $Q$ corresponds to a hyperedge with the same set of variables. With a slight abuse of notation, we identify atoms of $Q$ with hyperedges of $\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$ . A JQ $Q$ is acyclic if there exists a join tree for $\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$ ,otherwise it is cyclic. If we root the join tree,the subtree rooted at a node $U$ defines a subquery,i.e.,a JQ that contains only the atoms of descendants of $U$ . A partial query answer (for the subtree) rooted at $U$ is an answer to the subquery. If we materialize a relation ${R}_{U}$ for node $U$ ,a partial query answer (for the subtree) rooted at $t \in  {R}_{U}$ must additionally agree with $t$ .

连接树。超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {V,E}\right)$的连接树是满足以下条件的树$T$：其节点${}^{2}$对应超图的超边，且满足运行交集性质——对于所有$u \in  V$，集合$\{ e \in  E \mid  u \in  e\}$在树$T$中形成（连通的）子树。我们将JQ查询$Q$对应的超图$\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)  = \left( {V,E}\right)$定义为：顶点是查询变量，每个原子对应包含相同变量集的超边。在不严格区分符号的情况下，我们将查询原子与超边$\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$视为等同。当超图$\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$存在连接树时，JQ查询$Q$是无环的，否则为有环的。若对连接树进行根节点指定，则以节点$U$为根的子树定义了子查询（即仅包含该节点后代原子的JQ查询）。以$U$为根的（子树）部分查询答案即该子查询的解。若为节点$U$物化关系${R}_{U}$，则以$t \in  {R}_{U}$为根的部分查询答案还需与$t$保持一致。

Complexity. We measure complexity in the database size $n$ , while query size is considered constant. The model of computation is the standard RAM model with uniform cost measure. In particular, it allows for linear-time construction of lookup tables, which can be accessed in constant time. Following our prior work [7], we only consider comparison-based sorting, which takes quasilinear time.

复杂度。我们以数据库规模$n$作为复杂度度量基准，查询规模视为常数。计算模型采用标准RAM模型配合均匀成本度量。该模型特别支持线性时间构建查找表，并支持常数时间访问。延续我们先前工作[7]的设定，仅考虑基于比较的排序算法，其时间复杂度为准线性。

### 2.2 Orders over Query Answers

### 2.2 查询答案排序

To define $\% \mathrm{{JQs}}$ ,we assume an ordering of the query answers by a given ranking function. The ranking function is described by a 2-tuple $\left( {w, \leq  }\right)$ where a weight function $w : Q\left( D\right)  \rightarrow  {\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ maps the answers to a weight domain ${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ equipped with a total order $\leq$ . We denote the strict version of the total order by $<$ . Assuming consistent tie-breaking, the total order extends to query answers, i.e.,for ${q}_{1},{q}_{2} \in  Q\left( D\right) ,{q}_{1} \leq  {q}_{2}$ iff $w\left( {q}_{1}\right)  \prec  w\left( {q}_{2}\right)$ or $w\left( {q}_{1}\right)  = w\left( {q}_{2}\right)$ and ${q}_{1}$ is (arbitrarily but consistently) chosen to break the tie.

为定义$\% \mathrm{{JQs}}$，我们假定查询答案按给定排序函数进行排序。该排序函数由二元组$\left( {w, \leq  }\right)$描述：权重函数$w : Q\left( D\right)  \rightarrow  {\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$将答案映射至配备全序$\leq$的权重域${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$，其严格版本记作$<$。在采用一致平局打破规则的前提下，全序可扩展至查询答案——对于${q}_{1},{q}_{2} \in  Q\left( D\right) ,{q}_{1} \leq  {q}_{2}$，当且仅当$w\left( {q}_{1}\right)  \prec  w\left( {q}_{2}\right)$成立，或$w\left( {q}_{1}\right)  = w\left( {q}_{2}\right)$成立且${q}_{1}$被（任意但一致地）选定为平局打破依据时成立。

Weight aggregation model. We focus on the case of aggregate ranking functions where the query answer weights are computed by aggregating weights are assigned to the input database. In particular, an input-weight function ${w}_{x} :$ dom $\rightarrow  {\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ associates each value of variable $x$ with a weight in ${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ . An aggregate function ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$ : ${\mathbb{N}}^{{\operatorname{dom}}_{w}} \rightarrow  {\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ takes a multiset of weights and produces a single weight. Aggregate ranking functions are typically not sensitive to the order in which the input weights are given [11, 12], captured by the fact that their input is a multiset. Query answers map to ${\mathrm{{dom}}}_{w}$ by aggregating the weights of values assigned to a subset of the input variables ${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ with an aggregate function ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$ . Thus,the weight of a query answer $q \in  Q\left( D\right)$ is $w\left( q\right)  =$ ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( \left\{  {{w}_{x}\left( {q\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  x \in  {U}_{w}}\right\}  \right)$ . When we do not have a specific assignment from variables to values,we use $w\left( {U}_{w}\right)$ to refer to the expression ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( \left\{  {{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  \mid  x \in  {U}_{w}}\right\}  \right)$ . For example,if $\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)  =$ $\left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}  ,{U}_{w} = \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{3}}\right\}  ,{w}_{x}\left( x\right)$ is the identity function for all varaibles $x$ ,and ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$ is summation,then $w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  = {x}_{1} + {x}_{3}$ .

权重聚合模型。我们重点研究聚合排序函数的情况，其中查询答案的权重是通过对输入数据库分配的权重进行聚合计算得出的。具体而言，输入权重函数${w}_{x} :$ dom$\rightarrow  {\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$将变量$x$的每个值与${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$中的权重相关联。聚合函数${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$:${\mathbb{N}}^{{\operatorname{dom}}_{w}} \rightarrow  {\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$接收一个权重多重集并生成单一权重。聚合排序函数通常对输入权重的顺序不敏感[11,12]，这体现在其输入为多重集的特性上。查询答案通过使用聚合函数${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$对输入变量子集${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$所赋值权重的聚合，映射到${\mathrm{{dom}}}_{w}$。因此，查询答案$q \in  Q\left( D\right)$的权重为$w\left( q\right)  =$${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( \left\{  {{w}_{x}\left( {q\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  x \in  {U}_{w}}\right\}  \right)$。当变量到值的具体赋值关系不明确时，我们用$w\left( {U}_{w}\right)$指代表达式${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( \left\{  {{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  \mid  x \in  {U}_{w}}\right\}  \right)$。例如，若$\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)  =$$\left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}  ,{U}_{w} = \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{3}}\right\}  ,{w}_{x}\left( x\right)$是所有变量$x$的恒等函数，且${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$为求和函数，则$w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  = {x}_{1} + {x}_{3}$。

Concrete ranking functions. In this paper, we discuss three types of ranking functions:

具体排序函数。本文讨论三种排序函数类型：

(1) SUM: ${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ is $\mathbb{R}$ and ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$ is summation. We use the term full SUM when ${U}_{w} = \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ and partial SUM otherwise.

(1) 求和型(SUM)：${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$为$\mathbb{R}$且${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$为求和运算。当${U}_{w} = \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$时我们称其为完全求和，否则为部分求和。

(2) MIN/MAX: ${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ is $\mathbb{R}$ and ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$ is min or max.

(2) 最小/最大型(MIN/MAX)：${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$为$\mathbb{R}$且${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$为取最小值或最大值。

(3) LEX: Lexicographic orders fit into our framework by letting the domain ${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ consist of tuples in ${\mathbb{N}}^{\left| {U}_{w}\right| }$ . Every variable $x \in  {U}_{w}$ is mapped to ${\operatorname{dom}}_{w}$ as a tuple $\left( {0,\ldots ,{w}_{x}^{\prime }\left( x\right) ,\ldots ,0}\right)$ where ${w}_{x}^{\prime }\left( x\right)$ occupies the position of $x$ in the lexicographic order and ${w}_{x}^{\prime }$ is a function ${w}_{x}^{\prime } :$ dom $\rightarrow  \mathbb{N}$ that orders the domain of $x$ by mapping it to natural numbers. The aggregate function ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$ is then element-wise addition,while the order $\preccurlyeq$ compares these tuples lexicographically.

(3) 字典序型(LEX)：通过令域<b0></b1>由${\mathbb{N}}^{\left| {U}_{w}\right| }$中的元组构成，字典序可纳入本框架。每个变量$x \in  {U}_{w}$被映射为元组$\left( {0,\ldots ,{w}_{x}^{\prime }\left( x\right) ,\ldots ,0}\right)$，其中${w}_{x}^{\prime }\left( x\right)$占据字典序中$x$的位置，${w}_{x}^{\prime }$是函数${w}_{x}^{\prime } :$ dom$\rightarrow  \mathbb{N}$通过将$x$的域映射为自然数进行排序。聚合函数${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}$采用逐元素相加，而序关系$\preccurlyeq$按字典序比较这些元组。

Problem definition. Let $Q$ be a ${JQ}$ and $\left( {w, \leq  }\right)$ a ranking function. Given a database $D$ ,a query answer $q \in  Q\left( D\right)$ is a $\phi$ -quantile [21] of $Q\left( D\right)$ for some $\phi  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ if there exists a valid ordering of $Q\left( D\right)$ where there are $\lceil \phi \left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right| \rceil$ answers less-than or equal-to $q$ and $\lfloor \left( {1 - \phi }\right) \left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right| \rfloor$ answers greater than $q$ . A %JQ asks for a $\phi$ - quantile given $D$ and $\phi$ . Similarly,an $\epsilon$ -approximate %JQ asks for a $\left( {\phi  \pm  \epsilon }\right)$ -quantile for a given $D,\phi$ ,and $\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ .

问题定义。设$Q$为${JQ}$，$\left( {w, \leq  }\right)$为排序函数。给定数据库$D$，当存在$Q\left( D\right)$的有效排序使得其中小于等于$q$的答案有$\lceil \phi \left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right| \rceil$个、大于$q$的答案有$\lfloor \left( {1 - \phi }\right) \left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right| \rfloor$个时，查询结果$q \in  Q\left( D\right)$称为$Q\left( D\right)$的$\phi$分位数[21]。%JQ问题要求根据$D$和$\phi$返回对应的$\phi$分位数。类似地，$\epsilon$近似%JQ问题则要求对给定$D,\phi$和$\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$返回$\left( {\phi  \pm  \epsilon }\right)$分位数。

Monotonicity. Let $\uplus$ be multiset union. An (aggregate) ranking function is subset-monotone [23] if ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( {L}_{1}\right)  \preccurlyeq  {\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( {L}_{2}\right)$ implies that ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( {L \uplus  {L}_{1}}\right)  \leq  {\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( {L \uplus  {L}_{2}}\right)$ for all multisets $L,{L}_{1},{L}_{2}$ . All ranking functions we consider in this work have this property. We note that subset-monotonicity has been used as an assumption in ranked enumeration $\left\lbrack  {{15},{23}}\right\rbrack$ and is a stronger requirement than the more well-known monotonicity notion of Fagin et al. [10].

单调性。设$\uplus$为多重集并运算。若对任意多重集$L,{L}_{1},{L}_{2}$都有${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( {L}_{1}\right)  \preccurlyeq  {\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( {L}_{2}\right)$蕴含${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( {L \uplus  {L}_{1}}\right)  \leq  {\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( {L \uplus  {L}_{2}}\right)$，则称该(聚合)排序函数具有子集单调性[23]。本文涉及的所有排序函数均满足此性质。需说明的是，子集单调性在排序枚举$\left\lbrack  {{15},{23}}\right\rbrack$中常被作为假设条件，其要求强于Fagin等人[10]提出的经典单调性概念。

Tuple weights. Our ranking function definition uses attribute-weights but some of our algorithms are easier to describe when dealing with tuple weights. We can convert the former to the latter in linear time. First, we eliminate self-joins by materializing a fresh relation for every repeated symbol in the query $Q$ . Second,to avoid,to avoid giving the weight of a variable to tuples of multiple relations, we define a mapping $\mu$ that assigns each variable $x \in  {U}_{w}$ to a relation $R$ such that $x$ occurs in the $R$ -atom of $Q$ . The weight of a tuple $t \in  R$ is then the multiset of weights for variables assigned to $R$ : ${w}_{R}\left( t\right)  = \left\{  {{w}_{x}\left( {t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  x \in  {U}_{w},\mu \left( x\right)  = R}\right\}$ . ${}^{3}$ The total order $\leq$ can be extended to sets of tuples $\left( {{t}_{1},\ldots ,{t}_{r}}\right)$ (and thus query answers) by aggregating all individual weights contained in the tuple weights.

元组权重。虽然我们的排序函数定义使用属性权重，但部分算法在描述时更适合采用元组权重。两者可通过线性时间转换：首先通过为查询$Q$中重复符号实例化新关系来消除自连接；其次为避免将变量权重重复计入多个关系，我们定义映射$\mu$将每个变量$x \in  {U}_{w}$分配到$Q$中包含该变量的$R$关系原子中。元组$t \in  R$的权重即为分配给$R$关系的变量权重的多重集：${w}_{R}\left( t\right)  = \left\{  {{w}_{x}\left( {t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  x \in  {U}_{w},\mu \left( x\right)  = R}\right\}$。${}^{3}$通过聚合元组权重包含的所有个体权重，全序关系$\leq$可扩展至元组集合$\left( {{t}_{1},\ldots ,{t}_{r}}\right)$(进而扩展至查询结果)。

### 2.3 Known Bounds

### 2.3 已知界限

Certain upper and (conditional) lower bounds for $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ follow from our previous work [7] on the selection problem which asks for the query answer at index $k$ . The two problems are equivalent for acyclic JQs,since an index can be translated into a fraction $\phi$ ,and vice-versa,by knowing $\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$ ,which can be computed in linear time as we explain in Section 2.4.

关于$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$的某些上界及（条件性）下界源于我们先前对选择问题的研究[7]，该问题要求获取索引$k$处的查询结果。对于无环连接查询(JQs)，这两个问题是等价的——通过知晓$\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$（我们将在第2.4节解释其线性时间可计算性），索引可转换为分数$\phi$，反之亦然。

The lower bounds are based on two hypotheses:

下界基于两个假设：

(1) HYPERCLIQUE $\left\lbrack  {1,{16}}\right\rbrack$ : Let a $\left( {k + 1,k}\right)$ -hyperclique be a set of $k + 1$ vertices such that every subset of $k$ elements is a hyper-edge. For every $k \geq  2$ ,there is no $O\left( {m\text{polylog}m}\right)$ algorithm for deciding the existence of a $\left( {k + 1,k}\right)$ -hyperclique in a $k$ - uniform hypergraph with $m$ hyperedges.

(1) 超团假设(HYPERCLIQUE)$\left\lbrack  {1,{16}}\right\rbrack$：设$\left( {k + 1,k}\right)$-超团是由$k + 1$个顶点组成的集合，其中任意$k$个元素的子集都是超边。对于所有$k \geq  2$，不存在$O\left( {m\text{polylog}m}\right)$算法能判定具有$m$条超边的$k$-均匀超图中是否存在$\left( {k + 1,k}\right)$-超团。

(2) 3SUM [4,19]: For any $\epsilon  > 0$ ,we cannot decide in time $O\left( {m}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$ whether there exist $a \in  A,b \in  B,c \in  C$ from three integer sets $A,B,C$ ,each of size $\Omega \left( m\right)$ ,such that $a + b + c = 0$ .

(2) 三数和假设(3SUM)[4,19]：对任意$\epsilon  > 0$，我们无法在$O\left( {m}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$时间内判定三个大小为$\Omega \left( m\right)$的整数集$A,B,C$中是否存在满足$a + b + c = 0$的$a \in  A,b \in  B,c \in  C$元组。

HYPERCLIQUE implies that we cannot decide in $O\left( {n\text{polylog}n}\right)$ if a cyclic, self-join-free JQ has any answer [6]. For LEX, an acyclic %JQ can be answered in $O\left( n\right)$ [7]. For full SUM,an acyclic $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ can be answered in $O\left( {n\log n}\right)$ if its maximal hyperedges are at most 2,and the converse is true if it is also self-join-free, assuming 3SUM [7].

超团假设意味着在$O\left( {n\text{polylog}n}\right)$时间内无法判定循环无自连接JQ是否有解[6]。对于LEX查询，无环%JQ可在$O\left( n\right)$时间内求解[7]。在3SUM假设下，完全SUM查询中最大超边不超过2的无环$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$可在$O\left( {n\log n}\right)$时间内求解；若同时无自连接，则逆命题亦成立[7]。

### 2.4 Message Passing

### 2.4 消息传递

Message passing is a common algorithmic pattern that many algorithms for acyclic JQs follow. For example, it allows us to count the number of answers to an acyclic JQ in linear time [3, 20]. Some of the algorithms that we develop also follow this pattern, that we abstractly describe below.

消息传递是无环JQ算法常用的计算范式，例如能在线性时间内统计无环JQ的答案数量[3,20]。我们开发的若干算法也遵循此范式，其抽象流程如下所述。

Preprocessing. Choose an arbitrary root for a join tree $T$ of the JQ,and materialize a distinct relation for every $T$ -node. For every parent node ${V}_{p}$ and child node ${V}_{c}$ ,group the ${V}_{c}$ -relation by the ${V}_{p} \cap  {V}_{c}$ variables. We will refer to these groups of tuples as join groups; a join group shares the same values for variables that appear in the parent node. The algorithm visits the relations in a bottom-up order of $T$ ,sending children-to-parent messages. The goal is to compute a value $\operatorname{val}\left( t\right)$ for each tuple $t$ of these relations,initialized according to the specific algorithm. Sometimes, it is convenient to add an artificial root node ${V}_{0} = \varnothing$ to the join tree,which refers to a zero-arity relation with a single tuple ${t}_{0} = \left( \right)$ . Tuple ${t}_{0}$ joins with all tuples of the previous root and its purpose is only to gather the final result at the end of the bottom-up pass.

预处理。为JQ的连接树$T$任选根节点，并为每个$T$节点物化独立关系表。对每个父节点${V}_{p}$和子节点${V}_{c}$，将${V}_{c}$关系表按${V}_{p} \cap  {V}_{c}$变量分组（这些元组组称为连接组，组内元组在父节点变量上取值相同）。算法按$T$自底向上顺序访问关系表，传递子到父消息，目标是为每个元组$t$计算算法特定的初始值$\operatorname{val}\left( t\right)$。有时需在连接树中添加人工根节点${V}_{0} = \varnothing$，该节点引用包含单个元组${t}_{0} = \left( \right)$的零元关系，其作用仅是在自底向上遍历结束时收集最终结果——元组${t}_{0}$将与原根节点所有元组连接。

Messages. As we traverse the relations in bottom-up order, every tuple $t$ emits its $\operatorname{val}\left( t\right)$ . These messages are aggregated as follows:

消息传递。自底向上遍历过程中，每个元组$t$发送其$\operatorname{val}\left( t\right)$值，消息按以下方式聚合：

(1) Messages emitted by tuples ${t}^{\prime }$ in a join group are aggregated with an operator $\oplus$ . The result is sent to all parent-relation tuples that agree with the join values of the group.

(1) 同一连接组内元组${t}^{\prime }$发出的消息通过$\oplus$运算符聚合，结果传递给与该组连接值匹配的所有父关系元组。

(2) A tuple $t$ computes $\operatorname{val}\left( t\right)$ by aggregating the messages received from all children in the join tree, together with the initial value of $\operatorname{val}\left( t\right)$ ,with an operator $\otimes$ .

(2) 元组$t$通过运算符$\otimes$，聚合来自连接树中所有子节点接收到的消息以及$\operatorname{val}\left( t\right)$的初始值，计算出$\operatorname{val}\left( t\right)$。

Example 2.1 (Count). To count the JQ answers, we initialize $\operatorname{cnt}\left( t\right)  = 1$ for all tuples $t$ ,set $\oplus$ to product $\left( \prod \right)$ ,and $\otimes$ to sum $\left( \sum \right)$ . Figure 1 illustrates how messages are aggregated so that $\operatorname{cnt}\left( t\right)$ is the number of partial answers for the subtree rooted at $t$ . To get the final count,we sum the counts in the root relation $(9 + 4 = {13}$ in the example),e.g.,by introducing the artificial root-node tuple ${t}_{0}$ .

示例2.1（计数）。为了统计JQ答案数量，我们为所有元组$t$初始化$\operatorname{cnt}\left( t\right)  = 1$，将$\oplus$设为乘积$\left( \prod \right)$，$\otimes$设为求和$\left( \sum \right)$。图1展示了如何聚合消息，使得$\operatorname{cnt}\left( t\right)$成为以$t$为根的子树的局部答案数量。最终计数通过汇总根关系$(9 + 4 = {13}$中的数值获得（例如通过引入人工根节点元组${t}_{0}$）。

## 3 DIVIDE-AND-CONQUER FRAMEWORK

## 3 分治框架

We describe a general divide-and-conquer framework for acyclic $\% \mathrm{{JQs}}$ that applies to different ranking functions. It follows roughly the same structure as linear-time selection [5] in a given array of elements. This classic algorithm searches for the element at a desired index $k$ in the array by "pivoting". In every iteration,it selects a pivot element and creates three array partitions: elements that are lower, equal, and higher than the pivot. Depending on the partition sizes and the value of $k$ ,it chooses one partition and continues with that, thereby reducing the number of candidate elements. We adapt the high-level steps of this algorithm to our setting. The crucial challenge is that we do not have access to the materialized array of query answers (which can be very large), but only to the input database and JQ that produce them. In the following, we discuss the general structure of the algorithm and the subroutines that are required for it to work. In later sections, we then concretely specify these subroutines.

我们描述了一个适用于无环$\% \mathrm{{JQs}}$的通用分治框架，该框架可适配不同排序函数。其基本结构与线性时间选择算法[5]类似，后者通过在给定元素数组中"枢轴分割"来寻找目标索引$k$处的元素。每次迭代时，算法选取枢轴元素并创建三个数组分区：小于、等于和大于枢轴的元素。根据分区大小和$k$的值，算法选择其中一个分区继续处理，从而缩减候选元素规模。我们将此算法的高层步骤适配到当前场景，关键挑战在于无法直接访问具体化的查询答案数组（可能非常庞大），只能通过输入数据库和生成答案的JQ进行操作。下文将讨论算法的整体结构及所需子程序，具体子程序实现将在后续章节详述。

Pivot selection. We define what constitutes a "good" pivot. Intuitively, it is an element whose position is roughly in the middle of the ordering. With such a pivot, the partitioning step is guaranteed to eliminate a significant number of elements, resulting in quick convergence. Ideally, we would want to have the true median as our pivot because it is guaranteed to eliminate the largest fraction $\left( \frac{1}{2}\right)$ of elements. However,to achieve convergence in a logarithmic number of iterations, it is sufficient to choose any pivot that eliminates any constant fraction $c > 0$ of elements.

枢轴选择。我们定义了"优质"枢轴的标准：直观上应是排序中近似居中的元素。此类枢轴能确保分割步骤有效消除大量元素，实现快速收敛。理想情况下应选择真实中位数作为枢轴，因其可保证消除最大比例$\left( \frac{1}{2}\right)$的元素。但为实现对数级迭代次数的收敛，只需选择能消除固定比例$c > 0$元素的枢轴即可。

Definition 3.1 (c-pivot). For a constant $c \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ and a set $Z$ equipped with a total order $\leq$ ,a $c$ -pivot $p$ for $Z$ is an element of $Z$ such that $\left| {\{ z \in  Z \mid  p \leq  z\} }\right|  \geq  c\left| Z\right|$ and $\left| {\{ z \in  Z \mid  p \geq  z\} }\right|  \geq  c\left| Z\right|$ .

定义3.1（c-枢轴）。对于常数$c \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$和具有全序$\leq$的集合$Z$，$Z$的$c$-枢轴$p$是指满足$\left| {\{ z \in  Z \mid  p \leq  z\} }\right|  \geq  c\left| Z\right|$且$\left| {\{ z \in  Z \mid  p \geq  z\} }\right|  \geq  c\left| Z\right|$的元素。

Our goal is to find such a $c$ -pivot for the set of query answers $Q\left( D\right)$ ordered by the given ranking function.

我们的目标是为按给定排序函数排列的查询答案集$Q\left( D\right)$找到这样的$c$-枢轴。

Partitioning. Assuming an appropriate query answer $p$ as our pivot, we use it to partition the query answers. This means that we want to separate the answers into those whose weight is less than, equal to, and greater than the weight of the pivot. Since we do not have access to the query answers, this partitioning step must be performed on the input database and JQ. The less-than and greater-than partitions can be described by the original JQ, together with inequality predicates: $\left( 1\right) w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  \prec  w\left( p\right)$ and $\left( 2\right) w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  \succ  w\left( p\right)$ respectively. The equal-to partition can be assumed to contain all answers that do not fall into either of the other two.

分区处理。假设选取一个合适的查询答案$p$作为枢轴，我们利用它来划分查询答案。这意味着需要将答案分为权重小于、等于和大于枢轴权重的三部分。由于无法直接访问查询答案，此分区步骤必须在输入数据库和JQ（连接查询）上完成。小于和大于分区可通过原始JQ结合不等式谓词描述：分别用$\left( 1\right) w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  \prec  w\left( p\right)$和$\left( 2\right) w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  \succ  w\left( p\right)$表示。等于分区则默认包含不属于前两类的所有答案。

Trimming inequalities. If we materialize as database relations the inequalities that arise from the partitioning step, their size can be very large. For example,the inequality ${x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < 0$ for three variables ${x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}$ has a listing representation of size $O\left( {n}^{3}\right)$ . However, in certain cases it is possible to represent them more efficiently,e.g.,in space $O\left( {n\text{polylog}n}\right)$ ,by modifying the original JQ and database. We call this process "trimming."

不等式修剪。若将分区步骤产生的不等式具体化为数据库关系，其规模可能非常庞大。例如，针对三个变量${x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}$的不等式${x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < 0$，其列举表示的大小为$O\left( {n}^{3}\right)$。但在某些情况下，可通过修改原始JQ和数据库以更高效方式（如$O\left( {n\text{polylog}n}\right)$空间内）表示这些不等式，此过程称为"修剪"。

Definition 3.2 (Predicate Trimming). Given a JQ $Q$ and a predicate $P\left( U\right)$ with variables $U \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ ,a trimming of $P\left( U\right)$ from $Q$ receives a database $D$ and returns a ${JQ}{Q}^{\prime }$ of size $O\left( \left| Q\right| \right)$ and with $\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( {Q}^{\prime }\right)$ ,and a database ${D}^{\prime }$ for which there exists an $O\left( 1\right)$ -computable bijection from ${Q}^{\prime }\left( {D}^{\prime }\right)$ to $\left( {Q \land  P}\right) \left( D\right)$ . Trimming time is the time required to construct ${Q}^{\prime }$ and ${D}^{\prime }$ .

定义3.2（谓词修剪）。给定JQ$Q$和含变量$U \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$的谓词$P\left( U\right)$，从$Q$修剪$P\left( U\right)$的过程会接收数据库$D$，并返回大小为$O\left( \left| Q\right| \right)$且满足$\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( {Q}^{\prime }\right)$的${JQ}{Q}^{\prime }$，以及一个存在$O\left( 1\right)$可计算双射（从${Q}^{\prime }\left( {D}^{\prime }\right)$到$\left( {Q \land  P}\right) \left( D\right)$）的数据库${D}^{\prime }$。修剪时间指构建${Q}^{\prime }$和${D}^{\prime }$所需时间。

Efficient trimmings of predicates are for instance known for additive inequalities when the sum variables are found in adjacent JQ atoms [22] and for not-all-equals predicates [14], which are a generalization of non-equality $\left(  \neq  \right)$ . Ultimately,our ability to partition and the success of our approach relies on the existence of efficient trimmings of inequalities that involve the aggregate function.

高效的谓词修剪实例包括：当求和变量出现在相邻JQ原子中时的加法不等式[22]，以及非全等谓词[14]——这是非等式$\left(  \neq  \right)$的推广形式。最终，我们实现分区的能力及方法有效性取决于涉及聚合函数的不等式是否存在高效修剪方案。

Choosing a partition. After we obtain three new JQs and corresponding databases by trimming, we count their query answers to determine where the desired index (calculated from the given percentage) falls into. To ensure that this can be done in linear time, we want all JQs to be acyclic, and so we restrict ourselves to trimmings that do not alter the acyclicity of the JQs. To keep track of the candidate query answers, we maintain two weights low and high as bounds, which define a contiguous region in the sorted array of query answers. Every iteration then applies trimming for two additional inequalities $w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  >$ low and $w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  <$ high in order to restrict the search to the current candidate set.

选择分区。通过修剪获得三个新JQ及对应数据库后，我们统计其查询答案数量以确定目标索引（根据给定百分比计算）的落点。为保证线性时间复杂度，要求所有JQ均为无环结构，故仅采用不改变JQ无环性的修剪方案。为追踪候选查询答案，我们维护low和high两个权重边界值，用于定义查询答案排序数组中的连续区域。每次迭代会针对$w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  >$low和$w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  <$high两个新增不等式实施修剪，从而将搜索限定在当前候选集内。

Termination. The algorithm terminates when the desired index falls into the equal partition since any of its answers, including our pivot,is a $\phi$ -quantile. ${}^{4}$ It also terminates when the number of candidate answers is sufficiently small, by calling the Yannakakis algorithm [24] to materialize them and then applying linear-time selection [5]. With $c$ -pivots,we eliminate at least $c\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$ answers in every iteration; hence,the candidate query answers will be $O\left( n\right)$ after a logarithmic number of iterations. Notice that our trimming definition allows the new database to be larger than the starting one, so the database size may increase across iterations. However, the number of JQ answers decreases, ensuring termination.

终止条件。当目标索引落入均等分区时算法终止，因为包括枢轴点在内的任何答案都是一个$\phi$分位数。${}^{4}$当候选答案数量足够小时，算法也会通过调用Yannakakis算法[24]将其物化，再应用线性时间选择算法[5]来终止。使用$c$枢轴时，每次迭代至少消除$c\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$个答案；因此经过对数次迭代后，候选查询答案将缩减至$O\left( n\right)$。需注意，我们的修剪定义允许新数据库比初始库更大，因此数据库规模可能在迭代过程中增长。但JQ答案数量始终递减，确保算法必然终止。

It requires the implementation of two subroutines: (1) selecting a $c$ -pivot among the JQ answers, which we call "PIVOT", and (2) trimming of inequalities, which we call "TRIM". Thus, we have to continue searching in the index range from 401 to 1000 with ${k}^{\prime } = {500} - {400} - 1 = {99}$ . To create less-than and greater-than partitions in the next iteration, we will start with the original $Q$ and $D$ and apply inequalities $w\left( p\right)  \leq  {x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < w\left( {p}^{\prime }\right)$ and $w\left( {p}^{\prime }\right)  < {x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < \infty$ with some new pivot ${p}^{\prime }$ .

示例3.4. 假设$Q$是数据库$D$上的${R}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right)$查询，我们需要通过SUM函数以属性值作为权重计算中位数。首先调用PIVOT获取轴点答案$p$，据此创建两个分区：一个包含${x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < w\left( p\right)$，另一个包含${x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} > w\left( p\right)$。其次对这些不等式调用TRIM。根据已知构造方法[22]，这些不等式可在$O\left( {n\log n}\right)$时间内完成修剪。该构造会向两个关系添加新列和变量$v$。现在得到两个连接查询${Q}_{1\mathrm{t}}$和${Q}_{\mathrm{{gt}}}$，分别作用于数据库${D}_{1\mathrm{t}}$和${D}_{\mathrm{{gt}}}$。例如${Q}_{1\mathrm{t}}$即${R}_{{1}_{1\mathrm{t}}}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},v}\right) ,{R}_{{2}_{1\mathrm{t}}}\left( {v,{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right)$。假设$\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|  = {1001}$，则所需索引为$k = {500}$（采用零基索引）。若$\left| {{Q}_{1\mathrm{t}}\left( {D}_{1\mathrm{t}}\right) }\right|  = {400}$且$\left| {{Q}_{\mathrm{{gt}}}\left( {D}_{\mathrm{{gt}}}\right) }\right|  = {600}$，可推断中间分区包含1个权重为$w\left( p\right)$的答案。因此需在索引范围401至1000内继续搜索，此时${k}^{\prime } = {500} - {400} - 1 = {99}$。在下轮迭代中创建小于和大于分区时，将从原始$Q$和$D$出发，应用不等式$w\left( p\right)  \leq  {x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < w\left( {p}^{\prime }\right)$和$w\left( {p}^{\prime }\right)  < {x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < \infty$及新轴点${p}^{\prime }$。

In Section 4, we will show that an efficient algorithm for PIVOT exists for any subset-monotone ranking function. For TRIM, the situation is more tricky and no generic solution is known. For each ranking function, we design a trimming algorithm tailored to it. This is precisely where we encounter the known conditional hardness of SUM [7]. For example, a quasilinear trimming for $Q\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:-}} {R}_{1}\left( {x}_{1}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( {x}_{2}\right) ,{R}_{3}\left( {x}_{3}\right)$ and ${x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < 0$ would violate our 3sum hypothesis (see Section 2.3) because it would allow us to count the number of answers in the less-than and greater-than partitions. In Section 5, we will show that efficient trimmings exist for MIN/MAX and LEX, as well as (partial) SUM in certain cases.

在第4节中，我们将证明对于任意子集单调的排序函数，都存在针对PIVOT的高效算法。而对于TRIM而言，情况更为复杂，目前尚无通用解决方案。针对每种排序函数，我们都需要设计专门的修剪算法。正是在这里，我们遇到了SUM问题已知的条件性计算困难[7]。例如，若对$Q\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:-}} {R}_{1}\left( {x}_{1}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( {x}_{2}\right) ,{R}_{3}\left( {x}_{3}\right)$和${x}_{1} + {x}_{2} + {x}_{3} < 0$采用拟线性修剪，将违背我们的3sum假设（参见第2.3节），因为这将使我们能够统计小于和大于分区中的答案数量。第5节将展示，对于MIN/MAX和LEX排序函数，以及某些情况下的（部分）SUM，存在高效的修剪方法。

### 3.1 Adaptation for Approximate Quantiles

### 3.1 近似分位数的适配方法

Since $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ can be intractable (under our efficiency yardstick) for some ranking functions such as SUM [7],we aim for $\epsilon$ -approximate quantiles. We can obtain a randomized approximation by the standard technique of sampling answers uniformly and taking as estimate the $\phi$ -quantile of the sample (e.g.,as done by Doleschal et al. [9] for quantile queries in a different model). Concentration theorems such as Hoeffding Inequality imply that it suffices to collect $O\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ samples and repeat the process $O\left( {\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }\right)$ times (and select the median of the estimates) to get an $\epsilon$ -approximation with probability $1 - \delta$ . So,it suffices to be able to efficiently sample uniformly a random answer of an acyclic JQ; we can do so using linear-time algorithms for constructing a logarithmic-time random-access structure for the answers [6, 8].

由于对于SUM[7]等某些排序函数，$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$可能难以处理（根据我们的效率标准），我们转而寻求$\epsilon$-近似分位数。通过标准抽样技术，我们可以获得随机近似解：均匀采样答案并以样本的$\phi$-分位数作为估计值（如Doleschal等人[9]在不同模型的量化查询中所做）。霍夫丁不等式等浓度定理表明，只需收集$O\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$个样本并重复$O\left( {\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }\right)$次过程（选择估计值的中位数），就能以$1 - \delta$概率获得$\epsilon$-近似解。因此，关键在于能高效均匀采样无环JQ的随机答案，这可通过构建对数时间随机访问结构的线性时间算法实现[6,8]。

We will show that with our pivoting approach, we can obtain a deterministic approximation, which we found to be much more challenging than the randomized one. Pivot selection remains the same as in the exact algorithm, while for trimming (which as we explained is the missing piece for SUM), we introduce an approximate solution based on the notion of a lossy trimming. Intuitively, a lossy trimming does not retain all the JQ answers that satisfy a given predicate. Such a trimming results in some valid query answers being lost in each iteration and causes inaccuracy in the final index of the returned query answer. However, if the number of lost query answers is bounded, then we can also bound the error on the index.

我们将证明，通过枢轴选择方法可以获得确定性近似解——这比随机近似更具挑战性。枢轴选择与精确算法保持一致，而对于SUM问题缺失的修剪环节，我们提出了基于有损修剪概念的近似方案。直观而言，有损修剪不会保留满足给定谓词的所有JQ答案。这种修剪会导致每次迭代丢失部分有效查询答案，从而影响返回答案最终索引的准确性。但只要丢失答案数量可控，索引误差就能被限定。

## 4 GENERIC PIVOT SELECTION

## 4 通用枢轴选择

We describe a PIVOT algorithm for choosing a pivot element among the answers to an acyclic JQ. This is one of the two main subroutines of our quantile algorithm. We show that a $c$ -pivot can be computed in linear time for a large class of ranking functions.

我们提出一种PIVOT算法，用于在无环连接查询(JQ)的答案中选择枢轴元素。这是分位数算法中两个主要子例程之一。我们证明对于一大类排序函数，$c$-枢轴可在线性时间内计算完成。

LEMMA 4.1 (PIVOT SELECTION). Given an acyclic JQ Q over a database $D$ of size $n$ and a subset-monotone ranking function,a c-pivot of $Q\left( D\right)$ together with $c \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ can be computed in time $O\left( n\right)$ .

引理4.1(枢轴选择)。给定规模为$n$的数据库$D$上的无环连接查询Q，以及子集单调排序函数，可在$O\left( n\right)$时间内计算出$Q\left( D\right)$的c-枢轴及$c \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$。

### 4.1 Algorithm

### 4.1 算法

The key idea of our algorithm is the "median-of-medians", in similar spirit to classic linear-time selection [5] or selection for the $X + Y$ problem [13]. The main difference is that we apply the median-of-medians idea iteratively using message passing. The detailed pseudocode is given in Appendix B.

本算法的核心思想是"中位数的中位数"，其原理类似于经典线性时间选择算法[5]或$X + Y$问题的选择算法[13]。主要区别在于我们通过消息传递迭代应用中位数思想。详细伪代码见附录B。

Weighted median. An important operation for our algorithm is the weighted median, which selects the median of a set, assuming that every element appears a number of times equal to an assigned weight. ${}^{6}$ More formally,for a totally-ordered $\left(  \preccurlyeq  \right)$ set $Z$ and a multiplicity function $\beta  : Z \rightarrow  {\mathbb{N}}^{ + }$ ,the weighted median ${\operatorname{WMED}}_{ \preccurlyeq  }\left( {Z,\beta }\right)$ is the element at position $\left\lfloor  \frac{\left| B\right| }{2}\right\rfloor$ in the multiset $B = \left( {Z,\beta }\right)$ ordered by $\leq$ . The weighted median can be computed in time linear in $\left| Z\right|$ [13].

加权中位数。算法关键操作是加权中位数计算，该操作在假定每个元素出现次数等于指定权重的情况下选择集合的中位数。${}^{6}$严格定义为：对于全序集$Z$及其重数函数$\beta  : Z \rightarrow  {\mathbb{N}}^{ + }$，加权中位数${\operatorname{WMED}}_{ \preccurlyeq  }\left( {Z,\beta }\right)$是按$\leq$排序的多重集中位于$\left\lfloor  \frac{\left| B\right| }{2}\right\rfloor$位置的元素。加权中位数可在$\left| Z\right|$线性时间内计算完成[13]。

Message passing. Our algorithm employs the message-passing framework as outlined in Section 2.4 to compute $\operatorname{pivot}\left( t\right)$ for each tuple $t$ bottom-up. The computed pivot(t)is a partial query answer for the subtree rooted at $t$ and serves as a ${c}^{\prime }$ -pivot for these partial answers,for some ${c}^{\prime } \geq  c$ . Messages are aggregated as follows: (1) The $\oplus$ operator that combines pivots within a join group is the weighted median. The multiplicity function is given by the count of subtree answers and the order by the ranking function. The counts are also computed using message passing (see Section 2.4). (2) The $\otimes$ operator that combines pivots from different children is simply the union of (partial) assignments to variables.

消息传递。如2.4节所述，本算法采用消息传递框架自底向上计算每个元组$t$的$\operatorname{pivot}\left( t\right)$。计算所得pivot(t)是以$t$为根的子树的局部查询答案，并作为这些局部答案的${c}^{\prime }$-枢轴（其中${c}^{\prime } \geq  c$）。消息聚合规则如下：(1)连接组内合并枢轴的$\oplus$运算符采用加权中位数，重数函数由子树答案计数决定，排序依据为排序函数。计数同样通过消息传递计算（见2.4节）；(2)合并不同子节点枢轴的$\otimes$运算符仅需对变量(部分)赋值取并集。

Example 4.2. Consider the binary-join ${R}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right)$ under full SUM. Assume ${R}_{1}$ is the parent in the join tree with tuple weights ${x}_{1} + {x}_{2}$ ,while ${R}_{2}$ is the child with tuple weights ${x}_{3}$ . First, PIVOT groups the ${R}_{2}$ tuples by ${x}_{2}$ and,for each group,it finds the median ${x}_{3}$ value. The message from ${R}_{2}$ to ${R}_{1}$ is a mapping from ${x}_{2}$ values to (1) the count of ${R}_{2}$ tuples that contain the ${x}_{2}$ value and (2) the median ${x}_{3}$ value over these tuples. Then,every tuple ${r}_{1} \in  {R}_{1}$ unions its ${x}_{1},{x}_{2}$ values with the incoming ${x}_{3}$ ,obtaining a pivot $\left( {r}_{1}\right)  = \left( {{x1},{x2},{x3}}\right)$ tuple. To compute the final pivot,PIVOT takes the median of these pivot $\left( {r}_{1}\right)$ tuples,ranked by ${x1} + {x2} + {x3}$ , and weighted by the count of ${R}_{2}$ tuples that contain the ${x}_{2}$ value.

例4.2 考虑全SUM聚合下的二元连接${R}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right)$。假设${R}_{1}$是连接树中元组权重为${x}_{1} + {x}_{2}$的父节点，而${R}_{2}$是元组权重为${x}_{3}$的子节点。首先，PIVOT算法按${x}_{2}$对${R}_{2}$元组分组，并为每组找到${x}_{3}$的中位数值。从${R}_{2}$发送到${R}_{1}$的消息是${x}_{2}$值到（1）包含该${x}_{2}$值的${R}_{2}$元组计数和（2）这些元组${x}_{3}$中位数的映射。接着，每个元组${r}_{1} \in  {R}_{1}$将其${x}_{1},{x}_{2}$值与传入的${x}_{3}$取并集，得到枢轴$\left( {r}_{1}\right)  = \left( {{x1},{x2},{x3}}\right)$元组。为计算最终枢轴，PIVOT对这些按${x1} + {x2} + {x3}$排序且以包含${x}_{2}$值的${R}_{2}$元组计数加权的枢轴$\left( {r}_{1}\right)$元组取中位数。

Example 4.3. Figure 2 shows how an $R$ -tuple computes its pivot under full SUM for the example of Figure 1. Green values in brackets [.] represent the counts, while the orange assignments are the computed pivots for each tuple or join group. From a leaf node like $S$ or $U$ ,messages are simply the relation tuples,each with multiplicity 1. To see how a pivot is computed within a join group, consider the $T$ -node group. The pivot of tuple(1,6)is smaller than the pivot of tuple(1,7)according to the ranking function because $1 + 6 + 8 < 1 + 7 + 9$ . The weighted median selects the latter because it has multiplicity 2 (that is the group size for ${x}_{4} = 6$ in the child $U$ ).

例4.3 图2展示了图1示例中$R$元组在全SUM下计算枢轴的过程。方括号[.]内的绿色值表示计数，橙色标注则是各元组或连接组计算的枢轴值。对于$S$或$U$这样的叶节点，消息就是关系元组（每个 multiplicity为1）。以$T$节点组为例说明连接组内枢轴计算：根据排序函数，元组(1,6)的枢轴小于元组(1,7)，因为$1 + 6 + 8 < 1 + 7 + 9$。加权中位数选择后者因其multiplicity为2（即子节点$U$中${x}_{4} = 6$的组大小）。

Pivot accuracy. As we will show, applying our two operations (weighted median, union) results in a loss of accuracy for the pivots, captured by the $c$ parameter. The pivots computed for the leaf relations are the true medians (thus $\frac{1}{2}$ -pivots),but the $c$ parameter decreases as we go up the join tree. Fortunately, this can be bounded by a function of the query size,making our final result a $c$ -pivot with a $c$ value that is independent of the data size $n$ . The algorithm keeps track of the $c$ value for every node and upon termination,the $c$ value of the root is returned.

枢轴精度。如我们将证明的，应用加权中位数和并集这两个操作会导致枢轴精度损失，该损失由$c$参数表征。叶关系计算的枢轴是真实中位数（即$\frac{1}{2}$-枢轴），但随着沿连接树上溯，$c$参数递减。幸运的是，这可以被查询大小的函数限定，使得最终结果是具有与数据规模$n$无关的$c$值的$c$-枢轴。算法追踪每个节点的$c$值，终止时返回根节点的$c$值。

Running time. The time is linear in the database size. The weighted median and count operations are only performed once for every join group and both take linear time. Each tuple is visited only once, and all operations per tuple (e.g., number of child relations, finding the joining group, union) depend only on the query size.

运行时间。时间复杂度与数据库大小呈线性关系。加权中位数和计数操作仅对每个连接组执行一次，且均为线性时间。每个元组仅访问一次，所有元组操作（如子关系数量、查找连接组、并集）仅取决于查询大小。

### 4.2 Correctness

### 4.2 正确性证明

First, we show that PIVOT returns a valid query answer. The concern is that a variable $x$ may be assigned to different values in the pivots that are unioned from different branches of the tree. As we show next, this cannot happen because of the join tree properties.

首先证明PIVOT返回有效查询结果。潜在问题是变量$x$可能在来自树不同分支的并集枢轴中被赋予不同值。如下所示，由于连接树特性，这种情况不会发生。

LEMMA 4.4. Let $V$ be a join-tree node and ${R}_{V}$ the corresponding relation. For all $t \in  {R}_{V}$ ,the variable assignment pivot(t) computed by PIVOT is a partial query answer for the subtree rooted at $V$ .

引理4.4 设$V$为连接树节点，${R}_{V}$为对应关系。对于所有$t \in  {R}_{V}$，由PIVOT计算的变量赋值pivot(t)都是以$V$为根的子树的局部查询结果。

Next, we show how the accuracy of the pivot is affected by repeated weighted median and union operations.

接着我们证明重复加权中位数和并集操作如何影响枢轴点的准确性。

LEMMA 4.5. Given $r$ disjoint sets ${Z}_{1},\ldots ,{Z}_{r}$ equipped with a total order $\leq$ and corresponding c-pivots ${p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{r}$ ,then $\operatorname{WMED}\left( {\left\{  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{r}}\right\}  ,\beta }\right)$ with $\beta \left( {p}_{i}\right)  = \left| {Z}_{i}\right|$ ,for all $i \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ is a $\frac{c}{2}$ -pivot for ${Z}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Z}_{r}$ .

引理4.5 给定$r$个互斥集${Z}_{1},\ldots ,{Z}_{r}$（配备全序关系$\leq$）及其对应c-枢轴${p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{r}$，则对于所有$i \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$，$\operatorname{WMED}\left( {\left\{  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{r}}\right\}  ,\beta }\right)$（其中$\beta \left( {p}_{i}\right)  = \left| {Z}_{i}\right|$）构成${Z}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Z}_{r}$的$\frac{c}{2}$-枢轴。

LEMMA 4.6. Assume a join-tree node $V$ ,its corresponding relation ${R}_{V}$ ,its children ${V}_{1},\ldots ,{V}_{r}$ ,a subset-monotone ranking function,and $c$ -pivots ${p}_{i},i \in  r$ for the partial answers which are rooted at ${V}_{i}$ and restricted to those that agree with $t$ . Then, $t \cup  {p}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {p}_{r}$ is a ${c}^{r}$ -pivot for the partial answers rooted at $t$ .

引理4.6 假设连接树节点$V$及其对应关系${R}_{V}$、子节点${V}_{1},\ldots ,{V}_{r}$、子集单调排序函数，以及以${V}_{i}$为根且限定与$t$相符的局部结果${p}_{i},i \in  r$-枢轴，则$t \cup  {p}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {p}_{r}$构成以$t$为根的局部结果的${c}^{r}$-枢轴。

With the three above lemmas, we can complete the proof of Lemma 4.1 by induction on the join tree.

综合上述三个引理，通过对连接树进行归纳可完成引理4.1的证明。

## 5 EXACT TRIMMINGS

## 5 精确修剪

We now look into trimmings for different types of inequality predicates that arise in the partitioning step of our quantile algorithm (i.e., the TRIM subroutine). Our construction essentially removes these predicates from the query, while ensuring that the modified query can only produce answers that satisfy them.

现在研究分位数算法划分步骤（即TRIM子程序）中各类不等式谓词的修剪方法。我们的构造本质上是将这些谓词从查询中移除，同时确保修改后的查询仅生成满足原谓词的结果。

### 5.1 MIN/MAX

### 5.1 最小值/最大值

When the ranking function is MIN or MAX, then we need to trim predicates of the type $\min \left\{  {U}_{w}\right\}   < \lambda ,\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$ .

当排序函数为MIN或MAX时，需修剪$\min \left\{  {U}_{w}\right\}   < \lambda ,\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$类谓词。

Example 5.1. Suppose the ranking function is MAX, the weighted variables are ${U}_{w} = \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}$ ,attribute weights are equal to database values, and our pivot has weight 10 . To create the appropriate partitions,we trim predicates $\max \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}   < {10}$ and $\max \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}   > {10}$ . Enforcing $\max \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}   < {10}$ is straightforward by removing from the database all tuples with values greater than or equal to 10 for either of the three variables. For $\max \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}   > {10}$ ,there are three ways to satisfy the predicate: (1) ${x}_{1} > {10}$ ,(2) ${x}_{1} \leq  {10} \land  {x}_{2} > {10}$ ,or (3) ${x}_{1} \leq  {10} \land  {x}_{2} \leq  {10} \land  {x}_{3} > {10}$ . These three cases are disjoint and cover all possibilities. For each case, we create a fresh copy of the database and then enforce the predicates in linear time by filtering the tuples. Our JQ over one of these three databases produces a partition of the answers that satisfy the original inequality. To return a single database and JQ, we union the corresponding relations and distinguish between the three partitions by appending a partition identifier to every relation.

示例5.1 设排序函数为MAX，加权变量为${U}_{w} = \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}$，属性权重等于数据库值，枢轴点权重为10。创建分区时需修剪谓词$\max \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}   < {10}$和$\max \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}   > {10}$。对于$\max \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}   < {10}$，直接删除三个变量值大于等于10的元组即可；对于$\max \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}   > {10}$，存在三种满足方式：(1)${x}_{1} > {10}$，(2)${x}_{1} \leq  {10} \land  {x}_{2} > {10}$，或(3)${x}_{1} \leq  {10} \land  {x}_{2} \leq  {10} \land  {x}_{3} > {10}$。这三种情况互斥且完备。我们为每种情况创建数据库副本并通过线性时间元组过滤实施谓词。在这三个数据库上执行JQ将生成满足原不等式的答案分区。为返回单一数据库和JQ，我们合并相应关系并通过附加分区标识符加以区分。

Generalizing our example in a straightforward way, we show that trimmings of such inequalities exist for all acyclic JQs.

通过直接推广我们的示例，我们证明了对于所有无环JQ都存在此类不等式的修剪形式。

LEMMA 5.2. Given an acyclic $\mathcal{J}Q\mathcal{Q}$ ,variables ${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ , weight functions ${w}_{x} :$ dom $\rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$ for $x \in  {U}_{w}$ ,and $\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$ ,a trimming of $\mathop{\min }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  < \lambda ,\mathop{\min }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  > \lambda ,\mathop{\max }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  < \lambda ,$ or $\mathop{\max }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  > \lambda$ takes time $O\left( n\right)$ and returns an acyclic $\mathcal{J}Q$ .

引理5.2. 给定一个无环$\mathcal{J}Q\mathcal{Q}$、变量${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$、权重函数${w}_{x} :$定义域$\rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$作用于$x \in  {U}_{w}$、以及$\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$，对$\mathop{\min }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  < \lambda ,\mathop{\min }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  > \lambda ,\mathop{\max }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  < \lambda ,$或$\mathop{\max }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  > \lambda$进行修剪耗时$O\left( n\right)$并返回一个无环$\mathcal{J}Q$。

Combining Lemma 5.2 together with Lemma 4.1 and Lemma 3.3 gives us the following result:

将引理5.2与引理4.1及引理3.3相结合，我们得到以下结果：

THEOREM 5.3. Given an acyclic JQ over a database $D$ of size $n$ , MIN or MAX ranking function,and $\phi  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ ,the $\% \exists Q$ can be answered in time $O\left( {n\log n}\right)$ .

定理5.3. 给定作用于规模为$n$的数据库$D$上的无环JQ、MIN或MAX排序函数、以及$\phi  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$，可在$O\left( {n\log n}\right)$时间内解答$\% \exists Q$。

### 5.3 Partial SUM

### 5.3 部分求和

We now consider the case of SUM. While we previously gave a dichotomy [7] for all self-join-free JQs, this result is limited to full SUM. We now provide a more fine-grained dichotomy where certain variables may not participate in the ranking. For example, the 3-path JQ ${R}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right) ,{R}_{3}\left( {{x}_{3},{x}_{4}}\right)$ would be classified as intractable by the prior dichotomy, yet with weighted variables ${U}_{w} = \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}$ ,we show that it is in fact tractable.

现在我们讨论SUM的情况。虽然先前针对无自连接连接查询(JQ)给出了二分法[7]，但该结果仅限于完整SUM。本文提出更细粒度的二分法，允许部分变量不参与排序。例如，3路径JQ${R}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right) ,{R}_{3}\left( {{x}_{3},{x}_{4}}\right)$在原二分法中被归类为难解问题，但引入加权变量${U}_{w} = \left\{  {{x}_{1},{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right\}$后，我们证明其实际可解。

The positive side of our dichotomy requires a trimming of additive inequalities. We rely on a known algorithm that can be applied whenever the SUM variables appear in adjacent join-tree nodes.

二分法的积极面需要对加法不等式进行修剪。我们采用已知算法，当SUM变量出现在相邻连接树节点时即可应用。

LEMMA 5.5 ([22]). Given a set of variables ${U}_{w}$ ,let $Q$ be the class of acyclic $\mathcal{J}Q$ s $Q$ for which there exists a join tree where ${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ belong to adjacent join-tree nodes. Then,for all $Q \in  Q$ ,weight functions ${w}_{x} :$ dom $\rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$ for $x \in  {U}_{w}$ ,and $\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$ ,a trimming of $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  < \lambda$ or $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  > \lambda$ takes time $O\left( {n\log n}\right)$ and returns a $\mathcal{J}Q{Q}^{\prime } \in  Q$ .

引理5.5([22])。给定变量集${U}_{w}$，令$Q$为无环$\mathcal{J}Q$类$Q$，若存在连接树使${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$属于相邻节点，则对所有$Q \in  Q$、权重函数${w}_{x} :$定义域$\rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$、$x \in  {U}_{w}$及$\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$，修剪$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  < \lambda$或$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  > \lambda$耗时$O\left( {n\log n}\right)$并返回$\mathcal{J}Q{Q}^{\prime } \in  Q$。

We are now in a position to state our dichotomy:

现在我们可以陈述二分法：

THEOREM 5.6. Let $Q$ be a self-join-free $\mathcal{J}Q,\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$ its hypergraph, and ${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ the variables of a SUM ranking function.

定理5.6。设$Q$为无自连接$\mathcal{J}Q,\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$的超图，${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$为SUM排序函数的变量。

- If $\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$ is acyclic,any set of independent variables of ${U}_{w}$ is of size at most 2,and any chordless path between two ${U}_{w}$ variables is of length at most 3,then $\% {JQ}$ can be answered in $O\left( {n{\log }^{2}n}\right)$ .

- Otherwise, $\% {JQ}$ cannot be answered in $O\left( {n\text{polylog}n}\right)$ ,assuming 3SUM and HYPERCLIQUE.

- 否则，在3SUM和HYPERCLIQUE假设下，$\% {JQ}$无法在$O\left( {n\text{polylog}n}\right)$内求解。

We note that the positive side also applies to JQs with self-joins.

## 6 APPROXIMATE TRIMMING FOR SUM

## 6 SUM的近似修剪

We now move on to devise an $\epsilon$ -lossy trimming for additive inequalities in order to get a deterministic approximation for SUM and JQs beyond those covered by Theorem 5.6.

接下来我们设计$\epsilon$-有损修剪方法处理加法不等式，为定理5.6未覆盖的SUM和JQ提供确定性近似解。

LEMMA 6.1. Given an acyclic $\mathcal{J}Q\mathcal{Q}$ ,variables ${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ , weight functions ${w}_{x} : \operatorname{dom} \rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$ for $x \in  {U}_{w},\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$ ,and $\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ ,an $\epsilon$ -lossy trimming of $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  < \lambda$ or $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  > \lambda$ takes time $O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{2}n\log \frac{n}{\epsilon }}\right)$ and returns an acyclic $\mathcal{J}Q$ .

引理6.1. 给定一个无环的$\mathcal{J}Q\mathcal{Q}$、变量${U}_{w} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$、针对$x \in  {U}_{w},\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$的权重函数${w}_{x} : \operatorname{dom} \rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$以及$\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$，对$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  < \lambda$或$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {U}_{w}}}{w}_{x}\left( x\right)  > \lambda$进行$\epsilon$-有损修剪耗时$O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{2}n\log \frac{n}{\epsilon }}\right)$并返回一个无环的$\mathcal{J}Q$。

This, together with Lemmas 3.6 and 4.1 gives us the following:

结合引理3.6和引理4.1，我们得到以下结论：

THEOREM 6.2. Given an acyclic JQ Q over a database $D$ of size $n$ , SUM ranking function, $\phi  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ ,and $\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ ,the $\epsilon$ -approximate $\% {JQ}$ can be answered in time $O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{5}n\log \frac{n}{\epsilon }}\right)$ .

定理6.2. 给定一个作用于规模为$n$的数据库$D$上的无环连接查询Q、SUM排序函数$\phi  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$以及$\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$，可在$O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{5}n\log \frac{n}{\epsilon }}\right)$时间内回答$\epsilon$-近似$\% {JQ}$。

To achieve the trimming, we adapt an algorithm of Abo-Khamis et al. [2], which we refer to as ApxCNT. It computes an approximate count (or more generally, a semiring aggregate) over the answers to acyclic JQs with additive inequalities. In contrast, we need not only the count of answers, but an efficient relational representation of them as JQ answers over a new database. We only discuss the case of less-than $\left(  < \right)$ ,since the case of greater-than $\left(  > \right)$ is symmetric. The detailed pseudocode is given in Appendix D.

为实现修剪，我们改进了Abo-Khamis等人[2]的算法（称为ApxCNT）。该算法通过带加法不等式的无环连接查询答案计算近似计数（或更广义上的半环聚合）。与之不同，我们不仅需要答案计数，还需要将其高效地表示为新数据库上的连接查询答案关系。本文仅讨论小于$\left(  < \right)$的情况，因大于$\left(  > \right)$的情况对称。详细伪代码见附录D。

Message passing. APxCNT uses message passing (see Section 2.4). We first describe the exact, but costly, version of the algorithm. The message sent by a tuple is a multiset containing the (partial) sums of partial query answers in its subtree. Messages are aggregated as follows: (1) The $\oplus$ operator that combines multisets within a join group is multiset union $\left( \uplus \right)$ . (2) The $\otimes$ operator that combines multisets from different children is pairwise summation (applied as a binary operator). The messages emitted by the root-node tuples contain all query-answer sums, which can be leveraged to count the number of answers that satisfy the inequality.

消息传递. APxCNT采用消息传递机制（参见第2.4节）。我们首先描述精确但计算代价高的算法版本：元组传递的消息是其子树中部分查询答案（部分）和的多重集合。消息聚合规则如下：(1) 连接组内合并多重集合的$\oplus$运算符采用多重集并集$\left( \uplus \right)$；(2) 合并不同子节点多重集合的$\otimes$运算符采用两两求和（作为二元运算符应用）。根节点元组发出的消息包含所有查询答案和，可用于统计满足不等式的答案数量。

Sketching. Sending all possible sums up the join tree is intractable since, in the worst case, their number is equal to the number of JQ answers. For this reason, APxCNT applies sketching to compress the messages. The basic idea is to replace different elements in a multiset with the same element; the efficiency gain is due to the fact that an element $s$ that appears $r$ times can be represented as $s \times  r$ . In more detail,the multiset elements are split into buckets, and subsequently, each element within a bucket is replaced by the maximum element of the bucket. A sketched multi-set $L$ is denoted by ${\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right)$ ,where $\epsilon$ is a parameter that determines the number of buckets. Let ${ \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( L\right)$ be the number of elements of $L$ that are less than $\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$ . By choosing buckets appropriately,we can guarantee that ${ \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( {{\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right) }\right)$ is close to ${ \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( L\right)$ for all possible $\lambda$ .

草图技术。由于在最坏情况下，沿连接树发送所有可能的求和结果数量等同于连接查询(JQ)答案数，因此该操作具有计算不可行性。为此，APxCNT采用草图技术压缩消息流，其核心思想是将多重集中的不同元素替换为相同元素——这种优化的有效性源于：当元素$s$出现$r$次时，可表示为$s \times  r$。具体而言，首先将多重集元素划分至多个桶中，随后用各桶内最大元素替换该桶所有元素。经草图处理的多重集$L$记作${\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right)$，其中$\epsilon$为决定桶数量的参数。设${ \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( L\right)$表示$L$中小于$\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$的元素数量，通过合理设计桶划分策略，可确保对所有可能的$\lambda$，${ \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( {{\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right) }\right)$都能近似${ \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( L\right)$。

LEMMA 6.3 ( $\epsilon$ -SKETCH [2]). For a multiset $L \in  {\mathbb{N}}^{\mathbb{R}}$ and $\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ , we can construct a sketch ${\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right)$ with $O\left( {{\log }_{1 + \epsilon }\left| L\right| }\right)$ distinct elements such that for all $\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$ ,we have $\left( {1 - \epsilon }\right) { \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( L\right)  \leq  { \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( {{\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right) }\right)  \leq  { \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( L\right)$ .

引理6.3($\epsilon$-草图[2])。对于多重集$L \in  {\mathbb{N}}^{\mathbb{R}}$及参数$\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$，可构造仅含$O\left( {{\log }_{1 + \epsilon }\left| L\right| }\right)$个相异元素的草图${\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right)$，使得对所有$\lambda  \in  \mathbb{R}$都满足$\left( {1 - \epsilon }\right) { \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( L\right)  \leq  { \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( {{\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right) }\right)  \leq  { \downarrow  }_{\lambda }\left( L\right)$。

APXCNT sketches all messages and bounds the error incurred by the two message-passing operations $\left( {\oplus , \otimes  }\right)$ .

APXCNT对所有消息进行草图处理，并限定两种消息传递操作$\left( {\oplus , \otimes  }\right)$所产生的误差边界。

Relational representation. Our goal is to construct a relational representation of the JQ answers which satisfy the inequality that we want to trim. The key idea is to embed the sums contained in the messages of APxCNT into the database relations so that each tuple stores a unique sum and all answers in its subtree approximately have that sum. The reasoning behind this is that we can then remove from the database the root tuples whose associated sum does not satisfy the inequality. However, in APxCNT a message is a multiset of sums, and hence the main technical challenge we address below is how to achieve a unique sum per tuple (and its subtree).

关系表示。我们的目标是构建满足目标修剪不等式的JQ答案关系表示，其核心在于将APxCNT消息中的求和结果嵌入数据库关系，使每个元组存储唯一求和值，且其子树中所有答案近似具有该值。这样就能从数据库中移除关联求和值不满足不等式的根元组。然而APxCNT中的消息实为求和值的多重集，因此下文将重点解决如何实现每个元组(及其子树)对应唯一求和值这一技术难题。

Separating sums. Let $\sigma \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)$ be the message sent by a tuple ${t}^{\prime }$ in a child relation $S$ . Then,according to APxCNT,a tuple $t$ in the parent relation $R$ receives a message $\sigma \left( b\right)  = {\mathbb{S}}_{{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\left( {{ \uplus  }_{{t}^{\prime } \in  b}\sigma \left( {t}^{\prime }\right) }\right)$ for some ${\epsilon }^{\prime }$ and join group $b$ . We separate the sums in this multiset by creating a number of copies of $t$ ,equal to the number of distinct sums in $\sigma \left( b\right)$ . Each $t$ -copy is associated with a unique bucket $e$ ,described by a sum value ${e}_{s}$ and a multiplicity ${e}_{m}$ . To avoid duplicating query answers,we restrict each $t$ -copy to join only with the source tuples of its associated bucket $e$ ,i.e.,the child tuples ${t}^{\prime } \in  S$ whose messages were assigned to bucket $e$ during sketching.

分离求和。设$\sigma \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)$为子关系$S$中元组${t}^{\prime }$发送的消息。根据APxCNT算法，父关系$R$中的元组$t$会接收消息$\sigma \left( b\right)  = {\mathbb{S}}_{{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\left( {{ \uplus  }_{{t}^{\prime } \in  b}\sigma \left( {t}^{\prime }\right) }\right)$（对应某个${\epsilon }^{\prime }$和连接组$b$）。我们通过创建$t$的多个副本来分离这个多重集中的求和结果，副本数量等于$\sigma \left( b\right)$中不同求和值的数量。每个$t$副本与唯一桶$e$关联，该桶由求和值${e}_{s}$和重数${e}_{m}$描述。为避免重复查询结果，我们限制每个$t$副本仅能与其关联桶$e$的源元组连接，即那些在草图阶段消息被分配到桶$e$的子元组${t}^{\prime } \in  S$。

Example 6.4. Figure 3 illustrates how we embed messages into the database relations for a leaf relation $S$ and a parent relation $R$ with no other children, and assuming weights equal to attribute values. The messages from $S\left( {x,y}\right)$ to $R\left( {y,z}\right)$ are the sums $x + y$ (because $y$ -weights are assigned to $S$ ). After sketching their union using two buckets, sums 4 and 5 are both mapped to 5 ; we keep track of this with a multiplicity counter (shown as $\times  2$ ),reflecting the number of answers in the subtree. Upon reaching relation $R$ , the weight of the $R$ -tuple (which is the $z$ -value) is added to all sums. For our relational representation,we duplicate the $R$ -tuple and associate each copy with a unique sum. A copy corresponds to a bucket in the sketch,so we can trace its "source" $S$ -tuples,i.e.,those that belong to that bucket. Instead of joining with all $S$ -tuples like before,a copy now only joins with the source $S$ -tuples of the bucket via a new variable ${v}_{RS}$ that stores the sum and the multiplicity.

示例6.4。图3展示了如何将消息嵌入到无其他子节点的叶关系$S$和父关系$R$的数据库关系中（假设权重等于属性值）。从$S\left( {x,y}\right)$到$R\left( {y,z}\right)$的消息是求和结果$x + y$（因为$y$权重被分配给$S$）。使用两个桶草图化它们的并集后，求和值4和5都被映射到5；我们通过重数计数器（显示为$\times  2$）跟踪这个情况，反映子树中的答案数量。当到达关系$R$时，$R$元组的权重（即$z$值）被加到所有求和值上。在关系表示中，我们复制$R$元组并使每个副本对应唯一求和值。副本对应草图中的一个桶，因此可追溯其"源"$S$元组（即属于该桶的元组）。与先前和所有$S$元组连接不同，现在副本仅通过存储求和值和重数的新变量${v}_{RS}$与桶的源$S$元组连接。

Adjusting the sketch buckets. An issue we run into with the approach above is that the sum sent by a single tuple may be assigned to more than one bucket during sketching. To see why this is problematic,consider a tuple ${t}^{\prime }$ that sends $\sigma \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)  = 5 \times  {10}$ . For simplicity, assume these are the only values to be sketched and that the two buckets contain $5 \times  3$ and $5 \times  7$ . With these buckets,we will create two copies of a tuple $t$ in the parent and both will join with ${t}^{\prime }$ , because ${t}^{\prime }$ is the source tuple for both buckets. By doing so,we have effectively doubled the number of (partial) query answers since there are now 10 answers in the subtree of each copy. To resolve this issue,we need to guarantee that all elements in $\sigma \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)$ are assigned to the same bucket. We adjust the sketching ${\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right)$ of a multiset $L$ as follows. The $r$ buckets are determined by an increasing sequence of $r + 1$ indexes on an array that contains $L$ sorted. The first index is 0 and the last index is equal to $\left| L\right|  - 1$ (where $\left| L\right|$ takes into account the multiplicities). Consider three consecutive indexes $i,j,k$ which define two consecutive buckets where the values at the borders are the same,i.e., $L\left\lbrack  {j - 1}\right\rbrack   = L\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack$ . Let ${j}^{\prime }$ and ${j}^{\prime \prime }$ be the smallest and largest indexes that contain $L\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack$ in the buckets $i - j$ and $j - k$ , respectively. We replace the indexes $i,j,k$ with $i,{j}^{\prime },{j}^{\prime \prime } + 1,k$ (and if 2 consecutive indexes are the same, then we remove that bucket). As a result,all values $L\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack$ from these two buckets now fall into the same bucket. We repeat this process for every two consecutive buckets. This can at most double the number of buckets, which, as we show, does not affect the guarantee of Lemma 6.3.

调整草图桶。上述方法遇到的一个问题是，单个元组发送的求和值在草图绘制过程中可能被分配到多个桶中。理解其问题本质，假设元组${t}^{\prime }$发送了$\sigma \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)  = 5 \times  {10}$。为简化说明，假定这些是仅需绘制的值，且两个桶分别包含$5 \times  3$和$5 \times  7$。此时父节点会创建元组$t$的两个副本，由于${t}^{\prime }$是两个桶的源元组，这两个副本都会与${t}^{\prime }$连接。这实际上使（部分）查询答案数量翻倍，因为每个副本子树中现在有10个答案。为解决该问题，必须保证$\sigma \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)$中所有元素分配到同一桶。我们按以下方式调整多重集$L$的草图${\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right)$：通过排序后的$L$数组上递增的$r + 1$索引序列确定$r$个桶，首索引为0，末索引等于$\left| L\right|  - 1$（其中$\left| L\right|$考虑重复计数）。当三个连续索引$i,j,k$定义的两个相邻桶边界值相同时（即$L\left\lbrack  {j - 1}\right\rbrack   = L\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack$），取包含$L\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack$的最小索引${j}^{\prime }$和最大索引${j}^{\prime \prime }$分别对应桶$i - j$和$j - k$。我们将索引$i,j,k$替换为$i,{j}^{\prime },{j}^{\prime \prime } + 1,k$（若相邻索引相同则移除该桶），使得这两个桶中所有$L\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack$值归入同一桶。该过程最多使桶数量翻倍，但不影响引理6.3的保证。

Binary join tree. The running time of our algorithm (in particular the logarithmic-factor exponent) depends on the maximum number of children of a join-tree node. This is because we handle each parent-child node pair separately, and each child results in the parent relation growing by the size of the messages, which is logarithmic. To achieve the time bound stated in Lemma 6.1, we impose a binary join tree, i.e., every node has at most two children. Such a join tree can be constructed by creating copies of a node that has multiple children, connecting these copies in a chain, and distributing the original children among them. In the worst case, this doubles the number of nodes in the join tree (hence the number of relations that we materialize), but it does not affect the data complexity.

二叉连接树。算法运行时间（特别是对数因子指数）取决于连接树节点最大子节点数。因为我们独立处理每个父子节点对，且每个子节点会使父关系随消息规模（对数级）增长。为实现引理6.1的时间界，我们采用二叉连接树（每个节点至多两个子节点）。可通过复制多子节点构建此类树：将副本连成链并分配原子节点。最坏情况下节点数翻倍（即物化关系数增加），但不影响数据复杂度。

## 7 CONCLUSIONS

## 7 结论

We can often answer quantile queries over joins of multiple relations much more efficiently than it takes to materialize the result of the join. Here, we adopted quasilinear time as our yardstick of efficiency. With our divide-and-conquer technique, we recovered known results (for lexicographic orders) and established new ones (for partial sums, minimum, and maximum). We also showed how the approach can be adapted for deterministic approximations.

相较于物化连接结果，我们常能更高效地回答多关系连接的分位数查询。本文采用拟线性时间作为效率基准，通过分治技术重现了词典序的已知结果，并建立了部分和、最小值与最大值的新结论。同时展示了如何将该方法应用于确定性近似。

We restricted the discussion to JQs, that is, full Conjunctive Queries (CQs), and left open the treatment of non-full CQs (i.e., joins with projection). Most of our algorithms apply to CQs that are acyclic and free-connex, but it is not yet clear to us whether our results cover all tractable cases (under complexity assumptions). More general open directions are the generalization of the challenge to unions of CQs, and the establishment of nontrivial algorithms for general CQs beyond the acyclic ones.

我们限定讨论于JQ（即完全连接查询），未涉及非完全CQ（含投影的连接）。多数算法适用于无环且自由连接的CQ，但尚不确定结果是否覆盖所有可处理情形（基于复杂性假设）。更开放的探索方向包括：将挑战推广到CQ并集，以及为超越无环的一般CQ建立非平凡算法。

## A DETAILS OF DIVIDE-AND-CONQUER FRAMEWORK

## 分治框架实现细节

Algorithm 1 returns the desired (approximate) quantile for a given JQ, database, and ranking function, as presented in Section 3. The exact version is obtained by simply setting $\epsilon  = 0$ .

算法1如第3节所述，返回给定连接查询(JQ)、数据库和排序函数所需的(近似)分位数。精确版本只需设置$\epsilon  = 0$即可获得。

### A.1 Proof of Lemma 3.6

### A.1 引理3.6证明

Let ${Q}_{i}$ and ${D}_{i}$ be the JQ and database at the start of iteration $i$ , with $i \geq  0$ (these are the variables ${Q}^{\prime }$ and ${D}^{\prime }$ in Algorithm 1). Even though trimmings may increase the size of our queries by a constant factor (by the definition of trimming),all queries ${Q}_{i}$ have constant size. This is because we start every iteration with the original query $Q$ and every other query we construct is the result of applying at most two consecutive trimmings.

设${Q}_{i}$和${D}_{i}$分别表示第$i$次迭代开始时的连接查询和数据库，其中$i \geq  0$(对应算法1中的变量${Q}^{\prime }$和${D}^{\prime }$)。根据剪枝定义，虽然剪枝操作可能使查询规模以常数倍增长，但所有查询${Q}_{i}$始终保持常数规模。这是因为每次迭代都从原始查询$Q$开始，而后续构造的查询最多经过连续两次剪枝操作。

First,we bound the number of iterations. Iteration $i$ is guaranteed to eliminate at least $c\left| {{Q}_{i}\left( {D}_{i}\right) }\right|$ query answers because (i) we select a $c$ -pivot to partition and (ii) the lossy trimmings may result in more query answers being eliminated than they should, but never less. Consequently,at the beginning of iteration $i$ ,we have at most ${\left( 1 - c\right) }^{i}\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$ query answers remaining. The number of query answers is bounded by ${n}^{\ell }$ where $\ell$ is the number of atoms in $Q$ . If $I$ is the total number of iterations,then $I \leq  \left\lceil  {{\log }_{1/\left( {1 - c}\right) }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right| }\right\rceil   \leq$ $\left\lceil  {\ell {\log }_{1/\left( {1 - c}\right) }n}\right\rceil   = O\left( {\log n}\right)$ since $c$ and $\ell$ are constants.

首先界定迭代次数。第$i$次迭代至少能消除$c\left| {{Q}_{i}\left( {D}_{i}\right) }\right|$个查询结果，因为：(i)选择$c$-枢轴进行分区；(ii)有损剪枝可能消除多于预期的查询结果，但绝不会更少。因此第$i$次迭代开始时，最多剩余${\left( 1 - c\right) }^{i}\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$个查询结果。查询结果数量上限为${n}^{\ell }$，其中$\ell$表示$Q$中的原子数。若总迭代次数为$I$，则$I \leq  \left\lceil  {{\log }_{1/\left( {1 - c}\right) }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right| }\right\rceil   \leq$$\left\lceil  {\ell {\log }_{1/\left( {1 - c}\right) }n}\right\rceil   = O\left( {\log n}\right)$，因为$c$和$\ell$均为常数。

Second,we show that the returned answer is an $\epsilon$ -approximate quantile. The less-than partition ${Q}_{1\mathrm{t}}\left( {D}_{1\mathrm{t}}\right)$ is constructed by trimming the inequalities $w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  \prec  w\left( p\right)$ and $w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  \succ$ low with some error ${\epsilon }^{\prime }$ ,where low lower-bounds the weights of the candidate query answers. Because these two trimmings are lossy, we "lose" a number of query answers which are at most $2{\epsilon }^{\prime }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$ . These are the answers that satisfy the inequalities, but do not appear in ${Q}_{1\mathrm{t}}\left( {D}_{1\mathrm{t}}\right)$ . As Figure 4 illustrates,all answers not contained in the less-than or greater-than partition, including these lost query answers, are assumed to be contained in the equal-to partition which we do not explicitly count. We now bound the distance between the desired index and the index of the answer that our algorithm returns. Each iteration $i$ starts with an index ${k}_{i}$ and results in a new index ${k}_{i}^{\prime }$ ,which the following iteration is asked to retrieve (or,in case this is the last iteration, the index that is returned). Note that in Algorithm 1 the variable $k$ indexes the subarray of query answers that are currently candidates; thus it is offset by the index of the answer with weight low. Here,the indexes ${k}_{i}$ and ${k}_{i}^{\prime }$ refer to the original array that contains all the query answers. Suppose that ${k}_{i}$ falls into the less-than partition at the beginning of the iteration. Then,if ${k}_{i}^{\prime }$ is different than ${k}_{i}$ ,it has to be a higher index because of lost query answers that precede it and which are moved to the middle equal-to partition (see Figure 4). Thus, $\left| {{k}_{i} - {k}_{i}^{\prime }}\right|  \leq  2{\epsilon }^{\prime }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$ . If ${k}_{i}$ falls into the equal-to partition, then we still choose that partition and return the pivot because the size of the partition can only increase from the lossy trimmings. For the greater-than partition, the analysis is symmetric to lower-than since the lossy trimmings of the latter do not affect the indexes of the former. To conclude, the accumulated absolute error is $I \cdot  2{\epsilon }^{\prime }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|  \leq  2\left\lceil  {\ell {\log }_{1/\left( {1 - c}\right) }n}\right\rceil  {\epsilon }^{\prime }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$ . To obtain an $\epsilon$ -approximate quantile of $Q\left( D\right)$ ,we set ${\epsilon }^{\prime } = \frac{\epsilon }{2\left\lceil  {\ell {\log }_{1/\left( {1 - c}\right) }n}\right\rceil  }$ .

其次，我们证明返回的答案是一个$\epsilon$-近似分位数。通过以误差${\epsilon }^{\prime }$对不等式$w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  \prec  w\left( p\right)$和$w\left( {U}_{w}\right)  \succ$进行截断（其中low下界了候选查询答案的权重），构建出小于分区${Q}_{1\mathrm{t}}\left( {D}_{1\mathrm{t}}\right)$。由于这两次截断存在信息损失，我们最多会"丢失"$2{\epsilon }^{\prime }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$个查询答案——这些答案满足不等式条件但未出现在${Q}_{1\mathrm{t}}\left( {D}_{1\mathrm{t}}\right)$中。如图4所示，所有未被包含在小于或大于分区中的答案（包括这些丢失的查询答案）都被假定存在于未显式计数的等于分区中。现在界定目标索引与算法返回答案索引之间的距离：每次迭代$i$从索引${k}_{i}$开始，生成新索引${k}_{i}^{\prime }$作为后续迭代的检索目标（若为末次迭代则直接返回）。注意算法1中变量$k$索引的是当前候选查询答案的子数组，因此需用low权重答案的索引进行偏移。此处索引${k}_{i}$和${k}_{i}^{\prime }$指向包含所有查询答案的原始数组。假设${k}_{i}$在迭代开始时落入小于分区，若${k}_{i}^{\prime }$与${k}_{i}$不同，则由于前置丢失答案被移至中间等于分区（见图4），其索引值必然更大，故$\left| {{k}_{i} - {k}_{i}^{\prime }}\right|  \leq  2{\epsilon }^{\prime }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$。若${k}_{i}$初始位于等于分区，因信息损失截断只会扩大该分区范围，我们仍选择该分区并返回枢轴点。对于大于分区的分析对称于小于分区，因后者的信息损失截断不影响前者索引。综上，累计绝对误差为$I \cdot  2{\epsilon }^{\prime }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|  \leq  2\left\lceil  {\ell {\log }_{1/\left( {1 - c}\right) }n}\right\rceil  {\epsilon }^{\prime }\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$。为获得$Q\left( D\right)$的$\epsilon$-近似分位数，我们设定${\epsilon }^{\prime } = \frac{\epsilon }{2\left\lceil  {\ell {\log }_{1/\left( {1 - c}\right) }n}\right\rceil  }$。

Finally, we prove the running time. Since our trimmings return acyclic JQs, the answers of all queries we construct can be counted in linear time. Thus,the running time per iteration is $O\left( {{g}_{p}\left( n\right)  + }\right.$ $\left. {4{g}_{t}\left( {n,{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)  + n}\right)$ which is $O\left( {\max \left\{  {{g}_{p}\left( n\right) ,{g}_{t}\left( {n,{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right) }\right\}  \text{since}{g}_{p}\left( n\right) }\right.$ and ${g}_{t}\left( {n,{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$ are necessarily $\Omega \left( n\right)$ .

最后证明时间复杂度。由于截断操作返回无环连接查询，所构造所有查询的答案均可在线性时间内完成计数。因此单次迭代运行时间为$O\left( {{g}_{p}\left( n\right)  + }\right.$$\left. {4{g}_{t}\left( {n,{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)  + n}\right)$（因$O\left( {\max \left\{  {{g}_{p}\left( n\right) ,{g}_{t}\left( {n,{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right) }\right\}  \text{since}{g}_{p}\left( n\right) }\right.$与${g}_{t}\left( {n,{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$必然满足$\Omega \left( n\right)$）。

### B.1 Proof of Lemma 4.4

### B.1 引理4.4的证明

Let ${b}_{1},\ldots ,{b}_{r}$ be the joining groups from the children ${V}_{1},\ldots ,{V}_{r}$ of $V$ , We show by induction on the join tree that pivot $\left( {b}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,\operatorname{pivot}\left( {b}_{r}\right)$ , and $t$ all agree on their common variables. For the leaf relations,we have no children and pivot(t)is initialized to $t$ . For the inductive step,let $x$ be a common variable between two children ${V}_{i}$ and ${V}_{j}$ of $V,i,j \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ . Because of the running intersection property of the join tree, $x$ also needs to appear in the parent $V$ . Since the groups ${b}_{i},{b}_{j}$ join with $t$ ,all their tuples necessarily assign value $t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$ to variable $x$ . We show that pivot $\left( {b}_{i}\right)$ also assigns $t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$ to $x$ and the case of pivot $\left( {b}_{j}\right)$ is similar. We have that pivot $\left( {b}_{i}\right)  = \operatorname{pivot}\left( {t}_{i}\right)$ for some tuple ${t}_{i} \in  {b}_{i}$ where pivot $\left( {t}_{i}\right)$ is picked as the weighted median of the group. From the inductive hypothesis,pivot $\left( {t}_{i}\right)$ needs to agree with ${t}_{i}$ on the value of $x$ which we argued is equal to $t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$ .

设${b}_{1},\ldots ,{b}_{r}$为$V$子节点${V}_{1},\ldots ,{V}_{r}$的连接组，通过对连接树归纳可证枢轴$\left( {b}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,\operatorname{pivot}\left( {b}_{r}\right)$与$t$在其共有变量上取值一致。对于叶关系，无子节点且pivot(t)初始化为$t$。归纳步骤中，设$x$为$V,i,j \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$两子节点${V}_{i}$与${V}_{j}$的共有变量，根据连接树的运行相交性质，$x$也须出现在父节点$V$中。由于组${b}_{i},{b}_{j}$与$t$连接，其所有元组必然为变量$x$赋值$t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$。可证枢轴$\left( {b}_{i}\right)$同样为$x$赋值$t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$，枢轴$\left( {b}_{j}\right)$情况类似。其中枢轴$\left( {b}_{i}\right)  = \operatorname{pivot}\left( {t}_{i}\right)$来自元组${t}_{i} \in  {b}_{i}$，而枢轴$\left( {t}_{i}\right)$作为该组加权中位数选取。根据归纳假设，枢轴$\left( {t}_{i}\right)$需与${t}_{i}$在变量$x$上取值一致，前文已论证该值等于$t\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$。

### B.2 Proof of Lemma 4.5

### B.2 引理4.5的证明

Without loss of generality,let the indexing of the $r$ sets be consistent with the ordering of their $c$ -pivots,i.e., ${p}_{i} \leq  {p}_{j}$ for ${Z}_{i},{Z}_{j},1 \leq$ $i \leq  j \leq  r$ . Let ${p}_{m}$ be the weighted median,selected from set ${Z}_{m}$ for some $m \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ . We prove that ${p}_{m}$ is greater than or equal to (according to $\leq$ ) at least $\frac{c}{2}\left| {{Z}_{1} \cup  {Z}_{2} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Z}_{r}}\right|$ elements,and the case of less than or equal to is symmetric. Because of the indexing we enforced,we know that ${p}_{i} \leq  {p}_{m}$ for all $i \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ . Combining that with the definition of a $c$ -pivot (for ${p}_{i}$ ),we obtain that ${p}_{m}$ is greater than or equal to at least $c\left| {Z}_{i}\right|$ elements of $\left| {Z}_{i}\right|$ ,or $c\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack  }}\left| {Z}_{i}\right|$ in total. Now, because the median is weighted by the set sizes and there is no overlap between their elements, $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack  }}\left| {Z}_{i}\right|  \geq$ $\left| {{Z}_{1} \cup  {Z}_{2} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Z}_{r}}\right| /2$ . Thus, ${p}_{m}$ is greater than or equal to at least $c\left| {{Z}_{1} \cup  {Z}_{2} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Z}_{r}}\right| /2$ elements of ${Z}_{1} \cup  {Z}_{2} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Z}_{r}$ .

不失一般性，令$r$集合的索引与其$c$-主元的排序一致，即对${Z}_{i},{Z}_{j},1 \leq$$i \leq  j \leq  r$有${p}_{i} \leq  {p}_{j}$。设${p}_{m}$为加权中位数，从某个$m \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$的集合${Z}_{m}$中选出。我们证明${p}_{m}$至少大于等于（根据$\leq$）$\frac{c}{2}\left| {{Z}_{1} \cup  {Z}_{2} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Z}_{r}}\right|$个元素，小于等于的情况对称可得。由于强制设定的索引规则，可知对所有$i \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$有${p}_{i} \leq  {p}_{m}$。结合$c$-主元的定义（对于${p}_{i}$），可推出${p}_{m}$至少大于等于$\left| {Z}_{i}\right|$中的$c\left| {Z}_{i}\right|$个元素，总计$c\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack  }}\left| {Z}_{i}\right|$个。由于中位数按集合大小加权且元素互不重叠，故$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack  }}\left| {Z}_{i}\right|  \geq$$\left| {{Z}_{1} \cup  {Z}_{2} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Z}_{r}}\right| /2$。因此，${p}_{m}$至少大于等于${Z}_{1} \cup  {Z}_{2} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Z}_{r}$中的$c\left| {{Z}_{1} \cup  {Z}_{2} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Z}_{r}}\right| /2$个元素。

### B.3 Proof of Lemma 4.6

### B.3 引理4.6的证明

Let $M$ be the partial answers rooted at $t$ ,and let ${M}_{i}$ be the partial answers rooted at ${V}_{i}$ and restricted to those that agree with $t$ for all $i \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ . We only show that $t \cup  {p}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {p}_{r}$ is greater than or equal to at least ${c}^{r}\left| M\right|$ partial answers,since the case of less than or equal to is symmetric. For $i \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ ,let ${L}_{i}$ be the subset of ${M}_{i}$ answers that are less than or equal to ${p}_{i}$ .

设$M$为植根于$t$的部分解，${M}_{i}$为植根于${V}_{i}$且限制在所有$i \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$上与$t$一致的部分解。我们仅证明$t \cup  {p}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {p}_{r}$至少大于等于${c}^{r}\left| M\right|$个部分解，因小于等于的情况对称可证。对于$i \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$，令${L}_{i}$表示${M}_{i}$中小于等于${p}_{i}$的解的子集。

We first show that $w\left( {t \cup  {q}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {q}_{r}}\right)  \preccurlyeq  w\left( {t \cup  {p}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {p}_{r}}\right)$ whenever ${q}_{i} \in  {L}_{i},i \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ . We know that $w\left( {q}_{i}\right)  \leq  w\left( {p}_{i}\right)$ for all $i \in  \left\lbrack  r\right\rbrack$ . We proceed inductively in $i$ ,showing that $w\left( {{q}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {q}_{i}}\right)  \leq$ $w\left( {{p}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {p}_{i}}\right)$ . The inductive hypothesis is that $w\left( {{q}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {q}_{i - 1}}\right)  \leq$ $w\left( {{p}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {p}_{i - 1}}\right)$ . Each of these two terms is an aggregate over the values of variables ${U}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {U}_{i - 1}$ mapped to their weights, where ${U}_{i}$ is the subset of weighted variables ${U}_{w}$ that appear in the subtree rooteed at node ${V}_{i}$ . For example, $w\left( {{q}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {q}_{i - 1}}\right)$ is ${\operatorname{agg}}_{w}\left( \left\{  {{w}_{x}\left( {\left( {{q}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {q}_{i - 1}}\right) \left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  x \in  {U}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {U}_{i - 1}}\right\}  \right)$ . By subset-monotonicity, we can add to both aggregates the weighted values of ${p}_{i}$ without changing the inequality,i.e.,we obtain $w\left( {{q}_{1} \cup  }\right.$ $\left. {\ldots  \cup  {q}_{i - 1} \cup  {p}_{i}}\right)  \leq  w\left( {{p}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {p}_{i - 1} \cup  {p}_{i}}\right)$ (A). With a similar argument of subset-monotonicity,we start from $w\left( {q}_{i}\right)  \leq  w\left( {p}_{i}\right)$ and add to both sides the weighted values of ${q}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {q}_{i - 1}$ to obtain $w\left( {{q}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {q}_{i - 1} \cup  {q}_{i}}\right)  \leq  w\left( {{q}_{1} \cup  \ldots  \cup  {q}_{i - 1} \cup  {p}_{i}}\right)$ (B). ### C.3 Proof of Theorem 5.6

### C.3 定理5.6的证明

First, we prove that the condition in our dichotomy is equivalent to having the SUM variables on one or two adjacent join tree nodes.

首先，我们证明二分条件等价于SUM变量出现在一个或两个相邻的连接树节点上。

LEMMA C.1. Consider the hypergraph $\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$ of a $\mathcal{J}{QQ}$ and a set of variables ${U}_{w}$ . If $\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$ is acyclic,any set of independent variables of ${U}_{w}$ is of size at most 2,and any chordless path between two ${U}_{w}$ variables is of length at most 3,then there exists a join tree for $Q$ where $U$ appears on one or two adjacent nodes.

引理C.1. 给定$\mathcal{J}{QQ}$的超图$\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$和变量集${U}_{w}$。若$\mathcal{H}\left( Q\right)$是无环的，且${U}_{w}$的任意独立变量集大小不超过2，且任意两个${U}_{w}$变量间的无弦路径长度不超过3，则存在$Q$的连接树使得$U$出现在一个或两个相邻节点上。

Proof. If there is one query atom that contains all ${U}_{w}$ variables, then we are done. Otherwise, since any set of independent variables of ${U}_{w}$ is of size at most 2,then there are 2 atoms that together contain all ${U}_{w}$ variables. Indeed,consider any 3 atoms. If each of them has a ${U}_{w}$ variable that does not appear in the other two, then these three variables are an independent set of size 3 , which contradicts our condition. Thus, 2 of these atoms contain all ${U}_{w}$ variables that appear in the 3 atoms. By applying this repeatedly to the selected 2 atoms and an untreated atom until all atoms are treated,we get 2 atoms that contain all of ${U}_{w}$ variables.

证明. 若存在包含所有${U}_{w}$变量的查询原子，则得证。否则，由于${U}_{w}$的任意独立变量集大小不超过2，必存在两个原子共同包含所有${U}_{w}$变量。具体而言，考虑任意三个原子：若每个原子都存在一个${U}_{w}$变量不出现在另外两个原子中，则这三个变量构成大小为3的独立集，与条件矛盾。因此，这三个原子中有两个原子包含所有${U}_{w}$变量。通过将选定的两个原子与未处理的原子反复应用此逻辑，最终可得包含全部${U}_{w}$变量的两个原子。

Since $Q$ is acyclic,it has a join tree. Let ${R}^{\prime }$ and ${S}^{\prime }$ be two join-tree nodes that together contain all of ${U}_{w}$ . Consider the path ${P}^{\prime }$ from ${R}^{\prime }$ to ${S}^{\prime }$ in the join tree. Let $R$ be the last node on ${P}^{\prime }$ that contains all ${U}_{w}$ variables that are in ${R}^{\prime }$ ,and let $S$ be the first node on ${P}^{\prime }$ that contains all ${U}_{w}$ variables that are in ${S}^{\prime }$ . If $R$ and $S$ are neighbors, we are done. Otherwise, we show we can find an alternative join tree where they are neighbors. Consider the path $P$ from $R$ to $S$ in the join tree. Let $V$ be all the variables that appear on the path between $R$ and $S$ (not including $R$ and $S$ ),such that each variable in $V$ appears in either $R$ or $S$ (or both). We consider three cases. The first case is $V \subseteq  R$ . We directly connect $R$ and $S$ and remove the edge connecting $S$ to the node preceding it on the path from $R$ . The running intersection property is maintained as for each variable, the nodes containing this variable remain connected. The second case is $V \subseteq  S$ . It is handled similarly by directly connecting $R$ to $S$ and removing the edge from $R$ to its succeeding node on the path to $S$ . The third case is that a variable $u \in  V$ appears in $R$ but not in $S$ and another variable $v \in  V$ appears in $S$ but not in $R$ . Since $R$ is the last in $P$ to contain all ${U}_{w}$ variables of ${R}^{\prime }$ ,there is a variable $x \in  {U}_{w}$ that appears in $R$ but nowhere else in $P$ . Similarly,there is a variable $y \in  {U}_{w}$ that appears in $S$ and nowhere else in $P$ . If every two consecutive nodes on $P$ share a variable,then we have a chordless path $x - u - \ldots  - v - y$ of length at least 4,contradicting our condition. Otherwise, we remove the edge between the two nodes that do not share a variable,and add an edge between $R$ and $S$ ,which preserves the running intersection property.

由于$Q$是无环的，它存在一个连接树。设${R}^{\prime }$和${S}^{\prime }$为两个包含所有${U}_{w}$变量的连接树节点。考虑连接树中从${R}^{\prime }$到${S}^{\prime }$的路径${P}^{\prime }$。令$R$为路径${P}^{\prime }$上最后一个包含${R}^{\prime }$中所有${U}_{w}$变量的节点，$S$为路径上第一个包含${S}^{\prime }$中所有${U}_{w}$变量的节点。若$R$与$S$相邻，则得证。否则，我们可构造它们相邻的替代连接树。考察连接树中$R$到$S$的路径$P$，设$V$为$R$与$S$之间路径上（不含两端）出现的所有变量，这些变量必须存在于$R$或$S$（或两者）。我们分三种情况处理：第一种情况$V \subseteq  R$时，直接连接$R$与$S$并移除$S$与原前驱节点的边，运行相交性保持因各变量的包含节点仍连通；第二种情况$V \subseteq  S$类似处理，直接连接两端并移除$R$到后继节点的边；第三种情况是变量$u \in  V$存在于$R$但不在$S$，而变量$v \in  V$存在于$S$但不在$R$。由于$R$是$P$中最后一个包含${R}^{\prime }$全部${U}_{w}$变量的节点，存在变量$x \in  {U}_{w}$仅出现在$R$；同理存在变量$y \in  {U}_{w}$仅出现在$S$。若$P$上每对相邻节点共享变量，则会产生长度≥4的无弦路径$x - u - \ldots  - v - y$，与条件矛盾。否则移除不共享变量的两节点间边，添加$R$与$S$的边以保持运行相交性。

## We now show the dichotomy of Theorem 5.6.

## 现在证明定理5.6的二分性

For the positive side,we apply Lemma C.1. When all ${U}_{w}$ variables are contained in a single join-tree node, trimming can be done in linear time by filtering the corresponding relation. When they are contained in two adjacent join-tree nodes, $O\left( {n\log n}\right)$ trimming follows from Lemma 5.5. Combining these two cases with Lemmas 3.3 and 4.1 completes the proof of the positive side.

正向证明部分应用引理C.1：当所有${U}_{w}$变量包含在单个连接树节点时，通过过滤对应关系可在线性时间内完成修剪；当变量存在于两个相邻连接树节点时，$O\left( {n\log n}\right)$修剪由引理5.5可得。结合这两种情况与引理3.3、4.1即完成正向证明。

For the negative side,there are 3 cases. 1) If $Q$ is cyclic,an answer to $\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$ would also answer the decision problem of whether $Q$ has any answer,which precludes time $O\left( {n\text{polylog}n}\right)$ assuming Hyperclique [6]. Assume $Q$ is acyclic. 2) If there exists a set of independent variables of ${U}_{w}$ of size 3,selection by SUM is not possible in $O\left( {n}^{2 - \varepsilon }\right)$ for all $\varepsilon  > 0$ assuming 3sum [7,Corollary 7.11]. Since we can count the answers to an acyclic JQ in linear time, the selection problem and $\% {JQ}$ are equivalent. 3) If there is a chordless path between two ${U}_{w}$ variables of length 4 or more,we apply a known reduction [7, Lemma 7.13] to show that solving %JQ in quasi-linear time can be used to detect a triangle in a graph in quasilinear time, which is not possible assuming HYPERCLIQUE. There are two ways in which the statement of that lemma differs from our needs: first, all variables there were allowed to participate in the ranking. However, the reduction only assigns non-zero weights to the first and last variables in the path, so this difference is non-essential. Second, the path there contains exactly 3 atoms (i.e., 4 variables); if our path is longer, we simply make the the remaining relations equality, and the rest of the proof is the same.

负面情况有三种：1)若$Q$是循环的，对$\% \mathrm{{JQ}}$的解答将同时解决$Q$是否存在答案的判定问题，这在假设超团问题(Hyperclique)[6]成立时排除了$O\left( {n\text{polylog}n}\right)$时间复杂度。假设$Q$是非循环的。2)若存在大小为3的${U}_{w}$独立变量集，在假设3sum问题[7推论7.11]成立时，无法通过SUM方法在$O\left( {n}^{2 - \varepsilon }\right)$中对所有$\varepsilon  > 0$进行选择。由于我们能在线性时间内计算非循环连接查询(JQ)的答案，选择问题与$\% {JQ}$是等价的。3)若两个${U}_{w}$变量间存在长度≥4的无弦路径，我们应用已知归约方法[7引理7.13]证明：在拟线性时间内求解%JQ可用于检测图中的三角形，这在假设HYPERCLIQUE成立时不可能实现。该引理与我们的需求有两点差异：首先，原引理允许所有变量参与排序。但归约过程仅对路径首尾变量赋予非零权重，故此差异非本质；其次，原路径仅含3个原子关系(即4个变量)，若路径更长，我们只需将剩余关系设为等值连接，证明过程保持不变。

Recall that in our sketch ${\mathbb{S}}_{\epsilon }\left( L\right)$ of a multiset $L$ we made the adjustment that if $i,j,k$ are three consecutive indexes in the bucketization, $L\left\lbrack  {j - 1}\right\rbrack   = L\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack$ ,and ${j}^{\prime },{j}^{\prime \prime }$ are the smallest and largest indexes that contain $L\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack$ in the two consecutive buckets,then we replace $i,j,k$ with $i,{j}^{\prime },{j}^{\prime \prime } + 1,k$ . Also,let $\mathbb{S}$ be the original sketch with approximation error $\epsilon$ (see Lemma 6.3) and ${\mathbb{S}}^{\prime }$ be the resulting sketch. What we will show is that ${\mathbb{S}}^{\prime }$ is an $\epsilon$ -sketch of $L$ . We know inductively that ${ \uplus  }_{{t}_{j}^{\prime } \in  \operatorname{cp}\left( {t}^{\prime }\right) }\sigma \left( {t}_{j}^{\prime }\right)$ is a $\left( {{4}^{d - 1}{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$ -sketch of ${W}_{{t}^{\prime }}$ . The error bound of the sketch remains ${4}^{d - 1}{\epsilon }^{\prime }$ after the union,then becomes $2 \cdot  {4}^{d - 1}{\epsilon }^{\prime }$ after applying the ${\epsilon }^{\prime }$ -sketch,and finally $2 \cdot  2 \cdot  {4}^{d - 1}{\epsilon }^{\prime } = {4}^{d}{\epsilon }^{\prime }$ after taking the pairwise sums between the two children.

首先，我们通过归纳法证明：对于元组$t \in  R$（其中$R$是层级$d$上的关系，即从$R$到叶节点的最长路径为$d),{ \uplus  }_{{t}_{i} \in  \operatorname{cp}\left( t\right) }\sigma \left( {t}_{i}\right)$），该元组是${W}_{t}$的$\left( {{4}^{d}{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$-草图。${W}_{t}$中的每个权重是$t$的权重与来自子关系的连接部分答案（在原始数据库$D$中）的权重之和，即${W}_{t} = \{ w\left( t\right) \}  \otimes  \left( {{\bigotimes }_{S}\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{{t}^{\prime } \in  {\mathrm{{jg}}}_{S}\left( t\right) }}{W}_{{t}^{\prime }}}\right)$。若$t$与子关系$S$的元组${t}^{\prime }$连接，则需与处理$S$及其子节点时创建的所有副本$\operatorname{cp}\left( {t}^{\prime }\right)$连接。算法按如下方式计算值$\sigma \left( {t}_{i}\right)$：${ \uplus  }_{{t}_{i} \in  \operatorname{cp}\left( t\right) }\sigma \left( {t}_{i}\right)  = \{ w\left( t\right) \}  \otimes$$\left( {{\bigotimes }_{S}{\mathbb{S}}_{{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\left( {{ \uplus  }_{{t}^{\prime } \in  {\mathrm{{jg}}}_{S}\left( t\right) }{ \uplus  }_{{t}_{j}^{\prime } \in  \operatorname{cp}\left( {t}^{\prime }\right) }\sigma \left( {t}_{j}^{\prime }\right) }\right) }\right)$。根据归纳假设，${ \uplus  }_{{t}_{j}^{\prime } \in  \operatorname{cp}\left( {t}^{\prime }\right) }\sigma \left( {t}_{j}^{\prime }\right)$是${W}_{{t}^{\prime }}$的$\left( {{4}^{d - 1}{\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$-草图。该草图的误差界在并集操作后保持${4}^{d - 1}{\epsilon }^{\prime }$，应用${\epsilon }^{\prime }$-草图后变为$2 \cdot  {4}^{d - 1}{\epsilon }^{\prime }$，最终在两个子节点间进行成对求和后达到$2 \cdot  2 \cdot  {4}^{d - 1}{\epsilon }^{\prime } = {4}^{d}{\epsilon }^{\prime }$。

Second, we claim that the height of the binary join tree we construct is no more than $\ell$ ,where $\ell$ is the number of atoms of $Q$ . To see why, note that the new nodes we introduce in order to make the tree binary cannot be leaves and will always have 2 children. Suppose that there exists a root-to-leaf path of length greater than $\ell$ . For every new node on the path,there must be an original node that is a descendant of it, but not on this path. This implies that the number of original nodes would be greater than $\ell$ ,which is a contradiction. To conclude,we get $\epsilon$ -sketches of ${W}_{t}$ for tuples $t$ at the root level if we set ${\epsilon }^{\prime } = \frac{1}{{4}^{\ell }}\epsilon$ . Their union is an $\epsilon$ -sketch of $\{ w\left( q\right)  \mid  q \in  Q\left( D\right) \}$ .

其次，我们断言所构建的二叉连接树的高度不超过$\ell$（其中$\ell$是$Q$的原子数）。原因在于：为二叉化而引入的新节点不可能是叶节点，且必然有两个子节点。假设存在长度超过$\ell$的根到叶路径，则该路径上的每个新节点必然存在一个不属于该路径的原始后代节点。这将导致原始节点数超过$\ell$，产生矛盾。综上所述，若设置${\epsilon }^{\prime } = \frac{1}{{4}^{\ell }}\epsilon$，我们可获得根层级元组$t$对应的${W}_{t}$的$\epsilon$-草图，其并集构成$\{ w\left( q\right)  \mid  q \in  Q\left( D\right) \}$的$\epsilon$-草图。

Returned JQ properties. The fact that the JQ ${Q}^{\prime }$ that we return is acyclic is evident from the fact that every variable ${v}_{RS}$ that we introduce appears in two adjacent nodes of the join tree of $Q$ . Therefore, ${Q}^{\prime }$ also has a join tree.

返回的JQ属性。我们返回的JQ${Q}^{\prime }$具有无环性，这一事实可从每个引入的变量${v}_{RS}$都出现在$Q$连接树的两个相邻节点中得到验证。因此，${Q}^{\prime }$同样具备连接树结构。

Running time. The size of any relation is initially bounded by $n$ . Consider the step where we handle a relation and increase its size by creating copies of its tuples. The sizes of the child relations (which have already been handled) have size bounded by ${n}^{\prime } \geq  n$ . The total size of the messages sent from the children is $O\left( {{\log }_{1 + {\epsilon }^{\prime }}{n}^{\prime }}\right)$ because the messages are sketched. The parent relation receives the messages of a child and creates copies of its tuples whose number is equal to the message size. Since we have at most 2 children, the size of the parent relation becomes $O\left( {n{\left( {\log }_{1 + {\epsilon }^{\prime }}{n}^{\prime }\right) }^{2}}\right)$ . Applying this for every relation bottom-up, we can conclude that all relations after the algorithm terminates have size $O\left( {n{\left( {\log }_{1 + {\epsilon }^{\prime }}n\right) }^{2}}\right)$ (because the double-logarithmic terms are dominated). Changing base, this is $O\left( {n\frac{{\log }^{2}n}{{\log }^{2}\left( {1 + {\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right) }}\right)$ or $O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{2}n}\right)$ since ${\epsilon }^{\prime } = \Theta \left( \epsilon \right)$ and also $\log \left( {1 + \epsilon }\right)$ is very close to $\epsilon$ for small $\epsilon$ . All other operations of the algorithm are linear in this size, except for sketching, which is only done once for each join group. A sketch of a multiset $L = \left( {Z,\beta }\right)$ can be computed in $O\left( {\left| Z\right| \log \left| Z\right| }\right)$ by sorting. Since $O\left( {\log \left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{2}n}\right) }\right)  =$ $O\left( {\log \frac{n}{\epsilon }}\right)$ ,we get the desired time bound $O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{2}n\log \frac{n}{\epsilon }}\right)$ .

运行时间。任何关系的初始大小受$n$约束。在处理关系并通过复制元组增加其大小的步骤中，已处理的子关系大小受${n}^{\prime } \geq  n$限制。由于采用概要技术，子节点发送的消息总大小为$O\left( {{\log }_{1 + {\epsilon }^{\prime }}{n}^{\prime }}\right)$。父关系接收子节点消息后，会生成与消息大小相等的元组副本。鉴于最多存在2个子节点，父关系的大小将变为$O\left( {n{\left( {\log }_{1 + {\epsilon }^{\prime }}{n}^{\prime }\right) }^{2}}\right)$。自底向上逐层应用此过程，可得出算法终止后所有关系的大小为$O\left( {n{\left( {\log }_{1 + {\epsilon }^{\prime }}n\right) }^{2}}\right)$（因双对数项可忽略不计）。换算基数后，由于${\epsilon }^{\prime } = \Theta \left( \epsilon \right)$且$\log \left( {1 + \epsilon }\right)$在$\epsilon$较小时近似于$\epsilon$，故结果为$O\left( {n\frac{{\log }^{2}n}{{\log }^{2}\left( {1 + {\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right) }}\right)$或$O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{2}n}\right)$。除需对每个连接组执行一次的概要计算外，算法其他操作均与此大小呈线性关系。多重集$L = \left( {Z,\beta }\right)$的概要可通过排序在$O\left( {\left| Z\right| \log \left| Z\right| }\right)$时间内完成。由于$O\left( {\log \left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{2}n}\right) }\right)  =$$O\left( {\log \frac{n}{\epsilon }}\right)$，最终获得预期的时间界限$O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}n{\log }^{2}n\log \frac{n}{\epsilon }}\right)$。
