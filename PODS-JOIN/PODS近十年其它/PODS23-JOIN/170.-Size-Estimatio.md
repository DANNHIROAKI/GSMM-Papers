## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

We propose a new method for estimating the number of answers OUT of a small join query $Q$ in a large database $D$ ,and for uniform sampling over joins. Our method is the first to satisfy all the following statements.

我们提出了一种新方法，用于估算大型数据库$D$中小型连接查询$Q$的答案数量OUT，并实现连接操作的均匀采样。本方法是首个满足以下全部特性的方案：

- Support arbitrary $Q$ ,which can be either acyclic or cyclic, and contain binary and non-binary relations.

- 支持任意$Q$查询（包括无环和有环结构），可处理二元及多元关系

- Guarantee an arbitrary small error with a high probability always in $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ time,where ${AGM}$ is the AGM bound (an upper bound of OUT),and $\widetilde{O}$ hides the polylogarithmic factor of input size.

- 始终在$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间内以高概率保证任意小误差（其中${AGM}$为AGM边界值/OUT上界，$\widetilde{O}$包含输入规模的多对数因子）

We also explain previous join size estimators in a unified framework. All methods including ours rely on certain indexes on relations in $D$ ,which take linear time to build offline. Additionally, we extend our method using generalized hypertree decompositions (GHDs) to achieve a lower complexity than $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ when OUT is small, and present optimization techniques for improving estimation efficiency and accuracy.

我们在统一框架下解析了现有连接规模估算方法。包括本方法在内的所有方案都依赖于$D$关系上的特定索引（需线性时间离线构建）。此外，通过广义超树分解(GHDs)扩展本方法，在OUT较小时可获得优于$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$的复杂度，并提出提升估算效率与精度的优化技术

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

The evaluation of join queries is one of the most fundamental problems in databases $\left\lbrack  {1,{29} - {32},{34}}\right\rbrack$ . In theory,reducing the time complexity of evaluation algorithms has been the main goal $\lbrack 3,{19}$ , 31, 33]. This also applies to the counting problem [25], which we study in this paper. Given a join query $Q$ and a database $D$ ,an answer of $Q$ in $D$ is a mapping from free variables of $Q$ to attribute values in $D$ (or inversely as stated in [16]) constrained by the relations in $Q$ . For example,if a database $D$ of a social network has a binary relation $R\left( {A,B}\right)$ of friendship and a tuple $\left( {a,b}\right)  \in  R\left( {A,B}\right)$ indicates that two people $a,b$ are friends,finding all triples(a,b,c)of people who are all friends, is equivalent to finding the answers of the triangle join query $(R\left( {B,C}\right)$ and $R\left( {A,C}\right)$ are renamed relations of $R\left( {A,B}\right) )$ :

连接查询求值是数据库领域最基础的问题之一$\left\lbrack  {1,{29} - {32},{34}}\right\rbrack$。理论上，降低求值算法的时间复杂度始终是主要目标$\lbrack 3,{19}$，31, 33]。这同样适用于本文研究的计数问题[25]。给定连接查询$Q$和数据库$D$，查询$Q$在$D$中的解是指将$Q$的自由变量映射到$D$属性值的约束映射（或如[16]所述的逆映射）。例如，若社交网络数据库$D$包含表示友谊的二元关系$R\left( {A,B}\right)$，元组$\left( {a,b}\right)  \in  R\left( {A,B}\right)$表示两人$a,b$互为朋友，则找出所有互为朋友的三元组(a,b,c)等价于求解三角形连接查询$(R\left( {B,C}\right)$与$R\left( {A,C}\right)$（即$R\left( {A,B}\right) )$的重命名关系）的解：

$$
Q\left( {A,B,C}\right)  = R\left( {A,B}\right)  \land  R\left( {B,C}\right)  \land  R\left( {A,C}\right)  \tag{1}
$$

The complexity of evaluating join queries is commonly expressed as $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ where $\mathrm{{IN}}$ is the input size and $w\left( { \geq  1}\right)$ is a width of $Q$ (see $\$ 2$ for definition of IN and example widths). While acyclic queries can be evaluated with $w = 1$ [33],cyclic queries have higher $w$ values,i.e.,more difficult to solve. If $w$ is fractional edge cover number $\rho$ (see Definition 1), ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}$ becomes ${AGM}$ ,the AGM bound [7] which is an upper bound of OUT. Instead of evaluating join queries,the counting problem is to compute $\left| Q\right|$ ,the join size or the number of answers (e.g., triples above). It has its own application for answering COUNT queries in DBMS. Then, the complexity can be reduced from $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ to $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}\right)$ [25].

连接查询求值复杂度通常表示为$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$，其中$\mathrm{{IN}}$为输入规模，$w\left( { \geq  1}\right)$为$Q$的宽度（参见$\$ 2$了解IN定义及示例宽度）。无环查询可在$w = 1$时间内求值[33]，而有环查询因$w$值更高而更难求解。若$w$为分数边覆盖数$\rho$（见定义1），${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}$则变为${AGM}$——即AGM界[7]，这是OUT的上界。计数问题不直接求连接查询，而是计算$\left| Q\right|$（连接规模或解的数量，如前文的三元组），在DBMS中用于响应COUNT查询。此时复杂度可从$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$降至$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}\right)$[25]。

Approximate counting problem is to approximate $\left| Q\right|$ . Approximate algorithms are alternatively used whenever the exact counting is expensive and approximation is enough. In practice, a famous application is cost-based query optimization which requires efficient approximation of thousands of sub-queries [28]. In theory, going beyond the polynomial time algorithms, approximate algorithms established the complexity inversely proportional to OUT, under the requirement that guarantees an arbitrary small error with a high probability (see $§2$ for formal definitions). In a limited setting where every relation in $Q$ is binary and identical (e.g.,as in (1)),the complexity of $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}\right)$ has reduced to $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right) \left\lbrack  {6,{15}}\right\rbrack$ . However, in a general setting where $Q$ contains higher-arity relations,the complexity has reduced to $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w + 1}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ or $\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}} + \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$ only [11],with an additional multiplicative or additive factor of IN. Furthermore, all these methods have $w = \rho$ only (i.e., $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ ),and are not extended to other widths such as fractional hypertree width (see Definition 3) smaller than $\rho$ . In this paper,we propose a new method that achieves $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ complexity for $Q$ containing any-arity relations,under the same $w = \rho$ . As a corollary,ours achieve a linear or even constant complexity for a sufficiently large OUT. We also extend ours to incorporate fractional hypertree width by using generalized hypertree decompositions (GHDs), achieving a lower complexity than $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ when OUT is small.

近似计数问题的核心在于估算$\left| Q\right|$。当精确计数代价高昂而近似解足够时，通常会采用近似算法。实践中一个著名应用是基于成本的查询优化，该场景需要高效近似处理数千个子查询[28]。理论上，突破多项式时间算法限制后，近似算法在保证任意小误差且高概率的前提下（形式化定义见$§2$），建立了与OUT成反比的复杂度关系。在$Q$中所有关系均为二元且同构的受限场景下（如公式(1)），$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}\right)$的复杂度可降至$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right) \left\lbrack  {6,{15}}\right\rbrack$；而当$Q$包含更高元关系时，复杂度仅能降至$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w + 1}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$或$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}} + \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$[11]，且需额外乘以或加上IN因子。现有方法均仅支持$w = \rho$（即$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$），未能扩展至分数超树宽度等其他指标（见定义3）。本文提出新方法，在保持$w = \rho$前提下，对含任意元关系的$Q$实现$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$复杂度。推论表明：当OUT足够大时，本方法可获得线性乃至常数级复杂度。通过广义超树分解(GHDs)进一步扩展后，本方法在OUT较小时可获得优于$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$的复杂度。

## 2 TECHNICAL BACKGROUND

## 2 技术背景

Hypergraph. A join query $Q$ is commonly represented as a hyper-graph $\mathcal{H}\left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ ,where hypernodes $\mathcal{V}$ is the set of variables in $Q$ and hyperedges $\mathcal{E} \subseteq  {2}^{\mathcal{V}}$ [31]. Each hyperedge $F \in  \mathcal{E}$ is a subset of $\mathcal{V}$ and specifies the relation $R\left( F\right)$ (or ${R}_{F}$ for brevity) in $Q$ . For example, the query in (1) has $\mathcal{V} = \{ A,B,C\}$ and $\mathcal{E} = \{ \{ A,B\} ,\{ B,C\} ,\{ A,C\} \}$ . For any $I \subset  \mathcal{V}$ ,we define two notations used throughout the paper: ${\mathcal{E}}_{I} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ F \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  F \cap  I \neq  \varnothing \}$ and ${\mathcal{E}}_{I,\pi } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  {{\pi }_{I}F \mid  F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}\right\}$ ,i.e.,the set of hyperedges in $\mathcal{E}$ that intersect with $I$ and the projection of each edge in ${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$ onto $I$ .

超图(Hypergraph)。连接查询$Q$通常表示为超图$\mathcal{H}\left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$，其中超节点(hypernodes)$\mathcal{V}$是$Q$中的变量集合，超边(hyperedges)$\mathcal{E} \subseteq  {2}^{\mathcal{V}}$[31]。每条超边$F \in  \mathcal{E}$是$\mathcal{V}$的子集，用于指定$Q$中的关系$R\left( F\right)$（或简写为${R}_{F}$）。例如，(1)式中的查询包含$\mathcal{V} = \{ A,B,C\}$和$\mathcal{E} = \{ \{ A,B\} ,\{ B,C\} ,\{ A,C\} \}$。对于任意$I \subset  \mathcal{V}$，我们定义本文使用的两个符号：${\mathcal{E}}_{I} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ F \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  F \cap  I \neq  \varnothing \}$和${\mathcal{E}}_{I,\pi } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  {{\pi }_{I}F \mid  F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}\right\}$，即与$I$相交的$\mathcal{E}$中超边集合，以及${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$中每条边在$I$上的投影。

In relational algebra, $Q$ is equivalent to ${ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F} = {R}_{{F}_{1}} \bowtie  \ldots  \bowtie  {R}_{{F}_{\left| \mathcal{E}\right| }}$ . Here,join $\left(  \bowtie  \right)$ is a commutative binary operator between relations, allowing us to use ${ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}$ . Table 1 summarizes the operators used in this paper.

在关系代数中，$Q$等价于${ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F} = {R}_{{F}_{1}} \bowtie  \ldots  \bowtie  {R}_{{F}_{\left| \mathcal{E}\right| }}$。其中连接运算(join)$\left(  \bowtie  \right)$是关系间可交换的二元运算符，允许我们使用${ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}$。表1总结了本文使用的运算符。

Since all variables in $\mathcal{V}$ are free (output) variables in join queries, join queries are subsumed by full conjunctive queries. For a more general class of join-project (a.k.a. conjunctive) queries, the set of output variables is a subset of $\mathcal{V}$ . We assume $Q$ is a join query and extend to join-project queries in $§\mathrm{C}$ .

由于$\mathcal{V}$中的所有变量在连接查询中都是自由（输出）变量，因此连接查询被完全合取查询所包含。对于更一般的连接-投影（即合取）查询类，输出变量集是$\mathcal{V}$的一个子集。我们假设$Q$为连接查询，并在$§\mathrm{C}$中扩展到连接-投影查询。

Input and output size. We denote $\operatorname{IN}\left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}\left| {R}_{F}\right|$ as the input size $\left( {\left| {R}_{F}\right| \text{is the number of tuples in}{R}_{F}}\right)$ and $\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)  = \left| Q\right|$ , the number of query answers,as the output size of $Q$ (or $\mathcal{H}$ ) in $D$ . We drop $\mathcal{H}$ and $D$ if the context is clear,as well as in upcoming notations.

输入与输出规模。我们将$\operatorname{IN}\left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}\left| {R}_{F}\right|$表示为输入规模$\left( {\left| {R}_{F}\right| \text{is the number of tuples in}{R}_{F}}\right)$，将查询答案数量$\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)  = \left| Q\right|$作为$Q$（或$\mathcal{H}$）在$D$中的输出规模。若上下文明确，则省略$\mathcal{H}$和$D$，后续表示法同理。

Worst-case optimal (WCO) algorithms such as GenericJOIN [31] (Algorithm 4) achieve $w = \rho \left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)$ , called fractional edge cover number:

连接运算复杂度。连接评估算法的时间复杂度中，$Q$相较于$D$极小，故将$\left| \mathcal{V}\right|$和$\left| \mathcal{E}\right|$视为如$\left\lbrack  {6,{11},{31}}\right\rbrack$中的常数。最坏情况最优（WCO）算法（如GenericJOIN[31]的算法4）能达到$w = \rho \left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)$，称为分数边覆盖数：

Definition 1. [31]: The fractional edge cover number $\rho \left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)$ is the optimal solution of the linear program (LP): $\mathop{\min }\limits_{\left\{  {x}_{F} \mid  F \in  \mathcal{E}\right. }\}$ $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{x}_{F} \cdot  {\log }_{IN}\left| {R}_{F}\right|$ s.t. $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \ni  v}}{x}_{F} \geq  1 : \forall v \in  \mathcal{V}$ and $0 \leq  {x}_{F} \leq$ $1 : \forall F \in  \mathcal{E}$ .

定义1.[31]：分数边覆盖数$\rho \left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)$是线性规划（LP）的最优解：$\mathop{\min }\limits_{\left\{  {x}_{F} \mid  F \in  \mathcal{E}\right. }\}$$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{x}_{F} \cdot  {\log }_{IN}\left| {R}_{F}\right|$约束条件为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \ni  v}}{x}_{F} \geq  1 : \forall v \in  \mathcal{V}$且$0 \leq  {x}_{F} \leq$$1 : \forall F \in  \mathcal{E}$。

It is well known that $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{\left| {R}_{F}\right| }^{{x}_{F}}$ is an upper bound of OUT for any fractional edge cover $x$ satisfying the constraints of the LP $\left\lbrack  {7,{17}}\right\rbrack$ . The optimal value, ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\rho } = \mathop{\min }\limits_{x}\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{\left| {R}_{F}\right| }^{{x}_{F}}$ ,is called the AGM bound of $\mathcal{H}$ on $D$ denoted as ${AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H},D}\right)$ [7]. For example, the query in (1) has IN $= \left| {R}_{\{ A,B\} }\right|  = \left| {R}_{\{ B,C\} }\right|  = \left| {R}_{\{ A,C\} }\right|$ and $\rho  = 3/2$ from the optimal solution ${x}_{\{ A,B\} } = {x}_{\{ B,C\} } = {x}_{\{ A,C\} } = 1/2$ for the LP in Definition 1. Therefore,its $\mathrm{{AGM}}$ bound is ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\rho } = {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{3/2}$ . Generalized hypertree decomposition. As a special case, the YAN-NAKAKIS algorithm [33] achieves $w = 1$ if $Q$ is acyclic. It builds a join tree by assigning each hyperedge $F \in  \mathcal{E}$ to a tree node and performs dynamic programming (DP)-like join over the nodes' outputs (i.e., ${R}_{F}$ ’s) in a bottom-up manner.

To bridge the gap between $w = \rho$ and $w = 1$ when $Q$ is cyclic, generalized hypertree decompositions (GHDs, see Definition 2) [20] extend the tree for arbitrary joins by assigning multiple hy-peredges to a tree node, where the DP-like join is performed over these nodes' outputs (i.e., answers of the corresponding sub-query) computed by WCO algorithms. This way, $w$ decreases from $\rho$ to fractional hypertree width fhtw (see Definition 3). We refer to a comprehensive survey [31] for more details about these concepts and $§\mathrm{A}$ for our explanations of algorithms.

## 3 RELATED WORK

## 3 相关工作

In the context of approximate counting (and uniform sampling, which is closely related), there have been two lines of work: 1) determining the classes of queries of tractable and intractable cases and 2) reducing the degree of polynomial-time complexity for tractable cases, especially for join or join-project queries.

在近似计数（及与之密切相关的均匀采样）研究领域，存在两条工作主线：1) 确定可处理与难处理查询类别的划分；2) 降低可处理案例中多项式时间复杂度的阶数，特别是针对连接查询或连接-投影查询。

The first class of work widens the class of queries that admit fully polynomial-time randomized approximation scheme (FPRAS) and fully polynomial-time almost uniform sampler (FPAUS) [5]. Arenas et al. [5] showed that every class of conjunctive queries with bounded hypertree width admits FPRAS and FPAUS. Focke et al. [16] extended this to conjunctive queries with disequalities and negations, under some relaxations from FPRAS. These works focus on showing the existence of a polynomial-time algorithm over a wider class of queries, but not on reducing the exact degree of the polynomial complexity.

第一类工作拓展了允许完全多项式时间随机近似方案(FPRAS)和完全多项式时间近乎均匀采样器(FPAUS)的查询类别范围[5]。Arenas等人[5]证明所有具有有界超树宽度的合取查询类都允许FPRAS和FPAUS。Focke等人[16]将其扩展到包含不等式和否定的合取查询，在放松FPRAS某些条件的情况下。这些工作重点在于证明更广泛查询类别中存在多项式时间算法，而非降低多项式复杂度的具体阶数。

The second class of work $\left\lbrack  {4,6,8,{11},{14},{15}}\right\rbrack$ focuses on reducing the degree for a specific range of queries, and even further to making the complexity inversely proportional to OUT,e.g., $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ . However,they all consider $w = \rho$ only (i.e., $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ ), and the complexity $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ has been achieved for limited classes of queries. Assadi et al. [6] proposed a Simple Sublinear-Time Estimator (SSTE for short) with the complexity of $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ for unlabeled graph queries,i.e.,where $Q$ contains identical binary relations only as in the query in (1). However, we found that a missing factor in the proof by Assadi et al. [6] prevented SSTE from satisfying the $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ bound and thus notified the author to modify the proof (see $§\mathrm{E}$ in our full paper [26]). They also proposed some conjectures on labeled graphs (i.e., removing the identity assumption), but 1) no concrete algorithms were given and 2) the proposed bound for labeled graphs is larger than $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ (see $§\mathrm{D}{.2}$ ). Therefore,it is not known whether these methods can be extended to labeled graphs with $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{OUT}\right)$ time. Fichtenberger et al. [15] extended SSTE to sampling Subgraphs Uniformly in Sublinear Time (SUST for short) for uniform sampling while achieving the same complexity. Here, they said sublinear from the assumption that OUT is large as $\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\rho  - 1}\right)$ . Unlike join processing,it is unnecessary to scan the inputs for each query that requires $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ time.

第二类工作$\left\lbrack  {4,6,8,{11},{14},{15}}\right\rbrack$致力于降低特定查询范围的复杂度阶数，甚至使复杂度与OUT成反比，例如$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$。然而它们仅考虑$w = \rho$（即$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$是$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$），且$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$复杂度仅对有限查询类实现。Assadi等人[6]提出简单亚线性时间估计器(SSTE)，对无标记图查询实现$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$复杂度，即$Q$仅包含如查询(1)中相同的二元关系。但我们发现其证明存在遗漏因子导致SSTE无法满足$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$界，已通知作者修改证明（详见全文[26]中$§\mathrm{E}$）。他们也对标记图（即取消相同性假设）提出猜想，但1)未给出具体算法 2)所提标记图边界大于$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$（见$§\mathrm{D}{.2}$）。因此这些方法能否扩展到$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{OUT}\right)$时间复杂度的标记图尚属未知。Fichtenberger等人[15]将SSTE扩展为亚线性时间均匀子图采样(SUST)，保持相同复杂度。此处"亚线性"基于OUT足够大如$\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\rho  - 1}\right)$的假设。与连接处理不同，该方法无需为每个查询扫描需要$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$时间的输入。

Kim et al. [27] proposed ALLEY, a hybrid method that combines sampling and synopsis. We analyze the sampling part of ALLEY since analyzing the synopsis is out of scope. ALLEY solves the approximate subgraph counting problem (but not uniform sampling) for labeled graphs,where the relations in $Q$ are binary but not necessarily identical. The complexity,however,is $\widetilde{O}\left( {AGM}\right)$ instead of $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ , which we explain why in $\$ 5$ .

Kim等人[27]提出混合采样与概要的ALLEY方法。我们分析其采样部分，概要分析超出本文范围。ALLEY解决标记图的近似子图计数问题（非均匀采样），其中$Q$的二元关系不必相同。但其复杂度为$\widetilde{O}\left( {AGM}\right)$而非$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$，原因见$\$ 5$。

For a more general setting where relations in $Q$ can have higher arities, Chen & Yi [11] proposed GJ-SAMPLE that also performs uniform sampling,and achieved $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\mathrm{{IN}}{AGM}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ complexity. The additional factor of IN compared to SSTE and SUST results from computing sample probabilities (or weights) prior to sampling, where the number of candidates is $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ . We explain in detail in $\$ 5$ . Therefore, they do not achieve $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ complexity for arbitrary join queries and leave it as an open problem [11]. Note that it is important to remove the IN factor,for IN can be higher than $\frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$ when relation sizes are highly skewed. For example,for a triangle query $\mathcal{H}$ with $\mathcal{E} = \left\{  {{F}_{1},{F}_{2},{F}_{3}}\right\}  ,\left| {R}_{{F}_{1}}\right|  = {N}^{10},\left| {R}_{{F}_{2}}\right|  = \left| {R}_{{F}_{3}}\right|  = N$ ,and OUT $= N$ , then IN is ${N}^{10}$ and $\frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$ is $\frac{{N}^{2}}{N} = N$ for $\rho  = \frac{1}{5}$ .

针对$Q$中关系可具有更高元数的一般性场景，陈与易[11]提出的GJ-SAMPLE算法同样实现了均匀采样，并达成$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\mathrm{{IN}}{AGM}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$复杂度。相较于SSTE和SUST，额外的IN因子源于采样前计算样本概率（或权重）的过程，其中候选数量为$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$。我们将在$\$ 5$中详细说明。因此，他们未能实现任意连接查询的$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$复杂度，并将其列为开放性问题[11]。需注意消除IN因子至关重要，因为当关系规模高度倾斜时，IN可能超过$\frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$。例如，对于边数为$\mathcal{E} = \left\{  {{F}_{1},{F}_{2},{F}_{3}}\right\}  ,\left| {R}_{{F}_{1}}\right|  = {N}^{10},\left| {R}_{{F}_{2}}\right|  = \left| {R}_{{F}_{3}}\right|  = N$、输出量为$= N$的三角形查询$\mathcal{H}$，当$\rho  = \frac{1}{5}$时IN为${N}^{10}$而$\frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$为$\frac{{N}^{2}}{N} = N$。

We notice that Deng et al. [12] have independently pursued the same primary objective as ours,which is to attain $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ for uniform sampling and size estimation over joins. Both papers have been accepted in this PODS. The authors recursively 1) partition the input space into a constant number of sub-spaces and 2) sample a sub-space,to reduce the number of sample candidates from $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ to $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ . Our approach differs from theirs since we do not compute probabilities for candidates prior to sampling nor modify the set of candidates. Instead, we exploit known probability distributions to sample a candidate.

我们注意到邓等人[12]独立追求与我们相同的主要目标，即在连接操作上实现均匀采样和规模估计的$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$复杂度。两篇论文均被本届PODS会议收录。作者通过递归方式：1)将输入空间划分为恒定数量的子空间；2)对子空间采样，将候选样本数量从$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$降至$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$。我们的方法差异在于：既不预先计算候选概率，也不修改候选集，而是利用已知概率分布进行候选采样。

### 5.1 Generic Sampling-based Framework

### 5.1 通用基于采样的框架

We can classify existing sampling-based methods into three groups: variable-at-a-time (a.k.a., vertex-at-a-time) [11, 27], edge-at-a-time [29], and component-at-a-time [6, 15]. The first/second group samples a variable/hyperedge at a time until every variable/hyperedge in $\mathcal{H}$ is sampled,while the third group samples a component,either a cycle with an odd number of edges or a star, at a time, having a larger sample granularity than the other two groups.

现有基于采样的方法可分为三类：逐变量采样（亦称逐顶点采样）[11,27]、逐边采样[29]和逐组件采样[6,15]。前两类方法每次采样一个变量/超边直至$\mathcal{H}$中所有变量/超边被采样，而第三类方法每次采样一个组件（含奇数边的环或星型结构），其采样粒度大于前两类。

To analyze these groups in a unified manner, we propose a powerful sampling-based estimation framework GenericCardEst (Algorithm 1). It returns an unbiased estimate of $\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right)$ ,where ${\mathcal{H}}_{s}$ is the residual hypergraph given a current sample $s$ which is initially empty. 更重要的是，设定$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$使GJ-SAMPLE能在$Q$上实现均匀采样，每个$Q$的答案具有$\frac{1}{\operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }$的概率。令$s$为到达第2行的最终样本，${\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{1}},{\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{2}},\ldots ,{\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{n}}$为第8行生成的超图序列${\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}$。那么对于${\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{n}}$，从${\mathcal{E}}_{O} = {\mathcal{E}}_{\varnothing } = \varnothing$可得${AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{n}}\right)  = 1$，且

$$
P\left( s\right)  = \frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{1}}\right) }{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\ldots \frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{n}}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{n - 1}}\right) } = \frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{s}_{n}}\right) }{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) } = \frac{1}{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }.
$$

(3)

Since $\left| Q\right|  =$ OUT,a call to GENERICCARDEST succeeds to sample any answer of $Q$ with $\frac{\mathrm{{OUT}}}{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }$ probability and fails with 1 - $\frac{\text{ OUT }}{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }$ probability. Note that if $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ is set to $\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) /\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right)$ , then $P\left( s\right)  = \frac{1}{\text{ OUT }}$ ,so every call to GENERICCARDEST will succeed. However,computing $\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right)$ for every ${s}_{I} \in  {\Omega }_{I}$ is intractable, which is the reason for GJ-SAMPLE to use $\frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{S}I}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$ that can be computed in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time.

当$\left| Q\right|  =$为OUT时，调用GENERICCARDEST会以$\frac{\mathrm{{OUT}}}{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }$概率成功采样$Q$的任何答案，并以1-$\frac{\text{ OUT }}{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }$概率失败。注意若$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$设为$\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) /\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right)$，则$P\left( s\right)  = \frac{1}{\text{ OUT }}$，此时每次GENERICCARDEST调用都将成功。然而为每个${s}_{I} \in  {\Omega }_{I}$计算$\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right)$具有计算不可行性，这正是GJ-SAMPLE采用可在$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间内计算的$\frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{S}I}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$的原因。

However,computing $\frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$ values at Line 5 takes $\widetilde{O}\left( \left| {\Omega }_{I}\right| \right)  =$ $\widetilde{O}\left( \left| {{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{FGJ} \ltimes  s}\right) }\right| \right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ time. This results in an IN factor in GJ-SAMPLE’s runtime, $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right) \left\lbrack  {11}\right\rbrack$ . In order to remove this IN factor, Chen & Yi [11] separated out sequenceable queries, where all $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ values required in the sampling procedure can be precomputed in $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ time. This results in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}}\right)$ runtime for sequenceable queries and still $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ for non-sequenceable queries. Determining whether a query is sequenceable or not requires a brute-force algorithm of $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time,since the search space is bounded by the query size [11].

然而计算第5行的$\frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$值需要$\widetilde{O}\left( \left| {\Omega }_{I}\right| \right)  =$$\widetilde{O}\left( \left| {{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{FGJ} \ltimes  s}\right) }\right| \right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$时间，这导致GJ-SAMPLE运行时间中存在IN因子$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right) \left\lbrack  {11}\right\rbrack$。为消除该因子，Chen & Yi[11]分离出可序列化查询——其采样过程所需的所有$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$值均可通过$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$时间预计算。这使得可序列化查询的运行时降至$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}}\right)$，非序列化查询仍保持$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$。由于搜索空间受查询规模限制[11]，判断查询是否可序列化需要$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间的暴力算法。

### 5.4 Beyond the State of the Art with Degree-based Rejection Sampling

### 5.4 基于度数的拒绝采样技术突破现有水平

We propose degree-based rejection sampling (DRS) to avoid computing $\frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{S}I}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$ values in GJ-SAMPLE while achieving a similar $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ ,only a constant factor smaller. While we set $\left| I\right|  = 1$ and $k = 1$ as in GJ-SAMPLE,we sample ${F}^{ * }$ (a counterpart of ${F}^{GJ}$ in GJ-SAMPLE) uniformly from ${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$ and let ${\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}^{ * }} \ltimes  s}\right)$ . Therefore, $\left\{  {{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{F} \ltimes  s}\right)  \mid  F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}\right\}$ is our meta sample space. For each ${s}_{I} \in  {\Omega }_{I}$ and $F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}$ ,we define the relative degree of ${s}_{I}$ in ${R}_{F} \ltimes  s$ as ${\operatorname{rdeg}}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \frac{\left| {R}_{F} \ltimes  \left( s \uplus  {s}_{I}\right) \right| }{\left| {R}_{F} \ltimes  s\right| }.$

我们提出基于度的拒绝采样方法(DRS)，以避免在GJ-SAMPLE中计算$\frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{S}I}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$值，同时获得相似的$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$性能，仅存在恒定倍数的微小差距。虽然我们沿用GJ-SAMPLE中的$\left| I\right|  = 1$和$k = 1$参数设置，但改为从${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$中均匀采样${F}^{ * }$（对应GJ-SAMPLE中的${F}^{GJ}$），并令${\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}^{ * }} \ltimes  s}\right)$。因此，$\left\{  {{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{F} \ltimes  s}\right)  \mid  F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}\right\}$构成我们的元样本空间。对于每个${s}_{I} \in  {\Omega }_{I}$和$F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}$，我们将${s}_{I}$在${R}_{F} \ltimes  s$中的相对度定义为${\operatorname{rdeg}}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \frac{\left| {R}_{F} \ltimes  \left( s \uplus  {s}_{I}\right) \right| }{\left| {R}_{F} \ltimes  s\right| }.$

In order to use $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = {\operatorname{rdeg}}_{{F}^{ * },s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ in Line 5,we 1) uniformly sample a row $t$ from ${R}_{{F}^{ * }} \ltimes  s$ and 2) let ${s}_{I} = {\pi }_{I}t$ in Line 6,without computing $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ for every ${s}_{I} \in  {\Omega }_{I}$ . Then, ${rde}{g}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ for every $F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}$ is computed,and ${s}_{I}$ is rejected if ${F}^{ * } \neq  \arg \mathop{\max }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}}{\operatorname{rdeg}}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ (Line 7). We first assume that one $F$ has a higher ${\operatorname{rdeg}}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ than all other edges in ${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$ . Then,every $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ is multiplied by $\frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }$ at Line 7,resulting in $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }{\operatorname{rdeg}}_{{F}^{ * },s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ . Line 7 further uses $p = \frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{S}I}\right) }{{rde}{g}_{{F}^{ * },s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  \cdot  {AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }\left( { \leq  1\text{ from Lemma 2 }}\right)$ as the keeping probability of ${s}_{I}$ to make the final $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }\frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$ .

为了在第5行使用$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = {\operatorname{rdeg}}_{{F}^{ * },s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$，我们采取以下步骤：1) 从${R}_{{F}^{ * }} \ltimes  s$中均匀采样一行$t$；2) 在第6行直接赋值${s}_{I} = {\pi }_{I}t$，而无需为每个${s}_{I} \in  {\Omega }_{I}$计算$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$。随后，为每个$F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}$计算${rde}{g}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$，若满足条件${F}^{ * } \neq  \arg \mathop{\max }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}}{\operatorname{rdeg}}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$则舍弃${s}_{I}$（第7行）。我们首先假设某条边$F$的${\operatorname{rdeg}}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$值高于${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$中所有其他边。此时，每个$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$会在第7行乘以$\frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }$，生成$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }{\operatorname{rdeg}}_{{F}^{ * },s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$。第7行进一步将$p = \frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{S}I}\right) }{{rde}{g}_{{F}^{ * },s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  \cdot  {AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }\left( { \leq  1\text{ from Lemma 2 }}\right)$作为${s}_{I}$的保留概率，最终得到$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }\frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$。

Lemma 2. $\frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{f}}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) } \leq  \mathop{\max }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}}{rde}{g}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = {rde}{g}_{{F}^{ * },s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ if ${s}_{I} \in  { \bowtie  }_{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{F} \ltimes  s}\right)$ .

引理2. 当${s}_{I} \in  { \bowtie  }_{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{F} \ltimes  s}\right)$时，$\frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{f}}\right) }{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) } \leq  \mathop{\max }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{I}}}{rde}{g}_{F,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = {rde}{g}_{{F}^{ * },s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$成立。

From (3),any final sample $s$ that reaches Line 2 has $P\left( s\right)  =$ $\frac{1}{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{I \subset  \mathcal{V},\left| I\right|  = 1}}\frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }$ probability to be sampled,indicating a uniform sampling over $Q$ . Note that the added term $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }$ is regarded as a constant since it depends on the query size only. If we break our initial assumption so that $m > 1$ edges in ${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$ can have the same maximum relative degree,the $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ above will increase by $m$ times. Therefore,we simply decrease the keeping probability $p$ by $m$ times to preserve $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }\frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$ .

由(3)可知，任何到达第2行的最终样本$s$具有$P\left( s\right)  =$$\frac{1}{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{I \subset  \mathcal{V},\left| I\right|  = 1}}\frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }$的采样概率，表明在$Q$上实现了均匀采样。需注意，新增项$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }$被视为常数，因其仅取决于查询规模。若打破初始假设使得${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$中的$m > 1$条边可能具有相同的最大相对度，则上述$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$将增加$m$倍。因此，我们只需将保留概率$p$降低$m$倍即可维持$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }\frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) }$。

Example 1. Let $N$ and $M$ be two arbitrary numbers. Let $R\left( {A,B}\right)  =$ $\{ \left( {i,1}\right) \left| {i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,M}\right\rbrack  \} \cup \{ \left( {1,j}\right) }\right| j \in  \left\lbrack  {2,N}\right\rbrack  \}$ be a binary relation and $T\left( {A,C}\right)$ is obtained by renaming $B$ to $C$ in $R$ . Suppose we have a join query $Q\left( {A,B,C}\right)  = R\left( {A,B}\right)  \bowtie  T\left( {A,C}\right)$ . For ease of explanation,we use a hyperedge $F$ and its corresponding relation ${R}_{F}$ interchangeably. Then,we have an optimal fractional edge cover of $Q$ as ${x}_{R} = {x}_{T} = 1$ . We choose $I = \{ A\} ,I = \{ B\}$ ,and $I = \{ C\}$ in turn for DRS. For $I = \{ A\}$ and $s = \varnothing ,{\mathcal{E}}_{I} = \{ R,T\}$ ,so we sample a relation from ${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$ . Assume that $R$ is sampled,and then a row $t = \left( {1,1}\right)$ is sampled from $R$ . Then, ${s}_{I} = 1$ and ${\operatorname{rdeg}}_{R,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{N}{N + M - 1} = {\operatorname{rdeg}}_{T,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ ,so $R$ is not rejected. Since $R$ and $T$ tie w.r.t. ${s}_{I}$ ,we keep ${s}_{I}$ with probability $p =$ $\frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{{\operatorname{rdeg}}_{R,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right) }\frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s{ \uplus  }_{I}}\right) }{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) } = \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{{\operatorname{rdeg}}_{R,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right) }\frac{{N}^{2}}{{\left( N + M - 1\right) }^{2}}$ . Then, $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  =$ $P\left( {s}_{\{ A\} }\right)  = {rde}{g}_{R,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right) \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{{rde}{g}_{R,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right) }\frac{{N}^{2}}{{\left( N + M - 1\right) }^{2}} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{{N}^{2}}{{\left( N + M - 1\right) }^{2}}$ . Next, for $I = \{ B\}$ and $s = \{ A = 1\} ,{\mathcal{E}}_{I} = \{ R\}$ ,and rde ${g}_{R,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{1}{N}$ for any ${s}_{I} \in  {\pi }_{I}\left( {R \ltimes  s}\right)$ . Then, $P\left( {s}_{\{ B\} }\right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }\frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) } = \frac{N}{{N}^{2}} = \frac{1}{N}$ . Similarly, $P\left( {s}_{\{ C\} }\right)  = \frac{1}{N}$ for $I = \{ C\}$ . In total,a final samples that reaches Line 2 of GENERICCARDEST has $P\left( s\right)  = P\left( {s}_{\{ A\} }\right) P\left( {s}_{\{ B\} }\right) P\left( {s}_{\{ C\} }\right)  =$ $\frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{{\left( N + M - 1\right) }^{2}} = \mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }\frac{1}{\operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }.$

示例1. 设$N$和$M$为任意两个数。令$R\left( {A,B}\right)  =$$\{ \left( {i,1}\right) \left| {i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,M}\right\rbrack  \} \cup \{ \left( {1,j}\right) }\right| j \in  \left\lbrack  {2,N}\right\rbrack  \}$为二元关系，且$T\left( {A,C}\right)$是通过将$B$重命名为$C$从$R$中获得的。假设我们有一个连接查询$Q\left( {A,B,C}\right)  = R\left( {A,B}\right)  \bowtie  T\left( {A,C}\right)$。为便于说明，我们交替使用超边$F$及其对应关系${R}_{F}$。于是，我们得到$Q$的最优分数边覆盖为${x}_{R} = {x}_{T} = 1$。我们依次选择$I = \{ A\} ,I = \{ B\}$和$I = \{ C\}$进行DRS处理。对于$I = \{ A\}$和$s = \varnothing ,{\mathcal{E}}_{I} = \{ R,T\}$，我们从${\mathcal{E}}_{I}$中抽样一个关系。假定$R$被抽中，接着从$R$中抽取一行$t = \left( {1,1}\right)$。此时${s}_{I} = 1$且${\operatorname{rdeg}}_{R,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{N}{N + M - 1\right) }^{2}} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{{N}^{2}}{{\left( N + M - 1\right) }^{2}}$。接着处理$I = \{ B\}$和$s = \{ A = 1\} ,{\mathcal{E}}_{I} = \{ R\}$，且对于任意${s}_{I} \in  {\pi }_{I}\left( {R \ltimes  s}\right)$，rde值为${g}_{R,s}\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{1}{N}$。因此$P\left( {s}_{\{ B\} }\right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| }\frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s \uplus  {s}_{I}}\right) }{\operatorname{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{s}\right) } = \frac{N}{{N}^{2}} = \frac{1}{N}$。同理，$I = \{ C\}$的$P\left( {s}_{\{ C\} }\right)  = \frac{1}{N}$。最终，达到GENERICCARDEST算法第2行的抽样样本满足$P\left( s\right)  = P\left( {s}_{\{ A\} }\right) P\left( {s}_{\{ B\} }\right) P\left( {s}_{\{ C\} }\right)  =$$\frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{{\left( N + M - ### 5.5 Unified Analysis

### 5.5 统一分析

This section analyzes the bounds of variance and runtime $\left( {U}_{\text{var }}\right.$ and ${U}_{\text{time }}$ in $§2$ ) of sampling-based estimators in a unified manner. Note that Lemma 1 already states the unbiasedness of estimators. Theorem 1 can be proved from Propositions 4 and 8; ${U}_{\text{var }}{U}_{\text{time }}$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( {{AGM} \cdot  \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ .

本节统一分析基于采样的估计器在$\left( {U}_{\text{var }}\right.$和${U}_{\text{time }}$（位于$§2$中）的方差与运行时边界。需注意引理1已阐明估计器的无偏性。定理1可通过命题4和8得证；${U}_{\text{var }}{U}_{\text{time }}$即$\widetilde{O}\left( {{AGM} \cdot  \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$。

We first define two random variables,1) ${Z}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{s}}$ for the output of GENERICCARDEST given $s$ and 2) $Z = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{1 \leq  i \leq  N}}\frac{{Z}_{\mathcal{H}}^{i}}{N}$ for our final estimate. Here, ${Z}_{\mathcal{H}}^{i}$ ’s are independent and identical to ${Z}_{\mathcal{H}} = {Z}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{0}}$ , and $N$ is the number of initial calls to GENERICCARDEST with $s = \varnothing$ . Let ${T}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{s}}$ be the random variable for the number of core operations (including the four operations in §5.2) in GENERICCARDEST and $T$ for the total runtime of obtaining $Z$ . Then, $\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  Z\right\rbrack   = \frac{\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack  }{N}$ and $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  T\right\rbrack   = N \cdot  \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {T}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack$

我们首先定义两个随机变量：1) 给定$s$时GENERICCARDEST输出的${Z}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{s}}$，2) 最终估计值$Z = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{1 \leq  i \leq  N}}\frac{{Z}_{\mathcal{H}}^{i}}{N}$。此处${Z}_{\mathcal{H}}^{i}$与${Z}_{\mathcal{H}} = {Z}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{0}}$独立同分布，$N$表示使用$s = \varnothing$对GENERICCARDEST的初始调用次数。设${T}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{s}}$为GENERICCARDEST中核心操作（含§5.2所述四项操作）次数的随机变量，$T$为获取$Z$的总运行时。则有$\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  Z\right\rbrack   = \frac{\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack  }{N}$和$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  T\right\rbrack   = N \cdot  \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {T}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack$

Proposition 1. $\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack   \leq  {2}^{d} \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  {OUT}$ for SSTE and SUST where $d$ is the number of odd cycles and stars in $\mathcal{H}$ .

命题1. SSTE与SUST的$\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack   \leq  {2}^{d} \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  {OUT}$，其中$d$表示$\mathcal{H}$中奇环与星形结构的数量。

Proposition 2. $\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack   \leq  \frac{{t}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right| } - t}{t - 1} \cdot  {OU}{T}^{2}$ for ${ALLEY} +$ where $t = \frac{2\left( {1 - b}\right) }{b}$ .

命题2. ${ALLEY} +$的$\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack   \leq  \frac{{t}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right| } - t}{t - 1} \cdot  {OU}{T}^{2}$，其中$t = \frac{2\left( {1 - b}\right) }{b}$。

The upper bound ${U}_{\text{var }}$ of ALLEY+ explains the unique property of ALLEY+,that $\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack$ approaches to 0 as $b$ approaches to 1 . That is, $\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{b \rightarrow  1}}{U}_{\text{var }} = 0$ since $\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{b \rightarrow  1}}t = 0$ . In fact,this ${U}_{\text{var }}$ is tighter than the original bound ${U}_{\text{org }} = \frac{b}{1 - b}\left( {\frac{1}{{b}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right| }} - 1}\right)  \cdot  {\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2}$ proved in [27], where $\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{b \rightarrow  1}}{U}_{\text{org }} = \mathop{\lim }\limits_{{b \rightarrow  1}}\frac{1 + b + {b}^{2} + \ldots  + {b}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right|  - 1}}{{b}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right|  - 1}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2} = \left| \mathcal{V}\right|  \cdot  {\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2}$ .

ALLEY+的上界${U}_{\text{var }}$阐释了其独特性质：当$b$趋近于1时，$\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack$会趋近于0。换言之，$\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{b \rightarrow  1}}{U}_{\text{var }} = 0$因为$\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{b \rightarrow  1}}t = 0$。实际上，该${U}_{\text{var }}$比文献[27]中证明的原始边界${U}_{\text{org }} = \frac{b}{1 - b}\left( {\frac{1}{{b}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right| }} - 1}\right)  \cdot  {\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2}$更为严格，其中$\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{b \rightarrow  1}}{U}_{\text{org }} = \mathop{\lim }\limits_{{b \rightarrow  1}}\frac{1 + b + {b}^{2} + \ldots  + {b}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right|  - 1}}{{b}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right|  - 1}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2} = \left| \mathcal{V}\right|  \cdot  {\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2}$。

Proposition 3. $\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack   \leq  \left| \mathcal{V}\right|  \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  {OUT}$ for $G\mathcal{J}$ -SAMPLE.

命题3. 对于$G\mathcal{J}$-样本，$\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack   \leq  \left| \mathcal{V}\right|  \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  {OUT}$成立。

Proposition 4. $\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack   \leq  \left| \mathcal{V}\right|  \cdot  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right|  \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  {OUT}$ for DRS. Note that $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right|$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ .

命题4. DRS的$\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack   \leq  \left| \mathcal{V}\right|  \cdot  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right|  \cdot  {AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  {OUT}$。注意$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right|$是$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$。

Proposition 5. $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {T}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ for SSTE.

命题5. 对于SSTE，$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {T}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack$为$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$。

Proposition 6. $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {T}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( {{b}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right| }{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ for ${A}_{LLEY} +$ .

命题6. 对于${A}_{LLEY} +$，$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {T}_{\mathcal{H}}\right\rbrack$是$\widetilde{O}\left( {{b}^{\left| \mathcal{V}\right| }{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$。

Proposition 7. For $G\mathcal{J}$ -SAMPLE, ${T}_{\mathcal{H}}$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ for sequenceable queries and $\widetilde{O}\left( {IN}\right)$ for non-sequenceable queries.

命题7. 对于$G\mathcal{J}$-样本，可序列化查询的${T}_{\mathcal{H}}$为$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$，非序列化查询则为$\widetilde{O}\left( {IN}\right)$。

Proposition 8. ${T}_{\mathcal{H}}$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ for SUST and DRS.

命题8. SUST和DRS的${T}_{\mathcal{H}}$均为$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$。

Until now,we have assumed that OUT is given in computing $N =$ $\frac{{U}_{\text{var }}}{{\epsilon }^{2}\delta {\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2}}$ before invoking GENERICCARDEST,which is unrealistic since our goal is to estimate OUT itself. To tackle this, we use the geometric search by Assadi et al. [6]. They first assume that OUT is large as ${AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ and run estimation with a small $N$ [6]. Then, they perform a geometric search on OUT; assume a smaller OUT (decreasing OUT by 2),increase $N$ ,and run the estimation again. They repeat this until the assumed OUT becomes consistent with the algorithm output. While we implicitly assume that OUT $> 0$ , we can detect when $\mathrm{{OUT}} = 0$ in real applications whenever the assumed OUT falls below 1,in total $\widetilde{O}\left( {AGM}\right)$ time.

迄今为止，我们一直假设在调用GENERICCARDEST前已给定OUT值用于计算$N =$$\frac{{U}_{\text{var }}}{{\epsilon }^{2}\delta {\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2}}$，但这并不符合实际，因为我们的目标正是估算OUT本身。为解决此问题，我们采用Assadi等人[6]提出的几何搜索法。该方法首先假设OUT值较大如${AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$，并使用较小的$N$[6]进行估算。随后对OUT进行几何搜索：假设更小的OUT值（每次将OUT减半），增加$N$，并重新执行估算。该过程循环直至假设的OUT值与算法输出一致。虽然我们隐含假设OUT$> 0$，但在实际应用中，当假设的OUT值低于1时，我们可检测到$\mathrm{{OUT}} = 0$，整个过程仅需$\widetilde{O}\left( {AGM}\right)$时间。

This geometric search adds a constant factor $\left( { = 4}\right)$ in the $\widetilde{O}$ bound of ${T}_{\mathcal{H}}$ ,not a $\log \mathrm{{IN}}$ factor explained by Chen & $\mathrm{{Yi}}\left\lbrack  {11}\right\rbrack$ . Starting with an assumption $1 \leq  \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\text{ OUT }} < 2$ ,assume that the geometric search stops at ${2}^{k} \leq  \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}} < {2}^{k + 1}$ . Then,the total sample size used so far is $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{1 \leq  i \leq  k + 1}}\frac{{2}^{i}}{{\epsilon }^{2}\delta } \leq  \frac{{2}^{k + 2}}{{\epsilon }^{2}\delta } \leq  \frac{{4AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{{\epsilon }^{2}\delta \mathrm{{OUT}}}$ ,i.e.,only a constant factor (=4) is introduced.

这种几何搜索法在${T}_{\mathcal{H}}$的$\widetilde{O}$界中仅引入常数因子$\left( { = 4}\right)$，而非Chen&$\mathrm{{Yi}}\left\lbrack  {11}\right\rbrack$所解释的$\log \mathrm{{IN}}$因子。从初始假设$1 \leq  \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\text{ OUT }} < 2$出发，当几何搜索终止于${2}^{k} \leq  \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}} < {2}^{k + 1}$时，累计使用的样本总量为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{1 \leq  i \leq  k + 1}}\frac{{2}^{i}}{{\epsilon }^{2}\delta } \leq  \frac{{2}^{k + 2}}{{\epsilon }^{2}\delta } \leq  \frac{{4AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{{\epsilon }^{2}\delta \mathrm{{OUT}}}$，即仅引入常数因子(=4)。

Since GJ-SAMPLE can perform uniform sampling, it instead uses a simpler approach [11], by repeating the sampling until a constant number $c$ of trials succeed. Then,the number of total trials becomes a random variable,having $\frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  c}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$ as its expectation [11]. Therefore, $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  T\right\rbrack   = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  c}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}}\right)$ for sequenceable queries and $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  \operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  c}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ for non-sequenceable queries. Using the same approach,DRS requires $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| \frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  c}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$ trials in expectation and thus $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  T\right\rbrack   = \widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ ,asymptotically faster than GJ-SAMPLE.

由于GJ-SAMPLE支持均匀采样，其采用更简化的方法[11]：重复采样直至成功次数达到常数$c$。此时总试验次数成为期望值为$\frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  c}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$的随机变量[11]。因此，可序列化查询需$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  T\right\rbrack   = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  c}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}}\right)$次，非序列化查询需$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  \operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  c}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$次。采用相同方法时，DRS在期望中需要$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{I}\left| {\mathcal{E}}_{I}\right| \frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cdot  c}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$次试验，故其时间复杂度为$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  T\right\rbrack   = \widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$，渐进快于GJ-SAMPLE。

Finally,we give a simple explanation of why $T$ (or $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  T\right\rbrack$ ) of SSTE, SUST, GJ-SAMPLE, and DRS, is inversely proportional to OUT. Intuitively,if OUT is as large as ${AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,a set of samples ${S}_{I}$ are highly likely to be the actual join answers of $Q$ . In other words,the join answers are spread over a dense database. A small number of samples would give us enough information about the distribution of the join answers and estimating OUT accurately. In contrast, if OUT is small, the join answers would be skewed and sparsely distributed in certain regions of a database, and most samples would not be the join answers. Therefore, a small number of samples cannot effectively decrease the uncertainty of estimation, which may require a lot more sampling for an accurate estimation.

最后，我们简要解释为何SSTE、SUST、GJ-SAMPLE和DRS的$T$（或$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  T\right\rbrack$）与OUT成反比。直观而言，若OUT值大到${AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$，则样本集${S}_{I}$极可能是$Q$的实际连接结果。换言之，连接答案分布在稠密的数据库中。少量样本即可提供足够信息来掌握连接结果的分布特征并准确估算OUT值。反之，若OUT值较小，连接结果将呈现偏态分布，稀疏地集中在数据库某些区域，此时多数样本并非真实连接答案。因此，少量样本无法有效降低估算不确定性，可能需要大量采样才能实现精确估计。

### 5.6 Underlying Index Structure

### 5.6 底层索引结构

We explain the underlying index ${\operatorname{Index}}_{F}$ for each relation ${R}_{F}$ to evaluate the operation ${\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{F} \ltimes  s}\right)$ in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time,as mentioned in §5.2. We define ${F}_{I} = F \cap  I$ and ${F}_{s} = F \cap  {Attr}\left( s\right)$ ,where ${Attr}\left( s\right)$ is the set of attributes of $s$ . Note that ${F}_{I}$ and ${F}_{s}$ are disjoint in GenericCardEst. If ${F}_{s} = \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\ldots ,{A}_{m}}\right\}$ ,then ${R}_{F} \ltimes  s = {R}_{F} \ltimes$ ${\pi }_{{F}_{s}}\left( s\right)  = {\sigma }_{{A}_{1} = {\pi }_{{A}_{1}}s}{\sigma }_{{A}_{2} = {\pi }_{{A}_{2}}s}\ldots {\sigma }_{{A}_{m} = {\pi }_{{A}_{m}}s}\left( {R}_{F}\right) .$

如§5.2所述，我们阐释每个关系${R}_{F}$的底层索引${\operatorname{Index}}_{F}$，该索引可在$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间内完成${\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{F} \ltimes  s}\right)$运算。定义${F}_{I} = F \cap  I$和${F}_{s} = F \cap  {Attr}\left( s\right)$，其中${Attr}\left( s\right)$表示$s$的属性集合。注意在GenericCardEst中，${F}_{I}$与${F}_{s}$互不相交。若${F}_{s} = \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\ldots ,{A}_{m}}\right\}$，则${R}_{F} \ltimes  s = {R}_{F} \ltimes$${\pi }_{{F}_{s}}\left( s\right)  = {\sigma }_{{A}_{1} = {\pi }_{{A}_{1}}s}{\sigma }_{{A}_{2} = {\pi }_{{A}_{2}}s}\ldots {\sigma }_{{A}_{m} = {\pi }_{{A}_{m}}s}\left( {R}_{F}\right) .$

A mild assumption in [31] is to build a B-tree-like index (e.g., a trie index in [1]) under a global attribute order over the whole database. Then,if all attributes in ${F}_{s}$ precede all attributes in ${F}_{I},{R}_{F} \ltimes  s$ is readily available as an index lookup of ${\operatorname{Index}}_{F}\left( s\right)  = {\operatorname{Index}}_{F}\left( {{\pi }_{{F}_{s}}\left( s\right) }\right)$ . Furthermore,if no attribute in $F$ lies between ${F}_{s}$ and ${F}_{I},{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{F} \ltimes  s}\right)$ is an index lookup ${\operatorname{Index}}_{F}\left( s\right)$ up to depth $\left| {F}_{I}\right|$ . To exploit these,the selection of $I$ in each step of GENERICCARDEST should be consistent with the global order. Instead,we can build $\left| F\right|$ ! indexes for ${R}_{F}$ , one for each possible attribute order as in [1], to enable arbitrary selection of $I$ .

文献[31]提出一个温和假设：基于全局属性顺序构建类B树索引（如文献[1]中的字典树索引）。若${F}_{s}$所有属性都排列在${F}_{I},{R}_{F} \ltimes  s$之前，则${\operatorname{Index}}_{F}\left( s\right)  = {\operatorname{Index}}_{F}\left( {{\pi }_{{F}_{s}}\left( s\right) }\right)$可直接通过索引查找获得。进一步，若$F$中不存在位于${F}_{s}$与${F}_{I},{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{F} \ltimes  s}\right)$之间的属性，则${\operatorname{Index}}_{F}\left( s\right)$索引查找深度不超过$\left| {F}_{I}\right|$。为利用此特性，GENERICCARDEST每步选择的$I$需与全局顺序一致。替代方案是构建$\left| F\right|$!个索引（如文献[1]），为${R}_{F}$每种可能的属性顺序建立索引，从而实现$I$的任意选择。

Remark. The complexity of building an index for ${R}_{F}$ is linear,i.e., $\widetilde{O}\left( \left| {R}_{F}\right| \right)$ [1]. However,in contrast to the pre-computing overheads in GJ-SAMPLE, the indexing occurs once per database instead of per query. Therefore, its complexity is ignored from the analysis of ${T}_{\mathcal{H}}$ in $§{5.5}$ . Due to their simple structures,indexes can be easily extended to dynamic setting where tuples can be inserted and deleted, with a constant or logarithmic update time.

注记。为${R}_{F}$构建索引的复杂度是线性的，即$\widetilde{O}\left( \left| {R}_{F}\right| \right)$[1]。但与GJ-SAMPLE的预计算开销不同，索引构建是每个数据库一次性操作而非每查询执行。因此，在分析$§{5.5}$中的${T}_{\mathcal{H}}$时忽略该复杂度。由于结构简单，这些索引可轻松扩展至支持元组增删的动态场景，更新时间保持常数或对数级。

## 6 ACHIEVING A GENERALIZED BOUND

## 6 实现广义边界

In this section,we generalize our $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ bound for join size estimation using generalized hypertree decompositions (GHDs, see $§2)$ and achieve a better bound than $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ ,which is the bound of an exact aggregation algorithm AGGROGHDJOIN using GHDs (see §A.3). We leave uniform sampling using GHDs as a future work in $\$ 8$ . Our new bound may be better than our previous bound $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\text{ OUT }}\right)$ ,especially when OUT is small (see Example 3). We first provide background about GHDs and related algorithms.

本节中，我们利用广义超树分解(GHDs，参见$§2)$)推广了连接大小估计的$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$界，获得了优于$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$的界——后者是基于GHDs的精确聚合算法AGGROGHDJOIN的界(见附录A.3)。我们将GHDs均匀采样留作$\$ 8$中的未来工作。新界可能优于先前提出的$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\text{ OUT }}\right)$界，特别是在OUT较小时(见示例3)。首先介绍GHDs及相关算法的背景知识。

### 6.1 Aggregations over GHDs

### 6.1 基于GHDs的聚合计算

Instead of performing joins, OUT can be more efficiently computed by solving an AJAR (Aggregations and Joins over Annotated Relations) query [25]. AJAR queries assume that each relation ${R}_{F}$ is annotated; ${R}_{F} = \{ \left( {r,\lambda \left( r\right) }\right) \}$ where each tuple $r$ has an annotation $\lambda \left( r\right)$ (from some domain $\mathbb{K}$ ). If two tuples ${r}_{1}$ and ${r}_{2}$ are joined,the joined tuple ${r}_{1} \bowtie  {r}_{2}$ has $\lambda \left( {r}_{1}\right)  \otimes  \lambda \left( {r}_{2}\right)$ as its annotation. Hence, joining annotated relations results in another annotated relation:

相较于直接执行连接操作，通过求解AJAR(带标注关系上的聚合与连接)查询[25]可更高效计算OUT。AJAR查询假设每个关系${R}_{F}$都带有标注：${R}_{F} = \{ \left( {r,\lambda \left( r\right) }\right) \}$其中每个元组$r$拥有来自某域$\mathbb{K}$的标注$\lambda \left( r\right)$。若两个元组${r}_{1}$与${r}_{2}$连接，所得元组${r}_{1} \bowtie  {r}_{2}$的标注为$\lambda \left( {r}_{1}\right)  \otimes  \lambda \left( {r}_{2}\right)$。因此，带标注关系的连接将产生新的带标注关系：

$$
{ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F} = \left\{  {\left( {r,\lambda }\right)  \mid  r\text{ is a join answer,}\lambda  = { \otimes  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}\lambda \left( {{\pi }_{F}r}\right) }\right\}  . \tag{4}
$$

Furthermore, we can define an aggregation over an annotated relation $R$ with attributes $\mathcal{A}$ by a pair of attribute $A$ and sum operator $\oplus  \left\lbrack  {25}\right\rbrack$ . Let $G = \mathcal{A} \smallsetminus  \{ A\}$ . Then,the $\left( {A, \oplus  }\right)$ -aggregation of $R$ generates an annotated relation ${R}_{G}$ of attributes $G$ ,where $G$ is the grouping/output attributes,and ${\lambda }_{G}$ is an aggregated result for ${r}_{G}$ :

此外，我们可以通过属性$A$与求和运算符$\oplus  \left\lbrack  {25}\right\rbrack$的组合，定义带标注关系$R$(含属性$\mathcal{A}$)上的聚合。设$G = \mathcal{A} \smallsetminus  \{ A\}$，则$R$的$\left( {A, \oplus  }\right)$-聚合将生成带属性$G$的标注关系${R}_{G}$，其中$G$为分组/输出属性，${\lambda }_{G}$是对${r}_{G}$的聚合结果：

$$
\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{A, \oplus  }}R = \left\{  {\left( {{r}_{G},{\lambda }_{G}}\right)  : {r}_{G} \in  {\pi }_{G}R\text{ and }{\lambda }_{G} = {\bigoplus }_{\left( {r,\lambda }\right)  \in  R : {\pi }_{G}r = {r}_{G}}\lambda }\right\}  . \tag{5}
$$

By definition,no tuple ${r}_{G}$ is duplicated in the aggregation result. If multiple aggregation attributes $M = \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\ldots ,{A}_{n}}\right\}$ share the same $\oplus$ operator,we simply write $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{1}, \oplus  }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{2}, \oplus  }}\ldots \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{n}, \oplus  }}$ as $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{M, \oplus  }}$ . Here,attributes in $M$ are marginalized out and removed from the resulting relation. The output attributes $G$ becomes $\mathcal{A} \smallsetminus  M$ . Altogether,if $\left( {\mathbb{K},\oplus , \otimes  }\right)$ forms a commutative semiring [21],we can aggregate over joins (of annotated relations) [25] as $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{M, \oplus  }}{ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F}$ . If $\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{Z},\lambda \left( r\right)  = 1\forall r \in  {R}_{F}, \oplus   =  + , \otimes   =  \times$ ,and $M = \mathcal{V}$ ,the query $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\mathcal{V}, + }}{ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F}$ generates an annotated relation with a single tuple $r$ without any attribute. Here, $\lambda \left( r\right)  = \mathrm{{OUT}} = \left| {{ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F}}\right|$ . We hereafter omit $\oplus$ whenever possible without ambiguity. In addition, AJAR queries are known to be more general than FAQ queries [2], for a query can have multiple aggregation operators [25].

根据定义，聚合结果中不存在重复的元组${r}_{G}$。若多个聚合属性$M = \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\ldots ,{A}_{n}}\right\}$共享相同的$\oplus$运算符，我们简记为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{1}, \oplus  }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{2}, \oplus  }}\ldots \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{n}, \oplus  }}$为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{M, \oplus  }}$。此处，$M$中的属性被边缘化并从结果关系中移除。输出属性$G$将变为$\mathcal{A} \smallsetminus  M$。总体而言，当$\left( {\mathbb{K},\oplus , \otimes  }\right)$构成交换半环[21]时，我们可以对（带标注关系的）连接结果进行聚合[25]，记为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{M, \oplus  }}{ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F}$。若$\mathbb{K} = \mathbb{Z},\lambda \left( r\right)  = 1\forall r \in  {R}_{F}, \oplus   =  + , \otimes   =  \times$且$M = \mathcal{V}$，查询$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\mathcal{V}, + }}{ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F}$将生成仅含单个无属性元组$r$的标注关系。此时$\lambda \left( r\right)  = \mathrm{{OUT}} = \left| {{ \bowtie  }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{F}}\right|$。下文在不引起歧义的情况下将省略$\oplus$。此外，AJAR查询比FAQ查询[2]更具通用性，因为单个查询可包含多个聚合运算符[25]。

Joglekar et al. We prove in $§$ A. 3 that ${w}^{ * } \geq  {fhtw}$ for general AJAR queries and ${w}^{ * } = {fhtw}$ for computing OUT. Since we show in $§{6.3}$ that our new bound using GHDs is smaller than $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }}\right)$ , it is also smaller than $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{w}^{ * }}\right)$ .

Joglekar等人[25]提出了AggroGHDJoIN和AGGROYAN-NAKAKIS算法，用于在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{w}^{ * }} + {\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\text{agg }}}\right)$时间内求解AJAR查询，其中${w}^{ * }$表示算法宽度，${\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{agg}$为聚合结果的输出规模（通常小于OUT值，例如本案例中为1）。我们在$§$附录A.3中证明：通用AJAR查询的复杂度为${w}^{ * } \geq  {fhtw}$，计算OUT值的复杂度为${w}^{ * } = {fhtw}$。由于$§{6.3}$表明采用GHDs的新边界值小于$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }}\right)$，因此也必然小于$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{w}^{ * }}\right)$。

The key idea of GHDCARDEST is to pushdown the partial sum $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\chi \left( t\right)  \smallsetminus  G\left( t\right) }}$ from $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{\mathcal{V}}$ to each node $t$ in computing ${R}_{t}$ ,which is similar to the key idea of AGGROGHDJOIN that pushes down the aggregation attributes in GHD as deeply as possible. However, it is slightly different since we shrink each relation obtained from a node before the join phase in AGGROYANNAKAKIS; AGGROYANNAKAKIS aggregates after joining each pair of parent-child relations in GHD.

GHDCARDEST的核心思想是将部分和$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\chi \left( t\right)  \smallsetminus  G\left( t\right) }}$从$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{\mathcal{V}}$下推到计算${R}_{t}$时的每个节点$t$，这与AGGROGHDJOIN算法的关键思想相似——后者尽可能地将聚合属性在GHD中向下推送。但存在细微差异：在AGGROYANNAKAKIS中，我们会在连接阶段前收缩从节点获得的关系；而AGGROYANNAKAKIS是在GHD中逐对父子关系连接后才进行聚合。

We also argue that our definition of $G\left( t\right)$ is minimal; if an attribute in $G\left( t\right)$ is excluded from ${R}_{t}$ ,the node $t$ loses a join relationship with another node. Our $G\left( t\right)$ also minimizes the runtime of a non-sampling procedure, GENERICJOIN at Line 2 of Algorithm 2. Its runtime $\widetilde{O}\left( {{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{t}^{G\left( t\right) }\right) }\right)$ increases with the output attributes $G\left( t\right)$ due to the node-monotone property of the AGM bound [25].

我们还论证了$G\left( t\right)$的定义是最小的：如果从${R}_{t}$中排除$G\left( t\right)$的某个属性，节点$t$就会失去与其他节点的连接关系。我们的$G\left( t\right)$同时最小化了非抽样过程（算法2第2行的GENERICJOIN）的运行时间。由于AGM界[25]的节点单调性，其运行时$\widetilde{O}\left( {{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{t}^{G\left( t\right) }\right) }\right)$会随着输出属性$G\left( t\right)$的增加而增长。

### 6.3 Analysis of GHDCARDEST

### 6.3 GHDCARDEST算法分析

Using the analyses in $\$ {5.5}$ as building blocks,we analyze the variance and runtime of GHDCARDEST. For each $\left( {{g}_{t},Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right)  \in  {R}_{t}$ for a GHD node $t$ ,we attach two additional annotations $\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack$ and $T\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack$ (the runtime of obtaining $Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack$ ),and let ${\mathcal{H}}_{t,{g}_{t}}$ (or simply ${\mathcal{H}}_{{g}_{t}}$ ) denote the residual hypergraph of ${\mathcal{H}}_{t}$ given ${g}_{t}$ in Line 3 of Algorithm 2. Then, $Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack$ is an unbiased estimate of $\lambda \left( {g}_{t}\right)  = \operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{g}_{t}}\right)$ from Lemma 1. We also define $\left( {f,Z\left\lbrack  f\right\rbrack  }\right)$ as an annotated tuple in ${ \bowtie  }_{t \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}{R}_{t}$ so $Z\left\lbrack  f\right\rbrack   = \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{t \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}}Z\left\lbrack  {{\pi }_{G\left( t\right) }f}\right\rbrack  .$

以$\$ {5.5}$中的分析为基础模块，我们研究了GHDCARDEST的方差与运行时特征。针对GHD节点$t$的每个$\left( {{g}_{t},Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right)  \in  {R}_{t}$，我们附加两个额外注解$\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack$和$T\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack$（获取$Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack$的运行时），并令${\mathcal{H}}_{t,{g}_{t}}$（或简写为${\mathcal{H}}_{{g}_{t}}$）表示算法2第3行中给定${g}_{t}$时${\mathcal{H}}_{t}$的残差超图。根据引理1，$Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack$是$\lambda \left( {g}_{t}\right)  = \operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{g}_{t}}\right)$的无偏估计。我们还将$\left( {f,Z\left\lbrack  f\right\rbrack  }\right)$定义为${ \bowtie  }_{t \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}{R}_{t}$中的带注解元组，因此$Z\left\lbrack  f\right\rbrack   = \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{t \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}}Z\left\lbrack  {{\pi }_{G\left( t\right) }f}\right\rbrack  .$

Recall that our primary goal for join size estimation is to perform $\left( {1 \pm  \epsilon }\right)$ -approximation of OUT. To use Chebyshev’s inequality,we should bound $\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  Z\right\rbrack$ by ${\epsilon }^{2}\delta {\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2}$ as in $§{5.5}$ . Then the following questions arise: 1) Can we make this inequality hold for any $\epsilon$ and $\delta$ ? 2) How would the runtime $T$ be expressed? To answer these questions,we first express $Z$ using $Z\left\lbrack  f\right\rbrack$ values.

需知连接大小估算的主要目标是实现OUT的$\left( {1 \pm  \epsilon }\right)$近似。应用切比雪夫不等式时，需如$§{5.5}$所示将$\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  Z\right\rbrack$约束在${\epsilon }^{2}\delta {\mathrm{{OUT}}}^{2}$范围内。由此产生两个问题：1)该不等式是否对所有$\epsilon$和$\delta$成立？2)运行时$T$应如何表述？为解答这些问题，我们首先用$Z\left\lbrack  f\right\rbrack$值表示$Z$。

$$

$$

$$

$$

$$
\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  f\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack   = \mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{{g}_{t} \in  f}}Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack   \tag{8}

If there are multiple non-join attributes ${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\ldots ,{X}_{n}$ ,we skip all of these,sample for all join attributes only,then return $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{1 \leq  i \leq  n}}\left| {\Omega }_{\left\{  {X}_{i}\right\}  }\right|$ at Line 2 .

在GenericCardEst中，对任何非连接属性进行采样${s}_{I}$都是不必要的，因此可以安全跳过。以图1中的示例查询为例，${W}^{\prime }$是唯一的非连接属性。假设当前样本为$s = \left\{  {{x}^{\prime },{y}^{\prime },{z}^{\prime }}\right\}$（属性包括${X}^{\prime },{Y}^{\prime },{Z}^{\prime }$）和$I = \left\{  {W}^{\prime }\right\}$。那么，对任何${s}_{I} \in  {\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{{W}^{\prime }}\left( {{R}_{\left\{  {Z}^{\prime },{W}^{\prime }\right\}  } \ltimes  {z}^{\prime }}\right)$进行采样都不会影响其余顶点的样本空间和样本量，即$X,Y$和$Z$。因此我们跳过${W}^{\prime }$，直接在第二行返回$\frac{1}{P\left( {s}_{\left\{  {W}^{\prime }\right\}  }\right) } = \left| {\Omega }_{\left\{  {W}^{\prime }\right\}  }\right|  =$$\left| {{\pi }_{{W}^{\prime }}\left( {{R}_{\left\{  {Z}^{\prime },{W}^{\prime }\right\}  } \ltimes  {z}^{\prime }}\right) }\right|$而非第一行。若存在多个非连接属性${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\ldots ,{X}_{n}$，我们会跳过所有这些属性，仅对所有连接属性进行采样，然后在第二行返回$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{1 \leq  i \leq  n}}\left| {\Omega }_{\left\{  {X}_{i}\right\}  }\right|$。

In GHDCARDEST, calling GROUPBYCARDEST for single-edge GHD nodes can be safely skipped. If a GHD node $t$ is covered by a single edge,i.e., $\exists F \in  \mathcal{E} : \chi \left( t\right)  \subseteq  F$ ,we can directly obtain $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack   = \left| {{R}_{F} \propto  {g}_{t}}\right|$ for every ${g}_{t}$ in ${R}_{t}$ without sampling in Group-ByCARDEST. To be consistent,we regard ${R}_{t}$ as ${\pi }_{\chi \left( t\right) }{R}_{F}$ (with annotations) in order to remove any duplicates in ${R}_{t}$ as mentioned in §6.1. ${t}_{0}$ of ${\mathcal{T}}_{2}$ in Figure 1 is an example of a single-edge node,where $Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{{t}_{0}}\right\rbrack   = \left| {{R}_{\left\{  X,{X}^{\prime }\right\}  } \ltimes  {g}_{{t}_{0}}}\right|$ for ${g}_{{t}_{0}} \in  {R}_{{t}_{0}}$ . Therefore, $\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{{t}_{0}}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack   = 0$ and $T\left\lbrack  {g}_{{t}_{0}}\right\rbrack   = \widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ ,reducing both $\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  Z\right\rbrack$ and $T$ .

在GHDCARDEST中，对单边GHD节点调用GROUPBYCARDEST可安全跳过。若GHD节点$t$被单条边覆盖（即$\exists F \in  \mathcal{E} : \chi \left( t\right)  \subseteq  F$），我们无需通过Group-ByCARDEST采样，可直接从${R}_{t}$中获取每个${g}_{t}$对应的$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack   = \left| {{R}_{F} \propto  {g}_{t}}\right|$。为保持一致性，我们将${R}_{t}$视为带标注的${\pi }_{\chi \left( t\right) }{R}_{F}$，以消除如第6.1节所述的重复项。图1中${\mathcal{T}}_{2}$的${t}_{0}$就是单边节点的实例，其中${g}_{{t}_{0}} \in  {R}_{{t}_{0}}$对应的$Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{{t}_{0}}\right\rbrack   = \left| {{R}_{\left\{  X,{X}^{\prime }\right\}  } \ltimes  {g}_{{t}_{0}}}\right|$。因此，$\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{{t}_{0}}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack   = 0$且$T\left\lbrack  {g}_{{t}_{0}}\right\rbrack   = \widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$，同时减少了$\mathbb{V}\operatorname{ar}\left\lbrack  Z\right\rbrack$和$T$。

## 8 RESEARCH OPPORTUNITIES

## 8 研究机遇

We present six research opportunities based on our study. First, we could express our bound $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathop{\max }\limits_{t}{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{t}^{G\left( t\right) }\right)  + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{t}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\left( {{g}_{t},Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right)  \in  {R}_{t}}}}\right.$ $\left. \frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{g}_{t}}\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{g}_{t}}\right) }\right)$ in $§6$ in a more succinct form by removing internal ${g}_{t}$ terms,e.g.,to $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ . Then,we could more clearly characterize the gap between this bound and $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ or $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ .

基于我们的研究，我们提出六个探索方向。首先，可通过移除内部${g}_{t}$项（例如$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$）以更简洁形式表达$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathop{\max }\limits_{t}{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{t}^{G\left( t\right) }\right)  + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{t}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\left( {{g}_{t},Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right)  \in  {R}_{t}}}}\right.$$\left. \frac{{AGM}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{g}_{t}}\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{{g}_{t}}\right) }\right)$在$§6$中的边界。其次，可更清晰刻画该边界与$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{\operatorname{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$或$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$之间的差距。

Second, we could dynamically change the sampling approach based on the guess of OUT. Our bound $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\text{ OUT }}\right)$ in §5 can be small as a constant if OUT is large as ${AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,while the bound in $§6$ has advantages over small OUT (e.g.,Example 3). As stated in §5.5, we could start with GENERICCARDEST assuming a large OUT, increase the sample size, adjust the assumption, and change to GHDCARDEST if the estimated OUT becomes small.

第二，可根据OUT的预测动态调整抽样方法。当OUT值如${AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$般较大时，第5节的边界$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }{\text{ OUT }}\right)$可小至常数；而$§6$中的边界在小OUT场景（如例3）更具优势。如第5.5节所述，可先采用GENERICCARDEST假设较大OUT值，随后增加样本量、调整假设，并在估计OUT变小时切换至GHDCARDEST。

Third, we could even change between sampling and join, then apply the same analysis. While the join algorithms have zero error but +OUT term in their runtime, sampling algorithms have nonzero errors but $\frac{1}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$ factor in their runtime. Therefore,it might be possible to balance between sampling and join to reduce the runtime and error for both large- and small-OUT cases, i.e., select a sampling-like algorithm for large OUT and a join-like algorithm for small OUT.

第三，我们甚至能在抽样与连接操作间切换并保持分析框架不变。连接算法虽无误差但其运行时含+OUT项，而抽样算法存在非零误差但具$\frac{1}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}$因子。因此可通过平衡两种策略来降低大/小OUT情况下的运行时与误差——大OUT时选择类抽样算法，小OUT时选择类连接算法。

Fourth, we could develop join size estimators with lower complexities using more information about relations, e.g., degree constraints or functional dependencies [3] other than the cardinality constrains in Definition 1.

第四，可利用关系属性中的度约束或函数依赖[3]等额外信息（超越定义1的基数约束），开发复杂度更低的连接大小估计算法。

Fifth, we could extend GHDCARDEST to perform uniform sampling over arbitrary GHDs from the observations in $\$$ A. 2 that 1) EXACTWEIGHT [34] performs uniform sampling over certain types of GHDs for acyclic joins, and 2) GHDCARDEST can be easily modified for uniform sampling over the same GHDs. In Algorithm 6, the initial value of $W\left( {g}_{t}\right)$ (sampling weight for a tuple ${g}_{t}$ in ${R}_{t}$ ) is set to $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack   = \left| {{R}_{{F}_{t}} \ltimes  {g}_{t}}\right|$ . Therefore,we could extend ExACTWEIGHT and GHDCARDEST for arbitrary GHDs for even cyclic joins, starting from initializing $W\left( {g}_{t}\right)$ with an estimate $Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack$ instead of $\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack$ .

第五，根据$\$$A.2的观察，可将GHDCARDEST扩展至任意GHD上的均匀抽样：1)EXACTWEIGHT[34]能在无环连接的特定GHD类型上实现均匀抽样；2)GHDCARDEST稍作修改即可支持相同GHD的均匀抽样。算法6中$W\left( {g}_{t}\right)$（${R}_{t}$内元组${g}_{t}$的抽样权重）初始值设为$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack   = \left| {{R}_{{F}_{t}} \ltimes  {g}_{t}}\right|$。因此可通过将$W\left( {g}_{t}\right)$初始值改为估计量$Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack$而非$\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack  {Z\left\lbrack  {g}_{t}\right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack$，将ExACTWEIGHT与GHDCARDEST扩展至循环连接的任意GHD场景。

Sixth, we could extend our algorithms to more general problems, approximate counting and uniform sampling for conjunctive queries $\left\lbrack  {5,{16}}\right\rbrack$ or join-project queries ${\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right)$ ,where $O$ is the set of output attributes [11]. We explain in $\$ \mathrm{C}$ that our degree-based rejection sampling can be easily extended for join-project queries, following the same analysis in Section 3 of [11]. We achieve $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\left| {\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) \right| }\right)$ runtime which is again smaller than that of GJ-SAMPLE.

第六，我们可以将算法扩展到更一般性问题，即针对合取查询$\left\lbrack  {5,{16}}\right\rbrack$或连接-投影查询${\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right)$的近似计数与均匀采样，其中$O$是输出属性集[11]。我们在$\$ \mathrm{C}$中说明，基于度的拒绝采样方法可轻松扩展至连接-投影查询，其分析过程与文献[11]第3节相同。最终实现$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\left| {\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) \right| }\right)$的运行时复杂度，该值仍小于GJ-SAMPLE算法。

## 9 CONCLUSION

## 9 结论

In this paper, we have presented a new sampling-based method for join size estimation and uniform sampling. Our method solves an open problem in the literature [11], achieving (1± $\epsilon$ )-approximation of OUT in $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{OUT}\right)$ time for arbitrary join queries. We presented a unified approach that explains and analyzes four known methods: SSTE, SUST, ALLEY, and GJ-SAMPLE. We then extended our method using GHDs to achieve a better bound than $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ for small OUT and optimized the efficiency and accuracy using two approaches. Finally, we have highlighted several interesting research opportunities building on our results. We believe that our work may facilitate studies on achieving lower bounds on the uniform sampling and size estimation over joins.

本文提出了一种新的基于采样的连接大小估计与均匀采样方法。我们的方法解决了文献[11]中的开放性问题，可在$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{OUT}\right)$时间内对任意连接查询实现OUT的(1±$\epsilon$)近似。通过统一框架解释并分析了四种现有方法：SSTE、SUST、ALLEY和GJ-SAMPLE。随后利用广义超树分解(GHDs)扩展方法，针对小规模OUT获得了优于$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{AGM}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$的边界，并通过两种方案优化了效率与精度。最后基于研究成果指出了若干潜在研究方向。我们相信这项工作将推动连接操作上均匀采样与规模估计下界研究的进展。

Here,Val denotes the set of valid GHDs of $\mathcal{H}$ ,that preserve the aggregation ordering of the specified aggregation $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{1},{ \oplus  }_{1}}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{2},{ \oplus  }_{2}}}\ldots \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{n},{ \oplus  }_{n}}}$ ; changing the order of $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{i},{ \oplus  }_{i}}}$ and $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{j},{ \oplus  }_{j}}}$ might vary the aggregation result [25]. Since $\operatorname{fhtw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \mathop{\min }\limits_{\left( \mathcal{T},\chi \right) }\operatorname{fhtw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right)$ from Definition 3 and Val is a subset of the set of all GHDs, ${w}^{ * } \geq  \operatorname{fhtw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ . However,as in our case where all aggregation operators are the same $\left( {{ \oplus  }_{i} =  + }\right)$ ,thus commutative, any aggregation order gives the same aggregation result. Therefore, ${Val}$ becomes the set of all GHDs. This leads to ${w}^{ * } = {fhtw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ .

根据GENERICJOIN和AGGROYANNAKAKIS的运行时间，AggroGHDJoIN的运行时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) } + {\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\text{agg }}}\right)$。在文献[25]中，AGGROGHDJOIN的运行时间表示为$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{w}^{ * }} + {\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\text{agq }}}\right)$，其中${w}^{ * } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  \operatorname{Val}}}$fhtw(T,H)。此处，Val表示$\mathcal{H}$的有效GHD集合，这些GHD保持了指定聚合$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{1},{ \oplus  }_{1}}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{2},{ \oplus  }_{2}}}\ldots \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{n},{ \oplus  }_{n}}}$的排序；改变$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{i},{ \oplus  }_{i}}}$和$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{A}_{j},{ \oplus  }_{j}}}$的顺序可能会影响聚合结果[25]。由于根据定义3可知$\operatorname{fhtw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \mathop{\min }\limits_{\left( \mathcal{T},\chi \right) }\operatorname{fhtw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right)$，且Val是所有GHD集合的子集，因此${w}^{ * } \geq  \operatorname{fhtw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$。然而，在我们的案例中，所有聚合运算符都相同$\left( {{ \oplus  }_{i} =  + }\right)$且满足交换律，因此任何聚合顺序都会产生相同的聚合结果。因此，${Val}$就变成了所有GHD的集合。这导致了${w}^{ * } = {fhtw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$。

## C EXTENSION TO CONJUNCTIVE QUERIES

## C 对合取查询的扩展

From the introduction, the join query we have considered so far is a full conjunctive query where all attributes are output attributes. In this section, we easily extend our degree-based rejection sampling to approximate counting and uniform sampling for join-project queries (a.k.a. conjunctive queries) [5, 11, 16], following the same analysis of Section 3 in [11].

根据引言部分，我们迄今研究的连接查询均为全合取查询（所有属性皆为输出属性）。本节将基于文献[11]第3节的分析框架，将基于度数的拒绝采样法扩展应用于连接-投影查询（即合取查询）[5,11,16]的近似计数与均匀采样。

Let ${\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right)$ be a join-project query,where $O$ is the set of projection/output attributes and $Q$ is a join query. We can use the two-step approach in [11]: (Step 1) use Algorithm 1 to sample a join result $s$ from ${Q}_{O} \mathrel{\text{:=}} { \bowtie  }_{F \in  {E}_{O}}{\pi }_{O}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$ ,i.e.,join the relations that contain any attribute in $O$ only. Here,if ${Q}_{O}$ is disconnected,we take a sample from each connected component. (Step 2) check if ${Q}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}\left( s\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} { \bowtie  }_{F \in  {E}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}}\left( {{\pi }_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}\left( {{R}_{F} \bowtie  s}\right) }\right)$ is empty. If not,we return $s$ as a sampled result,otherwise,we repeat. Then,the final $s$ returned is a result in ${\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right)$ . Using the same analysis of [11],sampling a tuple in ${\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right)$ takes $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) }\right)$ time in expectation for our method.

设${\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right)$为连接-投影查询，其中$O$是投影/输出属性集，$Q$为连接查询。可采用文献[11]中的两步法：（步骤1）使用算法1从${Q}_{O} \mathrel{\text{:=}} { \bowtie  }_{F \in  {E}_{O}}{\pi }_{O}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$中采样连接结果$s$，即仅连接包含$O$中属性的关系。若${Q}_{O}$不连通，则从各连通分量分别采样。（步骤2）检查${Q}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}\left( s\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} { \bowtie  }_{F \in  {E}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}}\left( {{\pi }_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}\left( {{R}_{F} \bowtie  s}\right) }\right)$是否为空。若非空则返回$s$作为采样结果，否则重复该过程。最终返回的$s$即为${\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right)$中的结果。根据文献[11]的相同分析，本方法采样${\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right)$中元组的期望时间复杂度为$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) }\right)$。

Step 1 of our algorithm takes time $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) }\right)$ in expectation. Step 2 takes time $\widetilde{O}\left( {{AGM}\left( {{Q}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}\left( s\right) }\right) }\right)$ ,using any worst-case optimal join algorithm,e.g.,GENERICJOIN. Because each $s \in  {Q}_{O}$ is sampled with probability $\frac{1}{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) }$ ,the expected runtime of Step 2 is (the big-Oh of) $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{s \in  {Q}_{O}}}\frac{{AGM}\left( {{Q}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}\left( s\right) }\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) } \leq  \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) }$ where the inequality follows from the query decomposition lemma (Lemma 6). Please refer to Section 3 of [11] for the remaining part of the analysis proving the $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) }\right)$ time.

算法步骤1的期望时间复杂度为$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) }\right)$。步骤2采用任意最坏情况最优连接算法（如GENERICJOIN）耗时$\widetilde{O}\left( {{AGM}\left( {{Q}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}\left( s\right) }\right) }\right)$。由于每个$s \in  {Q}_{O}$的采样概率为$\frac{1}{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) }$，步骤2的期望运行时间复杂度为（大O记号）$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{s \in  {Q}_{O}}}\frac{{AGM}\left( {{Q}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  O}\left( s\right) }\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) } \leq  \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {Q}_{O}\right) }$，该不等式源自查询分解引理（引理6）。完整分析证明$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) }\right)$时间复杂度的其余部分请参阅文献[11]第3节。

Here, we would like to address the complexity of computing ${\pi }_{O}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$ before calling Algorithm 1. If there exists an index for ${R}_{F}$ with the attribute order such that the attributes in $O \cap  F$ precede the attributes in $F \smallsetminus  O$ ,then ${\pi }_{O}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$ is readily available (i.e.,in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time) as explained in §5.6. This results in total $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) }\right)$ time. Otherwise, we need an additional linear-time preprocessing to obtain ${\pi }_{O}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$ from ${R}_{F}$ ,resulting in total $\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) } + \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$ time. Nevertheless, we emphasize that this is still lower than the complexity of GJ-SAMPLE for non-sequenceable join-project queries, which is $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) } + \mathrm{{IN}}}\right) \left\lbrack  {11}\right\rbrack$ .

在此，我们拟探讨调用算法1前计算${\pi }_{O}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$的复杂性。若存在具有属性排序的${R}_{F}$索引，使得$O \cap  F$中的属性优先于$F \smallsetminus  O$中的属性，则如第5.6节所述，${\pi }_{O}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$可直接获取（即耗时$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$），总体耗时$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\mathrm{{OUT}}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) }\right)$。否则，需额外进行线性时间预处理以从${R}_{F}$获取${\pi }_{O}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$，总体耗时增至$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) } + \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$。但需强调的是，这仍低于不可序列化连接-投影查询的GJ-SAMPLE算法复杂度$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  \frac{{AGM}\left( Q\right) }{\operatorname{OUT}\left( {{\pi }_{O}\left( Q\right) }\right) } + \mathrm{{IN}}}\right) \left\lbrack  {11}\right\rbrack$。

## D COMPONENT-AT-A-TIME SAMPLING

## D 逐组件采样

For each component, GENERICCARDEST (Algorithm 1) is called in two recursions,e.g.,if $\mathcal{H}$ consists of four odd cycles and three stars, the maximum recursion depth is $2 \cdot  \left( {4 + 3}\right)  + 1 = {15}$ ,including the calls that reach Line 2. Since the components are vertex-disjoint, i.e.,variable-disjoint, ${R}_{F} \ltimes  s = {R}_{F}$ whenever $F$ and (the attributes of) $s$ are contained in different components. Therefore,we can safely drop $\ltimes  s$ from the following explanations for the component-at-a-time methods.

对每个组件，GENERICCARDEST（算法1）在两次递归中被调用。例如，若$\mathcal{H}$包含四个奇数环和三颗星形结构，最大递归深度为$2 \cdot  \left( {4 + 3}\right)  + 1 = {15}$（含执行到第2行的调用）。由于各组件顶点互不相交（即变量互斥），当$F$与$s$（的属性）分属不同组件时，${R}_{F} \ltimes  s = {R}_{F}$恒成立。因此，在后续逐组件方法说明中可安全省略$\ltimes  s$。

We first explain for a star $S$ of $n$ edges ${F}_{1},{F}_{2},\ldots ,{F}_{n}$ . Let $A$ be the center attribute,i.e., $\{ A\}  = { \cap  }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,n}\right\rbrack  }{F}_{i}$ . For SSTE,in the first call to GENERICCARDEST, $I$ becomes the (singleton set of) center attribute, i.e., $I = \{ A\} ,{\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{A}{R}_{{F}_{1}}$ and $k = 1$ . To sample a vertex $a$ from ${\Omega }_{I}$ , SSTE first samples an edge $e$ from ${R}_{{F}_{1}}$ ,and let $a = {\pi }_{A}\left( e\right)$ . Then, $P\left( a\right)  = \frac{\left| {R}_{{F}_{1}} \ltimes  a\right| }{\left| {R}_{{F}_{1}}\right| }$ since $\left| {{R}_{{F}_{1}} \ltimes  a}\right|$ edges in ${R}_{{F}_{1}}$ have $a$ as their projection onto $A$ . When GENERICCARDEST is called for the second time, $I$ becomes the remaining attributes of $S$ ,i.e., $I = { \cup  }_{i}{F}_{i} \smallsetminus  \{ A\} ,{\Omega }_{I} =$ $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{i}{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}_{i}} \ltimes  a}\right)$ ,and $k = 1$ ; a sequence of $n$ vertices $\left( {{b}_{1},{b}_{2},\ldots ,{b}_{n}}\right)$ is sampled from ${\Omega }_{I}$ once (i.e., ${b}_{i}$ is sampled from ${\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}_{i}} \ltimes  a}\right)$ ). If $\left( {a,{b}_{1},{b}_{2},\ldots ,{b}_{n}}\right)$ forms a star in the database, $\mathbb{I}\left\lbrack  \cdot \right\rbrack$ for these two calls become 1 , and the recursion proceeds to the remaining components, with $O \leftarrow  O \smallsetminus  \left( {{ \cup  }_{i}{F}_{i}}\right)$ and $s \leftarrow  s \uplus  \left( {a,{b}_{1},{b}_{2},\ldots ,{b}_{n}}\right)$ .

首先解释具有$S$条$n$边${F}_{1},{F}_{2},\ldots ,{F}_{n}$的星型结构。设$A$为中心属性，即$\{ A\}  = { \cap  }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,n}\right\rbrack  }{F}_{i}$。对于SSTE算法，在首次调用GENERICCARDEST时，$I$成为（单例集的）中心属性，即$I = \{ A\} ,{\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{A}{R}_{{F}_{1}}$与$k = 1$。要从${\Omega }_{I}$中采样顶点$a$，SSTE先自${R}_{{F}_{1}}$采样边$e$，并令$a = {\pi }_{A}\left( e\right)$。此时$P\left( a\right)  = \frac{\left| {R}_{{F}_{1}} \ltimes  a\right| }{\left| {R}_{{F}_{1}}\right| }$，因为${R}_{{F}_{1}}$中有$\left| {{R}_{{F}_{1}} \ltimes  a}\right|$条边的$a$在$A$上的投影相同。当第二次调用GENERICCARDEST时，$I$转为$S$的剩余属性，即$I = { \cup  }_{i}{F}_{i} \smallsetminus  \{ A\} ,{\Omega }_{I} =$$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{i}{\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}_{i}} \ltimes  a}\right)$及$k = 1$；从${\Omega }_{I}$批量采样$n$个顶点$\left( {{b}_{1},{b}_{2},\ldots ,{b}_{n}}\right)$（即从${\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}_{i}} \ltimes  a}\right)$采样${b}_{i}$）。若$\left( {a,{b}_{1},{b}_{2},\ldots ,{b}_{n}}\right)$在数据库中构成星型，这两次调用的$\mathbb{I}\left\lbrack  \cdot \right\rbrack$值变为1，递归处理剩余组件时携带$O \leftarrow  O \smallsetminus  \left( {{ \cup  }_{i}{F}_{i}}\right)$和$s \leftarrow  s \uplus  \left( {a,{b}_{1},{b}_{2},\ldots ,{b}_{n}}\right)$。

For SUST,in the first call to GENERICCARDEST, $I$ is simply the set of all attributes of $S$ and ${\Omega }_{I} = \mathop{\prod }\limits_{i}{R}_{{F}_{i}},P\left( \cdot \right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\Omega }_{I}\right| }$ ,and $k = 1$ ; a sequence of $n$ edges is sampled once. $\mathbb{I}\left\lbrack  \cdot \right\rbrack$ is 1 iff they share a common vertex for the center. The second call to GENERICCARDEST is a dummy that directly proceeds to the remaining components.

对于SUST算法，首次调用GENERICCARDEST时，$I$仅是$S$和${\Omega }_{I} = \mathop{\prod }\limits_{i}{R}_{{F}_{i}},P\left( \cdot \right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\Omega }_{I}\right| }$的所有属性集合及$k = 1$；一次性采样$n$条边序列。当这些边共享中心顶点时，$\mathbb{I}\left\lbrack  \cdot \right\rbrack$值为1。第二次调用GENERICCARDEST为虚设操作，直接跳转处理剩余组件。

We next explain for an odd cycle $C$ of ${2n} + 1$ edges with the vertex (attribute) sequence $\left( {{u}_{1},{v}_{1},{u}_{2},{v}_{2},\ldots ,{u}_{n},{v}_{n},w,{u}_{1}}\right)$ where ${F}_{i} =$ $\left\{  {{u}_{i},{v}_{i}}\right\}$ for $i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,n}\right\rbrack$ and ${F}_{0} = \left\{  {{u}_{1},w}\right\}$ . A constraint is that the sampled vertex (attribute value) for ${u}_{1}$ must have the smallest degree among the sampled vertices,where the degree of a vertex $a$ is $\left| {R \bowtie  a}\right|$ (note that $R = {R}_{{F}_{i}}$ for every $i$ ). In the first call to GENERICCARDEST, $I$ contains all ${u}_{i}$ ’s and ${v}_{i}$ ’s except $w,{\Omega }_{I} = \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,n}\right\rbrack  }}{R}_{{F}_{i}}$ ,and $k = 1$ ; a sequence of $n$ edges $\left( {\left\{  {{a}_{1},{b}_{1}}\right\}  ,\ldots ,\left\{  {{a}_{n},{b}_{n}}\right\}  }\right)$ is sampled where ${u}_{i}$ and ${v}_{i}$ map to ${a}_{i}$ and ${b}_{i}$ . When GENERICCARDEST is called for the second time, $I = \{ w\}$ . SSTE and SUST differ here.

接下来，我们针对一个具有${2n} + 1$条边的奇数环$C$进行说明，其顶点（属性）序列为$\left( {{u}_{1},{v}_{1},{u}_{2},{v}_{2},\ldots ,{u}_{n},{v}_{n},w,{u}_{1}}\right)$，其中对于$i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,n}\right\rbrack$和${F}_{0} = \left\{  {{u}_{1},w}\right\}$，满足${F}_{i} =$$\left\{  {{u}_{i},{v}_{i}}\right\}$。约束条件是：为${u}_{1}$采样的顶点（属性值）必须在所有采样顶点中拥有最小度数，此处顶点$a$的度数定义为$\left| {R \bowtie  a}\right|$（注意：对于每个$i$，都有$R = {R}_{{F}_{i}}$）。在首次调用GENERICCARDEST时，$I$包含除$w,{\Omega }_{I} = \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,n}\right\rbrack  }}{R}_{{F}_{i}}$和$k = 1$之外的所有${u}_{i}$与${v}_{i}$；此时会采样由$n$条边组成的序列$\left( {\left\{  {{a}_{1},{b}_{1}}\right\}  ,\ldots ,\left\{  {{a}_{n},{b}_{n}}\right\}  }\right)$，其中${u}_{i}$和${v}_{i}$分别映射到${a}_{i}$和${b}_{i}$。当第二次调用GENERICCARDEST时，$I = \{ w\}$。此处SSTE与SUST存在差异。

For SSTE, ${\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}_{0}} \ltimes  {a}_{1}}\right) ,P\left( \cdot \right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\Omega }_{I}\right| }$ ,and $k$ is $\left\lceil  \frac{\left| {\Omega }_{I}\right| }{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}\right\rceil$ . Therefore, $k$ vertices are uniformly sampled from ${\Omega }_{I}$ with replacement. For SUST, $k$ is fixed to 1,and ${\Omega }_{I}$ is defined based on the degree of ${a}_{1}$ . If the degree is smaller than $\sqrt{\left| R\right| }$ ,then ${\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}_{0}} \ltimes  {a}_{1}}\right)$ and $P\left( \cdot \right)  = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}$ . If the degree is larger than $\sqrt{\left| R\right| }$ ,then ${\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{I}{R}_{{F}_{0}}$ and $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{\left| R \bowtie  {s}_{I}\right| }{\left| R\right| }$ . Then,at Line 7,1) any ${s}_{I}$ with a smaller degree than ${a}_{1}$ is rejected due to the constraint,and 2) an accepted ${s}_{I}$ is further kept with probability $\frac{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}{\left| R \ltimes  {s}_{I}\right| }$ ,which is less than 1 from $\left| {R \ltimes  {s}_{I}}\right|  \geq$ $\left| {R \propto  {a}_{1}}\right|  \geq  \sqrt{\left| R\right| }$ . Therefore, $P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$ is again $\frac{\left| R \propto  {s}_{I}\right| }{\left| R\right| }\frac{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}{\left| R \propto  {s}_{I}\right| } = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}$ . For both SSTE and SUST,if $\left( {{a}_{1},{b}_{1},\ldots ,{a}_{n},{b}_{n},{s}_{\{ w\} },{a}_{1}}\right)$ forms a cycle in the database, the recursion proceeds to the remaining components.

对于SSTE算法，${\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}_{0}} \ltimes  {a}_{1}}\right) ,P\left( \cdot \right)  = \frac{1}{\left| {\Omega }_{I}\right| }$，且$k$为$\left\lceil  \frac{\left| {\Omega }_{I}\right| }{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}\right\rceil$。因此，$k$顶点采用有放回方式从${\Omega }_{I}$中均匀采样。对于SUST算法，$k$固定为1，${\Omega }_{I}$根据${a}_{1}$的度数定义。若度数小于$\sqrt{\left| R\right| }$，则${\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{I}\left( {{R}_{{F}_{0}} \ltimes  {a}_{1}}\right)$且$P\left( \cdot \right)  = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}$；若度数大于$\sqrt{\left| R\right| }$，则${\Omega }_{I} = {\pi }_{I}{R}_{{F}_{0}}$且$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)  = \frac{\left| R \bowtie  {s}_{I}\right| }{\left| R\right| }$。接着在第7行：1) 任何度数小于${a}_{1}$的${s}_{I}$因约束条件被拒绝；2) 被接受的${s}_{I}$会以$\frac{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}{\left| R \ltimes  {s}_{I}\right| }$概率保留（该概率由$\left| {R \ltimes  {s}_{I}}\right|  \geq$$\left| {R \propto  {a}_{1}}\right|  \geq  \sqrt{\left| R\right| }$可知小于1）。因此，$P\left( {s}_{I}\right)$再次$\frac{\left| R \propto  {s}_{I}\right| }{\left| R\right| }\frac{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}{\left| R \propto  {s}_{I}\right| } = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\left| R\right| }}$。对于SSTE和SUST算法，若$\left( {{a}_{1},{b}_{1},\ldots ,{a}_{n},{b}_{n},{s}_{\{ w\} },{a}_{1}}\right)$在数据库中形成环，递归过程会继续处理剩余组件。

SUST guarantees the uniformity of sampling odd cycles and stars. To sample an odd cycle,SUST relies on the constraint that ${a}_{1}$ has the smallest degree. This is allowed in unlabeled graphs since other non-qualifying cycles (e.g., ${a}_{2}$ having the smallest degree) can be found by permuting the vertices and edges (e.g.,constrain w.r.t. ${a}_{1}$ and let $\left. {{a}_{2} = {a}_{1}}\right)$ . The new proof of SSTE also uses this constraint to bound the variance (see $§\mathrm{E}$ in our full paper [26]). However,in non-identical relations, we cannot permute the edges with different labels.

SUST算法保证奇数长度环和星型结构的均匀采样。为采样奇数环，SUST依赖${a}_{1}$具有最小度数的约束条件。这在未标注图中可行，因为其他不符合条件的环（如${a}_{2}$具有最小度数）可通过置换顶点和边来发现（例如相对于${a}_{1}$进行约束并令$\left. {{a}_{2} = {a}_{1}}\right)$）。SSTE的新证明也利用该约束来限制方差（参见我们全文[26]中的$§\mathrm{E}$）。但在非等同关系中，无法置换具有不同标签的边。

The essence of component-at-a-time sampling is to use the divide-and-conquer approach by breaking a query $\mathcal{H}$ into smaller components (i.e., odd cycles and stars), solve simpler problems for these components, and estimate back the cardinality of the original query. Lemmas 8 and 9 allow us to set an induction hypothesis that bounds the variance of estimates in simpler problems. We explain these lemmas in $§\mathrm{D}{.1}$ and extend them for labeled graphs in $§\mathrm{D}{.2}$ .

逐组件采样的核心是采用分治法：将查询$\mathcal{H}$分解为更小的组件（奇数环和星型结构），解决这些组件的简化问题，再反推原查询的基数估计。引理8和9允许我们建立归纳假设，限定简化问题中估计值的方差范围。我们将在$§\mathrm{D}{.1}$中阐释这些引理，并在$§\mathrm{D}{.2}$中将其扩展至标注图情形。

Lemma 8. (Decomposition Lemma) [6]: Given a query hyper-graph $\mathcal{H}\left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ of identical binary relations (i.e., ${R}_{F} = R : \forall F \in  \mathcal{E}$ ), the LP in Definition 1 admits a half-integral optimal solution $x$ where 1) ${x}_{F} \in  \left\{  {0,\frac{1}{2},1}\right\}   : \forall F \in  \mathcal{E}$ and 2) the support of $x,\operatorname{supp}\left( x\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ F \in$ $\left. {\mathcal{E}\left| {\;{x}_{F} > 0}\right. }\right\}$ ,forms a set of vertex-disjoint odd cycles (i.e.,each cycle has an odd number of edges) and stars.

Lemma 9. [6]: Suppose that $\mathcal{H}$ (with identical binary relations as $\mathcal{E}$ ) is decomposed into odd cycles and stars by a half-integral optimal solution $x$ in Lemma 8. For arbitrary subsets of odd cycles $\mathbb{C}$ and stars $\mathbb{S}$ of $\mathcal{H}$ ,let ${\mathcal{H}}_{s} = \left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{s},{\mathcal{E}}_{s}}\right)$ be the induced subquery of $\mathcal{H}$ on vertices of $\mathbb{C}$ and $\mathbb{S}$ . 引理9.[6]：假设$\mathcal{H}$（具有与$\mathcal{E}$相同的二元关系）通过引理8中的半整数最优解$x$被分解为奇圈和星形结构。对于$\mathcal{H}$的任意奇圈子集$\mathbb{C}$和星形子集$\mathbb{S}$，令${\mathcal{H}}_{s} = \left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{s},{\mathcal{E}}_{s}}\right)$表示由$\mathbb{C}$和$\mathbb{S}$顶点诱导的子查询。则对于${\mathcal{H}}_{s},{OU}{T}_{s} \leq  {\left| R\right| }^{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}}}{x}_{F}}$的输出规模${OU}{T}_{s}$，以及$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{S}}}{x}_{F} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{C\left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{C},{\mathcal{E}}_{C}}\right)  \in  \mathbb{C}}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{C}}}{x}_{F} + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{S\left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{S},{\mathcal{E}}_{S}}\right)  \in  \mathbb{S}}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{S}}}{x}_{F}.$
