## Abstract

## 摘要

In this paper, we investigate space-time tradeoffs for answering conjunctive queries with access patterns (CQAPs). The goal is to create a space-efficient data structure in an initial preprocessing phase and use it for answering (multiple) queries in an online phase. Previous work has developed data structures that trades off space usage for answering time for queries of practical interest, such as the path and triangle query. However, these approaches lack a comprehensive framework and are not generalizable. Our main contribution is a general algorithmic framework for obtaining space-time tradeoffs for any CQAP. Our framework builds upon the PANDA algorithm and tree decomposition techniques. We demonstrate that our framework captures all state-of-the-art tradeoffs that were independently produced for various queries. Further, we show surprising improvements over the state-of-the-art tradeoffs known in the existing literature for reachability queries.

本文研究带访问模式的合取查询(CQAPs)应答中的时空权衡问题。目标是在预处理阶段构建空间高效的数据结构，用于在线阶段应答(多个)查询。现有研究已针对路径查询、三角查询等实际查询开发了空间使用与应答时间相权衡的数据结构，但这些方法缺乏统一框架且难以泛化。我们的核心贡献是提出适用于任意CQAP的通用算法框架，该框架基于PANDA算法与树分解技术。我们证明该框架能涵盖所有针对不同查询独立提出的先进权衡方案，并展示了在可达性查询方面超越现有文献记载权衡方案的突破性改进。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

We study a class of problems that splits an algorithmic task into two phases: the preprocessing phase, which computes a space-efficient data structure from the input, and the online phase, which uses the data structure to answer requests of a specific form over the input as fast as possible. Many important algorithmic tasks such as set intersection problems $\left\lbrack  {8,{15}}\right\rbrack$ ,reachability in directed graphs $\lbrack 2,3$ , 9], histogram indexing [7, 25], and problems related to document retrieval $\left\lbrack  {1,{26}}\right\rbrack$ can be expressed in this way. The fundamental algorithmic question related to these problems is to find the tradeoff between the space $S$ necessary for storing the data structures and the time $T$ for answering a request.

我们研究一类将算法任务划分为两个阶段的问题：预处理阶段从输入数据构建空间高效的数据结构，在线阶段则利用该数据结构快速响应特定形式的查询请求。诸如集合交集问题$\left\lbrack  {8,{15}}\right\rbrack$、有向图可达性$\lbrack 2,3$、直方图索引[7,25]及文档检索相关任务$\left\lbrack  {1,{26}}\right\rbrack$等重要算法问题均可由此框架表达。其核心算法问题在于探寻数据结构存储空间$S$与查询响应时间$T$之间的权衡关系。

Let us look at one of the simplest tasks in this setup. Consider the 2-Set Disjointness problem: given a universe of elements $U$ and a collection of $m$ sets ${S}_{1},\ldots ,{S}_{m} \subseteq  U$ ,we want to create a data structure such that for any pair of integers $1 \leq  i,j \leq  m$ , we can efficiently decide whether ${S}_{i} \cap  {S}_{j}$ is empty or not. It is well-known that the space-time tradeoff for 2-Set Disjointness is captured by the equation $S \cdot  {T}^{2} = O\left( {N}^{2}\right)$ ,where $N$ is the total size of all sets $\left\lbrack  {8,{15}}\right\rbrack$ . Similar tradeoffs have also been established for other data structure problems. In the $k$ -Reachability problem [8,15] we are given as input a directed graph $G = \left( {V,E}\right)$ ,an arbitrary pair of vertices $u,v$ ,and the goal is to decide whether there exists a path of length $k$ between $u$ and $v$ . The data structure obtained was conjectured to be optimal by [15], and the conjectured optimality was used to develop conditional lower bounds for other problems, such as approximate distance oracles $\left\lbrack  {2,3}\right\rbrack$ where no progress has been made in improving the upper bounds in the last decade. In the edge triangle detection problem [15], we are given as input a graph $G = \left( {V,E}\right)$ ,and the goal is to develop a data structure that can answer whether a given edge $e \in  E$ participates in a triangle or not. Each of these problems has been studied in isolation and therefore, the algorithmic insights are not readily generalizable into a comprehensive framework.

以该体系中最简单的问题之一为例。考虑2-集合互斥问题：给定元素全集$U$与$m$个集合${S}_{1},\ldots ,{S}_{m} \subseteq  U$，需构建数据结构使得对于任意整数对$1 \leq  i,j \leq  m$，可高效判定${S}_{i} \cap  {S}_{j}$是否为空集。已知2-集合互斥的时空权衡满足方程$S \cdot  {T}^{2} = O\left( {N}^{2}\right)$，其中$N$表示所有集合$\left\lbrack  {8,{15}}\right\rbrack$的总大小。类似权衡也见于其他数据结构问题。在$k$-可达性问题[8,15]中，给定有向图$G = \left( {V,E}\right)$和任意顶点对$u,v$，需判定$u$与$v$间是否存在长度为$k$的路径。文献[15]推测所得数据结构具有最优性，并据此为近似距离预言$\left\lbrack  {2,3}\right\rbrack$等问题建立条件性下界——这些问题的上限近十年来尚未取得突破。在边三角形检测问题[15]中，给定图$G = \left( {V,E}\right)$，需构建可判定边$e \in  E$是否参与三角形构成的数据结构。这些问题此前均被孤立研究，导致算法洞见难以整合为统一框架。

In this paper, we cast many of the above problems into answering Conjunctive Queries with Access Patterns (CQAPs) over a relational database. For example,by using the relation $R\left( {x,y}\right)$ to encode that element $x$ belongs to set $y,2$ -Set Disjointness can be captured by the following CQAP: $\varphi \left( {\mid {y}_{1},{y}_{2}}\right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,{y}_{1}}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,{y}_{2}}\right)$ . The expression $\varphi \left( {\mid {y}_{1},{y}_{2}}\right)$ can be interpreted as follows: given values for ${y}_{1},{y}_{2}$ , compute whether the query returns true or not. Different access patterns capture different ways of accessing the result of the CQ and result in different tradeoffs.

本文将上述问题转化为关系数据库上带访问模式的合取查询(CQAPs)。例如通过关系$R\left( {x,y}\right)$编码元素$x$属于集合$y,2$的事实，2-集合互斥问题可表示为CQAP：$\varphi \left( {\mid {y}_{1},{y}_{2}}\right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,{y}_{1}}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,{y}_{2}}\right)$。表达式$\varphi \left( {\mid {y}_{1},{y}_{2}}\right)$可解释为：给定${y}_{1},{y}_{2}$的取值，计算查询返回真值与否。不同访问模式对应CQ结果的不同访问方式，进而产生差异化权衡。

Tradeoffs for enumerating Conjunctive Query results under static and dynamic settings have been a subject of previous research $\left\lbrack  {{13},{17},{19} - {21},{30}}\right\rbrack$ . However,previous work either focuses on the tradeoff between preprocessing time and answering time [19- 21],or the tradeoff between space and delay in enumeration $\left\lbrack  {{13},{30}}\right\rbrack$ . In this paper, we focus explicitly on the tradeoff between space and answering time, without optimizing for preprocessing time. Most closely related to our setting is the problem of answering Boolean CQs [12]. In that work, the authors slightly improve upon the data structure proposed in [13] and adapt it for Boolean CQ answering. Further, [12] identified that the conjectured tradeoff for the $k$ -reachability problem is suboptimal by showing slightly improved tradeoffs for all $k \geq  3$ . The techniques used in this paper are quite different and a vast generalization of the techniques used in [12]. The proposed improvements in [12] for $k$ -reachability are already captured in this work and in many cases, surpass the ones from [12].

在静态和动态环境下枚举联合查询结果的权衡问题，已成为先前研究的主题$\left\lbrack  {{13},{17},{19} - {21},{30}}\right\rbrack$。然而，既往研究要么聚焦于预处理时间与应答时间的权衡[19-21]，要么关注枚举过程中空间与延迟的权衡$\left\lbrack  {{13},{30}}\right\rbrack$。本文明确研究空间与应答时间的权衡关系，且不优化预处理时间。与本研究最密切相关的是布尔联合查询应答问题[12]。该研究对文献[13]提出的数据结构稍作改进，使其适用于布尔联合查询应答。此外，文献[12]通过展示所有$k \geq  3$案例的轻微改进权衡，证明关于$k$可达性问题的推测权衡并非最优。本文采用的技术与文献[12]截然不同，是对其技术的重大泛化。文献[12]对$k$可达性问题的改进已被本研究涵盖，且在多数情况下超越了其成果。

Our Contribution. Our key contribution is a general algorithmic framework for obtaining space-time tradeoffs for any CQAP. Our framework builds upon the PANDA algorithm [24] and tree decomposition techniques [16, 28]. Given any CQAP, it calculates a tradeoff that can find the best possible time for a given space budget. To achieve this goal, we need two key technical contributions.

研究贡献。我们的核心贡献是提出适用于任何CQAP问题的通用时空权衡算法框架。该框架基于PANDA算法[24]和树分解技术[16,28]，能针对给定空间预算计算出最优时间权衡方案。实现该目标需要两项关键技术突破。

First, we introduce the novel notion of partially-materialized tree decompositions (PMTDs) that allow us to capture different possible materialization strategies on a given tree decomposition (Section 3). At a high level, a PMTD augments a tree decomposition with information on which bags should be materialized and which should be computed online. To use a PMTD, we propose a variant of the Yannakakis algorithm (Subsection 3.1) such that during the online phase we incur only the cost of visiting the non-materialized bags.

首先，我们提出部分物化树分解(PMTD)的新概念，用以捕捉给定树分解上的不同物化策略（第3节）。简言之，PMTD通过标注哪些节点袋应被物化、哪些需在线计算来增强树分解。为应用PMTD，我们改进Yannakakis算法（第3.1小节），确保在线阶段仅需处理非物化节点袋的计算开销。

The second key ingredient is an extension of the PANDA algorithm [24] that computes a disjunctive rule in two phases. The computation of a disjunctive rule allows placing an answer to any of the targets in the head of the rule. A key technical component in the PANDA algorithm is the notion of a Shannon-flow inequality. For any Shannon-flow inequality, one can construct a proof sequence that has a direct correspondence with relational operators. Consequently, a proof sequence can be transformed into a join algorithm. The disjunctive rules we consider are computed in two phases: in the first phase (preprocessing), we can place an answer only to targets that will be materialized during the preprocessing phase. In the second phase (online), we place an answer to the remaining targets. We call these rules 2-phase disjunctive rules (Subsection 4.1). To achieve this 2-phase computation, we introduce a type of Shannon-flow inequalities, called joint Shannon-flow inequalities (Section 5), such that each inequality gives rise to a space-time tradeoff. The joint Shannon-flow inequality generates two parallel proof sequences, one proof sequence for the preprocessing phase and another proof sequence for the answering phase. This transformation allows us to use the PANDA algorithm as a blackbox on each of the proof sequences independently and is instrumental in achieving space-time tradeoffs.

第二个关键要素是对PANDA算法[24]的扩展，该算法分两阶段计算析取规则。析取规则的计算允许将答案置于规则头部的任意目标处。PANDA算法的核心技术组件是香农流不等式概念。每个香农流不等式都可生成与关系运算符直接对应的证明序列，进而转化为连接算法。我们研究的析取规则分两阶段计算：预处理阶段仅物化部分目标答案，在线阶段处理剩余目标答案。这类规则称为两阶段析取规则（第4.1小节）。为实现该计算模式，我们引入联合香农流不等式（第5节），每个不等式对应一个时空权衡方案。该不等式生成两条并行证明序列，分别用于预处理阶段和应答阶段。这种转换使我们能独立将PANDA算法作为黑盒应用于各证明序列，是实现时空权衡的关键。

We demonstrate the versatility of our framework by recovering state-of-the-art space-time tradeoffs for Boolean CQAPs, 2-Set Disjointness as well as its generalization $k$ -Set Disjointness,and $k$ -Reachability (Section 6). We also apply our framework to the previously unstudied setting of space-time tradeoffs (in the static setting) for access patterns over a subset of hierarchical queries, a fragment of acyclic CQs that is of great interest $\left\lbrack  {5,6,{10},{11},{18},{20}}\right\rbrack$ . Interestingly, we can recover strategies that are very similar to how specialized enumeration algorithms with provable guarantees work for this class of CQs [11, 20]. More importantly, we improve state-of-the-art tradeoffs. Our most interesting finding is that we can obtain complex tradeoffs for $k$ -Reachability that exhibit different behavior for different regimes of $S$ . For the 3-Reachability problem,we show how to improve the tradeoff for a significant part of the spectrum. For the 4-Reachability problem, we are able to show (via a rather involved analysis) that the space-time tradeoff can be improved everywhere when compared to the conjectured optimal! These results falsify the proposed optimal tradeoff of $S \cdot  {T}^{2/\left( {k - 1}\right) } = \widetilde{O}\left( {\left| E\right| }^{2}\right)$ for $k$ -Reachability for regimes that are even larger than what was shown in [12].

我们通过恢复布尔CQAPs（Conjunctive Query Answering Problems）的先进时空权衡、2-集合不相交性及其广义化$k$-集合不相交性、以及$k$-可达性（第6节），展示了本框架的多功能性。我们还将该框架应用于层次查询子集的访问模式（静态环境下）这一尚未被研究的时空权衡场景，这类无环CQs片段具有重要研究价值$\left\lbrack  {5,6,{10},{11},{18},{20}}\right\rbrack$。值得注意的是，我们能恢复与针对此类CQs的专用枚举算法[11,20]工作原理高度相似的策略。更重要的是，我们改进了现有最优权衡方案。最有趣的发现是，针对$k$-可达性可以获得复杂权衡曲线，其在$S$的不同区间呈现差异化特性。对于3-可达性问题，我们展示了如何显著改善权衡曲线的大部分区间。对于4-可达性问题，我们通过严密分析证明：相较于猜想的最优解，时空权衡在所有区间均可改进！这些结果否定了文献[12]提出的$k$-可达性最优权衡猜想$S \cdot  {T}^{2/\left( {k - 1}\right) } = \widetilde{O}\left( {\left| E\right| }^{2}\right)$，且适用范围比该文献所示更广。

Conjunctive Query. We associate a Conjunctive Query (CQ) $\varphi$ with a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$ ,where $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   = \{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$ and $\mathcal{E} \subseteq  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ . The body of the query has atoms ${R}_{F}$ ,where $F \in  \mathcal{E}$ . To each node $i \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,we associate a variable ${x}_{i}$ . The CQ is then

合取查询。我们将合取查询(CQ)$\varphi$关联至超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$，其中$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   = \{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$且$\mathcal{E} \subseteq  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$。查询体包含原子式${R}_{F}$，此处$F \in  \mathcal{E}$。每个节点$i \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$对应变量${x}_{i}$，该CQ表达式为

$$
\varphi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) 
$$

where ${\mathbf{x}}_{I}$ denotes the tuple ${\left( {x}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  I}$ for any $I \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ . The variables in ${\mathbf{x}}_{H}$ are called the head variables of the CQ. The CQ is full if $H = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ and Boolean if $H = \varnothing$ . We use $\varphi$ to denote the output of the CQ $\varphi$ .

其中${\mathbf{x}}_{I}$表示任意$I \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$的元组${\left( {x}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  I}$。${\mathbf{x}}_{H}$中的变量称为CQ的头变量。当$H = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$时称该CQ为完全的，$H = \varnothing$时称为布尔型。用$\varphi$表示CQ$\varphi$的输出结果。

Degree Constraints. A degree constraint is a triple $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$ where $X \subset  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ and ${N}_{Y \mid  X}$ is a natural number. A relation ${R}_{F}$ is said to guard the degree constraint $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$ if $X \subset  Y \subseteq  F$ and for every tuple ${\mathbf{t}}_{X}$ (over $X$ ), $\mathop{\max }\limits_{{\mathbf{t}}_{X}}{\deg }_{F}\left( {Y \mid  {\mathbf{t}}_{X}}\right)  \leq  {N}_{Y \mid  X}$ ,where ${\deg }_{F}\left( {Y \mid  {\mathbf{t}}_{X}}\right)  = \left| {{\Pi }_{Y}\left( {{\sigma }_{X = {\mathbf{t}}_{X}}{R}_{F}}\right) }\right|$ . We use DC to denote a set of degree constraints and say that DC is guarded by a database instance $\mathcal{D}$ if every $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}$ is guarded by some relation in $\mathcal{D}$ . A degree constraint $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$ is a cardinality constraint if $X = \varnothing$ . Throughout this work, we make the following assumptions on DC guarded by a database instance $\mathcal{D}$ :

度约束条件。度约束是一个三元组$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$，其中$X \subset  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$且${N}_{Y \mid  X}$为自然数。当$X \subset  Y \subseteq  F$且对于每个元组${\mathbf{t}}_{X}$（基于$X$）满足$\mathop{\max }\limits_{{\mathbf{t}}_{X}}{\deg }_{F}\left( {Y \mid  {\mathbf{t}}_{X}}\right)  \leq  {N}_{Y \mid  X}$（其中${\deg }_{F}\left( {Y \mid  {\mathbf{t}}_{X}}\right)  = \left| {{\Pi }_{Y}\left( {{\sigma }_{X = {\mathbf{t}}_{X}}{R}_{F}}\right) }\right|$）时，称关系${R}_{F}$守护该度约束$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$。用DC表示度约束集合，若数据库实例$\mathcal{D}$中的某个关系守护每个$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}$，则称DC被$\mathcal{D}$守护。当$X = \varnothing$时，度约束$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$即为基数约束。本研究中，我们对被数据库实例$\mathcal{D}$守护的DC作如下假设：

- (best constraints assumption) w.l.o.g,for any $X \subset  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ , there is at most one $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}$ . This assumption can be maintained by only keeping the minimum ${N}_{Y \mid  X}$ if there is more than one.

- （最优约束假设）在不失一般性的前提下，对于任意$X \subset  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，至多存在一个$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}$。若存在多个约束，仅保留最小${N}_{Y \mid  X}$即可维持此假设。

- for every relation ${R}_{F} \in  \mathcal{D}$ ,there is a cardinality constraint $\left( {\varnothing ,F,\left| {R}_{F}\right| \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{N}_{F \mid  \varnothing }}\right)  \in$ DC. The size of the database $\mathcal{D}$ is denoted as $\left| \mathcal{D}\right| \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{{{R}_{F} \in  \mathcal{D}}}\left| {R}_{F}\right|$ .

- 对于每个关系${R}_{F} \in  \mathcal{D}$，DC中必存在一个基数约束$\left( {\varnothing ,F,\left| {R}_{F}\right| \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{N}_{F \mid  \varnothing }}\right)  \in$。数据库$\mathcal{D}$的规模记作$\left| \mathcal{D}\right| \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{{{R}_{F} \in  \mathcal{D}}}\left| {R}_{F}\right|$。

In this work, we use degree constraints to measure data complexity. All logs are in base 2, unless otherwise stated.

本研究采用度约束衡量数据复杂度。除特别说明外，所有对数均以2为底。

### 2.1 CQs with Access Patterns

### 2.1 带访问模式的联合查询

We define CQs with access patterns following the definition from [21]:

我们沿用文献[21]的定义给出带访问模式的联合查询：

Definition 2.1 (CQ with access patterns). A Conjunctive Query with Access Patterns (CQAP) is an expression of the form

定义2.1（带访问模式的联合查询）。带访问模式的联合查询（CQAP）是形如下的表达式

$$
\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) ,
$$

where $A \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ is called the access pattern of the query.

其中$A \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$称为该查询的访问模式。

The access pattern tells us how a user accesses the result of the CQ. In particular, the user will provide an instance of a relation ${Q}_{A}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{A}\right)$ ,which we call an access request. The task is then to return the result of the following CQ,denoted as $\varphi$ ,where

访问模式表征用户获取联合查询结果的方式。具体而言，用户将提供关系${Q}_{A}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{A}\right)$的实例（称为访问请求），系统需返回以下联合查询的结果（记作$\varphi$），其中

$$
\varphi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H}\right)  \leftarrow  {Q}_{A}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{A}\right)  \land  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) .
$$

We call $\varphi$ the access ${CQ}$ . The most natural access request is one where $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$ ; in other words,the user provides only one fixed value for every variable ${x}_{i},i \in  A$ . This can be thought of as using the CQ result as an index with search key ${\mathbf{x}}_{A}$ . By allowing the access request ${Q}_{A}$ to consist of more tuples,we can capture other scenarios. For example, one can take a stream of access requests of size 1 and batch them together to obtain a (possibly faster) answer for all of them at once. Prior work $\left\lbrack  {{13},{21}}\right\rbrack$ has only considered the case where $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$ .

我们将$\varphi$称为访问${CQ}$。最自然的访问请求是当$\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$时的情况；换言之，用户为每个变量${x}_{i},i \in  A$仅提供一个固定值。这可以视为将CQ结果作为具有搜索键${\mathbf{x}}_{A}$的索引使用。通过允许访问请求${Q}_{A}$包含更多元组，我们可以涵盖其他场景。例如，可以将多个大小为1的访问请求流批量处理，一次性获得（可能更快的）统一应答。先前研究$\left\lbrack  {{13},{21}}\right\rbrack$仅考虑了$\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$的情况。

### 2.2 Problem Statement

### 2.2 问题陈述

Let $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$ be a CQAP under degree constraints DC guarded by the input relations. In addition,we denote by $\mathrm{{AC}}$ another set of degree constraints known in prior, guarded by any access request ${Q}_{A}$ . Similar to DC,we that assume there is a cardinality constraint $\left( {\varnothing ,A,\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = {N}_{A \mid  \varnothing }}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{AC}}$ guarded by ${Q}_{A}$ . For example, the case where $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$ can be interpreted as a cardinality constraint $\left( {\varnothing ,A,1}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{AC}}$ . Given a database instance $\mathcal{D}$ guarding $\mathrm{{DC}}$ , our goal is to construct a data structure, such that we can answer any access request as fast as possible. More formally, we split query processing into two phases:

设$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$为输入关系守护的、受度数约束DC限制的CQAP问题。此外，我们用$\mathrm{{AC}}$表示另一组先验已知的度数约束，由任意访问请求${Q}_{A}$守护。类似于DC，我们假设存在由${Q}_{A}$守护的基数约束$\left( {\varnothing ,A,\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = {N}_{A \mid  \varnothing }}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{AC}}$。例如，当$\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$时可解释为基数约束$\left( {\varnothing ,A,1}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{AC}}$。给定守护$\mathrm{{DC}}$的数据库实例$\mathcal{D}$，我们的目标是构建一个数据结构，以便尽可能快速应答任何访问请求。更正式地，我们将查询处理分为两个阶段：

Preprocessing phase: it constructs a data structure in space $\widetilde{O}{\left( S\right) }^{1}$ . The overall space cost takes the form $\widetilde{O}\left( {S + \left| \mathcal{D}\right| }\right)$ ,where $S$ is called the intrinsic space cost of the data structure and $\left| \mathcal{D}\right|$ is the (unavoidable) space cost for storing the database.

预处理阶段：构建空间占用为$\widetilde{O}{\left( S\right) }^{1}$的数据结构。总空间成本表现为$\widetilde{O}\left( {S + \left| \mathcal{D}\right| }\right)$形式，其中$S$称为数据结构的固有空间成本，$\left| \mathcal{D}\right|$是存储数据库的（不可避免的）空间成本。

Online phase: given an access request ${Q}_{A}$ (guarding AC),it returns the results of the access $\mathrm{{CQ}}\varphi$ using the data structure built in the preprocessing phase. The (worst-case) answering time is then $\widetilde{O}\left( {T + \left| {Q}_{A}\right| }\right)  + O\left( \left| \varphi \right| \right)$ ,where $T$ is called the intrinsic time cost and $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  + \left| \varphi \right|$ is the (unavoidable) time cost of reading the access request ${Q}_{A}$ and enumerating the output. For the Boolean case and when $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$ ,the answering time simply becomes $\widetilde{O}\left( T\right)$ .

在线阶段：给定守护AC的访问请求${Q}_{A}$，利用预处理阶段构建的数据结构返回访问$\mathrm{{CQ}}\varphi$的结果。（最坏情况下）应答时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {T + \left| {Q}_{A}\right| }\right)  + O\left( \left| \varphi \right| \right)$，其中$T$称为固有时间成本，$\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  + \left| \varphi \right|$是读取访问请求${Q}_{A}$及枚举输出结果的（不可避免的）时间成本。对于布尔情形且当$\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$时，应答时间简化为$\widetilde{O}\left( T\right)$。

In this work, we study the tradeoffs between the two intrinsic quantities, $S$ and $T$ ,which we will call an intrinsic tradeoff. At one extreme,the algorithm stores nothing,thus $S = O\left( 1\right)$ ,and we answer each access request from scratch. At the other extreme, the algorithm stores the results of the CQ ${\varphi }_{M}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H \cup  A}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right)$ as a hash table with index key ${\mathbf{x}}_{A}$ . For any access request ${Q}_{A}$ ,we simply evaluate the query $\varphi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H}\right)  \leftarrow  {Q}_{A} \land  {\varphi }_{M}$ in the online phase by probing each tuple of ${Q}_{A}$ in the hash table. If $H \supseteq  A$ ,then any access request can be answered in (instance-optimal) time $O\left( {\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  + \left| \varphi \right| }\right)$ , in which case $T = O\left( 1\right)$ .

本研究中，我们探讨了两种内在量度$S$与$T$之间的权衡关系，称之为内在权衡。在极端情况下，算法不存储任何数据（即$S = O\left( 1\right)$），每个访问请求都需从头计算。另一极端是，算法将CQ${\varphi }_{M}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H \cup  A}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right)$结果以${\mathbf{x}}_{A}$为键值存储为哈希表。对于任意访问请求${Q}_{A}$，我们只需在线阶段通过哈希表探测${Q}_{A}$的每个元组来评估查询$\varphi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H}\right)  \leftarrow  {Q}_{A} \land  {\varphi }_{M}$。若$H \supseteq  A$，则所有访问请求都可在（实例最优）时间$O\left( {\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  + \left| \varphi \right| }\right)$内应答，此时$T = O\left( 1\right)$。

Example 2.2 (k-Set Disjointness). In this problem, we are given sets ${S}_{1},\ldots ,{S}_{m}$ with elements drawn from the same universe $U$ . Each access request asks whether the intersection between $k$ sets is empty or not. By encoding the family of sets as a binary relation $R\left( {y,x}\right)$ such that element $y$ belongs to set $x$ ,we can express the problem as the following CQAP:

示例2.2（k集合互斥性）。该问题给定从同一全域$U$抽取元素的集合族${S}_{1},\ldots ,{S}_{m}$，每次访问请求询问$k$个集合的交集是否为空。通过将集合族编码为二元关系$R\left( {y,x}\right)$（其中元素$y$属于集合$x$），该问题可表述为以下CQAP：

$$
\varphi \left( {\mid {\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}R\left( {y,{x}_{i}}\right) . \tag{1}
$$

If we also want to enumerate the elements in their intersection, we would instead use the non-Boolean version:

若需枚举交集中元素，则改用非布尔版本：

$$
\varphi \left( {y \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}R\left( {y,{x}_{i}}\right) . \tag{2}
$$

Example 2.3 (k-Reachability). Given a direct graph $G$ ,the $k$ - reachability problem asks,given a pair vertices(u,v),to check whether they are connected by a path of length $k$ . Representing the graph as a binary relation $R\left( {x,y}\right)$ ,we can model this problem through the following CQAP (the $k$ -path query):

示例2.3（k可达性）。给定有向图$G$，$k$可达性问题要求判断顶点对(u,v)是否存在长度为$k$的连通路径。将图表示为二元关系$R\left( {x,y}\right)$后，可通过以下CQAP（$k$路径查询）建模：

$$
{\phi }_{k}\left( {\mid {x}_{1},{x}_{k + 1}}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}R\left( {{x}_{i},{x}_{i + 1}}\right) .
$$

We can also check whether there is a path of length at most $k$ by combining the results of $k$ such queries (one for each $1,\ldots ,k$ ).

通过组合$k$个此类查询（每个对应$1,\ldots ,k$）的结果，还可检测是否存在长度不超过$k$的路径。

In this work,we focus on the CQAP such that $H \supseteq  A$ . If we are given a CQAP where $H \nsupseteq  A$ ,we replace the head of the CQAP with $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H \cup  A} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$ ,and simply project on the desired results in the end.

本文聚焦满足$H \supseteq  A$的CQAP。若给定CQAP中$H \nsupseteq  A$，则将其头部替换为$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H \cup  A} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$，最终仅投影所需结果。

## 3 PARTIALLY MATERIALIZED TREE DECOMPOSITIONS

## 3 部分物化树分解

In this section, we introduce a type of tree decomposition that augments a decomposition with information about what bags we want to materialize.

本节介绍一种通过物化标记增强的树分解方法。

Definition 3.1 (Tree Decomposition). A tree decomposition of a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$ is a pair $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ where $\left( i\right) \mathcal{T}$ is an undirected tree,and (ii) $\chi  : V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ is a mapping that assigns to every node $t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ a subset of $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,called the bag of $t$ ,such that

定义3.1（树分解）。超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$的树分解是二元组$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$，其中$\left( i\right) \mathcal{T}$为无向树，(ii)$\chi  : V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$为映射函数，为每个节点$t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$分配$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$的子集（称为$t$的包），满足：

(1) For every hyperedge $F \in  \mathcal{E}$ ,the set $F$ is contained in some bag; and

(1) 对每条超边$F \in  \mathcal{E}$，集合$F$包含于某个包中；且

(2) For each vertex $x \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,the set of nodes $\{ t \mid  x \in  \chi \left( t\right) \}$ forms a (non-empty) connected subtree of $\mathcal{T}$ .

(2) 对于每个顶点$x \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，节点集合$\{ t \mid  x \in  \chi \left( t\right) \}$构成$\mathcal{T}$的一个（非空）连通子树。

Take a tree decomposition $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ and a node $r \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ . We define ${\operatorname{TOP}}_{r}\left( x\right)$ as the highest node in $\mathcal{T}$ containing $x$ in its bag if we root the tree at $r$ . We now say that $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ is free-connex w.r.t. $r$ if for any $x \in  H$ and $y \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \smallsetminus  H,{\operatorname{TOP}}_{r}\left( y\right)$ is not an ancestor of ${\operatorname{TOP}}_{r}\left( x\right) \left\lbrack  {34}\right\rbrack$ . We say that $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ is free-connex if it is free-connex w.r.t. some $r \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ .

取树分解$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$和节点$r \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$。若将树根设为$r$，我们定义${\operatorname{TOP}}_{r}\left( x\right)$为$\mathcal{T}$中包含$x$的最高节点。若对任意$x \in  H$，$y \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \smallsetminus  H,{\operatorname{TOP}}_{r}\left( y\right)$不是${\operatorname{TOP}}_{r}\left( x\right) \left\lbrack  {34}\right\rbrack$的祖先，则称$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$关于$r$是自由连接的。若存在某个$r \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$使$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$自由连接，则称$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$是自由连接的。

We can now introduce our key concept of a partially materialized tree decomposition,tailored for CQAPs. Let $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$ be a CQAP such that $A \subseteq  H$ . Let $\mathcal{H}$ be the hypergraph associated with $\varphi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H}\right)$ , the access CQ.

现在引入为CQAP量身定制的部分物化树分解关键概念。设$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$是满足$A \subseteq  H$的CQAP，$\mathcal{H}$表示关联于访问查询$\varphi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H}\right)$的超图。

Definition 3.2 (PMTD). A Partially Materialized Tree Decomposition (PMTD) of the CQAP $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$ with $H \supseteq  A$ is a tuple $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,M,r}\right)$ such that the following properties hold:

定义3.2（PMTD）。具有$H \supseteq  A$的CQAP$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$的部分物化树分解（PMTD）是一个元组$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,M,r}\right)$，需满足以下性质：

(1) $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ is a free-connex tree decomposition of $\mathcal{H}$ w.r.t. node $r$ ,called the root ; and

(1) $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$是关于根节点$r$的$\mathcal{H}$的自由连接树分解；且

(2) $A \subseteq  \chi \left( r\right)$ ; and

(2) $A \subseteq  \chi \left( r\right)$；且

(3) $M \subseteq  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ such that whenever $t \in  M$ then all the nodes of its subtree (w.r.t. orienting the tree away from $r$ ) are in $M$ .

(3) $M \subseteq  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$满足：当$t \in  M$时，其子树所有节点（相对于以$r$为根的树方向）均属于$M$。

${}^{1}$ The notation $\widetilde{O}$ hides a polylogarithmic factor in $\left| \mathcal{D}\right|$ .

${}^{1}$ 记号$\widetilde{O}$隐藏了$\left| \mathcal{D}\right|$中的多对数因子。

Given a PMTD $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,M,r}\right)$ ,we call $M$ the materialization set. We also associate with each node $t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ a view with variables ${\mathbf{x}}_{v\left( t\right) }$ ,where the mapping $v : V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ is defined as follows. If the node $t \notin  M$ ,then $v\left( t\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\chi \left( t\right)$ and the view is of the form ${T}_{v\left( t\right) }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{v\left( t\right) }\right)$ ,called a $T$ -view. Otherwise, $t \in  M$ . If $t = r \in  M$ ,define $v\left( r\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\chi \left( t\right)  \cap  H$ . Let $p$ be the parent node of a non-root node $t \in  M$ and define

给定一个PMTD $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,M,r}\right)$，我们称$M$为物化集。同时为每个节点$t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$关联一个包含变量${\mathbf{x}}_{v\left( t\right) }$的视图，其中映射$v : V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$定义如下：若节点$t \notin  M$，则$v\left( t\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\chi \left( t\right)$且该视图形式为${T}_{v\left( t\right) }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{v\left( t\right) }\right)$，称为$T$-视图；否则$t \in  M$。当$t = r \in  M$时，定义$v\left( r\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\chi \left( t\right)  \cap  H$。设$p$为非根节点$t \in  M$的父节点，则定义

$v\left( t\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left\{  \begin{array}{ll} \chi \left( t\right)  \cap  \left( {H \cup  \chi \left( p\right) }\right) & \text{ if }p \notin  M \\  \chi \left( t\right)  \cap  H & \text{ if }p \in  M\text{ and }\chi \left( t\right)  \cap  H \nsubseteq  \chi \left( p\right)  \cap  H \\  \varnothing & \text{ if }p \in  M\text{ and }\chi \left( t\right)  \cap  H \subseteq  \chi \left( p\right)  \cap  H. \end{array}\right.$

The view (for each $t \in  M$ ) then is of the form ${S}_{v\left( t\right) }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{v\left( t\right) }\right)$ ,called the $S$ -view. This definition of $S$ -views corresponds to running a bottom-up semijoin-reduce pass of the Yannakakis algorithm in the materialization set $M$ of the free-connex tree decomposition $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ . Indeed,any variables in $\chi \left( t\right)  \smallsetminus  v\left( t\right)$ are safely projected out after the semijoin-reduce.

该视图（对每个$t \in  M$）的形式为${S}_{v\left( t\right) }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{v\left( t\right) }\right)$，称为$S$-视图。此$S$-视图定义对应于在自由连接树分解$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$的物化集$M$中运行Yannakakis算法的自底向上半连接归约过程。事实上，$\chi \left( t\right)  \smallsetminus  v\left( t\right)$中的任何变量在半连接归约后均可安全投影消除。

On a high level, $M$ specifies the type of views associated with each bag ( $S$ -view or $T$ -view),and $v\left( \cdot \right)$ pinpoints the schema of that view (possibly empty). A PMTD appoints its $S$ -views to be materialized in the preprocessing phase and its $T$ -views to be computed in the online phase. In the case where $M = \varnothing$ ,every view in the decomposition is obtained in the online phase. When $H = A$ or $H = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,the free-connex property does not put any additional restrictions on the tree decompositions for a PMTD.

高层次而言，$M$指定了每个包关联视图的类型（$S$-视图或$T$-视图），而$v\left( \cdot \right)$精确确定了该视图的模式（可能为空）。PMTD指定其$S$-视图在预处理阶段物化，$T$-视图在线计算阶段生成。当$M = \varnothing$时，分解中的所有视图均在线阶段获取。若$H = A$或$H = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，自由连接性质不会对PMTD的树分解施加额外限制。

示例3.3 我们以3可达性问题的CQAP为例：

Redundancy & Domination. We say that a tree decomposition is non-redundant if no bag is a subset of another bag. We say that a tree decomposition $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{1},{\chi }_{1}}\right)$ is dominated by another tree decomposition $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{2},{\chi }_{2}}\right)$ if every bag of $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{1},{\chi }_{1}}\right)$ is a subset of some bag of $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{2},{\chi }_{2}}\right)$ . Here, we will generalize both notions to PMTDs.

冗余与支配。若树分解中不存在一个包是另一个包的子集，则称该分解为非冗余的。若树分解$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{1},{\chi }_{1}}\right)$的每个包都是另一个树分解$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{2},{\chi }_{2}}\right)$中某个包的子集，则称$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{1},{\chi }_{1}}\right)$被$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{2},{\chi }_{2}}\right)$支配。下文将这两个概念推广至PMTD。

Definition 3.4 (PMTD Redundancy). A PMTD $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,M,r}\right)$ is non-redundant if (1) for $t \in  M,v\left( t\right)  \neq  \varnothing$ and no $v\left( t\right)$ is a subset of another; and (2) for $t \notin  M$ ,no $v\left( t\right)$ is a subset of another.

定义3.4（PMTD冗余性）。若满足以下条件，则称PMTD$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,M,r}\right)$是非冗余的：(1)对于$t \in  M,v\left( t\right)  \neq  \varnothing$，不存在$v\left( t\right)$是另一个的子集；(2)对于$t \notin  M$，不存在$v\left( t\right)$是另一个的子集。

Definition 3.5 (PMTD Domination). A PMTD $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{1},{\chi }_{1},{M}_{1},{r}_{1}}\right)$ is dominated by another PMTD $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{2},{\chi }_{2},{M}_{2},{r}_{2}}\right)$ if (1) for every node ${t}_{1} \in  {M}_{1}$ ,there is some node ${t}_{2} \in  {M}_{2}$ such that $v\left( {t}_{1}\right)  \subseteq  v\left( {t}_{2}\right)$ ,and (2) for every node ${t}_{1} \in  V\left( {\mathcal{T}}_{1}\right)  \smallsetminus  {M}_{1}$ ,there is some node ${t}_{2} \in  V\left( {\mathcal{T}}_{2}\right)  \smallsetminus  {M}_{2}$ such that $v\left( {t}_{1}\right)  \subseteq  v\left( {t}_{2}\right)$ .

定义3.5（PMTD支配性）。若满足以下条件，则称PMTD$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{1},{\chi }_{1},{M}_{1},{r}_{1}}\right)$被另一个PMTD$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{2},{\chi }_{2},{M}_{2},{r}_{2}}\right)$支配：(1)对于每个节点${t}_{1} \in  {M}_{1}$，存在某个节点${t}_{2} \in  {M}_{2}$使得$v\left( {t}_{1}\right)  \subseteq  v\left( {t}_{2}\right)$；(2)对于每个节点${t}_{1} \in  V\left( {\mathcal{T}}_{1}\right)  \smallsetminus  {M}_{1}$，存在某个节点${t}_{2} \in  V\left( {\mathcal{T}}_{2}\right)  \smallsetminus  {M}_{2}$使得$v\left( {t}_{1}\right)  \subseteq  v\left( {t}_{2}\right)$。

For PMTDs, both redundancy and domination are defined using the materialization set and views instead of the bags. For PMTDs with $M = \varnothing$ ,both PMTD redundancy and domination become equivalent to the standard definition.

对于PMTD而言，冗余性和支配性都是通过物化集和视图而非包来定义的。对于具有$M = \varnothing$的PMTD，其冗余性和支配性定义将与标准定义等价。

### 3.1 Online Yannakakis for PMTDs

### 3.1 PMTD的在线Yannakakis算法

We introduce an adaptation of the Yannakakis algorithm [36] for a non-redundant PMTD (so no empty views), called Online Yan-nakakis. Recall that for a non-redundant PMTD,the $S$ -views,one for each $t \in  M$ ,are stored in the preprocessing phase,while the $T$ -views,one for each $t \in  \mathcal{T} \smallsetminus  M$ ,and the access request ${Q}_{A}$ are accessible only in the online phase.

我们提出适用于非冗余PMTD（无空视图）的Yannakakis算法[36]变体，称为在线Yannakakis。需注意对于非冗余PMTD，预处理阶段存储每个$t \in  M$对应的$S$视图，而每个$t \in  \mathcal{T} \smallsetminus  M$对应的$T$视图及访问请求${Q}_{A}$仅在在线阶段可用。

THEOREM 3.7. Consider a PMTD $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,M,r}\right)$ and its view $v\left( \cdot \right)$ . Given S-views, we can preprocess them in space linear in their size such that we can compute the free-connex acyclic ${CQ}$

定理3.7。给定PMTD$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,M,r}\right)$及其视图$v\left( \cdot \right)$，若已知S-视图，我们可以用与其规模线性相关的存储空间进行预处理，从而计算自由连接无环的${CQ}$

$$
\psi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{H}\right)  \leftarrow  {Q}_{A} \land  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{t \in  M}}{S}_{v\left( t\right) } \land  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \smallsetminus  M}}{T}_{v\left( t\right) } \tag{3}
$$

for any $T$ -view and ${Q}_{A}$ in time $O\left( {\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \smallsetminus  M}}\left| {T}_{v\left( t\right) }\right|  + \left| {Q}_{A}\right|  + \left| \psi \right| }\right)$ , where $\left| \psi \right|$ is the output size of (3).

对于任意$T$视图及时间$O\left( {\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \smallsetminus  M}}\left| {T}_{v\left( t\right) }\right|  + \left| {Q}_{A}\right|  + \left| \psi \right| }\right)$中的${Q}_{A}$，其中$\left| \psi \right|$为(3)式的输出规模。

Note that the time cost has no dependence on the size of $S$ -views, because throughout Online Yannakakis, $S$ -views will be only used for hash probing in semijoin operations. We defer the details of the algorithm and the proof of its correctness to the full version of the paper [37].

需注意时间成本与$S$视图的规模无关，因为在Online Yannakakis算法过程中，$S$视图仅用于半连接操作中的哈希探测。我们将算法细节及其正确性证明留待论文完整版[37]阐述。

## 4 GENERAL FRAMEWORK

## 4 通用框架

Consider a CQAP $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right)$ with $H \supseteq  A$ . Recall that our goal is to find the best space-time tradeoffs under degree constraints DC (guarded by input relations) and AC (guarded by any access requests ${Q}_{A}$ ),as specified in Subsection 2.2. Our main algorithm is parameterized by:

考虑一个带有$H \supseteq  A$的CQAP$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right)$。根据第2.2小节规定，我们的目标是在输入关系约束DC和访问请求约束AC（由任意${Q}_{A}$请求守卫）下，寻找最优的时空权衡方案。主算法由以下参数确定：

- $\mathcal{P} = {\left\{  {P}_{i}\right\}  }_{i \in  I}$ ,a (finite) indexed set of non-redundant and non-dominant PMTDs such that ${P}_{i} = \left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{i},{\chi }_{i},{M}_{i},{r}_{i}}\right)$ for every $i \in  I$ . Including all such PMTDs in $\mathcal{P}$ (which are finite) will result in the best possible tradeoff. However, as we will see later, it is meaningful to consider smaller sets of PMTDs that result in more interpretable space-time tradeoffs.

- $\mathcal{P} = {\left\{  {P}_{i}\right\}  }_{i \in  I}$：一组（有限）非冗余且非支配的PMTD索引集，满足对每个$i \in  I$都有${P}_{i} = \left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{i},{\chi }_{i},{M}_{i},{r}_{i}}\right)$。包含$\mathcal{P}$中所有此类PMTD（数量有限）将实现最优权衡。但后文将表明，考虑更小的PMTD集以获得更可解释的时空权衡也具实际意义。

## $S$，空间预算

### 4.1 2-Phase Disjunctive Rules

### 4.1 两阶段析取规则

In this section, we define a specific type of disjunctive rule that will be necessary to acquire the $S$ -views and $T$ -views for PMTDs. We start by recalling the notion of a disjunctive rule. A disjunctive rule has the exact body of a CQ, while the head is a disjunction of output relations ${T}_{B}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{B}\right)$ ,which we call targets. Let $\mathrm{{BT}} \subseteq  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ be a non-empty set,then a disjunctive rule $\rho$ takes the form:

本节定义一类特殊析取规则，用于获取PMTD所需的$S$视图和$T$视图。首先回顾析取规则概念：其主体与CQ完全一致，而头部是输出关系${T}_{B}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{B}\right)$的析取（称为目标）。给定非空集$\mathrm{{BT}} \subseteq  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$，析取规则$\rho$形如：

$$
\rho  : \mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}}{T}_{B}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{B}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) . \tag{4}
$$

Given a database instance $\mathcal{D}$ ,a model of $\rho$ is a tuple ${\left( {T}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}$ of relations, one for each target, such that the logical implication indicated by (4) holds. More precisely, for any tuple a that satisfies the body,there is a target ${T}_{B} \in  {\left( {T}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{B}}$ such that ${\Pi }_{B}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  {T}_{B}$ . The size of a model is defined as the maximum size of its output relations and the output size of a disjunctive rule $\rho$ ,denoted as $\left| \rho \right|$ , is defined as the minimum size over all models.

给定数据库实例$\mathcal{D}$，$\rho$的模型是关系元组${\left( {T}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}$（每个目标对应一个关系），需满足(4)式所示的逻辑蕴含。具体而言，对任何满足主体的元组a，存在目标${T}_{B} \in  {\left( {T}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{B}}$使得${\Pi }_{B}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  {T}_{B}$。模型规模定义为其输出关系的最大规模，而析取规则$\rho$的输出规模记作$\left| \rho \right|$，定义为所有模型的最小规模。

For our purposes, we define a type of disjunctive rules, called 2-phase disjunctive rules.

基于需求，我们定义了一类称为两阶段析取规则的特定规则。

Definition 4.1 (2-phase Disjunctive Rules). A 2-phase disjunctive rule $\rho$ defined by a CQAP $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$ is a single disjunctive rule that takes the body of the access $\mathrm{{CQ}}\varphi$ ,while the head has two sets of output relations. In other words, $\rho$ takes the form

定义4.1（两阶段析取规则）。由CQAP$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{H} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$定义的两阶段析取规则$\rho$是一个单一析取规则，其主体采用访问$\mathrm{{CQ}}\varphi$的体部，而头部包含两组输出关系。换言之，$\rho$的形式为

$$
\rho  : \mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{B \in  \mathrm{{BS}}}}{S}_{B}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{B}\right)  \vee  \mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}}{T}_{B}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{B}\right)  \leftarrow  {Q}_{A}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{A}\right)  \land  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) , \tag{5}
$$

where BS, $\mathrm{{BT}} \subseteq  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ and at most one can be empty. A model of $\rho$ thus consists of two sets of output relations,i.e. the $S$ -targets ${\left( {S}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BS}}}$ and the $T$ -targets ${\left( {T}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}$ .

其中BS、$\mathrm{{BT}} \subseteq  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$至多可有一项为空。$\rho$的模型因此由两组输出关系构成，即$S$-目标${\left( {S}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BS}}}$与$T$-目标${\left( {T}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}$。

As the name suggests,a model of a 2-phase disjunctive rule $\rho$ is computed in two phases, the preprocessing and online phase:

顾名思义，两阶段析取规则$\rho$的模型分两个阶段计算：预处理阶段和在线阶段：

Preprocessing phase: we obtain the $S$ -targets ${\left( {S}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BS}}}$ using a preprocessing disjunctive rule

预处理阶段：通过预处理析取规则获取$S$-目标${\left( {S}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BS}}}$

$$
{\rho }_{S} : \;\mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{B \in  \mathrm{{BS}}}}{S}_{B}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{B}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) , \tag{6}
$$

The space cost for storing the $S$ -targets is $\widetilde{O}\left( {S}_{\rho }\right)$ ,and the overall space cost is $\widetilde{O}\left( {{S}_{\rho } + \left| \mathcal{D}\right| }\right)$ . The preprocessing phase has no knowledge of ${Q}_{A}$ except for the degree constraints AC,so as to explicitly force the $S$ -targets to be universal for any instance of access request.

存储$S$-目标的空间开销为$\widetilde{O}\left( {S}_{\rho }\right)$，总空间开销为$\widetilde{O}\left( {{S}_{\rho } + \left| \mathcal{D}\right| }\right)$。预处理阶段除度数约束AC外不感知${Q}_{A}$，从而显式确保$S$-目标对任何访问请求实例具有普适性。

Online phase: given an access request ${Q}_{A}$ (under AC),we obtain the $T$ -targets ${\left( {T}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}$ using an online disjunctive rule

在线阶段：给定访问请求${Q}_{A}$（在AC约束下），通过在线析取规则获取$T$-目标${\left( {T}_{B}\right) }_{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}$

$$
{\rho }_{T} : \;\mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{B \in  \mathrm{{BT}}}}{T}_{B}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{B}\right)  \leftarrow  {Q}_{A}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{A}\right)  \land  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right)  \tag{7}
$$

in time and space $\widetilde{O}\left( {T}_{\rho }\right)$ . The overall time is $\widetilde{O}\left( {{T}_{\rho } + \left| {Q}_{A}\right| }\right)$ .

时间与空间复杂度为$\widetilde{O}\left( {T}_{\rho }\right)$，总耗时为$\widetilde{O}\left( {{T}_{\rho } + \left| {Q}_{A}\right| }\right)$。

If $\mathrm{{BS}} = \varnothing$ ,then the model is computed from scratch in the online phase (and vice versa). As in Subsection 2.2, our focus is on analyzing the space-time tradeoffs between the two intrinsic quantities, ${S}_{\rho }$ and ${T}_{\rho }$ .

若$\mathrm{{BS}} = \varnothing$，则模型在在线阶段从头开始计算（反之亦然）。如第2.2小节所述，我们重点分析两个内在量${S}_{\rho }$与${T}_{\rho }$之间的时空权衡关系。

For the next part, assume that we have a 2-phase algorithm (called 2PP) that,given a space budget $S$ ,has a preprocessing procedure 2PP-Preprocess using space ${S}_{\rho } \leq  S$ and an online procedure 2PP-Online using time (and space) ${T}_{\rho }$ . We will discuss this algorithm in the next section.

对于下一部分，假设我们有一个两阶段算法（称为2PP），在给定空间预算$S$时，其预处理过程2PP-Preprocess使用${S}_{\rho } \leq  S$空间，在线过程2PP-Online使用${T}_{\rho }$时间（及空间）。我们将在下一节讨论该算法。

### 4.2 Preprocessing Phase

### 4.2 预处理阶段

As a first step,we construct from $\mathcal{P}$ a set of 2-phase disjunctive rules as follows. Let ${v}_{i}$ be the mapping for associated views of ${P}_{i}$ . Let us define the cartesian product $\mathbf{A} = { \times  }_{i \in  I}\left\{  {V\left( {\mathcal{T}}_{i}\right) }\right\}$ and let $M = \left| \mathbf{A}\right|$ . Informally,every element $\mathbf{a} \in  \mathbf{A}$ picks one view from every PMTD in the indexed set. For every $\mathbf{a} \in  \mathbf{A}$ ,we construct the following 2-phase disjunctive rule (recall that ${M}_{i}$ is the materialization set of PMTD $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{i},{\chi }_{i},{M}_{i},{r}_{i}}\right)$ ):

首先，我们从$\mathcal{P}$构建如下一组两阶段析取规则。令${v}_{i}$表示${P}_{i}$关联视图的映射，定义笛卡尔积$\mathbf{A} = { \times  }_{i \in  I}\left\{  {V\left( {\mathcal{T}}_{i}\right) }\right\}$并设$M = \left| \mathbf{A}\right|$。非正式地说，每个元素$\mathbf{a} \in  \mathbf{A}$从索引集的每个PMTD中选取一个视图。对于每个$\mathbf{a} \in  \mathbf{A}$，我们构建如下两阶段析取规则（注意${M}_{i}$是PMTD$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}_{i},{\chi }_{i},{M}_{i},{r}_{i}}\right)$的具体化集合）：

$$
\mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{{\mathbf{a}}_{i} \in  {M}_{i}}}{S}_{{v}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{i}\right) }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{{v}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{i}\right) }\right)  \vee  \mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{{\mathbf{a}}_{i} \notin  {M}_{i}}}{T}_{{v}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{i}\right) }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{{v}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{i}\right) }\right)  \leftarrow  {Q}_{A}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{A}\right)  \land  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) 
$$

The body of the rule is the same independent of $\mathrm{a} \in  \mathrm{A}$ . The head of the rule introduces an $S$ -target whenever the corresponding bag is in the materialization set of the PMTD (using the corresponding view); otherwise,it introduces a $T$ -target. There are exactly ${M2}$ - phase disjunctive rules constructed from the given set of PMTDs, which is a query-complexity quantity.

## 5 CONSTRUCTING THE TRADEOFFS

## 5 构建权衡模型

Let $\rho$ be a 2-phase disjunctive rule taking the form (5),under degree constraints DC (guarded by input relations) and degree constraints AC (guarded by ${Q}_{A}$ ). In this section,we will discuss how we can obtain a model of $\rho$ in two phases using PANDA,and the resulting space-time tradeoff. Due to limited space, we will keep the presentation informal and introduce the key ideas through an example. The full details and proofs are deferred to [37]. We will use the following rule as our running example,where $\left| {R}_{1}\right|  = \left| {R}_{2}\right|  = \left| \mathcal{D}\right|$ :

设$\rho$为遵循形式(5)的两阶段析取规则，受输入关系约束的度约束DC和${Q}_{A}$约束的度约束AC限制。本节将讨论如何利用PANDA分两阶段构建$\rho$模型，及其时空权衡。限于篇幅，我们将采用非形式化阐述，通过示例说明核心思想。完整细节与证明见[37]。以下规则将作为运行示例（其中$\left| {R}_{1}\right|  = \left| {R}_{2}\right|  = \left| \mathcal{D}\right|$）：

$$
{T}_{123} \vee  {S}_{13} \leftarrow  {Q}_{13}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{3}}\right) ,{R}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right) \text{.}
$$

This rule is the only rule we obtain from considering two PMTDs for the 2-reachability query. To compute a disjunctive rule, PANDA starts with a Shannon-flow inequality, which is an inequality over set functions $h : {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$ that must hold for any set function that is a polymatroid ${}^{2}$ . For our purposes,we need a joint Shannon-flow inequality,which holds over two set functions ${h}_{S},{h}_{T}$ that must be polymatroids. Intuitively, ${h}_{S}$ governs the preprocessing phase,while ${h}_{T}$ governs the online phase. The joint Shannon-flow inequality for our example is:

该规则是我们为2-可达性查询考虑两个PMTD时获得的唯一规则。计算析取规则时，PANDA从香农流不等式开始——该不等式对任意多拟阵集函数${}^{2}$的集合函数$h : {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$成立。我们需要联合香农流不等式，使其对两个必须为多拟阵的集合函数${h}_{S},{h}_{T}$成立。直观上，${h}_{S}$控制预处理阶段，而${h}_{T}$控制在线阶段。本例的联合香农流不等式为：

$$
\underset{{R}_{1}}{\underbrace{{h}_{S}\left( 1\right)  + {h}_{T}\left( {2 \mid  1}\right) }} + \underset{{R}_{2}}{\underbrace{{h}_{S}\left( 3\right)  + {h}_{T}\left( {2 \mid  3}\right) }} + 2\underset{{Q}_{13}}{\underbrace{{h}_{T}\left( {13}\right) }} \geq  \underset{{S}_{13}}{\underbrace{{h}_{S}\left( {13}\right) }} + 2\underset{{T}_{123}}{\underbrace{{h}_{T}\left( {123}\right) }}
$$

where $h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  = h\left( Y\right)  - h\left( X\right)$ . The right-hand side includes terms of ${h}_{S}$ that correspond to $S$ -targets and terms of ${h}_{T}$ that correspond to $T$ -targets. The left-hand side includes a term of ${h}_{T}$ that corresponds to the access request ${Q}_{A}$ ,and possibly terms of ${h}_{S}\left( {h}_{T}\right)$ that encode the degree constraints $\mathrm{{DC}}\left( {\mathrm{{DC}} \cup  \mathrm{{AC}}}\right)$ . More importantly,it contains terms that correlate the two polymatroids by splitting an input relation with attributes $Y$ into two parts,either (i) ${h}_{S}\left( X\right)  + {h}_{T}\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)$ , or (ii) ${h}_{T}\left( X\right)  + {h}_{S}\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)$ ,where $X \subseteq  Y$ . Intuitively,the first split materializes the heavy $X$ -values and sends everything else to the online phase,while the second split preprocesses the light $X$ -values and sends the heavy $X$ -values to the online phase. In our example, relation ${R}_{1}$ is split into ${h}_{S}\left( 1\right)  + {h}_{T}\left( {2 \mid  1}\right)$ ,and each part is sent to a different polymatroid. Using the coefficients of the above joint Shannon-flow inequality, we get the following intrinsic space-time tradeoff:

其中$h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  = h\left( Y\right)  - h\left( X\right)$。右侧包含对应于$S$目标的${h}_{S}$项，以及对应于$T$目标的${h}_{T}$项。左侧包含对应于访问请求${Q}_{A}$的${h}_{T}$项，可能还包含编码度约束$\mathrm{{DC}}\left( {\mathrm{{DC}} \cup  \mathrm{{AC}}}\right)$的${h}_{S}\left( {h}_{T}\right)$项。更重要的是，它包含通过将具有属性$Y$的输入关系拆分为两部分来关联两个多拟阵的项，即：(i)${h}_{S}\left( X\right)  + {h}_{T}\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)$，或(ii)${h}_{T}\left( X\right)  + {h}_{S}\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)$，其中$X \subseteq  Y$。直观上，第一种拆分物化重要的$X$值并将其他所有内容发送至在线阶段，而第二种拆分预处理轻量$X$值并将重要$X$值发送至在线阶段。在我们的示例中，关系${R}_{1}$被拆分为${h}_{S}\left( 1\right)  + {h}_{T}\left( {2 \mid  1}\right)$，每部分被发送至不同的多拟阵。利用上述联合香农流不等式的系数，我们得到以下内在时空权衡：

$$
S \cdot  {T}^{2} \cong  {\left| {Q}_{13}\right| }^{2} \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}
$$

We will use the $\cong$ notation to mean that $S \cdot  {T}^{2} = \widetilde{O}\left( {{\left| {Q}_{13}\right| }^{2} \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}}\right)$ . Generally, we show (for a formal definition, see the full version of the paper [37]):

我们将使用$\cong$表示法来表示$S \cdot  {T}^{2} = \widetilde{O}\left( {{\left| {Q}_{13}\right| }^{2} \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}}\right)$。通常，我们展示（正式定义参见论文[37]的完整版）：

THEOREM 5.1 (INFORMAL). Every joint Shannon-flow inequality for a 2-phase disjunctive rule implies a space-time tradeoff computed by reading the coefficients of the inequality.

The above theorem requires that we are given a joint Shannon-flow inequality to obtain a space-time tradeoff. We additionally show that,given a space budget $S$ ,we can also compute via a linear program the optimal inequality that will result in the best possible answering time.

The 2PP algorithm. We now present how our main algorithm works (see [37] for a detailed description). For the running example, we take $\left| {Q}_{13}\right|  = 1$ ,and $S$ is a fixed space budget.

2PP算法。现在我们介绍主算法的工作原理（详见[37]）。在运行示例中，我们取$\left| {Q}_{13}\right|  = 1$，且$S$为固定空间预算。

As a first step, 2PP scans the joint Shannon-flow inequality and partitions ${R}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right)$ (on ${x}_{1}$ ) into ${R}_{1}^{H}$ and ${R}_{1}^{L}$ ,where ${R}_{1}^{H}$ contains all $\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right)$ tuples where $\left| {{\sigma }_{{x}_{1} = t}\left( {R}_{12}\right) }\right|  \geq  \left| \mathcal{D}\right| /\sqrt{S}$ ,and ${R}_{1}^{L}$ contains the tuples that satisfy ${\deg }_{12}\left( {{x}_{2} \mid  {x}_{1}}\right)  \leq  \left| \mathcal{D}\right| /\sqrt{S}.{R}_{2}$ is partitioned symmetrically (on ${x}_{3}$ ) into ${R}_{2}^{H}$ and ${R}_{2}^{L}$ . In general,these splits will be done according to the correlated terms in the joint flow.

第一步，2PP算法扫描联合香农流不等式(Shannon-flow inequality)，将${R}_{1}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right)$（基于${x}_{1}$）划分为${R}_{1}^{H}$和${R}_{1}^{L}$，其中${R}_{1}^{H}$包含所有满足$\left| {{\sigma }_{{x}_{1} = t}\left( {R}_{12}\right) }\right|  \geq  \left| \mathcal{D}\right| /\sqrt{S}$的$\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right)$元组，而${R}_{1}^{L}$包含符合${\deg }_{12}\left( {{x}_{2} \mid  {x}_{1}}\right)  \leq  \left| \mathcal{D}\right| /\sqrt{S}.{R}_{2}$的元组。对称地（基于${x}_{3}$）将${R}_{2}^{H}$划分为${R}_{2}^{L}$，从而生成四个子问题$\left\{  {{R}_{1}^{H},{R}_{2}^{H}}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{R}_{1}^{H},{R}_{2}^{L}}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{R}_{1}^{L},{R}_{2}^{H}}\right\}$和$\left\{  {{R}_{1}^{L},{R}_{2}^{L}}\right\}$。通常，这些划分会依据联合流中的相关项进行。

 = {h}_{S}\left( {13}\right) \;\text{(composition)}

## 6 APPLICATIONS

## 6 应用场景

In this section, we apply our framework to obtain state-of-the-art space-time tradeoffs for several specific problems, as well as obtain new tradeoff results. We defer the discussion for hierarchical CQAPs to the full version of the paper [37].

本节将运用我们的框架，为若干具体问题获取最先进的时空权衡方案，同时得到新的权衡结果。关于分层CQAP的讨论将延至论文完整版[37]。

### 6.1 Tradeoffs for $k$ -Set Intersection

### 6.1 $k$集合交集的权衡分析

We will first study the CQAP (2) that corresponds to the non-Boolean $k$ -Set Disjointness problem (set $y = {x}_{k + 1}$ )

首先研究对应于非布尔型$k$集合不相交问题（集合$y = {x}_{k + 1}$）的CQAP(2)

$$
\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k + 1\right\rbrack  } \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}R\left( {{x}_{k + 1},{x}_{i}}\right) 
$$

From the decomposition with a single node $t$ with $\chi \left( t\right)  = \left\lbrack  {k + 1}\right\rbrack$ , we construct two PMTDs,one with ${M}_{1} = \varnothing$ ,another with ${M}_{2} = \{ t\}$ . Thus, ${v}_{1}\left( t\right)  = {v}_{2}\left( t\right)  = \left\lbrack  {k + 1}\right\rbrack$ . This gives rise to the following (only) two-phase disjunctive rule:

基于单节点$t$（含$\chi \left( t\right)  = \left\lbrack  {k + 1}\right\rbrack$）的分解结构，我们构建了两个PMTD：一个含${M}_{1} = \varnothing$，另一个含${M}_{2} = \{ t\}$。因此${v}_{1}\left( t\right)  = {v}_{2}\left( t\right)  = \left\lbrack  {k + 1}\right\rbrack$，由此产生以下（仅有的）两阶段析取规则：

$$
{T}_{\left\lbrack  k + 1\right\rbrack  } \vee  {S}_{\left\lbrack  k + 1\right\rbrack  } \leftarrow  {Q}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}R\left( {{x}_{k + 1},{x}_{i}}\right) 
$$

For this rule, we have the following joint Shannon-flow inequality:

针对该规则，我们得到如下联合香农流不等式：

$$
{h}_{S}\left( {k,k + 1}\right)  + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack  }}\left\{  {{h}_{S}\left( {i \mid  k + 1}\right)  + {h}_{T}\left( {k + 1}\right) }\right\}   + \left( {k - 1}\right)  \cdot  {h}_{T}\left( \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  \right) 
$$

$$
 \geq  {h}_{S}\left( \left\lbrack  {k + 1}\right\rbrack  \right)  + \left( {k - 1}\right)  \cdot  {h}_{T}\left( \left\lbrack  {k + 1}\right\rbrack  \right) .
$$

By Theorem 5.1,we get the tradeoff $S \cdot  {T}^{k - 1} \cong  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{k} \cdot  {\left| {Q}_{A}\right| }^{k - 1}$ .

根据定理5.1，可得出权衡关系$S \cdot  {T}^{k - 1} \cong  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{k} \cdot  {\left| {Q}_{A}\right| }^{k - 1}$。

### 6.2 Tradeoffs via Fractional Edge Covers

### 6.2 基于分数边覆盖的权衡方法

Let $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{A} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$ be a CQAP with hypergraph $\left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$ of $\varphi$ . A fractional edge cover of $S \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ is an assignment $\mathbf{u} = {\left( {u}_{F}\right) }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}$ such that (i) ${u}_{F} \geq  0$ ,and (ii) for every $i \in  S,\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F : i \in  F}}{u}_{F} \geq  1$ . For any fractional edge cover $\mathbf{u}$ of $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,we define the slack of $\mathbf{u}$ w.r.t. $A \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ :

设$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{A} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$为具有$\varphi$超图$\left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$的CQAP。$S \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$的分数边覆盖是指满足以下条件的赋值$\mathbf{u} = {\left( {u}_{F}\right) }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}$：(i)${u}_{F} \geq  0$，(ii)对于每个$i \in  S,\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F : i \in  F}}{u}_{F} \geq  1$。对于$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$的任意分数边覆盖$\mathbf{u}$，我们定义$\mathbf{u}$相对于$A \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$的松弛量：

$$
\alpha \left( {\mathbf{u},A}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\min }\limits_{{i \notin  A}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E} : i \in  F}}{u}_{F}.
$$

In other words, the slack is the maximum factor by which we can scale down the fractional cover $\mathbf{u}$ so that it remains a valid edge cover of the variables not in $A$ . Hence ${\left( {u}_{F}/\alpha \left( \mathbf{u},A\right) \right) }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}$ is a fractional edge cover of $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \smallsetminus  A$ . We always have $\alpha \left( {\mathbf{u},A}\right)  \geq  1$ .

换言之，松弛量是指在不影响非$A$变量有效边覆盖的前提下，分数覆盖$\mathbf{u}$可缩减的最大比例因子。因此${\left( {u}_{F}/\alpha \left( \mathbf{u},A\right) \right) }_{F \in  \mathcal{E}}$构成$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \smallsetminus  A$的分数边覆盖，且始终满足$\alpha \left( {\mathbf{u},A}\right)  \geq  1$。

THEOREM 6.1. Let $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{A} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$ be a CQAP. Let $\mathbf{u}$ be any fractional edge cover of the hypergraph of $\varphi$ . Then,for any input database $\mathcal{D}$ , and any access request, the following intrinsic tradeoff holds:

定理6.1 设$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{A} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$为CQAP（可组合查询评估问题）。设$\mathbf{u}$为$\varphi$超图的任意分数边覆盖。则对于任意输入数据库$\mathcal{D}$及任意访问请求，存在以下内在权衡关系：

$$
S \cdot  {T}^{\alpha \left( {\mathbf{u},A}\right) } \cong  {\left| {Q}_{A}\right| }^{\alpha \left( {\mathbf{u},A}\right) } \cdot  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{\left| {R}_{F}\right| }^{{u}_{F}}
$$

The above theorem can also be shown as a corollary of Theorem 1 in [13]. However, the data structure used in [13] is much more involved, since its goal is to also bound the delay during enumeration (while we are interested in total time instead). A simpler construction with the same tradeoff was shown in [12]. Our framework recovers the same result using a simple materialization strategy with two PMTDs.

该定理亦可作为文献[13]中定理1的推论。然而[13]使用的数据结构更为复杂，因其目标还需限定枚举过程中的延迟（而我们关注总耗时）。文献[12]展示了具有相同权衡的更简单构造。本框架通过采用含两个PMTD（部分物化树分解）的简单物化策略，复现了相同结论。

Example 6.2. Consider $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  } \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}R\left( {y,{x}_{i}}\right)$ (corresponds to the $k$ -Set Disjointness problem) with the fractional edge cover $\mathbf{u}$ ,where ${u}_{j} = 1$ for $j \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$ . The slack w.r.t. $\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ is $k$ , since the fractional edge cover $\widehat{\mathbf{u}}$ ,where ${\widehat{u}}_{i} = {u}_{i}/k = 1/k$ covers $x$ . Applying Theorem 6.1,we obtain a tradeoff of $S \cdot  {T}^{k} \cong  {\left| {Q}_{A}\right| }^{k} \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{k}$ . When $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$ ,this matches the best-known space-time tradeoff for the $k$ -Set Disjointness problem.

示例6.2 考虑$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  } \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}R\left( {y,{x}_{i}}\right)$（对应$k$-集合不相交问题）及其分数边覆盖$\mathbf{u}$，其中${u}_{j} = 1$对$j \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$成立。相对于$\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$的松弛量为$k$，因分数边覆盖$\widehat{\mathbf{u}}$（其中${\widehat{u}}_{i} = {u}_{i}/k = 1/k$覆盖$x$）。应用定理6.1可得$S \cdot  {T}^{k} \cong  {\left| {Q}_{A}\right| }^{k} \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{k}$的权衡关系。当$\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$时，这与$k$-集合不相交问题最著名的时空权衡一致。

### 6.3 Tradeoffs via Tree Decompositions

### 6.3 基于树分解的权衡关系

Let $\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{A} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$ be a CQAP. In the previous section,we recovered a space-time tradeoff using two trivial PMTDs. Here, we will show how our framework recovers a better space-time tradeoff by considering a larger set of PMTDs that corresponds to one decomposition.

设$\varphi \left( {{\mathbf{x}}_{A} \mid  {\mathbf{x}}_{A}}\right)$为CQAP。前节通过两个平凡PMTD复现了时空权衡。本节将展示如何通过对应单个分解的更大PMTD集合，在本框架中推导出更优的时空权衡。

Pick any arbitrary non-redundant free-connex decomposition $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,r}\right)$ . We start by taking any set of nodes that are not ancestors of each other in the decomposition as a materialization set. Then, for each node $t$ in the materialization set,we merge all bags in the subtree of $t$ into the bag of $t$ (and truncate the subtree). By ranging over all such materialization sets, we construct a fixed (finite) set of PMTDs. We say that this set of PMTDs is induced from $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,r}\right)$ . We now input the induced set of PMTDs to our general framework. To discuss the obtained space-time tradeoff, take any assignment of a fractional edge cover ${\mathbf{u}}_{t}$ to each node $t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ and let ${u}_{t}^{ * }$ be its total weight. Let ${A}_{t}$ denote the common variables between node $t$ and its parent (for the root, ${A}_{r} = A$ ),and define ${\alpha }_{t} = \alpha \left( {{\mathbf{u}}_{t},{A}_{t}}\right)$ to be the slack in node $t$ w.r.t. ${A}_{t}$ . Now,take the nodes $P$ of any root-to-leaf path in $\mathcal{T}$ . We can show that any such path $P$ generates the following intrinsic tradeoff:

任选非冗余自由连接分解$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,r}\right)$。首先选取分解中互非祖先关系的节点集作为物化集。然后对物化集中每个节点$t$，将其子树所有袋合并至$t$的袋（并截断子树）。遍历所有此类物化集，可构造固定的（有限）PMTD集合，称该集合由$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,r}\right)$导出。现将导出的PMTD集合输入通用框架。为讨论所得时空权衡，任取各节点$t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$的分数边覆盖分配${\mathbf{u}}_{t}$，设${u}_{t}^{ * }$为其总权重。令${A}_{t}$表示节点$t$与其父节点的共有变量（根节点时${A}_{r} = A$），定义${\alpha }_{t} = \alpha \left( {{\mathbf{u}}_{t},{A}_{t}}\right)$为节点$t$相对于${A}_{t}$的松弛量。现取$\mathcal{T}$中任意根到叶路径的节点$P$，可证任何此类路径$P$均产生以下内在权衡：

$$
{S}^{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t \in  P}}1/{\alpha }_{t}} \cdot  T \cong  \left| {Q}_{A}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t \in  P}}{u}_{t}^{ * }/{\alpha }_{t}}
$$

To obtain the final space-time tradeoff, we take the worst such tradeoff across all root-to-leaf paths. We show in [37] how to obtain this tradeoff, and also show why it recovers prior results [12]. Our framework guarantees that adding PMTDs to the induced set we considered here can only make this tradeoff better.

为获得最终的时空权衡结果，我们选取所有根节点到叶节点路径中最不理想的权衡值。在文献[37]中，我们阐述了如何推导该权衡曲线，并论证了其能涵盖前人研究成果[12]的原因。本框架确保，将PMTD（部分多路树分解）加入当前考虑的诱导集合后，只会优化该权衡关系。

The first bag has slack ${\alpha }_{1} = 1$ (w.r.t. ${x}_{1},{x}_{5}$ ),while the second has slack ${\alpha }_{2} = 2$ (w.r.t. ${x}_{2},{x}_{4}$ ). Here we have one root-to-leaf path, hence we get the tradeoff ${S}^{1 + 1/2} \cdot  T \cong  \left| {Q}_{A}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2/1 + 2/2}$ ,or equivalently ${S}^{3/2} \cdot  T \cong  \left| {Q}_{A}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{3}$ .

取树袋${t}_{1}$的边覆盖${u}_{1} = 1,{u}_{4} = 1$，及树袋${t}_{2}$的边覆盖${u}_{2} = 1,{u}_{3} = 1$。第一个树袋的松弛量为${\alpha }_{1} = 1$（相对于${x}_{1},{x}_{5}$），第二个树袋的松弛量为${\alpha }_{2} = 2$（相对于${x}_{2},{x}_{4}$）。此处我们有一条根到叶路径，因此得到权衡关系${S}^{1 + 1/2} \cdot  T \cong  \left| {Q}_{A}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2/1 + 2/2}$，等价于${S}^{3/2} \cdot  T \cong  \left| {Q}_{A}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{3}$。

In this part, we will consider the CQAP that corresponds to the $k$ -reachability problem described in Example 2.3:

本部分将研究对应于例2.3所述$k$-可达性问题的CQAP：

$$
{\phi }_{k}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{k + 1} \mid  {x}_{1},{x}_{k + 1}}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}R\left( {{x}_{i},{x}_{i + 1}}\right) .

Prior work [15] has shown the following tradeoff for a input $\mathcal{D}$ , which was conjectured to be asymptotically optimal for $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$ :

已有研究[15]针对输入$\mathcal{D}$提出了如下权衡关系，该关系被推测在$\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$情况下渐近最优：

$$
S \cdot  {T}^{2/\left( {k - 1}\right) } \cong  {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2} \cdot  {\left| {Q}_{A}\right| }^{2/\left( {k - 1}\right) }.

We will show that the above tradeoff can be significantly improved for $k \geq  3$ by applying our framework.

我们将证明，通过应用本框架，上述权衡关系在$k \geq  3$情况下可获得显著改进。

3-reachability. As a first step, we consider the set of all non-redundant and non-dominant PMTDs (five in total), as seen in Figure 3. Note that rules can admit two (or more) tradeoffs that do not dominate each other; hence, we need to pick the best tradeoff depending on the regime we consider (see [37] for how we prove each tradeoff). To understand the combined tradeoff we obtain from our analysis,we plot in Figure 4a each tradeoff curve by taking ${\log }_{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }$ and then taking the $x$ -axis as ${\log }_{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }\left( T\right)$ and the $y$ -axis as ${\log }_{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }\left( S\right)$ (fixing $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$ ). ## 7 RELATED WORK

## 7 相关工作

Set Intersection and Distance Oracles. Space-time tradeoffs for query answering (exact and approximate) has been an active area of research across multiple communities in the last decade $\left\lbrack  {1,8,{25},{26}}\right\rbrack$ . Cohen and Porat [8] introduced the fast intersection problem and presented a data structure to enumerate the intersection of two sets with guarantees on the total answering time. Goldstein et. al [15] formulated the $k$ -reachability problem on graphs,and showed a simple recursive data structure which achieves the $S \cdot  {T}^{2/\left( {k - 1}\right) } =$ $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}\right)$ tradeoff. They also conjectured that the tradeoff is optimal and used it to justify the optimality of an approximate distance oracle proposed by [2]. The study of (approximate) distance oracles over graphs was initiated by Patrascu and Roditty [32], where lower bounds are shown on the size of a distance oracle for sparse graphs based on a conjecture about the best possible data structure for a set intersection problem. Cohen and Porat [9] also connected set intersection to distance oracles. Agarwal et al. $\left\lbrack  {2,3}\right\rbrack$ introduced the notion of stretch of an oracle that controls the error allowed in the answer. Further, for stretch-2 and stretch-3 oracles, we can achieve tradeoffs $S \cdot  T = O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}\right)$ and $S \cdot  {T}^{2} = O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}\right)$ respectively, and for any integer $k > 0$ ,a stretch- $\left( {1 + 1/k}\right)$ oracle exhibits an $S \cdot  {T}^{1/k} = O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}\right)$ tradeoff. Unfortunately,no lower bounds are known for non-constant query time.

集合交集与距离预言机。在过去十年中，查询应答（精确与近似）的时空权衡已成为跨多个研究领域的活跃课题$\left\lbrack  {1,8,{25},{26}}\right\rbrack$。Cohen与Porat[8]提出了快速交集问题，并给出了一种能在保证总应答时间的前提下枚举两集合交集的数据结构。Goldstein等人[15]将图上的$k$-可达性问题形式化，展示了一种实现$S \cdot  {T}^{2/\left( {k - 1}\right) } =$$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}\right)$权衡的简单递归数据结构。他们进一步推测该权衡是最优的，并借此论证了[2]提出的近似距离预言机的最优性。Patrascu与Roditty[32]开创了图上（近似）距离预言机的研究，基于集合交集问题最佳数据结构的猜想，给出了稀疏图距离预言机尺寸的下界。Cohen与Porat[9]也建立了集合交集与距离预言机的联系。Agarwal等人$\left\lbrack  {2,3}\right\rbrack$引入了控制应答误差的预言机拉伸度概念。具体而言，对于拉伸度2和3的预言机，可分别实现$S \cdot  T = O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}\right)$和$S \cdot  {T}^{2} = O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}\right)$的权衡，而对任意整数$k > 0$，拉伸度$\left( {1 + 1/k}\right)$的预言机会呈现$S \cdot  {T}^{1/k} = O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{2}\right)$的权衡。遗憾的是，对于非常数查询时间尚无已知下界。

Space/Delay Tradeoffs. A different line of work considers the problem of enumerating query results of a non-Boolean query, with the goal of minimizing the delay between consecutive tuples of the output. In constant delay enumeration [4, 33], the goal is to achieve constant delay for a CQ after a preprocessing step of linear time (and space); however, only a subset of CQs can achieve such a tradeoff. Factorized databases [31] achieve constant delay enumeration after a more expensive super-linear preprocessing step for any CQ. If we want to reduce preprocessing time further, it is necessary to increase the delay. Kara et. al [20] presented a tradeoff between preprocessing time and delay for enumerating the results of any hierarchical CQ under static (and dynamic) settings. Deng et.al [14] initiates the study of the space-query tradeoffs for range subgraph counting and range subgraph listing problems. The problem of CQs with access patterns was first introduced by Deep and Koutris [13] (under the restriction $\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$ ),but the authors only consider full CQAPs. Previous work [35] considered the problem of constructing space-efficient views of graphs to perform graph analytics, but did not offer any theoretical guarantees. More recently, Kara et. al [21] studied tradeoffs between preprocessing time, delay, and update time for CQAPs. They characterized the class of CQAPs that admit linear preprocessing time, constant delay enumeration, and constant update time. All of the above results concern the tradeoff between space (or preprocessing time) and delay, while our work focuses on the total time to answer the query. Our work is most closely related to the non-peer-reviewed work in [12]. There, the authors also study the problem of building tradeoffs for Boolean CQs. The authors propose two results that slightly improve upon [13]. They were also the first to recognize that the $k$ -reachability tradeoff is not optimal by proposing a small improvement for $k \geq  3$ . The results in our work are a vast generalization that is achieved using a more comprehensive framework. For the dynamic setting, [5] initiated the study of answering CQs under updates. Recently, [19] presented an algorithm for counting the number of triangles under updates. [21] proposed dynamic algorithms for CQAPs and provided a syntactic characterization of queries that admit constant time per single-tuple update and whose output tuples can be enumerated with constant delay.

空间/延迟权衡。另一研究方向关注非布尔查询结果的枚举问题，其目标是最小化输出元组间的延迟间隔。在恒定延迟枚举[4,33]中，目标是通过线性时间（及空间）的预处理步骤，使CQ（合取查询）实现恒定延迟；但仅有部分CQ能达成此类权衡。因子化数据库[31]通过超线性预处理步骤，为任意CQ实现恒定延迟枚举。若需进一步减少预处理时间，则必须增加延迟。Kara等人[20]提出了静态（及动态）场景下层次化CQ结果枚举的预处理时间与延迟的权衡关系。Deng等人[14]开创性地研究了范围子图计数与范围子图列举问题的空间-查询权衡。带访问模式的CQ问题由Deep与Koutris[13]首次提出（受限于$\left| {Q}_{A}\right|  = 1$），但作者仅考虑完整CQAPs（带访问模式的合取查询）。先前研究[35]探讨了构建空间高效的图视图以执行图分析，但未提供理论保证。近期Kara等人[21]研究了CQAPs的预处理时间、延迟与更新时间的权衡，刻画了允许线性预处理时间、恒定延迟枚举及恒定更新时间的CQAPs类别。上述成果均聚焦空间（或预处理时间）与延迟的权衡，而本研究关注查询应答总耗时。我们的工作与文献[12]中的非同行评审研究最为相关，该研究同样探讨布尔CQ的权衡构建，提出了两项对[13]的微小改进成果，并首次指出$k$-可达性权衡非最优性，针对$k \geq  3$提出了小幅改进。本文成果通过更全面的框架实现了广泛泛化。动态场景下，文献[5]开创了更新环境下应答CQ的研究；近期[19]提出了更新环境下三角形计数的算法；[21]则针对CQAPs提出动态算法，并语法化表征了支持单元组恒定时间更新及恒定延迟枚举输出的查询类别。

${CQ}$ Evaluation. Our proposed framework is based on recent advances in efficient CQ evaluation, and in particular the PANDA algorithm [24]. This powerful algorithmic result follows a long line of work on query decompositions $\left\lbrack  {{16},{27},{28}}\right\rbrack$ ,worst-case optimal algorithms [29], and connections between CQ evaluation and information theory $\left\lbrack  {{22},{23}}\right\rbrack$ .

${CQ}$评估。我们提出的框架基于CQ高效求值的最新进展，特别是PANDA算法[24]。这一强大算法成果继承自查询分解$\left\lbrack  {{16},{27},{28}}\right\rbrack$、最坏情况最优算法[29]及CQ求值与信息论关联$\left\lbrack  {{22},{23}}\right\rbrack$等系列研究。

## 8 CONCLUSION

## 8 结论

In this paper, we present a framework for computing general spacetime tradeoffs for answering CQs with access patterns. We show the versatility of our framework by demonstrating how it can capture state-of-the-art tradeoffs for problems that have been studied separately. The application of our framework has also uncovered previously unknown tradeoffs. Many open problems remain, among which are obtaining (conditional) lower bounds that match our upper bounds, and investigating how to make our approach practical.

本文提出了一种计算通用时空权衡的框架，用于应答带访问模式的CQ。通过展示该框架如何涵盖各类独立研究问题的最先进权衡方案，我们证明了其多功能性。该框架的应用还揭示了先前未知的权衡关系。诸多开放问题尚待解决，包括获取匹配我们上界的（条件）下界证明，以及探索本方法的实际应用路径。

Many open problems remain that merit further work. In particular, there are no known lower bounds to prove the optimality of the space-time tradeoffs. The optimality of existing space-time tradeoffs for approximate distance oracles is also now an open problem again and we believe our proposed framework should be able to improve the upper bounds. It would also be very interesting to see the applicability of this framework in practice. In particular, our framework can be extended to also include views that have been precomputed, which is a common setting. In this regard, challenges remain to optimize the constants in the time complexity to ensure implementation feasibility.

许多值得深入研究的开放问题仍然存在。特别是目前缺乏能证明时空权衡最优性的已知下界。近似距离预言现有时空权衡的最优性也重新成为开放问题，我们相信所提框架应能改进上界。该框架的实际适用性验证也极具意义。特别地，我们的框架可扩展至包含预计算视图的常见场景，但优化时间复杂度中的常数因子以确保实施可行性仍面临挑战。
