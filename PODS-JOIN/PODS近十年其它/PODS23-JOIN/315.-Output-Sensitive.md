## Abstract

## 摘要

We present a dynamic index structure for join sampling. Built for an (equi-) join $Q$ - let IN be the total number of tuples in the input relations of $Q$ - the structure uses $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ space,supports a tuple update of any relation in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time,and returns a uniform sample from the join result in $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$ time with high probability (w.h.p.),where OUT and ${\rho }^{ * }$ are the join’s output size and fractional edge covering number,respectively; notation $\widetilde{O}\left( \text{.}\right) h$ ides a factor polylogarithmic to IN. We further show how our result justifies the $O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ running time of existing worst-case optimal join algorithms (for full result reporting) even when OUT $\ll  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$ . Specifically,unless the combinatorial $k$ -clique hypothesis is false,no combinatorial algorithms (i.e., algorithms not relying on fast matrix multiplication) can compute the join result in $O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$ time w.h.p. even if $\mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ ,regardless of how small the constant $\epsilon  > 0$ is.

我们提出了一种用于连接采样的动态索引结构。该结构针对(等值)连接$Q$构建——令IN表示$Q$输入关系中元组总数——其占用$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$空间，支持在$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间内完成任意关系的元组更新，并以高概率(w.h.p.)在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$时间内返回连接结果的均匀样本，其中OUT和${\rho }^{ * }$分别表示连接输出的规模与分数边覆盖数；记号$\widetilde{O}\left( \text{.}\right) h$表示IN的多对数因子。我们进一步证明，即使当OUT$\ll  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$时，该结果仍能验证现有最坏情况最优连接算法(用于完整结果报告)的$O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$时间复杂度。具体而言，除非组合$k$-团猜想不成立，否则即便$\mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$，也不存在能在$O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$时间内以高概率计算连接结果的组合算法(即不依赖快速矩阵乘法的算法)，无论常数$\epsilon  > 0$多小。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Joins, which combine the tuples across multiple tables based on equality conditions ${}^{1}$ ,are a fundamental operation in relational algebra and a main performance bottleneck in database systems. Research on joins has been a core field of database theory. Recent years have witnessed significant advances in this field. Particularly, in the realistic scenario where a join involves a constant number of attributes, the community has discovered join algorithms [6, 36, 42, ${44} - {47},{54}\rbrack$ that can achieve the asymptotically optimal performance (sometimes up to a polylogarithmic factor) in the worst case.

连接操作（join）是基于等值条件${}^{1}$将多表元组组合的关系代数基础运算，也是数据库系统的主要性能瓶颈。连接研究始终是数据库理论的核心领域，近年来该领域取得重大突破。特别是在涉及恒定数量属性的现实场景中，学界已发现能在最坏情况下实现渐进最优性能（有时可达多对数因子级）的连接算法[6,36,42,${44} - {47},{54}\rbrack$]。

Unfortunately, joins remain expensive even in the presence of worst-case optimal algorithms. The culprit is the output size: a join can produce $\Theta \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ tuples [8] where IN denotes the total number of tuples in the input relations,and ${\rho }^{ * }$ represents the join’s fractional edge covering number. We will defer the formal definition of ${\rho }^{ * }$ to Section 2,whereas,for our introductory discussion,it suffices to understand ${\rho }^{ * }$ as a constant at least 1 that is decided by the participating relations’ schemas. For instance, ${\rho }^{ * }$ equals 2 for a join between a relation with attributes $\mathrm{A},\mathrm{\;B}$ and another relation with attributes $B,C$ ,suggesting that the join may output up to $\Theta \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{2}\right)$ tuples. Even just listing the result necessitates $\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{2}\right)$ time in the worst case (regardless of which join algorithm is deployed). The phenomenon has a severe impact on database systems because the value IN is gigantic in today’s big-data era,and the value ${\rho }^{ * }$ can be considerably higher for other joins.

遗憾的是，即便存在最坏情况最优算法，连接操作仍代价高昂。其症结在于输出规模：一个连接可能产生$\Theta \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$个元组[8]，其中IN表示输入关系的总元组数，${\rho }^{ * }$代表连接的分数边覆盖数。我们将${\rho }^{ * }$的形式定义延至第2节，目前仅需理解${\rho }^{ * }$是由参与关系模式决定的、不小于1的常数。例如，当连接具有$\mathrm{A},\mathrm{\;B}$属性的关系与具有$B,C$属性的关系时，${\rho }^{ * }$值为2，意味着连接可能输出多达$\Theta \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{2}\right)$个元组。即使仅列举结果，最坏情况下也需$\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{2}\right)$时间（无论采用何种连接算法）。这种现象对数据库系统影响深远，因为在当今大数据时代IN值极为庞大，且其他连接的${\rho }^{ * }$值可能更高。

Fortunately, many downstream tasks of the join operation do not require a complete result, but can benefit significantly from random samples. A classical example is "approximate aggregation" (which arises in OLAP frequently), whose objective is to estimate the result tuples' total value on a selected attribute (e.g., sales). It is well-known that an accurate estimate can be derived from a small number of tuples drawn uniformly at random from the join result. Another, more modern, example is "fair representative reporting" [50], whose objective is to return a few tuples diverse enough to adequately illustrate the join output's overall distribution. Random samples again serve the purpose very well.

所幸，许多连接操作的下游任务并不需要完整结果，而能从随机采样中显著获益。经典案例是"近似聚合"（OLAP中常见），其目标是对选定属性（如销售额）的结果元组总值进行估算。众所周知，只需从连接结果中均匀随机抽取少量元组即可获得精确估计。另一个更现代的案例是"公平代表性报告"[50]，其目标是返回足够多样化的少量元组以充分展示连接输出的整体分布，随机采样同样能完美满足需求。

Our structure is different from all the previous solutions to join sampling. In particular, we take a perspective that can be viewed as the opposite of how Chen and Yi [21] approached the problem. They construct a random tuple by growing one attribute at a time, in a manner similar to Generic Join [47], which is a worst-case optimal join algorithm. Crucial to their strategy is the following subproblem: assuming that we have fixed the values ${x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{i}$ on attributes ${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\ldots ,{X}_{i}$ for some integer $i$ ,generate a (random) value ${x}_{i + 1}$ for the next attribute ${X}_{i + 1}$ according to a carefully crafted distribution ${}^{2}$ . The subproblem,however,is a major technical barrier, to which Chen and Yi’s solution incurs $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ time,which is the main reason behind the gap between their complexity (1) and the desired complexity in (2).

本结构与现有连接采样方案存在本质差异。我们采用了与Chen和Yi[21]截然相反的研究视角：他们通过逐属性扩展构建随机元组（类似最坏情况最优连接算法Generic Join[47]），其策略核心是解决以下子问题——假设已固定某整数$i$对应属性${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\ldots ,{X}_{i}$上的值${x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{i}$，需根据精心设计的分布${}^{2}$为下一属性${X}_{i + 1}$生成（随机）值${x}_{i + 1}$。该子问题构成重大技术障碍，Chen和Yi的解决方案需要$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$时间，这正是其复杂度(1)与目标复杂度(2)存在差距的主因。

- the maximum number of join result tuples in each subspace - characterized by the so-called "AGM bound" [8] (to be introduced in Section 2) - is at most half of the AGM bound of the original space, and

- 每个子空间内连接结果元组最大数量（以"AGM界"[8]表征，详见第2节）不超过原空间AGM界的一半；且

- the sum of the AGM bounds of the subspaces does not exceed the AGM bound of the original space

unless the AGM bound of the original space is already small enough to allow the join to be evaluated in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time. Recursively performing the partitioning leads to a sampling algorithm faster (and simpler) than the state of the art [21]. As a high-level idea, space partitioning has been leveraged to design join algorithms before $\left\lbrack  {6,{27},{36},{42},{44}}\right\rbrack$ . However,the idea’s deployment in those scenarios was for purposes drastically different from ours. The relevant algorithms, as well as their analysis, in the aforementioned works also differ from ours considerably. Our technical development is new and, we believe, clean enough for teaching at a graduate level.

递归执行该划分可获得比现有技术[21]更快速（且更简洁）的采样算法。虽然空间划分思想在早期连接算法设计$\left\lbrack  {6,{27},{36},{42},{44}}\right\rbrack$中已有应用，但其使用场景与目标与本研究截然不同，相关算法及分析框架也与本工作存在显著差异。我们的技术路线具有创新性，且我们认为其简洁性足以胜任研究生课程教学。

We also study how much further improvement over (2) is still possible. In fact, perhaps the most challenging step is to pose the right question in this regard. At first glance, (2) appears obviously optimal because OUT,as mentioned,can reach $\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ ,in which case (2) becomes $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ ,clearly the best achievable (we are not concerned with polylogarithmic factors in this work). Although correct, this argument tells us nothing in the realistic scenario where OUT $\ll  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$ . We instead ask a more meaningful question:

我们还研究了在(2)式基础上能实现多大程度的进一步改进。事实上，最具挑战性的步骤或许是提出正确的问题。初看之下，(2)式显然是最优的，因为如前所述OUT能达到$\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$，此时(2)式变为$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$，显然这是可达到的最佳结果（本研究不考虑多对数因子）。虽然正确，但该论证在OUT$\ll  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$的实际场景中并无指导意义。我们转而提出一个更有价值的问题：

The join sampling question. Is there a constant $\epsilon$ satisfying $0 < \epsilon  < 1/2$ ,under which we can find a structure that,after an initial $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$ -time preprocessing,extracts a uniformly random tuple from the join result in $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ time w.h.p. when $1 \leq  \mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ ?

连接采样问题。是否存在满足$0 < \epsilon  < 1/2$的常数$\epsilon$，使得我们能在初始$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$时间预处理后，当$1 \leq  \mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$时以高概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间内从连接结果中提取均匀随机元组？

Note that the structure does not need to guarantee anything for OUT $= 0$ or OUT $> {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ and has sampling time polynomially better than (2) for OUT $\in  \left\lbrack  {1,{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }}\right\rbrack$ .

注意该结构无需对OUT$= 0$或OUT$> {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$做出任何保证，且在OUT$\in  \left\lbrack  {1,{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }}\right\rbrack$时其采样时间需多项式优于(2)式。

We study the question within the class of combinatorial structures. These are structures whose preprocessing and sampling algorithms are combinatorial, namely, the algorithms do not rely on fast matrix multiplication (à la Strassen's). We prove that the answer to the question is "no" subject to the hypothesis below.

我们在组合结构类中研究该问题。这类结构的预处理和采样算法都是组合式的，即不依赖快速矩阵乘法（如Strassen算法）。基于下述假设，我们证明该问题的答案为"否"。

Combinatorial $k$ -clique hypothesis. There does not exist any fixed constant $\epsilon  > 0$ under which a combinatorial algorithm can achieve the following for every constant $k \geq  3$ : it can detect with probability at least $1/3$ in $O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$ time whether an undirected graph of $n$ vertices has a $k$ -clique.

组合$k$-团假设。对于任意常数$k \geq  3$，不存在任何固定常数$\epsilon  > 0$使得组合算法能以至少$1/3$的概率在$O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$时间内检测$n$顶点无向图中是否存在$k$-团。

The background behind the above hypothesis deserves some explanation. For $k$ being a (constant) multiple of $3,k$ -clique existence in an $n$ -vertex graph can be detected in $O\left( {n}^{{\omega k}/3}\right)$ time where $\omega$ is the matrix multiplication exponent [43] (see [28] for a more complex bound for arbitrary $k = O\left( 1\right)$ ). However,if only combinatorial algorithms are permitted,even beating the naive $O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$ -time approach is difficult: the fastest combinatorial algorithm [53] takes $O\left( {{n}^{k}/{\log }^{k - 1}n}\right)$ time for constant $k \geq  3$ . It is widely conjectured that no combinatorial algorithms can detect $k$ -cliques in $O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$ time for every constant $k \geq  3$ ; such a hypothesis has been applied to argue for computational hardness on a great variety of problems $\left\lbrack  {1,2,{10} - {13},{16},{17},{34},{35},{40},{41}}\right\rbrack$ . Our hypothesis (which requires success probability $1/3$ ) has been explicitly stated in [10,34].

该假设的背景需要说明。当$k$是$3,k$的（常数）倍数时，$n$顶点图中的团存在性可在$O\left( {n}^{{\omega k}/3}\right)$时间内检测，其中$\omega$是矩阵乘法指数[43]（对任意$k = O\left( 1\right)$的更复杂边界参见[28]）。但若仅允许组合算法，即使超越朴素$O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$时间方法也很困难：最快组合算法[53]对常数$k \geq  3$需要$O\left( {{n}^{k}/{\log }^{k - 1}n}\right)$时间。学界普遍推测不存在组合算法能在$O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$时间内检测$k$-团，该假设已被用于论证众多问题的计算难度$\left\lbrack  {1,2,{10} - {13},{16},{17},{34},{35},{40},{41}}\right\rbrack$。我们的假设（要求成功概率$1/3$）在[10,34]中已被明确阐述。

In fact, if answering the join sampling question was the sole purpose, our argument (in Section 5) could be shortened. However, the argument discloses an inherent connection between join sampling and output-sensitive join computation. For a precise explanation, let us introduce the notion of " $\epsilon$ -output sensitivity":

事实上，若仅回答连接采样问题，我们的论证（第5节）可简化。但该论证揭示了连接采样与输出敏感连接计算之间的内在联系。为准确说明，我们引入"$\epsilon$-输出敏感性"概念：

Let $\epsilon$ be a constant with $0 < \epsilon  < 1/2$ . An algorithm is $\epsilon$ -output sensitive if it can output all the tuples of the join result in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$ time w.h.p. whenever $\mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ .

设$\epsilon$为具有$0 < \epsilon  < 1/2$特性的常数。当算法能在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$时间内高概率输出连接结果的所有元组时（只要$\mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$），我们称该算法是$\epsilon$-输出敏感的。

Note that the above is different from demanding an algorithm to run in $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ time for all OUT values because the notion does not require the algorithm to guarantee anything when $\mathrm{{OUT}} > {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ . The significance of $\epsilon$ -output sensitive algorithms is that they convincingly enhance worst-case join algorithms. Although there has been research $\left\lbrack  {6,{36},{44},{49}}\right\rbrack$ on how to compute joins in time sensitive to OUT,none of the known algorithms is $\epsilon$ -output sensitive no matter how $\epsilon$ is chosen: those algorithms’ running time can still degenerate into $O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ even when $\mathrm{{OUT}} = 0$ .

需注意，这与要求算法对所有OUT值都在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间内运行不同，因为该定义不要求算法在$\mathrm{{OUT}} > {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$时提供任何性能保证。$\epsilon$-输出敏感算法的意义在于显著增强了最坏情况下的连接算法。尽管已有关于如何根据OUT值调整计算时间的研究$\left\lbrack  {6,{36},{44},{49}}\right\rbrack$，但无论怎样选择$\epsilon$，现有算法均非$\epsilon$-输出敏感：即使$\mathrm{{OUT}} = 0$时，这些算法的运行时间仍可能退化为$O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$。

### 2.1 The Problem of Join Sampling

### 2.1 连接采样问题

Denote by att a finite set whose elements are called attributes. Given a set $U \subseteq$ att,we define a tuple over $U$ as a function $\mathbf{u} : U \rightarrow  \mathbb{N}$ , where $\mathbb{N}$ is the set of integers. If $V$ is a subset of $U$ ,we define the projection of $u$ on $V -$ denoted as $\mathbf{u}\left\lbrack  V\right\rbrack   -$ to be the tuple $v$ over $V$ satisfying $\mathbf{u}\left( X\right)  = \mathbf{v}\left( X\right)$ for every attribute $X \in  V$ . A relation is a set $R$ of tuples over an identical set $U$ of attributes. We refer to $U$ as the schema of $R$ and represent this fact with $\operatorname{var}\left( R\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} U$ .

设att为有限集合，其元素称为属性。给定集合$U \subseteq$ att，我们定义$U$上的元组为函数$\mathbf{u} : U \rightarrow  \mathbb{N}$，其中$\mathbb{N}$为整数集。若$V$是$U$的子集，则定义$u$在$V -$上的投影为元组$v$，该元组满足对每个属性$X \in  V$都有$\mathbf{u}\left( X\right)  = \mathbf{v}\left( X\right)$。关系是$U$属性集上相同元组的集合$R$。我们将$U$称为$R$的模式，记作$\operatorname{var}\left( R\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} U$。

We define a join as a set $Q$ of relations with distinct schemas. Let $\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{R \in  \mathcal{Q}}}\operatorname{var}\left( R\right)$ . The result of the join,denoted as $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q), is a relation with schema $\operatorname{var}\left( \mathbf{Q}\right)$ given by

定义连接为具有不同模式的关系集合$Q$。令$\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{R \in  \mathcal{Q}}}\operatorname{var}\left( R\right)$。该连接的结果记为$\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)，其模式为$\operatorname{var}\left( \mathbf{Q}\right)$，由下式给出：

$$
\mathcal{J}\text{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ \text{tuple}\mathbf{u}\text{over}\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  \mid  \forall R \in  \mathcal{Q} : \mathbf{u}\left\lbrack  {\operatorname{var}\left( R\right) }\right\rbrack   \in  R\} \text{.}
$$

A join sample of $Q$ is a uniformly random tuple from $\operatorname{Join}\left( Q\right)$ .

$Q$的连接采样是从$\operatorname{Join}\left( Q\right)$中均匀随机抽取的元组。

The main problem we aim to solve is to design an index structure for $Q$ to support the two operations below:

我们的核心目标是设计$Q$的索引结构以支持以下两种操作：

- Extract a join sample of $Q$ . It is required that repeated extraction should produce mutually independent samples (i.e., every sample must be uniformly random even conditioned on all the samples already taken). In the case where $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q) is empty, this operation should declare so with a special output.

- 提取$Q$的连接采样。要求重复提取应产生相互独立的样本（即每个样本必须均匀随机，即使已获取所有先前样本）。当$\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)为空时，该操作应返回特殊输出标识。

- Insert or delete a tuple in any relation of $Q$ ,collectively called an "update".

- 在$Q$的任意关系中插入或删除元组，统称为"更新"。

Focusing on data complexities,we assume that $Q$ has a constant number of relations,and the schema of every relation in $Q$ has a constant number of attributes. We introduce

聚焦数据复杂度，我们假设$Q$具有恒定数量的关系，且每个关系的模式具有恒定数量的属性。我们引入

$$
\mathrm{{IN}} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{R \in  Q}}\left| R\right| \text{,and OUT} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left| {\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( Q\right) }\right| 
$$

and refer to them as the input and output size of $Q$ ,respectively.

并分别称其为$Q$的输入规模和输出规模。

### 2.2 The AGM Bound

### 2.2 AGM界

A fundamental question in join processing is how many tuples there can be in the join result. The AGM bound, derived by Atserias et al. [8], answers the question from the graph theory perspective.

连接处理的基本问题是连接结果可能包含多少元组。Atserias等人[8]提出的AGM界从图论角度回答了该问题。

To start with,given a join $Q$ ,we define a hypergraph $\mathcal{G} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ where $\mathcal{X} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ and $\mathcal{E} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ \operatorname{var}\left( R\right)  \mid  R \in  \mathcal{Q}\}$ . In other words, each vertex in $\mathcal{G}$ corresponds to an attribute involved in the join, and each (hyper) edge in $\mathcal{G}$ corresponds to the schema of an input relation of $Q$ . We will refer to $\mathcal{G}$ as the schema graph of $Q$ . For each edge $e \in  \mathcal{E}$ ,let ${R}_{e}$ be the (only) relation in $Q$ whose schema is $e$ .

首先，给定连接$Q$，我们定义超图$\mathcal{G} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E}}\right)$，其中$\mathcal{X} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$且$\mathcal{E} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ \operatorname{var}\left( R\right)  \mid  R \in  \mathcal{Q}\}$。换言之，$\mathcal{G}$中每个顶点对应连接涉及的属性，每条（超）边对应$Q$输入关系的模式。我们将$\mathcal{G}$称为$Q$的模式图。对每条边$e \in  \mathcal{E}$，令${R}_{e}$为$Q$中模式是$e$的唯一关系。

A fractional edge covering of $\mathcal{G}$ is a function $W : \mathcal{E} \rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$ ,where $\mathbb{R}$ is the set of real values such that

$\mathcal{G}$的分数边覆盖是函数$W : \mathcal{E} \rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$，其中$\mathbb{R}$为实数值集，满足：

- for each $e \in  \mathcal{E},W\left( e\right)  \geq  0$ ;

- 对于每个$e \in  \mathcal{E},W\left( e\right)  \geq  0$；

- for each $X \in  \mathcal{X},\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E} : X \in  e}}W\left( e\right)  \geq  1$ ,i.e.,the total weight of the edges covering vertex (a.k.a.,attribute) $X$ is at least 1 .

- 对于每个$X \in  \mathcal{X},\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E} : X \in  e}}W\left( e\right)  \geq  1$，即覆盖顶点（亦称属性）$X$的边总权重至少为1。

The AGM bound shows that any fractional edge covering of $\mathcal{G}$ yields an upper bound on the join result size OUT, as stated below:

AGM界表明，$\mathcal{G}$的任何分数边覆盖都会产生连接结果大小OUT的上界，如下所述：

LEMMA 1 (AGM BOUND [8]). Given any fractional edge covering $W$ of $\mathcal{G}$ ,we have $\left| {\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|  \leq  {\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ where

引理1（AGM界[8]）。给定$\mathcal{G}$的任意分数边覆盖$W$，我们有$\left| {\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|  \leq  {\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$，其中

$$
{\operatorname{AGM}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}{\left| {R}_{e}\right| }^{W\left( e\right) }. \tag{3}
$$

We will refer to ${\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ as the " $\mathrm{{AGM}}$ bound of $\mathcal{Q}$ " when $W$ is understood from the context.

当上下文明确时，我们将${\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$称为"$\mathcal{Q}$的$\mathrm{{AGM}}$界"。

Equation (3) expresses an upper bound of OUT using the concrete sizes of the relations in $Q$ . We often want to describe the upper bound more directly using the input size IN. For this purpose, we can apply the trivial fact $\left| {R}_{e}\right|  \leq  \mathrm{{IN}}$ (for all $e \in  \mathcal{E}$ ) to simplify Lemma 1 into OUT $\leq  {\operatorname{IN}}^{\sum {}_{e \in  \mathcal{E}}W\left( e\right) }$ . Note that the exponent $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}W\left( e\right)$ is exactly the total weight of all the edges in $\mathcal{E}$ assigned by $W$ . This motivates the concept of fractional edge covering number of $\mathcal{G} -$ denoted as ${\rho }^{ * } -$ which is the smallest $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}W\left( e\right)$ among all the fractional edge coverings $W$ . Hence,it always holds that OUT $\leq$ ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$ .

公式(3)利用$Q$中关系的具体大小表达了OUT的上界。我们常希望直接用输入大小IN来描述该上界。为此，可应用平凡事实$\left| {R}_{e}\right|  \leq  \mathrm{{IN}}$（对所有$e \in  \mathcal{E}$）将引理1简化为OUT$\leq  {\operatorname{IN}}^{\sum {}_{e \in  \mathcal{E}}W\left( e\right) }$。注意指数$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}W\left( e\right)$正是$W$分配给$\mathcal{E}$中所有边的总权重。这引出了$\mathcal{G} -$的分数边覆盖数概念，记作${\rho }^{ * } -$，即所有分数边覆盖$W$中最小的$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}W\left( e\right)$。因此始终有OUT$\leq$${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$。

The AGM bound is tight: given any integer $\operatorname{IN} \geq  1$ and hyper-graph $\mathcal{G}$ ,we can always find a join $\mathcal{Q}$ ,which has input size IN and schema graph $\mathcal{G}$ ,such that the output size of $Q$ reaches $\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ [8].

AGM界是紧致的：给定任意整数$\operatorname{IN} \geq  1$和超图$\mathcal{G}$，总能找到输入大小为IN、模式图为$\mathcal{G}$的连接$\mathcal{Q}$，其输出规模$Q$可达$\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$[8]。

### 2.3 Algorithms on Join Processing

### 2.3 连接处理算法

Full Join Computation. The tightness of the AGM bound establishes $\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ as a lower bound for the running time of any join algorithm because this amount of time is needed even just to output $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)in the worst case. Ngo et al. [45] were the first to discover a worst-case optimal algorithm that can evaluate any join in $O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ time. Since their invention,quite a number of algorithms with time complexity $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ have been subsequently developed $\left\lbrack  {6,{36},{42},{44},{46},{47},{54}}\right\rbrack$ .

全连接计算。AGM界的紧致性确立了$\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$作为任何连接算法运行时间的下界，因为即使在最坏情况下仅输出$\mathcal{J}$oin(Q)也需要这么多时间。Ngo等人[45]首次发现了能在$O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$时间内评估任意连接的最坏情况最优算法。此后，陆续开发出许多时间复杂度为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$的算法$\left\lbrack  {6,{36},{42},{44},{46},{47},{54}}\right\rbrack$。

In practice,a join’s output size OUT may be far less than ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$ . This motivates the study of output-sensitive algorithms whose running time is sensitive to both IN and OUT. As a notable success, Yannakakis [56] presented an algorithm to process any "acyclic join" in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ time. However,acyclic joins are a special type of joins on which stringent restrictions are imposed. Evaluating a join in $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ time in the absence of those restrictions, even for an arbitrarily small constant $\epsilon  > 0$ ,is still open.

实践中，连接操作的输出大小OUT可能远小于${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$。这促使研究者关注输出敏感型算法——其运行时间同时受IN和OUT影响。标志性成果是Yannakakis[56]提出的算法，能在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间内处理任何"无环连接"。但无环连接是施加了严格限制的特殊连接类型。对于不受这些限制的普通连接，即使$\epsilon  > 0$为任意小常数，能否在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间内完成计算仍是未解难题。

Several non-trivial attempts have been made to tackle the open challenge. When executed on a general join, all the known output-sensitive algorithms $\left\lbrack  {6,{36},{44},{49}}\right\rbrack$ have running time of the form

已有若干重要尝试攻克这一开放性问题。当应用于通用连接时，所有已知输出敏感型算法$\left\lbrack  {6,{36},{44},{49}}\right\rbrack$的运行时间均呈现如下形式

$$
\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{Cer}}}^{\text{width }} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right) 
$$

where width quantifies how much the schema graph $\mathcal{G}$ deviates from a tree (it can be defined using, e.g., tree width [24], query width [19], fractional hypertree width [31], etc.), and Cer is a value, called the "certificate size", measuring how difficult the input relations are for join processing in the instance-oriented sense (e.g., IN is a common value for Cer; see $\left\lbrack  {{36},{44}}\right\rbrack$ for other Cer definitions based on specialized "certificates"). Unfortunately, for all the algorithms in $\left\lbrack  {6,{36},{44},{49}}\right\rbrack$ ,the term Cer ${}^{\text{width }}$ always ends up being $\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ at some "unfriendly" joins. Therefore, as far as general joins are concerned,no algorithms with $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon } + }\right.$ OUT) time have been found,no matter how small the constant $\epsilon$ is.

其中width量化模式图$\mathcal{G}$与树的偏离程度（可用树宽[24]、查询宽[19]、分数超树宽[31]等定义），Cer称为"证书大小"，从实例导向角度衡量输入关系在连接处理中的难度（例如IN常作为Cer值；其他基于专用"证书"的Cer定义参见$\left\lbrack  {{36},{44}}\right\rbrack$）。遗憾的是，对于$\left\lbrack  {6,{36},{44},{49}}\right\rbrack$中所有算法，Cer${}^{\text{width }}$项总会在某些"不友好"连接中退化为$\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$。因此针对通用连接，无论常数$\epsilon$多小，迄今尚未发现能在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon } + }\right.$OUT)时间内完成的算法。

All the above algorithms are combinatorial. We are not aware of any non-combinatorial approaches for computing $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q). There exist,however,algorithms $\left\lbrack  {7,{25},{26}}\right\rbrack$ that use matrix multiplication to evaluate conjunctive queries with projections. A join in our definition can be understood as a conjunctive query without projections, in which case the algorithms of $\left\lbrack  {7,{25},{26}}\right\rbrack$ do not promise faster running time than the combinatorial methods discussed earlier.

上述算法均属组合数学范畴。我们尚未发现任何非组合方法可用于计算$\mathcal{J}$连接(Q)。但存在$\left\lbrack  {7,{25},{26}}\right\rbrack$类算法利用矩阵乘法处理含投影的连接查询。按本文定义，连接即无投影的连接查询，此时$\left\lbrack  {7,{25},{26}}\right\rbrack$类算法无法保证比前述组合方法更优。

Join Sampling. In 1999, Chaudhuri et al. [18] initialized the study on join sampling. They focused on joins with two relations, i.e., $Q = \left\{  {{R}_{1},{R}_{2}}\right\}$ ,and described a structure of $O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ space that allows extracting a sample from $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)in constant time. They also proved a lower bound that, if no preprocessing is allowed, taking a sample demands $\Omega \left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ time. Acharya et al. [3] considered joins with more than two relations, but their formal results apply only when the relations of $Q$ obey the so-called "star schema",namely,there is a "center relation" that has a foreign key to every other relation. Sampling is trivial on star-schema joins because it boils down to drawing a random tuple from a single relation (i.e., the center one).

连接采样。Chaudhuri等[18]于1999年开创连接采样研究，聚焦双关系连接（即$Q = \left\{  {{R}_{1},{R}_{2}}\right\}$），提出$O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$空间数据结构实现常数时间采样，并证明未预处理时采样需$\Omega \left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$时间下界。Acharya等[3]研究多关系连接，但形式化结果仅适用于$Q$符合"星型模式"的情形——存在包含所有外键的"中心关系"。星型模式连接采样简化为从单一关系（即中心关系）随机抽取元组。

No theoretical progress had been documented on join sampling in the next 18 years following the work of $\left\lbrack  {3,{18}}\right\rbrack$ (in the meantime, the problem had received a huge amount of attention from the system community, as discussed later). Theory advancement resumed in 2018. For any acyclic join $Q$ ,Zhao et al. [58] presented an $O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ - space structure that permits drawing a sample from $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)in constant time. By combining their structure with hypertree decompositions ${}^{3}$ ,one can obtain a structure for an arbitrary join $Q$ that has $O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ space and $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }}\right)$ sampling time,where fhtw is the fractional hypertree width of $Q$ ; in the worst case,however,fhtw $= {\rho }^{ * }$ , causing the sampling time to degenerate into $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ . In 2020, Chen and Yi [21] identified a class of joins under the name sequence-able joins,for which they obtained a (static) structure of $O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ space that can sample from the join result in $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$ time w.h.p. ${}^{4}$ . For general (non-sequenceable) joins,their structure still works,but the sampling time deteriorates by a factor of $O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ to $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } + 1}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$ .

在$\left\lbrack  {3,{18}}\right\rbrack$的工作之后长达18年间，连接采样领域未取得理论进展（期间该问题受到系统学界广泛关注，后文将详述）。理论突破于2018年重启。针对任意无环连接$Q$，赵等人[58]提出一种$O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$空间结构，可在常数时间内从$\mathcal{J}$连接(Q)中抽取样本。通过将该结构与超树分解${}^{3}$结合，可获得适用于任意连接$Q$的结构，其空间复杂度为$O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$，采样时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhtw }}\right)$，其中fhtw表示$Q$的分数超树宽度；但在最坏情况下，fhtw可能达到$= {\rho }^{ * }$，导致采样时间退化为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$。2020年，陈与易[21]定义了可序列化连接类，为此类连接设计了空间复杂度为$O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$的（静态）结构，能以高概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$时间内完成采样${}^{4}$。对于一般（不可序列化）连接，该结构仍适用，但采样时间会恶化$O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$倍至$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } + 1}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$。

Closely relevant to join sampling is the direct access (DA) problem on join computation. In that problem, there is a pre-agreed ordering on the tuples of $\mathop{\text{Join}}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ such that,given an integer $k \in  \left\lbrack  {1,\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right\rbrack$ ,a DA query returns the $k$ -th tuple in $\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ . If a structure can answer a DA query in ${T}_{\mathrm{{DA}}}$ time,we can use the query to draw a sample from $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)in $O\left( {T}_{\mathrm{{DA}}}\right)$ time with a random value $k \in  \left\lbrack  {1,\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right\rbrack$ , where the value OUT is available from preprocessing. When $Q$ is "free-connex",there is a structure of $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ space answering a DA query in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time $\left\lbrack  {{14},{15}}\right\rbrack$ ,which means that the structure also guarantees $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ sample time. However,because a free-connex $Q$ is necessarily acyclic, the sampling result is subsumed by that of [58].

与连接采样密切相关的是连接计算的直接访问(DA)问题。该问题要求对$\mathop{\text{Join}}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$的元组预先排序，给定整数$k \in  \left\lbrack  {1,\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right\rbrack$时，DA查询返回$\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$中的第$k$个元组。若某结构能在${T}_{\mathrm{{DA}}}$时间内响应DA查询，则可通过随机生成$k \in  \left\lbrack  {1,\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right\rbrack$值，在预处理已知OUT值的情况下，用$O\left( {T}_{\mathrm{{DA}}}\right)$时间从$\mathcal{J}$连接(Q)中抽取样本。当$Q$属于"自由连接"类时，存在空间复杂度为$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$的结构，可在$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间内响应DA查询$\left\lbrack  {{14},{15}}\right\rbrack$，这意味着该结构同样保证$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$的采样时间。但由于自由连接$Q$必为无环连接，该采样结果已被[58]的研究所涵盖。

Finally, we note that join sampling has been studied extensively in system research (see $\left\lbrack  {5,{20},{33},{38},{39},{48},{51},{55},{57},{59}}\right\rbrack$ and the references therein), which has produced numerous empirically efficient solutions. In the worst case, however, those solutions all require $\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ time to draw one sample,regardless of the value of OUT. All the above join sampling solutions, theoretical or empirical, are combinatorial.

最后需指出，连接采样在系统研究中已有大量探索（参见$\left\lbrack  {5,{20},{33},{38},{39},{48},{51},{55},{57},{59}}\right\rbrack$及其参考文献），产生了众多实证高效的解决方案。但在最坏情况下，无论OUT值如何，这些方案都需要$\Omega \left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$时间抽取单个样本。上述所有连接采样方案，无论是理论还是实证性的，均属于组合方法。

## 3 THE AGM SPLIT THEOREM

## 3 AGM分割定理

This section will establish the AGM split theorem, which serves as the technical core of our sampling algorithm. The theorem provides a simple and intuitive way to split the "attribute space", with the guarantee that the upper bound on the join result size given by the AGM bound gets (at least) halved in each subspace after the split.

本节将建立AGM分割定理，该定理作为我们采样算法的技术核心。该定理提供了一种简单直观的方法来划分"属性空间"，并保证分割后每个子空间中AGM边界给出的连接结果规模上界至少减半。

Recall that $\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ is the set of attributes involved in the join. Set $d \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left| {\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|$ . Let us impose an arbitrary ordering on the attributes of $\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ ,which can then be denoted as ${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\ldots ,{X}_{d}$ . This way, every tuple $\mathbf{u}$ in the join result Join(Q)can be interpreted as a point in ${\mathbb{N}}^{d}$ ,where $\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{i}\right)$ is the point’s $i$ -th coordinate for each $i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,d}\right\rbrack$ . The attribute space is now formally defined to be ${\mathbb{N}}^{d}$ .

记$\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$为连接操作涉及的属性集合。设$d \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left| {\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|$。我们对$\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$的属性施加任意排序后，可将其表示为${X}_{1},{X}_{2},\ldots ,{X}_{d}$。如此，连接结果Join(Q)中的每个元组$\mathbf{u}$可视为${\mathbb{N}}^{d}$空间中的一个点，其中$\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{i}\right)$表示该点在第$i$个维度上的坐标（对每个$i \in  \left\lbrack  {1,d}\right\rbrack$）。此时属性空间正式定义为${\mathbb{N}}^{d}$。

### 4.1 The Join Box-Tree

### 4.1 连接盒树

Our AGM split theorem shows how to split an arbitrary box in the attribute space. Next, we will repeatedly utilize the theorem to partition the space into sufficiently small boxes with "trivial AGM bounds". This will produce a tree $\mathcal{T}$ that we name the join box-tree.

我们的AGM划分定理展示了如何划分属性空间中的任意盒子。接下来，我们将反复应用该定理将空间分割为具有"平凡AGM界"的足够小盒子，由此生成名为连接盒树的树结构$\mathcal{T}$。

Fix an arbitrary fractional edge covering $W$ of the schema graph $\mathcal{G} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ of the input join $\mathcal{Q}$ . Define $\rho  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}W\left( e\right)$ ,i.e.,the total weight of the edges in $\mathcal{E}$ under $W$ . The value $\rho$ is a constant that does not depend on IN.

固定输入连接$\mathcal{Q}$的模式图$\mathcal{G} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E}}\right)$的任意分数边覆盖$W$。定义$\rho  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}W\left( e\right)$为$W$下$\mathcal{E}$中边的总权重。该值$\rho$是与输入无关的常数。

The join box-tree $\mathcal{T}$ is a tree dependent on $W$ . An internal node in $\mathcal{T}$ has at most ${2d} + 1$ child nodes,where $d \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left| {\operatorname{var}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|$ . Every node, no matter leaf or internal, is associated with a box, and no two nodes are associated with the same box. ### 4.2 The Sampling Algorithm

### 4.2 抽样算法

We emphasize that the join box-tree $\mathcal{T}$ is conceptual: its size is too large ${}^{7}$ such that we cannot afford to materialize it. To extract a sample from the join result $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q),our algorithm will generate - on the fly - a single root-to-leaf path of $\mathcal{T}$ in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time and then wipe off the path from memory immediately. The path generation may not always produce a sample, but it does so with probability $\mathrm{{OUT}}/{\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ ,where $\mathrm{{OUT}} = \left| {\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|$ . Thus, ${\operatorname{AGM}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) /$ OUT repeats will get us a sample in expectation.

需强调连接盒树$\mathcal{T}$是概念性的：其规模过大${}^{7}$导致无法物化。为从连接结果$\mathcal{J}$中抽取样本，本算法将动态生成$\mathcal{T}$的单个根到叶路径，耗时$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$并立即从内存清除该路径。路径生成未必总能产生样本，但成功概率为$\mathrm{{OUT}}/{\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$（其中$\mathrm{{OUT}} = \left| {\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|$）。因此，预期重复${\operatorname{AGM}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) /$次即可获得样本。

### 4.3 Oracles

### 4.3 预言机

It is time to clarify the oracles. In fact, the count and median oracles only require solving problems that are nowadays considered rudimentary in the data-structure area of computer science. We can, for example, maintain a set of range trees $\left\lbrack  {9,{23}}\right\rbrack$ to implement the count oracle and a set of binary search trees to implement the median oracle. All these trees occupy $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ space,can be built in $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ time,and can be modified in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time along with each update in the relations of $Q$ . Further details are available in Appendix B. We thus have established the following theorem.

现在需要澄清预言机的定义。实际上，计数和中位数预言机只需解决当今计算机科学数据结构领域中被视为基础的问题。例如，我们可以维护一组范围树$\left\lbrack  {9,{23}}\right\rbrack$来实现计数预言机，用一组二叉搜索树实现中位数预言机。这些树占用$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$空间，可在$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$时间内构建，并能在$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间内随$Q$关系的每次更新进行修改。更多细节见附录B。由此我们得出以下定理。

THEOREM 5. Consider an arbitrary (natural) join $Q$ involving a constant number of attributes. Let $W$ be an arbitrary fractional edge covering of the schema graph of $Q$ . There is an index structure of $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ space that can be used to extract,with high probability,a sample from the join result of $Q$ in $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( Q\right) /\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$ time,and supports an update in the relations of $\mathcal{Q}$ in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time, where IN and OUT, respectively, are the input and output sizes of $Q$ ,and ${\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( Q\right)$ is the ${AGM}$ bound of $Q$ under $W$ given by Lemma 1. The structure's update and sampling algorithms are combinatorial.

定理5. 考虑涉及恒定数量属性的任意（自然）连接$Q$。令$W$为$Q$模式图的任意分数边覆盖。存在一个占用$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$空间的索引结构，能以高概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( Q\right) /\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$时间内从$Q$的连接结果中抽取样本，并在$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间内支持$\mathcal{Q}$关系的更新，其中IN和OUT分别是$Q$的输入和输出大小，${\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( Q\right)$是引理1给出的$W$下$Q$的${AGM}$界。该结构的更新和采样算法均为组合式。

By choosing an optimal fractional edge covering $W$ ,the sample time in the theorem is bounded by the complexity in (2).

通过选择最优分数边覆盖$W$，定理中的采样时间受限于(2)式复杂度。

## 5 HARDNESS OF JOIN SAMPLING AND OUTPUT-SENSITIVE JOIN ALGORITHMS

## 5 连接采样与输出敏感连接算法的困难性

This section will provide evidence that Theorem 5 can no longer be improved significantly for all joins. For this purpose, we will argue that no combinatorial structure can give a "yes" answer to the join sampling question unless the combinatorial $k$ -clique hypothesis is wrong. To do so, we will take a de-tour to bridge join sampling with output-sensitive join evaluation.

本节将证明定理5对所有连接操作均无法显著改进。为此，我们将论证：除非组合$k$-团猜想错误，否则没有任何组合结构能对连接采样问题给出"是"的答案。为此，我们将迂回搭建连接采样与输出敏感连接评估之间的桥梁。

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a combinatorial algorithm for computing $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q). Recall from Section 1 that $\mathcal{A}$ is $\epsilon$ -output sensitive - where $0 < \epsilon  < 1/2$ - if it runs in time $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$ as long as $\mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ . The lemma below is proved in Appendix C.

设$\mathcal{A}$为计算$\mathcal{J}$连接(Q)的组合算法。回顾第1节，当$\mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$时，若$\mathcal{A}$在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$时间内运行，则称其为$\epsilon$-输出敏感（其中$0 < \epsilon  < 1/2$）。下述引理证明见附录C。

LEMMA 6. If a combinatorial structure can answer "yes" to the join sampling question for a constant $\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1/2}\right)$ ,there is a combinatorial $\epsilon$ -output sensitive algorithm for join computation.

引理6. 若某组合结构能对常数$\epsilon  \in  \left( {0,1/2}\right)$的连接采样问题回答"是"，则存在用于连接计算的组合式$\epsilon$-输出敏感算法。

An $\epsilon$ -output sensitive algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ does not need to be fast when OUT $> {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ . However,when OUT $> {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ ,the sampling algorithm in Theorem 5 runs in $\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$ time. In Appendix D, we combine the two algorithms to prove:

一种$\epsilon$输出敏感算法$\mathcal{A}$在输出量OUT$> {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$时无需保持高效。但当OUT$> {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$时，定理5中的采样算法可在$\widetilde{O}\left( \frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$时间内完成运行。在附录D中，我们通过结合两种算法证明：

LEMMA 7. Given a combinatorial $\epsilon$ -output sensitive algorithm,we can design a combinatorial algorithm to detect whether $\operatorname{Join}\left( Q\right)$ is empty in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$ time w.h.p.,regardless of the value OUT.

引理7. 给定组合式$\epsilon$输出敏感算法，我们可设计一个组合算法，以高概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$时间内检测$\operatorname{Join}\left( Q\right)$是否为空集，且不受OUT值影响。

As shown in Appendix F, however, the emptiness-detection algorithm in Lemma 7 breaks the combinatorial $k$ -clique hypothesis. In summary,finding a combinatorial $\epsilon$ -output sensitive algorithm is at least as hard as breaking the hypothesis. Lemma 6 indicates that it can be only harder to improve our result in Theorem 5 by a polynomial factor even when $\mathrm{{OUT}} \ll  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$ .

如附录F所示，引理7中的空集检测算法违背了组合式$k$团假设。综上，寻找组合式$\epsilon$输出敏感算法的难度至少等同于突破该假设。引理6表明，即使$\mathrm{{OUT}} \ll  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$时，要通过多项式级改进定理5的结果只会更加困难。

## 6 APPLICATIONS

## 6 应用场景

Our join sampling structure can be utilized to tackle many problems, a partial list of which is presented below.

我们的连接采样结构可应用于解决诸多问题，以下列举部分应用方向。

- (Join size estimation) It is standard [21] to use join sampling to estimate $\left| {\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|$ up to a relative error $\lambda$ . Our structure in Theorem 5 can be applied to produce an estimate in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{1}{{\lambda }^{2}}\frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}}{\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }}\right)$ time w.h.p.,improving the state of the art in [21] by an $O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ factor. We can also support each update in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time.

- (连接规模估计) 采用连接采样估计$\left| {\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) }\right|$至相对误差$\lambda$是标准方法[21]。运用定理5中的结构，可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{1}{{\lambda }^{2}}\frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}}{\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }}\right)$时间内高概率完成估计，较[21]的现有技术提升$O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$倍效率。每次更新也仅需$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间。

- (Subgraph sampling) Let $G \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,E}\right)$ be a simple undirected graph. Given a pattern graph $Q$ of a constant size,a query samples an occurrence ${}^{8}$ of $Q$ in $G$ uniformly at random. We obtain a structure of $\widetilde{O}\left( \left| E\right| \right)$ space that answers a query in $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\left| E\right| }^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OCC}}\} }\right)$ time w.h.p.,where ${\rho }^{ * }$ is the fractional edge covering number of $Q$ and OCC is the number of occurrences of $Q$ in $G$ . The structure supports an edge insertion and deletion in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time. Previously,a structure matching our guarantees was given in [29]. Our solution is drastically different and actually settles a problem we call "join sampling with predicates", which captures subgraph sampling as a (simple) special case. Details are available in Appendix E.

- (子图采样) 设$G \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,E}\right)$为简单无向图。给定常量大小的模式图$Q$，查询可均匀随机采样$Q$在$G$中的出现实例${}^{8}$。我们构建的空间复杂度为$\widetilde{O}\left( \left| E\right| \right)$的结构，能以高概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\left| E\right| }^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OCC}}\} }\right)$时间内响应查询，其中${\rho }^{ * }$是$Q$的分数边覆盖数，OCC表示$Q$在$G$中的出现次数。该结构支持$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间内完成边插入与删除操作。此前文献[29]提出了性能相当的结构，但我们的解决方案截然不同，实际上解决了一个称为"带谓词的连接采样"的问题，而子图采样只是其(简单)特例。详见附录E。

- (Joins with random enumeration) Carmeli et al. [15] proposed a variant of the join computation problem, where the objective is to design an algorithm that, after an initial pre-processing, can (i) produce a random permutation of the tuples in $\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ ,and (ii) do so with a small delay $\Delta$ (the maximum time gap between the reporting of two consecutive tuples in the permutation). We obtain the first algorithm that,after an initial $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ -time preprocessing, produces the whole permutation in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ time (i.e.,worst-case optimal up to a polylogarithmic factor) with a delay $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$ w.h.p.. Details are available in Appendix G.

- （与随机枚举结合）Carmeli等人[15]提出了一种连接计算问题的变体，其目标是设计一种算法，在初始预处理后能够：(i)生成$\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$中元组的随机排列，(ii)以较小延迟$\Delta$实现（即排列中连续两个元组输出之间的最大时间间隔）。我们获得了首个算法，经过初始$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$时间预处理后，能以$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$时间生成完整排列（即最坏情况下达到多对数因子的最优解），且高概率保证延迟$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OUT}}\} }\right)$。详见附录G。

- (Join Union Sampling) Let ${\mathcal{Q}}_{1},{\mathcal{Q}}_{2},\ldots ,{\mathcal{Q}}_{k}$ be joins with $\operatorname{var}\left( {\mathcal{Q}}_{1}\right)  =$ $\operatorname{var}\left( {\mathcal{Q}}_{2}\right)  = \ldots  = \operatorname{var}\left( {\mathcal{Q}}_{k}\right)$ ,where $k \geq  2$ is a constant. ## B ORACLE IMPLEMENTATION

## B ORACLE实现方案

The count oracle essentially deals with a problem known as orthogonal range counting. In that problem,the input is a set $P$ of $n$ points in $d$ -dimensional space ${\mathbb{R}}^{d}$ for some constant $d$ . Given an axis-parallel rectangle $q$ in ${\mathbb{R}}^{d}$ ,a query returns $\left| {P \cap  q}\right|$ ,namely,the number of points in $P$ that are covered by $q$ . The goal is to store $P$ in a data structure to answer queries efficiently. We refer the reader to [4] for a survey on the known data structures solving this problem. Among them is the range tree $\left\lbrack  {9,{23}}\right\rbrack$ ,which consumes $O\left( {n{\log }^{d - 1}n}\right)$ space, answers a query in $O\left( {{\log }^{d}n}\right)$ time,and supports a point insertion and deletion in $P$ using $O\left( {{\log }^{d}n}\right)$ time. It serves as a count oracle meeting our requirements.

计数oracle本质上处理的是正交范围计数问题。该问题的输入是某个常数维度空间${\mathbb{R}}^{d}$中的$n$个点集$P$。给定${\mathbb{R}}^{d}$中的轴对齐矩形$q$，查询返回$\left| {P \cap  q}\right|$，即被$q$覆盖的$P$中的点数。目标是将$P$存储在数据结构中以高效应答查询。关于解决该问题的已知数据结构综述，读者可参阅[4]。其中范围树$\left\lbrack  {9,{23}}\right\rbrack$占用$O\left( {n{\log }^{d - 1}n}\right)$空间，以$O\left( {{\log }^{d}n}\right)$时间应答查询，并支持用$O\left( {{\log }^{d}n}\right)$时间对$P$进行点插入和删除，这满足了我们所需的计数oracle要求。

Before implementing the median oracle, let us look at an alternative problem first. The input is a set $S$ of $n$ real values. Given an interval $q$ in $\mathbb{R}$ and an integer $k \geq  1$ ,a query returns the $k$ -th smallest integer in $S \cap  q$ (or returns nothing in the special case where $k > \left| {S \cap  q}\right|$ ). The goal is to store $S$ in a data structure to answer queries efficiently. We can create a binary search tree (BST) on $S$ and keep,at each node $v$ in the tree,the number of descendant nodes of $v$ . The tree occupies $O\left( n\right)$ space,answers a query in $O\left( {\log n}\right)$ time (see Chapter 14 "Augmenting Data Structures" of [22]),and supports an insertion or deletion in $S$ using $O\left( {\log n}\right)$ time. The same structure can also find the size of $S \cap  q$ in $O\left( {\log n}\right)$ time (see the above chapter of [22] again). It thus follows that we can report the median value in $S \cap  q$ in $O\left( {\log n}\right)$ time.

在实现中位数oracle之前，让我们先看一个替代问题。输入是$n$个实数值的集合$S$。给定$\mathbb{R}$中的区间$q$和整数$k \geq  1$，查询返回$S \cap  q$中第$k$小的整数（或在$k > \left| {S \cap  q}\right|$的特殊情况下不返回）。目标是将$S$存储在数据结构中以高效应答查询。我们可以在$S$上创建二叉搜索树(BST)，并在树的每个节点$v$处维护其子节点数量。该树占用$O\left( n\right)$空间，以$O\left( {\log n}\right)$时间应答查询（参见[22]第14章"增强数据结构"），并支持用$O\left( {\log n}\right)$时间对$S$进行插入或删除。该结构还能在$O\left( {\log n}\right)$时间内找出$S \cap  q$的大小（再次参见[22]上述章节），因此我们可以在$O\left( {\log n}\right)$时间内报告$S \cap  q$的中位数值。

To implement a median oracle,for every attribute $X \in  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ , we maintain the aforementioned (slightly-augmented) BST on the set $S$ of $X$ -values that appear in at least one relation in $Q$ . The structure occupies $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ space and,given a box $B$ ,finds the median of actdom(X,B)- which is the median of the values of $S$ covered by the interval $B\left( X\right)  -$ in $O\left( {\log \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$ time. It is straightforward to maintain the tree in $O\left( {\log \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$ time per update.

为实现中位数预言机，对于每个属性$X \in  \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$，我们在至少出现在$Q$中一个关系的$X$值集合$S$上维护前述（稍作扩展的）二叉搜索树。该结构占用$\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$空间，并能在给定框$B$时，于$O\left( {\log \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$时间内找到actdom(X,B)的中位数——即被区间$B\left( X\right)  -$覆盖的$S$值的中位数。每次更新时维护该树的时间复杂度为$O\left( {\log \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$。

Next,we will prove that ${\mathcal{A}}_{emp}$ ,w.h.p.,correctly decides if Join $\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  = \varnothing$ and runs in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$ time. Our analysis is through a case-by-case discussion on the value of OUT.

将上述空值检测算法记为${\mathcal{A}}_{emp}$。接下来我们将证明${\mathcal{A}}_{emp}$在高概率下能正确判定Join$\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  = \varnothing$是否为空，并在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$时间内完成。我们的分析基于对OUT值的分类讨论。

- If $\mathrm{{OUT}} = 0,{\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$ always returns "Join(Q)empty". Because $\mathcal{A}$ is $\epsilon$ -output sensitive,it terminates in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$ time w.h.p.. The cost of ${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$ is thus bounded by $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$ w.h.p..

- 若$\mathrm{{OUT}} = 0,{\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$始终返回"Join(Q)为空"。由于$\mathcal{A}$具有$\epsilon$输出敏感性，其高概率终止时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$。因此${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$的成本高概率不超过$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$。

- Consider now $0 <$ OUT $\leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ . Being $\epsilon$ -output sensitive, $\mathcal{A}$ must report the full $\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$ time w.h.p.. Hence, ${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$ finds a tuple of $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$ time w.h.p..

- 考虑$0 <$OUT≥$\leq  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$的情形。作为$\epsilon$输出敏感算法，$\mathcal{A}$必须高概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$时间内完整报告$\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$。故${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$高概率能在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$时间内找到$\mathcal{J}$oin(Q)的一个元组。

- The final case is OUT $> {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$ . By Theorem 5, ${\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$ must return a join sample of $\mathcal{Q}$ in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$ time w.h.p.. ${\mathcal{A}}_{emp}$ thus finds a tuple of $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$ time w.h.p..

- 最后是OUT∈$> {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\epsilon }$的情况。根据定理5，${\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$高概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }\right)$时间内返回$\mathcal{Q}$的连接样本。因此${\mathcal{A}}_{emp}$高概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$时间内发现$\mathcal{J}$oin(Q)的一个元组。

## E 子图抽样

Before discussing subgraph sampling, we first extend Theorem 5 to a scenario we call join sampling with predicates, where sampling is performed on only a subset of the join result. Let $Q$ be a join defined in Section 2.1. Given a boolean predicate $\sigma$ ,define $\mathcal{J}$ oin $\left( {\sigma ,\mathcal{Q}}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}}$ $\{ \mathbf{u} \in  \mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  \mid  \mathbf{u}$ satisfies $\sigma \}$ ,i.e.,the subset of tuples in $\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ passing the filtering condition $\sigma$ . 回顾一下，样本是从$\mathfrak{J}$ oin(Q)中均匀随机抽取$\mathbf{u}$的。因此，$\mathcal{J}$ oin $\left( {\sigma ,Q}\right)$中的每个元组（它是$\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)的一个子集）都有相同概率被选为$\mathbf{u}$。因此，$\sigma$-样本（若成功输出元组）将返回$Q$的一个$\sigma$-连接样本。分析其成功概率时，设${\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\sigma } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left| {\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( {\sigma ,\mathcal{Q}}\right) }\right|$。如第4.2节所示，样本以$\frac{\mathrm{{OUT}}}{{\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( Q\right) }$的概率输出$\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)的元组，这意味着$\sigma$-样本的成功概率为$\frac{\mathrm{{OUT}}}{{\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( Q\right) } \cdot  \frac{{\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\sigma }}{\mathrm{{OUT}}} = \frac{{\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\sigma }}{{\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( Q\right) }$。由此可得，通过重复算法直至成功，我们可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{AGM}}}_{W}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right) /\max \left\{  {1,{\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\sigma }}\right\}  }\right)$时间内获得$\mathcal{Q}$的$\sigma$-连接样本——通过最优选择分数边覆盖$W$，该时间可优化至$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \left\{  {1,{\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\sigma }}\right\}  }\right)$，其中${\rho }^{ * }$是$Q$模式图的分数边覆盖数。

We are ready to solve the subgraph sampling problem. Recall that the goal is to preprocess an undirected graph $G \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,E}\right)$ such that, given a constant-size pattern graph $Q$ ,we can uniformly sample an occurrence of $Q$ from $G$ . Let $\mathcal{X}$ be the set of vertices in $Q$ ,and $\mathcal{E}$ be the set of edges in $Q$ . It is worth reminding the reader that every edge in $G$ and $Q$ contains exactly two vertices.

现在开始解决子图采样问题。需预处理一个无向图$G \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,E}\right)$，使得给定恒定大小的模式图$Q$时，能从$G$中均匀采样$Q$的出现实例。设$\mathcal{X}$为$Q$的顶点集，$\mathcal{E}$为$Q$的边集。需要特别说明：$G$和$Q$中的每条边都严格包含两个顶点。

We create a join $Q$ with $\left| \mathcal{E}\right|$ relations as follows. For each edge $e = \{ X,Y\}$ in $Q$ (where $X$ and $Y$ are vertices in $Q$ ),create a relation ${R}_{e}$ with schema $\operatorname{var}\left( {R}_{e}\right)  = \{ X,Y\}$ . The size of ${R}_{e}$ is $2\left| E\right|$ ,i.e.,twice the number of edges in $G$ . Specifically,for every edge $\{ x,y\}$ in $G$ (where $x$ and $y$ are vertices in $G$ ),we create two tuples in ${R}_{e}$ : tuple ${\mathbf{u}}_{1}$ satisfying ${\mathbf{u}}_{1}\left( X\right)  = x$ and ${\mathbf{u}}_{1}\left( Y\right)  = y$ ,and tuple ${\mathbf{u}}_{2}$ satisfying ${\mathbf{u}}_{2}\left( X\right)  = y$ and ${\mathbf{u}}_{2}\left( Y\right)  = x$ . This completes the construction of $\mathcal{Q}$ , which has input size $\operatorname{IN} = \left| \mathcal{E}\right|  \cdot  \left( {2\left| E\right| }\right)  = O\left( \left| E\right| \right)$ .

我们按如下方式创建一个与$\left| \mathcal{E}\right|$关系的连接$Q$。对于$Q$中的每条边$e = \{ X,Y\}$（其中$X$和$Y$是$Q$中的顶点），创建一个模式为$\operatorname{var}\left( {R}_{e}\right)  = \{ X,Y\}$的关系${R}_{e}$。${R}_{e}$的大小为$2\left| E\right|$，即$G$中边数的两倍。具体来说，对于$G$中的每条边$\{ x,y\}$（其中$x$和$y$是$G$中的顶点），我们在${R}_{e}$中创建两个元组：满足${\mathbf{u}}_{1}\left( X\right)  = x$和${\mathbf{u}}_{1}\left( Y\right)  = y$的元组${\mathbf{u}}_{1}$，以及满足${\mathbf{u}}_{2}\left( X\right)  = y$和${\mathbf{u}}_{2}\left( Y\right)  = x$的元组${\mathbf{u}}_{2}$。这样就完成了$\mathcal{Q}$的构建，其输入大小为$\operatorname{IN} = \left| \mathcal{E}\right|  \cdot  \left( {2\left| E\right| }\right)  = O\left( \left| E\right| \right)$。

Every tuple $\mathbf{u}$ in the result $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)of $\mathcal{Q}$ can be thought of as mapping each edge $\{ X,Y\}$ in $Q$ to an edge $\{ \mathbf{u}\left( X\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right) \}$ in $G$ . If the set of (mapped) edges $\{ \{ \mathbf{u}\left( X\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right) \}  \mid  \{ X,Y\}  \in  \mathcal{E}\}$ induces an occurrence of $Q$ ,we say that $\mathbf{u}$ describes the occurrence.

在$\mathcal{Q}$的结果$\mathcal{J}$连接(Q)中，每个元组$\mathbf{u}$可视为将$Q$中的每条边$\{ X,Y\}$映射到$G$中的边$\{ \mathbf{u}\left( X\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right) \}$。若（映射后的）边集$\{ \{ \mathbf{u}\left( X\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right) \}  \mid  \{ X,Y\}  \in  \mathcal{E}\}$诱导出$Q$的一个实例，则称$\mathbf{u}$描述了该实例。

The following are two (folklore) facts about relationships between the tuples in $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)and the occurrences of $Q$ .

以下是关于$\mathcal{J}$连接(Q)中元组与$Q$实例之间关系的两个（公认）事实。

- Fact 1: Every occurrence of $Q$ is described by the same number $c$ of tuples in $\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ ,where $c \geq  1$ is a constant. For example,consider $Q$ to be a triangle with vertices $X,Y$ ,and $Z$ . An occurrence of $Q$ ,which is a triangle with vertices $x,y$ , and $z$ in $G$ ,is described by the tuple $\mathbf{u} \in  \mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)where $\mathbf{u}\left( X\right)  = x,\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right)  = y$ ,and $\mathbf{u}\left( Z\right)  = z$ . It is easy to see that the triangle is described by six tuples of $\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ in total,and six is exactly the number of automorhisms of $Q$ .

- 事实1：每个$Q$实例都由$\mathcal{J}\operatorname{oin}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$中相同数量$c$的元组描述，其中$c \geq  1$为常数。例如，设$Q$为具有顶点$X,Y$、$Z$的三角形。一个$Q$实例（即$G$中具有顶点$x,y$、$z$的三角形）由元组$\mathbf{u} \in  \mathcal{J}$连接(Q)描述，其中$\mathbf{u}\left( X\right)  = x,\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right)  = y$、$\mathbf{u}\left( Z\right)  = z$。易见该三角形总共由六个$\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$元组描述，而六恰好是$Q$的自同构数。

- Fact 2: It is possible for $\operatorname{Join}\left( Q\right)$ to contain tuples that do not describe any occurrence of $Q$ . For example,consider $Q$ to be a 4-cycle with vertices ${X}_{1},{X}_{2},{X}_{3}$ ,and ${X}_{4}$ . The tuple $\mathbf{u}$ with $\left( {\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{1}\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{2}\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{3}\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{4}\right) }\right)  = \left( {x,y,x,y}\right)$ ,where $\{ x,y\}$ is an edge in $G$ ,belongs to $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)but does not describe any occurrence of $Q$ .

- 事实2：$\operatorname{Join}\left( Q\right)$可能包含不描述任何$Q$出现情况的元组。例如，假设$Q$是一个具有顶点${X}_{1},{X}_{2},{X}_{3}$和${X}_{4}$的4-循环。当$\{ x,y\}$是$G$中的一条边时，带有$\left( {\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{1}\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{2}\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{3}\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( {X}_{4}\right) }\right)  = \left( {x,y,x,y}\right)$的元组$\mathbf{u}$属于$\mathcal{J}$连接(Q)，但并未描述任何$Q$的出现实例。

To sample occurrences, we create a structure of Theorem 5 on $Q$ ,which occupies $\widetilde{O}\left( \left| E\right| \right)$ space and can be easily maintained in $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ time per edge insertion and deletion. To draw a sample,we apply the $\sigma$ -sample algorithm by setting the predicate $\sigma$ to "tuple $\mathbf{u}$ should describe an occurrence of $Q$ ". The predicate can be evaluated in constant time. By Fact 1,the value of ${\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\sigma }$ equals $c \cdot  \mathrm{{OCC}}$ ,where OCC is the number of occurrences of $Q$ in $G$ . Our earlier discussion indicates that the sample time is $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\left| E\right| }^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OCC}}\} }\right)$ .

为采样出现实例，我们在$Q$上构建定理5的结构，该结构占用$\widetilde{O}\left( \left| E\right| \right)$空间且能轻松维护——每次边插入或删除仅需$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$时间。采样时，我们应用$\sigma$抽样算法，将谓词$\sigma$设为"元组$\mathbf{u}$应描述$Q$的出现实例"。该谓词可在常数时间内判定。根据事实1，${\mathrm{{OUT}}}_{\sigma }$的值等于$c \cdot  \mathrm{{OCC}}$（其中OCC表示$Q$在$G$中的出现次数）。前文讨论表明采样时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\left| E\right| }^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\max \{ 1,\mathrm{{OCC}}\} }\right)$。

## F ( 6-OUTPUT SENSITIVITY BREAKS COMBINATORIAL $k$ -CLIQUE HYPOTHESIS

## F（六输出敏感性打破组合$k$-团假设）

By Lemma 7,the existence of an $\epsilon$ -output sensitive algorithm implies a combinatorial algorithm ${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$ that,given any join $Q$ ,can decide whether $\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  = \varnothing$ in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$ time w.h.p.. Next, we show how to break the combinatorial $k$ -clique hypothesis with ${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$ for any constant $k \geq  3$ .

根据引理7，$\epsilon$输出敏感算法的存在意味着：给定任意连接$Q$，组合算法${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$能以$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * } - \epsilon }}\right)$时间高概率判定$\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)  = \varnothing$是否成立。接下来，我们将展示如何用${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$打破任何常数$k \geq  3$下的组合$k$-团假设。

Let $G \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,E}\right)$ be a simple undirected graph. In Appendix E,we presented a strategy to convert subgraph sampling to join sampling for any pattern graph $Q$ . Here,we apply the same ideas to construct a join $Q$ from $G$ ,setting $Q$ to $k$ -clique. As before,for each edge $e = \{ X,Y\}$ in $Q$ ,create a relation ${R}_{e}$ with schema $\operatorname{var}\left( {R}_{e}\right)  = \{ X,Y\}$ . For every edge $\{ x,y\}$ in $G$ ,relation ${R}_{e}$ contains two tuples: tuple ${\mathbf{u}}_{1}$ with ${\mathbf{u}}_{1}\left( X\right)  = x$ and ${\mathbf{u}}_{1}\left( Y\right)  = y$ ,and tuple ${\mathbf{u}}_{2}$ with ${\mathbf{u}}_{2}\left( X\right)  = y$ and ${\mathbf{u}}_{2}\left( Y\right)  = x$ . Recall that the conversion has two properties, presented as Facts 1 and 2 in Appendix E. Crucially, Fact 2 can now be strengthened into the claim below (for $Q = k$ -clique):

设$G \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,E}\right)$为简单无向图。附录E中我们提出了将任意模式图$Q$的子图采样转化为连接采样的策略。此处我们运用相同思路，从$G$构建连接$Q$，并设$Q$为$k$-团。如前所述，对$Q$中每条边$e = \{ X,Y\}$创建模式为$\operatorname{var}\left( {R}_{e}\right)  = \{ X,Y\}$的关系${R}_{e}$。对于$G$中每条边$\{ x,y\}$，关系${R}_{e}$包含两个元组：带${\mathbf{u}}_{1}\left( X\right)  = x$和${\mathbf{u}}_{1}\left( Y\right)  = y$的元组${\mathbf{u}}_{1}$，以及带${\mathbf{u}}_{2}\left( X\right)  = y$和${\mathbf{u}}_{2}\left( Y\right)  = x$的元组${\mathbf{u}}_{2}$。需注意该转换具有两个特性（附录E的事实1和事实2），关键的是，对于$Q = k$-团的情况，事实2可强化为下述主张：

## Every tuple in $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)describes an occurrence of $Q$ in $G$ .

## $\mathcal{J}$中oin(Q)的每个元组描述了$G$中$Q$的一次出现。

To explain why,consider any tuple $\mathbf{u} \in  \operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ . For each edge $e = \{ X,Y\}$ in $Q$ ,we know that $\{ \mathbf{u}\left( X\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right) \}$ is in ${R}_{e}$ and,hence,is an edge in $G$ . It thus follows that $G$ has an edge between vertices $\mathbf{u}\left( X\right)$ and $\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right)$ for any two distinct vertices $X$ and $Y$ in $Q$ . Therefore, $\mathbf{u}$ describes a $k$ -clique occurrence in $G$ .

为解释原因，考虑任意元组$\mathbf{u} \in  \operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$。对于$Q$中的每条边$e = \{ X,Y\}$，我们知道$\{ \mathbf{u}\left( X\right) ,\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right) \}$在${R}_{e}$中，因此是$G$的一条边。由此可推，对于$Q$中任意两个不同顶点$X$和$Y$，$G$在顶点$\mathbf{u}\left( X\right)$与$\mathbf{u}\left( Y\right)$之间存在边。因此，$\mathbf{u}$描述了$G$中一个$k$团的出现。

Combined with Fact 1,the above claim indicates that $G$ has a $k$ -clique if and only if $\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$ is non-empty. Therefore,we can apply ${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$ to detect the emptiness of $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)and then decide on the $k$ -clique presence in $G$ . To analyze running time,we note that the fractional edge covering number of $k$ -clique is $k/2$ ,as is also the fractional edge covering number ${\rho }^{ * }$ of the schema graph $Q$ of $Q$ . Therefore,the algorithm described earlier runs in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\left| E\right|  + {\left| E\right| }^{\frac{k}{2} - \epsilon }}\right)$ time. Applying the trivial fact $\left| E\right|  \leq  {\left| V\right| }^{2}$ ,we can relax the time complexity to $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\left| V\right| }^{2} + {\left| V\right| }^{k - {2\epsilon }}}\right)$ ,which is $\widetilde{O}\left( {\left| V\right| }^{k - {2\epsilon }}\right)$ because $k \geq  3$ and $\epsilon  < 1/2$ ,thus breaking the combinatorial $k$ -clique hypothesis.

结合事实1，上述论断表明$G$存在$k$团当且仅当$\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$非空。因此，我们可以应用${\mathcal{A}}_{\text{emp }}$检测oin(Q)中$\mathcal{J}$是否为空，进而判定$G$中$k$团的存在性。分析运行时间时，注意到$k$团的分数边覆盖数为$k/2$，这与$Q$的模式图$Q$的分数边覆盖数${\rho }^{ * }$相同。因此，前述算法运行时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\left| E\right|  + {\left| E\right| }^{\frac{k}{2} - \epsilon }}\right)$。根据平凡事实$\left| E\right|  \leq  {\left| V\right| }^{2}$，可将时间复杂度放宽至$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\left| V\right| }^{2} + {\left| V\right| }^{k - {2\epsilon }}}\right)$，即$\widetilde{O}\left( {\left| V\right| }^{k - {2\epsilon }}\right)$（因$k \geq  3$且$\epsilon  < 1/2$），从而打破组合$k$团假设。

## G JOINS WITH RANDOM ENUMERATION

## G与随机枚举的连接

Our solution to this problem combines ideas in Appendix C and a technique in [52] that adapts a delay-oblivious reporting algorithm for small-delay enumeration.

First,create a structure of Theorem 5 on the given join $Q$ in $\widetilde{O}\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ time. Then,we find out whether $\mathrm{{OUT}} = 0$ . If so, $\mathcal{A}$ must have done so in $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ time w.h.p.; obviously, $\mathrm{{OUT}} > 0$ in this scenario. Otherwise,OUT $= 0$ w.h.p.,in which case the execution time of $\mathcal{A}$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ ,and we have already solved the join.

The subsequent discussion focuses on OUT $> 0$ . We carry out two steps in the same fashion as in Appendix C.

后续讨论聚焦于OUT$> 0$的情况。我们将按照附录C的方式执行两个步骤。

- The first step obtains an estimate $\widehat{\mathrm{{OUT}}} \in  \left\lbrack  {\mathrm{{OUT}},2\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right\rbrack$ and, in the meantime,reports at least OUT/2 tuples in $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q). - 第一步获取一个估计值$\widehat{\mathrm{{OUT}}} \in  \left\lbrack  {\mathrm{{OUT}},2\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right\rbrack$，同时至少报告$\mathcal{J}$中OUT/2个元组的oin(Q)。算法与前述相同：持续以$\mathcal{A}$采样，直到连续遇到$\Delta  \mathrel{\text{:=}} O\left( {\log \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$个已见元组。该步骤成本为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}} \cdot  \mathrm{{OUT}} \cdot  \Delta }\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$。

- 第二步报告$\operatorname{Join}\left( \mathcal{Q}\right)$中尚未发现的剩余元组。如附录C所述，我们使用$\mathcal{A}$提取$s \mathrel{\text{:=}} O\left( {\widehat{\mathrm{{OUT}}} \cdot  \ln \mathrm{{IN}}}\right)$样本，仅当样本从未被报告过时才输出。该步骤成本为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}} \cdot  s}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$。

It is immediate from the analysis in Appendix C that the above two-step algorithm w.h.p. manages to output the entire $\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q) in a random permutation. However, it is not designed to achieve a small delay. In fact, Step 1 is fine: it enumerates at least OUT/2 result tuples with a delay $\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}} \cdot  \Delta }\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ . The trouble lies in Step 2 where we may fetch a large number of samples before hitting an unseen tuple.

根据附录C的分析可直接得出：上述两阶段算法大概率(w.h.p.)能以随机排列输出完整的$\mathcal{J}$ oin(Q)。但该算法并非为降低延迟而设计。实际上第一步表现良好：它以$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}}{\mathrm{{OUT}}} \cdot  \Delta }\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$延迟枚举至少OUT/2个结果元组。问题在于第二步——在遇到新元组前可能需要获取大量样本。

To eliminate the issue, we resort to a technique of Tao and Yi [52]. They defined a join reporting algorithm ${\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$ to be $\alpha$ -aggressive if,after $t$ running time ${}^{10}$ for any integer $t \geq  1,{\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$ must have discovered at least $\lfloor t/\alpha \rfloor$ distinct result tuples. 为解决此问题，我们采用Tao和Yi[52]提出的技术。他们将连接报告算法${\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$定义为$\alpha$-激进型：对于任意整数$t \geq  1,{\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$，在$t$运行时间${}^{10}$后必须发现至少$\lfloor t/\alpha \rfloor$个不同结果元组。他们证明任何此类${\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$都可转换为能以$O\left( \alpha \right)$延迟报告所有结果元组的算法，且转换后的算法输出顺序与原算法${\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$一致。

We claim that the two-step algorithm explained earlier is $\alpha$ - aggressive with

To understand the claim,first note that Step 1 is $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ - aggressive because,as mentioned,it ensures a delay $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ . 要理解该主张，首先注意步骤1是$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$——之所以称为激进，是因为如前所述，它确保了$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}/\mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$的延迟。考虑步骤2中的任意时刻；设$t$为从步骤1开始到该时刻的运行时间。由于$t = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$，我们可以将$\beta$提升至某个$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$因子以确保

$$
\lfloor t/\alpha \rfloor  \leq  \left\lfloor  {\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right) /\alpha }\right\rfloor   = \lfloor \mathrm{{OUT}} \cdot  \widetilde{O}\left( 1\right) /\beta \rfloor  \leq  \mathrm{{OUT}}/2
$$
