## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

Intersection joins over interval data are relevant in spatial and temporal data settings. A set of intervals join if their intersection is non-empty. In case of point intervals, the intersection join becomes the standard equality join.

区间数据的交集连接在空间和时间数据场景中具有重要意义。当一组区间的交集非空时，它们可进行连接。对于点区间而言，交集连接即退化为标准等值连接。

We establish the complexity of Boolean conjunctive queries with intersection joins by a many-one equivalence to disjunctions of Boolean conjunctive queries with equality joins. The complexity of any query with intersection joins is that of the hardest query with equality joins in the disjunction exhibited by our equivalence. This is captured by a new width measure called the ij-width.

我们通过将带交集连接的布尔连接查询多一等价转换为带等值连接的布尔连接查询的析取式，确立了其计算复杂性。任何带交集连接查询的复杂性，都等价于转换后析取式中最复杂的等值连接查询的复杂性。这一特性可通过称为ij宽度的新型度量指标来表征。

We also introduce a new syntactic notion of acyclicity called iota-acyclicity to characterise the class of Boolean queries with intersection joins that admit linear time computation modulo a poly-logarithmic factor in the data size. Iota-acyclicity is for intersection joins what alpha-acyclicity is for equality joins. It strictly sits between gamma-acyclicity and Berge-acyclicity. The intersection join queries that are not iota-acyclic are at least as hard as the Boolean triangle query with equality joins, which is widely considered not computable in linear time.

我们还引入了一种称为ι-无环性（iota-acyclicity）的新语法概念，用于刻画采用交集连接（intersection joins）的布尔查询类，这类查询允许在数据大小的多对数因子内进行线性时间计算。ι-无环性之于交集连接，就如同α-无环性（alpha-acyclicity）之于等值连接。它严格介于γ-无环性（gamma-acyclicity）和Berge-无环性之间。非ι-无环性的交集连接查询至少与采用等值连接的布尔三角形查询同等困难，后者被广泛认为无法在线性时间内计算。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Interval data is common in spatial and temporal databases. One important type of joins on intervals is the intersection join: A set of intervals join if their intersection is non-empty. In the case of point intervals, the intersection join becomes the classical equality join.

区间数据在空间和时态数据库中十分常见。区间上一种重要的连接类型是交集连接：若一组区间的交集非空，则它们可连接。对于点区间而言，交集连接即退化为经典的等值连接。

This paper establishes the complexity of Boolean conjunctive queries with Intersection Joins (denoted by IJ). Whereas the complexity of Boolean conjunctive queries with Equality Joins (denoted by EJ) has been extensively investigated in the literature by, e.g., Marx [21] and Abo Khamis et al. [4], the complexity of the more general IJ queries remained open for decades.

本文确立了带交集连接（简称IJ）的布尔合取查询的复杂度。尽管带等值连接（简称EJ）的布尔合取查询复杂度已被Marx[21]和Abo Khamis等人[4]在文献中深入研究，但更一般的IJ查询复杂度问题数十年来悬而未决。

The key tool aiding our investigation is a many-one equivalence of any IJ query to a disjunction of EJ queries. It uses a forward reduction (IJ-to-EJ) and a backward reduction (EJ-to-IJ), cf. Figure 1.

支撑本研究的关键工具是将任意IJ查询多一等价于EJ查询的析取。这通过前向归约（IJ到EJ）和后向归约（EJ到IJ）实现，参见图1。

The forward reduction takes an IJ query $Q$ and a database $\mathbf{D}$ of intervals. It reduces $Q$ to a disjunction of EJ queries,all over the same database $\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$ of numbers represented as bitstrings. The number and size of the EJ queries only depend on the structure of $Q$ ,and the size of $\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$ is within a poly-logarithmic factor from the size of $\mathbf{D}$ . This forward reduction enables us to use any algorithms and associated runtime upper bounds for the EJ queries as upper bounds for the IJ query $Q$ as well. Specifically, $Q$ ’s runtime is upper bounded by the maximum runtime upper bound among the generated EJ queries.

前向归约接收IJ查询$Q$和区间数据库$\mathbf{D}$，将$Q$归约为针对同一数值数据库$\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$（表示为位串）的EJ查询析取。生成的EJ查询数量和大小仅取决于$Q$的结构，且$\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$的大小与$\mathbf{D}$相比仅相差多对数因子。该前向归约使我们能将EJ查询的算法及运行时上界直接应用于IJ查询$Q$。具体而言，$Q$的运行时上界由生成的EJ查询中最大运行时上界决定。

The backward reduction takes an EJ query ${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$ ,whose structure matches that of one of the queries obtained by the forward reduction of an IJ query $Q$ without self-joins,and an arbitrary database ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{2}$ of numbers chosen independently from $\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$ and $\mathbf{D}$ in the forward reduction. It then reduces ${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$ to an IJ query $Q$ whose structure matches that of the original IJ query $Q$ ,and reduces ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{2}$ to some database ${\mathbf{D}}_{2}$ of intervals and size $O\left( \left| {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{2}\right| \right)$ . The backward reduction shows that we can use any lower bounds (i.e. hardness results) on any one of the EJ queries ${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$ constructed by the forward reduction as lower bounds on the IJ query $Q$ . Together with the upper bounds, this implies that the IJ query $Q$ is precisely at the same hardness level as the hardest EJ query ${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$ . Our forward reduction thus produces an optimal solution to $Q$ given optimal solutions to the queries ${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$ .

逆向归约处理一个EJ查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$（其结构与通过无自连接IJ查询$Q$正向归约所获查询之一的结构匹配）以及任意数值数据库${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{2}$（该数据库数值独立选自正向归约中的$\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$与$\mathbf{D}$）。随后将${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$归约为结构匹配原始IJ查询$Q$的IJ查询$Q$，并将${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{2}$归约为某个区间数据库${\mathbf{D}}_{2}$（其规模为$O\left( \left| {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{2}\right| \right)$）。该逆向归约表明：我们可以将正向归约构造的任意EJ查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$上的下界（即硬度结果）作为IJ查询$Q$的下界。结合上界分析可知，IJ查询$Q$的硬度等级与最复杂EJ查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$完全等同。因此，给定查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$的最优解，我们的正向归约能为$Q$生成最优解。

The quest for the optimality of EJ computation has a long history. The submodular width has been recently established as an optimality yardstick $\left\lbrack  {4,{21}}\right\rbrack$ . Let ${BCQ}\left( C\right)$ be the decision problem: Given an EJ query $Q \in  \mathcal{C}$ and a database $\mathbf{D}$ ,check whether $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ is true. This problem is fixed-parameter tractable (FPT; with parameter the query size $\left| Q\right|$ ) if there is an algorithm solving every $C$ -instance in time $f\left( \left| Q\right| \right)  \cdot  {\left| \mathbf{D}\right| }^{d}$ for some fixed constant $d$ and any computable function $f$ . The problem ${BCQ}\left( C\right)$ is FPT if and only if every $Q \in  C$ has bounded submodular width [21].

寻求EJ计算的最优性已有悠久历史。近年来，子模宽度(submodular width)被确立为最优性衡量标准$\left\lbrack  {4,{21}}\right\rbrack$。设${BCQ}\left( C\right)$为决策问题：给定EJ查询$Q \in  \mathcal{C}$和数据库$\mathbf{D}$，验证$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$是否成立。若存在算法能在时间$f\left( \left| Q\right| \right)  \cdot  {\left| \mathbf{D}\right| }^{d}$内求解任意$C$实例（其中$d$为固定常数，$f$为任意可计算函数），且参数为查询规模$\left| Q\right|$，则该问题属于固定参数可解类(FPT)。当且仅当所有$Q \in  C$具有有界子模宽度时，问题${BCQ}\left( C\right)$才属于FPT类[21]。

A natural question is then what would be an optimality yardstick for IJ computation. We settle this question with a new width notion called the ij-width. This is the maximum submodular width [21] of the EJ queries obtained by our forward reduction. Any IJ query $Q$ can then be computed in time $O\left( {{N}^{w}\text{polylog}N}\right)$ ,where $N$ is the size of the input database and $w$ is the ij-width of $Q$ . Each EJ query created by our forward reduction is computed over databases of size $O\left( {N\text{polylog}N}\right)$ and in time $O\left( {{N}^{w}\text{polylog}N}\right)$ .

一个自然的问题便是：如何为IJ计算确立最优性衡量标准。我们通过引入一种称为ij宽度的新型宽度概念解决了这一问题。该宽度即为我们前向约简所得EJ查询的最大子模宽度[21]。据此，任何IJ查询$Q$均可在$O\left( {{N}^{w}\text{polylog}N}\right)$时间内完成计算，其中$N$表示输入数据库的规模，$w$代表$Q$的ij宽度。通过前向约简生成的每个EJ查询，均在规模为$O\left( {N\text{polylog}N}\right)$的数据库上执行，并在$O\left( {{N}^{w}\text{polylog}N}\right)$时间内完成计算。

The relationship between the complexities of IJ and EJ is nontrivial. In general, IJ queries are, as expected, more expensive than their EJ counterparts. There are simple $\alpha$ -acyclic IJ queries whose complexity is on par with that of cyclic EJ queries. Consider the EJ counterparts of IJ queries, where we replace the intersection joins by equality joins. The triangle IJ query has ij-width 3/2 (Section 1.1), which matches the submodular width of the EJ triangle query. Yet for the IJ Loomis-Whitney query with four variables, the ij-width is $5/3$ while the EJ counterpart has submodular width $4/3$ .

IJ与EJ复杂性之间的关系并非显而易见。一般而言，IJ查询如预期那样比EJ查询代价更高。存在简单的$\alpha$-无环IJ查询，其复杂度与循环EJ查询相当。考虑将IJ查询中的交集连接替换为等值连接的EJ对应查询：三角形IJ查询的ij-width为3/2（第1.1节），这与EJ三角形查询的子模宽度一致。但对于含四个变量的IJ Loomis-Whitney查询，其ij-width为$5/3$，而EJ对应查询的子模宽度为$4/3$。

This raises the question of what is the natural counterpart of $\alpha$ -acyclic EJ queries,which are known to be the linear-time computable EJ queries [29]. We introduce an acyclicity notion called iota ( $\iota$ ) that syntactically characterises the class of IJ queries computable in linear time (modulo a polylog factor): An IJ query is $\iota$ -acyclic if and only if the incidence graph of its hypergraph does not have Berge cycles of length strictly greater than two. Our forward reduction maps $\iota$ -acyclic IJ queries to an equivalent disjunction of $\alpha$ -acyclic EJ queries,so by definition the ij-width of any $\iota$ -acyclic IJ query is one. $\iota$ -acyclicity implies $\gamma$ -acyclicity and is implied by Berge-acyclicity. The IJ queries that are not $\iota$ -acyclic are at least as hard as the EJ triangle query, which is widely considered not computable in linear time (based on the 3SUM conjecture) [25]. $\iota$ -acyclicity is thus for IJ queries what $\alpha$ -acyclicity is for EJ queries.

这引发了一个问题：已知可在线性时间内计算的$\alpha$-无环EJ查询，其自然对应物是什么？我们提出称为iota($\iota$)的无环性概念，它从语法上刻画了可在线性时间（模多对数因子）内计算的IJ查询类：当且仅当超图的关联图不包含严格大于2长度的Berge环时，IJ查询是$\iota$-无环的。我们的前向归约将$\iota$-无环IJ查询映射为$\alpha$-无环EJ查询的等价析取式，因此根据定义，任何$\iota$-无环IJ查询的ij-width均为1。$\iota$-无环性蕴含$\gamma$-无环性，且被Berge-无环性所蕴含。非$\iota$-无环的IJ查询至少与EJ三角形查询同等困难（基于3SUM猜想，普遍认为无法线性时间计算）[25]。因此，$\iota$-无环性之于IJ查询，正如$\alpha$-无环性之于EJ查询。

An intersection join can also be expressed as a disjunction of inequality joins, which can be evaluated using FAQ-AI [2]. However, FAQ-AI has a higher complexity than our approach: The exponents in the time complexity of FAQ-AI for the IJ triangle, Loomis-Whitney four,and the clique-four queries are: 2, 2, and respectively 3 ; whereas the ij-widths are: $3/2,5/3$ ,and respectively 2 .

交集连接也可表示为不等值连接的析取式，可通过FAQ-AI[2]评估。但FAQ-AI的复杂度高于我们的方法：FAQ-AI对IJ三角形、Loomis-Whitney四元及团四元查询的时间复杂度指数分别为2、2和3；而ij-width则分别为$3/2,5/3$和2。

### 1.1 Example: The Triangle Query

### 1.1 示例：三角形查询

We introduce our approach using the Boolean triangle query, where each join is an intersection join:

我们通过布尔三角形查询（其中每个连接均为交集连接）来介绍方法：

$$
{Q}_{\bigtriangleup } = R\left( {\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  S\left( {\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  T\left( {\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) 
$$

Brackets denote interval variables ranging over intervals with real-valued endpoints. The two occurrences of $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ in the query denote an intersection join on $A$ . An equality join on $A$ is expressed using the variable $A$ without brackets.

方括号表示取值范围为实值端点区间的区间变量。查询中$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$的两次出现表示对$A$进行交集连接，而等值连接则通过不带括号的变量$A$表示。

A common approach first computes the join of two of the three relations and then joins with the third relation. The first join can take $O\left( {N}^{2}\right)$ ,where $N$ is the size of the relations. An equivalent encoding of ${Q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ using inequality joins can be computed in time $O\left( {{N}^{2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$ using FAQ-AI [2] (Appendix E). Our approach takes time $O\left( {{N}^{3/2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$ ,which matches the complexity of the EJ triangle query (modulo polylog factor).

常规方法先计算三个关系中任意两个的连接，再与第三个关系连接。首次连接可能耗时$O\left( {N}^{2}\right)$（其中$N$为关系规模）。使用FAQ-AI[2]（附录E）对${Q}_{\bigtriangleup }$进行不等值连接编码的等效计算时间为$O\left( {{N}^{2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$。我们的方法耗时$O\left( {{N}^{3/2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$，这与EJ三角形查询复杂度（模多对数因子）一致。

Our approach is based on a decomposition of the tensor representing an intersection join. In our example, we decompose the three joins as follows. We construct three segment trees: one for the intervals from $R$ and $T$ for the interval variable $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ ,another for the intervals from $R$ and $S$ for the interval variable $\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack$ ,and the third one for the intervals from $S$ and $T$ for the interval variable $\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack$ . The segment tree for $O\left( N\right)$ intervals can be constructed in $O\left( {N\log N}\right)$ time and has depth $O\left( {\log N}\right)$ . Its nodes represent intervals called segments. A segment includes its descendant segments and is partitioned by its child segments. A property of a segment tree is that each input interval can be expressed as the disjoint union of at most $O\left( {\log N}\right)$ segments. The problem of checking whether two input intervals intersect now becomes the problem of finding two segments, one per interval, that lie along the same root-to-leaf path in the segment tree. There are three ways this can happen: The two segments are the same, one segment is an ancestor of the other, or the other way around; this corresponds to the possible permutations of the two segments along a path, with one permutation also including the case where the segments are the same. A further property is that each permutation can be expressed using equality joins, as explained next. We encode each node in the segment tree as a bitstring: the empty string represents the root, the strings " 0 " and " 1 " represent the left and right child respectively, the strings "00" and "01" represent the left and right child of the "0" respectively, and so on. Given two nodes ${n}_{1}$ and ${n}_{2}$ ,where ${n}_{1}$ is an ancestor of ${n}_{2}$ ,the bitstring for ${n}_{1}$ is then a prefix of that for ${n}_{2}$ . We can capture this relationship in a query with equality joins: We use one variable ${A}_{1}$ to stand for the bitstring for ${n}_{1}$ ,which is also a prefix of the bitstring for ${n}_{2}$ ,and ${A}_{2}$ to stand for the remaining bitstring for ${n}_{2}$ .

我们的方法基于对表示交集连接的张量进行分解。在本例中，我们将三个连接分解如下：构建三棵线段树——第一棵用于来自$R$和$T$的区间变量$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$，第二棵用于来自$R$和$S$的区间变量$\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack$，第三棵用于来自$S$和$T$的区间变量$\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack$。$O\left( N\right)$区间的线段树可在$O\left( {N\log N}\right)$时间内构建完成，其深度为$O\left( {\log N}\right)$。树节点所代表的区间称为段，一个段包含其所有后代段，并被其子段分割。线段树的特性是每个输入区间最多可表示为$O\left( {\log N}\right)$个互不相交的段的并集。检测两个输入区间是否相交的问题，现在转化为在线段树中寻找位于同一根到叶路径上的两个段（每个区间对应一个段）。存在三种可能情形：两段相同、一段是另一段的祖先、反之亦然；这对应着路径上两段的所有排列组合，其中一种排列也包含两段相同的情况。另一个特性是每种排列都可以通过等值连接表示，具体如下：我们用比特串编码线段树的每个节点——空串表示根节点，"0"和"1"分别表示左右子节点，"00"和"01"分别表示"0"的左右子节点，以此类推。给定节点${n}_{1}$和${n}_{2}$，当${n}_{1}$是${n}_{2}$的祖先时，${n}_{1}$的比特串就是${n}_{2}$比特串的前缀。我们可通过等值连接查询捕获这种关系：用变量${A}_{1}$表示${n}_{1}$的比特串（同时也是${n}_{2}$比特串的前缀），用${A}_{2}$表示${n}_{2}$比特串的剩余部分。

Each such case can be expressed using an EJ query. Our query ${Q}_{\Delta }$ is then equivalent to the disjunction ${\widetilde{Q}}_{ \vartriangle  } = \mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  8\right\rbrack  }}{\widetilde{Q}}_{i}$ of the following EJ queries:

The query is defined over new relations ${R}_{\mathcal{A};\mathcal{B}},{S}_{\mathcal{B};C}$ ,and ${T}_{\mathcal{A};C}$ that are transformations of the original relations $R,S$ ,and $T$ to hold the bitstrings in place of the original intervals. The subscript $\mathcal{A} = i$ stands for the indices $1,\ldots ,i$ of the variables ${A}_{1},\ldots ,{A}_{i}$ ranging over bitstrings; similarly for $\mathcal{B}$ and $C$ . To avoid clutter,in the remainder of this paper we will denote all such constructed new relations ${R}_{\mathcal{A};\mathcal{B}}$ by $\widetilde{R}$ and use their schema to identify them uniquely. For input relations of size $N$ ,the new relations have size $O\left( {N{\log }^{2}N}\right)$ ,with one logarithmic factor per join interval variable. We next explain the purpose of these bitstring relations. A tuple $\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2},{b}_{1},{b}_{2}}\right)$ is in ${R}_{2;2}$ if and only if there is a pair of intervals $\left( {{a}_{R},{b}_{R}}\right)$ in $R$ and the concatenations ${a}_{1} \circ  {a}_{2}$ and ${b}_{1} \circ  {b}_{2}$ reconstruct bitstrings of nodes covered by intervals ${a}_{R}$ and respectively ${b}_{R}$ . Similarly,a tuple $\left( {{b}_{1},{c}_{1},{c}_{2}}\right)$ in ${S}_{1;2}$ reconstructs the bitstrings ${b}_{1}$ and ${c}_{1} \circ  {c}_{2}$ of nodes covered by intervals ${b}_{S}$ and ${c}_{S}$ . A tuple $\left( {{a}_{1},{c}_{1}}\right)$ in ${T}_{1;1}$ specifies the bitstrings ${a}_{1}$ and ${c}_{1}$ of nodes covered by ${a}_{T}$ and ${c}_{T}$ . Therefore, ${\widetilde{Q}}_{1}$ holds in case: (1) there exists a node covered by an interval ${a}_{R}$ in $R$ and a node covered by an interval ${a}_{T}$ in $T$ such that the bitstring of the latter is a prefix of the former; (2) there exists a node covered by an interval ${b}_{R}$ in $R$ and a node covered by an interval ${b}_{S}$ in $S$ such that the bitstring of the latter is a prefix of the former; and (3) there exists a node covered by an interval ${c}_{S}$ in $S$ and a node covered by an interval ${c}_{T}$ in $T$ such that the bitstring of the latter is a prefix of the former. Equivalently, ${a}_{R}$ intersects with ${a}_{T},{b}_{R}$ intersects with ${b}_{S}$ ,and ${c}_{S}$ intersects with ${c}_{T}$ , i.e., $R\left( {{a}_{R},{b}_{R}}\right)  \land  S\left( {{b}_{S},{c}_{S}}\right)  \land  T\left( {{a}_{T},{c}_{T}}\right)$ holds.

该查询定义在通过转换原始关系$R,S$和$T$所得到的新关系${R}_{\mathcal{A};\mathcal{B}},{S}_{\mathcal{B};C}$与${T}_{\mathcal{A};C}$上，这些新关系用于存储比特串以替代原始区间。下标$\mathcal{A} = i$表示变量${A}_{1},\ldots ,{A}_{i}$在比特串上的索引$1,\ldots ,i$；同理适用于$\mathcal{B}$和$C$。为避免冗余，本文后续将统一用$\widetilde{R}$表示所有此类构造的新关系${R}_{\mathcal{A};\mathcal{B}}$，并通过其模式唯一标识。对于规模为$N$的输入关系，新关系的规模为$O\left( {N{\log }^{2}N}\right)$，每个连接区间变量对应一个对数因子。接下来阐述这些比特串关系的作用：当且仅当存在区间对$\left( {{a}_{R},{b}_{R}}\right)$属于$R$，且级联${a}_{1} \circ  {a}_{2}$与${b}_{1} \circ  {b}_{2}$能重构分别被区间${a}_{R}$和${b}_{R}$覆盖节点的比特串时，元组$\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2},{b}_{1},{b}_{2}}\right)$才属于${R}_{2;2}$。类似地，${S}_{1;2}$中的元组$\left( {{b}_{1},{c}_{1},{c}_{2}}\right)$重构被区间${b}_{S}$和${c}_{S}$覆盖节点的比特串${b}_{1}$与${c}_{1} \circ  {c}_{2}$；${T}_{1;1}$中的元组$\left( {{a}_{1},{c}_{1}}\right)$则指定被${a}_{T}$和${c}_{T}$覆盖节点的比特串${a}_{1}$与${c}_{1}$。因此，${\widetilde{Q}}_{1}$成立的条件是：(1)存在被$R$中区间${a}_{R}$覆盖的节点，其比特串是被$T$中区间${a}_{T}$覆盖节点比特串的前缀；(2)存在被$R$中区间${b}_{R}$覆盖的节点，其比特串是被$S$中区间${b}_{S}$覆盖节点比特串的前缀；(3)存在被$S$中区间${c}_{S}$覆盖的节点，其比特串是被$T$中区间${c}_{T}$覆盖节点比特串的前缀。等价而言，${a}_{R}$与${a}_{T},{b}_{R}$相交，${b}_{S}$与${c}_{S}$相交，且${c}_{S}$与${c}_{T}$相交，即$R\left( {{a}_{R},{b}_{R}}\right)  \land  S\left( {{b}_{S},{c}_{S}}\right)  \land  T\left( {{a}_{T},{c}_{T}}\right)$成立。

The hypergraph of each of the eight EJ queries admits a hy-pertree decomposition in the form of a star with the central bag $\left\{  {{A}_{1},{B}_{1},{C}_{1}}\right\}$ ,cf. Figure 2. In each of these decompositions,the materialisation of this bag requires solving the triangle join ${R}^{\prime }\left( {{A}_{1},{B}_{1}}\right)  \land$ ${S}^{\prime }\left( {{B}_{1},{C}_{1}}\right)  \land  {T}^{\prime }\left( {{A}_{1},{C}_{1}}\right)$ ,where ${R}^{\prime }$ is a projection of ${R}_{\mathcal{A};\mathcal{B}}$ to ${A}_{1},{B}_{1}$ and similarly for ${S}^{\prime }$ and ${T}^{\prime }$ . The new relations and their projections have size $O\left( {N{\log }^{2}N}\right)$ . The materialisation of the join takes time $O\left( {\left( N{\log }^{2}N\right) }^{3/2}\right)  = O\left( {{N}^{3/2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$ using existing worst-case optimal join algorithms [22]. Checking whether any of the eight EJ queries is true takes time linear in the maximum size of the bags of its decomposition. This gives an overall computation time $O\left( {{N}^{3/2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$ for ${\widetilde{Q}}_{ \bigtriangleup  }$ and also for ${Q}_{ \bigtriangleup  }$ .

八个EJ查询中每个的超图都允许采用星形结构的超树分解，其中心包为$\left\{  {{A}_{1},{B}_{1},{C}_{1}}\right\}$，参见图2。在这些分解中，该包的物化需要求解三角形连接${R}^{\prime }\left( {{A}_{1},{B}_{1}}\right)  \land$${S}^{\prime }\left( {{B}_{1},{C}_{1}}\right)  \land  {T}^{\prime }\left( {{A}_{1},{C}_{1}}\right)$，其中${R}^{\prime }$是${R}_{\mathcal{A};\mathcal{B}}$到${A}_{1},{B}_{1}$的投影，${S}^{\prime }$和${T}^{\prime }$同理。新关系及其投影的大小为$O\left( {N{\log }^{2}N}\right)$。利用现有最坏情况最优连接算法[22]，连接操作的物化耗时$O\left( {\left( N{\log }^{2}N\right) }^{3/2}\right)  = O\left( {{N}^{3/2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$。检查八个EJ查询是否为真的时间与其分解中最大包的大小成线性关系。这使得整体计算时间对${\widetilde{Q}}_{ \bigtriangleup  }$和${Q}_{ \bigtriangleup  }$均为$O\left( {{N}^{3/2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$。

We close the example with a discussion on an alternative encoding: Instead of $\widetilde{R}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{B}_{1},{B}_{2}}\right)$ ,we can use its lossless decomposition into ${\widetilde{R}}_{A}\left( {{Id},{A}_{1},{A}_{2}}\right)$ and ${\widetilde{R}}_{B}\left( {{Id},{B}_{1},{B}_{2}}\right)$ . Here,a tuple $\left( {i,{a}_{1},{a}_{2}}\right)  \in$ ${\widetilde{R}}_{A}$ encodes that the $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ -interval in the tuple of $R$ with identifier $i$ is mapped to the bitstring ${a}_{1} \circ  {a}_{2}$ in the segment tree for $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ . We thus avoid the explicit materialisation of all combinations of encodings for $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ and $\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack$ (and the same for the other two pairs of variables). This decomposition applies systematically to all constructed relations. Each of these new relations has size $O\left( {N\log N}\right)$ ,which is less than $O\left( {N{\log }^{2}N}\right)$ in our default encoding. In general,for each $m$ -way join interval variable,this encoding creates $m$ new relations regardless of whether such variables occur in the same relational atom in the query. In contrast,our default encoding creates ${m}^{k}$ new relations for each atom that contains $k$ such $m$ -way join variables. Although more space efficient, this encoding comes with the same data complexity (modulo log factors) as the one used in the paper.

我们通过讨论一种替代编码来结束本示例：不使用$\widetilde{R}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{B}_{1},{B}_{2}}\right)$，而是将其无损分解为${\widetilde{R}}_{A}\left( {{Id},{A}_{1},{A}_{2}}\right)$和${\widetilde{R}}_{B}\left( {{Id},{B}_{1},{B}_{2}}\right)$。这里，元组$\left( {i,{a}_{1},{a}_{2}}\right)  \in$${\widetilde{R}}_{A}$表示$R$中标识符为$i$的元组里的$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$区间被映射到$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$线段树中的位串${a}_{1} \circ  {a}_{2}$。这样避免了显式物化$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$和$\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack$所有编码组合（另两对变量同理）。该分解系统性地适用于所有构造的关系。每个新关系的大小为$O\left( {N\log N}\right)$，小于默认编码中的$O\left( {N{\log }^{2}N}\right)$。一般来说，对于每个$m$路连接区间变量，此编码会创建$m$个新关系，无论这些变量是否出现在查询的同一关系原子中。而我们的默认编码会为每个包含$k$个$m$路连接变量的原子创建${m}^{k}$个新关系。尽管更节省空间，该编码的数据复杂度（对数因子除外）与论文所用编码相同。

## 2 RELATED WORK

## 2 相关工作

Algorithms for intersection joins have been developed in the context of temporal [11] and spatial databases [13, 18]. In temporal databases, tuples can be associated with intervals that represent the valid time periods. Temporal or interval joins are used to match tuples that are valid at the same time. In spatial databases, tuples can be associated with 2D objects that are approximated by two intervals defining minimum bounding rectangles [19]. Spatial joins are used to find tuples with overlapping bounding rectangles. Temporal and spatial joins are thus intersection joins. Similarity joins under different distance metrics can be reduced to geometric containment, which is expressible using intersection joins [12].

交集连接算法已在时态数据库[11]和空间数据库[13,18]领域得到发展。在时态数据库中，元组可与表示有效时间段的区间相关联。时态或区间连接用于匹配同时有效的元组。在空间数据库中，元组可与由两个定义最小边界矩形[19]的区间近似的二维对象关联。空间连接用于查找边界矩形重叠的元组。因此时态连接和空间连接都属于交集连接。不同距离度量下的相似性连接可转化为几何包含问题，这可通过交集连接表达[12]。

Intersection joins. There is a wealth of work on algorithms for intersection joins, mostly binary joins computed one at a time and over relations with 1D or 2D intervals [18]. These algorithms use indices or partitioning and are typically disk-based with the objective of minimising I/O accesses. Examples of index-based algorithms include: the slot index spatial join [20], the seeded tree join [16], the R-tree join [8], and relational interval tree join [9]. Extensions of binary joins [8] to multi-way joins have also been considered [19]. Partition-based algorithms include: the partition based spatial-merge join [24], the spatial hash join [17], the size separation spatial join [15], the sweeping-based spatial join [5], and the plane-sweep method [27]. The partition-based algorithms can be naturally parallelised and distributed $\left\lbrack  {6,{26},{28}}\right\rbrack$ . These algorithms can compute two-way intersection joins in $O\left( {N\log N + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ , where OUT is the output size and $N$ is the input size. To sum up, there is no development on optimal algorithms for queries with intersection joins. Furthermore, most existing approaches focus on one join at a time, which can be suboptimal since they can produce intermediate results asymptotically larger than the final result, as in the case of equality joins [23]. Our approach escapes the limitation of existing intersection join algorithms and benefits from worst-case optimal algorithms for equality joins.

交集连接。现有大量关于交集连接算法的研究，主要是针对一维或二维区间关系逐个计算的二元连接[18]。这些算法采用索引或分区策略，通常基于磁盘存储并旨在最小化I/O访问。基于索引的算法包括：槽索引空间连接[20]、种子树连接[16]、R树连接[8]以及关系区间树连接[9]。二元连接[8]到多路连接的扩展也有研究[19]。基于分区的算法包括：基于分区的空间合并连接[24]、空间哈希连接[17]、尺寸分离空间连接[15]、扫描式空间连接[5]以及平面扫描法[27]。这些基于分区的算法可自然实现并行化与分布式$\left\lbrack  {6,{26},{28}}\right\rbrack$。这些算法能以$O\left( {N\log N + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间复杂度计算双向交集连接，其中OUT为输出规模，$N$为输入规模。总之，目前尚不存在针对交集连接查询的最优算法研究。此外，现有方法大多聚焦单次连接操作，这可能产生渐进大于最终结果的中间输出（如等值连接[23]），导致次优性能。我们的方法突破了现有交集连接算法的局限，并受益于等值连接的最坏情况最优算法。

Inequality joins. An intersection join can be expressed as a disjunction of inequality joins: Given two intervals $\left\lbrack  {{l}_{1},{r}_{1}}\right\rbrack$ and $\left\lbrack  {{l}_{2},{r}_{2}}\right\rbrack$ defined by their starting and ending points, checking whether they intersect can be expressed as $\left( {{l}_{1} \leq  {l}_{2} \leq  {r}_{1}}\right)  \vee  \left( {{l}_{2} < {l}_{1} \leq  {r}_{2}}\right)$ . IJ queries can thus be reformulated in the framework of Functional Aggregate Queries with Additive Inequalities (FAQ-AI) [2]. The hypergraph of an FAQ-AI has two types of hyperedges: normal hyperedges, one per relation in the query and that covers the nodes representing the variables of that relation, and relaxed hyperedges, one per inequality join and that covers the variables in the inequality. This hypergraph is subject to relaxed hypertree decompositions, which are fractional hypertree decompositions [21] where each normal hyperedge is covered by one bag and each relaxed hyperedge is covered by two adjacent bags. For a database of size $N$ ,an FAQ-AI can be solved in time $O\left( {N}^{{\text{subw }}_{\ell }}\right.$ polylog $\left. N\right)$ ,where ${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }$ is the relaxed submodular width of the FAQ-AI and corresponds to the submodular width of the FAQ-AI hypergraph computed over its possible relaxed hypertree decompositions [2]. For the triangle IJ query ${Q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ in Section 1.1,the ij-width is ijw $\left( {Q}_{\bigtriangleup }\right)  = 3/2$ yet ${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( {Q}_{\bigtriangleup }\right)  = 2$ (Appendix E). Furthermore, it can be shown that ijw is lower than ${\text{subw}}_{\ell }$ for the Loomis-Whitney 4,and the 4-clique IJ queries (see Table 1 for a summary and [14] for a full analysis).

不等式连接。交集连接可表述为不等式连接的析取：给定由起止点定义的两个区间$\left\lbrack  {{l}_{1},{r}_{1}}\right\rbrack$和$\left\lbrack  {{l}_{2},{r}_{2}}\right\rbrack$，检查其是否相交可表示为$\left( {{l}_{1} \leq  {l}_{2} \leq  {r}_{1}}\right)  \vee  \left( {{l}_{2} < {l}_{1} \leq  {r}_{2}}\right)$。因此IJ查询可在带加法不等式的函数聚合查询(FAQ-AI)框架[2]中重构。FAQ-AI的超图包含两类超边：常规超边（查询中各关系对应一个，覆盖该关系变量节点）和松弛超边（每个不等式连接对应一个，覆盖不等式中变量）。该超图适用松弛超树分解——即分数超树分解[21]，其中常规超边被单个包覆盖，松弛超边被两个相邻包覆盖。对于规模为$N$的数据库，FAQ-AI可在$O\left( {N}^{{\text{subw }}_{\ell }}\right.$乘以polylog$\left. ## 4 从交运算到等值运算

This forward reduction is used to give an upper bound on the time complexity for the former problem using the complexity of the latter problem. Section 5 then presents a backward reduction to give a corresponding lower bound on the time complexity of the IJ evaluation problem (recall Figure 1).

### 4.1 Rewriting the Intersection Predicate

### 4.1 重写交谓词

At the core of IJ evaluation lies the non-emptiness check of the intersection of $k$ intervals ${x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k} \in  \mathcal{I} : \left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{x}_{i}}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$ . We call this check the intersection predicate. In this section, we show how to rewrite this predicate into an equivalent form that uses the canonical partitions of the intervals in a segment tree ${\mathfrak{T}}_{\mathcal{I}}$ .

IJ求值的核心在于检查$k$个区间${x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k} \in  \mathcal{I} : \left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{x}_{i}}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$的交集非空性，该检查称为交谓词。本节展示如何将该谓词重写为利用线段树${\mathfrak{T}}_{\mathcal{I}}$中区间规范划分的等价形式。

Since the elementary segments that correspond to the leaves of ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ form a partition of $\mathbb{R}$ ,for any point $p \in  \mathbb{R}$ there is precisely one leaf node leaf(p)such that $p \in  \operatorname{seg}\left( {\operatorname{leaf}\left( p\right) }\right)$ . By Property 3.2(1), $\operatorname{anc}\left( {\operatorname{leaf}\left( p\right) }\right)  = \left\{  {v \in  V\left( {\mathfrak{T}}_{I}\right)  \mid  p \in  \operatorname{seg}\left( v\right) }\right\}$ . That is,the nodes whose segments contain the point $p$ are precisely the ancestors of $\operatorname{leaf}\left( p\right)$ .

由于对应${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$叶节点的基本线段构成$\mathbb{R}$的划分，对于任意点$p \in  \mathbb{R}$，存在唯一叶节点leaf(p)使得$p \in  \operatorname{seg}\left( {\operatorname{leaf}\left( p\right) }\right)$。根据性质3.2(1)，$\operatorname{anc}\left( {\operatorname{leaf}\left( p\right) }\right)  = \left\{  {v \in  V\left( {\mathfrak{T}}_{I}\right)  \mid  p \in  \operatorname{seg}\left( v\right) }\right\}$。即包含点$p$的线段节点恰好是$\operatorname{leaf}\left( p\right)$的祖先节点。

LEMMA 4.1 (INTERSECTION PREDICATE REWRITING 1). For any set of intervals $S = \left\{  {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathcal{I}$ ,the predicate $\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{x}_{i}}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$ is equivalent to:

引理4.1（交集谓词重写1）。对于任意区间集合$S = \left\{  {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathcal{I}$，谓词$\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{x}_{i}}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$等价于：

$$
\mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{\left( {{v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{k}}\right)  \in  \operatorname{anc}{\left( \operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right) \right) }^{k}}}\left( {\mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{\substack{{j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  } \\  {j \neq  i} }}{v}_{j} \in  C{P}_{\bar{I}}\left( {x}_{j}\right) }\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

Lemma 4.1 states the following. The intervals in $S$ intersect if and only if there is an interval ${x}_{i} \in  S$ such that the canonical partitions of each other interval in $S$ contain an ancestor of $\operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$ . By construction,this leaf contains the left endpoint of ${x}_{i}$ .

引理4.1阐述如下：当且仅当存在一个区间${x}_{i} \in  S$，使得$S$中其他区间的规范划分都包含$\operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$的祖先时，$S$中的区间才会相交。根据构造，该叶节点包含${x}_{i}$的左端点。

Property 4.2. Consider a set of intervals $S = \left\{  {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathcal{I}$ and a segment tree ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ . For any ${x}_{i} \in  S$ ,there can be at most one tuple of nodes ${v}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {\operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right) }\right)$ for $j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  ,j \neq  i$ that satisfy the conjunction of Lemma 4.1.

性质4.2。给定区间集合$S = \left\{  {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathcal{I}$与线段树${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$。对于任意${x}_{i} \in  S$，最多只存在一个满足引理4.1合取条件的节点元组${v}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {\operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right) }\right)$对应于$j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  ,j \neq  i$。

The conjunction in Lemma 4.1 can be satisfied by several $i$ -values when there are several intervals in $S$ that have the same left endpoint. The database can be transformed such that any two intervals from different relations have distinct left endpoints without affecting query evaluation [14]. If the intervals in $S$ have distinct left endpoints, then the conjunction in Lemma 4.1 can be satisfied by at most one $i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ ,namely the one with the maximum left endpoint of the intervals in $S$ ; this is also the left endpoint of the interval representing the intersection of all intervals in $S$ .

当$S$中存在多个左端点相同的区间时，引理4.1的合取条件可能被多个$i$值满足。通过数据库转换可使不同关系的区间具有相异左端点而不影响查询评估[14]。若$S$中各区间左端点唯一，则引理4.1的合取条件至多被一个$i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$满足——即$S$中具有最大左端点的区间；该端点同时也是$S$所有区间交集表示区间的左端点。

Consider a path from the root of the segment tree ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ down to $\operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$ from Lemma 4.1. The nodes ${v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{k}$ from Lemma 4.1 all lie on this path. These nodes satisfy ${v}_{j} \in  {\operatorname{CP}}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {x}_{j}\right)$ for all $j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack   - \{ i\}$ . WLOG let ${v}_{i} = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$ . Let ${u}_{1},\ldots ,{u}_{k}$ be a permutation of ${v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{k}$ listing them in order along the path where ${u}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {u}_{j + 1}\right)$ for all $j \in$ $\left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$ and ${u}_{k} = {v}_{i} = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$ . Let ${\sigma }_{1},\ldots ,{\sigma }_{k}$ be the corresponding permutation of the line segments ${x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}$ where ${u}_{j} \in  {\mathrm{{CP}}}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {\sigma }_{j}\right)$ for all $j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$ and ${\sigma }_{k} = {x}_{i}$ . Such a permutation always exists. Hence it is possible to reformulate Lemma 4.1 where on the righthand side we consider (a disjunction over) all such permutations ${\sigma }_{1},\ldots ,{\sigma }_{k}$ (subsuming the disjunction over $i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ ) and in return we get to assume that the inner disjunction is now over ordered $\left( {{u}_{1},\ldots ,{u}_{k}}\right)  \in  \operatorname{anc}{\left( \operatorname{leaf}\left( {\sigma }_{k}\right) \right) }^{k}$ ,meaning that ${u}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {u}_{j + 1}\right)$ for all $j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$ and ${u}_{k} = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {\sigma }_{k}\right)$ . This leads to the following variant of Lemma 4.1, which will be easier to utilize later in our reduction.

考虑从线段树根节点出发的一条路径${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$向下延伸至$\operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$（引理4.1所述）。引理4.1中的节点${v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{k}$均位于该路径上。这些节点满足${v}_{j} \in  {\operatorname{CP}}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {x}_{j}\right)$对所有$j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack   - \{ i\}$成立。不失一般性，设${v}_{i} = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$。令${u}_{1},\ldots ,{u}_{k}$为${v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{k}$沿路径顺序排列的置换，其中${u}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {u}_{j + 1}\right)$对所有$j \in$$\left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$及${u}_{k} = {v}_{i} = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$成立。令${\sigma }_{1},\ldots ,{\sigma }_{k}$为对应线段${x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}$的置换，满足${u}_{j} \in  {\mathrm{{CP}}}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {\sigma }_{j}\right)$对所有$j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$和${\sigma }_{k} = {x}_{i}$成立。此类置换必然存在。因此可将引理4.1重新表述为：在等式右侧我们考虑所有此类置换${\sigma }_{1},\ldots ,{\sigma }_{k}$（涵盖$i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$的析取），作为交换条件，我们可以假设内部析取现在作用于有序的$\left( {{u}_{1},\ldots ,{u}_{k}}\right)  \in  \operatorname{anc}{\left( \operatorname{leaf}\left( {\sigma }_{k}\right) \right) }^{k}$，即${u}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {u}_{j + 1}\right)$对所有$j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$和${u}_{k} = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {\sigma }_{k}\right)$成立。由此得到引理4.1的以下变体，这将更便于后续归约中使用。

LEMMA 4.3 (INTERSECTION PREDICATE REWRITING 2). For any set of intervals $S = \left\{  {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathcal{I}$ ,the predicate $\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{x}_{i}}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$ is equivalent to:

引理4.3（交集谓词重写2）。对于任意区间集$S = \left\{  {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathcal{I}$，谓词$\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{x}_{i}}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$等价于：

$$
\begin{matrix} \bigvee \bigvee \bigvee \\  \sigma  \in  \pi \left( S\right) \left( {{u}_{1},\ldots ,{u}_{k}}\right)  \in  \operatorname{anc}{\left( \operatorname{leaf}\left( {\sigma }_{k}\right) \right) }^{k}\left( {\mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack  }}{u}_{j} \in  C{P}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {\sigma }_{j}\right) }\right) \\  \forall j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack   : {u}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {u}_{j + 1}\right) \\  {u}_{k} = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {\sigma }_{k}\right)  \end{matrix}
$$

By Property 4.2,for a permutation $\sigma  \in  \pi \left( S\right)$ ,there can be at most one tuple ${\left( {u}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack  }$ that satisfies the conjunction. However, even if the intervals in $S$ have distinct left endpoints,the predicate of Lemma 4.3 may be satisfied by multiple permutations. To see this,suppose that $\sigma$ and ${\left( {u}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack  }$ satisfy the predicate. If there is $j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$ such that ${u}_{j} = {u}_{j + 1}$ ,then the permutation ${\sigma }^{\prime }$ ,obtained by swapping ${\sigma }_{j}$ and ${\sigma }_{j + 1}$ in $\sigma$ ,together with the tuple ${\left( {u}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack  }$ , also satisfy the predicate. It is possible to further restrict the permutations such that each tuple of segment tree nodes that satisfies the conjunction corresponds to exactly one permutation [14]. The equivalence in Lemma 4.3 can be alternatively expressed using the bitstrings of the nodes in the segment tree. By Property 3.2 (1) the expression ${u}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {u}_{j + 1}\right)$ can be equivalently stated as ${u}_{j}$ being a prefix of ${u}_{j + 1}$ . In other words,there exists a tuple of bitstrings $\left( {{b}_{1},\ldots ,{b}_{k}}\right)$ such that ${u}_{j} = {b}_{1} \circ  \cdots  \circ  {b}_{j}$ for $j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ . This observation leads to the following rewrite of Lemma 4.3.

根据性质4.2，对于排列$\sigma  \in  \pi \left( S\right)$，最多只能有一个元组${\left( {u}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack  }$满足该合取式。然而，即使$S$中的区间具有不同的左端点，引理4.3的谓词仍可能被多个排列满足。举例说明，假设$\sigma$和${\left( {u}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack  }$满足该谓词。若存在$j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$使得${u}_{j} = {u}_{j + 1}$，则通过交换$\sigma$中的${\sigma }_{j}$和${\sigma }_{j + 1}$得到的排列${\sigma }^{\prime }$与元组${\left( {u}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack  }$同样满足该谓词。可以进一步限制排列，使得每个满足合取式的线段树节点元组恰好对应唯一排列[14]。引理4.3的等价性亦可借助线段树节点的位串表示：根据性质3.2(1)，表达式${u}_{j} \in  \operatorname{anc}\left( {u}_{j + 1}\right)$等价于${u}_{j}$是${u}_{j + 1}$的前缀。换言之，存在位串元组$\left( {{b}_{1},\ldots ,{b}_{k}}\right)$使得对于$j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$有${u}_{j} = {b}_{1} \circ  \cdots  \circ  {b}_{j}$。这一观察引出了引理4.3的如下重写形式。

LEMMA 4.4 (INTERSECTION PREDICATE REWRITING 3). Consider a set of intervals $S = \left\{  {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right\}   \subseteq  I$ . The predicate $\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{x \in  S}}x}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$ is true if and only if there exists a permutation $\sigma  \in  \pi \left( S\right)$ and a tuple of bitstrings $\left( {{b}_{1},\ldots ,{b}_{k}}\right)$ such that:

引理4.4（交集谓词重写3）。给定区间集合$S = \left\{  {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right\}   \subseteq  I$，谓词$\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{x \in  S}}x}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$为真当且仅当存在排列$\sigma  \in  \pi \left( S\right)$和位串元组$\left( {{b}_{1},\ldots ,{b}_{k}}\right)$满足：

- $\left( {{b}_{1} \circ  \cdots  \circ  {b}_{j}}\right)  \in  C{P}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {\sigma }_{j}\right)$ for $j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$ ,and

- 对于$j \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$有$\left( {{b}_{1} \circ  \cdots  \circ  {b}_{j}}\right)  \in  C{P}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {\sigma }_{j}\right)$，且

- $\left( {{b}_{1} \circ  \cdots  \circ  {b}_{j}}\right)  = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {\sigma }_{j}\right)$ for $j = k$ .

- 对于$j = k$有$\left( {{b}_{1} \circ  \cdots  \circ  {b}_{j}}\right)  = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {\sigma }_{j}\right)$。

Section 4.2 lifts the rewriting in Lemma 4.4 to the level of queries.

第4.2节将引理4.4的重写提升至查询层面。

### 4.2 One-Step Forward Reduction

### 4.2 单步前向归约

For an IJ query $Q$ with hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ and a database $\mathbf{D}$ , the forward reduction proceeds iteratively on $Q$ and $\mathbf{D}$ and resolves one join interval variable at a time. Let this variable be $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ . The reduction yields a new query ${Q}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ and a new database ${\mathbf{D}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ such that ${Q}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\left( {\mathbf{D}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$ is true if and only if $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ is true.

对于超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$和数据库$\mathbf{D}$的IJ查询$Q$，前向约简过程在$Q$和$\mathbf{D}$上迭代进行，每次解析一个连接区间变量。设该变量为$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$。约简后生成新查询${Q}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$和新数据库${\mathbf{D}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$，使得${Q}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\left( {\mathbf{D}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$为真当且仅当$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$为真。

The core computation needed to evaluate $Q$ over $\mathbf{D}$ is the intersection predicate $\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{x \in  S}}x}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$ ,where $S$ consists of one input interval per relation involved in the intersection join on $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ . Lemma 4.4 explains how to express this computation for any subset $S$ of an input set of intervals $I$ for $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ using the segment tree ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ for $I$ .

评估$\mathbf{D}$上$Q$所需的核心计算是交集谓词$\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{x \in  S}}x}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$，其中$S$包含$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$上参与交集连接的每个关系对应的一个输入区间。引理4.4阐述了如何利用$I$的线段树${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$，对$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$的输入区间集$I$的任何子集$S$表达该计算。

Let $k = \left| {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right|$ be the number of hyperedges in $\mathcal{H}$ containing $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ . The reduction maps $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ to fresh point variables ${X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{k}$ that range over the possible bitstrings of the segment tree nodes from the canonical partitions of the intervals of $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ .

设$k = \left| {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right|$为包含$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$的$\mathcal{H}$中超边的数量。该归约将$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$映射至新点变量${X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{k}$，这些变量取值于$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$区间规范划分生成的线段树节点可能比特串。

Given a permutation $\sigma  = \left( {{\sigma }_{1},\ldots ,{\sigma }_{k}}\right)  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$ of the hy-peredges ${\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ containing $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ ,each hyperedge ${\sigma }_{i}$ induces a fresh hyperedge ${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}$ that has the fresh point variables ${X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{i}$ in place of the original interval variable $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack   : {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i} = {\sigma }_{i} \smallsetminus  \{ \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \}  \cup  \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{i}}\right\}$ .

给定包含$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$的超边${\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$的排列$\sigma  = \left( {{\sigma }_{1},\ldots ,{\sigma }_{k}}\right)  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$，每条超边${\sigma }_{i}$会生成新超边${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}$，其中用新点变量${X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{i}$替代原区间变量$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack   : {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i} = {\sigma }_{i} \smallsetminus  \{ \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \}  \cup  \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{i}}\right\}$。

Definition 4.5 (One-step Hypergraph Transformation). Given a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ ,an interval variable $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ ,and any permutation $\sigma  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$ ,the hypergraph ${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ has the set ${\mathcal{V}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ of vertices and the set ${\mathcal{E}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ of hyperedges,where ${\mathcal{V}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  } =$ $\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  \{ \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \}  \cup  \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{k}}\right\}$ and ${\mathcal{E}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) } = \mathcal{E} \smallsetminus  \left\{  {{\sigma }_{i} \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }\right\}   \cup  \left\{  {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right.  \mid$ $i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  \}$ . The set ${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  } = \left\{  {{\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) } \mid  \sigma  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right) }\right\}$ consists of all hypergraphs created from $\mathcal{H}$ by resolving the interval variable $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ .

定义4.5（单步超图转换）。给定超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$、区间变量$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$及任意排列$\sigma  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$，超图${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$具有顶点集${\mathcal{V}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$和超边集${\mathcal{E}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$，其中${\mathcal{V}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  } =$$\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  \{ \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \}  \cup  \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{k}}\right\}$且${\mathcal{E}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) } = \mathcal{E} \smallsetminus  \left\{  {{\sigma }_{i} \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }\right\}   \cup  \left\{  {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right.  \mid$$i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  \}$。集合${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  } = \left\{  {{\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) } \mid  \sigma  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right) }\right\}$包含通过解析区间变量$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$从$\mathcal{H}$生成的所有超图。

For a given permutation $\sigma$ ,there is a one-to-one correspondence between the hyperedges in $\mathcal{H}$ and those in ${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ . We obtain as many new hypergraphs as the number of permutations of ${\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ .

对于给定的排列$\sigma$，$\mathcal{H}$中的超边与${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$中的超边存在一一对应关系。我们获得的新超图数量等于${\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$排列的总数。

EXAMPLE 4.6. Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a hypergraph with vertices $\{ \left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  \}$ and edges ${e}_{1} = {e}_{2} = \{ \left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  \}$ and ${e}_{3} = \{ \left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  \}$ .

示例4.6 设$\mathcal{H}$是一个具有顶点集$\{ \left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  \}$和边集${e}_{1} = {e}_{2} = \{ \left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  \}$、${e}_{3} = \{ \left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  \}$的超图。

We reduce $\mathcal{H}$ by resolving the interval variable $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ . Since $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ occurs in three edges,we create three point variables ${A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3}$ and consider six permutations. The new edges created for the permutation $\sigma  = \left( {{e}_{1},{e}_{2},{e}_{3}}\right)$ are: ${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{1} = \left\{  {{A}_{1},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right\}  ,{\widetilde{\sigma }}_{2} = \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right\}$ , and ${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{3} = \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3}}\right\}$ . For the permutation $\left( {{e}_{3},{e}_{2},{e}_{1}}\right)$ ,the new edges are: $\left\{  {A}_{1}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right\}$ ,and $\left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right\}$ .

我们通过解析区间变量$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$来简化$\mathcal{H}$。由于$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$出现在三条边中，因此创建三个点变量${A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3}$并考虑六种排列。针对排列$\sigma  = \left( {{e}_{1},{e}_{2},{e}_{3}}\right)$生成的新边为：${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{1} = \left\{  {{A}_{1},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right\}  ,{\widetilde{\sigma }}_{2} = \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right\}$和${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{3} = \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3}}\right\}$；对于排列$\left( {{e}_{3},{e}_{2},{e}_{1}}\right)$，新边为：$\left\{  {A}_{1}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right\}$和$\left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right\}$。

Each hypergraph ${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ defines a new query ${Q}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ and the corresponding database ${\mathbf{D}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ . We next explain how to construct the new query and the new database.

每个超图${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$定义一个新查询${Q}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$及对应数据库${\mathbf{D}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$。接下来我们将阐述如何构建新查询与新数据库。

The query $Q$ is rewritten according to the new hypergraphs: For each permutation $\sigma$ ,we create an EIJ query ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ ,which has equality joins and possibly remaining intersection joins, whose hypergraph is ${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ . By taking all permutations,we thus create a query ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ that is a disjunction of EIJ queries such that each such query has one join interval variable less,namely $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ . Note that $Q$ need not be an IJ query: It may have both intersection and equality joins, for instance if it is the result of a previous rewriting step.

查询$Q$根据新超图进行重写：对于每个排列$\sigma$，我们创建一个EIJ查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$，该查询包含等值连接和可能的剩余交集连接，其超图为${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$。通过遍历所有排列，我们最终生成一个由EIJ查询组成的析取式查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$，其中每个此类查询减少一个连接区间变量，即$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$。注意$Q$不必是IJ查询：它可能同时包含交集连接和等值连接，例如作为前次重写步骤的结果。

Definition 4.7 (One-Step Query Rewriting). For any ElJ query $Q$ with hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ ,the EIJ query ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ with hyper-graph ${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) } = \left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  },{\mathcal{E}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }}\right)$ is defined by:

定义4.7（单步查询重写）。对于任何具有超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$的ElJ查询$Q$，带超图${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) } = \left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  },{\mathcal{E}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }}\right)$的EIJ查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$定义为：

$$
{\widetilde{Q}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{\widetilde{e} \in  {\widetilde{\mathcal{E}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }}}\widetilde{R}\left( \widetilde{e}\right) 
$$

The query ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ is the disjunction of the EIJ queries ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ over all possible permutations $\sigma  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$ :

查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$是所有可能排列$\sigma  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$上EIJ查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$的析取式：

$$
{\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{{\sigma  \in  \pi \left( {\mathcal{E}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right) }}{\widetilde{Q}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }.
$$

EXAMPLE 4.8. The IJ query $Q = R\left( {\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  S\left( {\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)$ $\land  T\left( \left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  \right)$ has the hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ in Example 4.6. The permutation $\left( {{e}_{1},{e}_{2},{e}_{3}}\right)$ yields ${\widetilde{Q}}_{1} = \widetilde{R}\left( {{A}_{1},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) ,\widetilde{S}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) ,\widetilde{T}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2}}\right.$ , $\left. {A}_{3}\right)$ . The permutation $\left( {{e}_{3},{e}_{2},{e}_{1}}\right)$ yields ${\widetilde{Q}}_{2} = \widetilde{R}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)$ , $\widetilde{S}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) ,\widetilde{T}\left( {A}_{1}\right)$ . The final query ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }$ is a disjunction of six EIJ queries,including ${\widetilde{Q}}_{1}$ and ${\widetilde{Q}}_{2}$ .

示例4.8：IJ查询$Q = R\left( {\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  S\left( {\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)$$\land  T\left( \left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  \right)$具有示例4.6中的超图$\mathcal{H}$。排列$\left( {{e}_{1},{e}_{2},{e}_{3}}\right)$生成${\widetilde{Q}}_{1} = \widetilde{R}\left( {{A}_{1},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) ,\widetilde{S}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) ,\widetilde{T}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2}}\right.$、$\left. {A}_{3}\right)$；排列$\left( {{e}_{3},{e}_{2},{e}_{1}}\right)$生成${\widetilde{Q}}_{2} = \widetilde{R}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)$、$\widetilde{S}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) ,\widetilde{T}\left( {A}_{1}\right)$。最终查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }$是六个EIJ查询的析取，包括${\widetilde{Q}}_{1}$和${\widetilde{Q}}_{2}$。

For each new hyperedge ${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}$ in ${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ ,there is a new relation ${\widetilde{R}}_{{\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}}\left( {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right)$ . To avoid clutter,we denote it by $\widetilde{R}\left( {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right)$ ; its schema ${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}$ uniquely identifies the transformation of the original relation $R$ .

对于${\widetilde{\mathcal{H}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$中的每个新超边${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}$，都存在一个新关系${\widetilde{R}}_{{\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}}\left( {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right)$。为避免混乱，我们将其记为$\widetilde{R}\left( {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right)$；其模式${\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}$唯一标识了原始关系$R$的转换结果。

Definition 4.9 (One-Step Database Transformation). The database ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ is constructed from the database $\mathbf{D}$ as follows. For each tuple $t \in  R\left( {\sigma }_{i}\right)$ ,we construct tuples $\widetilde{t} \in  \widetilde{R}\left( {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right)$ such that:

定义4.9（单步数据库转换）：数据库${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$按以下方式从数据库$\mathbf{D}$构建：对于每个元组$t \in  R\left( {\sigma }_{i}\right)$，我们构造满足以下条件的元组$\widetilde{t} \in  \widetilde{R}\left( {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right)$：

- $t\left( {{\sigma }_{i}\smallsetminus \{ \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \} }\right)  = \widetilde{t}\left( {\widetilde{{\sigma }_{i}} \smallsetminus  \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{i}}\right\}  }\right)$

- $\left( {\widetilde{t}\left( {X}_{1}\right)  \circ  \cdots  \circ  \widetilde{t}\left( {X}_{i}\right) }\right)  \in  C{P}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {t\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \right) }\right)$ if $i \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$

- 当$i \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$成立时$\left( {\widetilde{t}\left( {X}_{1}\right)  \circ  \cdots  \circ  \widetilde{t}\left( {X}_{i}\right) }\right)  \in  C{P}_{\mathcal{I}}\left( {t\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \right) }\right)$

- $\left( {\widetilde{t}\left( {X}_{1}\right)  \circ  \cdots  \circ  \widetilde{t}\left( {X}_{i}\right) }\right)  = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {t\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \right) }\right)$ if $i = k$

- $\left( {\widetilde{t}\left( {X}_{1}\right)  \circ  \cdots  \circ  \widetilde{t}\left( {X}_{i}\right) }\right)  = \operatorname{leaf}\left( {t\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \right) }\right)$ 如果 $i = k$

The relations whose schemas do not contain $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ are copied from $\mathbf{D}$ to ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ . The new database ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ is the set of all relations in the databases ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ .

模式中不包含$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$的关系从$\mathbf{D}$复制到${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$。新数据库${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$是数据库${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$中所有关系的集合。

The number of tuples $\widetilde{t}$ constructed for a tuple $t$ in Definition 4.9 depends on the size of the canonical partition ${\mathrm{{CP}}}_{I}\left( {t\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \right) }\right)$ of $t\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \right)$ and on the number of ways we can partition the bitstring of a node in the canonical partition into $i$ substrings. Overall,this number is poly-logarithmic in the number of input intervals $\mathcal{I}$ . This is made more precise in the next lemma.

根据定义4.9，为元组$t$构建的元组数量$\widetilde{t}$取决于$t\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \right)$的规范分区${\mathrm{{CP}}}_{I}\left( {t\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \right) }\right)$的大小，以及将规范分区中节点的比特串划分为$i$个子串的方式数量。总体而言，该数量相对于输入区间$\mathcal{I}$的数量呈多对数关系。这一点将在下一条引理中更精确地阐述。

LEMMA 4.10. Each new relation $\widetilde{R}\left( {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right)$ in database ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$ constructed from the database $\mathbf{D}$ following Definition 4.9 has the size: $O\left( {\left| {R\left( {\sigma }_{i}\right) }\right|  \cdot  {\log }^{i}\left| \mathcal{I}\right| }\right)$ if $i \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$ ; and $O\left( {\left| {R\left( {\sigma }_{i}\right) }\right|  \cdot  {\log }^{i - 1}\left| \mathcal{I}\right| }\right)$ if $i = k$ . It can be constructed in time proportional to its size.

引理4.10. 依据定义4.9从数据库$\mathbf{D}$构建的新数据库${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left( \left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\sigma \right) }$中，每个新关系$\widetilde{R}\left( {\widetilde{\sigma }}_{i}\right)$的规模为：若$i \in  \left\lbrack  {k - 1}\right\rbrack$，则$O\left( {\left| {R\left( {\sigma }_{i}\right) }\right|  \cdot  {\log }^{i}\left| \mathcal{I}\right| }\right)$；若$i = k$，则$O\left( {\left| {R\left( {\sigma }_{i}\right) }\right|  \cdot  {\log }^{i - 1}\left| \mathcal{I}\right| }\right)$。其构建时间与其规模成正比。

The transformations in Definitions 4.9 and 4.7 preserve the equivalence to the original evaluation problem: The result of $Q$ over $\mathbf{D}$ is the same as the result of ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ over ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ .

定义4.9和4.7中的转换保持了与原评估问题的等价性：在$\mathbf{D}$上执行$Q$的结果与在${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$上执行${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$的结果相同。

LEMMA 4.11. Given any ElJ query $Q$ ,interval variable $\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$ in $Q$ , and any database $\mathbf{D}$ ,let the EIJ query ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ and database ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$ be constructed as per Definitions 4.9 and 4.7. Then, $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ is true if and only if ${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$ is true.

引理4.11. 给定任意ElJ查询$Q$、$Q$中的区间变量$\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack$及任意数据库$\mathbf{D}$，若按定义4.9和4.7构建EIJ查询${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$和数据库${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }$，则当且仅当${\widetilde{Q}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  }\right)$为真时，$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$为真。

EXAMPLE 4.12. We demonstrate the database transformation for the query ${Q}_{\Delta }$ in Section 1.1. Consider the interval variable $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ . We convert ${Q}_{\Delta }$ into the disjunction of two queries with two new point variables ${A}_{1}$ and ${A}_{2}$ in lieu of the interval variable $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ :

示例4.12. 我们将演示第1.1节中查询${Q}_{\Delta }$的数据库转换过程。考虑区间变量$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$，将${Q}_{\Delta }$转换为由两个新点变量${A}_{1}$和${A}_{2}$替代区间变量$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$的两个查询析取式：

$$
{\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime } = {\widetilde{R}}_{1}\left( {{A}_{1},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  S\left( {\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  {\widetilde{T}}_{1}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) ,
$$

$$
{\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime \prime } = {\widetilde{R}}_{2}\left( {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  S\left( {\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right)  \land  {\widetilde{T}}_{2}\left( {{A}_{1},\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack  }\right) .
$$

Let $\mathbf{D}$ be the input database with relations $R,S$ and $T$ and let $N$ be the size of $\mathbf{D}$ . By Definition 4.9,we construct a new database instance ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }$ that consists of $S$ and four new relations ${\widetilde{R}}_{1},{\widetilde{T}}_{1},{\widetilde{R}}_{2}$ and ${\widetilde{T}}_{2}$ . By Lemma 4.11, ${Q}_{\Delta }\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ is true if and only if ${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$ or ${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime \prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$ are true.

设$\mathbf{D}$为包含关系$R,S$和$T$的输入数据库，$N$表示$\mathbf{D}$的规模。根据定义4.9，我们构建由$S$及四个新关系${\widetilde{R}}_{1},{\widetilde{T}}_{1},{\widetilde{R}}_{2}$、${\widetilde{T}}_{2}$组成的新数据库实例${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }$。根据引理4.11，当且仅当${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$或${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime \prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$为真时，${Q}_{\Delta }\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$成立。

Let $I$ be the set of all $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ -intervals in $R$ and $T$ . We have $\left| I\right|  \leq  2 \cdot  N$ . Let $\mathfrak{E}$ be the set of endpoints of these intervals. We have $\left| \mathfrak{E}\right|  \leq  2 \cdot  \left| I\right|$ . Assume that $\mathfrak{E} = \{ 0,1,2,\ldots ,k\}$ for some integer $k \leq  2 \cdot  N$ . This is without loss of generality since the intersection problem does not depend on the absolute values of the end points but only on their relative positioning. Assume also that $k$ is a power of 2 ; otherwise, replace $k$ with the smallest power of 2 that is $\geq  k$ . Let ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ be the segment tree whose root corresponds to the interval $\lbrack 0,k)$ ,and the left and right children of each node correspond to the left and right halves of the corresponding interval respectively. Assume without loss of generality that each interval $i \in  \mathcal{I}$ corresponds to a node in ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ ; otherwise, $i$ can be broken down into $O\left( {{\log }_{2}\left| k\right| }\right)  = O\left( {{\log }_{2}N}\right)$ intervals that correspond to nodes in ${\mathfrak{T}}_{\mathcal{I}}$ (Property 3.2 (3)). Each node $n$ in ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ can be encoded as a binary string,cf. Section 3: If $n$ corresponds to the string $b$ ,then its left and right children correspond to the strings " ${b0}$ " and " ${b1}$ " respectively. For an interval $i$ that corresponds to $n$ in ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ ,let $\operatorname{bin}\left( i\right)$ denote the binary string that encodes node $n$ . Let ${i}_{1}$ and ${i}_{2}$ be two intervals that correspond to the nodes ${n}_{1}$ and ${n}_{2}$ . They intersect if and only if one of the two nodes is an ancestor of the other (or the nodes are the same). That is, one of them contains the other (Property 3.3, part (1)). Equivalently, they intersect if and only if one of the two binary strings $\operatorname{bin}\left( {i}_{1}\right)$ and $\operatorname{bin}\left( {i}_{2}\right)$ is a prefix of the other (Property 3.2 (1)).

设$I$为$R$和$T$中所有$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$区间的集合，满足$\left| I\right|  \leq  2 \cdot  N$。令$\mathfrak{E}$表示这些区间的端点集，且$\left| \mathfrak{E}\right|  \leq  2 \cdot  \left| I\right|$。假设对于某个整数$k \leq  2 \cdot  N$有$\mathfrak{E} = \{ 0,1,2,\ldots ,k\}$——该假设不失一般性，因为交集问题不依赖于端点的绝对值，仅与其相对位置有关。同时假设$k$是2的幂次；否则将$k$替换为不小于$\geq  k$的最小2的幂次。设${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$是以区间$\lbrack 0,k)$为根节点的线段树，其中每个节点的左右子节点分别对应其代表区间的左右半区间。在不失一般性的前提下，假设每个区间$i \in  \mathcal{I}$都对应${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$中的某个节点；否则$i$可分解为$O\left( {{\log }_{2}\left| k\right| }\right)  = O\left( {{\log }_{2}N}\right)$个对应于${\mathfrak{T}}_{\mathcal{I}}$中节点的区间（性质3.2(3)）。根据第3节所述，${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$中的每个节点$n$可编码为二进制字符串：若$n$对应字符串$b$，则其左右子节点分别对应字符串"${b0}$"和"${b1}$"。对于${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$中对应$n$的区间$i$，用$\operatorname{bin}\left( i\right)$表示节点$n$的二进制编码字符串。设${i}_{1}$和${i}_{2}$为分别对应节点${n}_{1}$和${n}_{2}$的两个区间，当且仅当其中一个节点是另一个的祖先节点（或两节点相同）时，这两个区间相交。换言之，当且仅当两个二进制字符串$\operatorname{bin}\left( {i}_{1}\right)$和$\operatorname{bin}\left( {i}_{2}\right)$中有一个是另一个的前缀时，它们相交（性质3.2(1)）。

Define the new relations as follows (Definition 4.9):

按如下方式定义新关系（定义4.9）：

$$
{\widetilde{R}}_{1} = \{ \left( {\operatorname{bin}\left( \left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  \right) ,\left\lbrack  b\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  \left( {\left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  b\right\rbrack  }\right)  \in  R\} 
$$

$$
{\widetilde{T}}_{2} = \{ \left( {\operatorname{bin}\left( \left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  \right) ,\left\lbrack  c\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  \left( {\left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  c\right\rbrack  }\right)  \in  T\} 
$$

$$
{\widetilde{T}}_{1} = \left\{  {\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2},\left\lbrack  c\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  \left( {\left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  c\right\rbrack  }\right)  \in  T \land  {a}_{1} \circ  {a}_{2} = \operatorname{bin}\left( \left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  \right) }\right\}  
$$

$$
{\widetilde{R}}_{2} = \left\{  {\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2},\left\lbrack  b\right\rbrack  }\right)  \mid  \left( {\left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  ,\left\lbrack  b\right\rbrack  }\right)  \in  R \land  {a}_{1} \circ  {a}_{2} = \operatorname{bin}\left( \left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  \right) }\right\}  .
$$

By Lemma 4.10 and the assumption that each $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ -interval corresponds to exactly one node of ${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$ ,we have $\left| {\widetilde{R}}_{1}\right|  = \left| R\right| ,\left| {\widetilde{T}}_{2}\right|  = \left| T\right|$ , and both ${\widetilde{R}}_{1}$ and ${\widetilde{T}}_{2}$ can be constructed in linear time. Then, ${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$ holds if and only if $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ has a satisfying assignment where the $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ -interval from $R$ contains the $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ -interval from $T$ . Also, ${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime \prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$ holds if and only if $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ has a satisfying assignment where the $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ -interval from $R$ is contained in the $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ - interval from $T$ . 根据引理4.10及每个$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$区间对应${\mathfrak{T}}_{I}$中唯一节点的假设，可得$\left| {\widetilde{R}}_{1}\right|  = \left| R\right| ,\left| {\widetilde{T}}_{2}\right|  = \left| T\right|$，且${\widetilde{R}}_{1}$与${\widetilde{T}}_{2}$均可在线性时间内构建。此外，$\left| {\widetilde{T}}_{1}\right|  = O\left( {\left| T\right|  \cdot  \log N}\right)$因为存在$O\left( {\log N}\right)$种方式将长度为$O\left( {\log N}\right)$的二进制串$\operatorname{bin}\left( \left\lbrack  a\right\rbrack  \right)$一分为二。${\widetilde{T}}_{1}$可在$O\left( {\left| T\right|  \cdot  \log N}\right)$时间内构建。${\widetilde{R}}_{2}$同理（引理4.10）。当且仅当$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$存在满足条件的赋值使得$R$的$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$区间包含$T$的$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$区间时，${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$成立。同理，当且仅当$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$存在满足条件的赋值使得$R$的$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$区间被包含于$T$的$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$区间时，${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime \prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$成立。因此，$Q\left( D\right)$成立当且仅当${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$或${\widetilde{Q}}^{\prime \prime }\left( {\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack  }\right)$成立（引理4.11）。

## 5 FROM EQUALITIES TO INTERSECTIONS: REDUCTION OPTIMALITY

## 5 从等值关系到交集：归约最优性

In the previous section, we showed how to reduce the evaluation of an IJ query $Q$ over a database $\mathbf{D}$ of intervals to a disjunction $\widetilde{\mathbf{Q}}$ of EJ queries over a database $\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$ of numbers,where $\left| \widetilde{\mathbf{D}}\right|  = O(\left| \mathbf{D}\right|  \cdot$ polylog $\left| \mathbf{D}\right|$ ). We show that the runtime on $Q$ is also lower bounded by the maximum lower bound over all queries in $\widetilde{\mathbf{Q}}$ . We start with an EJ query $\widetilde{Q}$ ,whose query structure matches that of one of the queries in $\widetilde{\mathbf{Q}}$ ,and with an arbitrary database ${\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}}_{2}$ over the schema of $\widetilde{Q}$ . For any database $\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$ ,let $\Omega \left( {T\left( \left| \widetilde{\mathbf{D}}\right| \right) }\right)$ be a lower bound on the time complexity for computing $\widetilde{Q}$ ,where $T$ is a function of the size of the database $\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$ . There cannot be an algorithm ${\mathcal{A}}_{Q}$ that computes $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ in time $o\left( {T\left( \left| \mathbf{D}\right| \right) }\right)$ (i.e.,asymptotically strictly smaller),for any database $\mathbf{D}$ .

定理5.2 设$Q$为任意无自连接的IJ查询，其超图为$\mathcal{H}$；$\widetilde{Q}$为任意EJ查询，其超图属于$\tau \left( \mathcal{H}\right)$。对于任意数据库$\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$，令$\Omega \left( {T\left( \left| \widetilde{\mathbf{D}}\right| \right) }\right)$表示计算$\widetilde{Q}$的时间复杂度下界，其中$T$是数据库$\widetilde{\mathbf{D}}$规模的函数。不存在算法${\mathcal{A}}_{Q}$能在$o\left( {T\left( \left| \mathbf{D}\right| \right) }\right)$时间内（即渐进严格更小）计算$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$，该结论适用于任意数据库$\mathbf{D}$。

## 6 IOTA-ACYCLICITY

## 6 IOTA非循环性

In this section, we answer the following question: Which IJ queries can be computed in linear time (modulo a polylog factor)? To answer this question, we introduce a new notion of acyclicity, called $\iota$ -acylicity,which captures precisely the linear-time computable IJ queries. In other words, $\iota$ -acyclicity is for IJ queries what $\alpha$ - acyclicity is for EJ queries.

本节将回答：哪些IJ查询可在线性时间内（模多对数因子）完成计算？为此我们引入称为$\iota$非循环性的新概念，其精确刻画了线性时间可计算的IJ查询。换言之，$\iota$非循环性之于IJ查询，就如同$\alpha$非循环性之于EJ查询。

Definition 6.1 (IотА Acyclic Hypergraph). A hypergraph H is $\iota$ -acyclic if and only if each hypergraph in $\tau \left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ is $\alpha$ -acyclic.

定义6.1（IOTA非循环超图）。超图H是$\iota$非循环的，当且仅当$\tau \left( \mathcal{H}\right)$中的每个超图都是$\alpha$非循环的。

It is immediate to see why Definition 6.1 defines the hypergraphs of some linear-time computable IJ queries, namely those computed via our reduction. Theorem 6.6 later shows that Definition 6.1 defines in fact all linear-time computable IJ queries.

显然，定义6.1描述了部分线性时间可计算IJ查询的超图特性，即通过我们的归约方法可计算的查询。定理6.6后续将证明该定义实际上涵盖了所有线性时间可计算的IJ查询。

Since all hypergraphs in $\tau \left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ are $\alpha$ -acyclic,they correspond to EJ queries that can be computed in linear time [29]. Furthermore, the size of $\tau \left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ is independent of the input database and only depends on $\mathcal{H}$ . Definition 6.1 defines $\iota$ -acyclicity indirectly using our reduction. We next show that this is equivalent to a simple syntactic characterisation of the hypergraph of the given IJ query.

由于$\tau \left( \mathcal{H}\right)$中所有超图都是$\alpha$非循环的，它们对应着线性时间可计算的EJ查询[29]。此外，$\tau \left( \mathcal{H}\right)$的规模与输入数据库无关，仅取决于$\mathcal{H}$。定义6.1通过归约间接定义了$\iota$非循环性。接下来我们将证明这等价于给定IJ查询超图的简单语法特征。

Definition 6.2 (Berge Cycle [10]). A Berge cycle in $\mathcal{H}$ is a sequence $\left( {{e}^{1},{v}^{1},{e}^{2},{v}^{2},\ldots ,{e}^{n},{v}^{n},{e}^{n + 1}}\right)$ such that: ${v}^{1},\ldots ,{v}^{n}$ are distinct vertices in $\mathcal{V};{e}^{1},\ldots ,{e}^{n}$ are distinct hyperedges in $\mathcal{E}$ and ${e}^{n + 1} =$ ${e}^{1};n \geq  2$ ; and ${v}^{i}$ is in ${e}^{i}$ and ${e}^{i + 1}$ for each $1 \leq  i \leq  n$ .

定义6.2（Berge环[10]）。$\mathcal{H}$中的Berge环是指序列$\left( {{e}^{1},{v}^{1},{e}^{2},{v}^{2},\ldots ,{e}^{n},{v}^{n},{e}^{n + 1}}\right)$满足：${v}^{1},\ldots ,{v}^{n}$是$\mathcal{V};{e}^{1},\ldots ,{e}^{n}$中的不同顶点，$\mathcal{E}$是${e}^{n + 1} =$中的不同超边，且${e}^{1};n \geq  2$；同时${v}^{i}$位于${e}^{i}$和${e}^{i + 1}$中，对每个$1 \leq  i \leq  n$成立。

THEOREM 6.3 (IOTA ACYCLICITY CHARACTERISATION). A hyper-graph is $\iota$ -acyclic if and only if it has no Berge cycle of length strictly greater than two.

定理6.3（IOTA非循环性特征）。超图是$\iota$非循环的，当且仅当其不存在长度严格大于2的Berge环。

The smallest Berge cycle that makes a hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ not $\iota$ - acyclic has length three. This is a sequence $\left( {{e}^{1},{v}^{1},{e}^{2},{v}^{2},{e}^{3},{v}^{3},{e}^{4}}\right)$ , where ${v}^{1},{v}^{2},{v}^{3}$ are distinct vertices in the hypergraph, ${e}^{1},{e}^{2},{e}^{3}$ are distinct hyperedges in the hypergraph, ${e}^{4} = {e}^{1}$ ,and ${v}^{i} \in  {e}^{i} \cap  {e}^{i + 1}$ for $1 \leq  i \leq  3$ (Definition 6.2).

使超图$\mathcal{H}$不满足$\iota$-无环性的最小Berge环长度为三。该序列$\left( {{e}^{1},{v}^{1},{e}^{2},{v}^{2},{e}^{3},{v}^{3},{e}^{4}}\right)$中，${v}^{1},{v}^{2},{v}^{3}$是超图中的不同顶点，${e}^{1},{e}^{2},{e}^{3}$是超图中的不同超边，${e}^{4} = {e}^{1}$，且对于$1 \leq  i \leq  3$满足${v}^{i} \in  {e}^{i} \cap  {e}^{i + 1}$（定义6.2）。

As a corollary of Theorem 6.3, $\iota$ -acyclicity strictly sits between Berge-acyclicity and $\gamma$ -acyclicity $\left\lbrack  {7,{10}}\right\rbrack$ . A further corollary is that each $\iota$ -acyclic hypergraph is also $\alpha$ -acyclic.

作为定理6.3的推论，$\iota$-无环性严格介于Berge无环性与$\gamma$-无环性$\left\lbrack  {7,{10}}\right\rbrack$之间。进一步推论表明，每个$\iota$-无环超图同时也是$\alpha$-无环的。

COROLLARY 6.4. The class of $\iota$ -acyclic hypergraphs is a strict superset of the class of Berge-acyclic hypergraphs and it is a strict subset of the class of $\gamma$ -acyclic hypergraphs.

推论6.4 $\iota$-无环超图类是Berge无环超图类的严格超集，且是$\gamma$-无环超图类的严格子集。

IJ queries whose hypergraphs are not $\iota$ -acyclic cannot be computed in linear time (unless the 3SUM conjecture fails). This is shown by a reduction from the problem of computing the triangle EJ query, which takes super-linear time [3] unless the 3SUM conjecture fails [25]. The 3SUM problem asks,given a set $S$ of $n$ numbers, to find distinct $x,y,z \in  S$ such that $x + y = z$ . The problem can be solved in $O\left( {n}^{2}\right)$ time,and it is a long-standing conjecture that this quadratic complexity is essentially the best possible.

超图不具备$\iota$-无环性的IJ查询无法在线性时间内完成计算（除非3SUM猜想不成立）。这一结论通过将问题归约至计算三角形EJ查询得以证明，后者需要超线性时间[3]——除非3SUM猜想不成立[25]。3SUM问题指：给定包含$S$个<b2></b3>的集合，找出互异的$x,y,z \in  S$使得$x + y = z$。该问题可在$O\left( {n}^{2}\right)$时间内求解，而学界长期猜想这种二次复杂度本质上已是最优解。

THEOREM 6.6 (IOTA ACYCLICITY DICHOTOMY). Let $Q$ be any IJ query with hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ and let $\mathbf{D}$ be any database.

定理6.6（IOTA无环性二分法）。设$Q$为任意具有超图$\mathcal{H}$的IJ查询，$\mathbf{D}$为任意数据库。

If $\mathcal{H}$ is $\iota$ -acyclic,then $Q$ can be computed in time $O\left( {\left| \mathbf{D}\right|  \cdot  \operatorname{polylog}\left| \mathbf{D}\right| }\right)$ .

若$\mathcal{H}$满足$\iota$-无环性，则$Q$可在$O\left( {\left| \mathbf{D}\right|  \cdot  \operatorname{polylog}\left| \mathbf{D}\right| }\right)$时间内完成计算。

If $\mathcal{H}$ is not $\iota$ -acyclic,then there is no algorithm that can compute $Q$ in time $O\left( {\left| \mathbf{D}\right| }^{4/3 - \epsilon }\right)$ for $\epsilon  > 0$ ,unless the ${3SUM}$ conjecture fails.

若$\mathcal{H}$不满足$\iota$-无环性，则不存在能在$O\left( {\left| \mathbf{D}\right| }^{4/3 - \epsilon }\right)$时间内（对于$\epsilon  > 0$）计算$Q$的算法——除非${3SUM}$猜想不成立。

## 7 CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK

## 7 结论与未来工作

This paper pinpoints the complexity of Boolean conjunctive queries with intersection joins and characterises syntactically the class of such queries that can be computed in linear time modulo a poly-logarithmic factor. Core to our approach is a reduction of the evaluation problem for such queries to Boolean queries with equality joins. This reduction is robust: It also works for non-Boolean queries with both intersection and equality joins.

本文精确刻画了采用交集连接的布尔合取查询的复杂度，并通过语法特征界定了可在多对数因子近似的线性时间内计算的查询类别。我们方法的核心在于将此类查询的求值问题归约至等值连接的布尔查询。这种归约具有鲁棒性：它同样适用于同时包含交集连接和等值连接的非布尔查询。

A natural extension of this work is to refine the acyclicity notion in the presence of both intersection joins and equality joins. This notion necessarily lies between $\alpha$ -acyclicity and $\iota$ -acyclicity: It is the former when all joins are equality joins, as in the literature, and it is the latter when all joins are intersection joins, as in this paper. A further type of join that is naturally supported by the development in this paper is the membership join: This can be expressed by using a join variable to range over both intervals and points. Our reduction can be optimised to accommodate membership joins, in addition to intersection and equality joins. Characterising the linear-time computable Boolean queries with all three types of joins is an exciting venue of future research.

本工作的自然延伸是细化同时存在交集连接与等值连接时的无环性概念。该概念必然介于$\alpha$-无环性与$\iota$-无环性之间：当所有连接均为等值连接时即为前者（如文献所述），当所有连接均为交集连接时即为后者（如本文所示）。本文研究自然支持的另一种连接类型是成员资格连接：可通过使用同时覆盖区间和点的连接变量来表达。除交集连接和等值连接外，我们的归约方法可优化以支持成员资格连接。表征同时包含这三类连接的线性时间可计算布尔查询，是未来研究中令人振奋的方向。

## A 背景知识

Definition A. 1 (Inouced Set [7]). Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a family of sets. The induced set of $\mathcal{E}$ on $S \subseteq  \bigcup \mathcal{E}$ ,is the set $\mathcal{E}\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack   = \{ e \cap  S \mid  e \in  \mathcal{E}\}  \smallsetminus  \{ \varnothing \}$ .

定义A.1（诱导集[7]）。设$\mathcal{E}$为集合族，$\mathcal{E}$对$S \subseteq  \bigcup \mathcal{E}$的诱导集即集合$\mathcal{E}\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack   = \{ e \cap  S \mid  e \in  \mathcal{E}\}  \smallsetminus  \{ \varnothing \}$。

Definition A.2 (Minimisation of a Familiy of Sets [7]). Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a family of sets. The set $\mathcal{M}\left( \mathcal{E}\right)  = \{ e \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  \nexists f \in  \mathcal{E},e \subset  f\}$ is the minimization of $\mathcal{E}$ . That is,the subset of hyperedges that are maximal with respect to the inclusion order.

定义A.2（集合族极小化[7]）。设$\mathcal{E}$为集合族，集合$\mathcal{M}\left( \mathcal{E}\right)  = \{ e \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  \nexists f \in  \mathcal{E},e \subset  f\}$是$\mathcal{E}$的极小化。即关于包含序极大化的超边子集。

Definition A.3 (Conformal Hypergraph [7]). A hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ is conformal if there is no $S \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ ,with cardinality $\geq  3$ such that $\mathcal{M}\left( {\mathcal{E}\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack  }\right)  = \{ S \smallsetminus  \{ x\}  \mid  x \in  S\}$ .

定义A.3（共形超图[7]）：若超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$不存在基数$\geq  3$的$S \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$使得$\mathcal{M}\left( {\mathcal{E}\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack  }\right)  = \{ S \smallsetminus  \{ x\}  \mid  x \in  S\}$，则称其为共形超图。

Definition A.4 (Cycle-Free Hypergraph [7]). A hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ is cycle-free if there is no tuple $\left( {{v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{n}}\right)$ with $n \geq  3$ of pairwise distinct vertices such that $\mathcal{M}\left( {\mathcal{E}\left\lbrack  \left\{  {{v}^{i} \mid  1 \leq  i \leq  n}\right\}  \right\rbrack  }\right)  =$ $\left\{  {\left\{  {{v}^{i},{v}^{i + 1}}\right\}   \mid  1 \leq  i < n}\right\}   \cup  \left\{  \left\{  {{v}^{n},{v}^{1}}\right\}  \right\}  .$

定义A.4（无环超图[7]）：若超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$不存在由$n \geq  3$个两两相异顶点组成的元组$\left( {{v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{n}}\right)$满足$\mathcal{M}\left( {\mathcal{E}\left\lbrack  \left\{  {{v}^{i} \mid  1 \leq  i \leq  n}\right\}  \right\rbrack  }\right)  =$$\left\{  {\left\{  {{v}^{i},{v}^{i + 1}}\right\}   \mid  1 \leq  i < n}\right\}   \cup  \left\{  \left\{  {{v}^{n},{v}^{1}}\right\}  \right\}  .$，则称其为无环超图。

Definition A.5 (Alpha Acyclic Hypergraph [1,7,10]). Consider a hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ . The following statements are equivalent: (1) $\mathcal{H}$ is $\alpha$ -acyclic: (2) $\mathcal{H}$ is conformal and cycle-free; (3) $\mathcal{H}$ has a join tree; (4) $\mathcal{H}$ is GYO reducible to the empty hypergraph.

定义A.5（α-无环超图[1,7,10]）：对于超图$\mathcal{H}$，下列陈述等价：(1)$\mathcal{H}$是$\alpha$-无环的；(2)$\mathcal{H}$具有共形性且无环；(3)$\mathcal{H}$存在连接树；(4)$\mathcal{H}$可GYO归约为空超图。

We denote with $\operatorname{TD}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ the set of hypertree decompositions of $\mathcal{H}$ .

我们用$\operatorname{TD}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$表示$\mathcal{H}$的超树分解集合。

Definition A. 6 (Polymatroin [4]). Consider the vertex set $\mathcal{V}$ . A function $f : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ is a (non-negative) set function on $\mathcal{V}$ . A set function $f$ on $\mathcal{V}$ is: modular if $f\left( S\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{v \in  S}}f\left( {\{ v\} }\right)$ for all $S \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ ; monotone if $f\left( X\right)  \leq  f\left( Y\right)$ whenever $X \subseteq  Y$ ; and submodular if $f\left( {X \cup  Y}\right)  + f\left( {X \cap  Y}\right)  \leq  f\left( X\right)  + f\left( Y\right)$ for all $X,Y \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ .

定义A.6（多拟阵[4]）：给定顶点集$\mathcal{V}$，函数$f : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$是$\mathcal{V}$上的（非负）集函数。当满足以下条件时，集函数$f$称为：模函数——对所有$S \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$有$f\left( S\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{v \in  S}}f\left( {\{ v\} }\right)$；单调函数——若$X \subseteq  Y$则$f\left( X\right)  \leq  f\left( Y\right)$；次模函数——对所有$X,Y \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$有$f\left( {X \cup  Y}\right)  + f\left( {X \cap  Y}\right)  \leq  f\left( X\right)  + f\left( Y\right)$。

A monotone,submodular set function $h : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ with $h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$ is a polymatroid. We use ${\Gamma }_{V}$ to denote the set of all polymatroids $f : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ over the set $\mathcal{V}$ .

满足$h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$的单调次模集函数$h : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$称为多拟阵。我们用${\Gamma }_{V}$表示集合$\mathcal{V}$上所有多拟阵$f : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$的集合。

Definition A.7 (Edge Dominated Set Functions [4, 21]). Let $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ be a hypergraph. The set of edge dominated set functions is defined by: $\mathrm{{ED}}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  {h \mid  h : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + },h\left( S\right)  \leq  1,\forall S \in  \mathcal{E}}\right\}$ .

定义A.7（边支配集函数[4,21]）。设$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$为超图，边支配集函数集合定义为：$\mathrm{{ED}}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  {h \mid  h : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + },h\left( S\right)  \leq  1,\forall S \in  \mathcal{E}}\right\}$。

Definition A. 8 (Fractional Hypertree Width). Consider a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ . The fractional hypertree width of $\mathcal{H}$ is defined by:

定义A.8（分数超树宽度）。考虑超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$，其分数超树宽度定义为：

$$

$$

where ${\rho }_{\mathcal{E}}^{ * }\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right)$ denotes the fractional edge cover number of $\chi \left( t\right)$ .

其中${\rho }_{\mathcal{E}}^{ * }\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right)$表示$\chi \left( t\right)$的分数边覆盖数。

Definition A.9 (Submodular Winth [21]). Consider a hyper-graph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ . The submodular width of $\mathcal{H}$ is defined by:

定义A.9（子模宽度[21]）。考虑超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$，其子模宽度定义为：

$$
\operatorname{subw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  \mathrm{{ED}}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \cap  {\Gamma }_{\mathcal{V}}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  \mathrm{{TD}}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}h\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right) , \tag{2}
$$

where $\mathrm{{ED}}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ denotes the set of edge dominated set functions over $\mathcal{H}$ ,and ${\Gamma }_{\mathcal{V}}$ denotes the set of polymatroids over $\mathcal{V}$ .

其中$\mathrm{{ED}}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$表示$\mathcal{H}$上的边支配集函数集合，${\Gamma }_{\mathcal{V}}$表示$\mathcal{V}$上的多拟阵集合。

CLAIM D.5. The vertices $v\left( {\widetilde{v}}^{1}\right) ,\ldots ,v\left( {\widetilde{v}}^{k}\right)$ are pairwise distinct.

声明D.5：顶点$v\left( {\widetilde{v}}^{1}\right) ,\ldots ,v\left( {\widetilde{v}}^{k}\right)$两两互异。

最后，我们给出声明D.4和D.5的证明。

## E SOLVING THE TRIANGLE IJ USING FAQ-AI

## E 使用FAQ-AI求解三角形IJ

Consider the triangle intersection join query ${Q}_{\Delta }$ from Section 1.1. While we showed in Section 1.1 that our approach solves this query in time $O\left( {{N}^{3/2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$ ,we show here that the FAQ-AI approach [2] needs time $O\left( {{N}^{2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$ for this query.

考虑1.1节中的三角形交集连接查询${Q}_{\Delta }$。虽然在1.1节中我们证明了我们的方法可以在$O\left( {{N}^{3/2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$时间内解决该查询，但此处我们将展示FAQ-AI方法[2]需要$O\left( {{N}^{2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$时间来处理该查询。

To apply the FAQ-AI approach to query ${Q}_{\Delta }$ above,we have to express it as a query with inequality joins. In particular, each interval variable (say $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ ) is going to be replaced by two scalar variables (A.l and A.r) representing the beginning and end of $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ , i.e. $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack   = \left\lbrack  {A.l,A.r}\right\rbrack$ . Let $A.{l}^{\left( R\right) }$ and $A.{r}^{\left( R\right) }$ be the beginning and end of interval $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ in relation $R$ . Similarly,we define $A.{l}^{\left( T\right) }$ and $A.{r}^{\left( T\right) }$ . For the intervals $\left\lbrack  {A.{l}^{\left( R\right) },A.{r}^{\left( R\right) }}\right\rbrack$ and $\left\lbrack  {A.{l}^{\left( T\right) },A.{r}^{\left( T\right) }}\right\rbrack$ to overlap, the following condition must hold:

要将FAQ-AI方法应用于上述查询${Q}_{\Delta }$，需将其表示为带不等式连接的查询。具体而言，每个区间变量（例如$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$）将被表示区间起止点的两个标量变量（A.l和A.r）替代，即$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack   = \left\lbrack  {A.l,A.r}\right\rbrack$。设$A.{l}^{\left( R\right) }$和$A.{r}^{\left( R\right) }$为关系$R$中区间$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$的起止点，类似地定义$A.{l}^{\left( T\right) }$和$A.{r}^{\left( T\right) }$。要使区间$\left\lbrack  {A.{l}^{\left( R\right) },A.{r}^{\left( R\right) }}\right\rbrack$与$\left\lbrack  {A.{l}^{\left( T\right) },A.{r}^{\left( T\right) }}\right\rbrack$重叠，必须满足以下条件：

$$
\left( {A.{l}^{\left( T\right) } \leq  A.{l}^{\left( R\right) } \leq  A.{r}^{\left( T\right) }}\right)  \vee  \left( {A.{l}^{\left( R\right) } \leq  A.{l}^{\left( T\right) } \leq  A.{r}^{\left( R\right) }}\right)  \tag{3}
$$

For each variable $X \in  \{ A,B,C\}$ ,let $F\left( X\right)$ denote the set of relations containing $X$ ,i.e. $F\left( A\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ T,R\} ,F\left( B\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ R,S\}$ ,and $F\left( C\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ S,T\}$ . Note that (3) can be written equivalently as:

对于每个变量$X \in  \{ A,B,C\}$，令$F\left( X\right)$表示包含$X$的关系集合，即$F\left( A\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ T,R\} ,F\left( B\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ R,S\}$，且$F\left( C\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ S,T\}$。注意（3）式可等价表述为：

$$
\mathop{\bigvee }\limits_{\substack{{V \in  F\left( A\right) } \\  {W \in  F\left( A\right) -\{ V\} } }}A \cdot  {l}^{\left( W\right) } \leq  A \cdot  {l}^{\left( V\right) } \leq  A \cdot  {r}^{\left( W\right) } \tag{4}
$$

The same applies to the other two interval variables $\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack$ and $\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack$ .

该结论同样适用于另外两个区间变量$\left\lbrack  B\right\rbrack$和$\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack$。

Let:

设：

$$
\mathcal{R} \mathrel{\text{:=}} R\left( {A.{l}^{\left( R\right) },A.{r}^{\left( R\right) },B.{l}^{\left( R\right) },B.{r}^{\left( R\right) }}\right) ,
$$

$$
\mathcal{S} \mathrel{\text{:=}} S\left( {B.{l}^{\left( S\right) },B.{r}^{\left( S\right) },C.{l}^{\left( S\right) },C.{r}^{\left( S\right) }}\right) ,
$$

$$
\mathcal{T} \mathrel{\text{:=}} T\left( {A.{l}^{\left( T\right) },A.{r}^{\left( T\right) },C.{l}^{\left( T\right) },C.{r}^{\left( T\right) }}\right) .
$$

and

且

$$
\mathcal{A} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {A.{l}^{\left( {W}_{A}\right) } \leq  A.{l}^{\left( {V}_{A}\right) } \leq  A.{r}^{\left( {W}_{A}\right) }}\right) ,
$$

$$
\mathcal{B} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {B.{l}^{\left( {W}_{B}\right) } \leq  B.{l}^{\left( {V}_{B}\right) } \leq  B.{r}^{\left( {W}_{B}\right) }}\right) ,
$$

$$
C \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {C.{l}^{\left( {W}_{C}\right) } \leq  C.{l}^{\left( {V}_{C}\right) } \leq  C.{r}^{\left( {W}_{C}\right) }}\right) .
$$

After distributing disjunctions over conjunctions, ${Q}_{\Delta }$ can be written as follows:

将析取分配至合取后，${Q}_{\Delta }$可表述如下：

$$
{Q}_{\bigtriangleup } = \bigvee \;\mathcal{R} \land  \mathcal{S} \land  \mathcal{T} \land  \mathcal{A} \land  \mathcal{B} \land  \mathcal{C}. \tag{5}
$$

$$
\left( {{V}_{A},{V}_{B},{V}_{C}}\right)  \in  F\left( A\right)  \times  F\left( B\right)  \times  F\left( C\right) 
$$

$$
{W}_{A} \in  F\left( A\right)  - \left\{  {V}_{A}\right\}  
$$

$$
{W}_{B} \in  F\left( B\right)  - \left\{  {V}_{B}\right\}  
$$

$$
{W}_{C} \in  F\left( C\right)  - \left\{  {V}_{C}\right\}  
$$

For each $\left( {{V}_{A},{V}_{B},{V}_{C}}\right)  \in  F\left( A\right)  \times  F\left( B\right)  \times  F\left( C\right)$ ,the inner conjunction in (5) is an FAQ-AI query [2]. While solving each such query, it is possible to relax the definition of tree decompositions thus extending the set of valid tree decompositions and potentially reducing the fractional hypertree and submodular widths, ultimately resulting in the relaxed versions of these widths ${\mathrm{{fhtw}}}_{\ell }$ and ${\mathrm{{subw}}}_{\ell }$ respectively [2]. In particular, in a relaxed tree decomposition, we no longer require each inequality to have its variables contained in one bag of the tree. Instead, it suffices to have its variables contained in two adjacent bags in the tree.

对于每个$\left( {{V}_{A},{V}_{B},{V}_{C}}\right)  \in  F\left( A\right)  \times  F\left( B\right)  \times  F\left( C\right)$，（5）式中的内部合取式是一个FAQ-AI查询[2]。在求解此类查询时，可放宽树分解的定义，从而扩展有效树分解集合，潜在地降低分数超树宽度和次模宽度，最终得到放宽后的宽度版本${\mathrm{{fhtw}}}_{\ell }$和${\mathrm{{subw}}}_{\ell }$[2]。特别地，在放宽的树分解中，不再要求每个不等式的变量必须包含在树的单个袋中，只需其变量存在于树中两个相邻的袋即可。

Fix an arbitrary $\left( {{V}_{A},{V}_{B},{V}_{C}}\right)  \in  F\left( A\right)  \times  F\left( B\right)  \times  F\left( C\right)$ and let $\bar{Q}$ be the resulting FAQ-AI query corresponding to the inner conjunction in (5). Note that for every pair of the relations $R,S$ and $T$ ,the query $\bar{Q}$ contains at least one inequality between two variables from that pair. Hence if we distribute the relations $R,S$ and $T$ among three or more bags, there will be an inequality between two non-adjacent bags thus violating the condition for a relaxed tree decomposition. Therefore,every relaxed tree decomposition of $\bar{Q}$ must have at most two bags where each one the relations $R,S$ and $T$ falls within one bag. Consequently, there will be one bag with (at least) two relations. Noting that the variables of relations $R,S$ and $T$ are pairwise disjoint, this implies that ${\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  \geq  2$ . To minimize ${\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)$ ,an optimal tree decomposition would have two bags with two relations in one bag and the third relation in the other,resulting in ${\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  = 2$ .

固定任意$\left( {{V}_{A},{V}_{B},{V}_{C}}\right)  \in  F\left( A\right)  \times  F\left( B\right)  \times  F\left( C\right)$，令$\bar{Q}$表示（5）式中内部合取式对应的FAQ-AI查询。注意对于每对关系$R,S$和$T$，查询$\bar{Q}$至少包含该对变量间的一个不等式。因此若将关系$R,S$和$T$分配至三个或更多袋中，将导致两个非相邻袋之间存在不等式，从而违反放宽树分解的条件。故$\bar{Q}$的每个放宽树分解最多只能有两个袋，其中关系$R,S$和$T$各自位于一个袋内。这意味着必然存在一个袋包含（至少）两个关系。注意到关系$R,S$和$T$的变量两两不相交，由此可得${\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  \geq  2$。为最小化${\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)$，最优树分解应设两个袋：一个袋含两个关系，另一个袋含第三个关系，最终得到${\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  = 2$。

The relaxed submodular width ${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)$ is not any better in this case. Consider the following function $\bar{h} : {2}^{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ :

此情形下放宽的次模宽度${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)$并无改善。考虑以下函数$\bar{h} : {2}^{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$：

$$
\bar{h}\left( X\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \frac{\left| X\right| }{4},\;\forall X \subseteq  \operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) . \tag{6}
$$

Recall notation from Section A and [2,21]. The above $\bar{h}$ is a modular function hence it is submodular. Since it is also monotone, $\bar{h}$ is a polymatroid,i.e. $\bar{h} \in  {\Gamma }_{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) }$ where ${\Gamma }_{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) }$ denotes the set of polymatroids over the variables $\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) \left\lbrack  2\right\rbrack$ . Moreover for each finite input relation $E \in  \{ R,S,T\}$ ,we have $\bar{h}\left( E\right)  = 1$ since each one of these relations has four variables,i.e. $\left| E\right|  = 4$ . Therefore $\bar{h}$ is edge dominated,i.e. $\bar{h} \in  \mathrm{{ED}}\left( \bar{Q}\right)$ where $\mathrm{{ED}}\left( \bar{Q}\right)$ denotes the set of edge dominated functions $h : {2}^{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ . Recall the definition of ${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)$ for an FAQ-AI query $Q$ from [2] where ${\mathrm{{TD}}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)$ denotes the set of relaxed tree decompositions of $Q$ :

回顾A节及文献[2,21]中的符号表示。上述$\bar{h}$是模函数，因此具有次模性。因其同时具有单调性，$\bar{h}$构成多拟阵（polymatroid），即$\bar{h} \in  {\Gamma }_{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) }$，其中${\Gamma }_{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) }$表示变量集$\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) \left\lbrack  2\right\rbrack$上的多拟阵集合。对于每个有限输入关系$E \in  \{ R,S,T\}$，由于这些关系均包含四个变量（即$\left| E\right|  = 4$），故有$\bar{h}\left( E\right)  = 1$。因此$\bar{h}$是边支配的（edge dominated），即$\bar{h} \in  \mathrm{{ED}}\left( \bar{Q}\right)$，此处$\mathrm{{ED}}\left( \bar{Q}\right)$表示边支配函数集$h : {2}^{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$。根据文献[2]对FAQ-AI查询$Q$中${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)$的定义，其中${\mathrm{{TD}}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)$表示$Q$的松弛树分解集合：

$$
{\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  \mathrm{{ED}}\left( Q\right)  \cap  {\Gamma }_{\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }}}\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  {\mathrm{{TD}}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right) }}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}h\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right) . \tag{7}
$$

Based on the above definition and by choosing $\bar{h} \in  \mathrm{{ED}}\left( \bar{Q}\right)  \cap  {\Gamma }_{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) }$ , we have

基于上述定义并选取$\bar{h} \in  \mathrm{{ED}}\left( \bar{Q}\right)  \cap  {\Gamma }_{\operatorname{vars}\left( \bar{Q}\right) }$后，可得

$$
{\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  \geq  \mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  {\operatorname{TD}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right) }}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}\bar{h}\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right) .
$$

However for each relaxed tree decomposition $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  {\operatorname{TD}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)$ , we argued before that there must exist some bag ${t}^{ * } \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ containing at least two of the input relations $\{ R,S,T\}$ hence at least 8 distinct variables,meaning that $\left| {\chi \left( {t}^{ * }\right) }\right|  \geq  8$ . From (6),we have $\bar{h}\left( {\chi \left( {t}^{ * }\right) }\right)  \geq  2$ which implies that ${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  \geq  2$ . And since ${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)  \leq  {\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)$ for any query $Q$ according to [2],we have

然而对于每个松弛树分解$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  {\operatorname{TD}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)$，前文已论证必存在至少包含两个输入关系$\{ R,S,T\}$的袋（bag）${t}^{ * } \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$，这意味着至少涉及8个不同变量，即$\left| {\chi \left( {t}^{ * }\right) }\right|  \geq  8$。由式(6)可得$\bar{h}\left( {\chi \left( {t}^{ * }\right) }\right)  \geq  2$，由此推出${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  \geq  2$。根据文献[2]，对于任意查询$Q$均有${\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)  \leq  {\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right)$，故得

$$
{\operatorname{subw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  = {\operatorname{fhtw}}_{\ell }\left( \bar{Q}\right)  = 2\text{.} \tag{8}
$$

Finally according to Theorem 3.5 in [2], the time complexity in FAQ-AI involves an extra factor of ${\left( \log N\right) }^{\max \left( {k - 1,1}\right) }$ where $k$ is the number of inequalities that involve variables from two adjacent bags (i.e. that are not contained in a single bag) in an optimal relaxed tree decomposition. In query (5), when constructing any optimal relaxed tree decomposition involving two relations in one bag (say $R$ and $S$ ) and the third relation in another bag,there will be exactly 4 inequalities involving variables from both bags. Hence $k = 4$ and the overall time complexity of FAQ-AI for ${Q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ is $O\left( {{N}^{2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$ .

最终根据文献[2]定理3.5，FAQ-AI的时间复杂度包含额外因子${\left( \log N\right) }^{\max \left( {k - 1,1}\right) }$，其中$k$表示最优松弛树分解中涉及相邻两袋变量（即不共处于同一袋）的不等式数量。在查询(5)中，当构建包含两个关系（如$R$与$S$）同袋、第三个关系另袋的最优松弛树分解时，恰好存在4个涉及双袋变量的不等式。因此$k = 4$，FAQ-AI处理${Q}_{\bigtriangleup }$的总体时间复杂度为$O\left( {{N}^{2}{\log }^{3}N}\right)$。
