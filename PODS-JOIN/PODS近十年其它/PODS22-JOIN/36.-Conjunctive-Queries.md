## Abstract

## 摘要

Releasing the result size of conjunctive queries and graph pattern queries under differential privacy (DP) has received considerable attention in the literature, but existing solutions do not offer any optimality guarantees. We provide the first DP mechanism for this problem with a fairly strong notion of optimality, which can be considered as a natural relaxation of instance-optimality to a constant.

在差分隐私(DP)约束下发布连接查询和图模式查询的结果规模已获得学界广泛关注，但现有解决方案均无法提供最优性保证。我们首次提出具有强最优性保证的DP机制，该机制可视为对实例最优性进行常数松弛的自然扩展。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

The bulk of work on differential privacy (DP) has been devoted to counting queries $\left\lbrack  {{17},{35}}\right\rbrack$ . For a query $q$ ,let $q\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ be the results of evaluating $q$ on database instance $\mathbf{I}$ ,and let $\left| {q\left( \mathbf{I}\right) }\right|$ be the cardinality of $q\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$ . A DP mechanism ${\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$ for a counting query $q$ aims to release $\left| {q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right|$ masked with noise so as to satisfy the DP requirement. Formally, ${\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \cdot \right)$ is $\varepsilon$ -differentially private if

差分隐私(DP)研究主要集中于计数查询$\left\lbrack  {{17},{35}}\right\rbrack$。对于查询$q$，令$q\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$表示在数据库实例$\mathbf{I}$上执行$q$的结果，$\left| {q\left( \mathbf{I}\right) }\right|$表示$q\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$的基数。计数查询$q$的DP机制旨在发布经噪声处理的$\left| {q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right|$以满足DP要求。形式化地说，若满足以下条件，则称${\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \cdot \right)$具有$\varepsilon$-差分隐私：

$$
\Pr \left\lbrack  {{\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)  = y}\right\rbrack   \leq  {e}^{\varepsilon }\Pr \left\lbrack  {{\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( {\mathrm{I}}^{\prime }\right)  = y}\right\rbrack   \tag{1}
$$

for any $y$ and any pair of neighboring instances $\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$ ,i.e., $d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = 1$ . Here, $d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)$ denotes the minimum number of changes to turn $\mathbf{I}$ into ${\mathrm{I}}^{\prime }$ . DP policies may vary depending on what constitutes a "change". In strict DP [22, 26], a change can be inserting a tuple, deleting a tuple, or substituting a tuple by another, while in a relaxed version, only substitutions are allowed $\left\lbrack  {6,{25}}\right\rbrack$ . The latter is more relaxed since there are less neighboring instances that must satisfy (1). In particular, neighboring instances must have the same size, so a DP mechanism may use the instance size $N = \left| \mathbf{I}\right|$ directly; on the other hand, $N$ must be protected in strict DP. All the positive results in this paper hold for strict DP, while negative results hold even for the relaxed version. The privacy parameter $\varepsilon$ is usually taken as a constant, which in practice ranges from 0.1 to 10 .

对于任意$y$及任意相邻实例对$\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$(即$d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = 1$)。其中$d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)$表示将$\mathbf{I}$转换为${\mathrm{I}}^{\prime }$所需的最小变更次数。DP策略可能因"变更"定义而异：严格DP[22,26]中变更包括插入、删除或替换元组，而在宽松版本中仅允许替换操作$\left\lbrack  {6,{25}}\right\rbrack$。后者约束更宽松，因为需要满足(1)式的相邻实例更少。特别地，相邻实例必须保持相同规模，因此DP机制可直接使用实例大小$N = \left| \mathbf{I}\right|$；而严格DP中$N$必须受到保护。本文所有积极结论均适用于严格DP，消极结论即使在宽松版本下仍成立。隐私参数$\varepsilon$通常取常数值，实际取值范围为0.1至10。

Any (nontrivial) DP mechanism must be probabilistic by definition. Thus,the most common measure of the utility of ${\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \cdot \right)$ is its expected ${\ell }_{2}$ -error:

根据定义，任何（非平凡）差分隐私机制本质上都必须是概率性的。因此，衡量${\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \cdot \right)$效用最常用的指标是其期望${\ell }_{2}$误差：

$$
\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}_{q},\mathbf{I}}\right)  = \sqrt{\mathrm{E}\left\lbrack  {\left( {\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  - \left| q\left( \mathbf{I}\right) \right| \right) }^{2}\right\rbrack  }
$$

$$
 = \sqrt{{\left( \mathrm{E}\left\lbrack  {\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \mathrm{I}\right) \right\rbrack   - \left| q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) \right| \right) }^{2} + \operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {{\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right\rbrack  }\text{,}
$$

which consists of a bias term and a variance term. Between two mechanisms with the same $\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}_{q},\mathrm{I}}\right)$ ,the unbiased one is often preferred. All our DP mechanisms will be unbiased, in which case $\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}_{q},\mathrm{I}}\right)  = \sqrt{\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {{\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right\rbrack  }$ ,while our lower bounds hold even for biased mechanisms.

该误差由偏差项和方差项组成。在具有相同$\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}_{q},\mathrm{I}}\right)$的两个机制中，无偏机制通常更受青睐。我们所有的差分隐私机制都将保持无偏性，此时$\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}_{q},\mathrm{I}}\right)  = \sqrt{\operatorname{Var}\left\lbrack  {{\mathcal{M}}_{q}\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right\rbrack  }$，而我们的下界结论即使对有偏机制也成立。

The problem is now well understood for selection queries with various types of selection conditions [7, 10, 20, 31, 32, 36, 38]. However,the case when $q$ is a conjunctive query (CQ) is still largely open. Existing solutions $\left\lbrack  {{22},{28},{30}}\right\rbrack$ are heuristics without any notion of optimality. Notably, there have been extensive studies on graph pattern counting queries $\left\lbrack  {8,9,{11},{23},{24},{33},{37}}\right\rbrack$ ,which are a special case of CQs equipped with inequalities. But none of them has any theoretical guarantee on the utility, either.

目前对于含各类选择条件的查询问题已有充分研究[7,10,20,31,32,36,38]，但当$q$为连接查询(CQ)时，该领域仍存在大量空白。现有解决方案$\left\lbrack  {{22},{28},{30}}\right\rbrack$都是缺乏最优性理论支撑的启发式方法。值得注意的是，虽然针对图模式计数查询$\left\lbrack  {8,9,{11},{23},{24},{33},{37}}\right\rbrack$（即带有不等式的特殊连接查询）已有大量研究，但这些方法同样缺乏效用性的理论保证。

### 1.1 Sensitivity-based DP Mechanisms

### 1.1 基于敏感度的差分隐私机制

Let $\mathbf{I}$ be a database instance and $q$ a counting query. A widely used framework for designing an ${\mathcal{M}}_{q}$ is to first compute some measure of sensitivity ${S}_{q}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ ,and then add noise drawn from a certain zero-mean distribution calibrated to ${S}_{q}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$ . From now on,we may omit the subscript $q$ from ${\mathcal{M}}_{q}$ and ${S}_{q}$ when the context is clear. Note that all sensitivity-based mechanisms are unbiased. Various noise distributions (Section 2 gives more details) have been studied and they can all achieve $\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)  = \Theta \left( {S\left( \mathbf{I}\right) }\right)$ ,where the hidden constant depends on $\varepsilon$ and the particular distribution. Thus,the problem essentially boils down to finding the smallest $S\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ that satisfies the DP requirement. When we say that a particular sensitivity measure $S\left( \cdot \right)$ is optimal,this actually means that the DP mechanism $\mathcal{M}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ that adds noise drawn from a certain distribution calibrated to $S\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ achieves the optimal (in whatever sense) $\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)$ .

设$\mathbf{I}$为数据库实例，$q$为计数查询。设计${\mathcal{M}}_{q}$的通用框架是：先计算某种敏感度度量${S}_{q}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$，再添加与${S}_{q}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$校准的零均值分布噪声。下文在语境明确时可能省略${\mathcal{M}}_{q}$和${S}_{q}$中的下标$q$。需注意所有基于敏感度的机制都是无偏的。已有研究涉及多种噪声分布（第2章详述），它们都能实现$\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)  = \Theta \left( {S\left( \mathbf{I}\right) }\right)$，其中隐含常数取决于$\varepsilon$和具体分布。因此问题本质可归结为寻找满足DP要求的最小$S\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$。当称某敏感度度量$S\left( \cdot \right)$最优时，实指通过$S\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$校准噪声分布的DP机制$\mathcal{M}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$能达到某种意义下的最优$\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)$。

The main reason for the lack of a good theoretical analysis for CQs under DP is that standard notions of optimality are either useless or unattainable. It is well known that the global sensitivity ${GS}$ ,which is defined as the maximum difference between the query answers on any two neighboring instances, is worst-case optimal. Note that ${GS}$ is an instance-independent sensitivity measure. However,for any nontrivial $\mathrm{{CQ}}$ involving two or more relations, ${GS} = \infty$ in strict DP, as changing one tuple may affect an unbounded number of query results. In relaxed DP,where it is safe to use $N$ in designing the mechanism, ${GS}$ can be bounded,but can still be as high as $O\left( {N}^{n - 1}\right)$ ,where $n$ is the number of relations in $q$ .

连接查询在差分隐私下缺乏理论分析的主要原因在于：标准最优性概念要么失效要么不可实现。众所周知，全局敏感度${GS}$（定义为任意相邻实例间查询结果的最大差异）具有最坏情况最优性。需注意${GS}$是与实例无关的敏感度度量。但对于涉及两个及以上关系的非平凡$\mathrm{{CQ}}$，严格差分隐私中${GS} = \infty$会趋于无穷，因为单条元组可能影响无限量查询结果。在松弛差分隐私中，机制设计可采用$N$，此时${GS}$虽有界，但仍可能高达$O\left( {N}^{n - 1}\right)$（其中$n$表示$q$中的关系数量）。

For queries where ${GS}$ is unbounded or too high,a natural idea is to use an instance-specific sensitivity measure $\left\lbrack  {{23},{29},{37}}\right\rbrack$ . The local sensitivity (definition given in Section 2.3) ${LS}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$ is the most natural choice,and it can be much smaller than ${GS}$ on most real-world instances. However, Nissim et al. [29] point out that using ${LS}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ to calibrate noise violates DP; instead, they propose smooth sensitivity ${SS}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ ,which is always higher than ${LS}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ but lower than ${GS}$ ,and show that it satisfies DP.

当查询条件${GS}$无界或过高时，采用实例特定敏感度度量$\left\lbrack  {{23},{29},{37}}\right\rbrack$是自然思路。局部敏感度（定义见2.3节）${LS}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$是最自然的选择，在多数现实案例中它远小于${GS}$。但Nissim等人[29]指出，使用${LS}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$校准噪声会破坏差分隐私(DP)；他们转而提出平滑敏感度${SS}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$——该值始终高于${LS}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$但低于${GS}$，并证明其满足DP要求。

How do we quantify the utility of a DP mechanism that adds instance-specific noise? Ideally, we would like it to be instance-optimal [18]. More formally,a DP mechanism $\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$ is said to be c-instance-optimal if

如何量化添加实例特定噪声的DP机制效用？理想情况下，我们希望其达到实例最优[18]。严格来说，当满足以下条件时，DP机制$\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$可称为c-实例最优：

$$
\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)  \leq  c \cdot  \operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime },\mathbf{I}}\right) ,
$$

for every instance $\mathrm{I}$ and every DP mechanism ${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }\left( \cdot \right)$ .

对于任意实例$\mathrm{I}$和所有DP机制${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }\left( \cdot \right)$。

Unfortunately, as pointed out by Asi and Duchi [4], instance-optimal DP mechanisms do not exist, unless the query is trivial (i.e., it returns the same count on all instances), because for an I, one can always design a trivial DP mechanism ${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }\left( \cdot \right)  \equiv  \left| {q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right|$ . It works perfectly on I but fails miserably on other instances. Yet, such a trivial ${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }$ rules out instance-optimal mechanisms.

遗憾的是，Asi和Duchi[4]指出，除非查询是平凡的（即在所有实例返回相同计数），否则不存在实例最优DP机制——因为针对特定实例I，总能设计出在该实例完美工作但在其他实例完全失效的平凡DP机制${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }\left( \cdot \right)  \equiv  \left| {q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right|$。正是这类平凡的${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }$排除了实例最优机制存在的可能。

### 1.2 Neighborhood-optimal DP Mechanisms

### 1.2 邻域最优DP机制

To eliminate such a trivial ${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }$ ,Asi and Duchi [4] propose the following natural relaxation of instance-optimality, which requires ${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }$ to not just work well for $\mathbf{I}$ ,but also in its neighborhood.

为消除此类平凡的${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }$，Asi和Duchi[4]提出实例最优性的自然松弛形式：要求${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }$不仅对$\mathbf{I}$有效，还需在其邻域内保持性能。

Definition 1.1 ((r, c)-neighborhood optimal DP mechanisms). An $\varepsilon$ -DP mechanism $\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$ is said to be(r,c)-neighborhood optimal if for any instance $\mathrm{I}$ and any $\varepsilon$ -DP mechanism ${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }\left( \cdot \right)$ ,there exists an instance ${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$ with $d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  \leq  r$ such that

定义1.1（(r,c)-邻域最优DP机制）。称$\varepsilon$-DP机制$\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$为(r,c)-邻域最优，若对任意实例$\mathrm{I}$和任意$\varepsilon$-DP机制${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }\left( \cdot \right)$，总存在满足$d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  \leq  r$的实例${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$使得：

$$
\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathrm{I}}\right)  \leq  c \cdot  \operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime },{\mathrm{I}}^{\prime }}\right) . \tag{2}
$$

Note that neighborhood optimality smoothly interpolates between instance optimality $\left( {r = 0}\right)$ and worst-case optimality $\left( {r = N}\right)$ . It is also called local minimax optimality in [4], as it minimizes (up to a factor of $c$ ) the maximum error in the local neighborhood of $\mathbf{I}$ .

注意邻域最优性在实例最优性$\left( {r = 0}\right)$与最坏情况最优性$\left( {r = N}\right)$之间实现了平滑过渡。文献[4]中亦称其为局部极小极大最优性，因其在$\mathbf{I}$的局部邻域内将最大误差最小化（至多相差$c$倍）。

We adopt the convention of data complexity, i.e., all asymptotic notations suppress dependencies on the query size. We also take $\varepsilon$ to be a constant, as with most work on differential privacy. Thus, when we say that an $\varepsilon$ -DP mechanism is $\left( {O\left( 1\right) ,O\left( 1\right) }\right)$ -neighborhood optimal,this means that $r$ and $c$ may depend on $q$ and $\varepsilon$ ,but are independent of $N$ . As we are most interested in constant-factor approximations, we often use " $r$ -neighborhood optimal" as a shorthand of " $\left( {r,O\left( 1\right) }\right)$ -neighborhood optimal".

我们采用数据复杂度的惯例，即所有渐近表示法均忽略对查询规模的依赖。与大多数差分隐私研究一致，我们也将$\varepsilon$视为常数。因此，当我们称某个$\varepsilon$-差分隐私机制是$\left( {O\left( 1\right) ,O\left( 1\right) }\right)$-邻域最优时，这意味着$r$和$c$可能依赖于$q$和$\varepsilon$，但与$N$无关。由于我们最关注常数倍近似，常将"$r$-邻域最优"简写为"$\left( {r,O\left( 1\right) }\right)$-邻域最优"。

While more relaxed than instance optimality, neighborhood optimality is still not easy to achieve (for small $r$ ). For example,we can show that the MEDIAN query (i.e.,returning the median of $N$ elements in $\left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ ,for odd $N$ ) does not have any(r,c)-neighborhood optimal mechanisms for $r \leq  \lfloor N/2\rfloor$ and any $c$ . To see this,consider $\mathbf{I} = \left( {0,\ldots ,0}\right)$ and ${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }\left( \cdot \right)  \equiv  0$ . Note that all instances in the $r$ -neighborhood of $\mathbf{I}$ have output 0,so $\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime },{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = 0$ for all ${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$ with $d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  \leq  r$ . Thus,we must set $\mathcal{M}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  = 0$ to satisfy (2). We can apply the same argument on ${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime \prime } = \left( {1,\ldots ,1}\right)$ and conclude that $\mathcal{M}\left( {\mathbf{I}}^{\prime \prime }\right)  = 1$ . We have thus found two instances on which $\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$ returns different deterministic values,thus $\mathcal{M}$ cannot be DP by a standard argument [17].

虽然比实例最优性宽松，但邻域最优性（对于较小$r$）仍难以实现。例如，可证明MEDIAN查询（即返回$\left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$中$N$个元素的中位数，$N$为奇数时）对于$r \leq  \lfloor N/2\rfloor$和任意$c$都不存在(r,c)-邻域最优机制。具体而言，考察$\mathbf{I} = \left( {0,\ldots ,0}\right)$和${\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }\left( \cdot \right)  \equiv  0$。注意到$\mathbf{I}$的$r$-邻域内所有实例输出均为0，故对所有满足$d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  \leq  r$的${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$都有$\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime },{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = 0$。因此必须设置$\mathcal{M}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  = 0$以满足条件(2)。对${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime \prime } = \left( {1,\ldots ,1}\right)$应用相同论证可得$\mathcal{M}\left( {\mathbf{I}}^{\prime \prime }\right)  = 1$。至此我们发现两个实例使$\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$返回不同确定值，故根据标准论证[17]可知$\mathcal{M}$不满足差分隐私。

Acute readers would realize that the negative result for MEDIAN relies on the fact that there are certain instances (like $\left( {0,\ldots ,0}\right)$ ) with a "flat" neighborhood, i.e., the query output is the same within the neighborhood. Fortunately, for full CQs, which are the focus of this paper, these flat neighborhoods do not exist. This is because in any instance I, one can always add/remove a constant number (depending on $q$ ) of tuples to change $\left| {q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right|$ . However,this is merely a necessary condition for a query to admit neighborhood-optimal DP mechanisms. To actually design such a mechanism $\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$ ,we need an upper bound on $\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathrm{I}}\right)$ ,as well as a neighborhood lower bound on $\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{{\mathbf{l}}^{\prime } : d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  \leq  r}}\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime },{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)$ . Note that both the upper bound and the lower bound are instance specific (i.e., functions of I), and the worst-case (over all I) gap between the upper and lower bounds would become the optimality ratio $c$ .

敏锐的读者会意识到，MEDIAN的否定性结论源于存在具有"平坦"邻域的特定实例（如$\left( {0,\ldots ,0}\right)$），即查询输出在邻域内恒定。幸运的是，本文研究的完全合取查询不存在这种平坦邻域——在任何实例I中，总能通过增删有限个（取决于$q$）元组来改变$\left| {q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right|$。但这仅是查询允许邻域最优差分隐私机制的必要条件。要实际设计此类机制$\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$，我们既需要$\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathrm{I}}\right)$的上界，也需要$\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime }}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{{\mathbf{l}}^{\prime } : d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  \leq  r}}\operatorname{Err}\left( {{\mathcal{M}}^{\prime },{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)$的邻域下界。注意上下界都是实例相关的（即I的函数），而最坏情况下（对所有I）上下界间的差距将构成最优比$c$。

### 1.3 DP Mechanisms for Full CQs

### 1.3 完全合取查询的差分隐私机制

We relate both the upper and the lower bounds to the smooth sensitivity ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ . On the lower bound side,in Section 4,we show that ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is an $O\left( 1\right)$ -neighborhood lower bound. Thus,the smooth sensitivity based mechanism [29] is $O\left( 1\right)$ -neighborhood optimal. However, ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ in general requires exponential time to compute. In Section 3,we extend the residual sensitivity ${RS}\left( \cdot \right) \left\lbrack  {13}\right\rbrack$ to arbitrary full CQs, and show that it (1) satisfies DP, (2) is a constant-factor upper bound of ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ ,and (3) can be computed in polynomial time. Together with the lower bound, this yields our first main result:

我们将上下界均与平滑敏感度${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$建立关联。在下界方面，第4节证明${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$构成$O\left( 1\right)$-邻域下界，因此基于平滑敏感度的机制[29]具有$O\left( 1\right)$-邻域最优性。但${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$通常需要指数时间计算。第3节将残差敏感度${RS}\left( \cdot \right) \left\lbrack  {13}\right\rbrack$推广至任意完全CQ，证明其(1)满足差分隐私(DP)、(2)是${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$的常数倍上界、(3)可在多项式时间计算。结合下界分析，得出首个主要结论：

THEOREM 1.2. For any full ${CQq}$ ,there is an $\varepsilon$ -DP mechanism $\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$ that is $O\left( 1\right)$ -neighborhood optimal. For any instance $\mathbf{I},\mathcal{M}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ can be computed in poly( $N$ ) time.

定理1.2. 对任意完全${CQq}$，存在$\varepsilon$-差分隐私机制$\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$满足$O\left( 1\right)$-邻域最优性。对任意实例$\mathbf{I},\mathcal{M}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$可在poly($N$)时间内计算。

### 1.4 CQs with Predicates

### 1.4 带谓词的CQ

Next,we consider CQs with predicates. A predicate $P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$ is a computable function $P : \operatorname{dom}\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  \rightarrow  \{$ True,False $\}$ for a set of variables $\mathbf{y}$ . A valuation of $\mathbf{y}$ is a valid query result only if $P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$ evaluates to True.

接下来研究带谓词的CQ。谓词$P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$是变量集$\mathbf{y}$的可计算函数$P : \operatorname{dom}\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  \rightarrow  \{$真,假$\}$。仅当$P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$求值为真时，$\mathbf{y}$的赋值才构成有效查询结果。

Predicates are important for expressing graph pattern counting queries. Suppose we would like to count the number of length- 3 paths (no repeated vertices) in a directed graph whose edges are stored in a relation Edge. However,the CQ Edge $\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right)  \bowtie$ $\operatorname{Edge}\left( {{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right)  \bowtie  \operatorname{Edge}\left( {{x}_{3},{x}_{4}}\right)$ would not just count the number of length-3 paths, but also length-1 paths, length-2 paths, and triangles. We have to equip the CQ with inequalities,i.e., ${x}_{i} \neq  {x}_{j}$ for all $i \neq  j$ , to exclude these false positives. Another type of predicates are comparisons,i.e., ${x}_{i} \leq  {x}_{j}$ or ${x}_{i} < {x}_{j}$ ,which are common in queries over spatiotemporal databases.

谓词对图模式计数查询至关重要。假设需统计有向图中长度为3的路径（无重复顶点），边存储在Edge关系中。但CQ Edge$\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right)  \bowtie$$\operatorname{Edge}\left( {{x}_{2},{x}_{3}}\right)  \bowtie  \operatorname{Edge}\left( {{x}_{3},{x}_{4}}\right)$不仅会计数长度3路径，还包括长度1、2路径及三角形。必须为CQ添加不等谓词（即对所有$i \neq  j$设置${x}_{i} \neq  {x}_{j}$）以排除误报。另一类谓词是比较运算（如${x}_{i} \leq  {x}_{j}$或${x}_{i} < {x}_{j}$），这在时空数据库查询中常见。

In Section 5 we show how to modify ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ to take these predicates into consideration, while preserving its neighborhood optimality. The idea is conceptually easy: we just materialize each predicate $P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$ into a relation $\{ t \in  \mathbf{{dom}}\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  \mid  P\left( t\right) \}$ ,and then apply our DP mechanism in Theorem 1.2. However, this poses a computational issue, since this relation can be infinite (assuming infinite domains). To address this issue, we show that it is actually possible to compute ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ without materializing the $P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$ ’s.

第5节展示如何修正${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$以纳入谓词，同时保持邻域最优性。概念上很简单：将每个谓词$P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$物化为关系$\{ t \in  \mathbf{{dom}}\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  \mid  P\left( t\right) \}$后应用定理1.2的DP机制。但这会引发计算问题，因该关系可能无限（假设无限域）。我们证明实际上无需物化$P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$即可计算${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$。

THEOREM 1.3. For any full ${CQq}$ with predicates ${P}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,{P}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{K}\right)$ , there is an $O\left( 1\right)$ -neighborhood optimal $\varepsilon$ -DP mechanism $\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$ ,if for any $\mathbf{z} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( q\right)$ ,the satisfiability of ${\varphi }_{1} \land  \cdots  \land  {\varphi }_{S}$ is decidable,where each ${\varphi }_{i}$ is ${P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{u}}_{i}\right)$ for any $j$ and ${\mathbf{u}}_{i}$ is ${\mathbf{y}}_{j}$ after replacing all variables not in $\mathrm{z}$ by any constants. Furthermore, $\mathcal{M}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$ can be computed in $\operatorname{poly}\left( N\right)$ time if all predicates are inequalities or comparisons.

定理1.3. 对于任意包含谓词${P}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,{P}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{K}\right)$的完全${CQq}$，若对任意$\mathbf{z} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( q\right)$，${\varphi }_{1} \land  \cdots  \land  {\varphi }_{S}$的可满足性是可判定的（其中每个${\varphi }_{i}$对任意$j$和${\mathbf{u}}_{i}$均成立，且将所有不在$\mathrm{z}$中的变量替换为常量后${\mathbf{y}}_{j}$），则存在$O\left( 1\right)$邻域最优的$\varepsilon$-差分隐私机制$\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)$。此外，若所有谓词均为不等式或比较运算，$\mathcal{M}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$可在$\operatorname{poly}\left( N\right)$时间内计算得出。

### 1.5 Non-full CQs

### 1.5 非完全联立查询

To complete the picture, we finally study non-full CQs in Section 6. Prior work on non-full CQs simply ignores the projection, and uses the sensitivity of the full CQ to calibrate noise. We show how to extend ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ to handle the projection more effectively so as to reduce the noise. Unfortunately, our lower bound no longer holds for non-full CQs, hence losing neighborhood optimality. However, we show that this is unavoidable. In particular, even for the simple non-full query ${\pi }_{{x}_{2}}\left( {{R}_{1}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right) }\right)$ ,we show that it does not admit any $o\left( \sqrt{N}\right)$ -neighborhood optimal DP mechanisms.

为完善理论框架，我们最终在第6节研究了非完全联立查询。先前关于非完全联立查询的研究直接忽略投影操作，采用完全联立查询的敏感度来校准噪声。我们展示了如何扩展${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$以更有效地处理投影操作从而降低噪声。遗憾的是，我们的下界不再适用于非完全联立查询，因此丧失了邻域最优性。但我们证明这是不可避免的——即使对于简单的非完全查询${\pi }_{{x}_{2}}\left( {{R}_{1}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right) }\right)$，也不存在任何$o\left( \sqrt{N}\right)$邻域最优的差分隐私机制。

### 1.6 Related Work

### 1.6 相关工作

The notion of neighborhood optimality has been recently proposed in the machine learning community [4]. However, their focus is on high-dimensional queries whose output domain must be continuous. In particular, their lower bound does not hold for any query that returns discrete outputs, in particular, counting queries. Our lower bound (Section 4.1) holds for arbitrary 1-dimensional queries (with either continuous or discrete outputs), and is also better than their lower bound. On the upper bound side, their algorithm in general requires exponential time to compute, although they show how it can be made to run in polynomial time for some machine learning problems like mean-estimation and PCA.

邻域最优性概念最近在机器学习领域被提出[4]，但其研究聚焦于输出域必须连续的高维查询。特别地，他们的下界不适用于返回离散输出的查询（尤其是计数查询）。我们的下界（第4.1节）适用于任意一维查询（无论连续或离散输出），且优于他们的下界。在上界方面，他们的算法通常需要指数级计算时间，尽管他们证明了对于均值估计和主成分分析等机器学习问题可转化为多项式时间算法。

Our work builds upon smooth sensitivity ${SS}\left( \cdot \right) \left\lbrack  {29}\right\rbrack$ and residual sensitivity ${RS}\left( \cdot \right) \left\lbrack  {13}\right\rbrack$ . Although ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ has been shown to preserve DP in [29], two issues remain: whether it is optimal (in any sense) and whether it can be computed in polynomial time. We provide positive answers to both questions in the context of CQs. While our optimality of ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is the first of its kind,the computational issue has been addressed for some specific queries, such as MEDIAN (but ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is not neighborhood-optimal for MEDIAN!),triangle counting [29],and $t$ -star counting [23],the latter two being special cases of CQs (with inequalities). However, it is still an open problem whether ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ can be computed in polynomial time for an arbitrary CQ in terms of data complexity. To dodge the computational difficulty,elastic sensitivity ${ES}\left( \cdot \right) \left\lbrack  {22}\right\rbrack$ was introduced as a replacement. However,in Appendix $\mathrm{C}$ we show that ${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$ is not even worst-case optimal. Residual sensitivity ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ was proposed in [13],and it has been shown to be a constant-factor approximation of ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ while being polynomially computable. However, ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ as defined in [13] does not allow self-joins, which are challenging under DP, since changing one tuple corresponds to changing multiple logical relations when there are self-joins. This can be tricky for both the privacy guarantee and the mechanism's optimality. Second, no lower bound was provided in [13],so the optimality of ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is not known until this paper. Thirdly,we extend ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ to CQs with predicates and non-full CQs.

我们的研究建立在平滑敏感度${SS}\left( \cdot \right) \left\lbrack  {29}\right\rbrack$与残差敏感度${RS}\left( \cdot \right) \left\lbrack  {13}\right\rbrack$之上。尽管文献[29]已证明${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$能保持差分隐私(DP)，但仍存在两个问题：该方法是否（在任何意义上）最优，以及能否在多项式时间内计算。针对合取查询(CQs)场景，我们给出了这两个问题的肯定答案。虽然${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$的最优性属首次证明，但计算问题已在特定查询中得到解决，例如中位数查询(MEDIAN)（但${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$对MEDIAN并非邻域最优！）、三角形计数[29]和$t$-星型计数[23]——后两者是带不等式的合取查询特例。然而，就数据复杂度而言，任意合取查询能否在多项式时间内计算${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$仍是悬而未决的问题。为规避计算难题，弹性敏感度${ES}\left( \cdot \right) \left\lbrack  {22}\right\rbrack$被提出作为替代方案。但我们在附录$\mathrm{C}$中证明${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$甚至不具备最坏情况最优性。残差敏感度${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$由文献[13]提出，已被证明是${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$的常数因子近似且支持多项式计算。然而，文献[13]定义的${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$不允许自连接操作——这在差分隐私下具有挑战性，因为当存在自连接时，修改单个元组相当于同时修改多个逻辑关系，这对隐私保证和机制最优性都构成难题。其次，文献[13]未提供下界证明，因此直到本文才明确${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$的最优性。第三，我们将${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$扩展至支持谓词和非全合取查询。

The DP policy considered in this paper treats a change as the insertion, deletion, or substitution of a tuple, hence also called tuple-DP. In user-DP [26, 34], there is a designated primary private relation ${R}_{P} \in  \mathbf{R}$ that contains all the users,while tuples in other relations with a foreign key (FK) referencing the primary key (PK) of a tuple (user) ${t}_{P} \in  \mathbf{I}\left( {R}_{P}\right)$ ,directly or indirectly,are considered as data belonging to ${t}_{P}$ . Two instances $\mathbf{I}$ and ${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$ are considered neighbors if ${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$ can be obtained from $\mathbf{I}$ by adding/deleting/substituting a set of tuples,all of which reference the same user ${t}_{P} \in  \mathbf{I}\left( {R}_{P}\right)$ . Applying user-DP and tuple-DP on the graph schema $\mathbf{R} = \{ \operatorname{Node}\left( \mathrm{{ID}}\right)$ , Edge(src, dst)\}, where src and dst are both FKs referencing ID, yields the two well-known DP policies for graph data: node-DP [24] and edge-DP [23]. By designating Node as the primary private relation,user-DP on $\mathbf{R}$ becomes node-DP; by setting ${\mathbf{R}}_{P} = \{$ Edge $\}$ , tuple-DP becomes edge-DP (note that in tuple-DP, we ignore the FK constraints, which effectively means that Node is irrelevant). User-DP is more general than tuple-DP, hence potentially incurring a higher privacy cost. Very recently, a logarithmic-neighborhood optimal mechanism has been proposed for CQs under user-DP [12]. Meanwhile,it has also been shown that $O\left( 1\right)$ -neighborhood optimality is not achievable under user-DP [14] even for the simple query ${R}_{1}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right)$ where ${R}_{1}$ is the primary private relation and ${R}_{2}.{x}_{1}$ is an FK referencing ${R}_{1}.{x}_{1}$ . Therefore,combined with the result obtained in this paper, we now have a separation for CQs under tuple-DP and user-DP.

本文考虑的差分隐私(DP)策略将数据变更视为元组的插入、删除或替换，故也称为元组级差分隐私(tuple-DP)。在用户级差分隐私(user-DP)[26,34]中，存在一个指定的主私有关系${R}_{P} \in  \mathbf{R}$包含所有用户，而其他关系中通过外键(FK)直接或间接引用该主键(PK)的元组${t}_{P} \in  \mathbf{I}\left( {R}_{P}\right)$，均被视为属于${t}_{P}$的数据。若实例$\mathbf{I}$能通过添加/删除/替换一组引用同一用户${t}_{P} \in  \mathbf{I}\left( {R}_{P}\right)$的元组转换为${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$，则二者被视为相邻数据集。将user-DP和tuple-DP应用于图模式$\mathbf{R} = \{ \operatorname{Node}\left( \mathrm{{ID}}\right)$Edge(src,dst)}（其中src和dst均为引用ID的外键），可得到图数据中两种著名的DP策略：节点级差分隐私(node-DP)[24]和边级差分隐私(edge-DP)[23]。当指定Node为主私有关系时，$\mathbf{R}$上的user-DP即成为node-DP；若设置${\mathbf{R}}_{P} = \{$Edge$\}$，则tuple-DP转化为edge-DP（注意在tuple-DP中忽略FK约束意味着Node无关紧要）。User-DP比tuple-DP更具普适性，因此可能导致更高的隐私代价。最近文献[12]提出了user-DP下合取查询(CQs)的对数邻域最优机制，但同时研究[14]表明，即使对于${R}_{1}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{2}}\right)$这样的简单查询（其中${R}_{1}$是主私有关系，${R}_{2}.{x}_{1}$是引用${R}_{1}.{x}_{1}$的外键），user-DP下也无法实现$O\left( 1\right)$邻域最优性。因此结合本文结论，我们现已对tuple-DP和user-DP下的CQs完成了理论分离。

## 2 PRELIMINARIES

## 2 基础知识

### 2.1 Conjunctive Queries

### 2.1 合取查询

Let $\mathbf{R}$ be a database schema. A full conjunctive query(CQ)has the form

设$\mathbf{R}$为数据库模式，完全合取查询(CQ)具有如下形式

$$
q \mathrel{\text{:=}} {R}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{1}\right)  \bowtie  \cdots  \bowtie  {R}_{n}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{n}\right) ,
$$

where ${R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{n}$ are relation names in $\mathbf{R}$ ,and each ${\mathbf{x}}_{i}$ is a set of $\operatorname{arity}\left( {R}_{i}\right)$ variables/attributes ${}^{1}$ . We call each ${R}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$ an atom. We use $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ to denote $\{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$ ,and $\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack   = \{ i,\ldots ,j\}$ . For any $E \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ , $\bar{E} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   - E$ . For a variable $x$ ,we use $\operatorname{dom}\left( x\right)$ to denote the domain of $x$ . For $\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right)$ ,let $\mathbf{{dom}}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  = \mathbf{{dom}}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  \times  \cdots  \times  \mathbf{{dom}}\left( {x}_{k}\right)$ . Let $\operatorname{var}\left( q\right)$ denote the set of variables in $q$ .

其中${R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{n}$是$\mathbf{R}$中的关系名，每个${\mathbf{x}}_{i}$是一组$\operatorname{arity}\left( {R}_{i}\right)$变量/属性${}^{1}$。我们将每个${R}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$称为原子。使用$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$表示$\{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$和$\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack   = \{ i,\ldots ,j\}$。对于任意$E \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，$\bar{E} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   - E$。对于变量$x$，用$\operatorname{dom}\left( x\right)$表示其定义域。对于$\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right)$，令$\mathbf{{dom}}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  = \mathbf{{dom}}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  \times  \cdots  \times  \mathbf{{dom}}\left( {x}_{k}\right)$。用$\operatorname{var}\left( q\right)$表示$q$中的变量集合。

When considering self-joins,there can be repeats,i.e., ${R}_{i} = {R}_{j}$ . In this case,we assume ${\mathbf{x}}_{i} \neq  {\mathbf{x}}_{j}$ ; otherwise one of the two atoms is redundant. Let $\mathbf{I}$ be a database instance over $\mathbf{R}$ . For a relation name $R \in  \mathbf{R}$ ,let $\mathbf{I}\left( R\right)$ denote the relation instance of $R$ . We use ${I}_{i}$ as a shorthand for $\mathbf{I}\left( {R}_{i}\right)$ . I and the ${I}_{i}$ ’s are called physical instances. On the other hand,we use ${I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$ to denote ${I}_{i}$ after renaming its attributes to ${\mathbf{x}}_{i}$ . The ${I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$ ’s are called the logical instances. Note that if ${R}_{i}$ and ${R}_{j}$ are the same relation name,then ${I}_{i} = {I}_{j}$ ,but ${I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)  \neq  {I}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{j}\right)$ ,as ${I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$ and ${I}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{j}\right)$ have different attributes. For a self-join-free query, we may without loss of generality assume that ${\mathbf{x}}_{i} = \operatorname{sort}\left( {R}_{i}\right)$ for all $i \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ so the logical instances are the same as the physical instances, but for queries with self-joins, one physical relation instance may correspond to multiple logical relation instances. This distinction makes the problem more difficult under DP, as the distance between two logical instances may be larger than between the physical instances.

考虑自连接时可能出现重复，即${R}_{i} = {R}_{j}$。此时我们假设${\mathbf{x}}_{i} \neq  {\mathbf{x}}_{j}$，否则两个原子中有一个是冗余的。设$\mathbf{I}$为$\mathbf{R}$上的数据库实例。对于关系名$R \in  \mathbf{R}$，用$\mathbf{I}\left( R\right)$表示$R$的关系实例。${I}_{i}$是$\mathbf{I}\left( {R}_{i}\right)$的简写形式。I和${I}_{i}$被称为物理实例。另一方面，用${I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$表示将其属性重命名为${\mathbf{x}}_{i}$后的${I}_{i}$。${I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$被称为逻辑实例。注意若${R}_{i}$与${R}_{j}$是相同关系名，则${I}_{i} = {I}_{j}$，但${I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)  \neq  {I}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{j}\right)$，因为${I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$和${I}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{j}\right)$具有不同属性。对于无自连接查询，可假设所有$i \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$满足${\mathbf{x}}_{i} = \operatorname{sort}\left( {R}_{i}\right)$，此时逻辑实例等同于物理实例；但对于含自连接的查询，一个物理关系实例可能对应多个逻辑关系实例。这种差异使得DP下的问题更复杂，因为两个逻辑实例间的距离可能大于物理实例间的距离。

### 2.2 Differential Privacy in Relational Databases

### 2.2 关系数据库中的差分隐私

Differential privacy is already defined in (1). This notion can be applied to any problem by properly defining the distance function $d\left( {\cdot , \cdot  }\right)$ . As a database may contain both public and private relations, we use a more refined definition of $d\left( {\cdot , \cdot  }\right)$ . For two relation instances over the same relation name $I,{I}^{\prime }$ ,we use $d\left( {I,{I}^{\prime }}\right)$ to denote the distance between $I$ and ${I}^{\prime }$ ,which is the minimum number of steps to change $I$ into ${I}^{\prime }$ . Note that $d\left( {{I}_{j},{I}_{j}^{\prime }}\right)$ is the same for all $j \in  {D}_{i}$ ,for any $i \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ ,as they are the same physical relation instance.

差分隐私已在(1)中定义。通过正确定义距离函数$d\left( {\cdot , \cdot  }\right)$，这一概念可应用于任何问题。由于数据库可能同时包含公共和私有关系，我们使用更精细的$d\left( {\cdot , \cdot  }\right)$定义。对于相同关系名$I,{I}^{\prime }$上的两个关系实例，用$d\left( {I,{I}^{\prime }}\right)$表示$I$与${I}^{\prime }$之间的距离，即将$I$变为${I}^{\prime }$所需的最小步骤数。注意对于所有$j \in  {D}_{i}$和任意$i \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$，$d\left( {{I}_{j},{I}_{j}^{\prime }}\right)$都相同，因为它们属于同一物理关系实例。

We use ${P}^{m} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ to denote the set of private physical relations,while ${P}^{n} = { \cup  }_{i \in  {P}^{m}}{D}_{i}$ is the set of private logical relations. Let ${m}_{P} = \left| {P}^{m}\right| ,{n}_{P} = \left| {P}^{n}\right|$ . Two database instances can only differ in the private relations,i.e., $d\left( {{I}_{j},{I}_{j}^{\prime }}\right)  = 0$ for every $j \in  {\bar{P}}^{n}$ . In the DP definition (1), we must use the distance between the physical database instances,i.e., $d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }}d\left( {{I}_{{l}_{i}},{I}_{{l}_{i}}^{\prime }}\right)$ . Note that the distance between the logical instances,namely $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}d\left( {{I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right) ,{I}_{i}^{\prime }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right) }\right)$ ,can be larger than $d\left( {\mathrm{\;I},{\mathrm{I}}^{\prime }}\right)$ when self-joins are present.

我们用${P}^{m} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$表示私有物理关系集合，${P}^{n} = { \cup  }_{i \in  {P}^{m}}{D}_{i}$表示私有逻辑关系集合。设${m}_{P} = \left| {P}^{m}\right| ,{n}_{P} = \left| {P}^{n}\right|$。两个数据库实例只能在私有关系上存在差异，即对于每个$j \in  {\bar{P}}^{n}$有$d\left( {{I}_{j},{I}_{j}^{\prime }}\right)  = 0$。在差分隐私定义(1)中，必须使用物理数据库实例间的距离，即$d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }}d\left( {{I}_{{l}_{i}},{I}_{{l}_{i}}^{\prime }}\right)$。需注意当存在自连接时，逻辑实例间的距离$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}d\left( {{I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right) ,{I}_{i}^{\prime }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right) }\right)$可能大于$d\left( {\mathrm{\;I},{\mathrm{I}}^{\prime }}\right)$。

A simple but important observation is that a query with self-joins on instance ${\left\{  {I}_{i}\right\}  }_{i}$ can be considered as a query without self-joins on instance ${\left\{  {I}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right) \right\}  }_{i}$ . This allows us to reuse some of the technical results from [13] on self-join-free queries. However, the critical difference is that this conversion enlarges the distance, while the DP guarantee must hold with respect to the distance on the original, physical instance.

一个简单但重要的观察是：实例${\left\{  {I}_{i}\right\}  }_{i}$上带自连接的查询，可视为实例<b1></b1]上无自连接的查询。这使得我们能复用[13]中关于无自连接查询的部分技术成果。但关键区别在于，这种转换会扩大距离，而差分隐私保证必须基于原始物理实例的距离。

### 2.3 Sensitivity-based DP Mechanisms

### 2.3 基于敏感度的差分隐私机制

As mentioned in Section 1, the most common technique for designing DP mechanisms is to first compute some measure of sensitivity $S\left( \cdot \right)$ of the query,and then add noise drawn from a certain distribution calibrated to $S\left( \cdot \right)$ .

如第1节所述，设计差分隐私机制最常见的技术是首先计算查询的敏感度$S\left( \cdot \right)$度量，然后添加根据$S\left( \cdot \right)$校准的特定分布生成的噪声。

The local sensitivity of $q$ at instance $\mathrm{I}$ is how much $\left| {q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right|$ can change if we change one tuple in I, i.e.,

实例$\mathrm{I}$中$q$的局部敏感度是指当我们改变I中的一个元组时$\left| {q\left( \mathrm{I}\right) }\right|$能变化的最大幅度，即

$$
{LS}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{{{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime },d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = 1}}\left| \right| q\left( \mathbf{I}\right) \left| -\right| q\left( {\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }\right) \parallel . \tag{3}
$$

However,using ${LS}\left( \cdot \right)$ directly breaches privacy [29],because two neighboring instances may have very different ${LS}\left( \cdot \right)$ . On the other hand, it is safe to use the global sensitivity, which is the maximum ${LS}\left( \cdot \right)$ over all instances:

然而直接使用${LS}\left( \cdot \right)$会破坏隐私[29]，因为两个相邻实例可能具有完全不同的${LS}\left( \cdot \right)$。另一方面，使用全局敏感度是安全的，即所有实例中${LS}\left( \cdot \right)$的最大值：

$$
{GS} = \mathop{\max }\limits_{\mathbf{I}}{LS}\left( \mathbf{I}\right) .
$$

It is well known that one can achieve $\varepsilon$ -DP with $\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)  =$ $O\left( {GS}\right)$ by drawing noise from the Laplace distribution calibrated to ${GS}/\varepsilon \left\lbrack  {16}\right\rbrack$ . However,this loses the utility: the ${GS}$ for $\mathrm{{CQs}}$ can be as large as $O\left( {N}^{{n}_{P} - 1}\right)$ under relaxed DP,and $\infty$ under strict DP.

众所周知，通过从根据${GS}/\varepsilon \left\lbrack  {16}\right\rbrack$校准的拉普拉斯分布中提取噪声，可以实现$\varepsilon$-差分隐私。但这种方法会损失效用：在宽松差分隐私下$\mathrm{{CQs}}$的${GS}$可能高达$O\left( {N}^{{n}_{P} - 1}\right)$，在严格差分隐私下则可能达到$\infty$。

To address this issue, Nissim et al. [29] propose the smooth sensitivity $S{S}_{\beta }\left( \cdot \right)$ . To define $S{S}_{\beta }\left( \cdot \right)$ ,we first define the local sensitivity at distance $k$ :

$$

$$

Note that for CQs, (4) can be rewritten as

注意对于CQ，(4)式可以改写为

$$
L{S}^{\left( k\right) }\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{{{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime },d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = k}}{LS}\left( {\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }\right) , \tag{5}
$$

because if $d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  < k$ ,we can always insert dummy tuples so that $d\left( {\mathbf{I},{\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }}\right)  = k$ . Then for a parameter $\beta$ ,the smooth sensitivity is

$$

It is clear that $S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  \leq  {GS}$ . More importantly, $S{S}_{\beta }\left( \cdot \right)$ is "smooth", in the sense that $S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  \leq  {e}^{\beta }S{S}_{\beta }\left( {\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }\right)$ for any two neighboring instances $\mathbf{I}$ and ${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$ . Due to its smoothness,it has been shown that setting $\beta  = \varepsilon /{10}$ and drawing noise calibrated to $S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right) /\beta$ from a general Cauchy distribution,which has pdf $h\left( z\right)  \propto  \frac{1}{1 + {\left| z\right| }^{4}}$ ,achieves $\varepsilon$ -DP with $\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)  = \frac{S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right) }{\beta } = O\left( {S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right) }\right)$ . The choice of the constant 10 is arbitrary, which affects the tail properties of the noise distribution, but not the variance (asymptotically). In the rest of the paper,we omit the subscript $\beta$ from $S{S}_{\beta }\left( \cdot \right)$ for brevity.

显然$S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  \leq  {GS}$。更重要的是$S{S}_{\beta }\left( \cdot \right)$具有"平滑性"，即对于任意相邻实例$\mathbf{I}$和${\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }$都满足$S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right)  \leq  {e}^{\beta }S{S}_{\beta }\left( {\mathbf{I}}^{\prime }\right)$。由于其平滑特性，研究表明设定$\beta  = \varepsilon /{10}$并从广义柯西分布（其概率密度函数为$h\left( z\right)  \propto  \frac{1}{1 + {\left| z\right| }^{4}}$）中提取根据$S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right) /\beta$校准的噪声，能以$\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)  = \frac{S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right) }{\beta } = O\left( {S{S}_{\beta }\left( \mathbf{I}\right) }\right)$实现$\varepsilon$-差分隐私。常数10的选择是任意的，它影响噪声分布的尾部特性但不改变方差（渐近意义上）。后文为简洁起见将省略$S{S}_{\beta }\left( \cdot \right)$中的下标$\beta$。

## 3 完全CQ的残差敏感度

### 3.1 Residual Queries

### 3.1 残差查询

The residual sensitivity ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ can be considered as an instantiation of $\widehat{SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ for CQs,i.e.,it is defined as in (7) with a particular choice of ${\widehat{LS}}^{\left( k\right) }\left( \cdot \right)$ that has the smoothness property (8). ## 5 CQs WITH PREDICATES

## 5 含谓词的CQ

A CQ with predicates (CQP) has the form

含谓词的CQ（CQP）具有如下形式：

$$
q \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\sigma }_{{P}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{1}\right)  \land  \cdots  \land  {P}_{\kappa }\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{\kappa }\right) }\left( {{R}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{1}\right)  \bowtie  \cdots  \bowtie  {R}_{n}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{n}\right) }\right) ,
$$

where each ${P}_{j} : \operatorname{dom}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)  \rightarrow  \{$ True,False $\}$ is a computable function for some ${\mathbf{y}}_{j} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( q\right)  = {\mathbf{x}}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {\mathbf{x}}_{n}$ . By a slight abuse of notation, we also use $P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$ to denote the (possibly infinite) relation $\{ t \in$ $\operatorname{dom}\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  \mid  P\left( t\right) \}$ . This way,a CQP can be written as a normal CQ:

其中每个${P}_{j} : \operatorname{dom}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)  \rightarrow  \{$真值函数$\}$都是关于${\mathbf{y}}_{j} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( q\right)  = {\mathbf{x}}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {\mathbf{x}}_{n}$的可计算函数。在不引起歧义的情况下，我们用$P\left( \mathbf{y}\right)$表示（可能无限的）关系$\{ t \in$$\operatorname{dom}\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  \mid  P\left( t\right) \}$。因此，CQP可写作标准CQ形式：

$$
q \mathrel{\text{:=}} {R}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{1}\right)  \bowtie  \cdots  \bowtie  {R}_{n}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{n}\right)  \bowtie  {P}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{1}\right)  \bowtie  \cdots  \bowtie  {P}_{\kappa }\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{\kappa }\right) . \tag{17}
$$

Note that the ${P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)$ ’s are all public,since they only depend on the query and the domain, not on the instance.

注意${P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)$均为公开信息，因其仅取决于查询语句和定义域，而与具体实例无关。

The current approach to dealing with a CQP under DP $\left\lbrack  {{13},{22},{26}}\right\rbrack$ is to evaluate the CQP as given, but compute the sensitivity without considering the predicates. This yields a valid DP mechanism, but loses optimality. To see this, just consider an extreme case where a predicate always returns False. Then the query becomes a trivial query and the optimal (under any notion of optimality) mechanism is $\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)  \equiv  0$ ,i.e., $\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)  = 0$ for all $\mathbf{I}$ ,but the sensitivity of the query without the predicate must be nonzero.

当前处理差分隐私(DP)下条件查询谓词(CQP)的方法，是直接评估给定的CQP但在计算敏感度时不考虑谓词条件。这虽然能得到有效的DP机制，却牺牲了最优性。举例说明：假设存在一个始终返回False的谓词，此时查询退化为平凡查询，其最优机制应为$\mathcal{M}\left( \cdot \right)  \equiv  0$（即对所有$\mathbf{I}$都有$\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathbf{I}}\right)  = 0$），但忽略谓词计算的查询敏感度必然非零。

In this section,we show how to extend ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ to CQPs while preserving its $O\left( 1\right)$ -neighborhood optimality. The idea is simple, we just consider a CQP as a CQ as defined in (17), so optimality immediately follows from Theorem 1.2. The issue, however, is how to compute ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ when some relations are infinite. In Section 5.1 we first give a general algorithm, which may take exponential time,to compute ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ for arbitrary predicates under the technical condition of Theorem 1.3; in Section 5.2 we give a polynomial-time algorithm for the case where all the predicates are inequalities or comparisons, which proves the second part of Theorem 1.3.

本节将展示如何扩展${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$至CQPs并保持其$O\left( 1\right)$-邻域最优性。核心思路很简单：将CQP视为式(17)定义的常规查询(CQ)，其最优性可直接由定理1.2得出。关键难点在于当某些关系为无限集时如何计算${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$。5.1节首先给出通用算法（可能需指数时间），在定理1.3技术条件下计算任意谓词的${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$；5.2节针对谓词均为不等式比较的情况，给出多项式时间算法，由此证明定理1.3第二部分。

### 5.2 Comparison and Inequality Predicates

### 5.2 比较谓词与不等式谓词

For CQPs where the predicates are inequalities or comparisons, we may without loss of generality assume that the domain of all attributes in ${\mathbf{y}}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {\mathbf{y}}_{\kappa }$ is $\mathbb{Z}$ . We show in this subsection how to reduce the running time of the algorithm to $\operatorname{poly}\left( N\right)$ in this case. Let $\rho  = \left| {\partial {q}_{\bar{F}}^{2}}\right|$ . Then ${t}_{2}$ takes values from ${\mathbb{Z}}^{\rho }$ . The key to an efficient algorithm is thus to reduce this domain, and then apply the algorithm in $\left\lbrack  {3,{21}}\right\rbrack$ .

对于谓词为不等式或比较运算的CQP，不妨设${\mathbf{y}}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {\mathbf{y}}_{\kappa }$中所有属性的定义域为$\mathbb{Z}$。本节将算法时间复杂度降至$\operatorname{poly}\left( N\right)$。令$\rho  = \left| {\partial {q}_{\bar{F}}^{2}}\right|$，则${t}_{2}$取值自${\mathbb{Z}}^{\rho }$。高效算法的关键在于缩小该定义域后，应用$\left\lbrack  {3,{21}}\right\rbrack$中的算法。

To reduce the domain of ${t}_{2}$ ,one natural idea is to only consider the active domain [1]. Let ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$ be the set of integers appearing in $\mathbf{I}$ on attributes ${\mathbf{y}}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {\mathbf{y}}_{\kappa }$ ,and let ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( q\right)$ be the set of integers appearing in the predicates of $q$ . Then the active domain is ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathrm{I}}\right)  = {\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( q\right)  \cup  {\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( \mathrm{I}\right)  \cup  \{  - \infty ,\infty \}$ . However,only considering ${t}_{2} \in  {\left( {\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( q,\mathrm{I}\right) \right) }^{\rho }$ is not enough; Appendix E gives an example showing that ${T}_{\bar{E}}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$ may attain its maximum at some value between two consecutive values in the active domain. Thus, we augment the active domain to ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( {q,\mathrm{I}}\right)$ ,as follows. Let ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathrm{I},i}\right)$ be the $i$ th elements in ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right)$ in order. ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right)$ includes all elements in ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I},i}\right)$ ,plus ${2\kappa }$ arbitrary distinct elements between ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I},i}\right)$ and ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I},i + 1}\right)$ for all $i \in  \left\lbrack  {\left| {{\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right) }\right|  - 1}\right\rbrack$ . If there are less than ${2\kappa }$ elements between ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I},i}\right)$ and ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I},i + 1}\right)$ ,all elements in between are included.

为缩小${t}_{2}$的定义域，一个自然的思路是仅考虑活跃域[1]。设${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$为$\mathbf{I}$在属性${\mathbf{y}}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {\mathbf{y}}_{\kappa }$上出现的整数集合，${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( q\right)$为$q$谓词中出现的整数集合，则活跃域为${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathrm{I}}\right)  = {\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( q\right)  \cup  {\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( \mathrm{I}\right)  \cup  \{  - \infty ,\infty \}$。然而仅考虑${t}_{2} \in  {\left( {\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( q,\mathrm{I}\right) \right) }^{\rho }$并不足够，附录E的示例表明${T}_{\bar{E}}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)$可能在活跃域中两个相邻值之间的某点取得最大值。因此我们将活跃域扩展为${\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( {q,\mathrm{I}}\right)$，具体方法如下：令${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathrm{I},i}\right)$表示${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right)$中第$i$个有序元素，${\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right)$包含${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I},i}\right)$的所有元素，以及每对${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I},i}\right)$与${\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I},i + 1}\right)$之间任意${2\kappa }$个相异元素（对所有$i \in  \left\lbrack  {\left| {{\mathbb{Z}}^{ * }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right) }\right|  - 1}\right\rbrack$）。若其间元素不足${2\kappa }$个，则纳入全部中间元素。

We show that it suffices to use ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right)$ as the domain of ${t}_{2}$ .

我们证明使用${\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right)$作为${t}_{2}$的定义域已足够。

LEMMA 5.4. When all the predicates are inequalities and comparisons,

引理5.4. 当所有谓词均为不等式及比较运算时

$$
{T}_{\bar{E}}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{\substack{{{t}_{1} \in  {\pi }_{\partial {q}_{\bar{E}}^{1}}{q}_{\bar{E}}^{ \circ  }\left( \mathrm{I}\right) } \\  {{t}_{2} \in  {\left( {\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( q,\mathrm{I}\right) \right) }^{\rho }} }}\left| {{q}_{\bar{E}}^{ \circ  }\left( \mathrm{I}\right)  \bowtie  \left( {{ \bowtie  }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  \kappa \right\rbrack  }{P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right) }\right)  \bowtie  {t}_{1} \bowtie  {t}_{2}}\right| . \tag{21}
$$

Since ${\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right)  = O\left( {N + \kappa }\right)  = O\left( N\right)$ ,we can simply materialize each ${P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)$ into $\left\{  {t \in  {\left( {\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( q,\mathbf{I}\right) \right) }^{2} \mid  {P}_{j}\left( t\right) }\right\}$ ,which has size $O\left( {N}^{2}\right)$ . 由于${\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( {q,\mathbf{I}}\right)  = O\left( {N + \kappa }\right)  = O\left( N\right)$，我们可以直接将每个${P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)$物化为大小为$O\left( {N}^{2}\right)$的$\left\{  {t \in  {\left( {\mathbb{Z}}^{ + }\left( q,\mathbf{I}\right) \right) }^{2} \mid  {P}_{j}\left( t\right) }\right\}$。因此，使用$\left\lbrack  {3,{21}}\right\rbrack$中的算法计算(21)同样只需多项式时间，至此我们完成了定理1.3第二部分的证明。

As a practical improvement,observe that if a variable $y \in  \partial {q}_{\bar{F}}^{2}$ is only involved in inequality predicates, then it can always take a value such that all these inequalities hold. Thus, there is no need to materialize these predicates. In particular, we arrive at a simpler formula for computing ${T}_{\bar{E}}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ when all predicates are inequalities, e.g., graph pattern counting queries.

作为一项实际改进，可以观察到若变量$y \in  \partial {q}_{\bar{F}}^{2}$仅参与不等式谓词，则该变量总能取值满足所有不等式。因此无需具体化这些谓词。特别地，当所有谓词均为不等式时（如图模式计数查询），我们可推导出更简化的${T}_{\bar{E}}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$计算公式。

COROLLARY 5.5. For a CQP where all predicates are inequalities,

推论5.5. 对于所有谓词皆为不等式的CQP，

$$
{T}_{\bar{E}}\left( \mathrm{I}\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{{{t}_{1} \in  \operatorname{dom}\left( {\partial {q}_{\bar{E}}^{1}}\right) }}\left| {{q}_{\bar{E}}^{ \circ  }\left( \mathrm{I}\right)  \bowtie  \left( {{ \bowtie  }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  \kappa \right\rbrack  ,{\mathbf{y}}_{j} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( {q}_{\bar{E}}^{ \circ  }\right) }{P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right) }\right)  \bowtie  {t}_{1}}\right| .
$$

To compute ${T}_{\bar{E}}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ ,we compute ${q}_{\bar{E}}^{ \circ  }\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$ ,apply all predicates ${P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)$ ) for $j \in  \left\lbrack  \kappa \right\rbrack  ,{\mathbf{y}}_{j} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( {q}_{\bar{E}}^{ \circ  }\right)$ ),do a count group-by $\partial {q}_{\bar{E}}^{1}$ ,and return the maximum count.

计算${T}_{\bar{E}}\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$时，先计算${q}_{\bar{E}}^{ \circ  }\left( \mathbf{I}\right)$，应用$j \in  \left\lbrack  \kappa \right\rbrack  ,{\mathbf{y}}_{j} \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( {q}_{\bar{E}}^{ \circ  }\right)$的所有谓词${P}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)$，执行按$\partial {q}_{\bar{E}}^{1}$的分组计数，最后返回最大计数值。

## 7 PRACTICAL PERFORMANCE

## 7 实际性能

Besides its theoretical optimality, our DP mechanism also enjoys excellent practical performance compared with prior work. Some preliminary experimental results are provided in Appendix F, which show that the accuracy of ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is very close to that of ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ with order-of-magnitude reduction in computational cost. In fact, for most queries, ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is not even known to be polynomially computable. For these queries, ${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$ is the only known DP mechanism, and ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ offers drastic improvement in terms of utility. Finally,it is worth pointing out that our mechanism just requires the evaluation of a number of residual queries, whose results are then combined using a certain formula. Hence, it can be implemented in a relational DBMS easily (e.g., using PL/SQL or a plug-in).

除了理论上的最优性，我们的差分隐私(DP)机制相比先前工作还展现出卓越的实际性能。附录F提供的初步实验结果表明，${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$的准确度与${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$非常接近，同时计算成本降低了数量级。事实上，对于大多数查询而言，${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$甚至未被证明是多项式可计算的。针对这些查询，${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$是唯一已知的DP机制，而${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$在效用性上实现了显著提升。最后值得指出，我们的机制仅需评估若干残差查询，再通过特定公式组合其结果，因此可轻松在关系型数据库管理系统（如使用PL/SQL或插件）中实现。

## D NONEXISTENCE OF OPTIMAL DP MECHANISMS FOR CQs WITH ARBITRARY PREDICATES

## D 含任意谓词的CQs不存在最优差分隐私机制

We show that,when the $P\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)$ ’s are arbitrary (but still computable), it is undecidable to check if a given CQP is a trivial query. Recall that if the query is trivial,the optimal DP mechanism $\mathcal{M}$ is deterministic and achieves $\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathrm{I}}\right)  = 0$ for all $\mathrm{I}$ ; otherwise,the mechanism must be probabilistic. Since one cannot distinguish between the two cases, optimal (under any notion of optimality) DP mechanisms do not exist.

我们证明，当$P\left( {\mathbf{y}}_{j}\right)$为任意（但仍可计算）时，判定给定CQP是否为平凡查询是不可解的。需注意，若查询是平凡的，最优差分隐私机制$\mathcal{M}$将是确定性的，并对所有$\mathrm{I}$实现$\operatorname{Err}\left( {\mathcal{M},\mathrm{I}}\right)  = 0$；否则该机制必须为概率性。由于无法区分这两种情况，故不存在任何最优（无论采用何种最优性标准）的差分隐私机制。

For the undecidability result,just consider the simple CQP ${q}_{M} =$ $R\left( x\right)  \bowtie  {P}_{M}\left( x\right)$ ,where ${P}_{M}\left( x\right)  =$ True iff the Turing machine $M$ terminates in less than $x$ steps. Note that ${P}_{M}\left( x\right)$ is decidable. However, it is easy to see that $\left| {{q}_{M}\left( \cdot \right) }\right|  \equiv  0$ iff $M$ does not halt.

对于不可判定性结果，只需考虑简单CQP${q}_{M} =$$R\left( x\right)  \bowtie  {P}_{M}\left( x\right)$，其中${P}_{M}\left( x\right)  =$为真当且仅当图灵机$M$在$x$步内终止。注意${P}_{M}\left( x\right)$是可判定的。但显然$\left| {{q}_{M}\left( \cdot \right) }\right|  \equiv  0$成立当且仅当$M$不停止。

Our analysis in Section 4 shows that ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is at most a constant-factor larger than ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ ,both of which are $O\left( 1\right)$ -neighborhood optimal. At the same time, ${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$ does not have any optimal guarantee. In this section, we conduct an experimental study on the actual values of these sensitivities on some sub-graph counting queries over real-world graph data. Note that since the subsequent noise generation process is the same for all three sensitivity measures, it suffices to only compare the sensitivities instead of the ${\ell }_{2}$ -errors.

第4节分析表明，${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$至多是${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$的常数倍，二者均满足$O\left( 1\right)$-邻域最优性。同时${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$不具备任何最优性保证。本节我们通过实验研究这些敏感度在现实图数据子图计数查询中的实际取值。由于后续噪声生成过程对三种敏感度度量均相同，故仅需比较敏感度而非${\ell }_{2}$误差。

### F.1 Setup

### F.1 实验设置

Datasets. We use five graph network datasets: CondMat, AstroPh, HepPh, HepTh, and GrQc, which contain 23133, 18772, 12008, 9877, 5242 nodes and 186878, 396100, 236978, 51946, 28980 edges, respectively. These five datasets describe the collaboration between the authors on arXiv in Condensed Matter, Physics, High Energy Physics, High Energy Physics Theory and General Relativity categories. The datasets are obtained from SNAP [27]. The graphs are directed and we store all edges in a relation Edge(From, To).

数据集. 采用五个图网络数据集：CondMat（凝聚态物理）、AstroPh（天体物理）、HepPh（高能物理）、HepTh（高能物理理论）和GrQc（广义相对论），分别包含23133、18772、12008、9877、5242个节点和186878、396100、236978、51946、28980条边。这些数据集描述了arXiv上作者在相应领域的合作情况，数据来自SNAP[27]。图为有向图，所有边存储在Edge(From, To)关系中。

Queries. We experimented with 4 pattern counting queries as shown in Figure 2. We also added all inequalities between every two distinct variables. Since polynomial-time algorithms for computing ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ are known only for triangle counting [29] and $t$ -star counting [23],the results on ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ are available only on ${q}_{ \bigtriangleup  }$ and ${q}_{3 * }$ Note that the count returned by the graph pattern counting CQ is actually 3 times (resp. 6 times) the number of triangles and 3-stars in the graph,so we need to scale down ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ and ${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$ accordingly.

查询。我们实验了4种模式计数查询，如图2所示。同时为每对独立变量添加了所有不等式。由于目前仅针对三角形计数[29]和$t$星形计数[23]存在多项式时间算法，因此${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$的结果仅适用于${q}_{ \bigtriangleup  }$和${q}_{3 * }$。需注意，图模式计数CQ返回的数值实际是图中三角形数的3倍（对应3-星形时为6倍），故需相应调低${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$和${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$的数值。

Let’s first compare ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ and ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ . We see that ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is only $2\%$ larger than ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ in most cases,and the largest difference is 2 times for ${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ on the HepTh dataset. This shows that the constant factor derived in Lemma 4.8 is actually quite loose, and the practical utility of ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is close to that of ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ . On the other hand, ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is much more computational costly: the time for computing ${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$ is ${4.83} \sim  {145}$ times that of ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ .

首先比较${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$与${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$。可见在多数情况下${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$仅比${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$大$2\%$，最大差异出现在HepTh数据集的${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$上达2倍。这表明引理4.8推导的常数因子实际上相当宽松，且${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$的实用性与${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$相近。另一方面，${SS}\left( \cdot \right)$的计算成本高得多：其计算时间是${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$的${4.83} \sim  {145}$倍。

For the comparison between ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ and ${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$ ,for ${q}_{\Delta },{q}_{3 * }$ and ${q}_{2\Delta }$ , ${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$ is much larger than ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ . On ${q}_{3 * }$ ,all sensitivity measures are very close. This is because, for this query, the query result solely depends on the degrees (namely, this is an easy query), and so do all three sensitivities. Actually,the formulation of ${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$ essentially only makes use of the degree information, which can be verified by the fact that its values on ${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ and ${q}_{3 * }$ are equal. On the other hand, ${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$ and ${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$ exploits the actual structure of the graph.

关于${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$与${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$的对比，对于${q}_{\Delta },{q}_{3 * }$和${q}_{2\Delta }$，${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$显著大于${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$。而在${q}_{3 * }$上，所有敏感度度量值都非常接近。这是因为该查询结果仅取决于节点度数（即简单查询），三种敏感度亦然。实际上${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$的公式本质上仅利用度数信息，这可通过其在${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$与${q}_{3 * }$上的取值相等得以验证。而${RS}\left( \cdot \right)$和${ES}\left( \cdot \right)$则利用了图的实际结构。
