## Abstract

## 摘要

In addition to its theoretical interest, computing with circuits has found applications in many emerging domains. Yet, the exact circuit complexity of query evaluation had remained an unexplored topic. In this paper, we present circuit constructions for conjunctive queries under degree constraints, with polylogarithmic depth and size matching the polymatroid bound up to polylogarithmic factors. We also propose a definition of output-sensitive circuit families and obtain such circuits with sizes matching their RAM counterparts.

电路计算除理论价值外，已在诸多新兴领域获得应用。然而查询求值的精确电路复杂度仍是未解难题。本文提出在度约束条件下连接查询的电路构建方案，其多对数深度与规模匹配拟阵界至多对数因子。我们还定义了输出敏感的电路族，并实现了与RAM算法规模相匹配的电路构造。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

A classical result in database theory states that (the decision version of) any relational algebra query, when the query size is taken as a constant, is in NC, i.e., it can be solved by a circuit of polylog-arithmic depth and polynomial size (see e.g. Section 17.1 of [1]). While polynomial time/size used to be considered "efficient", many recent efforts have taken a more fine-grained approach, trying to determine the exact polynomial $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{k}\right)$ for a given query. In the past two decades, there has been tremendous progress in efficient RAM algorithms for conjunctive queries (CQs),and the optimal $k$ can be achieved in many cases $\left\lbrack  {3,{19},{28},{31}}\right\rbrack$ .

数据库理论的经典结论表明：当查询规模视为常数时，任何关系代数查询（决策版本）都属于NC类，即可通过具有多对数深度和多项式规模的电路求解（参见文献[1]第17.1节）。虽然多项式时间/规模曾被视为"高效"，但近年研究转向更细粒度分析，试图确定给定查询的精确多项式$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{k}\right)$。过去二十年间，连接查询(CQs)的RAM高效算法取得重大进展，多数情况下已能实现最优$k$$\left\lbrack  {3,{19},{28},{31}}\right\rbrack$。

Besides theoretical elegance, a circuit result is stronger than its counterparts in other models of computation: By Brent's Theorem [12],a circuit of size $W$ and depth $D$ can be evaluated on a $P$ - processor PRAM in $O\left( {W/P + D}\right)$ steps for any $P$ ,which in turn can be simulated in $O\left( W\right)$ steps on a single RAM. The opposite direction of both simulations is possible with a polynomial overhead [29], but it is a major open problem in complexity theory whether the overhead can be reduced to polylogarithmic in either case.

除理论价值外，电路模型的结果比其他计算模型更具优势：根据Brent定理[12]，规模$W$、深度$D$的电路可在$P$处理器PRAM上以$O\left( {W/P + D}\right)$步数求值，进而可在单RAM上以$O\left( W\right)$步数模拟。两种模拟的反向转换虽可能产生多项式开销[29]，但复杂度理论中尚存悬而未决的问题：是否可将开销降至多对数级别。

We see a gap here. The classical circuit construction [1] is actually quite naive,using $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{m}\right)$ gates where $m$ is the number of relations in the query; on the other hand, the recent worst-case efficient RAM algorithms have $k < m$ ,but they are all sequential algorithms, at least as written. There are some intuitive arguments that these algorithms have high levels of parallelism, but there is no formal proof. Notably, there have been a series of papers on query evaluation in the massively parallel computation (MPC) model $\left\lbrack  {{24},{26},{30}}\right\rbrack$ . Indeed,any MPC algorithm can be simulated on the PRAM, but we have not been able to match the best RAM running times with MPC algorithms for all CQs. In fact, a recent negative result [22] shows that, for some CQs, MPC is polynomially weaker than the RAM.

此处存在研究空白。经典电路构造[1]实际较为朴素，使用$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{m}\right)$个门电路（$m$为查询中关系数量）；而近期最坏情况高效的RAM算法虽具有$k < m$，但均为串行算法。虽有观点认为这些算法具有高度并行性，但缺乏形式化证明。值得注意的是，已有系列论文研究大规模并行计算(MPC)模型中的查询求值$\left\lbrack  {{24},{26},{30}}\right\rbrack$。虽然任何MPC算法都可在PRAM上模拟，但尚未实现所有CQs的MPC算法与最佳RAM运行时间匹配。事实上，近期负面结果[22]表明某些CQs在MPC模型中存在多项式级劣势。

We close this gap in this paper. Our main result is a circuit whose depth is polylogarithmic and whose size matches the polymatroid bound $\left\lbrack  {3,{19}}\right\rbrack$ ,which is the best known efficiently computable query size upper bound, up to polylogarithmic factors. By plugging this circuit into the generalized hypertree decomposition [20] framework, together with a new circuit version of the Yannakakis algorithm [34], we also obtain output-sensitive circuits with sizes matching their RAM counterparts ${}^{1}$ . Compared with the classical result [1], our new results more quantitatively formalize the intuition that CQs can be evaluated efficiently in parallel.

本文填补了这一空白。我们的主要成果是一个深度为多对数级、规模与多拟阵界$\left\lbrack  {3,{19}}\right\rbrack$相匹配的电路——该查询规模上界是目前已知最高效的可计算上限，仅相差多对数因子。通过将此电路嵌入广义超树分解[20]框架，并结合Yannakakis算法[34]的新型电路版本，我们还获得了输出敏感电路，其规模与对应的RAM实现${}^{1}$相当。相较于经典成果[1]，我们的新研究更量化地验证了"合取查询(CQs)可高效并行计算"这一直觉。

Beyond complexity theory, circuits of small depth and size have found applications in many emerging domains. We mention a few below.

除复杂性理论外，小深度与小规模电路已在诸多新兴领域得到应用。下文列举若干实例。

Query evaluation by hardware. Computations that require the best performance are often carried out using hardware. While traditionally it was expensive to build custom chips, new programmable hardware, such as PAL and FPGA, has made it much more economical, so one can build customized hardware for frequently asked queries. Here, the circuit size directly translates into the cost (both the fabrication cost and power consumption) of the hardware, while the depth corresponds to the time for query evaluation.

硬件化查询评估。对性能要求极高的计算常通过硬件实现。虽然传统定制芯片成本高昂，但PAL、FPGA等可编程硬件的出现大幅降低了成本，使得为高频查询定制硬件成为可能。此时电路规模直接对应硬件成本（包括制造成本与功耗），而深度则对应查询评估耗时。

Multi-party secure query evaluation. In the secure multi-party computation model [14], two or more users each hold some personal data, and they would like to collectively evaluate a function of their joint data, without revealing their own data to others. Nearly all generic protocols in this model, such as Yao's garbled circuits [35], ${GMW}\left\lbrack  {17}\right\rbrack$ ,and ${BGW}\left\lbrack  {11}\right\rbrack$ ,are based on expressing the function as a circuit. The communication and computation costs of these protocols are proportional to the size of the circuit, while the depth corresponds to the number of communication rounds.

多方安全查询评估。在安全多方计算模型[14]中，多个用户各自持有私有数据，希望在不泄露数据的前提下联合计算其数据的函数。该模型下几乎所有通用协议（如Yao混淆电路[35]${GMW}\left\lbrack  {17}\right\rbrack$和${BGW}\left\lbrack  {11}\right\rbrack$）都基于函数电路化实现。这些协议的通信与计算成本与电路规模成正比，深度则对应通信轮次。

Outsourced query evaluation. Suppose a client uploads her private data to a service provider (e.g., Amazon), and later asks queries on them. To protect her data, the client can encrypt them before uploading. To evaluate a query, the service provider needs to run a program on the encrypted data, which is now possible by homomorphic encryption $\left\lbrack  {{15},{16}}\right\rbrack$ . As homomorphic encryption handles data in a black-box fashion and the program cannot and should not see the plaintext, it cannot make any branching that depends on the actual data, namely, the program's access pattern must be oblivious to the input. There are two approaches towards obliviousness: The first is to use oblivious RAM (ORAM) [18], which translates each logical access into a polylogarithmic number of physical accesses to random locations of the memory. However, this translation must be done by the client (who knows the logical address to access), resulting in repeated interaction between the service provider and the client. On the other hand, if the program can be expressed as a circuit, which is oblivious by definition, then the process can be made much easier. In particular, evaluating the circuit requires no interaction, except sending the query at the beginning and receiving the query results (in encrypted form) in the end. Note that this requires the service provider to be able to generate the circuit from the given query and the input size, a property known as uniformity.

外包查询评估。假设客户将私有数据上传至服务商（如亚马逊）后发起查询。为保护数据，客户可预先加密数据。服务商需在加密数据上执行程序——这通过同态加密$\left\lbrack  {{15},{16}}\right\rbrack$实现。由于同态加密以黑盒方式处理数据，程序无法也不应接触明文，故不能进行依赖实际数据的分支操作，即程序访问模式必须与输入无关。实现这种无感知性有两种方法：一是使用茫然RAM(ORAM)[18]，将每次逻辑访问转化为对内存随机位置的多对数次物理访问，但该转换需由客户（知晓待访问逻辑地址）完成，导致服务商与客户需反复交互；二是将程序表达为电路（其定义即具无感知性），该方案仅需在开始时发送查询请求，最终接收加密结果，全程无需交互。此方案要求服务商能根据给定查询和输入规模生成电路，该特性称为均匀性。

## 2 RELATED WORK

## 2 相关工作

A number of worst-case efficient RAM algorithms for CQs have been proposed in the literature $\left\lbrack  {3,{28},{31}}\right\rbrack$ ; among them,we choose to "circuitize" the PANDA algorithm [3] because it can be turned into a relational circuit, as we show in Section 4.3. This allows us to focus on designing circuits for individual relational operators. Other algorithms are tuple based, and heavily rely on indexing to achieve the optimal running time, which has no corresponding circuit implementation. Another nice property of PANDA is that it naturally supports general degree constraints as opposed to only cardinality constraints.

文献中已提出多种针对CQ的最坏情况高效RAM算法$\left\lbrack  {3,{28},{31}}\right\rbrack$；我们选择将PANDA算法[3]进行"电路化"，因为如第4.3节所示，该算法可转换为关系电路。这使我们能专注于为单个关系运算符设计电路。其他算法基于元组，严重依赖索引实现最优运行时间，但缺乏对应的电路实现。PANDA的另一优势是天然支持广义度约束，而不仅限于基数约束。

Arasu and Kaushik [5] describe oblivious algorithms for common relational operators, including acyclic joins. However, as mentioned above, circuits directly translate to oblivious algorithms but not necessarily vice versa. Their model assumes the existence of a trusted module (TM) with $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ memory size,which allows the client to run some code on private data. Thus, ORAM simulation also works with a TM. Their only theoretical improvement over ORAM simulation is one logarithmic factor. But with the recent discovery of an optimal ORAM [6], this log-factor improvement disappears. ObliDB [13] provides the first end-to-end oblivious query processing system for general queries under the TM assumption, which also uses ORAM to further reduce the cost of some operators. We eliminate the need of a TM (more generally speaking, repeated interaction with the client) by expressing the query evaluation algorithm as a circuit.

Arasu和Kaushik[5]描述了包括无环连接在内的常见关系运算符的 oblivious算法。但如前所述，电路可直接转换为oblivious算法，反之则未必成立。他们的模型假设存在具有$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$内存的可信模块(TM)，允许客户端在私有数据上运行代码。因此ORAM模拟也适用于TM。相较于ORAM模拟，他们唯一的理论改进是降低了对数因子。但随着最优ORAM[6]的发现，这一对数优势已不复存在。ObliDB[13]在TM假设下首次实现了面向通用查询的端到端oblivious查询处理系统，该系统也使用ORAM进一步降低某些运算符成本。我们通过将查询评估算法表达为电路，消除了对TM（更广义地说，与客户端的重复交互）的需求。

SMCQL [10] is the first database system that supports query evaluation under the secure two-party computation model using Yao's garbled circuit. However, the circuit they use is the naive one of the size $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{m}\right)$ . Secure Yannakakis [32] improves the complexity to linear in the input size plus output size, when the query is a free-connex join-aggregate query. However, their protocol, in particular the one for joins, is not based on garbled circuits. Senate [33] is a query processing system under secure multi-party computation. Their protocol is not purely circuit-based, either.

SMCQL[10]是首个基于Yao混淆电路在安全两方计算模型下支持查询评估的数据库系统。但他们使用的电路是规模达$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{m}\right)$的原始电路。Secure Yannakakis[32]将复杂度改进为输入大小加输出大小的线性关系，但仅适用于自由连接聚合查询。其协议（特别是连接操作协议）并非基于混淆电路。Senate[33]是安全多方计算下的查询处理系统，其协议也非纯电路实现。

## 3 CONJUNCTIVE QUERIES

## 3 合取查询

### 3.1 Definition

### 3.1 定义

Let $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   = \{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$ . A conjunctive query (CQ) $Q$ is defined by a hypergraph $\mathcal{H}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right) ,\mathcal{E} \subseteq  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ and a non-negative integer $k \leq  n$ . Its variables are ${\left\{  {A}_{i}\right\}  }_{i = 1}^{n}$ . For each $F \in  \mathcal{E}$ ,let ${R}_{F}$ be a relation of arity $\left| F\right|$ . The query is:

设$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   = \{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$。合取查询(CQ)$Q$由超图$\mathcal{H}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right) ,\mathcal{E} \subseteq  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$和非负整数$k \leq  n$定义。其变量为${\left\{  {A}_{i}\right\}  }_{i = 1}^{n}$。对于每个$F \in  \mathcal{E}$，令${R}_{F}$为元数为$\left| F\right|$的关系。查询式为：

$$
Q\left( {{A}_{1},\cdots ,{A}_{k}}\right)  \leftarrow  \exists \left( {{A}_{k + 1},\cdots ,{A}_{n}}\right) \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{F \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{A}}_{F}\right) ,
$$

where ${\mathbf{A}}_{F}$ denotes the tuple ${\left( {A}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  F}.{A}_{1},\ldots ,{A}_{k}$ are called free variables and ${A}_{k + 1},\cdots ,{A}_{n}$ are the bound variables. When $k = n$ , i.e., the set of bound variables is empty, we call it a full query (FCQ). When $k = 0$ ,the query becomes a Boolean query (BCQ),which returns a Boolean value that indicates whether the result of FCQ is empty (false) or not (true).

其中${\mathbf{A}}_{F}$表示元组${\left( {A}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  F}.{A}_{1},\ldots ,{A}_{k}$称为自由变量，${A}_{k + 1},\cdots ,{A}_{n}$为绑定变量。当$k = n$（即绑定变量集为空）时称为完全查询(FCQ)。当$k = 0$时，查询退化为布尔查询(BCQ)，返回表示FCQ结果是否为空(false)的布尔值(true)。

We adopt the notion of data complexity, where the query size (i.e., the size of the hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ ) is considered a constant,and measure the complexity in terms of the data size (i.e., the relation cardinalities $\left| {R}_{F}\right|$ for $F \in  \mathcal{E}$ ). We assume that the tuples in the relations draw their values from the integer domain $\left\lbrack  u\right\rbrack$ . For simplicity we often ignore poly $\left( {\log \left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{F}\left| {R}_{F}\right| }\right) ,\log u,n}\right)$ factors,signified by the $\widetilde{O}\left( \cdot \right)$ notation.

我们采用数据复杂度的概念，其中查询规模（即超图$\mathcal{H}$的大小）被视为常数，并通过数据规模（即关系基数$\left| {R}_{F}\right|$对于$F \in  \mathcal{E}$）来衡量复杂度。假设关系中元组的值来自整数域$\left\lbrack  u\right\rbrack$。为简化起见，我们常忽略多项式$\left( {\log \left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{F}\left| {R}_{F}\right| }\right) ,\log u,n}\right)$因子，用$\widetilde{O}\left( \cdot \right)$符号表示。

In addition to the relation cardinalities, we also use degree constraints [3] to measure the complexity, which would yield much tighter bounds when available. For $X \subseteq  Y \in  \mathcal{E}$ and any $t \in  {\left\lbrack  u\right\rbrack  }^{\left| X\right| }$ , define $\deg \left( {Y \mid  t}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| {{\sigma }_{X = t}\left( {R}_{Y}\right) }\right|$ and $\deg \left( {Y \mid  X}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{t}\deg \left( {Y \mid  t}\right)$ . Then the 3-tuple $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$ represents the degree constraint $\deg \left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  \leq  {N}_{Y \mid  X}$ . Note that a cardinality constraint $\left| {R}_{Y}\right|  \leq  {N}_{Y}$ can be considered as a special degree constraint $\left( {\varnothing ,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  \varnothing } = {N}_{Y}}\right)$ , while a functional dependency ${\mathbf{A}}_{X} \rightarrow  {\mathbf{A}}_{Y}$ is also a special degree constraint $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X} = 1}\right)$ . Our definition is a slightly different from that in [3]. In their definition,they allow $Y \subseteq  F \in  \mathcal{E}$ . Our restriction $Y = F$ ,where $F \in  \mathcal{E}$ ,is without loss of generality: If there is a degree constraint on some $Y \subset  F \in  \mathcal{E}$ ,we just pre-compute ${R}_{Y} = {\Pi }_{Y}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$ ,and add it to the input relations. We also denote DC as the set of degree constraints given by the schema. All the degree constraints apply to the input relations; during our development, we may create new relations, and we use ${\deg }_{{R}_{Y}^{\prime }}\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{t}\left| {{\sigma }_{X = t}\left( {R}_{Y}^{\prime }\right) }\right|$ to denote the maximum degree from $X$ to $Y$ in some new relation ${R}_{Y}^{\prime }$ .

除关系基数外，我们还使用度约束[3]来度量复杂度，当可用时将产生更紧的界。对于$X \subseteq  Y \in  \mathcal{E}$和任意$t \in  {\left\lbrack  u\right\rbrack  }^{\left| X\right| }$，定义$\deg \left( {Y \mid  t}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| {{\sigma }_{X = t}\left( {R}_{Y}\right) }\right|$和$\deg \left( {Y \mid  X}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{t}\deg \left( {Y \mid  t}\right)$。三元组$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$表示度约束$\deg \left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  \leq  {N}_{Y \mid  X}$。注意基数约束$\left| {R}_{Y}\right|  \leq  {N}_{Y}$可视为特殊度约束$\left( {\varnothing ,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  \varnothing } = {N}_{Y}}\right)$，函数依赖${\mathbf{A}}_{X} \rightarrow  {\mathbf{A}}_{Y}$也是特殊度约束$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X} = 1}\right)$。我们的定义与文献[3]略有不同：其定义允许$Y \subseteq  F \in  \mathcal{E}$，而我们限制$Y = F$（其中$F \in  \mathcal{E}$）不失一般性——若存在对某些$Y \subset  F \in  \mathcal{E}$的度约束，只需预计算${R}_{Y} = {\Pi }_{Y}\left( {R}_{F}\right)$并加入输入关系。用DC表示模式给定的度约束集合，所有度约束作用于输入关系；在推导过程中可能创建新关系，用${\deg }_{{R}_{Y}^{\prime }}\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{t}\left| {{\sigma }_{X = t}\left( {R}_{Y}^{\prime }\right) }\right|$表示新关系${R}_{Y}^{\prime }$中从$X$到$Y$的最大度。

### 3.2 Output Size Bounds for FCQs

### 3.2 FCQ的输出规模上界

A central problem in database theory is to derive upper bounds for the output size of FCQs. The AGM bound is tight when there are only cardinality constraints [7]. However, when the database instance is subject to general degree constraints, the AGM bound is no longer tight nor the best possible. Instead, the following output size bounds have been introduced to take degree constraints into consideration:

数据库理论的核心问题是推导FCQ输出规模的上界。当仅存在基数约束时，AGM界是紧的[7]；但当数据库实例受一般度约束时，AGM界既非紧也非最优。为此引入以下考虑度约束的输出规模界：

Entropic bound. Given a query $Q$ defined by hypergraph $\mathcal{H} =$ $\left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$ ,a function $h : {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0}$ is said to be entropic if there is a joint distribution on $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ such that $h\left( F\right)$ is the marginal entropy on $F$ for any $F \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ . Let ${\Gamma }_{n}^{ * }$ be the set of entropic functions. Under degree constraints DC,we have the additional constraint $h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }$ $h\left( Y\right)  - h\left( X\right)  \leq  {n}_{Y \mid  X}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\log {N}_{Y \mid  X}$ for each $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}$ .

熵界(Entropic bound)。给定由超图$\mathcal{H} =$$\left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$定义的查询$Q$，若存在$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$上的联合分布使得对于任意$F \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，$h\left( F\right)$都是$F$的边际熵，则称函数$h : {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0}$为熵函数。记${\Gamma }_{n}^{ * }$为所有熵函数的集合。在度数约束DC下，我们对每个$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}$附加约束条件$h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }$$h\left( Y\right)  - h\left( X\right)  \leq  {n}_{Y \mid  X}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\log {N}_{Y \mid  X}$。

Denote

记

$$
\mathrm{{HDC}} = \left\{  {h : {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0} \mid  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}}}h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  \leq  {n}_{Y \mid  X}}\right\}  .
$$

Let $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$ be the output size of $Q$ on database instance $\mathbf{D}$ . The following is bound is known as the entropic bound [19], which is tight in the worst case [3]:

设$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$表示数据库实例$\mathbf{D}$上$Q$的输出规模。下述界被称为熵界[19]，在最坏情况下是紧致的[3]：

Polymatroid bound. Although the entropic bound is tight, it is not known how to compute it. To address this issue, Gottlob et al. [19] replace entropic functions with polymatroids. A polymatroid is a set function $h : {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0}$ with $h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$ that satisfies the following two rules (define $h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  = h\left( Y\right)  - h\left( X\right)$ as before):

多拟阵界(Polymatroid bound)。尽管熵界是紧致的，但其计算方法未知。为此，Gottlob等人[19]用多拟阵替代熵函数。多拟阵是满足以下两条规则的集合函数$h : {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0}$（$h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$定义同前）：

(R1) (Monotonicity): $h\left( Y\right)  \geq  h\left( X\right)$ for any $X \subseteq  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ;

(R1)(单调性)：对任意$X \subseteq  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，$h\left( Y\right)  \geq  h\left( X\right)$；

(R2) (Submodularity): $h\left( {I \mid  I \cap  J}\right)  \geq  h\left( {I \cup  J \mid  J}\right)$ for any $I,J \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ .

(R2)(子模性)：对任意$I,J \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，$h\left( {I \mid  I \cap  J}\right)  \geq  h\left( {I \cup  J \mid  J}\right)$。

Let ${\Gamma }_{n}$ be the set of polymatroid functions,then the polymatroid bound is defined as $\mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  {\Gamma }_{n} \cap  \mathrm{{HDC}}}}h\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)$ . Any entropic function is a polymatroid,so ${\Gamma }_{n}^{ * } \subseteq  {\Gamma }_{n}$ ,and the following inequalities hold:

设${\Gamma }_{n}$为多拟阵函数集合，则多拟阵界定义为$\mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  {\Gamma }_{n} \cap  \mathrm{{HDC}}}}h\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)$。由于所有熵函数都是多拟阵，故${\Gamma }_{n}^{ * } \subseteq  {\Gamma }_{n}$，且下列不等式成立：

$$
\log \left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  \leq  \mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  {\Gamma }_{n}^{ * } \cap  \mathrm{{HDC}}}}h\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)  \leq  \mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  {\Gamma }_{n} \cap  \mathrm{{HDC}}}}h\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right) .
$$

For simplicity we denote the (degree-aware) polymatroid bound as

为简化表述，我们将（度数感知的）多拟阵界记作

$$

$$

so that the polymatroid bound can be written as $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  \leq  \operatorname{DAPB}\left( Q\right)$ .

因此多拟阵界可表示为$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  \leq  \operatorname{DAPB}\left( Q\right)$。

The polymatroid bound degenerates into the AGM bound when there are only cardinality constraints, so it is tight in this case. However, Abo Khamis et al. [3] show that it is not tight under general degree constraints. Still, it is the best output size bound under degree constraints, and more importantly, the PANDA algorithm [3], to be reviewed in more detail in Section 4.4, has running time matching this bound up to polylogarithmic factors.

当仅存在基数约束时，多拟阵界退化为AGM界，此时是紧致的。但Abo Khamis等人[3]证明其在一般度数约束下非紧。尽管如此，这仍是度数约束下最优的输出规模界，更重要的是，4.4节将详述的PANDA算法[3]的时间复杂度与该界匹配至多对数因子。

### 3.3 Shannon-flow Inequality

### 3.3 香农流不等式(Shannon-flow Inequality)

We call the following inequality a Shannon-flow inequality if it holds for any polymatroid function $h \in  {\Gamma }_{n}$ :

若以下不等式对所有多拟阵函数$h \in  {\Gamma }_{n}$成立，则称其为香农流不等式：

$$
\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{X \subseteq  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}{\delta }_{Y \mid  X}h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  \geq  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}{\lambda }_{Y}h\left( Y\right)  \tag{1}
$$

where $\left\{  {\delta }_{Y \mid  X}\right\}$ and $\left\{  {\lambda }_{Y}\right\}$ are some non-negative real numbers. By representing $\left\{  {\delta }_{Y \mid  X}\right\}  ,\left\{  {\lambda }_{X}\right\}  ,\{ h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right) \}$ as vectors over(X,Y)pairs (recall that $h\left( Y\right)  = h\left( {Y \mid  \varnothing }\right)$ ,so ${\lambda }_{Y \mid  X} = 0$ for any $X \neq  \varnothing$ ),(1) can be concisely written as $\langle \mathbf{\delta },\mathbf{h}\rangle  \geq  \langle \mathbf{\lambda },\mathbf{h}\rangle$ ,where $\langle  \cdot  , \cdot  \rangle$ denotes dot product. The following theorem states that the polymatroid bound must be the LHS of (1) for some $\delta$ .

其中$\left\{  {\delta }_{Y \mid  X}\right\}$和$\left\{  {\lambda }_{Y}\right\}$为非负实数。通过将$\left\{  {\delta }_{Y \mid  X}\right\}  ,\left\{  {\lambda }_{X}\right\}  ,\{ h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right) \}$表示为(X,Y)对上的向量（注意到$h\left( Y\right)  = h\left( {Y \mid  \varnothing }\right)$，因此对于任意$X \neq  \varnothing$都有${\lambda }_{Y \mid  X} = 0$），式(1)可简洁表示为$\langle \mathbf{\delta },\mathbf{h}\rangle  \geq  \langle \mathbf{\lambda },\mathbf{h}\rangle$，此处$\langle  \cdot  , \cdot  \rangle$表示点积。下述定理表明，多拟阵界必须是式(1)左侧对于某个$\delta$的表达式。

THEOREM 1 ([3]). For any FCQ Q, there exists a non-negative vector $\delta$ such that $\langle \delta ,h\rangle  \geq  h\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)$ is a Shannon-flow inequality,and $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\left( {X,Y}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}}}{\delta }_{Y \mid  X} \cdot  {n}_{Y \mid  X} = \operatorname{LOGDAPB}\left( Q\right)$ .

定理1（文献[3]）：对于任何FCQ查询Q，存在非负向量$\delta$使得$\langle \delta ,h\rangle  \geq  h\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)$构成香农流不等式，且满足$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\left( {X,Y}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}}}{\delta }_{Y \mid  X} \cdot  {n}_{Y \mid  X} = \operatorname{LOGDAPB}\left( Q\right)$。

## 4 CIRCUITS

## 4 电路

### 4.1 Boolean Circuits

### 4.1 布尔电路

A Boolean circuit is a directed acyclic graph $C = \left( {V,E}\right)$ ,where the nodes in $V$ are called the gates. All nodes have in-degree ${}^{4}$ at most 2. Gates with no incoming edges are called the inputs, while gates with no outgoing edges are the outputs. Every gate with two incoming edges is associated with a Boolean operator from $\{  \land  , \vee  \}$ , and every gate with one incoming edge must be associated with $\neg$ . The truth value of an input gate is specified by the input, while the truth values of other gates are defined inductively in the natural way: the truth value of a gate $v$ is the result of applying the logical operator on the truth values of the two (or one) gates connected by the incoming edges to $v$ . The size of $C$ is $\left| V\right|$ ,while its depth is the length of the longest path from any input gate to any output gate.

布尔电路是一种有向无环图$C = \left( {V,E}\right)$，其中$V$的节点称为逻辑门。所有节点的入度${}^{4}$最多为2。无入边的门称为输入门，无出边的门称为输出门。每个具有两条入边的门关联着$\{  \land  , \vee  \}$中的一个布尔运算符，而每个具有单条入边的门必须关联$\neg$。输入门的真值由输入指定，其他门的真值按自然方式归纳定义：门$v$的真值是通过对其入边连接的两个（或一个）门的真值施加逻辑运算符得到的结果。$C$的规模为$\left| V\right|$，其深度则是从任意输入门到任意输出门的最长路径长度。

For certain applications, it is more efficient to use arithmetic circuits, where each edge (wire) carries an integer and the gates can perform basic arithmetic operations: addition, subtraction and multiplication. Since each tuple can be represented by $O\left( {\log u}\right)$ bits and we do not care about polylogarithmic factors, we will not make the distinction between Boolean circuits and arithmetic circuits. For convenience, we will allow each wire to carry an integer, a tuple, or a Boolean value, and each gate can perform any standard operation on them.

对于特定应用场景，使用算术电路更为高效——其中每条边（导线）传输整数，逻辑门可执行基本算术运算：加法、减法和乘法。由于每个元组可用$O\left( {\log u}\right)$位表示且我们不考虑多对数因子，本文将不区分布尔电路与算术电路。为方便起见，我们允许每条导线传输整数、元组或布尔值，且每个逻辑门可对它们执行任何标准操作。

### 4.2 Uniform Circuits

### 4.2 均匀电路

In order to use circuits as algorithms, they need to be uniform. Formally, a family of circuits is (logspace) uniform if there exists a deterministic Turing machine $M$ ,such that $M$ outputs a description of ${C}_{N}$ on input ${1}^{N}$ for any $N$ in $O\left( {\log N}\right)$ space,where ${C}_{N}$ is the circuit handling inputs of size $N$ . For the problem of query evaluation under degree constraints, the "input size" actually consists of all the degree constraints DC,together with the domain size $u$ . The Turing machine that generates the circuit is required to use space $O\left( {\log \left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{F}\left| {R}_{F}\right| }\right)  + \log u + n}\right)$ . In this paper,the circuits we propose are all uniform.

要将电路作为算法使用，它们需满足均匀性。形式上，若存在确定性图灵机$M$能在$O\left( {\log N}\right)$空间内对任意$N$输入${1}^{N}$输出${C}_{N}$的描述（其中${C}_{N}$是处理规模为$N$输入的电路），则该电路族是（对数空间）均匀的。在度约束下的查询评估问题中，"输入规模"实际包含所有度约束DC及域大小$u$。生成该电路的图灵机需使用$O\left( {\log \left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{F}\left| {R}_{F}\right| }\right)  + \log u + n}\right)$空间。本文提出的电路均满足均匀性。

### 4.3 Relational Circuits

### 4.3 关系电路

We will design our circuit in two steps. First,for any query $Q$ ,we will design a relational circuit, in which each wire carries a relation and each gate is one of the standard relational operators: selection, projection, join, and union. The in-degree of the first two gates is one while it is two for the latter two gates. In the second step (Section 5), we replace each relational gate by a Boolean circuit.

我们将分两步设计电路：首先针对任意查询$Q$设计关系电路，其中每条导线传输关系，每个逻辑门为标准关系运算符（选择、投影、连接、并集）之一。前两类门的入度为1，后两类为2。第二步（第5节）将每个关系门替换为布尔电路。

Pure relational circuits. Note that every CQ (or union of CQs) can be evaluated by a relational circuit of constant size, by definition. However, the evaluation cost can vary significantly over different circuits. To define the cost of a relational circuit more precisely, we first define the cost of each relational operator:

纯关系电路。根据定义，任何CQ（或CQ的并集）都可通过恒定大小的关系电路求值。但不同电路间的求值成本差异显著。为精确定义关系电路成本，我们首先定义各关系运算符的成本：

(1) The cost of selection ${\sigma }_{\varphi }\left( R\right)$ is $\left| R\right|$ .

(1) 选择操作${\sigma }_{\varphi }\left( R\right)$的成本为$\left| R\right|$。

(2) The cost of projection ${\Pi }_{X}\left( R\right)$ is $\left| R\right|$ .

(2) 投影操作${\Pi }_{X}\left( R\right)$的成本为$\left| R\right|$。

(3) The cost of join $R \bowtie  S$ is $\left| R\right|  + \left| S\right|  + \left| {R \bowtie  S}\right|$ .

(3) 连接操作$R \bowtie  S$的成本为$\left| R\right|  + \left| S\right|  + \left| {R \bowtie  S}\right|$。

(4) The cost of union $R \cup  S$ is $\left| R\right|  + \left| S\right|$ .

(4) 并集操作$R \cup  S$的成本为$\left| R\right|  + \left| S\right|$。

Then, the cost of a relational circuit is defined as the total costs of all its relational gates on the worst-case database instance. Note that standard algorithms for these operators in the RAM model match these costs asymptotically [1].

关系电路的总成本定义为最坏数据库实例下所有关系门成本之和。需注意，RAM模型中这些运算符的标准算法在渐进复杂度上符合上述成本[1]。

Extended relational circuits. Unfortunately, no existing algorithm using only the four relational operators above achieves a worst-case cost of $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{DAPB}}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ ,even for the triangle query ${Q}_{\bigtriangleup } = {R}_{AB} \bowtie$ ${R}_{BC} \bowtie  {R}_{AC}$ . Thus,we equip relational circuits with two more operators: aggregation and sorting. They are not defined in the pure relational algebra, but are routinely found in practical database systems. As we shall see, they can also be easily implemented by circuits.

扩展关系电路。遗憾的是，仅使用上述四种关系运算符的现有算法无法实现$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{DAPB}}\left( Q\right) }\right)$的最坏情况成本，即使是三角形查询${Q}_{\bigtriangleup } = {R}_{AB} \bowtie$${R}_{BC} \bowtie  {R}_{AC}$。因此我们为关系电路增设两个运算符：聚合与排序。它们虽未定义在纯关系代数中，但常见于实际数据库系统。如后文所示，它们也能被电路轻松实现。

An aggregation operator ${\Pi }_{F,\operatorname{agg}\left( A\right) }\left( R\right)$ first partitions the input relation $R$ according to a set of attributes $F$ ,and then computes the aggregate over attribute $A$ for each group. Common aggregations are count, sum, max and min. For the count aggregation, we simply write ${\Pi }_{F,\text{ count }}\left( R\right)$ without having to specify $A$ . Note that the output relation of ${\Pi }_{F,\operatorname{agg}\left( A\right) }\left( R\right)$ consists of $\left| F\right|  + 1$ attributes: all the attributes in $F$ plus the aggregate.

聚合运算符${\Pi }_{F,\operatorname{agg}\left( A\right) }\left( R\right)$首先根据属性集$F$对输入关系$R$进行分组，随后计算每组在属性$A$上的聚合值。常用聚合包括计数(count)、求和(sum)、最大值(max)和最小值(min)。计数聚合可简写为${\Pi }_{F,\text{ count }}\left( R\right)$而无需指定$A$。注意${\Pi }_{F,\operatorname{agg}\left( A\right) }\left( R\right)$的输出关系包含$\left| F\right|  + 1$个属性：$F$中所有属性加上聚合结果。

Since relations are sets of tuples, sorting takes the form of an ordering operator ${\tau }_{F}\left( R\right)$ . More precisely, ${\tau }_{F}\left( R\right)$ adds a new order column to $R$ ,which is the position number of the tuple after sorting $R$ by $F$ ,where ties can be broken arbitrarily.

由于关系是元组的集合，排序操作体现为排序运算符${\tau }_{F}\left( R\right)$。具体而言，${\tau }_{F}\left( R\right)$会为$R$添加新序列列，该列表示元组按$F$排序后的位置编号，相同值可任意排序。

This results in bounded wires and the cost of each gate is $O\left( {N}^{1.5}\right)$ . Relational circuits with bounded wires. There is another crucial difference between relational circuits and Boolean circuits. For any given query $Q$ ,we just design one relational circuit,but with the latter, we have to design a uniform family of circuits, parameterized by the input size (DC for the query evaluation problem). A related, and more technical, issue is that each wire in a relational circuit carries a relation, but each wire in a Boolean circuit can only carry one bit (or one tuple if polylogarithmic factors are ignored). This poses a difficulty in our second step, where we replace each relational gate by a Boolean circuit. In particular, a join gate may produce an output relation of size $\left| R\right|  \cdot  \left| S\right|$ ,so the Boolean circuit for a join must have at least this size in preparation for the worst case.

有限导线关系电路。关系电路与布尔电路还存在另一关键差异：对于任意给定查询$Q$，我们只需设计单一关系电路，而后者需要设计由输入规模参数化的电路族（查询求值问题中的DC）。更技术性的区别在于，关系电路中的每条导线承载的是关系，而布尔电路中每条导线仅能传输单比特（若忽略多对数因子则为单元组）。这为第二步工作带来挑战——当用布尔电路替代关系逻辑门时，连接门可能产生规模达$\left| R\right|  \cdot  \left| S\right|$的输出关系，因此对应布尔电路必须预留足够容量以应对最坏情况。

To resolve the difficulty, we require the relational circuits to have bounded wires, i.e., each wire is parameterized by certain degree constraints (including cardinality constraints) and only carries relations conforming to these constraints. As the input wires are bounded, the costs of the relational gates can also be bounded. More precisely, we define their costs as follows:

为解决该难题，我们要求关系电路具备有限导线特性，即每条导线均受特定度数约束（含基数约束）限制，仅传输符合约束的关系。由于输入导线受限，关系逻辑门的成本亦可界定。具体而言，我们按如下方式定义其成本：

(1) The cost of a selection, projection, aggregation, or sorting gate,that takes an input relation $R$ with the constraint $\left| R\right|  \leq$ $N$ ,is $N$ .

(1) 选择、投影、聚合或排序门的成本：当输入关系$R$满足约束$\left| R\right|  \leq$$N$时，其成本为$N$。

(2) The cost of a union gate,that takes two input relations $R$ and $S$ with the constraints $\left| R\right|  \leq  N,\left| S\right|  \leq  M$ ,is $M + N$ .

(2) 并集门的成本：当两个输入关系$R$和$S$满足约束$\left| R\right|  \leq  N,\left| S\right|  \leq  M$时，其成本为$M + N$。

(3) The cost of a join gate,that takes two input relations $R,S$ with the constraints $\left| R\right|  \leq  M,{\deg }_{F}\left( S\right)  \leq  N,\left| S\right|  \leq  {MN}$ where $F$ is the set of common attributes between $R$ and $S$ ,is ${MN}$ .

(3) 连接门的成本：当两个输入关系$R,S$满足约束$\left| R\right|  \leq  M,{\deg }_{F}\left( S\right)  \leq  N,\left| S\right|  \leq  {MN}$（其中$F$表示$R$与$S$间的共有属性集）时，其成本为${MN}$。

Note that the costs defined above only depend on the constraints on the input wires but not the actual instance. In Section 5 we will replace these relational gates by Boolean circuits whose size matches these costs, up to polylogarithmic factors. Thus, the overall size of the Boolean circuit for the query will be exactly the total costs of all the relational gates.

需注意上述成本仅取决于输入导线的约束条件，而与具体实例无关。在第5节中，我们将用布尔电路替代这些关系逻辑门，其规模在多项式对数因子内与这些成本相匹配。因此，查询对应的布尔电路总规模将精确等于所有关系逻辑门的总成本。

In the rest of the paper, a relational circuit always refers to one with bounded wires and extended with aggregation and sorting gates. Note that since the wires are now parameterized, for a given query $Q$ ,we will need to design a family of relational circuits,one for each set of degree constraints DC. To achieve uniformity, the corresponding relational circuit should be generated in log-space.

本文后续所述的关系电路均指具备有限导线特性且扩展了聚合与排序门的版本。由于导线现已参数化，对于给定查询$Q$，我们需要设计关系电路族——每组度数约束DC对应一个电路。为实现统一性，对应的关系电路应在对数空间内生成。

### 4.4 PANDA-C: Relational Circuits for FCQs

### 4.4 PANDA-C：面向FCQ的关系电路

The relational circuit for the triangle query in Figure 1 has constant size and the optimal cost $O\left( {\operatorname{DAPB}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ . However,it is an open question whether constant-sized relational circuits exist that achieve the optimal cost for an arbitrary FCQ, even if there are only cardinality constraints and all cardinality constraints are equal (to $N$ ). Note that in this case, $\operatorname{DAPB}\left( Q\right)  = {N}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$ where ${\rho }^{ * }$ is the minimum fractional edge cover number of the hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ of the query.

图1中三角形查询的关系电路具有恒定尺寸和最优成本$O\left( {\operatorname{DAPB}\left( Q\right) }\right)$。然而，对于任意FCQ是否存在能实现最优成本的恒定尺寸关系电路仍是一个开放性问题，即使仅存在基数约束且所有基数约束均相等（等于$N$）。需注意，此时$\operatorname{DAPB}\left( Q\right)  = {N}^{{\rho }^{ * }}$成立，其中${\rho }^{ * }$表示查询超图$\mathcal{H}$的最小分数边覆盖数。

Nevertheless, we are able to design a polylogarithmic-sized relational circuit for an arbitrary FCQ under general degree constraints while achieving cost $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{DAPB}}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ ,by appropriately modifying PANDA. We call the modified algorithm PANDA-C.

尽管如此，我们通过适当修改PANDA算法，能够在通用度约束下为任意FCQ设计出具有对数多项式尺寸的关系电路，同时实现$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{DAPB}}\left( Q\right) }\right)$成本。改进后的算法称为PANDA-C。

Data independence. PANDA-C takes $Q$ and DC as inputs,but not D. It will generate a relational circuit that works for any D that conforms to DC. In particular, this means that the sequence of operations can only depend on $Q$ and DC. To this end,we modify the original PANDA algorithm as shown in Algorithm 1, where the modified code are marked like this.

数据独立性。PANDA-C以$Q$和DC作为输入而非实际数据D，所生成的关系电路适用于所有符合DC约束的数据库D。这意味着操作序列仅能依赖$Q$和DC。为此，我们如算法1所示修改原始PANDA算法，修改部分以特殊格式标注。

## 5 FROM RELATIONAL CIRCUITS TO BOOLEAN CIRCUITS

## 5 从关系电路到布尔电路

In this section, we replace each relational gate in the relational circuit generated from the previous section with a Boolean circuit. Recall that each wire in the PANDA-C relational circuit carries a relation of size bounded by,say, $K$ . In the Boolean circuit,we replace it with Boolean wires that carry exactly $K$ tuples (which require $\widetilde{O}\left( K\right)$ Boolean wires). Of course,on any actual data instance $\mathbf{D}$ ,there may not be exactly $K$ tuples. In this case,we simply pad dummy tuples. One way to implement this is to add a special Boolean attribute $Z$ to each tuple $t$ ,and set $t.Z = 0$ if the tuple $t$ is dummy and $t.Z = 1$ if not. For conciseness,we will only say "set $t$ to dummy" or "check whether $t$ is dummy" while the underlying implementation is obvious.

本节将上一节生成的关系电路中每个关系门替换为布尔电路。需注意，PANDA-C关系电路中的每条导线承载的关系大小上限为$K$。在布尔电路中，我们将其替换为精确承载$K$个元组（需要$\widetilde{O}\left( K\right)$条布尔导线）的布尔导线。实际数据实例$\mathbf{D}$中元组数量可能不足$K$，此时通过填充虚拟元组解决。具体实现方式是为每个元组$t$添加特殊布尔属性$Z$：若$t$为虚拟元组则设为$t.Z = 0$，否则设为$t.Z = 1$。为简洁起见，我们仅表述为"将$t$设为虚拟"或"检查$t$是否为虚拟"，其底层实现逻辑显而易见。

### 5.1 Scan and Segmented Scan

### 5.1 扫描与分段扫描

For a sequence $X = {\left( {x}_{i}\right) }_{i = 1}^{N}$ and a binary associative operator $\oplus$ ,a $\oplus$ -scan on $X$ ,also known as the prefix sums problem,produces an output sequence $\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{1} \oplus  {x}_{2},\cdots ,{x}_{1} \oplus  {x}_{2} \oplus  \cdots  \oplus  {x}_{N}}\right)$ . The classical $\oplus$ -scan circuit [21] for this problem has depth $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ and size $\widetilde{O}\left( N\right)$ , as described in Algorithm 4.

对于序列$X = {\left( {x}_{i}\right) }_{i = 1}^{N}$和二元关联运算符$\oplus$，在$X$上进行的$\oplus$-扫描（也称为前缀和问题）会生成输出序列$\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{1} \oplus  {x}_{2},\cdots ,{x}_{1} \oplus  {x}_{2} \oplus  \cdots  \oplus  {x}_{N}}\right)$。如算法4所述，该问题的经典$\oplus$-扫描电路[21]具有深度$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$和规模$\widetilde{O}\left( N\right)$。

For our purpose, we need to extend the scan circuit above to compute a segmented scan: We are given a binary associative operator $\oplus$ and a sequence $X$ that consists of $k$ segments $X = \left( {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{k}}\right)$ . The $i$ -th segment has length ${N}_{i}$ ,and ${X}_{i} = {\left( \left( {a}_{i},{b}_{ij}\right) \right) }_{j = 1}^{{N}_{i}}$ where ${a}_{i} \neq  {a}_{j}$ for any $i \neq  j$ ,namely,the ${a}_{i}$ ’s delineate the segments and we would like to compute the prefix sums segment by segment. More precisely,a $\oplus$ -segmented scan takes $X$ as input,and produces an output sequence $Y = \left( {{Y}_{1},\ldots ,{Y}_{k}}\right)$ ,where ${Y}_{i}$ is the $\oplus$ -scan of ${\left\{  {b}_{ij}\right\}  }_{j = 1}^{{N}_{i}}$ . Since $X$ are represented by two sequences $A$ and $B$ where $X\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack   = \left( {A\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack  ,B\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack  }\right)$ ,we also call this operation as a $\oplus$ -scan on $B$ segmented by $A$ . Note that neither $k$ nor any ${N}_{i}$ is known to the circuit,so one cannot simply compute each ${Y}_{i}$ using a $\oplus$ -scan.

The reader may wonder why we do not use the scan circuit to combine the $C$ values,by defining ${c}_{1} \oplus  {c}_{2} = \left( {{c}_{1},{c}_{2}}\right)$ . The problem with this method is that the cost of each $\oplus$ operation is no longer constant,as the size of $C$ doubles after each level. In fact,doing so would result in a circuit of quadratic size. The key to avoiding the size blowup is the observation that,as more $C$ values are combined, the number of non-dummy tuples in $S$ must reduce. Then we throw away some dummy tuples to reduce the size of $S$ after each level, so that the size of the circuit is only logarithmically larger.

读者可能疑惑为何不通过定义${c}_{1} \oplus  {c}_{2} = \left( {{c}_{1},{c}_{2}}\right)$来使用扫描电路合并$C$值。此方法的问题在于每个$\oplus$操作的成本不再恒定，因为$C$的规模会逐层翻倍。实际上，这样做会导致电路规模呈平方级增长。避免规模膨胀的关键在于观察到：随着更多$C$值被合并，$S$中非虚拟元组数量必然减少。因此我们在每层处理后丢弃部分虚拟元组以缩减$S$规模，从而仅使电路规模呈对数级增长。

Correctness Proof. We prove that we never throw non-dummy tuples in line 14-15. As explained in Section 5.4,we have $\deg \left( {{BC} \mid  B}\right)  \leq$ ${2}^{n - i} + 1$ . Therefore,the number of tuples in $S$ that can join with $R$ is at most $\left( {{2}^{n - i} + 1}\right) M$ ,and tuples that cannot join have already been marked as dummy in line 1, hence line 14-15 do not throw non-dummy tuples.

正确性证明。我们证明第14-15行从未丢弃非虚拟元组。如第5.4节所述，我们有$\deg \left( {{BC} \mid  B}\right)  \leq$${2}^{n - i} + 1$。因此$S$中能与$R$连接的元组数量最多为$\left( {{2}^{n - i} + 1}\right) M$，且无法连接的元组已在第1行被标记为虚拟，故第14-15行不会丢弃非虚拟元组。

Complexity Analysis. We analyze the complexity of line 3-15 in Algorithm 7,as other parts obviously have $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ depth and $\widetilde{O}\left( {MN}\right)$ size. First note that $\left| S\right|  \leq  {MN}$ in line 3-15,so the depth of the circuit in each loop is dominated by that of sorting,which is $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ . In the $i$ -th loop,we have ${N}_{i} \leq  \left( {{2}^{n - i + 1} + 1}\right) M$ ,and the circuit size of line 7-11 and line 13 is $O\left( {2}^{i}\right)$ (the length of $C$ ); the circuit size of the sorting is $\widetilde{O}\left( {{N}_{i} \cdot  {2}^{i}}\right)$ . Therefore,the total circuit size of line 3-15 is $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{n}{N}_{i} \cdot  {2}^{i}}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{n}\left( {{2}^{n - i + 1} + 1}\right) M \cdot  {2}^{i}}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {MN}\right)$ .

复杂度分析。我们重点分析算法7中第3-15行的复杂度，其余部分显然具有$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$深度和$\widetilde{O}\left( {MN}\right)$规模。首先注意第3-15行中$\left| S\right|  \leq  {MN}$，因此每轮循环电路深度主要由排序操作决定，其深度为$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$。在第$i$次循环中，存在${N}_{i} \leq  \left( {{2}^{n - i + 1} + 1}\right) M$情况，第7-11行和第13行的电路规模为$O\left( {2}^{i}\right)$（$C$的长度）；排序操作的电路规模为$\widetilde{O}\left( {{N}_{i} \cdot  {2}^{i}}\right)$。因此，第3-15行的总电路规模为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{n}{N}_{i} \cdot  {2}^{i}}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{n}\left( {{2}^{n - i + 1} + 1}\right) M \cdot  {2}^{i}}\right)  = \widetilde{O}\left( {MN}\right)$。

## A OUTPUT-SENSITIVE CIRCUITS

## 附录A 输出敏感电路

Running times of RAM algorithms come in two flavors: $\widetilde{O}\left( {f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right) }\right)$ and $\widetilde{O}\left( {g\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)  + \left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right| }\right)$ ,where the latter is referred to as an output-sensitive running time, which is more attractive, both theoretically and practically: Theoretically,because $\left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right|$ is the unavoidable cost of reporting the output, $g\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)$ represents the "intrinsic" cost of the query. Indeed,for many queries we have $g\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)  < f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)$ , while $f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)$ usually matches the worst-case bound on $\left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right|$ ,so $\widetilde{O}\left( {f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right) }\right)$ is worst-case optimal simply because the output size can be this large on the worst-case D. In practice, an output-sensitive algorithm can benefit from the fact that $\left| {Q\left( \mathrm{\;D}\right) }\right|$ is much smaller than $f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)$ on most real-world instances.

RAM算法的运行时间分为两种类型：$\widetilde{O}\left( {f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right) }\right)$与$\widetilde{O}\left( {g\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)  + \left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right| }\right)$，后者被称为输出敏感运行时间，在理论和实践层面都更具吸引力：理论上，由于$\left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right|$是输出报告的固有成本，$g\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)$代表查询的"本质"成本。事实上，多数查询满足$g\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)  < f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)$，而$f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)$通常与$\left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right|$的最坏情况边界匹配，因此$\widetilde{O}\left( {f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right) }\right)$的最优性仅源于最坏情况数据库D可能产生如此大的输出规模。实践中，输出敏感算法的优势在于：对于大多数现实场景，$\left| {Q\left( \mathrm{\;D}\right) }\right|$远小于$f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right)$。

Unfortunately, since a circuit, hence its size, must be independent of $\mathrm{D}$ ,the size has to be $\widetilde{O}\left( {f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right) }\right)$ just to prepare for the worst-case input. Thus there is no counterpart of "output-sensitive" algorithms in the circuit model. Nevertheless, we observe that in many applications of circuits, such as parallel computing, secure multiparty computation, and querying outsourced data (not query evaluation by hardware though), one does not have to complete the entire query evaluation with a single circuit. With these applications in mind, we define an output-sensitive circuit to consist of two uniform families of circuits: The first family is parameterized by DC, and each circuit in this family computes $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$ for any $\mathbf{D}$ conforming to DC; the second family is parameterized by DC and OUT, and each circuit in this family computes $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ for any $\mathbf{D}$ subject to both the degree constraint DC and the output size constraint $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  =$ OUT. In any of the applications mentioned above, one uses the first circuit to compute $\left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right|$ (for BCQs,this step can be skipped since $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  = 1$ ),and then uses $\mathrm{{OUT}} = \left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$ as a parameter to build and evaluate the second circuit. Note that in secure multi-party computation or querying outsourced data,this would reveal $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$ , but this is allowed, since privacy in these models is defined to be the protection of anything beyond the query results, while the output size is certainly part of the query results.

遗憾的是，由于电路及其规模必须独立于$\mathrm{D}$，其规模必须$\widetilde{O}\left( {f\left( \mathrm{{DC}}\right) }\right)$以应对最坏情况输入。因此电路模型不存在"输出敏感"算法的对应概念。不过我们注意到，在电路诸多应用场景（如并行计算、安全多方计算、外包数据查询等，尽管硬件查询评估不在此列）中，并不需要单个电路完成全部查询评估。基于这些应用场景，我们将输出敏感电路定义为两个均匀电路族的组合：第一个电路族以DC为参数，该族中每个电路为符合DC约束的任意$\mathbf{D}$计算$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$；第二个电路族以DC和OUT为参数，该族中每个电路为同时满足度约束DC和输出规模约束$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  =$ OUT的$\mathbf{D}$计算$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$。在上述任何应用中，用户先用第一个电路计算$\left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right|$（对于BCQ可跳过此步，因$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  = 1$），再将$\mathrm{{OUT}} = \left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$作为参数构建并评估第二个电路。需注意在安全多方计算或外包数据查询时，这会暴露$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$，但这是允许的——因为这些模型的隐私定义仅要求保护查询结果之外的信息，而输出规模显然属于查询结果范畴。

Below, we first review output-sensitive algorithms in the RAM model. In Section A. 2 and A.3, we show how to construct the second circuit given DC and OUT. Then we discuss how to build the first circuit in Section A.4.

下文首先回顾RAM模型中的输出敏感算法。附录A.2和A.3章节将阐述如何根据DC与OUT构建第二类电路，A.4章节则讨论第一类电路的构建方法。

The only change we have to make is that in (6), $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ only ranges over free-connex GHDs. For a BCQ $Q$ ,all GHDs are free-connex (since there are no free variables) and $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  = 1$ ,so a $\mathrm{{BCQ}}$ can be evaluated in time $\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{\mathrm{{da}}\text{-}\mathrm{{fhtw}}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ without any restriction on the GHD.

对于非完全CQ，常用方法是限制GHD为自由连接[8]，直观来说是将所有自由变量置于绑定变量之上。唯一需要修改的是在(6)中，$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$仅遍历自由连接GHD。注意对于非完全CQ，$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$通常小于FCQ上的值，但da-fhtw(Q)会因GHD被限制为自由连接而增大。对于BCQ$Q$，所有GHD都是自由连接的（因无自由变量）且$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|  = 1$，因此$\mathrm{{BCQ}}$可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{\mathrm{{da}}\text{-}\mathrm{{fhtw}}\left( Q\right) }\right)$时间内完成计算，无需对GHD施加限制。

### A.2 Yannakakis by Circuits

### A.2 基于电路的Yannakakis算法

In order to turn GHD-based RAM algorithms to output-sensitive circuits (as defined above), we need two more components in addition to PANDA-C: The first is a circuit for computing $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$ given DC, and the second is a circuit version of the Yannakakis algorithm for given DC and OUT $= \left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right|$ . The first circuit has $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ depth and $\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{\mathrm{{da}}\text{-}\mathrm{{fhtw}}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ size,while the second has $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ depth and $\widetilde{O}\left( {{2}^{\mathrm{{da}}\text{-}\mathrm{{fhtw}}\left( Q\right) } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ size,Thus,the total size of the two circuits matches the RAM running time. We describe the second circuit, called Yannakakis-C, in this subsection; the first circuit is actually a simple variant of this circuit.

要将基于GHD的RAM算法转化为输出敏感电路（如上定义），除PANDA-C外还需两个组件：第一个是给定DC计算$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$的电路，第二个是给定DC和OUT$= \left| {Q\left( \mathrm{D}\right) }\right|$的Yannakakis算法电路版本。第一个电路具有$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$深度和$\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{\mathrm{{da}}\text{-}\mathrm{{fhtw}}\left( Q\right) }\right)$规模，第二个电路具有$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$深度和$\widetilde{O}\left( {{2}^{\mathrm{{da}}\text{-}\mathrm{{fhtw}}\left( Q\right) } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$规模，因此两个电路总规模与RAM运行时间匹配。本节描述称为Yannakakis-C的第二个电路；第一个电路实际上是该电路的简单变体。

We adopt the 3-phase version of the Yannakakis algorithm as described in [32]. Let $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ be a free-connect GHD of a given query $Q$ . In the first phase,which we call reduce,after computing the FCQ associated with each bag, it performs a bottom-up pass over $\mathcal{T}$ to remove all bounded variables by projections and semijoins. Now $\mathcal{T}$ becomes an acyclic FCQ. In the second phase,it makes two semijoin passes to remove all dangling tuples, i.e., tuples that will not appear in the query results. In the third phase, it performs another bottom-up pass of joins to compute the query results.

我们采用文献[32]描述的Yannakakis算法三阶段版本。设$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$为给定查询$Q$的自由连接GHD。第一阶段（称为归约阶段）在计算各包关联FCQ后，对$\mathcal{T}$执行自底向上遍历，通过投影和半连接移除所有绑定变量。此时$\mathcal{T}$变为无环FCQ。第二阶段通过两次半连接遍历移除所有悬空元组（即不会出现在查询结果中的元组）。第三阶段执行另一次自底向上的连接遍历以计算结果。

The circuit for the reduce phase, Reduce-C, is described in Algorithm 8. This is readily a circuit,observing that a semijoin ${R}_{X} \ltimes  {S}_{Y}$ can be implemented as ${R}_{X} \bowtie  {\Pi }_{X \cap  Y}\left( {S}_{Y}\right)$ . After the projection,the join becomes a primary-key join, so a semijoin can be implemented by a circuit of $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ depth and $\widetilde{O}\left( {\left| {R}_{X}\right|  + \left| {R}_{Y}\right| }\right)$ size. Thus,Reduce-C has $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ depth and $\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{\mathrm{{da}} - \operatorname{fhtw}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ size.

归约阶段电路Reduce-C的构造详见算法8。该电路可直接实现，注意到半连接${R}_{X} \ltimes  {S}_{Y}$可通过${R}_{X} \bowtie  {\Pi }_{X \cap  Y}\left( {S}_{Y}\right)$实现。投影后连接转为基于主键的连接，因此半连接可由深度$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$、规模$\widetilde{O}\left( {\left| {R}_{X}\right|  + \left| {R}_{Y}\right| }\right)$的电路实现。故Reduce-C具有$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$深度和$\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{\mathrm{{da}} - \operatorname{fhtw}\left( Q\right) }\right)$规模。

The second phase, described in line 2-9 of Algorithm 9, also only needs semijoins,so can be implemented by a circuit of $\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$ depth and linear size.

算法9第2-9行描述的第二阶段同样仅需半连接，因此可通过深度$\widetilde{O}\left( 1\right)$且线性规模的电路实现。

The third phase (line 10-18 of Algorithm 9) requires some more work since it uses joins (line 14) over relations whose degrees may not be bounded. In general, such a join needs a circuit of size $\left| {T}_{B}\right|  \cdot  \left| {T}_{{B}_{p}}\right|$ ,which may be greater than OUT. However,after all dangling tuples have been removed,it is guaranteed that $\mid  {T}_{B} \bowtie$ ${T}_{{B}_{p}} \mid   \leq$ OUT. Thus,we need a join circuit between two relations whose join size is bounded instead of the degrees.

第三阶段（算法9第10-18行）需要更多处理，因其在度数可能无界的关联关系上执行连接操作（第14行）。一般而言，此类连接需要规模$\left| {T}_{B}\right|  \cdot  \left| {T}_{{B}_{p}}\right|$的电路，该值可能大于OUT。但移除所有悬空元组后，可确保$\mid  {T}_{B} \bowtie$${T}_{{B}_{p}} \mid   \leq$OUT。因此我们需在两个连接规模有界（而非度数有界）的关联关系间构建连接电路。

### A.3 Output-bounded Join

### A.3 输出受限连接

Let $R\left( {A,B}\right)$ and $S\left( {B,C}\right)$ be two relations with $\left| R\right|  = M,\left| S\right|  = N$ and $\left| {R \bowtie  S}\right|  \leq$ OUT $\leq  {MN}$ . Note that an output-bounded join is more general than a degree-bounded join studied in Section 5.4: if ${\deg }_{S}\left( B\right)  \leq  d$ ,then OUT $\leq  {Md}$ ,so an output-bounded join circuit with OUT $= {Md}$ also works for a degree-bounded join.

### A.4 Computing OUT

### A.4 计算OUT

We can use a variant of Yannakakis-C to compute OUT $= \left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$ . We still first perform the reduce phase to remove all bound variables. Then we perform the bottom-up semijoin phase, except that in each semijoin ${R}_{X} \ltimes  {S}_{Y} = {R}_{X} \bowtie  {\Pi }_{X \cap  Y}\left( {S}_{Y}\right)$ ,we replace the projection with a sum aggregation, and after the join, we need to multiply the sub-join sizes together. ## B ACHIEVING SUBMODULAR WIDTH

## B 实现次模宽度

All the development in this section use one GHD. Marx [27] observe that using multiple GHDs can further reduce the running time. PANDA can be used to compute disjunctive datalog rules. Together with multiple GHDs, this leads to a running time where the width is the degree-aware submodular width [3]:

本节所有推导基于单个GHD。Marx[27]指出使用多个GHD可进一步降低运行时间。PANDA算法可用于计算析取数据日志规则，结合多GHD策略可将运行时间优化至度感知次模宽度[3]：

$$
\text{da-subw}\left( Q\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  \Gamma  \cap  \mathrm{{HDC}}}}\mathop{\min }\limits_{\left( \mathcal{T},\chi \right) }\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}}h\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right) \text{,}
$$

which is always no larger than da-fhtw(Q). As PANDA-C is a relational circuit for PANDA, the results in Theorem 5 also hold even if da-fhtw(Q)is replaced by da-subw(Q).

该值始终不大于da-fhtw(Q)。由于PANDA-C是PANDA的关系型电路，定理5中的结论即使将da-fhtw(Q)替换为da-subw(Q)仍然成立。

## C AGGREGATION QUERIES

## C 聚合查询

Join-aggregate queries over semirings, i.e., AJAR/FAQ queries as defined in $\left\lbrack  {2,{23}}\right\rbrack$ ,can also be handled by circuits: We first compute OUT using Algorithm 11. Then we invoke Yannakakis-C. To compute the aggregations correctly, we attach an annotation to each tuple. In Yannakakis-C, we replace each projection (including the one used in semijoins) by an $\oplus$ -aggregation circuit; after each join, we use a map circuit to compute the $\otimes$ -aggregation of the tuples comprising each join result. Here $\oplus$ and $\otimes$ are the semiring addition and multiplications. These additional circuits do not increase the overall depth/size by more than a constant factor. This way, the results in Theorem 5 also hold for join-aggregate queries. However, da-fhtw(Q)cannot be replaced by da-subw(Q)for join-aggregate queries, since using multiple GHDs cannot ensure that each annotation is aggregated only once. In fact, how to achieve the submodular width remains an open problem even in the RAM model [3].

半环上的连接聚合查询（即$\left\lbrack  {2,{23}}\right\rbrack$中定义的AJAR/FAQ查询）同样可通过电路处理：首先使用算法11计算OUT，然后调用Yannakakis-C。为确保聚合计算正确，我们为每个元组附加标注。在Yannakakis-C中，将每个投影（包括半连接中的投影）替换为$\oplus$聚合电路；每次连接后，使用映射电路计算构成连接结果的元组的$\otimes$聚合值。其中$\oplus$和$\otimes$分别代表半环加法和乘法运算。这些附加电路仅以常数倍增加总体深度/规模。因此定理5的结论同样适用于连接聚合查询。但da-fhtw(Q)不能替换为da-subw(Q)，因为使用多重GHD无法确保每个标注仅被聚合一次。事实上，即使在RAM模型[3]中，如何实现次模宽度仍是未解难题。
