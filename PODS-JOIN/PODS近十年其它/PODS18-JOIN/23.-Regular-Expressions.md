## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

Regular expressions with capture variables, also known as "regex formulas," extract relations of spans (interval positions) from text. These relations can be further manipulated via the relational Algebra as studied in the context of "document spanners," Fagin et al.'s formal framework for information extraction. We investigate the complexity of querying text by Conjunctive Queries (CQs) and Unions of CQs (UCQs) on top of regex formulas. Such queries have been investigated in prior work on document spanners, but little is known about the (combined) complexity of their evaluation.

带有捕获变量的正则表达式（又称"正则公式"）可从文本中提取跨度（区间位置）关系。这些关系可通过关系代数进一步操作，该研究属于Fagin等人提出的信息抽取形式化框架"文档张量"范畴。我们研究了在正则公式基础上，通过合取查询(CQs)及其联合查询(UCQs)来查询文本的复杂度。此类查询在先前文档张量研究中已有探讨，但其评估的（组合）复杂度尚不明确。

We show that the lower bounds (NP-completeness and W[1]- hardness) from the relational world also hold in our setting; in particular, hardness hits already single-character text. Yet, the upper bounds from the relational world do not carry over. Unlike the relational world, acyclic CQs, and even gamma-acyclic CQs, are hard to compute. The source of hardness is that it may be intractable to instantiate the relation defined by a regex formula, simply because it has an exponential number of tuples. Yet, we are able to establish general upper bounds. In particular, UCQs can be evaluated with polynomial delay, provided that every CQ has a bounded number of atoms (while unions and projection can be arbitrary). Furthermore, UCQ evaluation is solvable with FPT (Fixed-Parameter Tractable) delay when the parameter is the size of the UCQ.

我们证明关系数据库中的下界(NP完全性和W[1]难解性)在本场景中同样成立——即使是单字符文本也会遭遇难解性。然而关系数据库中的上界并不适用：与关系世界不同，无环CQs乃至γ-无环CQs都难以计算。难解性根源在于正则公式定义的关系可能因包含指数级元组而难以实例化。尽管如此，我们仍建立了通用上界：当每个CQ具有有限原子数时（允许任意联合与投影操作），UCQs可用多项式延迟进行评估；此外，当参数为UCQ规模时，其评估问题属于固定参数可解(FPT)延迟范畴。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Information Extraction (IE) conventionally refers to the task of automatically extracting structured information from text. While early work in the area focused largely on military applications [19], this task is nowadays pervasive in a plethora of computational challenges (especially those associated with Big Data), including social media analysis [5], healthcare analysis [38], customer relationship management [3], information retrieval [43], machine log analysis [17], and open-domain Knowledge Base construction [21, 35, 36, 42].

信息抽取（IE）传统上指从文本中自动提取结构化信息的任务。该领域早期研究主要集中于军事应用[19]，如今已广泛应用于众多计算挑战（特别是与大数据相关的领域），包括社交媒体分析[5]、医疗健康分析[38]、客户关系管理[3]、信息检索[43]、机器日志分析[17]以及开放领域知识库构建[21,35,36,42]。

One of the major commercial systems for rule-based IE is IBM's SystemT that exposes an SQL-like declarative language named ${AQL}$ (Annotation Query Language),along with a query plan optimizer [31] and development tooling [26]. Conceptually, AQL supports a collection of "primitive" extractors of relations from text (e.g., tokenizer, dictionary lookup, regex matcher and part-of-speech tagger), along with an algebra for relational manipulation (applied to the relations obtained from the primitive extractors). A similar approach is adopted by Xlog [34], where user-defined functions, playing the role of primitive extractors, are manipulated by non-recursive Datalog. DeepDive $\left\lbrack  {{32},{35}}\right\rbrack$ exposes a declarative language for rules and features, which are eventually translated into the factors of a statistical model where parameters (weights) are set by machine learning. There, the primitive extractors are defined in a scripting language, and the generated relations are again manipulated by relational rules.

IBM的SystemT是基于规则的IE主要商用系统之一，它提供名为${AQL}$（标注查询语言）的类SQL声明式语言，并配备查询计划优化器[31]和开发工具[26]。从概念上讲，AQL支持从文本中提取关系的多种"基础"抽取器（如分词器、词典查找、正则表达式匹配器和词性标注器），以及用于关系操作的关系代数（应用于基础抽取器获得的关系）。Xlog[34]采用类似方法，其中用户定义函数充当基础抽取器，通过非递归Datalog进行操作。DeepDive$\left\lbrack  {{32},{35}}\right\rbrack$提供用于规则和特征的声明式语言，最终转换为统计模型的因子，其参数（权重）通过机器学习设定。该系统的基础抽取器通过脚本语言定义，生成的关系再由关系规则操作。

Fagin et al. [12] proposed the framework of document spanners (or just spanners for short) that captures the relational philosophy of the aforementioned systems. Intuitively, a spanner extracts from a document $\mathbf{s}$ (which is a string over a finite alphabet) a relation over the spans of s. A span of s represents a substring of s that is identified by the start and end indices. An example of a spanner representation is a regex formula: a regular expression with embedded capture variables that are viewed as relational attributes. A regular spanner is one that can be expressed in the closure of the regex formulas under relational operators projection, union, and join.

法金等人[12]提出文档展宽器（简称展宽器）框架，体现了上述系统的关系哲学。直观上，展宽器从文档$\mathbf{s}$（有限字母表上的字符串）中提取关于s跨度的关系。s的跨度表示由起始和结束索引标识的s子串。正则表达式公式是展宽器的一种表示形式：这种带有嵌入式捕获变量的正则表达式被视为关系属性。常规展宽器可通过关系运算符投影、并集和连接在正则表达式公式闭包中表达。

For example, it is common to apply sentence boundary detection by evaluating a regex formula [37],which we denote as ${\alpha }_{\text{sen }}\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$ . When evaluating ${\alpha }_{\text{sen }}\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$ over a string $\mathbf{s}$ (representing text in natural language),the result is a relation with a single attribute, $x$ ,where each tuple consists of the left and right boundaries of a sentence.

例如，通常通过评估正则表达式公式[37]（记作${\alpha }_{\text{sen }}\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$）来实施句子边界检测。当在表示自然语言文本的字符串$\mathbf{s}$上评估${\alpha }_{\text{sen }}\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$时，结果是一个包含单属性$x$的关系，其中每个元组由句子的左右边界组成。

The research on spanners has been initiated with the motivation of exploring development architectures such as SystemT [25] and Xlog [34]. The basic queries in these architectures are Conjunctive Queries (CQs), and more generally Unions of CQs (UCQs), over regex formulas, expressed as SQL (SystemT) or acyclic Datalog (Xlog). Such UCQs also constitue the language underlying more recent theoretical extensions $\left\lbrack  {{13},{29}}\right\rbrack$ . Nevertheless,very little is known about the computational complexity of evaluating CQs and UCQs over regex formulas. In this paper, we explore this complexity.

抽取器研究的初始动机是探索SystemT[25]和Xlog[34]等开发架构。这些架构中的基础查询是正则表达式公式上的合取查询(CQs)及其并集(UCQs)，通过SQL(SystemT)或非循环Datalog(Xlog)表达。此类UCQs也构成了近期理论扩展$\left\lbrack  {{13},{29}}\right\rbrack$的基础语言。然而，关于正则表达式公式上CQs和UCQs求值计算复杂度的研究仍十分匮乏。本文正是针对这一复杂度展开探索。

To be more precise, in the framework of (regular and core) spanners, CQs and UCQs over regex formulas are defined similarly to the relational-database world, with two differences. First, the input is not a relational database, but rather a string. Second, the atoms are not relational symbols, but rather regex formulas. Therefore, the relations on which the query is applied are implicitly defined as those obtained by evaluating each regex formula over the input string. We refer to these queries as regex ${CQs}$ and regex ${UCQs}$ , respectively. As an example, the above query in (1) is a regex CQ. Under data complexity, where the query is assumed fixed, query evaluation is always doable in polynomial time. Here we focus on combined complexity where both the query and the data (string) are given as input. One might be tempted to claim that the literature on UCQ evaluation to date should draw the complete picture on complexity, by what we refer to as the canonical relational evaluation: evaluate each indvidual regex formula on the input string, and compute the UCQ as if it were query on an ordinary relational database. There are, however, several problems with this claim.

更准确地说，在（正则和核心）抽取器框架中，正则表达式公式上的CQs和UCQs定义与关系数据库领域类似，但存在两点差异：其一，输入并非关系数据库而是字符串；其二，原子项不是关系符号而是正则表达式公式。因此，查询所应用的关系隐式定义为通过对输入字符串求值每个正则表达式公式获得的结果。我们分别称这些查询为正则${CQs}$和正则${UCQs}$。例如，(1)式中的查询就是一个正则CQ。在数据复杂度（假设查询固定）下，查询求值总能在多项式时间内完成。本文重点研究查询和数据（字符串）同时作为输入的组合复杂度。有人可能认为现有UCQ求值文献通过所谓规范关系求值法——即在输入字符串上求值每个独立正则表达式公式后，将其视为普通关系数据库进行UCQ计算——应已完整描绘了复杂度图景。但这一主张存在若干问题。

The first problem is that lower bounds on UCQ evaluation do not carry over immediately to our setting, since the input is not an arbitrary relational database, but rather a very specific one: every relation is obtained by applying a regex to the (same) input string. But, quite expectedly, we show that the standard lower bound of NP-completeness [7] for Boolean CQs (we well as W[1]-hardness for some parameters) remains in our setting. The more surprising finding is that hardness holds even if the input string is a single character! Yet, a more fundamental problem with the canonical relational approach to evaluation is that it may be infeasible to materialize the relation defined by a regex formula, as the number of tuples in that relation may be exponential in the size of the input. This problem provably increases the complexity, as we show that Boolean regex-CQ evaluation is NP-complete even on acyclic CQs, and even on the more restricted gamma-acyclic CQs. In contrast, acyclic CQs (and more generally CQs of bounded hypertree width [18]) admit polynomial-time evaluation. Finally, even if we were guaranteed a polynomial bound on the result of each atomic regex formula, it would not necessarily mean that we can actually materialize the corresponding relation in polynomial time.

首要问题是UCQ求值的下界不能直接迁移到我们的设定，因为输入并非任意关系数据库，而是高度特定的：每个关系都是通过对（同一）输入字符串应用正则表达式获得。但正如预期，我们证明布尔CQs的NP完全性标准下界[7]（以及某些参数的W[1]-难度）在本设定中依然成立。更令人惊异的发现是：即使输入字符串仅为单个字符，计算难度仍然存在！然而，规范关系求值法更根本的问题在于，正则表达式公式定义的关系可能因元组数量呈输入规模指数级增长而无法物化。我们证明该问题确实会提升复杂度：即使对于非循环CQs乃至限制更强的γ-非循环CQs，布尔正则CQ求值仍是NP完全的。相比之下，非循环CQs（以及更广义的有界超树宽CQs[18]）允许多项式时间求值。最后，即便能保证每个原子正则表达式公式的结果有多项式边界，也不意味着能实际在多项式时间内物化相应关系。

Yet, in spite of the above daunting complexity we are able to establish some substantial upper bounds. Our upper bounds are based on a central algorithm that we devise in this paper for evaluating a variable-set automaton (vset-automaton for short) over a string. More formally,recall that a vset-automaton $A$ represents a spanner,which we denote as $\llbracket A\rrbracket$ . When evaluating $A$ on a string $\mathbf{s}$ , the result is a relation $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{s}\right)$ over the spans of $\mathrm{s}$ . The number of tuples in $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{s}\right) \left( \mathrm{s}\right)$ can be exponential in the size of the input ( $\mathrm{s}$ and $A$ ). Our algorithm takes as input a string $s$ and a vset-automaton $A$ (or,more precisely,a functional vset-automaton [16]) and enumerates the tuples of $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{s}\right)$ (s) with polynomial delay [23]. This is done by a nontrivial reduction to the problem of enumerating all the words of a specific length accepted by an NFA [2]. Our central algorithm implies several upper bounds, which we establish in two approaches: (a) via what we call the canonical relational evaluation, and (b) via compilation to automata.

尽管面临上述艰巨复杂性，我们仍能建立若干实质性上界。这些上界基于本文设计的核心算法，用于评估字符串上的变量集自动机（简称vset-automaton）。严格来说，vset-automaton $A$代表一个提取器（spanner），记作$\llbracket A\rrbracket$。当在字符串$\mathbf{s}$上评估$A$时，结果是对$\mathrm{s}$跨度进行关系运算$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{s}\right)$。该关系元组数量可能随输入规模（$\mathrm{s}$和$A$）呈指数增长。我们的算法以字符串$s$和vset-automaton $A$（更准确说是功能性vset-automaton[16]）为输入，通过多项式延迟[23]枚举$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{s}\right)$(s)的元组。这通过将问题归约至枚举NFA[2]接受特定长度单词的非平凡转化实现。核心算法通过两种方法推导多个上界：(a)规范关系评估法；(b)自动机编译法。

The first approach utilizes known algorithms for relational UCQ evaluation, by materializing the relations defined by the regex formulas. For that, we devise an efficient compilation of a regex formula into a vset-automaton, and establish that a regex formula can be evaluated in polynomial total time (and even polynomial delay). In particular, we can efficiently materialize the relations of each atom of a regex UCQ, whenever we have a polynomial bound on the cardinality of this relation. Hence, there is no need for a specialized algorithm for each cardinality guarantee-one algorithm fits all. Consequently, under such cardinality guarantees, canonical relational evaluation is efficient whenever the underlying UCQ is tractable (e.g., each CQ is acyclic).

第一种方法通过物化正则公式定义的关系，利用关系型UCQ评估的已知算法。为此，我们开发了将正则公式高效编译为vset-automaton的技术，并证明正则公式可在多项式总时间（甚至多项式延迟）内完成评估。特别地，当关系基数存在多项式边界时，我们能高效物化正则UCQ各原子的关系。因此无需为每种基数保证设计专用算法——一套算法即可通用。在此类基数保证下，只要底层UCQ可高效处理（如每个CQ均为无环查询），规范关系评估法即具备高效性。

In the second approach, compilation to automata, we compile the entire regex UCQ into a vset-automaton. Combining our polynomial-delay algorithm with known results $\left\lbrack  {{12},{15}}\right\rbrack$ ,we conclude that regex UCQs can be evaluated with Fixed-Parameter Tractable (FPT) delay when the size of the UCQ is the parameter. Moreover, we prove that the compilation is efficient if every disjunct (regex $\mathrm{{CQ}}$ ) has a bounded number atoms. In particular, we show that every join of a bounded number of vset-automata can be compiled in polynomial time into a single vset-automaton, and every projection and union (with no bounds) over vset-automata can be compiled in polynomial time into a single vset-automaton. Hence, we establish that for every fixed $k$ ,the evaluation of regex $k$ -UCQs (where each CQ has at most $k$ atoms) can be performed with polynomial delay.

第二种自动机编译法将整个正则UCQ编译为单个vset-automaton。结合我们的多项式延迟算法与已知成果$\left\lbrack  {{12},{15}}\right\rbrack$，可得出：当以UCQ规模为参数时，正则UCQ评估具有固定参数可处理(FPT)延迟特性。进一步证明，当每个析取项（正则$\mathrm{{CQ}}$）原子数有界时，编译过程高效。具体表现为：有界数量vset-automaton的连接运算、以及无限制的投影与并运算，均可多项式时间编译为单个vset-automaton。因此对任意固定参数$k$，含最多$k$个原子的正则$k$-UCQ评估均能以多项式延迟执行。

Finally, we generalize our results to allow regex-UCQ to include string-equality predicates, which are expressions that express the relationship " $x$ and $y$ span the same substring,possibly in different locations". For example, the following regex CQ with a string equality finds sentences that have the address as expressed in our previous example spanner (see (1) above), but they are not necessarily the same sentences (and in particular they may exclude the word police).

最后我们将结果推广至支持包含字符串等价谓词的正则-UCQ，这类谓词用于表达"$x$与$y$跨相同子串（位置可能不同）"的关系。例如以下带字符串等价的正则CQ，可查找包含前例提取器所示地址（见前文(1)）但未必相同的句子（尤其可能排除police一词）。

$$
{\pi }_{{x}^{\prime }}{\zeta }_{y,{y}^{\prime }}^{ = }\left( {Q\left\lbrack  {w,x,y,z}\right\rbrack   \boxtimes  {\alpha }_{\text{sen }}\left\lbrack  {x}^{\prime }\right\rbrack   \boxtimes  {\alpha }_{\text{sub }}\left\lbrack  {{y}^{\prime },{x}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right) 
$$

where $Q\left\lbrack  {w,x,y,z}\right\rbrack$ is the join expression inside the projection of (1) above. As shown in [16], adding an unbounded number of string equalities can make a tractable regex UCQ intractable, even if that regex UCQ is a single regex formula. We prove that now we no longer retain the above FPT delay, as the problem is W[1]-hard when the parameter is the size of the query.

其中$Q\left\lbrack  {w,x,y,z}\right\rbrack$为前文(1)投影运算中的连接表达式。如[16]所示，添加无界数量字符串等价谓词会使可处理正则UCQ转为难解问题——即使该正则UCQ仅为单个正则公式。我们证明此时不再保持前述FPT延迟特性，因为当查询规模作为参数时，该问题属于W[1]-难问题。

Nevertheless, much of the two evaluation approaches generalize to string equalities. While this generalization is immediate for the first approach, the second faces a challenge-it is impossible to compile string equality into a vset-automaton [12]. Yet, we show that with our compilation techniques, one can compile in string equality for the specific input string at hand (that is, not statically but rather at runtime). Hence,we conclude that regex $k$ -UCQs with a bounded number of string equalities can be evaluated with polynomial delay.

尽管如此，这两种评估方法大多可推广至字符串等价性。虽然第一种方法能直接实现这种推广，但第二种方法面临一个挑战——无法将字符串等价性编译为vset自动机[12]。然而，我们证明通过编译技术，可为特定输入字符串（即非静态而是运行时）实现字符串等价性编译。因此得出结论：具有有限数量字符串等价性的正则表达式$k$-UCQs可通过多项式延迟进行评估。

In Section 3 we give our complexity results for regex UCQs. We describe our polynomial-delay algorithm for evaluating vset-automata in Section 4. In Section 5 we generalize our complexity results to UCQs with string equalities. Finally, we conclude in Section 6.

## 2 BASICS OF DOCUMENT SPANNERS

## 2 文档解析器基础

We begin by recalling essentials of document spanners [12, 13, 16], a formal framework for Information Extraction (IE) inspired by declarative IE systems such as Xlog [34] and IBM's SystemT [24, 25, 31].

我们首先回顾文档解析器[12,13,16]的核心概念，这是受Xlog[34]和IBM SystemT[24,25,31]等声明式信息抽取系统启发的信息抽取(IE)形式化框架。

### 2.1 Strings, Spans and Spanners

### 2.1 字符串、文本跨度与解析器

Throughout this paper,we fix a finite alphabet $\sum$ . Unless explicitly stated,we assume that $\left| \sum \right|  \geq  2$ . A string is a sequence ${\sigma }_{1}{\sigma }_{2}\cdots {\sigma }_{N}$ of symbols from $\sum$ . The set of all strings is denoted by ${\sum }^{ * }$ . We use bold (non-italic) letters,such as $\mathbf{s}$ and $\mathbf{t}$ ,to denote strings,and $\epsilon$ to denote the empty string. The length $N$ of a string $\mathbf{s} \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\sigma }_{1}{\sigma }_{2}\cdots {\sigma }_{N}$ is denoted by $\left| \mathbf{s}\right|$ ; and ${\left| \mathbf{s}\right| }_{\sigma }$ denotes the number of occurrences of a symbol $\sigma$ in $\mathbf{s}$ .

全文设定有限字母表$\sum$。除非特别说明，假定$\left| \sum \right|  \geq  2$。字符串是由$\sum$中符号组成的序列${\sigma }_{1}{\sigma }_{2}\cdots {\sigma }_{N}$，所有字符串集合记作${\sum }^{ * }$。使用粗体（非斜体）字母如$\mathbf{s}$和$\mathbf{t}$表示字符串，$\epsilon$表示空字符串。字符串$\mathbf{s} \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\sigma }_{1}{\sigma }_{2}\cdots {\sigma }_{N}$的长度记作$\left| \mathbf{s}\right|$；${\left| \mathbf{s}\right| }_{\sigma }$表示符号$\sigma$在$\mathbf{s}$中的出现次数。

Let $\mathbf{s} \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\sigma }_{1}{\sigma }_{2}\cdots {\sigma }_{N}$ be a string. A span of $\mathbf{s}$ represents an interval of characters in $s$ by indicating the bounding indices. Formally,a span of $\mathbf{s}$ is an expression of the form $\lbrack i,j\rangle$ with $1 \leq  i \leq  j \leq  N + 1$ . For a span $\lbrack i,j\rangle$ of $\mathbf{s}$ ,we denote by ${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack i,j\rangle }$ the string ${\sigma }_{i}\cdots {\sigma }_{j - 1}$ . Note that two spans $\left\lbrack  {{i}_{1},{j}_{1}}\right\rangle$ and $\left\lbrack  {{i}_{2},{j}_{2}}\right\rangle$ are equal if and only if both ${i}_{1} = {i}_{2}$ and ${j}_{1} = {j}_{2}$ hold. In particular, ${\mathbf{s}}_{\left\lbrack  {i}_{1},{j}_{1}\right\rangle  } = {\mathbf{s}}_{\left\lbrack  {i}_{2},{j}_{2}\right\rangle  }$ does not necessarily imply that $\left\lbrack  {{i}_{1},{j}_{1}}\right\rangle   = \left\lbrack  {{i}_{2},{j}_{2}}\right\rangle$ .

设$\mathbf{s} \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\sigma }_{1}{\sigma }_{2}\cdots {\sigma }_{N}$为字符串，$\mathbf{s}$的文本跨度通过界定索引表示字符区间。形式上，$\mathbf{s}$的跨度表达式为$\lbrack i,j\rangle$且满足$1 \leq  i \leq  j \leq  N + 1$。对于$\mathbf{s}$的跨度$\lbrack i,j\rangle$，${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack i,j\rangle }$表示字符串${\sigma }_{i}\cdots {\sigma }_{j - 1}$。注意两个跨度$\left\lbrack  {{i}_{1},{j}_{1}}\right\rangle$与$\left\lbrack  {{i}_{2},{j}_{2}}\right\rangle$相等当且仅当${i}_{1} = {i}_{2}$和${j}_{1} = {j}_{2}$同时成立。特别地，${\mathbf{s}}_{\left\lbrack  {i}_{1},{j}_{1}\right\rangle  } = {\mathbf{s}}_{\left\lbrack  {i}_{2},{j}_{2}\right\rangle  }$并不必然意味着$\left\lbrack  {{i}_{1},{j}_{1}}\right\rangle   = \left\lbrack  {{i}_{2},{j}_{2}}\right\rangle$。

EXAMPLE 2.1. Our examples assume that $\sum$ consists of the Latin characters and some common additional symbols such as punctuation and commercial at (@). In addition, the symbol _ stands for whitespace.

例2.1. 示例假设$\sum$包含拉丁字符及常见附加符号（如标点和@符号）。此外，下划线_代表空白符。

Let $\mathbf{s}$ be the string chocolate_cookie. Then $\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  = {16}$ . The strings ${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack 4,6\rangle }$ and ${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack {11},{13}\rangle }$ are both equal (to the string co),and yet,we have $\lbrack 4,6\rangle  \neq  \lbrack {11},{13}\rangle$ . Likewise,we observe ${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack 1,1\rangle } = {\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack 2,2\rangle } = \epsilon$ ,but $\lbrack 1,1\rangle  \neq  \lbrack 2,2\rangle$ . Finally,note that $\mathbf{s}$ is the same as ${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack 1,{17}\rangle }$ .

设$\mathbf{s}$为字符串chocolate_cookie，则$\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  = {16}$。字符串${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack 4,6\rangle }$与${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack {11},{13}\rangle }$均等于字符串co，然而$\lbrack 4,6\rangle  \neq  \lbrack {11},{13}\rangle$。同理可观察到${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack 1,1\rangle } = {\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack 2,2\rangle } = \epsilon$，但$\lbrack 1,1\rangle  \neq  \lbrack 2,2\rangle$。最后注意$\mathbf{s}$与${\mathbf{s}}_{\lbrack 1,{17}\rangle }$相同。

We assume an infinite set Vars of variables,disjoint from $\sum$ . For a finite $V \subset$ Vars and $\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$ ,a(V,s)-tuple is a function $\mu$ that maps each variable in $V$ to a span of $\mathbf{s}$ . If context allows,we write $\mathbf{s}$ -tuple instead of(V,s)-tuple. A set of(V,s)-tuples is called a(V,s)-relation. A spanner is a function $P$ that is associated with a finite variable set $V$ ,denoted $\operatorname{Vars}\left( P\right)$ ,and that maps every string $\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$ to a (V,s)-relation $P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ .

假设存在与$\sum$互不相交的无限变量集Vars。对于有限变量集$V \subset$⊆Vars及字符串$\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$，(V,s)元组是函数$\mu$，它将$V$中每个变量映射到$\mathbf{s}$的片段。若上下文允许，可用$\mathbf{s}$元组替代(V,s)元组。(V,s)元组集合称为(V,s)关系。扩展器是函数$P$，其关联有限变量集$V$（记作$\operatorname{Vars}\left( P\right)$），并将每个字符串$\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$映射到(V,s)关系$P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$。

A spanner $P$ is Boolean if $\operatorname{Vars}\left( P\right)  = \varnothing$ . If $P$ is Boolean,then either $P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)  = \varnothing$ or $P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ contains only the empty $\left( {\varnothing ,\mathbf{s}}\right)$ -tuple; we interpret these two cases as false and true, respectively.

当$\operatorname{Vars}\left( P\right)  = \varnothing$时，扩展器$P$为布尔型。若$P$为布尔型，则$P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)  = \varnothing$或$P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$仅含空$\left( {\varnothing ,\mathbf{s}}\right)$元组，这两种情况分别解释为假与真。

### 2.2 Spanner Representations

### 2.2 扩展器表示法

In this paper, we use two models as basic building blocks for spanner representations: regex formulas and vset-automata. The two can be understood as extensions of regular expressions and NFAs, respectively, with variables. Both models were introduced by Fagin et al. [12], and following Freydenberger [15] we define the semantics of these models using so-called ref-words [33] (short for reference-words).

本文采用正则表达式公式与变量集自动机作为扩展器表示的基本模型。二者可分别理解为带变量的正则表达式与非确定有限自动机的扩展。这两个模型由Fagin等人[12]提出，遵循Freydenberger[15]的研究，我们使用引用词[33]（reference-words的缩写）来定义其语义。

2.2.1 Ref-words. For a finite variable set $V \subset$ Vars,ref-words are defined over the extended alphabet $\sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}$ ,where ${\Gamma }_{V}$ consists of two symbols, $x \vdash$ and $\neg x$ ,for each variable $x \in  V$ . We assume that $\sum$ and ${\Gamma }_{V}$ are disjoint. Intuitively,the letters $x \vdash$ and $\neg x$ represent opening or closing a variable $x$ . Hence,ref-words can be understood as terminal strings that are extended with encodings of variable operations. As we shall see, treating these variable operations as letters allows us to adapt techniques from automata theory.

2.2.1 引用词。对于有限变量集$V \subset$⊆Vars，引用词在扩展字母表$\sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}$上定义，其中${\Gamma }_{V}$包含每变量$x \in  V$对应的两个符号：$x \vdash$（开启）与$\neg x$（关闭）。假定$\sum$与${\Gamma }_{V}$不相交。直观上，符号$x \vdash$和$\neg x$分别表示变量的开启与关闭操作。因此，引用词可视为附加变量操作编码的终端字符串。如后文所示，将这些变量操作视为字母可实现自动机理论的技法迁移。

A ref-word $\mathbf{r} \in  {\left( \sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}\right) }^{ * }$ is valid for $V$ if each variable of $V$ is opened and then closed exactly once,or more formally,for each $x \in  \operatorname{Vars}\left( A\right)$ the string $\mathbf{r}$ has precisely one occurrence of $x \vdash$ , precisely one occurrence of $- x$ ,and the former occurrence takes place before (i.e., to the left of) the latter.

当引用词$\mathbf{r} \in  {\left( \sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}\right) }^{ * }$满足以下条件时对$V$有效：$V$中每个变量被开启并恰好关闭一次，或更形式化地说，对每个$x \in  \operatorname{Vars}\left( A\right)$，字符串$\mathbf{r}$恰好包含一个$x \vdash$和一个$- x$，且前者出现于后者左侧。

Note that ${\mathbf{r}}_{1}$ and ${\mathbf{r}}_{2}$ are not valid for ${V}^{\prime }$ with ${V}^{\prime } \supset  V$ ,as all variables of ${V}^{\prime }$ must be opened and closed.

By definition,every $\mathbf{r} \in  \operatorname{Ref}\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ has a unique factorization

$$
\alpha  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \varnothing \left| \epsilon \right| \sigma \left| \left( {\alpha  \vee  \alpha }\right) \right| \left( {\alpha  \cdot  \alpha }\right) \left| {\alpha }^{ * }\right| x\{ \alpha \}

If $\alpha$ is of the form $x\{ \beta \}$ ,then $\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} x \vdash  \mathcal{R}\left( \beta \right)  - x$ . Otherwise, $\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$ is defined like the language $\mathcal{L}\left( \alpha \right)$ of a regular expression; for example, $\mathcal{R}\left( {\alpha  \cdot  \beta }\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)  \cdot  \mathcal{R}\left( \beta \right) .$

我们将每个正则表达式公式$\alpha$解释为扩展字母表$\sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{\operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right) }$上引用词语言$\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$的生成器${}^{1}$。若$\alpha$形如$x\{ \beta \}$，则$\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} x \vdash  \mathcal{R}\left( \beta \right)  - x$。否则，$\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$的定义方式与正则表达式语言$\mathcal{L}\left( \alpha \right)$相同；例如$\mathcal{R}\left( {\alpha  \cdot  \beta }\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)  \cdot  \mathcal{R}\left( \beta \right) .$

We let $\operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$ denote the set of all ref-words in $\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$ that are valid for $\operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right)$ ; and for every string $\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$ ,we define $\operatorname{Ref}\left( {\alpha ,\mathbf{s}}\right)  =$ $\operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)  \cap  \operatorname{Ref}\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ . In other words, $\operatorname{Ref}\left( {\alpha ,\mathbf{s}}\right)$ contains exactly those valid ref-words from $\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$ that clr maps to $\mathbf{s}$ .

我们用$\operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$表示$\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$中所有对$\operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right)$有效的引用词集合；对于每个字符串$\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$，定义$\operatorname{Ref}\left( {\alpha ,\mathbf{s}}\right)  =$$\operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)  \cap  \operatorname{Ref}\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$。换言之，$\operatorname{Ref}\left( {\alpha ,\mathbf{s}}\right)$精确包含$\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$中那些被clr映射到$\mathbf{s}$的有效引用词。

Finally,the spanner $\llbracket \alpha \rrbracket$ is the one that defines the following $\left( {\operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right) ,\mathbf{s}}\right)$ -relation for every string $\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$ :

最终，扩展器$\llbracket \alpha \rrbracket$为每个字符串$\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$定义如下$\left( {\operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right) ,\mathbf{s}}\right)$关系：

$$

$$

We say that a regex-formula $\alpha$ is functional if $\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)  = \operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$ ; that is,every ref-word in $\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$ is valid. Fagin et al. [12] gave an algorithm for testing whether a regex formula is functional. By analyzing the complexity of their construction, we conclude the following.

当$\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)  = \operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$时，称正则表达式公式$\alpha$是功能性的，即$\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$中每个引用词均有效。Fagin等人[12]提出了检测正则表达式功能性的算法。通过分析其构造复杂度，我们得出以下结论。

THEOREM 2.4. [12] Whether a regex formula $\alpha$ with $v$ variables is functional can be tested in $O\left( {\left| \alpha \right|  \cdot  v}\right)$ time.

定理2.4. [12] 含$v$个变量的正则表达式公式$\alpha$是否具有功能性，可在$O\left( {\left| \alpha \right|  \cdot  v}\right)$时间内完成检测。

Following [12], we assume regex formulas are functional unless explicitly noted (in fact,[12] does not even define $\llbracket \alpha \rrbracket$ for nonfunctional regex formulas $\alpha$ ).

2.2.3 Variable-Set Automata. While the current paper is mostly motivated by spanners that are constructed using regex formulas, our upper bounds for these are obtained by converting regex formulas to the following automata model.

2.2.3 变量集自动机。虽然本文主要研究基于正则公式构建的扩展器，但我们通过将正则公式转换为以下自动机模型来获得其上界。

A variable-set automaton (vset-automaton for short) with variables from a finite set $V \subset$ Vars can be understood as an $\epsilon$ -NFA (i.e., an NFA with epsilon transitions allowed) that is extended with edges labeled with variable operations $x \vdash$ or $\neg x$ for $x \in  V$ . Formally, a vset-automaton is a tuple $A \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,Q,{q}_{0},{q}_{f},\delta }\right)$ ,where $V$ is a finite set of variables, $Q$ is the set of states, ${q}_{0},{q}_{f} \in  Q$ are the initial and the final states,respectively,and $\delta  : Q \times  \left( {\sum  \cup  \left\{  {\epsilon \}  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}}\right\}   \rightarrow  {2}^{Q}}\right)$ is the transition function. 变量集自动机（简称vset-automaton）从有限变量集$V \subset$Vars中获取变量，可理解为允许epsilon转移的$\epsilon$-NFA（即非确定性有限自动机），并通过标记变量操作$x \vdash$或$\neg x$的边进行扩展（其中$x \in  V$）。形式化定义下，vset-automaton是一个元组$A \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,Q,{q}_{0},{q}_{f},\delta }\right)$，其中$V$为有限变量集，$Q$为状态集合，${q}_{0},{q}_{f} \in  Q$分别表示初始状态和终止状态，$\delta  : Q \times  \left( {\sum  \cup  \left\{  {\epsilon \}  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}}\right\}   \rightarrow  {2}^{Q}}\right)$为转移函数。记$\operatorname{Vars}\left( A\right)$为集合$V$。

The vset-automaton $A \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,Q,{q}_{0},{q}_{f},\delta }\right)$ can be interpreted as a directed graph,where the nodes are the states,and every $q \in  \delta \left( {p,a}\right)$ is represented by an edge from $p$ to $q$ with the label $a$ . To define the semantics of $A$ ,we first interpret $A$ as an $\epsilon$ -NFA over the terminal alphabet $\sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}$ ,and define its ref-word language $\mathcal{R}\left( A\right)$ as the set of all ref-words $\mathbf{r} \in  {\left( \sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}\right) }^{ * }$ such that some path from ${q}_{0}$ to ${q}_{f}$ is labeled with $\mathbf{r}$ .

vset-automaton$A \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {V,Q,{q}_{0},{q}_{f},\delta }\right)$可视为有向图，节点对应状态，每条$q \in  \delta \left( {p,a}\right)$转换体现为从$p$到$q$的带标记边$a$。为定义$A$的语义，首先将其解释为基于终端字母表$\sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}$的$\epsilon$-NFA，并定义其引用词语言$\mathcal{R}\left( A\right)$为所有满足条件的引用词$\mathbf{r} \in  {\left( \sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}\right) }^{ * }$集合：存在从${q}_{0}$到${q}_{f}$的路径标记为$\mathbf{r}$。

We define the string equality selection ${\zeta }_{{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}^{ = }P$ by $\operatorname{Vars}\left( {{\zeta }_{{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}^{ = }P}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \operatorname{Vars}\left( P\right)$ and,for all $\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$ ,we have that ${\zeta }_{{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}^{ = }P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ is the set of all $\mu  \in  P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ for which ${\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {x}_{1}\right) } = \cdots  = {\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {x}_{k}\right) }$ holds.

选择：设${x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k} \in  \operatorname{Vars}\left( P\right)$。我们通过$\operatorname{Vars}\left( {{\zeta }_{{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}^{ = }P}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \operatorname{Vars}\left( P\right)$定义字符串相等选择${\zeta }_{{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}^{ = }P$，且对于所有$\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$，${\zeta }_{{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}^{ = }P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$是满足${\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {x}_{1}\right) } = \cdots  = {\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {x}_{k}\right) }$的所有$\mu  \in  P\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$的集合。

Note that the join operator joins tuples that have identical spans in their shared variables. In contrast, the selection operator compares the substrings of $\mathbf{s}$ that are described by the spans,and does not distinguish between different spans that span the same substrings.

注意连接运算符作用于共享变量中具有相同跨度的元组。而选择运算符比较的是由跨度描述的$\mathbf{s}$子字符串，且不区分跨越相同子字符串的不同跨度。

Following Fagin et al. A spanner algebra is a finite set of spanner operators. If $\mathrm{O}$ is a spanner algebra and $C$ is a class of primitive spanner representations,then ${C}^{\mathrm{O}}$ denotes the set of all spanner representations that can be constructed by (repeated) combination of the symbols for the operators from $\mathrm{O}$ with spanners from $C$ . For each spanner representation of the form $o\varrho$ or ${\varrho }_{1}o{\varrho }_{2}$ ,where $o \in  \mathrm{O}$ and $\varrho ,{\varrho }_{1},{\varrho }_{2} \in  C$ ,we define $\llbracket o\varrho \rrbracket  = o\llbracket \varrho \rrbracket$ and $\llbracket {\varrho }_{1}o{\varrho }_{2}\rrbracket  =$ $\left\lbrack  {\left\lbrack  {\varrho }_{1}\right\rbrack  ,o\left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {\varrho }_{2}\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack$ . Furthermore, $\left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {C}^{\mathrm{O}}\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack$ is the closure of $\llbracket C\rrbracket$ under the spanner operators in $\mathrm{O}$ .

依照Fagin等人[12]的表述，我们将正则表达式公式和vset自动机称为基本跨度表示器，并分别用${\mathrm{{VA}}}_{\text{set }}$和RGX表示所有vset自动机及所有功能性正则表达式公式的集合。跨度代数是一组有限的跨度运算符。若$\mathrm{O}$是跨度代数且$C$是基本跨度表示器的类，则${C}^{\mathrm{O}}$表示通过$\mathrm{O}$中的运算符符号与$C$中的跨度器（重复）组合可构造的所有跨度表示器的集合。对于形如$o\varrho$或${\varrho }_{1}o{\varrho }_{2}$的每个跨度表示器（其中$o \in  \mathrm{O}$和$\varrho ,{\varrho }_{1},{\varrho }_{2} \in  C$），我们定义$\llbracket o\varrho \rrbracket  = o\llbracket \varrho \rrbracket$和$\llbracket {\varrho }_{1}o{\varrho }_{2}\rrbracket  =$$\left\lbrack  {\left\lbrack  {\varrho }_{1}\right\rbrack  ,o\left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {\varrho }_{2}\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack  }\right\rbrack$。此外，$\left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {C}^{\mathrm{O}}\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack$是$\llbracket C\rrbracket$在$\mathrm{O}$中跨度运算符下的闭包。

Fagin et al. One of the main results of [12] is that

因此，${\mathrm{{VA}}}_{\text{set }}\left\{  {\pi ,{\zeta }^{ = },\cup , \bowtie  }\right\}$与核心跨度表示法具有相同的表达能力。正则跨度是指能用正则跨度表示法表示的跨度，核心跨度则指能用核心跨度表示法表示的跨度。Fagin等人[12]还证明了正则跨度类在差集运算符下是封闭的（即$\left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {\mathrm{{RGX}}}^{\{ \pi ,\cup , \bowtie  \} }\right. \right\rbrack   =$$\left. \left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {\operatorname{RGX}}^{\{ \pi ,\cup , \bowtie  , \smallsetminus  \} }\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack  \right)$），但核心跨度类不具备此性质。

## 3 COMPLEXITY OF UCQ EVALUATION

## 3 UCQ求值的复杂性

In this section we give our main complexity results for the evaluation of regex UCQs. We remark that in this section we do not allow string equalities; these will be discussed in Section 5. We begin with a description of the complexity measures we adopt.

本节将给出关于正则表达式联合查询求值的主要复杂性结论。需要说明的是，本节不允许字符串相等条件，相关内容将在第5节讨论。我们首先介绍采用的复杂度度量方法。

### 3.1 Complexity Measures

### 3.1 复杂度度量

Under the measure of data complexity,where the UCQ $q$ at hand is assumed fixed (and the string $\mathbf{s}$ is given as input),it is a straightforward observation that query evaluation can be done in polynomial time (see Freydenberger and Holldack [16]). Hence, our main measure of complexity is that of combined complexity where both $q$ and $s$ are given as input.

在数据复杂度度量下（假设当前UCQ$q$固定，字符串$\mathbf{s}$作为输入），可以直观观察到查询求值可在多项式时间内完成（参见Freydenberger和Holldack[16]）。因此，我们主要采用将$q$和$s$同时作为输入的联合复杂度度量。

The task of evaluating a regex query $q$ over an input $s$ requires the solver algorithm to produce all tuples in $\llbracket \gamma \left( \mathbf{s}\right) \rrbracket$ for each regex formula $\gamma$ in $q$ . In the worst case there could be exponentially many tuples, and so polynomial time is not a proper yardstick of efficiency. For such problems, Johnson, Papadimitriou and Yannakakis [23] introduced several complexity guarantees, which we recall here. An enumeration problem $\mathrm{E}$ is a collection of pairs(x,Y)where $x$ is an input and $Y$ is a finite set of answers for $x$ ,denoted by $\mathrm{E}\left( x\right)$ . In our case, $x$ has the form(q,s)and $\mathrm{E}\left( x\right)$ is $\llbracket q\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ . A solver for an enumeration problem $\mathbf{E}$ is an algorithm that,when given an input $x$ , produces a sequence of answers such that every answer in $\mathrm{E}\left( x\right)$ is printed precisely once. We say that a solver $S$ for an enumeration problem E runs in:

评估正则表达式查询$q$在输入$s$上的任务，要求求解算法为$q$中的每个正则表达式公式$\gamma$生成$\llbracket \gamma \left( \mathbf{s}\right) \rrbracket$中的所有元组。最坏情况下可能存在指数级数量的元组，因此多项式时间并非衡量效率的合理标准。对此类问题，Johnson、Papadimitriou和Yannakakis[23]提出了若干复杂度保证，现重述如下：枚举问题$\mathrm{E}$是由(x,Y)对组成的集合，其中$x$为输入，$Y$是$x$对应的有限答案集，记作$\mathrm{E}\left( x\right)$。本文案例中，$x$形式为(q,s)且$\mathrm{E}\left( x\right)$即$\llbracket q\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$。枚举问题$\mathbf{E}$的求解算法在接收输入$x$后，需生成答案序列并确保$\mathrm{E}\left( x\right)$中每个答案被精确输出一次。我们称枚举问题E的求解器$S$具有以下时间复杂度：

- polynomial total time if the total execution time of $S$ is polynomial in $\left( {\left| x\right|  + \left| {\mathrm{E}\left( x\right) }\right| }\right)$ ;

- 多项式总时间：当$S$的总执行时间在$\left( {\left| x\right|  + \left| {\mathrm{E}\left( x\right) }\right| }\right)$上呈多项式时

- polynomial delay if the time between every two consecutive answers produced is polynomial in $\left| x\right|$ .

- 多项式延迟：当每两个连续答案的生成间隔时间在$\left| x\right|$上呈多项式时

We also consider parameterized complexity $\left\lbrack  {{10},{20}}\right\rbrack$ for various parameters determined by $q$ . Formally,a parameterized problem is a decision problem where the input consists of a pair(x,k),where $x$ is an ordinary input and $k$ is a parameter (typically small,relates to a property of $x$ ). Such a problem is Fixed-Parameter Tractable (FPT) if there is a polynomial $p$ ,a computable function $f$ and a solver $S$ , such that $S$ terminates in time $f\left( k\right)  \cdot  p\left( \left| x\right| \right)$ on input(x,k). We similarly define FPT-delay for a parameterized enumeration algorithm: the delay between every two consecutive answers is bounded by $f\left( k\right)  \cdot  p\left( \left| x\right| \right)$ . A standard lower bound is $\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ -hardness,and the standard complexity assumption is that a W[1]-hard problem is not FPT [20].

我们还考虑由$q$确定的各类参数的参数化复杂度$\left\lbrack  {{10},{20}}\right\rbrack$。形式化而言，参数化问题是指输入为(x,k)对的决策问题，其中$x$为常规输入，$k$为参数（通常较小，与$x$的某个属性相关）。若存在多项式$p$、可计算函数$f$及求解器$S$，使得$S$在输入(x,k)时于$f\left( k\right)  \cdot  p\left( \left| x\right| \right)$时间内终止，则该问题属于固定参数可解(FPT)。类似地，对于参数化枚举算法，若每两个连续答案间的延迟受$f\left( k\right)  \cdot  p\left( \left| x\right| \right)$约束，则定义为FPT延迟。标准下界为$\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$难度，常规复杂度假设认为W[1]难问题不属于FPT[20]。

Whenever we give an upper bound, it applies to a general UCQ, and whenever we give a lower bound, it applies to Boolean CQs. When we give asymptotic running times, we assume the unit-cost RAM-model, where the size of each machine word is logarithmic in the size of the input. Regarding $\sum$ ,our lower bounds and asymptotic upper bounds assume that it is fixed with at least two characters; our "polynomial" upper bounds hold even if $\sum$ is given as part of the input.

We can now derive the nodes ${v}_{c\left( 1\right) }$ to ${v}_{c\left( k\right) }$ of the clique directly from the variables ${x}_{i,j}$ and ${y}_{i,j}$ ,as for each $l,\mu  \in  \llbracket {\delta }_{l}\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{\;s}\right)$ ensures that there is a unique $c\left( l\right)$ such that ${\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {y}_{i,l}\right) } = {\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {x}_{l,j}\right) } = {\mathbf{v}}_{c\left( l\right) }$ for all $1 \leq  i < l < j \leq  k$ . Furthermore, $\mu  \in  \llbracket \gamma \rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ ensures that for all $i < j,\mu \left( {x}_{i,j}\right)$ and $\mu \left( {y}_{i,j}\right)$ map to the encoding of the edge $\left\{  {{v}_{c\left( i\right) },{v}_{c\left( j\right) }}\right\}$ in s. Hence, $\left\{  {{v}_{c\left( 1\right) },\ldots ,{v}_{c\left( k\right) }}\right\}$ is a $k$ -clique in $G$ .

现假设$\mu  \in  \llbracket q\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$。可直接从变量${x}_{i,j}$和${y}_{i,j}$推导出团的节点${v}_{c\left( 1\right) }$至${v}_{c\left( k\right) }$，因为每个$l,\mu  \in  \llbracket {\delta }_{l}\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{\;s}\right)$确保存在唯一的$c\left( l\right)$使得对所有$1 \leq  i < l < j \leq  k$有${\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {y}_{i,l}\right) } = {\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {x}_{l,j}\right) } = {\mathbf{v}}_{c\left( l\right) }$。此外，$\mu  \in  \llbracket \gamma \rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$保证所有$i < j,\mu \left( {x}_{i,j}\right)$和$\mu \left( {y}_{i,j}\right)$都映射到s中边$\left\{  {{v}_{c\left( i\right) },{v}_{c\left( j\right) }}\right\}$的编码。因此，$\left\{  {{v}_{c\left( 1\right) },\ldots ,{v}_{c\left( k\right) }}\right\}$是$G$中的$k$-团。

Note that the we did not show W[1]-hardness with respect to the length of the query.

需注意我们未证明查询长度相关的W[1]-难性。

### 3.3 Upper Bounds

### 3.3 上界

3.3.1 Evaluation of Variable-Set-Automata. Our central complexity result states that functional vset-automata can be evaluated with polynomial delay.

3.3.1 变量集自动机的求值。我们的核心复杂度结果表明，功能性vset-自动机可在多项式延迟内完成求值。

THEOREM 3.3. Given a functional vset-automaton $A$ with $n$ states and $m$ transitions,and a string $\mathbf{s}$ ,one can enumerate $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ with polynomial delay of $O\left( {{n}^{2}\left| \mathbf{s}\right| }\right)$ ,following a polynomial preprocessing of $O\left( {{n}^{2}\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  + {mn}}\right)$ .

定理3.3. 给定一个具有$n$个状态和$m$条转移的函数型vset自动机$A$，以及字符串$\mathbf{s}$，可以在$O\left( {{n}^{2}\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  + {mn}}\right)$的多项式预处理后，以$O\left( {{n}^{2}\left| \mathbf{s}\right| }\right)$的多项式延迟枚举$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$。

Note that Losemann [27] introduced an algorithm that evaluates vset-automata with logarithmic delay, but only if the number of variables is bounded. ${}^{2}$ We discuss our algorithm and other details of the proof in Section 4. In the remainder of this section, we explain the implications of this theorem for both evaluation approaches.

需注意Losemann[27]提出的算法能以对数延迟评估vset自动机，但仅当变量数量有界时成立。${}^{2}$我们将在第4节讨论算法细节及证明过程。本节剩余部分将阐述该定理对两种评估方法的影响。

3.3.2 Canonical Relational Approach. It was already shown by Fagin et al. [12] that every regex formula can be converted into an equivalent vset-automaton (where a vset-automaton $A$ and a regex formula $\alpha$ are said to be equivalent if $\llbracket A\rrbracket  = \llbracket \alpha \rrbracket )$ . It is probably not at all surprising that this is possible in a way that is efficient and results in functional vset-automata (recall that we assume regex formulas to be functional by convention).

3.3.2 规范关系方法。Fagin等人[12]已证明每个正则表达式公式都可转换为等效的vset自动机（当$\llbracket A\rrbracket  = \llbracket \alpha \rrbracket )$时，称vset自动机$A$与正则表达式公式$\alpha$等效）。这种转换不仅能高效实现，还能生成函数型vset自动机（根据约定，我们假定正则表达式公式本身具有功能性）。

LEMMA 3.4. Given a regex formula $\alpha$ ,one can construct in time $O\left( \left| \alpha \right| \right)$ a functional vset-automaton $A$ with $\llbracket A\rrbracket  = \llbracket \alpha \rrbracket$ .

引理3.4. 给定正则表达式公式$\alpha$，可在$O\left( \left| \alpha \right| \right)$时间内构造出具有$\llbracket A\rrbracket  = \llbracket \alpha \rrbracket$特性的函数型vset自动机$A$。

Proof. Let $\alpha$ be a regex formula,and define $V \mathrel{\text{:=}} \operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right)$ . Assume that $\alpha$ is represented as its syntax tree. We first rewrite $\alpha$ into a regular expression $\widehat{\alpha }$ over the alphabet $\sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}$ with $\mathcal{L}\left( \widehat{\alpha }\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$ . This is done by recursively replacing every node that represents a variable binding $x\{ \beta \}$ with the concatenation $x \vdash   \cdot  \beta  \cdot  \neg x$ . Then the length of $\widehat{\alpha }$ is linear in the length of $\alpha$ ; and the rewriting is possible in linear time as well.

证明。设$\alpha$为正则表达式公式，定义$V \mathrel{\text{:=}} \operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right)$。假定$\alpha$以语法树形式表示。我们首先将$\alpha$重写为字母表$\sum  \cup  {\Gamma }_{V}$上具有$\mathcal{L}\left( \widehat{\alpha }\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$特性的正则表达式$\widehat{\alpha }$，具体方法是将每个表示变量绑定的节点$x\{ \beta \}$递归替换为连接式$x \vdash   \cdot  \beta  \cdot  \neg x$。此时$\widehat{\alpha }$的长度与$\alpha$呈线性关系，且重写过程同样可在线性时间内完成。

Next,we convert $\widehat{\alpha }$ into an $\epsilon$ -NFA $A$ with $\mathcal{L}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{L}\left( \widehat{\alpha }\right)$ . Using the Thompson construction (cf. e.g., [22]), this is possible in linear time. Furthermore, both the number of states and the number of transitions of $A$ are linear in the length of $\widehat{\alpha }$ (and,hence,also in the length of $\alpha$ ). Finally,note that the construction ensures that $A$ has only one accepting state (recall that vset-automata are required to have a single accepting state).

接着将$\widehat{\alpha }$转换为具有$\mathcal{L}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{L}\left( \widehat{\alpha }\right)$特性的$\epsilon$-NFA$A$。采用Thompson构造法（参见[22]），该转换可在线性时间内完成。此外，$A$的状态数和转移数均与$\widehat{\alpha }$长度（因而也与$\alpha$长度）呈线性关系。最后需注意，该构造法确保$A$仅含一个接受状态（根据定义，vset自动机必须具有单一接受状态）。

This allows us to interpret $A$ as a vset-automaton with variable set $V$ ,using $\mathcal{R}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{L}\left( A\right)$ . As $\mathcal{L}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{L}\left( \widehat{\alpha }\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$ holds by definition,we know that $\mathcal{R}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$ . Furthermore,as $\alpha$ is functional, $\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)  = \operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$ holds,which implies $\operatorname{Ref}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( A\right)  = \operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$ . This allows us to make two conclusions: Firstly,that $\operatorname{Ref}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( A\right)$ , hence $A$ is functional. And,secondly,that $\llbracket A\rrbracket  = \llbracket \alpha \rrbracket$ ,as $\operatorname{Ref}\left( A\right)  =$ $\operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$ implies $\operatorname{Ref}\left( {A,\mathbf{s}}\right)  = \operatorname{Ref}\left( {\alpha ,\mathbf{s}}\right)$ for all $\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$ .

这使我们能够将$A$解释为具有变量集$V$的vset自动机，使用$\mathcal{R}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{L}\left( A\right)$。根据定义，$\mathcal{L}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{L}\left( \widehat{\alpha }\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$成立，因此我们知道$\mathcal{R}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)$。此外，由于$\alpha$是功能性的，$\mathcal{R}\left( \alpha \right)  = \operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$成立，这意味着$\operatorname{Ref}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( A\right)  = \operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$。由此可得出两个结论：首先，$\operatorname{Ref}\left( A\right)  = \mathcal{R}\left( A\right)$成立，因此$A$是功能性的；其次，由于$\operatorname{Ref}\left( A\right)  =$$\operatorname{Ref}\left( \alpha \right)$对所有$\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$都意味着$\operatorname{Ref}\left( {A,\mathbf{s}}\right)  = \operatorname{Ref}\left( {\alpha ,\mathbf{s}}\right)$，故$\llbracket A\rrbracket  = \llbracket \alpha \rrbracket$成立。

We note that the result of Lemma 3.4 was shown independently by Morciano et al. [28].

我们注意到，引理3.4的结果已由Morciano等人[28]独立证明。

We note here that the complexity of the preprocessing stated in Theorem 3.3 holds for a general functional vset-automaton. If, however,the automaton is derived from a regex formula $\alpha$ by the construction we use for proving Lemma 3.4, then the time of this preprocessing drops to $O\left( {{n}^{2}\left| \mathbf{s}\right| }\right)$ ,where $n$ is $O\left( \left| \alpha \right| \right)$ .

此处需说明，定理3.3所述的预处理复杂度适用于一般功能性vset自动机。但若该自动机是通过引理3.4证明所用构造从正则表达式公式$\alpha$导出，则预处理时间将降至$O\left( {{n}^{2}\left| \mathbf{s}\right| }\right)$，其中$n$为$O\left( \left| \alpha \right| \right)$。

As a consequence, we conclude that the canonical relational approach to evaluation is actually efficient for UCQs, under two conditions. The first condition is that the regex CQs map to a tractable class of relational CQs. More formally, by tractable class of relational CQs we refer to a class $\mathrm{Q}$ of CQs that can be evaluated in polynomial total time, ${}^{3}$ such as acyclic CQs,or more generally CQs with bounded hypertree width [18]. But, as shown in Theorem 3.2, this condition is not enough. The second condition is that there is a polynomial bound on the number of tuples of each regex formula. More formally,we say that a class $\mathbf{A}$ of regex formulas is polynomi-ally bounded if there exists a positive integer $d$ such that for every regex formula $\alpha  \in  \mathbf{A}$ and string $\mathbf{s}$ we have $\left| {\llbracket \alpha \rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right) }\right|$ is $O\left( {\left| \mathbf{s}\right| }^{d}\right)$ .

由此我们得出结论：在两种条件下，规范关系式评估方法对UCQs实际上是高效的。第一条件是正则CQs映射到可处理的关系CQs类——更形式化地说，所谓可处理的关系CQs类$\mathrm{Q}$指可在多项式总时间内评估的CQs类${}^{3}$，如无环CQs或更广义的具有有界超树宽度的CQs[18]。但如定理3.2所示，仅此条件并不足够。第二条件是每个正则表达式公式的元组数量存在多项式界限——更准确地说，若存在正整数$d$使得对每个正则公式$\alpha  \in  \mathbf{A}$和字符串$\mathbf{s}$都有$\left| {\llbracket \alpha \rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right) }\right|$为$O\left( {\left| \mathbf{s}\right| }^{d}\right)$，则称正则公式类$\mathbf{A}$是多项式有界的。

Clearly, if every regex formula in A can be evaluated in polynomial time,then $\mathbf{A}$ is polynomially bounded. From Theorem 3.3 we conclude that the other direction also holds. Hence, from Theorem 3.3 and Lemma 3.4 we establish the following theorem.

显然，若类A中每个正则公式都可在多项式时间内评估，则$\mathbf{A}$是多项式有界的。由定理3.3可知其逆命题亦成立。因此，综合定理3.3与引理3.4可确立以下定理。

THEOREM 3.5. Let $\mathbf{P}$ be a class of regex UCQs. If the regex formulas in the UCQs of $\mathrm{P}$ belong to a polynomially bounded class,and $\mathrm{P}$ maps to a tractable class of relational UCQs, then UCQs in P can be evaluated in polynomial total time.

定理3.5. 设$\mathbf{P}$为正则UCQs类。若该UCQs中的正则公式属于多项式有界类，且$\mathrm{P}$映射到可处理的关系UCQs类，则P中的UCQs可在多项式总时间内完成评估。

Examples of polynomially bounded classes of regex formulas are the following.

以下是多项式有界正则公式类的示例：

- The class of regex formulas with at most $k$ variables,for some fixed $k$ .

- 变量数不超过固定值$k$的正则公式类。

- The class of regex formulas $\alpha$ with a key attribute,that is,a variable $x \in  \operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right)$ with the property that for all strings $\mathbf{s}$ and tuples $\mu$ and ${\mu }^{\prime }$ in $\llbracket \alpha \rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ ,if $\mu \left( x\right)  = {\mu }^{\prime }\left( x\right)$ then $\mu  = {\mu }^{\prime }$ .

- 具有关键属性的正则表达式类$\alpha$，即存在一个变量$x \in  \operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right)$，其特性为：对于所有字符串$\mathbf{s}$及元组$\mu$与${\mu }^{\prime }$，若$\llbracket \alpha \rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$中$\mu \left( x\right)  = {\mu }^{\prime }\left( x\right)$成立，则$\mu  = {\mu }^{\prime }$。

A key attribute implies a polynomial bound as the number of spans of a string $s$ is quadratic in $\left| s\right|$ . Interestingly,its existence can be tested in polynomial time.

关键属性意味着多项式约束，因为字符串$s$的跨度数量在$\left| s\right|$上呈二次方增长。值得注意的是，其存在性可在多项式时间内验证。

Proposition 3.6. Given a functional vset-automaton $A$ with $n$ states,and a variable $x \in  \operatorname{Vars}\left( A\right)$ ,it can be decided in time $O\left( {n}^{4}\right)$ whether $x$ is a key attribute.

命题3.6. 给定具有$n$个状态的功能性vset自动机$A$和变量$x \in  \operatorname{Vars}\left( A\right)$，可在$O\left( {n}^{4}\right)$时间内判定$x$是否为关键属性。

3.3.3 Compilation to Automata. We now discuss the second evaluation approach: compiling the regex UCQ to a functional vset-automaton, and then applying Theorem 3.3. An immediate consequence of a combination with past results is as follows. There are computable (but potentially exponential) conversions of spanners in a regular representation into a vset-automaton (Fagin et al. [12]), and of a vset-automaton into a functional vset-automaton (Freydenberger [15]). Hence, we get the following.

3.3.3 自动机编译。现讨论第二种求值方法：将正则表达式UCQ编译为功能性vset自动机后应用定理3.3。结合既有研究可得出：存在可计算（但可能指数级）的转换方法，可将正则表示下的扩展器转换为vset自动机（Fagin等人[12]），并将vset自动机转换为功能性vset自动机（Freydenberger[15]）。因此我们得到：

Corollary 3.7. Spanners $q$ in a regular representation (e.g.,regex UCQs) can be evaluated with FPT delay for the parameter $\left| q\right|$ .

推论3.7. 对于参数$\left| q\right|$，正则表示（如正则表达式UCQ）中的扩展器$q$可用FPT延迟求值。

The corollary should be contrasted with the traditional relational case, where Boolean CQ evaluation is W[1]-hard when the size of the query is the parameter [30]. Hence, in that respect regex UCQ evaluation is substantially more tractable than UCQ evaluation in the relational model.

该推论应与传统关系型案例对比——在查询规模作为参数时，布尔CQ求值是W[1]-难问题[30]。因此在这方面，正则表达式UCQ求值比关系模型中的UCQ求值更易处理。

Note that joining $k$ automata leads to a time of $O\left( {v{n}^{2k}}\right)$ ,which is only polynomial if $k$ is bounded. Due to Theorem 3.2, this is unavoidable under standard complexity theoretic assumptions.

本证明与文献[28]的证明均基于自动机交集的标准构造方法；关键区别在于我们的证明利用了变量配置的优势。需注意，连接$k$个自动机将导致$O\left( {v{n}^{2k}}\right)$的时间复杂度，仅当$k$有界时才呈现多项式特性。根据定理3.2，在标准计算复杂性理论假设下这是不可避免的。

This motivates the following definition. Let $k \geq  0$ be fixed. A regex $k - {CQ}$ is a regex $\mathrm{{CQ}}$ with at most $k$ atoms. A regex $k - {UCQ}$ is a UCQ where each CQ is a $k$ -CQ (i. e.,a disjunction of $k$ -CQs). From Lemmas 3.4 and 3.10 we conclude that we can convert, in polynomial time,a join of $k$ regex formulas into a single functional vset-automaton. Then, Lemma 3.8 implies that projection can also be efficiently pushed into the functional vset-automaton. Hence, in polynomial time we can translate a $k$ -CQ into a functional vset-automaton. Then, with Lemma 3.9 we conclude that this translation extends to $k$ -UCQs. Finally,by applying Theorem 3.3 we arrive at the following main result.

由此引出以下定义。设$k \geq  0$为固定值，正则表达式$k - {CQ}$是指最多包含$k$个原子的正则表达式$\mathrm{{CQ}}$。正则表达式$k - {UCQ}$是一个UCQ（联合查询），其中每个CQ都是$k$-CQ（即$k$-CQ的析取）。根据引理3.4和3.10，我们可以在多项式时间内将$k$个正则表达式公式的连接转换为单个功能型vset自动机。进而引理3.8表明投影操作也能高效地融入该自动机。因此，我们可在多项式时间内将$k$-CQ转换为功能型vset自动机，再通过引理3.9将此转换扩展到$k$-UCQ。最终应用定理3.3得到以下主要结论。

THEOREM 3.11. For every fixed $k$ ,regex $k$ -UCQs can be evaluated with polynomial delay.

定理3.11. 对于任意固定的$k$，正则表达式$k$-UCQ可在多项式延迟下求值。

Hence, where Theorem 3.11 applies, it is more powerful than Theorem 3.5: The former holds for all regex $k$ -UCQs,while the latter has additional requirements,even when limited to $k$ -UCQs.

因此，在定理3.11适用的情况下，其效力强于定理3.5：前者适用于所有正则表达式$k$-UCQ，而后者即使限于$k$-UCQ时仍有附加条件。

In the next section we describe the algorithm of Theorem 3.3. Then, in Section 5 we extend the main theorems of this section, Theorems 3.5 and 3.11, to incorporate string equalities.

下一节我们将阐述定理3.3的算法实现。随后在第5节中，将本节的主要定理（定理3.5和3.11）扩展至包含字符串等价性的情形。

## 4 EVALUATING VSET-AUTOMATA

## 4 VSET自动机求值

In this section, we give a high-level description of the proof of Theorem 3.3 and the resulting algorithm that, given a functional vset-automaton $A$ and a string $\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$ ,enumerates $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ with polynomial delay. Before we discuss the algorithm in Section 4.2, we first introduce the central notion of variable configurations.

本节将概要阐述定理3.3的证明过程及衍生算法，该算法在给定功能型vset自动机$A$和字符串$\mathbf{s} \in  {\sum }^{ * }$时，能以多项式延迟枚举$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$。在4.2节讨论具体算法前，我们首先引入变量配置的核心概念。

### 4.1 Sequences of Variable Configurations

### 4.1 变量配置序列

Before we present the actual algorithm, we first examine a property of functional vset-automata. Building on this, we introduce a novel way of working with vset-automata, which is the main part of our algorithm.

在正式呈现算法之前，我们先考察功能型vset自动机的一个特性。基于此，我们提出了一种处理vset自动机的新方法，这是本算法的核心部分。

In order to introduce the concept, we consider an arbitrary functional vset-automaton $A = \left( {V,Q,{q}_{0},{q}_{f},\delta }\right)$ ,and ensure that all states are reachable from ${q}_{0}$ ,and that ${q}_{f}$ is reachable from every state.

为阐明这一概念，我们考虑任意功能型vset自动机$A = \left( {V,Q,{q}_{0},{q}_{f},\delta }\right)$，确保所有状态均可从${q}_{0}$到达，且每个状态都能到达${q}_{f}$。

Then,for each state $q \in  Q$ and all $x \in  V$ ,each ref-word $\mathbf{r} \in$ ${\left( {\Gamma }_{V} \cup  \sum \right) }^{ * }$ that takes $A$ from ${q}_{0}$ to $q$ satisfies exactly one of these mutually exclusive conditions:

对于每个状态$q \in  Q$及所有$x \in  V$，能将$A$从${q}_{0}$转移到$q$的每个引用词$\mathbf{r} \in$${\left( {\Gamma }_{V} \cup  \sum \right) }^{ * }$都恰好满足以下互斥条件之一：

(1) ${\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{x \vdash  } = {\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{\exists x} = 0$ ,

(2) ${\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{x \vdash  } = 1$ and ${\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{\exists x} = 0$ ,or

(2) ${\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{x \vdash  } = 1$与${\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{\exists x} = 0$，或

(3) ${\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{x \vdash  } = {\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{\exists x} = 1$ ,and $x \vdash$ occurs before $\dashv  x$ .

(3) ${\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{x \vdash  } = {\left| \mathbf{r}\right| }_{\exists x} = 1$，且$x \vdash$先于$\dashv  x$出现

If neither of these conditions is met, $\mathbf{r}$ contains one of $x \vdash$ or $\neg x$ more than once, or the two symbols appear in the wrong order. Then we can choose any ref-word ${\mathbf{r}}^{\prime } \in  {\left( {\Gamma }_{V} \cup  \sum \right) }^{ * }$ that takes $A$ from $q$ to ${q}_{f}$ , and obtain a contradiction by observing that $\mathbf{r} \cdot  {\mathbf{r}}^{\prime } \in  \mathcal{R}\left( A\right)$ ,although $\mathbf{r} \cdot  {\mathbf{r}}^{\prime }$ is not valid. Furthermore,if we compare two ref-words ${\mathbf{r}}_{1}$ and ${\mathbf{r}}_{2}$ that both take $A$ from ${q}_{0}$ to a common state $q$ ,we know that both must satisfy the same of these three conditions (otherwise, ${\mathbf{r}}_{1} \cdot  {\mathbf{r}}^{\prime }$ or ${\mathbf{r}}_{2} \cdot  {\mathbf{r}}^{\prime }$ is not valid).

若均不满足上述条件，则$\mathbf{r}$中$x \vdash$或$\neg x$重复出现，或两符号顺序错误。此时可任选能将$A$从$q$转移到${q}_{f}$的引用词${\mathbf{r}}^{\prime } \in  {\left( {\Gamma }_{V} \cup  \sum \right) }^{ * }$，通过观察到$\mathbf{r} \cdot  {\mathbf{r}}^{\prime } \in  \mathcal{R}\left( A\right)$（尽管$\mathbf{r} \cdot  {\mathbf{r}}^{\prime }$无效）得出矛盾。此外，若比较两个都能将$A$从${q}_{0}$转移到共同状态$q$的引用词${\mathbf{r}}_{1}$和${\mathbf{r}}_{2}$，可知二者必满足这三个条件中的同一条件（否则${\mathbf{r}}_{1} \cdot  {\mathbf{r}}^{\prime }$或${\mathbf{r}}_{2} \cdot  {\mathbf{r}}^{\prime }$无效）。

In other words,in each run of $A$ ,the information which variables have been opened or closed is stored implicitly in the states. To formalize this notion,we define the set $\mathcal{V}$ of variable states as $\mathcal{V} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ \mathrm{w},\mathrm{o},\mathrm{c}\}$ (the symbols stand for waiting,open,and closed). A variable configuration (for $V$ ) is a function $\overrightarrow{c} : V \rightarrow  \mathcal{V}$ . For each state $q \in  Q$ ,we define its variable configuration ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q} : V \rightarrow  \mathcal{V}$ as follows: Choose any ref-word $\mathbf{r}$ that takes $A$ from ${q}_{0}$ to $q$ . We define ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}\left( x\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathrm{c}$ if $\mathrm{r}$ contains $\neg x,{\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}\left( x\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathrm{o}$ if $\mathrm{r}$ contains $x \vdash$ but not $\neg x$ , and ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}\left( x\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathrm{w}$ if $\mathbf{r}$ contains neither of the two symbols (as explained above, ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}$ is well-defined for each $q \in  Q$ ).

换言之，在$A$的每次运行中，变量开启或关闭的信息都隐式存储在状态中。为形式化这一概念，我们将变量状态集$\mathcal{V}$定义为$\mathcal{V} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ \mathrm{w},\mathrm{o},\mathrm{c}\}$（符号分别表示等待、开启和关闭）。变量配置（针对$V$）是函数$\overrightarrow{c} : V \rightarrow  \mathcal{V}$。对每个状态$q \in  Q$，其变量配置${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q} : V \rightarrow  \mathcal{V}$定义如下：任选能将$A$从${q}_{0}$转移到$q$的引用词$\mathbf{r}$。若$\mathrm{r}$包含$\neg x,{\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}\left( x\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathrm{o}$则定义${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}\left( x\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathrm{c}$；若$\mathrm{r}$含$x \vdash$但不含$\neg x$则定义其他值；若$\mathbf{r}$不含这两个符号则定义${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}\left( x\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathrm{w}$（如前所述，${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}$对每个$q \in  Q$都有明确定义）。

We say that a sequence of variable configurations for $V$ is valid if it respects the order of variables states; that is, ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{i}\left( x\right)  = \mathrm{c}$ implies ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{i + 1}\left( x\right)  = \mathrm{c}$ ,and ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{i}\left( x\right)  = \mathrm{o}$ implies ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{i + 1}\left( x\right)  \in  \{ \mathrm{o},\mathrm{c}\}$ for all $x \in  V$ . Like ref-words, sequences of variable configurations can be understood as an abstraction of spanner behavior. In fact, both can be seen as successive steps of generalization: Ref-words hide the actual behavior of primitive spanner representations (i. e., the actual sequence of states in the vset-automaton, or which parts of the regex are mapped to which part of the input); they only express in which order variables are opened and closed. Sequences of variable configurations take this one step further, and compress successive variable operations (without terminals in-between) into a single step. Hence,treating $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ as a language over the alphabet ${\mathcal{V}}^{\lceil V\rceil }$ is exactly the level of granularity that is needed to distinguish different tuples.

如同引用词，变量配置序列可视为跨度器行为的抽象表征。实际上，二者都可视为泛化的连续步骤：引用词隐藏了原始跨度器表示的实际行为（即vset自动机中的真实状态序列，或正则表达式各部分与输入内容的映射关系）；它们仅表达变量开启与关闭的顺序。变量配置序列更进一步，将连续的变量操作（中间无终结符）压缩为单一步骤。因此，将$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$视为字母表${\mathcal{V}}^{\lceil V\rceil }$上的语言，正是区分不同元组所需的粒度层级。

### 4.2 The Algorithm

### 4.2 算法

The algorithm enumerates the(V,s)-tuples of $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ ( $\mathbf{s}$ ) by enumerating the corresponding sequences of $\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  + 1$ variable configurations for $V$ . In order to do so,we interpret each variable configuration as a letter of the alphabet $\mathcal{K} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  {{\overrightarrow{c}}_{q} \mid  q \in  Q}\right\}$ (note that while there are ${3}^{\left| V\right| }$ possible letters that might occur in $\mathcal{K}$ ,its actual size is always bounded by $\left| Q\right|$ ). More specifically,the algorithm has the following two steps:

该算法通过枚举$V$对应的$\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  + 1$变量配置序列，来列举$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$($\mathbf{s}$)的(V,s)元组。具体实现时，我们将每个变量配置视为字母表$\mathcal{K} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  {{\overrightarrow{c}}_{q} \mid  q \in  Q}\right\}$中的一个字符（注意：虽然$\mathcal{K}$中可能出现${3}^{\left| V\right| }$种字符，但其实际规模始终受$\left| Q\right|$限制）。更精确地说，算法包含以下两个步骤：

(1) Given $A$ and $\mathbf{s}$ ,construct an NFA ${A}_{G}$ over the alphabet $\mathcal{K}$ such that $\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$ contains exactly those strings ${\kappa }_{1}\cdots {\kappa }_{\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  + 1}$ , ${\kappa }_{i} \in  \mathcal{K}$ ,that correspond to the elements of $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ .

(1) 给定$A$和$\mathbf{s}$，构建字母表$\mathcal{K}$上的NFA（非确定性有限自动机）${A}_{G}$，使得$\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$精确包含那些对应$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$元素的字符串${\kappa }_{1}\cdots {\kappa }_{\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  + 1}$,${\kappa }_{i} \in  \mathcal{K}$

(2) Enumerate $\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$ with polynomial delay.

(2) 以多项式延迟枚举$\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$

The algorithm constructs the NFA ${A}_{G}$ by first constructing a graph $G$ whose nodes are tuples(i,q),which encode that $A$ can be in state $q$ after reading ${\sigma }_{1}\cdots {\sigma }_{i}$ . The edges are drawn accordingly: There is an edge from(i,p)to $\left( {i + 1,q}\right)$ if $A$ can reach $q$ from $p$ by reading ${\sigma }_{i + 1}$ and then arbitrarily many variable operations. The NFA ${A}_{G}$ is then directly obtained from $G$ by interpreting every edge from(i,p)to $\left( {i + 1,q}\right)$ as a transition for the letter ${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}$ .

算法首先构建图$G$来构造NFA${A}_{G}$，其节点是元组(i,q)，表示读取${\sigma }_{1}\cdots {\sigma }_{i}$后$A$可能处于状态$q$。边的绘制规则为：若$A$通过读取${\sigma }_{i + 1}$及任意数量变量操作能从$p$到达$q$，则存在从(i,p)到$\left( {i + 1,q}\right)$的边。最终通过将(i,p)到$\left( {i + 1,q}\right)$的边解释为字母${\overrightarrow{c}}_{q}$的转移，直接从$G$得到NFA${A}_{G}$

Finally,to enumerate $\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$ without repetitions,we tailor an optimized version of the algorithm by Ackerman and Shallit [2] that,given $l \geq  0$ and an NFA $M$ over some alphabet $T$ ,enumerates $\mathcal{L}\left( M\right)  \cap  {T}^{l}$ .

最后，为实现无重复枚举$\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$，我们优化了Ackerman和Shallit[2]的算法版本，该算法在给定$l \geq  0$和字母表$T$上的NFA$M$时，能枚举$\mathcal{L}\left( M\right)  \cap  {T}^{l}$

In summary,the graph $G$ represents all runs of $A$ on $\mathbf{s}$ . By interpreting(V,s)-tuples as strings from ${\mathcal{K}}^{\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  + 1}$ ,we can treat $G$ as an NFA ${A}_{G}$ ,and enumerate $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{s}\right) \left( \mathrm{s}\right)$ by enumerating $\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$ . As there is a one-to-one correspondence between these two sets, the fact that $\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$ is enumerated without repetitions guarantees that no element of $\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ is repeated.

综上所述，图$G$表示$A$在$\mathbf{s}$上的所有运行路径。通过将(V,s)元组解释为${\mathcal{K}}^{\left| \mathbf{s}\right|  + 1}$的字符串，可将$G$视为NFA${A}_{G}$，并通过枚举$\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$来枚举$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathrm{s}\right) \left( \mathrm{s}\right)$。由于这两个集合存在一一对应关系，$\mathcal{L}\left( {A}_{G}\right)$的无重复枚举保证了$\llbracket A\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$元素不会重复出现

## 5 STRING EQUALITY

## 5 字符串等价性

Our results in the previous two sections only apply to regex UCQs. As these are not allowed to use string equality selections, these have the same expressive power as regular spanner representations. In this section, we discuss how our results can be extended to include string equality selections, which allows us reach the full expressive power of core spanners.

前两节的研究结果仅适用于正则表达式联合查询（regex UCQs）。由于这类查询不允许使用字符串等价选择，其表达能力与常规的抽取器表示相当。本节将探讨如何扩展研究成果以纳入字符串等价选择，从而实现核心抽取器的完整表达能力。

### 5.1 Lower Bound

### 5.1 下界

The main difficulty when dealing with string equality selections is that this operator quickly becomes computationally expensive, even without using joins. More specifically, Freydenberger and Holldack [16] showed that combined with string equalities, a single regex formula and a projection to a Boolean spanner already lead to an intractable evaluation problem.

处理字符串等价选择的主要难点在于，该运算符会迅速导致计算复杂度激增，即便不涉及连接操作。具体而言，Freydenberger和Holldack[16]的研究表明：当结合字符串等价条件时，仅需单个正则表达式公式和布尔抽取器的投影操作，就会导致计算问题变得棘手。

THEOREM 5.1. [16] Evaluation of Boolean regex CQs with string equalities is NP-complete, even if restricted to queries of the form ${\pi }_{0}{\zeta }_{{x}_{1},{y}_{1}}^{ = }\cdots {\zeta }_{{x}_{m},{y}_{m}}^{ = }\alpha .$

定理5.1. [16] 带字符串等价条件的布尔正则表达式联合查询的求值问题是NP完全的，即使限制为${\pi }_{0}{\zeta }_{{x}_{1},{y}_{1}}^{ = }\cdots {\zeta }_{{x}_{m},{y}_{m}}^{ = }\alpha .$形式的查询也不例外

In other words, even a single regex formula already leads to NP-hardness. The proof from [16] uses a reduction from the membership problem for so-called pattern languages. It was shown by Fernau et al. [14] that this membership problem is W[1]-complete for various parameters. We prove that the situation is analogou: for Boolean regex CQs with string equalities.

换言之，单个正则表达式公式就足以导致NP难问题。[16]的证明采用了从所谓模式语言的成员资格问题归约的方法。Fernau等人[14]已证明该成员资格问题对多种参数都是W[1]-完全的。我们证明这种情况具有类比性：对于带字符串等价条件的布尔正则表达式联合查询。

THEOREM 5.2. Evaluation of Boolean regex ${CQ}$ s $q$ with string equalities is W[1]-hard for the parameter $\left| q\right|$ ,even if restricted to queries of the form ${\pi }_{\varnothing }{\zeta }_{{x}_{1},{y}_{1}}^{ = }\cdots {\zeta }_{{x}_{m},{y}_{m}}^{ = }\alpha$ .

定理5.2. 带字符串等价条件的布尔正则表达式${CQ}$s$q$的求值问题对参数$\left| q\right|$是W[1]-难的，即使限制为${\pi }_{\varnothing }{\zeta }_{{x}_{1},{y}_{1}}^{ = }\cdots {\zeta }_{{x}_{m},{y}_{m}}^{ = }\alpha$形式的查询亦然。

Proof. We prove the claim by modifying the proof of Theorem 3.2: Let $\mathbf{s}$ and $\gamma$ be defined as in that proof. We then define our query $q$ as

$$
q \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\pi }_{\varnothing }{S}_{1}\cdots {S}_{k}\gamma 
$$

Note that being able to use string equality predicates, we do not need to iterate through all of the possible $\mathbf{v}$ as in the ${\delta }_{l}$ . Therefore the proof of correctness of the reduction used in the proof of Theorem 3.2 can be simply adapted to show correctness of this reduction. Observe that this is an FPT reduction with parameter $\left| q\right|$ since $\left| q\right|$ is of size $O\left( {k}^{2}\right)$ (i.e.,the number of string equality predicates we use depends only on the clique size). Finally, we remark that like the proof of Theorem 3.2, this proof can be adapted to a binary alphabet by using the standard coding techniques.

注意，由于可以使用字符串等价谓词，我们无需像${\delta }_{l}$中那样遍历所有可能的$\mathbf{v}$。因此定理3.2证明中使用的归约正确性论证经简单调整即可适用于本证明。可以观察到这是关于参数$\left| q\right|$的固定参数可归约（FPT reduction），因为$\left| q\right|$的规模为$O\left( {k}^{2}\right)$（即所用字符串等价谓词的数量仅取决于团的大小）。最后需要说明，与定理3.2的证明类似，本证明通过标准编码技术可适配到二元字母表。

Hence,while a regex $\mathrm{{CQ}}$ that consists of a single regex formula can be evaluated efficiently (due to Lemmas 3.4 and 3.8 and Theorem 3.3), even limited use of string equalities can become expensive. This result can be obtained by combining the reduction from [14] with the reduction from [16], the former proof requires encodings that are needlessly complicated for our purposes (this is caused by the comparatively low expressive power of pattern languages). Instead,we take the basic idea of an FPT-reduction from the $k$ -clique problem, and give a direct proof that is less technical. Note that the proof of Theorem 5.2 is different than that of Theorem 3.2 since in the former the query $q$ constructed in the reduction is determined solely by the parameter $k$ ,and not the size of the graph as in the latter.

因此，虽然由单一正则表达式$\mathrm{{CQ}}$组成的正则匹配可以高效执行（基于引理3.4、引理3.8和定理3.3），但即使有限使用字符串等价性也可能导致高昂代价。该结论可通过结合文献[14]与[16]的归约推导得出——前者的证明需要为我们的研究目的而言过于复杂的编码（这是由于模式语言表达能力相对较低所致）。我们转而采用$k$-团问题的FPT归约基本思想，给出一个技术性较弱的直接证明。注意定理5.2的证明与定理3.2不同，因为前者在归约中构建的查询$q$仅由参数$k$决定，而不像后者那样依赖于图的大小。

The reader should contrast Theorem 5.2 with Corollary 3.7. The combination of the two complexity results shows that, with respect to the parameter $\left| q\right|$ ,string equality considerably increases the parameterized complexity: Boolean regex CQ evaluation used to be FPT, and is now W[1]-hard.

读者应将定理5.2与推论3.7进行对比。这两个复杂度结果的结合表明，就参数$\left| q\right|$而言，字符串等价性显著增加了参数化复杂度：布尔正则联合查询评估原本属于FPT类，而现在属于W[1]-难问题。

Finally, note that queries as in Theorems 5.1 and 5.2 are always acyclic,as the variables of each equality atom also occur in $\alpha$ . Although they are not gamma acyclic, we can rewrite each query into an equivalent gamma-acyclic query with $k$ -ary string equalities,by merging each pair ${\zeta }_{X}^{ = }$ and ${\zeta }_{Y}^{ = }$ with sets $X \cap  Y \neq  \varnothing$ into ${\zeta }_{X \cup  Y}^{ = }$ until all equalities range over pairwise disjoint sets.

最后需注意，定理5.1和5.2中的查询总是无环的，因为每个等价原子的变量也出现在$\alpha$中。虽然它们不是γ-无环的，但我们可以通过将每对${\zeta }_{X}^{ = }$和${\zeta }_{Y}^{ = }$与集合$X \cap  Y \neq  \varnothing$合并为${\zeta }_{X \cup  Y}^{ = }$，将所有等价关系转换为作用于互不相交集上的$k$元字符串等价，从而将每个查询重写为等价的γ-无环查询。

### 5.2 Upper Bounds

### 5.2 上界

We now examine the upper bounds for our two evaluation strategies on regex UCQs with string equalities. For the canonical relational approach, we observe that each equality atom corresponds to a relation that is of polynomial size. Hence, we can directly use Theorem 3.5 to conclude the following.

现在我们考察带字符串等价性的正则联合查询两种评估策略的上界。对于规范关系方法，我们注意到每个等价原子对应一个多项式大小的关系。因此可以直接应用定理3.5得出以下结论。

COROLLARY 5.3. Let $\mathbf{P}$ be a class of regex UCQs with string equalities. If the regex formulas in the UCQs of $\mathrm{P}$ belong to a polynomially bounded class,and $\mathrm{P}$ maps to a tractable class of relational UCQs, then UCQs in $\mathrm{P}$ can be evaluated in polynomial total time.

推论5.3. 设$\mathbf{P}$为带字符串等价性的正则联合查询类。若$\mathrm{P}$中联合查询的正则公式属于多项式有界类，且$\mathrm{P}$映射到易处理的关系联合查询类，则$\mathrm{P}$中的联合查询可在多项式总时间内完成评估。

The upper bound for compilation to automata requires more effort. We first observe the following.

编译到自动机的上界证明需要更多工作。我们首先观察到：

THEOREM 5.4. For every fixed $m \geq  1$ there is an algorithm,that given a functional vset-automaton $A$ ,a sequence $S \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\zeta }_{{x}_{1},{y}_{1}}^{ = }\cdots {\zeta }_{{x}_{m},{y}_{m}}^{ = }$ of string equality selections over $\operatorname{Vars}\left( A\right)$ ,and a string $\mathrm{s}$ ,enumerates $\llbracket {SA}\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ with polynomial delay.

定理5.4. 对于每个固定参数$m \geq  1$，存在一个算法能在给定功能性vset自动机$A$、字符串$\mathrm{s}$及其上字符串等价选择序列$S \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\zeta }_{{x}_{1},{y}_{1}}^{ = }\cdots {\zeta }_{{x}_{m},{y}_{m}}^{ = }$时，以多项式延迟枚举$\llbracket {SA}\rrbracket \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$。

The main idea of the proof is to construct a vset-automaton ${A}_{\mathrm{{eq}}}$ that defines exactly the string equalities on $\mathbf{s}$ . Specifically, $\mu  \in$ $\left\lbrack  {A}_{\mathrm{{eq}}}\right\rbrack  \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ holds if and only if ${\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {x}_{i}\right) } = {\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {y}_{i}\right) }$ for all ${x}_{i},{y}_{i}$ where ${\zeta }_{{x}_{i},{y}_{i}}^{ = }$ is a selection in $S$ . We then use Lemma 3.10 to construct a vset-automaton ${A}_{\text{join }}$ with $\llbracket {A}_{\text{join }}\rrbracket  = \llbracket A \boxtimes  {A}_{\text{eq }}\rrbracket$ ,and use Theorem 3.3 to enumerate $\left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {A}_{\text{join }}\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack  \left( \mathbf{s}\right)  = \left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {SA}\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack  \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ . Note that the construction of ${A}_{\text{eq }}$ depends on $\mathbf{s}$ ; in fact, $\llbracket {A}_{\text{eq }}\rrbracket \left( {\mathbf{s}}^{\prime }\right)  = \varnothing$ for all strings ${\mathbf{s}}^{\prime } \neq  \mathbf{s}$ . This dependency on $s$ is unavoidable: Recall that as shown by Fagin et al. [12], regular spanners are strictly less expressive than core spanners (i. e., string equality adds expressive power). If we could construct an ${A}_{\text{eq }}$ that worked on every string ${\mathbf{s}}^{\prime }$ ,this would immediately lead to a contradiction.

该证明的核心思想是构建一个vset自动机${A}_{\mathrm{{eq}}}$，精确定义$\mathbf{s}$上的字符串等价关系。具体而言，当且仅当对于$S$中的所有选择${\zeta }_{{x}_{i},{y}_{i}}^{ = }$满足${\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {x}_{i}\right) } = {\mathbf{s}}_{\mu \left( {y}_{i}\right) }$时，$\mu  \in$$\left\lbrack  {A}_{\mathrm{{eq}}}\right\rbrack  \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$成立。随后我们运用引理3.10构建具有$\llbracket {A}_{\text{join }}\rrbracket  = \llbracket A \boxtimes  {A}_{\text{eq }}\rrbracket$的vset自动机${A}_{\text{join }}$，并利用定理3.3枚举$\left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {A}_{\text{join }}\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack  \left( \mathbf{s}\right)  = \left\lbrack  \left\lbrack  {SA}\right\rbrack  \right\rbrack  \left( \mathbf{s}\right)$。需注意${A}_{\text{eq }}$的构造依赖于$\mathbf{s}$；事实上，对于所有字符串${\mathbf{s}}^{\prime } \neq  \mathbf{s}$都有$\llbracket {A}_{\text{eq }}\rrbracket \left( {\mathbf{s}}^{\prime }\right)  = \varnothing$。这种对$s$的依赖性不可避免：如Fagin等人[12]所示，正则抽取器的表达能力严格弱于核心抽取器（即字符串等价增加了表达能力）。若能构造出适用于任意字符串${\mathbf{s}}^{\prime }$的${A}_{\text{eq }}$，将直接导致矛盾。

Analogously to the join in Section 3.3.3, the polynomial upper bound only holds for the construction if $m$ is fixed,and Theorem 5.2 suggests that this cannot be overcome under standard assumptions. Hence,for all fixed $k,m \geq  0$ ,we define the notion of a regex $k - {UCQ}$ with up to $m$ string equalities analogously to a regex $k$ -UCQ,with the additional requirement that each of the CQs uses at most $m$ binary string equality selections. It follows from Theorem 5.4 that for every constant $k$ ,adding a fixed number of string equalities to a regex $k$ -UCQ does not affect its enumeration complexity (in the sense that the complexity stays polynomial).

与第3.3.3节的连接操作类似，多项式上界仅适用于$m$固定的构造场景，定理5.2表明这在标准假设下无法突破。因此，对于所有固定的$k,m \geq  0$，我们类比regex$k$-UCQ定义了最多含$m$个字符串等价关系的regex$k - {UCQ}$概念，额外要求每个CQ至多使用$m$个二元字符串等价选择。根据定理5.4，对于任意常量$k$，向regex$k$-UCQ添加固定数量的字符串等价关系不会影响其枚举复杂度（即保持多项式复杂度）。

COROLLARY 5.5. For all fixed $m$ and $k$ ,regex $k$ -UCQs with up to $m$ string equalities can be evaluated with polynomial delay.

推论5.5 对于所有固定的$m$和$k$，含最多$m$个字符串等价关系的regex$k$-UCQ可通过多项式延迟求值。

In other words, Theorem 3.11 can be extended to cover string equalities.

换言之，定理3.11可扩展至涵盖字符串等价关系的情形。

## 6 CONCLUDING REMARKS

## 6 结论与展望

We have studied the combined complexity of evaluating regex CQs and regex UCQs. We showed that the complexity is not determined only by the structure of the CQ as a relational query; rather, complexity can go higher since an atomic regex formula can already define a relation that is exponentially larger than the combined size of the input and output. Our upper bounds are based on an algorithm for evaluating a vset-automaton with polynomial delay. These bounds are based on two altenative evaluation strategies-the canonical relational evaluation and query-to-automaton compilation. We conclude the paper by proposing several directions for future research.

我们研究了正则表达式CQ和UCQ的联合求值复杂度，证明其复杂度不仅取决于作为关系查询的CQ结构——由于原子正则公式可定义比输入输出总规模指数级更大的关系，复杂度可能更高。我们的上界基于多项式延迟vset自动机求值算法，采用两种替代评估策略：规范关系求值与查询到自动机的编译。最后提出若干未来研究方向。

One direction for future research is to generalize the ability to compile into a vset-automaton to a class of queries that is richer than regex $k$ -UCQs with string equality. In particular,we would like to have a robust definition of a class of algebraic expressions that we can efficiently translate into a vset-automaton.

未来研究方向之一是将vset自动机编译能力推广至比含字符串等价的regex$k$-UCQ更丰富的查询类别，特别是需要建立可高效转换为vset自动机的代数表达式类的鲁棒定义。

Fagin et al. [12] showed that regular spanners, or equivalently regex UCQs (as we established in Theorem 2.8), can be phrased as Unions of Conjunctive Regular Path Queries (UCRPQs) [4, 6, 8, 9]. However, their translation entails an exponential blowup. Moreover, even if the translation could be made efficiently, it is not at all clear that tractability properties of the regex UCQ (e.g., bounded number of atoms, or low hypertree width) would translate into tractable properties of UCRPQs. Importantly, in the UCRPQ every atom involves two variables, and so, the problem of intractable materialization of an atom (i.e., the main challenge we faced here) does not occur. So, another future direction is a deeper exploration of the relationship between the complexity results of the two frameworks.

Fagin等人[12]研究表明，正则选择器（或称等效的正则表达式联合查询，如定理2.8所述）可表述为联合正则路径查询(UCRPQs)[4,6,8,9]。然而，其转换过程会导致指数级膨胀。更重要的是，即便能实现高效转换，正则表达式联合查询的可处理性特征（如有限原子数或低超树宽度）是否对应UCRPQs的可处理性仍不明确。值得注意的是，UCRPQs中每个原子仅涉及两个变量，因此不存在原子难解物化问题（即本文主要挑战）。未来另一研究方向是深入探索两种框架复杂度结果间的关联。

Last but not least, there is the crucial future direction of translating the upper bounds we presented into algorithms that substantially outperform the state of the art, at least when our tractability conditions hold. Beyond optimizing our translation and polynomial-delay algorithm, we would like to incorporate techniques of aggressive filtering for matching regular expressions $\left\lbrack  {{39},{40}}\right\rbrack$ and parallelizing polynomial-delay enumeration [40].

最后但同样重要的是，关键的未来方向是将我们提出的上界转化为能显著超越现有技术的算法——至少在满足可处理性条件时。除优化转换过程和多项式延迟算法外，我们拟引入$\left\lbrack  {{39},{40}}\right\rbrack$正则表达式匹配的激进过滤技术，并并行化多项式延迟枚举[40]。
