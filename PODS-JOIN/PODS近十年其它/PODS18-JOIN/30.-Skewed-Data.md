## Abstract

## 摘要

Set similarity join, as well as the corresponding indexing problem set similarity search, are fundamental primitives for managing noisy or uncertain data. For example, these primitives can be used in data cleaning to identify different representations of the same object. In many cases one can represent an object as a sparse 0-1 vector, or equivalently as the set of nonzero entries in such a vector. A set similarity join can then be used to identify those pairs that have an exceptionally large dot product (or intersection, when viewed as sets). We choose to focus on identifying vectors with large Pearson correlation, but results extend to other similarity measures. In particular, we consider the indexing problem of identifying correlated vectors in a set $S$ of vectors sampled from $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ . Given a query vector $\mathbf{y}$ and a parameter $\alpha  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ ,we need to search for an $\alpha$ -correlated vector $\mathbf{x}$ in a data structure representing the vectors of $S$ . This kind of similarity search has been intensely studied in worst-case (non-random data) settings.

集合相似性连接及其对应的索引问题——集合相似性搜索，是管理噪声或不确定数据的基础原语。例如，这些原语可用于数据清洗，以识别同一对象的不同表示形式。在许多情况下，可以将对象表示为稀疏的0-1向量，或等效地表示为该向量中非零项的集合。集合相似性连接可用于识别那些具有异常大点积（或视为集合时的交集）的对。我们选择专注于识别具有高皮尔逊相关性的向量，但结果可推广到其他相似性度量。具体而言，我们考虑从$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$采样的向量集合$S$中识别相关向量的索引问题。给定查询向量$\mathbf{y}$和参数$\alpha  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$，我们需要在表示$S$向量的数据结构中搜索与$\mathbf{y}$具有$\alpha$相关性的向量$\mathbf{x}$。这种相似性搜索在最坏情况（非随机数据）设置中已被深入研究。

Existing theoretically well-founded methods for set similarity search are often inferior to heuristics that take advantage of skew in the data distribution,i.e.,widely differing frequencies of $1\mathrm{\;s}$ across the $d$ dimensions. The main contribution of this paper is to analyze the set similarity problem under a random data model that reflects the kind of skewed data distributions seen in practice, allowing theoretical results much stronger than what is possible in worst-case settings. Our indexing data structure is a recursive, data-dependent partitioning of vectors inspired by recent advances in set similarity search. Previous data-dependent methods do not seem to allow us to exploit skew in item frequencies, so we believe that our work sheds further light on the power of data dependence.

现有的理论完备的集合相似性搜索方法，往往不如利用数据分布偏斜（即$d$维度上$1\mathrm{\;s}$频率差异显著）的启发式方法有效。本文的主要贡献是在反映实践中常见偏斜数据分布的随机数据模型下分析集合相似性问题，从而获得比最坏情况设置更强有力的理论结果。我们的索引数据结构是一种递归的、数据依赖的向量划分方法，灵感来自集合相似性搜索的最新进展。以往的数据依赖方法似乎无法让我们利用项频率的偏斜，因此我们认为我们的工作进一步揭示了数据依赖的力量。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Data management is increasingly moving from a world of well-ordered, curated data sets to settings where data may be noisy, incomplete, or uncertain. This requires primitives that are able to work with notions of "approximate match", as opposed to the exact matches used in standard hash indexes and in equi-joins. Such functionality is particularly challenging to scale when data is high-dimensional, informally because of the curse of dimensionality which makes it hard to organize data in such a way that approximate matches can be efficiently identified. In this paper we consider the fundamental primitive set similarity join and more specifically the indexing problem set similarity search. A set similarity join is used to identify pairs of sets that are similar in the sense that they have an "exceptionally large intersection". Many notions of "exceptionally large intersection" exist, but from a theoretical point of view they are essentially equivalent [18]. We will consider the encoding of sets as sparse 0-1 vectors, and use Pearson correlation as the measure of similarity; we give more details below.

数据管理正逐渐从有序、规整的数据集世界转向数据可能存在噪声、不完整或不确定的环境。这要求基础操作能够处理"近似匹配"的概念，而非标准哈希索引和等值连接中使用的精确匹配。当数据具有高维度时，这种功能尤其难以扩展，非正式地说，这是由于维度灾难使得难以以可高效识别近似匹配的方式组织数据。本文考虑基础操作集合相似性连接，更具体地说是索引问题集合相似性搜索。集合相似性连接用于识别在"交集异常大"的意义上相似的集合对。存在多种"异常大交集"的定义，但从理论角度看它们本质上是等价的[18]。我们将集合编码为稀疏0-1向量，并使用皮尔逊相关系数作为相似性度量；下文将给出更多细节。

The information retrieval and database communities have extensively worked on designing scalable algorithms for similarity join, see e.g. the recent book by Augsten and Böhlen [10]. The state-of-the-art for practical set similarity join algorithms is reflected in the recent mini-survey and comprehensive empirical evaluation of Mann et al. [31]. The best methods in practice are ones that exploit the significant skew in frequencies of set elements that exists in many data sets. When there is insufficient skew these methods become inefficient, and in the worst case they degenerate to a trivial brute-force algorithm. In contrast, strong theoretical results are known when randomization and approximation of distances is allowed,e.g. $\left\lbrack  {2,{18},{22},{25},{46}}\right\rbrack$ . Even though existing randomized algorithms for set similarity search are superior to commonly used heuristics for difficult data distributions with small skew, it is clear that the heuristics will work much better (in theory and in practice) when the skew is large enough.

信息检索和数据库学界已广泛研究可扩展的相似性连接算法设计，例如参见奥格斯滕和伯伦的最新著作[10]。实践中最先进的集合相似性连接算法反映在曼等人的近期小规模综述和全面实证评估中[31]。实际应用中最佳的方法是那些利用许多数据集中存在的集合元素频率显著偏斜的方法。当偏斜不足时，这些方法效率低下，最坏情况下会退化为简单的暴力算法。相比之下，当允许随机化和距离近似时，已有强有力的理论结果，例如$\left\lbrack  {2,{18},{22},{25},{46}}\right\rbrack$。尽管现有的集合相似性搜索随机算法对于偏斜较小的困难数据分布优于常用启发式方法，但显然当偏斜足够大时，启发式方法在理论和实践上都会表现得更好。

In this paper we target this disconnect between theory and practice, presenting a new data structure that, in a certain way, can interpolate between the best existing methods for small skew and heuristics that work well with large skew. Our message is that modeling skew can lead to algorithms that takes advantage of structure in data in a way that is theoretically justified. This complements recent advances in the theory of data dependent methods for high-dimensional search, where clustering structure in data (rather than skew) is exploited to achieve faster algorithms, even in the worst case $\left\lbrack  {6,8}\right\rbrack$ .

本文针对理论与实践的脱节，提出一种新的数据结构，在某种意义上可以在适用于小偏斜的最佳现有方法与适用于大偏斜的启发式方法之间进行插值。我们的观点是，建模偏斜可以产生在理论上合理利用数据结构的算法。这补充了高维搜索依赖数据方法理论的最新进展，其中利用数据中的聚类结构（而非偏斜）来实现更快的算法，即使是在最坏情况下$\left\lbrack  {6,8}\right\rbrack$。

Probabilistic viewpoint. Establishing correlation between random variables is a fundamental and well-studied problem in statistics, but computational aspects of this problem are far from settled. In a breakthrough paper [42], Greg Valiant addressed the so-called light bulb problem originally posed in [43]:

概率视角。建立随机变量间的相关性是统计学中基础且深入研究的课题，但其计算层面的问题远未解决。Greg Valiant在突破性论文[42]中解决了[43]提出的所谓"灯泡问题"：

Given a set of $n$ vectors $S \subseteq  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ chosen uniformly and independently at random, with the exception of one pair of distinct vectors $\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y} \in  S$ that have correlation $\alpha  > 0$ ,identify the vectors $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$ .

给定一组$n$个随机均匀独立选取的向量$S \subseteq  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$，其中唯有一对相异向量$\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y} \in  S$具有相关性$\alpha  > 0$，需识别出这对向量$\mathbf{x}$和$\mathbf{y}$。

If $d$ is sufficiently high (e.g. $d \gg  \log \left( n\right) /\alpha$ ) the correlated vectors $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$ are,with high probability,the only pair with an inner product of around $\left( {1 + \alpha }\right) d/4$ ,while all other pairs have inner product around $d/4$ . From an algorithmic perspective the problem then becomes that of finding the pair of vectors with a significantly higher inner product. The problem of searching for correlated vectors has been intensely studied in recent years, in theory $\left\lbrack  {1 - 4,{26},{27},{32}}\right\rbrack$ and in practice $\left\lbrack  {{20},{21},{28},{37} - {41}}\right\rbrack$ . Practical solutions often address the search version, known as maximum inner product search, where the vector $\mathbf{y}$ is given as a query (often denoted $\mathbf{q}$ ) and the task is to search for the correlated vector $\mathbf{x}$ in a data structure enabling fast search among the vectors in $S$ .

当$d$足够大时（例如$d \gg  \log \left( n\right) /\alpha$），相关向量$\mathbf{x}$与$\mathbf{y}$极可能是唯一内积约为$\left( {1 + \alpha }\right) d/4$的向量对，其余向量对的内积则约为$d/4$。从算法角度看，问题转化为寻找具有显著更高内积的向量对。近年来相关向量搜索问题在理论$\left\lbrack  {1 - 4,{26},{27},{32}}\right\rbrack$和实践$\left\lbrack  {{20},{21},{28},{37} - {41}}\right\rbrack$领域都得到深入研究，实际解决方案常针对搜索版本（称为最大内积搜索），其中向量$\mathbf{y}$作为查询输入（常记作$\mathbf{q}$），任务是在支持快速搜索的数据结构中找出$S$中与$\mathbf{x}$相关的向量。

The light bulb problem is perhaps the cleanest and most fundamental correlation search problem. However, vectors of real-life data sets are usually not well described by a uniform distribution over $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ . Instead,such vectors are often sparse (assuming without loss of generality that 0 has probability at least $1/2$ in each coordinate),and the fraction of vectors having the value 1 in the $i$ th coordinate varies greatly with $i$ ,often following e.g. a Zipfian distribution (see Section 8). This kind of skew is exploited by practical solutions to correlation search $\left\lbrack  {{11},{31},{44}}\right\rbrack$ ,since high correlation between vectors $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$ will often be "witnessed" by ${\mathbf{x}}_{i} = {\mathbf{y}}_{i} = 1$ , where the set $\left\{  {\mathbf{z} \in  S \mid  {\mathbf{z}}_{i} = 1}\right\}$ is small. On the other hand,such methods do not perform well when the skew is small. In this paper we explore the computational problem of identifying correlations in random data with skew, focusing on the search version of the problem. Generalizing and modifying recent worst-case efficient data structures, we are able to get a smooth trade-off between "hard" queries and data sets with no skew, and "easy" queries and data sets of the kind often encountered in practice.

灯泡问题可能是最简洁、最基础的相关性搜索问题。然而现实数据集的向量通常不符合$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$上的均匀分布，这些向量往往具有稀疏性（假设各坐标中0出现的概率至少为$1/2$），且第$i$个坐标取值为1的向量比例随$i$变化显著，常遵循Zipf分布（参见第8节）。相关性搜索的实用解决方案$\left\lbrack  {{11},{31},{44}}\right\rbrack$正是利用这种偏态——当向量$\mathbf{x}$与$\mathbf{y}$高度相关时，其相关性常由${\mathbf{x}}_{i} = {\mathbf{y}}_{i} = 1$（其中集合$\left\{  {\mathbf{z} \in  S \mid  {\mathbf{z}}_{i} = 1}\right\}$规模较小）"见证"。但此类方法在偏态较小时表现不佳。本文探索具有偏态的随机数据中识别相关性的计算问题，聚焦于该问题的搜索版本。通过推广和改进最近的最坏情况高效数据结构，我们实现了"困难"查询（无偏态数据集）与"简单"查询（实践中常见数据集）之间的平滑权衡。

To model skewed data we adopt the model of Kirsch et al. [29] that was previously used to give statistical guarantees on data mining algorithms. We are not aiming at statistical guarantees, but instead use this model as an interesting "middle ground" between uniformly random and worst-case settings when analyzing algorithms dealing with high-dimensional data. Conceptually this model:

为建模偏态数据，我们采用Kirsch等人[29]提出的模型，该模型曾用于为数据挖掘算法提供统计保证。我们虽不追求统计保证，但在分析处理高维数据的算法时，将此模型作为均匀随机与最坏情况设定之间一种有趣的"中间地带"。概念上该模型：

Discussion. In the balanced case where all probabilities ${p}_{i}$ are identical, we recover the time bounds of the recently proposed CHOSENPATH algorithm [18], which are known to be optimal in this setting. In the very unbalanced case where some ${p}_{i}$ are $\Omega \left( 1\right)$ ,some ${p}_{i}$ are $O\left( {1/n}\right)$ ,and the expected number of items of both kinds are comparable, we match the well-known prefix filter algorithm [11], which beats CHOSENPATH in this setting. For skew between these extremes we get strict improvements over existing methods. (See Section 7 and Figure 1 for further discussion.)

讨论。在所有概率${p}_{i}$均相等的平衡情况下，我们重现了近期提出的CHOSENPATH算法[18]的时间边界，该边界在此设定下已被证明是最优的。在极端不平衡情况下，当部分${p}_{i}$为$\Omega \left( 1\right)$、部分${p}_{i}$为$O\left( {1/n}\right)$，且两类项目的期望数量相当时，我们的结果与著名的前缀过滤算法[11]一致——该算法在此情境下优于CHOSENPATH。对于介于这两个极端之间的偏斜分布，我们实现了对现有方法的严格改进。（详见第7节与图1的进一步讨论。）

Techniques. Our data structure is a natural, recursive variant of CHOSENPATH that is able to exploit skew by varying the recursion depth over the branches of the recursion tree and by aggressively favoring choices based on the given distribution that are more likely distinguish close and far elements. We stress that CHOSENPATH is not able to exploit skew, and in fact has the same tight running time guarantee independent of the data distribution. Because we cut the depth of the tree earlier based on the skew of the distribution, we must tighten the previous analysis to handle sampling without replacement, while also parameterizing our performance based on skew. This leads to significant challenges.

技术方法。我们的数据结构是CHOSENPATH的自然递归变体，能通过动态调整递归树分支的深度，并基于给定分布优先选择更可能区分远近元素的分支，从而有效利用数据偏斜。需要强调的是，CHOSENPATH无法利用偏斜特性，其严格时间复杂度保证实际上与数据分布无关。由于我们根据分布偏斜提前截断递归深度，必须加强原有分析以处理无放回抽样问题，同时基于偏斜参数化性能表现，这带来了重大技术挑战。

Adversarial queries. It may not always be reasonable to assume that a query is random and $\alpha$ -correlated,as in Theorem 1 . ### 1.2 相关工作

Correlation search on the unit sphere. After shifting vectors to have zero mean and normalizing them (which does not affect correlation),searching for a vector $\mathbf{x} \in  S$ that is $\alpha$ -correlated with a query vector $\mathbf{y}$ boils down to a search problem on the unit sphere ${S}^{d - 1}$ : Given $\mathbf{y} \in  {S}^{d - 1}$ find $\mathbf{x} \in  S$ such that $\mathbf{x} \cdot  \mathbf{y} \geq  \alpha$ . In turn,this is equivalent to near neighbor search under Euclidean distance on the unit sphere, which has been studied extensively. For "balanced" data structures with space (and construction time) $\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{1 + \rho }\right)$ and query time $\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{\rho }\right)$ the state-of-the-art is $\rho  = \frac{1 - \alpha }{1 + \alpha }$ [5],using a non-trivial generalization of Charikar's famous LSH for angular distances [15]. Generalizations to various time-space trade-offs are also known [7, 17].

单位球面上的相关性搜索。将向量平移至零均值并归一化后（此操作不影响相关性），寻找与查询向量$\mathbf{y}$具有$\alpha$相关性的向量$\mathbf{x} \in  S$可转化为单位球面${S}^{d - 1}$上的搜索问题：给定$\mathbf{y} \in  {S}^{d - 1}$，找到满足$\mathbf{x} \cdot  \mathbf{y} \geq  \alpha$的$\mathbf{x} \in  S$。这等价于单位球面上欧氏距离的近似最近邻搜索，该领域已有深入研究。对于空间（及构建时间）为$\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{1 + \rho }\right)$、查询时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{\rho }\right)$的"平衡"数据结构，当前最优方案是$\rho  = \frac{1 - \alpha }{1 + \alpha }$[5]，其采用了Charikar著名角距离LSH[15]的非平凡推广。针对不同时空权衡的泛化方案亦见于[7,17]。

Correlation search on sparse vectors. For sparse binary vectors, the framework of set similarity search captures search for different notions of similarity/correlation [16]. For vectors of fixed Hamming weight there is a 1-1 correspondence between Pearson correlation and standard set similarity measures such as Jaccard similarity and Braun-Blanquet similarity. It is known that methods such as MinHash $\left\lbrack  {{13},{14}}\right\rbrack$ that specifically target sparse vectors yield better search algorithms than more general methods when the fraction $\beta$ of non-zero entries is bounded by a sufficiently small constant. Recently it was shown that MinHash can be improved in this setting, yielding balanced similarity search data structure with $\rho  = \log (\beta  +$ $\alpha \left( {1 - \beta }\right) )/\log \beta \left\lbrack  {18}\right\rbrack$ .

稀疏向量的相关性搜索。对于稀疏二值向量，集合相似性搜索框架可捕捉不同相似性/相关性概念[16]。固定汉明重量的向量间，皮尔逊相关系数与标准集合相似性度量（如Jaccard相似性、Braun-Blanquet相似性）存在一一对应关系。已知当非零项比例$\beta$受足够小常数限制时，MinHash$\left\lbrack  {{13},{14}}\right\rbrack$等专门针对稀疏向量的方法能产生优于通用方案的搜索算法。最新研究表明该场景下MinHash可进一步优化，从而得到具有$\rho  = \log (\beta  +$$\alpha \left( {1 - \beta }\right) )/\log \beta \left\lbrack  {18}\right\rbrack$的平衡相似性搜索数据结构。

Batched search. The first subquadratic algorithm for the light bulb problem was given by Paturi et al. [36],yielding time ${n}^{2 - \Theta \left( \alpha \right) }$ . Valiant [42] showed that it is possible to remove $\alpha$ from the exponent, obtaining substantially subquadratic running time even for small values of $\alpha$ . Karppa et al. strenthened Valiant’s result to time $O\left( {{n}^{1.6}\operatorname{poly}\left( {1/\alpha }\right) }\right) \left\lbrack  {26}\right\rbrack$ ,for $\log n \ll  d = {n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$ (where the constant 1.6 reflects current matrix multiplication algorithms). A somewhat slower, but deterministic, subquadratic algorithm was subsequently presented [27]. With the exception of [36] these methods rely on fast rectangular matrix multiplication and seem inherently only applicable to batched search problems.

批量搜索。Paturi等人[36]首次提出了针对灯泡问题的亚二次方算法，其时间复杂度为${n}^{2 - \Theta \left( \alpha \right) }$。Valiant[42]证明了可以从指数中移除$\alpha$，即使对于较小的$\alpha$值也能获得显著亚二次方的运行时间。Karppa等人将Valiant的结果强化至$O\left( {{n}^{1.6}\operatorname{poly}\left( {1/\alpha }\right) }\right) \left\lbrack  {26}\right\rbrack$时间复杂度（其中常数1.6反映了当前矩阵乘法算法的水平）。随后文献[27]提出了一种速度稍慢但确定性的亚二次方算法。除[36]外，这些方法都依赖于快速矩形矩阵乘法，本质上似乎仅适用于批量搜索问题。

Worst-Case search. Much of previous theoretical work on set similarity search has focused on the worst case, where the point set and queries are adversarial rather than being drawn from a known distribution. The Chosen Path algorithm of Christiani and Pagh focuses on Braun-Blanquet similarity, which is the metric we use here. ${}^{4}$ In particular,if the dataset contains a point $p$ where $q$ and $p$ have Braun-Blanquet similarity at least ${b}_{1}$ ,the data structure returns a point ${p}^{\prime }$ such that $q$ and $p$ have Braun-Blanquet similarity at least ${b}_{2}$ . Their data structure achieves space $\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{1 + \rho }\right)$ and query time $\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{\rho }\right)$ for $\rho  = \left( {\log {b}_{1}}\right) /\log {b}_{2}$ . This result is a strict improvement over the classic MinHash algorithm [13, 14].

最坏情况搜索。以往关于集合相似性搜索的理论研究大多聚焦最坏情况，即点集和查询来自对抗性分布而非已知分布。Christiani与Pagh的"选择路径"算法专注于Braun-Blanquet相似度——这也是本文采用的度量标准。${}^{4}$具体而言，若数据集中存在点$p$满足$q$与$p$的Braun-Blanquet相似度至少为${b}_{1}$，该数据结构将返回点${p}^{\prime }$使得$q$与$p$的相似度不低于${b}_{2}$。其数据结构在$\rho  = \left( {\log {b}_{1}}\right) /\log {b}_{2}$条件下实现了$\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{1 + \rho }\right)$空间复杂度和$\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{\rho }\right)$查询时间，这一结果显著超越了经典的MinHash算法[13,14]。

Lower bounds. Cell probe data structure lower bounds achievable by current techniques are polylogarithmic in data size (assuming polylogarithmic word size). Tight bounds are only known for small query time, see e.g. [35] - for subquadratic space this leaves a large gap to the polynomial upper bounds that can be achieved by similarity search techniques. However, good conditional lower bounds based on the strong exponential time hypothesis (SETH) [23] are known. Ahle et al. [2] showed hardness of approximate maximum inner product search under SETH, but only for inner products very close to zero. Recently, Abboud, Rubinstein, and Williams [1] significantly improved this result, and in particular showed, again assuming SETH, that maximum inner product requires near-linear time even for vectors in $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ and allowing a large ${2}^{{\left( \log d\right) }^{1 - o\left( 1\right) }}$ approximation factor. This means that there is little hope of obtaining strong algorithms in the worst case, even for batched search problems. Of course, as the upper bounds for the light bulb problem show, the average case is easier.

下界限制。当前技术能达到的细胞探针数据结构下界是数据规模的多对数级别（假设字长为多对数）。紧致下界仅见于小查询时间场景[35]——对于亚二次方空间，这与相似性搜索技术能达到的多项式上界存在巨大差距。不过基于强指数时间假说(SETH)[23]的良好条件限制已被证实。Ahle等人[2]证明了SETH下近似最大内积搜索的困难性，但仅适用于接近零的内积值。最近Abboud、Rubinstein和Williams[1]大幅改进了该结果，特别在SETH假设下证明：即使对于$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$维向量且允许较大${2}^{{\left( \log d\right) }^{1 - o\left( 1\right) }}$近似因子时，最大内积搜索仍需近线性时间。这意味着在最坏情况下获得强算法希望渺茫，即便对批量搜索问题亦然。当然，如灯泡问题的上界所示，平均情况更为简单。

Heuristics. Above we have discussed related work with a theoretical emphasis. Many heuristics exist, especially for sparse vectors. Some of the most widely used ones are based on exact, deterministic filtering techniques, such as prefix filtering [11] which evaluates the similarity of every pair of vectors that share a 1 in a position that has a small fraction of $1\mathrm{\;s}$ . This is effective when vectors are sparse and skewed in the sense that there are many positions with a small fraction of $1\mathrm{\;s}$ . We refer to Mann et al. [31] for an overview of exact, heuristic techniques.

启发式方法。前文主要讨论理论导向的相关工作。实践中存在许多启发式方法，尤其针对稀疏向量。最广泛应用的某些方法基于精确的确定性过滤技术，如前缀过滤[11]——该方法评估每对在$1\mathrm{\;s}$占比较低位置同时出现1的向量相似度。当向量稀疏且存在大量$1\mathrm{\;s}$占比较低位置时效果显著。关于精确启发式技术的综述可参阅Mann等人[31]。

## 2 MODEL

## 2 模型

Our model closely follows the one in [29] - we describe it here for completeness. The elements in our set are $d$ -dimensional boolean vectors $\mathbf{x} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ . These vectors represent a subset of items from a universe $U = \{ 1,\ldots ,d\}$ . We generally consider our elements to be boolean vectors; however, we express some of our operations using set notation for convenience (for example, $\mathbf{x} \cap  \mathbf{q}$ represents the 1 bits in common between $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{q}$ ).

我们的模型严格遵循[29]中的设计——为完整起见在此详述。集合中的元素是$d$维布尔向量$\mathbf{x} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$，这些向量表示全域$U = \{ 1,\ldots ,d\}$中项目的子集。虽然我们通常将元素视为布尔向量，但为方便起见会采用集合符号表示某些运算（例如$\mathbf{x} \cap  \mathbf{q}$表示$\mathbf{x}$与$\mathbf{q}$共有的1比特位）。

We are given a distribution $\mathcal{D} = \mathcal{D}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}}\right\rbrack$ over $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ defined as follows: if $\mathbf{x}$ is a vector drawn from $\mathcal{D}$ ,then $\Pr \left\lbrack  {{\mathbf{x}}_{i} = 1}\right\rbrack   = {p}_{i}$ independently for each $i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$ . We denote by ${\mathcal{D}}^{n} = {\mathcal{D}}^{n}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{n}}\right\rbrack$ the distribution obtained by sampling $n$ vectors independently from $\mathcal{D}$ . The probabilities ${p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}$ are assumed to be known to the algorithm. We assume that all item-level probabilities are at most $1/2$ . The particular value $1/2$ is not important,and all of our results holds as long as there is some constant $M < 1$ such that all item-level probabilities are bounded by $M$ .

给定定义在$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$上的分布$\mathcal{D} = \mathcal{D}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}}\right\rbrack$：若$\mathbf{x}$是从$\mathcal{D}$抽取的向量，则对每个$i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$独立地有$\Pr \left\lbrack  {{\mathbf{x}}_{i} = 1}\right\rbrack   = {p}_{i}$。用${\mathcal{D}}^{n} = {\mathcal{D}}^{n}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{n}}\right\rbrack$表示从$\mathcal{D}$独立采样$n$个向量得到的分布。算法默认已知概率${p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}$，并假设所有项目级概率最大为$1/2$。具体阈值$1/2$并不关键，只要存在常数$M < 1$使得所有项目级概率都小于$M$，我们的结论均成立。

Definition 3 (Correlation). Let $\alpha  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ . Fix $\mathbf{x} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ . We say that $\mathbf{q}$ is $\alpha$ -correlated to $\mathbf{x}$ with respect to $\mathcal{D}$ ,written $\mathbf{q} \sim$ ${\mathcal{D}}_{\alpha }\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ ,if $\mathbf{q}$ is a random vector drawn as follows: For each $i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$ independently,with probability $\alpha$ let ${\mathbf{q}}_{i} = {\mathbf{x}}_{i}$ ,and with probability $1 - \alpha$ let ${\mathbf{q}}_{i} \sim$ Bernoulli $\left( {p}_{i}\right)$ .

定义3（相关性）。设$\alpha  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$，固定$\mathbf{x} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$。若$\mathbf{q}$是通过以下方式抽取的随机向量：对每个$i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$独立地，以概率$\alpha$令${\mathbf{q}}_{i} = {\mathbf{x}}_{i}$，以概率$1 - \alpha$令${\mathbf{q}}_{i} \sim$服从伯努利分布$\left( {p}_{i}\right)$，则称$\mathbf{q}$关于$\mathcal{D}$与$\mathbf{x}$具有$\alpha$相关性，记作$\mathbf{q} \sim$${\mathcal{D}}_{\alpha }\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$。

If $\mathbf{x} \sim  \mathcal{D}$ and $\mathbf{q} \sim  {\mathcal{D}}_{\alpha }\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ ,then the distribution of $\mathbf{q}$ is exactly $\mathcal{D}$ -in particular, $\Pr \left( {{\mathbf{q}}_{i} = 1}\right)  = {p}_{i}$ . Furthermore,for each $i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$ , the random variables ${\mathbf{q}}_{i}$ and ${\mathbf{x}}_{i}$ have Pearson correlation $\alpha$ .

当$\mathbf{x} \sim  \mathcal{D}$且$\mathbf{q} \sim  {\mathcal{D}}_{\alpha }\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$时，$\mathbf{q}$的分布恰为$\mathcal{D}$——特别地，$\Pr \left( {{\mathbf{q}}_{i} = 1}\right)  = {p}_{i}$。此外，对每个$i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$，随机变量${\mathbf{q}}_{i}$与${\mathbf{x}}_{i}$的皮尔逊相关系数为$\alpha$。

Our goal is to create an efficient data structure for vectors sampled from ${\mathcal{D}}^{n}$ which takes advantage of possible skew in the item-level probabilities. Our performance bounds (query and preprocessing times) are expected,and depend both on ${\mathcal{D}}^{n}$ and the data structure's random choices. Following [18], we will use Braun-Blanquet similarity,

我们的目标是构建高效数据结构，用于处理从${\mathcal{D}}^{n}$采样的向量，并利用项目级概率可能存在的偏态特征。性能界限（查询与预处理时间）均为期望值，其取决于${\mathcal{D}}^{n}$及数据结构的随机选择。遵循[18]的方法，我们将采用Braun-Blanquet相似度，

$$
B\left( {\mathbf{x},\mathbf{q}}\right)  = \frac{\left| \mathbf{x} \cap  \mathbf{q}\right| }{\max \{ \left| \mathbf{x}\right| ,\left| \mathbf{q}\right| \} },
$$

as our measure for similarity between sets. This similarity measure is closely related to Jaccard similarity, see [18] for details. We now formally define the two versions of the problem that we are considering:

作为集合间相似性度量标准。该度量与Jaccard相似度密切相关，详见[18]说明。下面正式定义我们研究的两个问题版本：

Adversarial query. Given a dataset $S \sim  {\mathcal{D}}^{n}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}}\right\rbrack$ of $n$ items sampled from $\mathcal{D}$ and a similarity threshold ${b}_{1}$ ,preprocess $S$ into a data structure with the following capability: Given $\mathbf{q} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ , return $\mathbf{x} \in  S$ such that $B\left( {\mathbf{x},\mathbf{q}}\right)  \geq  {b}_{1}$ if such an $\mathbf{x}$ exists. The data structure must succeed with probability at least $1 - {o}_{n}\left( 1\right)$ over its own internal randomness. In particular, the probability that the data structure succeeds must be independent of the dataset $S \in  {\mathcal{D}}^{n}$ .

对抗性查询。给定从$\mathcal{D}$采样的$n$个项组成的数据集$S \sim  {\mathcal{D}}^{n}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}}\right\rbrack$和相似度阈值${b}_{1}$，将$S$预处理为具有以下能力的数据结构：给定$\mathbf{q} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$，返回满足$B\left( {\mathbf{x},\mathbf{q}}\right)  \geq  {b}_{1}$的$\mathbf{x} \in  S$（若该$\mathbf{x}$存在）。该数据结构必须在其自身内部随机性上以至少$1 - {o}_{n}\left( 1\right)$的概率成功，且成功概率必须独立于数据集$S \in  {\mathcal{D}}^{n}$。

Correlated query. Given a dataset $S \sim  {\mathcal{D}}^{n}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}}\right\rbrack$ of $n$ items sampled from $\mathcal{D}$ and a correlation threshold $0 < \alpha  \leq  1$ ,preprocess $S$ into a data structure with the following capability: Let $\mathbf{x} \in  S$ and let $\mathbf{q} \sim  {\mathcal{D}}_{\alpha }\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ be $\alpha$ -correlated to $\mathbf{x}$ with respect to $\mathcal{D}$ . Then,given the query $\mathbf{q}$ ,the data structure must return $\mathbf{x}$ . The data structure must succeed in doing so with probability at least $1 - {o}_{n}\left( 1\right)$ over the choice of $\mathbf{q}$ ,the randomness of the dataset $S$ ,and its own internal randomness. ${}^{5}$

相关性查询。给定从$\mathcal{D}$采样的$n$个项组成的数据集$S \sim  {\mathcal{D}}^{n}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}}\right\rbrack$和相关性阈值$0 < \alpha  \leq  1$，将$S$预处理为具有以下能力的数据结构：设$\mathbf{x} \in  S$与$\mathbf{x}$在$\mathcal{D}$上具有$\alpha$相关性，则给定查询$\mathbf{q}$时，该数据结构必须返回$\mathbf{x}$。其成功概率（基于查询$\mathbf{q}$的选择、数据集$S$的随机性及自身内部随机性）必须至少为$1 - {o}_{n}\left( 1\right)$。${}^{5}$

As usual, the part of the success probability that depends only on the data structure's random choices may be boosted by a small number of repetitions. Thus, for adversarial queries, it suffices to build a data structure with success probability,say, $1/2$ ,and for correlated queries, it suffices that the part of the success probability depending only on the data structure's random choices is at least $1/2$ . However,the part of the success probability depending on the query and the dataset cannot be boosted by repetitions. Instead, we need to design a data structure such that under some reasonable assumptions on the item-level probabilities, we achieve the desired success probability.

通常，仅依赖数据结构随机选择的成功概率部分可通过少量重复操作提升。因此对于对抗性查询，构建成功概率为（例如）$1/2$的数据结构即可；对于相关性查询，仅需确保依赖数据结构随机选择的成功概率部分至少为$1/2$。但依赖查询与数据集的成功概率部分无法通过重复提升，而需在设计数据结构时，基于项级概率的合理假设达成目标成功概率。

We say that an event occurs with high probability if it occurs with probability $O\left( {1/{n}^{c}}\right)$ for a tunable ${}^{6}$ constant $c$ .

当某事件以概率$O\left( {1/{n}^{c}}\right)$（其中${}^{6}$为可调常数$c$）发生时，我们称该事件高概率发生。

## 3 DATA STRUCTURE

## 3 数据结构

The data structure follows the locality-sensitive mapping, or filtering,framework $\left\lbrack  {{12},{17},{18}}\right\rbrack$ . In this framework,each element $\mathbf{x}$ is mapped to a set of filters $F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ . This is distinct from locality-sensitive hashing,in which $\mathbf{x}$ would be mapped to a single hash value.

该数据结构遵循局部敏感映射（locality-sensitive mapping）或过滤框架$\left\lbrack  {{12},{17},{18}}\right\rbrack$。在此框架中，每个元素$\mathbf{x}$被映射到一组过滤器$F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$。这与局部敏感哈希不同——后者会将$\mathbf{x}$映射为单个哈希值。

While locality-sensitive filtering is distinct from locality-sensitive hashing, preprocessing and searching follows essentially the same high-level idea.

虽然局部敏感过滤与局部敏感哈希不同，但其预处理和搜索过程本质上遵循相同的高层理念。

To search for a query $\mathbf{q}$ we iterate through each filter $f \in  F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$ . For each such filter,we test all vectors $\mathbf{x} \in  S$ such that $f \in  F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ . In other words, we iterate through each vector that has a filter in common with $\mathbf{q}$ ,and test the similarity of $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{q}$ . If we find a sufficiently close $\mathbf{x}$ we return it; otherwise we return failure after exhausting all $f \in  F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$ .

搜索查询$\mathbf{q}$时，我们遍历每个过滤器$f \in  F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$。对于每个过滤器，测试所有满足$f \in  F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$的向量$\mathbf{x} \in  S$。换言之，我们遍历每个与$\mathbf{q}$拥有共同过滤器的向量，并测试$\mathbf{x}$与$\mathbf{q}$的相似度。若找到足够接近的$\mathbf{x}$则返回；否则在穷尽所有$f \in  F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$后返回失败。

Thus, the goal of preprocessing is to make it easy to find elements that have a filter in common with a given query. For each filter $f$ mapped to by some element of $S$ ,we store a list of all ${\mathbf{x}}^{\prime }$ such that $f \in  F\left( {\mathbf{x}}^{\prime }\right)$ . These lists can be stored and accessed easily (i.e. in a hash table),and this method takes space linear in $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  S}}\left| {F\left( \mathbf{x}\right) }\right|$ . We can preprocess quickly by calculating $F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ for all $\mathbf{x} \in  S$ .

因此，预处理的目标是便于查找与给定查询拥有共同过滤器的元素。对于$S$中元素映射到的每个过滤器$f$，我们存储所有满足$f \in  F\left( {\mathbf{x}}^{\prime }\right)$的${\mathbf{x}}^{\prime }$列表。这些列表可轻松存储访问（如通过哈希表实现），且该方法占用与$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  S}}\left| {F\left( \mathbf{x}\right) }\right|$线性相关的空间。通过为所有$\mathbf{x} \in  S$计算$F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$即可快速完成预处理。

Thus, the goal of our data structure is to define a randomized mapping of vectors to sets of filters satisfying:

因此，本数据结构的目标是定义向量到过滤器集合的随机映射，需满足：

- If $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{q}$ have low similarity,then $F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \cap  F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$ is small in expectation (guaranteeing small space and fast execution).

- 若$\mathbf{x}$与$\mathbf{q}$相似度低，则$F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \cap  F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$的期望值较小（确保空间效率与执行速度）

- If $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{q}$ have high similarity,then $F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \cap  F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$ is non-empty with high probability (guaranteeing correctness).

- 若$\mathbf{x}$与$\mathbf{q}$相似度高，则$F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \cap  F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$大概率非空（确保正确性）

Computing $F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ : the choice of paths. Our data structure is based on the Chosen Path data structure of Christiani and Pagh [18]. For our data structure,each $f \in  F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ corresponds to a path,i.e.,an ordered sequence $\left( {{i}_{1},\ldots ,{i}_{\ell }}\right)  \subseteq  {\left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }^{\ell }$ where each ${i}_{j} \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$ is the index of one of the $d$ dimensions. The construction of $F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ ensures that if $f \in  F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ ,then it must hold that ${\mathbf{x}}_{i} = 1$ for all $i \in  f$ . We say that the path $f$ was chosen by $\mathbf{x}$ if $f \in  F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ .

计算$F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$：路径选择。我们的数据结构基于Christiani和Pagh[18]的选定路径(Chosen Path)数据结构。在该结构中，每个$f \in  F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$对应一条路径，即有序序列$\left( {{i}_{1},\ldots ,{i}_{\ell }}\right)  \subseteq  {\left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }^{\ell }$，其中每个${i}_{j} \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$表示$d$个维度之一的索引。$F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$的构建确保当$f \in  F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$时，必然满足对所有$i \in  f$都有${\mathbf{x}}_{i} = 1$。若$f \in  F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$，则称路径$f$由$\mathbf{x}$选定。

The data structure comes with a (deterministic) function $s$ which maps each vector $\mathbf{x} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ ,path-length $j$ and bit $i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$ to a threshold $s\left( {\mathbf{x},j,i}\right)  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ . The choice of $s$ depends on the desired application and will be specified later on. In particular, $s$ is how our data structure adapts to the distribution-previous data structures essentially used a constant function for $s{.}^{7}$

该数据结构配备一个（确定性）函数$s$，将每个向量$\mathbf{x} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$、路径长度$j$和比特位$i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack$映射到阈值$s\left( {\mathbf{x},j,i}\right)  \in  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$。$s$的选择取决于具体应用场景，后续将详细说明。特别地，$s$是本结构适应数据分布的关键机制——先前数据结构基本采用恒定函数作为$s{.}^{7}$。

When initializing our data structure, we once and for all select $k$ hash functions, ${h}_{1},\ldots ,{h}_{k}$ ,where ${h}_{j} : {\left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }^{j} \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ ,each chosen independently from a family $\mathcal{H}$ of pairwise independent hash functions. These hash functions are fixed throughout.

初始化数据结构时，我们一次性选定$k$个哈希函数${h}_{1},\ldots ,{h}_{k}$（其中${h}_{j} : {\left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }^{j} \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$），每个函数均从成对独立哈希函数族$\mathcal{H}$中独立选取。这些哈希函数将全程固定使用。

We now explain how to recursively compute the set of paths $F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ . Initially,let ${F}_{0}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ consist of the empty path. We recursively grow the paths one step at a time; in particular, ${F}_{j}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ contains paths of length $j$ . To demonstrate our recursive process,let $v = \left( {{i}_{1},\ldots ,{i}_{j}}\right)$ be a path in ${F}_{j}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ . If $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 1}}^{j}{p}_{{i}_{k}} \leq  1/n$ ,we stop recursing,and $v$ is a filter of $\mathbf{x}$ . Otherwise,we independently consider each set bit $i$ of $\mathbf{x}$ which is not already in $v$ . With probability $s\left( {\mathbf{x},j,i}\right)$ ,we concatenate $i$ to the end of $v$ ; this results in a new filter ${v}^{\prime } \in  {F}_{j + 1}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ with ${v}^{\prime } = v \circ  i$ (where $\circ$ denotes concatenation). This probabilistic choice is made using ${h}_{j + 1}\left( {v \circ  i}\right)$ .

现阐述如何递归计算路径集合$F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$。初始时令${F}_{0}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$仅含空路径。我们逐级递归扩展路径：具体而言，${F}_{j}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$包含长度为$j$的路径。以${F}_{j}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$中的路径$v = \left( {{i}_{1},\ldots ,{i}_{j}}\right)$为例：若$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{k = 1}}^{j}{p}_{{i}_{k}} \leq  1/n$则终止递归，此时$v$成为$\mathbf{x}$的过滤器；否则，对$\mathbf{x}$中未被$v$包含的每个置位比特$i$，以概率$s\left( {\mathbf{x},j,i}\right)$将$i$拼接至$v$末端，从而生成新过滤器${v}^{\prime } \in  {F}_{j + 1}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$（其中${v}^{\prime } = v \circ  i$，$\circ$表示拼接操作）。该概率选择通过${h}_{j + 1}\left( {v \circ  i}\right)$实现。

We formally define this recursive process using the following equation.

我们通过以下等式正式定义该递归过程。

$$
{F}_{j + 1}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  = \left\{  {v \circ  i\left| {\;\begin{array}{l} v = \left( {{i}_{1},\ldots ,{i}_{j}}\right)  \in  {F}_{j}\left( \mathbf{x}\right) , \\  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{{i}_{k} = 1}}^{j}{p}_{{i}_{k}} > 1/n, \\  i \in  \mathbf{x} \smallsetminus  v, \\  {h}_{j + 1}\left( {v \circ  i}\right)  < s\left( {\mathbf{x},j,i}\right)  \end{array}}\right. }\right\}  .
$$

Finally,we define $F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ to be the union of all paths that stopped recursing.

最终，将终止递归的所有路径取并集定义为$F\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$。

$$

In order to simplify the discussion,assume that $\left| \mathbf{q}\right|  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i}$ (so that $\left| \mathbf{q}\right|$ equals the expected value of $\left| \mathbf{x}\right|$ when $\mathbf{x} \sim  \mathcal{D}$ ,meaning that all sets have roughly the same size). Now,for every $\varepsilon  > 0$ there exists a $C$ such that if $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i} \geq  C\log n$ ,then $B\left( {\mathbf{x},\mathbf{q}}\right)  =$ $\frac{1}{\left| \mathbf{q}\right| }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \mathbf{q}}}{p}_{i} \pm  \varepsilon$ for every $\mathbf{x} \in  S$ with high probability. Thus,we can solve the problem by solving the $\left( {{b}_{1},{b}_{2}}\right)$ -approximate Braun-Blanquet similarity search problem using the standard Chosen Path data structure,thereby obtaining a $\rho$ -value which,for sufficiently large $C$ ,is arbitrarily close to

接下来我们将探讨数据结构在多大程度上能够利用项目级概率中可能存在的偏态。为简化讨论，假设$\left| \mathbf{q}\right|  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i}$（即当$\mathbf{x} \sim  \mathcal{D}$时，$\left| \mathbf{q}\right|$等于$\left| \mathbf{x}\right|$的期望值，这意味着所有集合的规模大致相同）。现在，对于每个$\varepsilon  > 0$，都存在一个$C$，使得若$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i} \geq  C\log n$，则$B\left( {\mathbf{x},\mathbf{q}}\right)  =$$\frac{1}{\left| \mathbf{q}\right| }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \mathbf{q}}}{p}_{i} \pm  \varepsilon$对于每个$\mathbf{x} \in  S$以高概率成立。因此，我们可以通过使用标准Chosen Path数据结构解决$\left( {{b}_{1},{b}_{2}}\right)$-近似Braun-Blanquet相似性搜索问题来解决该问题，从而获得一个$\rho$值，当$C$足够大时，该值可无限接近于

$$
{\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} = \frac{\log \left( {b}_{1}\right) }{\log \left( {\frac{1}{\left| \mathbf{q}\right| }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \mathbf{q}}}{p}_{i}}\right) }.
$$

If the part of the input distribution relevant for the query $\mathbf{q}$ contains no skew,i.e.,if ${p}_{i} = p$ for all $i \in  \mathbf{q}$ ,then ${\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} = \rho$ . However, $\rho  <$ ${\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} = \log \left( {b}_{1}\right) /\log \left( p\right)$ in all other cases.

若与查询$\mathbf{q}$相关的输入分布部分不存在偏态，即对所有$i \in  \mathbf{q}$都有${p}_{i} = p$，则${\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} = \rho$。然而，$\rho  <$${\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} = \log \left( {b}_{1}\right) /\log \left( p\right)$在其他所有情况下均成立。

To our knowledge there is no closed-form expression for the solution $\rho$ to the equation $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \mathbf{q}}}{p}_{i}^{\rho } \leq  {b}_{1}\left| \mathbf{q}\right|$ . Instead,we will compute $\rho$ in a few settings to illustrate how skew influences our query time. Suppose that $\mathbf{q}$ consists of two types of bits: half the bits of $\mathbf{q}$ are set with probability ${p}_{a}$ in a random set from $S$ ,and the other half is set with probability ${p}_{b}$ . Furthermore,assume that $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i} = \left| \mathbf{q}\right|  = \Theta \left( {\log n}\right) .$

据我们所知，方程$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \mathbf{q}}}{p}_{i}^{\rho } \leq  {b}_{1}\left| \mathbf{q}\right|$的解$\rho$尚无闭式表达式。为此，我们将在几种设定下计算$\rho$以说明偏态如何影响查询时间。假设$\mathbf{q}$由两种比特组成：在$S$的随机集合中，$\mathbf{q}$的一半比特以概率${p}_{a}$置位，另一半以概率${p}_{b}$置位。此外，假定$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i} = \left| \mathbf{q}\right|  = \Theta \left( {\log n}\right) .$

Suppose first that the distribution has very significant skew, e.g., ${p}_{a} = 1/4,{p}_{b} = {n}^{-{0.9}}$ . Assume that we are searching for a set in $S$ with similarity at least,say, ${b}_{1} = 1/3$ . In this case, ${\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} \geq  \frac{\log \left( {1/3}\right) }{\log \left( {1/8}\right) } \geq$ 0.528,whereas we obtain a $\rho$ -value of $\rho  = \frac{\log \left( {2/3}\right) }{\log \left( {1/4}\right) } + {o}_{n}\left( 1\right)  \leq  {0.293}$ for $n$ sufficiently large. Prefix filtering does not give any non-trivial (worst-case) performance guarantee. This example shows that we can take advantage of the skew even when it is possible that the entire intersection between $\mathbf{q}$ and the close point $\mathbf{x}$ is within a part of $\mathbf{q}$ where all bits are set with the same probability in a datapoint. A more extreme example occurs if we increase ${b}_{1}$ to ${b}_{1} = 2/3$ so that a possible close point $\mathbf{x}$ must share some of the bits for which the associated probability is ${p}_{b} = {n}^{-{0.9}}$ . In this case,equation (7.1) is satisfied for $\rho$ arbitrarily close to zero. Thus,we achieve a very fast query time (in particular,our query time will be $O\left( {n}^{\varepsilon }\right)$ for every constant $\varepsilon  > 0$ ). In order to understand why,note that no path in $F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$ can contain more than two bits with associated item-level probability ${n}^{-{0.9}}$ . Furthermore,since ${b}_{1} > 1/2$ ,our sampling threshold $s\left( {\mathbf{q},j,i}\right)$ is low enough that we are very unlikely to create long paths consisting only of bits with associated probability $1/4$ . Indeed,the expected size of a path in $F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$ is $O\left( 1\right)$ in this case. Solving the problem by solving the $\left( {{b}_{1},{b}_{2}}\right)$ -approximate problem will yield a $\rho$ value of ${\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} = \log \left( {2/3}\right) /\log \left( {1/8}\right)  = {0.194}$ . Prefix filtering will need $\Omega \left( {n}^{0.1}\right)$ time to locate a possible close point if it exists.

首先假设该分布存在显著偏斜，例如${p}_{a} = 1/4,{p}_{b} = {n}^{-{0.9}}$。假定我们在$S$中寻找相似度至少为${b}_{1} = 1/3$的集合。此时${\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} \geq  \frac{\log \left( {1/3}\right) }{\log \left( {1/8}\right) } \geq$值为0.528，而当$n$足够大时，我们获得的$\rho$值为$\rho  = \frac{\log \left( {2/3}\right) }{\log \left( {1/4}\right) } + {o}_{n}\left( 1\right)  \leq  {0.293}$。前缀过滤技术无法提供任何有效的（最坏情况下）性能保证。该例表明，即使$\mathbf{q}$与邻近点$\mathbf{x}$的整个交集可能位于$\mathbf{q}$中所有比特位在数据点以相同概率设置的区域，我们仍可有效利用这种偏斜特性。更极端的情况是当${b}_{1}$增至${b}_{1} = 2/3$时，可能的邻近点$\mathbf{x}$必须共享关联概率为${p}_{b} = {n}^{-{0.9}}$的比特位。此时对于任意接近零的$\rho$值，方程(7.1)都能成立。因此我们实现了极快的查询速度（具体而言，对于任意常数$\varepsilon  > 0$，查询时间将为$O\left( {n}^{\varepsilon }\right)$）。究其原因，$F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$中任何路径最多只能包含两个关联项级概率为${n}^{-{0.9}}$的比特位。此外由于${b}_{1} > 1/2$，我们的采样阈值$s\left( {\mathbf{q},j,i}\right)$足够低，使得仅由关联概率为$1/4$的比特位构成长路径的概率极低——实际上此时$F\left( \mathbf{q}\right)$中路径的期望大小为$O\left( 1\right)$。通过求解$\left( {{b}_{1},{b}_{2}}\right)$近似问题来解决该问题，将得到$\rho$值为${\rho }_{\mathrm{{CP}}} = \log \left( {2/3}\right) /\log \left( {1/8}\right)  = {0.194}$。若存在可能邻近点，前缀过滤技术需要$\Omega \left( {n}^{0.1}\right)$时间进行定位。

### 7.2 Correlated query

### 7.2 关联查询

When the query is $\alpha$ -correlated with a point $\mathbf{x}$ in our dataset $S$ , the running time is (for $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i}$ sufficiently large) determined by the smallest $\rho$ such that $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i}^{1 + \rho }/{\widehat{p}}_{i} \leq  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i}$ where ${\widehat{p}}_{i} = \left( {1 - \alpha }\right) {p}_{i} + \alpha$

当查询与数据集$S$中的点$\mathbf{x}$存在$\alpha$相关性时（对于足够大的$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i}$），运行时间由满足$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i}^{1 + \rho }/{\widehat{p}}_{i} \leq  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  }}{p}_{i}$条件的最小$\rho$值决定，其中${\widehat{p}}_{i} = \left( {1 - \alpha }\right) {p}_{i} + \alpha$

As for the adversarial case, whenever there is significant skew in the input distribution, we can get very fast query time. Suppose for example that $\mathcal{D}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}}\right\rbrack$ is such that ${4C}\log \left( n\right)$ bits are set to 1 with probability ${p}_{a} = 1/4$ and ${n}^{9/{10}}C\log \left( n\right)$ bits are set to 1 with probability ${p}_{b} = {n}^{-9/{10}}$ . If $\alpha  = 2/3$ ,then we find that our expected query time is $O\left( {n}^{\varepsilon }\right)$ for every constant $\varepsilon  > 0$ . On the other hand, prefix filtering takes $\Omega \left( {n}^{0.1}\right)$ time.

对于对抗性案例，当输入分布存在显著偏态时，我们可以获得极快的查询时间。例如假设$\mathcal{D}\left\lbrack  {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{d}}\right\rbrack$满足：${4C}\log \left( n\right)$位以${p}_{a} = 1/4$概率置1，${n}^{9/{10}}C\log \left( n\right)$位以${p}_{b} = {n}^{-9/{10}}$概率置1。若$\alpha  = 2/3$，则发现对于任意常数$\varepsilon  > 0$，预期查询时间为$O\left( {n}^{\varepsilon }\right)$。而前缀过滤需要$\Omega \left( {n}^{0.1}\right)$时间。

Let us now consider examples where the input distribution is skewed but all probabilities are $\Omega \left( 1\right)$ ,meaning in particular that prefix filtering does not give any non-trivial worst-case guarantees. For example,suppose that $C\log n$ bits are set to 1 with probability ${p}_{a} = \Theta \left( 1\right)$ and $C\log n$ bits are set to 1 with probability ${p}_{b} = \Theta \left( 1\right)$ . We compare the performance of our data structure to that of using Chosen Path for solving the $\left( {{b}_{1},{b}_{2}}\right)$ -approximate problem where ${b}_{1}$ is the expected similarity between the correlated points and ${b}_{2}$ is the expected similarity between the query and an uncorrelated point. For ${p}_{a} = p$ and ${p}_{b} = p/8$ and $\alpha  = 2/3$ ,we plot the corresponding $\rho$ -values in Figure 1.

## 8 SKEW IN REAL DATA SETS

## 8 真实数据集中的偏态

In this section we examine how the ideas that inspired our model apply to the real-life sparse data sets used to evaluate similarity set methods by Mann et al. [31].

本节将探讨我们模型的理论基础如何应用于Mann等人[31]用于评估相似集方法的现实稀疏数据集。

Skew. For each data set we computed the empirical frequencies ${p}_{j}$ ,indexed such that frequencies decrease as $j$ grows. In Figure 2 we show the distribution of frequencies ${p}_{j}$ in two ways. On the left side we plot ${\log }_{n}\left( {{p}_{j}n}\right)$ against $j/d$ ,where $n$ is the number of vectors and $d$ is the dimension of the vectors. On the right side we show the distribution on a normalized log-log scale. As can be seen, all data sets display a significant skew. A plain Zipfian distribution would appear linear on this plot (with slope corresponding to the exponent in the distribution). Frequencies do not follow a Zipfian distribution, but are generally close to a "piecewise Zipfian" distribution. For almost all data sets, the frequencies outside of the very top can be thought of as being bounded by ${p}_{j} \leq  {n}^{-\gamma }$ for some constant $\gamma  > 0$ .

偏态分析。针对每个数据集，我们计算了按$j$递增而频率递减的经验频率${p}_{j}$。图2通过两种方式展示频率分布：左图绘制${\log }_{n}\left( {{p}_{j}n}\right)$与$j/d$的关系，其中$n$为向量数量，$d$为向量维度；右图采用标准化双对数坐标。可见所有数据集均呈现显著偏态。标准齐夫分布在此图中应呈线性（斜率对应分布指数），实际频率虽不符合齐夫分布，但接近"分段齐夫分布"。几乎所有数据集的高频部分之外，其频率均可视为受${p}_{j} \leq  {n}^{-\gamma }$约束（$\gamma  > 0$为常数）。

Approximate Independence. Our theoretical analysis is based on the assumption of independence between the set bits (i.e. the items) of the random vectors, and more specifically on the inequality:

近似独立性。我们的理论分析基于随机向量中置位（即数据项）相互独立的假设，更具体依赖于以下不等式：

$$
\mathop{\Pr }\limits_{{\mathbf{x} \in  S}}\left\lbrack  {{\forall }_{j \in  I}{\mathbf{x}}_{j} = 1}\right\rbrack   \leq  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{j \in  I}}{p}_{j},\text{ for }I \subseteq  \left\lbrack  d\right\rbrack  . \tag{2}
$$

The asymptotic analysis still applies if (2) holds only up to a constant factor. (Under some conditions we may even let this factor increase exponentially with $\left| I\right|$ .) To shed light on whether this assumption is realistic we considered random size-2 and size-3 subsets $I$ ,respectively,and computed the expected number of vectors $\mathbf{x}$ satisfying ${\forall }_{j \in  I}{\mathbf{x}}_{j} = 1$ under the independence assumption and in the data set, respectively. The ratio of these numbers is the constant factor needed,on average over all sets $I$ ,to make (2) hold.

只要(2)式在常数因子范围内成立，渐近分析依然适用（某些条件下该因子甚至可随$\left| I\right|$指数增长）。为验证该假设的合理性，我们分别考察大小为2和3的随机子集$I$，计算独立假设下与数据集中满足${\forall }_{j \in  I}{\mathbf{x}}_{j} = 1$的向量$\mathbf{x}$的期望数量之比，该比值即为使(2)式成立所需的平均常数因子。

The ratios are shown in Table 1. If the independence assumption were true, the ratios would be close to 1 . As can be seen all data sets have some kind of positive correlation between dimensions meaning that there are more pairs/triples than the independence model predicts. However, for most of the data sets it seems reasonable to assume that these correlations are weak enough that (2) holds up to a constant factor independent of $n$ ,at least for small constant $\left| I\right|$ . In those cases we expect our theoretical analysis based on independence to be indicative of actual running time.

各比率展示于表1中。若独立性假设成立，这些比率应接近1。可见所有数据集在维度间均存在某种正相关性，意味着实际出现的配对/三元组数量超出独立性模型的预测值。然而对于大多数数据集，这些相关性似乎足够微弱，使得(2)式在独立于$n$的常数因子范围内成立（至少对于较小的常数$\left| I\right|$）。在此情况下，我们基于独立性假设的理论分析应能反映实际运行时间。

## 9 CONCLUSION

## 9 结论

Several open problems remain about how to handle nearest neighbor search in skewed datasets.

关于如何处理偏态数据集中的最近邻搜索，仍存在若干待解决的问题。

One natural question is how to relax the assumption that all item probabilities ${p}_{i}$ are known to the algorithm beforehand. It seems likely that one can estimate each ${p}_{i}$ to very high precision by counting the occurrences in the dataset itself, leading to the same asymptotic bounds. We note that the set similarity join algorithm in [19] avoids using or estimating ${p}_{i}$ ,but we do not know how to analyze its performance in our setting.

一个自然的问题是：如何放宽算法需预先知晓所有项目概率${p}_{i}$的假设。通过统计数据集本身的出现频次，很可能能以极高精度估算每个${p}_{i}$，从而获得相同的渐近界。我们注意到文献[19]中的集合相似性连接算法避免了使用或估算${p}_{i}$，但尚不清楚如何在本研究框架下分析其性能。

The performance examples in this paper deal with distributions with two types of item: very rare and very common items. In practice, one more often encounters distributions with much more gradual skew, such as a Zipf distribution. Unfortunately, sets selected using a Zipf distribution have very small expected size, which trivializes the asymptotics. It would be interesting to find a class of distributions that accurately characterizes the skew of real data while remaining interesting for asymptotic analysis. Such a distribution would be an important use case for our algorithm.

本文中的性能示例涉及两种物品类型的分布：极稀有物品和极常见物品。实践中更常遇到的是倾斜度更为渐变的分布，例如Zipf分布。遗憾的是，基于Zipf分布选取的集合预期规模非常小，这使得渐近分析失去意义。若能找到一类既能准确刻画真实数据倾斜特征，又适用于渐近分析的分布，将具有重要意义。这类分布将成为我们算法的重要应用场景。

Finally, it would be interesting to relax the independence assumption. Our experiments gave some evidence that many of the datasets we studied have only mild dependencies, but for some this was not the case (particularly the Spotify dataset). In fact, the Spotify dataset has recently been observed to be a difficult case for a variant of the Chosen Path algorithm [19], possibly due to these correlations. It seems that if the correlations are "simple" and known ahead of time, there may be strategies to deal with them when sampling paths. Such an algorithm would loosen the independence assumption in our model, and has the potential to lead to much stronger performance in practice.

最后，放宽独立性假设将是一个有趣的方向。我们的实验表明，许多研究数据集仅存在微弱相关性，但部分数据集（尤其是Spotify数据集）并非如此。事实上，最近研究发现Spotify数据集对Chosen Path算法变体[19]构成挑战，可能正是源于这些相关性。若相关性"简单"且可预知，或许能在路径采样时采取应对策略。这类算法将放松我们模型中的独立性假设，有望在实践中实现更强劲的性能。
