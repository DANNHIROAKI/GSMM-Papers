## Abstract

## 摘要

We consider statistical estimations of a matrix product over the integers in a distributed setting, where we have two parties Alice and Bob; Alice holds a matrix $A$ and Bob holds a matrix $B$ ,and they want to estimate statistics of $A \cdot  B$ . We focus on the well-studied ${\ell }_{p}$ -norm,distinct elements $\left( {p = 0}\right) ,{\ell }_{0}$ -sampling,and heavy hitter problems. The goal is to minimize both the communication cost and the number of rounds of communication.

我们研究分布式环境下整数矩阵乘积的统计估计问题：参与者Alice持有矩阵$A$，Bob持有矩阵$B$，双方需协作估计$A \cdot  B$的统计特性。研究重点包括经典${\ell }_{p}$范数估计、相异元素$\left( {p = 0}\right) ,{\ell }_{0}$抽样及高频项识别问题，目标是最小化通信成本和通信轮次。

This problem is closely related to the fundamental set-intersection join problem in databases: when $p = 0$ the problem corresponds to the size of the set-intersection join. When $p = \infty$ the output is simply the pair of sets with the maximum intersection size. When $p = 1$ the problem corresponds to the size of the corresponding natural join. We also consider the heavy hitters problem which corresponds to finding the pairs of sets with intersection size above a certain threshold, and the problem of sampling an intersecting pair of sets uniformly at random.

该问题与数据库基础操作集合交集连接密切相关：当$p = 0$时对应集合交集连接的大小估计，$p = \infty$时输出具有最大交集规模的集合对，$p = 1$时则对应自然连接的大小计算。我们还研究高频项识别（寻找交集超过阈值的集合对）以及均匀随机抽样相交集合对的问题。

We study the problem of statistical estimations of a matrix product in the distributed setting. 我们研究分布式环境下矩阵乘积的统计估计问题。Alice和Bob分别持有矩阵$A \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$和$B \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$，通过消息交换协作计算定义在$A$和$B$上的函数$f$，目标是最小化总通信成本和交互轮次。

我们还研究了如下定义的近似频繁项问题。设

${\ell }_{p} - \left( {\phi ,\epsilon }\right)$-频繁项$\left( {0 < \epsilon  \leq  \phi  \leq  1}\right)$问题要求输出一个集合$S$，使得

$$
{\mathrm{{HH}}}_{\phi }^{p}\left( {AB}\right)  \subseteq  S \subseteq  {\mathrm{{HH}}}_{\phi  - \epsilon }^{p}\left( {AB}\right) .

As outputting the matrix product $C$ requires outputting ${n}^{2}$ numbers, it is natural to output the set $S$ as a sparse approximation of $C$ ; indeed this can be viewed as a matrix form of the well-studied compressed sensing problem.

由于输出矩阵乘积$C$需要输出${n}^{2}$个数字，自然地将集合$S$作为$C$的稀疏近似输出；这实际上可视为压缩感知问题的矩阵形式。

As mentioned, these basic statistical problems, being interesting for their own sake, have strong relationships to fundamental problems in databases. We describe such relationships more formally below.

如前所述，这些基础统计问题本身具有研究价值，同时与数据库中的基本问题存在紧密关联。下文将更正式地描述这些关系。

Despite a large amount of work on computing $p$ -norms and heavy hitters on frequency vectors in the streaming literature (see, e.g., [31] for a survey), we are not aware of any detailed study of these basic statistical functions on matrix products. The purpose of this paper is to introduce a systematic study of statistical estimations on matrix products.

尽管流计算领域已有大量关于频率向量$p$范数和频繁项计算的研究（例如综述[31]），但尚未发现针对矩阵乘积上这些基础统计函数的详细研究。本文旨在系统性地开展矩阵乘积统计估计的研究。

### 1.1 Motivation and Applications

### 1.1 研究动机与应用

Estimating the norm of a matrix product is closely related to two of the most important operations in relational databases - the composition and the natural join. Suppose we are given two relations $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{B}$ ,where $\mathcal{A}$ is defined over attributes(X,Y)and $B$ is defined over attributes(Y,Z). Assume for simplicity that $\operatorname{dom}\left( X\right)  = \operatorname{dom}\left( Y\right)  =$ $\operatorname{dom}\left( Z\right)  = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ . We thus have $\mathcal{A} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \times  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ and $\mathcal{B} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \times  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ . The composition of $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{B}$ is defined to be

矩阵乘积范数估计与关系数据库中两个最重要操作——复合运算和自然连接——密切相关。假设给定两个关系$\mathcal{A}$和$\mathcal{B}$，其中$\mathcal{A}$定义在属性(X,Y)上，$B$定义在属性(Y,Z)上。为简化起见，设$\operatorname{dom}\left( X\right)  = \operatorname{dom}\left( Y\right)  =$$\operatorname{dom}\left( Z\right)  = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$。因此有$\mathcal{A} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \times  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$和$\mathcal{B} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   \times  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$。$\mathcal{A}$与$\mathcal{B}$的复合运算定义为

$$
\mathcal{A} \circ  \mathcal{B} = \{ \left( {i,j}\right)  \mid  \exists k : \left( {i,k}\right)  \in  \mathcal{A} \land  \left( {k,j}\right)  \in  \mathcal{B}\} .
$$

The natural join is defined to be

自然连接定义为

$$
\mathcal{A} \bowtie  \mathcal{B} = \{ \left( {i,k,j}\right)  \mid  \left( {i,k}\right)  \in  \mathcal{A} \land  \left( {k,j}\right)  \in  \mathcal{B}\} .
$$

It is easy to see that the natural join corresponds to the composition together with the requirement that all the "witnesses" $k$ are output.

容易看出，自然连接对应于复合运算加上输出所有"见证"$k$的要求。

We further define "projection" sets ${A}_{i} = \{ k \mid  \left( {i,k}\right)  \in  \mathcal{A}\}$ for each $i \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,and ${B}_{j} = \{ k \mid  \left( {k,j}\right)  \in  \mathcal{B}\}$ for each $j \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ . Then we can rewrite the composition and natural joins as follows:

我们进一步为每个$i \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$定义"投影"集${A}_{i} = \{ k \mid  \left( {i,k}\right)  \in  \mathcal{A}\}$，为每个$j \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$定义${B}_{j} = \{ k \mid  \left( {k,j}\right)  \in  \mathcal{B}\}$。于是可将复合运算和自然连接改写如下：

$$
\mathcal{A} \circ  \mathcal{B} = \left\{  {\left( {i,j}\right)  \mid  {A}_{i} \cap  {B}_{j} \neq  \varnothing }\right\}  ,
$$

$$
\mathcal{A} \bowtie  \mathcal{B} = \left\{  {\left( {i,k,j}\right)  \mid  k \in  {A}_{i} \cap  {B}_{j}}\right\}  .
$$

We thus also refer to compositions as set-intersection joins, and natural joins as set-intersection joins with witnesses.

因此我们也称复合运算为集合交连接，称自然连接为带见证的集合交连接。

As an application of set-intersection joins, consider a job application scenario: we have $n$ applicants,with the $i$ -th applicant having a set of skills ${A}_{i}$ from the universe $\{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$ ,and $n$ jobs, with the $j$ -th job requiring a set of skills ${B}_{j}$ . Our goal is to find all the possible applicant-job matches,namely,those pairs(i,j)such that ${A}_{i} \cap  {B}_{j} \neq  \varnothing$ . One may also be interested in the number of such matches (the ${\ell }_{0}$ -norm) or the most qualified applicants (the entry realizing the ${\ell }_{\infty }$ -norm,or the heavy hitters).

作为集合交集连接的应用实例，考虑一个求职场景：我们有$n$位申请人，其中第$i$位申请人拥有来自全集$\{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$的技能集合${A}_{i}$；同时有$n$个职位，第$j$个职位需要技能集合${B}_{j}$。我们的目标是找出所有可能的申请人-职位匹配对，即满足${A}_{i} \cap  {B}_{j} \neq  \varnothing$条件的(i,j)组合。研究者可能还关注这类匹配的数量（即${\ell }_{0}$-范数）或最符合条件的申请人（实现${\ell }_{\infty }$-范数的条目，或称高频匹配项）。

We can further relate set-intersection joins to Boolean matrix multiplication. Let $A$ and $B$ be two $n \times  n$ matrices such that each row ${A}_{i, * }$ is the indicator vector of ${A}_{i}$ ,and each column ${B}_{*,j}$ is the indicator vector of ${B}_{j}$ . Then the non-zero entries of ${AB}$ exactly correspond to the outputs of the set-intersection joins on $\left\{  {{A}_{1},\ldots ,{A}_{n}}\right\}$ and $\left\{  {{B}_{1},\ldots ,{B}_{n}}\right\}$ . If we are interested in estimates to the sizes of the joins, which are very useful for guiding query optimization since they can be computed using much less communication than computing the actual joins, then we have

我们可以将集合交集连接与布尔矩阵乘法进一步关联。设$A$和$B$为两个$n \times  n$矩阵，其中每行${A}_{i, * }$是${A}_{i}$的指示向量，每列${B}_{*,j}$是${B}_{j}$的指示向量。那么${AB}$的非零项恰好对应$\left\{  {{A}_{1},\ldots ,{A}_{n}}\right\}$和$\left\{  {{B}_{1},\ldots ,{B}_{n}}\right\}$上集合交集连接的输出结果。若关注连接大小的估算值（这对查询优化极具指导意义，因其计算所需的通信量远低于实际连接操作），则可得到：

- $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{0} = \left| {\mathcal{A} \circ  \mathcal{B}}\right|$ ,that is,the ${\ell }_{0}$ -norm of ${AB}$ is the size of the composition of $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{B}$ ,

- $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{0} = \left| {\mathcal{A} \circ  \mathcal{B}}\right|$，即${AB}$的${\ell }_{0}$-范数等于$\mathcal{A}$与$\mathcal{B}$复合运算的结果大小

- $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{1} = \left| {\mathcal{A} \bowtie  \mathcal{B}}\right|$ ,that is,the ${\ell }_{1}$ -norm of ${AB}$ is the size of the natural join of $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{B}$ .

- $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{1} = \left| {\mathcal{A} \bowtie  \mathcal{B}}\right|$，即${AB}$的${\ell }_{1}$-范数等于$\mathcal{A}$与$\mathcal{B}$自然连接的结果大小

Finally, $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ corresponds to the pair(i,j)with the maximum overlap,and $\left\{  {\left( {i,j}\right)  \mid  {\left( AB\right) }_{i,j} \geq  \phi \parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{p}}\right\}$ for a threshold $\phi$ corresponds to the set of heavy hitters, i.e., those pairs of sets whose intersection size exceeds the threshold. These two problems have natural applications in inner product similarity joins on a set of vectors; we refer the reader to recent work [3] on inner product similarity joins and references therein.

最后，$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$对应具有最大交集量的(i,j)组合，而给定阈值$\phi$时的$\left\{  {\left( {i,j}\right)  \mid  {\left( AB\right) }_{i,j} \geq  \phi \parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{p}}\right\}$对应高频匹配项集合，即那些交集大小超过阈值的集合对。这两个问题在向量内积相似性连接中具有自然应用，读者可参阅近期关于内积相似性连接的研究[3]及其参考文献。

See Section 6 for more discussions.

注1. 需说明本文所有问题及结论均可直接推广至$\operatorname{dom}\left( X\right)  = {m}_{1},\operatorname{dom}\left( Z\right)  = {m}_{2}$和$\operatorname{dom}\left( Y\right)  = n$的广义情形，这对应于$A \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{{m}_{1} \times  n}$且$B \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  {m}_{2}}$时的${AB}$。更多讨论详见第6节。

### 1.2 Our Results

### 1.2 研究成果

For simplicity we use the notation $\widetilde{O}\left( \cdot \right)$ to hide poly $\left( {\log \frac{n}{\epsilon \delta }}\right)$ factors where $\epsilon$ is the multiplicative approximation ratio and $\delta$ is the error probability of a randomized communication algorithm. We say that $X$ approximates $Y$ within a factor of $\alpha$ if $X \in  \left\lbrack  {\frac{Y}{\beta },{\gamma Y}}\right\rbrack$ where $\beta ,\gamma  \geq  1$ and ${\beta \gamma } \leq  \alpha$ .

为简化表述，我们采用记号$\widetilde{O}\left( \cdot \right)$来隐藏多项式$\left( {\log \frac{n}{\epsilon \delta }}\right)$因子，其中$\epsilon$表示乘法近似比，$\delta$表示随机化通信算法的误差概率。当满足$X \in  \left\lbrack  {\frac{Y}{\beta },{\gamma Y}}\right\rbrack$时（其中$\beta ,\gamma  \geq  1$和${\beta \gamma } \leq  \alpha$），我们称$X$以$\alpha$因子近似$Y$。

Set-Intersection Join Size. We give a 2-round $\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\epsilon }\right)$ -bit algorithm that approximates $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{p},p \in  \left\lbrack  {0,2}\right\rbrack$ ,within a $\left( {1 + \epsilon }\right)$ factor. For the important case of $p = 0$ ,this provides a significant improvement over the previous $\widetilde{O}\left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ result in [16]. Also,due to the $\Omega \left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ lower bound in [16] for one-round algorithms (i.e., algorithms for which Alice sends a single message to Bob, who outputs the answer), this gives a separation in the complexity of this problem for one and two-round algorithms. As the algorithm in [16] is a direct application of an $\widetilde{O}\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ space streaming algorithm, our algorithm illustrates the power to go beyond streaming algorithms in this framework.

集合交连接大小。我们提出一种2轮$\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\epsilon }\right)$位算法，能以$\left( {1 + \epsilon }\right)$因子近似$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{p},p \in  \left\lbrack  {0,2}\right\rbrack$。对于$p = 0$这一重要情形，该算法较文献[16]中先前$\widetilde{O}\left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$结果有显著改进。此外，由于文献[16]针对单轮算法（即Alice向Bob发送单条消息后Bob输出结果的算法）给出了$\Omega \left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$下界，这揭示了一轮与两轮算法在该问题复杂度上的差异。鉴于[16]算法是$\widetilde{O}\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$空间流算法的直接应用，我们的算法证明了该框架下突破流算法限制的可能性。

Pair of Sets with Maximum Intersection Size. We first give a constant round $\widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{1.5}/\epsilon }\right)$ -bit algorithm that approximates $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ within a $\left( {2 + \epsilon }\right)$ factor. We complement our algorithm by showing a few different lower bounds that hold for algorithms with any (not necessarily constant) number of rounds. First, we show that any algorithm that approximates $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ within a factor of 2 needs $\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$ bits of communication,thus necessitating our $\left( {2 + \epsilon }\right)$ factor approximation. Moreover, we show that any algorithm achieving any constant factor approximation must use $\widetilde{\Omega }\left( {n}^{1.5}\right)$ bits of communication,which shows that our $\left( {2 + \epsilon }\right)$ factor approximation algorithm has optimal communication, up to polylogarithmic factors.

最大交集合对。我们首先给出常数轮$\widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{1.5}/\epsilon }\right)$位算法，能以$\left( {2 + \epsilon }\right)$因子近似$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$。通过证明适用于任意轮次（不限于常数轮）算法的若干下界，我们完善了该算法的理论支撑。首先证明：任何以2为近似因子的算法需要$\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$位通信量，这解释了为何必须采用$\left( {2 + \epsilon }\right)$因子近似。进一步证明：实现任意常数因子近似的算法必须使用$\widetilde{\Omega }\left( {n}^{1.5}\right)$位通信量，表明我们的$\left( {2 + \epsilon }\right)$因子近似算法在通信复杂度上达到多对数因子的最优性。

We next look at approximation algorithms that achieve approximation factors to $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ that are larger than constant. We show it is possible to achieve a $\kappa$ -approximation factor using $\widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$ bits of communication. We complement this with an $\Omega \left( {{n}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$ bit lower bound.

接下来研究实现超常数近似因子$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$的算法。我们证明利用$\widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$位通信可实现$\kappa$近似因子，并给出匹配的$\Omega \left( {{n}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$位下界。

Finally we show that the fact that the matrices $A$ and $B$ are binary is crucial. Namely, we first show that for general matrices $A$ and $B$ with poly(n)-bounded integer entries,there is an $\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$ lower bound for any constant factor approximation. For general approximation factors $\kappa$ that may be larger than constant,we show an upper and lower bound of $\widetilde{\Theta }\left( {{n}^{2}/{\kappa }^{2}}\right)$ communication. This shows an arguably surprising difference in approximation factor versus communication for binary and non-binary matrices.

最后证明矩阵$A$和$B$的二元性至关重要。首先表明：对于具有多项式有界整数项的一般矩阵$A$和$B$，任何常数因子近似都存在$\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$下界。针对可能超常数的一般近似因子$\kappa$，我们给出$\widetilde{\Theta }\left( {{n}^{2}/{\kappa }^{2}}\right)$通信量的上下界。这揭示了二元与非二元矩阵在近似因子与通信复杂度关系上的显著差异。

Heavy Hitters. We give an $O\left( 1\right)$ -round protocol that computes ${\ell }_{p} - \left( {\phi ,\epsilon }\right)$ -heavy-hitters, $0 < \epsilon  \leq  \phi  \leq  1$ ,and $p \in  (0,2\rbrack$ ,with various tradeoffs depending on whether Alice and Bob's matrices are arbitrary integer matrices, or whether they correspond to binary matrices. For arbitrary integer matrices,we achieve $\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{\sqrt{\phi }}{\epsilon }n}\right)$ bits of communication for $p = 1$ ,and $\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{\phi }{\epsilon }n}\right)$ bits of communication for every other $p \in  (0,2\rbrack  \smallsetminus  \{ 1\}$ .

高频项检测。我们提出一种$O\left( 1\right)$轮协议，用于计算${\ell }_{p} - \left( {\phi ,\epsilon }\right)$高频项、$0 < \epsilon  \leq  \phi  \leq  1$及$p \in  (0,2\rbrack$，其性能权衡取决于Alice和Bob的矩阵是任意整数矩阵还是二进制矩阵。对于任意整数矩阵，我们实现了$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{\sqrt{\phi }}{\epsilon }n}\right)$比特通信量用于$p = 1$，而对其他$p \in  (0,2\rbrack  \smallsetminus  \{ 1\}$则需$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{\phi }{\epsilon }n}\right)$比特通信量。

We are able to significantly improve these bounds for binary matrices, which as mentioned above, have important applications to database joins. Here we show for every $p \in  (0,2\rbrack$ an $O\left( 1\right)$ -round protocol with $\widetilde{O}\left( {n + \frac{\phi }{{\epsilon }^{2}}}\right)$ bits of communication.

针对二进制矩阵，我们能够显著提升这些界值——如前所述，这在数据库连接操作中具有重要应用。此处我们展示了对于每个$p \in  (0,2\rbrack$，采用$\widetilde{O}\left( {n + \frac{\phi }{{\epsilon }^{2}}}\right)$比特通信量的$O\left( 1\right)$轮协议。

### 1.3 Related Work

### 1.3 相关工作

Early work on studying joins in a distributed model can be found in [29] (Section 5) and [24]. Here the goal is to output the actual join rather than its size, and such algorithms, in the worst case, do not achieve communication better than the trivial algorithm in which Alice sends her entire input to Bob for a centralized computation.

早期关于分布式模型中连接操作的研究可参见[29]（第5节）和[24]。这些研究旨在输出实际连接结果而非其规模，且此类算法在最坏情况下无法超越朴素算法（即Alice将其全部输入发送给Bob进行集中式计算）的通信效率。

With the rise of the MapReduce-type models of computation, a number of works have been devoted to studying parallel and distributed computations of joins. Such works have looked at natural joins, multi-way joins, and similarity joins, in a model called the massively parallel computation model (MPC) [2, 9, 10, 17, 23, 25, 26]. Unlike our two-party communication model, in MPC there are multiple parties/machines, and the primary goal is to understand the round-load (maximum message size received by any server in any round) tradeoffs of the computation.

随着MapReduce类计算模型的兴起，大量研究致力于并行与分布式连接计算。这些工作在大规模并行计算模型（MPC）[2,9,10,17,23,25,26]中探讨了自然连接、多路连接及相似性连接。与我们的两方通信模型不同，MPC涉及多方/多机协作，主要目标是理解计算的轮次-负载（每轮中任意服务器接收的最大消息量）权衡关系。

In a recent paper [16] the authors and collaborators studied several join problems in the two-party communication model. The studied problems include set-intersection joins, set-disjointness joins, set-equality joins, and at-least-T joins. Our results can be viewed as a significant extension to the results in [16], as well as a systematic study of classical data stream problems in the context of matrix products. In particular,[16] did not study estimating the $p$ -norms of ${AB}$ ,for any $p$ other than $p = 0$ . For $p = 0$ ,they obtain an algorithm using $\widetilde{O}\left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ communication,which we significantly improve to $\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\epsilon }\right)$ communication,and extend to any $0 \leq  p \leq  2$ . Moreover,we obtain the first bounds for approximating $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ ,where perhaps surprisingly,we are able to obtain an $O\left( 1\right)$ -approximation in $\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{3/2}\right)$ communication,beating the naïve ${n}^{2}$ amount of communication. This leads us to the first algorithms for finding the frequent entries, or heavy hitters of ${AB}$ .

近期论文[16]中，作者与合作者研究了两方通信模型下的多个连接问题，包括集合交连接、集合不相交连接、集合等值连接及至少-T连接。我们的成果可视为对[16]结果的重大扩展，同时也是对矩阵乘积背景下经典数据流问题的系统研究。特别地，[16]未研究除$p = 0$外其他$p$范数下${AB}$的估计问题。对于$p = 0$，他们提出的算法需要$\widetilde{O}\left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$通信量，而我们将其显著优化至$\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\epsilon }\right)$通信量，并推广至任意$0 \leq  p \leq  2$。此外，我们首次给出了近似$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$的界值——令人惊讶的是，我们能在$\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{3/2}\right)$通信量下实现$O\left( 1\right)$近似，优于朴素的${n}^{2}$通信量。这引领我们提出了首个用于查找${AB}$高频项（或称重要元素）的算法。

While a number of recent works $\left\lbrack  {6,{11},{22},{27},{36}}\right\rbrack$ look at distributed linear algebra problems (for a survey, see [34]), in all papers that we are aware of,the matrix $C$ is distributed additively. What this means is that we want to estimate statistics of a matrix $C = A + B$ ,where $A$ and $B$ are held by Alice and Bob,respectively, who exchange messages with each other. In this paper, we instead study the setting for which we want to estimate statistics of a matrix $C = A \cdot  B$ ,where $A$ and $B$ are again held by Alice and Bob,respectively, who exchange messages with each other. Thus, in our setting the underlying matrix $C$ of interest is distributed multiplicatively. When $C$ is distributed additively,a common technique is for the players to agree on a random linear sketching matrix $S$ ,and apply it to their inputs to reduce their size. For example, if Alice has matrix $A$ and Bob has matrix $B$ ,then Alice can send $S \cdot  A$ to Bob,who can compute $S\left( {A + B}\right)$ . A natural extension of it in the multiplicative case is for Alice to send $S \cdot  A$ to Bob,who can compute $S \cdot  A \cdot  B$ . This is precisely how the algorithm for $p = 0$ of [16] proceeds. We show by using the product structure of $A \cdot  B$ and more than one round,it is possible to obtain significantly less expensive algorithms than this direct sketching approach.

尽管近期多项研究$\left\lbrack  {6,{11},{22},{27},{36}}\right\rbrack$关注分布式线性代数问题（综述参见[34]），但在我们已知的所有文献中，矩阵$C$均采用加法分布。这意味着我们需要估计矩阵$C = A + B$的统计量，其中$A$和$B$分别由Alice和Bob持有，双方通过消息交换进行协作。本文则研究另一种场景：在估计矩阵$C = A \cdot  B$统计量时，$A$与$B$仍分别由Alice和Bob持有并进行消息交互，此时目标矩阵$C$采用乘法分布。当$C$为加法分布时，常用技术是参与者协商随机线性素描矩阵$S$，并应用于输入数据以实现降维。例如若Alice持有$A$而Bob持有$B$，Alice可发送$S \cdot  A$给Bob以计算$S\left( {A + B}\right)$。乘法场景下的自然延伸是Alice发送$S \cdot  A$给Bob来计算$S \cdot  A \cdot  B$，这正是文献[16]中$p = 0$算法的实现方式。我们证明，通过利用$A \cdot  B$的乘积结构和多轮交互，可获得比直接素描法更高效的算法。

Finally, we would like to mention several papers considering similar problems but working in the centralized model. In [12], Cohen uses exponential random variables and applies a minimum operation to obtain an unbiased estimator of the number of nonzero entries in each column of a matrix product $C = {AB}$ . However, a direct adaptation of this algorithm to the distributed model would result $\widetilde{\Omega }\left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ bits of communication and 1-round,which is the same as using the 1-round ${\ell }_{0}$ -sketching protocol applied to each of the columns in earlier work [16]. In contrast we show that surprisingly,at least to the authors, $\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\epsilon }\right)$ bits of communication is possible with only 2 rounds. In [5], Amossen, Campagna, and Pagh improve the time complexity of [12],provided $\epsilon$ is not too small. However, a direct adaptation of this algorithm to the distributed model would result an even higher communication cost of $\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$ .

最后需指出，若干论文在集中式模型中研究了类似问题。文献[12]中，Cohen采用指数随机变量并通过最小化操作获得矩阵乘积$C = {AB}$各列非零项数量的无偏估计。但将该算法直接迁移至分布式模型将导致$\widetilde{\Omega }\left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$比特通信量和单轮交互，这与早期工作[16]中对每列应用单轮${\ell }_{0}$-素描协议的效果相同。而我们发现，至少对作者而言出乎意料的是，仅需2轮交互即可实现$\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\epsilon }\right)$比特通信量。文献[5]中，Amossen、Campagna和Pagh在$\epsilon$不太小的条件下改进了[12]的时间复杂度，但直接适配该算法将导致更高的$\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$通信开销。

In [13],the ${\ell }_{1}$ -sampling problem is considered. In this paper we do not emphasize estimation of $\parallel C{\parallel }_{1}$ ,since this quantity can be computed exactly using $O\left( {n\log n}\right)$ bits of communication,as stated in Remark 2. Similarly ${\ell }_{1}$ -sampling can also be done in $O\left( {n\log n}\right)$ bits of communication, as illustrated in Remark 3.

文献[13]研究了${\ell }_{1}$-采样问题。本文未重点探讨$\parallel C{\parallel }_{1}$的估计，因为如备注2所述，该量可通过$O\left( {n\log n}\right)$比特通信精确计算。同理，备注3表明${\ell }_{1}$-采样也可在$O\left( {n\log n}\right)$比特通信内完成。

In [32], it is shown how to apply CountSketch to the entries of a matrix product $C = {AB}$ where $A,B \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n \times  n}$ . The time complexity is $O\left( {\operatorname{nnz}\left( A\right)  + \operatorname{nnz}\left( B\right)  + n \cdot  k\log k}\right)$ ,where $\operatorname{nnz}\left( A\right)$ denotes the number of non-zero entries of $A$ ,and $k$ is the number of hash buckets in CountSketch which is at least $1/{\epsilon }^{2}$ . This outperforms the naïve time complexity of first computing $C$ and then hashing the entries of $C$ one-by-one. While interesting from a time complexity perspective, it does not provide an advantage over CountSketch in a distributed setting. Indeed,for each of the hashes on Alice’s side of the $n$ outer products computed in [32],the size of the hash is $\widetilde{\Theta }\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ , and consequently communicating this to Bob takes $\widetilde{\Theta }\left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$ bits in total.

在文献[32]中，展示了如何将CountSketch应用于矩阵乘积$C = {AB}$的各个元素，其中$A,B \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n \times  n}$。其时间复杂度为$O\left( {\operatorname{nnz}\left( A\right)  + \operatorname{nnz}\left( B\right)  + n \cdot  k\log k}\right)$，此处$\operatorname{nnz}\left( A\right)$表示$A$的非零元素数量，$k$是CountSketch中哈希桶的数量（至少为$1/{\epsilon }^{2}$）。这优于先计算$C$再逐项哈希处理的朴素方法时间复杂度。虽然从时间复杂度角度看颇具意义，但在分布式环境下相比CountSketch并无优势。实际上，对于[32]中计算的$n$个外积在Alice侧的每个哈希操作，其哈希规模为$\widetilde{\Theta }\left( {1/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$，因此向Bob传输这些数据总共需要$\widetilde{\Theta }\left( {n/{\epsilon }^{2}}\right)$比特。

Sketches. A sketch ${sk}\left( x\right)$ of a data object $x$ is a summary of $x$ of small size (sublinear or even polylogarithmic in the size of $x$ ) such that if we want to perform a query (denoted by a function $f$ ) on the original data object $x$ ,we can instead apply another function $g$ on ${sk}\left( x\right)$ such that $g\left( {{sk}\left( x\right) }\right)  \approx  f\left( x\right)$ . Sketches are very useful tools in the development of space-efficient streaming algorithms and communication-efficient distributed algorithms. Many sketching algorithms have been developed in the data stream literature. In this paper we will make use of the following.

素描。数据对象$x$的素描${sk}\left( x\right)$是其小型摘要（规模为亚线性甚至多对数级别），当需要对原始数据对象$x$执行查询（由函数$f$表示）时，可改为对${sk}\left( x\right)$应用另一函数$g$，使得$g\left( {{sk}\left( x\right) }\right)  \approx  f\left( x\right)$。素描技术在开发空间高效的流算法和通信高效的分布式算法中极具价值。数据流领域已发展出多种素描算法，本文将使用以下方法。

LEMMA 2.1 ([19,21], ${\ell }_{p} -$ SKETCH $\left( {0 \leq  p \leq  2}\right)$ ). For $p \in  \left\lbrack  {0,2}\right\rbrack$ and a data vector $x \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ ,there is a sketch $\operatorname{sk}\left( x\right)  = {Sx}$ where $S \in$ ${\mathbb{R}}^{O\left( {\frac{1}{{\epsilon }^{2}}\log \frac{1}{\delta }}\right)  \times  n}$ is a random sketching matrix,and a function $g$ such that with probability $1 - \delta ,g\left( {{sk}\left( x\right) }\right)$ approximates $\parallel x{\parallel }_{p}$ within a factor of $\left( {1 + \epsilon }\right)$ .

Communication Complexity. We will use two-party communication complexity to prove lower bounds for the problems we study. In the two-party communication complexity model, there are parties Alice and Bob. Alice gets an input $x \in  \mathcal{X}$ ,and Bob gets an input $y \in  \mathcal{Y}$ . They want to jointly compute a function $f : \mathcal{X} \times  \mathcal{Y} \rightarrow  \mathcal{Z}$ via a communication protocol. Let $\Pi$ be a (randomized) communication protocol,and let ${r}_{A},{r}_{B}$ be the private randomness used by Alice and Bob,respectively. Let ${\Pi }_{X,Y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}$ denote the transcript (the concatenation of all messages) when Alice and Bob run $\Pi$ on input (X,Y)using private randomness $\left( {{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}\right)$ ,and let $\Pi \left( {X,Y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}\right)$ denote the output of the protocol. We say $\Pi$ errs with probability $\delta$ if for all $\left( {x,y}\right)  \in  \mathcal{X} \times  \mathcal{Y}$ ,

通信复杂性。我们将采用两方通信复杂性理论来证明所研究问题的下界。在该模型中，参与者Alice和Bob分别获得输入$x \in  \mathcal{X}$和$y \in  \mathcal{Y}$，通过通信协议共同计算函数$f : \mathcal{X} \times  \mathcal{Y} \rightarrow  \mathcal{Z}$。设$\Pi$为（随机化）通信协议，${r}_{A},{r}_{B}$表示双方各自使用的私有随机数。${\Pi }_{X,Y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}$代表协议$\Pi$在输入(X,Y)和随机数$\left( {{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}\right)$运行时的通信记录（所有消息的串联），$\Pi \left( {X,Y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}\right)$为协议输出。若对所有$\left( {x,y}\right)  \in  \mathcal{X} \times  \mathcal{Y}$满足误差概率$\delta$，则称$\Pi$存在误差。

$$
\mathop{\Pr }\limits_{{{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}}\left\lbrack  {{\Pi }_{X,Y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}} \neq  f\left( {x,y}\right) }\right\rbrack   \leq  \delta \text{.}
$$

We define the randomized communication complexity of $f$ ,denoted by ${R}_{\delta }\left( f\right)$ ,to be $\mathop{\min }\limits_{\Pi }\mathop{\max }\limits_{{x,y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}}\left| {\Pi }_{X,Y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}\right|$ ,where $\left| z\right|$ denotes the length of the transcript $z$ .

我们将$f$的随机化通信复杂性记作${R}_{\delta }\left( f\right)$，其定义为$\mathop{\min }\limits_{\Pi }\mathop{\max }\limits_{{x,y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}}\left| {\Pi }_{X,Y,{r}_{A},{r}_{B}}\right|$，其中$\left| z\right|$表示通信记录$z$的长度。

We next introduce a concept called the distributional communication complexity. Let $\mu$ be a distribution over the inputs(X,Y). We say a deterministic protocol $\Pi$ computes $f$ with error probability $\delta$ on $\mu$ if

接下来引入分布通信复杂性概念。设$\mu$为输入(X,Y)上的概率分布，若确定性协议$\Pi$在$\mu$上计算$f$的误差概率为$\delta$，则称：

$$
\mathop{\Pr }\limits_{{\left( {X,Y}\right)  \sim  \mu }}\left\lbrack  {{\Pi }_{X,Y} \neq  f\left( {x,y}\right) }\right\rbrack   \leq  \delta .
$$

The $\delta$ -error distributional communication complexity under input distribution $\mu$ ,denoted by ${D}_{\delta }^{\mu }\left( f\right)$ ,is the minimum communication complexity of a deterministic protocol that computes $f$ with error probability $\delta$ on $\mu$ . The following lemma connects distributional communication complexity with randomized communication complexity.

在输入分布$\mu$下，$\delta$误差的分布通信复杂性记作${D}_{\delta }^{\mu }\left( f\right)$，即所有能在$\mu$上以$\delta$误差计算$f$的确定性协议的最小通信复杂度。下述引理揭示了分布通信复杂性与随机化通信复杂性的关系。

LEMMA 2.2 (YAO’s LEMMA). For any function $f$ and any $\delta  > 0$ , ${R}_{\delta }\left( f\right)  \geq  \mathop{\max }\limits_{\mu }{D}_{\delta }^{\mu }\left( f\right) .$

引理2.2（姚氏引理）。对于任意函数$f$和任意$\delta  > 0$，${R}_{\delta }\left( f\right)  \geq  \mathop{\max }\limits_{\mu }{D}_{\delta }^{\mu }\left( f\right) .$

A standard method to obtain randomized communication complexity lower bounds is to first find a hard input distribution $\mu$ for a function $f$ ,and then try to obtain a lower bound on the distributional communication complexity of $f$ under inputs $\left( {X,Y}\right)  \sim  \mu$ . By Yao's Lemma, this is also a lower bound on the randomized communication complexity of $f$ .

获取随机化通信复杂性下界的标准方法是：先为函数$f$寻找困难输入分布$\mu$，然后推导$f$在$\left( {X,Y}\right)  \sim  \mu$分布下的分布通信复杂性下界。根据姚氏引理，该下界同时也是$f$随机化通信复杂性的下界。

Gap- ${l}_{\infty }$ . The main change we make to Algorithm 2 is that we add a universe sampling step at the beginning. More precisely, we sample each column of $A$ with probability $q = \min \{ \alpha /\kappa ,1\}$ where $\alpha  =$ ${10}^{4}\log n$ ,and then replace all non-sampled columns in $A$ with all-0 vectors,obtaining a new matrix ${A}^{\prime }$ . Let $D = {A}^{\prime }B$ . Recall that $C = {AB}$ . We compute $\parallel C{\parallel }_{1}$ and $\parallel D{\parallel }_{1}$ .

对算法2的主要改进是在初始阶段增加全域采样步骤。具体而言，以$q = \min \{ \alpha /\kappa ,1\}$概率采样$A$的每列（其中$\alpha  =$${10}^{4}\log n$），随后将所有未采样列替换为全零向量，得到新矩阵${A}^{\prime }$。设$D = {A}^{\prime }B$。回顾$C = {AB}$，我们计算$\parallel C{\parallel }_{1}$和$\parallel D{\parallel }_{1}$。

With this new universe sampling step it is possible to have $\parallel D{\parallel }_{1} = 0$ . If this happens then we also check $\parallel C{\parallel }_{1}$ . If $\parallel C{\parallel }_{1} = 0$ then we simply output 0 ; otherwise we output 1 . If $\parallel D{\parallel }_{1} > 0$ ,then we follow Algorithm 2 to do further sampling on ${A}^{\prime }$ ,obtaining ${A}^{1},{A}^{2},\ldots$ Let ${C}^{\ell } = {A}^{\ell }B$ for $\ell  = 1,2,\ldots$ We again stop at the first level ${\ell }^{ * }$ for which ${\begin{Vmatrix}{C}^{{\ell }^{ * }}\end{Vmatrix}}_{1} \leq  \alpha {n}^{2}/\kappa$ ,and then exchange for each (surviving) universe item $j$ the indices of sets that contain $j$ , in exactly the same way as that in Algorithm 2.

该算法如算法3所示，我们得到以下定理。

THEOREM 4.3. 定理4.3. 对于任意$\kappa  \in  \left\lbrack  {4,n}\right\rbrack$，算法3能以0.9概率在$\widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$比特通信量和$O\left( 1\right)$轮次内，将两个布尔矩阵$A,B \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$的$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$近似至$\kappa$因子。

Correctness. For simplicity we assume that $\alpha /\kappa  \leq  1$ (and thus $q = \alpha /\kappa$ ),since otherwise $D = C$ and the arguments will follow those in Algorithm 2.

正确性分析。为简化起见，假设$\alpha /\kappa  \leq  1$（因而$q = \alpha /\kappa$），否则$D = C$时论证过程与算法2相同。

We define two events, and will show that each holds with probability $1 - 1/{n}^{4}$ .

定义两个事件，并证明每个事件以$1 - 1/{n}^{4}$概率成立。

${\mathcal{E}}_{4}$ : For all pairs(i,j),if ${D}_{i,j} \geq  \frac{1}{8}\alpha$ ,then ${D}_{i,j}/q$ approximates ${C}_{i,j}$ within a factor of 2 .

${\mathcal{E}}_{4}$：对所有(i,j)对，若${D}_{i,j} \geq  \frac{1}{8}\alpha$成立，则${D}_{i,j}/q$能在2倍因子内近似${C}_{i,j}$。

${\mathcal{E}}_{5}$：对于所有(i,j)对，若${D}_{i,j} < \frac{1}{8}\alpha$，则${C}_{i,j} < \frac{1}{4}\alpha /q$。

In the case that $\parallel D{\parallel }_{\infty } = 0$ ,by ${\mathcal{E}}_{5}$ we know that all entries in $C$ are less than $\kappa /4$ . 当$\parallel D{\parallel }_{\infty } = 0$时，根据${\mathcal{E}}_{5}$可知$C$中所有元素均小于$\kappa /4$。此时可检测是否满足$\parallel C{\parallel }_{1} > 0$：若成立则输出1（该结果已在$\kappa$倍误差范围内近似于$\parallel C{\parallel }_{\infty }$）；否则判定$C$为零矩阵并输出0。

${\mathcal{E}}_{4}$与${\mathcal{E}}_{5}$各自以$1 - 1/{n}^{4}$概率成立的证明过程，与引理4.2中${\mathcal{E}}_{2}$和${\mathcal{E}}_{3}$的证明方法类似。

Complexity. - If $\min \left\{  {{u}_{j},{v}_{j}}\right\}   \leq  \sqrt{n}$ ,then since the universe size is $\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\kappa }\right)$ , the total communication is upper bounded by $\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\kappa }\right)  \cdot  \sqrt{n} =$ $\widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$ .

- 若$\min \left\{  {{u}_{j},{v}_{j}}\right\}   \leq  \sqrt{n}$，由于全域大小为$\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\kappa }\right)$，总通信量上限为$\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\kappa }\right)  \cdot  \sqrt{n} =$$\widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$。

- If $\min \left\{  {{u}_{j},{v}_{j}}\right\}   > \sqrt{n}$ ,then since ${\begin{Vmatrix}{C}^{{\ell }^{ * }}\end{Vmatrix}}_{1} = \widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{2}/\kappa }\right)$ ,the total communication is upper bounded by

- 若$\min \left\{  {{u}_{j},{v}_{j}}\right\}   > \sqrt{n}$，因${\begin{Vmatrix}{C}^{{\ell }^{ * }}\end{Vmatrix}}_{1} = \widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{2}/\kappa }\right)$，总通信量上限为

${\begin{Vmatrix}{C}^{{\ell }^{ * }}\end{Vmatrix}}_{1}/\sqrt{n} = \widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right) .$

Therefore the total communication is bounded by $\widetilde{O}\left( {{n}^{2}/\kappa }\right)$ . The number of rounds is clearly bounded by $O\left( 1\right)$ .

### 4.2 Lower Bounds for Binary Matrices

### 4.2 二元矩阵下界

In this section we show that our algorithms for ${\ell }_{\infty }$ -norm estimation in Section 4.1 are almost tight in the sense that (1) $\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$ bits of communication is needed if we want to go beyond a $2 + \epsilon$ approximation,and (2) for any approximation $\kappa$ we need to use $\Omega \left( {{n}^{\frac{3}{2}}/\kappa }\right)$ bits of communication.

本节将证明，我们在4.1节提出的${\ell }_{\infty }$范数估计算法在以下意义下近乎最优：(1) 若要突破$2 + \epsilon$近似精度，需要$\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$比特通信量；(2) 对于任意$\kappa$近似精度，都需要消耗$\Omega \left( {{n}^{\frac{3}{2}}/\kappa }\right)$比特通信量。

#### 4.2.1 A Lower Bound for 2-Approximation.

#### 4.2.1 二倍近似的下界

THEOREM 4.4. Any algorithm that approximates $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ for two Boolean matrices $A,B \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$ within a factor of 2 with probability0.51needs $\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$ bits of communication,even if we allow an unbounded number of communication rounds.

定理4.4. 对于两个布尔矩阵$A,B \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$的$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$近似计算，任何能以0.51概率达到2倍近似精度的算法，即使允许无限轮次通信，仍需$\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$比特通信量。

Proof. We perform a reduction from the two-player set-disjointness (see Section 2) on strings of length ${\left( n/2\right) }^{2} = {n}^{2}/4$ ,where Alice has $x$ and Bob has $y$ . Alice creates an $n/2 \times  n/2$ matrix ${A}^{\prime }$ indexed by the coordinates in $x$ ,that is,the $i$ -th $\left( {i = 1,\ldots ,n/2}\right)$ row of ${A}^{\prime }$ consists of the $\left( {\left( {i - 1}\right) \frac{n}{2} + 1}\right)$ -th, $\ldots ,\frac{in}{2}$ -th coordinates of $x$ . Similarly,Bob creates an $n/2 \times  n/2$ matrix ${B}^{\prime }$ indexed by the coordinates in $y$ . Next,Alice creates an $n \times  n$ input matrix

证明. 我们通过长度为${\left( n/2\right) }^{2} = {n}^{2}/4$的字符串上的双玩家集合非交性问题（参见第2节）进行规约，其中Alice持有$x$，Bob持有$y$。Alice构建一个由$x$坐标索引的$n/2 \times  n/2$矩阵${A}^{\prime }$，即${A}^{\prime }$的第$i$个$\left( {i = 1,\ldots ,n/2}\right)$行由$x$的第$\left( {\left( {i - 1}\right) \frac{n}{2} + 1}\right)$、$\ldots ,\frac{in}{2}$个坐标组成。类似地，Bob构建由$y$坐标索引的$n/2 \times  n/2$矩阵${B}^{\prime }$。随后，Alice创建$n \times  n$输入矩阵

$$
A = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} {A}^{\prime } & I \\  \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{0} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack  
$$

where $I$ is an $n/2 \times  n/2$ identity matrix,and 0 is an $n/2 \times  n/2$ all-0 matrix. Bob creates an $n \times  n$ input matrix

其中$I$是$n/2 \times  n/2$阶单位矩阵，0是$n/2 \times  n/2$阶全零矩阵。Bob构建$n \times  n$输入矩阵

$$
B = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} I & \mathbf{0} \\  {B}^{\prime } & \mathbf{0} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack  .
$$

Note that $A$ and $B$ are both binary matrices,as needed for the reduction to the $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ problem.

注意$A$和$B$均为二元矩阵，这符合规约到$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$问题的要求。

The key is to observe that

关键在于观察到

$$
A \cdot  B = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} {A}^{\prime } + {B}^{\prime } & \mathbf{0} \\  \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{0} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack  . \tag{7}
$$

We thus have $\parallel A \cdot  B{\parallel }_{\infty } = {\begin{Vmatrix}{A}^{\prime } + {B}^{\prime }\end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty }$ ,which is 2 if $x \cap  y \neq  \varnothing$ ,and 1 otherwise. The claimed lower bound for approximating $\parallel C{\parallel }_{\infty }$ within a factor of 2 follows from the $\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$ lower bounds for two-player set-disjointness on strings of length $\Theta \left( {n}^{2}\right)$ for success probability 0.51 (Lemma 2.3).

由此得到$\parallel A \cdot  B{\parallel }_{\infty } = {\begin{Vmatrix}{A}^{\prime } + {B}^{\prime }\end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty }$，当$x \cap  y \neq  \varnothing$时为2，否则为1。根据长度为$\Theta \left( {n}^{2}\right)$的字符串上双玩家集合非交性问题在成功概率0.51时的$\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$下界（引理2.3），可推出2倍近似$\parallel C{\parallel }_{\infty }$的下界。

#### 4.2.2 A Lower Bound for General $\kappa$ -Approximation.

#### 4.2.2 通用$\kappa$近似的下界

THEOREM 4.5. 定理4.5. 对于任意$\kappa  \in  \left\lbrack  {1,n}\right\rbrack$，任何随机算法若要为两个布尔矩阵$A,B \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$的$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{\infty }$提供$\kappa$倍近似且成功概率达0.52，即使允许无限轮次通信，仍需$\widetilde{\Omega }\left( {{n}^{\frac{3}{2}}/\kappa }\right)$比特通信量。

Set $\beta  = \sqrt{{50}\log n/n}$ ,and set $k = 1/\left( {{4\kappa }{\beta }^{2}}\right)$ where $\kappa$ is the approximation ratio.

${\mu }_{1} :$ Set $\left( {X,Y}\right)  = \left( {0,0}\right)$ with probability $1/2$ ,and $\left( {X,Y}\right)  = \left( {1,1}\right)$ with probability $1/2$ .

${\mu }_{1} :$以$1/2$概率设定$\left( {X,Y}\right)  = \left( {0,0}\right)$，并以$1/2$概率设定$\left( {X,Y}\right)  = \left( {1,1}\right)$。

集合不交(DISJ)问题。回顾第2节介绍的集合不交问题，其中Alice持有$x = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right)  \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{k}$，Bob持有$y = \left( {{y}_{1},\ldots ,{y}_{k}}\right)  \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{k}$，他们需要计算$\operatorname{DISJ}\left( {x,y}\right)  = { \vee  }_{i = 1}^{k}\operatorname{AND}\left( {{x}_{i},{y}_{i}}\right)$。

Let $X = \left( {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{k}}\right)$ be Alice’s input,and $Y = \left( {{Y}_{1},\ldots ,{Y}_{k}}\right)$ be Bob’s input. We again define two input distributions for(X,Y).

设$X = \left( {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{k}}\right)$为Alice的输入，$Y = \left( {{Y}_{1},\ldots ,{Y}_{k}}\right)$为Bob的输入。我们再次为(X,Y)定义两种输入分布。

${v}_{k} : \operatorname{Set}\left( {{X}_{i},{Y}_{i}}\right)  \sim  {v}_{1}$ for each $i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ .

${v}_{k} : \operatorname{Set}\left( {{X}_{i},{Y}_{i}}\right)  \sim  {v}_{1}$对于每个$i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$。

### 5.2 Binary Matrices

### 5.2 二进制矩阵

In this section we show that we can do better for binary matrices by employing the idea we use for ${\ell }_{\infty }$ -norm estimation. Again Alice holds $A \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$ and Bob holds $B \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$ ,and let $C = {AB}$ . Due to the similarity of the approach compared with the ${\ell }_{\infty }$ -norm case (Section 4.1), we do not repeat some of the details.

本节将证明，通过采用${\ell }_{\infty }$范数估计的思路，我们可以在二进制矩阵上取得更好的效果。Alice持有$A \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$，Bob持有$B \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{n \times  n}$，令$C = {AB}$。由于该方法与${\ell }_{\infty }$范数情形（第4.1节）的相似性，部分细节不再赘述。

We first assume that $\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{p}^{p} \geq  {100\phi }\log n/{\epsilon }^{2}$ ,and will consider the other case later. The algorithm is as follows.

我们首先假设$\parallel {AB}{\parallel }_{p}^{p} \geq  {100\phi }\log n/{\epsilon }^{2}$，其他情形将在后续讨论。算法步骤如下。

Step 1: Alice and Bob first estimate ${L}_{p} = \parallel C{\parallel }_{p}$ within a factor of 2,denoted by ${L}_{p}^{\prime }$ .

步骤1：Alice和Bob首先以2倍精度估计${L}_{p} = \parallel C{\parallel }_{p}$，记为${L}_{p}^{\prime }$。

Step 2: Alice samples each column of $A$ with probability $\beta  =$ $\min \left\{  {\frac{\alpha }{{\phi }^{1/p}{L}_{p}^{\prime }},1}\right\}$ for $\alpha  = {\left( {10}^{4}\log n\right) }^{1/p}$ ,obtaining ${A}^{\prime }$ . Let ${C}^{\prime } = {A}^{\prime }B$ . 步骤2：Alice以概率$\beta  =$$\min \left\{  {\frac{\alpha }{{\phi }^{1/p}{L}_{p}^{\prime }},1}\right\}$对$A$的每列进行采样（其中$\alpha  = {\left( {10}^{4}\log n\right) }^{1/p}$），得到${A}^{\prime }$。令${C}^{\prime } = {A}^{\prime }B$。随后Alice和Bob按照算法2的步骤7-12交换包含$j$的集合索引（针对每个保留项$j \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$），最终得到满足${C}^{\prime } = {C}_{A} + {C}_{B}$的${C}_{A}$和${C}_{B}$。

Step 3: Alice and Bob try to verify for each non-zero entry in ${C}_{A}$ or ${C}_{B}$ whether it is indeed a heavy hitter. Let ${S}_{A},{S}_{B}$ consist of all the entries(i,j)in ${C}_{A},{C}_{B}$ for which ${\left( {C}_{A}\right) }_{i,j}^{p} \geq  {\beta }^{p}\phi {\left( {L}_{p}^{\prime }\right) }^{p}/{20}$ or ${\left( {C}_{B}\right) }_{i,j}^{p} \geq  {\beta }^{p}\phi {\left( {L}_{p}^{\prime }\right) }^{p}/{20}$ ,respectively. Then for each entry $\left( {i,j}\right)  \in$ ${S}_{A} \cup  {S}_{B}$ ,Alice and Bob try to estimate ${C}_{i,j}$ within a $\left( {1 + \epsilon /\left( {2\phi }\right) }\right)$ factor by sampling $\widetilde{O}\left( {1/{\left( \epsilon /\phi \right) }^{2}}\right)$ coordinates of their correponding row and column in $A$ and $B$ .

步骤3：对于${C}_{A}$或${C}_{B}$中的每个非零项，Alice和Bob尝试验证其是否为真正的heavy hitter。令${S}_{A},{S}_{B}$包含${C}_{A},{C}_{B}$中所有满足${\left( {C}_{A}\right) }_{i,j}^{p} \geq  {\beta }^{p}\phi {\left( {L}_{p}^{\prime }\right) }^{p}/{20}$或${\left( {C}_{B}\right) }_{i,j}^{p} \geq  {\beta }^{p}\phi {\left( {L}_{p}^{\prime }\right) }^{p}/{20}$的条目(i,j)。随后对于每个条目$\left( {i,j}\right)  \in$${S}_{A} \cup  {S}_{B}$，双方通过采样$A$和$B$中对应行列的$\widetilde{O}\left( {1/{\left( \epsilon /\phi \right) }^{2}}\right)$个坐标，以$\left( {1 + \epsilon /\left( {2\phi }\right) }\right)$倍精度估计${C}_{i,j}$。

根据切尔诺夫界(Chernoff bounds)，采样后以概率$\left( {1 - 1/{n}^{10}}\right)$满足：(1) $A$的采样列数（即存活的全局项数量）不超过$\widetilde{O}\left( {\beta n}\right)$；(2) ${\begin{Vmatrix}{C}^{\prime }\end{Vmatrix}}_{1} = \widetilde{O}\left( {\beta {L}_{1}}\right)$。

## 6 CONCLUDING REMARKS

## 六、结论与展望

In this paper we studied a set of basic statistical estimation problems of matrix products in the distributed model,including the ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms, distinct elements, ${\ell }_{0}$ -sampling and heavy hitters. These problems have a number of applications in database joins.

本文研究了分布式模型中矩阵乘积的基本统计估计问题，包括${\ell }_{p}$范数、相异元素、${\ell }_{0}$采样及重击项检测。这些问题在数据库连接操作中有广泛应用。

We would like to mention again that our algorithms for square matrices can be straightforwardly modified to handle rectangular matrices where $A \in  {\sum }^{m \times  n}\left( {m \geq  n}\right)$ and $B \in  {\sum }^{n \times  m}$ . We briefly list here how our main upper bounds look like on rectangular matrices. All the algorithms remain the same (we of course have to change some occurrences of $n$ to $m$ in several places).

我们需再次指出，针对方阵设计的算法可直接修改以处理矩形矩阵，其中$A \in  {\sum }^{m \times  n}\left( {m \geq  n}\right)$且$B \in  {\sum }^{n \times  m}$。此处简要列出主要上界在矩形矩阵上的表现形式。所有算法保持不变（仅需将部分代码中的$n$替换为$m$）。

- The communication cost for $\left( {1 + \epsilon }\right)$ -approximating ${\ell }_{p}(p \in$ $\left\lbrack  {0,2}\right\rbrack  )$ with $\sum  = \mathbb{Z}$ remains $\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\epsilon }\right)$ .

- $\left( {1 + \epsilon }\right)$近似${\ell }_{p}(p \in$$\left\lbrack  {0,2}\right\rbrack  )$（使用$\sum  = \mathbb{Z}$）的通信成本仍为$\widetilde{O}\left( {n/\epsilon }\right)$。

- The communication cost for $\left( {2 + \epsilon }\right)$ -approximating ${\ell }_{\infty }$ with $\sum  = \{ 0,1\}$ becomes $\widetilde{O}\left( {m}^{1.5}\right)$ ,and that for $\kappa$ -approximating ${\ell }_{\infty }$ with $\sum  = \{ 0,1\}$ becomes $\widetilde{O}\left( {{m}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$

- $\left( {2 + \epsilon }\right)$近似${\ell }_{\infty }$（使用$\sum  = \{ 0,1\}$）的通信成本变为$\widetilde{O}\left( {m}^{1.5}\right)$，而$\kappa$近似${\ell }_{\infty }$（使用$\sum  = \{ 0,1\}$）的成本变为$\widetilde{O}\left( {{m}^{1.5}/\kappa }\right)$

- The communication cost for ${\ell }_{p} - \left( {\phi ,\epsilon }\right)$ -heavy-hitters with $\sum  = \mathbb{Z}$ remains $\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{\phi }{\epsilon }n}\right)$ ,and that for ${\ell }_{p} - \left( {\phi ,\epsilon }\right)$ -heavy-hitters with $\sum  = \{ 0,1\}$ remains $\widetilde{O}\left( {n + \frac{\phi }{{\epsilon }^{2}}}\right)$ .

- ${\ell }_{p} - \left( {\phi ,\epsilon }\right)$密集项检测（使用$\sum  = \mathbb{Z}$）的通信成本保持$\widetilde{O}\left( {\frac{\phi }{\epsilon }n}\right)$，而${\ell }_{p} - \left( {\phi ,\epsilon }\right)$密集项检测（使用$\sum  = \{ 0,1\}$）的成本保持$\widetilde{O}\left( {n + \frac{\phi }{{\epsilon }^{2}}}\right)$。
