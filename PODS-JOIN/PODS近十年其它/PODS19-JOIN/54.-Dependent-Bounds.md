## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

In this paper, we prove topology dependent bounds on the number of rounds needed to compute Functional Aggregate Queries (FAQs) studied by Abo Khamis et al. [PODS 2016] in a synchronous distributed network under the model considered by Chattopadhyay et al. [FOCS 2014, SODA 2017]. Unlike the recent work on computing database queries in the Massively Parallel Computation model, in the model of Chat-topadhyay et al., nodes can communicate only via private point-to-point channels and we are interested in bounds that work over an arbitrary communication topology. This model, which is closer to the well-studied CONGEST model in distributed computing and generalizes Yao's two party communication complexity model, has so far only been studied for problems that are common in the two-party communication complexity literature.

本文证明了在Chattopadhyay等人[FOCS 2014, SODA 2017]提出的同步分布式网络模型下，计算Abo Khamis等人[PODS 2016]研究的函数聚合查询(FAQs)所需轮次的拓扑依赖界。与近期大规模并行计算模型中数据库查询的研究不同，在Chattopadhyay等人的模型中，节点仅能通过私有点对点通道通信，我们关注的是适用于任意通信拓扑的界。该模型更接近分布式计算中广泛研究的CONGEST模型，并推广了Yao的两方通信复杂度模型，此前仅针对两方通信复杂度文献中的常见问题进行研究。

This is the first work to consider more practically motivated problems in this distributed model. For the sake of exposition, we focus on two specific problems in this paper: Boolean Conjunctive Query (BCQ) and computing variable/factor marginals in Probabilistic Graphical Models (PGMs). We obtain tight bounds on the number of rounds needed to compute such queries as long as the underlying hy-pergraph of the query is $O\left( 1\right)$ -degenerate and has $O\left( 1\right)$ -arity. In particular,the $O\left( 1\right)$ -degeneracy condition covers most well-studied queries that are efficiently computable in the centralized computation model like queries with constant treewidth. These tight bounds depend on a new notion of 'width' (namely internal-node-width) for Generalized Hyper-tree Decompositions (GHDs) of acyclic hypergraphs, which minimizes the number of internal nodes in a sub-class of GHDs. To the best of our knowledge, this width has not been studied explicitly in the theoretical database literature. Finally, we consider the problem of computing the product of a vector with a chain of matrices and prove tight bounds on its round complexity (over a finite field of two elements) using a novel min-entropy based argument.

这是首个在该分布式模型中研究实际应用问题的成果。为阐述清晰，本文重点研究两个具体问题：布尔连接查询(BCQ)和概率图模型(PGMs)中变量/因子边际的计算。当查询底层超图具有$O\left( 1\right)$退化性且$O\left( 1\right)$元数时，我们获得了计算此类查询所需轮次的紧界。特别地，$O\left( 1\right)$退化性条件涵盖了集中式计算模型中可高效计算的大多数经典查询，如具有恒定树宽的查询。这些紧界依赖于无环超图广义超树分解(GHD)的新"宽度"概念(即内部节点宽度)，该概念在GHD子类中最小化内部节点数量。据我们所知，该宽度尚未在理论数据库文献中被明确研究。最后，我们研究了向量与矩阵链乘积的计算问题，通过基于最小熵的新论证方法，在二元有限域上证明了其轮复杂度的紧界。

Though our results are semiring agnostic, we mention two special problems that we consider in this paper. The first problem is when $\mathcal{F} = \varnothing$ and the semiring is the Boolean semiring $\left( {\mathbb{D} = \{ 0,1\} ,\vee , \land  }\right)$ . This corresponds to the Boolean Conjunctive Query (which we will call BCQ). ${}^{4}$ The other problem is when $\mathcal{F} = e$ for some $e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$ and the semiring is $\left( {{\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0},+, \cdot  }\right)$ ,which corresponds to computing a factor marginal in Probabilistic Graphical Models (or PGMs) - here we think of ${f}_{e}$ as a probability distribution. The FAQ setup (and even FAQ-SS) encompasses a large class of problems in varied domains. We refer the reader to the surveys $\left\lbrack  {3,{33}}\right\rbrack$ for an overview of these applications.

虽然我们的结果适用于任意半环，但本文重点讨论两类特殊问题。第一类是当$\mathcal{F} = \varnothing$且半环为布尔半环$\left( {\mathbb{D} = \{ 0,1\} ,\vee , \land  }\right)$时的情况，这对应于布尔合取查询（简称BCQ）。${}^{4}$ 第二类是当$\mathcal{F} = e$对某个$e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$成立且半环为$\left( {{\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0},+, \cdot  }\right)$时的情况，这对应于概率图模型（PGM）中的因子边际计算——此时我们将${f}_{e}$视为概率分布。FAQ框架（甚至FAQ-SS）涵盖了多领域中的大量问题，相关应用概述可参阅综述$\left\lbrack  {3,{33}}\right\rbrack$。

Given a query $q = \left( {\mathcal{H},{\left\{  {f}_{e}\right\}  }_{e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}},\mathcal{F}}\right)$ ,we will consider the number of rounds needed to compute $q$ in a distributed environment. In particular, the underlying communication topology ${}^{5}G = \left( {V,E}\right)$ is assumed to be a synchronous network and we would like to compute $q$ on $G$ with the following constraints $\left\lbrack  {{18},{19}}\right\rbrack$ . Initially,all functions ${\left\{  {f}_{e}\right\}  }_{e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}}$ are assigned to specific nodes $K \subseteq  V : 1 \leq  \left| K\right|  \leq  k$ (called players). In each round of communication, $O\left( {r \cdot  {\log }_{2}\left( D\right) }\right)$ bits ${}^{6}$ can be simultaneously communicated on each edge in $E$ (each such edge or channel is private to the nodes at its endpoints). At the end of the protocol,a pre-determined player in $K$ knows the answer to $q$ . Naturally,we would like to design protocols that minimize the total number of rounds of communication (rounds hereon) needed to compute $q$ on $G$ . More generally, we would like to obtain tight bounds depending on $\mathcal{H}$ and $G$ for this problem for every query topology $\mathcal{H}$ and every network topology $G$ . Note that we do not take into account the internal computation done by nodes in $G$ and we assume that all nodes in $V$ co-operatively compute the answer to $q$ . Due to space constraints, many of the technical details are deferred to the version of this paper [38], which we refer to as full paper.

给定查询$q = \left( {\mathcal{H},{\left\{  {f}_{e}\right\}  }_{e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}},\mathcal{F}}\right)$，我们将研究分布式环境下计算$q$所需的通信轮数。假设底层通信拓扑${}^{5}G = \left( {V,E}\right)$是同步网络，需要在满足约束条件$\left\lbrack  {{18},{19}}\right\rbrack$的$G$上计算$q$。初始时，所有函数${\left\{  {f}_{e}\right\}  }_{e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}}$被分配到特定节点$K \subseteq  V : 1 \leq  \left| K\right|  \leq  k$（称为参与者）。每轮通信中，$E$的每条边可同时传输${}^{6}$比特（每条边或信道仅对其端点节点可见）。协议结束时，$K$中的指定参与者将获知$q$的答案。我们致力于设计能最小化通信总轮数的协议，并期望针对每个查询拓扑$\mathcal{H}$和网络拓扑$G$，建立取决于$\mathcal{H}$和$G$的紧界。注意我们不考虑$G$中节点的内部计算，并假定$V$中所有节点协同计算$q$的答案。由于篇幅限制，技术细节详见本文完整版[38]。

### 1.1 Why this distributed model?

### 1.1 为何选择该分布式模型？

We believe that the strength of our model is its generality. Specifically, it captures query computation in three different paradigms, namely: (1) Computing the natural join query in the Massively Parallel Computation (MPC) model [2, 9, 10, ${31},{36},{37}\rbrack$ ,(2) Computing join and aggregation queries for sensor networks $\left\lbrack  {{13},{23},{40}}\right\rbrack$ and (3) Computation of FAQs on arbitrary topologies using software defined networks and optical reconfigurable networks like ProjecToR [24]. Before we discuss these in detail, we would like to mention that the CONGEST model in distributed computing has the same setup as ours [42] with one crucial difference. Unlike our case, where we can compute FAQs on any topology in the CONGEST model, the topologies for computing a fixed FAQ typically depend on the query itself.

我们认为该模型的优势在于其普适性。具体而言，它能捕捉三种不同范式下的查询计算：(1) 在大规模并行计算(MPC)模型[2,9,10,${31},{36},{37}\rbrack$]中计算自然连接查询；(2) 为传感器网络$\left\lbrack  {{13},{23},{40}}\right\rbrack$计算连接与聚合查询；(3) 利用软件定义网络及可重构光网络（如ProjecToR[24]）在任意拓扑结构上计算FAQ。需特别说明的是，分布式计算中的CONGEST模型[42]虽与我们架构相似，但存在关键差异：该模型只能针对特定查询设计拓扑，而我们的方案支持在任意拓扑上计算FAQ。

The sequence of works in the MPC setting focus on computing the natural join $q$ (which is a special case of FAQ-SS as mentioned earlier) on a topology $G$ with $p$ nodes,which is typically well-connected. Each round of communication has two phases - (1) internal computation among the nodes and (2) communication between the nodes bounded by a node capacity $L$ . The goal in MPC is to minimize the number of rounds $h$ needed for computing $q$ . There are two different lines of work in this regime - one where $p$ is fixed and the goal is to determine $h,L\left\lbrack  {9,{10},{36},{37}}\right\rbrack$ and the other is when $h,L$ are fixed and the goal is to determine $p\left\lbrack  2\right\rbrack$ . We compare both these classes of models with ours in Appendix A and present an executive summary here.

MPC环境下的系列研究聚焦于在具有$p$个节点（通常为高连通性拓扑$G$）上计算自然连接$q$（即前文所述FAQ-SS的特例）。每轮通信包含两个阶段——(1)节点内部计算；(2)受节点容量$L$约束的节点间通信。MPC的目标是最小化计算$q$所需轮数$h$。该领域存在两类研究方向：固定$p$时确定$h,L\left\lbrack  {9,{10},{36},{37}}\right\rbrack$，或固定$h,L$时确定$p\left\lbrack  2\right\rbrack$。我们将在附录A中对比这两类模型与我们的方案，并在此给出核心结论。

Roughly speaking, the MPC model defined in [9] is a special case of our model. We consider two different MPC models - one with no replication (which we call MPC(0) [9]) and one with replication (which we dub $\operatorname{MPC}\left( \epsilon \right) \left\lbrack  {2,{36}}\right\rbrack$ ). Both these models have some differences from ours and among themselves. For instance, both these models assume a specific network topology ${G}^{\prime }$ (as opposed to any topology $G$ in our case),work on node capacities $L$ (as opposed to edge capacities in our setting) and prove bounds for the natural join problem (in contrast, our bounds apply for the more general FAQ). The input functions are systematically assigned to players in $\operatorname{MPC}\left( 0\right)$ and are uniformly distributed among players in $\operatorname{MPC}\left( \epsilon \right)$ . The instantation of these models for the setting where $p$ is fixed and $h,L$ is to be determined is the closest to our model. In particular,when $\mathcal{H}$ is a star,our protocols obtain the same guarantees as $\operatorname{MPC}\left( 0\right)$ and are slightly worse in $\operatorname{MPC}\left( \epsilon \right)$ . Our model does not (yet) handle the scenario when $L$ is fixed and the goal is to determine $p$ . Sensor networks are typically tree-like topologies, where the goal is to efficiently and accurately report aggregate queries on data generated by the sensors. Since the sensors can store only little data, these queries are typically restrictive. We show in the full paper [38] that our results imply bounds for some of these queries. Recently, Internet of Things (IoT) devices [1] show the promise of expanding the data storage/class of queries that can be computed on sensor networks. We believe that our model/results will find more relevance in the IoT setting since the sensors used posess more computation power than those considered in [40]. Finally, our work initiates the study of computation on general topologies to be used in emerging technologies like ProjecToR [24], which has been proposed for use in data centers where topologies can be changed based on the workload.

### 1.2 Summary of Our Contributions

### 1.2 研究成果概述

Table 1 lists our results and Section 2 contains a detailed overview of techniques used to obtain the results. We summarize our contributions here. For the sake of brevity, we focus on the BCQ problem. Our main result is the following. For (hyper)graphs $\mathcal{H}$ with constant degeneracy ${}^{7}\left( d\right)$ and constant arity(r),we prove tight bounds (up to constant factors) for computing any BCQ on any network topology $G$ . Constant treewidth implies constant $d$ and,as a result,queries having constant $d$ encompass most well-studied queries that are efficiently computable in the centralized computation model.

表1列出我们的研究成果，第2节详细阐述了相关技术方法。我们在此总结主要贡献：针对BCQ问题，对于具有常数退化度${}^{7}\left( d\right)$和常数元数(r)的（超）图$\mathcal{H}$，我们证明了在任何网络拓扑$G$上计算BCQ的紧致边界（常数因子内）。常数树宽意味着常数$d$，因此具有常数$d$的查询涵盖了集中式计算模型中可高效计算的大多数经典查询。

Upper Bounds. Our upper bounds need protocols for solving the following two basic algorithmic tasks: (1) set intersection and (2) sending all inputs to a single node. For (1), our protocol is new in the FAQ literature and for (2), we use a standard protocol from flow networks. Interestingly, our results highlight a notion of width of acyclic queries- the number of internal nodes for a subclass of GHDs ${}^{8}$ (defined in Section 2.2.2), which to the best of our knowledge, has not been explicitly studied in the database literature.

上界。我们的上界需要解决两个基本算法任务的协议：（1）集合交运算（2）将所有输入发送至单一节点。对于（1），我们的协议在FAQ领域是创新性的；对于（2），我们采用流网络的标准协议。值得注意的是，我们的结果揭示了无环查询宽度的新概念——GHD子类${}^{8}$（定义见2.2.2节）的内部节点数，据我们所知，该概念尚未在数据库文献中被明确研究。

Lower Bounds. Our lower bounds follow from known lower bounds on the well-studied TRIBES function in two-party communication complexity literature (defined in Section 2.2.2) At a high level, we start with an arbitrary TRIBES instance and show that it can be reduced to a suitable BCQ instance in our model. We then prove lower bounds on the BCQ instance using known lower bounds on TRIBES.

下界。我们的下界源自双方通信复杂性理论中经典TRIBES函数（定义见2.2.2节）的已知下界。简言之，我们从任意TRIBES实例出发，证明其可归约为适合我们模型的BCQ实例，进而利用TRIBES的已知下界推导BCQ实例的下界。

We note here that the simplicity of our techniques allows us to extend our results to the general FAQ problem. Further, we would like to mention that extending our bounds to $d$ -degenerate graphs with non-constant $d$ has a known bottleneck of solving BCQ of $\mathcal{H}$ on $G$ when $\mathcal{H}$ is a clique and $G$ is an edge. In particular,the gaps dependent on $d$ in Table 1 cannot be resolved without addressing this bottleneck.

需要说明的是，我们方法的简洁性使得结果可推广至通用FAQ问题。此外需指出，将边界推广至具有非常数$d$的$d$-退化图时存在已知瓶颈：当$\mathcal{H}$为团而$G$为边时，需解决$G$上$\mathcal{H}$的BCQ问题。特别地，若不解决此瓶颈，表1中依赖$d$的间隙将无法消除。

Finally, we consider the following FAQ-SS problem of Chain Matrix-Vector Multiplication (MCM): computing ${\mathbf{A}}_{k}$ . ${\mathbf{A}}_{k - 1} \cdot  \ldots  \cdot  {\mathbf{A}}_{1} \cdot  \mathbf{x}$ ,where each player gets $\mathbf{x},{\mathbf{A}}_{1},\ldots ,{\mathbf{A}}_{k}$ in order and they would like to compute the product over the finite field ${\mathbb{F}}_{2}{.}^{9}$ Note that this problem is different from the wellknown Online Matrix Vector Multiplication problem ${}^{10}$ and is related to $k$ layer neural networks. ${}^{11}$ We prove a tight bound for this problem. The upper bound is simple but the lower bound argument (though conceptually simple) is technically the most involved part of the paper. We use an entropy-based argument using min-entropy instead of the standard Shannon's entropy. This requires more care since we can no longer use the chain rule.

最后，我们考虑链式矩阵-向量乘法（MCM）中的FAQ-SS问题：计算${\mathbf{A}}_{k}$。${\mathbf{A}}_{k - 1} \cdot  \ldots  \cdot  {\mathbf{A}}_{1} \cdot  \mathbf{x}$，其中每个参与者按序获取$\mathbf{x},{\mathbf{A}}_{1},\ldots ,{\mathbf{A}}_{k}$，他们需要在有限域${\mathbb{F}}_{2}{.}^{9}$上计算乘积。注意该问题不同于著名的在线矩阵向量乘法问题${}^{10}$，且与$k$层神经网络相关。${}^{11}$我们证明了该问题的紧界。上界证明简单，但下界论证（虽然概念简单）在技术上是本文最复杂的部分。我们采用基于最小熵而非标准香农熵的信息论论证方法，这需要更谨慎的处理，因为无法继续使用链式法则。

示例2.1。考虑查询超图${\mathcal{H}}_{0} = (\overline{\mathcal{V}} =$$\{ A\} ,\overline{\mathcal{E}} = \{ R\left( A\right) ,S\left( A\right) ,T\left( A\right) ,U\left( A\right) \} )$，即所有边均为$A$上的自循环，以及直线结构${G}_{1}$。我们需要在${G}_{1}$上求解${\mathcal{H}}_{0}$的布尔合取查询（BCQ），其Datalog格式为${q}_{0}\left( \right)  :  - R\left( A\right) ,S\left( A\right) ,T\left( A\right) ,U\left( A\right)$。在${G}_{1}$中，玩家${P}_{1}$获得$R,{P}_{2}$，$S,{P}_{3}$获得$T$，${P}_{4}$获得$U$。此时，求解${G}_{1}$上${\mathcal{H}}_{0}$的BCQ等价于验证集合交集$R\left( A\right)  \cap  S\left( A\right)  \cap  T\left( A\right)  \cap  U\left( A\right)$是否为空。假设玩家${P}_{4}$需要获知该查询结果。

We can solve this query in $N + 2$ rounds as follows. In the first round,player ${P}_{1}$ sends a value $a \in  \operatorname{Dom}\left( A\right)$ such that there exists $R\left( a\right)  = 1$ to player ${P}_{2}$ who then checks if $S\left( a\right)  = 1$ . More generally,in the $i$ -th round,player ${P}_{j}$ for $2 \leq  j \leq  4$ receives an $a$ from its left neighbor(j - 1)and checks if $a$ is present in its table. If so,it passes $a$ to its right neighbor $\left( {j + 1}\right)$ (if $j \leq  3$ ) in the next $\left( {i + 1}\right)$ -th round. Otherwise,it does not pass anything. Notice that this protocol will terminate once all matching values of $a$ are passed from ${P}_{1}$ to ${P}_{4}$ which takes $N + 2$ rounds in the worst case. In other words,we are computing the semijoin (see Definition 3.5) query $((R\left( A\right)  \ltimes$ $S\left( A\right) ) \ltimes  T\left( A\right) ) \ltimes  U\left( A\right)$ ,which is equivalent to computing $R\left( A\right)  \cap  S\left( A\right)  \cap  T\left( A\right)  \cap  U\left( A\right)$ . Note that this is much better than the trivial protocol for this case,which takes $3 \cdot  N + 2$ rounds.

我们可通过$N + 2$轮次解决此查询，具体步骤如下：首轮中，玩家${P}_{1}$向玩家${P}_{2}$发送值$a \in  \operatorname{Dom}\left( A\right)$（需满足存在$R\left( a\right)  = 1$），后者验证是否满足$S\left( a\right)  = 1$。更一般地，在第$i$轮，玩家${P}_{j}$（其中$2 \leq  j \leq  4$）从其左邻节点(j-1)接收$a$并核查该值是否存在于自身表中。若存在，则在下一轮（第$\left( {i + 1}\right)$轮）将$a$传递给右邻节点$\left( {j + 1}\right)$（当$j \leq  3$时）；否则不传递任何信息。注意该协议将在所有匹配的$a$值从${P}_{1}$传递至${P}_{4}$后终止，最坏情况下耗时$N + 2$轮。换言之，我们正在计算半连接（参见定义3.5）查询$((R\left( A\right)  \ltimes$$S\left( A\right) ) \ltimes  T\left( A\right) ) \ltimes  U\left( A\right)$，其等价于计算$R\left( A\right)  \cap  S\left( A\right)  \cap  T\left( A\right)  \cap  U\left( A\right)$。相较于该场景下的简单协议（需$3 \cdot  N + 2$轮），本方案效率显著提升。

At the end of this protocol, ${P}_{4}$ knows the answer to the query. It is not too hard to see that we can extend the above protocol to the case when any other player say ${P}_{i}$ for some $i \in  \left\lbrack  3\right\rbrack$ is designated to know the answer. In particular, we can orient ${G}_{1}$ in such a way that all paths are directed towards ${P}_{i}$ and then run the protocol above simultaneously on all paths (there are at most two) towards ${P}_{i}$ (recall that we assume knowledge of $G$ for all nodes). Note that ${P}_{i}$ would have the answer to the query and the new protocol takes $N + x$ rounds, where $x \leq  2$ depends on the choice of ${P}_{i}$ .

协议终止时，${P}_{4}$将获知查询结果。不难将该协议扩展至任意指定玩家（如某个$i \in  \left\lbrack  3\right\rbrack$对应的${P}_{i}$）获取结果的场景：通过定向调整${G}_{1}$使所有路径指向${P}_{i}$，并沿指向${P}_{i}$的所有路径（最多两条）同步执行上述协议（注意我们预设所有节点已知$G$）。此时${P}_{i}$将持有查询答案，新协议耗时$N + x$轮（具体轮数$x \leq  2$取决于${P}_{i}$的选择）。

It is not too hard to see that our protocol in the above example can be extended to the case when $\mathcal{H}$ is a star. We illustrate this in the following example.

显然上述示例协议可扩展至$\mathcal{H}$为星型拓扑的情形，下例将予以演示。

Example 2.2. Consider the star ${\mathcal{H}}_{1}$ and the line ${G}_{1}$ in Figure 1 . We now construct a corresponding ${\mathrm{{BCQ}}}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{1},\bar{X},\bar{Y}}$ instance from the TRIBES one as follows - we assign $R\left( {A,B}\right)  = {X}_{1} \times  \{ 1\} ,S\left( {A,C}\right)  = T\left( {A,D}\right)  = \left\lbrack  N\right\rbrack   \times  \{ 1\}$ and $U\left( {A,E}\right)  = {Y}_{1} \times  \{ 1\}$ . It is not too hard to see that ${\mathrm{{BCQ}}}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{1},\bar{X},\bar{Y}}$ is 1 iff ${\operatorname{TRIBES}}_{1,N}\left( {\bar{X},\bar{Y}}\right)$ is 1,implying that solving the BCQ instance would solve the TRIBES instance. Finally, to obtain a lower bound for computing ${\mathrm{{BCQ}}}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{1},\bar{X},\bar{Y}}$ on the line ${G}_{1}$ , we only need a cut where $R$ and $U$ are on different sides. We consider the cut $C = \left( {\left\{  {{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{P}_{3},{P}_{4}}\right\}  }\right)$ of ${G}_{1}$ and the assignment where ${P}_{1}$ gets $R,{P}_{2}$ gets $S,{P}_{3}$ gets $T$ and ${P}_{4}$ gets $U$ . Then,we can us (1) and Theorem 2.3 to obtain the required lower bound of $\Omega \left( N\right)$ since $\operatorname{MinCut}\left( {G,K}\right)  = 1$ . Note that the above lower bound holds for any star $\mathcal{H}$ . The same TRIBES instance can be used for Examples 2.1 and 2.3 as well. While a similar assignment holds for Example 2.1, Example 2.3 requires a different assignment where $C = \left\{  {P}_{1}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{P}_{2},{P}_{3},{P}_{4}}\right\}$ ) and ${P}_{1}$ gets $R$ and $S,{P}_{2}$ gets $T$ and ${P}_{3}$ gets $U$ . Note that more than one input function can be assigned to the same player in $G$ .

示例2.4 回顾我们证明了在${G}_{1}$上计算${\mathcal{H}}_{1}$的BCQ时存在$N + 2$的上界。我们从任意${\operatorname{TRIBES}}_{m = 1,N}\left( {\bar{X} = \left( {X}_{1}\right) ,\bar{Y} = \left( {Y}_{1}\right) }\right)$实例出发，稍作符号滥用，将${X}_{1},{Y}_{1}$视为$\left\lbrack  N\right\rbrack$的子集（而非$\{ 0,1{\} }^{N}$中的元素）。现按如下方式从TRIBES实例构造对应的${\mathrm{{BCQ}}}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{1},\bar{X},\bar{Y}}$实例——分配$R\left( {A,B}\right)  = {X}_{1} \times  \{ 1\} ,S\left( {A,C}\right)  = T\left( {A,D}\right)  = \left\lbrack  N\right\rbrack   \times  \{ 1\}$和$U\left( {A,E}\right)  = {Y}_{1} \times  \{ 1\}$。不难看出，当且仅当${\operatorname{TRIBES}}_{1,N}\left( {\bar{X},\bar{Y}}\right)$为1时${\mathrm{{BCQ}}}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{1},\bar{X},\bar{Y}}$为1，这意味着解决该BCQ实例即解决TRIBES实例。最后，为获得在线性结构${G}_{1}$上计算${\mathrm{{BCQ}}}_{{\mathcal{H}}_{1},\bar{X},\bar{Y}}$的下界，仅需一个满足$R$与$U$分处两侧的切割。考虑${G}_{1}$的$C = \left( {\left\{  {{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{P}_{3},{P}_{4}}\right\}  }\right)$切割及赋值方案：${P}_{1}$获得$R,{P}_{2}$，$S,{P}_{3}$获得$T$，${P}_{4}$获得$U$。由于$\operatorname{MinCut}\left( {G,K}\right)  = 1$，可利用式(1)和定理2.3得到所需下界$\Omega \left( N\right)$。注意该下界适用于任意星型结构$\mathcal{H}$。同一TRIBES实例亦可用于示例2.1和2.3。示例2.1适用类似赋值方案，而示例2.3需采用不同赋值：$C = \left\{  {P}_{1}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{P}_{2},{P}_{3},{P}_{4}}\right\}$），${P}_{1}$获得$R$，$S,{P}_{2}$获得$T$，${P}_{3}$获得$U$。需注意在$G$中，多个输入函数可分配给同一参与者。

For general $d$ -degenerate graphs $\mathcal{H}$ ,we start by recalling that $m$ (i.e.,size of the TRIBES instance) is a function of $\mathcal{H}$ . As mentioned in Section 2.2.1,we can decompose $\mathcal{H}$ into a forest and a core. We prove three different lower bounds on $\mathcal{H}$ ,where the size of the TRIBES instance $m$ used in our reduction is the maximum of three different bounds, each one on a different part of $\mathcal{H}$ . The first one is on $\mathcal{H}$ ’s forest part,the second and third ones are on $\mathcal{H}$ ’s core part - lower bounded by applying Moore's bound [5] and Turan's theorem [6] respectively. For each case, we show that we can reduce the TRIBES instance to a suitable two-party BCQ instance. Thus, known lower bounds on the TRIBES instance from Theorem 2.3 apply for the BCQ instance. Finally, to generalize our results from two-party BCQ to general $G$ ,we use ideas from $\left\lbrack  {{18},{19}}\right\rbrack$ to obtain an appropriate cut for $G$ and use lower bounds from the induced two-party communication complexity problem across the cut. Note that the assignment of functions depends on the cut. We present the details in Section 4.2.

针对一般$d$-退化图$\mathcal{H}$，我们首先回顾$m$（即TRIBES实例的规模）是$\mathcal{H}$的函数。如第2.2.1节所述，可将$\mathcal{H}$分解为森林部分和核心部分。我们证明了$\mathcal{H}$的三个不同下界，归约中使用的TRIBES实例规模$m$取这三个界中的最大值——每个界对应$\mathcal{H}$的不同部分：第一个界针对森林部分，第二、三个界通过分别应用摩尔界[5]和图兰定理[6]对核心部分进行下界估计。针对每种情况，我们证明可将TRIBES实例归约为合适的二方BCQ实例，因此定理2.3中TRIBES实例的已知下界适用于BCQ实例。最后，为将二方BCQ结果推广至一般$G$，我们运用$\left\lbrack  {{18},{19}}\right\rbrack$的思想获取$G$的适当分割，并利用分割诱导的二方通信复杂性问题的下界。需注意函数分配取决于分割方式，详见第4.2节。

For constant $d$ ,our upper and lower bounds match. However, for non-constant $d$ ,we have a gap of $\widetilde{O}\left( d\right)$ . We would like to note that there is a fundamental bottleneck in getting rid of this factor as the case of $\mathcal{H}$ being a clique is an outstanding open question ${}^{14}$ (even in Model 2.2) and seems beyond the reach of current communication complexity techniques [16].

当$d$为常数时，我们的上下界匹配；但对于非常数$d$，存在$\widetilde{O}\left( d\right)$的间隙。需要指出，消除这个因子存在根本性障碍——当$\mathcal{H}$为团簇时仍是悬而未决的开放问题${}^{14}$（即使在模型2.2中），这已超出当前通信复杂性技术的能力范围[16]。

### 2.3 Notion of Width

### 2.3 宽度概念

We start by defining the notion of ${GHD}$ s and acyclic (hyper)graphs.

我们首先定义${GHD}$和无环（超）图的概念。

Definition 2.4 (GHD). A GHD of $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ is defined by a triple $\langle \mathcal{T},\chi ,\lambda \rangle$ ,where $\mathcal{T} = \left( {V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) ,E\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }\right)$ is a tree, $\chi$ : $V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\mathcal{V}}$ is a function associating a set of vertices $\chi \left( v\right)  \subseteq$ $\mathcal{V}$ to each node $v$ of $\mathcal{T}$ ,and $\lambda  : V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\mathcal{E}}$ is a function associating a set of hyperedges to each node $v$ of $\mathcal{T}$ such that the following two properties hold. First,for each $e \in  \mathcal{E}$ ,there is at least one node $v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ such that $e \subseteq  \chi \left( v\right)$ and $e \in  \lambda \left( v\right)$ . Second,for every ${V}^{\prime } \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ ,the set $\left\{  {v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \mid  {V}^{\prime } \subseteq  \chi \left( v\right) }\right\}$ is connected in $\mathcal{T}$ ,called the running intersection property (RIP hereon). We only consider rooted GHDs.

定义2.4（GHD）。$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$的广义超树分解由三元组$\langle \mathcal{T},\chi ,\lambda \rangle$定义，其中$\mathcal{T} = \left( {V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) ,E\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }\right)$是树结构，$\chi$：$V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\mathcal{V}}$是将顶点集$\chi \left( v\right)  \subseteq$$\mathcal{V}$关联到$\mathcal{T}$各节点$v$的函数，$\lambda  : V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\mathcal{E}}$是将超边集关联到$\mathcal{T}$各节点$v$的函数，且满足两个性质：其一，对每个$e \in  \mathcal{E}$存在至少一个节点$v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$使得$e \subseteq  \chi \left( v\right)$且$e \in  \lambda \left( v\right)$；其二，对任意${V}^{\prime } \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$，集合$\left\{  {v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \mid  {V}^{\prime } \subseteq  \chi \left( v\right) }\right\}$在$\mathcal{T}$中连通（称为运行相交性，简称RIP）。本文仅考虑有根GHD。

A reduced-GHD has the additional property that every hyper-edge $e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$ has a unique node $v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ such that $\chi \left( v\right)  = e$ (note that this is an equality).

简化广义超树分解(GHD)还具有以下特性：每条超边$e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$都存在唯一节点$v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$使得$\chi \left( v\right)  = e$（注意这是等式关系）。

Definition 2.5 (Acyclicity). A hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\overline{\mathcal{V}},\overline{\mathcal{E}}}\right)$ is acyclic iff there exists a ${GHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,\lambda }\right)$ in which for every node $v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) ,\chi \left( v\right)$ is a hyperedge in $\overline{\mathcal{E}}$ .

定义2.5（无环性）。当且仅当存在${GHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi ,\lambda }\right)$使得对于每个节点$v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) ,\chi \left( v\right)$都是$\overline{\mathcal{E}}$中的超边时，超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\overline{\mathcal{V}},\overline{\mathcal{E}}}\right)$是无环的。

We now define the sub-classes of reduced-GHDs that we consider in this paper. In particular, we construct reduced-GHDs using the GYO algorithm [28, 46, 51] (GYOA, also called GYO-Elimination order) and call them GYO-GHDs. We start by defining the GYO-reduction ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ of a hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ .

现在定义本文研究的简化广义超树分解子类。具体而言，我们采用GYO算法[28,46,51]（简称GYOA，又称GYO消解序）构建简化广义超树分解，称为GYO-GHD。首先定义超图$\mathcal{H}$的GYO约简${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$。

Definition 2.6 (GYO-REDUCTION AND GYOA). For any hy-pergraph $\mathcal{H}$ ,the GYO-reduction ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ is defined as the leftover hypergraph after running GYOA on $\mathcal{H}$ . We describe GYOA here. The input to GYOA is $\mathcal{H}$ and its output is a hypergraph ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ . It performs the following two steps iteratively on ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ (starting with ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } = \mathcal{H}$ ): (a) Eliminate a vertex that is present in only one hyperedge and (b) Delete a hyperedge that is contained in another hyperedge. GYOA terminates when it cannot perform any of the above two steps on ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ .

定义2.6（GYO约简与GYOA）。对任意超图$\mathcal{H}$，GYO约简${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$定义为在$\mathcal{H}$上执行GYOA后剩余的超图。GYOA算法描述如下：输入为$\mathcal{H}$，输出为超图${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$。该算法在${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$上（初始为${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } = \mathcal{H}$）迭代执行：(a)消除仅存在于单条超边的顶点；(b)删除被其他超边包含的超边。当无法继续执行上述步骤时，GYOA终止。

We note here that running GYOA on an acyclic hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ returns an empty ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ . For general hypergraphs $\mathcal{H}$ ,we start by running GYOA on $\mathcal{H}$ ,which returns a hypergraph ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ . Note that the hyperedges removed in this process form a forest of acyclic hypergraphs. For each hypertree in the forest of acyclic hypergraphs, we can construct a reduced-GHD and root it arbitrarily. We now define $\mathrm{C}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ and $\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ based on the GYO-reduction ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ and the reduced-GHDs constructed for the original hyperedges of $\mathcal{H}$ removed during GYOA.

需注意，在无环超图$\mathcal{H}$上运行GYOA将返回空${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$。对于一般超图$\mathcal{H}$，首先运行GYOA得到${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$。被移除的超边构成无环超图森林，可为其中每个超树构建任意根节点的简化广义超树分解。现基于GYO约简${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$及为原超边构建的简化广义超树分解，定义$\mathrm{C}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$和$\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$。

Definition 2.7 (C(H), $\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ). $\mathrm{C}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ is the union of ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ and the root in each reduced-GHD we constructed. $\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \mathcal{H} \smallsetminus  {\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ .

定义2.7（C(H),$\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$）。$\mathrm{C}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$是${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$与各简化广义超树分解中根节点的并集。$\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \mathcal{H} \smallsetminus  {\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$。

We are now ready to construct GYO-GHDs.

现在可以构建GYO-GHD。

CONSTRUCTION 2.8. Let $\mathcal{T}$ be the GYO-GHD be obtained from the following procedure. We define the root ${r}^{\prime }$ of $\mathcal{T}$ with $\chi \left( {r}^{\prime }\right)  =$ $V\left( {\mathrm{C}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ . For each edge $e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$ with $e \subset  V\left( {\mathrm{C}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ ,we create a new node ${v}_{e}^{\prime }$ in $\mathcal{T}$ with $\chi \left( {v}_{e}^{\prime }\right)  = e$ and add the edge $\left( {{r}^{\prime },{v}_{e}^{\prime }}\right)$ to $\mathcal{T}$ in order to make it a reduced-GHD. For every reduced-GHD ${\mathcal{T}}^{\prime }$ in $\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,we add the edge $\left( {{r}^{\prime },{r}^{\prime \prime }}\right)$ to $\mathcal{T}$ ,where ${r}^{\prime \prime }$ is the root of ${\mathcal{T}}^{\prime }$ . We add all the remaining nodes and edges in each reduced-GHD in $\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ to $\mathcal{T}$ .

构造2.8 设$\mathcal{T}$为通过以下步骤获得的GYO-GHD。我们定义$\mathcal{T}$的根节点${r}^{\prime }$具有$\chi \left( {r}^{\prime }\right)  =$$V\left( {\mathrm{C}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$属性。对于每条具有$e \subset  V\left( {\mathrm{C}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$特性的边$e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$，我们在$\mathcal{T}$中创建新节点${v}_{e}^{\prime }$并赋予$\chi \left( {v}_{e}^{\prime }\right)  = e$属性，同时添加边$\left( {{r}^{\prime },{v}_{e}^{\prime }}\right)$使其成为约化GHD。对于$\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$中的每个约化GHD${\mathcal{T}}^{\prime }$，我们添加边$\left( {{r}^{\prime },{r}^{\prime \prime }}\right)$至$\mathcal{T}$，其中${r}^{\prime \prime }$是${\mathcal{T}}^{\prime }$的根节点。最后将$\mathrm{F}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$中各约化GHD的剩余节点和边全部加入$\mathcal{T}$。

We argue that the above procedure produces a reduced-GHD in the full paper [38]. Our new notion of width based on GYO-GHDs,which we call $y$ (Internal Node Width),is defined as follows.

我们在完整论文[38]中论证了上述流程可生成约化GHD。基于GYO-GHD提出的新宽度概念——$y$(内部节点宽度)，其定义如下。

DEFINITION 2.9.

定义2.9

$$
y\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \mathop{\min }\limits_{{\forall \mathcal{T} : \mathcal{T}\text{ is a GYO-GHD of }\mathcal{H}}}y\left( \mathcal{T}\right) 
$$

where $y\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ is the number of internal/non-leaf nodes in $\mathcal{T}$ .

其中$y\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$表示$\mathcal{T}$中内部/非叶节点的数量。

Unless specified otherwise, in the rest of the paper when we refer to GHDs, we are referring to GYO-GHDs. As an example in Figure 2,we consider two different GHDs ${\mathcal{T}}_{1}$ and ${\mathcal{T}}_{2}$ for the acyclic hypergraph ${\mathcal{H}}_{2}$ from Figure 1. Both are outcomes of Construction 2.8 and while ${\mathcal{T}}_{2}$ has two internal nodes, ${\mathcal{T}}_{1}$ has only one,implying $y\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = 1$ . For ${\mathcal{H}}_{1}$ in Figure 1, it is easy to construct a GHD with one internal node (i.e., $y\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = 1$ ) by keeping(A,B)as the root and $\left( {A,C}\right) ,\left( {A,D}\right) ,\left( {A,E}\right)$ as leaves. We show how this can be achieved for simple graphs $\mathcal{H}$ in Section 4.

### 2.4 Chain Matrix-Vector Multiplication

### 2.4 链式矩阵-向量乘法

Finally, in this work, we consider the problem of computing ${\mathbf{A}}_{k}\cdots {\mathbf{A}}_{1}\mathbf{x}$ where the computation is over ${\mathbb{F}}_{2}$ . The player ${P}_{i}$ gets ${\mathbf{A}}_{i}$ for $i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ and ${P}_{0}$ gets $\mathbf{x}$ . Player ${P}_{k + 1}$ wants to know the answer (and does not have any input). The topology $G$ is a line with ${P}_{i}$ connected to ${P}_{i + 1}$ for $0 \leq  i \leq  k$ . We show that when $k \leq  N$ the natural algorithm that computes the partial product ${\mathbf{A}}_{i}\cdots {\mathbf{A}}_{1}\mathbf{x}$ at ${P}_{i}$ taking $\Theta \left( {kN}\right)$ rounds is indeed optimal. By contrast, if the matrices are assigned randomly to the players then the optimal number of rounds is $\Theta \left( {{k}^{2}N}\right)$ (this follows from a trivial protocol). On the other extreme, if all matrices are assigned to one player, then the problem is trivial. So we are proving a tight lower-bound for arguably the simplest assignment of matrices to players that is not trivial.

最终，在本研究中，我们探讨了计算${\mathbf{A}}_{k}\cdots {\mathbf{A}}_{1}\mathbf{x}$的问题，其中计算过程基于${\mathbb{F}}_{2}$。参与者${P}_{i}$因$i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$获得${\mathbf{A}}_{i}$，而${P}_{0}$则得到$\mathbf{x}$。参与者${P}_{k + 1}$希望获知答案（且不提供任何输入）。拓扑结构$G$呈现为线性连接，其中${P}_{i}$与${P}_{i + 1}$相连，对应$0 \leq  i \leq  k$。我们证明当<b11></b1>时，在${P}_{i}$处计算部分积${\mathbf{A}}_{i}\cdots {\mathbf{A}}_{1}\mathbf{x}$的自然算法耗时$\Theta \left( {kN}\right)$轮确为最优。相比之下，若矩阵随机分配给参与者，则最优轮数为$\Theta \left( {{k}^{2}N}\right)$（这源于简单协议）。极端情况下，若所有矩阵分配给单一参与者，问题则无意义。因此，我们正在证明对于非平凡矩阵分配方案中最简单情形下的严格下界。

We note that the existing technique of [18] cannot prove a lower bound better than $\Omega \left( N\right)$ for this problem (see the full paper [38] for a more detailed description). To get a better lower bound of $\Omega \left( {kN}\right)$ ,we use an entropy based inductive argument to show that at end of the $\Omega \left( {iN}\right)$ rounds,in player ${P}_{i}$ ’s view, ${\mathbf{A}}_{i - 1}\cdots {\mathbf{A}}_{1}\mathbf{x}$ has very high entropy. However,Shannon's entropy is too weak for this argument to go through and we use the stronger notion of min-entropy, which is omnipresent in pseudorandomness and cryptography [49]. Unfortunately, this means that we can no longer appeal to the chain rule and the arguments become a bit more delicate. Finally, in the process we prove the following natural result: if $A$ and $x$ have high enough min-entropy,then ${Ax}$ has higher min-entropy than $\mathbf{x}$ . To the best of our knowledge this result is new, though it follows by combining known results in pseudorandomness. ${}^{15}$

我们注意到现有技术[18]无法为该问题证明优于$\Omega \left( N\right)$的下界（详见完整论文[38]）。为获得更优的$\Omega \left( {kN}\right)$下界，我们采用基于熵的归纳论证：经过$\Omega \left( {iN}\right)$轮后，在参与者${P}_{i}$的视角中，${\mathbf{A}}_{i - 1}\cdots {\mathbf{A}}_{1}\mathbf{x}$具有极高熵值。然而香农熵对此论证过于薄弱，故采用伪随机性与密码学[49]中普遍存在的最小熵概念。遗憾的是，这意味着我们无法继续沿用链式法则，论证过程需更精细处理。最终，我们在此过程中证明了以下自然结论：若$A$与$x$具有足够高的最小熵，则${Ax}$的最小熵高于$\mathbf{x}$。据我们所知，该结论虽可通过组合伪随机性已知结果得出，但仍属首次提出。${}^{15}$

Widths of GHDs. The Internal Node Width $y\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ of a GHD focuses on minimizing the number of internal (non-leaf) nodes in GHDs of acyclic hypergraphs. There is a related notion for Tree Decompositions called Lean Tree Decompositions (LTDs) [11, 22, 47]. The LTDs minimize the number of internal nodes in the following way - they try to retain only pairs of connected internal nodes whose intersection forms a bridge in the original graph $\mathcal{H}$ . The other nodes are forced to become leaves of one of the internal nodes. While our construction procedure of GYO-GHDs tries to convert existing internal nodes to become leaves of some internal nodes, we do not (yet) see an exact one-to-one mapping from GYO-GHDs to LTDs. We would like to mention here that both the goals of GYO-GHDs and LTDs are the same i.e., to minimize the number of internal nodes. We would like to note here that $y\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ can potentially reduce the depth of the GHD as well. Reducing depth of GHDs (sometimes by increasing the treewidth) has been considered before $\left\lbrack  {2,4,{12}}\right\rbrack$ .

where ${f}_{\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } = 1$ if ${v}^{\prime }$ is a internal node and 0 otherwise. It follows that ${f}_{\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$ can be computed in linear time in size of $\mathcal{T}$ . Given this setup, Theorem 3.4 in [29] proves that computing Minimal GHDs over HDs for arbitrary vertex aggregation functions is NP-Hard.

其中${f}_{\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } = 1$当${v}^{\prime }$为内部节点时取值为1，否则为0。由此可得${f}_{\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }$可在$\mathcal{T}$大小的线性时间内计算。基于此设置，文献[29]定理3.4证明了对任意顶点聚合函数计算HD上的最小GHD属于NP难问题。

However, this does not hold in our case since there is always a GHD with one internal node (containing all the variables in $\mathcal{H}$ ). As a result,considering the minimization over all GHDs for our case is trivial and doesn't give use tight results. In particular, our setup is a bit different and we minimize over GYO-GHDs (Construction 2.8). For the tightness of our bounds for $\mathcal{H}$ with constant degeneracy and constant arity,we only need an $O\left( 1\right)$ -factor approximation of Internal-Node-Width, which we achieve (details in the full paper [38]).

但这不适用于我们的情况，因总存在含单个内部节点（包含$\mathcal{H}$中所有变量）的GHD。因此在我们案例中考虑所有GHD的最小化是平凡的，无法得到紧致结果。实际上，我们的设置略有不同——针对GYO-GHD进行最小化（构造2.8）。对于具有常数退化度和常数元数的$\mathcal{H}$，我们仅需内部节点宽度的$O\left( 1\right)$倍近似值即能满足边界紧致性要求（详见全文[38]）。

We refer the reader to [27] for a recent survey on widths for GHD.

关于GHD宽度的最新综述，请参阅文献[27]。

Distributed Computing and Communication Complexity. As stated earlier, our model is similar to (and different from) the CONGEST model in distributed computing [42]. Recently, there has been work on the same model as ours but instead of minimizing the number of rounds, they focus on minimizing the total communication of the protocols $\lbrack {14},{19},{20},{43},{48}$ , 50]. Finally, [18] obtained results on minimizing the number of rounds of protocols in our setup for some well-studied functions in two-party communication complexity literature.

分布式计算与通信复杂度。如前所述，我们的模型类似于（又不同于）分布式计算中的CONGEST模型[42]。近期有研究采用相同模型，但其聚焦于最小化协议总通信量而非轮次数量$\lbrack {14},{19},{20},{43},{48}$,50]。最后，文献[18]针对两方通信复杂度中某些经典函数，在我们的设置下取得了最小化协议轮次数的成果。

Entropy in Communication Complexity. Information complexity by now is a well-established sub-field of communication complexity that uses Shannon entropy to measure the amount of information exchanges in a two-party communication protocol. Information complexity was introduced in the work of Chakrabarti et al. [15] and later used in a systematic way to tackle multiple problems in [8]. To the best of our knowledge, min-entropy has only been used very recently in communication complexity $\left\lbrack  {{25},{26}}\right\rbrack$ though it has found numerous applications in pseudorandomness and cryptography for at least two decades [49]. Our work adds to the recently growing body of work that uses min-entropy to prove communication complexity results [17].

通信复杂性中的熵。信息复杂性如今已成为通信复杂性中一个成熟的分支领域，它利用香农熵（Shannon entropy）来衡量两方通信协议中的信息交换量。信息复杂性的概念由Chakrabarti等人[15]首次提出，随后在文献[8]中被系统性地用于解决多个问题。据我们所知，最小熵（min-entropy）在通信复杂性中的应用直到最近才出现$\left\lbrack  {{25},{26}}\right\rbrack$，尽管它在伪随机性和密码学领域已有至少二十年的广泛应用[49]。我们的研究为近期利用最小熵证明通信复杂性结果的工作增添了新内容[17]。

## 8 FUTURE WORK

## 8 未来工作

We leave the following questions as future work: handling node failures, finding optimal assignments of functions to players in $G$ ,identifying the optimal topology for a given query and finally, closing the gap between our upper and lower bounds for $d$ -degenerate graphs for super-constant $d$ . We cannot (yet) handle the condition that $N$ has to be larger than the size of $G$ . We address the assumptions on the knowledge of $q$ and $G$ and the assumption that the input functions are completely assigned to players in $G$ in the full paper [38].

我们将以下问题留作未来研究方向：处理节点故障、在$G$中寻找函数对参与者的最优分配、为给定查询确定最佳拓扑结构，以及缩小超常数$d$退化图的上界与下界之间的差距。目前尚无法处理$N$必须大于$G$规模的条件。关于$q$和$G$的认知假设，以及输入函数完全分配给$G$中参与者的假设，我们将在完整论文[38]中予以探讨。

### A.1 Basic MPC model

### A.1 基础MPC模型

We formally define the MPC model used in [9] and the model adopted by [2] here, both in the language of our model (Model 2.1). We consider the MPC with no replication, which is known as the basic MPC model in the literature.

我们在此用模型2.1的表述方式，正式定义文献[9]采用的MPC模型及文献[2]采用的模型。考虑无复制的MPC，即文献中称为基础MPC的模型。

MODEL A. 1 (MPC(0)). We are given a query $q$ and its underlying hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\overline{\mathcal{V}},\overline{\mathcal{E}}}\right)$ with input functions ${f}_{e}$ having at most $N$ non-zero values for every $e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$ . We consider the network topology ${G}^{\prime }$ with $p + k$ nodes,defined as follows. There are $k$ nodes,each assigned a function ${f}_{e}$ for every $e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$ . We call this set $K$ . There are no edges between any pair of nodes in $K$ . All nodes in $K$ are directly connected by an edge to every node in a clique with $p$ nodes that are disjoint from $K$ (also a part of ${G}^{\prime }$ ). Each vnode in $K$ has capacity $N$ and all the remaining nodes have capacity $L$ . The capacity of a node bounds the number of bits it can receive in each round. Given this setup, we would like to compute BCQ (and more generally an FAQ) of $\mathcal{H}$ on ${G}^{\prime }$ .

模型A.1（MPC(0)）。给定查询$q$及其底层超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\overline{\mathcal{V}},\overline{\mathcal{E}}}\right)$，其中每个$e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$对应的输入函数${f}_{e}$至多有$N$个非零值。网络拓扑${G}^{\prime }$包含$p + k$个节点，定义如下：存在$k$个节点，每个节点为每个$e \in  \overline{\mathcal{E}}$分配函数${f}_{e}$，该集合记为$K$。$K$内节点间无连接边，而$K$中所有节点均通过边与一个含$p$个节点的团（与$K$不相交，也属于${G}^{\prime }$）直接相连。$K$内各虚拟节点容量为$N$，其余节点容量为$L$。节点容量限制其每轮可接收的比特数。在此设置下，我们需要在${G}^{\prime }$上计算BCQ（广义上是$\mathcal{H}$的FAQ）。

To design a protocol for this computation, we can assume that every node in ${G}^{\prime }$ has the knowledge of $q$ and ${G}^{\prime }$ . At the end of the protocol,a pre-determined player in $K$ knows the answer to $q$ .

设计计算协议时，可假定${G}^{\prime }$中每个节点知晓$q$和${G}^{\prime }$。协议结束时，$K$中指定参与者将获知$q$的答案。

Finally, given the above setup, our goal is to design protocols that minimize the number of rounds to compute $q$ on $G$ . This model does not take into account the internal computation done by the $p + k$ nodes and assumes the nodes co-operatively compute the answer to $q$ .

最终目标是在上述设置下，设计能最小化$G$上计算$q$所需轮数的协议。该模型不考虑$p + k$节点的内部计算，并假设节点协同计算$q$的答案。

We now summarize the differences of this model from ours. A.1.1 Differences from our Model.

现总结该模型与我们模型的差异：A.1.1 与本模型的区别

- MPC(0) assumes a specific choice of a network topology ${G}^{\prime }$ as opposed to general topology $G$ in our model.

- MPC(0)假设特定网络拓扑${G}^{\prime }$，而本模型采用通用拓扑$G$。

- MPC(0) assumes a specific assignment of functions in $\overline{\mathcal{E}}$ to players in ${G}^{\prime }$ . Our upper bound techniques can handle any assignment but our lower bounds are for a specific class of assignments. We would like to mention here that this is true for the models in $\left\lbrack  {2,9}\right\rbrack$ as well (i.e., upper bounds can handly any assignment whereas lower bounds are for a specific class of assignments) and we consider one such assignment in Model A.1.

- $\left\lbrack  {2,9}\right\rbrack$中的模型设计协议时，其轮次数量要么是常数，要么是$k$的函数。而我们模型的轮次数量是$N$的函数。
