## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

Motivated by fundamental applications in databases and relational machine learning, we formulate and study the problem of answering functional aggregate queries (FAQ) in which some of the input factors are defined by a collection of additive inequalities between variables. We refer to these queries as FAQ-AI for short.

受数据库和关系机器学习基础应用的启发，我们提出并研究了功能聚合查询（FAQ）的应答问题，其中部分输入因子由变量间的加法不等式集合定义。这类查询简称为FAQ-AI。

To answer FAQ-AI in the Boolean semiring, we define relaxed tree decompositions and relaxed submodular and fractional hypertree width parameters. We show that an extension of the InsideOut algorithm using Chazelle's geometric data structure for solving the semigroup range search problem can answer Boolean FAQ-AI in time given by these new width parameters. This new algorithm achieves lower complexity than known solutions for FAQ-AI. It also recovers some known results in database query answering.

为在布尔半环中应答FAQ-AI，我们定义了松弛树分解、松弛次模及分数超树宽度参数。通过扩展InsideOut算法并运用Chazelle几何数据结构解决半群区间搜索问题，该算法能在新宽度参数对应时间内完成布尔FAQ-AI应答。新算法较现有方案具有更低复杂度，同时复现了数据库查询应答中的若干已知结论。

Our second contribution is a relaxation of the set of poly-matroids that gives rise to the counting version of the submod-ular width, denoted by #subw. This new width is sandwiched between the submodular and the fractional hypertree widths. Any FAQ and FAQ-AI over one semiring can be answered in time proportional to #subw and respectively to the relaxed version of #subw.

第二项贡献是放宽多拟阵集合定义，由此导出次模宽度的计数版本#subw。该新宽度介于次模宽度与分数超树宽度之间。任何单半环上的FAQ及FAQ-AI均可分别在#subw及其松弛版本对应时间内应答。

We present three applications of our FAQ-AI framework to relational machine learning: $k$ -means clustering,training linear support vector machines, and training models using non-polynomial loss. These optimization problems can be solved over a database asymptotically faster than computing the join of the database relations.

我们展示了FAQ-AI框架在关系机器学习中的三个应用：$k$-均值聚类、线性支持向量机训练、以及使用非多项式损失函数训练模型。这些优化问题在数据库上的求解速度渐近快于计算数据库关系的连接操作。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

We consider the problem of computing functional aggregate queries with inequality joins, or FAQ-AI queries for short. This is a fundamental computational problem that goes beyond databases: core computation for supervised and unsupervised machine learning can be formulated in FAQ-AI.

本文研究带不等式连接的功能聚合查询（简称FAQ-AI）计算问题。这是超越数据库范畴的基础计算问题：监督与非监督机器学习的核心计算均可表述为FAQ-AI形式。

Inequalities occur naturally in scenarios involving temporal and spatial relationships between objects in databases. In a retail scenario (e.g., TPC-H), we would like to compute the revenue generated by a customer's orders whose dates closely precede the ship dates of their lineitems. In streaming scenarios, we would like to detect patterns of events whose time stamps follow a particular order [13]. In spatial data management scenarios, we would like to retrieve objects whose coordinates are within a multi-dimensional range or in close proximity of other objects [25]. The evaluation of Core XPath queries over XML documents amounts to the evaluation of conjunctive queries with inequalities expressing tree relationships in the pre/post plane [16].

不等式天然存在于涉及数据库对象时空关系的场景中。零售场景（如TPC-H）需计算客户订单日期紧邻货品发货日期所产生的收入；流处理场景需检测时间戳符合特定顺序的事件模式[13]；空间数据管理场景需检索位于多维区间或邻近其他对象的坐标[25]。XML文档Core XPath查询的求值可转化为在pre/post平面表达树形关系的合取查询不等式求值[16]。

### 1.1 Motivating examples

### 1.1 动机案例

A key insight of this paper is that the efficient computation of inequality joins can reduce the computational complexity of supervised and unsupervised machine learning.

本文核心观点是：高效计算不等式连接可降低监督与非监督机器学习的计算复杂度。

Example 1.1. The $k$ -means algorithm divides the input dataset $G$ into $k$ clusters of similar data points [19]. Each cluster ${\mathbf{G}}_{i}$ has a mean ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ ,which is chosen according to the following optimization (similarity is defined here with respect to the ${\ell }_{2}$ norm):

示例1.1：$k$-均值算法将输入数据集$G$划分为$k$个相似数据点的簇[19]。每个簇${\mathbf{G}}_{i}$具有均值${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$，其选择遵循以下优化准则（此处相似性基于${\ell }_{2}$范数定义）：

$$
\mathop{\min }\limits_{\left( {G}_{1},\ldots ,{G}_{k}\right) }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\mathbf{x} \in  {G}_{i}}}{\begin{Vmatrix}\mathbf{x} - {\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}\end{Vmatrix}}_{2}^{2}. \tag{1}
$$

Let ${\mu }_{i,\ell }$ be the $\ell$ ’th component of mean vector ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$ . For a data point $\mathbf{x} \in  G$ ,the function ${c}_{ij}$ computes the difference between the squares of the ${\ell }_{2}$ -distances from $\mathbf{x}$ to ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$ and from $\mathbf{x}$ to ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{j}$ :

设${\mu }_{i,\ell }$为均值向量${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$的第$\ell$个分量。对于数据点$\mathbf{x} \in  G$，函数${c}_{ij}$计算$\mathbf{x}$到${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$与$\mathbf{x}$到${\mathbf{\mu }}_{j}$的${\ell }_{2}$-距离平方差：

$$
{c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}\left\lbrack  {{\mu }_{i,\ell }^{2} - 2{x}_{\ell }\left( {{\mu }_{i,\ell } - {\mu }_{j,\ell }}\right)  - {\mu }_{j,\ell }^{2}}\right\rbrack  .
$$

A data point $\mathbf{x} \in  G$ is closest to mean ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$ from the set of $k$ means iff $\forall j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack   : {c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \leq  0$ .

当且仅当$\forall j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack   : {c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \leq  0$时，数据点$\mathbf{x} \in  G$在$k$个均值集合中最接近均值${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$。

To compute the mean vector ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$ ,we need to compute the sum of values for each dimension $\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ over ${G}_{i} : \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {G}_{i}}}{x}_{\ell }$ . If the dataset $G$ is the join of database relations ${\left( {R}_{p}\right) }_{p \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }$ over schemas ${S}_{p} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\forall p \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$ ,we can formulate this sum computation as a datalog-like query with aggregates [17]:

为计算均值向量${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$，需对${G}_{i} : \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {G}_{i}}}{x}_{\ell }$中每个维度$\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$的值求和。若数据集$G$是模式${S}_{p} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\forall p \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack$上数据库关系${\left( {R}_{p}\right) }_{p \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }$的连接，可将该求和计算表述为带聚合运算的类datalog查询[17]：

$$
{Q}_{1}^{\left( i,\ell \right) }\left( {\sum {x}_{\ell }}\right)  \leftarrow  \left( {\mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{p \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }}{R}_{p}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{{S}_{p}}\right) }\right)  \land  \left( {\mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \leq  0}\right) .
$$

The above notation means that the answer to query ${Q}_{1}^{\left( i,\ell \right) }$ is the sum of ${x}_{\ell }$ over all tuples $\mathbf{x}$ satisfying the conjunction on the right-hand side. Section 4 gives further queries necessary to compute the $k$ -means. As we show in this paper,such queries with aggregates and inequalities can be computed asymptotically faster than the join defining $G$ .

上述表示法指：查询${Q}_{1}^{\left( i,\ell \right) }$的答案是满足右侧合取条件的所有元组$\mathbf{x}$的${x}_{\ell }$之和。第4节将给出计算$k$-均值所需的其他查询。如本文所示，这类含聚合与不等式的查询可渐进地比定义$G$的连接运算更快计算。

如下所示，简单查询已能凸显当前评估技术的局限性。

Example 1.2. State-of-the-art techniques take time $O\left( {N}^{2}\right)$ to compute the following query over relations of size $\leq  N$ :

示例1.2：现有最优技术对规模为$\leq  N$的关系计算下列查询需耗时$O\left( {N}^{2}\right)$：

$$
{Q}_{2}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {a,b}\right)  \land  S\left( {b,c}\right)  \land  T\left( {c,d}\right)  \land  a \leq  d,
$$

Examples 3.9 and 3.19 show how to compute ${Q}_{2}$ and its counting version in time $O\left( {{N}^{1.5}\log N}\right)$ using the techniques introduced in this paper.

示例3.9和3.19将展示如何运用本文技术，在$O\left( {{N}^{1.5}\log N}\right)$时间内计算${Q}_{2}$及其计数版本。

### 1.2 The FAQ-AI problem

### 1.2 FAQ-AI问题

One way to answer the above queries is to view them as functional aggregate queries (FAQ) [5] formulated in sum-product form over some semiring. We therefore briefly introduce FAQ over a single semiring.

回答上述查询的一种方式，是将其视为在半环上以和积形式表述的函数型聚合查询(FAQ)[5]。因此我们简要介绍单一半环上的FAQ。

First we establish notation. For any positive integer $n$ , let $\mathcal{V} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ . For $i \in  \mathcal{V}$ ,let ${X}_{i}$ denote a variable/attribute, and ${x}_{i}$ denote a value in the discrete domain $\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)$ of ${X}_{i}$ . For any $K \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ ,define ${\mathbf{X}}_{K} = {\left( {X}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  K},{\mathbf{x}}_{K} = {\left( {x}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  K} \in$ $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  K}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)$ . That is, ${\mathbf{X}}_{K}$ is a tuple of variables and ${\mathbf{x}}_{K}$ is a tuple of values for these variables.

首先建立符号约定。对于任意正整数$n$，令$\mathcal{V} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$。对于$i \in  \mathcal{V}$，令${X}_{i}$表示变量/属性，${x}_{i}$表示离散域$\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)$中的取值。对任意$K \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$，定义${\mathbf{X}}_{K} = {\left( {X}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  K},{\mathbf{x}}_{K} = {\left( {x}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  K} \in$$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  K}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)$。即${\mathbf{X}}_{K}$是变量元组，${\mathbf{x}}_{K}$是这些变量的取值元组。

Let a semiring $\left( {\mathbf{D},\oplus ,\otimes ,\mathbf{0},\mathbf{1}}\right)$ and a multi-hypergraph $\mathcal{H} =$ $\left( {\mathcal{V} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$ . To each edge $K \in  \mathcal{E}$ we associate a function ${R}_{K} : \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{v \in  K}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)  \rightarrow  D$ called factor. An FAQ query over one semiring with free variables $F \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ has the form:

给定半环$\left( {\mathbf{D},\oplus ,\otimes ,\mathbf{0},\mathbf{1}}\right)$和多重超图$\mathcal{H} =$$\left( {\mathcal{V} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$。为每条边$K \in  \mathcal{E}$关联一个称为因子的函数${R}_{K} : \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{v \in  K}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)  \rightarrow  D$。具有自由变量$F \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$的单半环FAQ查询形式为：

$$
Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right)  = {\bigoplus }_{{\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  F} \in  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  \mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  F}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right) }{\bigotimes }_{K \in  \mathcal{E}}{R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right) . \tag{2}
$$

Under the Boolean semiring (\{true,false\}, $\vee  , \land$ ,false,true), the query (2) becomes a conjunctive query: The factors ${R}_{K}$ represent input relations,where ${R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right)  =$ true iff ${\mathbf{x}}_{K} \in  {R}_{K}$ , with some notational overloading. Under the sum-product semiring, the query (2) counts the number of tuples in the join result for each tuple ${\mathbf{x}}_{F}$ ,where the factors ${R}_{k}$ are indicator functions ${R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right)  = {\mathbf{1}}_{{x}_{K} \in  {R}_{K}}$ . To aggregate over some input variable,say ${X}_{k}$ ,we can designate an identity factor ${R}_{k}\left( {x}_{k}\right)  = {x}_{k}$ .

在布尔半环(\{真,假\},$\vee  , \land$,假,真)下，查询(2)转化为合取查询：因子${R}_{K}$表示输入关系，其中${R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right)  =$为真当且仅当${\mathbf{x}}_{K} \in  {R}_{K}$（存在符号重载）。在求和-乘积半环下，查询(2)对连接结果中每个元组${\mathbf{x}}_{F}$进行计数，此时因子${R}_{k}$是指示函数${R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right)  = {\mathbf{1}}_{{x}_{K} \in  {R}_{K}}$。若需对输入变量（如${X}_{k}$）进行聚合，可指定恒等因子${R}_{k}\left( {x}_{k}\right)  = {x}_{k}$。

Throughout the paper, we assume the query size to be a constant and state runtimes in data complexity. It is known [5] that over an arbitrary semiring, the query (2) can be answered in time $O\left( {{N}^{\operatorname{fhtw}\left( Q\right) } \cdot  \log N}\right)$ ,where fhtw denotes the fractional hypertree width of the query and $Q$ has no free variables [15]. If $Q$ has free variables,fhtw-width becomes FAQ-width instead [5]. Here $N$ is the size of the largest factor ${R}_{K}$ . Over the Boolean semiring,the time can be lowered to $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{\operatorname{subw}\left( Q\right) }\right) \left\lbrack  7\right\rbrack$ ,where subw is the submodular width [26] and $\widetilde{O}$ hides a polylogarithmic factor in $N$ .

全文假设查询规模为常数，时间复杂度按数据复杂度表述。已知[5]在任意半环上，查询(2)可在$O\left( {{N}^{\operatorname{fhtw}\left( Q\right) } \cdot  \log N}\right)$时间内求解，其中fhtw表示查询的分数超树宽度，且$Q$不含自由变量[15]。若$Q$含自由变量，则fhtw宽度替换为FAQ宽度[5]。此处$N$表示最大因子${R}_{K}$的规模。在布尔半环下，时间复杂度可降至$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{\operatorname{subw}\left( Q\right) }\right) \left\lbrack  7\right\rbrack$，其中subw表示子模宽度[26]，$\widetilde{O}$隐含了$N$的多对数因子。

Motivated by the examples in Section 1.1, we formulate a class of FAQ queries called FAQ-AI: the hyperedge multiset $\mathcal{E}$ is partitioned into two multisets $\mathcal{E} = {\mathcal{E}}_{s} \cup  {\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }$ ,where $s$ stands for "skeleton" and $\ell$ stands for "ligament". The input to our class of queries consists of the following: (1) to each hyperedge $K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}$ ,there corresponds a function ${R}_{K} : \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  K}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)  \rightarrow  D$ ,as in the FAQ case; (2) to each hyperedge $S \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }$ ,there corresponds $\left| S\right|$ functions ${\theta }_{v}^{S}$ : $\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{v}\right)  \rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$ ,one for every variable $v \in  S$ . The query we want to compute is the following:

受1.1节示例启发，我们提出一类称为FAQ-AI的查询：超边多重集$\mathcal{E}$被划分为两个多重集$\mathcal{E} = {\mathcal{E}}_{s} \cup  {\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }$，其中$s$代表"骨架"而$\ell$代表"韧带"。这类查询的输入包含：(1) 每个超边$K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}$对应一个函数${R}_{K} : \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  K}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)  \rightarrow  D$（如FAQ情形）；(2) 每个超边$S \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }$对应$\left| S\right|$个函数${\theta }_{v}^{S}$：$\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{v}\right)  \rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$（每个变量$v \in  S$对应一个）。待计算的查询如下：

$$
Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right)  = {\bigoplus }_{{\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{V}} \smallsetminus  F}\left( {{\bigotimes }_{K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}}{R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right) }\right)  \otimes  \left( {{\bigotimes }_{S \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }}{\mathbf{1}}_{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{v \in  S}}{\theta }_{v}^{S}\left( {x}_{v}\right)  \leq  0}}\right) . \tag{3}
$$

The summation $\bigoplus$ is over tuples ${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  F} \in  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  \mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  F}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)$ . The notation ${\mathbf{1}}_{A}$ denotes the indicator function of the event $A$ in the semiring $\left( {D,\oplus ,\otimes ,0,1}\right)  : {\mathbf{1}}_{A} = \mathbf{1}$ if $A$ holds,and 0 otherwise. The (uni-variate) functions ${\theta }_{v}^{S}$ can be user-defined functions,e.g., ${\theta }_{1}^{S}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  = {x}_{1}^{2}/2$ ,or binary predicates with one key in $\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{v}\right)$ and a numeric value,e.g.,a table salary(employee_id, salary_value) where employee_id is a key. The only requirement we impose is that,given $x$ , the value ${\theta }_{v}^{S}\left( x\right)$ can be accessed/computed in $O\left( 1\right)$ -time.

求和式$\bigoplus$针对元组${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  F} \in  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  \mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  F}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{i}\right)$进行。记号${\mathbf{1}}_{A}$表示半环$\left( {D,\oplus ,\otimes ,0,1}\right)  : {\mathbf{1}}_{A} = \mathbf{1}$中事件$A$的指示函数（若$A$成立则为1，否则为0）。单变量函数${\theta }_{v}^{S}$可以是用户自定义函数（如${\theta }_{1}^{S}\left( {x}_{1}\right)  = {x}_{1}^{2}/2$），或是包含$\operatorname{Dom}\left( {X}_{v}\right)$中键值与数值的二元谓词（例如员工薪资表salary(employee_id, salary_value)，其中employee_id为键）。唯一要求是给定$x$时，值${\theta }_{v}^{S}\left( x\right)$能在$O\left( 1\right)$时间内被访问/计算。

If ${\mathcal{E}}_{\ell } = \varnothing$ ,then we get back the FAQ formulation (2).

若${\mathcal{E}}_{\ell } = \varnothing$，则回归到FAQ公式(2)。

Example 1.3. The queries in Section 1.1 are instances of (3):

例1.3. 1.1节中的查询均为公式(3)的实例：

$$
{Q}_{1}^{\left( i,\ell \right) }\left( )\right)  = {\bigoplus }_{{\mathbf{x}}_{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}{x}_{\ell } \otimes  \left( {{\bigotimes }_{p \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }{R}_{p}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{{S}_{p}}\right) }\right)  \otimes  \left( {{\bigotimes }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }{\mathbf{1}}_{{c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \leq  0}}\right) ,
$$

### 1.3 Our contributions

### 1.3 研究贡献

To answer FAQ queries of the form (2), currently there are two dominant width parameters: fractional hypertree width (fhtw [15]) and submodular width (subw [26]). ${}^{1}$ It is known that subw $\leq$ fhtw for any query,and in the Boolean semiring we can answer (2) in $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{\text{subw }}\right)$ -time [7,26]. For non-Boolean semirings, the best known algorithm, called InsideOut [5, 6], evaluates (2) in time $O\left( {{N}^{\text{fhtw }}\log N}\right)$ . For queries with free variables, fhtw is replaced by the more general notion of FAQ-width (faqw) [5]; however, for brevity we discuss the non-free variable case here.

针对形式(2)的FAQ查询，现有两个主流宽度参数：分数超树宽度(fhtw[15])和次模宽度(subw[26])。${}^{1}$已知对于任何查询都有subw$\leq$fhtw，在布尔半环中我们能在$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{\text{subw }}\right)$时间内求解(2)[7,26]。对于非布尔半环，最佳算法InsideOut[5,6]以$O\left( {{N}^{\text{fhtw }}\log N}\right)$时间计算(2)。对于含自由变量的查询，fhtw被更通用的FAQ宽度(faqw)[5]取代；但为简洁起见，本文仅讨论无自由变量情形。

Following [6], both width parameters subw and fhtw can be defined via two constraint sets: the first is the set TD of all tree decompositions of the query hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ ,and the second is the set of polymatroids ${\Gamma }_{n}$ on $n$ vertices of $\mathcal{H}$ . The widths subw and fhtw are then defined as maximin or minimax optimization problems on the domain pair TD and ${\Gamma }_{n}$ ,subject to "edge domination" constraints for ${\Gamma }_{n}$ . Section 2 presents these notions and other related preliminary concepts in detail.

依据文献[6]，宽度参数subw和fhtw可通过两个约束集定义：第一个是查询超图$\mathcal{H}$所有树分解的集合TD，第二个是基于$\mathcal{H}$顶点集$n$的多拟阵集合${\Gamma }_{n}$。这两个宽度参数随后被定义为定义域对TD与${\Gamma }_{n}$上的极大极小或极小化优化问题，并受限于${\Gamma }_{n}$的"边支配"约束条件。第2节将详细阐述这些概念及其他相关预备知识。

Our contributions include the following:

我们的贡献包括：

Answering FAQ-AI over Boolean semiring. On the Boolean semiring, one way to answer query (3) is to apply the PANDA algorithm [26],using edge domination constraints on ${\mathcal{E}}_{s}$ and the set TD of all tree decompositions of $\mathcal{H} = (\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E} =$ ${\mathcal{E}}_{s} \cup  {\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }$ ). However,we can do better. In Section 3.2 we define a new notion of tree decomposition: relaxed tree decomposition,in which the hyperedges in ${\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }$ only have to be covered by adjacent TD bags. Then, we present a variant of the InsideOut algorithm running on these relaxed TDs, using Chazelle's classic geometric data structure [10] for solving the semigroup range search problem. We show that our InsideOut variant meets the "relaxed fhtw" runtime, which is the analog of fhtw on relaxed TD. The PANDA algorithm can use the InsideOut variant as a blackbox to meet the "relaxed subw" runtime. The relaxed widths are smaller than the non-relaxed counterparts, and are strictly smaller for some classes of queries, which means our algorithms yield asymptotic improvements over existing ones.

布尔半环上的FAQ-AI求解。在布尔半环上，求解查询(3)的一种方法是应用PANDA算法[26]，利用${\mathcal{E}}_{s}$的边支配约束和$\mathcal{H} = (\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E} =$${\mathcal{E}}_{s} \cup  {\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }$所有树分解的集合TD。但我们提出了更优方案：第3.2节定义新型树分解概念——松弛树分解，其中${\mathcal{E}}_{\ell }$的超边仅需被相邻TD包覆盖。随后我们提出运行于这类松弛TD上的InsideOut算法变体，采用Chazelle经典几何数据结构[10]解决半群区间搜索问题。该变体算法能达到与松弛TD上fhtw对应的"松弛fhtw"时间复杂度。PANDA算法可将此变体作为黑盒实现"松弛subw"时间复杂度。松弛宽度参数总小于传统参数，且对特定查询类严格更优，这意味着我们的算法实现了对现有方案的渐进改进。

### 2.1 Tree decompositions and polymatroids

### 2.1 树分解与拟阵

We briefly define tree decompositions, fhtw and subw parameters. We refer the reader to the recent survey by Gottlob et al. [14] for more details and historical contexts. In what follows,the hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ should be thought of as the hy-pergraph of the input query, although the notions of tree decomposition and width parameters are defined independently of queries.

我们简要定义树分解、fhtw和subw参数，详情及历史背景可参阅Gottlob等人近期综述[14]。下文所述超图$\mathcal{H}$应视为输入查询的超图，尽管树分解与宽度参数的定义独立于具体查询。

A tree decomposition of a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ is a pair $\left( {T,\chi }\right)$ ,where $T$ is a tree and $\chi  : V\left( T\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\mathcal{V}}$ maps each node $t$ of the tree to a subset $\chi \left( t\right)$ of vertices such that

超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$的树分解是二元组$\left( {T,\chi }\right)$，其中$T$为树结构，$\chi  : V\left( T\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\mathcal{V}}$将每个树节点$t$映射到顶点子集$\chi \left( t\right)$，满足：

(1) every hyperedge $S \in  \mathcal{E}$ is a subset of some $\chi \left( t\right) ,t \in$ $V\left( T\right)$ (i.e. every edge is covered by some bag),

(1) 每条超边$S \in  \mathcal{E}$都是某个$\chi \left( t\right) ,t \in$$V\left( T\right)$的子集（即所有边都被某个包覆盖）；

(2) for every vertex $v \in  \mathcal{V}$ ,the set $\{ t \mid  v \in  \chi \left( t\right) \}$ is a non-empty (connected) sub-tree of $T$ . This is called the running intersection property.

(2) 对每个顶点$v \in  \mathcal{V}$，集合$\{ t \mid  v \in  \chi \left( t\right) \}$构成$T$的非空连通子树，称为运行相交性。

The sets $\chi \left( t\right)$ are called the bags of the tree decomposition. Let $\operatorname{TD}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ denote the set of all tree decompositions of $\mathcal{H}$ . When $\mathcal{H}$ is clear from context,we use TD for brevity.

集合$\chi \left( t\right)$称为树分解的包。用$\operatorname{TD}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$表示$\mathcal{H}$所有树分解的集合，上下文明确时简记作TD。

To define width parameters, we use the polymatroid characterization from Abo Khamis et al. [7]. A function $f : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow$ ${\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$ is called a (non-negative) set function on $\mathcal{V}$ . A set function $f$ on $\mathcal{V}$ is modular if $f\left( S\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{v \in  S}}f\left( {\{ v\} }\right)$ for all $S \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ , is monotone if $f\left( X\right)  \leq  f\left( Y\right)$ whenever $X \subseteq  Y$ ,and is submod-ular if $f\left( {X \cup  Y}\right)  + f\left( {X \cap  Y}\right)  \leq  f\left( X\right)  + f\left( Y\right)$ for all $X,Y \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ . A monotone,submodular set function $h : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$ with $h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$ is called a polymatroid. Let ${\Gamma }_{n}$ denote the set of all polymatroids on $\mathcal{V} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ .

为定义宽度参数，我们采用Abo Khamis等人[7]提出的多拟阵特征描述。若函数$f : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow$${\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$在$\mathcal{V}$上满足非负集函数条件，则称其为（非负）集函数。当$f$对所有$S \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$满足模性条件$f\left( S\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{v \in  S}}f\left( {\{ v\} }\right)$，对$X \subseteq  Y$满足单调性条件$f\left( X\right)  \leq  f\left( Y\right)$，且对所有$X,Y \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$满足次模性条件$f\left( {X \cup  Y}\right)  + f\left( {X \cap  Y}\right)  \leq  f\left( X\right)  + f\left( Y\right)$时，该集函数分别称为模函数、单调函数和次模函数。若$h : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$同时满足单调性、次模性及$h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$条件，则称为多拟阵。用${\Gamma }_{n}$表示$\mathcal{V} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$上所有多拟阵的集合。

Given $\mathcal{H}$ ,define the set of edge dominated set functions:

给定$\mathcal{H}$，定义边支配集函数集合：

$$
\mathrm{{ED}} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  {h \mid  h : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + },h\left( S\right)  \leq  1,\forall S \in  \mathcal{E}}\right\}  . \tag{5}
$$

With this, we define the submodular width and fractional hypertree width of a given hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ :

由此可定义给定超图$\mathcal{H}$的次模宽度与分数超树宽度：

$$
\text{fhtw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {T,\chi }\right)  \in  \mathrm{{TD}}}}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  \mathrm{{ED}} \cap  {\Gamma }_{n}}}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( T\right) }}h\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right) \text{,} \tag{6}
$$

$$
\operatorname{subw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  \mathrm{{ED}} \cap  {\Gamma }_{n}}}\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {T,\chi }\right)  \in  \mathrm{{TD}}}}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( T\right) }}h\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right) . \tag{7}
$$

It is known [26] that subw $\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \leq$ fhtw(H),and there are classes of hypergraphs with bounded subw and unbounded fhtw. Furthermore, fhtw is strictly less than other width notions such as (generalized) hypertree width and tree width.

已知[26]次模宽度subw$\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \leq$分数超树宽度fhtw(H)，且存在一类超图具有有界subw和无界fhtw的特性。此外，fhtw严格小于其他宽度概念，如（广义）超树宽度和树宽度。

Definition 2.2 (F-connex tree decomposition [8,31]). ### 2.2 InsideOut and PANDA

### 2.2 InsideOut与PANDA算法

To answer the FAQ query (2), we need a model for the representation of the input factors ${R}_{K}$ . The support of the function ${R}_{K}$ is the set of tuples ${\mathbf{x}}_{K}$ such that $R\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right)  \neq  \mathbf{0}$ . We use $\left| {R}_{K}\right|$ to denote the size of its support. For example,if ${R}_{K}$ represents an input relation,then $\left| {R}_{K}\right|$ is the number of tuples in ${R}_{K}$ . In practice,there often are factors with infinite support,e.g., ${R}_{K}$ represents a built-in function in a database,an arithmetic operator, or a comparison operator as in (3). To deal with this more general setting,the edge set $\mathcal{E}$ is partitioned into two sets $\mathcal{E} = {\mathcal{E}}_{\infty } \cup  {\mathcal{E}}_{\infty }$ ,where $\left| {R}_{K}\right|$ is finite for all $K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{\infty }$ and $\left| {R}_{K}\right|  = \infty$ for all $K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{\infty }$ . For simplicity, we often state runtimes of algorithms in terms of the "input size" $N \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\max }\limits_{{K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{\infty }}}\left| {R}_{K}\right|$ . ### 2.3 Semigroup range searching

### 2.3 半群范围搜索

Orthogonal range counting (and searching) is a classic and ubiquitous problem in computational geometry [12]: given a set $S$ of $N$ points in a $d$ -dimensional space,build a data structure that,given any $d$ -dimensional rectangle,can efficiently return the number of enclosed points. More generally, there is the semigroup range searching problem [10], where each point $\mathbf{p} \in  S$ of the $N$ input points also has a weight $w\left( \mathbf{p}\right)  \in  G$ ,where $\left( {G, \oplus  }\right)$ is a semigroup. ${}^{2}$ The problem is: given a $d$ -dimensional rectangle $R$ ,compute ${\bigoplus }_{\mathbf{p} \in  S \cap  R}w\left( \mathbf{p}\right)$ .

正交范围计数（及搜索）是计算几何学中的经典普适性问题[12]：给定$d$维空间中包含$N$个点的集合$S$，需构建一个数据结构，使其能针对任意$d$维矩形高效返回所包围的点数。更广义的半群范围搜索问题[10]中，每个输入点$\mathbf{p} \in  S$还具有权重$w\left( \mathbf{p}\right)  \in  G$（其中$\left( {G, \oplus  }\right)$为半群）。${}^{2}$该问题的目标是：给定$d$维矩形$R$，计算${\bigoplus }_{\mathbf{p} \in  S \cap  R}w\left( \mathbf{p}\right)$。

Classic results by Chazelle [10] show that there are data structures for semigroup range searching which can be constructed in time $O\left( {N{\log }^{d - 1}N}\right)$ ,and answer rectangular queries in $O\left( {{\log }^{d - 1}N}\right)$ -time. Also,this is almost the best we can hope for [11]. There are more recent improvements to Chazelle's result (see, e.g., Chan et al. [9]), but they are minor (at most a log factor), as the original results were already very close to matching the lower bound.

Chazelle[10]的经典研究表明，存在可在$O\left( {N{\log }^{d - 1}N}\right)$时间内构建的半群范围搜索数据结构，其矩形查询响应时间为$O\left( {{\log }^{d - 1}N}\right)$。此外，这几乎是我们能达到的最佳性能[11]。虽然已有对Chazelle结果的改进研究（如Chan等人[9]），但由于原始结果已非常接近下界，这些改进均属微小优化（至多对数级别）。

Most of these range search/counting problems can be reduced to the dominance range searching problem (on semi-groups),where the query is represented by a point $\mathbf{q}$ ,and the objective is to return ${\bigoplus }_{q \leq  p,p \in  S}w\left( p\right)$ . Here, $\leq$ denotes the "dominance" relation (coordinate-wise $\leq$ ). We can think of $q$ as the lower-corner of an infinite rectangle query.

多数范围搜索/计数问题可归约为（半群上的）支配范围搜索问题，其中查询由点$\mathbf{q}$表示，目标是返回${\bigoplus }_{q \leq  p,p \in  S}w\left( p\right)$。此处$\leq$表示"支配"关系（坐标维度的$\leq$）。我们可以将$q$视为无限矩形查询的左下角。

Equipped with this basic case, we can now proceed to solve the general setting of (3). To this end, we define a new width parameter.

- The running intersection property holds: for each node $v \in  \mathcal{V}$ the set $\{ t \in  V\left( T\right)  \mid  v \in  \chi \left( t\right) \}$ is a connected subtree in $T$ .

- 满足运行相交性：对每个节点$v \in  \mathcal{V}$，集合$\{ t \in  V\left( T\right)  \mid  v \in  \chi \left( t\right) \}$在$T$中构成连通子树。

3.2.2 布尔半环上的FAQ-AI。在解释如何调整PANDA算法求解布尔半环上的FAQ-AI查询前，我们通过示例进行原理说明。

Example 3.9. Consider the following FAQ-AI:

示例3.9。考虑以下FAQ-AI查询：

$$

There are two relations in the head $U$ and $W$ ,and they form a solution to the rule iff the following holds: if(a,b,c,d) satisfies the body,then either $\left( {a,b,c}\right)  \in  U$ or $\left( {b,c,d}\right)  \in  W$ . Via information-theoretic inequalities [7], we are able to show that PANDA can compute a solution(U,W)to the above disjunctive Datalog rule in time $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{1.5}\right)$ . 头部存在两个关系$U$和$W$，当满足以下条件时它们构成该规则的解：若(a,b,c,d)满足主体，则必有$\left( {a,b,c}\right)  \in  U$或$\left( {b,c,d}\right)  \in  W$成立。通过信息论不等式[7]，我们可证明PANDA能在$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{1.5}\right)$时间内计算出上述析取Datalog规则的解(U,W)。特别地，$\left| U\right|$和$\left| W\right|$均受${N}^{1.5}$约束。

Given the solution(U,W)to(30),it is straightforward to verify that the following also holds, using the distributivity of $\vee$ over $\land$ :

给定(30)式的解(U,W)，利用$\vee$对$\land$的分配律，可直接验证下式成立：

$$
\left( {R\left( {a,b}\right)  \land  W\left( {b,c,d}\right) }\right)  \vee  \left( {U\left( {a,b,c}\right)  \land  T\left( {c,d}\right) }\right)

$$

$$

By semijoin-reducing $W$ against $S,T$ ,and semjoin-reducing $U$ against $R,S$ ,we conclude that

通过将$W$对$S,T$进行半连接归约，并将$U$对$R,S$进行半连接归约，我们得出：

$$
\left( {R\left( {a,b}\right)  \land  W\left( {b,c,d}\right) }\right)  \vee  \left( {U\left( {a,b,c}\right)  \land  T\left( {c,d}\right) }\right)  \equiv

$$

$$

Finally, we have a rewrite of the original body:

最终，我们对原主体进行如下重写：

$$
\left\lbrack  {R\left( {a,b}\right)  \land  W\left( {b,c,d}\right)  \land  a \leq  d}\right\rbrack   \vee  \left\lbrack  {U\left( {a,b,c}\right)  \land  T\left( {c,d}\right)  \land  a \leq  d}\right\rbrack

$$
 \equiv  R\left( {a,b}\right)  \land  S\left( {b,c}\right)  \land  T\left( {c,d}\right)  \land  a \leq  d. \tag{32}
$$

By defining intermediate rules,we can compute $Q$ from them:

通过定义中间规则，可从中计算出$Q$：

$$
{Q}_{1}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {a,b}\right)  \land  W\left( {b,c,d}\right)  \land  a \leq  d, \tag{33}
$$

$$
{Q}_{2}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  U\left( {a,b,c}\right)  \land  T\left( {c,d}\right)  \land  a \leq  d, \tag{34}

$$
Q\left( \right)  \leftarrow  {Q}_{1}\left( \right)  \vee  {Q}_{2}\left( \right) \text{.} \tag{35}
$$

${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are of the form (14),and thus they each can be answered in $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{1.5}\right)$ -time (since $\left| U\right| ,\left| W\right|  \leq  {N}^{1.5}$ ). This implies that $Q$ can be answered in $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{1.5}\right)$ -time overall.

${Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{2}$符合(14)式形式，因此各自可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{1.5}\right)$时间内求解（因$\left| U\right| ,\left| W\right|  \leq  {N}^{1.5}$）。这意味着$Q$整体可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{1.5}\right)$时间内求解。

### 3.3 Relaxed polymatroids

### 3.3 松弛多拟阵

A key step in the proof of Theorem 3.11 is to find the Boolean tensor decomposition (72) of the product over ${R}_{K}$ . In a non-Boolean semiring, this becomes a tensor decomposition on this semiring:

定理3.11证明的关键步骤是找到${R}_{K}$上乘积的布尔张量分解(72)。在非布尔半环中，这转化为该半环上的张量分解：

$$
{\bigotimes }_{K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}}{R}_{K} = {\bigoplus }_{\left( {T,\chi }\right)  \in  {\operatorname{TD}}_{F}^{\ell }}{\bigotimes }_{t \in  V\left( T\right) }{S}_{\chi \left( t\right) }^{\left( T,\chi \right) }. \tag{37}
$$

In order to compute this tensor decomposition, we can still follow the script of the proof of Theorem 3.11, working on the parameter space of the input factors ${R}_{K}$ ; however,for the equality in (37) to hold (it is an identity over the value-space of the factors), we have to ensure the following property:

为计算此张量分解，我们仍可遵循定理3.11证明的流程，在输入因子${R}_{K}$的参数空间上操作；但要使(37)式等式成立（该等式是因子值空间上的恒等式），必须确保以下性质：

For any ${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{V}}$ s.t. ${\bigotimes }_{K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}}{R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right)  \neq  \mathbf{0}$ ,there is exactly one tree decomposition $\left( {T,\chi }\right)  \in  {\mathrm{{TD}}}_{F}^{\ell }$ for which

对于任意满足${\bigotimes }_{K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}}{R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right)  \neq  \mathbf{0}$的${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{V}}$，存在唯一的树分解$\left( {T,\chi }\right)  \in  {\mathrm{{TD}}}_{F}^{\ell }$使得：

$$
{\bigotimes }_{t \in  V\left( T\right) }{S}_{\chi \left( t\right) }^{\left( T,\chi \right) }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\chi \left( t\right) }\right)  = {\bigotimes }_{K \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}}{R}_{K}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{K}\right) , \tag{38}
$$

while for the other TDs, the left-hand side above is 0 . Essentially, the property ensures that we do not have to perform inclusion-exclusion (IE) over the tree decompositions in ${\mathrm{{TD}}}_{F}^{\ell } \cdot  {}^{4}$ We do not know how to ensure this property in general. However, under a relaxed notion of polymatroids, the property above holds. Since this idea applies to FAQ queries in general, we start with our result on FAQ queries first, before specializing it to FAQ-AI.

而对于其他树分解TD，上式左侧为0。该性质本质上确保我们无需在${\mathrm{{TD}}}_{F}^{\ell } \cdot  {}^{4}$上对树分解进行容斥(IE)运算。我们尚不知如何保证该性质普遍成立。但在松弛多拟阵概念下，上述性质成立。由于此思想适用于一般FAQ查询，我们首先给出FAQ查询的结果，再专门针对FAQ-AI进行特化。

3.3.1 FAQ over an arbitrary semiring. To explain how we can guarantee the property (38) for an FAQ query over an arbitrary semiring, consider the following example. Suppose that we would like to evaluate the (aggregate) query

We write ${R}_{ij}$ instead of ${R}_{ij}\left( {{x}_{i},{x}_{j}}\right)$ for short. 对于"4-环"查询(39)仅存在两种非平凡树分解：一种包含包$\{ 1,2,3\}$和$\{ 3,4,1\}$，另一种包含包$\{ 1,2,4\}$和$\{ 2,3,4\} {.}^{5}$。查询评估时，我们首先在支撑集上求解关系方程(37)，即需要找到满足条件的关系${S}_{123},{S}_{341}$、${S}_{234}$和${S}_{412}$：

Let $N = \max \left\{  {\left| {R}_{12}\right| ,\left| {R}_{23}\right| }\right.$ , $\left. {\left| {R}_{34}\right| ,\left| {R}_{41}\right| }\right\}$ . 上述方案不适用于通用FAQ查询，因为我们无法保证PANDA返回的关系解满足(38)。（若能实现，则次模宽度的概念将适用于#CSP而不仅限于CSP。）但我们可以证明，在放宽的多拟阵定义及相应"锐次模(FAQ)宽度"条件下，该策略有效（即能确保(38)）。

Definition 3.12. ## 4 APPLICATIONS TO RELATIONAL MACHINE LEARNING

## 4 关系型机器学习的应用

Our FAQ-AI formalism and solution are directly applicable to learning a class of machine learning models, which includes supervised models (e.g., robust regression, SVM classification),and unsupervised models (e.g.,clustering via $k$ -means). In this section, we show that the core computation of these optimization problems can be formulated in FAQ-AI over the sum-product semiring.

我们的FAQ-AI形式化方法及解决方案可直接应用于学习一类机器学习模型，这类模型包括监督式模型（如鲁棒回归、支持向量机分类）和无监督式模型（如通过$k$-均值进行聚类）。本节将证明，这些优化问题的核心计算可转化为基于和积半环的FAQ-AI问题。

### 4.1 Training ML models over databases

### 4.1 基于数据库的机器学习模型训练

A typical machine learning model is learned over a training dataset $G$ . We consider the common scenario where the input data is a relational database $\mathbf{I}$ ,and the training dataset $G$ is the result of a feature extraction join query $Q$ over $I\left\lbrack  {2,3,{18},{24}}\right\rbrack$ . Each tuple $\left( {x,y}\right)  \in  G$ consists of a vector of features $x$ and a label $y$ . We consider that the feature extraction query $Q$ has the hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},{\mathcal{E}}_{s}}\right)$ ,where ${\mathcal{E}}_{s}$ is the set of its skeleton hyperedges.

典型的机器学习模型通过训练数据集$G$学习得到。我们考虑输入数据为关系数据库$\mathbf{I}$的常见场景，其中训练数据集$G$是对$I\left\lbrack  {2,3,{18},{24}}\right\rbrack$执行特征提取连接查询$Q$的结果。每个元组$\left( {x,y}\right)  \in  G$包含特征向量$x$和标签$y$。我们认为特征提取查询$Q$具有超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},{\mathcal{E}}_{s}}\right)$结构，其中${\mathcal{E}}_{s}$是其骨架超边集合。

A supervised machine learning model is a function ${f}_{\mathbf{\beta }}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ with parameters $\mathbf{\beta }$ that is used to predict the label $y$ for unlabeled data. The parameters are obtained by minimizing the objective function:

监督式机器学习模型是带参数$\mathbf{\beta }$的函数${f}_{\mathbf{\beta }}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$，用于预测未标注数据的标签$y$。这些参数通过最小化目标函数获得：

$$
J\left( \mathbf{\beta }\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\left( {\mathbf{x},y}\right)  \in  G}}\mathcal{L}\left( {y,{f}_{\mathbf{\beta }}\left( \mathbf{x}\right) }\right)  + {\lambda \Omega }\left( \mathbf{\beta }\right) , \tag{53}
$$

where $\mathcal{L}\left( {a,b}\right)$ is a loss function, $\Omega$ is a regularizer,e.g., ${\ell }_{1}$ or ${\ell }_{2}$ norm,and the constant $\lambda  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$ controls the influence of regularization.

其中$\mathcal{L}\left( {a,b}\right)$是损失函数，$\Omega$是正则化项（例如${\ell }_{1}$范数或${\ell }_{2}$范数），常数$\lambda  \in  \left( {0,1}\right)$控制正则化强度。

Previous work has shown that for polynomial loss functions,such as square loss $\mathcal{L}\left( {a,b}\right)  = {\left( a - b\right) }^{2}$ ,the core computation for optimizing the objective $J\left( \mathbf{\beta }\right)$ amounts to FAQ evaluation [3]. In many instances, however, the loss function is non-polynomial, either due to the structure of the loss, or the presence of non-polynomial components embedded within the model structure (e.g., ReLU activation function in neural nets) [27].

已有研究表明，对于平方损失$\mathcal{L}\left( {a,b}\right)  = {\left( a - b\right) }^{2}$等多项式损失函数，优化目标$J\left( \mathbf{\beta }\right)$的核心计算可归结为FAQ评估[3]。然而在许多情况下，由于损失函数结构或模型内嵌的非多项式组件（如神经网络中的ReLU激活函数），损失函数呈现非多项式特性[27]。

Examples of commonly used non-polynomial loss functions are: (1) hinge loss, used to learn classification models like linear support vector machines (SVM) [27], or generalized low rank models (glrm) with boolean principal component analysis (PCA) [33]; (2) Huber loss, used to learn regression models that are robust to outliers [27]; (3) scalene loss, used to learn quantile regression models [33]; (4) epsilon insensitive loss, used to learn SVM regression models [27]; and (5) ordinal hinge loss, used to learn ordinal regression models or ordinal PCA (another glrm) [33].

常用非多项式损失函数包括：(1) 铰链损失（用于训练线性支持向量机SVM[27]或布尔主成分分析PCA的广义低秩模型glrm[33]）；(2) Huber损失（用于构建抗异常值的回归模型[27]）；(3) 斜边损失（用于分位数回归模型[33]）；(4) ε不敏感损失（用于SVM回归模型[27]）；(5) 序数铰链损失（用于序数回归模型或序数PCA[33]）。

Any optimization problem with the above non-polynomial loss functions can benefit from our evaluation algorithm for FAQ-AI by reformulating computations in the optimization algorithm as FAQ-AI expressions over the feature extraction join query $Q$ . We next exemplify this reformulation for two such problems: (1) learning a robust linear regression model using Huber loss, which can be solved with gradient-descent optimization, and (2) learning a linear support vector machine (SVM) for binary classification using hinge loss, which can be solved with subgradient-based optimization algorithms or with a cutting-plane algorithm for the primal formulation of linear SVM classification. The full version [1] details the cases of the scalene, epsilon insensitive, and ordinal hinge loss functions.

通过将优化算法中的计算重构为基于特征提取连接查询$Q$的FAQ-AI表达式，上述所有非多项式损失函数的优化问题都能受益于我们的FAQ-AI评估算法。接下来我们以两个典型问题为例说明这种重构：(1) 使用Huber损失训练鲁棒线性回归模型（可通过梯度下降优化求解）；(2) 使用铰链损失训练二分类线性支持向量机SVM（可采用次梯度优化算法或切割平面算法求解）。完整版论文[1]详述了斜边损失、ε不敏感损失和序数铰链损失的情况。

We also consider $k$ -means unsupervised clustering and give an FAQ-AI reformulation of the computation done in an iteration of the algorithm over the dataset $G$ .

我们还考虑$k$-均值无监督聚类，并给出该算法在数据集$G$上单次迭代计算的FAQ-AI重构形式。

The advantage of FAQ-AI reformulation is that the FAQ-AI expressions for the aforementioned optimization problems can be evaluated over relaxed tree decompositions of the feature extraction query $Q$ and do not require the explicit materialization of its result $G$ . The size of and time to compute $G$ is $O\left( {\left| I\right| }^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( Q\right) }\right)$ [29]. The solution to these optimization problems can be computed in time sub-linear in the size of $G$ ,using InsideOut or #PANDA.

FAQ-AI重构的优势在于：针对前述优化问题的FAQ-AI表达式可在特征提取查询$Q$的松弛树分解上求值，无需显式物化其结果$G$。计算$G$的时间复杂度为$O\left( {\left| I\right| }^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( Q\right) }\right)$[29]，而利用InsideOut或#PANDA算法求解这些优化问题的时间复杂度可低于$G$规模的线性阶。

### 4.3 线性支持向量机

A linear SVM classification model is used for binary classification problems where the label $y \in  \{  \pm  1\}$ . Hinge loss is non-differentiable, and thus standard gradient descent optimization is not applicable. We next discuss two alternative approaches for solving this optimization.

The first approach is based on the observation that the loss function is convex, and the objective admits subgradient vectors, which generalize the standard notion of gradient. The optimization problem can be solved with subgradient based updates. Pegasos is a well-know algorithm for this approach [32].

第一种方法基于该损失函数是凸函数的观察，且目标函数存在次梯度向量（推广了标准梯度概念）。可通过基于次梯度的更新解决优化问题。Pegasos是该方法的经典算法[32]。

The alternative approach is to solve the primal formulation of the problem, which avoids the non-differentiable objective by turning it into a constraint optimization problem with slack variables. Joachims proposed a cutting-plane algorithm which solves this optimization problem efficiently [20].

第二种方法是求解问题的原始形式，通过引入松弛变量将其转化为约束优化问题以避免不可微目标。Joachims提出的切割平面算法能高效求解该优化问题[20]。

We next consider $k$ -means clustering,which is a popular example of an unsupervised machine learning algorithm.

接下来我们研究$k$均值聚类算法，这是无监督机器学习中的一个经典范例。

An unsupervised machine learning model is computed over a dataset $G \subseteq  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ ,for which each tuple $\mathbf{x} \in  G$ is a vector of features without a label. A clustering task aims to divide $G$ into $k$ clusters of "similar" data points with respect to the ${\ell }_{2}$ norm: $G = { \cup  }_{i = 1}^{k}{G}_{i}$ ,where $k$ is a given fixed positive integer. Each cluster ${G}_{i}$ is represented by a cluster mean ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ . One of the most ubiquitous clustering methods, Lloyd’s $k$ -means clustering algorithm (also known as the $k$ - means method), involves the optimization problem (1) with respect to the partition ${\left( {G}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$ and the $k$ means ${\left( {\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$ . Other norms or distance measures can be used, e.g., if we replace ${\ell }_{2}$ with ${\ell }_{1}$ -norm,then we get the $k$ -median problem. The subsequent development considers the ${\ell }_{2}$ -norm.

无监督机器学习模型在数据集$G \subseteq  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$上进行计算，其中每个元组$\mathbf{x} \in  G$都是无标签的特征向量。聚类任务旨在根据${\ell }_{2}$范数将$G$划分为$k$个"相似"数据点的簇：$G = { \cup  }_{i = 1}^{k}{G}_{i}$，其中$k$是给定的固定正整数。每个簇${G}_{i}$由簇均值${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i} \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$表示。最普遍的聚类方法之一——劳埃德(Lloyd)的$k$-均值聚类算法（也称为$k$-均值方法），涉及关于分区${\left( {G}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$和$k$个均值${\left( {\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$的优化问题(1)。也可以使用其他范数或距离度量，例如若将${\ell }_{2}$替换为${\ell }_{1}$-范数，则得到$k$-中位数问题。后续推导将采用${\ell }_{2}$-范数。

Lloyd's algorithm can be viewed as a special instantiation of the Expectation-Maximization (EM) algorithm. It iteratively computes two updating steps until convergence. First, it updates the cluster assignments for each ${\left( {G}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$ :

劳埃德算法可视为期望最大化(EM)算法的特例，通过迭代执行两个更新步骤直至收敛：首先更新每个${\left( {G}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$的簇分配：

$$
{G}_{i} = \left\{  {\mathbf{x} \in  G \mid  {\begin{Vmatrix}\mathbf{x} - {\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2} \leq  {\begin{Vmatrix}\mathbf{x} - {\mathbf{\mu }}_{j}\end{Vmatrix}}^{2},\forall j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  \smallsetminus \{ i\} }\right\}   \tag{66}
$$

and then it updates the corresponding $k$ -means ${\left( {\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$ :

随后更新对应的$k$个均值${\left( {\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$：

$$
{\mathbf{\mu }}_{i} = \frac{1}{\left| {G}_{i}\right| }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\mathbf{x} \in  {G}_{i}}}\mathbf{x}. \tag{67}
$$

Our observation is that we can reformulate the updating of the $k$ -means as FAQ-AI expressions,without explicitly computing the partitioning ${\left( {G}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$ . For a given set of $k$ - means ${\left( {\mathbf{\mu }}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$ ,let ${c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ be the following function:

我们的研究发现，无需显式计算分区${\left( {G}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$，可将$k$-均值更新重构为FAQ-AI表达式。对于给定的$k$个均值${\left( {\mathbf{\mu }}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }$集合，令${c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$为如下函数：

$$
{c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}\left\lbrack  {{\left( {x}_{\ell } - {\mu }_{i,\ell }\right) }^{2} - {\left( {x}_{\ell } - {\mu }_{j,\ell }\right) }^{2}}\right\rbrack  
$$

$$
 = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}\left\lbrack  {{\mu }_{i,\ell }^{2} - 2{x}_{\ell }\left( {{\mu }_{i,\ell } + {\mu }_{j,\ell }}\right)  - {\mu }_{j,\ell }^{2}}\right\rbrack  . \tag{68}
$$

where ${\mu }_{j,\ell }$ is the $\ell$ ’th component of mean vector ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{j}$ . A data point $\mathbf{x} \in  G$ is closest to center ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$ if and only if ${c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \leq  0$ holds $\forall j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ .

其中${\mu }_{j,\ell }$表示均值向量${\mathbf{\mu }}_{j}$的第$\ell$个分量。当且仅当${c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \leq  0$满足$\forall j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$时，数据点$\mathbf{x} \in  G$最接近中心${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$。

We use this inequality to reformulate the mean vector ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$ as $O\left( n\right)$ FAQ-AI expressions. First,we express $\left| {G}_{i}\right|$ as:

利用该不等式，我们将均值向量${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$重构为$O\left( n\right)$个FAQ-AI表达式。首先将$\left| {G}_{i}\right|$表示为：

$$
{Q}_{i}\left( \right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{\mathbf{x}}\left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{\mathbf{1}}_{{c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \leq  0}}\right) \left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) }\right) . \tag{69}
$$

Then,for each $\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,the sum $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {G}_{i}}}{x}_{\ell }$ can be reformulated in FAQ-AI as follows (similarly to (4)):

接着对于每个$\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，总和$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  {G}_{i}}}{x}_{\ell }$可按以下方式在FAQ-AI中重构（类似于式(4)）：

$$
{Q}_{i\ell }\left( \right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{\mathbf{x}}{x}_{\ell }\left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{j \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}{\mathbf{1}}_{{c}_{ij}\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \leq  0}}\right) \left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{F \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{s}}}{R}_{F}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{F}\right) }\right) . \tag{70}
$$

Each component ${\left( {\mu }_{i,\ell }\right) }_{\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ equals the division of ${Q}_{i\ell }$ by ${Q}_{i}$ .

每个分量${\left( {\mu }_{i,\ell }\right) }_{\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$等于${Q}_{i\ell }$除以${Q}_{i}$的值。

Overall,the mean vector ${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$ can be computed with $O\left( n\right)$ FAQ-AI expressions of the form (3).

总体而言，均值向量${\mathbf{\mu }}_{i}$可通过$O\left( n\right)$个形式如(3)的FAQ-AI表达式计算得出。

Theorem 4.4. Let $I$ be an input database where $N$ is the largest relation in $\mathbf{I}$ ,and $Q$ be a feature extraction query where $n$ is the number of its variables. Each iteration of Lloyd’s $k$ - means algorithm can be computed in time $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{\# {\operatorname{smfw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right) }\right)$ with #PANDA and in time $O\left( {{N}^{{\operatorname{faqw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right) }{\log }^{k - 1}N}\right)$ with InsideOut.

定理4.4。设$I$为输入数据库，其中$N$是$\mathbf{I}$中最大的关系，$Q$为特征提取查询，$n$表示其变量数。使用#PANDA时，Lloyd的$k$-均值算法每次迭代可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{\# {\operatorname{smfw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right) }\right)$时间内完成；使用InsideOut时则需$O\left( {{N}^{{\operatorname{faqw}}_{\ell }\left( Q\right) }{\log }^{k - 1}N}\right)$时间。
