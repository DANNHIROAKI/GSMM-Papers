Join evaluation is one of the most fundamental operations performed by database systems and arguably the most well-studied problem in the Database community. A staggering number of join algorithms have been developed, and commercial database engines use finely tuned join heuristics that take into account many factors including the selectivity of predicates, memory, IO, etc. However, most of the results have catered to either full join queries or non-full join queries but with degree constraints (such as PK-FK relationships) that makes join evaluation easier. Further, most of the algorithms are also not output-sensitive. In this paper, we present a novel, output-sensitive algorithm for the evaluation of acyclic Conjunctive Queries (CQs) that contain arbitrary free variables. Our result is based on a novel generalization of the Yannakakis algorithm and shows that it is possible to improve the running time guarantee of Yannakakis algorithm by a polynomial factor. Importantly, our algorithmic improvement does not depend on the use of fast matrix multiplication, as a recently proposed algorithm does. The application of our algorithm recovers known prior results and improves on known state-of-the-art results for common queries such as paths and stars. The upper bound is complemented with a matching lower bound for star queries, a restricted subclass of acyclic CQs, and a family of cyclic CQs conditioned on two variants of the $k$ -clique conjecture.

连接查询求值是数据库系统执行的最基本操作之一，也是数据库领域研究最深入的问题。已有大量连接算法被开发出来，商业数据库引擎采用精细调优的连接启发式策略，需考虑谓词选择性、内存、IO等诸多因素。然而多数成果要么针对全连接查询，要么针对带有使求值简化的度约束（如主键-外键关系）的非全连接查询。更重要的是，大多数算法不具备输出敏感性。本文提出一种新颖的、输出敏感的算法，用于求值含任意自由变量的无环合取查询（CQ）。我们的成果基于对Yannakakis算法的创新性推广，证明可通过多项式因子改进Yannakakis算法的时间复杂度保证。值得注意的是，我们的算法改进不依赖于快速矩阵乘法（这与最近提出的某算法不同）。本算法的应用不仅重现了已知先前结果，还对路径查询、星型查询等常见查询改进了现有最优结果。上界分析辅以针对星型查询（无环CQ的一个受限子类）的匹配下界，以及对两类$k$团猜想条件下的循环CQ族分析。

Despite its fundamental importance, no known improvements have been made to Yannakakis result until very recently. In an elegant result, $\mathrm{{Hu}}$ [19] showed that using fast matrix multiplication (i.e.,a matrix multiplication algorithm that multiplies two $n \times  n$ matrices in $O\left( {n}^{\omega }\right)$ time, $\omega  < 3$ ),we can evaluate any acyclic CQ in $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  + \left| {OUT}\right|  + \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| OUT\right| }^{5/6}}\right)$ (if $\omega  = 2$ ,which gives the strongest result in their framework). The key idea is to use the star query as a fundamental primitive that benefits from fast matrix multiplication, an insight also explored by prior work [5, 14], and evaluate any acyclic CQ in a bottom-up fashion by repeatedly applying the star query primitive. However, Hu's result has two principal limitations. First, the algorithm is non-combinatorial in nature, an undesirable property from a practical standpoint. Second, the algorithm does not support general aggregations (aka FAQs cf. Section 5), a common usecase in SQL [13]. It is also open whether the upper bound obtained is optimal. Therefore, the question of whether Hu's upper bound can be improved and whether the improvement can be made using a combinatorial ${}^{2}$ algorithm remains open.

尽管具有基础性重要意义，但直到最近才有人对Yannakakis的结果做出已知改进。$\mathrm{{Hu}}$[19]在一项精巧的研究中证明：利用快速矩阵乘法（即能在$O\left( {n}^{\omega }\right)$时间内完成两个$n \times  n$矩阵相乘的算法，$\omega  < 3$），我们可以在$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  + \left| {OUT}\right|  + \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| OUT\right| }^{5/6}}\right)$时间内评估任何无环连接查询（若满足$\omega  = 2$条件，这将产生其框架下的最强结果）。其核心思想是将星型查询作为能受益于快速矩阵乘法的基本原语——这一洞见也被先前研究[5,14]探讨过——并通过自底向上方式反复应用星型查询原语来评估任意无环连接查询。然而，胡氏的研究存在两个主要局限：首先，该算法本质上是非组合式的，从实践角度看这是不理想的特性；其次，该算法不支持通用聚合操作（即SQL中的常见用例FAQs，参见第5节）[13]。此外，所获上界是否最优仍属悬而未决。因此，能否改进胡氏上界、以及这种改进能否通过组合式${}^{2}$算法实现，这两个问题尚未得到解答。

Conjunctive Queries. We associate a Conjunctive Query (CQ) $Q$ to a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ , where $\mathcal{V} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   = \{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$ and $\mathcal{E}$ is a set of hyperedges; each hyperedge is a subset of $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ . The CQ is:

合取查询。我们将合取查询(CQ)$Q$关联至超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$，其中$\mathcal{V} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack   = \{ 1,\ldots ,n\}$且$\mathcal{E}$为超边集合；每条超边都是$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$的子集。查询的关系（亦称原子）是${R}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right) ,J \in  \mathcal{E}$，其中对于任意$J \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，${\mathbf{x}}_{J} = {\left( {x}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  J}$表示${R}_{J}$的模式（或变量）。该CQ表述为：

$$
Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{J \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right)  \tag{1}
$$

The variables in the head of the query ${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\left( {\mathcal{F} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }\right)$ are the free variables (or the projection variables). A CQ is full if $\mathcal{F} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,and it is Boolean if $\mathcal{F} = \varnothing$ ,simply written as $Q\left( \right)$ . A database $\mathcal{D}$ is a finite set of relations. We refer to the relational instance ${}^{3}$ for relation ${R}_{J}$ in database $\mathcal{D}$ using ${R}_{I}^{\mathcal{D}}$ . The size $\left| {R}_{I}^{\mathcal{D}}\right|$ of a relation ${R}_{I}^{\mathcal{D}}$ is the number of its tuples. The size of a database $\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{R}_{J} \in  \mathcal{D}}}\left| {R}_{J}^{\mathcal{D}}\right|$ . A schema $\mathcal{X} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{g}}\right)$ of a relation is a non-empty tuple of distinct variables where each variable can takes values from domain dom. We treat schemas and sets of variables interchangeably, assuming a fixed ordering of variables. We will say that a variable $x \in  \mathcal{V}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ that is present in at least two relations is a join variable, while a variable that is present in exactly one relation is isolated.

查询头部${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\left( {\mathcal{F} \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }\right)$中的变量称为自由变量（或投影变量）。当$\mathcal{F} = \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$时，该合取查询(CQ)为完全查询；当$\mathcal{F} = \varnothing$时则为布尔查询，简记为$Q\left( \right)$。数据库$\mathcal{D}$是关系的有限集合。我们使用${R}_{I}^{\mathcal{D}}$表示数据库$\mathcal{D}$中关系${R}_{J}$的关系实例${}^{3}$。关系${R}_{I}^{\mathcal{D}}$的大小$\left| {R}_{I}^{\mathcal{D}}\right|$即其元组数量。数据库的大小为$\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{R}_{J} \in  \mathcal{D}}}\left| {R}_{J}^{\mathcal{D}}\right|$。关系模式$\mathcal{X} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{g}}\right)$是由互异变量组成的非空元组，每个变量取值自域dom。我们将模式与变量集合视为等价，并假定变量存在固定排序。若变量$x \in  \mathcal{V}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$出现在至少两个关系中，则称其为连接变量；若仅出现在单一关系中，则称为孤立变量。

A tree decomposition of a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$ is a pair $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ where $\mathcal{T}$ is a tree and $\chi  : V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$ maps each node $t$ of the tree to a subset $\chi \left( t\right)$ of vertices such that

树分解。超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,\mathcal{E}}\right)$的树分解是一个二元组$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$，其中$\mathcal{T}$为树结构，$\chi  : V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }$将树的每个节点$t$映射到顶点子集$\chi \left( t\right)$，且满足：

(1) Every hyperedge $F \in  \mathcal{E}$ is a subset of some $\chi \left( t\right) ,t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ ,

(2) 对每个顶点$v \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$，集合$\{ t \mid  v \in  \chi \left( t\right) \}$构成$\mathcal{T}$的非空（连通）子树。

The sets $e = \chi \left( t\right)$ are called the bags of the tree decomposition. We use ${\chi }^{-1}\left( e\right)$ to recover the node of the tree with bag $e$ . A query is said to be $\alpha$ -acyclic ${}^{4}$ if there exists a tree decomposition such that every bag $B$ of the tree decomposition corresponds uniquely to an input relation ${R}_{B}$ . Such a tree decomposition is referred to as the join tree of the query. It is known that an $\alpha$ -acyclic query can be evaluated in time $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  + \left| {OUT}\right| }\right)$ . The subtree rooted at $t$ is denoted via ${\mathcal{T}}_{t}$ .

Consider a CQ $Q$ with hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ and free variables ${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}$ . We first define the reduced query of $Q$ ,denoted red $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ (in [19],this is called a cleansed query) via Algorithm 1. The while-loop of the procedure essentially follows the GYO algorithm [26, 31], with the difference that we can remove isolated variables only if they are not free. The reduced query is well-defined because the resulting hypergraph after the while-loop terminates is the same independent of the sequence of vertex and hyperedge removals. If $\mathcal{F} = V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,the procedure does not remove any variables (but may potentially remove hyperedges). If $\mathcal{F} = \varnothing$ ,the while-loop is identical to the GYO algorithm and will return the hypergraph $\left( {\varnothing ,\{ \{ \} \} }\right)$ . We say that $Q$ is reduced if $Q = \operatorname{red}\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ ,i.e.,the query cannot be further reduced.

## 4 Main Result

## 4 主要结果

In this section, we describe our main result that builds upon the celebrated Yannakakis algorithm. First, we recall the algorithm and its properties as outlined in [30]. The first step of the algorithm is to apply the full reducer which removes all tuples from the input database relations that do not contribute to the output. The main idea of the algorithm is to use a bottom-up evaluation strategy over the join tree. In particular,a node $s$ is processed once each of its children have been processed. Consider node $s$ whose children are all leaves. The key step of the algorithm is to do the join of relational instances corresponding to $s$ and each of its children but projecting the output on only the free variables in the subtree and the variables on node $s$ . The bottom-up process continues until we reach root and final join result is returned.

本节阐述基于著名Yannakakis算法的主要研究成果。首先回顾文献[30]所述算法及其特性：该算法第一步应用全约简器，剔除输入数据库关系中不参与输出的元组。其核心思想是沿连接树采用自底向上的评估策略——节点$s$必须在其所有子节点处理完毕后才会被处理。考虑子节点均为叶节点的$s$，算法关键步骤是对$s$及其各子节点对应的关系实例进行连接运算，但输出仅投影该子树中的自由变量及$s$节点上的变量。自底向上处理直至根节点，返回最终连接结果。

To complete the proof,let $W = \chi \left( s\right)  \cap  \left( {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\chi \left( {t}_{i}\right) }\right)$ denote the join variables of node $s$ that are also present in some leaf node ${t}_{1},\ldots ,{t}_{k}$ . We observe that the size of the intermediate bag ${\pi }_{\chi \left( s\right)  \cup  {\mathcal{F}}_{s}}\left( {{R}_{\chi \left( s\right) }^{\mathcal{D}} \land  {T}_{\chi \left( {t}_{1}\right) }^{\mathcal{D}} \land  \cdots  \land  {T}_{\chi \left( {t}_{k}\right) }^{\mathcal{D}}}\right)$ can be bounded by

为完成证明，令$W = \chi \left( s\right)  \cap  \left( {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\chi \left( {t}_{i}\right) }\right)$表示节点$s$中同时存在于某个叶节点${t}_{1},\ldots ,{t}_{k}$的连接变量。我们观察到中间包${\pi }_{\chi \left( s\right)  \cup  {\mathcal{F}}_{s}}\left( {{R}_{\chi \left( s\right) }^{\mathcal{D}} \land  {T}_{\chi \left( {t}_{1}\right) }^{\mathcal{D}} \land  \cdots  \land  {T}_{\chi \left( {t}_{k}\right) }^{\mathcal{D}}}\right)$的大小可被以下量约束：

$$
\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{w \in  {\pi }_{W}\left( {R}_{\chi \left( s\right) }^{\mathcal{D}}\right) }}\left| {{R}_{\chi \left( s\right) }^{\mathcal{D}} \ltimes  w}\right|  \cdot  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  }}\left| {{T}_{\chi \left( {t}_{i}\right) }^{\mathcal{D}} \ltimes  w}\right| 
$$

Discussion. Observe that Lemma 4.1 degenerates to the standard bound of Yannakakis algorithm for $\Delta  = 1$ . However,as we will show in the next part,choosing $\Delta  > 1$ can lead to better overall join processing algorithms. It is also interesting to note that the running time bound obtained in Lemma 4.1 can only be achieved when the root of the decomposition is a relation with the two properties as outlined in the statement. It can be shown that no other choice of the root node achieves a time better than $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  \left| {OUT}\right| }\right)$ . It is important to note that Lemma 4.1 requires the query to be reduced. Indeed, without the reduced query requirement, relations may contain variables that are neither free and nor join. The presence of such variables renders the join size computation incorrect.

讨论。注意到引理4.1退化为Yannakakis算法对$\Delta  = 1$的标准界。但如后文所示，选择$\Delta  > 1$可能产生更优的整体连接处理算法。值得注意的是，引理4.1中获得的时间界仅当分解树的根节点满足声明中所述两个属性时才能达成。可以证明其他根节点选择都无法获得优于$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  \left| {OUT}\right| }\right)$的时间复杂度。关键要强调的是引理4.1要求查询必须是归约后的——若非如此，关系中可能包含既非自由变量也非连接变量的无关变量，这类变量的存在会导致连接大小计算错误。

### 4.2 Our Algorithm

### 4.2 我们的算法

In this section,we will present the algorithm for our main result. We will first consider a CQ $Q$ that is existentially connected and reduced.

本节将展示实现主要结果的算法。我们首先考虑一个存在性连接且约简的合取查询(CQ) $Q$。

Algorithm 3 shows the improved procedure. It processes the nodes of the join tree in a leaf-to-root order just like Yannakakis algorithm,i.e.,a node $s$ is processed only after each of its children have been processed. However, our algorithm departs in the operations involved in the processing of each node. In particular,consider node $s$ whose children are all leaves. For every leaf node $t$ that is a child of $s$ ,we partition the relation ${T}_{\chi }^{\mathcal{D}}\left( t\right)$ assigned to the node into two disjoint partitions (the heavy partition ${T}_{\chi }^{\mathcal{D},H}\left( t\right)$ and the light partition ${T}_{\chi }^{\mathcal{D},L}\left( t\right)$ ) using a chosen degree threshold. Then,we use Lemma 4.1 to process ${T}_{\chi }^{\mathcal{D},H}\left( t\right)$ and add the produced output into a set $\mathcal{J}$ . This processing is done in lines 15-17 of the while loop. The crucial detail is that the processing of the heavy partition is done by reorienting the join tree to be rooted at $t$ ,and then applying Yannakakis in a bottom-up fashion.

算法3展示了改进后的流程。与Yannakakis算法类似，它按照从叶节点到根节点的顺序处理连接树节点，即节点$s$仅在其所有子节点处理完成后才被处理。但本算法在节点处理操作上有显著差异：对于所有子节点均为叶节点的$s$，我们使用选定度阈值将叶节点$t$对应的关系${T}_{\chi }^{\mathcal{D}}\left( t\right)$划分为两个互斥分区（重度分区${T}_{\chi }^{\mathcal{D},H}\left( t\right)$与轻度分区${T}_{\chi }^{\mathcal{D},L}\left( t\right)$）。随后运用引理4.1处理${T}_{\chi }^{\mathcal{D},H}\left( t\right)$，并将输出结果存入集合$\mathcal{J}$（见while循环第15-17行）。关键之处在于：通过将连接树根节点重置为$t$，以自底向上方式应用Yannakakis算法来处理重度分区。

Once all heavy sub-relations of the leaf nodes are processed, we are left with all light parts of the relations for the children of node $s$ . At this point,we join the relations in the subtree rooted at $s$ (line 22) and then remove the nodes for children of $s$ from the join tree $\mathcal{T}$ (line 24). The step on line 22 is identical to the one on line 8 of the Yannakakis algorithm. Note that modifying the structure of the join tree $\mathcal{T}$ by deleting nodes in our algorthm is a departure from Yannakakis algorithm. Although modification of $\mathcal{T}$ is not required for correctness of our algorithm,as we will show later,by controlling the node processing order $K$ (on line 5),one can use our algorithm in innovative ways. Therefore,when $K$ is a subset of $V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ instead of containing all the nodes,it is important to keep the database $\mathcal{D}$ and $\mathcal{T}$ up-to-date to reflect which nodes have been processed. We next state the main result (the proof can be found in the appendix).

当叶节点的所有重度子关系处理完毕后，剩余的是节点$s$子节点关系的轻度部分。此时我们连接以$s$为根的子树中的关系（第22行），并从连接树$\mathcal{T}$中移除$s$的子节点（第24行）。第22行步骤与Yannakakis算法第8行完全一致。需注意：本算法通过删除节点修改连接树$\mathcal{T}$结构的行为与Yannakakis算法不同。虽然$\mathcal{T}$的修改并非算法正确性的必要条件，但后文将证明：通过控制节点处理顺序$K$（第5行），可创新性地运用本算法。因此当$K$是$V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$的子集而非全部节点时，必须保持数据库$\mathcal{D}$和连接树$\mathcal{T}$的实时更新以反映已处理节点。接下来我们陈述主要结论（证明见附录）。

Lemma 4.2. Given an acyclic join query $Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\right)$ that is existentially connected and reduced,database $\mathcal{D}$ ,and an integer threshold $1 \leq  \Delta  \leq  \left| \mathcal{D}\right|$ ,Algorithm 3 computes the join result $Q\left( \mathcal{D}\right)$ in time $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\Delta }^{k - 1} + \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  \left| {OUT}\right| /\Delta }\right)$ ,where $k$ is the number of atoms in $Q$ .

引理4.2. 给定存在性连接且约简的无环连接查询$Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\right)$、数据库$\mathcal{D}$及整数阈值$1 \leq  \Delta  \leq  \left| \mathcal{D}\right|$，算法3可在$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\Delta }^{k - 1} + \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  \left| {OUT}\right| /\Delta }\right)$时间内计算出连接结果$Q\left( \mathcal{D}\right)$，其中$k$表示$Q$中的原子数。

Finding the optimal threshold $\Delta$ . To find the optimal threshold that minimizes the running time of Algorithm 3, we can equate the two terms in the running time expression of Lemma 4.2 to obtain $\Delta  = {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{1/k}$ ,giving us the running time as $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{1 - 1/k}}\right)$ . However,the value of |OUT| is not known apriori. To remedy this issue, we use the doubling trick [6] that was first introduced in the context of multi-armed bandit algorithms. The key idea is to guess the value of |OUT|. Suppose the guessed output size is $O$ and let $\alpha$ be a constant value that is an upper bound of the constant hidden in the big-O runtime complexity of Algorithm 3. We start with an estimate of ${O}_{1} = {2}^{0}$ in the first round and run the algorithm. If the algorithm does not finish execution in $\alpha  \cdot  \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {O}_{i}^{1 - 1/k}$ steps,then we terminate the algorithm and pick the new estimate to be ${O}_{i + 1} = 2 \cdot  {O}_{i}$ and re-run the algorithm. However,if the algorithm finishes,then we have successfully computed the query result. Note that $\alpha$ can be determined by doing an analysis of the program and counting the number of RAM model operations required for each line. It is easy to see that the algorithm will terminate within $\lceil \log \left( {2 \cdot  \left| {\mathrm{{OUT}} \mid  }\right| }\right) \rceil$ rounds and the total running time is $\alpha  \cdot  \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  \left\lceil  {{\log }_{2}\left( {2 \cdot  \left| {OUT}\right| }\right) }\right\rceil  \right\rbrack  }}{O}_{i}^{1 - 1/k} = \alpha  \cdot  \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in  \left\lbrack  \left\lceil  {{\log }_{2}\left( {2 \cdot  \left| {OUT}\right| }\right) }\right\rceil  \right\rbrack  }}{2}^{i \cdot  \left( {1 - 1/k}\right) } = O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| 0\mathrm{{UT}}\right| }^{1 - 1/k}}\right)$ for any $k \geq  2$ . General CQs. Finally, we discuss what happens for a general acyclic CQ that may not be reduced or existentially connected. In this case, we can relate the runtime of the algorithm to the projection width we defined in the previous section. The main insight here is that once a general acyclic CQ has been reduced and decomposed, we can evaluate each component separately. The query evaluation output of each component can be combined easily since the query can now be viewed as a free-connex acyclic query, whose evaluation is well understood [7]. Note that for a CQ that is reduced and existentially connected, pw is exactly the number of atoms in the query.

通用CQ处理。最后，我们讨论未化简或非存在连接的通用无环CQ的处理方法。此时，算法运行时间可与前文定义的投影宽度相关联。核心思路在于：当通用无环CQ被化简分解后，各组件可独立求值。由于查询此时可视为自由连接无环查询（其求值方法已被充分研究[7]），各组件求值结果能轻松合并。需注意，对于已化简且存在连接的CQ，投影宽度pw恰好等于查询中的原子数。

Self-Joins. So far, we have assumed that the query does not contain any repeated relations (i.e. no self-joins). However, our framework can handle self-joins as well by performing a few basic transformations. First, if a query contains repeated relations, we make copies of the input relation(s) in the database involved in the self-join, assign a unique relational name to each copy, and use the unique name for each occurrence of the repeated relation to rewrite the query. Then, we order the schema of each relation according to the variable order $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ . This operation is straightforward since reordering of the variables in the schema of a relation merely corresponds to shuffling each tuple in the relation to match the reordered schema. Finally,for all atoms ${R}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right) ,{S}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right) ,\ldots ,{V}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right)$ that have the same schema,we only keep one atom in query (say ${R}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right)$ ) and modify the instance ${R}_{J}^{D} = {R}_{J}^{D} \cap  {S}_{J}^{D} \cap  \cdots  \cap  {V}_{J}^{D}$ . Each step takes at most $O\left( \left| \mathcal{D}\right| \right)$ time and satisfies the formulation of a CQ as defined in Equation 1, and thus our main result can extend to self-joins.

自连接处理。此前我们假设查询不含重复关系（即无自连接），但本框架通过基础转换即可支持自连接。首先，若查询含重复关系，我们复制数据库中参与自连接的输入关系，为每个副本分配唯一关系名，并采用这些唯一名称重写查询。接着按变量顺序$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$调整各关系模式——该操作仅需对关系中的元组进行重排以匹配新模式。最后，对于模式相同的所有原子${R}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right) ,{S}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right) ,\ldots ,{V}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right)$，我们仅保留查询中的一个原子（如${R}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right)$）并修改实例${R}_{J}^{D} = {R}_{J}^{D} \cap  {S}_{J}^{D} \cap  \cdots  \cap  {V}_{J}^{D}$。每步操作至多消耗$O\left( \left| \mathcal{D}\right| \right)$时间，且符合公式1定义的CQ形式化要求，因此主要结论可推广至自连接情形。

关系中模式重复的变量也可以通过线性时间预处理步骤处理：修改模式使每个变量仅出现一次，并调整关系实例中的每个元组。

Semirings. A tuple $\sigma  = \left( {D,\oplus ,\otimes ,0,1}\right)$ is a (commutative) semiring if $\oplus$ and $\otimes$ are binary operators over $\mathbf{D}$ for which:

半环(Semirings)。若二元运算符$\oplus$和$\otimes$在集合$\mathbf{D}$上满足以下条件，则称元组$\sigma  = \left( {D,\oplus ,\otimes ,0,1}\right)$为(交换)半环：

(1) $\left( {D,\oplus ,0}\right)$ is a commutative monoid with additive identity0(i.e., $\oplus$ is associative and commutative,and $a \oplus  0 = a$ for all $a \in  D$ );

(1) $\left( {D,\oplus ,0}\right)$是以加法单位元0构成的交换幺半群(即$\oplus$满足结合律与交换律，且对所有$a \in  D$有$a \oplus  0 = a$)；

(2) $\left( {D,\otimes ,1}\right)$ is a (commutative) monoid with multiplicative identity1for $\otimes$ ;

(2) $\left( {D,\otimes ,1}\right)$是对于$\otimes$具有乘法单位元1的(交换)幺半群；

(3) $\otimes$ distributes over $\oplus$ ,i.e., $a \otimes  \left( {b \oplus  c}\right)  = \left( {a \otimes  b}\right)  \oplus  \left( {a \otimes  c}\right)$ for $a,b,c \in  D$ ; and

(3) $\otimes$对$\oplus$满足分配律，即对$a,b,c \in  D$有$a \otimes  \left( {b \oplus  c}\right)  = \left( {a \otimes  b}\right)  \oplus  \left( {a \otimes  c}\right)$；

(4) $a \otimes  \mathbf{0} = \mathbf{0}$ for all $a \in  \mathbf{D}$ .

(4) 对所有$a \in  \mathbf{D}$有$a \otimes  \mathbf{0} = \mathbf{0}$。

Such examples include the Boolean semiring $\mathbb{B} = \left( {\{ \text{false,true}\} ,\vee ,\land ,\text{false,true}}\right)$ ,the natural numbers semiring $\left( {\mathbb{N},+,\cdot ,0,1}\right)$ ,and the tropical semiring ${\operatorname{Trop}}^{ + } = \left( {{\mathbb{R}}_{ + }\cup \{ \infty \} ,\min ,+,\infty ,0}\right)$ .

### 6.1 Path Queries

### 6.1 路径查询

We will first study the path queries, a class of queries that has immense practical importance. The projection width of a path query ${P}_{k}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{k + 1}}\right)$ is $k$ . Therefore,applying our main result,we get:

我们首先研究具有重要实践价值的路径查询类。路径查询${P}_{k}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{k + 1}}\right)$的投影宽度为$k$。因此应用我们的主要结论可得：

Theorem 6.1. Given a path query ${P}_{k}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{k + 1}}\right)$ and a database $\mathcal{D}$ ,there exists an algorithm that can evaluate the path query in time $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  + \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{1 - 1/k}}\right)$ .

定理6.1. 给定路径查询${P}_{k}\left( {{x}_{1},{x}_{k + 1}}\right)$和数据库$\mathcal{D}$，存在可在$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  + \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{1 - 1/k}}\right)$时间内完成该路径查询的算法。

For $k = 2$ ,our result matches the bound shown in [5]. Comparing our result to Hu,the bound obtained in Theorem 6.1 strictly improves the results obtained by $\mathrm{{Hu}}$ for $3 \leq  k \leq  5$ and matches for $k = 6$ even if we assume $\omega  = 2$ . This result suggests there is room for further improvement in the use of fast matrix multiplication to obtain tighter bounds. For $k = 7$ ,our running time is better than the one obtained by $\mathrm{{Hu}}$ assuming the current best known value of $\omega  = {2.371552}\left\lbrack  {29}\right\rbrack$ .

对于$k = 2$，我们的结果与文献[5]所示界限一致。相较于Hu的研究，定理6.1获得的界限在$3 \leq  k \leq  5$情况下严格优于$\mathrm{{Hu}}$的结果，在$k = 6$时持平——即使假设$\omega  = 2$成立。这表明利用快速矩阵乘法获取更紧界限仍有改进空间。对于$k = 7$，在当前最佳已知值$\omega  = {2.371552}\left\lbrack  {29}\right\rbrack$假设下，我们的运行时间优于$\mathrm{{Hu}}$所得结果。

We show here the application of our result to hierarchical queries. A CQ is hierarchical if for any two of its variables, either their sets of atoms are disjoint or one is contained in the other. All hierarchical queries are acyclic,and all star queries ${Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }$ (defined in Example 3.2) are hierarchical queries. They have $\operatorname{pw}\left( {Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }\right)  = \ell$ ,thus:

此处展示我们的结果在层次化查询中的应用。若某CQ中任意两个变量的原子集要么互不相交，要么存在包含关系，则该查询为层次化查询。所有层次化查询都是无环的，且星型查询${Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }$（例3.2定义）均属层次化查询。这类查询具有$\operatorname{pw}\left( {Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }\right)  = \ell$特性，因此：

Theorem 6.2. Given a star query ${Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }\left( {x}_{\ell }\right)$ and a database $\mathcal{D}$ ,the star query can be evaluated in time $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  + \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{1 - 1/\ell }}\right)$ .

定理6.2 给定一个星型查询${Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }\left( {x}_{\ell }\right)$和数据库$\mathcal{D}$，该星型查询可在$O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  + \left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{1 - 1/\ell }}\right)$时间内完成求值。

In other words, Yannakakis algorithm also achieves the time bound as specified by Theorem 6.2 but the routinely used upper bound of $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  \left| {\mathrm{{OUT}} \mid  }\right| }\right)$ does not reflect that.

As another example,consider the query $Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{4567}\right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{124}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{124}\right)  \land  {R}_{125}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{125}\right)  \land  {R}_{136}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{136}\right)  \land$ ${R}_{137}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{137}\right)$ formed by the relations shown in Figure 5. ### 6.3 General Queries

### 6.3 通用查询

Submodular width. A function $f : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \mapsto  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$ is a non-negative set function on $\mathcal{V}\left( {\ell  \geq  1}\right)$ . The set function is monotone if $f\left( X\right)  \leq  f\left( Y\right)$ whenever $X \subseteq  Y$ ,and is submodular if $f\left( {X \cup  Y}\right)  + f\left( {X \cap  Y}\right)  \leq$ $f\left( X\right)  + f\left( Y\right)$ for all $X,Y \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ . A non-negative,monotone,submodular set function $h$ such that $h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$ is a polymatroid. Let $Q$ be a CQ and let ${\Gamma }_{\ell }$ be the set of all polymatroids $h$ on $\mathcal{V}$ such that $h\left( J\right)  \leq  1$ for all $J \in  \mathcal{E}$ . The submodular width of $Q$ is

子模宽度。函数$f : {2}^{\mathcal{V}} \mapsto  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$是定义在$\mathcal{V}\left( {\ell  \geq  1}\right)$上的非负集函数。当$X \subseteq  Y$时若满足$f\left( X\right)  \leq  f\left( Y\right)$则该集函数是单调的，若对所有$X,Y \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$满足$f\left( {X \cup  Y}\right)  + f\left( {X \cap  Y}\right)  \leq$$f\left( X\right)  + f\left( Y\right)$则是子模的。满足$h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$的非负、单调、子模集函数$h$称为多拟阵。设$Q$为合取查询，${\Gamma }_{\ell }$表示定义在$\mathcal{V}$上且对所有$J \in  \mathcal{E}$满足$h\left( J\right)  \leq  1$的多拟阵$h$的集合，则$Q$的子模宽度定义为

$$
\operatorname{subw}\left( Q\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  {\Gamma }_{\ell }}}\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  \mathfrak{F}}}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}h\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right) , \tag{6}
$$

where $\mathfrak{F}$ is the set of all non-redundant tree decompositions of $Q$ . A tree decomposition is non-redundant if no bag is a subset of another. Abo Khamis et al. [24] proved that non-redundancy ensures that $\mathfrak{F}$ is finite,hence the inner minimum is well-defined. Prior work [24] showed that given any $\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$ and database $\mathcal{D}$ ,the PANDA algorithm can decompose the query and database instance into a constant number of pairs $\left( {{Q}_{i},{\mathcal{D}}_{i}}\right)$ such that $Q\left( D\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{i}{Q}_{i}\left( {\mathcal{D}}_{i}\right)$ . Further,it is also guaranteed that each ${Q}_{i}$ is acyclic, $\left| {\mathcal{D}}_{i}\right|  = {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{\operatorname{subw}\left( Q\right) }$ (each ${\mathcal{D}}_{i}$ can be computed in $\widetilde{O}\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{\operatorname{subw}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ time),and $\left| {{Q}_{i}\left( {\mathcal{D}}_{i}\right) }\right|  \leq  \left| {Q\left( \mathcal{D}\right) }\right|$ . Since each ${Q}_{i}$ is acyclic,we can apply our main result and obtain the following theorem.

其中$\mathfrak{F}$表示$Q$的所有非冗余树分解的集合。若树分解中不存在一个包是另一个包的子集，则称该分解为非冗余的。Abo Khamis等人[24]证明了非冗余性保证了$\mathfrak{F}$是有限集合，因此内层最小值定义良好。前人工作[24]表明，给定任意$\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$和数据库$\mathcal{D}$，PANDA算法可将查询和数据库实例分解为常数数量的$\left( {{Q}_{i},{\mathcal{D}}_{i}}\right)$对，使得$Q\left( D\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{i}{Q}_{i}\left( {\mathcal{D}}_{i}\right)$。此外还保证每个${Q}_{i}$是无环查询，$\left| {\mathcal{D}}_{i}\right|  = {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{\operatorname{subw}\left( Q\right) }$（每个${\mathcal{D}}_{i}$可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{\operatorname{subw}\left( Q\right) }\right)$时间内计算），且满足$\left| {{Q}_{i}\left( {\mathcal{D}}_{i}\right) }\right|  \leq  \left| {Q\left( \mathcal{D}\right) }\right|$。由于每个${Q}_{i}$都是无环的，应用我们的主要结果可得以下定理。

Theorem 6.3. ## 7 A Faster Algorithm for Path Queries

## 7 路径查询的快速算法

In this section, we show a better algorithm that improves upon Theorem 6.1 by invoking Algorithm 3 in a novel way. Algorithm 4 shows the steps for evaluating a path query ${P}_{k}$ . The main idea of the algorithm is to carefully choose the ordering of how the nodes in a join tree are processed. This is in contrast with Algorithm 3 and the Yannakakis algorithm where any leaf-to-root order is sufficient to get join time guarantees. The key insight of the algorithm is the following: the reader may observe that Algorithm 3 only partitions a leaf node relation ${T}_{\chi \left( s\right) }$ over ${\chi }^{ \bowtie  }\left( s\right)$ . However,one could also partition a relation over the non-join variables (i.e. the isolated free variables) to further speed up query evaluation. We demonstrate that such a strategy can indeed be faster.

本节提出改进定理6.1的优化算法，通过创新性地调用算法3实现。算法4展示了路径查询${P}_{k}$的求值步骤。该算法的核心思想是精心选择连接树节点的处理顺序，这与算法3和Yannakakis算法中任意叶到根顺序即可保证连接时间的做法形成对比。关键洞见在于：读者可注意到算法3仅对叶节点关系${T}_{\chi \left( s\right) }$在${\chi }^{ \bowtie  }\left( s\right)$上进行划分，但实际上也可对非连接变量（即孤立自由变量）的关系进行划分以加速查询求值。我们将证明该策略确实能获得更快的速度。

## 8 Lower Bounds

## 8 下界证明

In this section, we demonstrate several lower bounds that show optimality for a subclass of CQs. First,we define the $k$ -clique problem that will be central to our lower bounds. Given an undirected graph $G = \left( {V,E}\right)$ and an integer $k \leq  \left| V\right|$ ,the $k$ -clique problem consists of deciding if the graph $G$ contains $k$ vertices such that each of the $k$ vertices are connected to each other via an edge in $E$ . We also define the minimum-weight $k$ -clique problem: suppose the graph $G$ is equipped with an edge-weight function that maps edges to weights in $\left\lbrack  {0,M}\right\rbrack$ for integer $M > 0$ ,find the $k$ -clique where the edge sum is minimized. We will use the following well-established conjectures from fine-grained complexity for the two $k$ -clique problems.

本节将展示证明合取查询(CQs)子类最优性的若干下界。首先定义$k$-团问题作为下界证明的核心：给定无向图$G = \left( {V,E}\right)$和整数$k \leq  \left| V\right|$，该问题需判定图$G$中是否存在$k$个顶点，使得每对顶点都通过$E$中的边相连。同时定义最小权重$k$-团问题：假设图$G$具有边权重函数（将边映射到整数$M > 0$的$\left\lbrack  {0,M}\right\rbrack$值域），需找出边权和最小的$k$-团。我们将基于细粒度复杂性理论的两个经典猜想来研究这两个$k$-团问题。

Definition 8.1 (Boolean k-Clique Conjecture). There is no real $\epsilon  > 0$ such that computing the $k$ -clique problem (with $k \geq  3$ ) over the Boolean semiring in an (undirected) $n$ -node graph requires time $O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$ using a combinatorial algorithm.

定义8.1（布尔k-团猜想）。不存在实数$\epsilon  > 0$使得在(无向)$n$节点图中，通过组合算法在布尔半环上计算$k$-团问题（当$k \geq  3$时）需要$O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$时间。

Definition 8.2 (Min-Weight $k$ -Clique Conjecture). There is no real $\epsilon  > 0$ such that computing the $k$ -clique problem (with $k \geq  3$ ) over the tropical semiring in an (undirected) $n$ -node graph with integer edge weights can be done in time $O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$ .

定义8.2（最小权重$k$-团猜想）。不存在实数$\epsilon  > 0$使得在具有整数边权重的（无向）$n$节点图中，计算热带半环上的$k$-团问题（带$k \geq  3$）可在$O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$时间内完成。

Our first lower bound tells us that the dependence in the bound of Theorem 6.3 on both the submodular width and projection width is somewhat necessary.

我们的首个下界表明，定理6.3中关于子模宽度和投影宽度的依赖关系在某种程度上是必要的。

Theorem 8.3. Take any integer $\ell  \geq  2$ and any rational $w \geq  1$ such that $\ell  \cdot  w$ is an integer. Then, there exists a query $Q$ with projection width $\ell$ free variables and submodular width $w$ such that no combinatorial algorithm can compute it over input $\mathcal{D}$ in time $O\left( {{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{w} \cdot  {\left| 0\mathrm{{UT}}\right| }^{1 - 1/\ell  - \epsilon }}\right)$ for any real $\epsilon  > 0$ ,assuming the Boolean k -Clique Conjecture.

定理8.3。任取整数$\ell  \geq  2$和有理数$w \geq  1$使得$\ell  \cdot  w$为整数。则存在一个具有$\ell$个自由变量的投影宽度和$w$子模宽度的查询$Q$，使得在布尔k-团猜想成立的前提下，任何组合算法都无法在$O\left( {{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{w} \cdot  {\left| 0\mathrm{{UT}}\right| }^{1 - 1/\ell  - \epsilon }}\right)$时间内对输入$\mathcal{D}$进行计算，其中$\epsilon  > 0$为任意实数。

We next prove a general result that gives output-sensitive lower bounds for arbitrary CQs. To this end,we utilize the notion of clique embedding [17]. We say that two sets of vertices $X,Y \subseteq  V\left( H\right)$ touch in $\mathcal{H}$ if either $X \cap  Y \neq  \varnothing$ or there is a hyperedge $e \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ that intersects both $X$ and $Y$ .

接下来我们证明一个通用结论，该结论为任意CQ提供了输出敏感的下界。为此，我们采用团嵌入的概念[17]。若两顶点集$X,Y \subseteq  V\left( H\right)$在$\mathcal{H}$中满足$X \cap  Y \neq  \varnothing$或存在同时与$X$和$Y$相交的超边$e \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$，则称它们在$\mathcal{H}$中接触。

Definition 8.4 (Clique Embedding). Let $k \geq  3$ and $\mathcal{H}$ be a hypergraph. A $k$ -clique embedding, denoted as ${C}_{k} \mapsto  \mathcal{H}$ ,is a mapping $\psi$ that maps every $v \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$ to a non-empty subset $\psi \left( v\right)  \subseteq  V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ such that the following hold:

定义8.4（团嵌入）。设$k \geq  3$和$\mathcal{H}$为超图。一个$k$-团嵌入（记作${C}_{k} \mapsto  \mathcal{H}$）是将每个$v \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$映射到非空子集$\psi \left( v\right)  \subseteq  V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$的映射$\psi$，且满足以下条件：

(1) $\psi \left( v\right)$ induces a connected subgraph;

(1) $\psi \left( v\right)$诱导出连通子图；

(2) for any two $u \neq  v \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$ then $\psi \left( u\right) ,\psi \left( v\right)$ touch in $\mathcal{H}$ .

(2) 对任意两个$u \neq  v \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$，则$\psi \left( u\right) ,\psi \left( v\right)$在$\mathcal{H}$中接触。

It is often convenient to describe a clique embedding $\psi$ by the reverse mapping ${\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right)  = \{ i \mid  x \in$ $\psi \left( i\right) \}$ ,for $x \in  V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ . For a hyperedge $J \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,its weak edge depth is ${d}_{\psi }\left( J\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left| {\{ v \mid  \psi \left( v\right)  \cap  J \neq  \varnothing \} }\right|$ , i.e.,the number of vertices from $V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ that map to some variable in $J$ . We also define the edge depth of $J \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ as ${d}_{\psi }^{ + }\left( J\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{v \in  J}}{d}_{\psi }\left( v\right)$ ,i.e. weak edge depth but counting multiplicity. For an embedding $\psi$ and a hyperedge $\mathcal{F} \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,the $\mathcal{F}$ -weak edge depth of $\psi$ is defined as ${\operatorname{wed}}^{\mathcal{F}}\left( \psi \right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}}$ $\mathop{\max }\limits_{{J \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \smallsetminus  \mathcal{F}}}{d}_{\psi }\left( J\right)$ ,i.e. the maximum of weak edge depths excluding $\mathcal{F}$ .

描述团嵌入$\psi$时，采用逆映射${\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right)  = \{ i \mid  x \in$$\psi \left( i\right) \}$通常较为便利，其中$x \in  V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$。对于超边$J \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$，其弱边深度为${d}_{\psi }\left( J\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left| {\{ v \mid  \psi \left( v\right)  \cap  J \neq  \varnothing \} }\right|$，即从$V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$映射到$J$中某变量的顶点数量。我们还将$J \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$的边深度定义为${d}_{\psi }^{ + }\left( J\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{v \in  J}}{d}_{\psi }\left( v\right)$，即考虑重复计数的弱边深度。对于嵌入$\psi$和超边$\mathcal{F} \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$，其$\mathcal{F}$-弱边深度定义为${\operatorname{wed}}^{\mathcal{F}}\left( \psi \right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}}$$\mathop{\max }\limits_{{J \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \smallsetminus  \mathcal{F}}}{d}_{\psi }\left( J\right)$，即排除$\mathcal{F}$后的最大弱边深度。

Theorem 8.5. Given any $\operatorname{CQ}Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{J \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right)$ and database $\mathcal{D}$ ,let $\psi$ be a $k$ -clique embedding of the hypergraph ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,E\left( \mathcal{H}\right) \cup \{ \mathcal{F}\} }\right)$ such that $x \cdot  {\operatorname{wed}}^{\mathcal{F}}\left( \psi \right)  + y \cdot  {d}_{\psi }^{ + }\left( \mathcal{F}\right)  \leq  k$ for positive $x,y > 0$ . Then,there is no combinatorial algorithm with running time $O\left( {{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{x - \epsilon } \cdot  {\left| 0\mathrm{{UT}}\right| }^{y - {\epsilon }^{\prime }}}\right)$ for evaluating $Q\left( \mathcal{D}\right)$ assuming the Boolean $k$ -Clique Conjecture,where $\epsilon ,{\epsilon }^{\prime }$ are non-negative with $\epsilon  + {\epsilon }^{\prime } > 0$ .

定理8.5。给定任意$\operatorname{CQ}Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\right)  \leftarrow  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{J \in  \mathcal{E}}}{R}_{J}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{J}\right)$和数据库$\mathcal{D}$，设$\psi$为超图${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,E\left( \mathcal{H}\right) \cup \{ \mathcal{F}\} }\right)$的$k$-团嵌入，且对正数$x,y > 0$满足$x \cdot  {\operatorname{wed}}^{\mathcal{F}}\left( \psi \right)  + y \cdot  {d}_{\psi }^{ + }\left( \mathcal{F}\right)  \leq  k$。则在布尔型$k$-团猜想成立的前提下，不存在运行时间为$O\left( {{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{x - \epsilon } \cdot  {\left| 0\mathrm{{UT}}\right| }^{y - {\epsilon }^{\prime }}}\right)$的组合算法可用于计算$Q\left( \mathcal{D}\right)$，其中$\epsilon ,{\epsilon }^{\prime }$为非负数且满足$\epsilon  + {\epsilon }^{\prime } > 0$。

We can show an analogous result for FAQ queries over the tropical semiring.

我们可以在热带半环上对FAQ查询展示类似结论。

Theorem 8.6. Given any FAQ $\varphi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\right)$ as (4) and database $\mathcal{D}$ ,let $\psi$ be a $k$ -clique embedding of the hypergraph ${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,E\left( \mathcal{H}\right) \cup \{ \mathcal{F}\} }\right)$ such that $x \cdot  {\operatorname{wed}}^{\mathcal{F}}\left( \psi \right)  + y \cdot  {d}_{\psi }^{ + }\left( \mathcal{F}\right)  \leq  k$ for positive $x,y > 0$ . Then, there exists no algorithm that evaluates $\varphi \left( \mathcal{D}\right)$ over the tropical semiring in time $O\left( {{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{x - \epsilon } \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{y - {\epsilon }^{\prime }}}\right)$ assuming the Min-Weight $k$ -Clique Conjecture,where $\epsilon ,{\epsilon }^{\prime }$ are non-negative with $\epsilon  + {\epsilon }^{\prime } > 0$ .

定理8.6。给定任意FAQ$\varphi \left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\mathcal{F}}\right)$如式(4)所示及数据库$\mathcal{D}$，设$\psi$为超图${\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } = \left( {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  ,E\left( \mathcal{H}\right) \cup \{ \mathcal{F}\} }\right)$的$k$-团嵌入，且对于正数$x,y > 0$满足$x \cdot  {\operatorname{wed}}^{\mathcal{F}}\left( \psi \right)  + y \cdot  {d}_{\psi }^{ + }\left( \mathcal{F}\right)  \leq  k$。则在最小权重$k$-团猜想成立的前提下，不存在任何算法能在$O\left( {{\left| \mathcal{D}\right| }^{x - \epsilon } \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{y - {\epsilon }^{\prime }}}\right)$时间内基于热带半环对$\varphi \left( \mathcal{D}\right)$求值，其中$\epsilon ,{\epsilon }^{\prime }$为非负数且满足$\epsilon  + {\epsilon }^{\prime } > 0$。

Hu defined the notion of freew of any acyclic query and proved that any "semiring algorithm" requires $\Omega \left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{1 - \frac{1}{\operatorname{freew}\left( Q\right) }} + \left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }\right)$ [19]. Our lower bound is complementary to Hu’s lower bound. Specifically, $\mathrm{{Hu}}$ showed a stronger result that the lower bound applies for every value of $\left| \mathcal{D}\right|$ and |OUT|, whereas our result shows the existence of a hard database instance. However, our lower bound also applies to cyclic CQs whereas Hu’s freew(Q)is not defined for cyclic queries. Therefore,the two results are incomparable. For the special case of $x = 1$ ,we match Hu’s lower bound (see Proposition D.1) by providing an alternate proof that there exists a clique embedding for ${Q}^{\prime }$ such that $y =$ $1 - \frac{1}{\operatorname{freew}\left( Q\right) }$ . We now apply Theorem 8.5 and Theorem 8.6 to get tight lower bounds for star queries and can be extended to hierarchical queries (note that star queries are also hierarchical queries).

胡定义了无环查询的自由度概念并证明任何"半环算法"需要$\Omega \left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }^{1 - \frac{1}{\operatorname{freew}\left( Q\right) }} + \left| \mathrm{{OUT}}\right| }\right)$[19]。我们的下界与胡的下界形成互补。具体而言，$\mathrm{{Hu}}$证明了更强的结果：该下界适用于$\left| \mathcal{D}\right|$和|OUT|的所有取值，而我们的结果表明存在困难数据库实例。但我们的下界同样适用于循环CQ，而胡的自由度(Q)未定义循环查询。因此两项结果不可直接比较。对于$x = 1$的特殊情况，我们通过提供替代证明（存在满足$y =$$1 - \frac{1}{\operatorname{freew}\left( Q\right) }$的${Q}^{\prime }$团嵌入）匹配了胡的下界（见命题D.1）。现应用定理8.5和定理8.6可获得星型查询的紧下界，并可推广至层次化查询（注意星型查询也属于层次化查询）。

For the star query ${Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }$ ,there exists a database $\mathcal{D}$ such that no algorithm can have runtime of $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| {Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }\left( \mathcal{D}\right) \right| }^{1 - 1/\ell  - \epsilon }}\right)$ for any real $\epsilon  > 0$ subject to the Boolean $k$ -clique conjecture.

For the star query ${Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }$ ,there exists a database $\mathcal{D}$ such that no algorithm can have runtime of $O\left( {\left| \mathcal{D}\right|  \cdot  {\left| {Q}_{\ell }^{ \star  }\left( \mathcal{D}\right) \right| }^{1 - 1/\ell  - \epsilon }}\right)$ for any real $\epsilon  > 0$ subject to the Min-Weight $k$ -Clique Conjecture.

## 9 Related Work

## 9 相关工作

Several prior works have investigated problems related to output sensitive evaluation. Deng et al. [16] presented a dynamic index structure for output sensitive join sampling. Riko and Stöckel [20] studied output-sensitive matrix multiplication (which is the two path query), a result that built upon the join-project query evaluation results from [5]. Deng et al. [15] presented output-sensitive evaluation algorithms for set similarity, an important practical class of queries used routinely in recommender systems, graph analytics, etc. An alternate way to evaluate join queries is to use delay based algorithms. As we saw in Section 5, our result is able to match the join running time obtained via [23] for star queries. Recent work [4] has also studied the problem of reporting $t$ patterns in graphs when the input is temporal, i.e., the input is changing over time. Output-sensitive algorithms has also been developed for the problem of maximal clique enumeration [11, 12, 25]. Several interesting results have been known for listing $k$ -cliques and $k$ -cycles in output-sensitive way. [10] designed output-sensitive algorithms for listing $t$ triangles,the simplest $k$ -clique,using fast matrix multiplication.The work of [22] and [1] show algorithms for list ${t4}$ -cycles,and [21] shows how to list ${t6}$ -cycles. The upper bounds were shown to be tight under the 3SUM hypothesis.

已有若干研究探讨了与输出敏感评估相关的问题。Deng等人[16]提出了一种用于输出敏感连接采样的动态索引结构。Riko和Stöckel[20]研究了输出敏感矩阵乘法（即双路径查询），该成果建立在文献[5]的连接-投影查询评估结果之上。Deng等人[15]针对集合相似性提出了输出敏感评估算法，这是推荐系统、图分析等领域常用的一类重要实用查询。另一种评估连接查询的方式是采用基于延迟的算法。如第5节所示，我们的研究成果能够匹配文献[23]在星型查询上获得的连接运行时间。近期研究[4]还探讨了当输入具有时序性（即随时间变化）时，如何报告$t$图模式的问题。针对极大团枚举问题[11,12,25]，研究者也开发了输出敏感算法。在输出敏感方式下列举$k$-团和$k$-循环方面，已有若干有趣成果。[10]利用快速矩阵乘法设计了列举$t$三角形（最简单的$k$-团）的输出敏感算法。[22]和[1]的研究展示了列举${t4}$-循环的算法，[21]则阐述了如何列举${t6}$-循环。根据3SUM假设，这些上界已被证明是紧致的。

## 10 Conclusion

## 10 结论

In this paper, we presented a novel generalization of Yannakakis algorithm that is provably faster for a large class of CQs. We show several applications of the generalized algorithm (which is combinatorial in nature) to recover state-of-the-art results known in existing literature, as well as new results for popular queries such as star queries and path queries. Surprisingly, our results show that for several queries, our combinatorial algorithm is better than known results that use fast matrix multiplication. We complement our upper bounds with a matching lower bound for a subclass of cyclic and acyclic CQs, for both Boolean semirings (a.k.a joins) as well as aggregations.

本文提出了一种Yannakakis算法的新颖泛化形式，经证明可显著加速一大类CQ的运算。我们展示了该泛化算法（本质上是组合算法）的多重应用：既能复现现有文献中的前沿成果，又能为星型查询、路径查询等常见查询提供新解法。令人惊讶的是，研究结果表明对于某些查询，我们的组合算法优于已知的快速矩阵乘法方案。我们还针对循环与非循环CQ的子类，在布尔半环（即连接运算）和聚合运算两方面，给出了与上界相匹配的下界证明。

Proposition 3.1. An acyclic $Q$ is free-connex acyclic if and only if all the variables of red $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ are free.

Proof. Consider an acyclic $\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$ such that all its variables in red $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ are free. Then,the hypergraph formed by adding a hyperedge containing all free variables is still acyclic, and thus satisfies the definition of free-connex acyclic. Indeed, there exists a join tree for the new hypergraph where the new hyperedge with all free variables is the root and all remaining hyperedges are its children.

证明。考虑一个无循环$\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$，其红色$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$中的所有变量均为自由变量。此时，通过添加包含所有自由变量的超边所构成的超图仍保持无循环性，因而满足自由连接无循环的定义。事实上，存在一个新超图的连接树，其中包含所有自由变量的新超边作为根节点，其余超边均为其子节点。

For the other direction,consider an acyclic $\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$ such that there exists a non-free variable (say $z$ ) in red $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ . Clearly,such a variable is not isolated. Let ${e}_{1}$ and ${e}_{2}$ be two hyperedges that contain $z$ . Let ${E}^{ * }$ be the hyperedge added to the hypergraph containing all the variables. We claim that the new hypergraph containing edge ${E}^{ * }$ is no longer acyclic. Indeed,suppose that ${E}^{ * }$ is the root of the join tree (if there exists a join tree,we can reorient it to make any node as the root). Note that ${e}_{1}$ and ${e}_{2}$ cannot be subsets of each other and thus,one cannot be in the subtree of the other in the join tree. This is because if (say) ${e}_{1}$ contains a free variable (say $f$ ) that is not contained in ${e}_{2}$ ,then having ${e}_{1}$ in the subtree of ${e}_{2}$ will violate the variable connectedness condition for $f$ ,as $f$ is present in ${E}^{ * }$ and ${e}_{1}$ but not in ${e}_{2}$ . Thus, ${e}_{1}$ and ${e}_{2}$ must be either be directly connected to ${E}^{ * }$ since both ${e}_{1}$ and ${e}_{2}$ may contain isolated free variables (all of which are also present in ${E}^{ * }$ ),or they may be connected to the root through a series of intermediate nodes. However,the variable $z$ is not present in ${E}^{ * }$ as ${E}^{ * }$ only contains free variables and thus,the connectedness condition cannot be satisfied for variable $z$ . Thus,a join tree cannot exist and the query cannot be free-connex acyclic.

对于另一方向，考虑一个无环的$\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$，使得红色$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$中存在非自由变量（例如$z$）。显然，该变量非孤立。设${e}_{1}$和${e}_{2}$为包含$z$的两个超边。令${E}^{ * }$为添加到包含所有变量的超图中的新超边。我们断言：包含边${E}^{ * }$的新超图不再无环。事实上，假设${E}^{ * }$是连接树的根（若存在连接树，可通过重定向使任意节点成为根）。注意${e}_{1}$与${e}_{2}$互不包含，因此在连接树中两者不能互为子树。这是因为若（例如）${e}_{1}$包含一个不在${e}_{2}$中的自由变量（例如$f$），将${e}_{1}$置于${e}_{2}$的子树中将违反$f$的变量连通条件——因$f$存在于${E}^{ * }$和${e}_{1}$却不在${e}_{2}$中。因此，${e}_{1}$与${e}_{2}$必须直接连接到${E}^{ * }$（因两者可能包含孤立自由变量，这些变量也存在于${E}^{ * }$），或通过中间节点序列连接到根。然而变量$z$不在${E}^{ * }$中（因${E}^{ * }$仅含自由变量），故无法满足$z$的连通条件。因此连接树不存在，该查询不可能是自由连接无环的。
