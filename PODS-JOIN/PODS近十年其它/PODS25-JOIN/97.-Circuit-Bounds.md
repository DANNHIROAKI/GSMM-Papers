# Circuit Bounds for Conjunctive Queries with Self-joins

含自连接合取查询的电路复杂度界限

AUSTEN Z. FAN, University of Wisconsin-Madison, USA

范奥斯汀·Z，美国威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校

赵杭东，美国威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校

In this paper, we study circuit size bounds for Conjunctive Queries (CQs) under different semiring semantics. Recent work established tight bounds for self-join-free CQs over the tropical semiring, among other results [16]. Here, we extend these results in two main directions. First, we prove a lower bound for any self-join-free CQ over the Boolean semiring by extending Razborov and Alon-Boppana's classic lower bound result for the $k$ -clique problem [1,38]. Second,we characterize the circuit complexity of CQs with self-joins by relating them to appropriate self-join free CQs. Interestingly, such correspondence crucially depends on the underlying semiring. To achieve this result,we present a novel technique of investigating the circuit complexity of a CQ with self-joins through the lens of endomorphisms.

本文研究不同半环语义下合取查询(CQs)的电路规模界限。近期研究[16]确立了热带半环上无自连接CQs的紧致界限。我们主要在两方面拓展这些成果：首先，通过扩展Razborov和Alon-Boppana关于$k$团问题的经典下界结果[1,38]，证明了布尔半环上任意无自连接CQ的下界；其次，通过将含自连接CQs关联到适当的无自连接CQs，刻画了其电路复杂性。值得注意的是，这种对应关系本质上取决于底层半环。为此，我们提出通过自同态视角研究含自连接CQs电路复杂性的新技术。

## 1 Introduction

## 1 引言

Recent work [16] considered the circuit size bounds for the sum-product polynomial for a self-join-free Conjunctive Query (CQ). Formally,the sum-product polynomial for a Boolean CQ $Q$ (with self-joins or not) is parameterized by an underlying semiring $\mathbb{S}$ and an instance $I$

近期研究[16]探讨了无自连接合取查询(CQ)的和积多项式电路规模界限。形式化定义中，布尔CQ$Q$(无论含自连接与否)的和积多项式由底层半环$\mathbb{S}$和实例$I$参数化

$$
{p}_{I}^{Q} \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\bigoplus }_{h \in  \operatorname{Hom}\left( {Q,I}\right) }{\bigotimes }_{R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \in  Q}{x}_{R\left( {h\left( \mathbf{x}\right) }\right) } \tag{1}
$$

where $\operatorname{Hom}\left( {Q,I}\right)$ is the set of all homomorphisms from $Q$ to $I$ and ${x}_{R\left( \mathrm{a}\right) }$ is a variable that annotates the tuple $R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ in $I$ ,taking values in the domain of the semiring.

其中$\operatorname{Hom}\left( {Q,I}\right)$表示从$Q$到$I$的所有同态集合，${x}_{R\left( \mathrm{a}\right) }$是标注实例$I$中元组$R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$的变量，其取值来自半环定义域

The central question we study in this paper is: given a ${CQ}$ (possibly with self-joins),a semiring $\mathbb{S}$ , and a set of constraints on an instance $I$ ,what is the smallest circuit over $\mathbb{S}$ for ${CQ}$ on $I$ ?

本文核心研究问题是：给定可能含自连接的${CQ}$、半环$\mathbb{S}$及实例$I$上的约束集，在$I$上计算${CQ}$的最小$\mathbb{S}$电路是什么？

We briefly introduce the notion of a circuit over a semiring. A circuit $F$ over a semiring $\mathbb{S}$ is a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) with input nodes the variables ${x}_{R\left( \mathrm{a}\right) }$ of fan-in 0 and every other node labelled by $\oplus$ or $\otimes$ of fan-in 2 ; these nodes are called $\oplus$ -gates and $\otimes$ -gates respectively [22]. A circuit $F$ that computes the sum-product polynomial has an output gate with fan-out 0 that,by sequentially going through all gates in a bottom-up fashion, evaluates the polynomial. The size of the circuit $F$ ,denoted as $\left| F\right|$ ,is the number of gates in $F$ . The study of circuits over a semiring is fruitful due to the following two reasons (see also [16] for a more detailed discussion):

简要介绍半环电路概念。半环$\mathbb{S}$上的电路$F$是有向无环图(DAG)，其输入节点为扇入0的变量${x}_{R\left( \mathrm{a}\right) }$，其他节点标记为扇入2的$\oplus$或$\otimes$门[22]。计算和积多项式的电路$F$具有扇出0的输出门，通过自底向上顺序遍历所有门来求值多项式。电路规模$\left| F\right|$指$F$中门的总数。研究半环电路具有双重意义(详见[16])：

(1) Circuits over a semiring can be seen as a model of computation that corresponds to algorithms that solely exploit the two algebraic operations in the underlying semiring. This model of computation is equivalent to monotone straight-line programs that computes a function given by a polynomial $\left\lbrack  {{20},{40}}\right\rbrack$ . In our setting,the polynomial is restricted to be the sum-product polynomial derived from a CQ on an instance $I$ over a semiring $\mathbb{S}$ .

(1) 半环上的电路可视为一种计算模型，对应仅利用底层半环中两种代数运算的算法。该计算模型等价于计算多项式$\left\lbrack  {{20},{40}}\right\rbrack$给定函数的单调直线程序。在本研究中，该多项式被限制为从半环$\mathbb{S}$上实例$I$导出的合查询(CQ)的和积多项式。

(2) Circuits that compute the sum-product polynomial of a CQ can be viewed as a concise representation of the corresponding provenance polynomial over a semiring, as introduced introduced by Green,Karvounarakis and Tannen [19]. It has been noted [16] that $d$ -representations in factorised databases $\left\lbrack  {5,{34},{35}}\right\rbrack$ can be seen as special cases of circuits for CQs where they are governed by exactly one tree decomposition.

(2) 计算合查询和积多项式的电路可视为半环上对应溯源多项式的简洁表示，由Green、Karvounarakis和Tannen[19]首次提出。已有研究指出[16]，因子化数据库$\left\lbrack  {5,{34},{35}}\right\rbrack$中的$d$表示可视为合查询电路的特例，这些电路严格受单一树分解控制。

The results in [16] have two limitations: they only work for self-join-free queries, and the lower bounds only apply to semirings that are at least as expressive as the tropical semiring (for instance, no lower bound was proved for the Boolean semiring $\mathbb{B}$ ). In this work,we take on both challenges.

文献[16]的研究存在双重局限：仅适用于无自连接查询，且下界仅对表达能力不低于热带半环的半环成立（例如未证明布尔半环$\mathbb{B}$的下界）。本研究将同时攻克这两个难题。

CQs with self-joins are notoriously difficult to deal with. Many areas, including enumeration complexity $\left\lbrack  {4,8,9}\right\rbrack$ ,consistent query answering $\left\lbrack  {{30},{31}}\right\rbrack$ and factorised databases $\left\lbrack  {5,{34},{35}}\right\rbrack$ enjoy a long line of success when restricted to self-join-free CQs, but immediately run into obstacles when trying to extend the results to CQs with self-joins. To have a slight taste of the difficulty, note that when a full $\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$ is self-join-free,a hypergraph suffices to encode all the information in $Q$ ; however,that is not the case for a CQ with self-joins,since different atoms might correspond to the same hyperedge. The fundamental technical difficulty is that the sum-product polynomials for CQs with self-joins are not multilinear and this makes the lower bound approach of [16] fail.

含自连接的合查询素以棘手著称。在枚举复杂度$\left\lbrack  {4,8,9}\right\rbrack$、一致性查询应答$\left\lbrack  {{30},{31}}\right\rbrack$和因子化数据库$\left\lbrack  {5,{34},{35}}\right\rbrack$等诸多领域，研究者在局限于无自连接合查询时取得系列成果，但一旦尝试将结论推广至含自连接的合查询便立即受阻。略窥其难度：当完整$\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$无自连接时，超图足以编码$Q$中所有信息；但对含自连接的合查询则不然，因不同原子可能对应相同超边。根本技术难点在于含自连接合查询的和积多项式并非多重线性，这使得文献[16]的下界方法失效。

The same technical issue occurs when we consider circuits over the Boolean semiring. In this setting, even for self-join-free CQs, the polynomial produced by the optimal circuit may not be multilinear. Hence, we have to use fundamentally different arguments compared to [16] in order to make progress in the lower bounds.

考虑布尔半环上的电路时同样存在该技术问题。在此情境下，即便对于无自连接合查询，最优电路产生的多项式也可能非多重线性。因此，为在下界研究中取得突破，我们必须采用与文献[16]截然不同的论证方法。

## 2 Preliminaries

## 2 预备知识

In this section, we present an overview of important terminologies used throughout the paper.

本节概述全文使用的重要术语。

Conjunctive Queries. A Conjunctive Query (CQ) is defined as an expression consisting of a conjunction of atoms:

合取查询。合取查询（CQ）定义为由原子公式合取组成的表达式：

$$
Q\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{U}\right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{1}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{1}\right)  \land  {R}_{2}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{2}\right)  \land  \cdots  \land  {R}_{\ell }\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{\ell }\right) 
$$

where ${\mathbf{x}}_{U},{\mathbf{x}}_{i}$ are tuples of variables or constants. The variables in ${\mathbf{x}}_{U}$ are called free variables. We say that $Q$ is Boolean if ${\mathbf{x}}_{U}$ is the empty tuple and full if the free variables are all the variables in the query body. The set of variables of $Q$ is denoted as $\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$ . A CQ is said to have self-joins if it contains two atoms with the same relational symbol; otherwise, it is called self-join-free. A CQ with self-joins can have an atom occur multiple times,e.g. the CQ $Q \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,y}\right)$ is allowed. This means that $Q$ can be viewed as a bag of atoms. Given an instance $I$ ,a homomorphism from $Q$ to $I$ is a function $h : \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)  \rightarrow  \operatorname{adom}{\left( I\right) }^{1}$ such that for every atom ${R}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$ in $Q$ ,it holds that ${R}_{i}\left( {h\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right) }\right)  \in  I$ . We denote by $\operatorname{Hom}\left( {Q,I}\right)$ the set of all homomorphisms from $Q$ to $I$ .

其中${\mathbf{x}}_{U},{\mathbf{x}}_{i}$为变量或常量元组。${\mathbf{x}}_{U}$中的变量称为自由变量。若${\mathbf{x}}_{U}$为空元组则称$Q$为布尔查询，若查询体中所有变量均为自由变量则称其为完全查询。$Q$的变量集合记为$\operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)$。若CQ包含两个相同关系符号的原子公式则称其具有自连接，否则称为无自连接。具有自连接的CQ允许原子公式重复出现，例如允许$Q \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,y}\right)$这样的查询。这意味着$Q$可视为原子公式的多重集。给定实例$I$，从$Q$到$I$的同态是一个函数$h : \operatorname{var}\left( Q\right)  \rightarrow  \operatorname{adom}{\left( I\right) }^{1}$，使得对于$Q$中的每个原子公式${R}_{i}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$，都有${R}_{i}\left( {h\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right) }\right)  \in  I$成立。用$\operatorname{Hom}\left( {Q,I}\right)$表示所有从$Q$到$I$同态的集合。

Given a CQ $Q$ ,we call the self-join-free counterpart of $Q$ ,denoted as $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ ,to be the CQ which replaces atoms that share the same relational symbol with distinct fresh relational symbols. For example,consider the CQ ${Q}_{1}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,w}\right)  \land  S\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  S\left( {w,z}\right)$ depicted in Figure 1a. Its self-join-free counterpart $\left\lbrack  {Q}_{1}\right\rbrack  \left( \right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{1}\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  {R}_{2}\left( {x,w}\right)  \land  {S}_{1}\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  {S}_{2}\left( {w,z}\right)$ is depicted in Figure 1b.

给定一个合取查询(CQ) $Q$，我们将其无自连接副本（记作$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$）定义为：用不同的新关系符号替换所有共享相同关系符号的原子所得到的CQ。例如，图1a所示的CQ${Q}_{1}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,w}\right)  \land  S\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  S\left( {w,z}\right)$，其无自连接副本$\left\lbrack  {Q}_{1}\right\rbrack  \left( \right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{1}\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  {R}_{2}\left( {x,w}\right)  \land  {S}_{1}\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  {S}_{2}\left( {w,z}\right)$如图1b所示。

With each self-join-free CQ $Q$ we can associate a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ ,where each node represents a variable in $Q$ and each hyperedge contains the variables of an atom in $Q$ .

对于每个无自连接CQ$Q$，可关联一个超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$：其中节点表示$Q$中的变量，超边包含$Q$中某个原子的所有变量。

Semirings. A (commutative) semiring is an algebraic structure $\mathbb{S} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathbf{D},\oplus ,\otimes ,\mathbf{0},\mathbf{1}}\right)$ ,where $\oplus$ and $\otimes$ are the addition and multiplication in $\mathbb{S}$ such that: $\left( 1\right) \left( {\mathbf{D},\oplus ,\mathbf{0}}\right)$ and $\left( {\mathbf{D},\otimes ,\mathbf{1}}\right)$ are commutative monoids,(2) $\otimes$ is distributive over $\oplus$ ,and (3)0is an annihilator of $\otimes$ in $\mathbf{D}$ . We say that $\mathbb{S}$ is idempotent if $x \oplus  x = x$ for every $x \in  \mathrm{D}$ and absorptive if $\mathbf{1} \oplus  x = \mathbf{1}$ for every $x \in  \mathrm{D}$ .

半环。一个(交换)半环是代数结构$\mathbb{S} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathbf{D},\oplus ,\otimes ,\mathbf{0},\mathbf{1}}\right)$，其中$\oplus$和$\otimes$分别表示$\mathbb{S}$上的加法与乘法运算，满足：(1)$\left( 1\right) \left( {\mathbf{D},\oplus ,\mathbf{0}}\right)$与$\left( {\mathbf{D},\otimes ,\mathbf{1}}\right)$构成交换幺半群，(2)$\otimes$对$\oplus$满足分配律，(3)0在$\mathbf{D}$中是$\otimes$的零化子。若对所有$x \in  \mathrm{D}$满足$x \oplus  x = x$则称$\mathbb{S}$是幂等的；若对所有$x \in  \mathrm{D}$满足$\mathbf{1} \oplus  x = \mathbf{1}$则称其具有吸收性。

Green et al. [19] observed that the semantics of CQ are in natural correspondence to the underlying semiring. For example, set semantics correspond to computing a query over the Boolean semiring $\mathbb{B} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\{ 0,1\} ,\vee ,\land ,\text{ FALSE,TRUE }}\right)$ ,while bag semantics correspond to the Counting semiring $\mathbb{C} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathbb{N},+,\cdot ,0,1}\right)$ where $\mathbb{N}$ is the set of natural numbers. Other important semirings include the Tropical semiring $\mathbb{T} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathbb{Z}\cup \{  + \infty \} ,\min ,+, + \infty ,0}\right)$ which is isomorphic to the Max semiring $\mathbb{M} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ \mathbb{Z} \cup  \{  - \infty \} ,\max , + , - \infty ,0{\} }^{2}$ ,the positive tropical semiring ${\mathbb{T}}^{ + } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {{\mathbb{R}}^{ + }\cup \{  + \infty \} ,\min , + , + \infty ,0}\right)$ and the positive Max semiring ${\mathbb{M}}^{ + } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathbb{N}\cup \{  - \infty \} ,\max ,+, - \infty ,0}\right)$ ,all of which are closely related to optimization problems $\left\lbrack  {{24},{25}}\right\rbrack$ . Our work follows the approach in [19],where an instance is interpreted as an instance tagged with annotations from a semiring ( i.e., a $K$ -relation).

Green等人[19]指出，CQ的语义与底层半环存在自然对应关系。例如，集合语义对应于在布尔半环$\mathbb{B} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\{ 0,1\} ,\vee ,\land ,\text{ FALSE,TRUE }}\right)$上计算查询，而包语义则对应于计数半环$\mathbb{C} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathbb{N},+,\cdot ,0,1}\right)$，其中$\mathbb{N}$表示自然数集。其他重要半环包括与Max半环$\mathbb{M} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ \mathbb{Z} \cup  \{  - \infty \} ,\max , + , - \infty ,0{\} }^{2}$同构的热带半环$\mathbb{T} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathbb{Z}\cup \{  + \infty \} ,\min ,+, + \infty ,0}\right)$、正热带半环${\mathbb{T}}^{ + } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {{\mathbb{R}}^{ + }\cup \{  + \infty \} ,\min , + , + \infty ,0}\right)$以及正Max半环${\mathbb{M}}^{ + } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\mathbb{N}\cup \{  - \infty \} ,\max ,+, - \infty ,0}\right)$，这些半环都与优化问题$\left\lbrack  {{24},{25}}\right\rbrack$密切相关。我们的研究遵循[19]的方法，将实例解释为带有半环标注的实例（即$K$-关系）。

A sum-product polynomial is always homogeneous: the sum of the exponents in every monomial is the same. We also say that it is multilinear if the exponent of every variable in a monomial is one. A crucial observation is that if $Q$ is self-join-free,then ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ must be multilinear; however,this is not true if the query has self-joins. We differentiate the notions of term and monomial for a sum-product polynomial carefully: a term is a formal product of variables,where the product $\otimes$ is interpreted over a semiring,whereas a monomial is a term with a coefficient $a \in  \mathbb{N}$ ,which is interpreted as the number of additions $\oplus$ of the term over the same semiring.

和积多项式总是齐次的：每个单项式中指数的总和相同。若单项式中每个变量的指数均为1，则称其为多重线性的。关键观察发现，若$Q$无自连接，则${p}_{I}^{Q}$必为多重线性；但查询存在自连接时此结论不成立。我们严格区分和积多项式的项与单项式概念：项是变量的形式乘积，其中乘积$\otimes$在半环上解释；而单项式是带系数$a \in  \mathbb{N}$的项，该系数解释为同一半环上项的加法次数$\oplus$。

Any sum-product polynomial $p$ over a semiring $\mathbb{S}$ with $n$ variables defines a function $\widehat{p} : {\mathbb{S}}^{n} \rightarrow  \mathbb{S}$ whose value $p\left( {{s}_{1},{s}_{2},\ldots ,{s}_{n}}\right)$ is obtained by substituting elements ${s}_{i} \in  \mathbb{S}$ for ${x}_{i}$ in $p$ . Two sum-product polynomials ${p}_{1},{p}_{2}$ are equivalent over $\mathbb{S}$ ,denoted as ${p}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{2}$ ,if $\widehat{{p}_{1}}\left( \mathbf{s}\right)  = \widehat{{p}_{2}}\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$ for all $\mathbf{s} \in  {\mathbb{S}}^{n}\left\lbrack  {22}\right\rbrack$ .

任何含$n$个变量的半环$\mathbb{S}$上的和积多项式$p$，都定义了通过将${x}_{i}$替换为元素${s}_{i} \in  \mathbb{S}$来获得值$p\left( {{s}_{1},{s}_{2},\ldots ,{s}_{n}}\right)$的函数$\widehat{p} : {\mathbb{S}}^{n} \rightarrow  \mathbb{S}$。若对所有$\mathbf{s} \in  {\mathbb{S}}^{n}\left\lbrack  {22}\right\rbrack$满足$\widehat{{p}_{1}}\left( \mathbf{s}\right)  = \widehat{{p}_{2}}\left( \mathbf{s}\right)$，则称两个和积多项式${p}_{1},{p}_{2}$在$\mathbb{S}$上等价，记作${p}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{2}$。

Circuits over Semirings. A circuit $F$ over a semiring $\mathbb{S}$ is a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) with input nodes (with fan-in 0 ) variables in a set ${S}_{x}$ containing ${x}_{R\left( \mathrm{a}\right) }$ ’s in the RHS of Equation 2 and the constants0,1. Every other node is labelled by $\oplus$ or $\otimes$ and has fan-in 2; these nodes are called $\oplus$ -gates and $\otimes$ -gates,respectively. An input gate of $F$ is any gate with fan-in 0 and an output gate of $F$ is any gate with fan-out 0 . The size of the circuit $F$ ,denoted as $\left| F\right|$ ,is the number of gates in $F$ . The depth of $F$ is the length of the longest path from an input to an output node.

半环上的电路。半环$\mathbb{S}$上的电路$F$是一个有向无环图(DAG)，其输入节点（扇入为0）包含方程2右侧的${x}_{R\left( \mathrm{a}\right) }$及常量0,1的变量集${S}_{x}$。其他节点标记为$\oplus$或$\otimes$且扇入为2，分别称为$\oplus$门和$\otimes$门。电路$F$的输入门指扇入为0的门，输出门指扇出为0的门。电路$F$的规模$\left| F\right|$指其包含的门数量，深度指从输入到输出节点的最长路径长度。

In this paper, the goal of circuits over some semiring is to compute sum-product polynomials over that semiring. A circuit $F$ can be viewed as a concise representation of a polynomial $\widehat{F}$ over the variable set ${S}_{x}$ . A circuit $F$ is said to compute a polynomial $p$ if $\widehat{F}$ and $p$ coincide as functions (interpreted over the semiring $\mathbb{S}$ ),and is said to produce a polynomial $p$ if $\widehat{F}$ and $p$ ,when written as sums of products of variables and coefficients, are exactly the same modulo the ordering of the summands and the ordering of variables and coefficients within each summand.

本文中，半环电路的目标是计算该半环上的和积多项式。电路$F$可视为变量集${S}_{x}$上多项式$\widehat{F}$的紧凑表示。若$\widehat{F}$与$p$作为半环$\mathbb{S}$上的函数等价，则称电路计算该多项式；若两者展开为变量与系数的积和式后完全一致（仅求和项顺序及各求和项内变量系数顺序不同），则称电路生成该多项式。

A circuit $F$ is homogeneous if every polynomial produced in each gate in $F$ is homogeneous,and is multilinear if for every $\otimes$ -gate $u = v \otimes  w$ ,the polynomials produced at gates $v$ and $w$ have a disjoint set of variables.

若电路$F$中每个门生成的多项式均为齐次式，则称该电路为齐次电路；若对每个$\otimes$门$u = v \otimes  w$，门$v$与$w$生成的多项式具有互不相交的变量集，则称该电路为多线性电路。

Let $\mathbb{S}\left( p\right)$ and $\mathbb{S}\left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ be the minimum size of a circuit over semiring $\mathbb{S}$ computing and producing $p$ , respectively. If $F$ produces $p$ ,then $F$ must compute $p$ ,but not necessarily the other way around.

设$\mathbb{S}\left( p\right)$和$\mathbb{S}\left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$分别为在半环$\mathbb{S}$上计算和生成$p$的电路最小规模。若$F$能生成$p$，则$F$必能计算$p$，反之则未必成立。

We also define $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)$ for some integer $N > 0$ to be the size of the smallest circuit computing the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ over $\mathbb{S}$ over all instances $I$ that have relations with size at most $N$ . In other words, $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)$ is a worst-case bound. The following tight circuit bounds were proved in [16].

我们还定义<b0></b1>为在规模不超过$N$的所有实例$I$上，计算多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$的最小电路规模。换言之，$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)$是最坏情况下的界。文献[16]证明了以下紧致电路界。

THEOREM 2.3 (Lower BOUND [16]). For any self-join-free ${CQQ}$ with hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ ,and any semiring $\mathbb{S} \in  \{ {\mathbb{B}}_{\text{lin }},\mathbb{T},\mathbb{C}{\} }^{3}$ it holds that $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ .

定理2.3（下界[16]）。对于任何无自连接的${CQQ}$及其超图$\mathcal{H}$，以及任意半环$\mathbb{S} \in  \{ {\mathbb{B}}_{\text{lin }},\mathbb{T},\mathbb{C}{\} }^{3}$，都有$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$成立。

Then,there exists a multilinear and homogeneous circuit $F$ of size $O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ that computes the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ over any idempotent semiring.

定理2.4（上界[16]）。设Q为无自连接的CQ，I是每个关系规模不超过$N$的实例。则存在规模为$O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$的多线性齐次电路$F$，可在任意幂等半环上计算多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$。

在证明上述结果时，文献[16]的作者运用了以下引理，将电路规模下界从一个半环转移到另一个半环。

引理2.5（[22]）。若$p$是多线性齐次多项式，则下列等式成立：${\mathbb{B}}_{\text{lin }}\left( p\right)  = \mathbb{T}\left( p\right)  = \mathbb{T}\left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack   = \mathbb{C}\left( p\right)  = \mathbb{C}\left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$。

引理2.7. 若存在满射半环同态$h : {\mathbb{S}}_{1} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{S}}_{2}$，则对任意多项式$p$有${\mathbb{S}}_{1}\left( p\right)  \geq  {\mathbb{S}}_{2}\left( p\right)$。

We call a semiring $\mathbb{S}$ positive if $\left( i\right) \mathbf{0} \neq  \mathbf{1}$ , $\left( {ii}\right) a \oplus  b = \mathbf{0}$ implies $a = \mathbf{0}$ and $b = \mathbf{0}$ ,and $\left( {iii}\right) a \otimes  b = \mathbf{0}$ implies $a = \mathbf{0}$ or $b = \mathbf{0}$ . Homomorphisms,Endomorphisms,Automorphisms. A mapping $h$ from the variables of a CQ ${Q}_{1}$ to the variables of a $\mathrm{{CQ}}{Q}_{2}$ is a homomorphism from ${Q}_{1}$ to ${Q}_{2}$ if $\left( 1\right) R\left( {h\left( \mathbf{x}\right) }\right)  \in  {Q}_{2}$ whenever $R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \in  {Q}_{1}$ and (2) $h\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{{U}_{1}}\right)  = h\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{{U}_{2}}\right)$ where ${\mathbf{x}}_{{U}_{1}}$ and ${\mathbf{x}}_{{U}_{2}}$ are the free variables of ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ respectively. An endomorphism of $Q$ is a homomorphism from $Q$ to itself. An automorphism of $Q$ is an endomorphism of $Q$ that is also an isomorphism.

同态、自同态与自同构。若映射$h$将合取查询${Q}_{1}$的变量映至$\mathrm{{CQ}}{Q}_{2}$的变量，且满足：(1)当$R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)  \in  {Q}_{1}$时$\left( 1\right) R\left( {h\left( \mathbf{x}\right) }\right)  \in  {Q}_{2}$；(2)$h\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{{U}_{1}}\right)  = h\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{{U}_{2}}\right)$（其中${\mathbf{x}}_{{U}_{1}}$和${\mathbf{x}}_{{U}_{2}}$分别是${Q}_{1}$与${Q}_{2}$的自由变量），则称$h$为从${Q}_{1}$到${Q}_{2}$的同态。$Q$的自同态是其到自身的同态，而自同构则是双射的自同态。

示例2.8. 考虑图1中的查询${Q}_{1}$。映射$x \rightarrow  x,y \rightarrow  w,w \rightarrow  y,z \rightarrow  z$是${Q}_{1}$的自同构，而映射$x \rightarrow  x,y \rightarrow  y,w \rightarrow  y,z \rightarrow  z$是自同态但非自同构。

CQ Equivalence. Given two CQs ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ ,we say they are syntactically equivalent if they are identical up to variable renaming. This notion is also called (body) isomorphism in [8, 12, 13]. For example,the CQs ${Q}_{1}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( x\right)  \land  S\left( {x,y}\right)$ and ${Q}_{2}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  S\left( {y,x}\right)  \land  R\left( y\right)$ are syntactically equivalent. In contrast,we say that two queries are semantically equivalent over a semiring $\mathbb{S}$ ,denoted as ${Q}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{Q}_{2}$ ,if upon any $\mathbb{S}$ -annotated input relation $I,{Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ return the same result.

CQ等价性。给定两个合取查询${Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{2}$，若它们仅存在变量重命名的差异，则称其具有语法等价性。该概念在文献[8,12,13]中也被称为（体）同构。例如，合取查询${Q}_{1}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( x\right)  \land  S\left( {x,y}\right)$与${Q}_{2}\left( \right)  \leftarrow  S\left( {y,x}\right)  \land  R\left( y\right)$语法等价。相对地，若两个查询在半环$\mathbb{S}$上对所有带$\mathbb{S}$标注的输入关系$I,{Q}_{1}$返回相同结果，则称其语义等价，记作${Q}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{Q}_{2}$。

For an endomorphism $h$ ,by $h\left( Q\right)$ we mean the query that contains all variables and atoms that are in the image of $h$ . In other words, $h$ is surjective when viewed both as mappings from $Q$ to $h\left( Q\right)$ between variables and atoms. The following lemma gives a characterization when two queries are syntactically equivalent in terms of homomorphisms (since the converse of the lemma is obvious).

对于自同态$h$，$h\left( Q\right)$表示包含$h$映射像中所有变量和原子的查询。换言之，当将$h$视为变量间和原子间的映射时，其从$Q$到$h\left( Q\right)$是满射的。以下引理通过同态给出了两个查询语法等价的判定条件（因该引理的逆命题显然成立）。

LEMMA 2.9. If there exists a homomorphism $h$ from ${Q}_{1}$ to ${Q}_{2}$ and a homomorphism $g$ from ${Q}_{2}$ to ${Q}_{1}$ such that $h\left( {Q}_{1}\right)  = {Q}_{2}$ and $g\left( {Q}_{2}\right)  = {Q}_{1}$ ,then ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are syntactically equivalent.

引理2.9. 若存在从${Q}_{1}$到${Q}_{2}$的同态$h$，以及从${Q}_{2}$到${Q}_{1}$的同态$g$，且满足$h\left( {Q}_{1}\right)  = {Q}_{2}$和$g\left( {Q}_{2}\right)  = {Q}_{1}$，则${Q}_{1}$与${Q}_{2}$语法等价。

Proof. Clearly, $h$ and $g$ are bijective when viewed as mappings between variables. Since $g \circ$ $h\left( {Q}_{1}\right)  = {Q}_{1}$ and $g \circ  h\left( {Q}_{2}\right)  = {Q}_{2}$ ,the mappings between atoms in ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ that are induced by $h$ and $g$ must be bijective as well. This implies that ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are the same up to variable renaming, as witnessed by $h$ and $g$ . Homomorphic Equivalence. Two CQs ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are said to be homomorphically equivalent if there exists a homomorphism $h$ from ${Q}_{1}$ to ${Q}_{2}$ and a homomorphism $g$ from ${Q}_{2}$ to ${Q}_{1}$ . This is a syntactic property between two CQs, and has been leveraged heavily in several prior works [3, 12, 14, 41]. It is not hard to show that for a large class of semirings, two homomorphically equivalent queries are semantically equivalent over any semiring in the class. In fact, Theorem 5.4 shows that over the class of semirings called ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ defined in Section 5,two CQs ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are homomorphically equivalent if and only if they are semantically equivalent. The situation could be different outside ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ . Consider the queries ${Q}_{1},{Q}_{2}$ and ${Q}_{3}$ in Figure 1. Note that all these queries are homomorphically equivalent to each other. However, ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are not semantically equivalent over $\mathbb{T} \notin  {\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ ,since ${Q}_{1}$ will always have 2 times the result of ${Q}_{2}$ . Moreover, ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{3}$ are semantically equivalent over $\mathbb{T}$ but not semantically equivalent over $\mathbb{C} \notin  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ . 证明。显然，当被视为变量间的映射时，$h$和$g$是双射的。由于$g \circ$$h\left( {Q}_{1}\right)  = {Q}_{1}$和$g \circ  h\left( {Q}_{2}\right)  = {Q}_{2}$，由$h$和$g$诱导的${Q}_{1}$与${Q}_{2}$中原子间的映射也必然是双射的。这意味着${Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{2}$在变量重命名下是等价的，这由$h$和$g$所见证。同态等价性。若存在从${Q}_{1}$到${Q}_{2}$的同态$h$及反向的同态$g$，则称两个CQ（合取查询）${Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{2}$是同态等价的。这是两个CQ间的语法属性，在先前多项研究中被广泛利用[3,12,14,41]。不难证明，对于一大类半环而言，两个同态等价的查询在该类任何半环上都是语义等价的。实际上，定理5.4表明，在第5节定义的${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$类半环上，两个CQ${Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{2}$同态等价当且仅当它们语义等价。在${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$之外情况可能不同。考虑图1中的查询${Q}_{1},{Q}_{2}$和${Q}_{3}$。注意这些查询彼此间都是同态等价的。然而，在$\mathbb{T} \notin  {\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$上${Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{2}$并非语义等价，因为${Q}_{1}$的结果总是${Q}_{2}$的两倍。此外，${Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{3}$在$\mathbb{T}$上语义等价，但在$\mathbb{C} \notin  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$上则不然。最后，即使在$\mathbb{B} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$上（此时${Q}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{B}}{Q}_{2}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{B}}{Q}_{3}$），${Q}_{1},{Q}_{2}$和${Q}_{3}$也非语法等价。

## 3 Lower Bounds for Boolean circuits for self-join-free CQs

## 3 无自连接合取查询的布尔电路下界

In this section, all CQs are assumed to be self-join-free, so we will identify the CQ with its hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ . Our goal is to prove lower bounds on the size of circuits over the Boolean semiring. This is quite challenging, since the techniques developed in [16] to show tight lower bounds cannot be applied to the Boolean semiring. The main technical issue is that a Boolean circuit that computes a multilinear polynomial (as is ${p}_{I}^{\mathcal{H}}$ ) does not need to be multilinear,and the arguments in [16] only apply for multilinear circuits.

本节中，所有CQ均假设为无自连接的，因此我们将CQ与其超图$\mathcal{H}$视为等同。目标是证明布尔半环上电路规模的下界。这极具挑战性，因为文献[16]中用于证明紧下界的技术无法应用于布尔半环。主要技术障碍在于，计算多元线性多项式（如${p}_{I}^{\mathcal{H}}$）的布尔电路本身不必是多元线性的，而[16]中的论证仅适用于多元线性电路。

### 3.1 Lower Bounds via Clique Embeddings

### 3.1 通过团嵌入证明下界

Fortunately, we have a starting point, which is a classical result by Razborov [38], later improved by Alon and Boppana [1]. To state the result,define the polynomial ${C}_{k}^{n}$ over $\left( \begin{array}{l} n \\  2 \end{array}\right)$ Boolean variables that takes as input an undirected graph on $n$ vertices (where each variable ${x}_{\{ i,j\} }$ corresponds to whether the edge $\{ i,j\}$ exists for $1 \leq  i \neq  j \leq  n$ ) and returns true iff the graph contains a $k$ -clique.

幸运的是，我们有一个起点，即Razborov[38]的经典结果，后由Alon和Boppana[1]改进。为陈述该结果，定义在$\left( \begin{array}{l} n \\  2 \end{array}\right)$个布尔变量上的多项式${C}_{k}^{n}$，其输入为$n$个顶点上的无向图（其中每个变量${x}_{\{ i,j\} }$对应边$\{ i,j\}$是否存在，其中$1 \leq  i \neq  j \leq  n$），当且仅当该图包含一个$k$-团时返回真值。

THEOREM 3.1 (ALON &BOPANA [1]). For any constant $k \geq  3$ ,every circuit $F$ over the Boolean semiring that computes ${C}_{k}^{n}$ must have size $\left| F\right|  = \Omega \left( \frac{{n}^{k}}{{\log }^{k}n}\right)$ .

定理3.1（ALON & BOPANA [1]）。对于任意常数$k \geq  3$，在布尔半环上计算${C}_{k}^{n}$的每个电路$F$必须具有规模$\left| F\right|  = \Omega \left( \frac{{n}^{k}}{{\log }^{k}n}\right)$。

In particular,we cannot have a Boolean circuit of size $O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$ for some constant $\epsilon  > 0$ . To be able to apply the above result to our setting, we need two steps. The first step is to show that the lower bound works for the $k$ -clique polynomial as defined over a $k$ -partite graph. Concretely,we define the polynomial ${P}_{k}^{n}$ as follows. The input is a $k$ -partite graph with vertex set ${V}_{1} \times  \cdots  \times  {V}_{k}$ , each ${V}_{i}$ of size $n$ (this means no edge exists within a partition ${V}_{i}$ ). The output is true if and only if the graph contains a $k$ -clique; such a clique must have exactly one vertex in each partition ${V}_{i}$ . It is clear that given a Boolean circuit $F$ that computes ${C}_{k}^{n}$ ,one can construct a Boolean circuit $F$ that computes ${P}_{k}^{n}$ . We show that the converse is also true.

特别地，我们无法获得规模为$O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$的布尔电路（其中$\epsilon  > 0$为某常数）。要将上述结果应用于我们的设定，需要两个步骤：首先证明该下界对于定义在$k$-分划图上的$k$-团多项式同样成立。具体而言，我们如下定义多项式${P}_{k}^{n}$：输入为具有顶点集${V}_{1} \times  \cdots  \times  {V}_{k}$的$k$-分划图，每个分划${V}_{i}$大小为$n$（这意味着分划${V}_{i}$内部不存在边），当且仅当该图包含一个$k$-团时输出真值；此类团必须在每个分划${V}_{i}$中恰好包含一个顶点。显然，给定计算${C}_{k}^{n}$的布尔电路$F$，可构造出计算${P}_{k}^{n}$的布尔电路$F$。我们证明其逆命题同样成立。

LEMMA 3.2. For any constant $\epsilon  > 0$ and $k \geq  3$ ,there exists no circuit that computes ${P}_{k}^{n}$ over the Boolean semiring with size $O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$ .

引理3.2。对于任意常数$\epsilon  > 0$和$k \geq  3$，不存在能在布尔半环上以规模$O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$计算${P}_{k}^{n}$的电路。

Proof of LEMMA 3.2. It suffices to show that given a circuit over $\mathbb{B}$ that computes the polynomial ${P}_{k}^{n}$ ,one can construct a circuit over $\mathbb{B}$ that computes ${C}_{k}^{n}$ . Let $F$ be a circuit over the Boolean semiring that computes ${P}_{k}^{n}$ . The input gates of the circuit are of the form ${y}_{\{ \left( {i,p}\right) ,\left( {j,q}\right) \} }$ where $1 \leq  i,j, \leq  n$ and $1 \leq  p \neq  q \leq  k$ : this is the gate that corresponds to the edge that connects vertex $i$ of partition ${V}_{p}$ to vertex $j$ of partition ${V}_{q}$ . We construct a circuit ${F}^{\prime }$ for ${C}_{k}^{n}$ as follows. It is identical to $F$ ,except for the input wires which we reroute to the new input gates. In particular,if the input gate is ${y}_{\{ \left( {i,p}\right) ,\left( {j,q}\right) \} }$ and $i = j$ we reroute it to0,otherwise we reroute it to the input gate ${x}_{\{ i,j\} }$ . It is easy to see that ${F}^{\prime }$ correctly computes ${C}_{k}^{n}$ ,since we have the property that every $k$ -clique formed in ${P}_{k}^{n}$ needs to have exactly one vertex on each partition (and of course all vertices are distinct).

引理3.2的证明。只需证明：给定一个在$\mathbb{B}$上计算多项式${P}_{k}^{n}$的电路，就能构造出在$\mathbb{B}$上计算${C}_{k}^{n}$的电路。设$F$为布尔半环上计算${P}_{k}^{n}$的电路，其输入门形式为${y}_{\{ \left( {i,p}\right) ,\left( {j,q}\right) \} }$，其中$1 \leq  i,j, \leq  n$且$1 \leq  p \neq  q \leq  k$：该门对应连接分区${V}_{p}$顶点$i$与分区${V}_{q}$顶点$j$的边。我们按如下方式构建计算${C}_{k}^{n}$的电路${F}^{\prime }$：除将输入线重定向至新输入门外，其余与$F$完全相同。具体而言，若输入门为${y}_{\{ \left( {i,p}\right) ,\left( {j,q}\right) \} }$且$i = j$则重定向至0，否则重定向至输入门${x}_{\{ i,j\} }$。显然${F}^{\prime }$能正确计算${C}_{k}^{n}$，因为${P}_{k}^{n}$中形成的每个$k$团需满足各分区恰好包含一个顶点（且所有顶点互异）。

For the second step, we need to apply the idea of a clique embedding [15]. Intuitively, a clique embedding embeds an instance of a clique problem inside a query, so that an algorithm that solves the query will also solve the clique problem. This idea was used to prove conditional lower bounds for query evaluation [15], but here we will use it to get unconditional lower bounds for circuits.

第二步需要应用团嵌入思想[15]。直观而言，团嵌入将团问题实例植入查询中，使得解决该查询的算法也能解决团问题。该思想曾用于证明查询求值的条件性下界[15]，但本文将用它获得电路的无条件性下界。

We will first need some definitions. We say that two sets of vertices $X,Y \subseteq  V\left( H\right)$ touch in $\mathcal{H}$ if either $X \cap  Y \neq  \varnothing$ or there is a hyperedge $e \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ that intersects both $X$ and $Y$ .

首先需要若干定义。在$\mathcal{H}$中，若顶点集$X,Y \subseteq  V\left( H\right)$满足$X \cap  Y \neq  \varnothing$或存在超边$e \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$同时与$X$和$Y$相交，则称这两个顶点集相接触。

Definition 3.3 (Clique Embedding). Let $k \geq  3$ and $\mathcal{H}$ be a hypergraph. A $k$ -clique embedding, denoted ${C}_{k} \mapsto  H$ ,is a mapping $\psi$ that maps every $v \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$ to a non-empty subset $\psi \left( v\right)  \subseteq$ $V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ such that the following hold:

定义3.3（团嵌入）。设$k \geq  3$与$\mathcal{H}$为超图，$k$团嵌入记作${C}_{k} \mapsto  H$，是满足以下条件的映射$\psi$：将每个$v \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$映射为非空子集$\psi \left( v\right)  \subseteq$$V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$：

(1) $\psi \left( v\right)$ induces a connected subgraph;

(1) $\psi \left( v\right)$诱导出连通子图；

(2) for any two $u \neq  v \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$ then $\psi \left( u\right) ,\psi \left( v\right)$ touch in $\mathcal{H}$ .

(2) 对任意两个$u \neq  v \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$，$\psi \left( u\right) ,\psi \left( v\right)$在$\mathcal{H}$中相接触。

It will often be convenient to describe a clique embedding $\psi$ by the reverse mapping ${\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right)  =$ $\{ i \mid  x \in  \psi \left( i\right) \}$ ,where $x$ is a vertex in $V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ . For a hyperedge $e \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,its weak edge depth is ${d}_{\psi }\left( e\right)  = \left| {\{ v \mid  \psi \left( v\right)  \cap  e \neq  \varnothing \} }\right|$ ,i.e. the number of colors from $\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ that map to some vertex in $e$ . The weak edge depth of an embedding $\psi$ can then be defined as wed $\left( \psi \right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{e}{d}_{\psi }\left( e\right)$ . Additionally,we define as $\operatorname{wed}\left( {{C}_{k} \mapsto  H}\right)$ the minimum weak edge depth of any clique embedding $\psi$ . We can now define the following quantity, which we call the clique embedding power:

描述团嵌入$\psi$时，采用逆映射${\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right)  =$$\{ i \mid  x \in  \psi \left( i\right) \}$往往更为便利，其中$x$表示$V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$中的一个顶点。对于超边$e \in  E\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$，其弱边深度定义为${d}_{\psi }\left( e\right)  = \left| {\{ v \mid  \psi \left( v\right)  \cap  e \neq  \varnothing \} }\right|$，即从$\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$映射到$e$中某顶点的颜色数量。团嵌入$\psi$的弱边深度可进一步定义为wed$\left( \psi \right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{e}{d}_{\psi }\left( e\right)$。此外，我们将$\operatorname{wed}\left( {{C}_{k} \mapsto  H}\right)$定义为任意团嵌入$\psi$的最小弱边深度。现可定义以下称为团嵌入幂的量：

$$
\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\sup }\limits_{{k \geq  3}}\frac{k}{\operatorname{wed}\left( {{C}_{k} \mapsto  \mathcal{H}}\right) }.
$$

It has been shown [15] that this quantity is well-defined. As an example, the CQ that corresponds to an cycle of length $\ell$ has clemb $= 2 - 1/\lceil \ell /2\rceil$ .

已有研究[15]表明该量是良定义的。例如，对应长度为$\ell$环的CQ具有clemb$= 2 - 1/\lceil \ell /2\rceil$。

Equipped with this definition, we will now show the main theorem of this section.

基于此定义，我们现在展示本节的主要定理。

THEOREM 3.4. For a self-join-free ${CQQ}$ with hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ and any constant $\epsilon  > 0$ ,we have that $\mathbb{B}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right) .$

定理3.4. 对于无自连接的${CQQ}$（其超图为$\mathcal{H}$）及任意常数$\epsilon  > 0$，我们有$\mathbb{B}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right) .$

Proof. From [15],we know that there exists some $k > 0$ such that $\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \frac{k}{\operatorname{wed}\left( {{C}_{k} \mapsto  \mathcal{H}}\right) }$ . Let $\psi$ be the $k$ -clique embedding from ${C}_{k}$ to $\mathcal{H}$ that achieves the clique embedding power for $\mathcal{H}$ ,with weak edge depth $\lambda  = \frac{k}{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }$ .

证明. 根据[15]，存在某个$k > 0$使得$\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \frac{k}{\operatorname{wed}\left( {{C}_{k} \mapsto  \mathcal{H}}\right) }$。设$\psi$为从${C}_{k}$到$\mathcal{H}$的$k$-团嵌入，该嵌入实现了$\mathcal{H}$的团嵌入幂，其弱边深度为$\lambda  = \frac{k}{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }$。

To prove the statement,we will reduce the $k$ -partite $k$ -clique problem to the problem of computing the query $\mathcal{H}$ ,but our reduction will be through circuits. The first step is to construct an input instance $I$ for $\mathcal{H}$ . We will do this following the same logic as [15]. For any variable $x \in  V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ , define the domain $\operatorname{Dom}\left( x\right)$ in $I$ as vectors over ${\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }^{\left| {\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right) \right| }$ . Let ${S}_{e} = \{ i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack   \mid  \psi \left( i\right)  \cap  e \neq  \varnothing \}$ . We have $\left| {S}_{e}\right|  \leq  \lambda$ by the definition of weak edge depth. The idea is that each input tuple for hyperedge $e$ corresponds to a clique between the partitions ${V}_{i}$ where $i \in  {S}_{e}$ ; note that these cliques will have size $\left| {S}_{e}\right|  \leq  \lambda$ and there are at most $O\left( {n}^{\lambda }\right)$ such cliques. Formally,for every $\mathbf{a} \in  {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }^{{S}_{e}}$ ,we introduce a tuple ${t}_{\mathrm{a}}$ in the instance $I$ over $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  {S}_{e}}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$ whose value at variable $x$ is $\left\langle  {{\mathrm{a}}_{\mathrm{i}} \mid  i \in  {\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right) }\right\rangle$ .

为证明该命题，我们将把$k$分团问题归约为查询$\mathcal{H}$的计算问题，但归约过程将通过电路实现。首先构造$\mathcal{H}$的输入实例$I$，具体方法遵循文献[15]的逻辑：对任意变量$x \in  V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$，在$I$中将其定义域$\operatorname{Dom}\left( x\right)$设为${\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }^{\left| {\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right) \right| }$上的向量空间。令${S}_{e} = \{ i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack   \mid  \psi \left( i\right)  \cap  e \neq  \varnothing \}$，根据弱边深度的定义可得$\left| {S}_{e}\right|  \leq  \lambda$。核心思想是超边$e$的每个输入元组对应于分区${V}_{i}$之间的团（其中$i \in  {S}_{e}$），注意这些团的规模为$\left| {S}_{e}\right|  \leq  \lambda$且最多存在$O\left( {n}^{\lambda }\right)$个此类团。形式化地，对每个$\mathbf{a} \in  {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }^{{S}_{e}}$，我们在实例$I$中引入$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i \in  {S}_{e}}}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$上的元组${t}_{\mathrm{a}}$，其在变量$x$处的取值为$\left\langle  {{\mathrm{a}}_{\mathrm{i}} \mid  i \in  {\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right) }\right\rangle$。

Now,consider the circuit $F$ that computes the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{\mathcal{H}}$ over the Boolean semiring. We will construct a circuit ${F}^{\prime }$ for ${P}_{k}^{n}$ as follows. ${F}^{\prime }$ can be seen as composition of two parts. The first part takes as input the input variables ${y}_{\{ \left( {i,p}\right) ,\left( {j,q}\right) \} }$ of ${P}_{k}^{n}$ . Then,for every hyperedge $e$ and $\mathrm{a} \in  {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }^{{S}_{e}}$ , it constructs an output gate that computes the "value" of the tuple ${t}_{\mathrm{a}}$ : in particular,it computes ${ \land  }_{p,q \in  {S}_{e},p \neq  q}{y}_{\left\{  \left( {\mathrm{a}}_{p},p\right) ,\left( {\mathrm{a}}_{q},q\right) \right\}  }$ . In other words,the gate for ${t}_{\mathrm{a}}$ will be true if and only if there is an ${S}_{e}$ -clique. The second part is identical to $F$ but connects the input wires to the corresponding output gate of the tuple computed in the first part. By the same argument as in [15],we can argue that ${F}^{\prime }$ correctly computes the $k$ -clique $k$ -partite problem,and so the desired polynomial.

现在考虑布尔半环上计算多项式${p}_{I}^{\mathcal{H}}$的电路$F$。我们将按如下方式构造${P}_{k}^{n}$的电路${F}^{\prime }$：该电路可视为两部分组合。第一部分以${P}_{k}^{n}$的输入变量${y}_{\{ \left( {i,p}\right) ,\left( {j,q}\right) \} }$为输入，对每个超边$e$和$\mathrm{a} \in  {\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }^{{S}_{e}}$构造输出门以计算元组${t}_{\mathrm{a}}$的"值"——具体而言是计算${ \land  }_{p,q \in  {S}_{e},p \neq  q}{y}_{\left\{  \left( {\mathrm{a}}_{p},p\right) ,\left( {\mathrm{a}}_{q},q\right) \right\}  }$。换言之，当且仅当存在${S}_{e}$团时，${t}_{\mathrm{a}}$对应的门才输出真值。第二部分与$F$结构相同，但将输入线连接到第一部分计算的元组对应输出门。根据文献[15]的论证方法，可证明${F}^{\prime }$能正确计算$k$分团问题，从而得到目标多项式。

We now argue about the size of the circuit. From our instance construction, $\left| I\right|  = O\left( {n}^{\lambda }\right)$ . This implies that the size of the first part of ${F}^{\prime }$ is $O\left( {n}^{\lambda }\right)$ . The second part of the circuit has size $\left| F\right|$ . Now, suppose for the sake of contradiction that $\mathbb{B}\left( {\mathcal{H},N}\right)  = O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$ for some $\epsilon  > 0$ . Then, $\left| F\right|  =$ $O\left( {\left| I\right| }^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$ . Hence,the resulting circuit $F$ for ${P}_{k}^{n}$ has size $O\left( {{n}^{\lambda } + {\left( {n}^{\lambda }\right) }^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }}\right)  = O\left( {n}^{k - {\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$ where ${\epsilon }^{\prime } = {\lambda \epsilon }$ . This contradicts Lemma 3.2 and thus completes the proof.

我们现在论证电路的规模。根据实例构造过程，$\left| I\right|  = O\left( {n}^{\lambda }\right)$。这意味着${F}^{\prime }$第一部分的大小为$O\left( {n}^{\lambda }\right)$。电路第二部分规模为$\left| F\right|$。现假设存在矛盾情形：对于某个$\epsilon  > 0$满足$\mathbb{B}\left( {\mathcal{H},N}\right)  = O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$。那么，$\left| F\right|  =$$O\left( {\left| I\right| }^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$。因此，所得电路$F$在${P}_{k}^{n}$条件下的规模为$O\left( {{n}^{\lambda } + {\left( {n}^{\lambda }\right) }^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }}\right)  = O\left( {n}^{k - {\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$，其中${\epsilon }^{\prime } = {\lambda \epsilon }$。这与引理3.2相矛盾，从而完成证明。

Using semiring homomorphisms and Lemma 2.7, we obtain as a corollary the following lower bound for all positive semirings:

利用半环同态和引理2.7，我们可推导出以下针对所有正半环的下界推论：

COROLLARY 3.5. Let $\mathbb{S}$ be a positive semiring. For a self-join-free ${CQ}$ with hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ and any constant $\epsilon  > 0$ ,we have that $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$ .

推论3.5. 设$\mathbb{S}$为正半环。对于具有超图$\mathcal{H}$的无自连接${CQ}$及任意常数$\epsilon  > 0$，我们有$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$。

Since the Boolean semiring is idempotent, by applying Theorem 2.4 we also get an upper bound of $\mathbb{B}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ for the Boolean semiring. This seems to imply that the clique embedding power is bounded by entropic width. We next give a direct proof of this fact that may be of independent interest, since the previously best upper bound known for clemb is that $\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \leq  \operatorname{subw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ .

由于布尔半环是幂等的，应用定理2.4可得出布尔半环的上界为$\mathbb{B}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$。这似乎意味着团嵌入能力受限于熵宽度。接下来我们将给出这一事实的直接证明（该证明可能具有独立价值），因为此前已知的clemb最佳上界是$\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \leq  \operatorname{subw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$。

THEOREM 3.6. For any hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ ,we have $\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \leq  \operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ .

定理3.6. 对于任意超图$\mathcal{H}$，我们有$\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \leq  \operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$。

Proof. Let wed $\left( {{C}_{k} \rightarrow  \mathcal{H}}\right)  = \alpha$ . Then,there is an embedding $\psi  : {C}_{k} \rightarrow  \mathcal{H}$ with weak edge depth $\alpha$ . We will show that entw $\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \geq  k/\alpha$ .

证明. 设wed$\left( {{C}_{k} \rightarrow  \mathcal{H}}\right)  = \alpha$。则存在一个弱边深度为$\alpha$的嵌入$\psi  : {C}_{k} \rightarrow  \mathcal{H}$。我们将证明entw$\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \geq  k/\alpha$。

To show this,we will need to define a vector $h \in  {\bar{\Gamma }}_{n}^{ * }$ over subsets of $V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ that is edge-dominated. To do this,we construct an instance $I$ as follows. For convenience,assume $V\left( {C}_{k}\right)  = \{ 1,2,\ldots ,k\}$ ; we call these vertices colors. For every variable $x \in  V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,let ${\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right)$ be the subset of $\{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$ mapping to $x$ . Then,we define the domain of variable $x,\operatorname{Dom}\left( x\right)$ to be $\{ 0,1{\} }^{\left| {\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right) \right| }$ . Now,for every vector $\mathbf{b} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{k}$ ,we introduce one tuple ${t}_{e}^{\mathbf{b}}$ in every relation ${R}_{e}$ such that ${t}_{e}^{\mathbf{b}}\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack   = \left\langle  {{\mathbf{b}}_{i} \mid  i \in  {\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right) }\right\rangle$ .

为此，我们需要定义一个在$V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$子集上且边支配的向量$h \in  {\bar{\Gamma }}_{n}^{ * }$。具体构造如下：为方便起见，假设$V\left( {C}_{k}\right)  = \{ 1,2,\ldots ,k\}$（我们称这些顶点为颜色）；对于每个变量$x \in  V\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$，令${\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right)$为映射到$x$的$\{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$子集；然后将变量$x,\operatorname{Dom}\left( x\right)$的定义域设为$\{ 0,1{\} }^{\left| {\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right) \right| }$。对于每个向量$\mathbf{b} \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{k}$，我们在每个满足${t}_{e}^{\mathbf{b}}\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack   = \left\langle  {{\mathbf{b}}_{i} \mid  i \in  {\psi }^{-1}\left( x\right) }\right\rangle$的关系${R}_{e}$中插入一个元组${t}_{e}^{\mathbf{b}}$。

It is easy to see that the output of the query over instance $I$ is exactly ${2}^{k}$ tuples. We define now a joint probability distribution over the output tuples by choosing each output tuple with the same uniform probability. The entropy of this distribution has $\bar{h}\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)  = \log {2}^{k} = k$ .

显然，实例$I$上的查询输出正好是${2}^{k}$个元组。我们通过均匀概率选择每个输出元组，定义输出元组上的联合概率分布。该分布的熵满足$\bar{h}\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)  = \log {2}^{k} = k$。

Now,consider any decomposition $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ of $\mathcal{H}$ . From Lemma 6 of [15],there is a node $t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ such that or every color $i \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\} ,\psi \left( i\right)  \cap  \chi \left( t\right)  \neq  \varnothing$ . This implies that the projections of the ${2}^{k}$ output tuples on $\chi \left( t\right)$ are pairwise disjoint,and hence $\bar{h}\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right)  = \bar{h}\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)  = k$ . Hence,for any decomposition $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right) ,\mathop{\max }\limits_{{v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}\bar{h}\left( {\chi \left( v\right) }\right)  = k$ .

现在，考虑$\mathcal{H}$的任意分解$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$。根据文献[15]中的引理6，存在一个节点$t \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$使得对于每种颜色$i \in  \{ 1,\ldots ,k\} ,\psi \left( i\right)  \cap  \chi \left( t\right)  \neq  \varnothing$。这意味着${2}^{k}$输出元组在$\chi \left( t\right)$上的投影是两两不相交的，因此$\bar{h}\left( {\chi \left( t\right) }\right)  = \bar{h}\left( \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \right)  = k$。故对于任意分解$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right) ,\mathop{\max }\limits_{{v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}\bar{h}\left( {\chi \left( v\right) }\right)  = k$。

Finally,we define $h = \bar{h}/\alpha$ . Since $\bar{h}$ is entropic, $h \in  {\bar{\Gamma }}_{n}^{ * }$ . We claim that $h$ is also edge-dominated. Indeed,since the weak edge depth is $\alpha$ ,each hyperedge $e$ contains at most $\alpha$ colors,and hence $\bar{h}\left( e\right)  \leq  \log {2}^{\alpha } = \alpha$ ,which implies that $h\left( e\right)  \leq  1$ . Finally,

最后，我们定义$h = \bar{h}/\alpha$。由于$\bar{h}$是熵的，$h \in  {\bar{\Gamma }}_{n}^{ * }$。我们断言$h$也是边支配的。事实上，由于弱边深度为$\alpha$，每条超边$e$最多包含$\alpha$种颜色，因此$\bar{h}\left( e\right)  \leq  \log {2}^{\alpha } = \alpha$，这意味着$h\left( e\right)  \leq  1$。最终，

$$
\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  \operatorname{TD}}}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}h\left( {\chi \left( v\right) }\right)  = k/\alpha 
$$

which implies that entw $\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \geq  k/\alpha$ .

这意味着entw$\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  \geq  k/\alpha$。

### 3.2 Tight Bounds

### 3.2 紧界

In this part, we show classes of self-join-free CQs for which we can obtain tight upper and lower bounds for any positive semiring (which essentially includes all semirings of interest). The first class of queries we consider are chordal queries.

本部分展示了几类无自连接的CQ（合取查询），对于这些查询我们能在任何正半环上（实质上包含所有重要的半环）获得紧致的上下界。我们考虑的第一类查询是弦查询。

Chordal Queries. A chord of a cycle $C$ of a graph $G$ is an edge that connects any two non-adjacent vertices in $C$ . We say that $G$ is a chordal graph if any cycle of length greater than 3 has a chord. We say that a hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ is chordal if its clique-graph is chordal,where the clique graph adds an edge between all pairs of vertices contained in the same hyperedge. Examples of chordal queries include the Loomis-Whitney join, all acyclic queries, and clique queries.

弦查询。图$G$中环$C$的弦是指连接该环任意两个非相邻顶点的边。若任何长度大于3的环都存在弦，则称$G$为弦图。若超图$\mathcal{H}$的团图是弦图（团图会在同一超边包含的所有顶点对之间添加边），则称该超图为弦图。弦查询的实例包括Loomis-Whitney连接、所有无环查询以及团查询。

Proposition 3.7. Let $Q$ be a self-join-free ${CQ}$ with a chordal hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ ,and $\mathbb{S}$ be a positive semiring. Then, $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ ,but for any $\epsilon  > 0,\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$ .

命题3.7。设$Q$为无自连接的${CQ}$，其超图$\mathcal{H}$为弦图，$\mathbb{S}$为正半环。则$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$，但对于任意$\epsilon  > 0,\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$。

Proof. It was shown in [16] that $O\left( {N}^{\mathrm{{fhw}}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ is an upper bound for the circuit size over any semiring $\mathbb{S}$ where fhw(H)is the fractional hypertree width of $\mathcal{H}$ . Moreover,for any $\epsilon  > 0$ , $\Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$ is a lower bound for the size of any Boolean semiring circuit. Since $\mathbb{S}$ is positive, this lower bound transfers to the semiring $\mathbb{S}$ . Finally,because $\mathcal{H}$ is chordal,it holds that clemb $\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  =$ fhw(H)[15]. This implies that we have a tight upper and lower bound for any positive semiring.

证明。文献[16]已表明$O\left( {N}^{\mathrm{{fhw}}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$是任意半环$\mathbb{S}$上电路大小的上界，其中fhw(H)表示$\mathcal{H}$的分数超树宽度。此外，对于任意$\epsilon  > 0$，$\Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right)$是布尔半环电路大小的下界。由于$\mathbb{S}$为正半环，该下界同样适用于$\mathbb{S}$。最后，因$\mathcal{H}$是弦状的，根据[15]有clemb$\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  =$fhw(H)。这意味着我们对任意正半环都获得了严格的上界与下界。

Queries with Subquadratic Submodular Width. Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a hypergraph such that each relation is binary ( $\mathcal{H}$ is a graph). In [7],the authors identified a class of graphs (precisely the graphs with subw $\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  < 2$ ) such that the corresponding CQ can be computed in time $O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ . We will show how to turn this result into a circuit upper bound for any semiring. For this, we need to define the notion of a full $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding,as in [7].

具有次二次次模宽度的查询。设$\mathcal{H}$为每个关系都是二元关系的超图（此时$\mathcal{H}$为普通图）。文献[7]中，作者识别了一类特定图（即满足subw$\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  < 2$的图），其对应的合取查询可在$O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$时间内计算。我们将展示如何将该结论推广至任意半环的电路上界。为此需如[7]所定义，引入完全$\mathcal{H}$编码的概念。

Definition 3.8 (Full $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding). Let $\mathcal{H}$ be the hypergraph of a self-join-free CQ $Q$ ,and $I$ be an instance. A partial $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding $\mathcal{E}$ is a tree decomposition $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ of $\mathcal{H}$ together with a collection of sets ${\left\{  {S}_{v}\right\}  }_{v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }$ of tuples. We say that a tuple $t \in  Q\left( I\right)$ is encoded by $\mathcal{E}$ if $t\left\lbrack  {\chi \left( v\right) }\right\rbrack   \in  {S}_{v}$ for every $v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ . A full $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding is a collection ${\left\{  {\mathcal{E}}_{i}\right\}  }_{i}$ of partial $\mathcal{H}$ -encodings such that each tuple $t \in  Q\left( I\right)$ is encoded by exactly one ${\mathcal{E}}_{i}$ . Moreover,the number of sets in the collection is finite and depends only on ${\mathcal{H}}^{4}$ .

定义3.8（完全$\mathcal{H}$编码）。设$\mathcal{H}$为无自连接合取查询$Q$的超图，$I$为实例。部分$\mathcal{H}$编码$\mathcal{E}$由$\mathcal{H}$的树分解$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$与元组集合${\left\{  {S}_{v}\right\}  }_{v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }$构成。若对每个$v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right)$都有$t\left\lbrack  {\chi \left( v\right) }\right\rbrack   \in  {S}_{v}$，则称元组$t \in  Q\left( I\right)$被$\mathcal{E}$编码。完全$\mathcal{H}$编码是部分$\mathcal{H}$编码的集合${\left\{  {\mathcal{E}}_{i}\right\}  }_{i}$，其中每个元组$t \in  Q\left( I\right)$仅被一个${\mathcal{E}}_{i}$编码，且集合大小仅取决于${\mathcal{H}}^{4}$。

Intuitively,if we have an $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding,we have a collection of tree decompositions where each bag is materialized. It is critical that an output tuple is encoded by exactly one partial $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding. The size of a full $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding is defined as the sum of the sizes of all sets ${S}_{v}$ .

直观而言，$\mathcal{H}$编码即包含多个树分解的集合，其中每个包都被实体化。关键要求是输出元组必须仅被一个部分$\mathcal{H}$编码所编码。完全$\mathcal{H}$编码的大小定义为所有集合${S}_{v}$的大小之和。

Proposition 3.9. Suppose that there exists a full $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding of an instance $I$ of size $S$ . Then,we can construct a circuit of size $O\left( S\right)$ that computes ${p}_{I}^{\mathcal{H}}$ over any positive semiring.

命题3.9。若存在大小为$S$的实例$I$的完全$\mathcal{H}$编码，则对任意正半环，我们可构造大小为$O\left( S\right)$的电路来计算${p}_{I}^{\mathcal{H}}$。

Proof. We follow the same construction as in [16]. The key difference is that the correctness does not depend on the idempotency of the semiring,since by the definition of a full $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding we are guaranteed that each tuple is produced exactly once across the circuit.

证明。我们沿用[16]的构造方法。关键区别在于正确性不依赖半环的幂等性，因为根据完全$\mathcal{H}$编码的定义，能确保每个元组在电路中仅被生成一次。

Note that the circuit we construct works over any positive semiring, even non-idempotent ones. We can show the following result.

需注意，我们所构建的电路适用于任何正半环（positive semiring），包括非幂等的情形。可证明如下结论。

THEOREM 3.10. Let $Q$ be a self-join-free ${CQ}$ with a hypergraph $\mathcal{H}$ with binary relations and $\operatorname{subw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  < 2$ . Let $\mathbb{S}$ be a positive semiring. Then, $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ ,but for any $\epsilon  > 0$ , $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right) .$

定理3.10. 设$Q$为无自连接的${CQ}$，其超图$\mathcal{H}$包含二元关系及$\operatorname{subw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  < 2$。令$\mathbb{S}$为正半环，则$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = O\left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$，但对任意$\epsilon  > 0$，$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  - \epsilon }\right) .$

Proof. The lower bound of $\Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ is obtained as before. For the upper bound,we use the result in [7] that shows that when subw $\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  < 2$ and $\mathcal{H}$ is binary,we can construct an $\mathcal{H}$ -encoding of size $\Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$ . From 3.9,this implies a circuit of the same size that computes the desired polynomial over every semiring.

证明. $\Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$的下界如前所述获得。对于上界，我们引用文献[7]的结果：当子模宽度$\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  < 2$且$\mathcal{H}$为二元时，可构造大小为$\Omega \left( {N}^{\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right) }\right)$的$\mathcal{H}$编码。根据3.9，这意味着能在相同规模电路上计算任意半环的期望多项式。

Note that for this class of queries,we also have that $\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \operatorname{subw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$ ,since the entropic width is sandwiched between the clique embedding power and the submodular width. Examples of queries in this class consist of all cycles, as well as all parallel-series graphs. We record this observation as the following theorem.

值得注意的是，对于此类查询，由于熵宽度介于团嵌入能力与子模宽度之间，故$\operatorname{clemb}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \operatorname{entw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)  = \operatorname{subw}\left( \mathcal{H}\right)$成立。这类查询的实例包括所有环结构及并串联图。我们将此观测记录为以下定理。

## 4 CQs with self-joins: Some Basic Results

## 4 含自连接的合取查询：基础性结论

We now switch our attention to CQs with self-joins and present some basic results. Since the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ may not be multilinear,we cannot apply directly the techniques in [16]. Instead, our approach is to characterize the circuit bounds for a query $Q$ via some other self-join-free $\mathrm{{CQ}}$ for which we have existing upper and lower bounds.

现将研究重心转向含自连接的合取查询（CQs），展示若干基础结论。由于多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$可能非多重线性，无法直接套用文献[16]的技术。我们的方法是通过其他已知上下界的无自连接查询$\mathrm{{CQ}}$，来表征查询$Q$的电路边界。

Recall that given a $\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$ ,we define $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ to be the corresponding self-join-free $\mathrm{{CQ}}$ where every relational symbol is distinct. For example,for the Boolean CQ $Q\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  R\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  R\left( {z,x}\right)$ , we have $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  \left( \right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{1}\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  {R}_{2}\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  {R}_{3}\left( {z,x}\right)$ . We first prove that constructing a circuit for $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ is at least as hard as constructing a circuit for $Q$ .

回顾给定$\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$时，我们将$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$定义为对应无自连接的$\mathrm{{CQ}}$，其中每个关系符号均唯一。例如对于布尔合取查询$Q\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  R\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  R\left( {z,x}\right)$，有$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  \left( \right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{1}\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  {R}_{2}\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  {R}_{3}\left( {z,x}\right)$。我们首先证明：为$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$构建电路至少与为$Q$构建电路同等困难。

THEOREM 4.1. Let $\mathbb{S}$ be any semiring and $Q$ be a Boolean ${CQ}$ . Then, $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  \leq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)$ .

定理4.1. 设$\mathbb{S}$为任意半环，$Q$为布尔${CQ}$。则$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  \leq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)$。

Proof. Let $I$ be an instance for $Q$ such that every relation has size at most $N$ . We construct an instance $J$ for $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ as follows: for each tuple $R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  I$ ,we add a tuple ${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ to $J$ for each atom ${R}_{i}$ in $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ . It is easy to see that the size of every relation in $J$ is still bounded by $N$ . Now,let ${F}^{\prime }$ be the smallest circuit that computes the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$ over $\mathbb{S}$ . By definition,we have that $\left| {F}^{\prime }\right|  \leq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)$ . We will construct a circuit $F$ for ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ as follows: it is identical to ${F}^{\prime }$ but identifies the input variables that come from the same tuple in $I$ (say ${R}_{1}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\ldots$ ) to the same variable in $I$ (the variable for the tuple $R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ ). We argue that the constructed circuit $F$ correctly computes ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ over $\mathbb{S}$ . Indeed,the circuit $F$ computes the polynomial ${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$ under the constraint that are variables that correspond to the same tuple in $I$ (say ${R}_{1}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\ldots$ ) must be equal to the variable ${x}_{R\left( \mathbf{a}\right) }$ . But this will give us a circuit for the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ .

证明. 设$I$是$Q$的一个实例，其中每个关系的大小至多为$N$。我们如下构造$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$的实例$J$：对于每个元组$R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  I$，我们为$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$中的每个原子${R}_{i}$向$J$添加元组${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$。易见$J$中每个关系的大小仍受限于$N$。现设${F}^{\prime }$为在$\mathbb{S}$上计算多项式${p}_{I}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$的最小电路。根据定义，我们有$\left| {F}^{\prime }\right|  \leq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)$。我们将如下构造${p}_{I}^{Q}$的电路$F$：其与${F}^{\prime }$完全相同，但将来自$I$中同一元组（如${R}_{1}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\ldots$）的输入变量标识为$I$中的同一变量（即元组$R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$对应的变量）。可证所构造电路$F$在$\mathbb{S}$上正确计算${p}_{I}^{Q}$。实际上，电路$F$在约束条件下计算多项式${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$，该约束要求对应于$I$中同一元组（如${R}_{1}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,{R}_{2}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) ,\ldots$）的变量必须等于变量${x}_{R\left( \mathbf{a}\right) }$。这将最终给出计算多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$的电路。

Note that since $Q$ might contain self-joins,the circuit $F$ in the above proof is not necessarily multilinear and homogeneous. The converse of Theorem 4.1 does not hold in general, but we show that it holds under certain restrictions.

注意由于$Q$可能包含自连接，上述证明中的电路$F$不一定是多重线性且齐次的。定理4.1的逆命题通常不成立，但我们证明其在特定限制下成立。

THEOREM 4.2. Let $\mathbb{S}$ be an idempotent semiring. Let $Q$ be a Boolean CQ s.t. every endomorphism of $Q$ is an automorphism. Then, $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\left| Q\right|  \cdot  N}\right)  \geq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)$ where $\left| Q\right|$ is the size of $Q$ .

定理4.2. 设$\mathbb{S}$为幂等半环，$Q$为布尔合取查询且其每个自同态皆为自同构。则$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\left| Q\right|  \cdot  N}\right)  \geq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)$，其中$\left| Q\right|$表示$Q$的规模。

Proof. First,note that $Q$ cannot have two atoms of the form $R\left( \mathbf{x}\right) ,R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ ,since then we would have a homomorphism to a subset of $Q$ . Thus,every atom of $Q$ is uniquely identified by its relational symbol $R$ and variable vector $\mathbf{x}$ . We will thus use ${R}_{\mathbf{x}}$ to talk about the relational symbol of $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ that corresponds to the atom $R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ in $Q$ .

证明. 首先注意$Q$不能同时具有形式为$R\left( \mathbf{x}\right) ,R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$的两个原子，否则将存在到$Q$某子集的同态映射。因此，$Q$的每个原子由其关系符号$R$和变量向量$\mathbf{x}$唯一确定。故我们将使用${R}_{\mathbf{x}}$来指代$Q$中对应于原子$R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$的关系符号$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$。

Suppose $J$ is an instance for $\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$ where every relation has size at most $N$ . Consider the tagged instance $I$ of $Q$ defined as follows $\left\lbrack  {4,8,9}\right\rbrack$ : For any tuple ${R}_{\mathbf{x}}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  J$ ,we add a tuple $R\left( {\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}}\right)$ to $I$ where $\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}$ is interpreted as the vector with entries $\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{i},{\mathbf{x}}_{i}}\right)$ . Clearly,in $I$ every relation has size at most $\left| Q\right|  \cdot  N$ ,where $\left| Q\right|$ is the number of atoms in $Q$ . Let $F$ be a circuit that computes ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ over $\mathbb{S}$ . Let $G$ be the circuit that,whenever a wire in $F$ connects to $R\left( {\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}}\right)$ ,it instead connects to ${R}_{\mathbf{x}}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ .

假设$J$是$\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack$的一个实例，其中每个关系的大小最多为$N$。考虑如下定义的$Q$的标记实例$I$：对于任何元组${R}_{\mathbf{x}}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  J$，我们向$I$添加一个元组$R\left( {\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}}\right)$，其中$\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}$被解释为具有条目$\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{i},{\mathbf{x}}_{i}}\right)$的向量。显然，在$I$中，每个关系的大小最多为$\left| Q\right|  \cdot  N$，其中$\left| Q\right|$是$Q$中的原子数。设$F$为在$\mathbb{S}$上计算${p}_{I}^{Q}$的电路。设$G$为这样一个电路：当$F$中的导线连接到$R\left( {\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}}\right)$时，它改为连接到${R}_{\mathbf{x}}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$。

To complete the proof,it suffices to prove that $G$ correctly computes ${p}_{I}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$ over $\mathbb{S}$ . For this,take any annotation $\mu  : J \rightarrow  \mathbb{S}$ and define the annotation $v : I \rightarrow  \mathbb{S}$ such that $v\left( {R\left( {\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}}\right) }\right)  = \mu \left( {{R}_{\mathbf{x}}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) }\right)$ . The following properties hold:

为完成证明，只需证明$G$在$\mathbb{S}$上正确计算了${p}_{I}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$。为此，取任意标注$\mu  : J \rightarrow  \mathbb{S}$并定义标注$v : I \rightarrow  \mathbb{S}$使得$v\left( {R\left( {\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}}\right) }\right)  = \mu \left( {{R}_{\mathbf{x}}\left( \mathbf{a}\right) }\right)$。以下性质成立：

- ${p}^{G}\left( \mu \right)  = {p}^{F}\left( v\right)$ : this holds directly from the construction of ${F}^{\prime }$ from $F$ .

- ${p}^{G}\left( \mu \right)  = {p}^{F}\left( v\right)$：这直接源于从$F$构造${F}^{\prime }$的方式。

- ${p}^{F}\left( v\right)  = {p}_{I}^{Q}\left( v\right)$ : this holds because $F$ computes ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ .

- ${p}^{F}\left( v\right)  = {p}_{I}^{Q}\left( v\right)$：这成立是因为$F$计算了${p}_{I}^{Q}$。

We will next show that ${p}_{I}^{Q}\left( v\right)  = {p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }\left( \mu \right)$ ; this implies that ${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }\left( \mu \right)  = {p}^{G}\left( \mu \right)$ . Indeed,consider a monomial $m$ in ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ with input tuples ${R}^{1}\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{1} \cdot  {\mathbf{x}}_{1}}\right) ,{R}^{2}\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{2} \cdot  {\mathbf{x}}_{2}}\right) ,\ldots$ . This monomial corresponds to an output tuple,hence there exists a valuation $\psi$ from the variables of $Q$ to the domain of $J$ . Since every value of the domain is a pair of constant and a variable, $\psi$ implicitly defines an endomorphism,i.e., a homomorphism from $Q$ to itself,which we call the tagging map [9]. Because of our assumption of $Q$ ,this endomorphism must also be an automorphism. But this implies that the monomial ${m}^{\prime }$ with tuples ${R}_{{\mathbf{x}}_{1}}^{1}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{1}\right) ,{R}_{{\mathbf{x}}_{2}}^{2}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{2}\right) ,\ldots$ exists in ${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$ . For the other direction,it is easy to see that if a monomial ${m}^{\prime }$ with tuples ${R}_{{\mathbf{x}}_{1}}^{1}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{1}\right) ,{R}_{{\mathbf{x}}_{2}}^{2}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{2}\right) ,\ldots$ exists in ${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$ ,then a monomial $m$ with input tuples ${R}^{1}\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{1} \cdot  {\mathbf{x}}_{1}}\right) ,{R}^{2}\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{2} \cdot  {\mathbf{x}}_{2}}\right) ,\ldots$ exists in ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ ,since any valuation $\psi  : \operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right)  \rightarrow  D$ can be extended to a valuation ${\psi }^{\prime } : \operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right)  \rightarrow  \operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right)  \times  D$ by using the identity automorphism (every variable maps to itself). It is now easy to see that $v\left( m\right)  = \mu \left( {m}^{\prime }\right)$ .

接下来我们将证明${p}_{I}^{Q}\left( v\right)  = {p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }\left( \mu \right)$；这意味着${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }\left( \mu \right)  = {p}^{G}\left( \mu \right)$。具体而言，考虑${p}_{I}^{Q}$中的单项式$m$，其输入元组为${R}^{1}\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{1} \cdot  {\mathbf{x}}_{1}}\right) ,{R}^{2}\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{2} \cdot  {\mathbf{x}}_{2}}\right) ,\ldots$。该单项式对应一个输出元组，因此存在从$Q$的变量到$J$域的赋值$\psi$。由于域中每个值都是常量与变量的有序对，$\psi$隐式定义了一个自同态（即从$Q$到自身的同态），我们称之为标记映射[9]。根据我们对$Q$的假设，该自同态必然也是自同构。由此可推得：具有元组${R}_{{\mathbf{x}}_{1}}^{1}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{1}\right) ,{R}_{{\mathbf{x}}_{2}}^{2}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{2}\right) ,\ldots$的单项式${m}^{\prime }$存在于${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$中。反之显然：若${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$中存在具有元组${R}_{{\mathbf{x}}_{1}}^{1}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{1}\right) ,{R}_{{\mathbf{x}}_{2}}^{2}\left( {\mathbf{a}}_{2}\right) ,\ldots$的单项式${m}^{\prime }$，则${p}_{I}^{Q}$中必然存在具有输入元组${R}^{1}\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{1} \cdot  {\mathbf{x}}_{1}}\right) ,{R}^{2}\left( {{\mathbf{a}}_{2} \cdot  {\mathbf{x}}_{2}}\right) ,\ldots$的单项式$m$——因为任何赋值$\psi  : \operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right)  \rightarrow  D$均可通过恒等自同构（每个变量映射到自身）扩展为赋值${\psi }^{\prime } : \operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right)  \rightarrow  \operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right)  \times  D$。至此容易得出$v\left( m\right)  = \mu \left( {m}^{\prime }\right)$。

We have thus shown that for every monomial in ${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$ there is one monomial in ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ with the same value,and vice versa. Since $\mathbb{S}$ is idempotent,this implies that ${p}_{I}^{Q}\left( v\right)  = {p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }\left( \mu \right)$ . Thus,we have ${p}^{G}\left( \mu \right)  = {p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }\left( \mu \right)$ for any annotation $\mu  : J \rightarrow  \mathbb{S}$ . This means ${p}^{G}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$ and thus $G$ correctly computes ${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$ over $\mathbb{S}$ .

由此我们证明，对于${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$中的每个单项式，在${p}_{I}^{Q}$中都存在一个等值的单项式，反之亦然。由于$\mathbb{S}$是幂等的，这意味着${p}_{I}^{Q}\left( v\right)  = {p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }\left( \mu \right)$。因此，对于任何标注$\mu  : J \rightarrow  \mathbb{S}$，我们有${p}^{G}\left( \mu \right)  = {p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }\left( \mu \right)$。这表明${p}^{G}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$，因此$G$能正确计算$\mathbb{S}$上的${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  }$。

Example 4.3. As an application of Theorem 4.2,consider the Boolean CQ $\Delta \left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land$ $R\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  R\left( {z,x}\right)$ . This query has three endomorphisms:

例4.3. 作为定理4.2的应用，考虑布尔合取查询$\Delta \left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land$$R\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  R\left( {z,x}\right)$。该查询具有三个自同态：

$$
{h}_{1} : x \rightarrow  x,y \rightarrow  y,z \rightarrow  z\;{h}_{2} : x \rightarrow  y,y \rightarrow  z,z \rightarrow  x\;{h}_{3} : x \rightarrow  z,y \rightarrow  x,z \rightarrow  y.
$$

All three endomorphisms are also automorphisms,and hence for $\Delta$ its optimal circuit is exactly characterized by the optimal circuit of its self-join-free counterpart $\left\lbrack  \Delta \right\rbrack  \left( \right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{xy}\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  {R}_{yz}\left( {y,z}\right)  \land$ ${R}_{zx}\left( {z,x}\right)$ for any idempotent semiring $\mathbb{S}$ .

这三个自同态同时也是自同构，因此对于$\Delta$，其最优电路完全由其无自连接对应项$\left\lbrack  \Delta \right\rbrack  \left( \right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{xy}\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  {R}_{yz}\left( {y,z}\right)  \land$${R}_{zx}\left( {z,x}\right)$在任意幂等半环$\mathbb{S}$上的最优电路所表征。

On the other hand,consider the Boolean CQ ${Q}_{\square }\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  S\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,w}\right)  \land  S\left( {w,z}\right)$ . This query has the endomorphism $h : x \rightarrow  x,y \rightarrow  y,w \rightarrow  y,z \rightarrow  z$ ,which is not an automorphism (since the four atoms are mapped in only two atoms of the query). Indeed, $\left\lbrack  {\mathrm{Q}}_{\square }\right\rbrack$ has circuit size $\Theta \left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$ for the tropical semiring,while as we will see ${Q}_{\square }$ admits a linear-size circuit.

另一方面，考虑布尔合取查询${Q}_{\square }\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  S\left( {y,z}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,w}\right)  \land  S\left( {w,z}\right)$。该查询存在自同态$h : x \rightarrow  x,y \rightarrow  y,w \rightarrow  y,z \rightarrow  z$，但这不是自同构（因为四个原子仅映射到查询中的两个原子）。事实上，在热带半环上，$\left\lbrack  {\mathrm{Q}}_{\square }\right\rbrack$的电路规模为$\Theta \left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$，而我们将看到${Q}_{\square }$允许线性规模电路。

Though powerful, Theorem 4.2 is only applicable for queries that are "minimal", in the sense that no endomorphism mapping to strictly smaller subset exists. To derive meaningful circuit lower bounds, we consider semirings where equivalency between queries is well-behaved.

尽管定理4.2功能强大，但仅适用于"最小化"查询——即不存在映射到严格更小子集的自同态。为了推导有意义的电路下界，我们考虑查询等价性表现良好的半环。

## 5 CQs with self-joins: Circuit Bounds for ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$

## 5 含自连接的合取查询：${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$的电路界限

Kostylev, Reutter and Salamon [29] classified the class of semirings where query containment is equivalent to the existence of a homomorphism between CQs. Formally,

Kostylev、Reutter和Salamon[29]对查询包含性等价于合取查询间存在同态的半环类别进行了分类。形式化定义为：

Definition 5.1. ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ is the class of semirings that satisfy the following axioms:

定义5.1. ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$是满足以下公理的半环类别：

(1) (8-idempotence) $x \otimes  x = x$ ;

(1) (8-幂等性) $x \otimes  x = x$；

(2) (1-annihilation) $\mathbf{1} \oplus  x = \mathbf{1}$ .

(2) (1-湮灭性) $\mathbf{1} \oplus  x = \mathbf{1}$。

Examples of semirings in ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ include $\mathbb{B},\operatorname{PosBool}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \left\lbrack  {19}\right\rbrack$ and any distributive lattice $\left\lbrack  {18}\right\rbrack$ . Note that any semiring that satisfies 1-annihilation is also $\oplus$ -idempotent,since $x \oplus  x = \mathbf{1} \otimes  x \oplus  \mathbf{1} \otimes  x =$ $\left( {\mathbf{1} \oplus  \mathbf{1}}\right)  \otimes  x = \mathbf{1} \otimes  x = x$ for any $x \in  \mathbb{S}$ ,where the third equality is due to1-annihilation.

在${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$中的半环示例包括$\mathbb{B},\operatorname{PosBool}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \left\lbrack  {19}\right\rbrack$及任何分配格$\left\lbrack  {18}\right\rbrack$。需注意，满足1-湮灭性质的半环同时也是$\oplus$幂等的，因为对于任意$x \in  \mathbb{S}$都有$x \oplus  x = \mathbf{1} \otimes  x \oplus  \mathbf{1} \otimes  x =$$\left( {\mathbf{1} \oplus  \mathbf{1}}\right)  \otimes  x = \mathbf{1} \otimes  x = x$，其中第三个等式成立源于1-湮灭性质。

Observe that for any polynomial $p$ over $\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ ,we lose no information by viewing $p$ as the set of its monomials, i.e., ignoring coefficients due to 1-annihilation, and further viewing each monomial as the set of its variables,i.e.,dropping exponents due to $\otimes$ -idempotence. In other words, we can identify any polynomial as a set of set of variables,and we write $p \subseteq  q$ for two polynomials $p,q$ in the sense of set containment. Finally,for any polynomial $p$ ,we denote ${p}_{\min }$ as the set of monomials not containing any other monomial in $p$ . Note that ${p}_{\min }$ is unique for every $p$ .

观察到对于$\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$上的任意多项式$p$，我们可以将其视为单项式集合而不丢失信息（即因1-湮灭性质忽略系数），并进一步将每个单项式视作其变量集合（即因$\otimes$幂等性省略指数）。换言之，任何多项式可表示为变量的集合之集合，我们记作$p \subseteq  q$表示两个多项式$p,q$之间的集合包含关系。最后，对任意多项式$p$，用${p}_{\min }$表示不包含$p$中其他单项式的单项式集合。注意${p}_{\min }$对每个$p$都是唯一的。

Example 5.2. The polynomial $p = 5{x}^{3} + 2{x}^{2}y + 3{y}^{3}$ is identified as the set $\{ \{ x\} ,\{ x,y\} ,\{ y\} \}$ and ${p}_{\min }$ is identified as the set $\{ \{ x\} ,\{ y\} \}$ .

例5.2 多项式$p = 5{x}^{3} + 2{x}^{2}y + 3{y}^{3}$被标识为集合$\{ \{ x\} ,\{ x,y\} ,\{ y\} \}$，而${p}_{\min }$被标识为集合$\{ \{ x\} ,\{ y\} \}$。

We write ${Q}_{2} \rightarrow  {Q}_{1}$ if there exists a homomorphism from ${Q}_{2}$ to ${Q}_{1}$ . The results in [29] can be stated as the following.

当存在从${Q}_{2}$到${Q}_{1}$的同态时，我们记作${Q}_{2} \rightarrow  {Q}_{1}$。文献[29]的结果可表述如下。

THEOREM 5.3 (KOSTYLEV,REUTTER &SLAMON [29]). A semiring $\mathbb{S}$ is in ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ if and only if for all ${CQs}{Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ ,we have that ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min } \subseteq  {\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$ for any instance $I$ if and only if ${Q}_{2} \rightarrow  {Q}_{1}$ .

定理5.3（KOSTYLEV, REUTTER & SLAMON [29]） 半环$\mathbb{S}$属于${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$当且仅当：对所有${CQs}{Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{2}$，任意实例$I$满足${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min } \subseteq  {\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$等价于${Q}_{2} \rightarrow  {Q}_{1}$成立。

It is straightforward to derive the following using their results.

根据他们的结果可直接推导出以下结论。

THEOREM 5.4. Fix any semiring $\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ . Then,two CQs ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are semantically equivalent over $\mathbb{S}$ if and only if they are homomorphically equivalent.

定理5.4 给定任意半环$\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$，则两个合取查询${Q}_{1}$与${Q}_{2}$在$\mathbb{S}$上语义等价当且仅当它们同态等价。

Proof. If ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are homomorphically equivalent,then ${Q}_{1} \rightarrow  {Q}_{2}$ and ${Q}_{2} \rightarrow  {Q}_{1}$ ,which implies that ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min } = {\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$ for any instance $I$ . Observe that $p{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{\min }$ for any $p$ . Therefore, ${p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}$ and thus ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are semantically equivalent over $\mathbb{S}$ .

证明。若${Q}_{1}$与${Q}_{2}$同态等价，则${Q}_{1} \rightarrow  {Q}_{2}$与${Q}_{2} \rightarrow  {Q}_{1}$成立，这意味着对于任意实例$I$都有${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min } = {\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$。注意到对于任意$p$都存在$p{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{\min }$。因此，${p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}$成立，故${Q}_{1}$与${Q}_{2}$在$\mathbb{S}$上语义等价。

If ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ are semantically equivalent over $\mathbb{S}$ ,we have ${p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}$ . By Theorem 5.3,it suffices to show ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min } = {\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$ . Suppose for contradiction that ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min } \neq  {\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$ . Then,there exists a set ${m}_{1}$ in ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min }$ that is not in ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$ . Setting every variable in ${m}_{1}$ to be 1 and every other variable to be0,since ${p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}$ ,we conclude that there exists a subset ${m}_{2} \varsubsetneq  {m}_{1}$ that is in ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$ . But then setting every variable in ${m}_{2}$ to be 1 and every other variable to be0,we have ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min }$ evaluates to 0 while ${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$ evaluates to 1,a contradiction. The proof is now complete.

若${Q}_{1}$与${Q}_{2}$在$\mathbb{S}$上语义等价，则有${p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}$。根据定理5.3，只需证明${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min } = {\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$。假设存在矛盾情况${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min } \neq  {\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$，则${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min }$中存在某个集合${m}_{1}$不属于${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$。将${m}_{1}$中所有变量赋值为1而其他变量为0时，由于${p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}$成立，可推出存在子集${m}_{2} \varsubsetneq  {m}_{1}$属于${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$。但若将${m}_{2}$中所有变量赋值为1而其他变量为0时，${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{1}}\right) }_{\min }$取值为0而${\left( {p}_{I}^{{Q}_{2}}\right) }_{\min }$取值为1，产生矛盾。至此证毕。

For any CQ $Q$ ,a minimal equivalent query ${Q}^{\prime }$ of $Q$ over a semiring $\mathbb{S}$ is any CQ such that ${Q}^{\prime } = {}_{\mathbb{S}}Q$ ,with no strictly smaller query of itself having this property. It is well-known that all minimal equivalent queries over $\mathbb{B}$ are isomorphic [10]. The following lemma generalizes this fact to any semiring in ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ .

对于任意CQ查询$Q$，在半环$\mathbb{S}$上，$Q$的最小等价查询${Q}^{\prime }$是指满足${Q}^{\prime } = {}_{\mathbb{S}}Q$且不存在更小子查询具有该性质的CQ。众所周知，在$\mathbb{B}$上所有最小等价查询都是同构的[10]。下述引理将该事实推广到${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$中的任意半环。

LEMMA 5.5. Let $Q$ be any ${CQ}$ and $\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ be a semiring. Then,all minimal equivalent queries of $Q$ over $\mathbb{S}$ are isomorphic.

引理5.5。设$Q$为任意${CQ}$，$\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$为半环，则$Q$在$\mathbb{S}$上的所有最小等价查询皆同构。

Proof. Let ${Q}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ be two minimal equivalent queries of $Q$ over $\mathbb{S}$ . By Theorem 5.4,there exists a homomorphism $h$ from ${Q}_{1}$ to ${Q}_{2}$ and there exists a homomorphism $g$ from ${Q}_{2}$ to ${Q}_{1}$ .

证明。设${Q}_{1}$和${Q}_{2}$是$Q$在$\mathbb{S}$上的两个最小等价查询。根据定理5.4，存在从${Q}_{1}$到${Q}_{2}$的同态映射$h$，以及从${Q}_{2}$到${Q}_{1}$的同态映射$g$。

Now,observe that the mapping $h \circ  g$ is an endomorphism for ${Q}_{2}$ . However,this endomorphism must also be an automorphism. Indeed,if not,then we have a query ${Q}_{2}^{\prime }$ with less atoms than ${Q}_{2}$ that by Theorem 5.4 is equivalent to $Q$ ,violating the minimality assumption. Symmetrically, $g \circ  h$ is an automorphism for ${Q}_{1}$ . But this can happen only if $h,g$ are isomorphisms.

现在注意到映射$h \circ  g$对于${Q}_{2}$而言是个自同态。但这个自同态必须也是个自同构。因为若非如此，根据定理5.4我们将得到原子数少于${Q}_{2}$的查询${Q}_{2}^{\prime }$与$Q$等价，这就违背了极小性假设。同理，$g \circ  h$是${Q}_{1}$的自同构。这种情况仅当$h,g$为同构映射时才成立。

${CQ}$ Core. The unique (up to isomorphism) minimal equivalent ${CQ}$ of $Q$ is called the core of $Q$ , denoted ${Q}_{\text{core }}$ . As in the Boolean case,the core can be constructed by removing one or more atoms from $Q$ (in fact,the core is the same for any semiring in ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ ).

${CQ}$核心。在$Q$所有（在同构意义下）唯一的极小等价${CQ}$称为其核心，记作${Q}_{\text{core }}$。与布尔情形类似，可通过从$Q$中移除一个或多个原子来构造核心（事实上，对于${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$中任何半环结构，核心都是相同的）。

LEMMA 5.6. Let $Q$ be any ${CQ}$ and let $\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ . Then,there exists a minimal equivalent query ${Q}_{\text{min }}$ to $Q$ over $\mathbb{S}$ that can be obtained from $Q$ by removing zero or more atoms.

引理5.6。设$Q$为任意${CQ}$，且令$\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$。则存在$Q$在$\mathbb{S}$上的极小等价查询${Q}_{\text{min }}$，该查询可通过从$Q$中移除零个或多个原子获得。

Proof. Let ${Q}_{\min }$ be any minimal equivalent query of $Q$ over ${\mathbb{S}}^{5}$ . By Theorem 5.4,there exists a homomorphism $h$ from $Q$ to ${Q}_{\min }$ and a homomorphism from ${Q}_{\min }$ to $Q$ . Consider the composed mapping $g \circ  h$ from $Q$ to $Q$ . Clearly $g \circ  h$ is a homomorphism from $Q$ to itself,and thus $g \circ  h\left( Q\right)$ is a subquery of $Q$ . It suffices to show that $g \circ  h\left( Q\right)$ is equivalent to $Q$ over $\mathbb{S}$ . This follows by Theorem 5.4 by noting that the identity map of $g \circ  h\left( Q\right)$ is a homomorphism to $Q$ .

证明。设${Q}_{\min }$是$Q$在${\mathbb{S}}^{5}$上的任意极小等价查询。根据定理5.4，存在从$Q$到${Q}_{\min }$的同态$h$，以及从${Q}_{\min }$到$Q$的同态。考虑复合映射$g \circ  h$从$Q$到$Q$。显然$g \circ  h$是$Q$到自身的同态，因此$g \circ  h\left( Q\right)$是$Q$的子查询。只需证明$g \circ  h\left( Q\right)$在$\mathbb{S}$上与$Q$等价。这由定理5.4可得，注意到$g \circ  h\left( Q\right)$的恒等映射是到$Q$的同态。

我们现在可以陈述本小节的主要结论。

THEOREM 5.7 (MAIN RESULT FOR ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ ). Let $Q$ be any Boolean ${CQ}$ and let $\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ . Then,

定理5.7（${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$主要结果）。设$Q$为任意布尔${CQ}$，且令$\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$。则有：

$$
\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Theta \left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right) .
$$

Proof. Since ${Q}_{\text{core }}$ is the minimal equivalent query to $Q$ over $\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ - in particular ${Q}_{\text{core }}$ is an equivalent query to $Q$ over $\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ - we have $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \mathbb{S}\left( {{Q}_{\text{core }},N}\right)$ . By Theorem 4.1,we know $\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)  \geq  \mathbb{S}\left( {{Q}_{\text{core }},N}\right)  = \mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)$ and thus $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right) ) = O\left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right)$ .

证明。由于${Q}_{\text{core }}$是$Q$在$\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$上的最小等价查询（特别地，${Q}_{\text{core }}$是$Q$在$\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathbf{C}}_{\text{hom }}$上的等价查询），因此我们有$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \mathbb{S}\left( {{Q}_{\text{core }},N}\right)$。根据定理4.1，可知$\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)  \geq  \mathbb{S}\left( {{Q}_{\text{core }},N}\right)  = \mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)$，从而得到$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right) ) = O\left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right)$。

We next claim that every endomorphism of ${Q}_{\text{core }}$ is an automorphism. Indeed,if an endomorphism $h$ exists that is not an automorphism,then the image $h\left( {Q}_{\text{core }}\right)$ is a strictly smaller equivalent query to $Q$ than ${Q}_{\text{core }}$ ,a contradiction. Therefore,Theorem 4.2 implies $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right|  \cdot  N}\right)  = \mathbb{S}\left( {{Q}_{\text{core }},\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right| }\right.$ . $N) \geq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)$ . Observe that $\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right|$ is a constant in terms of data complexity and the quantity $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right|  \cdot  N}\right)$ is an expression with $N$ being the only variable - specifically,the dependence on $N$ is polynomial. Thus,we have $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right) ) = \Theta \left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right|  \cdot  N}\right) }\right)  = \Omega \left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right)$ . We conclude that $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Theta \left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right)$ .

接下来我们断言：${Q}_{\text{core }}$的每个自同态都是自同构。事实上，若存在不是自同构的自同态$h$，则其像$h\left( {Q}_{\text{core }}\right)$将成为比${Q}_{\text{core }}$更小的等价查询，这与$Q$矛盾。故由定理4.2可得$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right|  \cdot  N}\right)  = \mathbb{S}\left( {{Q}_{\text{core }},\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right| }\right.$。$N) \geq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)$。注意到$\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right|$在数据复杂度意义下是常数，而量$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right|  \cdot  N}\right)$是以$N$为唯一变量的表达式——具体而言，其对$N$的依赖关系是多项式级的。因此我们得到$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right) ) = \Theta \left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\left| {Q}_{\text{core }}\right|  \cdot  N}\right) }\right)  = \Omega \left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right)$，最终可得出结论$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Theta \left( {\mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}_{\text{core }}\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right)$。

## 6 CQs with self-joins: Circuit Bounds for ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$

## 6 含自连接的合取查询：${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$的电路界

In this section, we study the behavior of CQs with self-joins for a different class of semirings, which we call ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ . Given two polynomials ${p}_{1}$ and ${p}_{2}$ over the same set of variables,we write ${p}_{1} \simeq  {p}_{2}$ when the sets of monomials in ${p}_{1}$ and ${p}_{2}$ are identical (thus ignoring coefficients).

本节我们研究自连接合取查询在另一类半环——称为${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$——中的表现。给定同一变量集上的两个多项式${p}_{1}$和${p}_{2}$，当${p}_{1}$与${p}_{2}$中的单项式集合相同时（此时忽略系数），我们记作${p}_{1} \simeq  {p}_{2}$。

Definition 6.1. ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ is the class of semirings $\mathbb{S}$ where ${p}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{2}$ implies ${p}_{1} \simeq  {p}_{2}$ .

定义6.1 ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$是指满足${p}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{2}$蕴含${p}_{1} \simeq  {p}_{2}$的半环类$\mathbb{S}$。

Any circuit $F$ that computes a polynomial $p$ over $\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ must produce all monomials in $p$ ,i.e. ${p}^{F} \simeq  p$ . Notable examples of ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ are the tropical semiring $\mathbb{T}$ ,the counting semiring $\mathbb{C}$ and $\mathbb{B}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \left\lbrack  {{20},{22},{29}}\right\rbrack$ . We also consider the arithmetic semiring where we overload the notation $\mathbb{Q} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {{\mathbb{Q}}_{ \geq  0},+,\cdot ,0,1}\right)$ whose underlying set is the set of non-negative rational numbers. The class ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ contains the class of semirings ${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$ defined in [29],as we show in Appendix A. However,we are unable to show the reverse direction of containment, nor can we find a counterexample. We thus leave the open problem whether ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ equals to ${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$ .

任何计算多项式$p$在$\mathbb{S} \in  {\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$上的电路$F$都必须生成$p$中的所有单项式，即${p}^{F} \simeq  p$。${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$的著名实例包括热带半环$\mathbb{T}$、计数半环$\mathbb{C}$和$\mathbb{B}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  \left\lbrack  {{20},{22},{29}}\right\rbrack$。我们还考虑算术半环，其中重载了符号$\mathbb{Q} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {{\mathbb{Q}}_{ \geq  0},+,\cdot ,0,1}\right)$，其基础集是非负有理数集。类${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$包含[29]中定义的半环类${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$，如附录A所示。然而，我们既无法证明包含关系的反向，也未能找到反例。因此我们留下这个开放性问题：${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$是否等于${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$。

The core of our lower bound construction for this section uses the following celebrated theorem, which says that computing all the partial derivatives of any polynomial collectively is asymptotically no harder than computing the polynomial itself [6]. We overload the notation $\mathbb{S}\left( {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{k}}\right)$ to mean the size of the optimal semiring circuit that computes all polynomials ${p}_{1},{p}_{2},\ldots ,{p}_{k}$ ,with one output gate for every polynomial.

本节下界构造的核心基于以下著名定理，该定理表明计算多项式所有偏导数的总体复杂度渐近地不高于计算多项式本身[6]。我们重载符号$\mathbb{S}\left( {{p}_{1},\ldots ,{p}_{k}}\right)$表示计算所有多项式${p}_{1},{p}_{2},\ldots ,{p}_{k}$的最优半环电路规模，其中每个多项式对应一个输出门。

THEOREM 6.2 (BAUR &STRASSEN BAURS83). For any polynomial $p$ with variables ${x}_{1},{x}_{2},\ldots ,{x}_{n}$ , $\mathbb{C}\left( {{\partial }_{1}\left( p\right) ,{\partial }_{2}\left( p\right) ,\ldots ,{\partial }_{n}\left( p\right) }\right)  \leq  5 \cdot  \mathbb{C}\left( p\right)$ ,where ${\partial }_{i}\left( p\right)$ is the partial derivative of $p$ w.r.t. ${x}_{i},1 \leq  i \leq  n$ .

定理6.2（BAUR & STRASSEN BAURS83）。对于任意含变量${x}_{1},{x}_{2},\ldots ,{x}_{n}$的多项式$p$，$\mathbb{C}\left( {{\partial }_{1}\left( p\right) ,{\partial }_{2}\left( p\right) ,\ldots ,{\partial }_{n}\left( p\right) }\right)  \leq  5 \cdot  \mathbb{C}\left( p\right)$，其中${\partial }_{i}\left( p\right)$是$p$关于${x}_{i},1 \leq  i \leq  n$的偏导数。

We now state and prove the main result in this section. In this section, we consider the circuit complexity of CQ with self-joins over the positive tropical semiring ${\mathbb{T}}^{ + } \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {{\mathbb{R}}^{ + }\cup \{  + \infty \} ,\min ,+, + \infty ,0}\right) .{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$ is idempotent and lies in neither ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ nor ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }.{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$ is an important semiring, because it captures finding the "minimum weight" valuation of a query.

Canonical Databases. We introduce here the notion of a canonical database $\left\lbrack  {{11},{18},{29}}\right\rbrack$ ,which is used to characterize query containment. We say a monomial $m$ in ${p}^{Q}$ sees an endomorphism of $Q$ ,if when viewing $m$ as a set of variables ${X}_{m}$ ,there exists an endomorphism $h$ of $Q$ such that ${X}_{m}$ equals to the image of $h$ . We will do this via the following iterative procedure: for each monomial $m \in  {p}^{Q}$ ,we check whether the polynomial ${p}^{\prime }$ ,which is the polynomial that we remove $m$ from ${p}^{Q}$ ,is semantically equivalent to ${p}^{Q}$ over ${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$ ; if so,set ${p}^{Q} = {p}^{\prime }$ . LEMMA 7.4 (Proposition 1.1 IN [21]). Even though [21] proves the case when the underlying set is ${\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ ,we can also adapt it to hold if we replace ${\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ by the positve rational numbers ${\mathbb{Q}}_{ \geq  0}$ . Indeed,all properties of ${\left( {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }\right) }^{d + 1}$ that are leveraged by the proof continue to hold in ${\mathbb{Q}}_{ \geq  0}^{d + 1}$ . For example,the property that there exists a closed ball within any open ball also holds in ${\mathbb{Q}}_{ \geq  0}^{d + 1}$ . 尽管[21]证明了当基础集为${\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$时的情况，但若将${\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$替换为正有理数${\mathbb{Q}}_{ \geq  0}$，该结论依然成立。事实上，证明所利用的${\left( {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }\right) }^{d + 1}$的所有性质在${\mathbb{Q}}_{ \geq  0}^{d + 1}$中仍然保持。例如，任何开球内存在闭球的性质在${\mathbb{Q}}_{ \geq  0}^{d + 1}$中也成立。然而，我们尚不清楚类似结果是否对正整数也成立。

Proof of LEMMA 7.3. We observe that the above procedure is nothing but picking the defining monomials for the facet of the polyhedron. Indeed,we can view the set $\mathcal{D}\left( p\right)$ in Lemma 7.4 as given by linear inequalities,one for each monomial of the form $\sum {a}_{i}{x}_{i} \geq  s$ . LEMMA 7.5. Any polynomial $p{ \equiv  }_{{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }}{p}^{Q}$ must contain ${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$ as a sub-polynomial (i.e.,all monomials of ${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$ occur in $p$ ).

引理7.5。任何多项式$p{ \equiv  }_{{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }}{p}^{Q}$都必须包含${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$作为子多项式（即${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$的所有单项式都出现在$p$中）。

Proof. By Lemma 7.4,we know that any polynomial $p$ that is equivalent to ${p}^{Q}$ over ${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$ must define the same full-dimensional closed convex polyhedron. Viewing them as a system of linear inequalities, this implies that both systems contain the same set of inequalities which defines the facets [33]. As in the proof of Lemma 7.3, ${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$ represents the unique minimal set of defining inequalities,therefore $p$ contains ${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$ as a sub-polynomial.

证明。根据引理7.4，任何在${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$上与${p}^{Q}$等价的多项式$p$必须定义相同的满维闭凸多面体。将其视为线性不等式系统时，这意味着两个系统包含相同的面定义不等式集[33]。如引理7.3证明所示，${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$代表唯一的最小定义不等式集，因此$p$包含${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$作为子多项式。

基于上述结论，我们现在可以为$Q$定义热带核的概念。

Definition 7.6 (Tropical Core). Given a Boolean CQ $Q$ ,its tropical core $\operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right)$ is the set of CQs that are seen by the monomials in the polynomial ${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$ .

定义7.6（热带核）。给定布尔合取查询$Q$，其热带核$\operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right)$是由多项式${p}_{\text{core }}^{Q}$中的单项式识别的合取查询集合。

Example 7.7. Consider the polynomial ${p}^{{Q}_{1}}$ for our running example. Then,one can show that:

例7.7。考虑运行示例中的多项式${p}^{{Q}_{1}}$。则可证明：

$$
{p}_{\text{core }}^{{Q}_{1}} \mathrel{\text{:=}} {\left( {x}_{1}{z}_{1}\right) }^{2} + {\left( {x}_{2}{z}_{2}\right) }^{2}.
$$

Hence,the tropical core for ${Q}_{1}$ consists of the CQs $R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  S\left( {y,z}\right)$ and $R\left( {x,w}\right)  \land  S\left( {w,z}\right)$ ,each one corresponding to an endomorphism of $Q$ .

因此，${Q}_{1}$的热带核由合取查询$R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  S\left( {y,z}\right)$和$R\left( {x,w}\right)  \land  S\left( {w,z}\right)$组成，每个查询对应$Q$的一个自同态。

It is worth contrasting the tropical core to the case of ${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$ ,where the core is a single CQ. Here, a tropical core may consist of multiple CQs, as the above example shows. We are now ready to characterize the circuit complexity of any $\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$ over ${\mathbb{T}}_{\mathbb{Q}}^{ + }$ .

值得将热带核与${\mathrm{C}}_{\text{hom }}$的情况进行对比——后者的核是单一合取查询。如示例所示，热带核可能包含多个合取查询。现在我们可以刻画任何$\mathrm{{CQ}}Q$在${\mathbb{T}}_{\mathbb{Q}}^{ + }$上的电路复杂性。

THEOREM 7.8 (MAIN RESULT FOR ${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$ ). Let $Q$ be any Boolean ${CQ}$ . Then,

定理7.8（${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$的主要结果）。设$Q$为任意布尔${CQ}$，则有：

$$
{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Theta \left( {\mathop{\max }\limits_{{{Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right) }}{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right) .
$$

Since ${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)  = \Theta \left( {{N}^{\text{entw }}\left( \left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  \right) }\right)$ ,the circuit complexity of $Q$ over ${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$ is characterized by the query ${Q}^{ \star  }$ in the tropical core with the highest entropic width.

自${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  ,N}\right)  = \Theta \left( {{N}^{\text{entw }}\left( \left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  \right) }\right)$以来，$Q$在${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$上的电路复杂度由热带核心中具有最大熵宽度的查询${Q}^{ \star  }$所表征。

We split the proof of Theorem 7.8 in two parts, the upper and lower bound.

我们将定理7.8的证明分为上下界两部分进行。

THEOREM 7.9 (UPPER BOUND). Let $Q$ be any Boolean CQ. Then,

定理7.9（上界）。设$Q$为任意布尔合取查询，则

$$
{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {Q,N}\right)  = O\left( {\mathop{\max }\limits_{{{Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right) }}{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right) .
$$

Proof. Consider an instance $I$ with relation sizes at most $N$ ,and the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ for which we want to construct a circuit $F$ . We will do this in two steps. First,we will construct a circuit ${F}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$ for each query ${Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right)$ . Second,we will compute the union of these circuits by taking the $\oplus$ -sum of their output gates. In other words,we are essentially encoding in the circuit the union of a set of CQs.

证明。考虑一个关系大小至多为$N$的实例$I$，以及需要为其构建电路$F$的多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$。我们将分两步完成：首先为每个查询${Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right)$构建电路${F}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$；其次通过对其输出门取$\oplus$和运算来计算这些电路的并集。本质上，我们是在电路中编码一组合取查询的并集。

We next describe the construction for ${F}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$ . Recall that each ${Q}^{ \star  }$ corresponds to an endomorphism ${h}^{ \star  }$ of $Q$ . For every atom $R$ in $Q$ ,let ${N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right)$ be the number of atoms from $Q$ that ${h}^{ \star  }$ maps to $R$ . As in the prior results,we construct an instance $J$ for $\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$ by adding for each tuple $R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  I$ a tuple ${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ for each atom ${R}_{i}$ in $\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$ . It is easy to see that the size of every relation in $J$ is still bounded by $N$ . We now construct a circuit ${\widehat{F}}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$ for $\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$ that runs on $J$ . To obtain ${F}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$ from ${\widehat{F}}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$ ,we identify the input variable that comes from a fact ${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ to the expression ${\left( {x}_{R\left( \mathbf{a}\right) }\right) }^{{N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right) }$ ; this is the variable for the tuple $R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  I$ multiplied ${N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right)$ times. If ${N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right)  = 0$ ,we can simply route this wire to 1 . If ${N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right)  > 0$ ,we compute the $\otimes$ -product of ${x}_{R\left( \mathrm{a}\right) }$ for as many times as prescribed. The size of the resulting circuit is still $O\left( {{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right)$ .

接下来描述${F}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$的构造。每个${Q}^{ \star  }$对应$Q$的一个自同态${h}^{ \star  }$。对于$Q$中的每个原子$R$，令${N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right)$表示${h}^{ \star  }$映射到$R$的原子数量。沿用先前结果，我们为$\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$构建实例$J$：为每个元组$R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  I$添加$\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$中每个原子${R}_{i}$对应的元组${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$。显然$J$中各关系的大小仍受$N$限制。现构造在$J$上运行的电路${\widehat{F}}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$，通过将来自事实${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$的输入变量标识为表达式${\left( {x}_{R\left( \mathbf{a}\right) }\right) }^{{N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right) }$，即可从${\widehat{F}}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$得到${F}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}$——该变量是元组$R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)  \in  I$乘以${N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right)$次的结果。若${N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right)  = 0$，直接将导线接至1；若${N}_{R}\left( {h}^{ \star  }\right)  > 0$，则按指定次数计算${x}_{R\left( \mathrm{a}\right) }$的$\otimes$积。最终电路规模仍为$O\left( {{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) }\right)$。

We now argue that the resulting circuit correctly computes the desired polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ . Given a tuple $t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right)$ ,we define ${I}_{t}$ to be the restriction of $I$ on the image of the valuation that produces the tuple $t$ . In other words,we get ${I}_{t}$ by setting the annotation of any input tuple that does not contribute to $t$ to 0 . Kostylev et. al [29] showed that ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ can be equivalently written as follows:

我们现在论证所得电路正确计算了目标多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$。给定一个元组$t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right)$，我们将${I}_{t}$定义为$I$在生成该元组$t$的赋值映射下的限制。换言之，通过将所有不参与生成$t$的输入元组标注设为0，即可得到${I}_{t}$。Kostylev等人[29]证明了${p}_{I}^{Q}$可等价表述为：

$$
{p}_{I}^{Q} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right) }}{p}_{{I}_{t}}^{Q}
$$

Now let us look at the polynomial ${p}^{F}$ produced by the circuit $F$ . Every tuple $t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right)$ can be traced back to a homomorphism ${h}_{t}$ from $Q$ to ${I}_{t}$ . Now consider the query ${Q}^{ \star  }$ . Since there is a homomorphism $h$ from ${Q}^{ \star  }$ to $Q$ (the identity homomorphism that maps each variable to itself), there exists a homomorphism ${\widehat{h}}_{t}$ from ${Q}^{ \star  }$ to ${I}_{t}$ . Hence,for each query ${Q}^{ \star  }$ in the tropical core, the polynomial ${p}^{F}$ will contain a monomial ${m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{t}$ . In the other direction,every monomial in ${p}^{F}$ corresponds to a homomorphism from some ${Q}^{ \star  }$ to $I$ . But,since there is a homomorphism from $Q$ to ${Q}^{ \star  }$ ,there is some tuple $t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right)$ that corresponds to that monomial. Hence, ${p}^{F}$ can be written as:

现在考察电路$F$生成的多项式${p}^{F}$。每个元组$t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right)$都可追溯为从$Q$到${I}_{t}$的同态映射${h}_{t}$。考虑查询${Q}^{ \star  }$时，由于存在从${Q}^{ \star  }$到$Q$的同态（将每个变量映射到自身的恒等同态），故存在从${Q}^{ \star  }$到${I}_{t}$的同态${\widehat{h}}_{t}$。因此，对于热带核心中的每个查询${Q}^{ \star  }$，多项式${p}^{F}$都会包含单项式${m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{t}$。反之，${p}^{F}$中的每个单项式都对应着从某个${Q}^{ \star  }$到$I$的同态。而由于存在从$Q$到${Q}^{ \star  }$的同态，故存在与该单项式对应的元组$t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right)$。因此，${p}^{F}$可表示为：

$$
{p}^{F} = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right) }}{m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{t}
$$

To finish the proof,it suffices to show that for every tuple $t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right) ,{p}_{{I}_{t}}^{Q}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{T}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right) }}{m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{t}$ . Indeed,by the definition of the tropical core,it holds that for the canonical instance $\llbracket Q\rrbracket$ and its output tuple $u$ ,

为完成证明，只需证对于任意元组$t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right) ,{p}_{{I}_{t}}^{Q}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{T}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right) }}{m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{t}$均成立。根据热带核心的定义，对于规范实例$\llbracket Q\rrbracket$及其输出元组$u$，显然有：

$$
{p}_{\parallel Q\parallel }^{Q}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{T}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right) }}{m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{u} \tag{3}
$$

But since $\llbracket Q\rrbracket$ is canonical,there is a homomorphism from $\llbracket Q\rrbracket$ to ${I}_{t}$ that can be viewed as a mapping $\phi$ of the variables in ${p}_{\llbracket Q\rrbracket }^{Q}$ to the variables in ${p}_{{I}_{t}}^{Q}$ . For $\phi$ ,we also have that $\phi \left( {m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{u}\right)  = {m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{t}$ . Hence,if we apply $\phi$ to Eq. (3) we obtain the desired result.

但由于$\llbracket Q\rrbracket$是规范化的，存在从$\llbracket Q\rrbracket$到${I}_{t}$的同态，该同态可视为将${p}_{\llbracket Q\rrbracket }^{Q}$中变量映射到${p}_{{I}_{t}}^{Q}$中变量的映射$\phi$。对于$\phi$，我们还满足$\phi \left( {m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{u}\right)  = {m}_{{Q}^{ \star  }}^{t}$。因此，将$\phi$代入式(3)即可得证。

THEOREM 7.10 (Lower Bounn). Let $Q$ be any Boolean ${CQ}$ . Then,for any ${Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right)$ ,we have that $\mathbb{T}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) .}\right.$

定理7.10（下界性）. 设$Q$为任意布尔${CQ}$，则对任意${Q}^{ \star  } \in  \operatorname{Tcore}\left( Q\right)$，必有$\mathbb{T}\left( {Q,N}\right)  = \Omega \left( {{\mathbb{T}}^{ + }\left( {\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  ,N}\right) .}\right.$

Proof. We follow the structure of the proof of Theorem 6.3.

证明. 我们沿用定理6.3的证明框架。

Let $J$ be an instance for $\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$ where every relation has size at most $N$ . Because $\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$ is self-join-free, we will assume without loss of generality that the constants that correspond to distinct variables are distinct, in other words, no output tuple can take the same value for two different variables. Consider the tagged instance $I$ of $Q$ as in the proof of Theorem 4.2. It is easy to see that in $I$ every relation has size $O\left( N\right)$ . Let $F$ be a circuit that computes ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ over ${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$ and let ${F}^{\prime }$ be the circuit that,whenever a wire in $F$ connects to $R\left( {\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}}\right)$ ,it connects to ${R}_{\mathbf{x}}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ ,as in the proof of Theorem 4.2. So far,it may not be true that ${F}^{\prime }$ computes the desired polynomial correctly. Thus,we are going to construct another circuit ${F}^{\prime \prime }$ for to do so.

设$J$为$\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$的一个实例，其中每个关系的大小至多为$N$。由于$\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$无自连接，不失一般性可假设对应不同变量的常量为互异值，即输出元组中不同变量不能取相同值。考虑定理4.2证明中所述$Q$的标记实例$I$。显然在$I$中每个关系的大小为$O\left( N\right)$。令$F$为在${\mathbb{T}}^{ + }$上计算${p}_{I}^{Q}$的电路，根据定理4.2证明，当$F$中的导线连接至$R\left( {\mathbf{a} \cdot  \mathbf{x}}\right)$时，令${F}^{\prime }$为将其改接至${R}_{\mathbf{x}}\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$的电路。目前${F}^{\prime }$可能无法正确计算目标多项式，因此需构造另一个电路${F}^{\prime \prime }$来实现。

We will follow again a running example,this time with the query $Q\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,z}\right)  \land$ $S\left( {z,w}\right)  \land  S\left( {y,w}\right) ,\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  \left( \right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{xy}\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  {S}_{yw}\left( {y,w}\right)$ and $J = \left\{  {{R}_{xy}\left( {a,b}\right) ,{S}_{yw}\left( {b,c}\right) }\right\}$ . Then,the tagged instance is $I = \{ R\left( {{ax},{by}}\right) ,S\left( {{by},{cw}}\right) \}$ . The two polynomials of interest are:

我们将再次沿用运行示例，本次采用查询$Q\left( \right)  \leftarrow  R\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  R\left( {x,z}\right)  \land$$S\left( {z,w}\right)  \land  S\left( {y,w}\right) ,\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  \left( \right)  \leftarrow  {R}_{xy}\left( {x,y}\right)  \land  {S}_{yw}\left( {y,w}\right)$和$J = \left\{  {{R}_{xy}\left( {a,b}\right) ,{S}_{yw}\left( {b,c}\right) }\right\}$。此时标记实例为$I = \{ R\left( {{ax},{by}}\right) ,S\left( {{by},{cw}}\right) \}$，两个目标多项式为：

$$
{p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  } = {x}_{ab} \cdot  {x}_{bc}
$$

$$
{p}_{I}^{Q} = {\left( {x}_{\left( ax,by\right) }\right) }^{2} \cdot  {\left( {x}_{\left( by,cw\right) }\right) }^{2}
$$

We now turn into the polynomial ${p}^{F}$ . Note that,in contrast to our proof for ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = },{p}^{F}$ may not be syntactically the same as ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ . But we can now apply Lemma 7.5 to help us. Indeed,consider any tuple $t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right)$ . Consider the instance ${I}_{t}$ as defined in the upper bound proof,and consider the polynomial ${p}_{{I}_{t}}^{F}$ produced by the circuit $F$ if we set to 0 all the tuples that are not in ${I}_{t}$ . For this polynomial,it must be that ${p}_{{I}_{t}}^{F} = {\mathbb{T}}^{ + }{p}_{{I}_{t}}^{Q}$ . The instance ${I}_{t}$ may not be isomorphic to the canonical instance $\llbracket Q\rrbracket$ ,but all variables in ${Q}^{ \star  }$ take distinct values in tuple $t$ . Hence,since there is a homomorphism from $\llbracket Q\rrbracket$ to ${I}_{t}$ and by Lemma 7.5, ${p}_{{I}_{t}}^{F}$ (and thus ${p}^{F}$ ) must contain a monomial that sees the endomorphism for ${Q}^{ \star  }$ for $t$ . Moreover,every monomial in ${p}^{F}$ that sees an endomorphism must correspond to some $t$ , otherwise the circuit would fail to output the correct value.

现在转向多项式${p}^{F}$。需注意与${\mathrm{C}}_{ = },{p}^{F}$的证明不同，${p}^{F}$在句法上可能与${p}_{I}^{Q}$不完全相同。但可应用引理7.5进行辅助：任取元组$t \in  \bar{Q}\left( I\right)$，考虑上界证明中定义的实例${I}_{t}$，若将不在${I}_{t}$中的所有元组设为0，则电路$F$生成的多项式${p}_{{I}_{t}}^{F}$必满足${p}_{{I}_{t}}^{F} = {\mathbb{T}}^{ + }{p}_{{I}_{t}}^{Q}$。实例${I}_{t}$虽可能与规范实例$\llbracket Q\rrbracket$不同构，但${Q}^{ \star  }$中所有变量在元组$t$中取值互异。因此根据从$\llbracket Q\rrbracket$到${I}_{t}$存在同态映射及引理7.5，${p}_{{I}_{t}}^{F}$（进而${p}^{F}$）必包含体现${Q}^{ \star  }$对$t$自同态的单项式。此外，${p}^{F}$中每个体现自同态的单项式必须对应某个$t$，否则电路将无法输出正确值。

We now apply the same differentiation method as in the proof of Theorem 6.3, but this time we differentiate on the variables that correspond to the atoms of $\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$ . Using the above reasoning,the only terms that will remain in the polynomial of the resulting circuit ${F}^{\prime \prime }$ will correspond to the ones that see the endomorphism for ${Q}^{ \star  }$ (any higher order terms that may exist will be zeroed out). In our running example, the polynomial after the differentiation will still be the same, i.e., we will have ${p}^{{F}^{\prime \prime }} = {x}_{\left( ax,by\right) }^{2} \cdot  {x}_{\left( by,cw\right) }^{2}$ .

我们现在采用与定理6.3证明中相同的微分方法，但此次对对应于$\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack$原子的变量进行微分。根据上述推理，最终电路${F}^{\prime \prime }$的多项式中仅会保留那些见证${Q}^{ \star  }$自同态的项（可能存在的高阶项将被消为零）。在我们的运行示例中，微分后的多项式仍保持不变，即得到${p}^{{F}^{\prime \prime }} = {x}_{\left( ax,by\right) }^{2} \cdot  {x}_{\left( by,cw\right) }^{2}$。

Our construction is not done yet,because the circuit ${F}^{\prime \prime }$ takes as an input powers of each variable,the power being the number of atoms of $Q$ mapped to one of ${Q}^{ \star  }$ via the endomorphism. However, because every variable will appear with exactly the same power in every monomial, it is straighforward to factorize the circuit to another circuit ${F}^{\prime \prime \prime }$ that computes the power of a variable before it is fed to the rest of the circuit. To obtain the desired circuit for ${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  }$ ,we now simply have to replace the subcircuit that computes the power of a variable $x,{\left( x\right) }^{n}$ ,with one that takes only $x$ as an input.

当前构造尚未完成，因为电路${F}^{\prime \prime }$以各变量的幂次作为输入，该幂次等于通过自同态映射到<b2></b1>的原子数量。但由于每个变量在所有单项式中均以相同幂次出现，可直接将电路因式分解为另一个电路${F}^{\prime \prime \prime }$，该电路在变量输入主电路前先计算其幂次。为获得${p}_{J}^{\left\lbrack  {Q}^{ \star  }\right\rbrack  }$的目标电路，现在只需将计算变量$x,{\left( x\right) }^{n}$幂次的子电路替换为仅接受$x$作为输入的电路。

## 8 Related Work

## 8 相关工作

Circuit Size Bounds for $\mathbf{{CQs}}$ . The circuit complexity of query processing has been investigated in depth, and there has been a lot of research in the space of tractable circuits and knowledge compilation [2]. In this work, all circuits are tractable and we focus on a fine-grained study of the exact polynomial size depending on the query and the semiring. Closer to ours is the work of Fan, Koutris and Zhao [16] that proved circuit size bounds for self-join-free CQs over different semirings, which we leverage intensively in this paper. Wang and Yi [42] constructed non-monotone Boolean circuits for CQs under degree constraints by instantiating PANDA [26]. Their circuit sizes match the polymatroid bound up to polylogarithmic factors. Finally, recent work [32] explored the instance-optimality of circuits with fan-out 1 (which are formulas).

$\mathbf{{CQs}}$的电路规模界限。查询处理的电路复杂性已被深入研究，在易处理电路和知识编译领域已有大量成果[2]。本研究中所有电路均具备易处理性，我们重点针对查询和半环进行精确多项式规模的细粒度分析。范、库特里斯和赵的工作[16]与本文最为接近，其证明了无自连接CQ在不同半环上的电路规模界限，我们对此进行了深度借鉴。王和易[42]通过实例化PANDA[26]，在度数约束下构建了CQ的非单调布尔电路，其电路规模与多对数因子内的多拟阵界相匹配。近期研究[32]则探讨了扇出为1的电路（即公式）的实例最优性。

Enumeration of CQs with Self-Joins. Computing CQs with self-joins has been studied in depth. Of particular interest to this paper is the work of Carmeli-Kröll [8] and Carmeli-Segoufin [9], which introduced the technique of a tagged instance that we use. This line of work studies the enumeration complexity of CQs with self-joins.

带自连接CQ的枚举。带自连接的CQ计算已得到深入研究。卡梅利-克勒尔[8]和卡梅利-塞古芬[9]提出的标记实例技术对本研究尤为重要，该技术被我们采用。这一系列工作主要研究带自连接CQ的枚举复杂性。

Factorised Databases. It has been noted in [16] that $d$ -representations and $f$ -representations in Factorised Databases (FD) [5, 34, 35] can be seen as special cases of circuits and formulas for CQs where they are governed by exactly one tree decomposition. Our results in this paper can thus be regarded as results in FDs for CQs with self-joins in this sense.

因子化数据库。文献[16]指出，因子化数据库(FD)[5,34,35]中的$d$表示和$f$表示可视为CQ电路和公式的特例，其严格受单一树分解支配。因此，本文成果可视为针对带自连接CQ的FD研究成果。

Circuits for Polynomials over Semirings. The problem of finding circuit size bounds for a polynomial has been studied intensively in the literature. A line of work that is particularly related to our problem is done by Jukna [22, 23]. For example, the notion of "pure dynamic programming" in Jukna’s language [23] translates to circuits over $\mathbb{T}$ in ours.

半环上多项式的电路。多项式电路规模界限问题在学界备受关注。尤克纳[22,23]的研究与我们的问题高度相关，例如其"纯动态规划"概念对应于本文中$\mathbb{T}$上的电路。

## 9 Conclusion

## 9 结论

In this paper, we studied circuit size bounds for CQs with self-joins. We characterized the circuit size of CQ with self-joins by an appropriate self-join free CQ for some classes of semirings. Several open questions remain. First, the exact fine-grained circuit size bound for self-join-free CQs over ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ is still open [16]. Such a result will immediately transfer to tight circuit size bound for CQs with self-joins over ${\mathbf{C}}_{ = }$ using our technique in Section 6. Another important question is whether we can close the gap between the upper and lower bound for self-join free CQs over $\mathbb{B}$ . It is possible that one can prove this using probabilistic arguments that are not explored in this paper [1, 37, 38]. Also, it is interesting to see if our endomorphism point of view can be applied to other scenarios when studying CQs with self-joins $\left\lbrack  {9,{17},{30},{36}}\right\rbrack$ .

本文研究了带自连接的CQs（Conjunctive Queries）电路规模界限问题。针对某些半环类别，我们通过适当的无自连接CQ刻画了带自连接CQ的电路规模。若干开放性问题仍待解决：首先，关于${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$的无自连接CQs的精确细粒度电路规模界限仍未确定[16]，该结果通过第6节的技术可立即转化为${\mathbf{C}}_{ = }$上带自连接CQs的紧致电路规模界限。另一个关键问题是能否缩小$\mathbb{B}$上无自连接CQs的上下界间隙，或可通过本文未涉及的概型论证方法予以证明[1,37,38]。此外，研究带自连接CQs时，我们的自同态视角能否拓展至其他场景$\left\lbrack  {9,{17},{30},{36}}\right\rbrack$也值得探讨。

## A Connections between Semiring Classes

## A 半环类别间的关联

In this part,we show the connection between two classes of semirings, ${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$ and ${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ .

本部分展示两类半环${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$与${\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$间的关联性。

Definition A. 1 ([29]). The class ${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$ contains all semirings $\mathbb{S}$ for which for any polynomial $p$ and any monomial ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}$ ,the inequality ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n} \preccurlyeq  p$ implies that $p$ contains the monomial ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}$ .

定义A.1([29])。类别${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$包含所有满足以下性质的半环$\mathbb{S}$：对任意多项式$p$与单项式${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}$，不等式${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n} \preccurlyeq  p$意味着$p$必包含单项式${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}$。

Proposition A.2. ${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}} \subseteq  {\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$ .

命题A.2。${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}} \subseteq  {\mathrm{C}}_{ = }$。

Proof. Note that all semirings in ${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$ are naturally ordered [29]. We show that if for any polynomial $p$ and any monomial ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}{ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}p$ ,we have ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n} \in  p$ ,then ${p}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{2}$ implies ${p}_{1} \simeq  {p}_{2}$ . Indeed,for any monomial ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n} \in  {p}_{1}$ ,we have ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}{ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}^{\text{nat }}{p}_{1}$ by the definition of ${ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}^{\text{nat }}$ and therefore ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}{ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{2}$ since ${ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}^{\text{nat }}$ is a subrelation of ${ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}$ for any naturally ordered semiring. Therefore ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n} \in  {p}_{2}$ and thus we conclude ${p}_{1} \simeq  {p}_{2}$ since the monomial ${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}$ is arbitrary.

证明。注意到${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$中的所有半环都是自然有序的[29]。我们证明若对任意多项式$p$和单项式${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}{ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}p$有${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n} \in  p$，则${p}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{2}$蕴含${p}_{1} \simeq  {p}_{2}$。事实上，根据${ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}^{\text{nat }}$的定义，对任意单项式${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n} \in  {p}_{1}$都有${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}{ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}^{\text{nat }}{p}_{1}$，又因在任何自然有序半环中${ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}^{\text{nat }}$都是${ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}$的子关系，故得${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}{ \preccurlyeq  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{1}{ \equiv  }_{\mathbb{S}}{p}_{2}$。因此${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n} \in  {p}_{2}$，由于单项式${x}_{1} \otimes  {x}_{2} \otimes  \cdots  \otimes  {x}_{n}$的任意性，我们最终得出${p}_{1} \simeq  {p}_{2}$。

We do not know whether the reverse inclusion is also true and leave this as an open question. An affirmative answer would be interesting and quite surprising, since that allows one to characterize ${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$ without ever mentioning the partial relation.

我们尚不清楚反向包含是否成立，将此留作开放性问题。若能得到肯定答案将非常有趣且出人意料，因为这意味着无需提及偏序关系即可刻画${\mathrm{C}}_{\mathrm{{bi}}}$。

## B Circuit Bounds with Degree Constraints

## B 带度数约束的电路界

In the main body of the paper, we only presented upper and lower circuit bounds when the only constraint is an upper bound $N$ on the size of each relation. Here,we will show how to extend all of our upper and lower bounds to the case where we have more general constraints, called degree constraints.

在论文主体部分，我们仅讨论了当约束条件仅为关系大小上限$N$时的电路上下界。此处我们将展示如何将所有上下界结果推广到具有更一般约束（称为度数约束）的情形。

We define the notion of degree constraints as in [27]. Let DC be a set of triples $\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$ for some $X \subset  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ and ${N}_{Y \mid  X} \in  \mathbb{N}$ . An instance $I$ satisfies the degree constraints DC if for every relation ${R}_{e}$ in $I$ with $X \subseteq  Y \subseteq  e$ and every tuple ${t}_{X}$ we have $\left| {{\pi }_{Y}\left( {{R}_{e} \ltimes  {t}_{X}}\right) }\right|  \leq  {N}_{Y \mid  X}$ . A constraint of the form $\left( {\varnothing ,e,{N}_{e}}\right)$ is simply a cardinality constraint. The degree constraints on an instance can be translated as constraints on entropic functions as follows:

我们采用[27]中定义的度数约束概念。设DC为由若干三元组$\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)$组成的集合，其中$X \subset  Y \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$且${N}_{Y \mid  X} \in  \mathbb{N}$。若实例$I$中每个关系${R}_{e}$对每个元组${t}_{X}$都满足$\left| {{\pi }_{Y}\left( {{R}_{e} \ltimes  {t}_{X}}\right) }\right|  \leq  {N}_{Y \mid  X}$，则称该实例满足度数约束DC。形如$\left( {\varnothing ,e,{N}_{e}}\right)$的约束即为基数约束。实例的度数约束可转化为熵函数约束如下：

$$
\mathrm{{HDC}} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  {h : {2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + } \mid  \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{\left( {X,Y,{N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right)  \in  \mathrm{{DC}}}}h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  \leq  \log {N}_{Y \mid  X}}\right\}  
$$

where $h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} h\left( Y\right)  - h\left( X\right)$ . For a given set of degree constraints HDC,we also define the "scaled-up" degree constraints $\mathrm{{HDC}} \times  k$ as the same set of constraints but with all the degree bounds multiplied by $k$ .

其中$h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} h\left( Y\right)  - h\left( X\right)$。对于给定度数约束集HDC，我们同时定义"放大版"度数约束$\mathrm{{HDC}} \times  k$，其约束形式相同但所有度数界均乘以$k$。

In analogy to the entropic and submodular width, we can define their degree-aware versions, called degree-aware entropic and degree-aware submodular width respectively:

类似于熵宽度与次模宽度，我们可以定义其度感知版本，分别称为度感知熵宽度与度感知次模宽度：

$$
\text{da-entw}\left( {\mathcal{H},\mathrm{{HDC}}}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  {\bar{\Gamma }}_{n}^{ * } \cap  \mathrm{{HDC}}}}\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  \mathrm{{TD}}}}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}h\left( {\chi \left( v\right) }\right) 
$$

$$
\text{da-subw}\left( {\mathcal{H},\mathrm{{HDC}}}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \mathop{\max }\limits_{{h \in  {\Gamma }_{n} \cap  \mathrm{{HDC}}}}\mathop{\min }\limits_{{\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)  \in  \mathrm{{TD}}}}\mathop{\max }\limits_{{v \in  V\left( \mathcal{T}\right) }}h\left( {\chi \left( v\right) }\right) 
$$

We can now generalize the theorems from [16] in terms of degree constraints.

现在我们可以根据度约束对文献[16]中的定理进行推广。

THEOREM B.1 ([16]). For any $\epsilon  > 0$ and any self-join-free CQQ,there exists an instance $I$ and $k > 0$ that satisfies the constraints $\mathrm{{HDC}} \times  k$ such that any circuit $F$ that computes the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ over a semiring in $\left\{  {{\mathbb{B}}_{\text{lin }},\mathbb{T},\mathbb{C}}\right\}$ has size

定理B.1([16])。对于任意$\epsilon  > 0$和无自连接的合取查询Q，存在满足约束$\mathrm{{HDC}} \times  k$的实例$I$和$k > 0$，使得在半环$\left\{  {{\mathbb{B}}_{\text{lin }},\mathbb{T},\mathbb{C}}\right\}$上计算多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$的任何电路$F$的规模满足

$$
\log \left| F\right|  \geq  \left( {1 - \epsilon }\right)  \cdot  \text{da-entw}\left( {Q,\mathrm{{HDC}} \times  k}\right) \text{.}
$$

THEOREM B.2 ([16]). Fix a self-join-free CQQ and a set of degree constraints DC. Let $I$ be any instance that satisfies DC. Then,there exists a multilinear and homogeneous circuit $F$ of size $O\left( {2}^{w}\right)$ that produces the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ over any idempotent semiring,where $w = {da}$ -entw(Q,HDC).

定理B.2([16])。给定无自连接的合取查询Q和度约束集DC。设$I$为满足DC的任意实例，则存在规模为$O\left( {2}^{w}\right)$的多线性齐次电路$F$，可在任何幂等半环上生成多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$，其中$w = {da}$-entw(Q,HDC)。

If $\mathrm{{DC}}$ is a set of degree constraints,we denote $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\mathrm{{DC}}}\right)$ to be the optimal size of a circuit that computes the polynomial ${p}_{I}^{Q}$ for a Boolean CQ $Q$ over $\mathbb{S}$ over all instances $I$ that satisfy the degree constraints. We now present the analogues of the results in the main body of the paper.

若$\mathrm{{DC}}$为度约束集，我们记$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\mathrm{{DC}}}\right)$表示在所有满足度约束的实例$I$上，计算布尔合取查询$Q$在$\mathbb{S}$上多项式${p}_{I}^{Q}$的电路最优规模。现给出本文主体结果的对应形式。

THEOREM B.3. Let $\mathbb{S}$ be any semiring and $Q$ be a Boolean ${CQ}$ . Then,for any degree constraints DC, $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\mathrm{{DC}}}\right)  \leq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,\mathrm{{DC}}}\right)$ .

定理B.3。设$\mathbb{S}$为任意半环，$Q$为布尔${CQ}$。则对于任意度约束DC，$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\mathrm{{DC}}}\right)  \leq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,\mathrm{{DC}}}\right)$。

Proof. Observe that the proof of Theorem 4.1 preserves all degree constraints, since it simply creates copies of each relation.

证明。注意定理4.1的证明过程保持了所有度约束，因其仅对每个关系创建副本。

THEOREM B.4. Let $\mathbb{S}$ be any idempotent semiring,and $Q$ be a Boolean ${CQ}$ s.t. every endomorphism is an automorphism. Then, $\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\mathrm{{DC}} \times  k}\right)  \geq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,\mathrm{{DC}}}\right)$ ,where $k$ is the number of atoms in $Q$ .

定理B.4。设$\mathbb{S}$为任意幂等半环，$Q$为布尔${CQ}$且每个自同态皆为自同构。则$\mathbb{S}\left( {Q,\mathrm{{DC}} \times  k}\right)  \geq  \mathbb{S}\left( {\left\lbrack  Q\right\rbrack  ,\mathrm{{DC}}}\right)$，其中$k$表示$Q$中的原子数。

Proof. The proof of Theorem 4.2 preserves the degree constraints if we scale them by a factor of $k$ .

证明。若将度约束按$k$倍缩放，定理4.2的证明仍保持度约束。

Analogous results can be shown for all of our main theorems, since the tagging construction maintains the degree-aware constraints if we scale them by a constant factor that depends only on the query.

我们的所有主要定理均可得到类似结果，因为标记构造法在按仅依赖于查询的常数因子缩放时，能保持度感知约束。
