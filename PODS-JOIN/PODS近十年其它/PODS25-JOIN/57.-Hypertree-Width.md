Hypertree decompositions provide a way to evaluate Conjunctive Queries (CQs) in polynomial time, where the exponent of this polynomial is determined by the width of the decomposition. In theory, the goal of efficient CQ evaluation therefore has to be a minimisation of the width. However, in practical settings, it turns out that there are also other properties of a decomposition that influence the performance of query evaluation. It is therefore of interest to restrict the computation of decompositions by constraints and to guide this computation by preferences. To this end, we propose a novel framework based on candidate tree decompositions, which allows us to introduce soft hypertree width (shw). This width measure is a relaxation of hypertree width (hw); it is never greater than hw and, in some cases, shw may actually be lower than hw. Most importantly, shw preserves the tractability of deciding if a given CQ is below some fixed bound, while offering more algorithmic flexibility. In particular, it provides a natural way to incorporate preferences and constraints into the computation of decompositions. A prototype implementation and preliminary experiments confirm that this novel framework can indeed have a practical impact on query evaluation.

超树分解为多项式时间内评估合取查询(CQs)提供了方法，其中多项式的指数由分解宽度决定。理论上，高效CQ评估的目标必然是宽度最小化。然而实践表明，分解的其他特性也会影响查询评估性能。因此，通过约束条件限制分解计算，并通过偏好引导该过程具有研究价值。为此，我们提出基于候选树分解的新框架，引入软超树宽度(shw)。该宽度度量是对超树宽度(hw)的松弛化处理，其值始终不大于hw，某些情况下shw可能实际低于hw。最关键的是，shw在保持判定给定CQ是否低于固定边界可处理性的同时，提供了更多算法灵活性。特别是为将偏好和约束融入分解计算提供了自然途径。原型实现与初步实验证实，该新框架确实能对查询评估产生实际影响。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Since their introduction nearly 25 years ago, hypertree decompositions (HDs) and hypertree width ( ${hw}$ ) have emerged as a cornerstone in the landscape of database research. Their enduring relevance lies in their remarkable ability to balance theoretical elegance with practical applicability. Hypertree width generalises $\alpha$ -acyclicity,a foundational concept for the efficient evaluation of conjunctive queries (CQs) [24]. Indeed, CQs of bounded hypertree width can be evaluated in polynomial time. Crucially,determining whether a CQ has ${hw} \leq  k$ for fixed $k \geq  1$ and,if so,constructing an HD of width $\leq  k$ can also be achieved in polynomial time [12].

自近25年前问世以来，超树分解(HDs)和超树宽度(${hw}$)已成为数据库研究领域的基石。其持久相关性源于其平衡理论优雅性与实践适用性的卓越能力。超树宽度推广了$\alpha$非循环性这一合取查询(CQs)高效评估的基础概念[24]。确实，有界超树宽度的CQs可在多项式时间内评估。关键的是，对于固定$k \geq  1$判定CQ是否具有${hw} \leq  k$，并在存在时构建宽度$\leq  k$的HD，同样可在多项式时间内完成[12]。

Consequently, practitioners naturally gravitate towards width measures that promise the smallest possible decomposition widths. Despite this motivation, HDs and ${hw}$ retain a crucial distinction: ${hw}$ is,to date,the most general width measure for it can be decided in polynomial time [12] if the width of a given query is below some fixed bound $k$ . In this work,we introduce a novel width notion-soft hypertree width(shw)- that overcomes this trade-off by retaining polynomial-time decidability while potentially reducing widths compared to ${hw}$ .

But this is only a first step towards a larger goal. Query evaluation based on decomposition methods works via a transformation of a given CQ with low width into an acyclic CQ by computing "local" joins for the bags at each node of the decomposition. The acyclic CQ is then evaluated by Yannakakis' algorithm [24]. From a theoretical perspective, the complexity of this approach solely depends on the width, while the actual cost of the local joins and the size of the relations produced by these joins is ignored. To remedy this short-coming, Scarcello et al. [20] introduced weighted ${HDs}$ to incorporate costs into HDs. More precisely,among the HDs with minimal width,they aimed at an HD that minimises the cost of the local joins needed to transform the CQ into an acyclic one and the cost of the (semi-)joins required by Yannakakis' algorithm. Promising first empirical results were obtained with this approach.

但这只是实现更大目标的第一步。基于分解方法的查询评估通过将低宽度的给定CQ转换为无环CQ来实现，具体方式是为分解树每个节点的包计算"局部"连接。随后通过Yannakakis算法[24]评估无环CQ。从理论角度看，此方法的复杂度仅取决于宽度，而忽略了局部连接的实际成本及这些连接产生的关系大小。为弥补这一缺陷，Scarcello等人[20]引入加权${HDs}$将成本纳入HD。更准确地说，在具有最小宽度的HD中，他们旨在找到能最小化将CQ转换为无环查询所需局部连接成本及Yannakakis算法要求的（半）连接成本的HD。该方法已获得初步实证成果。

However, there is more to decomposition-based query optimisation than minimising the width. For instance, ${hw} = 2$ means that we have to compute joins of two relations to transform the given query into an acyclic one. Regarding width, it makes no difference if the join is a Cartesian product of two completely unrelated relations or a comparatively cheap join along a foreign key relationship. This phenomenon is, in fact, confirmed by Scarcello et al. [20] when they report on decompositions with higher width but lower cost in their experimental results. Apart from the pure join costs at individual nodes or between neighbouring nodes in the decomposition, there may be yet other reasons why a decomposition with slightly higher width should be preferred. For instance, in a distributed environment, it makes a huge difference, if the required joins and semi-joins are between relations on the same server or on different servers. In other words, apart from incorporating costs of the joins at a node and between neighbouring nodes in the HD, a more general approach that considers constraints and preferences is called for.

然而，基于分解的查询优化不仅限于最小化宽度。例如，${hw} = 2$意味着必须计算两个关系的连接以将给定查询转换为无环查询。就宽度而言，无论该连接是两个完全不相关关系的笛卡尔积，还是沿外键关系的相对廉价连接，都没有区别。Scarcello等人[20]的实验结果中报告了宽度较大但成本较低的分解，证实了这一现象。除了分解中单个节点或相邻节点间的纯连接成本外，可能还有其他理由优先选择宽度稍大的分解。例如在分布式环境中，所需连接和半连接是在同一服务器还是不同服务器的关系间进行，会产生巨大差异。换言之，除了考虑HD中节点及相邻节点间的连接成本外，还需要能兼顾约束条件和偏好的更通用方法。

In this work, we propose a novel framework based on the notion of candidate tree decompositions (CTDs) from [10]. A CTD is a tree decomposition (TD) whose bags have to be chosen from a specified set of vertex sets (= the candidate bags). One can then reduce the problem of finding a particular decomposition (in particular, generalised or fractional hypertree decomposition) for given CQ $q$ to the problem of finding a CTD for an appropriately defined set of candidate bags. In [10], this idea was used (by specifying appropriate sets of candidate bags) to identify tractable fragments of deciding,for given $\mathrm{{CQ}}q$ and fixed $k$ ,if $\operatorname{ghw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ or $\operatorname{fhw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ holds. However, as was observed in [10], it is unclear how the idea of CTDs could be applied to hw-computation, where parent/child relationships between nodes in a decomposition are critical.

本工作中，我们基于[10]提出的候选树分解(CTD)概念提出新框架。CTD是一种树分解(TD)，其包必须从指定的顶点集集合(=候选包)中选择。通过为适当定义的候选包集合寻找CTD，可将为给定CQ$q$寻找特定分解(特别是广义或分数超树分解)的问题归约为此问题。[10]中通过指定适当的候选包集合，该思想被用于识别判定给定$\mathrm{{CQ}}q$和固定$k$时是否$\operatorname{ghw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$或$\operatorname{fhw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$成立的可处理片段。但如[10]所述，尚不清楚如何将CTD思想应用于hw计算——其中分解中节点间的父子关系至关重要。

We will overcome this problem by introducing a relaxed notion of HDs and ${hw}$ ,which we will refer to as soft HDs and soft hw (shw). The crux of this approach will be a relaxation of the so-called "special condition" of HDs (formal definitions of all concepts mentioned in the introduction will be given in Section 2): it will be restrictive enough to guarantee a polynomial upper bound on the number of candidate bags and relaxed enough to make the approach oblivious of any parent/child relationships of nodes in the decomposition. We thus create a novel framework based on CTDs, that paves the way for several improvements over the original approach based on HDs and ${hw}$ ,while retaining the crucial tractability of deciding,for fixed $k$ ,if the width is $\leq  k$ and,if so,computing a decomposition of the desired width.

我们将通过引入宽松化的HD概念和${hw}$（称为软HD和软hw（shw））来解决这个问题。该方法的核心在于放宽HD的所谓"特殊条件"（引言中所有概念的正式定义将在第2节给出）：这种放宽既要足够严格以保证候选包数量的多项式上界，又要足够宽松使该方法无需感知分解中节点的任何父子关系。由此我们创建了基于CTD的新框架，在保留关键可判定性的前提下（对于固定$k$，能判定宽度是否$\leq  k$并在满足时计算目标宽度的分解），为超越原始HD和${hw}$方法的多种改进铺平了道路。

First of all, shw constitutes an improvement in terms of the width, i.e., it is never greater than ${hw}$ and there exist CQs $q$ with strictly smaller ${shw}$ then ${hw}$ . The definition of soft HDs and soft ${hw}$ lends itself to a natural generalisation by specifying rules for extending a given set of candidate bags in a controlled manner. We thus define a whole hierarchy of width measures ${\left( sh{w}^{i}\right) }_{i \geq  0}$ with ${sh}{w}^{0} = {shw}$ . Moreover,will prove that ${sh}{w}^{\infty } = {ghw}$ holds. In other words,we extend the above mentioned hierarchy of width measures to the following extended hierarchy:

首先，shw在宽度维度实现了改进——其值永远不大于${hw}$，且存在严格满足${shw}$${hw}$的CQ$q$。软HD和软${hw}$的定义通过规范可控扩展候选包集的规则，自然衍生出广义化形式。由此我们定义了完整的宽度度量层级${\left( sh{w}^{i}\right) }_{i \geq  0}$（其中${sh}{w}^{0} = {shw}$），并将证明${sh}{w}^{\infty } = {ghw}$成立。换言之，我们将上述宽度度量层级扩展为如下结构：

$$
{fhw}\left( q\right)  \leq  {ghw}\left( q\right)  = {sh}{w}^{\infty }\left( q\right)  \leq  {sh}{w}^{i}\left( q\right)  \leq  {sh}{w}^{0}\left( q\right)  = {shw}\left( q\right)  \leq  {hw}\left( q\right) ,
$$

which holds for every CQ $q$ . Basing our new width notion shw on CTDs gives us a lot of computational flexibility. In particular, in [10], a straightforward algorithm was presented for computing a CTD for a given set of candidate bags. We will show how various types of constraints (such as disallowing Cartesian products) and preferences (which includes the minimisation of costs functions in [20] as an important special case) can be incorporated into the computation of CTDs without destroying the polynomial time complexity. We emphasise that even though our focus in this work is on soft HDs and shw, the incorporation of constraints and preferences into the CTD computation equally applies to any other use of CTDs - such as the above mentioned computation of generalised and fractional hypertree decomposition in certain settings proposed in [10]. We have also carried out first experimental results with a prototype implementation of our approach. In short, basing query evaluation on soft HDs and incorporating constraints and preferences into their construction may indeed lead to significant performance gains. Moreover the computation of soft HDs in a bottom-up fashion via CTDs instead of the top-down construction [6, 15] or parallel computation of HDs [8] is in the order of milliseconds and does not create a new bottleneck.

该结论对所有CQ$q$成立。基于CTD构建的新宽度概念shw赋予我们极大的计算灵活性。特别地，文献[10]提出了针对给定候选包集计算CTD的简明算法。我们将展示如何在保持多项式时间复杂度的前提下，将各类约束（如禁止笛卡尔积）和偏好（包含文献[20]成本函数最小化这一重要特例）融入CTD计算过程。需要强调的是，尽管本文聚焦软HD和shw，但将约束与偏好融入CTD计算的理念同样适用于其他CTD应用场景——例如文献[10]提出的特定场景下广义化及分数超树分解计算。我们已通过原型实现获得初步实验结果，表明基于软HD的查询评估结合约束与偏好确实能带来显著性能提升。此外，通过CTD自底向上计算软HD（相较于文献[6,15]的自顶向下构建或文献[8]的并行HD计算）仅需毫秒级时间，不会形成新瓶颈。

## 2 PRELIMINARIES

## 2 预备知识

Conjunctive queries (CQs) are first-order formulas whose only connectives are existential quantification and conjunction,i.e., $\forall , \vee$ ,and $\neg$ are disallowed. Likewise,Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs) are given in this form. Hypergraphs have proved to be a useful abstraction of CQs and CSPs. Recall that a hypergraph $H$ is a pair $\left( {V\left( H\right) ,E\left( H\right) }\right)$ where $V\left( H\right)$ is called the set of vertices and $E\left( H\right)  \subseteq  {2}^{V\left( H\right) }$ is the set of (hyper)edges. Then the hypergraph $H\left( \phi \right)$ corresponding to a CQ or CSP given by a logical formula $\phi$ has as vertices the variables occurring in $\phi$ . Moreover,every collection of variables jointly occurring in an atom of $\phi$ forms an edge in $H\left( \phi \right)$ . From now on,we will mainly talk about hypergraphs with the understanding that all results equally apply to CQs and CSPs.

合取查询(CQs)是一阶逻辑公式，其仅有的连接词是存在量词和合取，即不允许出现$\forall , \vee$和$\neg$。类似地，约束满足问题(CSPs)也以此形式给出。超图已被证明是CQs和CSPs的有效抽象。超图$H$是一个二元组$\left( {V\left( H\right) ,E\left( H\right) }\right)$，其中$V\left( H\right)$称为顶点集，$E\left( H\right)  \subseteq  {2}^{V\left( H\right) }$是(超)边集。对应于逻辑公式$\phi$的CQ或CSP的超图$H\left( \phi \right)$，其顶点是$\phi$中出现的变量。此外，$\phi$原子式中共同出现的每组变量构成$H\left( \phi \right)$中的一条边。下文将主要讨论超图，但所有结果同样适用于CQs和CSPs。

We write $I\left( v\right)$ for the set $\{ e \in  E\left( H\right)  \mid  v \in  e\}$ of edges incident to vertex $v$ . W.l.o.g.,we assume that hypergraphs have no isolated vertices,i.e.,every $v \in  V\left( H\right)$ is in some edge. A set of vertices $U \subseteq  V\left( H\right)$ induces the induced subhypergraph $H\left\lbrack  U\right\rbrack$ with vertices $U$ and edges $\{ e \cap  U \mid  e \in  E\left( H\right) \}  \smallsetminus  \varnothing$ .

我们用$I\left( v\right)$表示与顶点$v$关联的边集$\{ e \in  E\left( H\right)  \mid  v \in  e\}$。不失一般性，我们假设超图没有孤立顶点，即每个$v \in  V\left( H\right)$都位于某条边中。顶点集$U \subseteq  V\left( H\right)$导出诱导子超图$H\left\lbrack  U\right\rbrack$，其顶点为$U$，边为$\{ e \cap  U \mid  e \in  E\left( H\right) \}  \smallsetminus  \varnothing$。

Let $H$ be a hypergraph and let $S \subseteq  V\left( H\right)$ . We say that two vertices $u,v$ are $\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$ -connected if they are not in $S$ and there is a path from $u$ to $v$ without any vertices of $S$ . Two edges $e,f$ are $\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$ -connected if there are $\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$ -connected vertices $u \in  e,v \in  f$ . An $\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$ -component is a maximal set of pairwise $\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$ -connected edges. For a set of edges $\lambda  \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$ ,we will simply speak of $\left\lbrack  \lambda \right\rbrack$ -components in place of $\left\lbrack  {\bigcup \lambda }\right\rbrack$ -components .

设$H$为超图，$S \subseteq  V\left( H\right)$为顶点集。若两个顶点$u,v$不在$S$中且存在不经过$S$任何顶点的路径连接$u$和$v$，则称它们是$\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$-连通的。两条边$e,f$若存在$\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$-连通的顶点$u \in  e,v \in  f$，则称它们是$\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$-连通的。$\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$-分量是指两两$\left\lbrack  S\right\rbrack$-连通边的最大集合。对于边集$\lambda  \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$，我们简称为$\left\lbrack  \lambda \right\rbrack$-分量而非$\left\lbrack  {\bigcup \lambda }\right\rbrack$-分量。

A tree decomposition (TD) of hypergraph $H$ is a tuple(T,B)where $T$ is a tree and $B : V\left( T\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{V\left( H\right) }$ such that the following hold:

超图$H$的树分解(TD)是一个元组(T,B)，其中$T$是树，$B : V\left( T\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{V\left( H\right) }$满足以下条件：

(1) for every $e \in  E\left( H\right)$ ,there is a node $u$ in $T$ such that $e \subseteq  B\left( u\right)$ ,

(1) 对于每个$e \in  E\left( H\right)$，存在$T$中的节点$u$使得$e \subseteq  B\left( u\right)$，

(2) and for every $v \in  V\left( H\right) ,\{ u \in  V\left( T\right)  \mid  v \in  B\left( u\right) \}$ induces a non-empty subtree of $T$ .

(2) 且对于每个$v \in  V\left( H\right) ,\{ u \in  V\left( T\right)  \mid  v \in  B\left( u\right) \}$都会诱导出$T$的一个非空子树。

The latter condition is referred to as the connectedness condition. We sometimes assume that a tree decomposition is rooted. In this case we write ${T}_{u}$ for $u \in  V\left( T\right)$ to refer to the subtree rooted at $u$ . For subtree ${T}^{\prime }$ of $T$ ,we write $B\left( {T}^{\prime }\right)$ for $\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{u \in  V\left( {T}^{\prime }\right) }}B\left( u\right)$ .

后一条件被称为连通性条件。我们有时假设树分解是有根的，此时用${T}_{u}$表示$u \in  V\left( T\right)$以指代以$u$为根的子树。对于$T$的子树${T}^{\prime }$，我们用$B\left( {T}^{\prime }\right)$表示$\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{u \in  V\left( {T}^{\prime }\right) }}B\left( u\right)$。

A generalised hypertree decomposition (GHD) of a hypergraph $H$ is a triple $\left( {T,\lambda ,B}\right)$ ,such that (T,B)is a tree decomposition and $\lambda  : V\left( T\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{E\left( H\right) }$ satisfies $B\left( u\right)  \subseteq   \cup  \lambda \left( u\right)$ for every $u \in  V\left( T\right)$ . That is,every $\lambda \left( u\right)$ is an edge cover of $B\left( u\right)$ . A hypertree decomposition (HD) of a hypergraph $H$ is a GHD $\left( {T,\lambda ,B}\right)$ ,where the $T$ is rooted and the so-called special condition holds,i.e.,for every $u \in  V\left( T\right)$ ,we have $B\left( {T}_{u}\right)  \cap  \bigcup \lambda \left( u\right)  \subseteq  B\left( u\right)$ . Actually,in the literature on (generalised) hypertree decompositions,it is more common to use $\chi$ instead of $B$ . Moreover,rather than explicitly stating $B,\chi$ ,and $\lambda$ as functions,they are usually considered as labels - writing ${B}_{u},{\lambda }_{u}$ ,and ${\chi }_{u}$ rather than $B\left( u\right) ,\chi \left( u\right)$ ,and $\lambda \left( u\right)$ ,and referring to them as $B - ,\chi  -$ ,and $\lambda$ -label of node $u$ .

超图$H$的广义超树分解(GHD)是一个三元组$\left( {T,\lambda ,B}\right)$，其中(T,B)是树分解且每个$u \in  V\left( T\right)$对应的$\lambda  : V\left( T\right)  \rightarrow  {2}^{E\left( H\right) }$满足$B\left( u\right)  \subseteq   \cup  \lambda \left( u\right)$条件，即每个$\lambda \left( u\right)$都是$B\left( u\right)$的边覆盖。超图$H$的超树分解(HD)是一种带根GHD$\left( {T,\lambda ,B}\right)$，需满足特殊条件：对每个$u \in  V\left( T\right)$存在$B\left( {T}_{u}\right)  \cap  \bigcup \lambda \left( u\right)  \subseteq  B\left( u\right)$。实际上在（广义）超树分解文献中更常用$\chi$而非$B$，且通常将$B,\chi$和$\lambda$视为节点$u$的$B - ,\chi  -$标签与$\lambda$标签，而非显式声明为函数。

## 3 REVISITING CANDIDATE TREE DECOMPOSITIONS

## 3 重审候选树分解

We recall the candidate tree decomposition problem CANDIDATETD introduced in [10]. To that end we will be interested in tree decompositions of a specific structure.

我们回顾文献[10]提出的候选树分解问题CANDIDATETD。为此我们将关注特定结构的树分解。

DEFINITION 1. A rooted tree decomposition(T,B)is in component normal form (CompNF),if for each node $u \in  V\left( T\right)$ ,and for each child $c$ of $u$ ,there is exactly one $\left\lbrack  {B\left( u\right) }\right\rbrack$ -edge component ${C}_{c}$ such that $B\left( {T}_{c}\right)  =  \cup  {C}_{c} \cup  \left( {B\left( u\right)  \cap  B\left( c\right) }\right)$ . That is,each subtree "corresponds" to one $\left\lbrack  {B\left( u\right) }\right\rbrack$ -edge component plus the interface $B\left( u\right)  \cap  B\left( c\right)$ from the parent node $u$ into this component.

定义1. 有根树分解(T,B)若满足组件范式(CompNF)，则对每个节点$u \in  V\left( T\right)$及其子节点$c$，存在唯一$\left\lbrack  {B\left( u\right) }\right\rbrack$边组件${C}_{c}$使得$B\left( {T}_{c}\right)  =  \cup  {C}_{c} \cup  \left( {B\left( u\right)  \cap  B\left( c\right) }\right)$成立。即每个子树"对应"一个$\left\lbrack  {B\left( u\right) }\right\rbrack$边组件加上父节点$u$到该组件的接口$B\left( u\right)  \cap  B\left( c\right)$。

In the CANDIDATETD problem,we are given a hypergraph $H$ and a set $\mathbf{S} \subseteq  {2}^{V\left( H\right) }( =$ the set of candidate bags),and we have to decide whether there exists a CompNF tree decomposition(T,B) of $H$ ,such that,for every node $u \in  T$ ,we have $B\left( u\right)  \in  \mathrm{S}$ . That is,whether a CompNF TD of $H$ can be formed using only candidate bags. We refer to such a TD as candidate tree decomposition (CTD).

在CANDIDATETD问题中，给定一个超图$H$和一个候选包集合$\mathbf{S} \subseteq  {2}^{V\left( H\right) }( =$，需判定是否存在$H$的CompNF树分解(T,B)，使得对于每个节点$u \in  T$都满足$B\left( u\right)  \in  \mathrm{S}$。即能否仅用候选包构建$H$的CompNF树分解。此类分解称为候选树分解(CTD)。

It was also shown in [10] that the problem of deciding whether a hypergraph has width $\leq  k$ for various notions of decompositions can be reduced to the CANDIDATETD problem by an appropriate choice of the set $S$ of candidate bags. For instance,if $\mathrm{S}$ consists of the subsets of $V\left( H\right)$ of size at most $k + 1$ ,then the CANDIDATETD problem equals checking ${tw} \leq  k$ ; if $\mathrm{S}$ consists of sets of the form $\cup  \lambda$ for arbitrary set $\lambda$ of at most $k$ edges of $H$ ,then it checks ${ghw} \leq  k$ . It has been shown in [10] that the CANDIDATETD problem is decidable in polynomial time (w.r.t. the combined size of $H$ and S). Consequently, the CTD framework was used successfully in [10] to prove tractability results for certain kinds of decomposition problems by reducing them to the CANDIDATETD problem for a polynomially bounded set of candidate bags. For instance,tractability of deciding $\operatorname{ghw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ and fhw $\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ for fixed $k$ (that latter denoting "fractional hypertree width" introduced in [16]) for sets of hypergraphs with bounded multiple intersection has been proven in this fashion [9, 10].

文献[10]已证明：通过适当选择候选包集合$S$，可将判定超图各类分解宽度$\leq  k$的问题归约为CANDIDATETD问题。例如当$\mathrm{S}$由不超过$k + 1$规模的$V\left( H\right)$子集构成时，该问题等价于检验${tw} \leq  k$；若$\mathrm{S}$由最多$k$条$H$边的任意集合$\lambda$构成的$\cup  \lambda$形式集合组成，则对应检验${ghw} \leq  k$。[10]表明CANDIDATETD问题在多项式时间内可判定（相对于$H$与S的联合规模），因此该框架成功用于证明特定分解问题的可处理性——通过将其归约为候选包集合规模多项式有界的CANDIDATETD问题。例如对多重交集有界的超图集合，固定$k$时判定$\operatorname{ghw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$与分数超树宽度(fhw)$\left( H\right)  \leq  k$（后者引自[16]提出的概念）的可处理性即通过此方法得证[9,10]。

## 4 SOFT HYPERTREE WIDTH

## 4 软超树宽度

So far,it is not known whether there is a set of candidate bags ${\mathbf{S}}_{H,k}$ for a hypergraph $H$ such that there is a CTD for ${\mathbf{S}}_{H,k}$ if and only if ${hw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ . However,in [8],it was shown that there always exists a HD of minimal width such that all bags of nodes $c$ with parents $p$ are of the form ${B}_{c} = \left( {\cup {\lambda }_{c}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\cup {C}_{p}}\right)$ where ${C}_{p}$ is a $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$ -component of $H$ . In principle,this gives us a concrete way to enumerate a sufficient list of candidate bags. The number of all such bags is clearly polynomial in $H$ : there are at most ${\left| E\left( H\right) \right| }^{k + 1}$ sets of at most $k$ edges,and each of them cannot split $H$ into more than $\left| {E\left( H\right) }\right|$ components. The only point that is unclear when enumerating such a list of candidate bags,is how to decide beforehand whether two sets of edges ${\lambda }_{c},{\lambda }_{p}$ are in a parent/child relationship (and if so, which role they take). However, we observe that the parent/child roles are irrelevant for the polynomial bound on the number of candidate bags. Hence, we may drop this restriction and instead simply consider all such combinations induced by any two sets of at most $k$ edges. Concretely, this leads us to the following definitions.

目前尚不清楚是否存在一组针对超图$H$的候选包${\mathbf{S}}_{H,k}$，使得当且仅当${hw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$时存在${\mathbf{S}}_{H,k}$的CTD。然而文献[8]证明，总存在一个最小宽度的HD，其中所有具有父节点$p$的节点包$c$都呈现${B}_{c} = \left( {\cup {\lambda }_{c}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\cup {C}_{p}}\right)$形式，此处${C}_{p}$是$H$的$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$分量。理论上，这为枚举候选包提供了具体方法。此类包的数量在$H$中显然是多项式级别：最多存在${\left| E\left( H\right) \right| }^{k + 1}$组不超过$k$条边的集合，且每组边最多将$H$分割为$\left| {E\left( H\right) }\right|$个分量。唯一未明确的是枚举候选包时，如何预先判定两组边${\lambda }_{c},{\lambda }_{p}$是否构成父子关系（若是，则确定其角色）。但我们注意到父子角色对候选包数量的多项式约束并无影响，因此可放宽该限制，直接考虑所有由不超过$k$条边的任意两组边诱导的组合。具体而言，这引出了以下定义。

$$
B = \left( {\bigcup {\lambda }_{1}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\bigcup C}\right)  \tag{1}

This measure naturally generalises the notion of hypertree width in a way that remains tractable to check (for fixed $k$ ),but removes the need for the special condition.

THEOREM 1. Let $k \geq  1$ . Deciding,for given hypergraph $H$ ,whether $\operatorname{shw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ holds,is feasible in polynomial time in the size of $H$ . The problem even lies in the highly parallelisable class $\log {CFL}$ .

定理1。设$k \geq  1$。对于给定超图$H$，判定$\operatorname{shw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$是否成立的问题，在$H$规模的多项式时间内可解。该问题甚至属于高度可并行化的$\log {CFL}$类。

Proof Sketch. In [12],it is shown that checking if ${hw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ holds for fixed $k \geq  1$ is in LogCFL. The key part of the proof is the construction of an alternating Turing machine (ATM) that runs in Logspace and Ptime. The ATM constructs an HD in a top-down fashion. In the existential steps, one guesses a $\lambda$ -label of the next node in the HD. Given the label ${\lambda }_{c}$ of the current node and the label ${\lambda }_{p}$ of its parent node,one can compute the set of $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{c}\right\rbrack$ -components that lie inside a $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$ -component. In the following universal step,the ATM has to check recursively if all these ${\lambda }_{c}$ -components admit an HD of width $\leq  k$ .

证明概要。文献[12]指出对于固定$k \geq  1$，检验${hw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$是否成立属于LogCFL类。证明关键在于构建一个在对数空间和多项式时间运行的交替图灵机(ATM)。该ATM以自上而下方式构建HD：在存在性步骤中猜测HD下一节点的$\lambda$标签；根据当前节点标签${\lambda }_{c}$及其父节点标签${\lambda }_{p}$，可计算位于$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$分量内的$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{c}\right\rbrack$分量集合；在后续全称步骤中，ATM需递归验证所有这些${\lambda }_{c}$分量是否允许宽度为$\leq  k$的HD。

This ATM can be easily adapted to an ATM for checking if $\operatorname{shw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ holds. The main difference is,that rather than guessing the label ${\lambda }_{c}$ of the current node (i.e.,a collection of at most $k$ edges), we now simply guess an element from ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$ as the bag ${B}_{c}$ of the current node. The universal step is then again a recursive check for all components of ${B}_{c}$ that lie inside a $\left\lbrack  {B}_{p}\right\rbrack$ -component,if they admit a candidate TD of width $\leq  k$ . The crucial observation is that we only need Logspace to represent the bags $B \in  \operatorname{shw}\left( H\right)$ ,because every such bag is uniquely determined by the label ${\lambda }_{c}$ and a $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$ -component. Clearly, ${\lambda }_{c}$ can be represented by up to $k$ (pointers to) edges in $E\left( H\right)$ ,and a $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$ -component can be represented by up to $k + 1$ (pointers to) edges in $E\left( H\right)$ ,i.e.,up to $k$ edges in ${\lambda }_{p}$ plus 1 edge from the $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$ -component. Clearly,the latter uniquely identifies a component,since no edge can be contained in 2 components.

该自动柜员机(ATM)可轻松适配为用于验证$\operatorname{shw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$是否成立的ATM。主要差异在于：我们不再猜测当前节点的标签${\lambda }_{c}$(即最多包含$k$条边的集合)，而是直接从${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$中选取元素作为当前节点的包${B}_{c}$。随后，通用步骤将递归检查${B}_{c}$中位于$\left\lbrack  {B}_{p}\right\rbrack$组件内的所有子组件，验证它们是否允许存在宽度为$\leq  k$的候选树分解(TD)。关键发现是，我们仅需对数空间(Logspace)来表示包$B \in  \operatorname{shw}\left( H\right)$，因为每个包都由标签${\lambda }_{c}$和$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$组件唯一确定。显然，${\lambda }_{c}$可通过$E\left( H\right)$中最多$k$条边(的指针)表示，而$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$组件可通过$E\left( H\right)$中最多$k + 1$条边(的指针)表示，即${\lambda }_{p}$中最多$k$条边加上来自$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{p}\right\rbrack$组件的1条边。显然，后者能唯一标识组件，因为任何边都不可能同时属于两个组件。

The main argument in the proof of Theorem 1 was that the ATM for checking ${hw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ can be easily adapted to an algorithm for checking $\operatorname{shw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ . Actually,the straightforward adaptation of existing ${hw}$ -algorithms to ${shw}$ -algorithms is by no means restricted to the rather theoretical ATM of [12]. Also the det-k-dedcomp algorithm presented in [15] and the new-det-k-decomp algorithm used in [6] for creating the HyperBench benchmark are readily turned into an shw-algorithm. We just need to replace the search for $\lambda$ -labels of edges from $E\left( H\right)$ by a search for bags from Sof ${t}_{H,k}$ . Note that the parallel algorithm log-k-decomp from [8] (which "guesses" pairs of $\lambda$ -labels for parent and child nodes and has to keep track of the orientation of each subtree of the HD to be constructed) becomes unnecessarily complex for shw-computation, where we do not need to check the special condition. Hence, we may ignore the parent/child-relationships and, in particular, the orientation of subtrees. Instead, we may follow the philosophy of the much simpler BalancedGo algorithm in [14] for ghw-computation. That is, rather than putting all bags according to Definition 2 into ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$ ,we first only put balanced separators (i.e., ${\lambda }_{1}$ -labels that give rise to components containing at most half of the original edges) there. Then we actually compute the components and recursively determine balanced separators for the resulting "subproblems", etc. As with BalancedGo, we thus end up with a recursive procedure whose recursion depth is logarithmically bounded. In Section 6, we will extend the CTD-framework by constraints and preferences. It will turn out that we can thus also capture the opt-k-decomp approach from [20], that integrates a cost function into the computation of HDs. To conclude, the key techniques for modern decomposition algorithms are also applicable to shw-computation. Additionally, just as with other popular hypergraph width measures,it is possible to relate shw to a (extended) variant of the Robber and Marshals game, which we present in Appendix A.1.

定理1证明的核心论点是：验证${hw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$的ATM可轻松适配为验证$\operatorname{shw}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$的算法。实际上，将现有${hw}$算法直接适配为${shw}$算法的方法，绝不限于文献[12]中理论化的ATM。文献[15]提出的det-k-dedcomp算法，以及文献[6]中用于构建HyperBench基准的新det-k-decomp算法，都能直接转化为shw算法。我们只需将搜索$E\left( H\right)$中边的$\lambda$标签，替换为搜索${t}_{H,k}$集合中的包。值得注意的是，文献[8]的并行算法log-k-decomp(需"猜测"父子节点的$\lambda$标签对，并跟踪待构建超树分解(HD)中各子树的朝向)在shw计算中会变得不必要的复杂，因为我们无需检查特殊条件。因此，我们可以忽略父子关系及子树的朝向，转而采用文献[14]中更简单的BalancedGo算法思想来处理ghw计算。即不按定义2将所有包放入${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$，而是先仅放入平衡分隔符(即产生包含最多半数原始边的组件的${\lambda }_{1}$标签)，随后实际计算组件并递归确定这些"子问题"的平衡分隔符等。如BalancedGo所示，最终得到的递归过程其深度呈对数级受限。第6节我们将通过约束和偏好扩展CTD框架，由此也可涵盖文献[20]将成本函数集成到HD计算的opt-k-decomp方法。总之，现代分解算法的关键技术同样适用于shw计算。此外，与其他流行的超图宽度度量类似，shw也可关联到强盗与元帅游戏的(扩展)变体，详见附录A.1。

## 5 EVEN SOFTER HYPERTREE WIDTH

## 5 更宽松的超树宽度

In Definition 2,we have introduced a formalism to define a collection ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$ of bags that one could use in TDs towards the definition of a soft hypertree width of hypergraph $H$ . We now show how this process could be iterated in a natural way to get "softer and softer" notions of hypertree width. The key idea is to extend,for a given hypergraph $H$ ,the set $E\left( H\right)$ of edges by subedges that one could possibly use in a $\lambda$ -label of a soft HD to produce the desired $\chi$ -labels as $\chi  =  \cup  \lambda$ . It will turn out that we thus get a kind of interpolation between ${shw}$ and ${ghw}$ .

在定义2中，我们引入了一种形式化方法来定义包集合${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$，这些包可用于树分解（TDs）中以定义超图$H$的软超树宽度。现在我们将展示如何以自然方式迭代这个过程，从而获得"越来越软"的超树宽度概念。关键思想是对于给定超图$H$，通过可能在软超树分解（HD）的$\lambda$标签中使用的子边来扩展边集$E\left( H\right)$，以生成所需的$\chi$标签作为$\chi  =  \cup  \lambda$。这将导致我们在${shw}$和${ghw}$之间获得某种插值。

We first introduce the following useful notation:

我们首先引入以下实用符号：

DEFINITION 4. Let $A,B$ be two sets of sets. We write $A \boxtimes  B$ as shorthand for the set of all pairwise intersections of sets from $A$ with sets from $B$ ,i.e.:

定义4. 设$A,B$为两个集合的集合。我们用$A \boxtimes  B$表示来自$A$的集合与来自$B$的集合所有成对交集的简写，即：

$$
A \boxtimes  B \mathrel{\text{:=}} \{ a \cap  b \mid  a \in  A,b \in  B\} 
$$

We now define an iterative process of obtaining sets ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ of subedges of the edges in $E\left( H\right)$ and the corresponding collection Soft ${}_{H,k}^{i}$ of bags in TDs that one can construct with these subedges.

我们现在定义一个迭代过程来获取$E\left( H\right)$边集中子边的集合${E}^{\left( i\right) }$，以及可在树分解中用这些子边构造的对应软包集合Soft${}_{H,k}^{i}$。

DEFINITION 5. Consider a hypergraph $H$ . For $i > 0$ ,we set ${E}^{\left( i\right) } = {E}^{\left( i - 1\right) } \boxtimes  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i - 1}$ and we define ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ as the set that contains all sets of the form

定义5. 考虑一个超图$H$。对于$i \geq  0$，我们如下定义集合${E}^{\left( i\right) }$和${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$：当$i = 0$时，我们设定${E}^{\left( 0\right) } = E\left( H\right)$和${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{0} = {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$如定义2所述；当$i > 0$时，我们设定${E}^{\left( i\right) } = {E}^{\left( i - 1\right) } \boxtimes  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i - 1}$，并将${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$定义为包含所有形如下式的集合：

$$
B = \left( {\bigcup {\lambda }_{1}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\bigcup C}\right) 
$$

where $C$ is a $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$ -component of $H,{\lambda }_{1}$ is a set of at most $k$ elements of ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ ,and ${\lambda }_{2}$ is a set of at most $k$ elements of $E\left( H\right)$ .

其中$C$是$H,{\lambda }_{1}$的一个$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$分量，${E}^{\left( i\right) }$是至多$k$个元素的集合，$E\left( H\right)$是至多$k$个元素的集合。

We can then extend Definition 3 as follows: A soft hypertree decomposition of order $i$ of width $k$ of $H$ is a candidate tree decomposition for ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ . Moreover,the soft hypertree width of order $i$ of $H$ (denoted shw ${}^{i}\left( H\right)$ ) is the minimal $k$ ,such that there exists a soft hypertree decomposition of order $i$ of width $k$ of $H$ .

我们可以如下扩展定义3：一个宽度为$k$的$i$阶软超树分解是对${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$的候选树分解。此外，$H$的$i$阶软超树宽度（记作shw${}^{i}\left( H\right)$）是存在宽度为$k$的$i$阶软超树分解的最小$k$值。

Intuitively, ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ represents all the "interesting" subedges of the edges in $E\left( H\right)$ relative to the possible bags in ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i - 1}$ ,i.e.,every element $B \in  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i - 1}$ can be obtained as $B = {e}_{1}^{\prime } \cup  \cdots  \cup  {e}_{\ell }^{\prime }$ with $\ell  \leq  k$ and ${e}_{j}^{\prime } \in  {E}^{\left( i\right) }$ for every $j$ . In this way,we can iteratively refine the set of considered bags in a targeted way to arrive at an interpolation of ${ghw}$ . This idea will be made precise in Theorem 7 below. First,we establish certain monotonicity properties of ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ and ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ .

直观而言，${E}^{\left( i\right) }$代表了$E\left( H\right)$中相对于${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i - 1}$内可能存在的所有"有趣"子边，即每个元素$B \in  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i - 1}$都可表示为$B = {e}_{1}^{\prime } \cup  \cdots  \cup  {e}_{\ell }^{\prime }$，其中$\ell  \leq  k$且${e}_{j}^{\prime } \in  {E}^{\left( i\right) }$对于每个$j$成立。通过这种方式，我们可以有针对性地迭代优化所考虑的集合，最终得到${ghw}$的插值。这一思想将在下文定理7中精确表述。首先，我们确立${E}^{\left( i\right) }$与${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$的某些单调性性质。

LEMMA 3. Let $H$ be a hypergraph and $i \geq  0$ . Moreover,let ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ and ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ be defined according to Definition 5. Then the following subset relationships hold:

引理3. 设$H$为超图且$i \geq  0$。另设${E}^{\left( i\right) }$与${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$根据定义5确定。则下列子集关系成立：

$$
{E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) },\;{E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i},\;\text{ and }\;{\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i} \subseteq  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}
$$

Proof. We first prove ${E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H.k}^{i}$ . Indeed,let ${e}^{\prime }$ be an arbitrary edge in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ . Now pick ${\lambda }_{1} = \left\{  {e}^{\prime }\right\}$ and ${\lambda }_{2} = \varnothing$ . Hence,in particular, $C = E\left( H\right)$ itself is the only $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$ -component in $E\left( H\right)$ . We thus get ${e}^{\prime } = B$ with $B = \left( {\bigcup {\lambda }_{1}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\bigcup C}\right)$ and,therefore, ${e}^{\prime } \in  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ .

证明. 我们首先证明${E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H.k}^{i}$。任取${e}^{\prime }$作为${E}^{\left( i\right) }$中的任意边。现选取${\lambda }_{1} = \left\{  {e}^{\prime }\right\}$和${\lambda }_{2} = \varnothing$。因此特别地，$C = E\left( H\right)$本身是$E\left( H\right)$中唯一的$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$分量。由此可得${e}^{\prime } = B$（其中$B = \left( {\bigcup {\lambda }_{1}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\bigcup C}\right)$），故${e}^{\prime } \in  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$成立。

From this,the inclusion ${E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$ follows easily. Now let ${e}^{\prime }$ be an arbitrary edge in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ . Finally,consider an arbitrary element $B \in  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ ,i.e., $B$ is of the form $B = \left( {\bigcup {\lambda }_{1}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\bigcup C}\right)$ ,where $C$ is a $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$ -component of $H,{\lambda }_{1}$ is a set of at most $k$ elements of ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ ,and ${\lambda }_{2}$ is a set of at most $k$ elements of $E\left( H\right)$ . From ${E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$ ,it follows that ${\lambda }_{1}$ can also be considered as a set of at most $k$ elements of ${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$ . Hence, $B$ is also contained in ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$ .

最后，考虑一个任意元素$B \in  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$，即$B$的形式为$B = \left( {\bigcup {\lambda }_{1}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\bigcup C}\right)$，其中$C$是$H,{\lambda }_{1}$的一个$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$分量，$H,{\lambda }_{1}$是${E}^{\left( i\right) }$中最多$k$个元素的集合，而${\lambda }_{2}$是$E\left( H\right)$中最多$k$个元素的集合。由${E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$可知，${\lambda }_{1}$也可视为${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$中最多$k$个元素的集合。因此，$B$也包含于${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$中。

Now that we have seen that ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$ extends ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ ,a natural next question is,how expensive is it to get from ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ to ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$ . In particular,by how much does ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$ increase compared to ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ . The following lemma establishes a polynomial bound,if we consider $k$ as fixed.

既然我们已经看到${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$扩展了${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$，接下来自然要问：从${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$到${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$的代价有多高？具体而言，${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$相较于${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$增长了多少。若将$k$视为固定值，以下引理确立了一个多项式界限。

LEMMA 4. Let $H$ be a hypergraph and $i \geq  0$ and $k \geq  1$ . Then we have:

引理4. 设$H$为超图，$i \geq  0$和$k \geq  1$。则有：

$$
\left| {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}\right|  \leq  {\left| {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}\right| }^{{4k} + 6}\text{.}
$$

Proof. By Lemma 3, ${E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ holds and,by Definition 5,we have ${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) } = {E}^{\left( i\right) } \boxtimes  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ . Hence, ${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) } = {E}^{\left( i\right) } \boxtimes  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i} \subseteq  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i} \boxtimes  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ and,therefore, $\left| {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }\right|  \leq  {\left| {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}\right| }^{2}$ . According to Definition 5,Sof ${\mathrm{t}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$ contains all edges of the form $B = \left( {\bigcup {\lambda }_{1}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\bigcup C}\right)$ ,where $C$ is a $\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$ -component of $H,{\lambda }_{1}$ is a set of at most $k$ elements of ${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$ ,and ${\lambda }_{2}$ is a set of at most $k$ elements of $E\left( H\right)$ . 证明。根据引理3，${E}^{\left( i\right) } \subseteq  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$成立，且由定义5可得${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) } = {E}^{\left( i\right) } \boxtimes  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$。因此，${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) } = {E}^{\left( i\right) } \boxtimes  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i} \subseteq  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i} \boxtimes  {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$，进而$\left| {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }\right|  \leq  {\left| {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}\right| }^{2}$。根据定义5，${\mathrm{t}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$包含所有形如$B = \left( {\bigcup {\lambda }_{1}}\right)  \cap  \left( {\bigcup C}\right)$的边，其中$C$是$H,{\lambda }_{1}$的一个$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$分量，${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$中至多包含$k$个元素，$E\left( H\right)$中至多包含$k$个元素。故对于${\lambda }_{1}$至多有${\left| {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }\right| }^{k + 1}$种可能选择，对于${\lambda }_{2}$至多有${\left| E\left( H\right) \right| }^{k + 1}$种可能选择，且给定${\lambda }_{2}$时$\left\lbrack  {\lambda }_{2}\right\rbrack$分量的选择至多有$\left| {E\left( H\right) }\right|$种。综上，利用${E}^{\left( i\right) }$的单调性及关系式$E\left( H\right)  \subseteq  {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$，可得$\left| {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}\right|  \leq  {\left| {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }\right| }^{{2k} + 3}$。结合$\left| {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }\right|  \leq  {\left| {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}\right| }^{2}$，即得所求不等式$\left| {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}\right|  \leq  {\left( \left| {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}\right| \right) }^{{4k} + 6}$。

The following result on the complexity of recognizing low ${sh}{w}^{i}\left( H\right)$ is an immediate consequence.

关于识别低${sh}{w}^{i}\left( H\right)$复杂度的下述结果是直接推论。

THEOREM 5. Let $i$ and $k$ be fixed positive integers. Then,given a hypergraph $H$ ,it can be decided in polynomial time,if ${\operatorname{shw}}^{i}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ holds.

定理5。设$i$和$k$为固定正整数。则对于给定超图$H$，可在多项式时间内判定${\operatorname{shw}}^{i}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$是否成立。

Proof. It follows from Lemma 4,that we can compute ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ in polynomial time (for fixed $i,k$ ). Hence,by the tractability of the CANDIDATETD problem recalled in Section 3,deciding ${sh}{w}^{i}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$ is then also feasible in polynomial time.

证明。由引理4可知，对于固定$i,k$，可在多项式时间内计算${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$。因此，根据第3节所述CANDIDATETD问题的可处理性，判定${sh}{w}^{i}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$在多项式时间内亦可实现。

最终，我们证明${sh}{w}^{i}\left( H\right)$收敛于${ghw}\left( H\right)$。为此，首先证明给定超图$H,{\left( {\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}\right) }_{i \geq  0}$（因而${\left( sh{w}^{i}\left( H\right) \right) }_{i \geq  0}$）会收敛到一个不动点。第二步再证明对${sh}{w}^{i}\left( H\right)$而言，该不动点实际就是${ghw}\left( H\right)$。

## 6 CONSTRAINED HYPERTREE DECOMPOSITIONS

## 6 约束超树分解

Although hypertree decompositions and their generalisations have long been a central tool in identifying the asymptotic worst-case complexity of CQ evaluation, practical applications often demand more than simply a decomposition of low width. While width is the only relevant factor for the typically considered complexity upper bounds, structural properties of the decomposition can critically influence computational efficiency in practice.

尽管超树分解及其推广长期作为判定CQ评估渐近最坏情况复杂度的核心工具，实际应用往往需要超越单纯的低宽度分解。虽然宽度是通常考虑复杂度上界的唯一相关因素，但分解的结构特性会显著影响实际计算效率。

This query has multiple HDs of minimal width, but many of them are highly problematic for practical query evaluation. Some example computations resulting from various HDs of minimal width are illustrated below in (b)-(d).

Yannakakis’ algorithm for decompositions ${D}_{1}$ and ${D}_{3}$ requires the computation of a Cartesian product (by covering $w,x,y,z$ with two disjoint edges) of size $\left| T\right|  \cdot  \left| R\right|$ and $\left| S\right|  \cdot  \left| U\right|$ ,respectively. Yannakakis算法对分解${D}_{1}$和${D}_{3}$需要计算规模分别为$\left| T\right|  \cdot  \left| R\right|$和$\left| S\right|  \cdot  \left| U\right|$的笛卡尔积（通过用两条不相交边覆盖$w,x,y,z$）。在实际常见情况下，计算连接$S \ltimes  T$和$R \ltimes  U$的效率要高得多。

This issue is one of the simplest cases that highlights the necessity of imposing additional structural constraints on decompositions. By integrating such constraints, we can align decompositions more closely with practical considerations beyond worst-case complexity guarantees. We thus initiate the study of constrained decompositions. Specifically,for a constraint $\mathcal{C}$ and width measure width,we study $\mathcal{C}$ -width(H),the least width over all decompositions that satisfy the constraint $\mathcal{C}$ . In the first place,we thus study constrained shw. But we emphasise that the results in this section apply to any notion of decomposition and width that can be computed via CTDs.

此问题是最简单的案例之一，凸显了对分解施加额外结构约束的必要性。通过整合此类约束，我们能使分解更贴近超越最坏情况复杂度保证的实际需求。由此我们开创了约束分解研究领域。具体而言，对于约束条件$\mathcal{C}$和宽度度量width，我们研究$\mathcal{C}$-width(H)——即所有满足约束$\mathcal{C}$的分解中的最小宽度。我们首先研究约束shw，但强调本节结果适用于任何可通过CTDs计算的分解与宽度概念。

To guide the following presentation, we first identify various interesting examples of constraints for a TD(T,B),that we believe might find use in applications:

为引导后续阐述，我们首先列举TD(T,B)约束的若干有趣实例，这些约束有望在实际应用中使用：

Connected covers As motivated in Example 2, applications for query evaluation would want to avoid Cartesian products in the reduction to an acyclic query. This motivates constraint ConCov,that holds exactly for those CTDs where every bag ${B}_{u}$ has an edge cover ${\lambda }_{u}$ of size $\left| {\lambda }_{u}\right|  \leq  k$ ,such that the edges in ${\lambda }_{u}$ form a connected subhypergraph.

连通覆盖 如示例2所述，查询评估应用需要避免在归约为无环查询时产生笛卡尔积。这催生了ConCov约束，该约束严格适用于那些每个包${B}_{u}$都具有大小为$\left| {\lambda }_{u}\right|  \leq  k$的边覆盖${\lambda }_{u}$、且${\lambda }_{u}$中边形成连通子超图的CTD。

Shallow Cyclicity The constraint ShallowCyc ${}_{d}$ is satisfied by a CTD,if for every node $u$ at depth greater than $d$ ,the bag ${B}_{u}$ can be covered by a single edge of the hypergraph. In other words,if a CQ is cyclic,we can’t avoid bags that require an edge cover of size $\geq  2$ ,but we want to restrict the occurrence of such bags to a small upper part of the decomposition.

浅层循环性 若一个CTD满足ShallowCyc${}_{d}$约束，则对于深度超过$d$的每个节点$u$，其对应的包${B}_{u}$都能被超图的单条边覆盖。换言之，当CQ存在循环时，我们无法避免需要$\geq  2$规模边覆盖的包，但希望将此类包的出现限制在分解结构的上部小范围内。

Partition Clustering In distributed scenarios, where relations are partitioned across the network, query evaluation using a decomposition would benefit substantially from being able to evaluate entire subtrees of the CTD at a single partition. We can enforce this through a constraint of the following form: Let $\rho  : E\left( H\right)  \rightarrow$ Partitions label each edge of the hypergraph with a partition. The constraint PartClust holds in a CTD(T,B),if there exists a function $f : V\left( T\right)  \rightarrow$ Partitions such,that for every node $u$ in $T$ ,we have:

分区聚类 在关系表分散于网络的分布式场景中，若能在一个分区内评估CTD的完整子树，将极大提升基于分解的查询评估效率。可通过以下形式的约束实现：令$\rho  : E\left( H\right)  \rightarrow$Partitions为超图每条边标记分区号。当存在映射函数$f : V\left( T\right)  \rightarrow$Partitions使得对于$T$中的每个节点$u$都满足下列条件时，CTD(T,B)即满足PartClust约束：

(1) ${B}_{u}$ has a candidate cover using edges with label $f\left( u\right)$ .

(1) ${B}_{u}$存在使用$f\left( u\right)$标记边的候选覆盖

(2) For every $p \in$ Partitions,the set of nodes $u$ with $f\left( u\right)  = p$ induces a subtree of $T$ .

(2) 对于每个$p \in$Partitions，满足$f\left( u\right)  = p$的节点集$u$在$T$中形成子树

Introducing such constraints can,of course,increase the width. For example,for the 5-cycle ${C}_{5}$ , it is easy to verify that $\operatorname{ConCov} - \operatorname{ghw}\left( {C}_{5}\right)  = \operatorname{ConCov} - \operatorname{shw}\left( {C}_{5}\right)  = \operatorname{ConCov} - \operatorname{hw}\left( {C}_{5}\right)  = 3$ even though ${hw}\left( {C}_{5}\right)  = 2$ . However,in practice,adherence to such constraints can still be beneficial. In the ${C}_{5}$ example, the decomposition of width 2 forces a Cartesian product in the evaluation and is likely to be infeasible even on moderately sized data. Yet using a ConCov decomposition of width 3 might even outperform a typical query plan of two-way joins executed by a standard relational DBMS.

引入此类约束当然可能增加宽度。例如对于5环${C}_{5}$，容易验证$\operatorname{ConCov} - \operatorname{ghw}\left( {C}_{5}\right)  = \operatorname{ConCov} - \operatorname{shw}\left( {C}_{5}\right)  = \operatorname{ConCov} - \operatorname{hw}\left( {C}_{5}\right)  = 3$即使${hw}\left( {C}_{5}\right)  = 2$。但实际应用中遵守这类约束仍具优势。以${C}_{5}$为例，宽度为2的分解会强制产生笛卡尔积，中等规模数据即可能无法执行，而采用宽度3的ConCov分解甚至可能优于标准关系型DBMS执行双向连接的传统查询计划。

### 6.1 Tractable Constrained Decompositions

### 6.1 可处理的约束分解

Here, we are interested in the question of when it is tractable to find decompositions satisfying certain constraints. The bottom-up process of Algorithm 1 iteratively builds tree-decompositions for some induced subhypergraph of $H$ (recall that a satisfied block(B,C)corresponds to a TD for $H\left\lbrack  {B \cup  C}\right\rbrack  )$ . We will refer to such tree decompositions as partial tree decompositions of $H$ .

本节探讨在何种条件下可高效找到满足特定约束的分解。算法1的自底向上过程会迭代构建$H$某些诱导子超图的树分解（注意满足的块(B,C)对应$H\left\lbrack  {B \cup  C}\right\rbrack  )$的TD）。我们将此类树分解称为$H$的部分树分解。

A subtree constraint $\mathcal{C}$ (or,simply,a constraint) is a Boolean property of partial tree decompositions a given hypergraph $H$ . We write $\mathcal{T} \vDash  \mathcal{C}$ to say that the constraint holds true on the partial tree decomposition $\mathcal{T}$ . A tree decomposition(T,B)of $H$ satisfies $\mathcal{C}$ if ${T}_{u} \vDash  \mathcal{C}$ for every node the partial tree decomposition induced by ${T}_{u}$ .

子树约束$\mathcal{C}$（简称约束）是给定超图$H$的部分树分解的布尔属性。记作$\mathcal{T} \vDash  \mathcal{C}$表示该约束在部分树分解$\mathcal{T}$上成立。当${T}_{u}$诱导的部分树分解对每个节点都满足${T}_{u} \vDash  \mathcal{C}$时，$H$的树分解(T,B)即满足$\mathcal{C}$约束。

However,deciding the existence of a tree decomposition satisfying $\mathcal{C}$ might require the prohibitively expensive enumeration of all possible decompositions for a given set of candidate bags. To establish tractability for a large number of constraints, we additionally consider total orderings of partial tree decompositions ${\left( \text{toptds}\right) }^{1} \leq$ . A tree decomposition(T,B)is minimal w.r.t. $\leq$ ,if for every node $u$ ,there is no partial tree decomposition $\left( {{T}_{u}^{\prime },{B}^{\prime }}\right)$ of $H\left\lbrack  {B\left( {T}_{u}\right) }\right\rbrack$ with $\left( {{T}_{u}^{\prime },{B}^{\prime }}\right)  < \left( {{T}_{u},B}\right)$ .

然而，判定是否存在满足$\mathcal{C}$的树分解可能需要枚举给定候选包集合的所有可能分解，这种计算代价令人望而却步。为建立大量约束条件下的可处理性，我们额外考虑偏序树分解的全序关系${\left( \text{toptds}\right) }^{1} \leq$。当树分解(T,B)对于$\leq$是最小化的，意味着对于每个节点$u$，都不存在$H\left\lbrack  {B\left( {T}_{u}\right) }\right\rbrack$的偏序树分解$\left( {{T}_{u}^{\prime },{B}^{\prime }}\right)$满足$\left( {{T}_{u}^{\prime },{B}^{\prime }}\right)  < \left( {{T}_{u},B}\right)$。

An appropriate combination of subtree constraints $\mathcal{C}$ and toptds can simplify the search for decompositions that satisfy $\mathcal{C}$ . Formally,we say that a subtree constraint $\mathcal{C}$ is preference complete if there exists a toptd ${ \leq  }_{\mathcal{C}}$ such that: if there exists a TD for which $\mathcal{C}$ holds,then $\mathcal{C}$ holds for all minimal (w.r.t. ${ \leq  }_{\mathcal{C}}$ ) TDs. Subtree constraints - even with the additional condition of preference completeness - still include a wide range of constraints. For example, all three example constraints mentioned above are preference complete. Informally, for shallow cyclicity, those partial TDs that become acyclic from lower depth are preferred. For partition clustering, we prefer the root node of the partial TD to be in the same partition as one of the children over introducing a new partition.

子树约束$\mathcal{C}$与toptds的适当组合能简化满足$\mathcal{C}$的分解搜索。形式化地说，若存在toptd${ \leq  }_{\mathcal{C}}$使得：当存在满足$\mathcal{C}$的TD时，则该约束对所有${ \leq  }_{\mathcal{C}}$最小化TD均成立，此时称子树约束$\mathcal{C}$具有偏好完备性。即使附加偏好完备性条件，子树约束仍涵盖广泛类型。例如前文提及的三个约束均具偏好完备性。非形式化地说，对于浅层环性，我们倾向于选择从较低深度开始无环的偏序TD；对于分区聚类，则更偏好偏序TD根节点与某子节点同属一个分区，而非引入新分区。

However, efficient search for constrained decompositions is not the only use of toptds. Using the same algorithmic framework, one can see them as a way to order decompositions by preference as long as the respective toptds $\leq$ is monotone: that is,a partial tree decomposition(T,B)is minimal only if for each child $c$ of the root, $\left( {{T}_{c},B}\right)$ is minimal. A typical example for monotone toptds are cost functions for the estimated cost to evaluate a database query corresponding to the respective subtree. The cost of solving a query with a tree decomposition is made up of the costs for the individual subqueries of the child subtrees, plus some estimated cost of combining them, monotonicity is a natural simplifying assumption for this setting. For instance, consider an ordering of partial subtrees ${ \leq  }_{\text{cost }}$ that orders partial decompositions by such a cost estimate. In combination with the constraint ConCov, $\left( {\text{ ConCov,}{ \leq  }_{\text{cost }}}\right)$ forms a preference complete subtree constraint,such that a satisfying TD is a minimal cost tree decomposition where every bag has a connected edge cover of size at most $k$ . Thus,optimisation of tree decompositions for monotone toptds is simply a special case of preference complete subtree constraints.

然而，toptds的作用不仅限于约束分解的高效搜索。在相同算法框架下，只要对应toptd$\leq$具有单调性——即偏序树分解(T,B)最小化当且仅当其根节点的每个子节点$c$满足$\left( {{T}_{c},B}\right)$最小化——即可将其视为按偏好排序分解的方法。单调toptd的典型实例是评估数据库子查询的预估成本函数，其总成本由子树的各子查询成本加上组合成本构成，单调性在此情境下是自然的简化假设。例如考虑按此成本估计排序偏序子树的${ \leq  }_{\text{cost }}$，与ConCov约束组合时，$\left( {\text{ ConCov,}{ \leq  }_{\text{cost }}}\right)$构成偏好完备的子树约束，使得满足条件的TD成为边覆盖规模不超过$k$的最小成本树分解。因此，单调toptd的树分解优化仅是偏好完备子树约束的特例。

Our main goal in introducing the above concepts is the general study of the complexity of incorporating constraints into the computation of CTDs. We define the $\left( {\mathcal{C}, \leq  }\right)$ -CANDIDATETD problem as the problem of deciding for given hypergraph $H$ and set $S$ of candidate bags whether there exists a CompNF CTD that satisfies $\mathcal{C}$ and is minimal for $\leq$ . We want to identify tractable fragments of $\left( {\mathcal{C},{ \leq  }_{\mathcal{C}}}\right)$ -CANDIDATETD. Let us call a constraint and toptds tractable if one can decide in polynomial time w.r.t. the size of the original hypergraph $H$ and the set $S$ of candidate bags both, whether $\mathcal{C}$ holds for a partial tree decomposition of $H$ ,and whether $\left( {{T}^{\prime },{B}^{\prime }}\right) { < }_{\mathcal{C}}\left( {T,B}\right)$ holds for partial tree decompositions of $H$ .

引入上述概念的主要目的是系统研究在计算CTD时融入约束的复杂度问题。我们将$\left( {\mathcal{C}, \leq  }\right)$-候选树分解问题定义为：给定超图$H$和候选包集合$S$，判定是否存在满足$\mathcal{C}$且对$\leq$极小的CompNF树分解。我们致力于识别$\left( {\mathcal{C},{ \leq  }_{\mathcal{C}}}\right)$-候选树分解的可处理片段。若能在多项式时间内（相对于原超图$H$和候选包集$S$的规模）同时判定约束条件$\mathcal{C}$对$H$的部分树分解是否成立，以及$\left( {{T}^{\prime },{B}^{\prime }}\right) { < }_{\mathcal{C}}\left( {T,B}\right)$对$H$的部分树分解是否成立，则称该约束与最优树分解具有可处理性。

THEOREM 10. Let $\mathcal{C}, \leq$ be a tractable constraint and toptds. Then $\left( {\mathcal{C}, \leq  }\right)$ -CANDIDATETD $\in$ PTIME.

定理10. 设$\mathcal{C}, \leq$为可处理约束及最优树分解，则$\left( {\mathcal{C}, \leq  }\right)$-候选树分解问题属于$\in$多项式时间可解类。

For a block(B,C)with basis $X$ in Algorithm 2,the basis property of every block induces a (unique) tree decomposition for $B \cup  C$ ,denoted as $\operatorname{Decomp}\left( {B,C,X}\right)$ . Where $\operatorname{Algorithm}1$ used dynamic programming to simply check for a possible way to satisfy the root block $\left( {\varnothing ,V\left( H\right) }\right)$ . It is straightforward to verify the correctness of Algorithm 2. Our analysis here brings us back to one of the initially raised benefits of soft hypertree width: algorithmic flexibility. Our analysis, and Theorem 10 in particular, applies to any setting where a width measure can be effectively expressed in terms of CompNF candidate tree decompositions.

推论11. 设$\mathcal{C}$为可处理且偏好完备的子树约束，$k,i$为非负整数，则判定$\mathcal{C} - {\operatorname{shw}}^{i}\left( H\right)  \leq  k$属于多项式时间可解问题。

### 6.2 Constraints in Other Approaches for Computing Decompositions

### 6.2 其他分解计算方法的约束机制

Bag-level constraints such as ConCov are simple to enforce with existing combinatorial algorithms such as det-k-decomp [15] and new-det-k-decomp [6]. These algorithms try to construct an HD in a top-down manner by combining up to $k$ edges into $\lambda$ -labels. Enforcing the ConCov is trivial in this case. However, with more global constraints such as PartClust, it is unclear how to adapt these algorithms while retaining tractability. Also the incorporation of a cost function is not obvious. These algorithms critically rely on caching for pairs of a bag $B$ and vertex set $C$ , whether there is an HD of $H\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack$ rooted at $B$ . With constraints that apply to a larger portion of the decomposition, the caching mechanism no longer works. Other prominent algorithms are also not suitable for this analysis: $\log  - \mathrm{k}$ -decomp [8] is the fastest current algorithm for HD computation in practice, even though in theory only quasi-polynomial. It applies a divide-and-conquer strategy that keeps splitting the hypergraph to be decomposed into smaller pieces until the base case is reached. But it never analyses larger portions of the decomposition. The HtdSMT solver by Schidler and Szeider [21, 22] searches for a decomposition of minimal width by making use of an SMT solver. However, it is unclear how to state constraints over these encodings. Furthermore, due to the reliance on SAT/SMT solvers, the method is not suited to a theoretical analysis of tractable constrained decomposition methods.

诸如ConCov（覆盖约束）这类包级约束，可通过现有组合算法（如det-k-decomp[15]和new-det-k-decomp[6]）轻松实现。这些算法采用自顶向下方式构建层次分解(HD)，通过将最多$k$条边组合成$\lambda$标签。在此情境下，实施ConCov约束是直观的。但对于PartClust（部分聚类）等全局性约束，如何在保持可处理性的前提下调整算法尚不明确，且成本函数的融入也非显而易见。这些算法核心依赖于对包$B$与顶点集$C$的缓存机制，以判断是否存在以$B$为根的$H\left\lbrack  C\right\rbrack$HD。当约束作用于分解的更大部分时，缓存机制即告失效。其他主流算法亦不适用此分析：$\log  - \mathrm{k}$-decomp[8]虽是当前实践中HD计算最快的算法（尽管理论仅为准多项式），其采用分治策略持续分割超图直至基例，但从不分析分解的宏观结构。Schidler与Szeider提出的HtdSMT求解器[21,22]利用SMT求解器搜索最小宽度分解，但难以表述此类编码约束，且依赖SAT/SMT求解器的特性使其不适于可约束分解方法的理论分析。

The algorithm that comes closest to our algorithm for constructing a constrained decomposition is the opt-k-decomp algorithm from [20]. It introduces the notion of "weighted HDs" and aims at constructing an $\mathrm{{HD}}$ of minimal cost among all $\mathrm{{HDs}}$ up to a given width. The cost of an $\mathrm{{HD}}$ is defined by a function that assigns a cost to each node and each edge in the HD. The natural cost function assigns the cost of the join computation of the relations in ${\lambda }_{u}$ to each node $u$ and the cost of the (semi-)join between the bags at a node and its parent node to the corresponding edge. We also consider this type of cost function (and the goal to minimise the cost) as an important special case of a preference relation in our framework. However, our CTD-based construction of constrained decomposition allows for a greater variety of preferences and constraints, and the simplicity of Algorithm 2 facilitates a straightforward complexity analysis. Moreover, opt-k-decomp has been specifically designed for HDs. This is in contrast to our framework, which guarantees tractability for any combination of decompositions with tractable, preference complete subtree constraints as long as the set of candidate sets is polynomially bounded. As mentioned above, this is, for instance, the case for the tractable fragments of generalised and fractional HDs identified in [9, 10].

与我们的约束分解构建算法最接近的是文献[20]提出的opt-k-decomp算法。该算法引入"加权HD"概念，旨在给定宽度范围内构建成本最低的$\mathrm{{HD}}$。其成本函数为HD中每个节点和边分配代价：节点$u$的成本源自${\lambda }_{u}$关系集合的连接运算，边的成本则取决于父子节点包间的（半）连接操作。我们将此类成本函数（及最小化成本目标）视为框架中偏好关系的重要特例。但基于CTD的约束分解构建支持更丰富的偏好与约束组合，且算法2的简洁性便于直接复杂度分析。opt-k-decomp专为HD设计，而我们的框架保证：只要候选集多项式有界，任何可处理分解与偏好完备子树约束的组合均具可处理性。如文献[9,10]所证，广义HD与分数HD的可处理片段即属此列。

## 7 EXPERIMENTS

## 7 实验研究

While the focus of this paper is primarily on the theory of tractable decompositions of hypergraphs, the ultimate motivation of this work is drawn from applications to database query evaluation. We therefore include a focused experimental analysis of the practical effect of constraints and optimisation on candidate tree decompositions. The aim of our experiments is to gain insights into the effects of constraints and costs on candidate tree decompositions in practical settings. We perform experiments with cyclic queries over standard benchmarks (TPC-DS [19], LSQB [18] as well as a queries over the Hetionet Biomedical Knowledge Graph [17] from a recent cardinality estimation benchmark [3]. In all experiments, we first compute candidate tree decompositions as in Algorithm 2 for the candidate bags as in Definition 2, i.e., we compute constrained soft hypertree decompositions. We then build on a recent line of research on rewriting the execution of Yannakakis' algorithm in standard SQL $\left\lbrack  {4,7}\right\rbrack$ ,to execute Yannakakis’ algorithm for these decompositions on standard relational DBMSs. ## 8 CONCLUSION

## 8 结论

In this work, we have introduced the concept of soft hypertree decompositions (soft HDs) and the associated measure of soft hypertree width (shw). Despite avoiding the special condition of HDs, we retained the tractability of deciding whether a given CQ has width at most $\mathrm{k}$ . At the same time, ${shw}$ is never greater than ${hw}$ and it may allow for strictly smaller widths. Most importantly,it provides more algorithmic flexibility. Building on the framework of candidate tree decompositions (CTDs), we have demonstrated how to incorporate diverse constraints and preferences into the decomposition process, enabling more specialised decompositions that take application-specific concerns beyond width into account. We have additionally performed an experimental evaluation that confirms that this approach can yield significant performance gains in practice, without introducing new computational bottlenecks. Taken together, these contributions enhance our understanding of tractable CQ evaluation and offer a more flexible and principled foundation for a wide range of future research in database query optimization and related applications.

本研究提出了软超树分解（soft HDs）概念及相应度量指标软超树宽度（shw）。尽管规避了HD的特殊条件，我们仍保留了判定给定CQ宽度是否≤$\mathrm{k}$的可追踪性。同时${shw}$始终≤${hw}$，且可能实现更小的严格宽度。最重要的是该方法提供了更强的算法灵活性。基于候选树分解（CTDs）框架，我们展示了如何将多样化约束与偏好融入分解过程，从而生成考虑应用特定需求（超越宽度指标）的专项分解方案。实验评估证实该方法能在不引入新计算瓶颈的前提下，带来显著的实践性能提升。这些成果共同深化了我们对可追踪CQ评估的理解，为数据库查询优化及相关应用的未来研究提供了更灵活、更系统化的理论基础。

On a purely theoretical level,we additionally introduce a hierarchy of refinements of ${shw}$ that still yields tractable width measures for every fixed step on the hierarchy. In particular, we show that ${ghw}$ emerges as the limit of this hierarchy. We believe that this interpolation of ${ghw}$ contributes to a better understanding of hypergraph decompositions and opens up a number of intriguing theoretical questions. A yet unexplored facet of this hierarchy is that Lemma 6 can possibly be strengthened to bound $\alpha$ more tightly with respect to structural properties of hypergraphs. ### A.1 Institutional Robber and Marshals Games

### A.1 制度性盗匪与执法官博弈

Many important hypergraph width measures admit characterisations in form of games. Robertson and Seymour characterised treewidth in terms of Robber and Cops Games as well as in terms of a version of the game that is restricted to so-called monotone moves [23]. Gottlob et al. [12] introduced Robber and Marshals Games and showed that their monotone version characterised hypertree width. Adler [1] showed that without the restriction to monotone moves, the Robber and Marshals Game provides a lower-bound for generalised hypertree width. In this section we further extend Robber and Marshals Games to Institutional Robber and Marshals Games (IRMGs) and relate soft hypertree width to IRMGs. Our presentation loosely follows the presentation of Gottlob et al. [11].

许多重要的超图宽度度量可通过博弈形式表征。Robertson与Seymour通过盗匪与警察博弈及其限制于所谓单调移动的变体来描述树宽[23]。Gottlob等人[12]提出盗匪与执法官博弈，证明其单调版本可表征超树宽度。Adler[1]指出若取消单调移动限制，该博弈能为广义超树宽度提供下界。本节我们将盗匪与执法官博弈扩展为制度性盗匪与执法官博弈（IRMGs），并将软超树宽度与之关联。论述框架大体遵循Gottlob等人[11]的表述。

For a positive integer $k$ and hypergraph $H$ ,the $k$ -Institutional Robber and Marshals Game (kIRMG) on $H$ is played by three players $\mathbf{I},\mathbf{{II}}$ ,and III. Player $\mathbf{I}$ plays $k$ administrators, $\mathbf{{II}}$ plays $k$ marshals, and III plays the robber. The administrators and marshals move on the edges of $H$ ,and together attempt to catch the robber. The robber moves on vertices of $H$ ,trying to evade the marshals. Players I and II aim to catch the robber, by having marshals on the edge that the robber moved to. However, marshals are only allowed to catch the robber when they are in an (edge) component designated by the administrators. That is, the difference to the classic Robber and Marshals Games comes from this additional interplay between administrators and marshals.

对于正整数$k$和超图$H$，其$k$-制度性盗匪与执法官博弈（kIRMG）由三位玩家$\mathbf{I},\mathbf{{II}}$参与。玩家$\mathbf{I}$操控$k$名管理员，$\mathbf{{II}}$操控$k$名执法官，III扮演盗匪。管理员与执法官在$H$的边集行动，协同追捕盗匪；盗匪则在顶点集移动以逃避追捕。玩家I和II通过使执法官占据盗匪移动目标边来实现抓捕，但执法官仅能在管理员指定的（边）组件内实施抓捕。该博弈与经典版本的核心差异即在于管理员与执法官间的这种新增互动机制。

More formally,a game position is a 4-tuple $p = \left( {A,C,M,r}\right)$ ,where $A,M \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$ are sets of at most $k$ administrators and marshals,respectively), $C$ is an $\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$ -edge component and $r \in  V\left( H\right)$ is the position of the robber. The effectively marshalled space is the set ${\eta }_{p} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\cup C}\right)  \cap  \left( {\cup M}\right)$ . We call $p$ a capture position if $r \in  {\eta }_{p}$ . If $p$ is a capture position,then it has escape space $\varnothing$ . Otherwise,the escape space of $p$ is the $\left\lbrack  {\eta }_{p}\right\rbrack$ -component that contains $r$ .

形式化定义中，博弈位置是四元组$p = \left( {A,C,M,r}\right)$，其中$A,M \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$分别表示不超过$k$名管理员与执法官的集合，$C$为$\left\lbrack  A\right\rbrack$-边组件，$r \in  V\left( H\right)$是盗匪位置。有效执法空间为集合${\eta }_{p} \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left( {\cup C}\right)  \cap  \left( {\cup M}\right)$。当$r \in  {\eta }_{p}$时，称$p$为捕获位置。若$p$为捕获位置，其逃脱空间为$\varnothing$；否则，$p$的逃脱空间是包含$r$的$\left\lbrack  {\eta }_{p}\right\rbrack$-组件。

The initial position is $\left( {\varnothing ,E\left( H\right) ,\varnothing ,v}\right)$ ,where $v$ is an arbitrary vertex. In every turn,the position $p = \left( {A,C,M,r}\right)$ is updated to a new position $\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime },{r}^{\prime }}\right)$ as follows:

初始位置为$\left( {\varnothing ,E\left( H\right) ,\varnothing ,v}\right)$，其中$v$为任意顶点。每回合中，位置$p = \left( {A,C,M,r}\right)$按以下规则更新为新位置$\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime },{r}^{\prime }}\right)$：

(1) I picks up to $k$ edges ${A}^{\prime }$ to place administrators on and designates an $\left\lbrack  {A}^{\prime }\right\rbrack$ -edge component ${C}^{\prime }$ as the administrated component.

(1) 我选取最多$k$条边${A}^{\prime }$部署管理员，并指定$\left\lbrack  {A}^{\prime }\right\rbrack$边组件${C}^{\prime }$作为受管辖组件。

(2) II picks up to $k$ edges ${M}^{\prime }$ to place marshals on.

(2) 你选取最多$k$条边${M}^{\prime }$部署元帅。

(3) III moves the robber from $r$ to any $\left\lbrack  {{\eta }_{p} \cap  \bigcup {M}^{\prime } \cap  \bigcup {C}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack$ -connected vertex ${r}^{\prime }$ .

(3) 他将盗贼从$r$移动到任意$\left\lbrack  {{\eta }_{p} \cap  \bigcup {M}^{\prime } \cap  \bigcup {C}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack$连通顶点${r}^{\prime }$。

Formally it is simpler to think of $\mathbf{I}$ and $\mathbf{{II}}$ as one player that makes both of their moves,we refer to this combined player as $\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}}$ . Players $\mathbf{I}$ and $\mathbf{{II}}$ win together when a capture position is reached (and thus also the combined player $\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}}$ wins in this situation). Player III wins the game if a capture position is never reached.

形式上更简单的做法是将$\mathbf{I}$和$\mathbf{{II}}$视为执行双方行动的单一玩家，我们称该合并玩家为$\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}}$。当达到捕获状态时，玩家$\mathbf{I}$与$\mathbf{{II}}$共同获胜（此时合并玩家$\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}}$亦获胜）。若始终未达捕获状态，则玩家III赢得游戏。

It is clear from the definition of III's possible moves that the only relevant matter with respect to whether the robber can win, is the escape space the robber is in. It will therefore be convenient to represent positions compactly simply as $\left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$ where $\varepsilon$ is an escape space. A strategy (for $\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}})$ is a function $\sigma$ that takes a compact position $p = \left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$ as input and returns new sets $\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime }}\right)$ with ${A}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime } \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$ representing the placement of the $k$ administrators and the $k$ marshals,respectively; and ${C}^{\prime }$ is an $\left\lbrack  {A}^{\prime }\right\rbrack$ -edge component.

根据III可行走法的定义可知，盗贼能否获胜的关键仅取决于其所在的逃逸空间。因此宜将局势简记为$\left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$，其中$\varepsilon$表示逃逸空间。一个（针对$\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}})$的）策略是函数$\sigma$，其输入简略局势$p = \left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$后返回新集合$\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime }}\right)$——${A}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime } \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$分别代表$k$名管理员与$k$名元帅的部署位置；而${C}^{\prime }$是$\left\lbrack  {A}^{\prime }\right\rbrack$边组件。

A game tree $T$ of strategy $\sigma$ is a rooted tree where the nodes are labelled by compact positions. The label of the root is $\left( {\varnothing ,E\left( H\right) ,\varnothing ,V\left( H\right) }\right)$ . Let $p = \left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$ be the label of a non-leaf node $n$ of $T$ . Let $\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime }}\right)  = \sigma \left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$ . For every $\left\lbrack  {\bigcup {M}^{\prime } \cap  \bigcup {C}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack$ -component ${\varepsilon }^{\prime }$ is $\left\lbrack  {\eta }_{p}\right\rbrack$ -connected to $\varepsilon ,n$ has a child with label $\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime },{\varepsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$ . If no such component exists,then $n$ has a single child with label $\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime },\varnothing }\right)$ . All nodes labelled with capture configurations are leafs. We call a strategy monotone, if in its game tree, the escape space of a node label is always a subset of the escape space of the parent label. A winning strategy (for $\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}}$ ) is a strategy for which the game tree is finite.

策略$\sigma$的游戏树$T$是节点标注简略局势的有根树，其根节点标注为$\left( {\varnothing ,E\left( H\right) ,\varnothing ,V\left( H\right) }\right)$。设$p = \left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$为$T$中非叶节点$n$的标注，令$\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime }}\right)  = \sigma \left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$。对于每个$\left\lbrack  {\bigcup {M}^{\prime } \cap  \bigcup {C}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack$组件${\varepsilon }^{\prime }$，若其与$\varepsilon ,n$保持$\left\lbrack  {\eta }_{p}\right\rbrack$连通，则生成标注为$\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime },{\varepsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$的子节点；若无此类组件，则$n$仅生成标注为$\left( {{A}^{\prime },{C}^{\prime },{M}^{\prime },\varnothing }\right)$的子节点。所有标注捕获配置的节点皆为叶节点。若某策略的游戏树中，节点标注的逃逸空间恒为父节点逃逸空间的子集，则称该策略单调。致胜策略（针对$\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}}$）指对应游戏树有限的策略。

We are now ready to define the institutional robber and marshal width $\operatorname{irmw}\left( H\right)$ of a hypergraph as the least $k$ such that there is a winning strategy in the $k$ IRMG. Analogously,the monotone institutional robber and marshal width mon-irmw(H)of a hypergraph as the least $k$ such that there is a monotone winning strategy in the $k$ IRMG.

我们现在可以定义超图的机构强盗与执法官宽度（institutional robber and marshal width）$\operatorname{irmw}\left( H\right)$为满足在$k$ IRMG中存在必胜策略的最小$k$值。类似地，超图的单调机构强盗与执法官宽度mon-irmw(H)定义为在$k$ IRMG中存在单调必胜策略的最小$k$值。

定理12. 对于任意超图$H$

Proof Sketch. Consider a CompNF tree decomposition(T,B)with bags from ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$ . Let $r$ be the root of the decomposition. We show that the decomposition induces a (monotone) winning strategy for $\mathbf{I} + \mathbf{{II}}$ in the $k$ IRMG.

In their first move,the administrators and marshals play $A,C,M$ such that $A = {\lambda }_{2},M = {\lambda }_{1},C = C$ in the expression Equation (1) that gives $B = {B}_{r}$ . That is we have ${\eta }_{p} = {B}_{r}$ at the corresponding positions $p$ . The robber will then be in some $\left\lbrack  {\eta }_{p}\right\rbrack$ -vertex component $\varepsilon$ .

在第一回合中，管理员与执法官选择$A,C,M$使得表达式(1)中给出$B = {B}_{r}$的$A = {\lambda }_{2},M = {\lambda }_{1},C = C$成立。即在对应位置$p$处有${\eta }_{p} = {B}_{r}$。此时强盗将位于某个$\left\lbrack  {\eta }_{p}\right\rbrack$-顶点分量$\varepsilon$中。

For each possible escape space $\varepsilon$ of the robber,there is a child node $c$ of the root of the tree decomposition that corresponds to a $\left\lbrack  {B}_{r}\right\rbrack$ -edge component $C$ such that $\bigcup C \supseteq  \varepsilon$ . By CompNF there is a one-to-one mapping of such components to children (in the root). Since the possible escape spaces are vertex components of the same separator, they are necessarily included in exactly one such edge component. That is the positions after the first move are of the form $p = \left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$ where ${B}_{r} = {\eta }_{p}$ and $\varepsilon$ is subset of exactly one $B\left( {T}_{c}\right)$ where $c$ is a child of $r$ .

对于强盗每个可能的逃逸空间$\varepsilon$，树分解根节点都存在对应的子节点$c$，该子节点关联着满足$\bigcup C \supseteq  \varepsilon$的$\left\lbrack  {B}_{r}\right\rbrack$-边分量$C$。根据CompNF性质，这些分量与子节点存在一一映射关系。由于逃逸空间属于同一分隔器的顶点分量，它们必然被精确包含在一个边分量中。因此首轮移动后的位置形式为$p = \left( {A,C,M,\varepsilon }\right)$，其中${B}_{r} = {\eta }_{p}$且$\varepsilon$是$r$子节点$c$对应$B\left( {T}_{c}\right)$的真子集。

In terms of strategy, the administrators and marshals then continue to play according to the corresponding child node $c$ of the position. Again choosing their positions according to the construction of the bag ${B}_{c}$ by Equation (1). Play in this form continues as above. The only additional consideration beyond the root node is,that there might be additional $\left\lbrack  {B}_{u}\right\rbrack$ -components that do not match a subtree of $u$ ,namely those that occur only above $u$ in the decomposition. However, these are not reachable from the corresponding escape space since this would require the robber to move through the interface between $u$ and its parent,which is exactly what is not allowed in the definition of III's moves.

策略层面，管理员与执法官随后根据当前位置对应的子节点$c$继续行动。再次依据表达式(1)的构造选择袋${B}_{c}$的位置。后续回合按上述模式进行。与根节点相比唯一的额外考量是：可能存在不匹配$u$子树的$\left\lbrack  {B}_{u}\right\rbrack$-分量，即那些仅出现在分解中$u$上方的分量。但由于强盗无法穿越$u$与其父节点间的接口（这正是III移动定义所禁止的），这些分量无法从当前逃逸空间到达。

It also follows that the strategy is monotone. If the escape space were to gain some vertex $v$ from some position $p$ to successor ${p}^{\prime }$ ,then this would violate the assumption of CompNF (or connectedness) on the original tree decomposition: recall from above that the escape space is the corresponding vertex component of the edge component associated with the TD node. So if $v$ is not in the escape space for $p$ ,then it is either not in the associated component ${C}_{p}$ ,or it is in ${B}_{p}$ itself. The second would mean we are actually in a capture position as ${\eta }_{p} = {B}_{p}$ (and thus have no children). Hence, $v \nmid  n{C}_{p}$ . As discussed above,the robber cannot escape this component when we play according to our strategy,and thus they can never reach $v$ .

由此可证该策略具有单调性。若逃逸空间从位置$p$到后继${p}^{\prime }$时获得了顶点$v$，这将违反原树分解的CompNF（或连通性）假设：如前所述，逃逸空间是TD节点对应边分量的顶点分量。若$v$不在$p$的逃逸空间中，则其要么不在关联分量${C}_{p}$内，要么位于${B}_{p}$自身。后者意味着实际处于捕获位置${\eta }_{p} = {B}_{p}$（因此无子节点）。故$v \nmid  n{C}_{p}$。如前述，按此策略行动时强盗无法逃离该分量，因此永远无法到达$v$。

### B.1 Proof of Lemma 6

### B.1 引理6的证明

Proof. To avoid expressions of the form $\cap   \cup$ ,we introduce the following notation: for a set $C$ of edges,we write $V\left( C\right)$ to denote the set of all vertices contained in $C$ ,i.e., $V\left( C\right)  =  \cup  C$ .

证明。为避免出现$\cap   \cup$形式的表达式，我们引入以下记号：对于边集$C$，记$V\left( C\right)$表示$C$包含的所有顶点集合，即$V\left( C\right)  =  \cup  C$。

The proof proceeds in three steps.

证明过程分为三个步骤。

Claim A. For every $i \geq  0$ ,the elements in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ can be represented in the form

断言A. 对于每个$i \geq  0$，集合${E}^{\left( i\right) }$中的元素可表示为以下形式

$$
e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1} \in  {E}_{1}}}{e}_{1} \cap  \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{C}_{1} \in  {\mathcal{C}}_{1}}}V\left( {C}_{1}\right) }\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{m} \in  {E}_{m}}}{e}_{m} \cap  \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{C}_{m} \in  {\mathcal{C}}_{m}}}V\left( {C}_{m}\right) }\right\rbrack  }\right) 
$$

for some $m \geq  0$ ,where $e \in  E\left( H\right) ,{E}_{1},\ldots ,{E}_{m} \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$ ,and $\mathcal{C}$ is a set of sets of edges,such that every element $C \in  \mathcal{C}$ is a $\left\lbrack  \lambda \right\rbrack$ -component,where $\lambda$ is a set of at most $k$ edges from $E\left( H\right)$ .

存在某个$m \geq  0$，其中$e \in  E\left( H\right) ,{E}_{1},\ldots ,{E}_{m} \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$，且$\mathcal{C}$是边集的集合，使得每个元素$C \in  \mathcal{C}$都是$\left\lbrack  \lambda \right\rbrack$分量，这里$\lambda$是来自$E\left( H\right)$的最多$k$条边的集合。

The claim is easily proved by induction on $i$ . For the induction begin,we have ${E}^{\left( 0\right) } = E\left( H\right)$ and every element $e \in  E\left( H\right)$ clearly admits such a representation,namely by setting $m = 0$ . For the induction step,we use the induction hypothesis for the elements in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ ,expand the definition of the elements in ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H.k}^{i}$ and ${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$ according to Definition 5,and then simply apply distributivity of $\cup$ and $\cap$ .

该断言通过对$i$的归纳法易证。归纳基础阶段，我们有${E}^{\left( 0\right) } = E\left( H\right)$且每个元素$e \in  E\left( H\right)$显然允许这种表示，即通过设定$m = 0$。归纳步骤中，我们对${E}^{\left( i\right) }$中的元素应用归纳假设，根据定义5展开${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H.k}^{i}$和${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$中元素的定义，然后直接应用$\cup$与$\cap$的分配律。

The above claim implicitly confirms that all elements contained in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ are either edges in $E\left( H\right)$ or subedges thereof. Let us now ignore for a while the sets $\mathcal{C}$ of sets of components in the above representation of elements in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ . The next claims essentially state that after $n$ iterations,for the sets ${E}_{j}$ in the above representation,we may use any non-empty subset $E\left( H\right)$ and,after another $n$ iterations,no new elements (assuming all sets ${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$ to be empty) can be produced.

上述断言隐式确认了${E}^{\left( i\right) }$包含的所有元素要么是$E\left( H\right)$中的边，要么是其子边。现在暂时忽略元素表示中${E}^{\left( i\right) }$的分量集$\mathcal{C}$。后续断言本质上表明：经过$n$次迭代后，对于上述表示中的集合${E}_{j}$，我们可以使用任意非空子集$E\left( H\right)$；再经过$n$次迭代后，将无法产生新元素（假设所有集合${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$为空）。

Claim B. Let $i \geq  0$ . Then ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ contains all possible vertex sets $S$ that can be represented as $S = \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}^{\prime } \in  {E}^{\prime }}}{e}^{\prime }$ for some ${E}^{\prime } \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$ with $\left| {E}^{\prime }\right|  \leq  i$ .

断言B. 设$i \geq  0$，则${E}^{\left( i\right) }$包含所有可表示为$S = \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}^{\prime } \in  {E}^{\prime }}}{e}^{\prime }$的顶点集$S$，其中${E}^{\prime } \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$满足$\left| {E}^{\prime }\right|  \leq  i$。

Again,the claim is easily proved by induction on $i$ . In the definition of ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ ,we always choose ${\lambda }_{1}$ as a singleton subset of $E\left( H\right)$ and ${\lambda }_{2} = \varnothing$ . Hence,in particular,after $n$ iterations,we have all possible sets of the form $S = \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}^{\prime } \in  {E}^{\prime }}}{e}^{\prime }$ for all possible subsets ${E}^{\prime } \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$ at our disposal. This fact will be made use of in the proof of the next claim.

同样地，该断言通过对$i$的归纳法易证。在${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$的定义中，我们总是选择${\lambda }_{1}$作为$E\left( H\right)$和${\lambda }_{2} = \varnothing$的单例子集。因此特别地，经过$n$次迭代后，我们将掌握所有形式为$S = \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}^{\prime } \in  {E}^{\prime }}}{e}^{\prime }$的可能集合，这些集合可由任意子集${E}^{\prime } \subseteq  E\left( H\right)$构成。这一事实将在下个断言的证明中被使用。

Claim C. Suppose that in the definition of ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ in Definition 5,we only allow ${\lambda }_{2} = \varnothing$ ,i.e.,the only component we can thus construct is $C = E\left( H\right)$ and,therefore, $V\left( C\right)  = V\left( H\right)$ holds. Then,for every $i \geq  {2n}$ ,we have ${E}^{\left( i\right) } = {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$ .

断言C. 假设在定义5的${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$定义中，我们仅允许${\lambda }_{2} = \varnothing$，即由此构造的唯一分量是$C = E\left( H\right)$，因而$V\left( C\right)  = V\left( H\right)$成立。那么对于每个$i \geq  {2n}$，都有${E}^{\left( i\right) } = {E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$。

To prove the claim,we inspect the above representation of elements in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ ,but assuming $V\left( C\right)  = V\left( H\right)$ for every $C$ . That is,all elements are of the form

为证明该论断，我们考察${E}^{\left( i\right) }$中元素的上述表示形式，但假设对于每个$C$都有$V\left( C\right)  = V\left( H\right)$。换言之，所有元素皆具如下形式

$$
e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1} \in  {E}_{1}}}{e}_{1}}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{m} \in  {E}_{m}}}{e}_{m}}\right\rbrack  }\right) ,
$$

for some $m \geq  0$ . By Claim B,we know that from $i \geq  n$ on,we may choose as set ${E}_{j}$ any subset of $E\left( H\right)$ . It is easy to show,by induction on $i$ ,that we can produce any element of the form $e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1} \in  {E}_{1}}}{e}_{1}}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{i} \in  {E}_{i}}}{e}_{i}}\right\rbrack  }\right)$ in ${E}^{\left( n + i\right) }$ . Then,to prove Claim C,it suffices to show that choosing $i$ bigger than $n$ does not allow us to produce new subedgdes of $e$ . To the contrary, assume that there exists ${n}^{\prime } > n$ and a set ${e}^{\prime } = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime }}}{e}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right)$ that cannot be represented as ${e}^{\prime } = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1} \in  {E}_{1}}}{e}_{1}}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{n} \in  {E}_{n}}}{e}_{n}}\right\rbrack  }\right)$ . Moreover,assume that ${n}^{\prime }$ is minimal with this property. Then we derive a contradiction by inspecting the sequence ${s}_{1} = e \cap  \left( \left\lbrack  {{\bigcap }_{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  \right)$ , ${s}_{2} = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack   \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{2}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{2}^{\prime }}}{e}_{2}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right) ,\ldots ,{s}_{{n}^{\prime }} = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime }}}{e}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right)$ . Clearly,this sequence is monotonically increasing and all elements ${s}_{j}$ are subedges of $e$ . By ${n}^{\prime } > n \geq  \left| {V\left( H\right) }\right|$ , the sequence cannot be strictly increasing. Hence,there exists $j$ ,with ${s}_{j} = {s}_{j - 1}$ . But then we can leave $\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{j}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{j}^{\prime }}}{e}_{j}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack$ out from the representation ${e}^{\prime } = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{n}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{n}^{\prime }}}{e}_{n}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right)$ and still get ${e}^{\prime }$ . This contradicts the minimality of ${n}^{\prime }$ .

对于某些$m \geq  0$，根据命题B可知，从$i \geq  n$开始，我们可以将$E\left( H\right)$的任意子集选作集合${E}_{j}$。通过对$i$进行归纳法不难证明，我们能在${E}^{\left( n + i\right) }$中生成形如$e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1} \in  {E}_{1}}}{e}_{1}}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{i} \in  {E}_{i}}}{e}_{i}}\right\rbrack  }\right)$的任何元素。为证明命题C，只需说明选择大于$n$的$i$并不会让我们生成$e$的新子边。反之，假设存在${n}^{\prime } > n$和集合${e}^{\prime } = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime }}}{e}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right)$无法表示为${e}^{\prime } = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1} \in  {E}_{1}}}{e}_{1}}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{n} \in  {E}_{n}}}{e}_{n}}\right\rbrack  }\right)$，且${n}^{\prime }$是具有该性质的最小值。通过考察序列${s}_{1} = e \cap  \left( \left\lbrack  {{\bigcap }_{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  \right)$和${s}_{2} = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack   \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{2}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{2}^{\prime }}}{e}_{2}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right) ,\ldots ,{s}_{{n}^{\prime }} = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime }}}{e}_{{n}^{\prime }}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right)$，我们导出了矛盾：显然该序列单调递增且所有元素${s}_{j}$都是$e$的子边。根据${n}^{\prime } > n \geq  \left| {V\left( H\right) }\right|$，该序列不可能严格递增。因此存在$j$使得${s}_{j} = {s}_{j - 1}$成立，此时我们可以从表示式${e}^{\prime } = e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{1}^{\prime }}}{e}_{1}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{n}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{n}^{\prime }}}{e}_{n}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack  }\right)$中移除$\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{j}^{\prime } \in  {E}_{j}^{\prime }}}{e}_{j}^{\prime }}\right\rbrack$仍能得到${e}^{\prime }$，这与${n}^{\prime }$的最小性矛盾。

Completion of the proof of the lemma. From Claim C it follows that after ${2n}$ iterations,any new elements can only be obtained via the intersections with sets of the form $\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{C \in  \mathcal{C}}}V\left( C\right)$ ,where $\mathcal{C}$ is a set of components. Recall that, according to Definition 5, components are only built w.r.t. sets ${\lambda }_{2}$ of sets of edges from $E\left( H\right)$ . Hence,all possible components $C$ are available already in the first iteration. Hence,analogously to Claim B,we can show that after $n$ successive iterations,we can choose any sets ${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$ with $\left| {\mathcal{C}}_{j}\right|  \leq  i$ for constructing elements of the form

引理证明的完成。由主张C可知，经过${2n}$次迭代后，任何新元素只能通过与形如$\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{C \in  \mathcal{C}}}V\left( C\right)$的集合相交获得，其中$\mathcal{C}$是组件集合。根据定义5，组件仅针对来自$E\left( H\right)$的边集族${\lambda }_{2}$构建。因此，所有可能的组件$C$在第一次迭代时已完备。类似于主张B，可证经过$n$次连续迭代后，能选取任意满足$\left| {\mathcal{C}}_{j}\right|  \leq  i$的集合${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$来构造形如

$$
e \cap  \left( {\left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{1} \in  {E}_{1}}}{e}_{1} \cap  \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{C}_{1} \in  {\mathcal{C}}_{1}}}V\left( {C}_{1}\right) }\right\rbrack   \cup  \cdots  \cup  \left\lbrack  {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{e}_{m} \in  {E}_{m}}}{e}_{m} \cap  \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{{C}_{m} \in  {\mathcal{C}}_{m}}}V\left( {C}_{m}\right) }\right\rbrack  }\right) ,
$$

in ${E}^{\left( 2n + i\right) }$ . In particular,in ${E}^{\left( 3n\right) }$ ,we get any element of the above form for any possible choice of ${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$ with $\left| {\mathcal{C}}_{j}\right|  \leq  n$ . Finally,analogously to Claim $\mathrm{C}$ ,we can show that taking sets ${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$ with $\left| {\mathcal{C}}_{j}\right|  > n$ cannot contribute any new element. Again,we make use of the fact that $n \geq  \left| {V\left( H\right) }\right|$ and it only makes sense to add an element $C$ to ${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$ if the intersection with $V\left( C\right)$ allows us to delete another vertex from $e$ . But we cannot delete more than $n$ vertices.

于${E}^{\left( 2n + i\right) }$中的元素。特别地，在${E}^{\left( 3n\right) }$中，对任意满足$\left| {\mathcal{C}}_{j}\right|  \leq  n$的${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$选择，均可获得上述形式的元素。最后，类比主张$\mathrm{C}$，可证选取满足$\left| {\mathcal{C}}_{j}\right|  > n$的集合${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$不会产生新元素。这里再次利用$n \geq  \left| {V\left( H\right) }\right|$的特性：仅当与$V\left( C\right)$的交集能让我们从$e$中删除另一顶点时，向${\mathcal{C}}_{j}$添加元素$C$才有意义。但可删除的顶点数不超过$n$。

### B.2 Proof of Theorem 7

### B.2 定理7的证明

Proof. Let $\mathcal{G} = \left( {T,\lambda ,\chi }\right)$ be a GHD of width ${ghw}\left( H\right)$ of hypergraph $H$ . For a node $u$ in $T$ ,we write ${\lambda }_{u}$ and ${\chi }_{u}$ to denote the $\lambda$ -label and $\chi$ -label at $u$ . It suffices to show that,for some $m \geq  0$ ,the following property holds: for every node $u$ in $T$ and every $e \in  E\left( H\right)$ with $e \in  {\lambda }_{u}$ ,the subedge $e \cap  {\chi }_{u}$ is contained in ${E}^{\left( m\right) }$ . Clearly,if this is the case,then every bag ${\chi }_{u}$ is contained in ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H.k}^{m}$ ,since we can define ${\lambda }_{1}$ by replacing every $e$ in $\lambda$ by $e \cap  {\chi }_{u}$ and setting ${\lambda }_{2} = \varnothing$ . Then ${\mathcal{G}}^{\prime } = \left( {T,\chi }\right)$ is a candidate TD of ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{m}$ ,and,therefore,we get ${ghw}\left( H\right)  = {sh}{w}^{m}\left( H\right)$ .

证明。设$\mathcal{G} = \left( {T,\lambda ,\chi }\right)$为超图$H$的宽度${ghw}\left( H\right)$的GHD。对于$T$中的节点$u$，记${\lambda }_{u}$和${\chi }_{u}$分别表示该节点的$\lambda$标签和$\chi$标签。只需证明存在某个$m \geq  0$使得：对$T$中每个节点$u$及每个满足$e \in  {\lambda }_{u}$的$e \in  E\left( H\right)$，子边$e \cap  {\chi }_{u}$都包含于${E}^{\left( m\right) }$。显然此时每个包${\chi }_{u}$都包含于${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H.k}^{m}$，因为可通过将$\lambda$中的每个$e$替换为$e \cap  {\chi }_{u}$并设${\lambda }_{2} = \varnothing$来定义${\lambda }_{1}$。这样${\mathcal{G}}^{\prime } = \left( {T,\chi }\right)$就成为${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{m}$的候选TD，因此得到${ghw}\left( H\right)  = {sh}{w}^{m}\left( H\right)$。

To obtain such an $m$ with $\left( {e \cap  {\chi }_{u}}\right)  \in  {E}^{\left( m\right) }$ for every node $u$ in $T$ and edge $e \in  {\lambda }_{u}$ ,we recall the following result from [10],Lemma 5.12: Let $e \in  {\lambda }_{u}$ with $e \notin  {\chi }_{u}$ for some node $u$ and let ${u}^{\prime }$ be a node with $e \subseteq  {\chi }_{{u}^{\prime }}$ (such a node ${u}^{\prime }$ must exist in a GHD). Moreover,let $u = {u}_{0},\ldots ,{u}_{\ell } = {u}^{\prime }$ be the path from $u$ to ${u}^{\prime }$ in $T$ . Then the following property holds: $e \cap  {\chi }_{u} = e \cap  \left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{\ell } \cup  {\lambda }_{{u}_{j}}}\right)$ .

为在$T$中对每个节点$u$和边$e \in  {\lambda }_{u}$获得具有$\left( {e \cap  {\chi }_{u}}\right)  \in  {E}^{\left( m\right) }$的$m$，我们引用[10]中引理5.12的结果：设对于某节点$u$存在$e \notin  {\chi }_{u}$的$e \in  {\lambda }_{u}$，并令${u}^{\prime }$为具有$e \subseteq  {\chi }_{{u}^{\prime }}$的节点（在GHD中此节点${u}^{\prime }$必然存在）。此外，设$u = {u}_{0},\ldots ,{u}_{\ell } = {u}^{\prime }$为$T$中从$u$到${u}^{\prime }$的路径。则以下性质成立：$e \cap  {\chi }_{u} = e \cap  \left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{\ell } \cup  {\lambda }_{{u}_{j}}}\right)$。

Clearly,for every $j$ ,the bag $\cup  {\lambda }_{{u}_{j}}$ is in ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$ and,therefore,the subedge ${s}_{j} = e \cap   \cup  {\lambda }_{{u}_{j}}$ of $e$ is in ${E}^{\left( 1\right) }$ . We claim that,for every $i \geq  1$ ,it holds that $\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{i}{s}_{j}}\right)$ is contained in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ .

显然，对于每个$j$，包$\cup  {\lambda }_{{u}_{j}}$位于${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}$中，因此$e$的子边${s}_{j} = e \cap   \cup  {\lambda }_{{u}_{j}}$属于${E}^{\left( 1\right) }$。我们断言：对于每个$i \geq  1$，$\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{i}{s}_{j}}\right)$都包含在${E}^{\left( i\right) }$中。

Proof of the claim. We proceed by induction on $i$ . For $i = 1$ ,the claim reduces to the property that ${s}_{1} = e \cap  \bigcup {\lambda }_{{u}_{1}}$ is in ${E}^{\left( 1\right) }$ ,which we have just established. Now suppose that the claim holds for arbitrary $i \geq  1$ . We have to show that $\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{{i + 1}}{s}_{j}}\right)$ is contained in ${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$ . On the one hand,we have that every ${s}_{j} = e \cap  \bigcup {\lambda }_{{u}_{j}}$ is in ${E}^{\left( 1\right) }$ and,therefore,also in ${E}^{\left( i\right)  + 1}$ and in ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$ . On the other hand, by the induction hypothesis, $\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{i}{s}_{j}}\right)$ is contained in ${E}^{\left( i\right) }$ and,therefore,also in ${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$ . Hence, by Definition 5, $\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{{i + 1}}{s}_{j}}\right)$ is contained in ${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$ .

断言证明。我们对$i$进行归纳。当$i = 1$时，该断言简化为${s}_{1} = e \cap  \bigcup {\lambda }_{{u}_{1}}$位于${E}^{\left( 1\right) }$的性质——这正是我们已证明的。现假设断言对任意$i \geq  1$成立，需证$\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{{i + 1}}{s}_{j}}\right)$包含于${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$。一方面，每个${s}_{j} = e \cap  \bigcup {\lambda }_{{u}_{j}}$都在${E}^{\left( 1\right) }$中，故亦在${E}^{\left( i\right)  + 1}$和${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i + 1}$中；另一方面，根据归纳假设，$\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{i}{s}_{j}}\right)$包含于${E}^{\left( i\right) }$，因而也包含于${\operatorname{Soft}}_{H,k}^{i}$。故由定义5可知，$\left( {\mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{{i + 1}}{s}_{j}}\right)$包含于${E}^{\left( i + 1\right) }$。

The system consists of two major components - a Python library providing an interface to the user, which handles the search for the best decompositions, and a Scala component which connects to the DBMS and makes use of Apache Calcite to parse the SQL query and extract its hypergraph structure, as well as extracing from the DBMS node cost information (which we will detail further below) and generate the rewritings.

系统包含两大组件：提供用户接口的Python库（负责搜索最优分解），以及连接DBMS的Scala组件（利用Apache Calcite解析SQL查询并提取其超图结构，同时从DBMS节点获取成本信息[下文详述]，最终生成查询重写）。

The Python library - referred to as QueryRewriterPython - acts as a proxy to the DBMS, and is thus initialised like a standard DBMS connection. The library is capable of returning not just the optimal rewriting,but can provide the top- $n$ best rewritings,as reported on in Appendix D. As seen in Figure 9, on start up QueryRewriterPython starts QueryRewriterScala as a sub-process. QueryRewriterPython communicates with it via RPC calls using Py4J. QueryRewriterScala obtains the JDBC connection details from QueryRewriterPython, and connects to the DBMS using Apache Calcite, in order to be able to access the schema information required later. The input SQL query is then passed to QueryRewriterScala. Using Apache Calcite, and the schema information from the database, the input SQL query is parsed and converted into a logical query plan. After applying simple optimiser rules in order to obtain a convenient representation, the join structure of the plan is extracted and used to construct a hypergraph. Subtrees in the logical representation of the query, which are the inputs to joins, such as table scans followed by projection and filters, are kept track of, and form the hyperedges of this hypergraph. Later, when creating the rewriting, the corresponding SQL queries for these subtrees is used in the generation of the leaf node VIEW expressions. After retrieving the hypergraph, the QueryRewriterPython enumerates the possible covers,i.e.,hypertree nodes,whose size is based on the width parameter $k$ . The list of nodes is sent to the QueryRewriterScala, where the costs of each of these nodes are estimated. We will detail the two used cost functions in the next subsection. Once all the cost functions have been computed, the QueryRewriterPython follows an algorithm in the vein of Algorithm 2, in order to find the best or the top- $n$ best decompositions. Finally,the decompositions are passed to the QueryRewriterScala in order to generate the rewritings.

### C.2 Cost Functions for Section 7

### C.2 第七章成本函数

In this section we provide full details for the cost functions implemented for our optimisation experiments in Section 7.

本节将详细说明第七章优化实验所实现的成本函数。

C.2.1 A Cost Function Based on DBMS-Estimates. To estimate the costs of the decompositions, we make use of Postgres' cost estimates, as they are internally used by the system for finding a good query plan. Postgres estimates the costs of a query plan in abstract units based on assumptions about lower-level costs in the system, such as disk I/O, or CPU operations. By making use of EXPLAIN statements, it is possible to retrieve the total cost of a plan estimated by the DBMS.

C.2.1 基于DBMS估算的成本函数。我们利用Postgres内部查询规划使用的成本估算机制来评估分解方案成本。Postgres基于磁盘I/O、CPU操作等底层系统假设，以抽象单位估算查询计划成本。通过EXPLAIN语句可获取数据库管理系统估算的计划总成本。

When estimating the costs of bags in the decomposition, we construct the join query corresponding to the bag, and let Postgres estimate the cost of this query in an EXPLAIN statement.

估算分解中数据包成本时，我们构建与数据包对应的连接查询，通过EXPLAIN语句获取Postgres对该查询的成本估算。

$$
\operatorname{cost}\left( u\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  \begin{array}{ll} C\left( {{\pi }_{{B}_{u}}\left( {{R}_{1} \boxtimes  {R}_{2} \boxtimes  \cdots  \boxtimes  {R}_{n}}\right) }\right) & \text{ if }n > 1 \\  0 & \text{ if }n = 1 \end{array}\right.  \tag{5}
$$

where $C\left( q\right)$ corresponds to the costs estimated by Postgres for the query $q$ .

其中$C\left( q\right)$代表Postgres对查询$q$的估算成本。

To estimate the costs of a subtree, we also consider the costs of computing the bottom-up semijoins. Because Postgres includes the costs of scanning the relations which are semijoined, and any joins inside the bags, in the total costs, we have to subtract these costs from the semijoin-costs. Due to noisy estimates, we have to avoid the total cost becoming negative, and thus set a minimum cost.

估算子树成本时还需考虑自底向上半连接的计算成本。由于Postgres在总成本中已包含扫描半连接关系及数据包内连接操作的成本，需从半连接成本中扣除这些部分。鉴于估算存在噪声，我们设置最低成本阈值以避免总成本出现负值。

$$
\operatorname{cost}\left( {T}_{p}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \operatorname{cost}\left( p\right)  + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\left( {\operatorname{cost}\left( {T}_{{c}_{i}}\right)  + \min \left( {C\left( {{J}_{p} \ltimes  {J}_{{c}_{i}}}\right)  - C\left( {J}_{p}\right)  - C\left( {J}_{{c}_{i}}\right) ,1}\right) }\right)  \tag{6}
$$

C.2.2 A Cost Function Based on Actual Cardinalities. Because we observe problematic unreliability of cost estimates from the DBMS, we also consider an idealised cost function, that is omniscient about bag sizes and uses these to estimate cost of individual operations. This has the weakness of not taking physical information, such as whether relations are already in memory (or even fit wholly in memory) or specific implementation behaviour (which we surprisingly observe to be highly relevant in our experiments). Nonetheless, we find it instructive to compare these costs to the estimate based cost function to correct for cases where estimates are wildly inaccurate.

C.2.2 基于实际基数的成本函数。针对DBMS成本估算不可靠的问题，我们设计了知晓数据包大小的理想化成本函数。该函数虽未考虑物理信息（如关系是否已驻留内存）和具体实现行为（实验证明这些因素影响显著），但通过对比基于估算的成本函数，可校正严重失准的估算案例。

First, the cost for bags. We will assume we know the cardinality of the bag (simulating a good query planner) and combine this with the size of the relations that make up the join. The idea is simple, to compute the join we need to at least scan every relation that makes up the join (that is, linear effort in the size of the relations). And then we also need to create the new relation, which takes effort linear in the size of the join result. We thus get for a node $u$ of a decomposition,whose bag is created by cover ${R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{n}$ :

首先定义数据包成本：假设已知数据包基数（模拟优质查询规划器），结合构成连接的各个关系大小进行估算。基本原理是：连接操作至少需要扫描所有参与连接的关系（成本与关系大小线性相关），同时生成新关系需付出与连接结果规模线性相关的成本。对于由覆盖${R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{n}$创建数据包的分解节点$u$，其成本计算公式为：

$$
\operatorname{cost}\left( u\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  \begin{array}{ll} \left| {J}_{u}\right|  + \left| {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{n}\left| {R}_{i}\right|  \cdot  \log \left| {R}_{i}\right| }\right| & \text{ if }n > 1 \\  0 & \text{ if }n = 1 \end{array}\right.  \tag{7}
$$

where ${J}_{u} = {\pi }_{{B}_{u}}\left( {{R}_{1} \boxtimes  {R}_{2} \boxtimes  \cdots  \boxtimes  {R}_{n}}\right)$ ,i.e.,table for the node.

其中${J}_{u} = {\pi }_{{B}_{u}}\left( {{R}_{1} \boxtimes  {R}_{2} \boxtimes  \cdots  \boxtimes  {R}_{n}}\right)$，即该节点对应的表。

The cost for a subtree then builds on this together with some estimate of how effective the semi-joins from the subtrees will be. We will estimate this very simply, by seeing how many non primary-key attributes of the parent appear in a subtree. The point being that if something is a FK in the parent, we assume that every semi-join will do nothing. If something is not a PK/FK relationship in this direction, then we assume some multiplicative reduction in tuples for each such attribute. For every node $p$ in a rooted decomposition we thus define ReduceAttrs(p)as the set containing every attribute $A$ in ${B}_{p}$ ,for which $A$ appears in a subtree rooted at a child of $p$ ,where it does not come from a relation where $A$ is the primary key.

子树成本的计算基于此，并结合对来自子树的半连接操作效能的预估。我们采用简易估算方法：统计父节点非主键属性在子树中的出现次数。核心逻辑是——若父节点中存在外键关系，则假定所有半连接操作无效；若非主键/外键关系，则假定每个此类属性都会使元组数量呈倍数缩减。对于根分解中的每个节点$p$，我们定义ReduceAttrs(p)为包含${B}_{p}$中所有属性$A$的集合，这些属性需满足：出现在$p$子节点为根的子树中，且不源自以$A$为主键的关系。

With this in hand we then estimate the size of the node after the up-phase semi-joins have reached it:

基于此，我们预估上行阶段半连接操作抵达后的节点规模：

$$
\operatorname{ReducedSz}\left( u\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  \begin{array}{ll} 0 & \text{ if }\operatorname{ReducedSz}\left( c\right)  = 0\text{ for any child }c\text{ of }u \\  \frac{\left| {J}_{u}\right| }{1 + \left| {\operatorname{ReduceAttrs}\left( u\right) }\right| } & \text{ otherwise } \end{array}\right.  \tag{8}
$$

Importantly,if relation $C$ is empty,PostgreSQL never scans $U$ in the semi-join $U \ltimes  C$ ,to capture this define the following for nodes $u$ .

关键点：若关系$C$为空，PostgreSQL在半连接$U \ltimes  C$中绝不会扫描$U$。为此针对节点$u$定义如下规则：

$$
\operatorname{ScanCost}\left( u\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \left\{  \begin{array}{ll} 0 & \mathop{\min }\limits_{{c \in  \operatorname{children}\left( u\right) }}\operatorname{ReducedSz}\left( c\right)  = 0 \\  \left| {J}_{u}\right|  \cdot  \log \left| {J}_{u}\right| & \text{ otherwise } \end{array}\right. 
$$

The cost for a subtree ${T}_{p}$ rooted at $p$ ,with children ${c}_{1},\ldots ,{c}_{k}$ is then as follows:

以$p$为根、${c}_{1},\ldots ,{c}_{k}$为子节点的子树${T}_{p}$成本计算公式如下：

$$
\operatorname{cost}\left( {T}_{p}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} \operatorname{cost}\left( p\right)  + \operatorname{ScanCost}\left( p\right)  + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\left( {\operatorname{cost}\left( {T}_{{c}_{i}}\right)  + \operatorname{ReducedSz}\left( {c}_{i}\right)  \cdot  \log \operatorname{ReducedSz}\left( {c}_{i}\right) }\right)  \tag{9}
$$

which corresponds to computing the bag for $p$ ,computing the subtrees for each child,and then finally also using Reduced ${Sz}\left( {c}_{i}\right)$ as a proxy for the cost of the semi-join $p \ltimes  {c}_{i}$ . From experiments, it seems like PostgreSQL actually does the initial loop over the right-hand side of the semi-join. So when ${c}_{i}$ is empty,it stops instantly,but when $p$ is empty it does a full scan of ${c}_{i}$ . This is better reflected by simply taking the reduced child size as the cost.

这对应于计算$p$的数据包、各子节点的子树成本，最终使用Reduced${Sz}\left( {c}_{i}\right)$作为半连接$p \ltimes  {c}_{i}$的成本代理。实验表明PostgreSQL实际会遍历半连接右侧数据集：当${c}_{i}$为空时立即终止，但当$p$为空时会完整扫描${c}_{i}$。直接采用缩减后的子节点规模作为成本更能准确反映该特性。

### D.1 Experimental Setup

### D.1 实验设置

Our experiments use the well-known and publicly available open-source database system Post-greSQL as our target DBMS.

我们选用知名开源数据库系统PostgreSQL作为目标DBMS进行实验。

Experimental Setup. The tests were run on a test server with an AMD EPYC-Milan Processor with 16 cores,run at a max. $2\mathrm{{GHz}}$ clock speed. It had ${128}\mathrm{{GBRAM}}$ and the test data and database was stored and running off of a 500GB SSD. The operating system was Ubuntu 22.04.2 LTS, running Linux kernel with version 5.15.0-75-generic. We executed the tests using a JupyterLab Notebook, which we make available anonymous in https://anonymous.4open.science/r/softhw-pods25.This also includes the source code of the Scala library that handles the tasks of parsing the input query, producing the hypergraph and extracting costs from the target DBMS. We also include all experimental data collected in this repository.

实验环境。测试服务器搭载16核AMD EPYC-Milan处理器，最高主频$2\mathrm{{GHz}}$，配备${128}\mathrm{{GBRAM}}$内存，测试数据及数据库存储在500GB固态硬盘上运行。操作系统为Ubuntu 22.04.2 LTS，Linux内核版本5.15.0-75-generic。测试通过JupyterLab Notebook执行，相关资源已匿名发布于https://anonymous.4open.science/r/softhw-pods25，包含解析输入查询、生成超图及从目标DBMS提取成本的Scala库源代码，以及全部实验数据。

Benchmarks. For the benchmarks we consider three datasets. There is TPC-DS, a benchmark created for decision support systems ${}^{3}$ . For our experiments we use the scaling factor 10 . Furthermore, we also consider the Hetionet database, where we specifically focus on queries already presented in [3]. There is no scaling factors for Hetionet, the entire dataset was used as-is. Finally, we also feature the "Labelled Subgraph Query Benchmark", or LSQB for short, which was first presented in [18]. As with TPC-DS, we also used the scaling factor 10 .

基准测试。我们采用三个数据集：面向决策支持系统的TPC-DS基准测试${}^{3}$（使用缩放因子10）；Hetionet数据库（完整使用其数据集，重点关注文献[3]中的查询）；以及文献[18]提出的"标记子图查询基准"(LSQB)（同样采用缩放因子10）。

Queries. Since the focus is on queries that exhibit high join costs, we opted to manually construct such challenging yet still practical examples, due to the fact that many benchmarks, such as TPC-DS opt to focus on cases where joins are always along primary or foreign keys, and in case of foreign keys only to join them with the respect table they are a key of. This means that joins do not produce significantly more rows than are present in the involved tables: to see this, we just observe that a join over a key can, by the uniqueness condition, only find exactly one match (and due "presence condition", must always succeed). While this scenario makes sense when users have carefully prepared databases with a clear schema, we believe and indeed users reports suggest, that queries with a large number of tables with very high intermediate join sizes occur in practice. So to get any useful insights into our frameworks applicability, we forgo the default provided queries and pick a number of handcrafted "tough" join queries, putting more focus on their evaluation complexity and less on their semantics. A notable exception here are the queries from Hetionet, which we directly sourced together with data from [3], which are used as-is, since they already exhibit complex, cyclic join queries.

查询设计。由于研究重点在于高连接成本的查询，我们选择手动构建这类具有挑战性但仍具实用性的案例。这是因为TPC-DS等基准测试往往仅关注主外键连接场景，且外键连接时仅关联其对应的主表。这意味着连接操作产生的行数不会显著多于原表数据量——只需注意到基于键的连接在唯一性约束下仅能匹配到一条记录（且参照完整性要求连接必须成功）。虽然这种场景适用于用户精心设计的规范数据库，但实际应用中确实存在涉及多表连接且中间结果集庞大的查询案例。为验证框架适用性，我们放弃默认查询集，选取了一批手工设计的"高难度"连接查询，更侧重评估其执行复杂度而非语义价值。其中Hetionet的查询是个特例，我们直接采用文献[3]提供的原始查询及数据，因其本身已包含复杂的循环连接结构。

This is not meant to be a thorough, systematic analysis, which would involve a very large number of such queries, tested across multiple systems and in various scenarios. That goes beyond the scope of this paper, which only aims to show the practical potential of our framework. Implementing it in a system which enables reliable, robust performance across all kinds of queries is left as future work, once its potential has been proven.

本文并非要进行全面系统的分析——那需要跨系统、多场景的海量查询测试。这超出了本文范畴，我们仅旨在展示框架的实用潜力。待其潜力验证后，将其实现为能稳定处理各类查询的系统将是未来工作方向。
