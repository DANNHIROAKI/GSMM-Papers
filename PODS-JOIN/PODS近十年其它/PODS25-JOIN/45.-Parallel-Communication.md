# Parallel Communication Obliviousness: One Round and Beyond

并行通信无感知性：单轮及更远

YUFEI TAO, RU WANG, and SHIYUAN DENG, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, China

陶宇飞、王茹与邓诗源，香港中文大学，中国

This paper studies communication-oblivious algorithms under the massively parallel computation (MPC) model. The communication patterns of these algorithms follow a distribution dependent only on the definition of the underlying problem,the problem size $N$ ,and the number $p$ of machines,but not on the specific input elements. Our objective is to understand when obliviousness necessitates - or does not necessitate - heavier communication compared to the traditional MPC model that does not enforce such a requirement.

本文研究大规模并行计算（MPC）模型下的通信无感知算法。这些算法的通信模式仅取决于底层问题的定义、问题规模$N$及机器数量$p$，而与具体输入元素无关。我们的目标是理解：相较于不强制此要求的传统MPC模型，无感知性何时会必然（或非必然）导致更高的通信开销。

The first part of our investigation focuses on single-round algorithms. We prove that skew-free hashing, a fundamental problem solvable with load $\widetilde{O}\left( {N/p}\right)$ (with high probability or w.h.p. for short) under the traditional model,demands a load of nearly $\Omega \left( N\right)$ under communication obliviousness. Intriguingly,we show that hashing can still be applied in an oblivious manner to process any natural join in one round with a load complexity matching that of the best traditional MPC algorithm. The second part of our investigation studies compilation methods that convert a traditional MPC algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ into a communication-oblivious counterpart. Given an $\mathcal{A}$ that operates within $\ell  = \operatorname{poly}\left( p\right)$ rounds and entails a load at most $L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$ w.h.p.,we can produce w.h.p. a communication-oblivious version running in $2\ell$ rounds with a load at most $\left( {1 + \delta }\right) L$ , where $\delta  > 0$ can be an arbitrarily small constant. Additionally,we establish hardness results indicating that the theoretical guarantees of our compilation can no longer be significantly improved.

研究第一部分聚焦单轮算法。我们证明：传统模型下可轻松实现负载$\widetilde{O}\left( {N/p}\right)$（高概率或简称w.h.p.）的基础问题——无偏哈希，在通信无感知条件下需要接近$\Omega \left( N\right)$的负载。有趣的是，我们展示哈希仍能以无感知方式处理任何自然连接，其单轮负载复杂度与最佳传统MPC算法相当。第二部分研究将传统MPC算法$\mathcal{A}$转换为通信无感知版本的编译方法。给定一个在$\ell  = \operatorname{poly}\left( p\right)$轮内运行且负载最高$L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$（w.h.p.）的$\mathcal{A}$，我们能生成w.h.p.运行的通信无感知版本，其在$2\ell$轮内完成且负载不超过$\left( {1 + \delta }\right) L$，其中$\delta  > 0$可为任意小常数。此外，我们建立的硬度结果表明编译的理论保证已无法显著提升。

## 1 引言

Two primary topics come to the forefront in the discourse on privacy control in cloud computing:

云计算隐私控制讨论中两大核心议题凸显：

- Confidential computing: This aims to mitigate two forms of privacy leakage that may occur on an individual server. First, rogue administrators could exploit their positions to inspect sensitive data within the server. Second, even if encryption hinders direct data accesses, administrators could still infer valuable information from the memory access patterns that the computation generates.

- 机密计算：旨在缓解个体服务器上可能发生的两种隐私泄露形式。其一，恶意管理员可能利用职权窥探服务器内的敏感数据；其二，即使加密阻碍了直接数据访问，管理员仍可通过计算产生的内存访问模式推断出有价值的信息。

- Oblivious communication: This aims to prevent another two forms of privacy leakage that may occur during communication across different servers. First, malicious network managers might scrutinize the data flowing through the gateways. Second, even if the network traffic is encrypted, these managers could still deduce sensitive information from the statistics extracted from the communication.

- 无感知通信：旨在防止跨服务器通信时可能发生的另两种隐私泄露。其一，恶意网络管理者可能审查流经网关的数据；其二，即使网络流量被加密，这些管理者仍能从通信统计中推导出敏感信息。

Robust solutions to confidential computing are already in place, including systems that employ fully homomorphic encryption (e.g., IBM's HE4Cloud) or hardware-based trusted execution environments (nowadays offered by the leading cloud-computing platforms). When integrated with oblivious RAM techniques $\left\lbrack  {5,{11},{20},{21}}\right\rbrack$ ,these systems safeguard sensitive information throughout the entire lifecycle of data at an individual server: whether stored in the disk, transiting through the memory hierarchy, or in use by the CPU.

目前已有成熟的机密计算解决方案，包括采用全同态加密的系统（如IBM的HE4Cloud）或基于硬件的可信执行环境（现由主流云计算平台提供）。当与无感知随机存取存储器技术$\left\lbrack  {5,{11},{20},{21}}\right\rbrack$结合时，这些系统能在数据于单个服务器的全生命周期内提供保护——无论数据存储在磁盘中、在内存层次间传输，还是被CPU使用时。

In this work, we will concentrate on oblivious communication, with the aim to understand how much communication is needed to enforce this requirement. To pave the foundation of our discussion, next we will formalize the concept of oblivious communication and the class of algorithms to be studied.

本工作将聚焦无感知通信，旨在探究满足该需求所需的最小通信量。为奠定讨论基础，下文将形式化无感知通信概念及待研究算法类别。

### 1.1 Communication-Oblivious MPC

### 1.1 通信无感知多方安全计算

Let us first clarify some math conventions used in this paper. Given positive integers $x$ and $y$ ,let $\left( \begin{array}{l} x \\  y \end{array}\right)$ be the number of ways to choose $y$ elements from a set of size $x$ ; specially,if $y > x$ ,define $\left( \begin{array}{l} x \\  y \end{array}\right)$ $= 0$ . For an integer $x \geq  1$ ,the notation $\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$ denotes the set $\{ 1,2,\ldots ,x\}$ . We use double-curly braces to represent multi-sets,e.g., $\{ \{ 1,1,1,2,2,3\} \}$ is a multi-set with 6 elements. Sets ${S}_{1},{S}_{2},\ldots ,{S}_{n}\left( {n \geq  2}\right)$ form a partition of set $S$ if ${S}_{i} \cap  {S}_{j} = \varnothing$ for any distinct $i,j \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ and $\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{n}{S}_{i} = S$ . Every $\log \left( \cdot \right)$ has base 2. The notation $\operatorname{poly}\left( n\right)$ denotes the class of functions polynomial in $n$ .

首先明确本文数学约定：给定正整数$x$和$y$，令$\left( \begin{array}{l} x \\  y \end{array}\right)$表示从大小为$x$的集合中选取$y$个元素的组合数；特别地，若$y > x$，定义$\left( \begin{array}{l} x \\  y \end{array}\right)$$= 0$。对整数$x \geq  1$，符号$\left\lbrack  x\right\rbrack$表示集合$\{ 1,2,\ldots ,x\}$。双花括号表示多重集，如$\{ \{ 1,1,1,2,2,3\} \}$是含6个元素的多重集。当${S}_{1},{S}_{2},\ldots ,{S}_{n}\left( {n \geq  2}\right)$对任意相异$i,j \in  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$和$\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{n}{S}_{i} = S$满足${S}_{i} \cap  {S}_{j} = \varnothing$时，称这些集合构成$S$的划分。所有$\log \left( \cdot \right)$均以2为底数。符号$\operatorname{poly}\left( n\right)$表示关于$n$的多项式函数类。

The MPC Model. Our analysis will be under the massively parallel computation (MPC) model, which has been extensively adopted in the database literature, as will be surveyed in Section 1.3.

MPC模型。我们的分析基于大规模并行计算（MPC）模型，该模型在数据库领域已被广泛采用，详见第1.3节综述。

In this model, $p$ share-nothing machines are connected via a high-speed network. Initially, the input data is distributed across the $p$ machines. An algorithm then executes in rounds,each consisting of two phases: in the first phase, every machine performs computation on its local data, while in the second phase, the machines exchange messages via the network. Importantly, if machine $i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ plans to send machine $j \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ a message (in the second phase),machine $i$ must prepare the message in the first phase. This restriction prevents machine $i$ from,for example, deciding what to send based on the messages received during the second phase. The load of a round is the maximum number of words communicated (sent and received combined) by one machine in that round. The algorithm's performance is measured by two metrics: (i) the total number of rounds executed, and (ii) the maximum load of all rounds, which is termed as the algorithm's (overall) load.

该模型中，$p$台无共享机器通过高速网络连接。初始时输入数据分布在$p$台机器上。算法以轮次执行，每轮含两个阶段：第一阶段各机器对本地数据进行计算，第二阶段通过网络交换消息。关键约束是：若机器$i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$计划向机器$j \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$发送消息（第二阶段），必须在第一阶段准备好该消息。此限制防止机器$i$根据第二阶段接收的消息动态决定发送内容。每轮的负载定义为该轮中单台机器通信（收发合计）的最大字数。算法性能通过两个指标衡量：(i)总执行轮数；(ii)所有轮次中的最大负载，称为算法（总体）负载。

Randomization is modeled by introducing a sequence of random bits, which is agreed upon by all machines. Such an agreement can be reached before receiving the input and therefore does not require communication during the algorithm's execution. In the first phase of a round, each machine can guide its local computation according to the random-bit sequence. When a random event is said to occur "with high probability" (w.h.p.), we require that the probability must be at least $1 - 1/{p}^{c}$ where $c$ can be an arbitrarily large constant chosen before seeing the input data.

随机化通过引入一个所有机器都预先达成一致的随机比特序列来建模。这种共识可在接收输入前达成，因此算法执行期间无需额外通信。在每轮的第一阶段，各机器根据该随机比特序列指导本地计算。当某随机事件被称为"以高概率"(w.h.p.)发生时，我们要求该概率必须至少为$1 - 1/{p}^{c}$，其中$c$是查看输入数据前可任意指定的足够大常数。

The size $N$ of the input data is commonly assumed to be significantly larger than $p$ ,which typically means $N \geq  {p}^{c}$ where $c$ is a constant dependent on the problem studied.

通常假设输入数据规模$N$显著大于$p$，这意味着$N \geq  {p}^{c}$，其中$c$是所研究问题相关的常数。

Communication-Oblivious Algorithms. We will now formalize the notion of communication obliviousness, building upon a similar formulation in [10].

通信无感知算法。我们将基于文献[10]的类似形式化表述，严格定义通信无感知性概念。

We consider the existence of a countably infinite set called the input domain, whose concrete definition depends on the problem we aim to study. The members of the input domain are referred to as inputs. Every input $\mathcal{I}$ is associated with an integer size. While the meaning of input size depends on the underlying problem, it should always be proportional to the number of words needed to describe $\mathcal{I}$ . Denote by $\mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$ the collection of all possible inputs with the same size $N$ .

我们假设存在称为输入域的可数无限集，其具体定义取决于所研究的问题。输入域成员称为输入项，每个输入项$\mathcal{I}$关联一个整数规模。虽然输入规模的含义因具体问题而异，但始终应与描述$\mathcal{I}$所需字词数成正比。用$\mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$表示所有规模为$N$的可能输入项的集合。

Fix an input size $N \geq  p$ (where $p$ is the number of machines in the MPC model) and an algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ . We demand that every input of size $N$ should be presented to $\mathcal{A}$ with exactly the same number of words stored on each machine initially. For this purpose,we assume a function $W\left( N\right)$ (determined by the underlying problem) mapping $N$ to a positive integer such that $W\left( N\right)  = \Theta \left( {N/p}\right)$ . For each input $\mathcal{I} \in  \mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$ ,we call $\sum  = \left( {{\sum }_{1},{\sum }_{2},\ldots ,{\sum }_{p}}\right)$ a legal initial state of $\mathcal{I}$ if

给定输入规模$N \geq  p$(其中$p$是MPC模型中的机器数量)和算法$\mathcal{A}$，我们要求每个规模为$N$的输入必须用完全相同数量的初始字词分配给各机器。为此假设存在由问题决定的函数$W\left( N\right)$，将$N$映射到满足$W\left( N\right)  = \Theta \left( {N/p}\right)$的正整数。对于每个输入$\mathcal{I} \in  \mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$，当满足以下条件时称$\sum  = \left( {{\sum }_{1},{\sum }_{2},\ldots ,{\sum }_{p}}\right)$为$\mathcal{I}$的合法初始状态：

- each ${\sum }_{i}\left( {i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack  }\right)$ is a sequence of $W\left( N\right)$ words;

- 每个${\sum }_{i}\left( {i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack  }\right)$是由$W\left( N\right)$个字词组成的序列

- the $p$ sequences ${\sum }_{1},{\sum }_{2},\ldots$ ,and ${\sum }_{p}$ collectively describe the input $\mathcal{I}$ with no ambiguity;

- $p$个序列${\sum }_{1},{\sum }_{2},\ldots$与${\sum }_{p}$能无歧义地共同描述输入$\mathcal{I}$

- placing (an encrypted version of) ${\sum }_{i}$ on machine $i$ for each $i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ is permitted by the underlying problem.

- 根据问题设定允许将(加密版的)${\sum }_{i}$置于机器$i$上

Each input $\mathcal{I}$ can have multiple legal initial states,and the algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ must be designed to work with all of them. Specifically,when the algorithm is executed on a legal initial state $\sum  =$ $\left( {{\sum }_{1},{\sum }_{2},\ldots ,{\sum }_{p}}\right)$ ,machine $i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ sees ${\sum }_{i}$ in the first phase in the first round (of $\mathcal{A}$ ). From there,the algorithm then performs the necessary computation and communication to solve the problem with strong performance guarantees.

每个输入$\mathcal{I}$可对应多个合法初始状态，算法$\mathcal{A}$必须设计为能处理所有情况。具体而言，当算法在合法初始状态$\sum  =$$\left( {{\sum }_{1},{\sum }_{2},\ldots ,{\sum }_{p}}\right)$上执行时，机器$i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$在首轮第一阶段将看到${\sum }_{i}$，随后通过必要的计算和通信来解决问题，并确保强性能保证。

Suppose that,when given a legal initial state $\sum$ ,an MPC algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ finishes in $\ell$ rounds where, in the $r$ -th round $\left( {r \in  \left\lbrack  \ell \right\rbrack  }\right)$ ,machine $i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ sends message ${\mathbf{M}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$ to machine $j \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ (for $i = j$ , ${\mathbf{M}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$ is an empty message). Define ${\mathbf{L}}_{r}$ as the $p \times  p$ matrix where ${\mathbf{L}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$ equals the length of ${\mathbf{M}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$ ,measured in the number of words $\left( {{\mathbf{L}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack  }\right.$ is always 0 for $\left. {i = j}\right)$ . Every message ${\mathbf{M}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$ is encrypted such that an adversary, who observes the communication, can only use the sequence

假设给定一个合法的初始状态$\sum$时，MPC算法$\mathcal{A}$将在$\ell$轮次内完成运算，其中在第$r$轮$\left( {r \in  \left\lbrack  \ell \right\rbrack  }\right)$中，机器$i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$向机器$j \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$发送消息${\mathbf{M}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$（当$i = j$时，${\mathbf{M}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$为空消息）。定义${\mathbf{L}}_{r}$为一个$p \times  p$矩阵，其中${\mathbf{L}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$等于${\mathbf{M}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$的长度（以字数计量），对于$\left. {i = j}\right)$而言$\left( {{\mathbf{L}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack  }\right.$始终为0。每条消息${\mathbf{M}}_{r}\left\lbrack  {i,j}\right\rbrack$均经过加密，使得观测通信的敌手仅能利用该序列

$$
\pi  = \left( {{\mathbf{L}}_{1},{\mathbf{L}}_{2},\ldots ,{\mathbf{L}}_{\ell }}\right) 
$$

to infer about $\mathcal{I}$ . We refer to $\pi$ as the communication pattern of $\mathcal{A}$ when executed on $\sum$ . For a randomized algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ ,the sequence $\pi$ may be a random variable.

来推断$\mathcal{I}$。我们将$\pi$称为$\mathcal{A}$在$\sum$上执行时的通信模式。对于随机化算法$\mathcal{A}$，序列$\pi$可能是个随机变量。

The algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ is communication oblivious under the input size $N$ if its communication pattern $\pi$ follows exactly the same probabilistic distribution,regardless of the choice of $\mathcal{I} \in  \mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$ and the legal initial state of $\mathcal{I}$ . We say that $\mathcal{A}$ is a single-round algorithm if $\ell$ is never greater than 1, regardless of the choice of $\mathcal{I}$ and $\sum$ ; otherwise, $\mathcal{A}$ is a multi-round algorithm.

当算法$\mathcal{A}$的通信模式$\pi$遵循完全相同的概率分布（无论$\mathcal{I} \in  \mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$的选择和$\mathcal{I}$的合法初始状态如何变化）时，称该算法在输入规模$N$下具有通信不可区分性。若对于任意$\mathcal{I}$和$\sum$的选择，$\ell$始终不大于1，则称$\mathcal{A}$为单轮算法；否则称为多轮算法。

### 1.2 Our Contributions

### 1.2 我们的贡献

Our primary objective is to discern the inherent connections between the traditional MPC model and its communication-oblivious counterpart. The core is to understand when obliviousness would necessitate a provably higher load than the traditional, non-oblivious, MPC model. Next, we provide an overview of our findings.

我们的核心目标是揭示传统MPC模型与其通信不可区分版本之间的内在联系，关键在于理解不可区分性何时会必然导致比传统非不可区分MPC模型更高的可证明负载。接下来概述我们的研究发现。

Single-Round Hashing. Our first contribution is a hardness result proving that hashing - a building brick deployed by many MPC algorithms (in both theory and practice) - is significantly more expensive under communication obliviousness as far as single-round algorithms are concerned.

单轮哈希。我们的首个贡献是证明硬度结果：就单轮算法而言，哈希——作为众多MPC算法（理论与实践中的基础构件）——在通信不可区分性约束下的实现成本显著更高。

To pinpoint the source of hardness, we introduce a problem named skew-free gathering. Fix arbitrary integers $N$ and $p$ such that $N$ is a multiple of $p$ ,and $p \geq  t$ where $t$ is a constant integer at least 2. The input collection $\mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$ has only a single input $\mathcal{I}$ : this is a set of $N$ elements,each of which - denoted as $e -$ is associated with an integer $\operatorname{key}\left( e\right)$ ,referred to as the $\operatorname{key}$ of $e$ . The set of keys fulfills two conditions:

为定位硬度根源，我们引入名为无偏聚集的问题。给定任意整数$N$和$p$（其中$N$是$p$的倍数），以及$p \geq  t$（$t$为不小于2的常数整数）。输入集合$\mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$仅包含单个输入$\mathcal{I}$：这是$N$个元素的集合，每个元素（记作$e -$）关联一个整数$\operatorname{key}\left( e\right)$，称为$e$的$\operatorname{key}$。键集合满足两个条件：

- There is one special key that is possessed by $t \cdot  \left( {N/p}\right)$ elements,called the special elements.

- 存在一个由$t \cdot  \left( {N/p}\right)$个元素持有的特殊键，这些元素称为特殊元素；

- Every other key is possessed by exactly one element.

- 其余每个键仅被一个元素持有。

A legal initial state $\left( {{\sum }_{1},\ldots ,{\sum }_{p}}\right)$ of $\mathcal{I}$ meets the following conditions:

合法的初始状态$\left( {{\sum }_{1},\ldots ,{\sum }_{p}}\right)$需满足以下条件：

- ${\sum }_{1},\ldots ,{\sum }_{p}$ are word sequences with the same length,which is $\Theta \left( {N/p}\right)$ ;

- ${\sum }_{1},\ldots ,{\sum }_{p}$是长度均为$\Theta \left( {N/p}\right)$的词序列；

- each ${\sum }_{i}\left( {i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack  }\right)$ describes a subset ${\mathcal{I}}_{i} \subseteq  \mathcal{I}$ of size $N/p$ ;

- 每个${\sum }_{i}\left( {i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack  }\right)$描述了一个大小为$N/p$的子集${\mathcal{I}}_{i} \subseteq  \mathcal{I}$；

- ${\mathcal{I}}_{1},{\mathcal{I}}_{2},\ldots ,{\mathcal{I}}_{p}$ form a partition of $\mathcal{I}$ ;

- ${\mathcal{I}}_{1},{\mathcal{I}}_{2},\ldots ,{\mathcal{I}}_{p}$构成$\mathcal{I}$的一个划分；

- there are $t$ different machine ids $j \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ such that ${\mathcal{I}}_{j}$ contains nothing but $N/p$ special elements (i.e.,the $t \cdot  N/p$ special elements are placed on $t$ distinct machines).

- 存在$t$个不同的机器标识符$j \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$，使得${\mathcal{I}}_{j}$仅包含$N/p$个特殊元素（即这$t \cdot  N/p$个特殊元素分布在$t$台不同机器上）。

The goal of an algorithm is to move all the special elements to one (arbitrary) machine.

算法的目标是将所有特殊元素移至一台（任意选择的）机器。

In the conventional MPC model,the problem can be solved trivially with load $O\left( {N/p}\right)$ : simply ask the $t$ machines where the special elements are initially stored to send their data to a common machine. In contrast, we prove that if a single-round communication-oblivious algorithm is required to succeed with probability at least $2/3$ ,its load must be at least $\Omega \left( {N/{p}^{1/t}}\right)$ with a constant probability. As $t$ increases,the load approaches $\Omega \left( N\right)$ up to a factor sub-polynomial in $p$ . More precisely,there does not exist any constant $\epsilon  > 0$ such that one can design an algorithm promising a load of $O\left( {N/{p}^{\epsilon }}\right)$ with a constant probability for all constant $t$ . We also show that the lower bound $\Omega \left( {N/{p}^{1/t}}\right)$ can be matched by a deterministic algorithm.

在传统MPC模型中，该问题可轻松以$O\left( {N/p}\right)$负载解决：只需让初始存储特殊元素的$t$台机器将数据发送至同一台机器。然而我们证明，若要求单轮通信无关算法至少以$2/3$概率成功，则其负载必须至少达到$\Omega \left( {N/{p}^{1/t}}\right)$（存在常数概率）。随着$t$增大，负载将趋近于$\Omega \left( N\right)$（相差$p$的次多项式因子）。更准确地说，不存在任何常数$\epsilon  > 0$能保证设计出对所有常数$t$都承诺$O\left( {N/{p}^{\epsilon }}\right)$负载（具有常数概率）的算法。我们也证明确定性算法可匹配这个$\Omega \left( {N/{p}^{1/t}}\right)$下界。

Let us now turn to skew-free hashing,where each input in $\mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$ is a set $\mathcal{I}$ of $N$ elements,each with an integer key. It is guaranteed that every possible key is possessed by $O\left( {N/p}\right)$ elements of $\mathcal{I}$ (hence,skew "free"). Initially,each machine receives at most $\lceil N/p\rceil$ elements ${}^{1}$ . The goal is to move all the elements of the same key to a common machine. In the traditional MPC model, the problem can be settled (with hashing) in one round using load $\widetilde{O}\left( {N/p}\right)$ w.h.p. $\left\lbrack  {8,9}\right\rbrack$ ,where $\widetilde{O}\left( \cdot \right)$ hides a polylog $p$ factor. As skew-free hashing generalizes skew-free gathering ${}^{2}$ ,our lower bound on the latter indicates that oblivious skew-free hashing demands a load of nearly $\Omega \left( N\right)$ ,thus creating a huge separation from the traditional model.

现在讨论无偏哈希，其中$\mathcal{I}\left( N\right)$的每个输入是包含$N$个元素的集合$\mathcal{I}$，每个元素具有整数键。保证每个可能键值由$\mathcal{I}$的$O\left( {N/p}\right)$个元素持有（故称"无偏"）。初始时每台机器最多接收$\lceil N/p\rceil$个元素${}^{1}$。目标是将同键元素移至同一台机器。传统MPC模型中，该问题可通过哈希单轮解决，负载为$\widetilde{O}\left( {N/p}\right)$（高概率$\left\lbrack  {8,9}\right\rbrack$），其中$\widetilde{O}\left( \cdot \right)$隐含多对数因子$p$。由于无偏哈希推广了无偏聚集${}^{2}$，我们对后者证明的下界表明：通信无关的无偏哈希需要接近$\Omega \left( N\right)$的负载，与传统模型形成巨大差距。

Single-Round Joins. The hardness of skew-free hashing under communication obliviousness is alarming because the existing one-round join algorithms $\left\lbrack  {3,4,7 - 9,{19}}\right\rbrack$ in the (conventional) MPC model depend on a method named ${}^{3}$ Share $\left\lbrack  {4,9}\right\rbrack$ ,which is closely relevant to hashing. Because the community has nearly resolved the one-round communication complexity of (natural) joins [19], it would be a huge pity if the topic had to be re-opened in the name of obliviousness.

单轮连接。通信无关条件下无偏哈希的困难性令人警觉，因为现有（传统）MPC模型中的单轮连接算法$\left\lbrack  {3,4,7 - 9,{19}}\right\rbrack$依赖于名为${}^{3}$共享$\left\lbrack  {4,9}\right\rbrack$的方法，该方法与哈希密切相关。鉴于学界已近乎解决（自然）连接的单轮通信复杂度[19]，若因通信无关性需重新研究该课题，将是重大遗憾。

Specifically, if a value is "too heavy" in the sense that it is possessed by $\Omega \left( {N/p}\right)$ tuples,our algorithm will always dissipate those tuples across different machines, rather than gathering them on a single machine. Round-Doubling Compilation and Token Passing. Our next contribution is a round compilation method that translates a traditional MPC algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ into the communication-oblivious model while preserving the number of rounds of $\mathcal{A}$ by a factor of 2 and its load by a factor of $1 + \delta$ for an arbitrarily small constant $\delta  > 0$ . Specifically,if $\mathcal{A}$ performs at most $\ell  = \operatorname{poly}\left( p\right)$ rounds and,w.h.p., incurs a load of at most $L$ ,our method yields an oblivious algorithm ${\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$ that computes the same information as $\mathcal{A}$ in $2\ell$ rounds with a load $\left( {1 + \delta }\right) L$ . Our compilation succeeds w.h.p. as long as $L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$ ,a condition satisfied by nearly all the existing MPC algorithms we are aware of.

轮次倍增编译与令牌传递。我们的第二项贡献是轮次编译方法：将传统MPC算法 $\mathcal{A}$ 转换为通信无关模型时，保持其轮次数乘以2倍，负载乘以 $1 + \delta$ 倍（其中 $\delta  > 0$ 为任意小常数）。具体而言，若 $\mathcal{A}$ 最多执行 $\ell  = \operatorname{poly}\left( p\right)$ 轮且高概率下负载不超过 $L$，我们的方法将生成通信无关算法 ${\mathcal{A}}^{\prime }$，该算法在 $2\ell$ 轮内以 $\left( {1 + \delta }\right) L$ 负载计算与 $\mathcal{A}$ 相同的信息。只要满足 $L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$（现有MPC算法普遍满足该条件），我们的编译就能高概率成功。

We also study how much improvement can still be expected over our compilation method. First, it is clear from our earlier discussion on skew-free gathering that the blow-up factor 2 in the round number cannot be improved in general. Specifically, for skew-free gathering, it is easy to achieve load $O\left( {N/p}\right)$ in one round in the traditional MPC model,but under communication obliviousness,every single-round algorithm must entail a load of nearly $\Omega \left( N\right)$ with a constant probability. Given the above,the primary remaining question is whether the condition $L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$ can be significantly relaxed. Our last contribution is to answer the question in the negative by establishing a hardness result on the following token passing problem.

在传统模型中，该问题可在一轮内以负载$L = O\left( {p}^{1 - \epsilon }\right)$轻松解决：只需让机器$x$将所有令牌发送至机器$y$。然而我们证明，若要求两轮通信无感知算法以至少$2/3$的概率成功，其负载必须以恒定概率达到$\Omega \left( {p}^{1 - \epsilon /2}\right)$。

The polynomial gap between $\Omega \left( {p}^{1 - \epsilon /2}\right)$ and $O\left( {p}^{1 - \epsilon }\right)$ indicates that the condition $L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$ of our round-doubling compilation is tight up to a factor sub-polynomial in $p$ . For example,suppose that one could relax the condition to $L = \Omega \left( {p}^{0.99}\right)$ ,while still preserving our round blow-up factor 2 and load blow-up factor $1 + \delta$ w.h.p.. Then,for the token passing problem with $\epsilon  = {0.01}$ ,we would be able to compile the aforementioned trivial one-round algorithm into a communication-oblivious counterpart that w.h.p. solves the problem in two rounds with a load of $O\left( {p}^{0.99}\right)$ w.h.p.. This, however,contradicts our negative result,which states that the load needs to be $\Omega \left( {p}^{0.995}\right)$ with at least a constant probability.

$\Omega \left( {p}^{1 - \epsilon /2}\right)$与$O\left( {p}^{1 - \epsilon }\right)$之间的多项式差距表明，我们轮次倍增编译的条件$L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$在$p$的亚多项式因子范围内是紧致的。例如，假设将条件放宽至$L = \Omega \left( {p}^{0.99}\right)$，同时仍保持轮次膨胀系数2和大概率负载膨胀系数$1 + \delta$。那么对于$\epsilon  = {0.01}$的令牌传递问题，我们本可将前述简单单轮算法编译为通信无感知版本，以大概率在两轮内用$O\left( {p}^{0.99}\right)$负载解决问题。但这与我们的否定结论相矛盾，该结论表明负载必须以至少恒定概率达到$\Omega \left( {p}^{0.995}\right)$。

### 1.3 Previous Results and Relevance to Ours

### 1.3 先前成果与本文关联性

Closely related to our work is a compilation method by Chan et al. [10]. Our compilation draws inspiration from theirs but makes several improvements. First, the approach of [10] was designed for the "SODA MPC" model [17], which resembles the model in Section 1.1 but does away the notion of "load". In that model,each machine is equipped with $s = \Omega \left( {N/p}\right)$ words of memory,and the amount of communication is not a main concern as long as each machine receives no more than $s$ words in each round from all other machines combined. The analysis of [10] was carried out under the condition of $s = {N}^{\epsilon }$ for some constant $\epsilon  > 0$ . When translated into our scenario,the analysis fails to capture the regime where $L \ll  s$ . Second,our compilation is substantially simpler, restores the clarity for the approach underneath [10] in the scenario where $L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$ ,and explicitly determines the blow-up factors $1 + \delta$ and 2 (for preserving the load and number of rounds, respectively ${)}^{5}$ . Finally,our discussion (through the token passing problem) on the condition that $L$ needs to satisfy to enable round-doubling load-preserving compilation is new.

与我们的研究密切相关的是一项由Chan等人提出的编译方法[10]。我们的编译工作受其启发但做出了若干改进。首先，文献[10]的方法专为"SODA MPC"模型[17]设计，该模型与1.1节所述模型类似但摒弃了"负载"概念。在该模型中，每台机器配备$s = \Omega \left( {N/p}\right)$字内存，只要每台机器每轮从其他所有机器接收的总数据量不超过$s$字，通信量就不是主要考量。文献[10]的分析是在$s = {N}^{\epsilon }$（其中$\epsilon  > 0$为常数）条件下进行的，若将该分析移植到我们的场景中，则无法捕捉$L \ll  s$时的状态。其次，我们的编译方案显著简化，在$L = \Omega \left( {p\log p}\right)$场景下还原了文献[10]方法的清晰性，并明确计算出膨胀系数$1 + \delta$和2（分别对应保持负载和轮次不变${)}^{5}$）。最后，我们通过令牌传递问题探讨的关于$L$需满足的条件以实现轮次倍增且负载保持的编译，是全新的研究贡献。

It should be further noted that the study in [10] does not address one-round MPC algorithms. Through our round compilation method, one can see that a primary distinction between the conventional MPC model and its oblivious counterpart lies in the realm of one-round algorithms. Exploring fundamental problems that can characterize this difference represents an interesting direction. Our work can be seen as a step in that direction.

需特别指出的是，文献[10]的研究并未涉及单轮MPC算法。通过我们的轮次编译方法可以看出，传统MPC模型与其无感知变体之间的核心差异体现在单轮算法领域。探索能表征这种差异的基础性问题是个值得关注的研究方向，我们的工作可视为该方向的阶段性成果。

### 3.2 A Non-Oblivious Join Algorithm

This subsection will present an algorithm, which is not communication oblivious, to compute the result of a(G,N)-join $\mathcal{Q}$ with load $\widetilde{O}\left( {N/{p}^{1/\psi }}\right)$ w.h.p.. The reason for describing this non-oblivious version is to allow the reader to draw a direct comparison with the solution of [19]. As will be clear, a key new idea is to apply the concentration bound of Lemma 3.2 locally on each machine, rather than globally on the entire join (as was done in [19]). This in turn requires us to decrease the "heavy threshold" from $N/p$ to $N/{p}^{2}$ . ### 3.3 Making the Algorithm Oblivious

### 3.3 实现算法的 oblivious（无意识）化

Our algorithm in Section 3.2 enjoys a simple communication pattern. It can be modified into a communication-oblivious version by padding enough dummy words to make the length of each message consistent with the "worst" case.

我们在3.2节的算法具有简单的通信模式。通过填充足够的虚拟字使每条消息长度与"最坏"情况一致，可将其修改为通信 oblivious（无意识）版本。

Let us start with step $\mathbf{{S1}}$ ,where machine $i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ transmits the same message,which contains ${R}^{\left( i,\text{ heavy }\right) }$ for each $R \in  Q$ ,to every other machine. This message can have a maximum length of ${\text{len}}_{\text{heavy }} = O\left( {p}^{2\alpha }\right)$ words. Whenever this message is shorter than ${\text{len}}_{\text{heavy }}$ ,expand it with dummy words into length ${\text{len}}_{\text{heavy }}$ .

从步骤$\mathbf{{S1}}$开始，机器$i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$向其他每台机器传输相同消息，其中包含针对每个$R \in  Q$的${R}^{\left( i,\text{ heavy }\right) }$。该消息最大长度为${\text{len}}_{\text{heavy }} = O\left( {p}^{2\alpha }\right)$字。若消息短于${\text{len}}_{\text{heavy }}$，则用虚拟字扩展至${\text{len}}_{\text{heavy }}$长度。

In step $\mathbf{{S2}}$ ,for each proper subset $\mathcal{Z} \subset  \mathcal{V}$ ,machine $i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ transmits a possibly different message to each other machine $j \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ ,whose length is bounded by an integer ${\operatorname{len}}_{\mathcal{Z}} = \widetilde{O}\left( {M/{p}^{1/\tau \left( {\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}\right) }}\right)$ w.h.p. (Lemma 3.3). Whenever this message is shorter than ${\operatorname{len}}_{Z} -$ including an empty message - expand it with dummy words into length ${le}{n}_{Z}$ . However,it is possible for the message to be actually longer than ${\operatorname{len}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$ (this happens when the high-probability event in Lemma 3.3 does not occur). In that scenario,machine $i$ (arbitrarily) trims the message at the length of ${\operatorname{len}}_{Z}$ and sends the trimmed message to machine $j$ anyway; when this happens,we say that the algorithm errs.

在步骤$\mathbf{{S2}}$中，对于每个真子集$\mathcal{Z} \subset  \mathcal{V}$，机器$i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$向其他每台机器$j \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$传输可能不同的消息，其长度高概率(w.h.p.)受整数${\operatorname{len}}_{\mathcal{Z}} = \widetilde{O}\left( {M/{p}^{1/\tau \left( {\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}\right) }}\right)$限制（引理3.3）。当消息短于${\operatorname{len}}_{Z} -$（包括空消息）时，用虚拟字扩展至${le}{n}_{Z}$长度。但消息实际可能超过${\operatorname{len}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$（当引理3.3中的高概率事件未发生时）。此时机器$i$（任意地）将消息截断至${\operatorname{len}}_{Z}$长度后仍发送给机器$j$；这种情况称为算法出错。

In the above modified algorithm,every machine sends precisely ${le}{n}_{\text{heavy }} + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Z \subset  \mathcal{V}}}{le}{n}_{Z}$ words to every other machine. It therefore exhibits a deterministic communication pattern for all(G,N)-joins - this is true regardless of whether the algorithm errs. The load is always $p \cdot  \left( {{\operatorname{len}}_{\text{heavy }} + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Z \subset  \mathcal{V}}}{\operatorname{len}}_{Z}}\right)$ , which is $\widetilde{O}\left( {N/{p}^{1/\psi }}\right)$ as analyzed in the previous subsection. The algorithm computes the join result correctly if it does not err (an adversary cannot tell whether the algorithm has erred because everything is encrypted). Due to Lemma 3.3, by choosing constants appropriately, we can reduce the probability that the algorithm errs to at most $1/{p}^{c}$ for an arbitrarily large constant $c$ . This completes the proof of Theorem 3.1.

在上述修改后的算法中，每台机器精确发送${le}{n}_{\text{heavy }} + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Z \subset  \mathcal{V}}}{le}{n}_{Z}$字给其他每台机器。因此对所有(G,N)-连接都呈现确定性通信模式——无论算法是否出错皆成立。负载恒为$p \cdot  \left( {{\operatorname{len}}_{\text{heavy }} + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Z \subset  \mathcal{V}}}{\operatorname{len}}_{Z}}\right)$，即前文分析的$\widetilde{O}\left( {N/{p}^{1/\psi }}\right)$。若算法未出错则正确计算连接结果（因全程加密，敌手无法判断是否出错）。根据引理3.3，通过适当选择常数，可将算法出错概率降至任意大常数$c$对应的$1/{p}^{c}$。至此完成定理3.1的证明。

## A Basic Concepts of Hypergraphs

## 超图基本概念

Let $\mathcal{G} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ be a hypergraph with $\mathcal{E}$ being a multi-set where each element,called an edge,is a non-empty subset of $\mathcal{V}$ (the set of vertices). A fractional edge packing is a function $w : \mathcal{E} \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ (mapping each edge $e \in  \mathcal{E}$ to a weight $w\left( e\right)$ between 0 and 1) such that,for any attribute $X \in  \mathcal{V}$ , we have $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E} : X \in  e}}w\left( e\right)  \leq  1$ (i.e.,the weight sum of all the edges containing $X$ is at most 1). The total weight of $w$ is $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}w\left( e\right)$ ,i.e.,the weight sum of all the edges in $\mathcal{E}$ . The fractional edge packing number of $\mathcal{G}$ ,denoted as $\tau \left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ ,is defined as the maximum total weight of all the fractional edge packings of $\mathcal{G}$ .

设$\mathcal{G} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$为超图，其中$\mathcal{E}$是多集合，其每个元素（称为边）都是$\mathcal{V}$（顶点集）的非空子集。分数边填充是一个函数$w : \mathcal{E} \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$（将每条边$e \in  \mathcal{E}$映射到0到1之间的权重$w\left( e\right)$），使得对于任意属性$X \in  \mathcal{V}$，满足$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E} : X \in  e}}w\left( e\right)  \leq  1$（即包含$X$的所有边的权重和至多为1）。$w$的总权重为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  \mathcal{E}}}w\left( e\right)$，即$\mathcal{E}$中所有边的权重和。$\mathcal{G}$的分数边填充数记作$\tau \left( \mathcal{G}\right)$，定义为$\mathcal{G}$所有分数边填充的最大总权重。

Dual to fractional edge packing is the notion of fractional vertex cover, which is a function $w : \mathcal{V} \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ (mapping each vertex $X \in  \mathcal{V}$ to a weight $w\left( X\right)$ between 0 and 1) such that,for any edge $e \in  \mathcal{E}$ ,we have $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{X \in  \mathcal{V} : X \in  e}}w\left( X\right)  \geq  1$ (i.e.,the weight sum of all the vertices in $e$ is at least 1). The total weight of $w$ is $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{X \in  \mathcal{V}}}w\left( X\right)$ ,i.e.,the weight sum of all the vertices in $\mathcal{V}$ . The fractional vertex cover number of $\mathcal{G}$ is defined as the minimum total weight of all the fractional vertex covers of $\mathcal{G}$ . It can be shown [23] that the fractional vertex cover number of $\mathcal{G}$ is always identical to the fractional edge packing number $\tau \left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ .

与分数边填充对偶的是分数顶点覆盖的概念，这是一个函数$w : \mathcal{V} \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$（将每个顶点$X \in  \mathcal{V}$映射到0到1之间的权重$w\left( X\right)$），使得对于任意边$e \in  \mathcal{E}$，满足$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{X \in  \mathcal{V} : X \in  e}}w\left( X\right)  \geq  1$（即$e$中所有顶点的权重和至少为1）。$w$的总权重为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{X \in  \mathcal{V}}}w\left( X\right)$，即$\mathcal{V}$中所有顶点的权重和。$\mathcal{G}$的分数顶点覆盖数定义为$\mathcal{G}$所有分数顶点覆盖的最小总权重。文献[23]证明$\mathcal{G}$的分数顶点覆盖数始终等于分数边填充数$\tau \left( \mathcal{G}\right)$。

Given a proper subset $\mathcal{Z} \subset  \mathcal{V}$ ,we define ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$ as the hypergraph obtained by "removing" the attributes of $\mathcal{Z}$ from $\mathcal{G}$ . Specifically:

给定真子集$\mathcal{Z} \subset  \mathcal{V}$，我们定义${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$为通过从$\mathcal{G}$"移除"$\mathcal{Z}$属性得到的超图。具体而言：

- The vertex set of ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$ is $\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  \mathcal{Z}$ .

- ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$的顶点集是$\mathcal{V} \smallsetminus  \mathcal{Z}$

- The edges of ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$ are decided as follows: for each edge $e \in  \mathcal{G}$ ,we add the edge $e \smallsetminus  \mathcal{Z}$ to ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$ if $e \smallsetminus  Z \neq  \varnothing$ .

- ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$的边按如下规则确定：对于每条边$e \in  \mathcal{G}$，当$e \smallsetminus  Z \neq  \varnothing$时，我们将边$e \smallsetminus  \mathcal{Z}$加入${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$

We will refer to ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$ as a residual graph of $\mathcal{G}$ . Specially,if $\mathcal{Z} = \varnothing$ ,then ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}} = \mathcal{G}$ .

我们将把${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$称为$\mathcal{G}$的残差图。特别地，若$\mathcal{Z} = \varnothing$，则${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}} = \mathcal{G}$。

For each proper subset $\mathcal{Z} \subset  \mathcal{V}$ ,the residual graph ${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$ has its own fractional edge packing number $\tau \left( {\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}\right)$ . The maximum such number of all possible $\mathcal{Z}$ is the edge quasi-packing number $\psi \left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ of $\mathcal{G}$ ,or formally:

对于每个真子集$\mathcal{Z} \subset  \mathcal{V}$，残差图${\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}$都有其自身的分数边填充数$\tau \left( {\mathcal{G}}_{\mathcal{Z}}\right)$。所有可能$\mathcal{Z}$中的最大该数值即为$\mathcal{G}$的边拟填充数$\psi \left( \mathcal{G}\right)$，形式化表述为：

$$
\psi \left( \mathcal{G}\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{{Z \subset  \mathcal{V}}}\tau \left( {\mathcal{G}}_{Z}\right)  \tag{26}
$$

Due to the equivalence between fractional edge packing number and fractional vertex cover number, the value $\psi \left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ can alternatively be defined as the maximum fractional vertex cover number of all residual graphs ${Gz}$ of $\mathcal{G}$ .

由于分数边填充数与分数顶点覆盖数等价，值$\psi \left( \mathcal{G}\right)$也可定义为$\mathcal{G}$所有残差图${Gz}$的最大分数顶点覆盖数。

## B A Skew-Free Gathering Algorithm

## B 无偏斜聚集算法

In this section, we present a deterministic communication-oblivious algorithm to solve the skew-free gathering problem (parameterized by integer $t$ ) with load $O\left( {N/{p}^{1/t}}\right)$ ,matching the lower bound in Theorem 2.1.

本节提出一种确定性通信无关算法，以负载$O\left( {N/{p}^{1/t}}\right)$解决无偏斜聚集问题（由整数$t$参数化），达到定理2.1中的下界。

Our algorithm is in essence an adaptation of the "hyper-cube algorithm" in [4]. Define $h = \left\lfloor  {p}^{1/t}\right\rfloor$ , and order the points in the $t$ -dimensional space ${\left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack  }^{t}$ lexicographically. For each $i \in  \left\lbrack  {h}^{t}\right\rbrack$ ,assign machine $i$ to the $i$ -th point in ${\left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack  }^{t}$ under the lexicographic order. Note that some machines (precisely, $p - {h}^{t}$ machines) are not assigned to any point.

本算法本质上是文献[4]中"超立方体算法"的改编。定义$h = \left\lfloor  {p}^{1/t}\right\rfloor$，并按字典序排列$t$维空间${\left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack  }^{t}$中的点。对每个$i \in  \left\lbrack  {h}^{t}\right\rbrack$，将机器$i$分配给字典序下${\left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack  }^{t}$中的第$i$个点。注意部分机器（准确说是$p - {h}^{t}$台）未被分配任何点。

Divide domain $\left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ into $h$ disjoint intervals ${I}_{1},{I}_{2},\ldots ,{I}_{h}$ ,ordered in ascending order of their starting points, such that:

将域$\left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$划分为$h$个互不相交的区间${I}_{1},{I}_{2},\ldots ,{I}_{h}$，按其起点升序排列，满足：

- each of ${I}_{1},\ldots ,{I}_{h - 1}$ covers $\lfloor p/h\rfloor$ integers;

- 每个${I}_{1},\ldots ,{I}_{h - 1}$覆盖$\lfloor p/h\rfloor$个整数；

- ${I}_{h}$ covers $p - \left( {h - 1}\right) \lfloor p/h\rfloor$ integers.

- ${I}_{h}$覆盖$p - \left( {h - 1}\right) \lfloor p/h\rfloor$个整数。

For each interval ${I}_{c}$ with $c \in  \left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack$ ,we use $\left| {I}_{c}\right|$ to denote the number of integers covered by ${I}_{c}$ . Consider any point $\left( {{c}_{1},{c}_{2},\ldots ,{c}_{t}}\right)  \in  {\left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack  }^{t}$ . Remember that the point corresponds to a machine. We associate the machine with a $t$ -dimensional box ${I}_{{c}_{1}} \times  {I}_{{c}_{2}} \times  \ldots  \times  {I}_{{c}_{t}}$ and will refer to ${I}_{{c}_{j}}$ the machine’s projection on dimension $j \in  \left\lbrack  t\right\rbrack$ .

对于每个满足$c \in  \left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack$的区间${I}_{c}$，用$\left| {I}_{c}\right|$表示${I}_{c}$覆盖的整数数量。考虑任意点$\left( {{c}_{1},{c}_{2},\ldots ,{c}_{t}}\right)  \in  {\left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack  }^{t}$，该点对应一台机器。我们将该机器与$t$维盒子${I}_{{c}_{1}} \times  {I}_{{c}_{2}} \times  \ldots  \times  {I}_{{c}_{t}}$关联，并称${I}_{{c}_{j}}$为机器在第$j \in  \left\lbrack  t\right\rbrack$维上的投影。

Given a legal initial state of the skew-free gathering problem, each machine - say the one with id $i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack   -$ sends the $N/p$ elements in its local storage to every machine whose projection on at least one dimension covers the value $i$ - there are at most $t \cdot  {h}^{t - 1}$ such machines. This completes the description of our algorithm.

给定无偏斜聚集问题的合法初始状态，每台机器（假设ID为$i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack   -$）会将其本地存储中的$N/p$个元素发送给所有在至少一个维度上的投影覆盖值$i$的机器——这类机器最多有$t \cdot  {h}^{t - 1}$台。至此算法描述完成。

To see the algorithm's correctness, suppose that machines where the special elements are initially placed have ids ${z}_{1},{z}_{2},\ldots ,{z}_{t} \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ . For each $j \in  \left\lbrack  t\right\rbrack$ ,let ${c}_{j}$ be the integer in $\left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack$ such that the interval ${I}_{{c}_{j}}$ covers ${z}_{j}$ . Our algorithm ensures that the machine associated with the $t$ -dimensional box ${I}_{{c}_{1}} \times  {I}_{{c}_{2}} \times  \ldots  \times  {I}_{{c}_{t}}$ receives all the special elements from the machines ${z}_{1},\ldots ,{z}_{t}$ . The algorithm’s communication obliviousness can be verified from the fact that,for each $i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ ,machine $i$ sends exactly $N/p$ elements to a set of machines that is determined in a way independent of how the input elements are initially distributed.

为验证算法正确性，假设初始存放特殊元素的机器ID为${z}_{1},{z}_{2},\ldots ,{z}_{t} \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$。对于每个$j \in  \left\lbrack  t\right\rbrack$，令${c}_{j}$表示$\left\lbrack  h\right\rbrack$中满足区间${I}_{{c}_{j}}$覆盖${z}_{j}$的整数。本算法确保与$t$维盒子${I}_{{c}_{1}} \times  {I}_{{c}_{2}} \times  \ldots  \times  {I}_{{c}_{t}}$关联的机器会接收来自机器${z}_{1},\ldots ,{z}_{t}$的所有特殊元素。算法的通信无关性可通过以下事实验证：对于每个$i \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$，机器$i$会向一组机器发送恰好$N/p$个元素，该机器组的确定方式与输入元素的初始分布无关。

It remains to analyze the algorithm’s load. In terms of sending,each machine delivers $N/p$ elements to at most $t \cdot  {h}^{t - 1} = O\left( {h}^{t - 1}\right)  = O\left( {p}^{\left( {t - 1}\right) /t}\right)$ machines and,thus,transmits $O(\left( {N/p}\right)$ . $\left. {p}^{\left( {t - 1}\right) /t}\right)  = O\left( {N/{p}^{1/t}}\right)$ words. In terms of receiving,the machine corresponding to the $t$ -dimensional box ${I}_{{c}_{1}} \times  {I}_{{c}_{2}} \times  \ldots  \times  {I}_{{c}_{t}}$ receives at most $\left( {N/p}\right)  \cdot  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{t}\left| {I}_{{c}_{j}}\right|$ elements. Among all the intervals ${I}_{1},\ldots ,{I}_{h}$ , the interval ${I}_{h}$ is the longest and covers

接下来分析算法负载。在发送方面，每台机器向最多$t \cdot  {h}^{t - 1} = O\left( {h}^{t - 1}\right)  = O\left( {p}^{\left( {t - 1}\right) /t}\right)$台机器传送$N/p$个元素，因此传输量为$O(\left( {N/p}\right)$个$\left. {p}^{\left( {t - 1}\right) /t}\right)  = O\left( {N/{p}^{1/t}}\right)$字。在接收方面，对应$t$维盒子${I}_{{c}_{1}} \times  {I}_{{c}_{2}} \times  \ldots  \times  {I}_{{c}_{t}}$的机器最多接收$\left( {N/p}\right)  \cdot  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{t}\left| {I}_{{c}_{j}}\right|$个元素。在所有区间${I}_{1},\ldots ,{I}_{h}$中，区间${I}_{h}$最长且覆盖

$$
