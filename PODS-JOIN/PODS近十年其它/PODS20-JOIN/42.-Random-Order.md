## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

As data analytics becomes more crucial to digital systems, so grows the importance of characterizing the database queries that admit a more efficient evaluation. We consider the tractability yardstick of answer enumeration with a polylogarithmic delay after a linear-time preprocessing phase. Such an evaluation is obtained by constructing, in the preprocessing phase, a data structure that supports polylogarithmic-delay enumeration. In this paper, we seek a structure that supports the more demanding task of a "random permutation": polylogarithmic-delay enumeration in truly random order. Enumeration of this kind is required if downstream applications assume that the intermediate results are representative of the whole result set in a statistically valuable manner. An even more demanding task is that of a "random access": polylogarithmic-time retrieval of an answer whose position is given.

随着数据分析在数字系统中的关键性日益凸显，界定可高效评估的数据库查询标准愈发重要。我们采用线性时间预处理后多对数延迟的答案枚举作为易处理性衡量标准。这种评估通过在预处理阶段构建支持多对数延迟枚举的数据结构实现。本文中，我们寻求支持更严苛"随机排列"任务的结构：实现真正随机顺序的多对数延迟枚举。当下游应用需要中间结果以具有统计价值的方式代表整个结果集时，此类枚举成为必需。更为严苛的是"随机访问"任务：根据给定位置在多对数时间内检索答案。

We establish that the free-connex acyclic CQs are tractable in all three senses: enumeration, random-order enumeration, and random access; and in the absence of self-joins, it follows from past results that every other CQ is intractable by each of the three (under some fine-grained complexity assumptions). However, the three yardsticks are separated in the case of a union of CQs (UCQ): while a union of free-connex acyclic CQs has a tractable enumeration, it may (provably) admit no random access. For such UCQs we devise a random-order enumeration whose delay is logarithmic in expectation. We also identify a subclass of UCQs for which we can provide random access with polylogarithmic access time. Finally, we present an implementation and an empirical study that show a considerable practical superiority of our random-order enumeration approach over state-of-the-art alternatives.

我们证实自由连接无环合取查询(CQ)在三种意义上均具易处理性：枚举、随机顺序枚举及随机访问；在无自连接条件下，根据已有研究可推知其他所有CQ在三种标准下均属难解(基于某些精细复杂度假设)。然而在联合合取查询(UCQ)场景中，这三项标准出现分离：虽然自由连接无环CQ的联合具有易处理枚举，但(可证明)可能不支持随机访问。针对此类UCQ，我们设计了期望延迟为对数的随机顺序枚举方案。同时确定了支持多对数访问时间随机访问的UCQ子类。最后通过实现与实证研究，证明我们的随机顺序枚举方法较现有技术方案具有显著实践优势。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

In the effort of reducing the computational cost of database queries to the very least possible, recent years have seen a substantial progress in understanding the fine-grained data complexity of enumerating the query answers. The seminal work of Bagan, Durand, and Grandjean [6] has established that the free-connex acyclic conjunctive queries (or just free-connex ${CQs}$ for short) can be evaluated using an enumeration algorithm with a constant delay between consecutive answers, after a linear-time preprocessing phase. Moreover, their work, combined with that of Brault-Baron [8], established that, in the absence of self-joins (i.e., when every relation occurs at most once), the free-connex CQs are precisely the CQs that have such an evaluation. The lower-bound part of this dichotomy requires some lower-bound assumptions in fine-grained complexity (namely, that neither sparse Boolean matrix-multiplication, nor triangle detection, nor hyperclique detection can be done in linear time). Later generalizations consider unions of CQs (UCQs) [7, 11] and the presence of constraints $\left\lbrack  {7,{10}}\right\rbrack$ .

为将数据库查询的计算成本降至最低，近年来在理解枚举查询答案的细粒度数据复杂度方面取得了重大进展。巴甘、杜兰德和格兰让的开创性工作[6]表明，自由连接无环合取查询(简称自由连接${CQs}$)可通过线性时间预处理后、以恒定延迟连续输出答案的枚举算法进行评估。此外，他们的工作与布劳特-巴伦[8]的研究共同证明：在无自连接条件下(即每个关系至多出现一次时)，自由连接合取查询正是具备此类评估特性的查询。该二分法的下界证明需要基于细粒度复杂度中的某些假设(即稀疏布尔矩阵乘法、三角形检测和超团检测均无法在线性时间内完成)。后续研究将结论推广至合取查询的并集(UCQs)[7,11]及带约束条件$\left\lbrack  {7,{10}}\right\rbrack$的情况。

As a query-evaluation paradigm, the enumeration approach has the important guarantee that the number of intermediate results is proportional to the elapsed processing time. This guarantee is useful when the query is a part of a larger analytics pipeline where the answers are fed into downstream processing such as machine learning, summarisation, and search. The intermediate results can be used to save time by invoking the next-step processing (e.g., as in streaming learning algorithms [28]), computing approximate summaries that improve in time (e.g., as in online aggregation [20, 24]), and presenting the first pages of search results (e.g., as in keyword search over structured data $\left\lbrack  {{19},{21}}\right\rbrack  )$ . Yet,at least the latter two applications make the implicit assumption that the collection of intermediate results is a representative of the entire space of answers. In contrast, the aforementioned constant-delay algorithms enumerate in an order that is a merely an artifact of the tree selected to utilize free-connexity, and hence, intermediate answers may feature an extreme bias. Importantly, there has been a considerable recent progress in understanding the ability to enumerate the answers not just efficiently, but also with a guarantee on the order [14, 29].

作为查询评估范式，枚举方法的重要保证在于中间结果数量与处理时间成正比。当查询作为分析管道的组成部分、其输出将用于机器学习、摘要生成和搜索等下游处理时，该保证尤为实用：中间结果可用于节省后续处理时间(如流式学习算法[28])、生成随时间改进的近似摘要(如在线聚合[20,24])、以及展示搜索结果首页(如结构化数据关键词搜索$\left\lbrack  {{19},{21}}\right\rbrack  )$)。但后两种应用至少隐含假设中间结果集能代表全体答案空间。而现有恒定延迟算法遵循的枚举顺序仅反映利用自由连接特性所选的树结构，可能导致中间结果存在极端偏差。值得注意的是，近期研究在"高效枚举"基础上，进一步探索了"保证输出顺序"的枚举能力[14,29]。

Yet, to be a statistically meaningful representation of the space of answers, the enumeration order needs to be provably random. In this paper, we investigate the task of enumerating answers in a uniformly random order. To be more precise, the goal is to enumerate the answers without repetitions, and the output induces a uniform distribution over the space of permutations over the answer set. We refer to this task as random permutation. Similarly to the recent work on ranked enumeration $\left\lbrack  {{14},{29}}\right\rbrack$ ,our focus here is on achieving a logarithmic or polylogarithmic delay after a linear preprocessing time. Hence, more technically, the goal we seek is to construct in linear-time a data structure that allows to sample query answers without replacement, with a (poly)logarithmic-time per sample. Note that sampling with replacement has been studied in the past $\left\lbrack  {2,{12}}\right\rbrack$ and recently gained a renewed attention [33].

要使枚举顺序成为答案空间的统计学有效表征，必须确保其可证明的随机性。本文研究按均匀随机顺序枚举答案的任务，具体而言：无重复枚举答案，且输出结果在答案集排列空间上形成均匀分布。我们称此任务为随机排列枚举。与近期排序枚举研究$\left\lbrack  {{14},{29}}\right\rbrack$类似，我们聚焦于线性预处理后实现对数或多对数延迟。更技术性地说，目标是构建线性时间数据结构，支持以(多)对数时间/样本的速度进行无放回抽样。值得注意的是，有放回抽样已有既往研究$\left\lbrack  {2,{12}}\right\rbrack$，近期又获新关注[33]。

One way of achieving a random permutation is via random access-a structure that is tied to some enumeration order and, given a position $i$ ,returns the $i$ th answer in the order. Random access, in general, can be seen as an efficient way of accessing the query answers as if they are already computed and stored in an array. One could imagine additional uses for an efficient random-access algorithm. For example, a server implementing a random-access algorithm can provide answers to concurrent users in a stateless manner: the users ask for a range of indices, and the server does not need to keep track of the answers already sent to each user. To satisfy our target of an efficient permutation, we need a random-access structure that can be constructed in linear time (preprocessing) and supports answer retrieval (given $i$ ) in polylogarithmic time. We show that, having this structure at hand, we can use the Fisher-Yates shuffle [16] to design a random permutation with a negligible additive overhead over the preprocessing and enumeration phases.

实现随机排列的一种方法是通过随机访问结构——该结构与某种枚举顺序绑定，给定位置$i$时，能返回该顺序中第$i$个答案。广义而言，随机访问可视为高效获取查询结果的方式，仿佛这些结果已预先计算并存储于数组中。高效的随机访问算法还可能存在其他应用场景，例如：实现该算法的服务器能以无状态方式为并发用户提供响应——用户请求特定索引范围时，服务器无需追踪已向各用户发送的答案。为实现高效排列目标，我们需要支持线性时间构建（预处理）且能在多对数时间内完成答案检索（给定$i$）的随机访问结构。研究表明，借助该结构配合Fisher-Yates洗牌算法[16]，可在预处理和枚举阶段之外以可忽略的附加开销实现随机排列。

So far, we have mentioned three tasks of an increasing demand: (a) enumeration,(b)random permutation,and(c)random access. We show that all three tasks can be performed efficiently (i.e., linear preprocessing time and evaluation with polylogarithmic time per answer) over the class of free-connex CQs. We conclude that within the class of CQs without self-joins, it is the same precise set of queries where these tasks are tractable-the free-connex CQs. (We remind the reader that all mentioned lower bounds are under assumptions in fine-grained complexity.) The existence of a random access for free-connex CQs has been established by Brault-Baron [8]. Here, we devise our own random-access algorithm for free-connex CQs that is simpler and better lends itself to a practical implementation. Moreover, we design our algorithm in such a way that it is accompanied by an inverted access that is needed for our later results on UCQs.

目前我们已探讨三种需求递增的任务：(a)枚举、(b)随机排列、(c)随机访问。研究证明，在自由连接CQs类查询中，这三个任务均可高效完成（即线性预处理时间与多对数时间/答案的评估效率）。由此得出结论：在无自连接的CQs类查询中，这些任务的可处理性边界与自由连接CQs完全重合（需注意所有提及的下界均基于细粒度复杂性假设）。Brault-Baron[8]已证实自由连接CQs存在随机访问可能。本文提出更简洁、更易工程化的自由连接CQs随机访问算法，并通过反向访问机制设计为后续UCQs研究奠定基础。

Note that an alternative approach to our algorithm for random permutation would be to repeatedly sample tuples uniformly with replacement (using known techniques, e.g., [33]) and reject tuples that have already been produced. In expectation, this alternative would have the same total time as our algorithm, namely $O\left( {M\log M}\right)$ where $M$ is the number of answers,due to the coupon collector argument and the fact that the delay of our algorithm is $O\left( {\log M}\right)$ . However,this alternative approach would not have the strict (and deterministic) guarantee that we provide on the delay, and would not even be counted as an enumeration algorithm with a sublinear delay.

值得注意的是，随机排列算法的替代方案可采用带放回的均匀采样技术（如[33]），并拒绝已生成的重复元组。根据赠券收集理论及算法延迟$O\left( {\log M}\right)$的特性，该方案期望总时间$O\left( {M\log M}\right)$（其中$M$为答案数量）与本算法相当。然而，替代方案无法提供我们给出的严格（确定性）延迟保证，甚至不能被归类为具有次线性延迟的枚举算法。

The tractability of enumeration generalizes from free-connex CQs to unions of free-connex CQs [7, 11]. Interestingly, this is no longer the case for random access! The reason is as follows. An efficient random access allows to count the answers; while counting can be done in linear time for free-connex CQs, we show the existence of a union of free-connex CQs where linear-time counting can be used for linear-time triangle detection in a graph. At this point, we are investigating two questions:

枚举的可处理性可从自由连接CQs推广至其并集[7,11]，但随机访问却存在本质差异：高效随机访问允许答案计数，而自由连接CQs的线性时间计数特性，可转化为图中线性时间三角形检测。这引发出两个核心问题：

(1) Can we identify a nontrivial class of UCQs with an efficient random access?

(1) 能否界定具有高效随机访问的非平凡UCQs类？

(2) Can we get an efficient random permutation for unions of free-connex CQs, without requiring a random access?

(2) 能否在不依赖随机访问的前提下，实现自由连接CQs并集的高效随机排列？

For the first question, we identify the class of mutually-compatible ${UCQs}$ (mc-UCQs) and show that every such UCQ has an efficient random access. As for the second question, we show that the answer is positive under the following weakening of the delay guarantee: there is a random permutation where each delay is a geometric random variable with a logarithmic mean. In particular, each delay is logarithmic in expectation.

针对问题一，我们提出互兼容${UCQs}$类(mc-UCQs)，证明该类UCQs均支持高效随机访问。对于问题二，在延迟保证弱化条件下（每次延迟为对数均值的几何随机变量）可获得肯定答案，此时各延迟期望值呈对数级。

Finally, we present an implementation of our random-access and random-permutation algorithms, and present an empirical evaluation. Over the TPC-H benchmark, we compare our random permutation to the approach of using a state-of-the-art random sampler [33], which is designed to produce a uniform sample with replacement, and then remove duplicates as they are detected. The experiments show that our algorithms are not only featuring complexity and statistical guarantees, but also a significant practical improvement. Moreover, the theoretical improvement of Fisher-Yates over our random access for mc-UCQs, compared to our generic algorithm for UCQ random permutation, is not consistently evident in the experiments; there is work to be done on how to leverage the advantages of this class in practice.

最后，我们实现了随机访问与随机排列算法并进行实证评估。在TPC-H基准测试中，相较于采用最先进随机采样器[33]（设计用于带放回均匀采样并去重）的方案，实验表明我们的算法不仅具备复杂度与统计保证优势，更展现出显著的实践改进。值得注意的是，mc-UCQs类查询中Fisher-Yates算法相对于通用UCQ随机排列算法的理论优势，在实验中未呈现一致性体现——如何实践利用该类优势仍需探索。

## 2 PRELIMINARIES

## 2 预备知识

In this section, we provide basic definitions and notation that we will use throughout this paper. For integers $\ell ,m$ we write $\left\lbrack  {\ell ,m}\right\rbrack$ for the set of all integers $i$ with $\ell  \leq  i \leq  m$ .

本节将给出全文使用的基本定义与符号体系。对于整数$\ell ,m$，我们用$\left\lbrack  {\ell ,m}\right\rbrack$表示满足条件$\ell  \leq  i \leq  m$的所有整数$i$构成的集合。

Databases and Queries. A (relational) schema S is a collection of relation symbols $R$ ,each with an associated arity $\operatorname{ar}\left( R\right)$ . A relation $r$ is a set of tuples of constants, where each tuple has the same arity (length). A database $D$ (over the schema S) associates with each relation symbol $R$ a finite relation $r$ ,which we denote by ${R}^{D}$ ,such that $\operatorname{ar}\left( R\right)  = \operatorname{ar}\left( {R}^{D}\right)$ . Notationally,we identify a database $D$ with its finite set of facts $R\left( {{c}_{1},\ldots ,{c}_{k}}\right)$ ,stating that the relation ${R}^{D}$ over the $k$ -ary relation symbol $R$ contains the tuple $\left( {{c}_{1},\ldots ,{c}_{k}}\right)$ .

数据库与查询。关系模式S是关系符号$R$的集合，每个符号具有对应的元数$\operatorname{ar}\left( R\right)$。关系$r$是由常量元组构成的集合，其中每个元组具有相同元数（长度）。数据库$D$（基于模式S）为每个关系符号$R$关联一个有限关系$r$（记作${R}^{D}$），满足$\operatorname{ar}\left( R\right)  = \operatorname{ar}\left( {R}^{D}\right)$。在表示上，我们将数据库$D$等同于其有限事实集$R\left( {{c}_{1},\ldots ,{c}_{k}}\right)$，即声明：基于$k$元关系符号$R$的关系${R}^{D}$包含元组$\left( {{c}_{1},\ldots ,{c}_{k}}\right)$。

A conjunctive query (CQ) over the schema $\mathrm{S}$ is a relational query $Q$ defined by a first-order formula of the form $\exists \overrightarrow{y}\varphi \left( {\overrightarrow{x},\overrightarrow{y}}\right)$ ,where $\varphi$ is a conjunction of atomic formulas of the form $R\left( \overrightarrow{t}\right)$ with variables among those in $\overrightarrow{x}$ and $\overrightarrow{y}$ . We write a CQ $Q$ shortly as a logic rule, that is,an expression of the form $Q\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)  :  - {R}_{1}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,{R}_{n}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{n}\right)$ where each ${R}_{i}$ is a relation symbol of $\mathrm{S}$ ,each ${\overrightarrow{t}}_{i}$ is a tuple of variables and constants with the same arity as ${R}_{i}$ ,and $\overrightarrow{x}$ is a tuple of $k$ variables from ${\overrightarrow{t}}_{1},\ldots ,{\overrightarrow{t}}_{n}$ . We call $Q\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)$ the head of $Q$ ,and ${R}_{1}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,{R}_{n}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{n}\right)$ the body of $Q$ . Each ${R}_{i}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{i}\right)$ is an atom of $Q$ . We use $\operatorname{Vars}\left( Q\right)$ and $\operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right)$ to denote the sets of variables that occur in the CQ $Q$ and the atom $\alpha$ ,respectively. The variables occurring in the head are called the head variables, and we make the standard safety assumption that every head variable occurs at least once in the body. The variables occurring in the body but not in the head are existentially quantified, and are called the existential variables. A CQ with no existential variables is called a full join query.

模式$\mathrm{S}$上的合取查询(CQ)是通过形如$\exists \overrightarrow{y}\varphi \left( {\overrightarrow{x},\overrightarrow{y}}\right)$的一阶公式定义的关系查询$Q$，其中$\varphi$是由原子公式$R\left( \overrightarrow{t}\right)$构成的合取式，这些原子公式的变量来自$\overrightarrow{x}$和$\overrightarrow{y}$。我们将CQ$Q$简写为逻辑规则，即形如$Q\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)  :  - {R}_{1}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,{R}_{n}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{n}\right)$的表达式：其中每个${R}_{i}$是模式$\mathrm{S}$的关系符号，每个${\overrightarrow{t}}_{i}$是与${R}_{i}$同元数的变量和常量元组，$\overrightarrow{x}$是来自${\overrightarrow{t}}_{1},\ldots ,{\overrightarrow{t}}_{n}$的$k$个变量构成的元组。$Q\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)$称为查询$Q$的头部，${R}_{1}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,{R}_{n}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{n}\right)$称为查询$Q$的主体。每个${R}_{i}\left( {\overrightarrow{t}}_{i}\right)$都是查询$Q$的原子。我们用$\operatorname{Vars}\left( Q\right)$和$\operatorname{Vars}\left( \alpha \right)$分别表示CQ$Q$和原子$\alpha$中出现的变量集合。出现在头部的变量称为头部变量，我们采用标准安全性假设：每个头部变量至少在主体中出现一次。出现在主体但未在头部出现的变量是存在量化的，称为存在变量。不含存在变量的CQ称为完全连接查询。

We usually omit the explicit specification of the schema S, and simply assume that it is the one that consists of the relation symbols that occur in the query at hand.

我们通常省略模式S的显式说明，仅假定其由当前查询中出现的关系符号构成。

A homomorphism from a CQ $Q$ to a database $D$ is a mapping of the variables in $Q$ to the constants of $D$ ,such that every atom of $Q$ is mapped to a fact of $D$ . Each such homomorphism $h$ yields an answer to $Q$ ,which is obtained from $\overrightarrow{x}$ by replacing every variable in $\overrightarrow{x}$ with the constant it is mapped to by $h$ . We denote by $Q\left( D\right)$ the set of all answers to $Q$ on $D$ .

从CQ查询$Q$到数据库$D$的同态映射，是将$Q$中的变量映射到$D$常量的过程，使得$Q$的每个原子都被映射为$D$中的一个事实。每个这样的同态映射$h$都会生成$Q$的一个答案，该答案通过$h$将$\overrightarrow{x}$中每个变量替换为其映射的常量而获得。我们用$Q\left( D\right)$表示$Q$在$D$上所有答案的集合。

We say that a database $D$ is globally consistent with respect to $Q$ if each fact in $D$ agrees with some answer in $Q\left( D\right)$ ; that is,there exists a homomorphism from $Q$ to $D$ and an atom of $Q$ such that the homomorphism maps the atom to the fact.

当数据库$D$中每个事实都与$Q\left( D\right)$中某个答案一致时，我们称$D$相对于$Q$是全局一致的；即存在从$Q$到$D$的同态映射以及$Q$的一个原子，使得该同态将原子映射为该事实。

A self-join in a CQ $Q$ is a pair of distinct atoms over the same relation symbol. We say that $Q$ is self-join free if it has no self-joins, that is, every relation symbol occurs at most once in the body.

CQ查询$Q$中的自连接是指同一关系符号上的一对不同原子。若$Q$不含自连接（即每个关系符号在查询体中至多出现一次），则称其为无自连接查询。

To each CQ $Q\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)  :  - {\alpha }_{1}\left( {\overrightarrow{x},\overrightarrow{y}}\right) ,\ldots ,{\alpha }_{k}\left( {\overrightarrow{x},\bar{y}}\right)$ we associate a hyper-graph ${\mathcal{H}}_{Q}$ where the nodes are the variables in $\operatorname{Vars}\left( Q\right)$ ,and the edges are $E = \left\{  {{e}_{1},\ldots ,{e}_{k}}\right\}$ such that ${e}_{i} = \operatorname{Vars}\left( {\alpha }_{i}\right)$ . Hence,the nodes of ${\mathcal{H}}_{Q}$ are $\overrightarrow{x} \cup  \overrightarrow{y}$ ,and the hyperedge ${e}_{i}$ includes all the variables that appear in ${\alpha }_{i}$ . A CQ $Q$ is acyclic if its hypergraph is acyclic. That is,there exists a tree $T$ (called a join-tree of $Q$ ) such that $\operatorname{nodes}\left( T\right)  = \operatorname{edges}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{Q}\right)$ ,and for every $v \in  \operatorname{nodes}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{Q}\right)$ ,the nodes of $T$ that contain $v$ form a (connected) subtree of $T$ . The CQ $Q$ is free-connex if $Q$ is acyclic and ${\mathcal{H}}_{Q}$ remains acyclic when adding a hyperedge that consists of the free variables of $Q$ .

为每个CQ查询$Q\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)  :  - {\alpha }_{1}\left( {\overrightarrow{x},\overrightarrow{y}}\right) ,\ldots ,{\alpha }_{k}\left( {\overrightarrow{x},\bar{y}}\right)$关联一个超图${\mathcal{H}}_{Q}$，其节点是$\operatorname{Vars}\left( Q\right)$中的变量，边是满足${e}_{i} = \operatorname{Vars}\left( {\alpha }_{i}\right)$的$E = \left\{  {{e}_{1},\ldots ,{e}_{k}}\right\}$。因此${\mathcal{H}}_{Q}$的节点是$\overrightarrow{x} \cup  \overrightarrow{y}$，而超边${e}_{i}$包含${\alpha }_{i}$中出现的所有变量。当超图无环时，称CQ查询$Q$是无环的——即存在树结构$T$（称为$Q$的连接树），使得$\operatorname{nodes}\left( T\right)  = \operatorname{edges}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{Q}\right)$，且对于每个$v \in  \operatorname{nodes}\left( {\mathcal{H}}_{Q}\right)$，包含$v$的$T$节点构成$T$的（连通）子树。若$Q$无环且加入由$Q$自由变量组成的超边后${\mathcal{H}}_{Q}$仍保持无环，则称$Q$是自由连接的。

A union of ${CQs}\left( \mathrm{{UCQ}}\right)$ is a query of the form ${Q}_{1}\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)$ , where every ${Q}_{i}$ is a CQ with the sequence $\overrightarrow{x}$ of head variables. The set of answers to ${Q}_{1}\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)$ over a database $D$ is,naturally, the union ${Q}_{1}\left( D\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( D\right)$ .

${CQs}\left( \mathrm{{UCQ}}\right)$的并集是形如${Q}_{1}\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)$的查询，其中每个${Q}_{i}$都是具有头部变量序列$\overrightarrow{x}$的CQ查询。在数据库$D$上，${Q}_{1}\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( \overrightarrow{x}\right)$的答案集合自然是各查询答案的并集${Q}_{1}\left( D\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( D\right)$。

Computation Model. An enumeration problem $P$ is a collection of pairs(I,Y)where $I$ is an input and $Y$ is a finite set of answers for $I$ , denoted by $P\left( I\right)$ . An enumeration algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ for an enumeration problem $P$ is an algorithm that consists of two phases: preprocessing and enumeration. During preprocessing, $\mathcal{A}$ is given an input $I$ ,and it builds certain data structures. During the enumeration phase, $\mathcal{A}$ can access the data structures built during preprocessing, and it emits the answers $P\left( I\right)$ ,one by one,without repetitions. We denote the running time of the preprocessing phase by ${t}_{p}$ . The time between printing any two answers during the enumeration phase is called delay,and is denoted by ${t}_{d}$ .

计算模型。枚举问题$P$是由输入$I$与其对应有限答案集$Y$构成的二元组(I,Y)集合，记作$P\left( I\right)$。枚举算法$\mathcal{A}$包含预处理和枚举两个阶段：预处理阶段接收输入$I$并构建数据结构；枚举阶段通过访问这些结构逐个无重复地输出答案$P\left( I\right)$。预处理时间记为${t}_{p}$，枚举阶段任意两个答案间的输出间隔称为延迟，记作${t}_{d}$。

In this paper, an enumeration problem will refer to a query (namely,a CQ or a UCQ) $Q$ ,the input $I$ is a database $D$ ,and the answer set $Y$ is $Q\left( D\right)$ . Hence,we adopt data complexity,where the query is treated as fixed. We use a variant of the Random Access Machine (RAM) model with uniform cost measure that has been adopted as a standard computation model in a substantial part of the database theory literature, cf. e.g. [5, 17, 22]. This model enables the construction of lookup tables of polynomial size that can be queried in constant time. In particular, it is possible to compute the semi-join of two relations in linear time. For the randomized version of this model it is reasonable to assume that it takes constant time to draw a random number of size polynomial in the input size.

本文中，枚举问题特指查询（即CQ或UCQ）$Q$，其输入$I$为数据库$D$，答案集$Y$即$Q\left( D\right)$。采用数据复杂度视角（查询视为固定），使用数据库理论文献[5,17,22]中标准的随机存取机(RAM)模型变体——该模型支持在常数时间内查询多项式规模的查找表，并能线性时间计算两个关系的半连接。其随机版本可假设在输入规模多项式范围内生成随机数耗时恒定。

Complexity Hypotheses. Our conditional optimality results rely on the following hypotheses on the hardness of algorithmic problems.

复杂性假设。我们的条件最优性结果基于以下算法问题难解性假设。

The hypothesis SPARSE-BMM states that there is no algorithm that multiplies two Boolean matrices (represented as lists of their non-zero entries) over the Boolean semiring in time ${m}^{1 + o\left( 1\right) }$ ,where $m$ is the number of non-zero entries in $A,B$ ,and ${AB}$ . The best known running time for this problem is $O\left( {m}^{4/3}\right) \left\lbrack  4\right\rbrack$ ,which remains true even if the matrix multiplication exponent ${}^{1}\omega$ is equal to 2 .

SPARSE-BMM假设断言：不存在在布尔半环上以${m}^{1 + o\left( 1\right) }$时间（其中$m$表示$A,B$中非零项数量，且${AB}$）计算两个布尔矩阵（以非零项列表表示）乘积的算法。该问题当前最佳时间复杂度为$O\left( {m}^{4/3}\right) \left\lbrack  4\right\rbrack$，即使矩阵乘法指数${}^{1}\omega$等于2时亦然。

By TRIANGLE we denote the hypothesis that there is no $O\left( m\right)$ time algorithm that detects whether a graph with $m$ edges contains a triangle. The best known algorithm for this problem runs in time ${m}^{{2\omega }/\left( {\omega  + 1}\right)  + o\left( 1\right) }\left\lbrack  3\right\rbrack$ ,which is $\Omega \left( {m}^{4/3}\right)$ even if $\omega  = 2$ . The TriANGLE hypothesis is also implied by a slightly stronger conjecture in [1].

TRIANGLE假设指：不存在$O\left( m\right)$时间算法可检测含$m$条边的图中是否存在三角形。当前最佳算法时间复杂度为${m}^{{2\omega }/\left( {\omega  + 1}\right)  + o\left( 1\right) }\left\lbrack  3\right\rbrack$，即使$\omega  = 2$时仍为$\Omega \left( {m}^{4/3}\right)$。该假设亦由文献[1]中更强猜想所蕴含。

A $\left( {k + 1,k}\right)$ -hyperclique is a set of $k + 1$ vertices in a hypergraph such that every $k$ -element subset is a hyperedge. By HYPERCLIQUE we denote the hypothesis that for every $k \geq  3$ there is no time $O\left( m\right)$ algorithm for deciding the existence of a $\left( {k + 1,k}\right)$ -hyperclique in an $k$ -uniform hypergraph with $m$ hyperedges. This hypothesis is implied by the(l,k)-HYPERCLIQUE conjecture proposed in [25].

$\left( {k + 1,k}\right)$-超团指超图中满足每$k$元子集皆为超边的$k + 1$个顶点集合。HYPERCLIQUE假设认为：对任意$k \geq  3$，不存在$O\left( m\right)$时间算法可判定含$m$条超边的$k$-均匀超图中是否存在$\left( {k + 1,k}\right)$-超团。此假设由文献[25]提出的(l,k)-HYPERCLIQUE猜想所蕴含。

While the three hypotheses are not as established as classical complexity assumptions (like $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ ),their refutation would lead to unexpected breakthroughs in algorithms, which would be achieved when improving the relevant methods in our paper.

尽管这三个假设不如经典复杂性假设（如$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$）那样确立，但若能证伪它们，将意味着算法领域取得意外突破——这正是改进本文相关方法所能达成的。

## 3 枚举问题类别

In this section, we define three classes of enumeration problems and discuss the relationship between them.

本节定义三类枚举问题并探讨其相互关系。

### 3.1 Definitions

### 3.1 定义

We write $d$ to denote a function from the positive integers ${\mathbb{N}}_{ \geq  1}$ to the non-negative reals ${\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0}$ ,and $d =$ const, $d = \operatorname{lin},d = {\log }^{c}$ (for $c \geq  1)$ mean $d\left( n\right)  = 1,d\left( n\right)  = n,d\left( n\right)  = {\log }^{c}\left( n\right)$ ,respectively.

我们用$d$表示从正整数集${\mathbb{N}}_{ \geq  1}$到非负实数集${\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0}$的函数，其中$d =$为常数，$d = \operatorname{lin},d = {\log }^{c}$（对于$c \geq  1)$分别表示$d\left( n\right)  = 1,d\left( n\right)  = n,d\left( n\right)  = {\log }^{c}\left( n\right)$）。

Definition 3.1. Let $d$ be a function from ${\mathbb{N}}_{ \geq  1}$ to ${\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0}$ . We define $\operatorname{Enum}\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$ to be the class of enumeration problems for which there exists an enumeration algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ such that for every input $I$ it holds that ${t}_{p} \in  O\left( \left| I\right| \right)$ and ${t}_{d} \in  O\left( {d\left( \left| I\right| \right) }\right)$ . Furthermore,Enum $\langle \operatorname{lin},$ polylog $\rangle$ is the union of $\operatorname{Enum}\left\langle  {\operatorname{lin},{\log }^{c}}\right\rangle$ for all $c \geq  1$ .

定义3.1. 设$d$为从${\mathbb{N}}_{ \geq  1}$到${\mathbb{R}}_{ \geq  0}$的函数。定义$\operatorname{Enum}\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$为满足以下条件的枚举问题类：存在枚举算法$\mathcal{A}$，使得对于每个输入$I$都满足${t}_{p} \in  O\left( \left| I\right| \right)$和${t}_{d} \in  O\left( {d\left( \left| I\right| \right) }\right)$。此外，Enum$\langle \operatorname{lin},$polylog$\rangle$是所有$c \geq  1$对应的$\operatorname{Enum}\left\langle  {\operatorname{lin},{\log }^{c}}\right\rangle$的并集。

A random-permutation algorithm for an enumeration problem $P$ is an enumeration algorithm where every emission is done uniformly at random. That is, at every emission, every until then not yet emitted tuple has equal probability of being emitted. As a result, if $\left| {P\left( I\right) }\right|  = n$ ,every ordering of the answers $P\left( I\right)$ has probability $\frac{1}{n!}$ of representing the order in which $\mathcal{A}$ prints the answers.

针对枚举问题$P$的随机排列算法，其每次输出都是均匀随机生成的。即在每次输出时，所有此前未输出的元组都有相同的被选概率。因此若$\left| {P\left( I\right) }\right|  = n$，则答案序列$P\left( I\right)$的每种排列对应$\mathcal{A}$输出答案顺序的概率均为$\frac{1}{n!}$。

### 3.2 Random-Access and Random-Permutation

### 3.2 随机访问与随机排列

We now show that, under certain conditions, it suffices to devise a random-access algorithm in order to obtain a random-permutation algorithm. To achieve this, we need to produce a random permutation of the indices of the answers.

现证明在特定条件下，设计随机访问算法即可获得随机排列算法。为此需要生成答案索引的随机排列。

Note that the trivial approach of producing the permutation upfront will not work: the length of the permutation is the number of answers, which can be much larger than the size of the input; however, we want to produce the first answer after linear time in the size of the input.

注意首先生成完整排列的简单方法不可行：排列长度等于答案数量，可能远大于输入规模；而我们需要在输入规模的线性时间内输出首个答案。

Instead, we adapt a known random-permutation algorithm, the Fisher-Yates Shuffle [16], so that it works with constant delay after constant preprocessing time. The original version of the Fisher-Yates Shuffle (also known as Knuth Shuffle) [16] generates a random permutation in time linear in the number of items in the permutation, which in our setting is polynomial in the size of the input. It initializes an array containing the numbers $0,\ldots ,n - 1$ . Then,at each step $i$ ,it chooses a random index, $j$ ,greater than or equal to $i$ and swaps the chosen cell with the $i$ th cell. At the end of this procedure, the array contains a random permutation. Proposition 3.6 describes an adaptation of this procedure that runs with constant delay and constant preprocessing time in the RAM model.

## 4 RANDOM-ACCESS FOR CQS

## 4 CQS的随机访问

In this section, we discuss random access for CQs. For enumeration, the characterization of CQs with respect to Enum $\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$ follows from known results of Bagan, Durand, Grandjean, and Brault-Baron.

本节探讨合取查询(CQs)的随机访问。关于Enum$\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$的CQs分类，可基于Bagan、Durand、Grandjean及Brault-Baron的已知结论推导。

THEOREM 4.1 ([6,8]). Let $Q$ be a ${CQ}$ . If $Q$ is free-connex,then it is in Enum $\langle$ lin,const $\rangle$ . Otherwise,if it is also self-join-free,then it is not in Enum (lin, polylog) assuming SPARSE-BMM, TRIANGLE, and HYPERCLIQUE.

定理4.1([6,8])。设$Q$为${CQ}$。若$Q$是自由连接的，则属于Enum$\langle$线性常数类$\rangle$；否则对于无自连接的情况，在SPARSE-BMM、TRIANGLE及HYPERCLIQUE假设下不属于Enum(线性, 多对数)类。

Indeed,if the query $Q$ is self-join-free and non-free-connex, there are two cases. If $Q$ is cyclic,then it is not possible to determine whether there exists a first answer to $Q$ in linear time assuming Triangle and Hyperclique [8]. Therefore, $Q$ is not in Enum $\langle \operatorname{lin},\operatorname{lin}\rangle$ . Otherwise,if $Q$ is acyclic,the proof follows along the same lines as the one presented by Bagan et al. [6]. Using the same reduction as defined there, if any acyclic non-free-connex $\mathrm{{CQ}}$ is in $\operatorname{Enum}\left\langle  {\operatorname{lin},{\log }^{c}}\right\rangle$ ,then any two Boolean matrices of size $n \times  n$ can be multiplied in $O\left( {{m}_{1} + {m}_{2} + {m}_{3} \cdot  {\log }^{c}\left( n\right) }\right)$ time,where ${m}_{1},{m}_{2}$ ,and ${m}_{3}$ are the number of non-zero entries in $A,B$ ,and ${AB}$ , respectively. This contradicts spARSE-BMM.

事实上，若查询$Q$不含自连接且非自由连接，则存在两种情况。若$Q$为循环查询，则基于三角形和超团假设[8]，无法在线性时间内判定$Q$是否存在首个答案。因此，$Q$不属于枚举类$\langle \operatorname{lin},\operatorname{lin}\rangle$。反之，若$Q$为无环查询，其证明逻辑与Bagan等人[6]提出的方法一致。采用相同的归约方法，若任何无环非自由连接查询$\mathrm{{CQ}}$属于$\operatorname{Enum}\left\langle  {\operatorname{lin},{\log }^{c}}\right\rangle$类，则大小为$n \times  n$的任意两个布尔矩阵可在$O\left( {{m}_{1} + {m}_{2} + {m}_{3} \cdot  {\log }^{c}\left( n\right) }\right)$时间内完成乘法运算，其中${m}_{1},{m}_{2}$和${m}_{3}$分别表示矩阵$A,B$与${AB}$中非零元素的数量。这与稀疏布尔矩阵乘法(spARSE-BMM)理论相矛盾。

An implication of Theorem 4.1 is that free-connex CQs can be answered with logarithmic delay. Since Brault-Baron [8] proved that there exists a random-access algorithm that works with linear preprocessing and logarithmic access time, we get a strengthening of that fact: free-connex CQs belong to RAccess $\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$ . According to Theorem 3.7, this also shows the tractability of a random-order enumeration, that is, membership in REnum (lin, log).

定理4.1的推论表明：自由连接查询(CQs)可在对数延迟时间内应答。由于Brault-Baron[8]已证明存在一种随机访问算法，仅需线性预处理时间和对数访问时间即可运行，我们进一步强化该结论：自由连接CQs属于随机访问类$\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$。根据定理3.7，这同时证明了随机顺序枚举的可处理性，即其属于线性预处理-对数延迟枚举类(REnum (lin, log))。

In this section, we present a random-access algorithm for free-connex CQs that, compared to Brault-Baron [8], is simpler and better lends itself to a practical implementation. In addition, we devise the algorithm in such a way that it is accompanied by an inverted-access that is needed for our results on UCQs in Section 5. An inverted-access ${I}_{A}$ is an enhancement of a random-access algorithm $A$ with the inverse operation: given an element $e$ ,the inverted-access returns ${I}_{A}\left\lbrack  e\right\rbrack   = j$ such that $A\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack   = e$ . That is,the $j$ th answer in the random-access is $e$ . If $e$ is not an answer,then the inverted-access indicates so by returning "not-an-answer."

本节提出一种针对自由连接查询(Free-connex CQs)的随机访问算法，相较于Brault-Baron[8]的方案更简洁且更易于实践实现。此外，我们特别设计了该算法使其附带反向访问功能，这是第5节处理联合查询(UCQs)时所需的关键组件。反向访问${I}_{A}$是对随机访问算法$A$的增强，支持逆向操作：给定元素$e$时，返回满足$A\left\lbrack  j\right\rbrack   = e$的${I}_{A}\left\lbrack  e\right\rbrack   = j$。即随机访问中第$j$个答案应为$e$。若$e$非有效答案，反向访问将返回"非答案"标识。

Given a relation $R$ ,denote by pAtt ${\mathrm{s}}_{\mathrm{R}}$ the attributes that appear both in $R$ and in its parent. If $R$ is the root,then ${\mathrm{{pAtts}}}_{\mathrm{R}} = \varnothing$ . Given a relation $R$ and an assignment $a$ ,we denote by bucket $\left\lbrack  {S,a}\right\rbrack$ all tuples in $S$ that agree with $a$ over the attributes that $S$ and $a$ have in common. We use this notation also when $a$ is a tuple,by treating the tuple as an assignment from the attributes of its relation to the values it holds (intuitively this is $S \ltimes  a$ ).

The preprocessing starts by partitioning every relation to buckets according to the different assignments to the attributes shared with the parent relation. This can be done in linear time in the RAM model. Then,we compute a weight $\mathrm{w}\left( t\right)$ for each tuple $t$ . This weight represents the number of different answers this tuple agrees with when only joining the relations of the subtree rooted in the current relation. The weight is computed in a leaf-to-root order, where tuples of a leaf relation have weight 1 . The weight of a tuple $t$ in a non-leaf relation $R$ is determined by the product of the weights of the corresponding tuples in the children's relations. These corresponding tuples are the ones that agree with $t$ on the attributes that $R$ shares with its child. The weight of each bucket is the sum of the weights of the tuples it contains. In addition, we assign each tuple $t$ with an index range that starts with startIndex(t) and ends with the startIndex of the following tuple in the bucket (or the total weight of the bucket if this is the last tuple). This represents a partition of the indices from 0 to the bucket weight, such that the length of the range of each tuple is equal to its weight. At the end of preprocessing, the root relation has one bucket (since ${\text{pAtts}}_{\text{root }} = \varnothing$ ),and the weight of this bucket represents the number of answers to the query.

预处理首先根据与父关系共享属性的不同赋值将每个关系划分至桶中。在RAM模型下该操作可在线性时间内完成。接着，我们为每个元组$t$计算权重$\mathrm{w}\left( t\right)$，该权重表示仅连接以当前关系为根的子树关系时，该元组能匹配的不同答案数量。权重按从叶到根的顺序计算，其中叶关系的元组权重为1。非叶关系$R$中元组$t$的权重由其子关系对应元组权重的乘积决定，这些对应元组需在$R$与子关系共享的属性上与$t$一致。每个桶的权重是其包含元组权重之和。此外，我们为每个元组$t$分配起止于startIndex(t)至桶内下一元组startIndex的索引范围（若为末位元组则使用桶总权重），这构成从0到桶权重的索引划分，使得各元组范围长度等于其权重。预处理结束时，根关系仅有一个桶（因${\text{pAtts}}_{\text{root }} = \varnothing$），该桶权重即代表查询的答案总数。

The random-access is done recursively in a root-to-leaf order: we start from the single bucket at the root. At each step we find the tuple $t$ in the current relation that holds the required index in its range (we denote by $t + 1$ the tuple that follows $t$ in the bucket). Then, we assign the rest of the search to the children of the current relation,restricted to the bucket that corresponds to $t$ . Finding $t$ can be done in logarithmic time using binary search ${}^{3}$ . The remaining index ${j}^{\prime } = j -$ startIndex(t)is split into search tasks for the children using the method SPLITINDEX. The split can be seen as representing ${j}^{\prime }$ in a mixed radix numeral system where the units are the bucket weights. In other words, it is done in the same way as an index is split in standard multidimensional arrays: if the last bucket is of weight $m$ ,its index is ${j}^{\prime }{\;\operatorname{mod}\;m}$ ,and the other buckets recursively split between them the index $\left\lfloor  \frac{{j}^{\prime }}{m}\right\rfloor$ .

随机访问以根到叶的递归顺序完成：我们从根节点的单一存储桶开始。每一步在当前关系中查找包含目标索引范围的元组$t$（用$t + 1$表示该存储桶中$t$的后继元组）。然后将剩余搜索任务分配给当前关系的子节点，限定在与$t$对应的存储桶内。通过二分查找${}^{3}$可在对数时间内定位$t$。剩余索引${j}^{\prime } = j -$startIndex(t)通过SPLITINDEX方法拆分为子节点的搜索任务。这种拆分可视为以存储桶权重为基数的混合进制表示法${j}^{\prime }$。换言之，其原理与标准多维数组的索引拆分相同：若末存储桶权重为$m$，其索引为${j}^{\prime }{\;\operatorname{mod}\;m}$，其余存储桶递归拆分索引$\left\lfloor  \frac{{j}^{\prime }}{m}\right\rfloor$。

## 5 UNIONS OF CQS

## 5 CQ的并集

In this section, we discuss the availability of random-order enumeration and random-access in UCQs. We first show that not all UCQs that have efficient enumeration also have efficient random-access. Then we relax the delay requirements and provide an algorithm for the enumeration in random order of a union of sets, and show that the algorithm can be applied for such UCQs. In addition, we identify a subclass of UCQs that do allow for an efficient random-access.

本节讨论UCQ中随机顺序枚举与随机访问的可行性。首先证明并非所有具备高效枚举的UCQ都支持高效随机访问。随后放宽延迟要求，提出针对集合并集随机顺序枚举的算法，并证明该算法适用于此类UCQ。此外，我们还识别出允许高效随机访问的UCQ子类。

If several CQs are in Enum $\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$ ,for some $d$ ,then their union can also be enumerated within the same time bounds [11, 15]. Since our goal is to answer queries in random order, a natural question arises: does the same apply to queries in RAccess $\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$ and $\operatorname{REnum}\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$ ? We show that it does not apply to CQs in RAccess $\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$ . This means that for UCQs we cannot rely on random-access to achieve an efficient random-permutation algorithm as we did for CQs. The following is an example of two free-connex CQs (therefore, each one admits efficient counting, enumeration, random-order enumeration and random-access), but we show that their union is not in RAccess $\langle \operatorname{lin},\operatorname{lin}\rangle$ under TRIANGLE.

若多个CQ（Conjunctive Queries，合取查询）处于枚举$\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$状态，对于某些$d$而言，它们的并集也能在相同时间范围内完成枚举[11, 15]。由于我们的目标是以随机顺序应答查询，一个自然问题随之产生：这一结论是否同样适用于RAccess（随机访问）$\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$和$\operatorname{REnum}\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$中的查询？我们证明该结论不适用于RAccess$\langle \operatorname{lin},d\rangle$中的CQ。这意味着对于UCQ（Union of Conjunctive Queries，合取查询的并集），我们无法像处理CQ那样依赖随机访问来实现高效的随机排列算法。以下是一对自由连接CQ的示例（因此每个查询都支持高效计数、枚举、随机顺序枚举和随机访问），但我们证明在TRIANGLE条件下，它们的并集不属于RAccess$\langle \operatorname{lin},\operatorname{lin}\rangle$。

Note that $\left| {{Q}_{ \cup  }\left( D\right) }\right|  = \left| {{Q}_{1}\left( D\right) }\right|  + \left| {{Q}_{2}\left( D\right) }\right|  - \left| {{Q}_{1}\left( D\right)  \cap  {Q}_{2}\left( D\right) }\right|$ . Example 5.1 shows that (assuming TRIANGLE) RAccess $\langle$ lin, $\log \rangle$ is not closed under union. LEMMA 5.2. Let ${S}_{1},\ldots ,{S}_{k}$ be sets,each supports sampling,testing, deletion and counting in time t. Then, it is possible to enumerate $\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{k}{S}_{j}$ in uniformly random order with expected $O\left( {kt}\right)$ delay.

Algorithm 5 enumerates the union of several sets in uniformly random order. Every iteration starts by choosing a random set and a random element it contains. The choice of set is weighted by the number of elements it contains. If the algorithm would have always printed the element at that stage (after line 3), then an element that appears in two sets would have had twice the probability of being chosen compared to an element that appears in only one set. The following lines correct this bias. We denote by providers all sets that contain the chosen element. Then, the algorithm assigns one owner to this element out of its providers (as the choice of the owner is not important, we arbitrarily choose to take the provider with the minimum index). The element is then deleted from non-owners, and is printed only if the algorithm chooses its owner in line 2 . If the element was reached through a non-owner, then the current iteration "rejects" by printing nothing.

算法5以均匀随机顺序枚举多个集合的并集。每次迭代首先随机选择一个集合及其包含的随机元素，集合选择权重按其元素数量分配。若算法始终在此阶段（第3行后）输出元素，则出现在两个集合中的元素被选概率将是仅出现在单个集合中元素的两倍。后续代码修正此偏差：将所有包含选定元素的集合标记为提供者，并从中任意指定最小索引的提供者作为该元素的归属者。元素随后从非归属者中删除，仅当算法在第2行选择其归属者时才输出。若通过非归属者访问到该元素，则当前迭代以空输出实现"拒绝"。

Algorithm 5 prints the results in a uniformly random order since, in every iteration, every answer remaining in the union has equal probability of being printed. Denote by Choices the set of all possible (chosen, element) pairs that the algorithm may choose in lines 2 and 3. The probability of such a pair is $\frac{\left| {S}_{\text{chosen }}\right| }{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{k}\left| {S}_{j}\right| }\frac{1}{\left| {S}_{\text{chosen }}\right| } = \frac{1}{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{k}\left| {S}_{j}\right| }$ , which is the same for all pairs in Choices. Denote by AccChoices $\subseteq$ Choices the pairs for which ${S}_{\text{chosen }}$ is the owner of element. Line 8 guarantees that an element is printed only when the selections the algorithm makes are in AccChoices. Since every possible answer only appears once as an element in AccChoices, the probability of each element to be printed is $\frac{1}{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{k}\left| {S}_{j}\right| }$ . Therefore,all answers have the same probability of being printed. A printed answer is deleted from all sets containing it, so it will not be printed twice.

算法5实现均匀随机输出的原理在于：每次迭代中并集内剩余答案的打印概率均等。用Choices表示算法第2-3行可能选择的所有(集合,元素)对，每对概率为$\frac{\left| {S}_{\text{chosen }}\right| }{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{k}\left| {S}_{j}\right| }\frac{1}{\left| {S}_{\text{chosen }}\right| } = \frac{1}{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{k}\left| {S}_{j}\right| }$且全局一致。AccChoices$\subseteq$Choices表示元素归属者正确的配对子集，第8行确保仅当选择落入AccChoices时才输出元素。由于每个答案在AccChoices中仅出现一次，其输出概率恒为$\frac{1}{\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{k}\left| {S}_{j}\right| }$，故所有答案输出概率相同。已输出答案将从所有包含集合中删除避免重复。

We now discuss the time complexity. If some iteration rejects an answer, this iteration also deletes it from all non-owner sets. This guarantees that each unique answer will only be rejected once, as it only has one provider in the second time it is seen. This means that the total number of iterations Algorithm 5 performs is bound by twice the number of answers. The number of operations between successive answers is therefore amortized constant. In addition, since by definition $\left| \text{ Choices }\right|  \leq  k\left| \text{ AccChoices }\right|$ ,in every iteration the probability that an answer will be printed is $\frac{\left| \text{ AccChoices }\right| }{\left| \text{ Choices }\right| } \geq  \frac{1}{k}$ . The delay between two successive answers therefore comprises of a constant number of operations both in expectation and in amortized complexity. This proves Lemma 5.2.

时间复杂度分析：当迭代拒绝某答案时，会同步从所有非归属者集合中删除该元素，确保每个唯一答案最多被拒绝一次（因第二次出现时仅有一个提供者）。因此算法5总迭代次数不超过答案数量的两倍，连续答案间的操作次数摊还为常数。根据定义$\left| \text{ Choices }\right|  \leq  k\left| \text{ AccChoices }\right|$，每次迭代中答案输出概率为$\frac{\left| \text{ AccChoices }\right| }{\left| \text{ Choices }\right| } \geq  \frac{1}{k}$，故连续答案间的延迟在期望值和摊还复杂度下均为常数操作量，由此证得引理5.2。

We describe the construction of the data structure. First, we count the number of answers $n$ . As in Algorithm 1,our data structure contains an array $a$ of length $n$ and an integer $i$ . The values $a\left\lbrack  0\right\rbrack  ,\ldots ,a\left\lbrack  {i - 1}\right\rbrack$ represent the indices of the deleted elements,while $a\left\lbrack  i\right\rbrack  ,\ldots ,a\left\lbrack  {n - 1}\right\rbrack$ hold the indices that remain in the set. Similarly, " $a = A$ .NEXT(   )" means that the next output element of the enumeration algorithm for $A$ is produced and that $a$ shall be that element. In case that all elements of $A$ have already been enumerated, A.NEXT(   ) will return the end-of-enumeration message EOE; and in case that $A$ is the empty set,A.FIRST(   ) will return EOE.

我们基于Durand和Strozecki提出的并集技巧[15]，其原理如下：假设$A$和$B$是某个全域$U$的两个（不一定互斥的）子集，且对每个集合都存在枚举其元素的算法。此外，假设对于集合$B$还存在一个成员资格测试算法。目标是枚举$A \cup  B$（按照惯例，所有枚举均不重复）。并集技巧的伪代码见算法6。其中"$a = A$.FIRST()"表示启动$A$的枚举算法并将$a$作为首个输出元素；"$a = A$.NEXT()"表示生成$A$枚举算法的下一个输出元素，且$a$即为该元素。若$A$所有元素均已枚举完毕，A.NEXT()将返回枚举结束信号EOE；若$A$为空集，A.FIRST()将直接返回EOE。

This algorithm starts by enumerating all elements of $A$ in the same order as the enumeration algorithm for $A$ ,but every time it encounters $a \in  A \cap  B$ ,it ignores this element and instead outputs the next available element produced by the enumeration algorithm for $B$ . Once the enumeration of $A$ has terminated,the algorithm proceeds by producing the remaining elements of $B$ . Clearly,Algorithm 6 enumerates all elements in $A \cup  B$ ; and the algorithm’s delay is $O\left( {{d}_{A} + {d}_{B} + {t}_{B}}\right)$ where ${d}_{A}$ and ${d}_{B}$ are the delay of the enumeration algorithms for $A$ and $B$ ,respectively,and ${t}_{B}$ is the time needed for testing membership in $B$ .

该算法首先按照$A$枚举算法的原始顺序输出所有元素，但每当遇到$a \in  A \cap  B$时跳过该元素，转而输出$B$枚举算法生成的下一个可用元素。当$A$枚举完成后，算法继续输出$B$的剩余元素。显然，算法6能枚举$A \cup  B$中的所有元素；其延迟时间为$O\left( {{d}_{A} + {d}_{B} + {t}_{B}}\right)$，其中${d}_{A}$和${d}_{B}$分别是$A$和$B$枚举算法的延迟时间，${t}_{B}$是$B$成员资格测试所需时间。

## 6 IMPLEMENTATION AND EXPERIMENTS

Second, we examine the empirical overhead of RENUM(UCQ) and RENUM(MCUCQ) compared to the cumulative cost of running each RENUM(CQ) for each CQ separately. We describe our implementation of RENUM(CQ), RENUM(UCQ), and RENUM(MCUCQ) in Section 6.1, the experimental setup in Section 6.2, and the results in Section 6.3.

本节介绍本文提出的随机顺序枚举算法的实现与实验评估。${}^{4}$第4章提出的随机顺序CQ枚举算法记为RENUM(CQ)，第5.1节的UCQ算法记为RENUM(UCQ)，第5.2节的mc-UCQ算法记为RENUM(MCUCQ)。实验目标有二：首先比较RENUM(CQ)与重复应用先进采样算法（无放回）[33]及去重操作的实际执行成本；其次分析RENUM(UCQ)和RENUM(MCUCQ)相较于独立运行各CQ的RENUM(CQ)累计成本的实证开销。6.1节描述算法实现，6.2节说明实验设置，6.3节展示结果。

### 6.1 Implementation

### 6.1 实现方案

RENUM(CQ), RENUM(UCQ), and RENUM(MCUCQ) are implemented in c++14 using the standard library (STL), and mainly the unordered STL containers. For instance, we use an unordered map to partition a table into the buckets of Algorithms 2 and 3. Other than STL, the implementation uses Boost to hash complex types such as vectors.

RENUM(CQ)、RENUM(UCQ)和RENUM(MCUCQ)采用C++14标准库(STL)实现，主要使用无序STL容器。例如通过无序映射将表格分割为算法2和3的桶结构。除STL外，实现还使用Boost库对向量等复杂类型进行哈希处理。

The RENUM(CQ) implementation uses a query compiler that generates c++ code for the specific CQ and database schema. Specifically, the code is generated via templates, which are files of c++ code with placeholders. These placeholders stand for query-specific parameters such as the relation names, the attributes and their types, the tree structure of the query, and its head variables. Once these placeholders are filled in and function calls are ordered according to the tree structure,the result is valid $\mathrm{c} +  +$ code.

RENUM(CQ)实现采用了一个查询编译器，该编译器会针对特定CQ和数据库模式生成C++代码。具体而言，代码通过模板生成，这些模板是带有占位符的C++代码文件。这些占位符代表查询特定参数，如关系名称、属性及其类型、查询的树状结构及其头部变量。一旦这些占位符被填充且函数调用根据树状结构排序，结果就是有效的$\mathrm{c} +  +$代码。

As described in Algorithm 5, RENUM(UCQ) uses CQ enumerators as black boxes with an interface of four methods: count, sample, test, and delete. Therefore, in addition to the counting and sampling provided by RENUM(CQ), we implemented deletion and testing as explained in Section 5.1. The latter two require an inverted-access, which we described in Algorithm 4. The inverted-access is compiled only when needed as part of a UCQ enumeration, as it requires nonnegligible preprocessing (to support line 4 of Algorithm 4). Hence, RENUM(CQ) meets the four requirements when inverted-access is activated and the shuffler capable of deletion is used. Other than that, our implementation of Algorithm 5 is fairly straightforward.

如算法5所述，RENUM(UCQ)将CQ枚举器作为具有四种方法接口的黑盒使用：计数、采样、测试和删除。因此，除了RENUM(CQ)提供的计数和采样功能外，我们还按照第5.1节的说明实现了删除和测试功能。后两者需要反向访问机制，该机制在算法4中已作描述。反向访问仅在UCQ枚举需要时才会编译，因为它需要不可忽略的预处理（以支持算法4的第4行）。因此，当激活反向访问并使用支持删除功能的混洗器时，RENUM(CQ)满足全部四项要求。除此之外，我们对算法5的实现相当直接。

RENUM(MCUCQ) uses the underlying index RENUM(CQ) for random-access, testing, and inverted-access of all CQs, as well as all intersection CQs. We created RENUM(MCUCQ) by using the shuffler described in Algorithm 1 on the random-access for mcUCQs described in Section 5.2. Doing so requires knowing the number of answers after linear time preprocessing. The cardinality of a mcUCQ ${Q}_{1}\left( I\right)  \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( I\right)$ is simple to compute recursively via the formula $\left| {{Q}_{1}\left( I\right) }\right|  + \left| {{Q}_{2}\left( I\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( I\right) }\right|  - \left| {{Q}_{1}\left( I\right)  \cap  \left( {{Q}_{2}\left( I\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( I\right) }\right) }\right|$ , for which we have all elements after linear time preprocessing. A minor difference between the implementation and the definition in Section 5.2 instead of computing the largest answer less than or equal to our current answer and then applying inverted-access on it, we compute that index directly (using the same binary-search concept as in the proof of Theorem 5.5).

RENUM(MCUCQ)利用底层索引RENUM(CQ)实现对所有CQ及交集CQ的随机访问、测试和反向访问。我们通过在第5.2节描述的mcUCQ随机访问上应用算法1中的混洗器，创建了RENUM(MCUCQ)。这需要在线性时间预处理后获知答案数量。mcUCQ${Q}_{1}\left( I\right)  \cup  \ldots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( I\right)$的基数可通过公式$\left| {{Q}_{1}\left( I\right) }\right|  + \left| {{Q}_{2}\left( I\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( I\right) }\right|  - \left| {{Q}_{1}\left( I\right)  \cap  \left( {{Q}_{2}\left( I\right)  \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}\left( I\right) }\right) }\right|$递归计算得出，在线性时间预处理后我们已获得所有元素。实现与第5.2节定义的一个微小区别在于：我们直接计算该索引（采用与定理5.5证明中相同的二分搜索概念），而非先计算小于等于当前答案的最大答案再对其应用反向访问。

### 6.2 Experimental Setup

### 6.2 实验设置

We now describe the setup of our experimental study.

下面描述我们实验研究的设置。

Algorithms. To the best of our knowledge, this paper is the first to suggest a provably uniform random-order algorithm for CQ enumeration. Therefore, we compare our RENUM(CQ) to a sampling algorithm by Zhao et al. [33] via an implementation from their public repository. Their algorithm generates a uniform sample, and we naively transform it into a sampling-without-replacement algorithm by duplicate elimination (i.e., rejecting previously encountered answers). ${}^{5}$ Zhao et al. [33] suggest four different ways to initialize their algorithm,denoted ${RS},{EO},{OE}$ ,and ${EW}$ . We compare our algorithm to EW as it consistently outperformed all other methods in our experiments (see Section B.2 in the Appendix). We denote this variant by SAMPLE(EW). SAMPLE(EW) samples by iterating over the join tree from root to leaves and maintaining an assignment of the variables. At each relation, the algorithm chooses a tuple that agrees with the assignment such that the probability of each tuple being chosen is proportional to its weight. The weights signify the number of answers the tuple produces when only examining the sub-query rooted in its relation. This sampling algorithm is also implemented in c++14. Hence, we consider four algorithms: RENUM(CQ), SAMPLE(EW), RENUM(UCQ), and RENUM(MCUCQ).

算法。据我们所知，本文首次提出了可证明均匀随机顺序的CQ枚举算法。因此，我们将RENUM(CQ)与Zhao等人[33]通过其公开仓库实现的采样算法进行对比。他们的算法生成均匀样本，我们通过去重（即拒绝已遇到答案）将其简单转换为无放回采样算法。${}^{5}$Zhao等人[33]提出四种初始化方法，分别记为${RS},{EO},{OE}$和${EW}$。我们选择EW方法进行对比，因为实验中其表现始终优于其他方法（见附录B.2节）。该变体记为SAMPLE(EW)。SAMPLE(EW)通过从根到叶遍历连接树并维护变量赋值进行采样。在每个关系处，算法选择与当前赋值一致的元组，其选择概率与权重成正比。权重表示仅检查以其关系为根的子查询时该元组产生的答案数量。该采样算法同样用C++14实现。因此我们对比四种算法：RENUM(CQ)、SAMPLE(EW)、RENUM(UCQ)和RENUM(MCUCQ)。

Dataset. We used the TPC-H benchmark as the database for the experiments. We generated a database using the TPC-H dbgen tool with a scale factor of ${sf} = 5$ . The database has been instantiated once in memory, and all experiments use the exact same database.

数据集。实验采用TPC-H基准作为数据库。使用TPC-H dbgen工具生成规模因子为${sf} = 5$的数据库。数据库在内存中实例化一次，所有实验使用完全相同的数据库。

Queries. We compare our RENUM(CQ) to SAMPLE(EW) using the six free-connex CQs on which SAMPLE(EW) is implemented in the online repository. These are full-join (projection-free) CQs over the TPC-H schema. For lack of benchmarks, we phrased UCQs that we believed would form a natural extension to the TPC-H queries. The algorithms suggested in this paper adhere to set semantics with projection included. In SAMPLE(EW), projection is applied on top of sampling, and the uniform distribution applies to the results prior to projection. As projection may result in duplicates, the sampling can be interpreted as over bag semantics. In order to mitigate this difference,we add attributes from the LineItem relation to ${Q}_{3},{Q}_{7}$ , ${Q}_{9}$ ,and ${Q}_{10}$ . This addition achieves an equivalence between set semantics and bag semantics. The full description of our queries can be found in the Appendix (Section B.1). Each result is the average over three runs, except for Figures 2 and 3 that show a single run.

查询对比。我们将RENUM(CQ)与SAMPLE(EW)在在线代码库中实现的六个自由连接CQ上进行对比。这些是基于TPC-H模式的无投影全连接CQ。由于缺乏基准测试，我们设计了自认为能自然扩展TPC-H查询的UCQ。本文算法采用包含投影的集合语义。SAMPLE(EW)在抽样后应用投影，均匀分布作用于投影前结果。由于投影可能导致重复，抽样可视为基于包语义。为消除差异，我们在${Q}_{3},{Q}_{7}$、${Q}_{9}$和${Q}_{10}$中添加了LineItem关系的属性，实现集合语义与包语义的等效。完整查询描述见附录（B.1节）。除图2和图3显示单次运行外，其余结果均为三次运行平均值。

Hardware and system. The experiments were executed on an Intel(R) Xeon(R) CPU 2.50GHz machine with 768KB L1 cache, 3MB L2 cache, 30MiB L3 cache, and 496 GB of RAM, running Ubuntu 16.04.01 LTS. Code compilations used the O3 flag and no other optimization flag.

硬件与系统。实验在配备Intel(R) Xeon(R) 2.50GHz CPU（768KB L1缓存/3MB L2缓存/30MiB L3缓存）和496GB内存的Ubuntu 16.04.01 LTS系统上运行，代码编译使用O3优化标志。

### 6.3 Experimental Results

### 6.3 实验结果

We now describe the results of our experimentation with CQs and UCQs. The CQ experiments analyze RENUM(CQ) in terms of the total enumeration time (Section 6.3.1) and delay (Section 6.3.2), while the UCQ experiments analyze RENUM(UCQ) and RENUM(MCUCQ) in terms of the total enumeration time, as well as the rejection rate of RENum(UCQ) (Section 6.3.3). We omit from all preprocessing times the portion devoted to reading the relations.

本节阐述CQ与UCQ的实验结果。CQ实验从总枚举时间（6.3.1节）和延迟（6.3.2节）角度分析RENUM(CQ)；UCQ实验则分析RENUM(UCQ)和RENUM(MCUCQ)的总枚举时间，以及RENum(UCQ)的拒绝率（6.3.3节）。所有预处理时间均不含关系数据读取耗时。

6.3.1 CQ running time. To characterize the total enumeration time of RENUM(CQ), we compare it to that of SAMPLE(EW) for the TPC-H CQs. In the experiment,we task each algorithm with enumerating $k$ distinct answers for increasing values of $k$ . The different values of $k$ were chosen as a percentage of the query results $(1\% ,5\% ,{10}\% ,{30}\%$ , ${50}\% ,{70}\% ,{90}\% )$ . For each task,we measure the total enumeration time, that is, the time elapsed from the beginning of the preprocessing phase to when $k$ distinct answers were supplied. The results of this experiment are presented in Figure 1 with a chart per query. The results indicate that,as $k$ grows,the total enumeration time of SAMPLE(EW) grows more rapidly in comparison to RENUM(CQ). Generally, the total time of RENUM(CQ) increases slower as it does not reject answers. Hence, SAMPLE(EW) seems better or comparable for smaller $k$ values,but is consistently outperformed by $\operatorname{RENUM}\left( \mathrm{{CQ}}\right)$ for larger values of $k$ . This is especially true when preprocessing time becomes negligible in comparison to the time it takes to enumerate $k$ distinct answers. RENUM(CQ) performs better, relative to SAMPLE(EW),on queries with more relations $\left( {{Q}_{2},{Q}_{7}}\right.$ , ${Q}_{9}$ ) than ones with fewer relations.

6.3.1 CQ运行时间。为表征RENUM(CQ)的总枚举时间，我们将其与SAMPLE(EW)在TPC-H CQ上的表现对比。实验中，各算法需枚举$k$个不同答案（$k$值递增）。$k$取值设定为查询结果$(1\% ,5\% ,{10}\% ,{30}\%$、${50}\% ,{70}\% ,{90}\% )$的百分比。测量从预处理开始到输出$k$个答案的总耗时，结果见图1（每查询单独图表）。数据显示：随着$k$增大，SAMPLE(EW)的总枚举时间增速显著快于RENUM(CQ)。由于不拒绝答案，RENUM(CQ)耗时增长更平缓。对于较小$k$值，SAMPLE(EW)表现更优或相当；但$k$较大时，$\operatorname{RENUM}\left( \mathrm{{CQ}}\right)$始终更优——当预处理时间相对枚举时间可忽略时尤为明显。相较于少关系查询，RENUM(CQ)在多关系查询（$\left( {{Q}_{2},{Q}_{7}}\right.$、${Q}_{9}$）中相对SAMPLE(EW)的优势更大。

6.3.2 ${CQ}$ delay analysis. To examine the delay of RENUM(CQ) and SAMPLE(EW), we record the delay of each answer and depict it in box-and-whisker plots. Each query has two box plots: enumeration of all answers (Figure 2) and enumeration of ${50}\%$ of the answers (Figure 3). Outliers that fell outside the whiskers are not shown, since some are several orders of magnitude larger than the median. Further information regarding the results of this experiment, as well as the outliers dropped, is in the Appendix (Section B.3). We can see that in a full enumeration, RENUM(CQ) always shows a lower median value, smaller variation, and a smaller interquartile range (IQR). Smaller IQR and whiskers show that half of the delay samples fall within a smaller range, meaning that the delay is more stable and predictable. When enumerating ${50}\%$ of the answers,the variation and IQR remain smaller across all queries. However, in ${Q}_{0}$ we see that SAMPLE(EW) actually exhibits a smaller median. In addition, RENUM(CQ) usually shows better results on larger queries, in comparison to SAMPLE(EW). For instance, SAMPLE(EW) has a better median in ${Q}_{0}$ than in its larger counterpart ${Q}_{2}$ .

6.3.2 ${CQ}$ 延迟分析。为考察RENUM(CQ)与SAMPLE(EW)的延迟表现，我们记录每个应答的延迟时间并通过箱线图呈现。每个查询对应两个箱图：全量答案枚举（图2）与${50}\%$比例答案枚举（图3）。由于部分异常值远超中位数数量级，箱须范围外的离群点未予显示。本实验结果的详细数据及剔除的离群点参见附录（B.3节）。可见在全枚举场景中，RENUM(CQ)始终呈现更低的中位数、更小的离散度及更窄的四分位距(IQR)。较小的IQR和箱须范围表明半数延迟样本集中在较窄区间，意味着延迟更稳定且可预测。当枚举${50}\%$比例的答案时，所有查询的离散度与IQR仍保持较低水平。但在${Q}_{0}$场景中，SAMPLE(EW)实际展现出更小的中位数。此外，相比SAMPLE(EW)，RENUM(CQ)通常在较大规模查询中表现更优。例如SAMPLE(EW)在${Q}_{0}$场景的中位数优于其更大规模的对应场景${Q}_{2}$。

6.3.3 UCQ enumeration. This section analyzes the total enumeration time of RENUM(UCQ) and RENUM(MCUCQ), as well as the time spent on rejection of RENUM(UCQ) in three experiments, shown in Figures 4a, 4b, and 5, respectively. The 1st experiment measures the length of a full enumeration (with RENUM(UCQ) or RENUM(MCUCQ)) in three UCQs, while the second focuses on one UCQ and measures the total time of both UCQ algorithms as it varies when producing a different portion of the answers (as in Section 6.3.1). In both experiments, we compare RENUM(UCQ) and RENUM(MCUCQ) to the cumulative running time of RENUM(CQ) on the CQs comprising the union. We stress that running RENUM(CQ) on the independent CQs is not an alternative to an actual UCQ enumeration-it produces duplicates and does not provide a uniform random order. We perform this comparison to measure the overhead of the UCQ algorithms over RENUM(CQ). The 3rd experiment examines the time that RENUM(UCQ) spends on producing rejected answers during a single run. It shows how this time changes along the course of a full enumeration.

6.3.3 UCQ枚举。本节通过图4a、4b和5分别展示的三个实验，分析RENUM(UCQ)与RENUM(MCUCQ)的总枚举时间，以及RENUM(UCQ)在拒绝环节耗时。第一项实验测量三个UCQ中完整枚举（使用RENUM(UCQ)或RENUM(MCUCQ)）的时长，第二项实验聚焦单个UCQ，测量两种UCQ算法在生成不同比例答案时的总耗时变化（如6.3.1节所述）。两项实验中，我们将RENUM(UCQ)和RENUM(MCUCQ)与组成并集的各CQ上RENUM(CQ)累计运行时间进行对比。需要强调的是，在独立CQ上运行RENUM(CQ)并非实际UCQ枚举的替代方案——它会产生重复项且无法提供均匀随机顺序。此对比旨在衡量UCQ算法相较RENUM(CQ)的开销。第三项实验检测RENUM(UCQ)单次运行中生成被拒答案的耗时，展示该耗时在完整枚举过程中的变化趋势。

The difference in preprocessing time between RENUM(CQ) and RENUM(UCQ) is that for the latter, we need to build an index that supports Line 4 in Algorithm 5. Figure 4a shows that this difference can be quite small,as seen in ${Q}_{7}^{S} \cup  {Q}_{7}^{C}$ and ${Q}_{A} \cup  {Q}_{E}$ . Meanwhile, the preprocessing of RENUM(MCUCQ) adds to that of RENUM(UCQ) the need to preprocess CQs defined by intersection of CQs from the union. Hence, RENUM(MCUCQ) always has the largest preprocessing time. Nevertheless, we see that the difference in the enumeration phase is more significant for both algorithms.

RENUM(CQ)与RENUM(UCQ)预处理时间的差异在于，后者需构建支持算法5第4行的索引。图4a显示该差异可能极小，如${Q}_{7}^{S} \cup  {Q}_{7}^{C}$和${Q}_{A} \cup  {Q}_{E}$所示。同时，RENUM(MCUCQ)的预处理在RENUM(UCQ)基础上增加了对并集中CQ交集定义部分的预处理，因此RENUM(MCUCQ)预处理时间始终最长。但可见两种算法在枚举阶段的耗时差异更为显著。

The slowdown of RENUM(UCQ) compared to RENUM(CQ) is mostly attributed to the effort to avoid duplicates by multiple CQs. A union of $m$ CQs calls the inverted-access $m - 1$ times per answer. Also, the enumeration phase is slowed down by the deletion mechanism and rejections. Figure 4a also shows that the slowdown between RENUM(CQ) and RENUM(UCQ) depends largely on the intersection size. ${Q}_{2}^{N} \cup  {Q}_{2}^{P} \cup  {Q}_{2}^{S}$ has a large intersection and ${Q}_{A} \cup  {Q}_{E}$ has no intersection at all. In general, two disjoint queries will be much faster than two identical queries because for two identical queries the algorithm will reject half of the answers on average.

RENUM(UCQ)相较RENUM(CQ)的减速主要源于避免多CQ重复答案的机制。$m$个CQ的并集每生成一个答案需调用$m - 1$次倒排访问。此外，删除机制和拒绝操作也会延缓枚举阶段。图4a还显示RENUM(CQ)与RENUM(UCQ)的减速程度主要取决于交集规模——${Q}_{2}^{N} \cup  {Q}_{2}^{P} \cup  {Q}_{2}^{S}$具有大交集而${Q}_{A} \cup  {Q}_{E}$无交集。通常，两个互斥查询会比两个相同查询快得多，因为对于相同查询，算法平均会拒绝半数答案。

Figure 4a demonstrates that the difference in running time between RENUM(MCUCQ) and RENUM(UCQ) depends on the number of CQs in the union. For two CQs, RENUM(MCUCQ) outperforms RENum(UCQ). ${Q}_{A} \cup  {Q}_{E}$ is a disjoint union,while ${Q}_{7}^{S} \cup  {Q}_{7}^{C}$ is not. Finally, Figure 5 shows that the time RENUM(UCQ) spends on producing rejected answers decays over time. The reason for this decay is that the number of answers that belong to multiple CQs (shared answers) drops faster than that of non-shared answers, for two reasons. First, shared answers have a higher probability of being selected. Second, when a non-shared answer is selected, it is deleted everywhere, while a shared answer may become non-shared.

最后图5表明，RENUM(UCQ)用于生成被拒答案的时间会逐渐减少。这种衰减源于属于多个CQ的共享答案数量比非共享答案下降更快，原因有二：首先共享答案被选中的概率更高；其次当非共享答案被选中时会被全局删除，而共享答案可能转变为非共享状态。

6.3.4 Conclusions. Our experimental study indicates that the merits of RENUM(CQ) are not only in its complexity and statistical guarantees-a fairly simple implementation of it features a significant improvement in practical performance compared to the state-of-the-art approach. Moreover, the overhead of RENum(UCQ) is non-negligible. While this overhead is reasonable for the case of binary union, it is an important future challenge to reduce this overhead for larger unions. Finally, although RENUM(MCUCQ) has the advantage of guaranteed delay (unlike that of RENUM(UCQ) which is expected), our empirical evaluation shows that RENUM(UCQ) is usually comparable to RENUM(MCUCQ) or more efficient.

6.3.4 结论。实验研究表明，RENUM(CQ)的优势不仅体现在复杂度与统计保证上——其简洁的实现相比现有技术方案带来了显著的性能提升。虽然RENUM(UCQ)在二元联合情况下的开销尚可接受，但降低大规模联合查询的开销仍是未来重要挑战。尽管RENUM(MCUCQ)具有延迟确定性优势（不同于RENUM(UCQ)的期望延迟），实证评估显示RENUM(UCQ)通常与RENUM(MCUCQ)性能相当或更优。

## 7 CONCLUSIONS

## 7 结论

We studied the problems of answering queries in a random permutation and via a random-access. We established that for CQs without self-joins it holds that $\operatorname{Enum}\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle  = \operatorname{RAccess}\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle  =$ REnum $\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$ . We also studied the generalization to unions of free-connex CQs where,in contrast,we have $\operatorname{Enum}\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle  \neq$ RAccess $\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$ and random-access may be intractable even if tractable for each CQ in the union. We then studied two alternatives: (1) RENUM(MCUCQ) uses the random-access approach for the restricted class of mc-UCQs and achieves guaranteed ${\log }^{2}$ delay; (2) RENum(UCQ) finds a random permutation directly for any UCQ comprising of free-connex CQs and achieves log delay in expectation. Our experimental study shows that the two solutions are comparable on a union of two CQs, but RENUM(UCQ) preforms better on larger unions. We described an implementation of our algorithms, and presented an experimental study showing that our algorithms outperform the sampling-with-rejection alternatives.

我们研究了随机排列与随机访问下的查询应答问题。证实对于无自连接的CQ，$\operatorname{Enum}\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle  = \operatorname{RAccess}\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle  =$ REnum $\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$成立。而在自由连接CQ的联合查询中，情况相反——$\operatorname{Enum}\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle  \neq$ RAccess $\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$，即使单个CQ可高效处理，联合查询的随机访问仍可能难以处理。我们继而研究两种方案：(1) RENUM(MCUCQ)针对受限的mc-UCQ类采用随机访问方法，确保${\log }^{2}$延迟；(2) RENUM(UCQ)直接为自由连接CQ组成的任意UCQ生成随机排列，实现期望对数延迟。实验显示两种方案在双CQ联合中性能相当，但RENUM(UCQ)在更大规模联合中表现更佳。我们实现了算法并通过实验证明其性能优于拒绝采样替代方案。

It seems conceivable that by using van Emde Boas trees [30], the delay can be improved from log to log log; we plan to provide details in this paper's journal version. Concerning the restricted class of mc-UCQs, an obvious future task is to find syntactic properties which ensure that a given UCQ is an mc-UCQ. A simple, but very restrictive sufficient condition is a union comprising of the same CQ with different selection conditions (like we have done in our experiments).

可以设想，通过使用van Emde Boas树[30]，延迟可从对数级优化至对数对数级；我们计划在本文的期刊版本中提供详细说明。针对受限类别的mc-UCQ，一个明显的未来研究方向是寻找能确保给定UCQ属于mc-UCQ的语法特性。一个简单但限制性很强的充分条件是：由相同CQ搭配不同选择条件组成的并集（如我们在实验中所做的那样）。

We know that (under complexity assumptions) non-free-connex CQs do not have a random permutation algorithm with linear preprocessing and polylogarithmic delay. However, it would be interesting to understand more precisely the times required for guaranteeing enumeration in a random permutation, for example, by building on top of recent work on sampling cyclic CQs [13]. Regarding UCQs, it is an open problem to find which UCQs admit efficient fine-grained enumeration, even without order guarantees [11]. However, we do know that UCQs comprising of free-connex CQs admit efficient enumeration. This work opens the question of finding an exact characterisation for when such a UCQ admits random-access or random-permutation in polylogarithmic delay.

已知（在复杂度假设下）非自由连接CQ不存在具有线性预处理时间和多对数延迟的随机排列算法。然而，更精确地理解保证随机排列枚举所需的时间将很有意义，例如基于近期关于循环CQ采样的研究[13]。对于UCQ而言，即使不保证顺序[11]，确定哪些UCQ允许高效细粒度枚举仍是未解难题。但我们确实知道，由自由连接CQ组成的UCQ允许高效枚举。本研究提出了一个新问题：如何精确描述在多对数延迟下，何种UCQ允许随机访问或随机排列。

证明。设$Q = {Q}_{1} \cup  \cdots  \cup  {Q}_{m}$为给定的mc-UCQ（多核联合查询），对于所有$\varnothing  \neq  I \subseteq  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack$，令${\mathcal{A}}_{I}$为RAccess$\langle \operatorname{lin},\log \rangle$算法，这些算法共同验证$Q$构成一个mc-UCQ。

Upon input of a database $D$ we perform the linear-time preprocessing of all the algorithms ${\mathcal{A}}_{I}$ input $D$ . Now consider an arbitrary $\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack$ and an $I \subseteq  \left\lbrack  {\ell  + 1,m}\right\rbrack$ . For the sets ${S}_{\ell } \mathrel{\text{:=}} {Q}_{\{ \ell \} }\left( D\right)$ and ${T}_{\ell ,I} \mathrel{\text{:=}} {Q}_{\{ \ell \}  \cup  I}\left( D\right)$ ,we then immediately know that all the assumptions of Lemma A. 2 are satisfied, except for the last one (i.e., the last bullet point in item (3) of Lemma A.2). Furthermore, we know that each of the time bounds ${t}_{\text{test }},{t}_{\text{acc }}$ ,and ${t}_{\text{inv-acc }}$ are at most logarithmic in the size $\left| D\right|$ of $D$ . To finish the proof,it therefore suffices to show the following for each $\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack$ and each $\varnothing  \neq  I \subseteq  \left\lbrack  {\ell  + 1,m}\right\rbrack$ :

在输入数据库$D$时，我们对所有算法${\mathcal{A}}_{I}$的输入$D$执行线性时间预处理。现考虑任意$\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack$和$I \subseteq  \left\lbrack  {\ell  + 1,m}\right\rbrack$。对于集合${S}_{\ell } \mathrel{\text{:=}} {Q}_{\{ \ell \} }\left( D\right)$和${T}_{\ell ,I} \mathrel{\text{:=}} {Q}_{\{ \ell \}  \cup  I}\left( D\right)$，我们可立即确认引理A.2中除最后一项（即引理A.2第(3)项最后要点）外的所有假设均成立。此外，各时间界限${t}_{\text{test }},{t}_{\text{acc }}$和${t}_{\text{inv-acc }}$至多为$D$规模$\left| D\right|$的对数级别。因此，要完成证明，只需对每个$\ell  \in  \left\lbrack  {1,m}\right\rbrack$和$\varnothing  \neq  I \subseteq  \left\lbrack  {\ell  + 1,m}\right\rbrack$证明以下结论：

(*) On input of an arbitrary $a \in  {S}_{\ell }$ ,within time $O\left( {{\log }^{2}\left| D\right| }\right)$ we can output the particular $c \in  {T}_{\ell ,I}$ such that $c$ is the largest element of ${T}_{\ell ,I}$ that is less than or equal to $a$ according to the enumeration order of ${S}_{\ell }$ .

(*) 对于任意输入$a \in  {S}_{\ell }$，我们能在$O\left( {{\log }^{2}\left| D\right| }\right)$时间内输出特定$c \in  {T}_{\ell ,I}$，使得根据${S}_{\ell }$的枚举顺序，$c$是${T}_{\ell ,I}$中小于等于$a$的最大元素。

We can achieve this by doing a binary search on indexes w.r.t. the enumeration orders on ${S}_{\ell }$ and ${T}_{\ell ,I}$ by using the routines ${T}_{\ell ,I}$ .Access and ${S}_{\ell }$ .INvAcc. More precisely,we start by letting $j = {S}_{\ell }$ .INvAcc(a), $c = {T}_{\ell ,I}$ .ACCESS(1),and ${j}_{c} = {S}_{\ell }$ .INvAcc(c). If ${j}_{c} = j$ we can safely return $c$ . If ${j}_{c} > j$ ,we return an error message indicating that ${T}_{\ell ,I}$ does not contain any element less than or equal to $a$ . If ${j}_{c} < j$ ,we let ${k}_{c} = 1,{k}_{d} = \left| {T}_{\ell ,I}\right| ,d = {T}_{\ell ,I}$ .Access $\left( {k}_{d}\right)$ , and ${j}_{d} = {S}_{\ell }$ .INvAcc(d). If ${j}_{d} \leq  j$ we can safely return $d$ . Otherwise,we do a binary search based on the invariant that $c,d$ are elements of ${T}_{\ell ,I}$ with $c < a < d$ (where $<$ refers to the enumeration order of ${S}_{\ell }$ ), ${j}_{c},{j}_{d}$ are their indexes in ${S}_{\ell }$ ,and ${k}_{c},{k}_{d}$ are their indexes in ${T}_{\ell ,I}$ : we let ${k}^{\prime } = \left\lfloor  {\left( {{k}_{c} + {k}_{d}}\right) /2}\right\rfloor$ ,and in case that ${k}^{\prime } = k$ we can safely terminate with output $c$ . Otherwise, we let ${c}^{\prime } = {T}_{\ell ,I}$ .Access $\left( {k}^{\prime }\right)$ and ${j}^{\prime } = {S}_{\ell }$ .InvAcc $\left( {c}^{\prime }\right)$ . If ${j}^{\prime } = j$ we can safely terminate and return ${c}^{\prime }$ . If ${j}^{\prime } < j$ we proceed letting $\left( {c,d,{j}_{c},{j}_{d},{k}_{c},{k}_{d}}\right)  = \left( {{c}^{\prime },d,{j}^{\prime },{j}_{d},{k}^{\prime },{k}_{d}}\right)$ . If ${j}^{\prime } > j$ we proceed letting $\left( {c,d,{j}_{c},{j}_{d},{k}_{c},{k}_{d}}\right)  = \left( {c,{c}^{\prime },{j}_{c},{j}^{\prime },{k}_{c},{k}^{\prime }}\right)$ . The number of iterations is logarithmic in $\left| {T}_{\ell ,I}\right|$ ,and each iteration invokes a constant number of calls to ${T}_{\ell ,I}$ .Access and ${S}_{\ell }$ .InvAcc. Since each such call is answered in time $O\left( {\log \left| D\right| }\right)$ ,we have achieved $\left( *\right)$ . Theorem 5.5 now follows from Lemma A.2.

我们可以通过对${S}_{\ell }$和${T}_{\ell ,I}$上的枚举顺序索引进行二分查找来实现这一点，具体使用${T}_{\ell ,I}$.Access和${S}_{\ell }$.INvAcc例程。更准确地说，我们首先设$j = {S}_{\ell }$.INvAcc(a)、$c = {T}_{\ell ,I}$.ACCESS(1)和${j}_{c} = {S}_{\ell }$.INvAcc(c)。如果${j}_{c} = j$，我们可以安全返回$c$。如果${j}_{c} > j$，则返回错误信息表明${T}_{\ell ,I}$不包含任何小于或等于$a$的元素。如果${j}_{c} < j$，我们设${k}_{c} = 1,{k}_{d} = \left| {T}_{\ell ,I}\right| ,d = {T}_{\ell ,I}$.Access$\left( {k}_{d}\right)$和${j}_{d} = {S}_{\ell }$.INvAcc(d)。若${j}_{d} \leq  j$则可安全返回$d$。否则，基于以下不变式进行二分查找：$c,d$是${T}_{\ell ,I}$中满足$c < a < d$的元素（其中$<$指代${S}_{\ell }$的枚举顺序），${j}_{c},{j}_{d}$是它们在${S}_{\ell }$中的索引，${k}_{c},{k}_{d}$是它们在${T}_{\ell ,I}$中的索引：我们设${k}^{\prime } = \left\lfloor  {\left( {{k}_{c} + {k}_{d}}\right) /2}\right\rfloor$，若${k}^{\prime } = k$则可终止并输出$c$。否则，设${c}^{\prime } = {T}_{\ell ,I}$.Access$\left( {k}^{\prime }\right)$和${j}^{\prime } = {S}_{\ell }$.InvAcc$\left( {c}^{\prime }\right)$。若${j}^{\prime } = j$则终止并返回${c}^{\prime }$。若${j}^{\prime } < j$则继续设$\left( {c,d,{j}_{c},{j}_{d},{k}_{c},{k}_{d}}\right)  = \left( {{c}^{\prime },d,{j}^{\prime },{j}_{d},{k}^{\prime },{k}_{d}}\right)$。若${j}^{\prime } > j$则继续设$\left( {c,d,{j}_{c},{j}_{d},{k}_{c},{k}_{d}}\right)  = \left( {c,{c}^{\prime },{j}_{c},{j}^{\prime },{k}_{c},{k}^{\prime }}\right)$。迭代次数与$\left| {T}_{\ell ,I}\right|$成对数关系，每次迭代调用固定次数的${T}_{\ell ,I}$.Access和${S}_{\ell }$.InvAcc。由于每次调用耗时$O\left( {\log \left| D\right| }\right)$，我们实现了$\left( *\right)$。定理5.5现在由引理A.2得出。

When SAMPLE(EO) timed-out, the corresponding bar in Figure 6 is omitted. In addition, we omit ${Q}_{1}0$ ,as SAMPLE(EO) did not produce $1\%$ of the answers within the time limit. Figure 6 shows that with the exception of ${Q}_{3}$ at $1\% ,{EO}$ is significantly slower than both RENUM(CQ) and SAMPLE(EW).

B.2.1 EO。由于SAMPLE(EO)可能拒绝抽样，其采样时间显著更长（实验数据可证明）。图6复现了6.3.1节（图1所示）的实验，并加入SAMPLE(EO)。我们省略了SAMPLE(EO)的预处理阶段——如赵等人[33]所做的那样——因为其性能始终不如SAMPLE(EW)。运行SAMPLE(EO)时，我们设置了超时机制，当采样时间超过${EW}$对应值的100倍时即终止。若SAMPLE(EO)超时，则图6中相应柱状图被省略。此外，我们省略了${Q}_{1}0$，因为SAMPLE(EO)未能在时限内生成$1\%$的答案。图6显示，除了${Q}_{3}$在$1\% ,{EO}$时的情况外，SAMPLE(EO)明显慢于RENUM(CQ)和SAMPLE(EW)。

B.2.2 OE. Out of our six queries, SAMPLE(OE) was implemented on ${Q}_{3}$ alone. Figure 7 shows the results of section 6.3.1 with SAMPLE(OE) added. In our experiments with ${Q}_{3}$ ,SAMPLE(EW) has always out-preformed SAMPLE(OE).

B.2.2 OE。在我们的六个查询中，SAMPLE(OE)仅针对${Q}_{3}$实现。图7展示了加入SAMPLE(OE)后的6.3.1节实验结果。在${Q}_{3}$的实验中，SAMPLE(EW)始终优于SAMPLE(OE)。

B.2.3 RS. SAMPLE(RS) was also implemented only on ${Q}_{3}$ . SAMPLE(RS) was unable to produce a sample of $1\%$ of the answers to ${Q}_{3}$ in less than an hour. It took SAMPLE(RS) about 6.8 seconds to gather a sample of 100000 distinct answers,which is roughly ${0.33}\%$ of all answers. Therefore, SAMPLE(RS) would be slower than SAMPLE(EW) even if it were to proceed and sample $1\%$ with no deterioration due to repeating samples.

B.2.3 RS抽样方法(SAMPLE(RS))同样仅在${Q}_{3}$上实施。该抽样方法无法在一小时内生成针对${Q}_{3}$问题答案的$1\%$样本。采集10万个不重复答案样本耗时约6.8秒，约占全部答案的${0.33}\%$。因此即使不考虑重复样本导致的效率衰减，RS抽样法的处理速度仍会慢于EW抽样法。

### B.3 More on the CQ delay experiment

### B.3 CQ延迟实验补充说明

The following tables show the mean, standard deviation (SD) and number of delay samples that counted as outliers during the enumeration of ${50}\%$ of the answers (on the left of Figure 8) and a full enumeration (on the right of Figure 8). The smaller number of outliers and lower standard deviation indicates the predictability of the delay, as it does not grow rapidly.
