## ABSTRACT

## 摘要

The resilience of a Boolean query on a database is the minimum number of tuples that need to be deleted from the input tables in order to make the query false. A solution to this problem immediately translates into a solution for the more widely known problem of deletion propagation with source-side effects. In this paper, we give several novel results on the hardness of the resilience problem for conjunctive queries with self-joins, and, more specifically, we present a dichotomy result for the class of single-self-join binary queries with exactly two repeated relations occurring in the query. Unlike in the self-join free case, the concept of triad is not enough to fully characterize the complexity of resilience. We identify new structural properties, namely chains, confluences and permutations, which lead to various NP-hardness results. We also give novel involved reductions to network flow to show certain cases are in P. Although restricted, our results provide important insights into the problem of self-joins that we hope can help solve the general case of all conjunctive queries with self-joins in the future.

布尔查询在数据库中的韧性是指为使查询结果为假，需要从输入表中删除的最小元组数。该问题的解决方案可直接转化为更广为人知的、具有源端效应的删除传播问题的解。本文针对带自连接的合取查询的韧性难题给出了若干新发现，特别针对包含两个重复关系的单自连接二元查询类提出了二分性结论。与无自连接情况不同，三元组概念已不足以完整刻画韧性复杂度。我们发现了链式结构、汇流结构和排列结构等新特性，这些特性导致了不同的NP难结果。同时通过新颖复杂的网络流归约，证明了特定案例属于P类问题。尽管研究范围受限，这些成果为自连接问题提供了重要洞见，有望助力未来解决带自连接的所有合取查询的一般情况。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Various problems in database research, such as causality, explanations, and deletion propagation, examine how interventions in the input to a query impact the query's output. An intervention constitutes a change (update, addition, or deletion) to the input tuples. In this paper, we study the resilience of a Boolean query with respect to tuple deletions. Resilience is a variant of deletion propagation that focuses on Boolean queries: it corresponds to the minimum number of tuples whose deletion causes the query to evaluate to false. In previous work [14], we provided a full characterization of the complexity of resilience for the family of self-join-free conjunctive queries (sj-free CQs) with functional dependencies. In this paper, we augment the previous results to account for a restricted class of self-joins.

数据库研究中的诸多问题——如因果分析、解释生成和删除传播——都探讨查询输入端的干预如何影响输出结果。干预表现为对输入元组的修改（更新、添加或删除）。本文重点研究布尔查询针对元组删除的弹性问题。弹性是删除传播的布尔查询变体，其核心在于确定使查询结果为假所需删除的最小元组数。前期工作[14]中，我们已完整刻画了无自连接且含函数依赖的连接查询（sj-free CQs）的弹性复杂度。本文将进一步扩展该成果，涵盖特定类别的自连接查询。

Self-joins have long plagued the complexity study of many problems in database theory research: for example, on the topic of consistent query answering, Kolaitis and Pema [26] proved a dichotomy into PTIME and coNP-complete cases for the family of queries with only two atoms and no self-joins. Koutris and Su-ciu [27] extended the dichotomy to the larger class of self-join-free conjunctive queries, where each atom has as primary key either a single attribute or all the attributes. Koutris and Wijsen [29, 31] further extended the dichotomy to the full class of sj-free Boolean CQs, and queries with negated atoms [30]. To the best of our knowledge, there is no known result on this problem for a query family that permits self-joins. As another example, complexity results on the problem of query-based pricing [28] are also restricted to the class of sj-free CQs. On the closely related topic of deletion propagation with view side-effects, Kimelfeld et al. [24] used a characteristic of the query structure (head domination) to formalize a complexity dichotomy for the family of sj-free CQs, and indicated that self-joins can significantly harden approximation in the problem of deletion propagation. Extensions to the cases of functional dependencies [23] and multi-tuple deletions [25] also focused on the same query class. These examples offer strong indication that self-joins introduce significant hurdles in the study of a variety of problems, and progress in cases that account for self-joins is rare. ${}^{1}$

自连接问题长期困扰着数据库理论研究的复杂度分析：例如在一致性查询应答领域，Kolaitis和Pema[26]证明了仅含两个原子且无自连接的查询族存在PTIME与coNP完全问题的二分法。Koutris和Suciu[27]将该二分法扩展至更广泛的无自连接连接查询类，其中每个原子主键为单一属性或全属性。Koutris与Wijsen[29,31]进一步将二分法推广至全体无自连接布尔CQs及含否定原子的查询[30]。据我们所知，目前尚无针对允许自连接的查询族的相关研究成果。类似地，基于查询的定价问题[28]的复杂度结论也仅限于无自连接CQs类别。在密切相关的带视图副作用删除传播研究中，Kimelfeld等[24]利用查询结构特征（头部支配性）形式化了无自连接CQs的复杂度二分法，并指出自连接会极大增加删除传播问题的近似求解难度。针对函数依赖[23]和多元组删除[25]的扩展研究同样聚焦于该查询类别。这些例证充分表明，自连接为各类问题的研究设置了重大障碍，相关突破性进展寥寥。${}^{1}$

In this paper, we give several novel results on the hardness of the resilience problem for CQs with self-joins. We show some results that hold for any CQ with self-join but later we focus on the class of binary ${CQs}$ (those where relations are either unary or binary). We provide various complexity results for binary CQs where only one relation name can be repeated, which we denote by single-self-join (ssj). We analyze the case of ssj binary queries in general but emphasize that for the case with at most 2 instances of the repeated relation,we prove that a $\mathrm{P}$ versus $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete dichotomy exists. We further provide a unifying criterion for hardness (a "proof template"), and we conjecture that it subsumes and generalizes the criterion of triads from Sj-free queries, and that it provides a sufficient criterion of hardness for any CQ.

## 2 BACKGROUND AND PRIOR RESULTS

## 2 背景与已有成果

This section introduces our notation, defines the resilience of a query, and summarizes prior complexity results for sj-free queries.

本节介绍我们的符号体系，定义查询的弹性(resilience)，并总结关于无自连接(sj-free)查询的既有复杂度结果。

Standard database notations. We use boldface to denote tuples or ordered sets,(e.g., $\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right)$ ) and use both subscripts and superscripts as indices (e.g., ${a}^{1}$ and ${a}_{1}$ ). We fix a relational vocabulary $\mathbf{R} = \left( {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{\ell }}\right)$ ,and denote arity $\left( {R}_{i}\right)$ the arity of a relation ${R}_{i}$ . We call unary and binary those relations with arity 1 or 2,respectively. We call "binary queries" those queries that contain only unary or binary relations. A database instance over $\mathbf{R}$ is $D = \left( {{R}_{1}^{D},\ldots ,{R}_{\ell }^{D}}\right)$ , where each ${R}_{i}^{D}$ is a finite relation. We call the elements of ${R}_{i}^{D}$ tuples and write ${R}_{i}$ instead of ${R}_{i}^{D}$ when $D$ is clear from the context. With some abuse of notation we also denote $D$ as the set of all tuples,i.e. $D = \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{i}{R}_{i}$ ,where the union is understood to be a disjoint union (thus each tuple belongs to only one relation). The active domain $\operatorname{dom}\left( D\right)$ is the set of all constants occurring in $D$ . The size of the database instance is $n = \left| D\right|$ ,i.e. the number of tuples in the database. ${}^{2}$

标准数据库符号。我们用粗体表示元组或有序集合（如$\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right)$），并使用下标和上标作为索引（如${a}^{1}$和${a}_{1}$）。我们固定一个关系词汇表$\mathbf{R} = \left( {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{\ell }}\right)$，并用$\left( {R}_{i}\right)$表示关系${R}_{i}$的元数。将元数为1或2的关系分别称为一元和二元关系。将仅含一元或二元关系的查询称为"二元查询"。在$\mathbf{R}$上的数据库实例是$D = \left( {{R}_{1}^{D},\ldots ,{R}_{\ell }^{D}}\right)$，其中每个${R}_{i}^{D}$都是有限关系。我们将${R}_{i}^{D}$的元素称为元组，当上下文明确时用${R}_{i}$代替${R}_{i}^{D}$。在不严格区分的情况下，我们也用$D$表示所有元组的集合，即$D = \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{i}{R}_{i}$（此处并集理解为不相交并集，故每个元组仅属于一个关系）。活动域$\operatorname{dom}\left( D\right)$是$D$中出现的所有常量的集合。数据库实例的大小为$n = \left| D\right|$，即数据库中元组的数量。${}^{2}$

A conjunctive query(CQ)is a first-order formula $q\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  = \exists \mathbf{x}\left( {{g}_{1} \land  }\right.$ $\left. {\ldots  \land  {g}_{m}}\right)$ where the variables $\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right)$ are called existential variables, $\mathbf{y} = \left( {{y}_{1},\ldots ,{y}_{c}}\right)$ are called the head variables (or free variables),and each atom (also called subgoal) ${g}_{i}$ represents a relation ${g}_{i} = R\left( {\mathbf{z}}_{i}\right)$ where ${\mathbf{z}}_{i} \subseteq  \mathbf{x} \cup  \mathbf{y}{.}^{3}$ A self-join-free ${CQ}$ (sj-free $\mathrm{{CQ}}$ ) is one where no relation symbol occurs more than once and thus every atom represents a different relation. In turn,a self-join ${CQ}$ is one where at least one relation symbol is repeated, and a single-self-join (ssj) ${CQ}$ is one where only one relation symbol can be repeated in a query. We write $\operatorname{var}\left( {g}_{j}\right)$ for the set of variables occurring in atom ${g}_{j}$ . As usual,we abbreviate a non-Boolean query in Datalog notation by $q\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} {g}_{1},\ldots ,{g}_{m}$ where $q$ has head variables $\mathbf{y}$ and ${g}_{1},\ldots ,{g}_{m}$ represents the body of the query.

合取查询(CQ)是一阶公式$q\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  = \exists \mathbf{x}\left( {{g}_{1} \land  }\right.$$\left. {\ldots  \land  {g}_{m}}\right)$，其中变量$\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{k}}\right)$称为存在变量，$\mathbf{y} = \left( {{y}_{1},\ldots ,{y}_{c}}\right)$称为头部变量（或自由变量），每个原子（也称子目标）${g}_{i}$表示一个关系${g}_{i} = R\left( {\mathbf{z}}_{i}\right)$（其中${\mathbf{z}}_{i} \subseteq  \mathbf{x} \cup  \mathbf{y}{.}^{3}$）。无自连接${CQ}$(sj-free$\mathrm{{CQ}}$)指没有关系符号重复出现，因此每个原子表示不同关系。反之，自连接${CQ}$是至少有一个关系符号重复的查询，而单自连接(ssj)${CQ}$是查询中仅允许一个关系符号重复的情况。我们用$\operatorname{var}\left( {g}_{j}\right)$表示原子${g}_{j}$中出现的变量集合。按照惯例，我们用Datalog表示法$q\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} {g}_{1},\ldots ,{g}_{m}$缩写非布尔查询，其中$q$包含头部变量$\mathbf{y}$，${g}_{1},\ldots ,{g}_{m}$表示查询主体。

Unless otherwise stated, a query in this paper denotes a Boolean CQ $q$ (i.e., $\mathbf{y} = \varnothing$ ). We write $D \vDash  q$ to denote that the query $q$ evaluates to true over the database instance $D$ ,and $D \mathrel{\text{\vDash \not{} }} q$ to denote that $q$ evaluates to false. For a Boolean query $q$ ,we write $q\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ to indicate that $\mathbf{x}$ represents the set of all existentially quantified variables. We write $\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ as short notation for the set $\{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$ .

除非另有说明，本文中的查询均指布尔合取查询(Boolean CQ)$q$（即$\mathbf{y} = \varnothing$）。我们用$D \vDash  q$表示查询$q$在数据库实例$D$上评估为真，用$D \mathrel{\text{\vDash \not{} }} q$表示评估为假。对于布尔查询$q$，$q\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$表示$\mathbf{x}$代表所有存在量化变量的集合。简记$\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$为集合$\{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$。

### 2.1 Query resilience

### 2.1 查询弹性

In this paper, we focus on the problem of resilience, a variant of the problem of deletion propagation focusing on Boolean queries: Given $D \vDash  q$ ,what is the minimum number of endogenous tuples that have to be removed from $D$ to make the query false? A large minimum set implies that the query is more "resilient" and requires the deletion of more tuples to change the query output. In order to study the complexity of resilience, we focus on the decision problem:

本文重点研究弹性问题——针对布尔查询的删除传播问题变体：给定$D \vDash  q$，需要从$D$中移除的最少内生元组数量是多少才能使查询为假？最小集越大意味着查询越"弹性"，需要删除更多元组才能改变查询结果。为研究弹性复杂度，我们关注以下判定问题：

Definition 1 (Resilience Decision). Given a query $q$ ,database $D$ ,and an integer $k$ . We say that $\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ if and only if $D \vDash  q$ and there exists a set $\Gamma$ with at most $k$ endogenous tuples s.t. $D - \Gamma  \mathrel{\text{\vDash \not{} }} q$ . We define $\rho \left( {D,q}\right)$ as the size of a minimum contingency set for input D and $q$ .

定义1（韧性决策）。给定查询$q$、数据库$D$及整数$k$。当且仅当$D \vDash  q$且存在至多含$k$个内生元组的集合$\Gamma$使得$D - \Gamma  \mathrel{\text{\vDash \not{} }} q$时，我们称$\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$。我们将$\rho \left( {D,q}\right)$定义为输入D和$q$的最小应急集规模。

In other words, $\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ means that there is a set of $k$ or fewer endogenous tuples whose removal makes the query false. We refer to such a set of tuples $\Gamma$ as a "contingency set." Observe that, for a fixed $q$ ,we can talk about data complexity and $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \in  \mathrm{{NP}}$ when $q$ is computable in PTIME.

换言之，$\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$意味着存在一个$k$或更少内生元组的集合，其移除会使查询结果为假。我们称此类元组集合$\Gamma$为"应急集"。需注意，对于固定$q$，当$q$可在PTIME内计算时，我们可以讨论数据复杂性和$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \in  \mathrm{{NP}}$。

A central result of the prior work on resilience [14] is that the complexity of resilience of an sj-free CQ can be exactly characterized via a natural property of its dual hypergraph $\mathcal{H}\left( q\right)$ . The hypergraph of an sj-free query $q$ is usually defined with its vertices being the variables of $q$ and the hyperedges being the atoms [1]. The dual hypergraph, $\mathcal{H}\left( q\right)$ ,has vertex set $V = \left\{  {{g}_{1},\ldots ,{g}_{m}}\right\}$ ,and each variable ${x}_{i} \in  \operatorname{var}\left( q\right)$ determines the hyperedge consisting of all those atoms in which ${x}_{i}$ occurs: ${e}_{i} = \left\{  {{g}_{j} \mid  {x}_{i} \in  \operatorname{var}\left( {g}_{j}\right) }\right\}$ . A path in the graph is an alternating sequence of vertices and edges, ${g}_{1},{x}_{1},{g}_{2},{x}_{2},\ldots ,{g}_{n - 1},{x}_{n - 1},{g}_{n}$ ,such that for all $i,{x}_{i} \in$ $\operatorname{var}\left( {g}_{i}\right)  \cap  \operatorname{var}\left( {g}_{i + 1}\right)$ ,i.e.,the hyperedge ${x}_{i}$ joins vertices ${g}_{i}$ and ${g}_{i + 1}$ . We explicitly list the hyperedges in the path, because more than one hyperedge may join the same pair of vertices. Since we only consider dual hypergraphs, we use the shorter term "hypergraph" from now on.

先前关于韧性的研究[14]的核心成果是：无自连接CQ的韧性复杂度可通过其对偶超图$\mathcal{H}\left( q\right)$的自然属性精确表征。无自连接查询$q$的超图通常定义为：顶点是$q$的变量，超边是原子[1]。对偶超图$\mathcal{H}\left( q\right)$的顶点集为$V = \left\{  {{g}_{1},\ldots ,{g}_{m}}\right\}$，每个变量${x}_{i} \in  \operatorname{var}\left( q\right)$决定由包含${x}_{i}$的所有原子组成的超边：${e}_{i} = \left\{  {{g}_{j} \mid  {x}_{i} \in  \operatorname{var}\left( {g}_{j}\right) }\right\}$。图中的路径是顶点和边的交替序列${g}_{1},{x}_{1},{g}_{2},{x}_{2},\ldots ,{g}_{n - 1},{x}_{n - 1},{g}_{n}$，使得对所有$i,{x}_{i} \in$$\operatorname{var}\left( {g}_{i}\right)  \cap  \operatorname{var}\left( {g}_{i + 1}\right)$，即超边${x}_{i}$连接顶点${g}_{i}$和${g}_{i + 1}$。我们显式列出路径中的超边，因为同一对顶点可能被多条超边连接。由于仅考虑对偶超图，下文将简称为"超图"。

### 2.2 Domination

### 2.2 支配关系

We may mark some relations in an input database as exogenous and, the remaining relations are endogenous. However, some relations are "implicitly" exogenous. For example,the relation $W$ in ${q}_{T}$ is given as endogenous, but is never needed in minimum contingency sets. We next define a syntactic property, called domination, that captures when endogenous relations are implicitly exogenous.

我们可将输入数据库中的某些关系标记为外生的，其余关系则为内生的。但某些关系是"隐式"外生的。例如，${q}_{T}$中的关系$W$虽被指定为内生，却从未出现在最小应急集中。接下来我们定义称为支配关系的句法属性，用以捕捉内生关系实为隐式外生的情况。

DEFINITION 3 (DOMINATION). If a query $q$ has endogenous atoms $A,B$ such that $\operatorname{var}\left( A\right)  \subset  \operatorname{var}\left( B\right)$ ,we say that $A$ dominates $B$ .

定义3（支配关系）。若查询$q$存在满足$\operatorname{var}\left( A\right)  \subset  \operatorname{var}\left( B\right)$的内生原子$A,B$，则称$A$支配$B$。

For example, $A\left( x\right)$ dominates $W\left( {x,y,z}\right)$ in ${q}_{T}$ . Whenever a contingency set contains tuples from $W$ ,they can always be replaced with a smaller than,or equal,number of tuples from $A$ .

例如，$A\left( x\right)$在${q}_{T}$中支配$W\left( {x,y,z}\right)$。当应急集包含来自$W$的元组时，总能用数量更少或相等的$A$元组替换它们。

Proposition 4 ( Domination for Resilience [32]). Let $q$ be an sj-free ${CQ}$ and ${q}^{\prime }$ the query resulting from labeling some dominated atoms as exogenous. Then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \equiv  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$ .

命题4（韧性支配关系[32]）。设$q$为无自连接${CQ}$，${q}^{\prime }$是将部分被支配原子标记为外生变量后得到的查询，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \equiv  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$。

When studying resilience, we follow the convention that all dominated atoms are made exogenous, and we consider that the normal form of a query. As we have seen, $A$ dominates $W$ in ${q}_{\mathrm{T}}$ . Similarly,the atom $A$ dominates both $R$ and $T$ in ${q}_{\text{rats }}$ . We thus transform the queries so that the dominated atoms are exogenous. Exogenous atoms have the superscript "x".

研究韧性时，我们约定所有被支配原子均视为外生变量，并将此视为查询的规范形式。如前所述，$A$在${q}_{\mathrm{T}}$中支配$W$。类似地，原子$A$在${q}_{\text{rats }}$中同时支配$R$和$T$。因此我们转换查询使被支配原子成为外生变量，外生原子用上标"x"标注。

$$
{q}_{\mathrm{T}}^{\prime } \mathrel{\text{:-}} A\left( x\right) ,B\left( y\right) ,C\left( z\right) ,{W}^{\mathrm{X}}\left( {x,y,z}\right) 
$$

$$
{q}_{\text{rats }}^{\prime } \mathrel{\text{:-}} {R}^{\mathrm{X}}\left( {x,y}\right) ,A\left( x\right) ,{T}^{\mathrm{X}}\left( {z,x}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right)

Proposition 4 implies that $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{rats }}\right)  \equiv  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{rats }}^{\prime }\right)$ .

### 2.3 Triads and hardness

### 2.3 三元组与复杂性

We showed in [14] that $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\Delta }\right)$ and $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\mathrm{T}}\right)$ from Example 2 are NP-complete. While ${q}_{\Delta }$ and ${q}_{\mathrm{T}}$ appear to be quite different,they share a key common structural property which alone is responsible for hardness for sj-free CQs.

我们在文献[14]中证明，示例2中的$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\Delta }\right)$和$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\mathrm{T}}\right)$具有NP完全性。虽然${q}_{\Delta }$与${q}_{\mathrm{T}}$看似差异显著，但它们共享一个关键结构特性——该特性正是导致无自连接合取查询复杂性的唯一原因。

DEFINITION 5 (TRIAD). A triad is a set of three endogenous atoms, $\mathcal{T} = \left\{  {{S}_{0},{S}_{1},{S}_{2}}\right\}$ such that for every pair $i,j$ ,there is a path from ${S}_{i}$ to ${S}_{j}$ in $\mathcal{H}\left( q\right)$ that uses no variable occurring in the other atom of $\mathcal{T}$ .

定义5（三元组）。三元组是由三个内生原子$\mathcal{T} = \left\{  {{S}_{0},{S}_{1},{S}_{2}}\right\}$构成的集合，其中对于任意原子对$i,j$，在$\mathcal{H}\left( q\right)$中存在从${S}_{i}$到${S}_{j}$的路径，且该路径不使用$\mathcal{T}$中另一原子所含的任何变量。

Intuitively, a triad is a triple of points with "robust connectivity." Observe that atoms $R,S,T$ form a triad in ${q}_{\Delta }$ and atoms $A,B,C$ form a triad in ${q}_{\mathrm{T}}$ (see Fig. 1). For example,there is a path from $R$ to $S$ in ${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ (across hyperedge $y$ ) that uses only variables (here $y$ ) that are not contained in the other atom $\left( {y \notin  \operatorname{var}\left( T\right) }\right)$ . We showed that triads are responsible for hardness:

直观上，三元组是具有"强连通性"的三元点集。观察发现，原子$R,S,T$在${q}_{\Delta }$中构成三元组，原子$A,B,C$在${q}_{\mathrm{T}}$中亦构成三元组（见图1）。例如在${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$中，存在从$R$到$S$的路径（通过超边$y$），该路径仅使用未包含于另一原子$\left( {y \notin  \operatorname{var}\left( T\right) }\right)$的变量（此处为$y$）。我们证明三元组是导致复杂性的根源：

### 2.4 Linear queries

### 2.4 线性查询

A query $q$ is linear if its atoms can be arranged in a linear order s.t. each variable occurs in a contiguous sequence of atoms. Geometrically, a query is linear if all of the vertices of its hypergraph can be drawn along a straight line and all of its hyperedges can be drawn as convex regions (thus the variables form intervals on a line of relations). For example ${q}_{\text{lin }}$ is linear (see Fig. 1d).

当查询$q$的原子能排列成线性序列且每个变量出现在连续的原子序列中时，该查询是线性的。几何意义上，若超图所有顶点可沿直线排列且所有超边可表示为凸区域（即变量在关系线上形成区间），则该查询是线性的。例如${q}_{\text{lin }}$即为线性查询（见图1d）。

It was shown in [32] that for any sj-free CQ that is linear, $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ may be computed in a natural way using network flow. Thus all such queries are easy.

文献[32]表明，对于任何无自连接的线性合取查询$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$，可通过网络流自然计算。因此这类查询均属易解问题。

If all sj-free CQs without a triad were linear, then this would complete the dichotomy theorem for resilience. While this is not the case, we completed the proof of Theorem 7, by showing that every triad-free sj-free ${CQ}$ may be transformed to a linear query of equivalent resilience.

若所有不含三元组的无自连接合取查询都是线性的，这将完善弹性问题的二分法定理。虽然事实并非如此，但我们通过证明每个无三元组的无自连接查询${CQ}$都可转化为具有等效弹性的线性查询，最终完成定理7的证明。

### 2.5 Dichotomy Theorem

### 2.5 二分法定理

Now we can present the full characterization of the complexity of sj-free CQs proved in [14].

现在我们可以完整呈现文献[14]证明的无自连接合取查询复杂度特征。

THEOREM 7 ( DICHOTOMY OF RESILIENCE FOR SJ-FREE CQS [14]). Let $q$ be an sj-free ${CQ}$ and let ${q}^{\prime }$ be the result of making all "dominated" atoms exogenous. If ${q}^{\prime }$ has a triad,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete, otherwise it is in PTIME.

定理7（无自连接合取查询弹性问题的二分法[14]）。设$q$为无自连接查询${CQ}$，${q}^{\prime }$为所有"被支配"原子外生化后的结果。若${q}^{\prime }$存在三元组，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$属于$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全问题，否则属于PTIME问题。

## 3 SELF-JOINS CHANGE EVERYTHING

## 3 自连接改变一切

Queries with self-joins are far more complicated than sj-free queries for at least 4 reasons: (1) For the sj-free case, triads alone were shown to determine hardness. Triads need at least 3 existential variables and at least 3 subgoals. Section 3.1 shows that already 2 atoms or 2 variables can be enough for hardness; (2) Linear sj-free queries can be solved using a natural reduction to network flow. For self-join queries, linear queries can be hard. Furthermore, Section 3.3 shows that we may need more elaborate reductions to network flow, even when they are easy. (3) The previous definition of domination does not lead to the desired properties in the presence of self-joins. Section 3.2 explains why dominated atoms may still be relevant when computing the minimum contingency set. (4) Our previous crucial concept of the dual hypergraph is no longer sufficient to characterize queries when relations appear multiple times. The position at which a variable appears in a subgoal may influence the complexity of resilience, including whether an atom has repeated variables,e.g.," $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,y}\right)$ ."

含自连接的查询比无自连接查询复杂得多，主要原因有四：(1)无自连接情况下，仅三元组即可判定问题难度。而三元组至少需要3个存在变量和3个子目标。第3.1节证明仅需2个原子或2个变量即可导致难解；(2)无自连接的线性查询可通过自然归约到网络流求解。含自连接的线性查询却可能难解。第3.3节进一步表明，即使问题简单时，我们仍可能需要更复杂的网络流归约；(3)原有支配定义在存在自连接时不再具备理想性质。第3.2节解释为何计算最小应急集时被支配原子仍可能相关；(4)当关系多次出现时，我们先前提出的对偶超图关键概念不足以表征查询特征。变量在子目标中出现的位置可能影响弹性复杂度，例如"$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,y}\right)$"中原子是否含重复变量。

In the cases where the variable position is relevant and we are restricted to binary queries, we naturally represent queries as labeled direct graphs. This representation captures all relevant structural information of the binary queries, especially the relative position of variables, which the hypergraph representation does not reflect.

### 4.1 Minimal queries

### 4.1 最小化查询

Given queries ${q}_{1}$ and ${q}_{2}$ ,we say that ${q}_{1}$ is contained in ${q}_{2}\left( {{q}_{1} \subseteq  {q}_{2}}\right)$ if answers to ${q}_{1}$ over any database instance $D$ are always a subset of the answers to ${q}_{2}$ over $D$ . We say ${q}_{1}$ is equivalent to ${q}_{2}\left( {{q}_{1} \equiv  {q}_{2}}\right)$ if ${q}_{1} \subseteq  {q}_{2}$ and ${q}_{2} \subseteq  {q}_{1}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ . We say a conjunctive query $q$ is minimal if for every other conjunctive query ${q}^{\prime }$ such that $q \equiv  {q}^{\prime },{q}^{\prime }$ has at least as many atoms as $q$ . For every query $q$ ,there exists a minimal equivalent CQ ${q}^{\prime }$ that can be obtained from $q$ by removing zero or more atoms [6].

给定查询${q}_{1}$和${q}_{2}$，若对于任意数据库实例$D$，${q}_{1}$的答案总是${q}_{2}$在$D$上答案的子集，则称${q}_{1}$包含于${q}_{2}\left( {{q}_{1} \subseteq  {q}_{2}}\right)$。当${q}_{1} \subseteq  {q}_{2}$且${q}_{2} \subseteq  {q}_{1}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$时，称${q}_{1}$等价于${q}_{2}\left( {{q}_{1} \equiv  {q}_{2}}\right)$。若对于所有满足$q \equiv  {q}^{\prime },{q}^{\prime }$且原子数不少于$q$的合取查询${q}^{\prime }$，称合取查询$q$为最小化查询。任何查询$q$都存在可通过移除零或多个原子获得的最小等价CQ${q}^{\prime }$[6]。

From now on, we focus only on minimal queries. This is WLOG, since any non-minimal query can always be minimized as a preprocessing step. The reason is that our hardness evaluation relies on identifying certain subqueries (or patterns) in a query that make this query hard. However, if a pattern is in a subquery that is removed during minimization, then, this pattern has no effect on the resilience of the query.

下文仅关注最小化查询。这具有普遍性（WLOG），因为非最小化查询总可通过预处理步骤最小化。原因在于：我们的硬度评估依赖于识别查询中导致计算困难的特定子查询（或模式）。若某模式存在于最小化过程中被移除的子查询中，则该模式对查询的弹性无影响。

### 4.2 Query components

### 4.2 查询组件

A connected component of $q$ (or "component" in short) is a nonempty subset of atoms that are connected via existential variables. A query $q$ is disconnected if its atoms can be partitioned into two or more components that do not share any existential variables. For example,

查询$q$的连通组件（简称"组件"）指通过存在变量连接的原子非空子集。若某查询的原子可划分为两个及以上不共享存在变量的组件，则称该查询$q$为非连通。例如：

$$
{q}_{\text{comp }} \mathrel{\text{:-}} A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right) ,B\left( w\right) 
$$

is disconnected and has two components:

该查询非连通且含两个组件：

$$
{q}_{\text{comp }}^{1} \mathrel{\text{:-}} A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) 
$$

$$
{q}_{\text{comp }}^{2} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {z,w}\right) ,B\left( w\right) .
$$

The resilience of a query is determined by taking the minimum of the resiliences of each of its components. In the following, let $\rho \left( {q,D}\right)$ stand for the resilience of query $q$ over database $D$ ,which is the size of the minimum contingency set for(q,D).

查询弹性通过取其各组件弹性的最小值确定。下文用$\rho \left( {q,D}\right)$表示查询$q$在数据库$D$上的弹性，即(q,D)的最小应急集规模。

LEMMA 14 (● QUERY COMPONENTS). Let $q :  - {q}_{1},\ldots ,{q}_{k}$ be a query that consists of $k$ components ${q}_{i},i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ . Then $\rho \left( {q,D}\right)  = \mathop{\min }\limits_{i}\rho \left( {{q}_{i},D}\right)$ .

引理14（●查询组件）。设$q :  - {q}_{1},\ldots ,{q}_{k}$是由$k$个组件${q}_{i},i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$构成的查询，则$\rho \left( {q,D}\right)  = \mathop{\min }\limits_{i}\rho \left( {{q}_{i},D}\right)$。

We can now show that the complexity of a query is determined by the hardest of its components if the query is minimal:

现可证明：对于最小化查询，其计算复杂度由最复杂组件决定：

LEMMA 15 (● QUERY COMPONENTS COMPLEXITY). Let $q$ be a minimal query that consists of $k$ query components. $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete if there is at least one component $i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ for which $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{i}\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ - complete. Conversely,if $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{i}\right)$ is in $\mathrm{P}$ for all $i$ ,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is in $\mathrm{P}$ .

引理15（●查询组件复杂度）。设$q$是由$k$个查询组件构成的最小化查询。若存在至少一个$i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$组件使$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{i}\right)$是$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全的，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$是$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全的；反之若所有$i$的$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{i}\right)$均属于$\mathrm{P}$，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$属于$\mathrm{P}$。

In the remainder of the paper we assume queries to be connected.

本文后续部分默认讨论连通查询。

### 4.3 SJ-domination

### 4.3 自连接支配

As discussed in Section 3.2, we need to consider the position of the variables in the attribute list of each atom in a sj-query. We write ${\operatorname{pos}}_{g}^{q}\left( i\right)  = x$ to express that the $i$ -th attribute of atom $g$ is variable $x$ for a query $q$ and omit $q$ when $q$ is clear from the context.

如第3.2节所述，需考虑自连接查询中各原子属性列表中变量的位置。记${\operatorname{pos}}_{g}^{q}\left( i\right)  = x$表示查询$q$中原子$g$的第$i$个属性为变量$x$，当上下文明确时可省略$q$。

Definition 16 (Domination with Self-Joins). Let relations $A$ and $B$ be endogenous relations in query $q$ . We say that $A$ dominates $B$ if there exists a function

定义16（自连接支配）。设关系$A$和$B$是查询$q$中的内生关系。若存在函数使得...

$$
f : \left\lbrack  {\operatorname{arity}\left( A\right) }\right\rbrack   \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {\operatorname{arity}\left( B\right) }\right\rbrack  
$$

such that for each $B$ atom ${g}_{B}$ ,there exists an $A$ atom ${h}_{A}$ satisfying ${\operatorname{pos}}_{{h}_{A}}\left( i\right)  = {\operatorname{pos}}_{{g}_{B}}\left( {f\left( i\right) }\right) ,\forall i \in  \left\lbrack  {\operatorname{arity}\left( A\right) }\right\rbrack$ . In other words,each $B$ atom that occurs in $q$ has a corresponding $A$ atom,and each of these pairs will have matching variables accordingly to function $f$ .

对于每个$B$原子${g}_{B}$，都存在一个$A$原子${h}_{A}$满足${\operatorname{pos}}_{{h}_{A}}\left( i\right)  = {\operatorname{pos}}_{{g}_{B}}\left( {f\left( i\right) }\right) ,\forall i \in  \left\lbrack  {\operatorname{arity}\left( A\right) }\right\rbrack$。换言之，出现在$q$中的每个$B$原子都有对应的$A$原子，这些原子对将根据函数$f$具有相应的匹配变量。

Notice that when $B$ appears only once,the definition of domination is equivalent to the sj-free definition: $\operatorname{var}\left( A\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( B\right)$ .

注意当$B$仅出现一次时，支配定义等价于无自连接定义：$\operatorname{var}\left( A\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( B\right)$。

EXAMPLE 17. To illustrate the new self-join domination, consider the following queries:

示例17. 为说明新的自连接支配，考虑以下查询：

$$
{q}_{1} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,A\left( y\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) 
$$

$$
{q}_{2} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,A\left( y\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) 
$$

By following the definition above,A doesn’t dominate $R$ in ${q}_{1}$ but it does in ${q}_{2}$ ,whereas $S$ is dominated in both queries. Notice that in ${q}_{2}$ , a tuple $R\left( {a,b}\right)$ will always join with tuple $A\left( b\right)$ so we can always choose $A\left( b\right)$ instead to be in the contingency set. The same is not true for ${q}_{1}$ ,where a tuple $R\left( {a,b}\right)$ could join with $A\left( a\right)$ or $A\left( b\right)$ .

根据上述定义，A在${q}_{1}$中不支配$R$，但在${q}_{2}$中支配；而$S$在两个查询中均被支配。注意在${q}_{2}$中，元组$R\left( {a,b}\right)$总会与元组$A\left( b\right)$连接，因此我们总能选择$A\left( b\right)$作为应急集。但${q}_{1}$中情况不同，元组$R\left( {a,b}\right)$可能与$A\left( a\right)$或$A\left( b\right)$连接。

Proposition 18 (● Domination for resilience with Self- -JOIN). Let $q$ be a ${CQ}$ and ${q}^{\prime }$ the result of labeling some dominated relations exogenous. Then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \equiv  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$ .

命题18（●含自连接的韧性支配）。设$q$为${CQ}$，${q}^{\prime }$为标记某些被支配外生关系的结果，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \equiv  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$。

### 4.4 Outline of our plan of attack

### 4.4 研究方案概述

To obtain a dichotomy result for the resilience of binary queries in the presence of a single-self-join, we proceed as follows. (1) Section 5 shows that triads in any conjunctive queries with self-joins still imply hardness (Theorem 24) and furthermore, when triads are absent, the endogenous atoms are linearly connected. We call such queries pseudo-linear (Theorem 25). We conjecture that pseudo-linear queries may be transformed to linear queries of equivalent resilience (Conjecture 26). In any case, it suffices to study the criteria for hardness of pseudo-linear queries. (2) Section 6 generalizes the hardness pattern behind ${q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}$ to a more general class of hard ssj binary queries that contain "paths" between repeated atoms. (3) We then focus on the complexity of the resilience of ssj binary CQs with at most a single repetition of a single relation. Section 7 gives a complete characterization of the complexity for the cases of 2 occurrences of a repeated relation. This is a dichotomy theorem: we show that for all such queries, $q,\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is either $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete or $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is reducible to network flow and is thus in P. Section 8 presents the remaining challenges that must be overcome in order to characterize all queries with 3 occurrences of a repeated relation. In Section 9, we present a "template" for hardness proofs that we believe will help us make progress in the general self-join case.

为获得含单自连接的二元查询韧性二分结果，我们按以下步骤推进：(1)第5节证明含自连接的合取查询中三元组仍导致问题难解（定理24），且无三元组时内禀原子呈线性连接，称此类查询为伪线性（定理25）。我们推测伪线性查询可转化为韧性等效的线性查询（猜想26）。无论如何，研究伪线性查询的难解性标准已足够。(2)第6节将${q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}$的难解模式推广至更广泛的含重复原子间"路径"的难解ssj二元查询类。(3)随后聚焦最多含单个关系单次重复的ssj二元CQ韧性复杂度。第7节完整刻画了重复关系出现2次时的复杂度，这是二分定理：证明所有此类查询中，$q,\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$要么是$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全的，要么$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$可归约为网络流从而属于P类。第8节提出刻画重复关系出现3次查询时需解决的剩余挑战。第9节给出难解性证明的"模板"，这将助力推进一般自连接情形研究。

## 5 NON-LINEAR QUERIES: NP-COMPLETE

## 5 非线性查询：NP完全问题

In this section we prove that queries containing triads remain hard in the presence of self-joins (Theorem 24). We then show that for any query that does not contain a triad, its endogenous atoms are arranged linearly. We call such a query pseudo-linear. Thus, we conclude that either a query contains a triad in which case its resilience problem is NP-complete, or it is pseudo-linear. In the following sections, we can thus safely restrict our attention to pseudo-linear queries.

本节证明含三元组的查询在自连接情形下仍保持难解性（定理24）。继而证明不含三元组的查询，其内禀原子呈线性排列，称此类查询为伪线性。因此结论是：查询要么含三元组（此时韧性问题是NP完全的），要么是伪线性的。后续章节可安全地专注于伪线性查询研究。

Definition 19 (Self-join variation of a CQ). Let $q$ be a sj-free ${CQ}$ and let ${q}^{sj}$ result from $q$ by replacing some atoms ${S}_{i}\left( \bar{v}\right)$ from $q$ with the atom ${R}_{i}\left( \bar{v}\right)$ ,where the relation ${R}_{i}$ occurs elsewhere in $q$ . We say that ${q}^{sj}$ is a self-join variation of $q$ .

定义19（CQ的自连接变体）。设$q$为无自连接的${CQ}$，若${q}^{sj}$是通过将$q$中的某些原子${S}_{i}\left( \bar{v}\right)$替换为原子${R}_{i}\left( \bar{v}\right)$而得到（其中关系${R}_{i}$在$q$其他位置出现），则称${q}^{sj}$是$q$的自连接变体。

EXAMPLE 20 (SELF-JOIN VARIATIONS). Consider sj-free query ${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ . The following are all its possible self-join variations:

示例20（自连接变体）。考虑无自连接查询${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$，以下是其所有可能的自连接变体：

$$
{q}_{\bigtriangleup } \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) ,T\left( {z,x}\right) \;\text{ (Triangle) }
$$

$$
{q}_{\Delta }^{s{j}_{1}} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right) ,R\left( {z,x}\right) 
$$

$$
{q}_{\Delta }^{s{j}_{2}} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right) ,T\left( {z,x}\right) 
$$

$$
{q}_{\Delta }^{s{j}_{3}} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) ,R\left( {z,x}\right) 
$$

We first observe that the resilience of self-join variations of a query can only be harder than their sj-free counterpart:

我们首先观察到，查询自连接变体的弹性计算必然比其无自连接版本更困难：

LEMMA 21 (● SJ CAN ONLY MAKE RESILIENCE HARDER). Let $q$ be an sj-free ${CQ}$ and let ${q}^{sj}$ be a self-join variation of $q$ . If ${q}^{sj}$ is minimal, then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \leq  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{sj}\right)$ .

引理21（● 自连接只会增加弹性计算难度）。设$q$为无自连接的${CQ}$，${q}^{sj}$是$q$的自连接变体。若${q}^{sj}$是极小的，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \leq  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{sj}\right)$。

We need to rely on the fact that ${q}^{\mathrm{{sj}}}$ is minimal for the result to hold, as we see in the example below:

需要依赖${q}^{\mathrm{{sj}}}$的极小性才能使结论成立，如下例所示：

EXAMPLE 22. Consider query $q :  - R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {z,y}\right) ,T\left( {z,w}\right) ,A\left( {x,w}\right)$ , and observe that $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete because $q$ contains a triad. $A$ possible self-join variation is ${q}^{sj} :  - R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right) ,R\left( {x,w}\right)$ . Note that ${q}^{sj}$ is not minimal,and is equivalent to $R\left( {x,y}\right)$ . So $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{sj}\right)$ is trivially in $\mathrm{P}$ .

示例22。考虑查询$q :  - R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {z,y}\right) ,T\left( {z,w}\right) ,A\left( {x,w}\right)$，注意到$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全性，因为$q$包含三元组。$A$可能自连接变体是${q}^{sj} :  - R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right) ,R\left( {x,w}\right)$。注意${q}^{sj}$非极小，等价于$R\left( {x,y}\right)$，因此$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{sj}\right)$显然属于$\mathrm{P}$。

By Lemma 21,the resilience of the self-join variations of ${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ are NP-complete. Recall from Definition 5 that a triad is set of three endogenous atoms, so we can say that the self-join variations of ${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$ all have triads. However,it does not immediately follow from Lemma 21 that every sj-query with a triad is hard. The missing cases are when an sj-query includes a triad, but it is not a self-join variation of an sj-free query with a triad. We next explore this situation.

根据引理21，${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$自连接变体的弹性计算均为NP完全问题。由定义5可知，三元组是由三个内生原子构成的集合，因此可以说${q}_{\bigtriangleup }$的所有自连接变体都包含三元组。但引理21并不能直接推出所有含三元组的自连接查询都是困难的——当自连接查询包含三元组，但并非某个含三元组的无自连接查询的变体时，就属于遗漏情况。接下来我们将探讨这种情形。

### 5.1 Self-join variations of ${q}_{\text{rats }}$ and ${q}_{\text{brats }}$

### 5.1 ${q}_{\text{rats }}$与${q}_{\text{brats }}$的自连接变体

Recall two important sj-free queries:

回顾两个重要的无自连接查询：

$$
{q}_{\text{rats }} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,A\left( x\right) ,T\left( {z,x}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right)  \tag{Rats}
$$

$$
{q}_{\text{brats }} \mathrel{\text{:-}} B\left( y\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,A\left( x\right) ,T\left( {z,x}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) \;\text{ (Brats) } \tag{Brats}
$$

$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\Delta }\right)$ is NP-complete because it contains the triad, $R,S,T$ . However, ${q}_{\text{rats }}$ and ${q}_{\text{brats }}$ are easy because $A$ dominates $R,T$ and $B$ dominates $S$ so they only have two endogenous atoms each and thus no triad.

$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\Delta }\right)$因包含三元组$R,S,T$而具有NP完全性。但${q}_{\text{rats }}$和${q}_{\text{brats }}$是简单的，因为$A$支配$R,T$且$B$支配$S$，故它们各自仅含两个内生原子，不构成三元组。

The same doesn’t occur with some self-join variations of ${q}_{\text{rats }}$ and ${q}_{\text{brats }}$ . Below we list two example of variations which contain triads.

这种情况在${q}_{\text{rats }}$和${q}_{\text{brats }}$的某些自连接变体中并不成立。下面列出两个包含三元组的变体示例：

$$
{q}_{\text{rats }}^{{\mathrm{{sj}}}_{1}} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right) ,R\left( {z,x}\right) 
$$

$$
{q}_{\text{brats }}^{{\mathrm{{sj}}}_{1}} \mathrel{\text{:-}} B\left( y\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {z,x}\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right) 
$$

In these examples,relation $R$ is now more robust and not dominated by $A$ or $B$ . Therefore,they still contain triads consisting of their three $R$ -atoms. The presence of a triad is a strong indication that these queries are hard but we cannot use Lemma 21 to show this because their sj-free counterparts, ${q}_{\text{rats }}$ and ${q}_{\text{brats }}$ ,are easy. We now proceed to show their complexity is hard.

在这些示例中，关系$R$现在更具鲁棒性，不受$A$或$B$支配。因此它们仍包含由三个$R$原子构成的三元组。三元组的存在强烈暗示这些查询是困难的，但由于其对应的无自连接版本${q}_{\text{rats }}$和${q}_{\text{brats }}$是简单的，我们无法直接使用引理21证明。接下来将论证它们的计算复杂度确实属于困难类。

Proposition 23. Let $q$ be a self-join variation of ${q}_{\text{rats }}$ or ${q}_{\text{brats }}$ . If $q$ has a triad,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete.

命题23. 设$q$是${q}_{\text{rats }}$或${q}_{\text{brats }}$的自连接变体。若$q$存在三元组，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全性。

Using Proposition 23, we now generalize the fact that triads make sj-free queries hard (Lemma 6) to the same result for general CQs.

基于命题23，我们现在将"三元组导致无自连接查询变难"的结论（引理6）推广至一般合取查询。

### 5.2 Triads Make Queries Hard

### 5.2 三元组导致查询困难

THEOREM 24 (● SJ-QUERIES WITH TRIADS). If $q$ has a triad,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is NP-complete.

定理24（●含三元组的自连接查询）。若$q$存在三元组，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$是NP完全的。

Proof Sketch. We argue that there are only two cases to consider when a sj-query $q$ has a triad. Consider that $q$ is a self-join variations of a sj-free query ${q}^{\prime }$ . The first case is when the resilience of ${q}^{\prime }$ is hard,i.e.,RES $\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$ is NP-complete. Then,we can use Lemma 21 to show $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete. The second case is when $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$ is in $\mathrm{P}$ . Then,we argue that we can show a reduction from $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime \prime }\right)  \leq  \operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ ,where ${q}^{\prime \prime }$ is a self-join variation of either ${q}_{\text{rats }}$ or ${q}_{\text{brats }}$ that contains a triad. From Proposition 23, $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime \prime }\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete,which thus implies that $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is also NP-complete.

证明概要。我们论证自连接查询$q$存在三元组时只需考虑两种情况。设$q$是无自连接查询${q}^{\prime }$的自连接变体。第一种情况是当${q}^{\prime }$的韧性判定本身是困难问题，即RES$\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$为NP完全。此时可运用引理21证明$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全性。第二种情况是当$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$属于$\mathrm{P}$类。此时可通过从$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime \prime }\right)  \leq  \operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$归约来证明，其中${q}^{\prime \prime }$是含有三元组的${q}_{\text{rats }}$或${q}_{\text{brats }}$的自连接变体。根据命题23，$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime \prime }\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全性，故$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$也是NP完全的。

Thus, if a query contains a triad, it is hard. In the next section, we discuss queries that do not contain triads and how they are similar to linear queries, since their endogenous atoms are linearly connected.

因此，含有三元组的查询必为困难问题。下节将讨论不含三元组的查询，由于其内蕴原子呈线性连接，它们与线性查询具有相似性。

### 5.3 No Triad Means Pseudo-Linear

### 5.3 无三元组意味着伪线性

In [14],we proved that if a sj-free $\mathrm{{CQ}}q$ has no triad,then $q$ may be transformed to a sj-free CQ query ${q}^{\prime }$ which is linear and such that $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \leq  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$ . Since linear sj-free CQ’s are easy,it follows that $q$ is easy.

文献[14]证明：若无自连接查询$\mathrm{{CQ}}q$不含三元组，则可将其转换为等价的无自连接线性合取查询${q}^{\prime }$，且满足$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \leq  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$。由于无自连接线性合取查询易解，故$q$亦为易解问题。

This argument no longer works in the presence of self-joins because linear queries can be easy or hard. However, we can extend the theorem from [14] to show the following,

该论证在存在自连接时不成立，因线性查询可能难易并存。但我们可以扩展文献[14]的定理以证明：

Theorem 25 (● No Triad Means Pseudo-Linear). Let $q$ be a CQ with no triad. Then all endogenous atoms in $q$ are connected linearly.

定理25（●无三元组意味着伪线性）。设$q$为不含三元组的合取查询，则其所有内蕴原子均呈线性连接。

We conjecture that pseudo-linearity is equivalent to linearity when considering resilience. What makes a query pseudo-linear, instead of linear or containing a triad, is the presence of some exogenous atoms. However, the exogenous atoms of a query are mostly only connecting the endogenous atoms, and also, if necessary, ensuring that $q$ is a minimal query,so we believe they can be modified to obtain a linear query without altering the complexity of a query.

我们推测在韧性判定问题上，伪线性等价于线性。查询之所以呈现伪线性而非线性或含三元组，源于某些外蕴原子的存在。但这些外蕴原子主要起连接内蕴原子的作用，必要时也确保$q$为极小查询。因此我们认为可通过修改外蕴原子获得线性查询，且不改变原查询复杂度。

Conjecture 26 (● No Triad Means Linear). Let $q$ be a ${CQ}$ with no triad. Then we can transform $q$ to a linear ${CQ}{q}^{\prime }$ with $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \equiv$ $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$ .

猜想26（●无三元组意味着线性）。设$q$为不含三元组的${CQ}$，则可将其转换为满足$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)  \equiv$$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}^{\prime }\right)$的线性查询${CQ}{q}^{\prime }$。

## 6 PATHS ARE HARD

## 6 路径查询亦为难题

Section 3.1 presented two linear queries that are hard, unlike in the sj-free case where all linear queries are easy. Note that these queries are binary and, in both, only one relation is part of a self-join. In other words, these are single-self-join binary queries.

第3.1节展示了两个困难的线性查询，这与无自连接情形下所有线性查询均易解的特性不同。需注意这些查询均为二元形式，且各自仅有一个关系参与自连接。换言之，它们都是单自连接的二元查询。

We now identify a pattern characteristic of ${q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}$ that we call a path. The main result of this section is that every ssj binary query containing a path is hard. We start by showing the case where the self-join relation is unary.

我们现在识别出${q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}$中一种称为路径的模式特征。本节主要结论是：每个包含路径的ssj二元查询都是难解的。我们首先展示自连接关系为一元的情况。

THEOREM 27 ([D UNARY PATH). Let $q$ be a minimal ssj-CQ. If $q$ contains distinct atoms $R\left( x\right)$ and $R\left( y\right)$ ,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete.

定理27（[D 一元路径）。设$q$为最小ssj-CQ。若$q$包含不同原子$R\left( x\right)$和$R\left( y\right)$，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全性。

Proof sketch. Let $R\left( x\right)$ and $R\left( y\right)$ be the first two occurrences of the relation $R$ in $q$ . Since $q$ is connected, $R\left( x\right)$ and $R\left( y\right)$ are connected by at least one non-self-join relation, $S$ (see Fig. 4a). We prove that $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}\right)  \leq  \operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ . Details are in the full version [15],but it is not hard to see that any database $D \vDash  {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}$ can be transformed to a database ${D}^{\prime } \vDash  q$ that exactly preserves resilience. Here ${R}^{\prime },{S}^{\prime }$ in ${D}^{\prime }$ come from $A$ and $R$ in $D$ ,and all the other atoms of $q$ (including any additional occurrences of the self-join relation, $R$ ,to the right of $R\left( y\right)$ ) are covered by multiple,extra values which complete the joins but are never chosen in minimum contingency sets. Note that this proof doesn't make any assumption about the existence or not of triads.

证明概要。令$R\left( x\right)$和$R\left( y\right)$为关系$R$在$q$中的前两次出现。由于$q$是连通的，$R\left( x\right)$与$R\left( y\right)$之间至少通过一个非自连接关系$S$相连（见图4a）。我们证明$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}\right)  \leq  \operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$。完整细节见文献[15]，但显然任何数据库$D \vDash  {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}$都可转换为精确保持弹性的数据库${D}^{\prime } \vDash  q$。此处${D}^{\prime }$中的${R}^{\prime },{S}^{\prime }$源自$D$中的$A$和$R$，而$q$的其他所有原子（包括$R\left( y\right)$右侧自连接关系$R$的额外出现）均由多个辅助值覆盖，这些值补全连接但绝不会被选入最小应急集。注意该证明未对三元组存在与否做任何假设。

When the self-join relation is binary, if two consecutive atoms, $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right)$ ,are disjoint,then we call this a binary path. "Overlapping" consecutive atoms with shared variables,such as $R\left( {x,y}\right)$ , $R\left( {y,z}\right)$ in ${q}_{\text{chain }}$ ,can also cause hardness and are studied in later sections.

当自连接关系为二元时，若两个连续原子$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right)$互不相交，则称其为二元路径。具有共享变量的"重叠"连续原子（如${q}_{\text{chain }}$中的$R\left( {x,y}\right)$、$R\left( {y,z}\right)$）同样会导致难解性，这将在后续章节研究。

THEOREM 28 ([D BINARY PATH). Let $q$ be a minimal ssj-CQ. If $q$ has distinct consecutive sj atoms $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right)$ with $\{ x,y\}  \cap  \{ z,w\}  = \varnothing$ , then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ complete.

定理28（[D 二元路径）。设$q$为最小ssj-CQ。若$q$存在满足$\{ x,y\}  \cap  \{ z,w\}  = \varnothing$的不同连续sj原子$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right)$，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全性。

Proof Sketch. Given $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right)$ as in the statement of the theorem,there must be an atom $S\left( {u,v}\right)$ ,with $S \neq  R$ on the path between them,and $u \in  \{ x,y\}$ and $v \notin  \{ x,y\}$ . Now,as in the proof of Theorem 27,we reduce $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}\right)$ to $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ . We map any database $D \vDash  {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}$ to a database ${D}^{\prime } \vDash  q$ ,where ${R}^{\prime }$ contains $\{ \left( {a,a}\right)$ $A\left( a\right)  \in  D\}$ plus other multiple,extra values for any other atoms of the relation $R$ in $q$ to the left of $R\left( {x,y}\right)$ or the right of $R\left( {z,w}\right)$ and ${S}^{\prime } = \{ \left( {a,b}\right)  \mid  R\left( {a,b}\right)  \in  D\}$ . Same as in the unary case,there is no assumption about the linearity of the query. Details are the full version [15].

证明概要。给定定理陈述中的$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right)$，必然存在原子$S\left( {u,v}\right)$，其路径上有$S \neq  R$，且满足$u \in  \{ x,y\}$与$v \notin  \{ x,y\}$。参照定理27的证明方法，我们将$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}\right)$归约为$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$。将任意数据库$D \vDash  {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}$映射到数据库${D}^{\prime } \vDash  q$，其中${R}^{\prime }$包含$\{ \left( {a,a}\right)$$A\left( a\right)  \in  D\}$，以及关系$R$在$q$中位于$R\left( {x,y}\right)$左侧或$R\left( {z,w}\right)$与${S}^{\prime } = \{ \left( {a,b}\right)  \mid  R\left( {a,b}\right)  \in  D\}$右侧的其他原子的多个额外值。与一元情形相同，查询的线性性不作假设。详见完整版[15]。

Unary and Binary Paths are the simplest of the hard patterns. By Theorem 27 and Theorem 28, they always force their queries to be hard.

一元与二元路径是最基础的困难模式。根据定理27和定理28，它们总会导致查询具有计算复杂性。

Since we have established that an sj- query either has a triad or is pseudo-linear (Theorem 25) and because we have proved that triads imply hardness (Theorem 24), we can now focus on the pseudo-linear queries.

既然我们已证明sj查询要么包含三元组要么是伪线性的（定理25），且三元组意味着计算困难性（定理24），现在可以聚焦于伪线性查询的研究。

In the next sections we study the more subtle pseudo-linear ssj binary queries, which do not contain paths.

后续章节将探讨更复杂的伪线性ssj二元查询，这类查询不包含路径结构。

## 7 QUERIES WITH EXACTLY TWO R-ATOMS

## 7 仅含两个R原子的查询

In this section we cover the complexity of pseudo-linear ssj binary queries with exactly two atoms referring to the same relation. We will refer to this relation as $R$ . As always,we assume that our query is minimal and connected,and from now on also assume that $q$ does not contain a triad or a path as described in Theorem 27 and Theorem 28; otherwise we would already know that $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ - complete. Even in this restricted setting, we will see that there is a surprisingly rich variety of structures, requiring different strategies to determine their complexity.

本节研究具有两个同关系原子的伪线性ssj二元查询的计算复杂性。该关系记作$R$。始终假设查询是极小且连通的，并额外排除定理27和定理28描述的三元组或路径存在的情况——否则已知$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全性。即便在这种限制条件下，我们仍将发现存在惊人的结构多样性，需要不同策略来判定其复杂性。

Because there are no paths, $R$ must be a binary relation and the two $R$ -atoms must have at least one variable in common.

由于不存在路径，$R$必为二元关系，且两个$R$原子至少共享一个变量。

- Chains have one common variable and join in different attributes,e.g., $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right)$ ;

- 链式结构共享一个变量并通过不同属性连接，例如$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right)$；

- Confluences have one common variable and join in the same attribute,e.g., $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right)$ ;

- 汇合结构共享一个变量并通过相同属性连接，例如$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right)$；

- Permutations share both variables but join in different attributes,e.g., $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,x}\right)$ .

- 置换结构共享全部变量但通过不同属性连接，例如$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,x}\right)$。

- Queries with repeated variables (REP) have repeated variables in at least one $R$ -atom e.g., $R\left( {x,x}\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,B\left( y\right)$

- 含重复变量的查询（REP）在至少一个$R$原子中存在重复变量，例如$R\left( {x,x}\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,B\left( y\right)$

Figure 5 shows the binary graphs for each these patterns, which helps visualize the subtle variations in how the $R$ -atoms can join. We consider each of these possibilities in turn and characterize their complexity.

图5展示了各类模式的二元图，直观呈现$R$原子间连接的微妙差异。我们将依次分析这些可能性并界定其复杂性。

### 7.1 2-Chains

### 7.1 双链结构

The chain query is the simplest possible minimal sj-query with two atoms and we proved earlier that its resilience is NP-complete (Proposition 10). In this section we prove that the chain structure is quite robust and that any of its expansions remains NP-complete.

链式查询是最简单的双原子极小sj查询，前文已证明其弹性问题是NP完全的（命题10）。本节将证明链式结构具有强健壮性，其任何扩展形式仍保持NP完全性。

We call "expansions" of ${q}_{\text{chain }}$ any query obtained by adding new relations to it, i.e. relations that do not self-join. We start by presenting the expansions obtained by adding unary relations and then generalize that to any expansion.

我们将${q}_{\text{chain }}$的"扩展"定义为通过添加新关系（即非自连接关系）得到的查询。首先讨论添加一元关系形成的扩展，再推广至任意扩展情形。

Figure 6a shows how unary relations can be added to ${q}_{\text{chain }}$ . Each one can appear by itself or combined with others. While the proof involves several subcases, the important take-away is that all 8 of these expansions are hard.

图6a展示了如何将一元关系添加到${q}_{\text{chain }}$中。每个关系可以单独出现或与其他关系组合。虽然证明涉及多个子情况，但关键结论是这8种扩展都属于难解问题。

Proposition 29 (Chains with unary relations). Any expansion of ${q}_{\text{chain }}$ with unary relations is NP-complete.

命题29（带一元关系的链）。任何对${q}_{\text{chain }}$添加一元关系的扩展都是NP完全的。

Proof Sketch. We prove these expansions are hard by a reduction from 3SAT. The same idea used to prove that $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{chain }}\right)$ is hard will work here as long as we adapt the variable and clause gadgets to deal with the existence of the unary relations. Details are in the full version [15].

证明概要。我们通过3SAT归约证明这些扩展的难解性。只要调整变量和子句构件来处理一元关系的存在性，证明$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{chain }}\right)$难解性的相同思路在此依然适用。详见完整版[15]。

Now we can generalize this hardness result to any chain expansion using a reduction idea similar to the ones used for the proofs of Theorems 27 and 28 for paths.

现在我们可以通过类似定理27和28中路径证明的归约思路，将这一难解性结果推广到任何链扩展。

Proposition 30 (Q: Chains). If a query $q$ contains a 2-chain as its only self-join,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete.

命题30（Q：链）。若查询$q$包含仅作为自连接的2-链，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$是$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全的。

### 7.2 2-Confluences

### 7.2 二合流

Confluences are defined by relation $R$ joining only in the same attribute. We refer to this pattern as ${q}_{\text{conf }}$ (Fig. 6b).

合流由仅在相同属性上连接的关系$R$定义。我们将此模式称为${q}_{\text{conf }}$（图6b）。

Note that as a stand-alone query ${q}_{\text{conf }}$ is not minimal,so we need other atoms connected to both $x$ and $z$ . An example of a minimal query containing a confluence is ${q}_{\text{conf }}^{AC} \mathrel{\text{:=}} A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right) ,C\left( z\right)$ .

注意作为独立查询时${q}_{\text{conf }}$非最小化，因此需要其他同时连接$x$和$z$的原子。包含合流的最小化查询示例是${q}_{\text{conf }}^{AC} \mathrel{\text{:=}} A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right) ,C\left( z\right)$。

We next show that the standard flow algorithm without any modifications works correctly for linear queries with no self-join other than one 2-confluence, thus generalizing the idea of Proposition 12.

接下来我们将证明，未经修改的标准流算法可正确处理除单个二合流外无其他自连接的线性查询，从而推广命题12的思路。

Proposition 31 (Q: ${q}_{\mathrm{{CONF}}}$ ). RES(q)for any linear query $q$ with ${q}_{\text{conf }}$ as its only self-join pattern can be solved in $\mathrm{P}$ by standard network flow.

命题31（Q：${q}_{\mathrm{{CONF}}}$）。任何以${q}_{\text{conf }}$为唯一自连接模式的线性查询$q$的RES(q)问题，可通过标准网络流在$\mathrm{P}$时间内求解。

In Proposition 31 we assume that $q$ is linear,thus guaranteeing that every path in $q$ from $x$ to $z$ involves the variable $y$ ,and therefore we are able to create a network flow to solve the problem. Note that this is not true in general for pseudo-linear queries containing ${q}_{\text{conf }}$ . For example,consider $c{f}_{p} :  - R\left( {x,y}\right) {H}^{\mathrm{x}}\left( {x,z}\right) R\left( {z,y}\right)$ . It is easy to see that $c{f}_{p}$ is pseudo-linear but we have $\operatorname{RES}\left( {c{f}_{p}}\right)  \equiv  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}\right)$ . Thus,we cover all possible cases for ${q}_{\text{conf }}$ by observing,

命题31中我们假定$q$是线性的，从而保证从$x$到$z$的每条路径都涉及变量$y$，因此能构建网络流解决问题。注意这对包含${q}_{\text{conf }}$的伪线性查询通常不成立。例如考虑$c{f}_{p} :  - R\left( {x,y}\right) {H}^{\mathrm{x}}\left( {x,z}\right) R\left( {z,y}\right)$，显然$c{f}_{p}$是伪线性的但存在$\operatorname{RES}\left( {c{f}_{p}}\right)  \equiv  \operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}}\right)$。因此我们通过观察覆盖${q}_{\text{conf }}$的所有可能情况：

Proposition 32 (Q):). Let $q$ be a pseudo-linear query with ${q}_{\text{conf }}$ as its only self-join pattern. If $q$ contains an exogenous path from $x$ to $z$ not involving the variable $y$ ,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete; otherwise it is in $\mathrm{P}$ .

命题32（Q）：）。设$q$是以${q}_{\text{conf }}$为唯一自连接模式的伪线性查询。若$q$包含不涉及变量$y$的从$x$到$z$的外生路径，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$是$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全的；否则属于$\mathrm{P}$类。

### 7.3 2-Permutations

### 7.3 双排列

We call two $R$ -atoms sharing both variables a permutation. The smallest pattern that has this property is $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,x}\right)$ (Fig. 5). We show that permutations have both NP-complete and PTIME instances.

我们将共享两个变量的$R$原子称为排列。具有此属性的最小模式是$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,x}\right)$（图5）。我们将证明排列同时存在NP完全和PTIME实例。

Easy permutations. We start with two easy permutations.

简单排列。我们从两个简单排列开始。

$$
{q}_{\text{perm }} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,x}\right) \;{q}_{\text{perm }}^{A} \mathrel{\text{:-}} A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,x}\right) 
$$

Proposition 33. $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{perm }}\right)$ and $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}\right)$ are in $\mathrm{P}$ .

命题33。$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{perm }}\right)$和$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}\right)$属于$\mathrm{P}$。

Proof. Given a database ${D}_{1}$ satisfying ${q}_{\text{perm }}$ ,each tuple that is part of a witness for ${D}_{1},{q}_{\text{perm }}$ is part of exactly one witness. Therefore the size of a minimum contingency set for ${D}_{1},{q}_{\text{perm }}$ is exactly the number of witnesses.

证明。给定满足${q}_{\text{perm }}$的数据库${D}_{1}$，每个作为${D}_{1},{q}_{\text{perm }}$见证的元组都恰好属于一个见证。因此${D}_{1},{q}_{\text{perm }}$的最小应急集大小正好等于见证数量。

Given a database ${D}_{2}$ satisfying ${q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}$ ,for each join(a,b),we have 2 possible choices. Either $A\left( a\right)$ will be in the min $\Gamma$ or either one of $R\left( {a,b}\right)$ and $R\left( {b,a}\right)$ but never both. Therefore we can reduce $\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}\right)$ to vertex cover in a bipartite graph,which is in P.

给定满足${q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}$的数据库${D}_{2}$，对于每个连接(a,b)，我们有两种选择：要么$A\left( a\right)$位于最小$\Gamma$中，要么是$R\left( {a,b}\right)$或$R\left( {b,a}\right)$其中之一，但绝不会两者兼有。因此我们可以将$\operatorname{RES}\left( {q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}\right)$归约为二分图中的顶点覆盖问题，后者属于P类。

Hard permutations. Surprisingly, adding another unary atom to ${q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}$ ,thus bounding it on both ends.leads to a hard query.

困难排列。令人惊讶的是，在${q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}$上再添加一个一元原子使其两端受限，会导致查询变得困难。

$$
{q}_{\text{perm }}^{AB} \mathrel{\text{:-}} A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,x}\right) ,B\left( y\right) 
$$

It is still true that for any pair $R\left( {a,b}\right) ,R\left( {b,a}\right)$ participating in a join, a minimum contingency set will only contain one tuple from the pair. This might lead to the wrong conclusion that network flow could solve this problem. We will next show that this is incorrect.

对于参与连接的任意元组对$R\left( {a,b}\right) ,R\left( {b,a}\right)$，最小应急集仍只会包含该对中的一个元组。这可能导致错误结论认为网络流可以解决此问题。接下来我们将证明这是不正确的。

Proposition 34. RES $\left( {q}_{\text{perm }}^{AB}\right)$ is NP-complete.

命题34。RES$\left( {q}_{\text{perm }}^{AB}\right)$是NP完全的。

The criterion. The main structural difference between the hard and easy permutations defined above is whether or not there are relations that "bound" the permutation on both ends, i.e. whether there are endogenous relations $S,T$ ,such that $S$ contains variable $x$ but not $y$ ,and $T$ contains variable $y$ but not $x$ . Thus,the hard permutation, ${q}_{\text{perm }}^{AB}$ ,is bound,but the easy ones, ${q}_{\text{perm }},{q}_{\text{perm }}^{A}$ , are not bound. ### 7.4 Queries with REP

### 7.4 含REP的查询

We call queries with repeated variables (or REP in short) those where atoms contain the same variable twice,e.g. occurrences of $R\left( {x,x}\right)$ . Note that this is only relevant for the case where $R$ is part of a self-join,otherwise it could be considered as $R\left( x\right)$ .

我们将原子中包含重复变量（简称REP）的查询称为重复变量查询，例如出现$R\left( {x,x}\right)$的情形。需注意，仅当$R$作为自连接部分时此特性才有意义，否则可视为$R\left( x\right)$处理。

There are only three patterns to consider when we are restricted to two $R$ -atoms,either one or both atoms have repeated variables. The following queries are the smallest examples of this class of queries:

当限定为两个$R$原子时，仅需考虑三种模式：单个原子含重复变量或两个原子均含重复变量。以下是此类查询的最小示例：

$$
{z}_{1} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,x}\right) ,S\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,y}\right) 
$$

$$
{z}_{2} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,x}\right) ,S\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right) 
$$

$$
{z}_{3} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,x}\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,A\left( y\right) 
$$

Notice that queries ${z}_{1}$ and ${z}_{2}$ satisfy the condition for hardness of binary paths (Theorem 28), since their set of variables is disjoint. Therefore,we can conclude that $\operatorname{RES}\left( {z}_{1}\right)$ and $\operatorname{RES}\left( {z}_{2}\right)$ are NP-complete, as well as any expansion of those queries. We show that any REP queries that contain ${z}_{3}$ are in $\mathrm{P}$ .

注意查询${z}_{1}$和${z}_{2}$满足二元路径困难性条件（定理28），因其变量集互不相交。因此可判定$\operatorname{RES}\left( {z}_{1}\right)$和$\operatorname{RES}\left( {z}_{2}\right)$属于NP完全问题，其任何扩展查询亦然。我们证明所有包含${z}_{3}$的REP查询均属于$\mathrm{P}$类。

Proposition 36 (Q:). Any pseudo-linear query $q$ with exactly two $R$ -atoms that contains ${z}_{3}$ is in $\mathrm{P}$ .

命题36（问题：）。任何精确包含两个$R$原子且含有${z}_{3}$的伪线性查询$q$均属于$\mathrm{P}$类。

### 7.5 二分性

综合现有研究成果，在自连接关系最多出现两次的情况下，我们已完整刻画了弹性问题的复杂度特征：

## 8 QUERIES WITH EXACTLY THREE $R$ -ATOMS

## 8 含恰好三个$R$原子的查询

In Theorem 37 we completely characterized the complexity of resilience of all CQs with at most one repetition of a single relation, thus extending the dichotomy for sj-free CQs into the land of self-joins.

定理37中我们完整刻画了所有最多含单个关系一次重复的CQ（Conjunctive Query）的弹性复杂度，从而将无自连接CQ的二分性扩展到自连接领域。

In this section, we present an overview of what can happen when we allow a third $R$ -atom to self-join. Since we only have to consider pseudo-linear queries that do not have a path,all three $R$ -atoms must connect to each other directly or through the third $R$ -atom. Even though this is still a restrictive setting, we will see that it brings non-trivial complications to the characterization. We will present some complexity results; but also some remaining open problems.

本节概述引入第三个$R$自连接原子时可能出现的情况。由于只需考虑无路径的伪线性查询，这三个$R$原子必须直接或通过第三个原子相互连接。尽管仍是受限场景，但我们将看到这会给复杂度刻画带来非平凡的复杂性。我们将呈现部分复杂度结果，同时指出若干未决问题。

### 8.1 3-Chains

### 8.1 三链结构

We obtain a 3-chain by adding an extra $R$ -atom to a 2-chain in a way such that the new atom joins in a different attribute from the other two.

通过在二链结构中添加一个$R$原子，并使新原子通过不同属性与其他两个原子连接，即构成三链。

$$
{q}_{3\text{ chain }} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {y,z}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right) 
$$

Analogous to the 2-chain case, 3-chains are always hard. In fact this holds for 4 -chains, 5 -chains, etc.

类比二链情形，三链始终具有高复杂度。该性质同样适用于四链、五链等结构。

Proposition 38 (Q). For all $k \geq  2$ ,if $q$ contains a $k$ -chain as its only self-join,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete.

命题38 (Q). 对所有$k \geq  2$，若$q$包含$k$链作为其唯一自连接，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完备性。

### 8.2 3-Confluences

### 8.2 三合流结构

Adding a third $R$ -atom to a 2-confluence and making sure that it joins in the same attribute with one of the two existing $R$ -atoms produces a 3-confluence.

在二合流基础上添加第三个$R$原子，并确保其与现有两个原子之一通过相同属性连接，即形成三合流。

$$
{q}_{3\text{ conf }} \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right) 
$$

As in the 2-confluence case, ${q}_{3\text{ conf }}$ is not minimal,so other atoms are required to make it minimal. Here are a few examples of minimal queries containing ${q}_{3\text{conf }}$ .

与二合流情况类似，${q}_{3\text{ conf }}$不具备最小性，因此需要其他原子使其最小化。以下是包含${q}_{3\text{conf }}$的最小查询示例。

$$
{q}_{3\text{ conf }}^{AC} \mathrel{\text{:-}} A\left( x\right) ,R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right) ,C\left( w\right)

$$
{q}_{3\text{ conf }}^{TS} :  - T{\left( x,y\right) }^{\mathrm{x}},R\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,y}\right) ,R\left( {z,w}\right) ,S{\left( z,w\right) }^{\mathrm{x}}

These queries are very similar but one of them is hard, while the other one is easy.

这些查询高度相似，但其中一个属于难解问题，另一个则属于易解问题。

命题40 (Q). ${}^{\because }$)。通过包含一元关系获得的${q}_{\text{3conf }}^{AC}$任何变体均为NP完全问题。

### 8.3 三链汇合

With ${3R}$ -atoms,it is possible that different patterns will occur at the same time. This feature of this case makes it harder to analyze the queries, since the result of these interactions might diverge from what we expect when we see each pattern in isolation.

当存在${3R}$原子时，不同模式可能同时出现。这种情况的特性使得查询分析更为困难，因为这些交互产生的结果可能与我们单独观察每个模式时的预期相背离。

### 8.4 三置换加$\mathrm{R}$

## 9 INDEPENDENT JOIN PATHS: A UNIFYING HARDNESS CRITERION

## 9 独立连接路径：统一的硬度判定准则

Motivation. We now define a particular "template" for hardness reductions which we call Independent Join Paths or IJPs. The idea is that if we can construct a particular database that fulfills 5 criteria for a query $q$ ,then we can conclude safely that $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is NP-complete.

研究动机。我们定义了一种称为独立连接路径(IJPs)的特殊硬度归约模板。其核心思想是：若能构建满足查询$q$五项条件的特定数据库，则可安全判定$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$属于NP完全问题。

This recent development is exciting for several reasons: 1) In our earlier attempts to prove hardness for queries, we amassed a plethora of different hardness proofs, with little immediate intuition of how one hardness proof immediately helps facilitate the hardness proof of another query. Now we expect that the task can be simplified to the task of searching for any particular database that serves as "proof" of hardness based on a generalized reduction from Vertex Cover. 2) We were able to look at our existing hardness proofs and post-hoc identify some part in some gadget that formed an IJP. In other words, IJPs were already present in our hardness proofs (we give examples in Appendix A). Thus IJPs are really a unifying common denominator for all hard queries known so far. 3) The search for hardness proofs could now, in theory, be automated. While we have not yet explored this idea, we give the intuition in Appendix A. 4) The hardness based on IJPs is not restricted to the particular fragment of CQs that we have analyzed in this paper; rather they are a universal criterion. Even the original criterion of triads for sj-free CQs can be subsumed under IJPs. 5) We conjecture that the inability to form IJPs for those queries that are in PTIME can be deduced from the structure of a query, and future work will discover the reason.

这一最新进展令人振奋的原因在于：1）早期证明查询硬度的尝试中，我们积累了大量不同证明，但缺乏直观体现不同证明间关联性的方法。现在可将该任务简化为寻找基于顶点覆盖广义归约的硬度"证明"数据库；2）在现有硬度证明中，我们可后验识别构成IJP的特定组件（附录A含示例），表明IJPs实则是迄今所有已知困难查询的统一共性特征；3）理论上硬度证明的搜索可实现自动化（附录A阐述该构想）；4）基于IJPs的硬度判定不仅限于本文分析的CQ特定片段，而是普适性准则——甚至适用于无自连接CQ的三元组原始判定标准；5）我们推测PTIME类查询无法形成IJPs的特性可从查询结构推导，这将是未来研究方向。

The intuition of IJPs. We have already seen that paths between two subgoals ${g}_{1}$ and ${g}_{n}$ that refer to the same relation are a sufficient condition for hardness under "certain circumstances". Recall our simplest example for a path implying hardness: ${q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}} :  - R\left( x\right) ,S\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( y\right)$ . The intuition of our construction is now as follows: Take any minimal VC problem for a graph $G\left( {V,E}\right)$ (see Fig. 8a). Replace any existing arc with 3 arcs instead to create ${G}^{\prime }$ (see Fig. 8b). Then $G$ has a VC of size $k$ iff ${G}^{\prime }$ has a VC of size $k + \left| E\right|$ . Similarly,replace each arc with 5 instead of 3 arcs,then the condition for ${G}^{\prime }$ is $k + 2\left| E\right|$ . The key property we needed for this to work is the fact that 3 arcs form a particular path with the following "OR-property" (see Fig. 8c): As long as at least one end point of the path is removed, then the minimal VC is exactly one additional node per path.

独立连接路径(IJPs)的直观理解。我们已经知道，在"特定条件下"，指向同一关系的两个子目标${g}_{1}$与${g}_{n}$之间的路径是导致问题复杂化的充分条件。回顾我们关于路径隐含复杂性的最简单示例：${q}_{\mathrm{{vc}}} :  - R\left( x\right) ,S\left( {x,y}\right) ,R\left( y\right)$。本构建的直观原理如下：取图$G\left( {V,E}\right)$的最小顶点覆盖(VC)问题（见图8a），将每条现有弧替换为3条弧以创建${G}^{\prime }$（见图8b）。当且仅当${G}^{\prime }$存在大小为$k + \left| E\right|$的VC时，$G$才存在大小为$k$的VC。类似地，若将每条弧替换为5条而非3条弧，则${G}^{\prime }$的条件变为$k + 2\left| E\right|$。此方法有效的关键特性在于：3条弧构成的特定路径具有以下"或属性"（见图8c）：只要移除路径的至少一个端点，每条路径的最小VC恰好只需额外增加一个节点。

Formalization of IJPs. We next use this idea to define a particular canonical database instance which we call "Independent Join Path." We conjecture that whenever a query has such a canonical database, then resilience is hard by a proof that generalizes the idea from above. We give the formal definition here and provide intuition for each of the conditions in Appendix A. In the following, we write ${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathbf{j}}$ to denote the subvector of $\mathbf{x}$ that retains only the entries indexed by $\mathbf{j}$ . For example if $\mathbf{x} = \left( {1,2,3,4,5}\right)$ and $\mathbf{j} = \left( {2,4,5}\right)$ then ${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathbf{j}} = \left( {2,4,5}\right)$

独立连接路径的形式化定义。接下来我们将运用该思想定义特定的规范数据库实例，称为"独立连接路径"。我们推测，当查询存在此类规范数据库时，通过推广上述思想的证明可判定其弹性问题具有复杂性。此处给出形式化定义，各条件的直观解释见附录A。下文用${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathbf{j}}$表示向量$\mathbf{x}$中仅保留$\mathbf{j}$索引项的子向量。例如若$\mathbf{x} = \left( {1,2,3,4,5}\right)$且$\mathbf{j} = \left( {2,4,5}\right)$，则${\mathbf{x}}_{\mathbf{j}} = \left( {2,4,5}\right)$

Definition 48 (Independent Join Path). A database D forms an Independent Join Path for query $q$ if the following conditions hold:

定义48（独立连接路径）。若满足以下条件，则称数据库D构成查询$q$的独立连接路径：

(1) There is a relation $R$ containing at least two tuples $R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ and $R\left( \mathbf{b}\right)$ with $\mathbf{a} \nsubseteq  \mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{b} \nsubseteq  \mathbf{a}$ .

(1) 存在关系$R$至少包含两个元组$R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$和$R\left( \mathbf{b}\right)$，且满足$\mathbf{a} \nsubseteq  \mathbf{b}$与$\mathbf{b} \nsubseteq  \mathbf{a}$

(2) In $D,R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ and $R\left( \mathbf{b}\right)$ each participate in exactly one witness ${\mathbf{w}}_{a},{\mathbf{w}}_{b}$ of $D \vDash  q$ . Both ${\mathbf{w}}_{a}$ and ${\mathbf{w}}_{b}$ have exactly $m$ tuples, where $m$ is the number of atoms in $q$ .

(2) 在$D,R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$和$R\left( \mathbf{b}\right)$中，每个元组仅参与$D \vDash  q$的一个见证${\mathbf{w}}_{a},{\mathbf{w}}_{b}$。${\mathbf{w}}_{a}$与${\mathbf{w}}_{b}$均精确包含$m$个元组，其中$m$为$q$中的原子数

(3) There is no endogenous relation $S$ containing a tuple $S\left( \mathbf{c}\right)$ with $\mathbf{c} \subset  \mathbf{a}$ or $\mathbf{c} \subset  \mathbf{b}$ .

(3) 不存在包含元组$S\left( \mathbf{c}\right)$的内生关系$S$，且该元组满足$\mathbf{c} \subset  \mathbf{a}$或$\mathbf{c} \subset  \mathbf{b}$

(4) If there is an exogenous relation ${T}^{\mathrm{x}}$ containing a tuple ${T}^{\mathrm{x}}\left( \mathbf{d}\right)$ with $\mathbf{d} = {\mathbf{a}}_{\mathbf{j}}$ for some $\mathbf{j}$ ,then ${T}^{\mathrm{X}}$ also contains ${T}^{\mathrm{X}}\left( \mathbf{e}\right)$ with $\mathbf{e} = {\mathbf{b}}_{\mathbf{j}}$ .

(4) 若存在外生关系${T}^{\mathrm{x}}$包含元组${T}^{\mathrm{x}}\left( \mathbf{d}\right)$，且对某些$\mathbf{j}$满足$\mathbf{d} = {\mathbf{a}}_{\mathbf{j}}$，则${T}^{\mathrm{X}}$必须同时包含满足$\mathbf{e} = {\mathbf{b}}_{\mathbf{j}}$的${T}^{\mathrm{X}}\left( \mathbf{e}\right)$

(5) Let $c$ be the resilience of $q$ on $D : \rho \left( {q,D}\right)  = c$ . Then the resilience is $c - 1$ in all 3 cases of removing either $R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$ ,or $R\left( \mathbf{b}\right)$ ,or both.

(5) 设$c$为$q$在$D : \rho \left( {q,D}\right)  = c$上的韧性值，则无论移除$R\left( \mathbf{a}\right)$、$R\left( \mathbf{b}\right)$或两者时，该韧性值在三种情况下均为$c - 1$。

CONJECTURE 49 (IJPs IMPLY HARDNESS). If there is a database $D$ that forms an IJP for a query $q$ ,then $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ is $\mathrm{{NP}}$ -complete.

猜想49（IJP蕴含硬度）。若存在数据库$D$能为查询$q$形成独立连接路径(IJP)，则$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$具有$\mathrm{{NP}}$完全性。

The conjecture. For the fragment of CQs we are considering in this paper, we have been able to simplify some hardness proofs, which at times use very different constructions (reductions from VC, 3-SAT, Max 2-SAT), by looking at our existing hardness proofs and identifying IJPs in our existing gadgets.

核心猜想。针对本文研究的合取查询片段，我们通过分析现有硬度证明并在现有构件中识别独立连接路径，成功简化了部分依赖不同构造（如顶点覆盖归约、3-SAT归约、最大2-SAT归约）的硬度证明过程。

We conjecture that the existence of IJPs for a query is also a necessary condition for hardness, that there is an algorithm to verify whether a query can form IJPs or not, and that the fact that a query cannot form IJPs (such as linear SJ-free CQs) translates immediately into a PTIME algorithm for solving $\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$ .

我们推测查询存在独立连接路径也是硬度判定的必要条件，且存在算法可验证查询能否形成独立连接路径。若某类查询（如无自连接的线性合取查询）无法形成独立连接路径，则意味着可直接采用PTIME算法求解$\operatorname{RES}\left( q\right)$。

## 10 RELATED WORK

## 10 相关工作

In prior work [14], we identified the concept of a triad, a novel structure that allowed us to fully characterize the complexity of resilience (and consequentially for deletion propagation) for the class of self-join-free conjunctive queries with potential functional dependencies. Our work in this paper considers self-joins, which have long-plagued the study of many problems in database theory; results for such queries have been few and far between.

前期研究[14]中我们提出了三元组(triad)概念，这种新型结构能完整刻画无自连接合取查询在存在函数依赖时的韧性复杂度（进而解决删除传播问题）。本文研究则聚焦长期困扰数据库理论研究的自连接问题，该类查询的研究成果迄今仍寥寥无几。

Deletion propagation and view updates. The problem of resilience is a special case of deletion propagation, focusing on Boolean queries. Deletion propagation generally refers to non-Boolean queries. Given a non-Boolean query $q$ and database $D$ , the typical goal is to determine the minimum number of tuples that must be removed from $D$ ,so that a tuple $\mathbf{t}$ is no longer in the query result $\left\lbrack  {5,{12}}\right\rbrack$ (source side-effects). Variants of deletion propagation consider side-effects in the query result rather than the source $\left\lbrack  {{23},{24}}\right\rbrack$ ,and multi-tuple deletions $\left\lbrack  {9,{25}}\right\rbrack$ . Resilience and deletion propagation are special cases of the view update problem $\left\lbrack  {3,9,{10},{12},{13},{16},{22}}\right\rbrack$ ,which consists of finding the set of operations that should be applied to the database in order to obtain a certain modification in the view.

删除传播与视图更新。韧性问题是布尔查询场景下的特殊删除传播问题。广义删除传播针对非布尔查询，给定查询$q$和数据库$D$，其核心目标是确定从$D$中移除元组的最小数量，使得元组$\mathbf{t}$不再出现在查询结果$\left\lbrack  {5,{12}}\right\rbrack$中（源端效应变体）。其他变体包括考虑查询结果端效应而非源端$\left\lbrack  {{23},{24}}\right\rbrack$，以及多元组删除场景$\left\lbrack  {9,{25}}\right\rbrack$。韧性与删除传播本质都是视图更新问题$\left\lbrack  {3,9,{10},{12},{13},{16},{22}}\right\rbrack$的特例，后者旨在寻找使视图产生特定变更的数据库操作集合。

Causality and explanations. Database causality is geared towards providing explanations for query results, but typically relies on the concept of responsibility $\left\lbrack  {{32},{33}}\right\rbrack$ ,which is harder than resilience. The idea of interventions appears in other explanation settings, but often apply to queries instead of the data [34, 35, 37]. Finally, the problem of explaining missing query results [7, 19- 21, 36] is a problem analogous to deletion propagation, but in this case, we want to add, rather than remove tuples from the view.

因果性与解释。数据库因果关系旨在解释查询结果，但通常依赖比韧性更复杂的责任度概念$\left\lbrack  {{32},{33}}\right\rbrack$。干预思想见于其他解释框架，但多作用于查询而非数据[34,35,37]。解释缺失查询结果的问题[7,19-21,36]与删除传播类似，区别在于需要向视图添加而非移除元组。

Provenance and view updates. Data provenance studies formalisms that can characterize the relation between the input and the output of a given query $\left\lbrack  {4,8,{11},{17}}\right\rbrack$ . "Why-provenance" is the provenance type most closely related to resilience. The motivation behind Why-provenance is to find the "witnesses" for the query answer, i.e., the tuples or group of tuples in the input that can produce the answer. Resilience, searches to find a minimum set of input tuples that can make a query false.

溯源与视图更新。数据溯源研究旨在形式化表征查询$\left\lbrack  {4,8,{11},{17}}\right\rbrack$输入与输出的关系。"Why溯源"是与韧性最相关的溯源类型，其核心是寻找查询答案的"见证"——即输入中能产生该答案的元组或元组组。而韧性研究则寻求使查询失效的最小输入元组集合。

## 11 FINAL REMARKS

## 11 结论

In this paper, we studied the problem of resilience for conjunctive queries with self-joins. We identified fundamental query structures that impact hardness, and proved a complete dichotomy for the restricted class of single-self-join binary CQs where exactly two atoms can correspond to the same relation.

本文研究了含自连接的合取查询的韧性问题，揭示了影响硬度的基础查询结构，并针对仅含两个原子对应同一关系的单自连接二元合取查询类，证明了完整的二分性定理。

We also present results towards the for the case of binary CQs with a single self-join relation that appears in 3 atoms, and identifies some open problems and challenges towards completing the dichotomy for this class (Section 8).

我们还针对二元CQs（含单个自连接关系且出现在3个原子中的情况）呈现了相关结果，并指出了完成该类二分法研究尚待解决的开放性问题与挑战（第8节）。

Our work also presents a roadmap for tackling the analysis of more extended query families. Section 9 provides towards a possible generalization of our results to all class of self-join queries, by using a unifying criterion that we call Independent Join Paths.

本研究还提出了分析更广泛查询家族的路线图。第9节通过我们称为"独立连接路径"的统一标准，探讨了将研究成果推广至所有自连接查询类别的可能性。

Overall, our work in this paper contributes important progress in the theoretical analysis of self-joins, which has long been stalled for many related problems. We hope that our results, even though they apply to a restricted class, will provide the foundations to help solve the general case for CQs with self-joins in the future.

总体而言，本文在自连接理论分析领域取得了重要进展——该领域许多相关问题长期停滞不前。尽管当前成果仅适用于特定类别，我们期望这些发现能为未来解决自连接CQs的一般情况奠定基础。
