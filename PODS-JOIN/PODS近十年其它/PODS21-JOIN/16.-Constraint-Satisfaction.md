## Abstract

## 摘要

Conditional lower bounds based on $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ ,the Exponential-Time Hypothesis (ETH), or similar complexity assumptions can provide very useful information about what type of algorithms are likely to be possible. Ideally, such lower bounds would be able to demonstrate that the best known algorithms are essentially optimal and cannot be improved further. In this tutorial, we overview different types of lower bounds, and see how they can be applied to problems in database theory and constraint satisfaction.

基于$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$指数时间假设（ETH）或其他复杂性假设的条件性下界，能为判断哪些算法可能实现提供重要依据。理想情况下，这类下界可以证明现有最优算法本质上已无法改进。本教程将综述不同类型的下界技术，并展示它们在数据库理论和约束满足问题中的应用。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

The design of efficient algorithms is in the focus of a large part of theoretical computer science research. The practical need to solve computational problems efficiently makes the systematic study of algorithmic efficiency highly motivated. Decades of research in algorithm design discovered mathematically beautiful and sometimes very practical algorithmic techniques that gave us deep insights into efficient computation in a wide range of contexts and application domains. The field of computational complexity treats algorithmic problems and computation as formal mathematical objects and tries to prove relationships between them $\left\lbrack  {8,{55}}\right\rbrack$ .

高效算法设计是理论计算机科学研究的核心课题。实际应用中解决计算问题的需求，使得对算法效率的系统研究具有强烈动机。数十年的算法设计研究发现了数学上优美且有时极具实用性的算法技术，使我们对广泛领域中的高效计算有了深刻理解。计算复杂性领域将算法问题和计算视为形式化的数学对象，试图证明它们之间的关系$\left\lbrack  {8,{55}}\right\rbrack$。

Given the abundance of computation in our modern word, it is justified to consider algorithms and computation as a fundamental mathematical objects, on par with basic objects in geometry, algebra, and combinatorics. Researchers in computational complexity try to learn as much as possible about the mathematical nature of computation. But more pragmatically, computational complexity can give very important messages to algorithm designers. By giving information about limits of computation, it can prevent researchers from wasting time in dead ends of study: trying to design algorithm for problems that cannot be efficiently solved.

鉴于计算在现代社会中的普遍性，将算法和计算视为与几何、代数和组合数学基础对象同等重要的数学对象是合理的。计算复杂性研究者致力于揭示计算的数学本质。更实际地，计算复杂性能为算法设计者提供关键指导：通过阐明计算极限，可避免研究者将时间浪费在无解问题上——即试图为无法高效求解的问题设计算法。

Having techniques to prove negative results can profoundly change the way research in algorithms is done. For example, the theory of NP-hardness changed the search for polynomial-time algorithms from a hit and miss effort to a more systematically doable project. Without NP-hardness, we would not be able to distinguish problems that do not admit polynomial-time algorithms from problems where we just were not yet successful in finding algorithms. But with the possibility of giving negative evidence in the form of NP-hardness, the lack of a known answer means that the question is still an active research problem: we typically expect that the algorithmic problem at hand can be eventually classified as either polynomial-time solvable or NP-hard, and it is worth trying to resolve the question one way or the other.

掌握证明负面结果的技术能深刻改变算法研究的方式。例如，NP难理论将多项式时间算法的探索从碰运气转变为系统性工程。若无NP难理论，我们将无法区分本就不存在多项式算法的问题与尚未找到算法的问题。但借助NP难理论的否定性证明，未知答案的问题就仍是活跃研究课题——我们通常预期算法问题终将被归类为多项式时间可解或NP难问题，因而值得持续探索其最终归属。

In a sense, computation complexity has not progressed much in the past 50 years despite intense efforts: the core questions underlying the hardness of computation,such as the celebrated $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ problem, are still wide open. However, by accepting certain wellchosen complexity assumptions,such as the $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ hypothesis, we can obtain conditional lower bounds explaining the apparent complexity of a large number of problems. As a general theme in computational complexity research, we can see a proliferation of new assumptions. These assumptions typical postulate that a certain type of algorithm does not exist for a particular fundamental problem (e.g., for Boolean satisfiability). The assumptions are chosen to be both plausible and have strong explanatory power: in many cases, they are able to show that the algorithms that we currently have are optimal and cannot be improved any further. Indeed, from the viewpoint of algorithm design, this is precisely the role of computation complexity: to separate problems where our current knowledge is complete from problems where there are still algorithmic ideas waiting to be discovered.

从某种意义上说，尽管付出了巨大努力，计算复杂性在过去50年间并未取得太大进展：关于计算难度的核心问题，诸如著名的$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$问题，至今仍悬而未决。然而通过接受某些精心选择的复杂性假设（如$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$假说），我们能获得解释众多问题明显复杂性的条件性下界。作为计算复杂性研究的普遍现象，我们可以看到新假设的激增。这些假设通常断言某类算法无法解决特定基础问题（例如布尔可满足性问题），其选择标准既要可信又需具备强大解释力——在许多情况下，它们能证明现有算法已达最优且无法进一步改进。事实上，从算法设计的视角来看，这正是计算复杂性的核心作用：区分当前认知已完备的问题与仍待发现新算法思想的问题。

Some of the complexity assumptions are standard (such as $\mathrm{P} \neq$ NP), while others may be more controversial (such as the Strong Exponential-Time Hypothesis (SETH)). Therefore, the reader may wonder about the usefulness of proving conditional lower bounds based on unproven assumptions. It is important to point out that these conditional lower bounds are valuable even if we have doubts about the validity of the assumptions. Suppose we use a complexity assumption $X$ to prove that a certain type of algorithm does not exists for a specialized problem $P$ ,perhaps in an application domain such as database theory. Even if we do not believe in the validity of the assumption $X$ ,this conditional lower bound shows that the difficulties we face when attacking problem $P$ have nothing to do with the specific details of problem $X$ or the application area: we are really facing assumption $X$ in disguise and we need to disprove that first before any progress can be made on problem $P$ . In other words, the conditional lower bound shows that we can stop trying to obtain the desired algorithm for problem $P$ ,as any such effort would be better spent on trying to disprove the (typically more fundamental) assumption $X$ .

部分复杂性假设是标准化的（如$\mathrm{P} \neq$NP），而另一些可能更具争议性（如强指数时间假说/SETH）。因此读者可能会质疑基于未证实假设证明条件性下界的实用性。需要强调的是，即便我们对假设有效性存疑，这些条件性下界仍具重要价值。假设我们使用复杂性假设$X$来证明某类算法无法解决特定问题$P$（可能出现在数据库理论等应用领域），即使不认同假设$X$的正确性，该条件性下界也表明：我们在攻克问题$P$时遇到的困难与问题$X$的具体细节或应用领域无关——我们实际遭遇的是假设$X$的变体，必须首先证伪该假设才能在问题$P$上取得进展。换言之，条件性下界提示我们应停止寻求问题$P$的理想算法，因为任何此类努力都应转向尝试证伪（通常更基础的）假设$X$。

The purpose of this article is to highlight some of the lower bound techniques and show what kind of results they can deliver in the context of database theory. We will introduce a number of assumptions, contrast them, and show examples of their use. It has to be emphasized that this article does not aim to be an up to date survey of lower bounds in the area of database theory. The focus is more on the diverse set of assumptions and lower bound techniques that exist, rather than on presenting an exhaustive list of applications for each technique. Some of these example applications come directly from the literature on database query evaluation, but others were stated in essentially equivalent forms in other domains: for constraint satisfaction problems (CSP) or for graph-theoretic problems. Therefore, we begin with introducing the terminology for all these domains and then discuss the results using the most appropriate terminology.

本文旨在重点介绍若干下界技术，并展示它们在数据库理论背景下能取得的研究成果。我们将引入多种假设进行对比，并举例说明其应用。必须强调的是，本文并非对数据库理论领域下界研究的最新综述，其重点在于展现假设体系与下界技术的多样性，而非穷举每种技术的所有应用场景。部分示例直接来自数据库查询评估文献，另一些则以本质等效的形式出现在其他领域：如约束满足问题(CSP)或图论问题。因此，我们首先介绍所有相关领域的术语体系，再选用最合适的术语展开讨论。

## 2 THE FOUR DOMAINS

## 2 四大领域

Conjunctive query evaluation is a fundamental problem in database theory. This problem can be equivalently seen as a CSP instance and therefore some of the results in the CSP literature are directly relevant. A large part of the CSP literature uses a formulation using the homomorphism of relational structures, which in some special cases degenerate to traditional graph problems. In this section, we introduce the terminology for all these domains and show how they are connected to each other.

合取查询评估是数据库理论的基础问题。该问题可等价视为CSP实例，因此CSP文献中的部分成果具有直接相关性。大量CSP研究采用关系结构同态的表述方式，某些特例会退化为传统图问题。本节将介绍所有这些领域的术语体系，并阐明它们之间的关联。

### 2.1 Database queries

### 2.1 数据库查询

A join query $Q$ is an expression of the form

连接查询$Q$是形如以下的表达式

$$
{R}_{1}\left( {{a}_{11},\ldots ,{a}_{1{r}_{1}}}\right)  \bowtie  \cdots  \bowtie  {R}_{m}\left( {{a}_{m1},\ldots ,{a}_{m{r}_{m}}}\right) ,
$$

where the ${R}_{i}$ are relation names with attributes ${a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}$ . Let $A$ be the set of all attributes occurring in $Q$ and $n = \left| A\right|$ . A database instance $\mathbf{D}$ for $Q$ consists of a domain $\operatorname{dom}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ and relations ${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{dom}{\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }^{{r}_{i}}$ of arity ${r}_{i}$ . It is common to think of the relation ${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ as a table whose columns are labeled by the attributes ${a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}$ and whose rows are the tuples in the relation. The answer,or set of solutions,of the query $Q$ in $\mathbf{D}$ is the $n$ -ary relation $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ with attributes $A$ consisting of all tuples $t$ whose projection on the attributes of ${R}_{i}$ belongs to the relation ${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ ,for all $i$ . Given the query $Q$ and the database $D$ ,the task in the JOIN QUERY problem is to compute the set $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ . In the Boolean Join Query problem, we only need to decide if $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ is empty or not. One can also define the counting version of the problem (i.e,compute $\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$ ).

其中${R}_{i}$是带有属性${a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}$的关系名称。设$A$为$Q$和$n = \left| A\right|$中出现的所有属性集合。数据库实例$\mathbf{D}$由域$\operatorname{dom}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$和元数为${r}_{i}$的关系${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{dom}{\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }^{{r}_{i}}$组成。通常将关系${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$视为一张表，其列由属性${a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}$标记，行由关系中的元组构成。查询$Q$在$\mathbf{D}$中的答案（即解集）是具有属性$A$的$n$元关系$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$，包含所有满足条件的元组$t$——这些元组在${R}_{i}$属性上的投影均属于关系${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$。给定查询$Q$和数据库$D$，连接查询(JOIN QUERY)问题的任务是计算集合$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$。在布尔连接查询问题中，只需判定$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$是否为空。亦可定义该问题的计数版本（即计算$\left| {Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right) }\right|$）。

The primal graph of the query has the set $A$ of attributes as vertex set and two variables are adjacent if there is a relation containing both of them. The hypergraph of the instance is defined similarly: the vertex set is $A$ ,and each relation ${R}_{i}\left( {{a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}}\right)$ is represented by a hyperedge $\left\{  {{a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}}\right\}$ .

查询的原图以属性集$A$为顶点集，若两个变量存在于同一关系中则相邻。实例的超图类似定义：顶点集为$A$，每个关系${R}_{i}\left( {{a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}}\right)$表示为超边$\left\{  {{a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}}\right\}$。

### 2.2 Constraint satisfaction problems

### 2.2 约束满足问题

Constraint satisfaction is a general framework that includes many standard algorithmic problems such as satisfiability, graph coloring, database queries, etc. A constraint satisfaction problem (CSP) instance consists of a set $V$ of variables,a domain $D$ ,and a set $C$ of constraints, where each constraint is a relation on a subset of the variables. The task is to assign a value from $D$ to each variable in such a way that every constraint is satisfied. For example, 3SAT can be interpreted as a CSP instance where the domain is $\{ 0,1\}$ and the constraints in $C$ correspond to the clauses (thus the arity of each constraint is 3 ).

约束满足是涵盖可满足性、图着色、数据库查询等标准算法问题的通用框架。约束满足问题(CSP)实例由变量集$V$、值域$D$和约束集$C$构成，其中每个约束是变量子集上的关系。任务是为每个变量分配$D$中的值，使所有约束被满足。例如3SAT可解释为CSP实例：值域为$\{ 0,1\}$，约束集$C$对应子句（故每个约束的元数为3）。

Formally,an instance $I$ of a constraint satisfaction problem is a triple $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ ,where:

形式化定义下，约束满足问题的实例$I$是三元组$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$，其中：

- $V$ is a set of variables,

- $V$是变量集合

- $D$ is a domain of values,

- $D$是值域

- $C$ is a set of constraints, $\left\{  {{c}_{1},{c}_{2},\ldots ,{c}_{q}}\right\}$ . Each constraint ${c}_{i} \in$ $C$ is a pair $\left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$ ,where:

- $C$是约束集$\left\{  {{c}_{1},{c}_{2},\ldots ,{c}_{q}}\right\}$。每个约束${c}_{i} \in$$C$是二元组$\left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$，其中：

- ${s}_{i}$ is a tuple of variables of length ${m}_{i}$ ,called the constraint scope, and

- ${s}_{i}$是长度为${m}_{i}$的变量元组（称为约束作用域）

- ${R}_{i}$ is an ${m}_{i}$ -ary relation over $D$ ,called the constraint relation.

- ${R}_{i}$是$D$上的${m}_{i}$元关系（称为约束关系）

For each constraint $\left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$ the tuples of ${R}_{i}$ indicate the allowed combinations of simultaneous values for the variables in ${s}_{i}$ . The length ${m}_{i}$ of the tuple ${s}_{i}$ is called the arity of the constraint. A solution to a constraint satisfaction problem instance is a function $f$ from the set of variables $V$ to the domain $D$ of values such that for each constraint $\left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$ with ${s}_{i} = \left( {{v}_{{i}_{1}},{v}_{{i}_{2}},\ldots ,{v}_{{i}_{m}}}\right)$ ,the tuple $\left( {f\left( {v}_{{i}_{1}}\right) ,f\left( {v}_{{i}_{2}}\right) ,\ldots ,f\left( {v}_{{i}_{m}}\right) }\right)$ is a member of ${R}_{i}$ . Given a CSP instance $I$ ,we can consider the problem of deciding if a solution exists,the problem of finding all solutions, or the problem of counting the number of solutions.

对于每个约束$\left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$，元组${R}_{i}$表示变量集${s}_{i}$中变量同时取值的允许组合。元组${s}_{i}$的长度${m}_{i}$称为约束的元数(arity)。约束满足问题实例的解是一个从变量集$V$到值域$D$的函数$f$，使得对于每个带${s}_{i} = \left( {{v}_{{i}_{1}},{v}_{{i}_{2}},\ldots ,{v}_{{i}_{m}}}\right)$的约束$\left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$，元组$\left( {f\left( {v}_{{i}_{1}}\right) ,f\left( {v}_{{i}_{2}}\right) ,\ldots ,f\left( {v}_{{i}_{m}}\right) }\right)$都是${R}_{i}$的成员。给定CSP实例$I$，我们可以考虑判定解是否存在、寻找所有解或统计解数量这三类问题。

We say that an instance is binary if each constraint relation is binary,that is, ${m}_{i} = 2$ for every constraint ${}^{1}$ . The primal graph (or Gaifman graph) of a CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ is a graph $G$ with vertex set $V$ ,where $x,y \in  V$ form an edge if and only if there is a constraint $\left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle   \in  C$ with $x,y \in  {s}_{i}$ . The hypergraph of an instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ has $V$ as its vertex set and for every constraint in $C$ a hyperedge that consists of all variables occurring in the constraint.

若每个约束关系都是二元的（即对每个约束${}^{1}$都有${m}_{i} = 2$），则称该实例为二元实例。CSP实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$的原图（或称盖斯曼图）是顶点集为$V$的图$G$，当且仅当存在带$x,y \in  {s}_{i}$的约束$\left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle   \in  C$时，$x,y \in  V$才构成边。实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$的超图以$V$为顶点集，并为$C$中的每个约束建立包含该约束所有变量的超边。

Given a join query $Q$ and a database $\mathbf{D}$ ,we can turn the query problem into a CSP instance $I$ in a straightforward way: the domain of $I$ is $\operatorname{dom}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ ,the set of variables correspond to the attributes $A$ of $Q$ ,and for each relation ${R}_{i}$ ,there is a corresponding constraint ${c}_{i}$ on the variables ${a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}$ . It is clear that the tuples in the answer set of $Q$ in $\mathbf{D}$ are in one to one correspondence with the solutions of the CSP instance $I$ . This establishes a correspondence between the basic algorithmic problems of the two domains.

给定连接查询$Q$和数据库$\mathbf{D}$，可通过以下方式将查询问题转化为CSP实例$I$：实例$I$的域是$\operatorname{dom}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$，变量集对应$Q$的属性$A$，且对每个关系${R}_{i}$，在变量${a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}$上存在对应约束${c}_{i}$。显然，$\mathbf{D}$中查询$Q$的答案集元组与CSP实例$I$的解存在一一对应关系，这确立了两个领域基本算法问题间的对应关系。

It is worth pointing out that even though the two problems are equivalent, a large part of CSP research focuses on problem instances where the domain has small constant size and the number of constraints is large (for example, 3SAT is such a problem). This has to be contrasted with the typical setting in database theory research where we assume that there are only a small number of attributes and relations have low arity, but the domain can be large and the number of tuples in a relation can be large.

值得指出的是，尽管这两个问题等价，但CSP研究主要关注域大小为常数且约束数量庞大的问题实例（例如3SAT问题），这与数据库理论研究通常假设属性数量少、关系元数低但域规模大、关系元组量大的典型设定形成对比。

### 2.3 Graph problems

### 2.3 图问题

Given a binary CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ ,we can equivalently formulate it as a graph problem. We construct a graph $G$ the following way: let us introduce $\left| V\right|  \cdot  \left| D\right|$ vertices ${w}_{v,d}\left( {v \in  V,d \in  D}\right)$ and for ever constraint ${c}_{i} = \left\langle  {\left( {u,v}\right) ,{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$ ,let us make ${w}_{u,{d}_{1}}$ and ${w}_{v,{d}_{2}}$ adjacent if and only if $\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2}}\right)  \in  {R}_{i}$ . Let ${W}_{i} = \left\{  {{w}_{i,d} \mid  d \in  D}\right\}$ and consider the partition $\mathcal{P} = \left\{  {{W}_{1},\ldots ,{W}_{\left| V\right| }}\right\}$ . We say that a subgraph $H$ of $G$ respects the partition $\mathcal{P}$ if every class of the partition $\mathcal{P}$ contains exactly one vertex of $H$ .

给定一个二元约束满足问题(CSP)实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$，我们可以等价地将其表述为图论问题。按如下方式构建图$G$：引入$\left| V\right|  \cdot  \left| D\right|$个顶点${w}_{v,d}\left( {v \in  V,d \in  D}\right)$，对于每个约束${c}_{i} = \left\langle  {\left( {u,v}\right) ,{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$，当且仅当$\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2}}\right)  \in  {R}_{i}$时使${w}_{u,{d}_{1}}$与${w}_{v,{d}_{2}}$相邻。设${W}_{i} = \left\{  {{w}_{i,d} \mid  d \in  D}\right\}$并考虑划分$\mathcal{P} = \left\{  {{W}_{1},\ldots ,{W}_{\left| V\right| }}\right\}$。若子图$H$满足每个划分类$\mathcal{P}$恰好包含$H$的一个顶点，则称该子图尊重划分$\mathcal{P}$。

Let $f : V \rightarrow  D$ be a solution of $I$ . If we consider the vertices $\left\{  {{w}_{v,f\left( v\right) } \mid  v \in  V}\right\}$ ,then it is easy to see that they induce a subgraph $H$ that respects $\mathcal{P}$ and isomorphic to the primal graph of $I$ . Conversely,it is not difficult to see that if $G$ has a subgraph that respects $\mathcal{P}$ and is isomorphic to the primal graph of $I$ ,the it describes a solution of $I$ . Therefore,the CSP instance can be described by an instance of partitioned subgraph isomorphism: given graphs $H$ and $G$ ,and partition $\mathcal{P}$ of $V\left( G\right)$ into $\left| {V\left( H\right) }\right|$ classes,find a subgraph of $V$ that respects $\mathcal{P}$ and is isomorphic to $H$ . This problem is a natural variant of the standard subgraph isomorphism problem (find a subgraph of $G$ isomorphic to $H$ ) and,as we have seen,its complexity is tightly connected to the complexity of CSP instances where the primal graph is $H$ .

设$f : V \rightarrow  D$为$I$的解。考察顶点集$\left\{  {{w}_{v,f\left( v\right) } \mid  v \in  V}\right\}$时，显然它们导出的子图$H$既尊重划分$\mathcal{P}$，又与$I$的原始图同构。反之，若$G$存在既尊重划分$\mathcal{P}$又与$I$原始图同构的子图，则该子图即对应$I$的一个解。因此，该CSP实例可转化为划分子图同构问题实例：给定图$H$和$G$，以及将$V\left( G\right)$划分为$\left| {V\left( H\right) }\right|$个类的分区$\mathcal{P}$，需找出$V$中既尊重$\mathcal{P}$又与$H$同构的子图。此问题是标准子图同构问题（在$G$中寻找与$H$同构的子图）的自然变体，且其计算复杂度与原始图为$H$的CSP实例复杂度密切相关。

There is another way in which graph-theoretic notions can describe the solutions of a CSP instance. Consider a binary CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ where every constraint ${c}_{i} = \left\langle  {\left( {u,v}\right) ,{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$ contains the same binary relation ${R}_{i} = R$ ,which we further assume to be symmetric (that is, $\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2}}\right)  \in  R$ if and only $\left( {{d}_{2},{d}_{1}}\right)  \in  R$ ). Let $H$ be the primal graph of $I$ and let $G$ be a graph with vertex set $D$ where ${d}_{1},{d}_{2} \in  D$ are adjacent if and only if $\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2}}\right)  \in  R$ . A homomorphism from $H$ to $G$ is a mapping $f : V\left( H\right)  \rightarrow  V\left( G\right)$ such that if $u$ and $v$ are adjacent in $H$ ,then $f\left( u\right)$ and $f\left( v\right)$ are adjacent in $G$ . Note that $f$ does not have to be injective (i.e., $f\left( {u}_{1}\right)  = f\left( {u}_{2}\right)$ is possible) and if $u$ and $v$ are not adjacent,then we do not require that $f\left( u\right)$ and $f\left( v\right)$ be nonadjacent as well. It is easy to see that every solution $f : V \rightarrow  D$ of $I$ describes a homomophism from $H$ to $G$ , in fact, these homomorphisms are in one to one correspondence with the solutions of $I$ . Therefore,the complexity of finding a homomorphism from a fixed graph $H$ to the input graph $G$ is tightly connected to the complexity of CSP for instances with primal graph $H$ where the same symmetric relation $R$ appears in every constraint. If the relation $R$ is not symmetric,then a similar connection can be made to the homomorphism problem in directed graphs.

图论概念还可通过另一种方式描述CSP实例的解。考虑一个二元CSP实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$，其中每个约束${c}_{i} = \left\langle  {\left( {u,v}\right) ,{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$都包含相同的二元关系${R}_{i} = R$（我们进一步假设该关系具有对称性，即$\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2}}\right)  \in  R$当且仅当$\left( {{d}_{2},{d}_{1}}\right)  \in  R$）。令$H$表示$I$的原始图，$G$是以$D$为顶点集的图，其中${d}_{1},{d}_{2} \in  D$相邻当且仅当$\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2}}\right)  \in  R$。从$H$到$G$的同态是一个映射$f : V\left( H\right)  \rightarrow  V\left( G\right)$，满足：若$u$与$v$在$H$中相邻，则$f\left( u\right)$与$f\left( v\right)$在$G$中也相邻。注意该映射不必是单射（即允许$f\left( {u}_{1}\right)  = f\left( {u}_{2}\right)$），且若$u$与$v$不相邻，我们也不要求$f\left( u\right)$与$f\left( v\right)$必须不相邻。显然，$I$的每个解$f : V \rightarrow  D$都对应一个从$H$到$G$的同态，实际上这些同态与$I$的解呈一一对应关系。因此，从固定图$H$到输入图$G$的同态求解复杂度，与原始图为$H$且每个约束包含相同对称关系$R$的CSP实例复杂度密切相关。若关系$R$非对称，则可类比有向图中的同态问题建立类似关联。

### 2.4 Relational structures

### 2.4 关系结构

The connection between CSP and graph homomorphisms that we have seen in the previous section has two major limitations: it worked only for binary CSP instances and only if every constraint contained the same relation $R$ . These limitations can be removed if we move from graphs to the much more general setting of relational structures.

前文所述CSP与图同态的关联存在两大局限：仅适用于二元CSP实例，且要求所有约束包含相同关系$R$。若将研究载体从图推广至更广义的关系结构，这些局限便可消除。

A vocabulary $\tau$ is a finite set of relation symbols of specified arities. The arity of $\tau$ is the maximum of the arities of all relational symbols it contains. A $\tau$ -structure A consists of a finite set $A$ called the universe of $\mathbf{A}$ and for each relation symbol $R \in  \tau$ ,say,of arity $k$ ,a $k$ -ary relation ${R}^{\mathrm{A}} \subseteq  {A}^{k}$ . A homomorphism from a $\tau$ -structure A to a $\tau$ -structure $\mathbf{B}$ is a mapping $h : A \rightarrow  B$ from the universe of $\mathbf{A}$ to the universe of $\mathbf{B}$ that preserves all relations,that is,for all $R \in  \tau$ ,say,of arity $k$ ,and all tuples $\left( {{a}_{1},\ldots ,{a}_{k}}\right)  \in  {R}^{\mathbf{A}}$ it holds that $\left( {h\left( {a}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,h\left( {a}_{k}\right) }\right)  \in  {R}^{\mathbf{B}}$ . Note that if $\tau$ contains only a single relational symbol,which has arity 2,then $\tau$ -structures are essentially directed graphs and the homomorphism problem between $\tau$ -structures is equivalent to the homomorphism problem on directed graphs.

词汇表$\tau$是具特定元数的关系符号有限集，其元数为所含符号的最大元数。$\tau$-结构A由有限集$A$（称为$\mathbf{A}$的论域）及每个元数为$k$的关系符号$R \in  \tau$对应的$k$元关系${R}^{\mathrm{A}} \subseteq  {A}^{k}$组成。从$\tau$-结构A到$\tau$-结构$\mathbf{B}$的同态是保持所有关系的映射$h : A \rightarrow  B$，即对任意元数为$k$的$R \in  \tau$及元组$\left( {{a}_{1},\ldots ,{a}_{k}}\right)  \in  {R}^{\mathbf{A}}$，恒有$\left( {h\left( {a}_{1}\right) ,\ldots ,h\left( {a}_{k}\right) }\right)  \in  {R}^{\mathbf{B}}$。当$\tau$仅含一个二元关系符号时，$\tau$-结构本质上即为有向图，此时结构间的同态问题等价于有向图同态问题。

More generally,we can express every CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ as a homomorphism problem the following way. Let $c = \left| C\right|$ be the number of constraints. Let the vocabulary $\tau$ contain $c$ symbols ${Q}_{1}$ , ... ${Q}_{c}$ ,where symbol ${Q}_{i}$ has the same arity ${m}_{i}$ as the constraint ${c}_{i} = \left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$ . We define $\tau$ -structure $\mathbf{A}$ over the universe $V$ such that ${Q}_{i}^{\mathbf{A}}$ contains only the tuple ${s}_{i}$ . We define $\tau$ -structure $\mathbf{B}$ over the universe $D$ such that ${Q}_{i}^{\mathbf{B}}$ is precisely the relation ${R}_{i}$ appearing in constraint ${c}_{i}$ . Now it can be verified that a mapping $f : V \rightarrow  D$ is a solution of $I$ if and only if $f$ is a homomorphism from $\mathbf{A}$ to $\mathbf{B}$ .

更一般地，我们可以将每个CSP实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$表述为如下同态问题。设$c = \left| C\right|$为约束数量。令词汇表$\tau$包含$c$个符号${Q}_{1}$,...,${Q}_{c}$，其中符号${Q}_{i}$的元数${m}_{i}$与约束${c}_{i} = \left\langle  {{s}_{i},{R}_{i}}\right\rangle$相同。我们定义域集$V$上的$\tau$-结构$\mathbf{A}$，使得${Q}_{i}^{\mathbf{A}}$仅包含元组${s}_{i}$。我们定义域集$D$上的$\tau$-结构$\mathbf{B}$，使得${Q}_{i}^{\mathbf{B}}$恰好是约束${c}_{i}$中出现的关系${R}_{i}$。现可验证：映射$f : V \rightarrow  D$是$I$的解，当且仅当$f$是从$\mathbf{A}$到$\mathbf{B}$的同态。

## 3 UNCONDITIONAL LOWER BOUNDS

## 3 无条件下限

Ideally, we would like to prove negative results and lower bounds that are incontestably true mathematical statements. However, our inability to prove the $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ hypothesis is a major barrier that prevents us from proving most negative statements of interest. For all we know,it is still possible that $\mathrm{P} = \mathrm{{NP}}$ and we can solve all database query and CSP instances in polynomial time, and hence at the moment we cannot expect to unconditionally prove any result that rules out such algorithms. As long as we are in the classical setting of computation typically studied in computational complexity (algorithm is given an input, needs to compute a yes-no output), there is little hope in proving strong unconditional lower bounds.

理想情况下，我们希望证明那些无可争议的数学否定结论与下限。然而，无法证明$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$假说这一重大障碍，使我们难以证明大多数有价值的否定命题。就目前所知，仍有可能$\mathrm{P} = \mathrm{{NP}}$，使得我们能在多项式时间内解决所有数据库查询和CSP实例，因此现阶段无法期望无条件证明任何排除此类算法的结果。只要处于计算复杂性理论研究的经典计算框架下（算法接收输入并需计算布尔输出），证明强无条件下限的希望就十分渺茫。

We can hope to obtain unconditional lower bounds only if we deviate from the classical setting: for example, the problem involves the cost of accessing the input or the cost of communication. We show a particular, very simple setting in which we have tight unconditional lower bounds. If the task is to compute the answer to a join query, then the size of the answer is obviously an unconditional lower bound on the number of steps needed for computing the answer. This raises the question: what bounds can we give on the size of the answer and are there query evaluation algorithms that match this lower bound?

唯有脱离经典计算框架，我们才有望获得无条件下限：例如涉及输入访问成本或通信成本的问题。我们将展示一个能获得紧致无条件下限的特定简单场景。若任务是计算连接查询的答案，则答案规模显然就是计算所需步骤数的无条件下限。这引出一个问题：我们能给出怎样的答案规模界限？是否存在匹配该下限的查询评估算法？

Formally,let $Q = {R}_{1}\left( {{a}_{11},\ldots ,{a}_{1{r}_{1}}}\right)  \bowtie  \cdots  \bowtie  {R}_{m}\left( {{a}_{m1},\ldots ,{a}_{m{r}_{m}}}\right)$ be a join query and let $\mathbf{D}$ be a database instance for $Q$ such that every relation ${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ contains at most $N$ tuples. What can we say about the size of the answer? It is easy to see that ${N}^{m}$ is an obvious upper bound: every tuple appearing in the answer chooses one of the at most $N$ possibilities in each of the $m$ relations. But this bound is often very far from being tight. For example, for the query $Q = {R}_{1}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{3}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{3}\left( {{a}_{2},{a}_{3}}\right)$ ,it is known that the upper bound is ${N}^{3/2}$ instead of ${N}^{3}$ . The fractional number $3/2$ in the exponent of $N$ suggests that obtaining the bound cannot be completely obvious. Still, precise bounds can be obtained in a clean way using known combinatorial techniques.

形式化地，设$Q = {R}_{1}\left( {{a}_{11},\ldots ,{a}_{1{r}_{1}}}\right)  \bowtie  \cdots  \bowtie  {R}_{m}\left( {{a}_{m1},\ldots ,{a}_{m{r}_{m}}}\right)$为连接查询，$\mathbf{D}$是$Q$的数据库实例，其中每个关系${R}_{i}\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$至多包含$N$个元组。关于答案规模，容易看出${N}^{m}$是显然的上界：答案中每个元组在$m$个关系中各选择至多$N$种可能性。但该上界往往远非紧致。例如对于查询$Q = {R}_{1}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{3}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{3}\left( {{a}_{2},{a}_{3}}\right)$，已知上界是${N}^{3/2}$而非${N}^{3}$。指数项中的分数$3/2$表明，该界限的获得并非完全显而易见。不过借助已知组合技术，仍能以清晰方式获得精确界限。

Let us define the hypergraph $H$ of the query $Q$ the following way: the vertices are the attributes and we introduce a hyperedge $\left\{  {{a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}}\right\}$ for each relation ${R}_{i}\left( {{a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}}\right)$ . A fractional cover of a hypergraph $H$ is a mapping $f : V\left( H\right)  \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$ such that for every vertex $v \in  V\left( H\right)$ ,we have $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  E\left( H\right) ,v \in  e}}f\left( e\right)  \geq  1$ . That is, $f$ is a weight assignment on the edges such that the total weight put on each vertex $v$ is at least 1 . The weight of $f$ is $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  E\left( H\right) }}f\left( e\right)$ and the fractional edge cover number ${\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right)$ of $H$ is the minimum weight of a fractional edge cover of $H$ . For example,for the query $Q = {R}_{1}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{3}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{3}\left( {{a}_{2},{a}_{3}}\right)$ ,the hypergraph $H$ is a triangle and ${\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right)  = 3/2$ (assigning weight $1/2$ to each edge is a fractional edge cover and a quick analysis shows that this is optimal).

我们按如下方式定义查询$Q$的超图$H$：顶点代表属性，为每个关系${R}_{i}\left( {{a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}}\right)$引入一条超边$\left\{  {{a}_{i1},\ldots ,{a}_{i{r}_{i}}}\right\}$。超图$H$的分数覆盖是一个映射$f : V\left( H\right)  \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack$，使得对于每个顶点$v \in  V\left( H\right)$，满足$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  E\left( H\right) ,v \in  e}}f\left( e\right)  \geq  1$。即$f$是对边的权重分配，使得每个顶点$v$上的总权重至少为1。$f$的权重为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{e \in  E\left( H\right) }}f\left( e\right)$，而$H$的分数边覆盖数${\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right)$是其所有分数边覆盖中的最小权重。例如，对于查询<b11></b1>，超图$H$是一个三角形，且${\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right)  = 3/2$（给每条边分配权重$1/2$即构成分数边覆盖，快速分析表明这是最优解）。

Using a simple application of Shearer's Lemma [24], which is a purely combinatorial statement on entropy, one can show that ${N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right) }$ is an upper bound on the number of solutions.

通过Shearer引理[24]的简单应用——这是一个关于熵的纯组合数学陈述——可以证明${N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right) }$是解数量的上界。

THEOREM 3.1 (ATSERIAS,GROHE,MARX [9]). Let $Q$ be a join query with hypergraph $H$ . Let $\mathrm{D}$ be a database for $Q$ where every relation has at most $N$ tuples. Then the answer of $Q$ in $\mathrm{D}$ has size at most $N{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right)$ .

定理3.1（ATSERIAS,GROHE,MARX[9]）。设$Q$是带有超图$H$的连接查询。若$Q$的数据库$\mathrm{D}$中每个关系最多有$N$个元组，则$Q$在$\mathrm{D}$中的答案大小至多为$N{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right)$。

Conversely,we can show that ${N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right) }$ is essentially a tight lower bound. As it is usual with lower bound statements, we have to be a bit more careful with the formulation.

反之，我们可以证明${N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right) }$本质上是一个紧下界。与下界陈述的惯例相同，我们在表述时需要更加谨慎。

THEOREM 3.2 (ATSERIAS, GROHE, MARX [9]). Let $Q$ be a join query with hypergraph $H$ . For infinitely many $N \geq  1$ ,there is a database ${\mathrm{D}}_{N}$ for $Q$ where every relation has at most $N$ tuples and the answer of $Q$ in ${\mathrm{D}}_{N}$ has size at least ${N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right) }$ .

定理3.2（ATSERIAS,GROHE,MARX[9]）。设$Q$是带有超图$H$的连接查询。对于无限多个$N \geq  1$，存在$Q$的数据库${\mathrm{D}}_{N}$，其中每个关系最多含$N$个元组，且$Q$在${\mathrm{D}}_{N}$中的答案大小至少为${N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right) }$。

Theorem 3.2 provides an unconditional lower bound for any algorithm computing the full answer of $Q$ (but of course it does not provide any bound on algorithms that just decide whether the answer is empty or compute the size of the answer). Are there algorithms that match this lower bound? The combinatorial proof of Theorem 3.1 can be turned into an algorithm with a constant overhead in the exponent,that is,to obtain ${N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right)  + O\left( 1\right) }$ running time. With additional techniques, it is possible to give tight algorithms that tightly match the lower bound.

定理3.2为任何计算$Q$完整答案的算法提供了无条件下界（当然它不适用于仅判定答案是否为空或计算答案大小的算法）。是否存在匹配该下界的算法？定理3.1的组合证明可转化为指数上带常数开销的算法，即获得${N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right)  + O\left( 1\right) }$的运行时间。通过附加技术，可以给出严格匹配该下界的紧算法。

THEOREM 3.3 ([54,61]). Let $Q$ be a join query with hypergraph $H$ . Let $\mathrm{D}$ be a database for $Q$ where every relation has at most $N$ tuples. Then the answer of $Q$ in $\mathrm{D}$ can be computed in time $O\left( {N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right) }\right)$ .

定理3.3（[54,61]）。设$Q$为具有超图$H$的连接查询，$\mathrm{D}$为$Q$的数据库，其中每个关系最多包含$N$个元组。则$Q$在$\mathrm{D}$中的答案可在$O\left( {N}^{{\rho }^{ * }\left( H\right) }\right)$时间内计算得出。

## 4 NP-HARDNESS

## 4 NP难问题

Since its conception and development in the early 70s [26, 38, 47], NP-hardness has been the main workhorse of providing intractability results for computational problems. The class NP contains decision problems that can be solved in nondeterministic polynomial time. This robust definition covers (the decision version of) most combinatorial and optimization problems of interest. If a problem $P$ is NP-hard,then this means in particular that a polynomial-time algorithm for $P$ would give polynomial-time algorithms for every problem in NP, which we take as strong evidence that such an algorithm is unlikely. We prove NP-hardness of $P$ by giving a polynomial-time reduction from a known NP-hard problem $Q$ ; this reduction shows that a polynomial-time algorithm for $P$ would give a polynomial-time algorithm for $Q$ and hence for every problem in NP.

自20世纪70年代初提出并发展以来[26,38,47]，NP难问题始终是证明计算问题不可解性的核心工具。NP类包含能在非确定性多项式时间内解决的判定问题，这一定义涵盖了大多数具有实际意义的组合与优化问题（的判定版本）。若问题$P$具有NP难性，则意味着针对$P$的多项式时间算法将可推导出NP类中所有问题的多项式时间解法——这被视作该算法不可能存在的强有力证据。我们通过从已知NP难问题$Q$进行多项式时间归约来证明$P$的NP难性，该归约表明：针对$P$的多项式时间算法将可转化为针对$Q$乃至NP类中所有问题的多项式时间解法。

Most of the problems studied in database theory or in CSP research are obviously NP-hard, as they contain basic hard problems as special cases. Therefore, it is not completely obvious how to ask reasonable questions about NP-hardness where the answer is not trivial. One direction is to consider restricted parameter values. For example, for CSP problems, one may ask if the problem remains NP-hard if we restrict the domain size $\left| D\right|$ to 2 (yes,as 3SAT is still a special case), or restrict the constraints to binary (yes, 3-COLORING is a special case),or we apply both restrictions (no,with $\left| D\right|  = 2$ and binary constraints the problem becomes the polynomial-time solvable 2SAT). Restricting the number $\left| V\right|$ of variables to any constant, say 10 , gives a polynomial-time solvable special case even if the domain size $\left| D\right|$ is arbitrarily large (as we can try the at most ${\left| D\right| }^{10}$ possible assignments in polynomial time).

数据库理论或CSP研究中探讨的问题大多显然具有NP难性，因其均包含基础难解问题作为特例。因此，如何提出非平凡结论的NP难性问题成为关键研究方向。一种思路是对参数取值进行限制：例如在CSP问题中，可探讨当限定值域大小$\left| D\right|$为2时（仍具NP难性，因3SAT为其特例），或限定约束为二元时（仍具NP难性，因3-COLORING为其特例），或同时施加两种限制（此时问题退化为多项式时间可解的2SAT）。若将变量数量$\left| V\right|$限制为任意常数（如10），即使值域大小$\left| D\right|$任意，也将产生多项式时间可解的特例（因最多只需在多项式时间内尝试${\left| D\right| }^{10}$种可能赋值）。

More generally, we can introduce restrictions on the problem in a systematic way and determine which of the restrictions lead to polynomial-time solvable and NP-hard special cases. In CSP research, a very well studied family of special cases arise from restricting the type of relations that are allowed in the constraints. Formally,let $D$ be a finite domain and let $\mathcal{R}$ be a finite set of relations over $D$ . Then we denote by $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{R}\right)$ the special case of the general CSP problem where the instance is allowed to contain only constraints $c = \langle s,R\rangle$ where $R \in  \mathcal{R}$ . Equivalently,we can state this restriction in the language of the homomorphism problem in a very compact way. Let $\tau$ be a vocabulary and let $\mathbf{B}$ be a $\tau$ -structure. Then $\mathrm{{HOM}}\left( {\_ ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$ is the special case of the general homomorphism problem where the input is a pair(A,B)of $\tau$ -structures,where $\mathbf{A}$ is arbitrary.

更系统的方法是引入问题限制条件，进而判定哪些限制会导致多项式时间可解或NP难的特例。在CSP研究中，通过约束允许的关系类型可产生一系列被深入研究的特例。形式化定义为：设$D$为有限值域，$\mathcal{R}$为$D$上有限关系集合，则$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{R}\right)$表示通用CSP问题的特例，其中实例仅允许包含满足$R \in  \mathcal{R}$的约束$c = \langle s,R\rangle$。等价地，可用同态问题语言简洁表述该限制：设$\tau$为词汇表，$\mathbf{B}$为$\tau$-结构，则$\mathrm{{HOM}}\left( {\_ ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$表示通用同态问题的特例，其输入为$\tau$-结构对(A,B)，其中$\mathbf{A}$可任意。

A classic result of Schaefer [59] characterized the complexity of $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{R}\right)$ for any fixed finite set $\mathcal{R}$ of relation over the Boolean domain (i.e., $\left| D\right|  = 2$ ). More precisely,Schaefer’s Dichotomy Theorem showed that every such $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{R}\right)$ problem is either polynomial-time solvable or NP-hard, and gave a clean characterization of the two cases. For several years, it was an outstanding open problem to prove an analog of this result for larger fixed domains (the Feder-Vardi Conjecture [35]). After partial progress, the conjecture was resolved in 2016 independently by Bulatov [21] and Zhuk [63].

Schaefer的经典成果[59]刻画了布尔域（即$\left| D\right|  = 2$）上任意固定有限关系集$\mathcal{R}$对应$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{R}\right)$问题的复杂度。具体而言，Schaefer二分定理证明此类$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{R}\right)$问题要么多项式时间可解，要么具有NP难性，并清晰界定了两类情形。多年来，证明该结论在更大固定值域上的推广形式（即Feder-Vardi猜想[35]）一直是悬而未决的难题。经过部分进展，该猜想最终由Bulatov[21]和Zhuk[63]于2016年分别独立解决。

While these classification results are cornerstones of modern CSP research, translating them into the language of database theory does not give much useful insight. Indeed, these characterization results would concern special cases where the domain of the attributes have constant size and every database relations is of constant size. From the viewpoint of database theory, a more relevant family of special cases can be obtained by restricting the structure of the query. Let us focus on Boolean JOIN QUERY, the problem of deciding if the answer set is empty or not. If we assume, for example, that the primal graph of the query is a tree (acyclic graph), then it is easy to solve the problem in polynomial time, while the problem may remain NP-hard under other restrictions (for example, under the assumption that the primal graph has maximum degree 3 or is a planar graph etc.). Formally,in the language of CSPs,if $\mathcal{G}$ is any class of graphs, we may want to understand the complexity of the problem $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ ,which is $\operatorname{CSP}$ under the restriction that the primal graph belongs to class $\mathcal{G}$ .

尽管这些分类结果是现代约束满足问题(CSP)研究的基石，但将其转化为数据库理论的语言并未提供太多有价值的洞见。实际上，这些特征化结果仅涉及属性域为恒定大小且每个数据库关系均为恒定规模的特殊情况。从数据库理论视角来看，更相关的特殊情形族可通过限制查询结构获得。让我们聚焦于布尔连接查询(Boolean JOIN QUERY)——判断结果集是否为空的问题。例如若假设查询的主图是树结构(无环图)，则该问题可在多项式时间内轻松求解，而其他限制条件下(如主图最大度为3或为平面图等)问题可能仍保持NP难。形式上，用CSP术语表述，若$\mathcal{G}$是任意图类，我们需理解问题$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$的复杂度，即在主图属于$\mathcal{G}$类限制下的$\operatorname{CSP}$。

Can we identify every class $\mathcal{G}$ that leads to polynomial-time solvable special cases and use NP-hardness to give evidence of hardness for every other case? It is known that if $\mathcal{G}$ contains only graphs of bounded treewidth,then $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ becomes polynomial-time solvable. Treewidth is a combinatorial measure of graphs that can be though of as a number expressing how treelike the graph is: treewidth is 1 if and only if the graph is an acyclic forest, while other fixed values mean that the graph is similar to a tree with each node being replaced by a small graph. While the formal definition of treewidth is technical, it models very faithfully the requirements that make the algorithmic paradigm "split on small separators and recurse" work and its mathematical naturality is further evidenced by the fact that it was independently discovered in equivalent formulations at least three times $\left\lbrack  {{14},{41},{57}}\right\rbrack$ . The precise definition of treewidth is not essential for this paper; we include the definition here only for completeness.

能否识别所有导致多项式时间可解特殊情形的图类$\mathcal{G}$，并利用NP难性证明其他情形的计算困难度？已知若$\mathcal{G}$仅包含有限树宽的图，则$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$变为多项式时间可解。树宽是图的组合度量指标，可视为表达图与树相似程度的数值：当且仅当图为无环森林时树宽为1，其他固定值意味着图类似于每个节点被小型图替换的树结构。虽然树宽的形式化定义具有技术性，但它精确建模了"基于小分隔集分裂递归"算法范式的要求，其数学自然性还体现于该概念被至少三次以等价形式独立发现$\left\lbrack  {{14},{41},{57}}\right\rbrack$。本文无需树宽的精确定义，此处仅为完整性予以说明。

Definition 4.1. A tree decomposition of a graph $G$ is a pair(B,T) where $T$ is a tree and $\mathcal{B} = \left\{  {{B}_{t} \mid  t \in  V\left( T\right) }\right\}$ is a collection of subsets of $V\left( G\right)$ such that:

定义4.1 图$G$的树分解是由(B,T)构成的二元组，其中$T$是树结构，$\mathcal{B} = \left\{  {{B}_{t} \mid  t \in  V\left( T\right) }\right\}$是$V\left( G\right)$的子集集合，满足：

- $\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( T\right) }}{B}_{t} = V\left( G\right)$ ,

- for each edge ${xy} \in  E\left( G\right) ,\{ x,y\}  \subseteq  {B}_{t}$ for some $t \in  V\left( T\right)$ ;

- 对于每条边${xy} \in  E\left( G\right) ,\{ x,y\}  \subseteq  {B}_{t}$，存在某个$t \in  V\left( T\right)$；

- for each $x \in  V\left( G\right)$ the set $\left\{  {t \mid  x \in  {B}_{t}}\right\}$ induces a connected subtree of $T$ .

- 对于每个$x \in  V\left( G\right)$，集合$\left\{  {t \mid  x \in  {B}_{t}}\right\}$在$T$上导出连通子树。

The width of the tree decomposition is $\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( T\right) }}\left\{  {\left| {B}_{t}\right|  - 1}\right\}$ . The treewidth of a graph $G$ is the minimum width over all tree decompositions of $G$ . We denote by $\operatorname{tw}\left( G\right)$ the treewidth of graph $G$ .

树分解的宽度为$\mathop{\max }\limits_{{t \in  V\left( T\right) }}\left\{  {\left| {B}_{t}\right|  - 1}\right\}$。图$G$的树宽是其所有树分解中的最小宽度，记作$\operatorname{tw}\left( G\right)$。

Freuder [37] showed that if the primal graph has bounded treewidth, then the instance can be solved in polynomial time. By now, the result can be obtained by standard dynamic programming techniques on tree decompositions.

Freuder[37]证明若主图具有有限树宽，则该实例可在多项式时间内求解。目前该结果可通过树分解上的标准动态规划技术获得。

THEOREM 4.2 (FREUDER [37]). For every fixed $k$ ,a CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ can be solved in time $O\left( {\left| V\right|  \cdot  {\left| D\right| }^{k + 1}}\right)$ if the primal graph has treewidth at most $k$ .

定理4.2(FREUDER[37]) 对于每个固定$k$，当主图树宽至多为$k$时，CSP实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$可在$O\left( {\left| V\right|  \cdot  {\left| D\right| }^{k + 1}}\right)$时间内求解。

It follows from Theorem 4.2 that $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ is polynomial-time solvable if $\mathcal{G}$ has bounded treewidth,and it is easy to find graph classes $\mathcal{G}$ with unbounded treewidth (e.g.,cliques) where the problem remains NP-hard. But, surprisingly, there seem to be cases that are neither polynomial-time solvable or NP-hard, thus a full classification into these two categories does not seem to be possible.

根据定理4.2可知，当$\mathcal{G}$的树宽有界时，$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$是多项式时间可解的；而容易找到树宽无界的图类（如团），使得该问题仍保持NP难性。但出人意料的是，似乎存在既非多项式时间可解也非NP难的情形，因此完全划分为这两类似乎不可行。

In the introduction, we mentioned that typically we expect that the problem at hand can be eventually classified as either polynomial-time solvable or NP-hard. While this may be true in most cases, there is no mathematical reason why this should be true in general. In fact,Ladner’s Theorem [48] states that if $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ , then there are NP-intermediate problems in the class NP: problems that are neither polynomial-time solvable nor NP-hard. The proof of Ladner's Theorem produces NP-intermediate problems that are highly artificial, so it is a different question whether there are natural problems that are NP-intermediate. There are two problems that are often highlighted as natural candidates for being NP-intermediate: GRAPH ISOMORPHISM and INTEGER FACTORIZATION. These two problems are not expected to be polynomial-time solvable, and the fact that they can be solved much more efficiently than brute force $\left\lbrack  {{10},{11},{49}}\right\rbrack$ suggests that they are not NP-hard either.

引言中我们提到，通常期望问题最终可归类为多项式时间可解或NP难。虽然多数情况如此，但从数学角度并无普适性依据。事实上，Ladner定理[48]指出：若$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$，则NP类中存在NP中间问题——既非多项式时间可解也非NP难的问题。该定理证明构造的NP中间问题具有高度人为性，因此自然存在的NP中间问题成为另一议题。有两个问题常被视为NP中间问题的天然候选者：图同构(GRAPH ISOMORPHISM)和整数分解(INTEGER FACTORIZATION)。这两个问题不被认为存在多项式时间解法，且其求解效率远高于暴力穷举$\left\lbrack  {{10},{11},{49}}\right\rbrack$的事实表明它们也非NP难问题。

One could say that the reason why GRAPH ISOMORPHISM and INTEGER FACTORIZATION are NP-intermediate is that the deep algebraic and number-theoretic structures underlying these problems make them occupy a special place in the complexity landscape of NP problems. However, it is important to point out that problems can be (probably) NP-intermediate for more pedestrian reasons: it is possible to scale down an NP-hard problem in a way that it no longer NP-hard, but still not sufficiently easy to be polynomial-time solvable. We will refer to the following (artificial) example also in later sections.

可以说，图同构与整数分解之所以成为NP中间问题，是因为其深层的代数与数论结构使它们在NP问题复杂度图谱中占据特殊位置。但需指出，问题成为（可能的）NP中间问题也可能源于更平凡的原因：可以通过缩减NP难问题的规模使其不再NP难，但仍不足以达到多项式时间可解。后文将反复引用以下（人为构造的）示例。

Definition 4.3. A graph $G$ is special if it has exactly two connected components: a clique of size $k$ for some integer $k \geq  1$ and a path of exactly ${2}^{k}$ vertices. SPECIAL CSP and SPECIAL BOOLEAN JOIN QUERY are the restricted cases of the general problems where we assume that the primal graph is special.

定义4.3. 若图$G$恰好包含两个连通分量：一个规模为$k$（$k \geq  1$为某整数）的团和一条含${2}^{k}$个顶点的路径，则称该图为特殊图。SPECIAL CSP和SPECIAL BOOLEAN JOIN QUERY是假定原图为特殊图时，相应一般问题的受限情形。

Let us give some intuitive arguments why these problems could be NP-intermediate (we will make this more formal in later sections). First, the path part can be solved efficiently in polynomial time. Then we need to solve the clique part, which can certainly be done by brute force in time $O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$ ,where $n$ is the total length of the input. But as already the primal graph has size larger than ${2}^{k}$ ,we have $n \geq  {2}^{k}$ and hence $k \leq  \log n$ . Thus we can solve the problem in quasipolynomial time ${n}^{O\left( {\log n}\right) }$ ,which would be an exceptionally unusual property of an NP-hard problem. Moreover, it is not clear what substantial improvements we can expect on this algorithm: one would need to solve the clique part significantly faster than brute force. Therefore, it seems that these problems variants are likely to be NP-intermediate with best possible running time around ${n}^{O\left( {\log n}\right) }$ .

我们给出这些问题可能属于NP中间问题的直观论证（后文将形式化阐述）。首先，路径部分可在多项式时间内高效求解。而团部分虽可通过$O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$时间暴力求解（$n$为输入总长度），但由于原图规模已超过${2}^{k}$，故有$n \geq  {2}^{k}$，从而$k \leq  \log n$。因此问题可在拟多项式时间${n}^{O\left( {\log n}\right) }$内求解——这对NP难问题而言是极其罕见的特性。此外，该算法难有实质性改进：需显著快于暴力解法处理团部分。因此，这些问题变体很可能是NP中间问题，最佳运行时间约为${n}^{O\left( {\log n}\right) }$。

This example shows that even if we just want to understand which special cases are polynomial-time solvable, then NP-hardness may not be sufficient for this purpose. As we shall see in later sections, we need to use other lower bound techniques for this type of classification. Additionally, these lower bound techniques can provide stronger lower bounds beyond just ruling out polynomial-time algorithms, showing the optimality of certain algorithms in a tighter way.

此例表明，即便仅需理解哪些特例是多项式时间可解的，NP难性也可能不足。后文将展示，此类分类需借助其他下界技术。这些技术不仅能排除多项式时间算法，还能给出更强下界，更精确地证明特定算法的最优性。

## 5 PARAMETERIZED INTRACTABILITY

## 5 参数化难解性

Parameterized complexity considers algorithmic problems where each input instance has a parameter $k$ associated with it. This parameter is typically either the size of the solution we are looking for or some measure of the input, such as the number of variables in a formula, the dimension of the input point set, the maximum degree of the input graph, or perhaps the alphabet size of the input strings. The central goal of parameterized complexity is to develop algorithms that are efficient on instances where the value of the parameter is small. Formally, we say that a parameterized problem is fixed-parameter tractable (FPT) if it can be solved in time $f\left( k\right)  \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ ,where $n$ is the size of the input and $f$ is a computable function depending only on $k$ . This form of running time has to be contrasted with the running time ${n}^{O\left( k\right) }$ of brute force algorithms that are often easily achievable if $k$ is the size of the solution we are looking for. Research in the past three decades has shown that many of the natural NP-hard problems are FPT with various parameterizations, leading to algorithms that are often highly nontrivial and combinatorially deep $\left\lbrack  {{29},{31},{32},{36}}\right\rbrack$ .

参数化复杂度研究的是每个输入实例都关联一个参数$k$的算法问题。该参数通常是我们寻找的解的规模或输入的某种度量，例如公式中的变量数量、输入点集的维度、输入图的最大度数，亦或是输入字符串的字母表大小。参数化复杂度的核心目标是开发在参数值较小时高效的算法。形式化地说，若一个参数化问题能在$f\left( k\right)  \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$时间内求解（其中$n$表示输入规模，$f$是仅依赖于$k$的可计算函数），则称该问题具有固定参数可处理性(FPT)。这种时间复杂度形式需要与暴力算法的${n}^{O\left( k\right) }$时间复杂度形成对比——当$k$表示所求解的规模时，后者往往容易实现。过去三十年的研究表明，许多自然NP难问题在不同参数化下都具有FPT性质，由此产生的算法通常高度非平凡且具有深刻的组合数学内涵$\left\lbrack  {{29},{31},{32},{36}}\right\rbrack$。

As an example, let us consider VERTEX COVER: given a graph $G$ and an integer $k$ ,the task is to find a vertex cover $S$ of size at most $k$ ,that is,a set $S$ of at most $k$ vertices such that every edge of $G$ has at least one endpoint in $S$ . Clearly,we can solve the problem by brute force on an $n$ -vertex graph by trying each of the $O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$ sets $S$ of size at most $k$ . But more efficient algorithms are available: a standard application of the bounded-depth search tree technique already delivers a ${2}^{k} \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ algorithm,which can be further refined with additional techniques [23]. This means that VERTEX COVER is FPT parameterized by the size of the solution. For CLIQUE,the trivial $O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$ brute force search can be improved to about $O\left( {n}^{{\omega k}/3}\right)$ ) (where $\omega  < {2.3729}$ is the exponent for matrix multiplication $\left\lbrack  {6,{53},{53}}\right\rbrack$ ),but no FPT algorithm is known despite significant efforts.

以顶点覆盖问题(VERTEX COVER)为例：给定图$G$和整数$k$，任务是找到规模不超过$k$的顶点覆盖$S$，即满足图$G$中每条边至少有一个端点属于$S$的顶点子集。显然，我们可以在$n$个顶点的图上通过暴力枚举所有不超过$k$规模的$O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$个子集来求解。但存在更高效的算法：应用有界深度搜索树技术即可得到${2}^{k} \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$算法，结合其他技术还可进一步优化[23]。这意味着顶点覆盖问题在解规模参数化下是FPT的。对于团问题(CLIQUE)，虽然暴力搜索的$O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$时间复杂度可改进至约$O\left( {n}^{{\omega k}/3}\right)$（其中$\omega  < {2.3729}$是矩阵乘法$\left\lbrack  {6,{53},{53}}\right\rbrack$的指数），但经过大量努力仍未找到FPT算法。

Motivated by this apparent difference between VERTEX COVER and CLIQUE, Downey and Fellows introduced the notion of W[1]- hardness and the FPT $\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ hypothesis [31]. We omit here the technical definitions related to the class $\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ ; for the purpose of proving negative evidence,it is sufficient to know that FPT $\neq$ $\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ is equivalent to the statement "CLIQUE is not FPT" (or to "INDEPENDENT SET is not FPT", as the two problems are equivalent by taking the complement of the graph). To define W[1]-hardness, we need first the following notion of reduction:

基于顶点覆盖与团问题之间的这种显著差异，Downey和Fellows提出了W[1]难度的概念以及FPT$\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$假设[31]。此处我们省略关于复杂度类$\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$的技术定义；为证明负面结论，只需知道FPT$\neq$$\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$等价于命题"团问题不是FPT的"（或"独立集问题不是FPT的"，因为这两个问题通过取图补集相互等价）。要定义W[1]难度，首先需要以下归约概念：

Definition 5.1. Let $P$ and $Q$ be two parameterized problems. A parameterized reduction transforms an instance $x$ of $P$ with parameter $k$ to an instance ${x}^{\prime }$ of $Q$ with parameter ${k}^{\prime }$ such that

定义5.1. 设$P$和$Q$为两个参数化问题。参数化归约将带有参数$k$的$P$实例$x$转换为带有参数${k}^{\prime }$的$Q$实例${x}^{\prime }$，满足：

(1)(x,k)is a yes-instance of $P$ if and only if $\left( {{x}^{\prime },{k}^{\prime }}\right)$ is a yes-instance of $Q$ .

(1) (x,k)是$P$的肯定实例当且仅当$\left( {{x}^{\prime },{k}^{\prime }}\right)$是$Q$的肯定实例。

(2) The running time of the reduction is $f\left( k\right) {\left| x\right| }^{O\left( 1\right) }$ for some computable function $f$ .

(2) 归约过程的运行时间为某个可计算函数$f$对应的$f\left( k\right) {\left| x\right| }^{O\left( 1\right) }$。

(3) We have ${k}^{\prime } \leq  f\left( k\right)$ for some computable function $f$ .

(3) 对于某个可计算函数$f$，我们有${k}^{\prime } \leq  f\left( k\right)$。

The third requirement is what makes this notion very different from usual polynomial-time reductions: we have to pay extra attention not to blow up too much the parameter $k$ in the reduction. Parameterized reductions were designed in a way that they transfer the property of being FPT: it can be shown that if there is a parameterized reduction from $P$ to $Q$ and $Q$ is FPT,then $P$ is FPT as well. We can define $\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ -hardness by saying that a problem $P$ is $\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ - hard if there is a parameterized reduction from CLIQUE to $P$ . We can interpret this as evidence that $P$ is not FPT: an FPT algorithm for $P$ would show that CLIQUE is FPT,violating the FPT $\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ hypothesis.

第三个要求使得这一概念与常规多项式时间归约截然不同：我们必须格外注意避免在归约过程中过度放大参数$k$。参数化归约的设计初衷是传递FPT性质：可以证明，若存在从$P$到$Q$的参数化归约且$Q$属于FPT，则$P$同样属于FPT。我们可通过将问题$P$定义为$\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$难（若存在从CLIQUE到$P$的参数化归约）来界定$\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$难度。这可视作$P$不属于FPT的证据：针对$P$的FPT算法将证明CLIQUE属于FPT，从而违背FPT$\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$假设。

Let us have a look at the complexity of CSP via the lens of parameterized complexity. Given a instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ ,we can introduce the number $k = \left| V\right|$ of variables as the parameter of the instance. We can decide if there is a solution by trying each of the ${\left| D\right| }^{k} = O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$ possible assignments. The NP-hardness of the problem implies that this cannot be improved to ${n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ (assuming $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ ),but this does not rule out the possibility that the problem is FPT parameterized by $k$ ,that is,there is a $f\left( k\right)  \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ time algorithm. Such an algorithm would be certainly of interest in contexts where we can assume that $k$ is small,but $D$ is large (which is typically true in database applications). However, it is easy to see that the problem of finding a clique of size $k$ in a graph $G$ can be expressed as a CSP problem with $k$ variables, $\left( \begin{array}{l} k \\  2 \end{array}\right)$ constraints,and domain $D = V\left( G\right)$ . That is,there is a parameterized reduction from CLIQUE to CSP parameterized by the number of variables, showing that the latter problem is unlikely to be FPT either.

让我们从参数化复杂度视角审视CSP的复杂性。给定实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$，可将变量数量$k = \left| V\right|$设为实例参数。通过尝试${\left| D\right| }^{k} = O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$种可能的赋值即可判定解的存在性。问题的NP难性表明无法改进至${n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$（假设$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$），但这不排除问题对于参数$k$是FPT的可能性，即存在$f\left( k\right)  \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$时间算法。在$k$较小而$D$较大的场景（如数据库应用中），此类算法极具价值。然而显然，在图$G$中寻找规模为$k$的团可转化为具有$k$个变量、$\left( \begin{array}{l} k \\  2 \end{array}\right)$个约束且定义域为$D = V\left( G\right)$的CSP问题。这表明存在从CLIQUE到以变量数为参数的CSP的归约，故后者很可能也非FPT问题。

We can now return to the question left open at the end of Section 4: what are those classes $\mathcal{G}$ for which $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ is polynomial-time solvable? As we have seen, NP-hardness does not seem to be sufficiently strong to highlight all the negative cases. Instead, let us look at the fixed-parameter tractability of $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ ,parameterized by the number $k$ of variables. Now if $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ can be proved to be $\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ -hard for some $\mathcal{G}$ ,then this implies in particular that it is not polynomial-time solvable,assuming FPT $\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ .

现在我们可以回到第4节末尾悬而未决的问题：对于哪些类<b0></b1>，$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$是多项式时间可解的？如前所述，NP难性似乎不足以涵盖所有负面情况。让我们转而考察以变量数$k$为参数的$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$的固定参数可解性。若能证明$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$对某些$\mathcal{G}$是$\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$难的，则在FPT$\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$假设下，这尤其意味着该问题非多项式时间可解。

For example,let $\mathcal{G}$ contain every special graph,as defined in Definition 4.3,and let us consider SPECIAL CSP (that is, $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ ) parameterized by the number of variables. Given an instance of CLIQUE (a graph $G$ where it has to be decided if there is a clique of size $k$ ),then we can express it as a SPECIAL CSP instance as follows. We introduce $k$ variables connected by $\left( \begin{array}{l} k \\  2 \end{array}\right)$ binary constraints to express the problem of finding a $k$ -clique,and additionally we introduce ${2}^{k}$ dummy variables connected by constraints forming a path. The primal graph is a $k$ -clique plus a path on ${2}^{k}$ vertices, as required in SPECIAL CSP. The reduction turns the problem of finding a $k$ -clique to a SPECIAL CSP instance on $f\left( k\right)  = k + {2}^{k}$ variables, hence this is a proper parameterized reduction. It follows that SPECIAL CSP is W[1]-hard parameterized by the number of variables, and hence unlikely to be polynomial-time solvable.

例如，令$\mathcal{G}$包含定义4.3中所有的特殊图，并考虑以变量数量为参数的SPECIAL CSP（即$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$）。给定一个CLIQUE问题实例（需要判断图$G$中是否存在大小为$k$的团），可将其转化为SPECIAL CSP实例：引入$k$个通过$\left( \begin{array}{l} k \\  2 \end{array}\right)$个二元约束连接的变量来表达寻找$k$-团的问题，并额外添加${2}^{k}$个通过路径约束连接的哑变量。原始图由$k$-团和${2}^{k}$个顶点组成的路径构成，符合SPECIAL CSP要求。该归约将寻找$k$-团的问题转化为含$f\left( k\right)  = k + {2}^{k}$个变量的SPECIAL CSP实例，故为恰当的参数化归约。由此可得，以变量数为参数的SPECIAL CSP属于W[1]-难问题，因而基本不存在多项式时间解法。

More generally, Grohe, Schwentick, and Segoufin [40] proved that if $\mathcal{G}$ has unbounded treewidth,then $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ is $\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ -hard, leading to a complete classification.

更广义地说，Grohe、Schwentick和Segoufin[40]证明：若$\mathcal{G}$具有无界树宽，则$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$属于$\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$-难问题，从而实现了完全分类。

Theorem 5.2 (Grohe,Schwentick,and Segoufin [40]). Let $\mathcal{G}$ be a decidable class of graphs. Assuming FPT $\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ ,the following are equivalent:

定理5.2（Grohe、Schwentick和Segoufin[40]）。设$\mathcal{G}$为可判定图类。假设FPT$\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$，以下条件等价：

(1) $\mathcal{G}$ has bounded treewidth,

(1) $\mathcal{G}$具有有界树宽

(2) $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ is polynomial-time solvable,

(2) $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$存在多项式时间解法

(3) $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ is FPT parameterized by the number $k$ of variables.

(3) $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$以变量数$k$为参数时属于FPT类

Observe that there is a major coincidence here: the polynomial-time solvable cases are exactly the same as the FPT cases (which in principle could have been a more general class). This coincidence makes it possible to use W[1]-hardness to identify those cases that are not polynomial-time solvable.

注意此处存在重大巧合：多项式时间可解的情形与FPT情形完全重合（理论上后者可能构成更广的类别）。这一巧合使得我们可以利用W[1]-难性来识别非多项式时间可解的情形。

A more general result gives a classification in the framework of homomorphism problem for relational structures (which can be directly translated to results for Boolean JOIN QUERY). Let $\tau$ be a vocabulary and let $\mathcal{A}$ be a class of $\tau$ -structures. Then $\operatorname{HOM}\left( {\mathcal{A},\_ }\right)$ is the special case of the general homomorphism problem where given two $\tau$ -structures (A,B) with $\mathbf{A} \in  \mathcal{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ arbitrary,the task is to decide if there is a homomorphism from $\mathbf{A}$ to $\mathbf{B}$ . The polynomial-time solvable cases again depend on treewidth, but in a slightly more complicated manner. If ${\mathbf{A}}^{\prime }$ is a substructure of $\mathbf{A}$ such that there is a homomorphism from $\mathbf{A}$ to ${\mathbf{A}}^{\prime }$ ,then the problem instances (A,B)and $\left( {{\mathrm{A}}^{\prime },\mathrm{B}}\right)$ are equivalent. The smallest such substructure ${\mathrm{A}}^{\prime }$ of $\mathrm{A}$ is called the core of $\mathrm{A}$ (it is known to be unique up to isomorphism). It is the treewidth of this core that determines the complexity of the problem.

一个更普遍的结果在关系结构同态问题的框架下给出了分类（可直接转化为布尔连接查询的结果）。设$\tau$为词汇表，$\mathcal{A}$为$\tau$-结构类，则$\operatorname{HOM}\left( {\mathcal{A},\_ }\right)$是一般同态问题的特例：给定两个$\tau$-结构(A,B)，其中$\mathbf{A} \in  \mathcal{A}$和$\mathbf{B}$任意，需判定是否存在从$\mathbf{A}$到$\mathbf{B}$的同态映射。多项式时间可解情形仍取决于树宽，但以更复杂的方式。若${\mathbf{A}}^{\prime }$是$\mathbf{A}$的子结构且存在$\mathbf{A}$到${\mathbf{A}}^{\prime }$的同态，则问题实例(A,B)与$\left( {{\mathrm{A}}^{\prime },\mathrm{B}}\right)$等价。$\mathrm{A}$的最小此类子结构${\mathrm{A}}^{\prime }$称为其核心（已知在同构意义下唯一），正是该核心的树宽决定了问题的复杂度。

THEOREM 5.3 (GROHE [39]). Let $\tau$ be a finite vocabulary and let $\mathcal{A}$ be a decidable class of $\tau$ -structures. Assuming FPT $\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ ,the following are equivalent:

定理5.3（GROHE [39]）。设$\tau$为有限词汇表，$\mathcal{A}$为可判定的$\tau$-结构类。假设FPT$\neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$，则以下条件等价：

(1) the cores of the structures in $\mathbf{A}$ have bounded treewidth,

(1) $\mathbf{A}$中结构的核心具有有界树宽

(2) $\operatorname{HOM}\left( {\mathcal{A},\_ }\right)$ is polynomial-time solvable,

(2) $\operatorname{HOM}\left( {\mathcal{A},\_ }\right)$是多项式时间可解的

(3) $\operatorname{HOM}\left( {\mathcal{A},\_ }\right)$ is FPT parameterized by the size $k$ of the universe of A.

(3) $\operatorname{HOM}\left( {\mathcal{A},\_ }\right)$关于结构A的域大小$k$是FPT参数化的

Again, we have that the polynomial-time solvable and FPT cases coincide,allowing the use of $\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ -hardness for the classification of both properties.

再次表明多项式时间可解情形与FPT情形重合，允许使用$\mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$-硬度对两类性质进行分类。

## 6 THE EXPONENTIAL-TIME HYPOTHESIS

## 6 指数时间假设

Parameterized complexity gives a finer understanding of the complexity of problems: for example, the negative results not only tell us that CLIQUE is not polynomial-time solvable, but they rule out algorithms with running time $f\left( k\right) {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ . However,this is still a qualitative result that rules out a certain running time, but does not tell us the exact complexity of the problem: can the ${n}^{k}$ brute force search be improved to,say, ${n}^{\sqrt{k}}$ ,or to ${n}^{O\left( {\log k}\right) }$ ,or to ${n}^{O\left( {\log \log \log \log k}\right) }$ , or to ...? For all we know, such algorithms cannot be ruled out based on the $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ or $\mathrm{{FPT}} \neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$ conjectures. Similarly,for FPT problems such as VERTEX COVER where the best known algorithms have running time of the form ${2}^{O\left( k\right) } \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ ,we cannot rule out that these algorithms can be significantly improved to, say, ${2}^{O\left( \sqrt{k}\right) } \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ .

参数化复杂度使问题复杂度的理解更精细：例如负面结果不仅表明CLIQUE非多项式时间可解，还排除了运行时间为$f\left( k\right) {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$的算法。但这仍是定性结果——虽排除特定运行时间，却未给出问题的精确复杂度：能否将${n}^{k}$暴力搜索改进为${n}^{\sqrt{k}}$、${n}^{O\left( {\log k}\right) }$、${n}^{O\left( {\log \log \log \log k}\right) }$或更优？据我们所知，基于$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$或$\mathrm{{FPT}} \neq  \mathrm{W}\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack$猜想无法排除这类算法。同理，对于VERTEX COVER等FPT问题（已知最佳算法运行时间为${2}^{O\left( k\right) } \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$），我们无法排除其可显著改进至${2}^{O\left( \sqrt{k}\right) } \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$的可能性。

The Exponential-Time Hypothesis (ETH), formulated by Impagli-azzo,Paturi,and Zane $\left\lbrack  {{43},{44}}\right\rbrack$ ,makes the assumption $\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$ more quantitative: informally, it not only tells us that NP-hard problems do not have polynomial-time algorithms, but it postulates that NP-hard problems really require exponential time and cannot be solved in subexponential time. The formal statement of the ETH is somewhat technical and for most applications it is more convenient to use the following assumption instead, which is an easy consequence of the ETH:

指数时间假设(ETH)由Impagli-azzo、Paturi和Zane$\left\lbrack  {{43},{44}}\right\rbrack$提出，该假设将$\mathrm{P} \neq  \mathrm{{NP}}$的量化程度提升：通俗而言，它不仅指明NP难问题不存在多项式时间算法，更断言NP难问题本质上需要指数时间求解，无法在亚指数时间内解决。ETH的形式化表述较为技术性，在多数应用场景下，采用以下推论假设更为便利（该推论可直接由ETH导出）：

HYPOTHESIS 1 (CONSEQUENCE OF THE ETH, IMPAGLIAZZO, PATURI, AND ZANE $\left\lbrack  {{43},{44}}\right\rbrack  )$ . 3SAT with $n$ variables cannot be solved in time ${2}^{o\left( n\right) }$ .

假设1(ETH推论，Impagliazzo、Paturi和Zane$\left\lbrack  {{43},{44}}\right\rbrack  )$)。含$n$个变量的3SAT问题无法在${2}^{o\left( n\right) }$时间内求解。

3SAT is the fundamental satisfiability problem where, given a Boolean formula in conjunctive normal form with at most 3 literals in each clause (e.g., $\left( {{x}_{1} \vee  {\bar{x}}_{3} \vee  {x}_{5}}\right)  \land  \left( {{\bar{x}}_{1} \vee  {x}_{2} \vee  {x}_{3}}\right)  \land  \left( {{\bar{x}}_{2} \vee  {x}_{3} \vee  {x}_{4}}\right)$ ), the task is to decide whether a satisfying assignment exists. For completeness, let us recall the formal statement of the ETH, of which Hypothesis 1 is an easy consequence. Let ${s}_{k}$ be the infinum of all real numbers $\delta$ for which there exists an $O\left( {2}^{\delta n}\right)$ time algorithm for $k$ -SAT. Then the ETH is the assumption that ${s}_{k} > 0$ for every $k \geq  3$ . It is easy to show that this assumption implies Hypothesis 1, hence if we can show that some statement would refute Hypothesis 1 , then it would refute the ETH as well.

3SAT是最基本的可满足性问题，其输入为每个子句最多含3个文字（例如$\left( {{x}_{1} \vee  {\bar{x}}_{3} \vee  {x}_{5}}\right)  \land  \left( {{\bar{x}}_{1} \vee  {x}_{2} \vee  {x}_{3}}\right)  \land  \left( {{\bar{x}}_{2} \vee  {x}_{3} \vee  {x}_{4}}\right)$）的合取范式布尔公式，需判定是否存在满足赋值。为完备性计，我们回顾ETH的形式化表述（假设1即其直接推论）。设${s}_{k}$为所有满足$\delta$-SAT存在$O\left( {2}^{\delta n}\right)$时间算法的实数$k$的下确界，则ETH假设对任意${s}_{k} > 0$都有$k \geq  3$。易证该假设蕴含假设1，故若某命题可推翻假设1，则同样可推翻ETH。

As 3SAT can interpreted as a special case of CSP with domain size 2 and constraints of arity 3 , we can translate Hypothesis 1 into the language of CSPs to obtain a lower bound for solving instances with constant domain size.

由于3SAT可视为域大小为2、约束元数为3的CSP特例，我们可将假设1转化为CSP表述，从而得到恒定域规模实例求解的时间下界。

COROLLARY 6.1. Assuming the ETH, there is no algorithm that solves every CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ in time ${2}^{o\left( \left| V\right| \right) } \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ ,even if $\left| D\right|  = 2$ and every constraint has arity at most 3 .

推论6.1。在ETH成立前提下，不存在能在${2}^{o\left( \left| V\right| \right) } \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$时间内求解所有CSP实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$的算法，即使$\left| D\right|  = 2$且每个约束的元数至多为3。

Hypothesis 1 rules out the existence of algorithms that are subex-ponential in the number $n$ of variables. But the number $m$ of clauses in a 3SAT instance can be up to cubic in the number of variables, thus the length of the instance can be much larger than $O\left( n\right)$ . Therefore, Hypothesis 1 does not rule out the existence of algorithms that are subexponential in the length of the instance: it could be potentially the case that all the really hard instances of 3SAT have, say, $\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$ clauses,hence a ${2}^{o\left( \sqrt{n + m}\right) }$ algorithm would be still compatible with Hypothesis 1. Impagliazzo, Paturi and Zane [44] showed that this is not the case: the Sparsification Lemma implies that, for the purposes of Hypothesis 1, 3SAT remains hard already when restricted to instances with a linear number of clauses. With the Sparsification Lemma, the following stronger assumption follows from Hypothesis 1:

假设1排除了变量数$n$的亚指数时间算法。但3SAT实例的子句数$m$可达变量数的三次方，故实例长度可能远大于$O\left( n\right)$。因此假设1并不排除实例长度的亚指数时间算法：理论上3SAT的真正困难实例可能均含$\Omega \left( {n}^{2}\right)$量子句，此时${2}^{o\left( \sqrt{n + m}\right) }$算法仍与假设1兼容。Impagliazzo、Paturi和Zane[44]证明此情形不成立：稀疏化引理表明就假设1而言，3SAT在子句数线性增长时仍保持困难性。结合稀疏化引理，可得到比假设1更强的推论：

HYPOTHESIS 2 (CONSEQUENCE OF THE ETH + SPARSIFICATION LEMMA, IMPAGLIAZZO, PATURI, AND ZANE [44]). 3SAT with $n$ variables and $m$ clauses cannot be solved in time ${2}^{o\left( {n + m}\right) }$ .

假设2(ETH+稀疏化引理推论，Impagliazzo、Paturi和Zane[44])。含$n$个变量和$m$个子句的3SAT问题无法在${2}^{o\left( {n + m}\right) }$时间内求解。

This stronger assumption turns out to be very useful to prove lower bounds for other problems. Reductions from 3SAT to other problems typically create instances whose size depends not only on the number $n$ of variables,but also on the number $m$ of clauses, hence it is important to have lower bounds on 3SAT in terms of both $n$ and $m$ . For example,if we look at textbook reductions from 3SAT to 3-ColorING,then they transform a formula with $n$ variables and $m$ clauses into a graph with $O\left( {n + m}\right)$ vertices and $O\left( {n + m}\right)$ edges. Such a reduction together with Hypothesis 2 implies a lower bound for binary CSP over a constant domain size.

这一更强的假设被证明在证明其他问题的下界时极为有用。从3SAT到其他问题的归约通常生成的实例规模不仅取决于变量数$n$，还取决于子句数$m$，因此基于$n$和$m$两个参数对3SAT进行下界分析至关重要。例如，教科书中的3SAT到3-ColorING归约会将含$n$个变量和$m$条子句的公式转化为具有$O\left( {n + m}\right)$个顶点和$O\left( {n + m}\right)$条边的图。此类归约结合假设2，可推导出在常量域大小下二元CSP问题的下界。

COROLLARY 6.2. Assuming the ETH, there is no algorithm that solves every CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ in time ${2}^{o\left( {\left| V\right|  + \left| C\right| }\right) } \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ , even if $\left| D\right|  = 3$ and every constraint is binary.

推论6.2. 在ETH假设下，不存在能在${2}^{o\left( {\left| V\right|  + \left| C\right| }\right) } \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$时间内求解所有CSP实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$的算法，即使$\left| D\right|  = 3$且所有约束均为二元。

Let us turn our attention now to parameterized problems. A key result in parameterized complexity states that, assuming ETH, the ${n}^{k}$ brute force search for CLIQUE cannot be improved better than a constant factor in the exponent,even if we allow an arbitrary $f\left( k\right)$ factor in the running time.

现在我们将注意力转向参数化问题。参数化复杂度的一个关键结论表明：在ETH假设下，针对CLIQUE问题的${n}^{k}$暴力搜索算法，其指数时间复杂度无法通过常数因子优化改进，即便允许运行时间中存在任意$f\left( k\right)$因子。

THEOREM 6.3 (CHEN ET AL. [22]). Assuming ETH, CLIQUE cannot be solved in time $f\left( k\right)  \cdot  {n}^{o\left( k\right) }$ for any computable function $f$ .

定理6.3 (Chen等[22]). 在ETH假设下，对于任何可计算函数$f$，CLIQUE问题无法在$f\left( k\right)  \cdot  {n}^{o\left( k\right) }$时间内求解。

The same is true for the PARTITIONED CLIQUE, which, as we have seen in Section 2.3, is essentially equivalent to a binary CSP instance where the primal graph is clique. Therefore, we can translate Theorem 6.3 into the language of CSPs.

该结论同样适用于PARTITIONED CLIQUE问题——如第2.3节所述，该问题本质上等价于原始图为完全图的二元CSP实例。因此我们可以将定理6.3转化为CSP表述。

THEOREM 6.4. Assuming ETH, there is no algorithm that solves every binary CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ in time $f\left( \left| V\right| \right)  \cdot  {\left| D\right| }^{o\left( \left| V\right| \right)  \mid  }$ . ${n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ ,where $f$ is an arbitrary computable function.

定理6.4. 在ETH假设下，不存在能在$f\left( \left| V\right| \right)  \cdot  {\left| D\right| }^{o\left( \left| V\right| \right)  \mid  }$时间内求解所有二元CSP实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$的算法。${n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$，其中$f$为任意可计算函数。

Moreover,we have seen in Section 5 that $k$ -CLIQUE can be reduced to a SPECIAL CSP instance with $k + {2}^{k}$ variables. Together with Theorem 6.3 it follows that, assuming the ETH, there is no $f\left( \left| V\right| \right) {n}^{o\left( {\log \left| V\right| }\right) }$ time algorithm for SPECIAL CSP. This makes the NP-intermediate status of the problem very precise: it is indeed ${n}^{O\left( {\log \left| V\right| }\right) }$ the best possible running time we can hope for this problem.

此外，第5节已证明$k$-CLIQUE可归约为含$k + {2}^{k}$个变量的SPECIAL CSP实例。结合定理6.3可知：在ETH假设下，SPECIAL CSP不存在$f\left( \left| V\right| \right) {n}^{o\left( {\log \left| V\right| }\right) }$时间算法。这精确界定了该问题的NP中间状态——事实上${n}^{O\left( {\log \left| V\right| }\right) }$就是我们期望的最佳时间复杂度。

Note that the treewidth of a $k$ -clique is $k - 1$ . Therefore,Theorem 6.4 shows that if $k$ is the treewidth of the primal graph of the CSP instance,then the ${n}^{O\left( k\right) }$ time algorithm of Freuder [37] is essentially optimal in the sense that the exponent cannot be improved by more than a constant factor.

注意$k$-完全图的树宽为$k - 1$。因此定理6.4表明：若CSP实例原始图的树宽为$k$，则Freuder[37]提出的${n}^{O\left( k\right) }$时间算法本质上已最优，其指数项至多只能通过常数因子改进。

THEOREM 6.5. Assuming ETH, there is no algorithm that solves every binary CSP instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ in time $f\left( \left| V\right| \right)  \cdot  {n}^{o\left( k\right) }$ ,where $k$ is the treewidth of the primal graph and $f$ is an arbitrary computable function $f$ .

定理6.5. 在ETH假设下，不存在能在$f\left( \left| V\right| \right)  \cdot  {n}^{o\left( k\right) }$时间内求解所有二元CSP实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$的算法，其中$k$表示原始图的树宽，$f$为任意可计算函数$f$。

One could interpret Theorem 6.5 as saying that the treewidth-based algorithm of Freuder is an optimal way of solving CSP instances. However, this interpretation is misleading. What Theorem 6.5 really says is that there is one type of primal graphs, namely cliques,where the ${n}^{O\left( k\right) }$ running time that follows using that treewidth-based algorithm is essentially optimal. This does not rule out the possibility that there are some graph classes, maybe planar graphs, bounded-degree graphs, interval graphs, etc. where it is possible to solve the problem ${n}^{o\left( k\right) }$ time,where $k$ is the treewidth of the primal graph. Formally, we can approach this possibility in the spirit of Theorem 5.2,by considering the problem $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ ,where the primal graph is restricted some class $\mathcal{G}$ . The following lower bound shows that the treewidth-based algorithm is still optimal for any such $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ ,up to a logarithmic factor in the exponent.

人们可能将定理6.5解读为：Freuder基于树宽的算法是解决CSP实例的最优方法。但这种理解具有误导性。定理6.5实际表明，仅对一类原始图（即团簇）而言，基于树宽算法的${n}^{O\left( k\right) }$时间复杂度本质上是不可优化的。这并不排除某些图类（如平面图、有限度图、区间图等）可能存在${n}^{o\left( k\right) }$时间复杂度的解法，其中$k$表示原始图的树宽。形式上，我们可以遵循定理5.2的思路，通过研究限制原始图属于某类$\mathcal{G}$的问题$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$来探讨这种可能性。接下来的下界证明表明，基于树宽的算法对此类$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$仍是最优解，至多存在指数中对数因子的差异。

THEOREM 6.6 ([52]). Let $\mathcal{G}$ be a class of graphs with unbounded treewidth. Assuming ETH, there is no algorithm that solves every instance $I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$ of $\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$ in time $f\left( \left| V\right| \right) {n}^{o\left( {k/\log k}\right) }$ ,where $k$ is the treewidth of the primal graph and $f$ is an arbitrary computable function.

定理6.6（[52]）。设$\mathcal{G}$为树宽无界的图类。在ETH假设下，不存在能在$f\left( \left| V\right| \right) {n}^{o\left( {k/\log k}\right) }$时间内求解$\operatorname{CSP}\left( \mathcal{G}\right)$所有实例$I = \left( {V,D,C}\right)$的算法，其中$k$是原始图的树宽，$f$为任意可计算函数。

The formulation of Theorem 6.6 was originally chosen in a way to be analogous to the formulation of Theorem 5.2. However, it later turned out that it is possible to state it in a slightly more robust and expressive way that shows the precise complexity of individual primal graphs.

定理6.6的表述最初是为与定理5.2形成类比而设计。但后续研究表明，可以采用更具鲁棒性和表现力的方式重新表述，从而精确揭示单个原始图的复杂度特征。

THEOREM 6.7 ([25]). Assuming the ETH, there exists a universal constant $\alpha  > 0$ such that for any fixed primal graph $G$ with treewidth $k \geq  2$ ,there is no algorithm deciding the binary CSP instances $I =$ (V,D,C)whose primal graph is $G$ in time $O\left( {\left| D\right| }^{\alpha  \cdot  k/\log k}\right)$ .

定理6.7（[25]）。在ETH假设下，存在通用常数$\alpha  > 0$，使得对于任何固定树宽为$k \geq  2$的原始图$G$，不存在能在$O\left( {\left| D\right| }^{\alpha  \cdot  k/\log k}\right)$时间内判定原始图为$G$的二元CSP实例$I =$(V,D,C)的算法。

## 7 THE STRONG EXPONENTIAL-TIME HYPOTHESIS

## 7 强指数时间假设

Despite the usefulness of the ETH, there are complexity lower bounds that seem to be beyond the reach of what can be proved as a consequence of this hypothesis. Impagliazzo, Paturi, and Zane [44] proposed an even stronger assumption on the complexity of NP-hard problems: the so-called Strong Exponential-Time Hypothesis (SETH). Using the notation introduced at the beginning of Section 6, the SETH assumes that $\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{k \rightarrow  \infty }}{s}_{k} = 1$ . The following consequence of the SETH is a convenient formulation that can be used as a starting point for lower bounds on other problems:

尽管ETH极具实用性，但有些复杂性下界似乎超出了该假设的推导能力。Impagliazzo、Paturi和Zane[44]提出了关于NP难问题复杂性的更强假设——强指数时间假设（SETH）。沿用第6节开篇的符号体系，SETH假定$\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{k \rightarrow  \infty }}{s}_{k} = 1$。该假设的以下推论可作为其他问题下界研究的起点：

Hypothesis 3 (Consequence of the SETH, Impagliazzo, Pa-TURI,AND ZANE [44]). SAT with $n$ variables and $m$ clauses cannot be solved in time ${\left( 2 - \epsilon \right) }^{n} \cdot  {m}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ for any $\epsilon  > 0$ .

假设3（SETH推论，Impagliazzo、Paturi与Zane[44]）。对于含$n$个变量和$m$个子句的SAT问题，不存在对任意$\epsilon  > 0$能在${\left( 2 - \epsilon \right) }^{n} \cdot  {m}^{O\left( 1\right) }$时间内求解的算法。

Intuitively, Hypothesis 3 states that there is no better algorithm for SAT than the brute force search of trying each of the ${2}^{n}$ possible assignments. Note that here SAT is the satisfiability problem with unbounded clause length. For fixed clause length, algorithms better than ${2}^{n}$ are known: for example,the best known algorithms for 3SAT and 4SAT have running times ${1.308}^{n}$ and ${1.469}^{n}$ ,respectively [42]. The SETH states that the base of the exponent has to get closer and closer to 1 as the clause length increases, and it is not possible to have an algorithm with base $2 - \epsilon$ that works for arbitrary large clause length.

直观而言，假设3断言SAT算法的最优解就是暴力枚举${2}^{n}$种可能赋值。需注意此处SAT指子句长度无界的可满足性问题。对于固定子句长度，已知存在优于${2}^{n}$的算法：例如3SAT和4SAT的最佳算法时间复杂度分别为${1.308}^{n}$和${1.469}^{n}$[42]。SETH指出随着子句长度增加，指数底数必须无限趋近于1，且不存在适用于任意长子句的、底数为$2 - \epsilon$的通用算法。

It is important to note that there is no known analogue of the Sparsification Lemma for the SETH. That is, we cannot assume that the hard instances stipulated by Hypothesis 3 have only a linear number of clauses: for all we know, the number of clauses can be exponential in the number $n$ of variables. This severely limits the applicability of lower bounds based on the SETH as any reduction from the SAT instance would create instances whose sizes are potentially exponentially large in $n$ . Nevertheless,the SETH has found applications in parameterized complexity, for example, giving tight lower bounds on how the running time has to depend on treewidth $\left\lbrack  {{15},{27},{28},{30},{33},{45},{46},{51}}\right\rbrack$ .

需要特别指出的是，目前尚未发现适用于SETH（强指数时间假设）的稀疏化引理类似物。这意味着我们不能假设假设3所规定的困难实例仅包含线性数量的子句：就我们所知，子句数量可能随变量数$n$呈指数级增长。这严重限制了基于SETH的下界应用范围，因为从SAT实例的任何归约都可能产生规模随$n$指数级膨胀的实例。尽管如此，SETH在参数化复杂度领域仍有重要应用，例如为树宽$\left\lbrack  {{15},{27},{28},{30},{33},{45},{46},{51}}\right\rbrack$相关的时间复杂度下界提供了紧约束。

An important parameterized problem for which the SETH gives a very tight lower bound is DomINATING SET. The closed neighborhood $N\left\lbrack  v\right\rbrack   = N\left( v\right)  \cup  \{ v\}$ of a vertex $v$ consists of the vertex itself and its neighbors. A dominating set $S$ is a set of vertices that contains a vertex from the closed neighborhood of every vertex, in other words, every vertex is either selected or has a selected neighbor. In the Dominating Set problem,given a graph $G$ and an integer $k$ , the task is to find a dominating set $S$ of size at most $k$ . If $G$ is an $n$ -vertex graph,then the trivial brute force algorithm enumerates the $O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$ subsets of size at most $k$ and needs $O\left( {n}^{2}\right)$ time for each of them to check if they form a solution. This results in a $O\left( {n}^{k + 2}\right)$ time algorithm,which can be improved to ${n}^{k + o\left( 1\right) }\left\lbrack  {34}\right\rbrack$ . The following lower bound shows that any small constant improvement beyond $k$ in the exponent would violate the SETH.

SETH能给出极紧下界的一个重要参数化问题是支配集问题。顶点$v$的闭邻域$N\left\lbrack  v\right\rbrack   = N\left( v\right)  \cup  \{ v\}$包含该顶点及其所有邻居。支配集$S$是指满足每个顶点的闭邻域中至少包含一个该集合成员的顶点集，换言之，每个顶点要么被选中，要么与某个被选中的顶点相邻。在支配集问题中，给定图$G$和整数$k$，目标是找到规模不超过$k$的支配集$S$。若$G$是有$n$个顶点的图，暴力算法会枚举所有不超过$k$规模的$O\left( {n}^{k}\right)$个子集，每个子集验证需$O\left( {n}^{2}\right)$时间，最终得到$O\left( {n}^{k + 2}\right)$时间复杂度算法，该结果可优化至${n}^{k + o\left( 1\right) }\left\lbrack  {34}\right\rbrack$。下述下界表明，若指数部分在$k$基础上获得任何微小常数改进，都将违背SETH。

THEOREM 7.1 (PATRASCU AND WILLIAMS [56]). If there is an integer $k \geq  3$ and a real number $\epsilon  > 0$ such that $k$ -DOMINATING SET can be solved in time $O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$ on $n$ -vertex graphs,then the SETH is false.

定理7.1（帕特拉斯库与威廉姆斯[56]）。若存在整数$k \geq  3$和实数$\epsilon  > 0$，使得$k$-支配集问题能在$n$顶点图上以$O\left( {n}^{k - \epsilon }\right)$时间求解，则SETH不成立。

作为演示，我们展示定理7.1如何帮助强化定理6.5的紧密度。我们不仅排除了${\left| D\right| }^{o\left( k\right) }$时间复杂度，更排除了定义域规模指数部分在$k$之上的任何潜在改进，从而更接近定理4.2的上界。

Therefore,we obtain an algorithm for solving $t$ -Dominating SET in time $O\left( {n}^{t - \epsilon }\right)$ . By Theorem 7.1, this violates SETH.

所构造实例${I}^{\prime }$的规模可宽松地界定为小于$O\left( {n}^{t - \epsilon }\right)$的$O\left( {n}^{{2g} + 1}\right)$。上述规约过程的时间复杂度与${I}^{\prime }$规模呈线性关系，因此规约本身的运行时间由采用假定算法求解${I}^{\prime }$的时间主导。由此，我们得到在$O\left( {n}^{t - \epsilon }\right)$时间内求解$t$-支配集问题的算法。根据定理7.1，这与SETH相矛盾。

In recent years, the SETH has been successfully used to give lower bounds for polynomial-time solvable problems, for example,by showing that the textbook $O\left( {n}^{2}\right)$ dynamic programming algorithm for EDIT DISTANCE cannot be significantly improved: it cannot be solved in time $O\left( {n}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$ for any $\epsilon  > 0$ ,unless the SETH fails $\left\lbrack  {{12},{19}}\right\rbrack$ . Many other tight results of this form can be found in the recent literature under the name "fine-grained complexity" $\left\lbrack  {1,3,5,{12},{17} - {20},{56},{58},{62}}\right\rbrack$ .

近年来，强指数时间假说（SETH）已成功用于为多项式时间可解问题提供下界证明，例如通过表明教科书式$O\left( {n}^{2}\right)$动态规划算法求解编辑距离（EDIT DISTANCE）时无法显著改进：除非SETH不成立$\left\lbrack  {{12},{19}}\right\rbrack$，否则该问题无法在$O\left( {n}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$时间内解决（对于任意$\epsilon  > 0$）。此类精确结论的许多其他范例可见于近年文献中称为"细粒度复杂性"$\left\lbrack  {1,3,5,{12},{17} - {20},{56},{58},{62}}\right\rbrack$的研究领域。

## 8 OTHER CONJECTURES

## 8 其他猜想

We finish the overview of lower bound techniques with a few other complexity conjectures that have appeared recently in the literature. This section does not contain any strong results or nontrivial reductions; the goal is to present assumptions that have direct consequences when translated into the language of database theory and CSP. The aim is to raise awareness of the existence of these conjectures, which may be the starting point of future research.

我们将以近期文献中出现的若干其他复杂性猜想作为下界技术概述的收尾。本节不包含任何强结论或非平凡归约，旨在介绍那些转化为数据库理论和CSP语言后具有直接影响的假设，以提高学界对这些猜想存在的认知——它们可能成为未来研究的起点。

The $k$ -clique conjecture. Matrix multiplication techniques can be used to detect if a graph contains a triangle: if $A$ is the adjacency matrix of $G$ ,then $G$ contains a triangle if and only if ${A}^{3}$ has a nonzero value on the diagonal. Therefore, if we have an algorithm for multiplying two $n \times  n$ matrices in time $O\left( {n}^{\omega }\right)$ for some $\omega$ (the current best known algorithm has $\omega  < {2.3729}\left\lbrack  6\right\rbrack$ ),then we can detect in time $O\left( {n}^{3}\right)$ if an $n$ -vertex graph contains a triangle. Ne-setril and Poljak [53] showed in 1985 that this can be generalized further for detecting a clique of size $k$ in time $O\left( {n}^{{\omega k}/3}\right)$ (if $k$ is divisible by 3). As no significant improvement over this approach appeared in the past 35 years, one can conjecture that there is no $O\left( {n}^{\left( {\omega  - \epsilon }\right) k/3 + c}\right)$ time algorithm for $k$ -CLIQUE for any $\epsilon ,c > 0$ . Abboud, Backurs, and Vassilevska Williams [2] used this conjecture to give evidence that Valiant’s $O\left( {n}^{\omega }\right)$ time parsing algorithm [60] from 1975 is optimal.

$k$-团猜想。矩阵乘法技术可用于检测图中是否存在三角形：若$A$是$G$的邻接矩阵，则$G$包含三角形当且仅当${A}^{3}$对角线上存在非零值。因此，若我们能在$O\left( {n}^{\omega }\right)$时间内（当前最佳算法为$\omega  < {2.3729}\left\lbrack  6\right\rbrack$）完成两个$n \times  n$阶矩阵乘法，就能在$O\left( {n}^{3}\right)$时间内检测$n$阶图是否包含三角形。Ne-setril与Poljak[53]于1985年证明该技术可推广至用$O\left( {n}^{{\omega k}/3}\right)$时间检测规模为$k$的团（当$k$能被3整除时）。由于过去35年该方法未见显著改进，可猜想对于任意$\epsilon ,c > 0$，不存在$O\left( {n}^{\left( {\omega  - \epsilon }\right) k/3 + c}\right)$时间算法求解$k$-团问题。Abboud、Backurs和Vassilevska Williams[2]利用该猜想证明Valiant于1975年提出的$O\left( {n}^{\omega }\right)$时间解析算法[60]具有最优性。

As discussed in Section 2.3, $k$ -CLIQUE on an $n$ -vertex graph can be represented as a CSP with $k$ variables, $\left( \begin{array}{l} k \\  2 \end{array}\right)$ constraints,and domain size $n$ . Therefore,the $k$ -clique conjecture further refines Theorem 6.4 by ruling out not only a ${\left| D\right| }^{o\left( \left| V\right| \right) }$ dependence in the running time,but also ${\left| D\right| }^{\left( {\omega  - \epsilon }\right) \left| V\right| /3 + c}$ for any $\epsilon ,c > 0$ .

如第2.3节所述，$n$阶图上的$k$-团问题可表示为具有$k$个变量、$\left( \begin{array}{l} k \\  2 \end{array}\right)$个约束且定义域大小为$n$的CSP。因此$k$-团猜想通过排除运行时间中不仅${\left| D\right| }^{o\left( \left| V\right| \right) }$依赖关系、还包括任意$\epsilon ,c > 0$的${\left| D\right| }^{\left( {\omega  - \epsilon }\right) \left| V\right| /3 + c}$可能性，进一步细化了定理6.4。

The $d$ -uniform hyperclique conjecture. A hypergraph is $d$ - uniform if every hyperedge contains exactly $d$ vertices. The analog of a $k$ -clique in a $d$ -uniform hypergraph is a set $S$ of $k$ vertices such that each of the $\left( \begin{array}{l} k \\  d \end{array}\right)$ possible hyperedges are present in $S$ . Somewhat surprisingly, matrix multiplication techniques seem to speed up the search for $k$ -cliques only for $d = 2$ (ordinary graphs). For any fixed $d \geq  3$ ,nothing substantially better is known than trying every set of size $k$ . This suggests the conjecture that there is no $O\left( {n}^{\left( {1 - \epsilon }\right) k + c}\right)$ time algorithm for detecting $k$ -cliques in $d$ -uniform hypergraphs for any fixed $d \geq  2$ and $\epsilon ,c > 0$ [50]. We can again translate this conjecture into the language of CSPs: we can show that even if the arity of every constraint is at most 3 , there is no $f\left( \left| V\right| \right)  \cdot  {\left| D\right| }^{\left( {1 - \epsilon }\right) \left| V\right|  + c} \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ algorithm for CSP for any $\epsilon ,c > 0$ and computable function $f$ . Therefore,we get very tight lower bounds showing that essentially the brute force search of all assignments cannot be avoided. The $d$ -uniform hyperclique conjecture was used to rule out the possibility of constant-delay enumeration algorithms $\left\lbrack  {{13},{16}}\right\rbrack$ .

关于$d$均匀超图的超团猜想。若超图中每条超边恰好包含$d$个顶点，则称其为$d$均匀超图。在$d$均匀超图中，$k$团（clique）的类比是指由$k$个顶点组成的集合$S$，其中所有$\left( \begin{array}{l} k \\  d \end{array}\right)$种可能的超边都存在于$S$中。令人惊讶的是，矩阵乘法技术似乎仅能加速$d = 2$（普通图）中$k$团的搜索。对于任何固定参数$d \geq  3$，目前所知的最佳方法仍是穷举所有大小为$k$的集合。这暗示了一个猜想：对于任何固定参数$d \geq  2$和$\epsilon ,c > 0$[50]，不存在$O\left( {n}^{\left( {1 - \epsilon }\right) k + c}\right)$时间复杂度的算法能检测$d$均匀超图中的$k$团。我们可将该猜想转化为约束满足问题（CSP）的表述：即使每个约束的元数（arity）最多为3，对于任意$\epsilon ,c > 0$和可计算函数$f$，也不存在$f\left( \left| V\right| \right)  \cdot  {\left| D\right| }^{\left( {1 - \epsilon }\right) \left| V\right|  + c} \cdot  {n}^{O\left( 1\right) }$时间复杂度的CSP求解算法。因此我们得到了非常紧的下界，证明本质上无法避免对所有赋值进行暴力搜索。该$d$均匀超团猜想被用于排除恒定延迟枚举算法$\left\lbrack  {{13},{16}}\right\rbrack$存在的可能性。

The triangle conjecture. In database query problems it is more relevant to express the running time in terms of the size of the database relations rather than the size of the domain. For example, given the query $Q = {R}_{1}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{3}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{3}\left( {{a}_{2},{a}_{3}}\right)$ whose primal graph is the triangle, matrix multiplication can be used to check in time $O\left( {d}^{\omega }\right)$ if the answer is empty,where $d$ is the size of the domain of the attributes. But what can we say about the running time expressed as a function $N$ of the maximum size of the relations? As we have seen in Section 3, the size of the solution is $O\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$ ,we can enumerate it in time $O\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$ ,and this is tight. However, this does not rule out the possibility that there are faster algorithms for deciding if the answer is empty. In particular, can this be solved in linear time? Note that this question is equivalent to asking for the best possible running time for detecting triangles, where the running time is now expressed as a function of the number $m$ of edges. The best known algorithm of this form detects the existence of a triangle in time $O\left( {m}^{{2\omega }/\left( {\omega  + 1}\right) }\right) \left\lbrack  7\right\rbrack$ and one can state as a conjecture (Strong Triangle Conjecture [4]) that this is indeed best possible.

三角形猜想。在数据库查询问题中，用数据库关系的大小而非域（domain）的大小来表示运行时间更为合理。例如给定查询$Q = {R}_{1}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{2}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{2}\left( {{a}_{1},{a}_{3}}\right)  \bowtie  {R}_{3}\left( {{a}_{2},{a}_{3}}\right)$（其原图是三角形），可利用矩阵乘法在$O\left( {d}^{\omega }\right)$时间内判断答案是否为空，其中$d$表示属性域的大小。但若以关系最大尺寸的函数$N$表示运行时间，我们能得出什么结论？如第3节所述，解的规模为$O\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$时，可在$O\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$时间内完成枚举且该结果是最优的。但这并不排除存在更快判断答案是否为空的算法，尤其是否能在线性时间内解决？该问题等价于寻求检测三角形的最佳时间复杂度（此时运行时间表示为边数$m$的函数）。目前最优算法能在$O\left( {m}^{{2\omega }/\left( {\omega  + 1}\right) }\right) \left\lbrack  7\right\rbrack$时间内检测三角形存在性，而强三角形猜想[4]认为这已是理论极限。

## 9 CONCLUSIONS

## 9 结论

We have seen a sequence of complexity lower bounds of various strengths. The results were based on assumptions with different levels of plausibility, going all the way from unconditional bounds, classic NP-completeness, to novel conjectures. The results also differ in the tightness of the lower bound: they can be only qualitative results (polynomial-time vs. NP-hard, FPT vs. W[1]-hard) or quantitative lower bounds showing the optimality of current algorithms to various levels of tightness.

我们考察了一系列强度各异的计算复杂度下界。这些结果基于可信度不同的假设——从无条件界限、经典NP完全性理论到新提出的猜想。下界的紧致程度也各不相同：既有定性结论（多项式时间与NP困难性对比、FPT与W[1]困难性对比），也有定量下界以不同精度证明现有算法的最优性。

What can we learn from all these results? First, when aiming for a lower bound, we need to select a precise form of the bound. Ideally, we would like to have negative results that rule out the possibility of any improved algorithm compared to what is known currently, showing that they are already optimal. The meaning of "any improved algorithm" needs to be clarified precisely and the choice of this meaning can greatly influence the technical difficulty of the lower bound proof and the required assumptions. Second, we need to choose a suitable complexity assumption that we can base the result on. There are established conjectures, such as the ETH, that are widely used in different domains. But we should be ready to connect our database theory or CSP problem at hand with other, less celebrated open question as well. The general theme of conditional lower bounds is to transform a relatively specialized question to a more fundamental question that was studied from multiple directions. If we can formally establish that the main challenge in understanding our problem is really some other, more fundamental problem in disguise, then this means that spending further efforts on finding improved algorithms is not timely and we can assume for the time being that any algorithm matching the lower bound is optimal. This is a common situation in complexity theory: as it is often said, computational complexity progresses by reducing the number of questions, without increasing the number of answers.

从这些结果中我们能汲取什么？首先，在设定下界目标时，必须明确界定边界的具体形式。理想情况下，我们希望得出否定性结论，以排除任何优于现有已知算法的改进可能性，证明它们已达最优状态。其中"任何改进算法"的定义需精确阐明，这一概念的界定将显著影响下界证明的技术难度及所需假设条件。其次，需选择适当的复杂性假设作为理论根基。学界已确立诸如ETH等猜想，这些被广泛应用于不同领域。但我们还应做好准备，将手头的数据库理论或CSP问题与其他尚未广受关注的开放性问题建立关联。条件性下界的核心逻辑在于：将相对专门化的问题转化为已被多角度研究的更基础性问题。若能正式论证——理解本问题的核心挑战实则是另一更基础性问题的伪装形态，则意味着投入精力寻找改进算法为时尚早，可暂时认定达到该下界的任何算法即为最优解。这正是计算复杂性理论的常态：正如常言道，该领域的进展在于减少问题数量，而非增加答案数目。
