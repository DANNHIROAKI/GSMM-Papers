Estimating the output size of a query is a fundamental yet longstanding problem in database query processing. Traditional cardinality estimators used by database systems can routinely underestimate the true output size by orders of magnitude, which leads to significant system performance penalty. Recently, upper bounds have been proposed that are based on information inequalities and incorporate sizes and max-degrees from input relations, yet their main benefit is limited to cyclic queries, because they degenerate to rather trivial formulas on acyclic queries.

查询输出规模估算是数据库查询处理中基础且长期存在的难题。传统基数估计器常将真实输出规模低估数个数量级，导致系统性能严重下降。近年提出的基于信息不等式、结合输入关系表大小与最大度数的上界方法，其主要优势仅适用于环形查询——对于无环查询会退化为简单公式。

We introduce a significant extension of the upper bounds,by incorporating ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms of the degree sequences of join attributes. Our bounds are significantly lower than previously known bounds, even when applied to acyclic queries. These bounds are also based on information theory, they come with a matching query evaluation algorithm, are computable in exponential time in the query size, and are provably tight when all degrees are "simple".

我们通过引入连接属性度序列的${\ell }_{p}$-范数，显著扩展了上界方法。新边界值远低于已知结果，即使应用于无环查询亦然。这些基于信息理论的边界配有匹配的查询评估算法，可在查询规模指数级时间内计算，且当所有度数均为"简单"时可证明是紧致的。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Cardinality estimation is a central yet longstanding open problem in database systems. It allows query optimizers to select a query plan that minimizes the size of the intermediate results and therefore the necessary time and memory to compute the query. Yet traditional estimators present in virtually all database management systems routinely underestimate the true cardinality by orders of magnitude,which can lead to inefficient query plans [14, 17, 21].

基数估算是数据库系统中核心且长期未解的难题。准确的估算能使查询优化器选择最小化中间结果规模的执行计划，从而降低查询所需时间和内存。但现有数据库管理系统普遍采用的传统估算器常将真实基数低估数个数量级，导致低效查询计划[14,17,21]。

The past two decades introduced worst-case upper bounds on the output size of a join query. The first such bound is the AGM bound, which is a function of the sizes of the input tables [5]. It was further refined in the presence of functional dependencies [3, 13]. A more general bound is the PANDA bound, which is a function of both the sizes of the input tables and the max degrees of attributes in these tables [4]. These are powerful methods as they can be applied to arbitrary

However, these theoretical bounds have not had practical impact. One reason is that most queries in practice are acyclic queries, where upper bounds become trivial: they simply multiply the size of one relation with the maximum degrees of the joining relations. This is not new for a practitioner: standard estimators do the same, but use the average degrees instead of the max degrees. A second, related reason, is that they use essentially the same statistics as existing cardinality estimators: cardinalities and max or average degrees. There have been a few implementations under the name pessimistic cardinality estimators $\left\lbrack  {7,{15}}\right\rbrack$ ,but their empirical evaluation showed that they remain less accurate than other estimators $\left\lbrack  {8,{14}}\right\rbrack$ .

然而，这些理论界限并未产生实际影响。一个重要原因是实践中大多数查询都是无环查询，此时上界变得微不足道：它们仅将单个关系的大小与连接关系的最大度数相乘。这对实践者并不陌生：标准估算器采用相同方法，但使用平均度数而非最大度数。第二个相关原因是它们使用的统计量与现有基数估算器基本相同：基数及最大或平均度数。虽然已有若干以悲观基数估算器$\left\lbrack  {7,{15}}\right\rbrack$命名的实现，但实证评估表明其准确性仍低于其他估算器$\left\lbrack  {8,{14}}\right\rbrack$。

In this paper,we introduce new upper bounds on the query output size that use ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms of degree sequences. The degree sequence of a graph is the sorted list of the degrees of the nodes, ${d}_{1} \geq  {d}_{2} \geq  \cdots$ ,where ${d}_{1}$ the largest degree, ${d}_{2}$ the next largest,etc. The ${\ell }_{p}$ -norm of a degree sequence is defined as ${\left( {d}_{1}^{p} + {d}_{2}^{p} + \cdots \right) }^{1/p}$ . Our method computes an upper bound in terms of ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms of the degree sequences of the join columns; to the best of our knowledge, these are the first upper bounds that use arbitrary ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms on the relations. They strictly generalize previous bounds based on cardinalities and max-degrees [4],because the ${\ell }_{1}$ -norm of an attribute $R.A$ is the size $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{i}{d}_{i}$ of $R$ , and the ${\ell }_{\infty }$ -norm is the max degree ${d}_{1}$ of $A$ . However,our method can use any other norm,which leads to a much tighter upper bound. We follow the standard assumption in cardinality estimation, and assume that several ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms are pre-computed,and available during cardinality estimation.

本文提出基于${\ell }_{p}$范数（degree sequence norms）的查询输出规模新上界。图的度数序列是节点度数的排序列表${d}_{1} \geq  {d}_{2} \geq  \cdots$，其中${d}_{1}$表示最大度数，${d}_{2}$表示次大度数等。度数序列的${\ell }_{p}$范数定义为${\left( {d}_{1}^{p} + {d}_{2}^{p} + \cdots \right) }^{1/p}$。我们的方法通过连接列度数序列的${\ell }_{p}$范数计算上界——据我们所知，这是首个在关系上应用任意${\ell }_{p}$范数的上界。它们严格推广了基于基数和最大度数的先前界限[4]，因为属性$R.A$的${\ell }_{1}$范数是$R$的尺寸$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{i}{d}_{i}$，而${\ell }_{\infty }$范数是$A$的最大度数${d}_{1}$。但我们的方法可采用其他范数，从而得到更紧凑的上界。遵循基数估算的标准假设，我们假定多个${\ell }_{p}$范数已预计算并在估算期间可用。

Like the AGM [5] and the PANDA [4] bounds, our method relies on information inequalities. The computed bound is the optimal solution of a linear program, and can be computed in time exponential in the size of the query. Our method applies to arbitrary join queries (cyclic or not), but, unlike AGM and PANDA, it leads to completely new bounds even for acyclic queries, and uses new kinds of statistics, which makes it more likely for these theoretical bounds to have impact in practical scenarios.

与AGM[5]和PANDA[4]界限类似，我们的方法依赖信息不等式。计算所得界限是线性规划的最优解，可在查询规模指数级时间内求解。该方法适用于任意连接查询（无论是否含环），但与AGM和PANDA不同，即使对无环查询也能产生全新界限，并采用新型统计量，从而更可能使这些理论界限在实际场景中产生影响。

### 1.1 A Motivating Example

### 1.1 动机案例

The standard illustration for size upper bounds is the triangle query:

规模上界的经典示例是三角形查询：

$$
Q\left( {X,Y,Z}\right)  = R\left( {X,Y}\right)  \land  S\left( {Y,Z}\right)  \land  T\left( {Z,X}\right) , \tag{1}
$$

for which the AGM bound [5] (based on the ${\ell }_{1}$ -norm) is:

其AGM界限[5]（基于${\ell }_{1}$范数）为：

$$
\left| Q\right|  \leq  {\left( \left| R\right|  \cdot  \left| S\right|  \cdot  \left| T\right| \right) }^{1/2} \tag{2}
$$

and the PANDA bound [4] (based on the ${\ell }_{1}$ and ${\ell }_{\infty }$ norms) is:

而PANDA界限[4]（基于${\ell }_{1}$和${\ell }_{\infty }$范数）为：

$$
\left| Q\right|  \leq  \left| R\right|  \cdot  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{S}\left( Z \mid  Y\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty } \tag{3}
$$

where ${\deg }_{S}\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)  = \left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2},\ldots ,{d}_{m}}\right)$ is the degree sequence of $Y$ in $S$ ,more precisely ${d}_{i}$ is the number of occurrences of the $i$ ’th most frequent value $Y = y$ . If the ${\ell }_{2}$ - and ${\ell }_{3}$ -norms of the degree sequences are also available, then we can derive new upper bounds, for example:

其中${\deg }_{S}\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)  = \left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2},\ldots ,{d}_{m}}\right)$表示$S$中$Y$的度数序列，更准确地说${d}_{i}$是第$i$高频值$Y = y$的出现次数。若度数序列的${\ell }_{2}$范数和${\ell }_{3}$范数也可用，则可推导新上界，例如：

$$
\left| Q\right|  \leq  {\left( {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( Y \mid  X\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{2}^{2} \cdot  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{S}\left( Z \mid  Y\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{2}^{2} \cdot  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{T}\left( X \mid  Z\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{2}^{2}\right) }^{1/3} \tag{4}
$$

$$
\left| Q\right|  \leq  {\left( {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( Y \mid  X\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{3}^{3} \cdot  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{S}\left( Y \mid  Z\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{3}^{3} \cdot  {\left| T\right| }^{5}\right) }^{1/6} \tag{5}
$$

Assuming the ${\ell }_{1},{\ell }_{2},{\ell }_{3},{\ell }_{\infty }$ norms are precomputed,then all formulas above give us upper bounds on the query output size, and we can take the minimal one; which one is the smallest depends on the actual data.

假定${\ell }_{1},{\ell }_{2},{\ell }_{3},{\ell }_{\infty }$范数已预计算，则上述公式均能提供查询输出规模上界，我们可取最小值；具体哪个最小取决于实际数据。

### 1.2 Problem Definition

### 1.2 问题定义

Before we define the problem investigated in this paper, we introduce the class of queries and the statistics under consideration.

在定义本文研究问题前，我们先介绍所考虑的查询类别及统计量。

For a number $n$ ,let $\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\{ 1,2,\ldots ,n\}$ . We use upper case $X$ for variable names,and lower case $x$ for values of these variables. We use boldface for sets of variables,e.g., $X$ ,and of constants,e.g., $x$ .

对于一个数$n$，令$\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\{ 1,2,\ldots ,n\}$。我们使用大写字母$X$表示变量名，小写字母$x$表示这些变量的值。集合变量（如$X$）和常量集合（如$x$）采用粗体表示。

A full conjunctive (or join) query is defined by:

完全合取（或连接）查询定义为：

$$
Q\left( \mathbf{X}\right)  = \mathop{\bigwedge }\limits_{{j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }}{R}_{j}\left( {\mathbf{Y}}_{j}\right)  \tag{6}
$$

where ${Y}_{j}$ is the tuple of variables in ${R}_{j}$ and $X = \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }}{Y}_{j}$ is the set of $n\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| X\right|$ variables in the query $Q$ .

其中${Y}_{j}$是${R}_{j}$中的变量元组，$X = \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }}{Y}_{j}$表示查询$Q$中$n\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| X\right|$变量的集合。

For a relation $S$ and subsets $\mathbf{U},\mathbf{V}$ of its attributes,let ${\deg }_{S}\left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right)$ be the degree sequence of $\mathbf{U}$ in the projection ${\Pi }_{UV}S$ . Formally,let $G\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left( {{\Pi }_{U}\left( S\right) ,{\Pi }_{V}\left( S\right) ,E}\right)$ be the bipartite graph whose edges $E$ are all pairs $\left( {\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}}\right)  \in  {\Pi }_{UV}\left( S\right)$ . Then ${\deg }_{S}\left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2},\ldots ,{d}_{m}}\right)$ is the degree sequence of the $\mathbf{U}$ -nodes of the graph.

对于关系$S$及其属性子集$\mathbf{U},\mathbf{V}$，令${\deg }_{S}\left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right)$表示投影${\Pi }_{UV}S$中$\mathbf{U}$的度序列。形式化定义为：设$G\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left( {{\Pi }_{U}\left( S\right) ,{\Pi }_{V}\left( S\right) ,E}\right)$为二分图，其边$E$包含所有$\left( {\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}}\right)  \in  {\Pi }_{UV}\left( S\right)$对。则${\deg }_{S}\left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2},\ldots ,{d}_{m}}\right)$是该图中$\mathbf{U}$节点的度序列。

Fix $\mathbf{X}$ a set of variables. An abstract conditional,or simply conditional,is an expression of the form $\sigma  = \left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right)$ . We say that $\sigma$ is guarded by a relation $R\left( Y\right)$ if $\mathbf{U},\mathbf{V} \subseteq  \mathbf{Y}$ ; then we write ${\deg }_{R}\left( \sigma \right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{\deg }_{R}\left( {V \mid  U}\right)$ . An abstract statistics is a pair $\tau  = \left( {\sigma ,p}\right)$ ,where $p \in  (0,\infty \rbrack$ . If $B \geq  1$ is a real number,then we call the pair $\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$ a concrete statistics,and call $\left( {\tau ,b}\right)$ ,where $b\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\log B$ ,a concrete log-statistics. If $R$ is a relation guarding $\sigma$ ,then we say that $R$ satisfies $\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$ if ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( \sigma \right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{p} \leq  B$ . When $p = 1$ then the statistics is a cardinality assertion on $\left| {{\Pi }_{UV}\left( R\right) }\right|$ ,and when $p = \infty$ then it is an assertion on the maximum degree. We write $\sum  = \left\{  {{\tau }_{1},\ldots ,{\tau }_{s}}\right\}$ for a set of abstract statistics, and $\mathbf{B} = \left\{  {{B}_{1},\ldots ,{B}_{s}}\right\}$ for an associated set of real numbers; thus,every pair $\left( {{\tau }_{i},{B}_{i}}\right)$ is a concrete statistics. We will call the pair $\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$ a set of (concrete) statistics,and call $\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{b}}\right)$ ,where ${b}_{i}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\log {B}_{i}$ , a set of concrete log-statistics. We say that $\sum$ is guarded by a relational schema $\mathbf{R} = \left( {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}}\right)$ if every ${\tau }_{i} \in  \sum$ has a guard ${R}_{{j}_{i}}$ ,and we say that a database instance $D = \left( {{R}_{1}^{D},\ldots ,{R}_{m}^{D}}\right)$ satisfies the statistics $\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$ ,denoted by $\mathbf{D} \vDash  \left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$ ,if ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{{R}_{{j}_{i}}^{D}}\left( \sigma \right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{{p}_{i}} \leq  {B}_{{\tau }_{i}}$ for all ${\tau }_{i} = \left( {{\sigma }_{i},{p}_{i}}\right)  \in  \sum$ ,where ${R}_{{j}_{i}}$ is the guard of ${\sigma }_{i}$ . We can now state the problem investigated in this paper:

固定$\mathbf{X}$一组变量。抽象条件式（或简称条件式）是形如$\sigma  = \left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right)$的表达式。若$\mathbf{U},\mathbf{V} \subseteq  \mathbf{Y}$，则称$\sigma$由关系$R\left( Y\right)$守护，此时记作${\deg }_{R}\left( \sigma \right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{\deg }_{R}\left( {V \mid  U}\right)$。抽象统计量是二元组$\tau  = \left( {\sigma ,p}\right)$，其中$p \in  (0,\infty \rbrack$。若$B \geq  1$为实数，则称二元组$\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$为具体统计量，而将$\left( {\tau ,b}\right)$（其中$b\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\log B$）称为具体对数统计量。当$R$是守护$\sigma$的关系时，若${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( \sigma \right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{p} \leq  B$，则称$R$满足$\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$。当$p = 1$时，该统计量表示对$\left| {{\Pi }_{UV}\left( R\right) }\right|$的基数断言；当$p = \infty$时，则为对最大度的断言。用$\sum  = \left\{  {{\tau }_{1},\ldots ,{\tau }_{s}}\right\}$表示抽象统计量集合，$\mathbf{B} = \left\{  {{B}_{1},\ldots ,{B}_{s}}\right\}$表示关联的实数集合，因而每个二元组$\left( {{\tau }_{i},{B}_{i}}\right)$都是具体统计量。称二元组$\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$为（具体）统计量集合，而将$\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{b}}\right)$（其中${b}_{i}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\log {B}_{i}$）称为具体对数统计量集合。若每个${\tau }_{i} \in  \sum$都有守护关系${R}_{{j}_{i}}$，则称$\sum$由关系模式$\mathbf{R} = \left( {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}}\right)$守护；若数据库实例$D = \left( {{R}_{1}^{D},\ldots ,{R}_{m}^{D}}\right)$对所有${\tau }_{i} = \left( {{\sigma }_{i},{p}_{i}}\right)  \in  \sum$（其中${R}_{{j}_{i}}$是${\sigma }_{i}$的守护关系）满足${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{{R}_{{j}_{i}}^{D}}\left( \sigma \right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{{p}_{i}} \leq  {B}_{{\tau }_{i}}$，则称该实例满足统计量$\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$，记作$\mathbf{D} \vDash  \left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$。至此可阐述本文研究的问题：

Problem 1. Given a join query $Q$ and a set of statistics $\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$ guarded by the (schema of the) query $Q$ ,find a bound $U \in  \mathbb{R}$ such that for all database instances $D$ ,if $D \vDash  \left( {\sum ,B}\right)$ ,then $\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|  \leq  U$ .

问题1. 给定一个连接查询$Q$和一组由查询$Q$模式约束的统计量$\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$，寻找一个边界值$U \in  \mathbb{R}$，使得对于所有数据库实例$D$，若满足$D \vDash  \left( {\sum ,B}\right)$，则$\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|  \leq  U$。

The bound $U$ is tight,if there exists a database instance $D$ such that $D \vDash  \left( {\sum ,B}\right)$ and $U =$ $O\left( \left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right| \right)$ .

当存在数据库实例$D$使得$D \vDash  \left( {\sum ,B}\right)$且$U =$$O\left( \left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right| \right)$时，称边界值$U$是紧致的。

### 1.3 Main Results

### 1.3 主要研究成果

We solve Problem 1 for arbitrary join queries $Q$ ,databases $D$ with relations of arbitrary arities, and statistics $\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$ consisting of arbitrary ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms of degree sequences. We make the following contributions.

我们针对任意连接查询$Q$、包含任意元数关系的数据库$D$，以及由任意度序列${\ell }_{p}$-范数构成的统计量$\left( {\sum ,\mathbf{B}}\right)$，解决了问题1。主要贡献如下：

Contribution 1: ${\ell }_{p}$ Bounds on Query Output Size. Our key observation is that the concrete statistics ${\left| \left| \deg \left( \mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}\right) \right| \right| }_{p} \leq  B$ implies the following inequality in information theory:

贡献1：${\ell }_{p}$查询输出规模的边界。关键发现是具体统计量${\left| \left| \deg \left( \mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}\right) \right| \right| }_{p} \leq  B$隐含了以下信息论不等式：

$$
\frac{1}{p}h\left( \mathbf{U}\right)  + h\left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right)  \leq  \log B \tag{7}
$$

The Degree Sequence Bound (DSB) [9] is a tight upper bound of a query $Q$ in terms of the degree sequences of its join attributes. ### 2.1 Cardinality Estimation

### 2.1 基数估计

Our main intended application of Theorem 1.1 is for pessimistic cardinality estimation: given a query and statistics on the database, compute an upper bound on the query output size. A bound is good if it is as small as possible, i.e. as close as possible to the true output size. We follow the common assumption in cardinality estimation that the statistics are precomputed ${}^{1}$ and available at estimation time. For example the system may have precomputed the ${\ell }_{2},{\ell }_{5},{\ell }_{\infty }$ -norms of ${\deg }_{R}\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)$ and the ${\ell }_{1},{\ell }_{10}$ -norms of ${\deg }_{S}\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)$ . We give several examples of upper bounds of the from (9) that improve significantly previously known bounds. For presentation purposes we describe all bounds in this section using (9). A system would instead rely on (12), i.e. it will compute the numerical value of the upper bound by optimizing a linear program, as we explain in Sec. 5. To reduce clutter, in this section we abbreviate $\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$ with $\left| Q\right|$ ,and drop the superscript $D$ from an instance ${R}^{D}$ when no confusion arises.

定理1.1的主要应用是悲观基数估计：给定查询及数据库统计信息，计算查询输出大小的上界。理想的上界应尽可能小，即尽可能接近真实输出规模。我们遵循基数估计领域的通用假设，即统计量已预计算${}^{1}$并在估计时可用。例如系统可能已预计算${\deg }_{R}\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)$的${\ell }_{2},{\ell }_{5},{\ell }_{\infty }$-范数和${\deg }_{S}\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)$的${\ell }_{1},{\ell }_{10}$-范数。我们给出了若干基于公式(9)的上界实例，这些上界较现有已知边界有显著提升。为便于说明，本节所有边界均采用(9)表示，实际系统将依赖公式(12)，即通过优化线性规划计算上界数值，如第5节所述。为简化表述，本节将$\left| {Q\left( D\right) }\right|$缩写为$\left| Q\right|$，并在无歧义时省略实例${R}^{D}$的上标$D$。

Our framework derives several new upper bounds,by using ${\ell }_{p}$ -statistics other than ${\ell }_{1}$ and ${\ell }_{\infty }$ . We start with the simplest:

我们的框架通过使用${\ell }_{1}$和${\ell }_{\infty }$之外的${\ell }_{p}$-统计量，推导出若干新上界。首先展示最简单的：

$$
\left| Q\right|  \leq  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( X \mid  Y\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{2} \cdot  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{S}\left( Z \mid  Y\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{2} \tag{18}
$$

The reader may notice that this inequality is Cauchy-Schwartz, but, in the framework of Th. 1.1, it follows from a Shannon inequality:

读者可能注意到该不等式符合柯西-施瓦茨形式，但在定理1.1框架下，它源自香农不等式：

$$
\frac{1}{2}\left( {h\left( Y\right)  + {2h}\left( {X \mid  Y}\right) }\right)  + \frac{1}{2}\left( {h\left( Y\right)  + {2h}\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right) }\right)  \geq  h\left( {XYZ}\right) 
$$

The inequality can be simplified to $h\left( Y\right)  + h\left( {X \mid  Y}\right)  + h\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)  \geq  h\left( {XYZ}\right)$ ,which holds because $h\left( Y\right)  + h\left( {X \mid  Y}\right)  = h\left( {XY}\right) ,h\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)  \geq  h\left( {Z \mid  {XY}}\right)$ ,and $h\left( {XY}\right)  + h\left( {Z \mid  {XY}}\right)  = h\left( {XYZ}\right)$ ; we review Shannon inequalities in Sec. 3. Depending on the data, (18) can be asymptotically better than (17). A simple example where this happens is when $Q$ is a self-join,i.e. $R\left( {X,Y}\right)  \land  R\left( {Z,Y}\right)$ . Then,the two degree sequences are equal, ${\deg }_{R}\left( {X \mid  Y}\right)  = {\deg }_{R}\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)$ ,and (18) becomes an equality,because $\left| Q\right|  = \left| \right| {\deg }_{R}\left( {X \mid  Y}\right) {\left| \right| }_{2}^{2}$ . Thus, (18) is exactly $\left| Q\right|$ ,while (17) continues to be an over approximation of $\left| Q\right|$ ,and can be asymptotically worse (see Appendix B.1).

该不等式可简化为$h\left( Y\right)  + h\left( {X \mid  Y}\right)  + h\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)  \geq  h\left( {XYZ}\right)$，其成立的原因是$h\left( Y\right)  + h\left( {X \mid  Y}\right)  = h\left( {XY}\right) ,h\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)  \geq  h\left( {Z \mid  {XY}}\right)$且$h\left( {XY}\right)  + h\left( {Z \mid  {XY}}\right)  = h\left( {XYZ}\right)$；我们将在第3节回顾香农不等式。根据数据不同，(18)式可能渐进优于(17)式。当$Q$是自连接（即$R\left( {X,Y}\right)  \land  R\left( {Z,Y}\right)$）时即为简单示例，此时两个度序列相等${\deg }_{R}\left( {X \mid  Y}\right)  = {\deg }_{R}\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)$，(18)式转化为等式因为$\left| Q\right|  = \left| \right| {\deg }_{R}\left( {X \mid  Y}\right) {\left| \right| }_{2}^{2}$。因此，(18)式精确等于$\left| Q\right|$，而(17)式仍是对$\left| Q\right|$的高估近似，且可能渐进更差（见附录B.1）。

A more sophisticated inequality for the join query is the following,which holds for all $p,q \geq  0$ s.t. $\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} \leq  1$

针对连接查询更复杂的不等式如下所示，该式对所有满足$\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} \leq  1$的$p,q \geq  0$成立

$$
\left| Q\right|  \leq  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( X \mid  Y\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{p}^{p} \cdot  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{S}\left( Z \mid  Y\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{q}^{\frac{q}{p\left( {q - 1}\right) }}{\left| S\right| }^{1 - \frac{q}{p\left( {q - 1}\right) }} \tag{19}
$$

Depending on the concrete statistics on the data, this new bound can be much better than both (17) and (18). We prove this bound in Appendix B.1, where we also use this bound to study the connection between our ${\ell }_{p}$ -bounds on the Degree Sequence Bound in [9].

根据具体数据统计特性，这个新边界可能远优于(17)和(18)式。我们在附录B.1中证明该边界，并借此研究[9]中度序列边界与${\ell }_{p}$边界的关系。

The new bounds (18)-(19) are just two examples, and other inequalities exist. In the full version [2], we provide some empirical evidence showing that, even for a single join, these new formulas indeed give better bounds on real data.

新边界(18)-(19)仅是两例，还存在其他不等式。完整版[2]中我们提供的实证表明，即使对于单次连接，这些新公式在实际数据中确实能给出更优边界。

该边界对所有$p \geq  2$成立，源于如下香农不等式：

$$
 + \left( {h\left( {X}_{n - 1}\right)  + {ph}\left( {{X}_{n} \mid  {X}_{n - 1}}\right) }\right)  \geq  {ph}\left( {{X}_{1}\ldots {X}_{n}}\right)  \tag{20}
$$

特别地，上述不等式成立是因为它是以下基本香农不等式的和：

$$

$$

$$

$$

$$
h\left( {{X}_{1} \mid  {X}_{2}}\right)  + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 2,n - 2}}h\left( {X}_{i}\right)  + h\left( {X}_{n - 1}\right)  + h\left( {{X}_{n} \mid  {X}_{n - 1}}\right)  \geq  h\left( {{X}_{1}\ldots {X}_{n}}\right) 
$$

路径查询还存在其他边界[2]。令人惊讶的是，当我们在完整版[2]中进行实证评估时，发现系统使用了$p \in  \{ 1,2,\ldots ,{29},\infty \}$范围内的多种${\ell }_{p}$范数。这说明基数估计需要丰富多样的${\ell }_{p}$范数统计量。这也引出一个理论问题：是否对于每个$p$，都存在某个查询/数据库的最优边界需要使用${\ell }_{p}$范数？下文我们给出肯定回答。

ExaMPLE 2.3. For every $p$ ,there exists a query and a database instance where the ${\ell }_{p}$ -norm on degree sequences leads to the best upper bound. Consider the cycle query of length $p + 1$ :

示例2.3 对于每个$p$，都存在某个查询和数据库实例，其中度序列的${\ell }_{p}$范数能产生最佳上界。考虑长度为$p + 1$的环形查询：

$$
Q\left( {{X}_{0},\ldots ,{X}_{p}}\right)  = {R}_{0}\left( {{X}_{0},{X}_{1}}\right)  \land  \ldots  \land  {R}_{p - 1}\left( {{X}_{p - 1},{X}_{p}}\right)  \land  {R}_{p}\left( {{X}_{p},{X}_{0}}\right) 
$$

For every $q \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ ,the following is an upper bound (generalizing (4)):

对于每一个$q \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$，以下是一个上界（推广自(4)式）：

$$
\left| Q\right|  \leq  \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i = 0,p}}{\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{{R}_{i}}\left( {X}_{\left( {i + 1}\right) {\;\operatorname{mod}\;\left( {p + 1}\right) }} \mid  {X}_{i}\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{q}^{\frac{q}{q + 1}} \tag{21}
$$

To prove (21), we show the following Shannon inequality, where the arithmetic on the indices is taken modulo $p + 1$ ,i.e. $i + 1$ means $\left( {i + 1}\right) {\;\operatorname{mod}\;\left( {p + 1}\right) }$ etc:

为证明(21)式，我们展示以下香农不等式，其中指标运算采用模$p + 1$算法，即$i + 1$表示$\left( {i + 1}\right) {\;\operatorname{mod}\;\left( {p + 1}\right) }$，以此类推：

$$
\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0,p}}\left( {h\left( {X}_{i}\right)  + {qh}\left( {{X}_{i + 1} \mid  {X}_{i}}\right) }\right)  \geq  \left( {q + 1}\right) h\left( {{X}_{0}\ldots {X}_{p}}\right)  \tag{22}
$$

To prove the inequality,we proceed as follows. First,we observe that,for each $i = 0,p$ ,the following is a Shannon inequality:

为证明该不等式，我们按如下步骤进行。首先观察到，对于每个$i = 0,p$，以下香农不等式成立：

$$
h\left( {X}_{i}\right)  + h\left( {{X}_{i + 1} \mid  {X}_{i}}\right)  + \ldots  + h\left( {{X}_{i + q} \mid  {X}_{i + q - 1}}\right)  \geq  h\left( {{X}_{i}{X}_{i + 1}\ldots {X}_{i + q}}\right) 
$$

All indices are taken modulo $p + 1$ ,for example $i + q$ means $\left( {i + q}\right) {\;\operatorname{mod}\;\left( {p + 1}\right) }$ . Each inequality above can be easily checked. Next,we add up these $p + 1$ inequalities,and make two observations. First, the sum of their LHS is precisely the LHS of (22). Second, after adding up their RHS, we use the following Shannon inequality $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0,p}}h\left( {{X}_{i}\ldots {X}_{i + q}}\right)  \geq  \left( {q + 1}\right) h\left( {{X}_{0}{X}_{1}\ldots {X}_{p}}\right)$ (which holds because each variable ${X}_{k}$ occurs exactly $q + 1$ times on the left,hence this is a Shearer-type inequality). Together, these observations prove (22). We compare now the upper bound (21) to the AGM and PANDA bounds. To reduce the clutter we will assume that ${R}_{0} = {R}_{1} = \cdots  = {R}_{p}$ . Then the AGM bound and the PANDA bounds are:

所有指标均按模$p + 1$计算，例如$i + q$表示$\left( {i + q}\right) {\;\operatorname{mod}\;\left( {p + 1}\right) }$。上述每个不等式均可轻松验证。接着我们将这$p + 1$个不等式相加，并得到两个观察结果：第一，其左侧之和恰好等于(22)式左侧；第二，在累加右侧后，我们使用以下香农不等式$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0,p}}h\left( {{X}_{i}\ldots {X}_{i + q}}\right)  \geq  \left( {q + 1}\right) h\left( {{X}_{0}{X}_{1}\ldots {X}_{p}}\right)$（该式成立是因为每个变量${X}_{k}$在左侧恰好出现$q + 1$次，故属于Shearer型不等式）。综合这些观察即可证明(22)式。现在我们将上界(21)式与AGM界和PANDA界进行比较。为简化表述，假设${R}_{0} = {R}_{1} = \cdots  = {R}_{p}$，此时AGM界和PANDA界分别为：

$$
\left| Q\right|  \leq  {\left| R\right| }^{\frac{p + 1}{2}}\;\left| Q\right|  \leq  \left| R\right|  \cdot  {\left| \left| {\deg }_{R}\left( Y \mid  X\right) \right| \right| }_{\infty }^{p - 1} \tag{23}
$$

They follow from the following straightforward Shannon inequalities:

它们源自以下直接推导的香农不等式：

$$
h\left( {{X}_{0}{X}_{1}}\right)  + h\left( {{X}_{1}{X}_{2}}\right)  + \cdots  + h\left( {{X}_{p}{X}_{0}}\right)  \geq  {2h}\left( {{X}_{0}{X}_{1}{X}_{2}\ldots {X}_{p}}\right) 
$$

$$
h\left( {{X}_{0}{X}_{1}}\right)  + h\left( {{X}_{2} \mid  {X}_{1}}\right)  + \cdots  + h\left( {{X}_{p} \mid  {X}_{p - 1}}\right)  \geq  h\left( {{X}_{0}{X}_{1}{X}_{2}\ldots {X}_{p}}\right) 
$$

在附录B.2中我们证明：对于任意p，存在一个数据库实例，其中$q \mathrel{\text{:=}} p$的界(21)是通过所有度序列的${\ell }_{1},{\ell }_{2},\ldots ,{\ell }_{p},{\ell }_{\infty }$-范数统计所能获得的理论最优界。

### 2.2 Query Evaluation

### 2.2 查询评估

The second application is to query evaluation: we show that, if inequality (8) holds for all polyma-troids, then we can evaluate the query in time bounded by (9) times a polylogarithmic factor in the data and an exponential factor in the sum of the $p$ values of the statistics. Our algorithm generalizes the PANDA’s algorithm [4] from ${\ell }_{1}$ and ${\ell }_{\infty }$ norms to arbitrary norms. Recall that PANDA starts from an inequality of the form (8),where every ${p}_{i}$ is either 1 or $\infty$ ,and computes $Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$ in time $O\left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{i}{B}_{i}^{{w}_{i}}}\right)$ if the database satisfies $\left| {{\Pi }_{{U}_{i}{V}_{i}}\left( {R}_{{j}_{i}}\right) }\right|  \leq  {B}_{i}$ when ${p}_{i} = 1$ and ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{{R}_{{j}_{i}}}\left( {V}_{i} \mid  {U}_{i}\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty } \leq  {B}_{i}$ when ${p}_{i} = \infty$ .

第二个应用涉及查询评估：我们证明，若不等式(8)对所有多拟阵成立，则可在数据量的多对数倍与统计量$p$值之和的指数倍(即(9)式)的时间范围内完成查询评估。本算法将PANDA算法[4]从${\ell }_{1}$范数和${\ell }_{\infty }$范数推广至任意范数。需知PANDA算法始于形如(8)的不等式（其中每个${p}_{i}$取值非1即$\infty$），当数据库满足$\left| {{\Pi }_{{U}_{i}{V}_{i}}\left( {R}_{{j}_{i}}\right) }\right|  \leq  {B}_{i}$（若${p}_{i} = 1$）或${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{{R}_{{j}_{i}}}\left( {V}_{i} \mid  {U}_{i}\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty } \leq  {B}_{i}$（若${p}_{i} = \infty$）时，能在$O\left( {\mathop{\prod }\limits_{i}{B}_{i}^{{w}_{i}}}\right)$时间内计算出$Q\left( \mathbf{D}\right)$。

Our algorithm uses PANDA as a black box, as follows. It first partitions the relations on the join columns so that, within each partition, all degrees are within a factor of two, and each statistics defined by some ${\ell }_{p}$ -norm on the degree sequence of the join column can be expressed alternatively using only ${\ell }_{1}$ and ${\ell }_{\infty }$ . The original query becomes a union of queries,one per combination of parts of different relations. The algorithm then evaluates each of these queries using PANDA. We describe next the details of data partitioning and the reduction to PANDA.

本算法将PANDA作为黑盒调用，具体流程如下：首先按连接列对关系进行分区，确保每个分区内所有度数值相差不超过两倍，且连接列度序列上任意${\ell }_{p}$范数定义的统计量均可仅用${\ell }_{1}$和${\ell }_{\infty }$表示。原始查询由此转化为各关系分区组合的查询并集，随后使用PANDA逐一评估这些查询。下文将详述数据分区方法及向PANDA的归约过程。

Consider a relation $R$ with attributes $X$ ,and consider a concrete statistics $\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$ ,where $\tau  =$ $\left( {\left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right) ,p}\right)$ . We say that $R$ strongly satisfies $\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$ ,in notation $R{ \vDash  }_{s}\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$ ,if there exists a number $d > 0$ such that ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( V \mid  U\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty } \leq  d$ and $\left| {{\Pi }_{U}\left( R\right) }\right|  \leq  {B}^{p}/{d}^{p}$ . If $R{ \vDash  }_{s}\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$ then $R \vDash  \left( {\tau ,B}\right)$ because:

设关系$R$具有属性$X$，给定具体统计量$\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$（其中$\tau  =$$\left( {\left( {\mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}}\right) ,p}\right)$）。若存在数值$d > 0$使得${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( V \mid  U\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty } \leq  d$且$\left| {{\Pi }_{U}\left( R\right) }\right|  \leq  {B}^{p}/{d}^{p}$成立，则称$R$强满足$\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$，记作$R{ \vDash  }_{s}\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$。当$R{ \vDash  }_{s}\left( {\tau ,B}\right)$成立时，必有$R \vDash  \left( {\tau ,B}\right)$，原因如下：

$$
{\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( \mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{p}^{p} \leq  \left| {{\Pi }_{\mathbf{U}}\left( R\right) }\right|  \cdot  {\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( \mathbf{V} \mid  \mathbf{U}\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty }^{p} \leq  \frac{{B}^{p}}{{d}^{p}}{d}^{p} = {B}^{p} \tag{24}

## 3 BACKGROUND ON INFORMATION THEORY

## 3 信息论基础

Consider a finite probability space(D,P),where $P : D \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack  ,\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  D}}P\left( x\right)  = 1$ ,and denote by $X$ the random variable with outcomes in $D$ . The entropy of $X$ is:

设有限概率空间(D,P)，其中$P : D \rightarrow  \left\lbrack  {0,1}\right\rbrack  ,\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  D}}P\left( x\right)  = 1$，并用$X$表示取值在$D$的随机变量。$X$的熵定义为：

$$
H\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = } - \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{x \in  D}}P\left( x\right) \log P\left( x\right)  \tag{25}
$$

If $N\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| D\right|$ ,then $0 \leq  H\left( X\right)  \leq  \log N$ ,the equality $H\left( X\right)  = 0$ holds iff $X$ is deterministic,and $H\left( X\right)  = \log N$ holds iff $X$ is uniformly distributed. Given $n$ jointly distributed random variables $\mathbf{X} = \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{n}}\right\}$ ,we denote by $\mathbf{h} \in  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$ the following vector: ${h}_{\alpha }\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }H\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{\alpha }\right)$ for $\alpha  \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$ ,where

若$N\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| D\right|$，则$0 \leq  H\left( X\right)  \leq  \log N$，等式$H\left( X\right)  = 0$成立当且仅当$X$是确定性的，而$H\left( X\right)  = \log N$成立当且仅当$X$服从均匀分布。给定$n$个联合分布的随机变量$\mathbf{X} = \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{n}}\right\}$，我们用$\mathbf{h} \in  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$表示以下向量：对$\alpha  \subseteq  \left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack$有${h}_{\alpha }\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }H\left( {\mathbf{X}}_{\alpha }\right)$，其中

${X}_{\alpha }$ is the joint random variable ${\left( {X}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \alpha }$ ,and $H\left( {X}_{\alpha }\right)$ is its entropy; such a vector $\mathbf{h} \in  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$ is called entropic. We will blur the distinction between a vector in ${\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$ ,a vector in ${\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{X}}$ ,and a function ${2}^{X} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$ ,and write interchangeably ${\mathbf{h}}_{\alpha },{\mathbf{h}}_{{X}_{\alpha }}$ ,or $h\left( {X}_{\alpha }\right)$ . A polymatroid is a vector $\mathbf{h} \in  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$ that satisfies the following basic Shannon inequalities:

${X}_{\alpha }$ 是联合随机变量 ${\left( {X}_{i}\right) }_{i \in  \alpha }$，$H\left( {X}_{\alpha }\right)$ 为其熵；这样的向量 $\mathbf{h} \in  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$ 称为熵向量。我们将模糊处理 ${\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$ 中的向量、${\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{X}}$ 中的向量与函数 ${2}^{X} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }$ 之间的区别，交替使用 ${\mathbf{h}}_{\alpha },{\mathbf{h}}_{{X}_{\alpha }}$ 或 $h\left( {X}_{\alpha }\right)$ 表示。多拟阵是指满足以下基本香农不等式的向量 $\mathbf{h} \in  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$：

$$
h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0 \tag{26}
$$

$$
h\left( {\mathbf{U} \cup  \mathbf{V}}\right)  \geq  h\left( \mathbf{U}\right)  \tag{27}
$$

$$
h\left( \mathbf{U}\right)  + h\left( \mathbf{V}\right)  \geq  h\left( {\mathbf{U} \cup  \mathbf{V}}\right)  + h\left( {\mathbf{U} \cap  \mathbf{V}}\right)  \tag{28}
$$

The last two inequalities are called called monotonicity and submodularity respectively.

最后两个不等式分别称为单调性与子模性。

For any set $V \subseteq  \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{n}}\right\}$ ,the step function ${\mathbf{h}}^{V}$ is:

对于任意集合 $V \subseteq  \left\{  {{X}_{1},\ldots ,{X}_{n}}\right\}$，其阶跃函数 ${\mathbf{h}}^{V}$ 定义为：

$$
{h}^{\mathbf{V}}\left( \mathbf{U}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left\{  \begin{array}{ll} 1 & \text{ if }\mathbf{V} \cap  \mathbf{U} \neq  \varnothing \\  0 & \text{ otherwise } \end{array}\right.  \tag{29}
$$

There are ${2}^{n} - 1$ non-zero step functions (since ${\mathbf{h}}^{0} \equiv  0$ ). A normal polymatroid is a positive linear combination of step functions. When $\mathbf{V}$ is a singleton set, $\mathbf{V} = \left\{  {X}_{i}\right\}$ for some $i = 1,n$ ,then we call ${\mathbf{h}}^{{X}_{i}}$ a basic modular function. A modular function is a positive linear combination of ${\mathbf{h}}^{{X}_{1}},\ldots ,{\mathbf{h}}^{{X}_{n}}$ . The following notations are used in the literature: ${M}_{n}$ is the set of modular functions, ${N}_{n}$ is the set of normal polymatroids, ${\Gamma }_{n}^{ * }$ is the set of entropic vectors, ${\bar{\Gamma }}_{n}^{ * }$ is its topological closure,and ${\Gamma }_{n}$ is the set of polymatroids. It is known that ${M}_{n} \subset  {N}_{n} \subset  {\Gamma }_{n}^{ * } \subset  {\bar{\Gamma }}_{n}^{ * } \subset  {\Gamma }_{n} \subset  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{{2}^{\left\lbrack  n\right\rbrack  }}$ ,that ${M}_{n},{N}_{n},{\Gamma }_{n}$ are polyhedral cones, ${\bar{\Gamma }}_{n}^{ * }$ is a closed,convex cone,and ${\Gamma }_{n}^{ * }$ is not a cone. ${}^{2}$

## 5 COMPUTING THE BOUND

## 5 边界计算

In this section we prove Theorem 1.2. Recall that the main problem in our paper, problem 1, asks for an upper bound to the query's output, given a set of concrete statistics on the database. So far we have proven Theorem 1.1, which says that, for any valid information inequality of the form (8), we can infer some bound. The best bound is their minimum, over all valid inequalities (8), and depends on the concrete statistics of the database. In this section we describe how to compute the best bound, by using the dual of information inequalities.

## 7 CONCLUSIONS

## 7 结论

We have described a new upper bound on the size of the output of a multi-join query,using ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms of degree sequences. Our techniques are based on information inequalities, and extend prior results in $\left\lbrack  {3 - 5,{13}}\right\rbrack$ . This is complemented by a query evaluation algorithm whose runtime matches the size bound. The bound can be computed by optimizing a linear program whose size is exponential in the size of the query. This bound is tight in the case when all degree sequences are simple.

我们利用度序列的${\ell }_{p}$范数，提出了多连接查询输出规模的新上界。我们的技术基于信息不等式，并扩展了$\left\lbrack  {3 - 5,{13}}\right\rbrack$中的先前研究成果。与之配套的是一个运行时间匹配该规模界限的查询评估算法。该界限可通过优化一个规模随查询大小呈指数增长的线性规划问题来计算。当所有度序列均为简单序列时，这个界限是紧致的。

Our new bounds significantly extend the previously known upper bounds, especially for acyclic queries. We have also conducted some very preliminary experiments on real datasets in the full version [2], which showed significantly better upper bounds for acyclic queries than the AGM and PANDA bounds from prior work.

我们的新界限显著扩展了先前已知的上界，尤其针对无环查询。在完整版本[2]中，我们还对真实数据集进行了初步实验，结果显示无环查询的上界明显优于以往研究中的AGM和PANDA界限。

In future work,we will incorporate our ${\ell }_{p}$ -bounds into a cardinality estimation system.

在未来的工作中，我们将把${\ell }_{p}$界限整合到基数估计系统中。

## A EQUIVALENCE OF ${\ell }_{p}$ -NORMS AND DEGREE SEQUENCES

## 附录A ${\ell }_{p}$范数与度序列的等价性

The following is a standard result, establishing a 1-to-1 correspondence between a sequence of length $m$ and its first $m$ norms. We include it here for completeness.

以下标准结论建立了长度为$m$的序列与其前$m$个范数之间的一一对应关系。为求完整，我们在此列出。

LEMMA A.1. Denote by $S \subseteq  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{m}$ the set of sorted sequences ${d}_{1} \geq  {d}_{2} \geq  \cdots  \geq  {d}_{m} \geq  0$ . The mapping $\varphi  : S \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{m}$ defined by $\varphi \left( \mathbf{d}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left( {\parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{1},\parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{2}^{2},\ldots ,\parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{m}^{m}}\right)$ is injective.

引理A.1。用$S \subseteq  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{m}$表示已排序序列集合${d}_{1} \geq  {d}_{2} \geq  \cdots  \geq  {d}_{m} \geq  0$。由$\varphi \left( \mathbf{d}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left( {\parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{1},\parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{2}^{2},\ldots ,\parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{m}^{m}}\right)$定义的映射$\varphi  : S \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}_{ + }^{m}$是单射的。

In other words,having the full degree sequence ${d}_{1} \geq  {d}_{2} \geq  \cdots  \geq  {d}_{m}$ is equivalent to having the ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms for $p = 1,2,\ldots ,m$ .

换言之，拥有完整的度序列${d}_{1} \geq  {d}_{2} \geq  \cdots  \geq  {d}_{m}$等价于拥有${\ell }_{p}$-范数对于$p = 1,2,\ldots ,m$。

Proof. We make use of the elementary symmetric polynomials

$$

$$

$$

$$

$$
{e}_{2}\left( \mathbf{d}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{1 \leq  i < j \leq  m}}{d}_{i}{d}_{j}
$$

...

$$
{e}_{m}\left( \mathbf{d}\right)  = {d}_{1} \cdot  {d}_{2} \cdot  \ldots  \cdot  {d}_{m}
$$

$$

$$

$$

$$
{\mathbf{e}}_{0}\left( \mathbf{d}\right)  = 1
$$

$$
1 \cdot  {e}_{1}\left( \mathbf{d}\right)  = {e}_{0}\left( \mathbf{d}\right) \parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{1}^{1}
$$

$$
2 \cdot  {e}_{2}\left( \mathbf{d}\right)  = {e}_{1}\left( \mathbf{d}\right) \parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{1}^{1} - {e}_{0}\left( \mathbf{d}\right) \parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{2}^{2}

...

$$
m \cdot  {e}_{m}\left( \mathbf{d}\right)  = {e}_{m - 1}\left( \mathbf{d}\right) \parallel d{\parallel }_{1}^{1} - {e}_{m - 2}\left( \mathbf{d}\right) \parallel d{\parallel }_{2}^{2} + \cdots  + {\left( -1\right) }^{m - 1}{e}_{0}\left( \mathbf{d}\right) \parallel d{\parallel }_{m}^{m}

$$

$$

$$
{\deg }_{S}\left( {Z \mid  Y}\right)  = {b}_{1} \geq  {b}_{2} \geq  \cdots  \geq  {b}_{M}
$$

If the system has full access to both degree sequences, then the Degree-Sequence Bound (DSB) defined in [9] is the following quantity:

若系统能完整访问两个度序列，则文献[9]定义的度序列上界(DSB)为：

$$
{DSB}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1,M}}{a}_{i}{b}_{i} \tag{47}
$$

In general the degree sequences are too large to store, and the DSB bound needs to use compression [10], but for the purpose of our discussion we will assume that we know both degree sequences, and ${DSB}$ is given by the formula above. It is easy to check that $\left| Q\right|  \leq  {DSB}$ . Our bound (18) becomes:

通常度序列规模过大难以存储，DSB需采用压缩技术[10]，但为便于讨论，我们假设已知两个度序列，且${DSB}$由上述公式给出。易验证$\left| Q\right|  \leq  {DSB}$。本文上界(18)可表示为：

$$

$$

Thus,the DSB and the ${\ell }_{2}$ -bound above are the two sides of the Cauchy-Schwartz inequality; DSB is obviously the better one. DSB is also better than the PANDA bound (17), which in our notation is $\min \left( {{a}_{1}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{i}{b}_{i},{b}_{1}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{i}{a}_{i}}\right)$ (assuming ${a}_{1}$ and ${b}_{1}$ are the largest degrees). Can we compute a better ${\ell }_{p}$ -bound? We will show that (19) can improve over both (17),and (18),however,it remains strictly weaker than the DSB bound. This may be surprising, given the 1-1 correspondence between the statistics and the ${\ell }_{p}$ -bounds that we described in Appendix A. The mapping between a degree sequence of length $M$ and its ${\ell }_{1},{\ell }_{2},\ldots ,{\ell }_{M}$ -norms is 1-to-1,and,moreover,both bounds are tight: tightness of the DSB bound was proven in [9], while tightness of the polymatroid bound holds because both degrees are simple, and it follows from our discussion in Sec. 6. So, one expects that some ${\ell }_{p}$ -bounds will match the ${DSB}$ expression (47). However,this is not the case,for a rather subtle reason: it is because the set of databases to which these two bounds apply, differ. The 1-to-1 mapping from degrees to ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms is monotone in one direction,but not in the other. For example, consider the degree sequence $\mathbf{d} = \left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2}}\right)  = \left( {a + \varepsilon ,a - \varepsilon }\right)$ ,where $\parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{1} = {2a},\parallel \mathbf{d}{\parallel }_{2}^{2} = 2{a}^{2} + 2{\varepsilon }^{2}$ . We show next that the polymatroid bound that we can obtain from the ${\ell }_{p}$ -norms can be strictly worse than the DSB. 现在考虑$\{ 1,\infty \}$界，换言之我们仅掌握$\left| \right| {\deg }_{R}\left( {Y\left| {X)}\right| {|}_{1}}\right)$（即$\left| R\right|$）和${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( Y \mid  X\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty }$的统计量。我们证明式(23)中的PANDA界确为最优。事实上我们证明了一个更普遍的结论：当$\left| R\right|  \leq$$N,{\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}Y\left| X\right| \end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty } \leq  D$且$N,D$满足${D}^{2} \leq  N$时，循环查询的$\{ 1,\infty \}$界为$\left| Q\right|  \leq  N \cdot  {D}^{p - 1}$。在本例中$D = {N}^{\frac{1}{p + 1}}$成立，该结论表明$\{ 1,\infty \}$界即为${N}^{1 + \frac{p - 1}{p + 1}} = {N}^{\frac{2p}{p + 1}}$。为证明此结论，我们将引用定义5.1中的多拟阵上界与多拟阵下界。例2.3证明的香农不等式蕴含Log-U界${}_{{\Gamma }_{n}}\left( Q\right)  \leq  \log N + \left( {p - 1}\right) \log D$。根据定理6.1可得Log-U界${}_{{\Gamma }_{n}}\left( Q\right)  =$ Log-U界${}_{{N}_{n}}\left( Q\right)$，其中${N}_{n}$为正规多拟阵，而定理5.2表明$\operatorname{Log-U-Bound}{}_{{N}_{n}}\left( Q\right)  = \operatorname{Log-L-Bound}{}_{{N}_{n}}\left( Q\right)$。我们断言存在满足$\{ 1,\infty \}$统计量且$h\left( {{X}_{0}\ldots {X}_{p}}\right)  = \log N + \left( {p - 1}\right) \log D$的正规多拟阵：该断言蕴含$\log N + \left( {p - 1}\right) \log D \leq$ Log-L界${\mathrm{r}}_{n}\left( Q\right)$，由此证得$\{ 1,\infty \}$界为$N \cdot  {D}^{p - 1}$。为证明该断言，考虑如下多拟阵：

$$
h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0,\;\forall \mathbf{W} \neq  \varnothing ,h\left( \mathbf{W}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\log N + \left( {\left| \mathbf{W}\right|  - 2}\right) \log D
$$

Then $h$ satisfies the required statistics:

则$h$满足所需统计量：

$$
\forall i : h\left( {{X}_{i}{X}_{i + 1}}\right)  \leq  \log N\;h\left( {{X}_{i + 1} \mid  {X}_{i}}\right)  \leq  \log D
$$

and $h\left( {{X}_{0}{X}_{1}\ldots {X}_{p}}\right)  = \log N + \left( {p - 1}\right) \log D$ . It remains to observe that $h$ is a normal polymatroid, which follows by writing it as $\mathbf{h} = \left( {\log N - 2\log D}\right)  \cdot  {h}^{X} + \log D \cdot  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0,p}}{h}^{{X}_{i}}$ .

且$h\left( {{X}_{0}{X}_{1}\ldots {X}_{p}}\right)  = \log N + \left( {p - 1}\right) \log D$。最后只需注意到$h$作为正规多拟阵，可通过将其表示为$\mathbf{h} = \left( {\log N - 2\log D}\right)  \cdot  {h}^{X} + \log D \cdot  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0,p}}{h}^{{X}_{i}}$得证。

Finally,we prove that,if we have available all statistics ${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( Y \mid  X\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{q}$ for $q = 1,2,\ldots ,p,\infty$ ,then the best query upper bound is (21). Fix a number $q \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$ ,and let $N,L,D$ be three positive numbers satisfying $L \leq  N$ and $L \leq  {D}^{q + 1}$ . Then we claim that the $\{ 1,2,\ldots ,q,\infty \}$ -bound of the cyclic query in Example 2.3,when the input relation satisfies the statistics $\left| R\right|  \leq  N,{\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( Y \mid  X\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{r}^{r} \leq  L$ ,for all $r \leq  q$ ,and $\parallel {\deg }_{R}\left( {Y \mid  X}\right) {\parallel }_{\infty } \leq  D$ ,is $\left| Q\right|  \leq  {L}^{\frac{\left( {p + 1}\right) q}{q + 1}}$ . The claim applies to our database instance $\left( {\alpha ,\beta }\right)$ for $\alpha  = \beta  = \frac{1}{p + 1}$ ,because we have $L = \left( {1 + o\left( N\right) }\right) N$ and $D = {N}^{\frac{1}{p + 1}}$ ,and implies that the $\{ 1,2,\ldots ,q\}$ -bound is ${L}^{\frac{\left( {p + 1}\right) q}{q + 1}}$ . To prove the claim,we use the same reasoning as above: it suffices to describe a polymatroid satisfying the statistics

最终，我们证明，若可获得所有关于${\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( Y \mid  X\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{q}$的统计量$q = 1,2,\ldots ,p,\infty$，则最佳查询上界为(21)。设定一个数值$q \in  \left\lbrack  p\right\rbrack$，令$N,L,D$为满足$L \leq  N$与$L \leq  {D}^{q + 1}$的三个正数。此时我们断言，在输入关系满足统计量$\left| R\right|  \leq  N,{\begin{Vmatrix}{\deg }_{R}\left( Y \mid  X\right) \end{Vmatrix}}_{r}^{r} \leq  L$（对所有$r \leq  q$及$\parallel {\deg }_{R}\left( {Y \mid  X}\right) {\parallel }_{\infty } \leq  D$）的情况下，例2.3中循环查询的$\{ 1,2,\ldots ,q,\infty \}$-界为$\left| Q\right|  \leq  {L}^{\frac{\left( {p + 1}\right) q}{q + 1}}$。该断言适用于我们针对$\alpha  = \beta  = \frac{1}{p + 1}$的数据库实例$\left( {\alpha ,\beta }\right)$，因为我们具备$L = \left( {1 + o\left( N\right) }\right) N$与$D = {N}^{\frac{1}{p + 1}}$，这意味着$\{ 1,2,\ldots ,q\}$-界即为${L}^{\frac{\left( {p + 1}\right) q}{q + 1}}$。为证明该断言，我们沿用上述推理逻辑：只需构造一个满足统计量的多拟阵即可

$$
h\left( {{X}_{i}{X}_{i + 1}}\right)  \leq  \log N
$$

$$
\forall r = 2,q : h\left( {{X}_{i}{X}_{i + 1}}\right)  + \left( {r - 1}\right) h\left( {{X}_{i + 1} \mid  {X}_{i}}\right)  \leq  \log L
$$

$$
h\left( {{X}_{i + 1} \mid  {X}_{i}}\right)  \leq  \log D
$$

The desired polymatroid is the following modular function: $h\left( W\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\frac{\left| W\right|  \cdot  \log L}{q + 1}$ . In other words, $\mathbf{h} = \frac{1}{q + 1}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0,p}}{\mathbf{h}}^{{X}_{i}}$ . Then,the first inequality above is $\frac{2\log L}{q + 1} \leq  \log N$ ,and it holds because $L \leq  N$ . The second inequality is $\left( {r + 1}\right) \frac{\log L}{q + 1} \leq  \log L$ ,which holds because $r \leq  q$ . And the third inequality is $\frac{\log L}{q + 1} \leq  \log D$ ,which holds by the assumption $L \leq  {D}^{q + 1}$ .

所需的多拟阵为如下模函数：$h\left( W\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\frac{\left| W\right|  \cdot  \log L}{q + 1}$。换言之，$\mathbf{h} = \frac{1}{q + 1}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 0,p}}{\mathbf{h}}^{{X}_{i}}$。此时，第一个不等式为$\frac{2\log L}{q + 1} \leq  \log N$，其成立原因是$L \leq  N$。第二个不等式是$\left( {r + 1}\right) \frac{\log L}{q + 1} \leq  \log L$，成立依据为$r \leq  q$。第三个不等式则为$\frac{\log L}{q + 1} \leq  \log D$，其有效性由假设$L \leq  {D}^{q + 1}$保证。
