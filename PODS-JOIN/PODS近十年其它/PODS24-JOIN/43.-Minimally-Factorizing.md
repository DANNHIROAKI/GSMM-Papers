# Minimally Factorizing the Provenance of Self-join Free Conjunctive Queries

自连接自由合取查询溯源的最小化因式分解

NEHA MAKHIJA, D Northeastern University , USA

NEHA MAKHIJA，美国东北大学

WOLFGANG GATTERBAUER, DOrtheastern University, USA

WOLFGANG GATTERBAUER，美国东北大学

We consider the problem of finding the minimal-size factorization of the provenance of self-join-free conjunctive queries, i.e., we want to find a formula that minimizes the number of variable repetitions. This problem is equivalent to solving the fundamental Boolean formula factorization problem for the restricted setting of the provenance formulas of self-join free queries. While general Boolean formula minimization is $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{2}^{p}$ -complete, we show that the problem is NP-complete in our case. Additionally, we identify a large class of queries that can be solved in PTIME, expanding beyond the previously known tractable cases of read-once formulas and hierarchical queries.

我们研究自连接自由合取查询溯源的最小规模因式分解问题，即寻找使变量重复次数最小化的公式表示。该问题等价于针对自连接自由查询溯源公式这一受限场景，解决布尔公式因式分解的基础性问题。虽然一般布尔公式最小化是$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{2}^{p}$完全问题，但我们证明该问题在本文设定下是NP完全的。此外，我们识别出可在PTIME内求解的大类查询，突破了现有可处理案例（只读公式和分层查询）的边界。

We describe connections between factorizations, Variable Elimination Orders (VEOs), and minimal query plans. We leverage these insights to create an Integer Linear Program (ILP) that can solve the minimal factorization problem exactly. We also propose a Max-Flow Min-Cut (MFMC) based algorithm that gives an efficient approximate solution. Importantly, we show that both the Linear Programming (LP) relaxation of our ILP, and our MFMC-based algorithm are always correct for all currently known PTIME cases. Thus, we present two unified algorithms (ILP and MFMC) that can both recover all known PTIME cases in PTIME, yet also solve NP-complete cases either exactly (ILP) or approximately (MFMC), as desired.

我们阐述了因式分解、变量消除序(VEO)和最小查询计划之间的关联，并据此构建能精确求解最小因式分解问题的整数线性规划(ILP)。同时提出基于最大流最小割(MFMC)的近似算法。值得注意的是，我们证明ILP的线性规划松弛解和MFMC算法在当前所有已知PTIME案例中均保持正确性。因此，本文提出的两种统一算法(ILP与MFMC)既能以PTIME处理所有已知可解案例，又能根据需求对NP完全案例进行精确(ILP)或近似(MFMC)求解。

## 1 INTRODUCTION

## 1 引言

Given the provenance formula for a Boolean query, what is its minimal size equivalent formula? And under what conditions can this problem be solved efficiently? This paper investigates the complexity of minFACT, i.e. the problem of finding a minimal factorization for the provenance of self-join-free conjunctive queries (sj-free CQs). While the general Boolean formula minimization is ${\sum }_{2}^{p}$ -complete [7],several important tractable subclasses have been identified,such as read-once formulas [32]. In this paper, we identify additional tractable cases by identifying a large class of queries for which the minimal factorization of any provenance formula can be found in PTIME.

给定布尔查询的溯源公式，其最小规模的等价公式是什么？该问题在何种条件下可高效求解？本文研究minFACT问题的复杂度——即自连接自由合取查询(sj-free CQs)溯源的最小因式分解问题。虽然一般布尔公式最小化是${\sum }_{2}^{p}$完全的[7]，但已发现若干重要可解子类（如只读公式[32]）。本文通过识别可在PTIME内求解溯源最小因式化的大类查询，进一步扩展了可处理案例的范围。

We focus on provenance formulas for two key reasons: 1) Provenance computation and storage is utilized in numerous database applications. The issue of storing provenance naturally raises the question: How can provenance formulas be represented minimally? This problem has previously been investigated in this context $\left\lbrack  {{50},{51}}\right\rbrack$ ,where algorithms were described for factorizations with asymptotically optimal sizes, leading to work on factorized databases. However, finding instance-optimal factorizations i.e. factorizations that are guaranteed to be the smallest possible, for any arbitrary input, remains an open challenge, and is the focus of our work.

聚焦溯源公式基于两大要因：1)溯源计算与存储广泛应用于数据库领域，其存储自然引出的核心问题是：如何最精简地表示溯源公式？该问题在$\left\lbrack  {{50},{51}}\right\rbrack$中已有研究，描述了渐近最优规模的因式分解算法，并催生因子化数据库研究。然而，寻找实例最优因式分解——即保证对任意输入都能获得最小可能规模的解——仍是开放难题，这正是我们的研究重点。

2) Minimal factorizations of provenance formulas can be used to obtain probabilistic inference bounds. Prior approaches for approximate probabilistic inference are either incomplete i.e. focus on just PTIME cases [17, 54, 56], or do not solve all PTIME cases exactly [17, 28]. As we show, using minimal factorization as a preprocessing step achieves the best of both worlds: It is complete (i.e. it applies to easy and hard cases) while recovering all known PTIME cases exactly.

2) 溯源公式的最小因子分解可用于获取概率推断边界。现有近似概率推断方法要么不完整（即仅关注PTIME情形[17,54,56]），要么无法精确求解所有PTIME情形[17,28]。如我们所示，将最小因子分解作为预处理步骤可实现两全其美：该方法具有完备性（即适用于简单与困难情形），同时能精确复现所有已知PTIME情形。

In this paper, we prove that the minimal factorization problem is NP-complete (NP-C) for provenance formulas,and give two algorithms for all sj-free CQs that are unified algorithms in the sense that they solve all known tractable cases in PTIME, and provide approximations for hard cases. We further place the set of tractable queries firmly between the tractable queries for two other related problems: resilience [24] and probabilistic query evaluation [16] (Fig. 1).

本文证明了溯源公式的最小因子分解问题具有NP完全性(NP-C)，并为所有无自连接CQs提出两种统一算法——这些算法既能以PTIME精确求解所有已知易处理案例，又能为困难案例提供近似解。我们进一步将易处理查询集明确界定于另两个相关问题（弹性[24]与概率查询评估[16]）的易处理查询集之间（图1）。

Contributions & Outline. 1 The minFACT problem has strong ties to the diverse problems of Boolean factorization, factorized databases, probabilistic inference, and resilience, among others. Section 2 explains these connections before Section 3 formalizes the problem. 2 Section 4 describes connections between provenance factorizations, variable elimination orders (VEOs) and query plans. These connections allow us to reformulate minFACT as the problem of assigning each witness to one of several "minimal VEOs." 3 Section 5 develops an ILP encoding to solve minFACT for any sj-free CQ exactly. We are not aware of any prior ILP formulation for minimal-size encodings of propositional formulas, for restricted cases like for monotone formulas. 4 Section 6 describes our two unified PTIME algorithms that are exact for all known PTIME cases, and approximations otherwise. The first one encodes the problem in the form of a "factorization flow graph" s.t. a minimal cut of the graph corresponds to a valid factorization of the instance. We refer to this algorithm as the MFMC (Max-Flow Min-Cut) based algorithm. The second is an LP relaxation of our ILP encoding, for which we also prove a guaranteed constant factor approximation for hard queries.

## 2 RELATED WORK

## 2 相关工作

Boolean Factorization. Minimum Equivalent Expression (MEE) is the problem of deciding whether a given Boolean formula $\varphi$ (note that we use the terms expressions and formulas interchangeably) has a logically equivalent formula ${\varphi }^{\prime }$ that contains $\leq  k$ occurrences of literals. It was known to be at least NP-hard for over 40 years [26,Section 7.2] and was shown to be ${\sum }_{p}^{2}$ -complete only 10 years ago [7]. The problem is more tractable for certain restrictions like Horn formulas [38] as input, or if allowing arbitrary Boolean functions as connectors [39], or if posed as the Minimum Formula Size Problem (MFSP) that takes the uncompressed truth table as input [2, 40]. There is a lot of work on approximate Boolean function factorization [45, 47], however efficient, exact methods are limited to classes such as read-once [32] and read-polarity-once formulas [9] (see [14, Section 10.8] for a detailed historical overview). Our problem restricts the formula to be minimized to the provenance of a sj-free conjunctive query (i.e. a monotone, $m$ -partite DNF that follows join dependencies),with the goal of uncovering important classes that permit a PTIME exact evaluation. An illustration of an overview of known results can be found in the full online appendix. [43, Fig 7] To the best of our knowledge, no prior work has provided a general approach for finding the minimal factorization of monotone $k$ -partite Boolean formulas given as DNFs,and we are unaware of prior work that provides an ILP for the problem, even under restricted settings.

布尔因子分解。最小等价表达式(MEE)问题旨在判定给定布尔公式$\varphi$（注：表达式与公式在此通用）是否存在逻辑等价但仅含$\leq  k$个文字出现的公式${\varphi }^{\prime }$。该问题被证实至少具有NP难度已逾40年[26第7.2节]，而${\sum }_{p}^{2}$完备性的证明仅在十年前完成[7]。对于某些限制条件如以Horn公式[38]为输入、允许任意布尔函数作为连接符[39]、或以未压缩真值表为输入的最小公式规模问题(MFSP)[2,40]等形式，该问题更易处理。近似布尔函数因子分解已有大量研究[45,47]，但高效精确方法仅限于只读一次[32]和极性只读一次公式[9]等类别（详见[14第10.8节]历史综述）。我们的研究将待最小化公式限制为无自连接合取查询的溯源公式（即遵循连接依赖的单调$m$部DNF），旨在发现允许PTIME精确求解的重要类别。完整在线附录[43图7]展示了现有成果概览。据我们所知，尚无研究提出针对以DNF形式给出的单调$k$部布尔公式的最小因子分解通用方法，也未发现任何研究——包括受限场景下——为该问题提供整数线性规划(ILP)解法。

Factorized Databases and Related Work on Factorization. Our problem has been studied before in the context of Factorized Databases (FDBs) [49-52]. Five key differences in focus are: (i) The tight bounds provided through that line of work are on "readability" i.e. the lowest $k$ such that each variable in the factorized formula is repeated at most $k$ times. The work shows that the class of queries with bounded readability is strictly that of hierarchical queries [51]. In contrast, we focus on the minimal number of variable repetitions and show this can be calculated in PTIME for a strict superset of hierarchical queries. (ii) For bounds on the minimal length (as is our focus), FDBs focus only asymptotic bounds on the size of query result representations [52] whereas we focus on minimizing the exact number of variables (e.g. whether a provenance is read-once or has a factor 2 bigger size is of no relevance in the asymptotic analysis of FDBs). (iii) variants of FDBs permit the reuse of intermediate results, i.e. they focus on the corresponding circuit size, while we focus on formulas. (iv) Intuitively, FDBs study the trade-offs between applying one of several factorizations (or query plans or variable elimination orders) to the entire query results at once, whereas we may factorize each witness in different ways. (v) Except for [50], the work on FDBs focuses on factorizations in terms of domain values whereas provenance formulas are defined in terms of tuple variables (e.g, a tuple from an arity-3 relation has 3 different domains, but is still represented by a single tuple variable). These discrepancies lead to different technical questions and answers. Also related is the very recently studied problem of finding a factorized representation of all the homomorphisms between two finite relational structures [5]. Similar to FDBs, that work also differs from ours in that it focuses on the asymptotic factorization size (and proves lower bounds and allows a circuit factorization instead of a formula). Our problem is also different from the problem of calculating a " $p$ -minimal query" for a given query [3]: The solution to our problem depends on the database instance and factorizes a given provenance formula, whereas the latter problem is posed irrespective of any given database, chooses among alternative polynomials, and becomes trivial for queries without self-joins.

因子化数据库及相关研究。我们的问题曾在因子化数据库(FDBs)[49-52]背景下被研究，但存在五个关键差异：(i)FDB研究提供的紧界针对"可读性"——即因子化公式中每个变量至多重复$k$次的最小值，证明具有有限可读性的查询类严格对应于层次化查询[51]。而我们关注变量重复的最小次数，并证明这对层次化查询的严格超集仍可PTIME求解。(ii)针对最小长度边界（我们的焦点），FDBs仅关注查询结果表示规模的渐近边界[52]，而我们追求变量数量的精确最小化（例如溯源公式是只读一次还是规模两倍在FDBs渐近分析中无差别）。(iii)FDBs变体允许中间结果复用（侧重电路规模），我们则聚焦公式结构。(iv)直观而言，FDBs研究对整个查询结果应用某类因子化（或查询计划/变量消解顺序）的权衡，而我们允许对每个见证采用不同因子化方式。(v)除[50]外，FDBs研究基于域值的因子化，而溯源公式使用元组变量（如三元关系中的元组虽涉及三个域但仍用单一变量表示）。这些差异导致了不同的技术命题与解法。最新研究[5]关于有限关系结构间同态的因子化表示也与FDBs类似——聚焦渐近规模下界并允许电路因子化。我们的问题亦不同于计算给定查询的"$p$最小查询"[3]：我们的解法依赖数据库实例并因子化特定溯源公式，而后者不依赖具体数据库、在备选多项式中选择，对无自连接查询则退化为平凡问题。

Probabilistic Inference, Read-Once Formulas, and Dissociation. Probabilistic query evaluation (PROB) is $\#$ P-hard in general [17]. However,if a provenance formula $\varphi$ can be represented in read-once form then its marginal probability $\mathbb{P}\left\lbrack  \varphi \right\rbrack$ can be computed in linear time in the number of literals. Olteanu and Huang [48] showed that the previously known tractable queries called hierarchical queries lead to read-once factorizations. A query $Q$ is called hierarchical [17] iff for any two existential variables $x,y$ ,one of the following three conditions holds: at $\left( x\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{at}\left( y\right)$ , at $\left( x\right)  \supseteq  \operatorname{at}\left( y\right)$ ,or at $\left( x\right)  \cap  \operatorname{at}\left( y\right)  = \varnothing$ ,where $\operatorname{at}\left( x\right)$ is the set of atoms of $Q$ in which $x$ participates. Roy et al. [54] and Sen et al. [56] independently proposed algorithms for identifying read-once provenance for non-hierarchical queries in PTIME. Notice that finding the read-once form of a formula (if it exists) is just an extreme case of representing a Boolean function by a minimum length $\left( {\vee , \land  }\right)$ -formula. Our solution is a natural generalization that is guaranteed to return a read-once factorization in PTIME should there be one. We give an interesting connection by proving that the tractable queries for our problem are a strict superset of hierarchical queries and thus the tractable queries for probabilistic query evaluation.

概率推理、一次读取公式与解离。概率查询评估(PROB)在一般情况下是$\#$ P难问题[17]。然而，若溯源公式$\varphi$能以一次读取形式表示，则其边际概率$\mathbb{P}\left\lbrack  \varphi \right\rbrack$可在文字数量的线性时间内计算得出。Olteanu与Huang[48]证明，先前被称为层次化查询的可处理查询会导致一次读取分解。当且仅当任意两个存在变量$x,y$满足以下三个条件之一时，查询$Q$被称为层次化查询[17]：在$\left( x\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{at}\left( y\right)$处、在$\left( x\right)  \supseteq  \operatorname{at}\left( y\right)$处、或在$\left( x\right)  \cap  \operatorname{at}\left( y\right)  = \varnothing$处，其中$\operatorname{at}\left( x\right)$是$Q$中包含$x$的原子集合。Roy等人[54]与Sen等人[56]分别提出了在PTIME内识别非层次化查询的一次读取溯源的算法。需注意，寻找公式的一次读取形式（若存在）仅是用最小长度$\left( {\vee , \land  }\right)$公式表示布尔函数的极端案例。我们的解决方案是自然泛化，保证在PTIME内返回一次读取分解（若存在）。通过证明本问题的可处理查询是层次化查询的严格超集，我们建立了有趣关联，因此这些也是概率查询评估的可处理查询。

Resilience. The resilience problem $\left\lbrack  {{24},{25}}\right\rbrack$ is a variant of the deletion propagation problem $\left\lbrack  {8,{19}}\right\rbrack$ focusing on Boolean queries: Given $D \vDash  Q$ ,what is the minimum number of tuples to remove (called a "contingency set") in order to make the query false? We give an interesting connection by proving that the tractable queries for our problem are a strict subset of the tractable queries for resilience. Concretely, we show that the structural hardness criterion for resilience also makes the factorization problem hard. We achieve separation by giving a query that is easy for resilience yet hard to factorize. Our approach has an interesting conceptual connection to "holistic" join algorithms [1] that rely on not just a single tree decomposition (thus one query plan) but rather multiple tree decompositions (thus multiple plans) for different output tuples. Very similarly, our approach also carefully assigns different witnesses to different query plans.

### 3.1 Standard database notations

### 3.1 标准数据库符号

We write $D$ for the database,i.e. the set of tuples in the relations. A conjunctive query (CQ) is a first-order formula $Q\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  = \exists \mathbf{x}\left( {{g}_{1} \land  \ldots  \land  {g}_{m}}\right)$ where the variables $\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{\ell }}\right)$ are called existential variables, $\mathbf{y}$ are called the head variables and each atom ${g}_{i}$ represents a relation ${g}_{i} = {R}_{{j}_{i}}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$ where ${\mathbf{x}}_{i} \subseteq  \mathbf{x} \cup  {\mathbf{y}}_{ \cdot  }^{1}$ W.l.o.g.,we discuss only connected queries. ${}^{2}$ We write $\operatorname{var}\left( X\right)$ for the set of variables occurring in atom/ relation/ query/ formula $X$ and at(x)for the set of atoms that contain variable $x$ . We write $\left\lbrack  {\mathbf{w}/\mathbf{x}}\right\rbrack$ as a valuation (or substitution) of query variables $\mathbf{x}$ by constants $\mathbf{w}$ . These substitutions may be written explicitly by "domain-annotating" variables with domain constants as subscripts. Domain-annotated tuples use such domain-annotated variables as subscripts,e.g. ${r}_{{x}_{1},{y}_{2}}$ represents a tuple of relation $R\left( {x,y}\right)$ with $x = 1$ and $y = 2$ . We sometimes informally omit the variables and use the notation ${r}_{{v}_{1}{v}_{2}\ldots {v}_{a}}$ where ${v}_{1}{v}_{2}\ldots {v}_{a}$ are the domain values of $\operatorname{var}\left( R\right)$ in the order that they appear in atom $R$ . Thus, ${r}_{12}$ also represents $R\left( {1,2}\right)$ . A self-join-free (sj-free) ${CQ}$ is one where no relation symbol occurs more than once and thus every atom represents a different relation. Thus, for sj-free CQs, one may refer to atoms and relations interchangeably. We focus on Boolean queries (i.e.,where $\mathrm{y} = \varnothing$ ),since the problem of finding the minimal factorization of the provenance for one particular output tuple of a non-Boolean query immediately reduces to the Boolean query case (see e.g. [57]). ${}^{3}$ Unless otherwise stated,a query in this paper denotes a sj-free Boolean CQ. Appendix A defines further notation.

我们用$D$表示数据库，即关系中元组的集合。合取查询(CQ)是一个一阶公式$Q\left( \mathbf{y}\right)  = \exists \mathbf{x}\left( {{g}_{1} \land  \ldots  \land  {g}_{m}}\right)$，其中变量$\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{\ell }}\right)$称为存在变量，$\mathbf{y}$称为头部变量，每个原子${g}_{i}$表示一个关系${g}_{i} = {R}_{{j}_{i}}\left( {\mathbf{x}}_{i}\right)$。不失一般性，我们仅讨论连通查询。${}^{2}$我们用$\operatorname{var}\left( X\right)$表示出现在原子/关系/查询/公式$X$中的变量集合，用at(x)表示包含变量$x$的原子集合。将查询变量$\mathbf{x}$通过常量$\mathbf{w}$进行赋值(或替换)记为$\left\lbrack  {\mathbf{w}/\mathbf{x}}\right\rbrack$。这些替换可通过为变量添加域常量下标来显式表示。域标注元组使用此类带域标注的变量作为下标，例如${r}_{{x}_{1},{y}_{2}}$表示关系$R\left( {x,y}\right)$的一个元组，其中包含$x = 1$和$y = 2$。我们有时会非正式地省略变量，使用记号${r}_{{v}_{1}{v}_{2}\ldots {v}_{a}}$，其中${v}_{1}{v}_{2}\ldots {v}_{a}$是$\operatorname{var}\left( R\right)$在原子$R$中出现顺序对应的域值。因此，${r}_{12}$也表示$R\left( {1,2}\right)$。无自连接(sj-free)的${CQ}$是指关系符号仅出现一次的查询，因此每个原子代表不同关系。对于无自连接CQ，原子和关系可以互换指代。我们聚焦于布尔查询(即$\mathrm{y} = \varnothing$的情况)，因为非布尔查询特定输出元组的溯源最小分解问题可立即归约为布尔查询情形(参见[57])。${}^{3}$除非特别说明，本文中的查询均指无自连接的布尔合取查询。附录A定义了更多符号。

### 3.2 Boolean and Provenance formulas

### 3.2 布尔与溯源公式

The terms provenance and lineage are used in the literature with slightly different meanings. While lineage was originally formalized in [15], we follow the modern treatment of data provenance as denoting a proposition formula that corresponds to the Boolean provenance semiring of Green et al. $\left\lbrack  {{33},{34}}\right\rbrack$ ,which is the commutative semiring of positive Boolean expressions $\left( {\mathbb{B}\left\lbrack  X\right\rbrack  ,\vee ,\land ,0,1}\right)$ . ### 3.3 Minimal factorization minFACT

### 3.3 最小因式分解minFACT

For a provenance ${\varphi }_{p} = \operatorname{Prov}\left( {Q,D}\right)$ as DNF,we want to find an equivalent formula ${\varphi }^{\prime } \equiv  {\varphi }_{p}$ with the minimum number of literals.

对于DNF形式的来源表达式${\varphi }_{p} = \operatorname{Prov}\left( {Q,D}\right)$，我们需要找到具有最少文字量的等价公式${\varphi }^{\prime } \equiv  {\varphi }_{p}$。

Definition 3.1 (FACT). Given a query $Q$ and database $D$ ,we say that $\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{FACT}\left( Q\right)$ if there is a formula ${\varphi }^{\prime }$ of length $\operatorname{len}\left( {\varphi }^{\prime }\right)  \leq  k$ that is equivalent to the expression ${\varphi }_{p} = \operatorname{Prov}\left( {Q,D}\right)$ .

定义3.1（因式分解）。给定查询$Q$和数据库$D$，当存在长度为$\operatorname{len}\left( {\varphi }^{\prime }\right)  \leq  k$的公式${\varphi }^{\prime }$与表达式${\varphi }_{p} = \operatorname{Prov}\left( {Q,D}\right)$等价时，我们称$\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{FACT}\left( Q\right)$。

Our focus is to determine the difficulty of this problem in terms of data complexity [60], i.e., we treat the query size $\left| Q\right|$ as a constant. We are interested in the optimization version of this decision problem: given $Q$ and $D$ ,find the minimum $k$ such that $\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{FACT}\left( Q\right)$ . We refer to this optimization variant as the minFACT problem and use minFACT(Q,D)to refer to the length of the minimal size factorization for the provenance of database $D$ under query $Q$ .

我们的研究重点是从数据复杂度[60]角度确定该问题的难度，即我们将查询规模$\left| Q\right|$视为常数。我们关注该决策问题的优化版本：给定$Q$和$D$，寻找满足$\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{FACT}\left( Q\right)$的最小$k$。我们将该优化变体称为minFACT问题，并使用minFACT(Q,D)表示查询$Q$下数据库$D$的溯源最小规模分解长度。

## 4 SEARCH SPACE FOR minFACT

## 4 minFACT的搜索空间

Factorizations. In order to find the minimal factorization of a provenance formula, we first define a search space of all permissible factorizations. Each factorized formula can be represented as a factorization tree (or FT),where each literal of the formula corresponds to a leaf node, ${}^{6}$ and internal nodes denote the $\oplus$ and $\otimes$ operators of the commutative provenance semiring. The length (size) of a FT is the number of leaves. We allow the semiring operations to be $k$ -ary (thus even unary) and use prefix notation for the operators when writing FTs in linearized text. Notice that the space of FTs is strictly larger than the space of factorized expressions: E.g.,the FT $\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1},{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right)$ is not equivalent to $\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1}, \otimes  \left( {{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right) }\right)$ ,although they represent the same formula ${r}_{1}{s}_{1}{t}_{1}$ . We consider FTs as equivalent under commutativity i.e. we treat $\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1},{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right)$ as equivalent to $\otimes  \left( {{s}_{1},{t}_{1},{r}_{1}}\right)$ . Furthermore,w.l.o.g.,we only consider trees in which the operators $\oplus  , \otimes$ alternate: E.g., $\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1}, \otimes  \left( {{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right) }\right)$ is not alternating but represents the same formula as the alternating tree $\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1}, \oplus  \left( {\otimes \left( {{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right) }\right) }\right)$ using unary $\oplus$ . Henceforth, we use factorization trees or FTs as a short form for alternating factorization trees.

分解形式。为寻找溯源公式的最小分解，我们首先定义所有允许分解的搜索空间。每个分解公式可表示为分解树（FT），其中公式的每个字面对应叶节点，${}^{6}$内部节点表示交换式溯源半环的$\oplus$和$\otimes$运算符。FT的长度（规模）即叶节点数量。我们允许半环运算采用$k$元形式（甚至一元），并在线性文本中使用运算符前缀表示法。注意FT空间严格大于分解表达式空间：例如FT$\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1},{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right)$虽表示相同公式${r}_{1}{s}_{1}{t}_{1}$，但与$\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1}, \otimes  \left( {{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right) }\right)$不等价。我们认为FT在交换律下等价，即视$\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1},{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right)$与$\otimes  \left( {{s}_{1},{t}_{1},{r}_{1}}\right)$等价。此外，不失一般性，我们仅考虑运算符$\oplus  , \otimes$交替出现的树：例如$\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1}, \otimes  \left( {{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right) }\right)$非交替树，但通过一元$\oplus$可转化为表示相同公式的交替树$\otimes  \left( {{r}_{1}, \oplus  \left( {\otimes \left( {{s}_{1},{t}_{1}}\right) }\right) }\right)$。下文将交替分解树简称为分解树或FT。

Variable Elimination Order (VEO). FTs describe tuple-level factorizations, however, they fail to take into account the structure (and resulting join dependencies) of the query producing the provenance. For this purpose, we define query-specific Variable Elimination Orders (VEOs). They are similar to VEOs in general reasoning algorithms, such as bucket elimination [20] and VEOs defined in FDBs [49] for the case of no caching (i.e. corresponding to formulas, not circuits). However, our formulation allows each node to have a set of variables instead of a single variable. This allows VEOs to have a 1-to-1 correspondence to the sequence of variables projected away in an "alternating" query plan, in which projections and joins alternate, just as in our FTs (details in [43]). Furthermore, we show VEOs can be "annotated" with a data instance and "merged" to form forests that describe a minimal factorization tree of any provenance formula.

变量消除序（Variable Elimination Order，VEO）。因子树（FTs）描述元组级分解，但未能考虑生成出处的查询结构（及由此产生的连接依赖）。为此我们定义查询特定的变量消除序（VEOs）。它们类似于通用推理算法中的VEO，例如无缓存情况下的桶消元[20]和FDBs[49]中定义的VEO（即对应公式而非电路）。但我们的形式化允许每个节点包含一组变量而非单一变量，这使得VEO能与"交替"查询计划中投影消除变量的序列形成一一对应——正如我们的因子树所示，该计划中投影与连接交替进行（详见[43]）。此外，我们证明VEO可通过数据实例"标注"并"合并"形成森林，用以描述任何出处公式的最小分解树。

Definition 4.1 (Variable Elimination Order (VEO)). A VEO $v$ of a query $Q$ is a rooted tree whose nodes are labeled with non-empty sets of query variables s.t. (i) each variable of $Q$ is assigned to exactly one node of $v$ ,and (ii) all variables $\mathbf{x}$ for any atom $R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$ in $Q$ must occur in the prefix of some node of $v$ .

定义4.1（变量消除序（VEO））。查询$Q$的VEO$v$是带根节点的树，其节点被非空查询变量集标记，满足：(i)$Q$的每个变量恰好分配至$v$的一个节点；(ii)$Q$中任意原子$R\left( \mathbf{x}\right)$的所有变量$\mathbf{x}$必须出现在$v$某节点的前缀路径上。

Definition 4.2 (VEO instance). Given a VEO $v$ and witness $\mathbf{w}$ ,a VEO instance $v\langle \mathbf{w}\rangle$ is the rooted tree resulting from annotating the variables $\mathbf{x}$ in $v$ with the domain values of $\mathbf{w}$ .

定义4.2（VEO实例）。给定VEO$v$与见证$\mathbf{w}$，VEO实例$v\langle \mathbf{w}\rangle$是通过将$\mathbf{w}$的域值标注到$v$中变量$\mathbf{x}$上所形成的带根树。

In order to refer to a VEO in-text, we use a linear notation with parentheses representing sets of children. To make it a unique serialization, we need to assume an ordering on the children of each parent. For notational convenience, we leave out the parentheses for nodes with singleton sets. For example, $x \leftarrow  y$ ,(instead of $\{ x\}  \leftarrow  \{ y\}$ ) and $\{ x,y\}$ are two valid VEOs of ${Q}_{2}^{ \star  }$ . We refer to the unique path of a node to the root as its prefix.

为在文中引用VEO，我们采用括号表示子节点集合的线性记法。为确保序列唯一性，需假定每个父节点的子节点存在序关系。记法便利性上，单元素集合节点可省略括号。例如$x \leftarrow  y$（而非$\{ x\}  \leftarrow  \{ y\}$）与$\{ x,y\}$都是${Q}_{2}^{ \star  }$的有效VEO。我们将节点到根的唯一路径称为其前缀。

THEOREM 4.5 (FACTORIZATIONS AND VEOS). There exist transformations from FTs to VEOFFs and back such that the transformations can recover the original FT for at least one minimal size $\mathrm{{FT}}{\varphi }^{\prime }$ of any provenance formula ${\varphi }_{p}$ .

定理4.5（因子化与VEOs）：存在从FT（因子树）到VEOFF的双向转换方法，该转换能针对任意溯源公式${\varphi }_{p}$的至少一个最小尺寸$\mathrm{{FT}}{\varphi }^{\prime }$恢复原始FT。

Proof INTUITION. We describe a transformation from FTs to VEOFFs via domain-annotated FTs as intermediate step (Fig. 3). A domain-annotated FT is constructed as follows: We first replace the $\otimes$ operator with a join $\left(  \bowtie  \right)$ and the $\oplus$ operator with a projection $\left( \pi \right)$ and label the leaves with the domain-annotated variables. We then recursively label each join and projection bottom-up as follows: (1) label each $\bowtie$ by the union of variables of its children,and (2) label each $\pi$ with the subset of variables of its children that are not required for subsequent joins (this can be inferred from the query). To get the VEOFF instance, we remove all variables on joins that appear in ancestor joins. We remove the leaves and absorb all non-join (projection) nodes into their parents (eliminating the root projection node).

证明思路：通过域标注FT作为中间步骤实现FT到VEOFF的转换（图3）。域标注FT构建方法：首先将$\otimes$运算符替换为连接$\left(  \bowtie  \right)$，$\oplus$运算符替换为投影$\left( \pi \right)$，并为叶子节点标注域变量；然后自底向上递归标注：(1)每个$\bowtie$标注为其子节点变量的并集，(2)每个$\pi$标注为子节点中后续连接不需要的变量子集（可通过查询推断）。最终通过移除祖先连接中的重复变量、删除叶子节点、将非连接（投影）节点合并至父节点（消除根投影节点）得到VEOFF实例。

We show that if this transformation succeeds then it is a bijection and can be reversed. The only case when this transformation fails is when it results in an empty annotation for a node, i.e. when there is a join after which no variable is projected away (since by design VEOs do not permit empty nodes). In that case,the FT can always be simplified by removing $a \oplus$ node and merging two $\otimes$ nodes.

我们证明若此转换成功，则其为双射且可逆。唯一失败情形是转换导致节点出现空标注，即连接操作后未投影掉任何变量（因VEO设计上不允许空节点）。此时，通过移除$a \oplus$节点并合并两个$\otimes$节点总能简化该FT。

THEOREM 4.6 (minFACT WITH VEOs). There exists a transformation that constructs FTs of a provenance ${\varphi }_{p}$ from mappings of each witness of ${\varphi }_{p}$ to a VEO of $Q$ ,and there exists a mapping that is transformed into a minimal size factorization tree ${\varphi }^{\prime }$ of ${\varphi }_{p}$ under this transformation.

定理4.6（含VEO的最小因子分解树）。存在一种变换，将溯源公式${\varphi }_{p}$的每个见证映射到$Q$的VEO来构造FT，且存在一个映射在该变换下可转化为${\varphi }_{p}$的最小规模因子分解树${\varphi }^{\prime }$。

Proof INTUITION. From Theorem 4.5,we know that for every provenance formula ${\varphi }_{p}$ there exists a minimal size $\mathrm{{FT}}$ that has a reversible transformation to a VEOFF. We show all such VEOFFs can be constructed by assigning a VEO to each witness of ${\varphi }_{p}$ . This is constructed by defining a merge operation on VEOs that greedily merges common prefixes.

证明思路。由定理4.5可知，每个溯源公式${\varphi }_{p}$都存在可逆转换为VEOFF的最小规模$\mathrm{{FT}}$。我们证明所有此类VEOFF可通过为${\varphi }_{p}$的每个见证分配VEO来构建，具体通过对VEO定义贪婪合并共同前缀的归并操作实现。

Minimal Variable Elimination Orders (mveo). By reducing the problem of finding the minimal factorization to that of assigning a VEO to each witness, we have so far shown that FACT is in NP with respect to data complexity. ${}^{7}$ However,we can obtain a more practically efficient result by showing that we need not consider all VEOs, but only the Minimal Variable Elimination Orders of a query or $\operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$ . We can define a partial order $\preccurlyeq$ on VEOs of a query $Q$ as follows: ${v}_{1} \preccurlyeq  {v}_{2}$ if for every relation ${R}_{i} \in  Q$ the variables in the ${R}_{i}$ table prefix of ${v}_{1}$ are a subset of the variables of the ${R}_{i}$ table prefix of ${v}_{2}$ ,i.e. $\forall {R}_{i} \in  Q : \operatorname{var}\left( {v}_{1}^{{R}_{i}}\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( {v}_{2}^{{R}_{i}}\right)$ . mveo(Q)then is the set of all VEOs of $Q$ that are minimal with respect to this partial order $\preccurlyeq$ . For ${Q}_{2}^{ \star  }$ ,there are only two minimal variable elimination orders $x \leftarrow  y$ and $y \leftarrow  x$ ,but not $\{ x,y\}$ ,and for ${Q}_{3}^{ \star  }$ ,there are only 6,despite 13 possible VEOs in total. Interestingly, $\operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$ corresponds exactly to Minimal Query Plans as defined in work on probabilistic databases [28], and we can use this connection to leverage prior algorithms for computing mveo(Q).

最小变量消序（mveo）。通过将寻找最小因子分解问题转化为给每个见证分配VEO的问题，我们已证明FACT在数据复杂度上属于NP类。${}^{7}$但实际可获得更高效结果：只需考虑查询的极小变量消序集$\operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$而非所有VEO。可定义查询$Q$上VEO的偏序关系$\preccurlyeq$：若对每个关系${R}_{i} \in  Q$，${v}_{1}$的${R}_{i}$表前缀变量是${v}_{2}$对应前缀的子集（即$\forall {R}_{i} \in  Q : \operatorname{var}\left( {v}_{1}^{{R}_{i}}\right)  \subseteq  \operatorname{var}\left( {v}_{2}^{{R}_{i}}\right)$），则${v}_{1} \preccurlyeq  {v}_{2}$。mveo(Q)即为该偏序$\preccurlyeq$下所有极小VEO的集合。例如${Q}_{2}^{ \star  }$仅有$x \leftarrow  y$和$y \leftarrow  x$两个极小消序，而${Q}_{3}^{ \star  }$在13种可能VEO中仅有6个极小消序。值得注意的是，$\operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$恰好对应概率数据库文献[28]定义的极小查询计划，可利用该关联调用既有算法计算mveo(Q)。

THEOREM 4.7 (minFACT WITH mveos). There exists a transformation that constructs FTs of a provenance ${\varphi }_{p}$ from mappings of each witness of ${\varphi }_{p}$ to a $\operatorname{VEO}v \in  \operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$ ,and there exists a mapping that is transformed into a minimal size factorization tree ${\varphi }^{\prime }$ of ${\varphi }_{p}$ under this transformation.

定理4.7（含mveo的最小因子分解树）。存在一种变换，将溯源公式${\varphi }_{p}$的每个见证映射到$\operatorname{VEO}v \in  \operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$来构造FT，且存在一个映射在该变换下可转化为${\varphi }_{p}$的最小规模因子分解树${\varphi }^{\prime }$。

## 5 ILP FORMULATION FOR minFACT

## 5 最小因子分解树的整数线性规划形式化

Given a set of witnesses $W =$ witnesses(Q,D)for a query $Q$ over some database $D$ ,we can use the insight of Theorem 4.7 to describe a 0-1 Integer Linear Program (ILP) minFACT_ILP (3) that chooses a $v \in$ mveo or equivalently a minimal query plan,for each $\mathbf{w} \in  W$ ,s.t. the resulting factorization is of minimal size. The size of the ILP is polynomial in $n = \left| D\right|$ and exponential in the query size.

给定查询$Q$在数据库$D$上的一组见证元组$W =$witnesses(Q,D)，我们可以运用定理4.7的洞见，构建一个0-1整数线性规划(ILP)minFACT_ILP(3)，为每个$\mathbf{w} \in  W$选择$v \in$最小变量消除序(mveo)或等价的最小查询计划，使得最终生成的因式分解具有最小规模。该ILP的规模与$n = \left| D\right|$呈多项式关系，与查询规模呈指数关系。

ILP Decision Variables. The ILP is based on two sets of binary variables: Query Plan Variables (QPV) $q$ use a one-hot encoding for the choice of a minimal VEO (or equivalently minimal query plan) for each witness,and Prefix Variables (PV) $p$ encode sub-factorizations that are a consequence of that choice. Intuitively, shared prefixes encode shared computation through factorization.

ILP决策变量。该ILP基于两组二元变量：查询计划变量(QPV)$q$采用独热编码为每个见证元组选择最小VEO（或等价的最小查询计划），前缀变量(PV)$p$则编码该选择产生的子因式分解。直观而言，共享前缀通过因式分解实现了计算共享。

(1) Query Plan Variables (QPV): For each witness $\mathbf{w} \in  W$ and each minimal VE0 $v \in  \operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$ we define a binary variable $q\left\lbrack  {v\langle \mathbf{w}\rangle }\right\rbrack$ ,which is set to 1 iff VEO $v$ is chosen for witness ${\mathbf{w}}^{8}$

(1) 查询计划变量(QPV)：对于每个见证元组$\mathbf{w} \in  W$和每个最小VEO$v \in  \operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$，我们定义二元变量$q\left\lbrack  {v\langle \mathbf{w}\rangle }\right\rbrack$，当且仅当为见证元组${\mathbf{w}}^{8}$选择VEO$v$时其值为1。

(2) Prefix Variables (PV): All witnesses must be linked to a set of prefix variables, by creating instances of VEO prefixes that are in a query-specific set called the Prefix Variable Format (PVF). This set ${PVF}$ is composed of all table prefixes of all minimal VEOs $v \in  \operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$ . Notice that prefix variables can be shared by multiple witnesses, which captures the idea of joint factorization. Additionally, we define a weight (or ${\operatorname{cost}}^{9}$ ) $c\left( {v}^{R}\right)$ for each table prefix ${v}^{R} \in  {PVF}$ ; this weight is equal to the number of tables that have the same table prefix for a given VEO. From that PVF set, then binary prefix variables $p\left\lbrack  {{v}^{R}\langle \mathbf{w}\rangle }\right\rbrack$ are defined for each table prefix ${v}^{R} \in  {PVF}$ and $\mathbf{w} \in  W$ .

(2) 前缀变量(PV)：所有见证元组必须通过创建VEO前缀实例与一组前缀变量关联，这些前缀属于查询特定的前缀变量格式(PVF)集合。该集合${PVF}$由所有最小VEO$v \in  \operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$的表前缀构成。注意前缀变量可被多个见证元组共享，体现了联合因式分解思想。此外，我们为每个表前缀${v}^{R} \in  {PVF}$定义权重（或称${\operatorname{cost}}^{9}$）$c\left( {v}^{R}\right)$，其值等于给定VEO下具有相同表前缀的表数量。基于PVF集合，我们为每个表前缀${v}^{R} \in  {PVF}$和$\mathbf{w} \in  W$定义二元前缀变量$p\left\lbrack  {{v}^{R}\langle \mathbf{w}\rangle }\right\rbrack$。

### 6.1 MFMC-based Algorithm for minFACT

### 6.1 基于MFMC的minFACT算法

Given witnesses $W$ and $\operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$ ,we describe the construction of a factorization flow graph $F$ s.t. any minimal cut of $F$ corresponds to a factorization of $W$ . A minimal cut of a flow graph is the smallest set of nodes whose removal disconnects the source $\left(  \bot  \right)$ and target $\left( \top \right)$ nodes [62]. Since minimal cuts of flow graphs can be found in PTIME [18], we obtain is a PTIME approximation for minFACT.

给定见证$W$和$\operatorname{mveo}\left( Q\right)$，我们构建因子分解流图$F$，使得该图的任何最小割都对应$W$的一个因子分解。流图的最小割是指断开源节点$\left(  \bot  \right)$与目标节点$\left( \top \right)$所需移除的最小节点集[62]。由于流图最小割可在多项式时间内求解[18]，因此我们获得了minFACT的多项式时间近似算法。

6.1.1 Construction of a factorization flow graph We construct a flow graph $F$ s.t. there exists a valid factorization of $W$ of length $\leq  c$ if the graph has a cut of size $c.F$ is constructed by transforming decision variables in the ILP into "cut" nodes in the flow graph that may be cut at a penalty equal to their weight. Any valid cut of the graph selects nodes that fulfill all constraints of the ILP. We prove this by describing the construction of $F$ (Fig. 6).

6.1.1 因子分解流图的构建 我们构造流图$F$，使得当图中存在大小为$c.F$的割时，存在长度为$\leq  c$的$W$有效因子分解。该图通过将ILP中的决策变量转化为流图中可被切割的"割节点"（切割代价等于其权重）来实现。任何有效割选择的节点都满足ILP所有约束。我们通过描述$F$的构建过程（图6）来证明这一点。

(1) mveo order: We use $\Omega$ to describe a total order on mveo (i.e. a total order on the set of minimal query plans). In Fig. 6,mveo is ordered by $\Omega  = \left( {{v}_{1},{v}_{2},\ldots ,{v}_{k}}\right)$ where $k = \left| \text{mveo}\right|$ .

(1) mveo序：我们使用$\Omega$来描述mveo上的全序（即最小查询计划集合上的全序）。在图6中，mveo通过$\Omega  = \left( {{v}_{1},{v}_{2},\ldots ,{v}_{k}}\right)$进行排序，其中$k = \left| \text{mveo}\right|$。

(2) QPV: For each witness w,connect the query plan variables(QPV)as defined by $\Omega$ . Since all paths from source to target must be disconnected, at least one mveo must be cut from this path. Thus, $F$ enforces the Query Plan Constraints.

(2) 查询计划变量(QPV)：对于每个见证w，按照$\Omega$定义的规则连接查询计划变量。由于从源到目标的所有路径都必须断开，至少需要从该路径中切断一个mveo。因此，$F$强制执行查询计划约束。

(3) ${PV}$ : For each witness $\mathbf{w}$ and prefix variable $p$ ,identify the first and last query variable for which $p$ is a prefix and connect the corresponding prefix variable node to connector nodes before and after these query variables. For example,in Fig. 6,for ${\mathbf{w}}_{2},{p}_{1}$ starts at $q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{1}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$ and ends at $q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{2}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$ implying that ${p}_{1}$ is a prefix for $q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{1}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$ and $q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{2}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$ ,but no query plan after that. Now if either of $q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{1}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$ or $q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{2}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$ are in the minimal cut,the graph is not disconnected until ${p}_{1}$ is added to the cut as well. These nodes guarantee that $F$ enforces Prefix Constraints.

(3) ${PV}$：对于每个见证$\mathbf{w}$和前缀变量$p$，识别$p$作为前缀的首尾查询变量，并将对应的前缀变量节点连接到这些查询变量前后的连接节点。例如图6中，${\mathbf{w}}_{2},{p}_{1}$起始于$q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{1}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$终止于$q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{2}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$，这意味着${p}_{1}$是$q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{1}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$和$q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{2}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$的前缀，但后续无查询计划。若$q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{1}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$或$q\left\lbrack  {{v}_{2}\left\langle  {\mathbf{w}}_{2}\right\rangle  }\right\rbrack$处于最小割中，则必须同时将${p}_{1}$加入割集才能使图断开。这些节点确保$F$强制执行前缀约束。

(4) Weights: Assign each $q$ and $p$ node in $F$ the same weight as in the ILP objective. Recall that this weight is the number of tables with the same table prefix under the same VEO and that it helps calculate the correct factorization length.

(4) 权重分配：为$F$中的每个$q$和$p$节点赋予与ILP目标函数相同的权重。需注意该权重表示同一VEO下具有相同表前缀的表数量，用于计算正确的因子分解长度。

Thus,a min-cut of $F$ contains at least one plan for each witness,along with all the prefixes that are necessary for the plan. This guarantees a valid (although not necessarily minimal) factorization. ${}^{11}$

因此，$F$的最小割至少包含每个见证的一个计划，以及该计划所需的所有前缀。这保证了因子分解的有效性（但不一定是最小化的）。${}^{11}$

6.1.2 When is the MFMC-based algorithm optimal? In the previous subsection we saw that a min-cut of $F$ always represents a valid factorization. However,the converse is not true: there can be factorizations that do not correspond to a cut. The reason is that spurious constraints might arise by the interaction of paths; those additional constraints no longer permit the factorization. There are two types of spurious paths:

6.1.2 基于MFMC算法的最优性条件 前文已论证$F$的最小割始终对应有效因子分解。但反之不成立：可能存在不对应任何割的因子分解。这是由于路径交互可能产生伪约束，这些额外约束会使因子分解失效。伪路径分为两类：

(1) Spurious Prefix Constraints. Spurious prefix constraints arise when a prefix node $p$ is in parallel with a query node $q$ of which it is not a prefix. This happens when a $q$ is not prefixed by $p$ , but other query plans before and after are. To avoid this,the ordering $\Omega$ must be a Running-Prefixes (RP) ordering. ${}^{12}$

(1) 伪前缀约束。当前缀节点$p$与非前缀查询节点$q$并联时产生。当$q$不以$p$为前缀，但其前后查询计划均以$p$为前缀时发生。为避免此情况，排序$\Omega$必须满足运行前缀(RP)序。${}^{12}$

Definition 6.1 (Running-Prefixes (RP) ordering). An ordering $\Omega  = \left( {{q}_{1},{q}_{2},\ldots ,{q}_{k}}\right)$ is an RP Ordering and satisfies the RP-Property iff for any $p$ that is a prefix for both ${q}_{i}$ and ${q}_{j}\left( {i < j}\right) ,p$ is a prefix for all ${q}_{k}$ with $i \leq  k \leq  j$ .

定义6.1（运行前缀(RP)序）。当且仅当对于任何同时作为${q}_{i}$和${q}_{j}\left( {i < j}\right) ,p$前缀的$p$，其也是所有满足$i \leq  k \leq  j$的${q}_{k}$的前缀时，排序$\Omega  = \left( {{q}_{1},{q}_{2},\ldots ,{q}_{k}}\right)$称为RP序并满足RP性质。

Example 7 (RP ORDERING). Assume m $\left. {\operatorname{mveo} = \{ \left( {x \leftarrow  y \leftarrow  z}\right) ,\left( {x \leftarrow  z \leftarrow  y}\right) ,\left( {z \leftarrow  y \leftarrow  x}\right) \} \text{. In contrast, ${\Omega }_{2} = \left( {\left( {x \leftarrow  y \leftarrow  z}\right) ,\left( {x \leftarrow  z \leftarrow  y}\right) ,\left( {z \leftarrow  y \leftarrow  x}\right) }\right)$ is an RP ordering.

It turns out that for some queries RP-Orderings are impossible (such as ${Q}_{6WE}^{ \circ  }\left\lbrack  {43}\right\rbrack$ ). However,we are able to adapt our algorithm for such queries with a simple extension called nested orderings. We first define two query plans as nestable if each query plan can be "split" into paths from root to leaf such that they have an equal number of resulting paths, and that the resulting paths can be mapped to each other satisfying the property that corresponding paths use the same set of query variables. Nested orderings then are partial orders of query plans such that the pair of query plans may be uncomparable iff they are nestable. Finally, we define Nested RP-orderings as those such that all paths in the partial nested order satisfy the RP property. Intuitively, nested orderings add parallel paths for a single witness to model independent decisions. We can now prove that there always is an ordering that avoids spurious prefix constraints.

事实证明，某些查询无法构建RP序（如${Q}_{6WE}^{ \circ  }\left\lbrack  {43}\right\rbrack$）。但通过称为嵌套序的简单扩展，我们能使算法适配此类查询。首先定义两个可嵌套的查询计划：若每个计划都能从根到叶"拆分"为路径，且拆分后的路径数量相等，并能按"对应路径使用相同查询变量集"的性质相互映射。嵌套序是查询计划的部分序，当且仅当两个查询计划可嵌套时，它们才允许不可比。最后，我们将满足所有路径均具备RP性质的嵌套序定义为嵌套RP序。直观上，嵌套序通过为单个见证添加并行路径来建模独立决策。由此可证明：总存在能避免虚假前缀约束的序。

THEOREM 6.2 (RUNNING PREFIXES (RP) PROPERTY). For any query, there is a simple or nested ordering $\Omega$ that satisfies the RP Property.

定理6.2（运行前缀RP性质）。对于任意查询，存在满足RP性质的简单序或嵌套序$\Omega$。

(2) Spurious Query Constraints. Query Plan constraints are enforced by paths from source to target such that at least one node from each path must be chosen for a valid factorization. Due to sharing of prefix variables, these paths can interact and can lead to additional spurious paths that place additional spurious constraints on the query nodes. The existence of spurious query constraints does not necessarily imply that the algorithm is not optimal. In fact,Section 8 shows that ${Q}_{U}^{ \vartriangle  }$ and ${Q}_{4}^{\infty }$ have spurious query paths, yet the min-cut is guaranteed to correspond to the minimal factorization. However, if the presence of spurious query paths prevents any of the minimal factorizations to be a min-cut for $F$ ,then we say that the factorization flow graph $F$ has "leakage" [43]. Since all paths along one witness are cut by construction, a leakage path must contain nodes from at least two different witnesses.

(2)虚假查询约束。查询计划约束通过源到目标的路径实施，有效分解要求每条路径至少选一个节点。由于前缀变量共享，这些路径会相互作用，产生对查询节点施加额外虚假约束的寄生路径。虚假约束的存在未必意味着算法非最优——第8章显示${Q}_{U}^{ \vartriangle  }$和${Q}_{4}^{\infty }$虽有虚假查询路径，但最小割仍保证对应最小分解。然而，若虚假路径导致任何最小分解都无法成为$F$的最小割，则称分解流图$F$存在"泄漏"[43]。由于单见证的所有路径在构造时已被切断，泄漏路径必须包含至少两个不同见证的节点。

Definition 6.3 (Leakage). Leakage exists in a factorization flow graph if no minimal factorization is a valid cut of the graph. A leakage path is a path from source to target such that a valid minimal factorization is possible without using any node on the path.

### 6.2 LP relaxation for minFACT and an LP relaxation-based approximation

### 6.2 minFACT的线性规划松弛及基于LP松弛的近似解法

Linear Programming relaxation and rounding is a commonly-used technique to find PTIME approximations for NP-C problems [61]. The LP relaxation for minFACT simply removes the integrality constraints on all the problem variables. The LP relaxation may pick multiple query plans for a given witness, each with fractional values. We present a rounding scheme for minFACT and show it to be a |mveo|-factor approximation of the optimal solution. The rounding algorithm simply picks the maximum fractional value of query plan variables for each witness, breaking ties arbitrarily. Finally, it counts only the prefix variables necessitated by the chosen query plans.

线性规划松弛与取整是求解NP-C问题PTIME近似的常用技术[61]。minFACT的LP松弛只需移除所有问题变量的整数约束，允许为每个见证选择多个带分数值的查询计划。我们提出minFACT的取整方案，证明其是最优解的|MVEO|倍近似。该算法简单选取每个见证查询计划变量的最大分数值（随机处理平局情况），最终仅统计所选查询计划所需的前缀变量。

THEOREM 6.4. The described rounding scheme gives a PTIME, |mveo|-factor approximation for minFACT.

定理6.4. 所述取整方案为minFACT提供PTIME级别的|MVEO|倍近似解。

6.2.1 When is the LP relaxation optimal? Experimentally, we observed that the LP solution of many queries are equal to the integral ILP solution. This is surprising since the ILP does not satisfy any of the known requirements for tractable ILPs such as Total Unimodularity, Balanced Matrices, or even Total Dual Integrality [55]. Interestingly, we next prove that the LP relaxation has the same objective value as the original ILP whenever the MFMC-based algorithm is optimal.

6.2.1 LP松弛何时最优？实验观测到，多数查询的LP解与整数规划ILP解相等。这一现象令人惊讶，因为该ILP并不满足任何已知的可处理条件，如全单模性（Total Unimodularity）、平衡矩阵（Balanced Matrices）或全对偶整性（Total Dual Integrality）[55]。值得注意的是，我们接下来将证明：当基于最大流最小割（MFMC）的算法最优时，LP松弛的目标值始终与原ILP相同。

LEMMA 6.5. If all database instances can be solved exactly by the MFMC-based algorithm for a given query $Q$ (i.e. for each database instance there exists an ordering that generates a leakage-free graph), then the LP relaxation of minFACT always has the same objective as the original ILP.

引理6.5 若给定查询$Q$的所有数据库实例都能通过MFMC算法精确求解（即每个实例都存在生成无泄漏图的排序），则minFACT的LP松弛目标值始终与原ILP一致。

This result is important as it exposes cases for which the optimal objective value of minFACT_ILP is identical to the optimal objective value of a simpler LP relaxation. The only example we know of where this has been shown using flow graphs is in recent work on resilience [44]. In such cases, standard ILP solvers return the optimal solution to the original ILP in PTIME. This is due to ILP solvers using an LP-based branch and bound approach which starts by computing the LP relaxation bound and then exploring the search space to find integral solutions that move closer to this bound. If an integral solution is encountered that is equal to the LP relaxation optimum, then the solver is done [36]. We use this knowledge in Section 8 to show that ILP solvers can solve all known PTIME cases in PTIME.

该结论具有重要意义，它揭示了minFACT_ILP最优目标值与更简单LP松弛最优值相等的情形。目前已知唯一通过流图证明此现象的案例来自近期关于弹性计算的研究[44]。此类情况下，标准ILP求解器可在多项式时间内返回原问题的最优解。这是因为ILP求解器采用基于LP的分支定界法：先计算LP松弛界，再搜索逼近该界的整数解。当遇到等于LP松弛最优解的整数解时，求解立即终止[36]。我们将在第8章运用这一原理，证明ILP求解器能以多项式时间处理所有已知的可多项式时间求解案例。

## 9 HARD QUERIES FOR minFACT

## 9 minFACT的难解查询

In this section, we first prove that all queries that contain a structure called "an active triad" (e.g. ${Q}_{3}^{ \star  }$ and ${Q}^{\Delta }$ ) are NP-C. We then prove another query to be NP-C that does not contain an active triad, but a "co-deactivated triad." We thus show that while active triads are sufficient for FACT of a query to be NP-C, they are not necessary, and minFACT is a strictly harder problem than RES.

本节首先证明所有包含"活跃三元组"结构（如${Q}_{3}^{ \star  }$和${Q}^{\Delta }$）的查询都是NP完全的。继而证明另一个不含活跃三元组但含"共钝化三元组"的查询也是NP完全的。由此表明：虽然活跃三元组足以导致查询的FACT问题为NP完全，但并非必要条件，且minFACT是严格难于RES的问题。

Queries with Active triads. We repeat here the necessary definitions introduced in the context of resilience under bag semantics [44]. A triad is a set of three atoms, $\mathcal{T} = \left\{  {{R}_{1},{R}_{2},{R}_{3}}\right\}$ s.t. for every pair $i \neq  j$ ,there is a path from ${R}_{i}$ to ${R}_{j}$ that uses no variable occurring in the third atom of $\mathcal{T}$ . Here a path is an alternating sequence of relations and variables ${R}_{1} - {\mathbf{x}}_{1} - {R}_{2} - \cdots {\mathbf{x}}_{p - 1} - {R}_{p}$ s.t. all adjacent relations ${R}_{i},{R}_{i + 1}$ share variables ${\mathbf{x}}_{i}$ . In a query $Q$ with atoms $R$ and $S$ ,we say $R$ dominates $S$ iff $\operatorname{var}\left( R\right)  \subset  \operatorname{var}\left( S\right)$ . We call an atom $g$ in a query independent iff there is no other atom in the query that contains a strict subset of its variables (and hence it is not dominated). A triad is active iff none of its atoms are dominated.

活跃三元组查询。此处我们复述在包语义下韧性研究中提出的必要定义[44]。三元组是指由三个原子组成的集合$\mathcal{T} = \left\{  {{R}_{1},{R}_{2},{R}_{3}}\right\}$，其中对于每对$i \neq  j$，都存在一条从${R}_{i}$到${R}_{j}$的路径，该路径不使用$\mathcal{T}$第三个原子中的任何变量。这里的路径是指关系与变量交替出现的序列${R}_{1} - {\mathbf{x}}_{1} - {R}_{2} - \cdots {\mathbf{x}}_{p - 1} - {R}_{p}$，其中所有相邻关系${R}_{i},{R}_{i + 1}$共享变量${\mathbf{x}}_{i}$。在包含原子$R$和$S$的查询$Q$中，当且仅当$\operatorname{var}\left( R\right)  \subset  \operatorname{var}\left( S\right)$时，我们说$R$支配$S$。若查询中某个原子$g$的变量集合不被其他原子严格包含（即不被支配），则称该原子是独立的。当三元组中所有原子都不被支配时，该三元组称为活跃的。

THEOREM 9.1 (ACTIVE TRIADS ARE HARD). FACT(Q)for a query $Q$ with an active triad is NP-C.

定理9.1（活跃三元组的难解性）。含有活跃三元组的查询$Q$的FACT(Q)问题是NP完全的。

Separation between RES and minFACT. A triad is deactivated if any of the three atoms is dominated. A triad is co-deactivated if all three atoms are dominated only by the same (nonempty) set of atoms. The co-deactivated triangle query ${Q}_{\text{cod }}^{\Delta } :  - A\left( w\right) ,R\left( {w,x,y}\right) ,S\left( {w,y,z}\right) ,T\left( {w,z,x}\right)$ contains no active triads: notice that the tables $R,S$ and $T$ are not independent and have no independent paths to each other. Thus, $\operatorname{RES}\left( {Q}_{\text{cod }}^{\Delta }\right)$ is PTIME. However, ${Q}_{\text{cod }}^{\Delta }$ contains a co-deactivated triad since $R,S$ and $T$ are all dominated only by atom $A$ . We next prove that $\operatorname{FACT}\left( {Q}_{\text{cod }}^{\Delta }\right)$ is NP-C, thus showing a strict separation in the complexities of the two problems.

RES与minFACT的分离性。若三元组中任一原子被支配，则该三元组失活。若三个原子仅被同一（非空）原子集合支配，则称为共失活三元组。共失活的三角查询${Q}_{\text{cod }}^{\Delta } :  - A\left( w\right) ,R\left( {w,x,y}\right) ,S\left( {w,y,z}\right) ,T\left( {w,z,x}\right)$不含活跃三元组：注意表$R,S$与$T$非独立且无相互独立的路径，因此$\operatorname{RES}\left( {Q}_{\text{cod }}^{\Delta }\right)$属于PTIME问题。但由于$R,S$和$T$均仅被原子$A$支配，${Q}_{\text{cod }}^{\Delta }$包含共失活三元组。后续我们将证明$\operatorname{FACT}\left( {Q}_{\text{cod }}^{\Delta }\right)$属于NP完全问题，从而揭示两类问题在复杂度上的严格差异。

THEOREM 9.2 (CO-DEACTIVATED TRIADS ARE HARD). FACT(Q)for a query $Q$ with a co-deactivated triad is NP-C.

定理9.2（共失活三元组的难解性）。含有共失活三元组的查询$Q$的FACT(Q)问题是NP完全的。

## 10 CONCLUSION

## 10 结论

We propose an ILP framework for minimizing the size of provenance polynomials for sj-free CQs. We show that our problem is NP-C and thus in a lower complexity class than the general Minimum Equivalent Expression (MEE) problem. Key to our formulation is a way to systematically constrain a space of possible minimum factorizations thus allowing us to build an ILP, and connecting minimal variable elimination orders to minimal query plans developed in the context of probabilistic databases. We complement our hardness results with two unified PTIME algorithms that can recover exact solutions to a strict superset of all prior known tractable cases.

我们提出了一种ILP框架，用于最小化无半连接聚合查询的溯源多项式规模。研究表明该问题是NP完全的，其复杂度低于广义最小等价表达式(MEE)问题。该框架的关键在于系统化约束可能的最小分解空间，从而构建ILP模型，并将最小变量消序与概率数据库领域开发的最小查询计划相关联。我们还提出了两个统一的多项式时间算法，能精确求解所有已知可处理案例的严格超集，为硬度结果提供了补充。

We write $\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack$ as short notation for the set $\{ 1,\ldots ,k\}$ and use boldface to denote tuples or ordered sets,(e.g., $\mathbf{x} = \left( {{x}_{1},\ldots ,{x}_{\ell }}\right)$ ). We fix a relational vocabulary $\mathbf{R} = \left( {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}}\right)$ ,and denote with $\operatorname{arity}\left( {R}_{i}\right)$ the number of attributes of a relation ${R}_{i}$ . For notational convenience,we assume w.l.o.g. that there are no two atoms ${R}_{i}$ and ${R}_{j}$ with $\operatorname{var}\left( {R}_{i}\right)  = \operatorname{var}\left( {R}_{j}\right)$ . A database instance over $\mathbf{R}$ is $D = \left( {{R}_{1}^{D},\ldots ,{R}_{m}^{D}}\right)$ ,where each ${R}_{i}^{D}$ is a finite relation. We call the elements of ${R}_{i}^{D}$ tuples and write ${R}_{i}$ instead of ${R}_{i}^{D}$ when $D$ is clear from the context. With some abuse of notation we also denote $D$ as the set of all tuples,i.e. $D = \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{i}{R}_{i}$ . The active domain $\operatorname{dom}\left( D\right)$ is the set of all constants occurring in $D$ . W.l.o.g.,we commonly use $\operatorname{dom}\left( D\right)  \subset  \mathbb{N} \cup  \{ a,b,\ldots ,z\}$ . The size of the database instance is $n = \left| D\right|$ ,i.e. the number of tuples in the database. ${}^{15}$

### B.1 Boolean Factorization

### B.1 布尔因子分解

Fig. 9 illustrates the landscape of known results for the problem of Minimum Equivalent Expressions (MEE) applied to formulas.

图9展示了应用于公式的最小等价表达式(MEE)问题的已知研究成果概览。

The general problem of MEE has been long known to be NP-hard [26]. However, only relatively recently it has been proved to be ${\sum }_{p}^{2}$ -complete [7]. Various important classes of this problem have been studied, a fundamental one being the factorization of DNF expressions. The MinDNF problem [59], deals with finding the minimum equivalent DNF expression of an input DNF formula, and is also known to be ${\sum }_{p}^{2}$ -complete. However,if the input to the MinDNF is the truth table (or set of all true assignments of the formula) then the problem is NP-C [2]. If we take away the restriction that the factorized formula must be a DNF, then the problem of finding the minimum factorization of an input table is known as the Minimum Formula Size Problem (MFSP) and is shown to be in NP and (ETH)-hard [40].

MEE问题长期以来被认为是NP难问题[26]，但直到最近才被证明是${\sum }_{p}^{2}$完全问题[7]。该问题的多个重要子类已被研究，其中基础性的是DNF表达式的因子分解。MinDNF问题[59]涉及寻找输入DNF公式的最小等价DNF表达式，同样被证明是${\sum }_{p}^{2}$完全问题。然而，若MinDNF的输入是真值表（或公式所有真赋值的集合），则该问题属于NP-C[2]。若取消因子化公式必须为DNF的限制，则输入表的最小因子化问题称为最小公式规模问题(MFSP)，已被证明属于NP且(ETH)难[40]。

Another important class of restrictions is over monotone formulas (thus we do not allow negatives in input or output formulas). Surprisingly, we do not know of any work that proves the complexity of the general monotone boolean factorization problem. However, there are many interesting and important restrictions for which complexity results are known. One such important sub-class is that of read-once formulas, which can be factorized in PTIME [32]. For Monotone formulas with DNF input and output restrictions, the problem can be solved in logspace by eliminating monomials [29]. Interestingly the problem monotone formula factorization of an arbitrary formula with a DNF restriction on the output only has differing complexity based on the input encoding of the length of the factorization. Checking if the minimum size of a DNF for a monotone formula is at most $\mathrm{k}$ is PP-complete,but for $\mathrm{k}$ in unary,the complexity of the problem drops to coNP [29]. The intuition is that in this problem, (which can be seen as "dual" of minFACT since it has a DNF output restriction instead of a DNF input restriction), the optimal output (a DNF) can be exponentially larger than the input (any monotone formula).

另一类重要限制是针对单调公式（即不允许输入或输出公式中出现否定）。令人惊讶的是，目前尚无研究证明一般单调布尔因子分解问题的复杂度。但对于许多重要限制子类，其复杂度结果已知。其中一个重要子类是只读公式，可在PTIME内完成因子化[32]。对于具有DNF输入输出限制的单调公式，该问题可通过消除单项式在对数空间内求解[29]。有趣的是，仅对输出施加DNF限制的任意单调公式因子化问题，其复杂度会因因子化长度的输入编码方式而异：检查单调公式的最小DNF规模是否至多为$\mathrm{k}$是PP完全的，但对于一元编码的$\mathrm{k}$，问题复杂度降至coNP[29]。其原理在于该问题（可视为minFACT的"对偶"问题，因其具有DNF输出限制而非输入限制）的最优输出（DNF）可能相对于输入（任意单调公式）呈指数级增长。

Our problem of minFACT is a further restriction on the MEE problem applied to a monotone DNF. Provenance formulas for sj-free CQs are $k$ -partite monotone formulas that satisfy join dependencies. We prove in this paper that the problem is NP-C, in general, and further identify interesting PTIME subcases.

我们的minFACT问题是对应用于单调析取范式(DNF)的最小等价表达式(MEE)问题的进一步限制。针对无自连接连接查询(sj-free CQ)的溯源公式是满足连接依赖性的$k$部单调公式。本文证明该问题在一般情况下属于NP完全问题(NP-C)，并进一步识别出具有多项式时间(PTIME)复杂性的有趣子案例。

### B.2 Probabilistic Inference and Dissociation

### B.2 概率推理与解离

Given a provenance that is not read-once, one can still upper and lower bound its probability efficiently via dissociation [27]: Let $\varphi$ and ${\varphi }^{\prime }$ be two Boolean formulas with variables $\mathbf{x}$ and ${\mathbf{x}}^{\prime }$ , respectively. Then ${\varphi }^{\prime }$ is a dissociation of $\varphi$ if there exists a substitution $\theta  : {\mathbf{x}}^{\prime } \rightarrow  \mathbf{x}$ s.t. ${\varphi }^{\prime }\left\lbrack  \theta \right\rbrack   = \varphi$ . If ${\theta }^{-1}\left( x\right)  = \left\{  {{x}_{1}^{\prime },\ldots ,{x}_{d}^{\prime }}\right\}$ ,then variable $x$ dissociates into $d$ variables ${x}_{1}^{\prime },\ldots ,{x}_{d}^{\prime }$ . Every provenance expression has a unique read-once dissociation up to renaming of variables. One application of compiling provenance polynomials into their smallest representation is motivated by the following known results on "oblivious bounds" [27]: (i) lower and upper bounds for intractable expressions can be found very efficiently; and(ii)those bounds work better the fewer times variables are repeated. Similarly, anytime approximation schemes based on branch-and-bound provenance decomposition methods $\left\lbrack  {{22},{23}}\right\rbrack$ give tighter bounds if Shannon expansions need to be run on fewer variables.

对于非一次性读取的溯源表达式，仍可通过解离技术[27]高效计算其概率上下界：设$\varphi$和${\varphi }^{\prime }$分别是具有变量$\mathbf{x}$和${\mathbf{x}}^{\prime }$的布尔公式。若存在替换$\theta  : {\mathbf{x}}^{\prime } \rightarrow  \mathbf{x}$使得${\varphi }^{\prime }\left\lbrack  \theta \right\rbrack   = \varphi$，则称${\varphi }^{\prime }$是$\varphi$的解离。当${\theta }^{-1}\left( x\right)  = \left\{  {{x}_{1}^{\prime },\ldots ,{x}_{d}^{\prime }}\right\}$时，变量$x$将解离为$d$个变量${x}_{1}^{\prime },\ldots ,{x}_{d}^{\prime }$。每个溯源表达式在变量重命名后都具有唯一的一次性读取解离形式。将溯源多项式编译为最小表示的应用动机源于以下关于"无意识边界"[27]的已知结论：(i)难解表达式的上下界可被高效计算；(ii)变量重复出现次数越少，边界效果越好。类似地，基于分支定界溯源分解方法的近似算法$\left\lbrack  {{22},{23}}\right\rbrack$在需要展开的变量较少时能获得更紧致的边界。

### B.3 Resilience

### B.3 韧性

The resilience of a Boolean query measures the minimum number of tuples in database $D$ ,the removal of which makes the query false. The optimization version of this decision problem is then: given $Q$ and $D$ ,find the minimum $k$ so that $\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{RES}\left( Q\right)$ . A larger $k$ implies that the query is more "resilient" and requires the deletion of more tuples to change the query output. We know from [24] that all hard queries must have a "triad" which is a set of three non-dominated atoms, $\mathcal{T} = \left\{  {{R}_{1},{R}_{2},{R}_{3}}\right\}$ s.t. for every pair $i \neq  j$ ,there is a path from ${R}_{i}$ to ${R}_{j}$ that uses no variable occurring in the other atom of $\mathcal{T}$ . In return,the tractable queries for RES are exactly those that are triad-free.

布尔查询的韧性指使查询结果为假所需删除的数据库$D$中元组的最小数量。该决策问题的优化形式为：给定$Q$和$D$，寻找最小$k$使得$\left( {D,k}\right)  \in  \operatorname{RES}\left( Q\right)$。$k$值越大表明查询"韧性"越强，需要删除更多元组才能改变查询结果。根据[24]的研究，所有困难查询都必须包含由三个非支配原子组成的"三元组"$\mathcal{T} = \left\{  {{R}_{1},{R}_{2},{R}_{3}}\right\}$，其中对于每对$i \neq  j$，都存在不涉及$\mathcal{T}$中另一原子变量的连接路径从${R}_{i}$到${R}_{j}$。反之，具有多项式时间复杂性的韧性查询恰好都是无三元组结构的。

EXAMPLE 9 (RESILIENCE). The resilience for our example from Fig. 2 is 2 because removing the set $\Gamma  = \left\{  {{r}_{1},{t}_{3}}\right\}$ removes one tuple from each witness and there is no smaller set that achieves that.

例9(韧性). 图2示例的韧性值为2，因为删除集合$\Gamma  = \left\{  {{r}_{1},{t}_{3}}\right\}$会从每个见证中移除一个元组，且不存在更小的集合能实现该目标。
