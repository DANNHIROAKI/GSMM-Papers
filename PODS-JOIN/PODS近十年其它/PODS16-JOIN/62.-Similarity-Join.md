## Abstract

## 摘要

A number of tasks in classification, information retrieval, recommendation systems, and record linkage reduce to the core problem of inner product similarity join (IPS join): identifying pairs of vectors in a collection that have a sufficiently large inner product. IPS join is well understood when vectors are normalized and some approximation of inner products is allowed. However, the general case where vectors may have any length appears much more challenging. Recently, new upper bounds based on asymmetric locality-sensitive hashing (ALSH) and asymmetric embeddings have emerged, but little has been known on the lower bound side. In this paper we initiate a systematic study of inner product similarity join, showing new lower and upper bounds. Our main results are:

分类、信息检索、推荐系统和记录链接中的诸多任务都可归结为内积相似性连接(IPS连接)的核心问题：在向量集合中找出具有足够大内积的向量对。当向量经过归一化处理且允许内积近似计算时，IPS连接问题已有较深入理解。然而，对于向量长度不受限的一般情况，该问题显得更具挑战性。最近基于非对称局部敏感哈希(ALSH)和非对称嵌入的新上界方法相继出现，但下界方面的研究仍十分有限。本文首次对内积相似性连接展开系统研究，提出新的上下界结果。主要贡献包括：

- Approximation hardness of IPS join in subquadratic time, assuming the strong exponential time hypothesis.

- 在强指数时间假设下，证明亚二次时间内解决IPS连接问题存在近似计算困难性

- New upper and lower bounds for (A)LSH-based algorithms. In particular, we show that asymmetry can be avoided by relaxing the LSH definition to only consider the collision probability of distinct elements.

- 针对基于(非对称)局部敏感哈希算法的上下界新结果，特别论证通过放宽LSH定义仅考虑相异元素的碰撞概率可避免非对称性

- A new indexing method for IPS based on linear sketches, implying that our hardness results are not far from being tight.

- 基于线性草图的新型IPS索引方法，表明我们的困难性结论接近紧界

Our technical contributions include new asymmetric embed-dings that may be of independent interest. At the conceptual level we strive to provide greater clarity, for example by distinguishing among signed and unsigned variants of IPS join and shedding new light on the effect of asymmetry.

技术贡献包括可能具有独立价值的新型非对称嵌入方法。在概念层面，我们通过区分IPS连接的有符号/无符号变体、重新阐释非对称性影响等方式提升理论清晰度

## 1. 引言

This paper is concerned with inner product similarity join (IPS join) where,given two sets $P,Q \subseteq  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$ ,the task is to find

本文研究内积相似性连接(IPS连接)问题：给定两个集合$P,Q \subseteq  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$，目标是找出

for each point $q \in  Q$ at least one pair ${}^{1}\left( {p,q}\right)  \in  P \times  Q$ where the inner product (or its absolute value) is larger than a given threshold $s$ . Our results apply also to the problem where for each $q \in  Q$ we seek the vector $p \in  P$ that maximizes the inner product, a search problem known in literature as maximum inner product search (MIPS) [43, 45].

## Motivation

## 研究动机

Similarity joins have been widely studied in the database and information retrieval communities as a mechanism for linking noisy or incomplete data. Considerable progress, in theory and practice, has been made to address metric spaces where the triangle inequality can be used to prune the search space (see e.g. $\left\lbrack  {6,{61}}\right\rbrack$ ). In particular,it is now known that in many cases it is possible to improve upon the quadratic time complexity of a naive algorithm that explicitly considers all pairs of tuples. The most prominent technique used to achieve provably subquadratic running time is locality-sensitive hashing (LSH) [25, 24]. In the database community the similarity join problem was originally motivated by applications in data cleaning $\left\lbrack  {{17},{10}}\right\rbrack$ . However,since then it has become clear that similarity join is relevant for a range of other data processing applications such as clustering, semi-supervised learning, query refinement, and collaborative filtering (see e.g. [44] for references and further examples). We refer to the recent book by Augsten and Böhlen [11] for more background on similarity join algorithms in database systems.

相似性连接作为关联噪声或不完整数据的机制，已在数据库和信息检索领域得到广泛研究。在可利用三角不等式剪枝搜索空间的度量空间领域（参见$\left\lbrack  {6,{61}}\right\rbrack$），理论与实践均已取得显著进展。特别地，现已证明在许多情况下可以改进显式考虑所有元组对的朴素算法的二次时间复杂度。实现可证明次二次运行时间的最主要技术是局部敏感哈希（LSH）[25,24]。在数据库领域，相似性连接问题最初源于数据清洗应用$\left\lbrack  {{17},{10}}\right\rbrack$。但后来人们逐渐认识到，该技术还适用于聚类、半监督学习、查询优化和协同过滤等其他数据处理场景（参见[44]等文献）。关于数据库系统中相似性连接算法的更多背景知识，建议参阅Augsten与Böhlen的最新著作[11]。

Inner product is an important measure of similarity between real vectors, particularly in information retrieval and machine learning contexts $\left\lbrack  {{31},{48}}\right\rbrack$ ,but not captured by techniques for metric similarity joins such as $\left\lbrack  {{26},{61}}\right\rbrack$ . Teflioudi et al. [50] studied the IPS join problem motivated by applications in recommender systems based on latent-factor models. In this setting, a user and the available items are represented as vectors and the preference of a user for an item is given by the inner product of the two associated vectors. Other examples of applications for IPS join are object detection [23] and multi-class prediction [22, 28]. IPS join also captures the so-called maximum kernel search, a general machine learning approach with applications such as image matching and finding similar protein/DNA sequences [20].

内积是实值向量间重要的相似性度量指标，尤其在信息检索和机器学习领域$\left\lbrack  {{31},{48}}\right\rbrack$，但无法通过$\left\lbrack  {{26},{61}}\right\rbrack$等度量空间相似性连接技术实现。Teflioudi等人[50]基于潜在因子模型的推荐系统应用研究了IPS连接问题。在该场景中，用户与待选项目被表示为向量，用户对项目的偏好程度由对应向量的内积决定。IPS连接的其他应用场景还包括目标检测[23]和多类预测[22,28]。该技术还可实现最大核搜索——这种通用机器学习方法适用于图像匹配、蛋白质/DNA序列比对等场景[20]。

## Challenges of IPS join

## IPS连接的挑战

Large inner products do not correspond to close vectors in any metric on the vector space, so metric space techniques cannot directly be used. In fact, there are reasons to believe that inner product similarity may be inherently more difficult than other kinds of similarity search: Williams [56, 4] has shown that a truly subquadratic exact algorithm for IPS join would contradict the Strong Exponential Time Hypothesis, an important conjecture in computational complexity. On the upper bound side new reductions of (special cases of) approximate IPS join to fast matrix multiplication have appeared $\left\lbrack  {{51},{29}}\right\rbrack$ ,resulting in truly subquadratic algorithms even with approximation factors asymptotically close to 1 . However, the approach of reducing to fast matrix multiplication does not seem to lead to practical algorithms, since fast matrix multiplication algorithms are currently not competitive on realistic input sizes. From a theoretical viewpoint it is of interest to determine how far this kind of technique might take us by extending lower bounds for exact IPS join to the approximate case.

大内积值并不对应向量空间任何度量下的邻近向量，因此度量空间技术无法直接适用。事实上，有理由认为内积相似性搜索可能本质上比其他类型更困难：Williams[56,4]已证明，若存在真正次二次时间的精确IPS连接算法，将违背计算复杂性领域的重要猜想——强指数时间假设。在上界方面，近似IPS连接（特例）与快速矩阵乘法的新约简方法已经出现$\left\lbrack  {{51},{29}}\right\rbrack$，即使近似因子渐近趋近于1时仍能产生真正次二次算法。但由于快速矩阵乘法算法在当前实际输入规模下尚不具备竞争力，这种约简方法难以转化为实用算法。从理论角度看，通过将精确IPS连接的下界扩展到近似情况，可以探索此类技术的潜力边界。

Another approach to IPS join would be to use LSH, which has shown its utility in practice. The difficulty is that inner products do not admit locality-sensitive hashing as defined by Indyk and Motwani [45, Theorem 1]. Recently there has been progress on asymmetric LSH methods for inner products, resulting in subquadratic IPS join algorithms in many settings. The idea is to consider collisions between two different hash functions, using one hash function for query vectors and another hash function for data vectors [45, 46, 39]. However, existing ALSH methods give very weak guarantees in situations where inner products are small relative to the lengths of vectors. It is therefore highly relevant to determine the possibilities and limitations of this approach.

另一种实现IPS连接的方法是采用局部敏感哈希(LSH)，该方法已在实际应用中展现出其价值。难点在于内积运算不符合Indyk和Motwani[45, Theorem 1]所定义的局部敏感哈希特性。近期非对称LSH方法在内积计算领域取得进展，使得多种场景下都能实现次二次方的IPS连接算法。其核心思想是采用两种不同哈希函数的碰撞机制——分别对查询向量和数据向量施用不同的哈希函数[45,46,39]。然而现有ALSH方法在向量内积值相对于向量长度较小时提供的保证非常有限，因此深入探究该方法的潜力与局限具有重要研究意义。

## Problem definitions

## 问题定义

We are interested in two variants of IPS join that slightly differ in the formulation of the objective function. For notational simplicity, we omit the term IPS and we simply refer to IPS join as join. Let $s > 0$ be a given value. The first variant is the signed join, where the goal is to find at least one pair $\left( {p,q}\right)  \in  P \times  Q$ for each point $q \in  Q$ with ${p}^{T}q \geq  s$ . The second variant is the unsigned join which finds, for each point $q \in  Q$ ,at least one pair $\left( {p,q}\right)  \in  P \times  Q$ where $\left| {{p}^{T}q}\right|  \geq  s$ . We observe that the unsigned version can be solved with the signed one by computing the join between $P$ and $Q$ and between $P$ and $- Q$ ,and then returning only pairs where the absolute inner products are larger than $s$ . Signed join is of interest when searching for similar or preferred items with a positive correlation, like in recommender systems. On the other hand, unsigned join can be used when studying relations among phenomena where even a large negative correlation is of interest. Previous works do not make the distinction between signed and unsigned versions since they focus on settings where there are no negative dot products.

我们关注IPS连接的两种变体，其目标函数表述略有差异。为简化表示，下文将IPS连接简称为连接。给定阈值$s > 0$，第一种是有符号连接，其目标是为每个点$q \in  Q$找到至少一个满足${p}^{T}q \geq  s$的配对$\left( {p,q}\right)  \in  P \times  Q$；第二种是无符号连接，要求为每个点$q \in  Q$找到至少一个满足$\left| {{p}^{T}q}\right|  \geq  s$的配对$\left( {p,q}\right)  \in  P \times  Q$。值得注意的是，无符号版本可通过有符号连接实现：分别计算$P$与$Q$、$P$与$- Q$的连接，然后筛选内积绝对值大于$s$的配对。有符号连接适用于推荐系统等需要寻找具有正相关性的相似项或偏好项的场景，而无符号连接则适用于研究现象间关系时需关注强负相关性的情况。既往研究未区分这两种变体，因其聚焦于不存在负点积的场景。

Our focus is on approximate algorithms for signed and unsigned joins. Indeed, approximate algorithms allow us to overcome, at least in some cases, the curse of dimensionality without significantly affecting the final results. Approximate signed joins are defined as follows.

我们重点研究有符号与无符号连接的近似算法。事实上，近似算法至少能在部分情况下克服维度灾难问题，且不会显著影响最终结果。近似有符号连接定义如下：

Definition 1 (Approximate signed join). Given two point sets $P,Q$ and values $0 < c < 1$ and $s > 0$ ,the signed(cs,s)join returns,for each $q \in  Q$ ,at least one pair $\left( {p,q}\right)  \in  P \times  Q$ with ${p}^{T}q \geq  {cs}$ if there exists ${p}^{\prime } \in  P$ such that ${p}^{\prime T}q \geq  s$ . No guarantee is provided for $q \in  Q$ where there is no ${p}^{\prime } \in  P$ with ${p}^{\prime T}q \geq  s$ .

定义1(近似有符号连接)。给定两个点集$P,Q$及参数$0 < c < 1$和$s > 0$，有符号(cs,s)连接需确保：对于每个$q \in  Q$，若存在${p}^{\prime } \in  P$满足${p}^{\prime T}q \geq  s$，则返回至少一个满足${p}^{T}q \geq  {cs}$的配对$\left( {p,q}\right)  \in  P \times  Q$；对于不存在此类${p}^{\prime } \in  P$的$q \in  Q$，则不提供保证。

The unsigned(cs,s)join is defined analogously by taking the absolute value of dot products. Indexing versions of signed/unsigned exact/approximate joins can be defined in a similar way. For example,the signed(cs,s)search is defined as follows: given a set $P \subset  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$ of $n$ vectors,construct a data structure that efficiently returns a vector $p \in  P$ such that ${p}^{T}q > {cs}$ for any given query vector $q \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$ ,under the promise that there is a point ${p}^{\prime } \in  P$ such that ${p}^{T}q \geq  s$ (a similar definition holds for the unsigned case).

无符号(cs,s)连接可通过取点积绝对值进行类似定义。有符号/无符号的精确/近似连接的索引版本也可类似定义。例如有符号(cs,s)搜索定义如下：给定包含$n$个向量的集合$P \subset  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$，构建一个数据结构，能针对任意查询向量$q \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$高效返回满足${p}^{T}q > {cs}$的向量$p \in  P$，前提是存在满足${p}^{T}q \geq  s$的点${p}^{\prime } \in  P$(无符号版本可作类似定义)。

As already mentioned, LSH is often used for solving similarity joins. In this paper, we use the following definition of asymmetric LSH based on the definition in [45].

如前所述，LSH常被用于解决相似性连接问题。本文采用基于文献[45]的非对称LSH定义如下：

Definition 2 (Asymmetric LSH). Let ${\mathcal{U}}_{p}$ denote the data domain and ${\mathcal{U}}_{q}$ the query domain. Consider a family $\mathcal{H}$ of pairs of hash functions $h = \left( {{h}_{p}\left( \cdot \right) ,{h}_{q}\left( \cdot \right) }\right)$ . Then $\mathcal{H}$ is said $\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$ -asymmetric LSH for a similarity function sim if for any $p \in  {\mathcal{U}}_{p}$ and $q \in  {\mathcal{U}}_{q}$ we have:

定义2（非对称局部敏感哈希）。设${\mathcal{U}}_{p}$表示数据域，${\mathcal{U}}_{q}$表示查询域。考虑一个由哈希函数对$h = \left( {{h}_{p}\left( \cdot \right) ,{h}_{q}\left( \cdot \right) }\right)$组成的族$\mathcal{H}$。若对于任意$p \in  {\mathcal{U}}_{p}$和$q \in  {\mathcal{U}}_{q}$满足以下条件，则称$\mathcal{H}$为相似度函数sim的$\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$-非对称局部敏感哈希：

1. if $\operatorname{sim}\left( {p,q}\right)  \geq  s$ then $\mathop{\Pr }\limits_{\mathcal{H}}\left\lbrack  {{h}_{p}\left( p\right)  = {h}_{q}\left( q\right) }\right\rbrack   \geq  {P}_{1}$ ;

1. 若$\operatorname{sim}\left( {p,q}\right)  \geq  s$则$\mathop{\Pr }\limits_{\mathcal{H}}\left\lbrack  {{h}_{p}\left( p\right)  = {h}_{q}\left( q\right) }\right\rbrack   \geq  {P}_{1}$；

2. if $\operatorname{sim}\left( {p,q}\right)  < {cs}$ then $\mathop{\Pr }\limits_{\mathcal{H}}\left\lbrack  {{h}_{p}\left( p\right)  = {h}_{q}\left( q\right) }\right\rbrack   \leq  {P}_{2}$ .

2. 若$\operatorname{sim}\left( {p,q}\right)  < {cs}$则$\mathop{\Pr }\limits_{\mathcal{H}}\left\lbrack  {{h}_{p}\left( p\right)  = {h}_{q}\left( q\right) }\right\rbrack   \leq  {P}_{2}$。

When ${h}_{p}\left( \cdot \right)  = {h}_{q}\left( \cdot \right)$ ,we get the traditional (symmetric) LSH definition. The $\rho$ value of an (asymmetric) LSH is defined as usual with $\rho  = \log {P}_{1}/\log {P}_{2}\left\lbrack  6\right\rbrack$ . Two vectors $p \in  {\mathcal{U}}_{p}$ and $q \in  {\mathcal{U}}_{q}$ are said to collide under a hash function from $\mathcal{H}$ if ${h}_{p}\left( p\right)  = {h}_{q}\left( q\right)$ .

当${h}_{p}\left( \cdot \right)  = {h}_{q}\left( \cdot \right)$时，即得到传统（对称）局部敏感哈希定义。（非对称）局部敏感哈希的$\rho$值按常规方式用$\rho  = \log {P}_{1}/\log {P}_{2}\left\lbrack  6\right\rbrack$定义。若${h}_{p}\left( p\right)  = {h}_{q}\left( q\right)$，则称两个向量$p \in  {\mathcal{U}}_{p}$和$q \in  {\mathcal{U}}_{q}$在$\mathcal{H}$的哈希函数下发生碰撞。

### 1.1 Overview of results

### 1.1 研究成果概述

## Hardness results

## 硬度结果

The first results of the paper are conditional lower bounds for approximate signed and unsigned IPS join that rely on a conjecture about the Orthogonal Vectors Problem (OVP). This problem consists in determining if two sets $A,B \subseteq$ $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ ,each one with $n$ vectors,contain $x \in  A$ and $y \in  B$ such that ${x}^{T}y = 0$ . It is conjectured that there cannot exist an algorithm that solves OVP in $O\left( {n}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$ time as soon as $d = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$ ,for any given constant $\epsilon  > 0$ . Indeed,such an algorithm would imply that the Strong Exponential Time Hypothesis (SETH) is true [56].

本文首个成果是基于正交向量问题(OVP)猜想的近似有符号/无符号内积相似度连接的条件性下界。该问题需要判定两个各含$n$个向量的集合$A,B \subseteq$$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$中是否存在满足${x}^{T}y = 0$的$x \in  A$和$y \in  B$。学界猜想当$d = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$时（其中$\epsilon  > 0$为任意给定常数），不存在能在$O\left( {n}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$时间内求解OVP的算法——该结论若成立将意味着强指数时间假设(SETH)为真[56]。

Many recent hardness results rely on reductions from OVP, however we believe ours is the first example of using the conjecture to show the conditional hardness for an approximate problem. In particular we show the following result:

近年许多硬度结果都基于OVP归约，但我们首次利用该猜想证明了近似问题的条件性硬度。具体而言我们得到：

THEOREM 1. Let $\alpha  > 0$ be given and consider sets of vectors $P,Q$ with $\left| Q\right|  = n,\left| P\right|  = {n}^{\alpha }$ . Suppose there exists a constant $\epsilon  > 0$ and an algorithm with running time at most ${d}^{O\left( 1\right) }{n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }$ ,when $d$ and $n$ are sufficiently large and for all $s > 0$ ,for at least one of the following IPS join problems.

定理1。给定$\alpha  > 0$，考虑向量集$P,Q$（其中$\left| Q\right|  = n,\left| P\right|  = {n}^{\alpha }$）。若存在常数$\epsilon  > 0$和算法在$d$和$n$足够大时对所有$s > 0$能在${d}^{O\left( 1\right) }{n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }$时间内求解以下至少一种内积相似度连接问题：

1. Signed(cs,s)join of $P,Q \subseteq  \{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ where $c > 0$ .

1. $P,Q \subseteq  \{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$的有符号(cs,s)连接，其中$c > 0$。

2. Unsigned(cs,s)join of $P,Q \subseteq  \{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ where $c =$ ${e}^{-o\left( {\sqrt{\log n}/\log \log n}\right) }.$

2. $P,Q \subseteq  \{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$的无符号(cs,s)连接，其中$c =$ ${e}^{-o\left( {\sqrt{\log n}/\log \log n}\right) }.$

3. Unsigned(cs,s)join of $P,Q \subseteq  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ where $c = 1 -$ $o\left( 1\right)$ .

3. $P,Q \subseteq  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$的无符号(cs,s)连接，其中$c = 1 -$ $o\left( 1\right)$ 。

Then the OVP conjecture is false.

则OVP猜想不成立。

Discussion. For the search problem, Theorem 1 implies that, assuming the OVP conjecture, there does not exist a data structure for signed/unsigned(cs,s)inner product search with ${\left( nd\right) }^{O\left( 1\right) }$ construction time and ${n}^{1 - \epsilon }{d}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ query time,for constant $\epsilon  > 0$ . This follows by considering a join instance with $\alpha$ constant small enough that we can build the data structure on $P$ in time $o\left( {nd}\right)$ . We can then query over all the points of $Q$ in time ${n}^{1 + \alpha \left( {1 - \epsilon }\right) }{d}^{O\left( 1\right) }$ ,contradicting the OVP conjecture. Our result can be seen as an explanation of why all LSH schemes for IPS have failed to provide sub-linear query times for small $s$ . As the theorem however does not cover the case where $c$ is very small,e.g. ${n}^{-\delta }$ for unsigned $\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ ,we show in Section 4 that for such approximation requirements, there are indeed useful data structures.

讨论。对于搜索问题，定理1表明：在OVP猜想成立的前提下，不存在具有${\left( nd\right) }^{O\left( 1\right) }$构建时间和${n}^{1 - \epsilon }{d}^{O\left( 1\right) }$查询时间的有符号/无符号(cs,s)内积搜索数据结构（其中$\epsilon  > 0$为常数）。这个结论是通过考虑一个连接实例得出的，该实例的$\alpha$常数足够小，使得我们能在$P$时间内完成$o\left( {nd}\right)$上的数据结构构建。随后我们可以在$Q$时间内查询${n}^{1 + \alpha \left( {1 - \epsilon }\right) }{d}^{O\left( 1\right) }$的所有点，这与OVP猜想矛盾。我们的研究结果可以解释为何所有IPS的LSH方案都无法为较小的$s$提供次线性查询时间。由于该定理未涵盖$c$极小的情况（例如无符号$\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$的${n}^{-\delta }$），我们在第4节证明了对于这类近似要求，确实存在实用的数据结构。

We stress that the hardness result holds for algorithms solving signed/unsigned(cs,s)joins for any $c$ in the specified range and all $s > 0$ . It is possible to show complete relations between hard values of $c,s$ and $d$ ,but for the sake of clearness, we have preferred to optimize the largest range of hard $c$ ’s. For intuition we can say,that the exact instances of(cs,s) joins that are hard,turn out to be the ones where $s/d$ and ${cs}/d$ are very small,that is when we have to distinguish nearly orthogonal vectors from very nearly orthogonal vectors. If we inspect the proofs of Theorem 1 and Lemma 3, we see that for unsigned join in $\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ ,the hard case has ${cs}/d$ around ${n}^{1/\log \log n}$ . Similarly for $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ join, ${cs}$ ends up at just barely $\omega \left( 1\right)$ ,while the $d$ is as high as ${n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$ . It is interesting to note for $\{ 0,1\}$ that if ${cs}$ had been slightly lower,at $O\left( 1\right)$ ,we could have solved the OVP problem exact in subquadratic time using an $n\left( \begin{matrix} {n}^{o\left( 1\right) } \\  O\left( 1\right)  \end{matrix}\right)  = {n}^{1 + o\left( 1\right) }$ algorithm.

需要强调的是，该硬度结果适用于在指定范围内对所有$s > 0$和任意$c$求解有符号/无符号(cs,s)连接的算法。虽然可以展示$c,s$与$d$硬度值之间的完整关系，但为了清晰起见，我们优先优化了最大范围的硬$c$值。直观而言，(cs,s)连接中难以处理的实例恰好是$s/d$和${cs}/d$极小的情形——即需要区分近乎正交的向量与极度接近正交的向量。通过分析定理1和引理3的证明可见，在$\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$中进行无符号连接时，硬度案例的${cs}/d$约为${n}^{1/\log \log n}$；而对于$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$连接，${cs}$最终仅略高于$\omega \left( 1\right)$，而$d$高达${n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$。值得注意的是，对于$\{ 0,1\}$而言，若${cs}$稍低于$O\left( 1\right)$，我们本可采用$n\left( \begin{matrix} {n}^{o\left( 1\right) } \\  O\left( 1\right)  \end{matrix}\right)  = {n}^{1 + o\left( 1\right) }$算法在次二次时间内精确求解OVP问题。

It is interesting to compare our conditional lower bound to the recent upper bounds by Karppa et al. [29], who get sub-quadratic running time for unsigned join of normalized vectors in $\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ ,when $\log \left( {s/d}\right) /\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right)$ is a constant smaller than $1.{}^{2}$ Our next Theorem 2 shows that we cannot hope to do much better than this, though it does not completely close the gap. A hardness result for $\log \left( {s/d}\right) /\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right)  = 1 - o\left( 1\right)$ is still an interesting open problem. However, while the algorithm of Karppa et al. works even for dimension ${n}^{1/3}$ ,our bound only requires the dimension to be slightly larger than polylog, so it may well be that their algorithm is optimal, while another algorithm with a higher dependency on the dimension matches our bound from above.

将我们的条件性下界与Karppa等人[29]近期提出的上界进行对比颇具意义，他们在$\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$中实现了归一化向量无符号连接的次二次运行时间，当$\log \left( {s/d}\right) /\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right)$是小于$1.{}^{2}$的常数时。我们的定理2表明，虽然无法完全弥合差距，但很难指望取得比这更好的结果。关于$\log \left( {s/d}\right) /\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right)  = 1 - o\left( 1\right)$的硬度证明仍是一个有趣的开放性问题。值得注意的是，Karppa等人的算法甚至在维度${n}^{1/3}$时仍有效，而我们的下界仅要求维度略大于多对数级，因此他们的算法很可能是最优的，而另一种对维度依赖更高的算法可能从上界匹配我们的结果。

THEOREM 2. Let $\alpha  > 0$ be given and consider sets of vectors $P,Q$ with $\left| Q\right|  = n,\left| P\right|  = {n}^{\alpha }$ . Suppose there exists a constant $\epsilon  > 0$ and an algorithm with running time at most ${d}^{O\left( 1\right) }{n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }$ ,when $d$ and $n$ are sufficiently large and for all $s > 0$ ,for at least one of the following IPS join problems:

定理2. 给定$\alpha  > 0$，考虑具有$\left| Q\right|  = n,\left| P\right|  = {n}^{\alpha }$的向量集$P,Q$。假设存在常数$\epsilon  > 0$和运行时间至多为${d}^{O\left( 1\right) }{n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }$的算法，当$d$和$n$足够大时，对于以下至少一个IPS连接问题对所有$s > 0$成立：

1. Unsigned(cs,s)over $\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ where $\frac{\log \left( {s/d}\right) }{\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right) } = 1 -$ $o\left( {1/\sqrt{\log n}}\right)$

1. 在$\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$上的无符号(cs,s)连接，其中$\frac{\log \left( {s/d}\right) }{\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right) } = 1 -$$o\left( {1/\sqrt{\log n}}\right)$

2. Unsigned(cs,s)over $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ where $\frac{\log \left( {s/d}\right) }{\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right) } = 1 -$ $o\left( {1/\log n}\right)$

2. 在$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$上的无符号(cs,s)连接，其中$\frac{\log \left( {s/d}\right) }{\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right) } = 1 -$$o\left( {1/\log n}\right)$

Then the OVP conjecture is false.

则OVP猜想不成立。

The $\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ case seems to be harder than the $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ case. In fact Valiant [51] reduces the general case of $P,Q \subseteq  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$ to the case $P,Q \subseteq  \{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ using the Charikar hyperplane LSH [15]. Another piece of evidence is that we can achieve runtime ${n}^{1 + \frac{\log \left( {s/d}\right) }{\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right) }}$ using LSH for $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ ,but it is not known to be possible for $\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ . Furthermore there appears to be some hope for even better data dependent LSH, as we show in Section 4.2. The $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ case is particularly interesting, as it is occurs often in practice, for example when the vectors represent sets. A better understanding of the upper and lower bounds for this case is a nice open problem. For an elaborate comparison of the different upper and lower bounds, see Table 1.

$\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$情形似乎比$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$情形更复杂。事实上Valiant[51]通过Charikar超平面LSH[15]将$P,Q \subseteq  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$的一般情况归约到$P,Q \subseteq  \{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$情形。另一个证据是我们可以利用LSH对$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$实现${n}^{1 + \frac{\log \left( {s/d}\right) }{\log \left( {{cs}/d}\right) }}$运行时间，但对$\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$尚未知是否可行。如第4.2节所示，对于数据依赖型LSH的进一步优化仍存在可能。$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$情形特别值得关注，因其在实践中频繁出现，例如当向量表示集合时。深入理解该情形的上下界是个很好的开放性问题。不同上下界的详细对比见表1。

Techniques. From a technical point of view, the proof uses a number of different algebraic techniques to expand the gap between orthogonal and non-orthogonal vectors from the OVP problem. For the $\{  - 1,1\}$ we use an enhanced, deterministic version of the "Chebyshev embedding" [51], while for the interesting $\{ 0,1\}$ part,we initiate a study of embeddings for restricted alphabets.

技术方法。从技术角度看，该证明运用了多种代数技巧来扩大OVP问题中正交与非正交向量间的差距。对于$\{  - 1,1\}$，我们采用确定性增强版的"切比雪夫嵌入"[51]，而针对关键的$\{ 0,1\}$部分，我们开创了受限字母表嵌入的研究。

## Inner product LSH lower bounds

## 内积LSH下界

In the second part of the paper we focus on LSH functions for signed and unsigned IPS. We investigate the gap between the collision probability ${P}_{1}$ of vectors with inner product (or absolute inner product) larger than $s$ and the collision probability ${P}_{2}$ of vectors with inner product (or absolute inner product) smaller than ${cs}$ . As a special case,we get the impossibility result in $\left\lbrack  {{39},{45}}\right\rbrack$ ,that there cannot exist an asymmetric LSH for unbounded query vectors. Specifically we get the following theorem:

在论文第二部分，我们重点研究带符号与无符号内积相似性搜索(IPS)的局部敏感哈希(LSH)函数。我们探究了内积(或绝对内积)大于$s$的向量碰撞概率${P}_{1}$与内积(或绝对内积)小于${cs}$的向量碰撞概率${P}_{2}$之间的差距。作为特例，我们得出$\left\lbrack  {{39},{45}}\right\rbrack$中的不可能性结果：对于无界查询向量不存在非对称LSH。具体而言，我们得到以下定理：

THEOREM 3. Consider an $\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$ -asymmetric LSH for signed IPS when data and query domains are d-dimensional balls with unit radius and radius $U$ respectively. Then,the following upper bounds on ${P}_{1} - {P}_{2}$ apply:

定理3. 当数据域和查询域分别为单位半径和$U$半径的d维球体时，考虑带符号IPS的$\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$-非对称LSH，则${P}_{1} - {P}_{2}$存在以下上界：

1. if $d \geq  1$ and $s \leq  \min \{ {cU},U/(4\sqrt{d}\}$ ,we have ${P}_{1} - {P}_{2} =$ $O\left( {1/\log \left( {d{\log }_{1/c}\left( {U/s}\right) }\right) }\right)$ for signed and unsigned IPS;

1. 若$d \geq  1$且$s \leq  \min \{ {cU},U/(4\sqrt{d}\}$，则带符号与无符号IPS满足${P}_{1} - {P}_{2} =$$O\left( {1/\log \left( {d{\log }_{1/c}\left( {U/s}\right) }\right) }\right)$；

2. if $d \geq  2$ and $s \leq  U/\left( {2d}\right)$ ,we have ${P}_{1} - {P}_{2} =$ $O\left( {1/\log \left( {{dU}/\left( {s\left( {1 - c}\right) }\right) }\right) }\right)$ for signed IPS;

2. 若$d \geq  2$且$s \leq  U/\left( {2d}\right)$，则带符号IPS满足${P}_{1} - {P}_{2} =$$O\left( {1/\log \left( {{dU}/\left( {s\left( {1 - c}\right) }\right) }\right) }\right)$；

3. if $d > \Theta \left( {{U}^{5}/\left( {{c}^{2}{s}^{5}}\right) }\right)$ and $s \leq  U/8$ ,we have ${P}_{1} - {P}_{2} =$ $O\left( \sqrt{s/U}\right)$ for signed and unsigned IPS.

3. 若$d > \Theta \left( {{U}^{5}/\left( {{c}^{2}{s}^{5}}\right) }\right)$且$s \leq  U/8$，则带符号与无符号IPS满足${P}_{1} - {P}_{2} =$$O\left( \sqrt{s/U}\right)$。

It follows that,for any given dimension $d$ ,there cannot exist an asymmetric ${LSH}$ when the query domain is unbounded.

由此可得，对于任意给定维度$d$，当查询域无界时不存在${LSH}$非对称LSH。

Discussion. The upper bounds for ${P}_{1} - {P}_{2}$ translate into lower bounds for the $\rho$ factor,as soon as ${P}_{2}$ is fixed. To the best of our knowledge,this is the first lower bound on $\rho$ that holds for asymmetric LSH. Indeed, previous results [37, 40] have investigated lower bounds for symmetric LSH and it is not clear if they can be extended to the asymmetric case.

讨论. 只要${P}_{2}$固定，${P}_{1} - {P}_{2}$的上界即可转化为$\rho$因子的下界。据我们所知，这是首个适用于非对称LSH的$\rho$下界。先前研究[37,40]仅探讨了对称LSH的下界，尚不清楚能否推广至非对称情形。

Techniques. The starting point of our proof is the same as in [39]: Use a collision matrix given by two sequences of data and query vectors that force the gap to be small. The proof in [39] then applies an asymptotic analysis of the margin complexity of this matrix [49], and it shows that for any given value of ${P}_{1} - {P}_{2}$ there are sufficiently large data and query domains for which the gap must be smaller. Unfortunately, due to their analysis, an upper bound on the gap for a given radius $U$ of the query domain is not possible, and so the result does not rule out very large gaps for small domains. Our method also highlights a dependency of the gap on the dimension, which is missing in [39]. In addition, our proof holds for $d = 1$ and only uses purely combinatorial arguments.

技术方法. 我们的证明起点与[39]相同：使用由数据和查询向量序列构成的碰撞矩阵迫使差距缩小。[39]中的证明随后对该矩阵的边际复杂度进行渐近分析[49]，表明对于任意给定${P}_{1} - {P}_{2}$值，存在足够大的数据域和查询域使得差距必须更小。遗憾的是，由于其分析方法，无法得出给定查询域半径$U$时的差距上界，因此该结果不能排除小域存在极大差距的可能性。我们的方法还揭示了差距对维度的依赖性，这是[39]未涉及的。此外，我们的证明适用于$d = 1$且仅使用纯组合论证。

## IPS upper bounds

## IPS上界

In the third part we provide some insights on the upper bound side. We first show that it is possible to improve the asymmetric LSH in $\left\lbrack  {{39},{46}}\right\rbrack$ by just plugging the best known data structure for Approximate Near Neighbor for ${\ell }_{2}$ on a sphere [9] into the reduction in [39,12]. With data/query points in the unit ball,this LSH reaches $\rho  =$ $\left( {1 - s}\right) /\left( {1 + \left( {1 - {2c}}\right) s}\right)$ . In the $\{ 0,1\}$ domain,this LSH improves upon the state of the art [46] for some ranges of $c$ and $s$ .

第三部分我们针对上界问题提出若干见解。首先证明通过将球面${\ell }_{2}$近似最近邻的最佳已知数据结构[9]直接套用到[39,12]的归约中，可以改进$\left\lbrack  {{39},{46}}\right\rbrack$中的非对称局部敏感哈希。当数据/查询点位于单位球内时，该LSH达到$\rho  =$$\left( {1 - s}\right) /\left( {1 + \left( {1 - {2c}}\right) s}\right)$。在$\{ 0,1\}$领域，该LSH在某些$c$和$s$范围内超越了现有技术[46]。

Then we show how to circumvent the impossibility results in $\left\lbrack  {{39},{45}}\right\rbrack$ by showing that there exists a symmetric LSH when the data and query space coincide by allowing the bounds on collision probability to not hold when the data and query vectors are identical.

随后我们展示了如何规避$\left\lbrack  {{39},{45}}\right\rbrack$中的不可能性结果：当数据空间与查询空间重合时，通过允许数据向量与查询向量相同时碰撞概率边界失效，从而构造出对称LSH。

We conclude by describing a data structure based on the linear sketches for ${\ell }_{p}$ in [5] for unsigned(cs,s)search: for any given $0 < \kappa  \leq  1/2$ ,the data structure yields a $c =$ $1/{n}^{\kappa }$ approximation with $\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{2 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$ construction time and $\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$ query time. Theorem 1 suggests that we cannot substantially improve the approximation with similar performance.

最后我们描述了基于文献[5]中${\ell }_{p}$线性素描的无符号(cs,s)搜索数据结构：对于任意给定$0 < \kappa  \leq  1/2$，该结构可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{2 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$构建时间和$\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$查询时间内实现$c =$$1/{n}^{\kappa }$近似。定理1表明我们无法在保持同等性能的情况下显著提升该近似度。

The last data structure allows us to reach truly sub-quadratic time for $c = 1/{n}^{\kappa }$ for the unsigned version in the $\{ 0,1\}$ and $\{  - 1,1\}$ domains for all value $s$ . We note that the result in [29] also reaches subquadratic time for the $\{  - 1,1\}$ case. However,it exploits fast matrix multiplication, whereas our data structure does not.

最终数据结构使我们在$\{ 0,1\}$和$\{  - 1,1\}$领域对所有$s$值的无符号版本$c = 1/{n}^{\kappa }$实现真正的次二次时间复杂度。需注意文献[29]的结果在$\{  - 1,1\}$情况下同样达到次二次时间，但其利用了快速矩阵乘法技术，而我们的数据结构无需此技术。

### 1.2 Previous work

### 1.2 前人工作

## Similarity join

## 相似性连接

Similarity join problems have been extensively studied in the database literature (e.g. [17, 18, 19, 26, 27, 34, 36, 47, ${52},{53},{58}\rbrack )$ ,as well as in information retrieval (e.g. [14,21, 59]), and knowledge discovery (e.g. [3, 13, 54, 60, 62]). Most of the literature considers algorithms for particular metrics (where the task is to join tuples that are near according to the metric), or particular application areas (e.g. near-duplicate detection). A distinction is made between methods that approximate distances in the sense that we only care about distances up to some factor $c > 1$ ,and methods that consider exact distances. Known exact methods do not guarantee subquadratic running time. It was recently shown how approximate LSH-based similarity join can be made I/O-efficient [41].

相似性连接问题在数据库领域（如[17,18,19,26,27,34,36,47,${52},{53},{58}\rbrack )$）、信息检索（如[14,21,59]）及知识发现（如[3,13,54,60,62]）研究中被广泛探讨。多数文献关注特定度量下的算法（要求按该度量连接相近元组）或特定应用领域（如近重复检测）。研究方法分为两类：仅关注$c > 1$倍距离内的近似距离方法，以及考虑精确距离的方法。已知精确方法无法保证次二次时间复杂度。近期研究[41]展示了如何使基于LSH的近似相似性连接具备I/O效率。

## IPS join

## IPS连接

The inner product similarity for the case of normalized vectors is known as "cosine similarity" and it is well understood $\left\lbrack  {{16},{33},{43}}\right\rbrack$ . While the general case where vectors may have any length appears theoretically challenging, practically efficient indexes for unsigned search were proposed in [43, 30], based on tree data structures combined with a branch-and-bound space partitioning technique similar to $k$ -d trees, and in [12] based on principal component axes trees. For document term vectors Low and Zheng [35] showed that unsigned search can be sped up using matrix compression ideas. However, as many similarity search problems, the exact version considered in these papers suffers from the curse of dimensionality [55].

归一化向量情况下的内积相似度被称为"余弦相似度"，这一概念已被充分理解$\left\lbrack  {{16},{33},{43}}\right\rbrack$。虽然理论上处理任意长度向量的通用情况具有挑战性，但文献[43,30]提出了基于树形数据结构与类似$k$-d树的分支定界空间划分技术的无符号搜索高效索引方案，文献[12]则提出了基于主成分轴树的方案。对于文档词项向量，Low和Zheng[35]证明利用矩阵压缩思想可加速无符号搜索。然而与多数相似性搜索问题相同，这些论文研究的精确版本仍受维度灾难[55]困扰。

The efficiency of approximate IPS approaches based on LSH is studied in $\left\lbrack  {{45},{39}}\right\rbrack$ . These papers show that a traditional LSH does exist when the data domain is the unit ball and the query domain is the unit sphere, while it does not exist when both domains are the unit ball (the claim automatically applies to any radius by suitably normalizing vectors). On the other hand an asymmetric LSH exists in this case, but it cannot be extended to the unbounded domain ${\mathbb{R}}^{d}$ . An asymmetric LSH for binary inner product is proposed in [46]. The unsigned version is equivalent to the signed one when the vectors are non-negative.

基于局部敏感哈希(LSH)的近似内积相似度搜索效率在$\left\lbrack  {{45},{39}}\right\rbrack$中得到研究。这些论文表明：当数据域为单位球且查询域为单位超球面时存在传统LSH方案；而当两者均为单位球时则不存在（通过适当向量归一化，该结论适用于任意半径情况）。另一方面，此时存在非对称LSH方案，但无法扩展至无界域${\mathbb{R}}^{d}$。文献[46]提出了针对二元内积的非对称LSH。当向量非负时，无符号版本与有符号版本等价。

## Algebraic techniques

## 代数技术

Finally, recent breakthroughs have been made on the (unsigned) join problem in the approximate case as well as the exact. Valiant [51] showed how to reduce the problem to matrix multiplication,when ${cs} \approx  O\left( \sqrt{n}\right)$ and $s \approx  O\left( n\right)$ , significantly improving on the asymptotic time complexity of approaches based on LSH. Recently this technique was improved by Karppa et al. [29], who also generalized the sub-quadratic running time to the case when $\log \left( s\right) /\log \left( {cs}\right)$ is small. In another surprising development Alman and Williams [4] showed that for $d = O\left( {\log n}\right)$ dimensions,truly subquadratic algorithms for the exact IPS join problem on binary vectors is possible. Their algorithm is based on an algebraic technique (probabilistic polynomials) and tools from circuit complexity.

最近在近似和精确情况下的（无符号）连接问题均取得突破性进展。Valiant[51]证明了当${cs} \approx  O\left( \sqrt{n}\right)$和$s \approx  O\left( n\right)$时，如何将该问题转化为矩阵乘法问题，显著改进了基于LSH方法的渐近时间复杂度。Karppa等人[29]近期改进该技术，并将次二次运行时间推广至$\log \left( s\right) /\log \left( {cs}\right)$较小的情况。另一突破性进展中，Alman和Williams[4]证明对于$d = O\left( {\log n}\right)$维情况，二元向量精确内积连接问题存在真正的次二次算法，其方案基于代数技术（概率多项式）和电路复杂性工具。

## 2. HARDNESS OF IPS JOIN

## 2. 内积连接问题的计算难度

We first provide an overview of OVP and of the associated conjecture in next Section 2.1. Then, in Section 2.2, we prove Theorems 1 and 2 by describing some reductions from the OVP to signed/unsigned joins.

我们首先在第2.1节概述正交向量问题(OVP)及相关猜想，随后在第2.2节通过描述从OVP到有符号/无符号连接的若干归约，证明定理1和2。

### 2.1 Preliminaries

### 2.1 预备知识

The Orthogonal Vectors Problem is defined as follows:

正交向量问题定义如下：

DEFINITION 3 (OVP). Given two sets $P$ and $Q$ ,each one containing $n$ vectors in $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ ,detect if there exist vectors $p \in  P$ and $q \in  Q$ such that ${p}^{T}q = 0$ .

定义3(OVP). 给定两个集合$P$和$Q$，各包含$n$个$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$维向量，检测是否存在向量$p \in  P$与$q \in  Q$满足${p}^{T}q = 0$。

OVP derives its hardness from the Strong Exponential Time Hypothesis (Williams [56]), but could potentially be true even if SETH is not. We will therefore assume the following plausible conjecture: ${}^{3}$

OVP的计算难度源于强指数时间假设(SETH)(Williams[56])，但即使SETH不成立，OVP仍可能成立。因此我们采用以下合理猜想：${}^{3}$

CONJECTURE 1 ([56]). For every constant $\epsilon  > 0$ ,there is no algorithm for OVP with $\left| P\right|  = \left| Q\right|  = n$ and dimension $d = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$ running in $O\left( {n}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$ time.

猜想1([56]). 对于任意常数$\epsilon  > 0$，当$\left| P\right|  = \left| Q\right|  = n$且维度为$d = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$时，不存在$O\left( {n}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$时间复杂度的OVP算法。

The conjecture does not hold for $d = O\left( {\log n}\right)$ : recently Abboud et al. [1] have proposed an algorithm for OVP running in time ${n}^{2 - 1/O\left( {\gamma {\log }^{2}\gamma }\right) }$ ,when $d = \gamma \log n$ . Thus,in order to disprove OVP, an algorithm must be strongly subquadratic when $d = \gamma \log n$ for all constant $\gamma  > 0$ .

该猜想不适用于$d = O\left( {\log n}\right)$情况：近期Abboud等人[1]提出当$d = \gamma \log n$时时间复杂度为${n}^{2 - 1/O\left( {\gamma {\log }^{2}\gamma }\right) }$的OVP算法。因此，要证伪OVP猜想，算法必须在$d = \gamma \log n$对所有常数$\gamma  > 0$均具有强次二次时间复杂度。

The OVP conjecture, as usually stated, concerns the case where the two sets have equal size. However in order to eventually show hardness for data structures, we consider the following generalization of OVP:

通常表述的OVP猜想针对两集合规模相等的情况。但为最终证明数据结构的计算难度，我们考虑以下OVP推广形式：

LEMMA 1 (GENERALIZED OVP). Suppose there exist constants $\epsilon  > 0$ and $\alpha  > 0$ ,and an algorithm such that for $d = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$ the algorithm solves OVP for $P,Q \subseteq  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ where $\left| P\right|  = {n}^{\alpha }$ and $\left| Q\right|  = n$ in time $O\left( {n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }\right)$ . Then ${OVP}$ is false.

引理1（广义OVP）。假设存在常数$\epsilon  > 0$和$\alpha  > 0$，以及一个算法，使得对于$d = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$，该算法能在时间$O\left( {n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }\right)$内求解$P,Q \subseteq  \{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$的OVP问题，其中$\left| P\right|  = {n}^{\alpha }$且$\left| Q\right|  = n$。则${OVP}$不成立。

Proof. Without loss of generality assume $\alpha  \leq  1$ (otherwise is enough to invert the role of $P$ and $Q$ ). Suppose we have an algorithm running in time $O\left( {n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }\right)$ for some $\epsilon  > 0$ . Take a normal OVP instance with $\left| P\right|  = \left| Q\right|  = n$ . Split $P$ into chunks ${P}_{i}$ of size ${n}^{\alpha }$ and run the OVP algorithm on all pairs $\left( {{P}_{i},Q}\right)$ . By our assumption this takes time ${n}^{1 - \alpha }O\left( {n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }\right)  = O\left( {n}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$ ,contradicting OVP.

证明。不失一般性假设$\alpha  \leq  1$（否则只需交换$P$与$Q$的角色）。假设存在一个在时间$O\left( {n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }\right)$内运行的算法，其中$\epsilon  > 0$。取一个标准OVP实例$\left| P\right|  = \left| Q\right|  = n$，将$P$分割为大小为${n}^{\alpha }$的块${P}_{i}$，并对所有块对$\left( {{P}_{i},Q}\right)$运行OVP算法。根据假设，这将耗时${n}^{1 - \alpha }O\left( {n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }\right)  = O\left( {n}^{2 - \epsilon }\right)$，与OVP矛盾。

### 2.2 Reductions from OVP

### 2.2 基于OVP的归约

In this section we prove Theorems 1 and 2, about hardness of approximate joins. We will do this by showing the existence of certain efficient embeddings of the OVP problem. We need the following definition:

本节我们将通过构造OVP问题的高效嵌入映射，证明关于近似连接难度的定理1和定理2。需要以下定义：

DEFINITION 4. An unsigned $\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2},{cs},s}\right)$ -embedding into the domain $\mathcal{A}$ is a pair of functions $\left( {f,g}\right)  : \{ 0,1{\} }^{{d}_{1}} \rightarrow  {\mathcal{A}}^{{d}_{2}}$ , where ${d}_{1} \leq  {d}_{2},\mathcal{A} \subseteq  \mathbb{R}$ ,and for any $x,y \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{{d}_{1}}$ :

定义4。无符号$\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2},{cs},s}\right)$-嵌入到域$\mathcal{A}$是指函数对$\left( {f,g}\right)  : \{ 0,1{\} }^{{d}_{1}} \rightarrow  {\mathcal{A}}^{{d}_{2}}$，其中${d}_{1} \leq  {d}_{2},\mathcal{A} \subseteq  \mathbb{R}$，且对于任意$x,y \in  \{ 0,1{\} }^{{d}_{1}}$满足：

$$
\left| {f{\left( x\right) }^{T}g\left( y\right) }\right|  \geq  s\;\text{ when }\;{x}^{T}y = 0
$$

$$
\left| {f{\left( x\right) }^{T}g\left( y\right) }\right|  \leq  {cs}\;\text{ when }\;{x}^{T}y \geq  1
$$

A signed $\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2},{cs},s}\right)$ -embedding is analogous,but without the absolute value symbols. We further require that the functions $f$ and $g$ can be evaluated in time polynomial to ${d}_{2}$ .

有符号$\left( {{d}_{1},{d}_{2},{cs},s}\right)$-嵌入的定义类似，但无需绝对值符号。我们进一步要求函数$f$和$g$可在${d}_{2}$的多项式时间内求值。

Gap embeddings connect to the join problem, by the following technical lemma:

间隙嵌入通过以下技术引理与连接问题相关联：

LEMMA 2. Suppose there exist a join algorithm for (un)signed (cs,s)-join over $\mathcal{A}$ and a family of(un)signed $\left( {d,{2}^{o\left( d\right) },{cs},s}\right)$ - embeddings into $\mathcal{A}$ ,for all $d$ large enough,with the following properties:

引理2。假设存在$\mathcal{A}$上的（无）符号(cs,s)-连接算法，以及对于所有足够大的$d$，存在满足以下性质的（无）符号$\left( {d,{2}^{o\left( d\right) },{cs},s}\right)$-嵌入族：

- The embedding can be evaluated in time ${2}^{o\left( d\right) }$ .

- 嵌入映射可在时间${2}^{o\left( d\right) }$内求值

- For given constants $\alpha  \geq  0$ ,and $\epsilon  > 0$ ,the join algorithm has running time ${d}^{O\left( 1\right) }{n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }$ when $\left| Q\right|  = n$ and $\left| P\right|  =$ ${n}^{\alpha }$ for all $n$ and $d$ large enough.

- 对于给定常数$\alpha  \geq  0$和$\epsilon  > 0$，当$\left| Q\right|  = n$且$\left| P\right|  =$${n}^{\alpha }$对所有足够大的$n$和$d$成立时，连接算法运行时间为${d}^{O\left( 1\right) }{n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }$

Then OVP can be solved in ${n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }$ time,and the conjecture is false.

则OVP可在${n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon }$时间内求解，该猜想不成立。

Proof. First notice that for any function ${d}_{2} = {2}^{o\left( d\right) }$ we can take $d = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$ growing slowly enough that ${d}_{2} = {n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$ .

证明。首先注意对于任意函数${d}_{2} = {2}^{o\left( d\right) }$，我们可取增长足够缓慢的$d = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$使得${d}_{2} = {n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$。

To see this,assume ${d}_{2}\left( d\right)  = {2}^{f\left( d\right) }$ where $f\left( d\right)  = o\left( d\right)$ . Then we have $f\left( {d\left( n\right) }\right)  = o\left( {d\left( n\right) }\right)  = o\left( 1\right) d\left( n\right)$ that is $f\left( {d\left( n\right) }\right)  =$ ${f}^{\prime }\left( n\right) d\left( n\right)$ for some ${f}^{\prime }\left( n\right)  = o\left( 1\right)$ . Now take $d\left( n\right)  = \frac{\log n}{\sqrt{{f}^{\prime }\left( n\right) }} =$ $\omega \left( {\log n}\right)$ and we get ${d}_{2}\left( {d\left( n\right) }\right)  = {2}^{{f}^{\prime }\left( n\right) d\left( n\right) } = {2}^{\sqrt{{f}^{\prime }\left( n\right) }\log n} =$ ${n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$ as desired.

要理解这一点，假设${d}_{2}\left( d\right)  = {2}^{f\left( d\right) }$其中$f\left( d\right)  = o\left( d\right)$。于是我们得到$f\left( {d\left( n\right) }\right)  = o\left( {d\left( n\right) }\right)  = o\left( 1\right) d\left( n\right)$即对于某些${f}^{\prime }\left( n\right)  = o\left( 1\right)$存在$f\left( {d\left( n\right) }\right)  =$${f}^{\prime }\left( n\right) d\left( n\right)$。现取$d\left( n\right)  = \frac{\log n}{\sqrt{{f}^{\prime }\left( n\right) }} =$$\omega \left( {\log n}\right)$，便可如预期获得${d}_{2}\left( {d\left( n\right) }\right)  = {2}^{{f}^{\prime }\left( n\right) d\left( n\right) } = {2}^{\sqrt{{f}^{\prime }\left( n\right) }\log n} =$${n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$。

Hence,there is a family of $\left( {d\left( n\right) ,{d}_{2}\left( n\right) ,{cs},s}\right)$ -gap embed-dings for all $n$ ,where $d\left( n\right)  = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$ and ${d}_{2}\left( n\right)  = {n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$ . By the generalized OVP lemma,for large enough $n$ ,we can thus take a hard OVP instance with $\left| Q\right|  = n,\left| P\right|  = {n}^{\alpha }$ and dimension $d\left( n\right)$ . Apply the corresponding gap embedding, (f,g),to the instance,such that the maximum inner product between $f\left( P\right) ,g\left( Q\right)$ is at least $s$ if the OVP instance has an orthogonal pair and $\leq  {cs}$ otherwise. Now run the algorithm for (un)signed(cs,s)join on $\left( {f\left( P\right) ,g\left( Q\right) }\right)$ ,which produces the orthogonal pair, if it exists.

因此，对于所有$n$存在一族$\left( {d\left( n\right) ,{d}_{2}\left( n\right) ,{cs},s}\right)$-间隙嵌入，其中$d\left( n\right)  = \omega \left( {\log n}\right)$且${d}_{2}\left( n\right)  = {n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$。根据广义OVP引理，对于足够大的$n$，我们可以选取一个具有$\left| Q\right|  = n,\left| P\right|  = {n}^{\alpha }$和维度$d\left( n\right)$的困难OVP实例。对该实例应用相应的间隙嵌入(f,g)，使得当OVP实例存在正交对时，$f\left( P\right) ,g\left( Q\right)$之间的最大内积至少为$s$，否则为$\leq  {cs}$。现在对$\left( {f\left( P\right) ,g\left( Q\right) }\right)$运行(无)符号(cs,s)连接算法，若存在正交对，该算法将输出该正交对。

It remains to show that the running time of the above procedure is $O\left( {n}^{1 + \alpha  - {\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$ for some ${\epsilon }^{\prime } > 0$ . But this is easy, since by assumption, performing the embedding takes time ${n}^{1 + o\left( 1\right) }$ ,and running the algorithm on vectors of dimension ${n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$ takes time ${n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon  + o\left( 1\right) }$ . So letting ${\epsilon }^{\prime } = \epsilon /2$ suffices.

还需证明上述过程的运行时间对于某个${\epsilon }^{\prime } > 0$是$O\left( {n}^{1 + \alpha  - {\epsilon }^{\prime }}\right)$。这很容易，因为根据假设，执行嵌入需要时间${n}^{1 + o\left( 1\right) }$，而在维度${n}^{o\left( 1\right) }$的向量上运行算法需要时间${n}^{1 + \alpha  - \epsilon  + o\left( 1\right) }$。因此令${\epsilon }^{\prime } = \epsilon /2$即可满足。

The last ingredient we need to prove Theorems 1 and 2 is a suitable family of embeddings to use with Lemma 2.

证明定理1和定理2所需的最后一个要素，是要找到适合与引理2配合使用的嵌入族。

LEMMA 3. We can construct the following embeddings:

引理3. 我们可以构造以下嵌入：

1. A signed(d,4d - 4,0,4)-embedding into $\{  - 1,1\}$ .

1. 一个有符号(d,4d-4,0,4)-嵌入到$\{  - 1,1\}$。

2. An unsigned $\left( {d,{\left( 9d\right) }^{q},{\left( 2d\right) }^{q},{\left( 2d\right) }^{q}{e}^{q/\sqrt{d}}/2}\right)$ -embedding into $\{  - 1,1\}$ ,for any $q \geq  1$ and $d > 1$ .

2. 对于任意$q \geq  1$和$d > 1$，一个无符号$\left( {d,{\left( 9d\right) }^{q},{\left( 2d\right) }^{q},{\left( 2d\right) }^{q}{e}^{q/\sqrt{d}}/2}\right)$-嵌入到$\{  - 1,1\}$。

3. An unsigned $\left( {d,k{2}^{d/k},k - 1,k}\right)$ -embedding into $\{ 0,1\}$ , for any integer $1 \leq  k \leq  d$ .

3. 一个无符号的$\left( {d,k{2}^{d/k},k - 1,k}\right)$嵌入到$\{ 0,1\}$中，对于任意整数$1 \leq  k \leq  d$。

Proof. We will use the following notation in our constructions: Let $x \boxplus  y$ be the concatenation of vectors $x$ and $y{;}^{4}$ Let ${x}^{n}$ mean $x$ concatenated with itself $n$ times ${;}^{5}$ And let $x \boxtimes  y$ mean the vectorial representation of the outer product $x{y}^{T}$ . Tensoring is interesting because of the following folklore property: ${\left( {x}_{1} \boxtimes  {x}_{2}\right) }^{T}\left( {{y}_{1} \boxtimes  {y}_{2}}\right)  = \operatorname{trace}{\left( {x}_{1}{x}_{2}^{T}\right) }^{T}\left( {{y}_{1}{y}_{2}^{T}}\right)  =$ trace ${x}_{2}\left( {{x}_{1}^{T}{y}_{1}}\right) {y}_{2}^{T} = \left( {{x}_{1}^{T}{y}_{1}}\right) \left( {{x}_{2}^{T}{y}_{2}}\right)$ .

## 3. LIMITATIONS OF LSH FOR IPS

## 3. 面向IPS的LSH局限性

We provide an upper bound on the gap between ${P}_{1}$ and ${P}_{2}$ for an $\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$ -asymmetric LSH for signed/unsigned IPS. For the sake of simplicity we assume the data and query domains to be the $d$ -dimensional balls of radius 1 and $U \geq  1$ , respectively. The bound holds for a fixed set of data vectors, so it applies also to data dependent LSH [9]. A consequence of our result is that there cannot exist an asymmetric LSH for any dimension $d \geq  1$ when the set of query vectors is unbounded, getting a result similar to that of [39], which however requires even the data space to be unbounded and $d \geq  2$ .

我们针对带符号/无符号IPS的$\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$-非对称LSH，给出了${P}_{1}$与${P}_{2}$间差距的上界。为简化起见，假设数据和查询域分别是半径为1和$U \geq  1$的$d$维球体。该界限适用于固定数据集，因此也适用于数据依赖型LSH[9]。我们的研究结果表明：当查询向量集无界时，任何维度$d \geq  1$下都不存在非对称LSH，这与[39]的结论类似，但后者还要求数据空间无界且$d \geq  2$。

We firsts show in Lemma 4 that the gap ${P}_{1} - {P}_{2}$ can be expressed as a function of the length $h$ of two sequences of query and data vectors with suitable collision properties. Then we provide the proof of the aforementioned Theorem 3, where we derive some of such sequences and then apply the lemma.

我们首先在引理4中证明：差距${P}_{1} - {P}_{2}$可表示为具有特定碰撞性质的查询向量序列与数据向量序列长度$h$的函数。继而通过构造此类序列并应用该引理，完成前述定理3的证明。

LEMMA 4. Suppose that there exists a sequence of data vectors $P = \left\{  {{p}_{0},\ldots ,{p}_{n - 1}}\right\}$ and a sequence of query vectors $Q = \left\{  {{q}_{0},\ldots ,{q}_{n - 1}}\right\}$ such that ${q}_{i}^{T}{p}_{j} \geq  s$ if $j \geq  i$ and ${q}_{i}^{T}{p}_{j} \leq$ ${cs}$ otherwise (resp., $\left| {{q}_{i}^{T}{p}_{j}}\right|  \geq  s$ if $j \geq  i$ and $\left| {{q}_{i}^{T}{p}_{j}}\right|  \leq  {cs}$ otherwise). Then any $\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$ -asymmetric LSH for signed IPS (resp.,unsigned IPS) must satisfy ${P}_{1} - {P}_{2} \leq$ $1/\left( {8\log n}\right)$ .

引理4. 若存在数据向量序列$P = \left\{  {{p}_{0},\ldots ,{p}_{n - 1}}\right\}$和查询向量序列$Q = \left\{  {{q}_{0},\ldots ,{q}_{n - 1}}\right\}$满足：当$j \geq  i$时${q}_{i}^{T}{p}_{j} \geq  s$，否则${q}_{i}^{T}{p}_{j} \leq$${cs}$（相应地，对于无符号IPS，当$j \geq  i$时$\left| {{q}_{i}^{T}{p}_{j}}\right|  \geq  s$，否则$\left| {{q}_{i}^{T}{p}_{j}}\right|  \leq  {cs}$）。则任何带符号IPS（相应无符号IPS）的$\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$-非对称LSH必须满足${P}_{1} - {P}_{2} \leq$$1/\left( {8\log n}\right)$。

Proof. For the sake of simplicity we assume that $n =$ ${2}^{\ell } - 1$ for some $\ell  \geq  1$ ; the assumption can be removed by introducing floor and ceiling operations in the proof. Let $\mathcal{H}$ denote an $\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$ -asymmetric LSH family of hash functions,and let $h$ be a function in $\mathcal{H}$ . The following argument works for signed and unsigned IPS.

证明. 为简化起见，假设对某$\ell  \geq  1$有$n =$${2}^{\ell } - 1$；该假设可通过在证明中引入取整运算消除。设$\mathcal{H}$表示$\left( {s,{cs},{P}_{1},{P}_{2}}\right)$-非对称LSH哈希函数族，$h$为$\mathcal{H}$中的函数。下述论证对带符号与无符号IPS均适用。

Consider the $n \times  n$ grid representing the collisions between $Q \times  P$ ,that is,a node(i,j)denotes the query-data vectors ${q}_{i}$ and ${p}_{j}$ . We say that a node(i,j),with $0 \leq  i,j < n$ ,collides under $h$ if vectors ${q}_{i}$ and ${p}_{j}$ collide under $h$ . We use lower triangle to refer to the part of the grid with $j \geq  i$ and ${P}_{1}$ -nodes to refer to the nodes within it; we refer to the remaining nodes as ${P}_{2}$ -nodes.

We define the mass ${m}_{i,j}$ of a node(i,j)to be the collision probability,under $\mathcal{H}$ ,of ${q}_{i}$ and ${p}_{j}$ . The shared mass ${m}_{i,j}^{s}$ is the sum of probabilities of all(i,j)- shared functions. ## 4. UPPER BOUNDS

## 4. 上界分析

This section contains three observations with implications for IPS join and its indexing version. We first notice in Section 4.1 that by plugging the best known LSH for ${\ell }_{2}$ distance on a sphere [9] into a reduction presented in [12, 39], we get a data structure based on LSH for signed IPS with search time exponent $\rho  = \left( {1 - s}\right) /\left( {1 + \left( {1 - {2c}}\right) s}\right)$ .

本节阐述三个对IPS连接及其索引版本有启示意义的观察结果。首先在第4.1节指出，通过将球面${\ell }_{2}$距离最著名的LSH（局部敏感哈希）[9]嵌入到[12,39]提出的归约框架中，我们得到了带符号IPS的LSH数据结构，其搜索时间指数为$\rho  = \left( {1 - s}\right) /\left( {1 + \left( {1 - {2c}}\right) s}\right)$。

Then, in Section 4.2, we show how to circumvent the results in $\left\lbrack  {{39},{45}}\right\rbrack$ showing that symmetric LSH is not possible when the data and query domains coincide (while an asymmetric LSH does exist). We use an slightly modified definition of LSH that disregards the collision probability of 1 for pairs of identical vectors, and assume that vectors are represented with finite precision. The LSH construction uses explicit incoherent matrices built using Reed-Solomon codes [38] to implement a symmetric version of the reduction in [12, 39].

随后在第4.2节，我们展示了如何规避$\left\lbrack  {{39},{45}}\right\rbrack$中的结论——当数据域与查询域重合时对称LSH不可行（尽管非对称LSH确实存在）。采用经微调的LSH定义（忽略相同向量对的碰撞概率1），并假设向量以有限精度表示。该LSH构造利用Reed-Solomon码[38]构建的显式非相干矩阵，实现了[12,39]归约的对称版本。

Finally,in Section 4.3 we solve unsigned(cs,s)join using linear sketches for ${\ell }_{p}$ norms from [5]. Given $\kappa  \geq  2$ we obtain a approximation factor $c \geq  1/{n}^{1/\kappa }$ using $\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{2 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$ time. Although this trade-off is not that strong, it is not far from the conditional lower bound in Theorem 1.

最后在第4.3节，我们采用[5]提出的${\ell }_{p}$范数线性草图解决了无符号(cs,s)连接问题。给定$\kappa  \geq  2$时，以$\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{2 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$时间获得$c \geq  1/{n}^{1/\kappa }$近似因子。虽然该权衡关系不强，但与定理1的条件下限相去不远。

### 4.1 Asymmetric LSH for signed IPS

### 4.1 带符号IPS的非对称LSH

We assume the data and query domains to be $d$ -dimensional balls with respective radius 1 and $U$ . Vectors are embedded into a $\left( {d + 2}\right)$ -dimensional unit sphere using the asymmetric map as in [39]: a data vector $p$ is mapped to $\left( {p,\sqrt{1 - \parallel p{\parallel }^{2}},0}\right)$ , while a query $q$ is mapped to $\left( {q/U,0,\sqrt{1 - \parallel q{\parallel }^{2}/{U}^{2}}}\right)$ . This transformation just scales the inner product by a factor $U$ ,and hence signed inner product search can be seen as an instance of ANN in ${\ell }_{2}$ with distance threshold $r =$ $\sqrt{2\left( {1 - s/U}\right) }$ and approximation ${c}^{\prime } = \sqrt{\left( {1 - {cs}/U}\right) /\left( {1 - s/U}\right) }$ . The latter can be solved in space $O\left( {{n}^{1 + \rho } + {dn}}\right)$ and query time $O\left( {n}^{\rho }\right)$ using the LSH construction of [9]. We get the following $\rho$ value (for the LSH gap as well as for the exponent of the running time):

我们假设数据域和查询域分别为$d$维球体，其半径分别为1和$U$。采用[39]中的非对称映射将向量嵌入$\left( {d + 2}\right)$维单位球面：数据向量$p$映射为$\left( {p,\sqrt{1 - \parallel p{\parallel }^{2}},0}\right)$，查询向量$q$映射为$\left( {q/U,0,\sqrt{1 - \parallel q{\parallel }^{2}/{U}^{2}}}\right)$。该变换仅将内积缩放$U$倍，因此带符号内积搜索可视为${\ell }_{2}$空间中的近似最近邻问题，其距离阈值为$r =$$\sqrt{2\left( {1 - s/U}\right) }$，近似比为${c}^{\prime } = \sqrt{\left( {1 - {cs}/U}\right) /\left( {1 - s/U}\right) }$。利用[9]的局部敏感哈希方案，可在$O\left( {{n}^{1 + \rho } + {dn}}\right)$空间和$O\left( {n}^{\rho }\right)$查询时间内求解。由此得到如下$\rho$值（既作为LSH间隙参数，也作为时间复杂度指数）：

$$
\rho  = \frac{1}{2{c}^{\prime 2} - 1} = \frac{1 - s/U}{1 + \left( {1 - {2c}}\right) s/U}. \tag{3}
$$

In Figure 2,we plot the $\rho$ values of three LSH constructions: the one proposed here (with $U = 1$ ),the one from [39], and the one from [46]. The latter works only for binary vectors. We point out that our bound is always stronger than the one from [39] and sometimes stronger than the one from [46], despite that the latter is tailored for binary vectors. The latter conclusion is somewhat surprising, since the data structure we obtain works for non-binary vectors as well.

图2展示了三种LSH方案的$\rho$值对比：本文方案（含$U = 1$参数）、[39]方案及[46]方案。后者仅适用于二值向量。值得注意的是，尽管[46]方案专为二值向量设计，但我们的边界始终优于[39]，有时甚至优于[46]。这一结论颇具启发性，因为我们获得的数据结构同样适用于非二值向量。

In practice, one may want to use a recent LSH family from [7] that-both in theory and in practice - is superior to the hyperplane LSH from [16] used in [39].

实际应用中，建议采用[7]提出的新型LSH族——无论在理论还是实践层面，该方案都优于[39]所采用的[16]超平面LSH。

### 4.2 Symmetric LSH for almost all vectors

### 4.2 适用于绝大多数向量的对称LSH

Neyshabur and Srebro [39] show that an asymmetric view on LSH for signed IPS is required. Indeed they show that a symmetric LSH for signed IPS does not exist when data and query domains are balls of the same radius, while an asymmetric LSH does exist. (On the other hand, when the data domain is a ball of given radius $U$ and the query domain is a sphere of same radius, a symmetric LSH does exist.) In this section we show that even when data and query spaces coincide a nontrivial symmetric LSH does exist if we disregard the trivial collision probability of 1 when data and query vectors are identical.

Neyshabur与Srebro[39]证明带符号内积搜索必须采用非对称LSH。他们指出当数据域与查询域为同半径球体时，对称LSH不存在，而非对称LSH存在。（但当数据域为半径$U$的球体且查询域为同半径球面时，对称LSH存在。）本节将证明：即使数据与查询空间重合，只要忽略向量相同时平凡碰撞概率为1的情况，非平凡对称LSH依然存在。

We first show how to reduce signed IPS to the case where data and query vectors lie on a unit sphere. The reduction is deterministic and maintains inner products up to an additive error $\varepsilon$ for all vectors $x,y$ with $x \neq  y$ . We then plug in any Euclidean LSH for ANN on the sphere, for example the one from [9]. This reduction treats data and query vectors identically, unlike the one from [39], and thus we are able to obtain a symmetric LSH.

首先展示如何将带符号内积搜索规约至单位球面情形。该确定性规约对所有满足$x \neq  y$的向量$x,y$保持内积加性误差$\varepsilon$。随后可接入任意球面近似最近邻的欧氏LSH（如[9]方案）。与[39]不同，本规约对数据与查询向量进行对称处理，故能构造对称LSH。

Assume that vector coordinates are encoded as $k$ -bit numbers, and that data and query vectors are in the unit ball. The idea is the following. There are at most $N = {2}^{O\left( {dk}\right) }$ possible data vectors and queries. Imagine a collection of $N$ unit vectors ${v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{N}$ such that for every $i \neq  j$ one has $\left| {{v}_{i}^{T}{v}_{j}}\right|  \leq  \varepsilon$ . Then,it is easy to check that a map of a vector $p$ to $f\left( p\right)  = \left( {p,\sqrt{1 - \parallel p{\parallel }^{2}} \cdot  {v}_{p}}\right)$ maps a vector from a unit ball to a unit sphere and,moreover,for $p \neq  q$ one has $\left| {f{\left( p\right) }^{T}f\left( q\right)  - {p}^{T}q}\right|  \leq  \varepsilon$

假设向量坐标被编码为$k$位数字，且数据向量和查询向量都位于单位球内。其核心思想如下：最多存在$N = {2}^{O\left( {dk}\right) }$种可能的数据向量和查询。设想存在一组$N$个单位向量${v}_{1},\ldots ,{v}_{N}$，使得对于任意$i \neq  j$都满足$\left| {{v}_{i}^{T}{v}_{j}}\right|  \leq  \varepsilon$。此时容易验证，将向量$p$映射为$f\left( p\right)  = \left( {p,\sqrt{1 - \parallel p{\parallel }^{2}} \cdot  {v}_{p}}\right)$的操作会将单位球内的向量映射到单位球面上，并且当$p \neq  q$时满足$\left| {f{\left( p\right) }^{T}f\left( q\right)  - {p}^{T}q}\right|  \leq  \varepsilon$。

What remains is to construct such a collection of vectors ${v}_{i}$ . Moreover, our collection of vectors must be explicit in a strong sense: we should be able to compute ${v}_{u}$ given a vector $u$ (after interpreting it as an ${dk}$ -bit string). Such a constructions are well known, e.g., in [38] it is shown how to build such vectors using Reed-Solomon codes. The resulting dimension is $O\left( {{\varepsilon }^{-2}\log N}\right)  = O\left( {{kd}/{\varepsilon }^{2}}\right) \left\lbrack  {{32},{38}}\right\rbrack$ .

现在需要构建这样一组向量集合${v}_{i}$。此外，该向量集合必须具有强显式性：在将向量$u$解释为${dk}$位字符串后，我们应能计算出${v}_{u}$。这类构造方法已广为人知，例如文献[38]展示了如何利用里德-所罗门码构建此类向量，最终得到的维度为$O\left( {{\varepsilon }^{-2}\log N}\right)  = O\left( {{kd}/{\varepsilon }^{2}}\right) \left\lbrack  {{32},{38}}\right\rbrack$。

After performing such a reduction we can apply any state-of-the-art LSH (or data structure for ANN) for ${\ell }_{2}$ norm on a sphere,e.g. from $\left\lbrack  {9,7}\right\rbrack$ ,with distance threshold ${r}^{2} =$ $2\left( {1 - s + \epsilon }\right)$ ,approximation factor ${c}^{\prime 2} = \left( {1 - {cs} - \epsilon }\right) /{r}^{2}$ . If $\varepsilon$ is sufficiently small we get a $\rho$ value close to the one in (3). The final result is therefore a symmetric LSH for symmetric domains that does not provide any collision bound for all pairs(q,p)with $q = p$ since the guarantees on the inner product fail for these pairs. This LSH can used for solving signed(cs,s)IPS as a traditional LSH [6],although it is required an initial step that verifies whether a query vector is in the input set and, if this is the case, returns the vector $q$ itself if ${q}^{T}q \geq  s$ .

完成此约简后，我们可以对球面上的${\ell }_{2}$范数应用最先进的LSH（或近似最近邻数据结构），例如来自$\left\lbrack  {9,7}\right\rbrack$的方案，其距离阈值为${r}^{2} =$$2\left( {1 - s + \epsilon }\right)$，近似因子为${c}^{\prime 2} = \left( {1 - {cs} - \epsilon }\right) /{r}^{2}$。若$\varepsilon$足够小，我们将获得接近式(3)的$\rho$值。因此最终结果是针对对称域的对称LSH，由于内积保证对这些配对失效，该方法无法为所有$q = p$的查询-数据对(q,p)提供碰撞边界。虽然需要初始步骤验证查询向量是否在输入集中（若是则当${q}^{T}q \geq  s$时返回向量$q$本身），但该LSH可像传统LSH[6]那样用于求解带符号的(cs,s)IPS问题。

### 4.3 Unsigned IPS via linear sketches

### 4.3 基于线性素描的无符号IPS

In this section we propose a linear sketch for unsigned $c$ - ${MIPS}$ ,that can be used for solving unsigned(cs,s)join. The unsigned $c$ -MIPS is defined as follows: given a set $P \subset  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$ of $n$ vectors, construct a data structure that efficiently returns, for a given query vector $q$ ,a vector $p \in  P$ where $\left| {{p}^{T}q}\right|  \geq  c\left( {{p}^{\prime T}q}\right)$ , where ${p}^{\prime }$ is the vector in $P$ with maximum absolute inner product with $q$ . The unsigned(cs,s)join between sets $P$ and $Q$ can be computed by constructing a data structure for unsigned $c$ -MIPS for vectors in $P$ and then performs a query for each vector in $Q$ .

本节提出用于无符号$c$-${MIPS}$的线性素描方法，可解决无符号(cs,s)连接问题。无符号$c$-MIPS定义如下：给定包含$n$个向量的集合$P \subset  {\mathbb{R}}^{d}$，构建一个数据结构，使其能针对查询向量$q$高效返回向量$p \in  P$（其中$\left| {{p}^{T}q}\right|  \geq  c\left( {{p}^{\prime T}q}\right)$），这里${p}^{\prime }$表示$P$中与$q$具有最大绝对内积的向量。通过为$P$中的向量构建无符号$c$-MIPS数据结构，并对$Q$中的每个向量执行查询，即可计算集合$P$与$Q$间的无符号(cs,s)连接。

Of independent interest,we notice that unsigned $c$ -MIPS can be solved by a data structure for unsigned(cs,s)search. Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a data structure for unsigned(cs,s)search on the data set $P$ ,and suppose we are given a query $q$ and the promise that there exists ${p}^{\prime } \in  P$ such that ${p}^{\prime T}q > \gamma$ . Then,unsigned $c$ -MIPS can be solved by performing on $\mathcal{D}$ the queries $q/{c}^{i}$ for any $0 \leq  i \leq  \left\lceil  {{\log }_{1/c}\left( {s/\gamma }\right) }\right\rceil$ . Intuitively, we are scaling up the query $q$ until the largest inner product becomes larger than the threshold $s$ . We notice that $\gamma$ can be also considered as the smallest inner product that can be stored according to the numerical precision of the machine.

值得注意的是，我们发现无符号$c$-最大内积搜索(MIPS)可以通过无符号(cs,s)搜索的数据结构解决。设$\mathcal{D}$为数据集$P$上无符号(cs,s)搜索的数据结构，给定查询$q$且存在${p}^{\prime } \in  P$满足${p}^{\prime T}q > \gamma$时，无符号$c$-MIPS可通过在$\mathcal{D}$上执行查询$q/{c}^{i}$（对任意$0 \leq  i \leq  \left\lceil  {{\log }_{1/c}\left( {s/\gamma }\right) }\right\rceil$）来解决。直观而言，我们通过等比放大查询$q$直至最大内积超过阈值$s$。需注意$\gamma$也可视为根据机器数值精度可存储的最小内积值。

Our data structure requires $\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{2 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$ construction time and $\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$ query time and provide a $c \geq  1/{n}^{1/\kappa }$ approximation with high probability,for any $\kappa  \geq  2$ . This gives an algorithm for unsigned(cs,s)join on two sets of size $n$ requiring time $\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{2 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$ . As shown in Theorem 1,we are unlikely to significant improve further the approximation factor if the OVP conjecture is true.

本数据结构需要$\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{2 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$构建时间和$\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$查询时间，能以高概率提供$c \geq  1/{n}^{1/\kappa }$近似解（适用于任意$\kappa  \geq  2$）。由此得到的无符号(cs,s)连接算法，处理规模为$n$的两个集合时需$\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{2 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$时间。如定理1所示，若OVP猜想成立，我们不太可能显著改进该近似因子。

First, suppose we are only interested in approximating the value of $\mathop{\max }\limits_{p}\left| {{q}^{t}p}\right|$ and not to find the corresponding vector. Then,the problem is equivalent to estimating $\parallel {Aq}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ ,where $A$ is an $n \times  d$ matrix,whose rows are data vectors. This problem can be tackled using linear sketches (for an overview see $\left\lbrack  {{57},8}\right\rbrack  )$ . More specifically,we use the following result from [5]: for every $2 \leq  \kappa  \leq  \infty$ there exists a distribution over $\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }\right)  \times  n$ matrices $\Pi$ such that for every $x \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$ one has:

首先，假设我们仅需近似$\mathop{\max }\limits_{p}\left| {{q}^{t}p}\right|$值而无需定位对应向量。此时该问题等价于估计$\parallel {Aq}{\parallel }_{\infty }$，其中$A$是由数据向量组成的$n \times  d$矩阵。此问题可采用线性素描技术解决（概述见$\left\lbrack  {{57},8}\right\rbrack  )$）。具体而言，我们引用文献[5]的结论：对于任意$2 \leq  \kappa  \leq  \infty$，存在$\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }\right)  \times  n$矩阵$\Pi$的概率分布，使得对每个$x \in  {\mathbb{R}}^{n}$满足：

$$
\mathop{\Pr }\limits_{\Pi }\left\lbrack  {\left( {1 - c}\right) \parallel x{\parallel }_{\kappa } \leq  \parallel {\Pi x}{\parallel }_{\infty } \leq  \left( {1 + c}\right) \parallel x{\parallel }_{\kappa }}\right\rbrack   \geq  {0.99}
$$

for a suitable constant $0 < c < 1$ . Thus,to build a data structure for computing $\parallel {Aq}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ ,we sample a matrix $\Pi$ according to the aforementioned result in [5] and compute the $\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }\right)  \times  d$ matrix ${A}_{s} = {\Pi A}$ . Then,for every query $q$ ,we compute ${\begin{Vmatrix}{A}_{s}q\end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty }$ in time $\widetilde{O}\left( {d \cdot  {n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$ ,which is a $O\left( {n}^{1/\kappa }\right)$ -approximation to $\parallel {Aq}{\parallel }_{\infty }$ with probability at least 0.99 . Note that we can reduce the probability of error from 0.01 to $\delta  > 0$ as usual,by building $O\left( {\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }\right)$ independent copies of the above data structure and reporting the median estimate.

其中$0 < c < 1$为适当常数。因此构建计算$\parallel {Aq}{\parallel }_{\infty }$的数据结构时，我们根据文献[5]所述方法采样矩阵$\Pi$，并计算$\widetilde{O}\left( {n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }\right)  \times  d$矩阵${A}_{s} = {\Pi A}$。对于每个查询$q$，我们可在$\widetilde{O}\left( {d \cdot  {n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$时间内计算${\begin{Vmatrix}{A}_{s}q\end{Vmatrix}}_{\infty }$，该结果以至少0.99概率成为$\parallel {Aq}{\parallel }_{\infty }$的$O\left( {n}^{1/\kappa }\right)$近似值。注：通过构建$O\left( {\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }\right)$个独立副本并取中位数估计，可将误差概率从0.01降至$\delta  > 0$。

We now consider the recovery of the vector that almost maximizes $\left| {{p}^{t}q}\right|$ . We recover the index of the desired vector bit by bit. That is,for every bit index $0 \leq  i < \log n$ ,we consider every binary sequence $b$ of length $i$ and build a data structure for the dataset containing only the vectors in $\mathcal{P}$ for which the binary representations of their indexes have prefix $b$ . Although the number of data structures is $n$ ,the total required space is still $\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$ since each vector appears in only $\log n$ data structures. The claim stated at the beginning follows.

我们现在考虑恢复那个几乎最大化$\left| {{p}^{t}q}\right|$的向量。我们逐位恢复目标向量的索引。也就是说，对于每个位索引$0 \leq  i < \log n$，我们考虑所有长度为$i$的二进制序列$b$，并为仅包含$\mathcal{P}$中索引的二进制表示具有前缀$b$的向量的数据集构建数据结构。尽管数据结构的数量是$n$，但由于每个向量仅出现在$\log n$个数据结构中，所需的总空间仍为$\widetilde{O}\left( {d{n}^{1 - 2/\kappa }}\right)$。由此可证得开篇所述结论。

## 5. CONCLUSION

## 5. 结论

This paper has investigated different aspects of the complexity of approximate similarity join with inner product. In particular, we have related the hardness of this problem to the OVP conjecture. Under some assumptions on $c$ and $s$ , the proposed conditional lower bounds rule out algorithms for signed/unsigned(cs,s)IPS join running in ${n}^{2 - \epsilon }$ time,for a constant $\epsilon  > 0$ ,unless the OVP conjecture is false. Nevertheless, the data structures in section 4 show that it still possible to reach weak subquadratic time, and even truly subquadratic time for small values of the approximation factor.

本文从多个维度研究了基于内积的近似相似连接问题的复杂性。特别地，我们将该问题的难度与OVP猜想相关联。在对$c$和$s$的某些假设下，所提出的条件性下界排除了在${n}^{2 - \epsilon }$时间内运行的有符号/无符号(cs,s)IPS连接算法，除非OVP猜想不成立。然而，第4节中的数据结构表明，仍有可能实现弱次二次时间，对于近似因子较小的情况甚至可实现真正的次二次时间。

The hardness of signed/unsigned IPS holds even for weak approximation factors when the vector domain is $\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$ . Indeed,the result holds if $c \geq  0$ for signed join,and if $c \geq$ ${e}^{-o\left( {\sqrt{\log n}/\log \log n}\right) }$ for unsigned join. When $c < 1/{n}^{\Omega \left( 1\right) }$ ,the data structure for unsigned IPS in section 4.3 reaches strongly subquadratic time and this gives evidence that the constraint on $c$ of the hardness result cannot be significantly relaxed. On the other hand,when vectors are in the $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ domain,a stronger assumption is required on the approximation factor. In this case,the conditional lower bound holds for $c = 1 - o\left( 1\right)$ and hence it does not rule out an algorithm running in ${n}^{2 - \epsilon }$ time for a constant approximation factor. We believe that a different approach is required to show the hardness of IPS for constant approximation: indeed, the proposed reduction from OVP to IPS in the $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ domain strongly relies on the ability to distinguish inner products smaller than $k - 1$ and larger than $k$ for some $k = \omega \left( 1\right)$ ,implying $c \geq  1 - o\left( 1\right)$ . From an upper bound point of view, the LSH proposed in section 4.1 improves upon the state of the art [46] based on minwise hashing for different values (e.g.,when $s \geq  d/3$ and $c \geq  {0.83}$ ); however,it still gives weak subquadratic algorithm for signed/unsigned IPS with constant $c$ . An interesting open question is therefore to assess if strongly subquadratic time is possible when the approximation is constant (or smaller) in the $\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$ domain.

即使对于弱近似因子，当向量域为$\{  - 1,1{\} }^{d}$时，有符号/无符号IPS的难度依然成立。具体而言，对于有符号连接，若满足$c \geq  0$则结果成立；对于无符号连接，若满足$c \geq$${e}^{-o\left( {\sqrt{\log n}/\log \log n}\right) }$则结果成立。当$c < 1/{n}^{\Omega \left( 1\right) }$时，第4.3节中无符号IPS的数据结构实现了强次二次时间，这表明难度结果中对$c$的约束条件无法显著放宽。另一方面，当向量处于$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$域时，需要对近似因子提出更强假设。此时，条件性下界适用于$c = 1 - o\left( 1\right)$，因此不排除在恒定近似因子下存在${n}^{2 - \epsilon }$时间运行算法的可能性。我们认为需要采用不同方法来证明恒定近似下IPS的难度：实际上，在$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$域中从OVP到IPS的规约强烈依赖于区分小于$k - 1$和大于$k$的内积的能力（对于某些$k = \omega \left( 1\right)$），这意味着$c \geq  1 - o\left( 1\right)$。从上界角度看，第4.1节提出的LSH针对不同参数值（例如当$s \geq  d/3$和$c \geq  {0.83}$时）改进了基于minwise哈希的现有技术[46]；但对于恒定$c$的有符号/无符号IPS，其仍属于弱次二次算法。因此，一个有趣的开放问题是：在$\{ 0,1{\} }^{d}$域中，当近似因子为常数（或更小）时，是否可能实现强次二次时间。
