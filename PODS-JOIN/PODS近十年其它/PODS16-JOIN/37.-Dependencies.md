## Abstract

## 摘要

Recently, Gottlob, Lee, Valiant, and Valiant (GLVV) presented an output size bound for join queries with functional dependencies (FD), based on a linear program on polyma-troids. GLVV bound strictly generalizes the bound of At-serias, Grohe and Marx (AGM) for queries with no FD, in which case there are known algorithms running within the AGM-bound and thus are worst-case optimal.

最近，戈特洛布、李、瓦利安特和瓦利安特（GLVV）基于多拟阵线性规划，提出了带函数依赖（FD）的联接查询输出规模界限。GLVV界限严格推广了阿瑟里亚斯、格罗赫和马克思（AGM）针对无FD查询的界限，后者已有算法能在AGM界限内运行，因此是最坏情况最优的。

A main result of this paper is an algorithm for computing join queries with FDs, running within GLVV bound up to a poly-log factor. In particular, our algorithm is worst-case optimal for any query where the GLVV bound is tight. As an unexpected by-product, our algorithm manages to solve a harder problem, where (some) input relations may have prescribed maximum degree bounds, of which both functional dependencies and cardinality bounds are special cases.

本文主要成果是提出一种带FD的联接查询算法，其运行时间在GLVV界限内至多相差多对数因子。特别地，当GLVV界限紧致时，我们的算法对所有查询都是最坏情况最优的。作为意外收获，该算法还能解决更复杂的问题——允许（部分）输入关系存在预设的最大度数约束，其中函数依赖和基数约束都是特例。

We extend Gottlob et al. framework by replacing all variable subsets with the lattice of closed sets (under the given FDs). This gives us new insights into the structure of the worst-case bound and worst-case instances. While it is still open whether GLVV bound is tight in general, we show that it is tight on distributive lattices and some other simple lattices. Distributive lattices capture a strict superset of queries with no FD and with simple FDs. We also present two simpler algorithms which are also worst-case optimal on distributive lattices within a single-log factor, but they do not match GLVV bound on a general lattice. Our algorithms are designed based on a novel principle: we turn a proof of a polymatroid-based output size bound into an algorithm.

我们通过用闭集格（在给定FD下）替代所有变量子集，扩展了戈特洛布等人的框架。这为理解最坏情况界限和实例结构提供了新视角。尽管GLVV界限是否普遍紧致尚待研究，但我们证明其在分配格和某些简单格上是紧致的。分配格涵盖了无FD查询和简单FD查询的严格超集。我们还提出两种更简单的算法，它们在分配格上也能以单对数因子差距实现最坏情况最优，但无法在一般格上匹配GLVV界限。这些算法基于创新原理设计：将多拟阵输出规模界限的证明转化为算法。

## 1. INTRODUCTION

## 1. 引言

Several results published in the last ten years or so have lead to tight worst-case output size bounds ( ${AGM}$ -bound [5, 13]) and the development of a new class of query processing algorithms running within the bound’s time budget $\lbrack {18},{19}$ , 23]. These new worst-case optimal algorithms are quite different from traditional query plans, in the sense that they no longer compute one pairwise join at a time, but instead process the query globally. The runtime is bounded by $O\left( {N}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$ , where $N$ is the size of the database,while ${\rho }^{ * }$ is the value of the optimal fractional edge cover of the query. For example, they compute the query $Q\left( {x,y,z}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) ,T\left( {z,x}\right)$ in time $O\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$ ,while any traditional query plan requires worst-case time $\Omega \left( {N}^{2}\right) \left\lbrack  {19}\right\rbrack$ .

近十年发表的多项研究得出了严格的最坏情况输出大小界限（${AGM}$界[5,13]），并发展出在界限时间预算内运行的新型查询处理算法类$\lbrack {18},{19}$,23]。这些最坏情况最优算法与传统查询计划截然不同——它们不再逐对计算连接，而是全局处理查询。其时间复杂度受限于$O\left( {N}^{{\rho }^{ * }}\right)$，其中$N$表示数据库规模，${\rho }^{ * }$为查询最优分数边覆盖值。例如处理查询$Q\left( {x,y,z}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:=}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) ,T\left( {z,x}\right)$仅需$O\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$时间，而传统查询计划在最坏情况下需要$\Omega \left( {N}^{2}\right) \left\lbrack  {19}\right\rbrack$时间。

While the vast majority of database engines today still rely on traditional query plans, new, complex data analytics engines increasingly switch to worst-case optimal algorithms: LogicBlox's engine [4] is built on a worst-case optimal algorithm called LeapFrog Triejoin [23] (LFTJ), the Myria data analytics platform supports a variant of LFTJ [8], and the increased role of SIMD instructions in modern processors also favors this new class of join algorithms [1].

尽管当前绝大多数数据库引擎仍依赖传统查询计划，但新兴的复杂数据分析引擎正转向最坏情况最优算法：LogicBlox引擎[4]基于称为LeapFrog Triejoin[23](LFTJ)的算法构建，Myria数据分析平台支持LFTJ变体[8]，现代处理器中SIMD指令比重的提升也促进了这类新型连接算法的发展[1]。

When there are functional dependencies (FDs), however, the AGM-bound is no-longer tight. Gottlob, Lee, Valiant, and Valiant [12] initiated the study of the worst-case output size of a query in the presence of FDs and described an upper bound (reviewed in Sec.2), called the GLVV-bound henceforth. It remains open whether the GLVV-bound is tight; at present, it is the best known upper bound for the output size of a join query with FDs. A recent result by Gogacz and Toruńczyk [11] proved a weaker statement, namely that if we replace the polymatroidal constraints in the GLVV-bound with entropic constraints (of which there are infinitely many), then the bound is tight, albeit uncomputable.

然而存在函数依赖(FD)时，AGM界不再严格。Gottlob、Lee、Valiant和Valiant[12]开创了FD环境下查询最坏情况输出规模的研究，提出了称为GLVV界的上界（详见第2节）。GLVV界是否严格仍属开放问题，目前它仍是FD连接查询输出规模的最佳已知上界。Gogacz和Toruńczyk[11]近期证明了较弱结论：若将GLVV界中的多拟阵约束替换为熵约束（存在无限多个），则该界限虽不可计算但严格成立。

Our paper proposes an approach to studying join queries in the presence of FDs by using lattice theory. The GLVV-bound is reformulated with a very simple linear program on the lattices' polymatroids, and output size bounds are obtained by studying formal inequalities satisfied by all these polymatroids. This approach allows us to derive both new lower and upper bounds on the worst-case output size. Furthermore, we present an algorithm which runs in time proportional to the GLVV-bound, within a polylogarithmic factor. We also describe two special cases where the polyloga-rithmic factor is reduced to a single log. Before discussing our results, we explain their significance.

本文提出运用格理论研究FD环境下的连接查询。通过格上多拟阵的简单线性规划重构GLVV界，并分析这些多拟阵满足的形式不等式来获取输出规模界限。该方法不仅能推导最坏情况输出规模的新上下界，还提出运行时间在多重对数因子内与GLVV界成正比的算法。我们还描述了两种能将多重对数因子降为单对数的情况。在讨论成果前，先阐明其重要性。

### 1.1 Motivation

### 1.1 研究动机

User-Defined Functions. UDF's, or interpreted predicates, significantly affect the runtime of a query. Consider:

用户自定义函数。UDF（即解释型谓词）会显著影响查询运行时。例如：

$$
Q\left( {x,y,z,u}\right)  \mathrel{\text{:-}} R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) ,T\left( {z,u}\right) ,u = f\left( {x,z}\right) ,x = g\left( {y,u}\right) .
$$

(1)

The predicates $u = f\left( {x,z}\right)$ and $x = g\left( {y,u}\right)$ represent two user-defined functions, $f$ and $g$ ; for example $f\left( {x,z}\right)$ could be $x + z$ ,or the concatenation of two strings $x$ and $z$ . Any UDF can be modeled as a relation with a primary key, for example the function $f$ can be viewed as a relation $F\left( {x,z,u}\right)$ of cardinality ${N}^{2}$ (with one entry for every $x,z$ pair in their effective domains) satisfying the FD ${xz} \rightarrow  u$ ; similarly $g$ introduces the FD ${yu} \rightarrow  x$ . The addition of these two FD’s significantly affects the output size and the query evaluation complexity: if we computed the intermediate query $R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) ,T\left( {z,u}\right)$ ,and then applied the two predicates $u = f\left( {x,z}\right)$ and $x = g\left( {y,u}\right)$ ,then the runtime can be as high as ${N}^{2}$ because the size of the intermediate result is ${N}^{2}$ for $R = \{ \left( {i,1}\right)  \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  N\right\rbrack  \} ,S = \{ \left( {1,1}\right) \} ,T = \{ \left( {1,i}\right)  \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  N\right\rbrack  \} .$ However,as we will show,the GLVV-bound is $\left| Q\right|  \leq  {N}^{3/2}$ . Our new algorithms run in $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$ ; and as we shall see this is worst-case optimal.

谓词$u = f\left( {x,z}\right)$和$x = g\left( {y,u}\right)$表示两个用户自定义函数$f$与$g$；例如$f\left( {x,z}\right)$可以是$x + z$，或是两个字符串$x$与$z$的拼接。任何用户定义函数(UDF)都可建模为含主键的关系，如函数$f$可视为基数${N}^{2}$的关系$F\left( {x,z,u}\right)$（在其有效域中每个$x,z$键值对对应一个条目），满足函数依赖(FD)${xz} \rightarrow  u$；同理$g$引入函数依赖${yu} \rightarrow  x$。这两个函数依赖的加入会显著影响输出规模与查询评估复杂度：若先计算中间查询$R\left( {x,y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) ,T\left( {z,u}\right)$再应用谓词$u = f\left( {x,z}\right)$和$x = g\left( {y,u}\right)$，由于中间结果规模在$R = \{ \left( {i,1}\right)  \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  N\right\rbrack  \} ,S = \{ \left( {1,1}\right) \} ,T = \{ \left( {1,i}\right)  \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  N\right\rbrack  \} .$情况下可达${N}^{2}$，运行时耗可能高达${N}^{2}$。但我们将证明GLVV界为$\left| Q\right|  \leq  {N}^{3/2}$，而新算法运行时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$，且该复杂度是最坏情况最优的。

A related problem is querying relations with restricted access patterns [7]. In that setting, some of the relations in the database can only be read by providing the values of one or more attributes. As shown above, a user-defined function $f\left( {x,z}\right)$ can be modeled as an infinite relation $F\left( {u,x,z}\right)  \equiv$ $\left( {u = f\left( {x,z}\right) }\right)$ with the restriction that $F$ can be accessed only by providing inputs for the variables $x$ and $z$ . The work on querying under restricted access patterns has focused on the answerability question (whether a query can or cannot be answered). Our work extends that, by finding algorithms for answering the query within the GLVV-bound.

相关研究涉及受限访问模式下的关系查询[7]。该场景中，数据库某些关系仅能通过提供特定属性值进行读取。如上所述，用户自定义函数$f\left( {x,z}\right)$可建模为无限关系$F\left( {u,x,z}\right)  \equiv$$\left( {u = f\left( {x,z}\right) }\right)$，其限制条件是必须为变量$x$和$z$提供输入值才能访问$F$。现有受限访问模式查询研究主要关注可回答性问题（查询是否可被解答）。我们的工作通过开发GLVV界内的查询应答算法对此进行了扩展。

Known Frequencies. Systems often know an upper bound on the frequencies (or degrees) in the database. For example, consider the triangle query above, and assume that the binary graph defined by the relation $R\left( {x,y}\right)$ has a bounded degree: all outdegrees are $\deg \left( x\right)  \leq  {d}_{1}$ and all indegrees are $\deg \left( y\right)  \leq  {d}_{2}$ . One can model this scenario by introducing an artificial color ${c}_{1}$ on outgoing edges,and a color ${c}_{2}$ for incoming edges of $R$ :

已知频率。系统通常掌握数据库中频率（或度数）的上界。例如前文的三角形查询，假设关系$R\left( {x,y}\right)$定义的二元图存在度数限制：所有出度为$\deg \left( x\right)  \leq  {d}_{1}$，入度为$\deg \left( y\right)  \leq  {d}_{2}$。可通过在出边添加人工颜色${c}_{1}$，并为$R$的入边设置颜色${c}_{2}$来建模该场景：

$$
Q\left( {x,y,z,{c}_{1},{c}_{2}}\right)  :  - R\left( {x,{c}_{1},{c}_{2},y}\right) ,S\left( {y,z}\right) ,T\left( {z,x}\right) ,{C}_{1}\left( {c}_{1}\right) ,
$$

$$
{C}_{2}\left( {c}_{2}\right) ,x{c}_{1} \rightarrow  y,y{c}_{2} \rightarrow  x,{xy} \rightarrow  {c}_{1}{c}_{2} \tag{2}
$$

where $\left| R\right|  = \left| S\right|  = \left| T\right|  = N$ and $\left| {C}_{1}\right|  = {d}_{1},\left| {C}_{2}\right|  = {d}_{2}$ . Thus, each outgoing edge from $x$ is colored with some distinct color ${c}_{1}$ ,and similarly each incoming edge to $y$ is colored with some distinct color ${c}_{2}$ . The new predicates ${C}_{1},{C}_{2}$ limit the number of colors to ${d}_{1},{d}_{2}$ respectively. We can show that the worst-case query output decreases from ${N}^{3/2}$ to $\min \left( {{N}^{3/2},N{d}_{1},N{d}_{2}}\right)$ [2]. Alternatively,one can use the linear program and algorithm in Sec 5.2 to capture queries with known maximum degree bounds. We note that, by adapting AYZ algorithm [3], degree information was used in [15] to obtain a sub-quadratic time algorithm for join on a special class of query graphs called 1-series-parallel graphs. Their approach also uses a linear program similar to the one in Sec 5.2, minus the sub-modularity constraints.

其中$\left| R\right|  = \left| S\right|  = \left| T\right|  = N$和$\left| {C}_{1}\right|  = {d}_{1},\left| {C}_{2}\right|  = {d}_{2}$。因此，从$x$出发的每条出边都被赋予某种独特颜色${c}_{1}$，同理进入$y$的每条入边也被赋予某种独特颜色${c}_{2}$。新增谓词${C}_{1},{C}_{2}$将颜色数量限制为${d}_{1},{d}_{2}$。我们可以证明最坏情况下的查询输出从${N}^{3/2}$降至$\min \left( {{N}^{3/2},N{d}_{1},N{d}_{2}}\right)$[2]。或者，可采用第5.2节中的线性规划与算法来处理已知最大度数限制的查询。值得注意的是，通过改进AYZ算法[3]，文献[15]利用度数信息在称为1-系列平行图的特殊查询图类上实现了次平方时间连接算法。该方法同样采用了类似第5.2节的线性规划，但未包含子模约束。

### 1.2 Overview of the Results

### 1.2 研究成果概述

Grohe and Marx [13] and later Atserias, Grohe and Marx [5] derived an elegant tight upper bound on the output size of a join query: $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{\left| {R}_{j}\right| }^{{w}_{j}}$ ,where ${R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}$ are the input relations to the query,and ${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ is any fractional edge cover of the query's hypergraph. This bound is known today as the AGM bound. They proved that the bound is tight by describing a simple database instance for any query, such that the query's output matches the bound. In that instance, every relation is a cross product of sets, one set per variable; we call it a product instance.

Grohe与Marx[13]以及后续Atserias、Grohe和Marx[5]推导出了连接查询输出大小的精确上界：$\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{\left| {R}_{j}\right| }^{{w}_{j}}$，其中${R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}$表示查询的输入关系，${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$为查询超图的任意分数边覆盖。该界现称为AGM界。他们通过为任意查询构造简单数据库实例（其中每个关系都是变量对应集合的笛卡尔积，称为积实例）证明该界是紧致的，查询输出恰好达到该界。

An obvious open question was whether a query $Q$ can be evaluated on any database instance $D$ in time that is no larger than the AGM bound of $Q$ on databases with the same cardinalities as $D$ ; such an algorithm is called worst-case optimal. Ngo, Porat, Ré, and Rudra [18] described the first worst-case optimal algorithm; later Veldhuizen [23] proved that LFTJ, an algorithm already implemented at LogicBlox earlier, is also worst-case optimal. A survey and unification of these two algorithms can be found in [19].

一个明显的开放性问题在于：对于任意数据库实例$D$，查询$Q$能否在不超过与$D$基数相同数据库上AGM界的时间内完成计算——满足此条件的算法称为最坏情况最优算法。Ngo、Porat、Ré和Rudra[18]提出了首个最坏情况最优算法；随后Veldhuizen[23]证明LogicBlox已实现的LFTJ算法同样具备该特性。这两种算法的综述与统一性分析可参见[19]。

Neither the AGM-bound nor the associated algorithms analytically exploit FDs in the database. ${}^{1}$ Such FDs can provably reduce the worst-case output of a query, but the upper bound and algorithms mentioned above cannot use this information, and instead treat the query by ignoring the FDs. Gottlob et al. [12] studied the upper bound of the query size in the presence of FDs, and established two classes of results. The first was a characterization of this bound in the case when the FD's are restricted to simple keys; as we will show, this case can be solved entirely using the AGM bound by simply replacing each relation with its closure. The second was a novel approach to reasoning about the worst-case output of a query, using information theory. They viewed the query output as a multivariate probability space, and introduced two constraints on the marginal entropies: a cardinality constraint for each input relation $R$ ,stating that the entropy of its variables cannot exceed the uniform entropy $H\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( R\right) }\right)  \leq  {\log }_{2}\left| R\right|$ ,and one constraint for each FD $X \rightarrow  Y$ ,stating $H\left( {XY}\right)  = H\left( X\right)$ . (Note that $X$ and $Y$ are sets of variables.) The largest answer to the query $Q$ is then given by the largest possible value of ${2}^{H\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }\right) }$ ,over all choices of entropic functions $H$ that satisfy these constraints. Characterizing the space of all entropic functions $H$ is a long-standing open problem in information theory [24]; to circumvent that, they relax the function $H$ by allowing it to be any function that satisfies the Shannon inequalities [24]. Such a function is called a polymatroid in the literature, and we denote it with lower case $h$ to distinguish it from entropic functions $H$ . Thus, the problem in [12] can be stated equivalently as: find the maximum value $h\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ where $h$ ranges over all polyma-troids satisfying the given constraints.

无论是AGM界还是相关算法，都未在理论上利用数据库中的函数依赖(FDs)。${}^{1}$ 这类FDs可证明能缩减查询的最坏情况输出，但前述上界和算法无法利用此信息，反而通过忽略FDs来处理查询。Gottlob等人[12]研究了存在FDs时的查询规模上界，确立了两类结果：首先是针对FDs仅限于简单键情形的上界特征描述——如我们将展示的，该情形可通过用闭包替换每个关系，完全利用AGM界求解；其次是通过信息论推查询最坏情况输出的新方法。他们将查询输出视为多元概率空间，并对边际熵施加两类约束：每个输入关系的基数约束$R$（声明其变量熵不超过均匀熵$H\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( R\right) }\right)  \leq  {\log }_{2}\left| R\right|$），以及每个FD对应的约束$X \rightarrow  Y$（声明$H\left( {XY}\right)  = H\left( X\right)$）。（注意$X$和$Y$是变量集合。）查询$Q$的最大答案即满足这些约束的所有熵函数$H$中${2}^{H\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }\right) }$可能达到的最大值。特征化所有熵函数$H$的构成空间是信息论中长期未决的难题[24]；为此他们放宽要求，允许$H$为满足香农不等式[24]的任意函数，这类函数在文献中称为多拟阵，我们以小写$h$标记以区别于熵函数$H$。因此[12]中的问题等价于：在满足给定约束的所有多拟阵$h$中，找出最大值$h\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }\right)$。

In this paper, we continue the study of query evaluation under general FDs. We establish both bounds and algorithms. Our novelty is to model FDs as a lattice $\mathbf{L}$ of the closed sets of attributes, and to study polymatroids on lattices. The function $h$ is now any non-negative and lattice-submodular function ${}^{2}$ that satisfies all cardinality constraints. FD constraints are enforced automatically by the lattice structure,and the upper bound on the query size is ${2}^{\max h\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }\right) }$ . When there are no FDs, the lattice is a Boolean algebra.

本文延续对一般FDs下查询评估的研究，同时建立界和算法。我们的创新在于将FDs建模为属性闭集的格$\mathbf{L}$，并研究格上的多拟阵。此时函数$h$是满足所有基数约束的非负格次模函数${}^{2}$。FD约束通过格结构自动实施，查询规模上界为${2}^{\max h\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }\right) }$。当不存在FDs时，该格为布尔代数。

Our first question is whether the elegant AGM bound and worst-case product instance carry over to arbitrary FDs. We answer this question completely, by proving that both sides of the AGM bound hold iff the lattice has a special structure, which we call a normal lattice. Both upper and lower bounds require minor extensions to be applicable to normal lattices. The standard AGM upper bound is given in terms of fractional edge covers of the query's hypergraph, but in a normal lattice one needs to consider a dual hypergraph, whose nodes are L's co-atoms. In a Boolean algebra, these two hy-pergraphs are isomorphic, but in a general lattice they can be significantly different. The notion of normal lattice seems novel, and strictly includes all distributive lattices, which in turn include all lattices corresponding to simple FDs (each FD is of the form $a \rightarrow  b$ ,where $a,b$ are attributes). The second minor change is that one needs to allow for a slight generalization of product instances, to what we call quasi-product instances. Importantly, both these properties fail on non-normal lattices; in particular, on non-normal lattices worst-case instances are not quasi-product instances.

我们首先探讨优雅的AGM界和最坏情况积实例是否适用于任意FDs。通过证明当且仅当格具有我们称为正规格的特殊结构时AGM界两侧成立，我们完整回答了该问题。上下界均需微小扩展以适用于正规格：标准AGM上界通过查询超图的分数边覆盖给出，但在正规格中需考虑其对偶超图（其节点为L的共原子）。布尔代数中这两个超图同构，但一般格中可能显著不同。正规格概念似属首创，严格包含所有分配格（后者又包含对应简单FDs的所有格，即每个FD形如$a \rightarrow  b$，其中$a,b$为属性）。第二处微调是需要允许积实例的轻微推广——即我们所谓的拟积实例。关键是非正规格上这两个性质均不成立，尤其非正规格的最坏情况实例并非拟积实例。

Next, we examine algorithms whose runtime is bounded by the GLVV bound. We propose a novel methodology for designing such algorithms, starting from the observation that such an algorithm must provide a proof of the query's upper bound, equivalently, a proof of an inequality of the form $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{w}_{j}h\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right)  \geq  h\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }\right)$ ,where ${R}_{1},{R}_{2},\ldots$ are the input relations. In the case of a Boolean algebra, Shearer's lemma [9] is of this form; in a normal lattice this corresponds to a fractional edge cover of the co-atomic hy-pergraph; and, for a general lattice it is a general inequality. A key motivation behind NPRR [18] was to prove inequalities algorithmically. This paper completes the cycle by proceeding in the opposite direction: given a proof method for such inequalities, we design algorithms whose steps correspond to the proof steps. We design three such algorithms, corresponding to three methods for proving the above type of inequalities.

接下来我们研究运行时间受GLVV界约束的算法。基于这类算法必须提供查询上界证明（即形式为$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{w}_{j}h\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right)  \geq  h\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( Q\right) }\right)$的不等式证明，其中${R}_{1},{R}_{2},\ldots$表示输入关系）的观察，我们提出了一种新颖的设计方法。在布尔代数中，Shearer引理[9]即属此类；在正规格中对应共原子超图的分数边覆盖；而在一般格中则是普适不等式。NPRR[18]的核心动机正是算法化证明不等式。本文逆向完成这个循环：给定此类不等式的证明方法，我们设计其步骤与证明步骤对应的算法。据此开发了三种算法，分别对应上述不等式的三种证明方法。

The first algorithm called the chain algorithm runs within the chain bound; the proof technique is adapted from Radhakrishnan's proof [20] of Shearer's lemma to general lattices. Both the chain bound and algorithm strictly generalize AGM-bound and worst-case optimal algorithms for join queries without FDs. The second algorithm, called the sub-modular algorithm, runs within the sub-modularity bound; the proof technique is that of Balister and Bollobás's [6]. The third algorithm, called the conditional sub-modularity algorithm (CSMA), runs within the general GLVV-bound, up to a polylogarithmic factor; the proof technique is our own, based on linear programming duality. As a bonus, CSMA can be used straightforwardly to handle input relations with known maximum degree bounds, which is a strict generalization of the problem of computing join queries with FDs. While GLVV bound is tighter than the other two in general lattices, we show that in distributive lattices all of them are tight, and thus our algorithms are worst-case optimal.

首个称为链式算法的方案运行于链界内，其证明技术是将Radhakrishnan证明Shearer引理[20]的方法推广至一般格。链界与算法均严格推广了无FD连接查询的AGM界和最坏情况最优算法。第二种子模算法运行于子模性界内，采用Balister和Bollobás[6]的证明技术。第三种条件子模性算法(CSMA)运行于GLVV界内（至多相差多对数因子），其基于线性规划对偶性的原创证明技术。作为额外优势，CSMA可直接处理已知最大度约束的输入关系，这是带FD连接查询问题的严格推广。虽然GLVV界在一般格中比其他两者更紧，但我们证明在分配格中三者均紧致，因此我们的算法都是最坏情况最优的。

The AGM Bound. A series of results over the last ten years $\left\lbrack  {5,{13},{18},{19},{23}}\right\rbrack$ has established tight connections between the maximum output size of a query and the runtime of a query evaluation algorithm. The query hypergraph of a query $Q$ is ${H}_{Q} = \left( {\mathbf{X},\mathbf{R}}\right)$ ; its nodes are the variables and its hyperedges are the input relations (where each ${R}_{j} \in  \mathbf{R}$ is viewed as a set of variables). Consider the following two linear programs (LP's):

$$
\text{(Weighted) Edge Cover (Weighted) Ver. Packing}
$$

$$
\text{ minimize }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{w}_{j}{n}_{j}\;\text{ maximize }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{v}_{i}
$$

$$
\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j : {x}_{i} \in  {R}_{j}}}{w}_{j} \geq  1,\forall i \in  \left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  \;\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i : {x}_{i} \in  {R}_{j}}}{v}_{i} \leq  {n}_{j},\forall j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack

Functional Dependencies. A query with functional dependencies is a pair(Q,FD),where $Q$ is a query and $\mathrm{{FD}}$ is a set of functional dependencies (fd), which are expressions of the form $U \rightarrow  V$ where $U,V \subseteq  \mathbf{X}$ . An fd can be defined either by some input relation ${R}_{j}$ ,in which case we call it guarded (in particular $U,V \subseteq  {R}_{j}$ ),or can be defined by a UDF (as we saw in Sec. 1.1), and then we call it unguarded. A simple fd is of the form $u \rightarrow  v$ where both $u,v$ are variables,and a simple key is a simple fd guarded in some ${R}_{j}$ s.t. $u$ is a key for ${R}_{j}$ .

函数依赖。带函数依赖的查询是一个二元组(Q,FD)，其中$Q$是查询，$\mathrm{{FD}}$是函数依赖(fd)集合，这些依赖是形如$U \rightarrow  V$的表达式（其中$U,V \subseteq  \mathbf{X}$）。fd可由某个输入关系${R}_{j}$定义（此时称其为受保护的，特别地$U,V \subseteq  {R}_{j}$），或由UDF定义（如第1.1节所见），此时称其为无保护的。简单fd形如$u \rightarrow  v$（其中$u,v$均为变量），简单键是在某个${R}_{j}$中受保护的简单fd，满足$u$是${R}_{j}$的键。

Gottlob et al. [12] studied output size bounds in the presence of FDs. Their bound is defined only in terms of the maximum cardinality, $\mathop{\max }\limits_{j}{N}_{j}$ ,but in this paper we slightly generalize the discussion to all cardinalities ${\left( {N}_{j}\right) }_{j}$ . The key technique introduced in [12] is to use information theory to upper bound the size of the query, as reviewed next.

Gottlob等人[12]研究了存在FD时的输出大小界。他们的界仅依据最大基数$\mathop{\max }\limits_{j}{N}_{j}$定义，但本文将其稍作推广至所有基数${\left( {N}_{j}\right) }_{j}$。[12]引入的关键技术是使用信息论来给出查询大小的上界，如下所述。

Let ${Q}^{D}$ be the query answer over some instance $D$ . Define a probability distribution over $\mathop{\prod }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\operatorname{Dom}\left( {x}_{i}\right)$ by randomly drawing one tuple from ${Q}^{D}$ with probability $1/\left| {Q}^{D}\right|$ each. Then,the joint entropy of the $k$ random variables $\mathbf{X}$ is $H\left( \mathbf{X}\right)  = {\log }_{2}\left| {Q}^{D}\right|$ . Each subset of variables $X \subseteq  \mathbf{X}$ defines a (marginal) distribution,with entropy $H\left( X\right)$ . If $X = {R}_{j}$ ,then $H$ must satisfy the following cardinality constraint $H\left( {R}_{j}\right)  \leq  {\log }_{2}{N}_{j} = {n}_{j}$ ,because ${\Pi }_{{R}_{j}}\left( {Q}^{D}\right)  \subseteq  {R}_{j}^{D}$ and the marginal entropy is maximized when it is uniform. In addition,for any fd $U \rightarrow  V$ the entropy must satisfy the fd-constraint $H\left( U\right)  = H\left( {UV}\right)$ .

GLVV Bound. Gottlob et al. [12] observe that $\log \left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq$ $\mathop{\max }\limits_{H}H\left( \mathbf{X}\right)$ ,where $H$ ranges over all entropic functions that satisfy the cardinality constraints and the fd-constraints, and this bound has recently been shown to be tight [11]. However, we do not know how to compute this upper bound. The entropic cone requires an infinite number of (independent) constraints to characterize, and the precise characterization is a long-standing open problem in information theory [24]. To circumvent this difficulty, in [12] the entropic function $H$ is replaced with a polymatroid function h. A polymatroid over a set of variables $\mathbf{X}$ is a function $h : {2}^{\mathbf{X}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ that satisfies the following inequalities,called the Shannon inequalities:

GLVV界。Gottlob等人[12]指出，$\log \left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq$ $\mathop{\max }\limits_{H}H\left( \mathbf{X}\right)$ ，其中$H$遍历所有满足基数约束和函数依赖约束的熵函数，且该界最近被证明是紧致的[11]。然而我们尚不知如何计算这个上界。熵锥需要无限个（独立）约束来表征，其精确刻画是信息论中长期悬而未决的难题[24]。为规避此困难，文献[12]用多拟阵函数h替代熵函数$H$。变量集$\mathbf{X}$上的多拟阵是满足下列不等式（称为香农不等式）的函数$h : {2}^{\mathbf{X}} \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$：

$$
h\left( X\right)  + h\left( Y\right)  \geq  h\left( {X \cap  Y}\right)  + h\left( {X \cup  Y}\right) \;\text{Sub-modularity}
$$

$$
h\left( {X \cup  Y}\right)  \geq  h\left( X\right) \;\text{ Monotonicity }
$$

$h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$ Zero Every entropic function $H$ is a polymatroid,and the converse fails when $\mathbf{X}$ has four or more variables [25]. Define GLVV $\left( {Q,\mathrm{{FD}},{\left( {N}_{j}\right) }_{j}}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{h}h\left( \mathbf{X}\right)$ ,where $h$ ranges over all polymatroids that satisfy all cardinality constraints $h\left( {X}_{j}\right)  \leq$ ${n}_{j}$ ,and all fd-constraints $h\left( {UV}\right)  = h\left( U\right)$ for $U \rightarrow  V \in  \mathrm{{FD}}$ . Formally, Gottlob et al. [12] proved:

$h\left( \varnothing \right)  = 0$ 零值性 每个熵函数$H$都是多拟阵，但当$\mathbf{X}$具有四个及以上变量时逆命题不成立[25]。定义GLVV$\left( {Q,\mathrm{{FD}},{\left( {N}_{j}\right) }_{j}}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\max }\limits_{h}h\left( \mathbf{X}\right)$，其中$h$遍历所有满足基数约束$h\left( {X}_{j}\right)  \leq$${n}_{j}$及函数依赖约束$h\left( {UV}\right)  = h\left( U\right)$（对于$U \rightarrow  V \in  \mathrm{{FD}}$）的多拟阵。形式化地，Gottlob等人[12]证明了：

Theorem 2.2 (GLVV-bound). Let $Q$ be a join query with functional dependencies ${FD}$ and input relations ${R}_{j}$ of size $\left| {R}_{j}\right|  = {N}_{j}$ . Then, $\left| Q\right|  \leq  {GLVV}\left( {Q,{FD},{\left( {N}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}}\right)$ .

定理2.2（GLVV界）。设$Q$为带有函数依赖${FD}$的连接查询，输入关系${R}_{j}$的规模为$\left| {R}_{j}\right|  = {N}_{j}$，则$\left| Q\right|  \leq  {GLVV}\left( {Q,{FD},{\left( {N}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}}\right)$。

We abbreviate the bound by $\operatorname{GLVV}\left( Q\right)$ when $\mathrm{{FD}}$ and ${\left( {N}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ are clear from the context. Note the important fact that GLVV(Q)can be computed by solving a linear program (with ${2}^{k} - 1$ variables). As we shall show,the bound is tight on normal lattices and some non-normal lattices we examined in this paper; but in general it is open whether the bound is always tight.

当$\mathrm{{FD}}$和${\left( {N}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$在上下文中明确时，我们将该界简记为$\operatorname{GLVV}\left( Q\right)$。需注意关键事实：GLVV(Q)可通过求解线性规划（含${2}^{k} - 1$个变量）来计算。如本文所示，该界在正规格及我们考察的部分非正规格上是紧致的；但该界是否普遍紧致仍是未解难题。

Closure. Fix a set $\mathsf{{FD}}$ . The closure of a set $X \subseteq  \mathbf{X}$ is the smallest set ${X}^{ + }$ such that $X \subseteq  {X}^{ + }$ ,and that $U \rightarrow  V \in  \mathrm{{FD}}$ and $U \subseteq  {X}^{ + }$ imply $V \subseteq  {X}^{ + };X$ is closed if ${X}^{ + } = X$ ; the intersection of closed sets is closed. Denote by ${Q}^{ + }$ the query obtained by replacing each relation ${R}_{j}\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right)$ with ${R}_{j}\left( {\operatorname{vars}{\left( {R}_{j}\right) }^{ + }}\right)$ ,then forgetting all functional dependencies; then a simple upper bound is $\mathop{\max }\limits_{D}\left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq  \operatorname{AGM}\left( {Q}^{ + }\right)$ . This bound is tight when all fd's are simple keys, because any product database over the schema of ${Q}^{ + }$ can be converted into a database over the schema for $Q$ that satisfies all simple keys. Thus,for simple keys, $\operatorname{AGM}\left( {Q}^{ + }\right)$ is a tight upper bound on $\left| {Q}^{D}\right|$ . Theorem 4.4 in [12] proves essentially the same result. However, for fd's other than simple keys this technique fails,as illustrated by $Q\left( {x,y,z}\right)  =$ $R\left( x\right) ,S\left( y\right) ,T\left( {x,y,z}\right)$ where ${xy}$ is a key in $T$ (i.e. ${xy} \rightarrow  z$ ): when $\left| R\right|  = \left| S\right|  = N,\left| T\right|  = {N}^{100}$ we have ${Q}^{ + } = Q$ and $\operatorname{AGM}\left( {Q}^{ + }\right)  = {N}^{100}$ ,yet one can easily verify that $\left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq  {N}^{2}$ .

闭包。给定一个集合$\mathsf{{FD}}$。集合$X \subseteq  \mathbf{X}$的闭包是最小的集合${X}^{ + }$，使得$X \subseteq  {X}^{ + }$，且$U \rightarrow  V \in  \mathrm{{FD}}$和$U \subseteq  {X}^{ + }$蕴含$V \subseteq  {X}^{ + };X$。若${X}^{ + } = X$则称该集合为闭集；闭集的交集仍是闭集。用${Q}^{ + }$表示将每个关系${R}_{j}\left( {\operatorname{vars}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right)$替换为${R}_{j}\left( {\operatorname{vars}{\left( {R}_{j}\right) }^{ + }}\right)$后、再忽略所有函数依赖所得到的查询，则简单上界为$\mathop{\max }\limits_{D}\left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq  \operatorname{AGM}\left( {Q}^{ + }\right)$。当所有函数依赖都是简单键时，这个界是紧致的，因为${Q}^{ + }$模式上的任何乘积数据库都能转换为满足所有简单键的$Q$模式数据库。因此对于简单键，$\operatorname{AGM}\left( {Q}^{ + }\right)$是$\left| {Q}^{D}\right|$的紧致上界。文献[12]中的定理4.4实质上证明了相同结论。但对于非简单键的函数依赖，该技术失效，如$Q\left( {x,y,z}\right)  =$$R\left( x\right) ,S\left( y\right) ,T\left( {x,y,z}\right)$所示：其中${xy}$是$T$中的键（即${xy} \rightarrow  z$），当$\left| R\right|  = \left| S\right|  = N,\left| T\right|  = {N}^{100}$时我们有${Q}^{ + } = Q$和$\operatorname{AGM}\left( {Q}^{ + }\right)  = {N}^{100}$，但可轻易验证$\left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq  {N}^{2}$。

The Expansion Procedure. All our algorithms use the following simple pre-processing subroutine, called an expansion. Fix a relation $R\left( X\right)$ and a database instance $D.R$ may be an input relation,or some intermediate relation generated during query evaluation. An expansion of ${R}^{D}$ is some relation ${\left( {R}^{ + }\right) }^{D}$ over attributes ${X}^{ + }$ such that ${\Pi }_{{X}^{ + }}\left( {Q}^{D}\right)  \subseteq  {\left( {R}^{ + }\right) }^{D}$ and ${\Pi }_{X}\left( {\left( {R}^{ + }\right) }^{D}\right)  \subseteq  {R}^{D}$ . If ${X}^{ + } = X$ , then the expansion could be ${R}^{D}$ itself,or any partial semi-join reduction that removes dangling tuples from ${R}^{D}$ (which do not join with tuples in other relations). If $X \neq  {X}^{ + }$ ,then the expansion fills in the extra attributes, by repeatedly applying functional dependencies $X \rightarrow  y$ : if the fd is guarded in ${R}_{j}$ ,then it joins $R$ with ${\Pi }_{Xy}\left( {R}_{j}\right)$ ; otherwise,if the fd corresponds to a UDF $y = f\left( X\right)$ then it simply computes $f$ for each tuple in $R$ . The expansion of $R$ can be done in time $\widetilde{O}\left( N\right)$ using trivial techniques.

扩展过程。我们所有算法都使用以下简单的预处理子程序，称为扩展。固定关系$R\left( X\right)$和数据库实例$D.R$（可能是输入关系，或是查询执行过程中生成的中间关系）。${R}^{D}$的扩展是指属性集${X}^{ + }$上的某个关系${\left( {R}^{ + }\right) }^{D}$，满足${\Pi }_{{X}^{ + }}\left( {Q}^{D}\right)  \subseteq  {\left( {R}^{ + }\right) }^{D}$且${\Pi }_{X}\left( {\left( {R}^{ + }\right) }^{D}\right)  \subseteq  {R}^{D}$。若${X}^{ + } = X$，扩展可以是${R}^{D}$自身，或是从${R}^{D}$中移除悬空元组（无法与其他关系元组连接）的部分半连接约简。若$X \neq  {X}^{ + }$，则通过重复应用函数依赖$X \rightarrow  y$来填充额外属性：若该函数依赖在${R}_{j}$中被守护，则将$R$与${\Pi }_{Xy}\left( {R}_{j}\right)$连接；否则若该函数依赖对应UDF$y = f\left( X\right)$，则直接计算$R$中每个元组的$f$。$R$的扩展可在$\widetilde{O}\left( N\right)$时间内通过基础技术完成。

We briefly review some notions of lattice theory needed later and refer the reader to [22] for an extensive treatment. Let $\land  , \vee  ,\widehat{0},\widehat{1}$ denote the greatest lower bound (meet),least upper bound (join), minimum and maximum elements of the lattice. The following hold in ${\mathbf{L}}_{\mathrm{{FD}}} : X \land  Y = X \cap  Y$ , $X \vee  Y = {\left( X \cup  Y\right) }^{ + },\widehat{0} = \varnothing$ ,and $\widehat{1} = \mathbf{X}$ . We write $X \nleq  Y$ to mean $X$ and $Y$ are incomparable. An element $U$ is said to cover an element $V$ of $\mathbf{L}$ if $U \succ  V$ and $U \succcurlyeq  W \succcurlyeq  V$ implies $W = U$ or $W = V$ . A chain is a sequence of elements, one below the next in partial order. A maximal chain is a sequence of elements from $\widehat{0}$ to $\widehat{1}$ where one is covered by the next. An atom is an element $X$ that covers $\widehat{0}$ ; a co-atom is an element covered by $\widehat{1}.X$ is called a join-irreducible if $Y \vee  Z = X$ implies $Y = X$ or $Z = X.X$ is called a meet-irreducible if $Y \land  Z = X$ implies $Y = X$ or $Z = X$ . For any $X \in  \mathbf{L}$ ,let ${\Lambda }_{X}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\{ Z \mid  Z \preccurlyeq  X,Z$ is a join-irreducible $\}$ . The mapping $X \mapsto  {\Lambda }_{X}$ defines a 1-1 mapping from $\mathbf{L}$ to the order ideals of the poset of join-irreducibles, with inverse ${\Lambda }_{X} \mapsto  \bigvee {\Lambda }_{X} = X$

Lattice representations and queries with FDs are in 1-to- 1 correspondence, up to the addition/removal of variables functionally equivalent to some other set of variables. To see this in one direction,consider any pair(L,R)where $\mathbf{R} = \left\{  {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}}\right\}   \subseteq  \mathbf{L}$ and $\bigvee \mathbf{R} = \widehat{1}$ . In the other direction,consider a query with functional dependencies,(Q,FD). Call a variable $x$ redundant if $Y \leftrightarrow  x$ for some set $Y$ that does not contain $x$ . ### 3.3 The Lattice Linear Program

### 3.3 格线性规划

Given a query(L,R)and log cardinalities ${\left( {n}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ ,define the following linear program

$$
\text{maximize}h\left( \widehat{1}\right)  \tag{4}
$$

$$
\forall X \nleq  Y : h\left( {X \land  Y}\right)  + h\left( {X \vee  Y}\right)  \leq  h\left( X\right)  + h\left( Y\right) 
$$

$$
\forall j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack   : h\left( {R}_{j}\right)  \leq  {n}_{j}
$$

over non-negative variables ${\left( h\left( X\right) \right) }_{X \in  \mathbf{L}}$ . This is called the Lattice Linear Program, or LLP; its optimal objective will be shown to be exactly GLVV-bound.

作用于非负变量${\left( h\left( X\right) \right) }_{X \in  \mathbf{L}}$。该规划称为格线性规划（LLP），其最优目标值将被证明恰好等于GLVV界。

A feasible solution $h$ to LLP is called a (non-negative) $\mathbf{L}$ -sub-modular function. If $h$ is also $\mathbf{L}$ -monotone,i.e. $X \preccurlyeq$ $Y \Rightarrow  h\left( X\right)  \leq  h\left( Y\right)$ ,and $h\left( \widehat{0}\right)  = 0$ ,then $h$ is called an $\mathbf{L}$ - polymatroid. When $\mathbf{L}$ is clear from context we drop the $\mathbf{L}$ - prefix from L-monotone, $\mathbf{L}$ -submodular,and $\mathbf{L}$ -polymatroid, respectively. LLP did not require $h$ to be a polymatroid because,at optimality, ${h}^{ * }$ can always be taken to be a poly-matroid thanks to Lovász’s monotonization: if $h$ is nonnegative $\mathbf{L}$ -submodular,then the function $\bar{h}\left( \widehat{0}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }0$ and $\bar{h}\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\min }\limits_{{Y : X \preccurlyeq  Y}}h\left( Y\right) ,X \neq  \widehat{0}$ ,is an $\mathbf{L}$ -polymatroid (see e.g. [21],pp. 774) and satisfies: $\bar{h}\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = h\left( \widehat{1}\right)$ and $\forall X$ , $\bar{h}\left( X\right)  \leq  h\left( X\right)$ . The full version [2] has the (very short) proof of the following lattice-based formulation of GLVV-bound:

LLP的一个可行解$h$被称为（非负）$\mathbf{L}$-子模函数。若$h$同时满足$\mathbf{L}$-单调性，即$X \preccurlyeq$$Y \Rightarrow  h\left( X\right)  \leq  h\left( Y\right)$，且$h\left( \widehat{0}\right)  = 0$，则称$h$为$\mathbf{L}$-多拟阵。当上下文明确时，我们省略L-单调、$\mathbf{L}$-子模和$\mathbf{L}$-多拟阵的前缀$\mathbf{L}$。LLP不要求$h$必须是多拟阵，因为在最优情况下，得益于Lovász单调化定理，${h}^{ * }$总可转化为多拟阵：若$h$是非负$\mathbf{L}$-子模函数，则函数$\bar{h}\left( \widehat{0}\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }0$与$\bar{h}\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\min }\limits_{{Y : X \preccurlyeq  Y}}h\left( Y\right) ,X \neq  \widehat{0}$构成$\mathbf{L}$-多拟阵（参见[21]第774页），并满足：$\bar{h}\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = h\left( \widehat{1}\right)$和$\forall X$，$\bar{h}\left( X\right)  \leq  h\left( X\right)$。完整版[2]给出了基于格的GLVV界公式的（极简短）证明：

Proposition 3.4. Let ${h}^{ * }$ be an optimal solution to the LLP of a query $\left( {Q,{FD}}\right)  = \left( {\mathbf{L},\mathbf{R}}\right)$ . Then, ${2}^{h * \left( \widehat{1}\right) } = {GLVV}\left( Q\right)$ .

命题3.4。设${h}^{ * }$为查询$\left( {Q,{FD}}\right)  = \left( {\mathbf{L},\mathbf{R}}\right)$的LLP最优解，则${2}^{h * \left( \widehat{1}\right) } = {GLVV}\left( Q\right)$。

When $\mathrm{{FD}} = \varnothing ,\mathbf{L}$ is a Boolean algebra in which case we show in [2] that $\operatorname{AGM}\left( Q\right)  = {2}^{{h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right) }$ .

### 3.4 Embeddings

### 3.4 嵌入方法

The main goal in this paper is to design algorithms that compute a query(Q,FD)(i.e.(L,R)) in time $\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{{h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right) }\right)$ . Whenever GLVV-bound is tight such an algorithm is worst-case optimal, hence our second goal is to study cases when the bound is tight. This happens if and only if the LLP has some optimal solution ${h}^{ * }$ that is materializable w.r.t. (L,R). A secondary goal in this paper is to find sufficient conditions for $h$ to be materializable. Embeddings allow us to "transfer" materializability of polymatroids from one lattice to another.

本文主要目标是设计在$\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{{h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right) }\right)$时间内计算查询(Q,FD)（即(L,R)）的算法。当GLVV界紧致时，此类算法具有最坏情况最优性，因此我们的次要目标是研究该界紧致的情形。当且仅当LLP存在关于(L,R)可物化的最优解${h}^{ * }$时，该界才会紧致。本文的另一目标是寻找$h$可物化的充分条件。嵌入技术使我们能够将多拟阵的物化性质从一个格"转移"至另一个格。

Definition 3.5. An embedding between two lattices $\mathbf{L}$ and ${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$ is a function $f : \mathbf{L} \rightarrow  {\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$ such that $f\left( {\bigvee X}\right)  = \bigvee f\left( X\right)$ forall $X \subseteq  \mathbf{L}$ ,and $f\left( {\widehat{1}}_{\mathbf{L}}\right)  = {\widehat{1}}_{{\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }}$ . An embedding between two queries $f : \left( {\mathbf{L},\mathbf{R}}\right)  \rightarrow  \left( {{\mathbf{L}}^{\prime },{\mathbf{R}}^{\prime }}\right)$ is an embedding from $\mathbf{L}$ to ${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$ that is a bijection from $\mathbf{R}$ to ${\mathbf{R}}^{\prime }$ .

定义3.5. 两个格$\mathbf{L}$与${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$间的嵌入(embedding)是指满足$f\left( {\bigvee X}\right)  = \bigvee f\left( X\right)$对所有$X \subseteq  \mathbf{L}$成立、且$f\left( {\widehat{1}}_{\mathbf{L}}\right)  = {\widehat{1}}_{{\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }}$的函数$f : \mathbf{L} \rightarrow  {\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$。两个查询(query)$f : \left( {\mathbf{L},\mathbf{R}}\right)  \rightarrow  \left( {{\mathbf{L}}^{\prime },{\mathbf{R}}^{\prime }}\right)$间的嵌入是指从$\mathbf{L}$到${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$的双射(bijection)$\mathbf{R}$→${\mathbf{R}}^{\prime }$。

If $f$ is an embedding from $\mathbf{L}$ to ${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$ and ${h}^{\prime }$ is a non-negative, sub-modular function on ${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$ ,then one can check that $h\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }$ ${h}^{\prime } \circ  f$ is also sub-modular. If $f$ is an embedding between queries, then their relation symbols are in 1-1 correspondence: ${R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}$ and ${R}_{1}^{\prime },\ldots ,{R}_{m}^{\prime }$ respectively. Call a function $\mathcal{L} : {2}^{\mathbf{X}} \rightarrow  {2}^{{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }}$ a variable renaming if $\mathcal{L}\left( X\right)  =$ $\mathcal{L}\left( {X}^{ + }\right) ,\mathcal{L}\left( X\right)  = {\left( \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right) \right) }^{ + }(\mathcal{L}$ is uniquely defined by its values on single variables),and $\mathcal{L}\left( \mathbf{X}\right)  = {\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }$ (all variables in ${Q}^{\prime }$ are used). 若$f$是从$\mathbf{L}$到${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$的嵌入，且${h}^{\prime }$是${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$上的非负次模函数(sub-modular function)，则可验证$h\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }$${h}^{\prime } \circ  f$也是次模的。当$f$是查询间的嵌入时，其关系符号呈一一对应关系：分别为${R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}$和${R}_{1}^{\prime },\ldots ,{R}_{m}^{\prime }$。给定变量集分别为$\mathbf{X},{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }$的两个查询$\left( {Q,\mathrm{{FD}}}\right) ,\left( {{Q}^{\prime },{\mathrm{{FD}}}^{\prime }}\right)$，若函数$\mathcal{L} : {2}^{\mathbf{X}} \rightarrow  {2}^{{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }}$满足$\mathcal{L}\left( X\right)  =$$\mathcal{L}\left( {X}^{ + }\right) ,\mathcal{L}\left( X\right)  = {\left( \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right) \right) }^{ + }(\mathcal{L}$（由单变量取值唯一确定）且$\mathcal{L}\left( \mathbf{X}\right)  = {\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }$（${Q}^{\prime }$中所有变量均被使用），则称其为变量重命名。可以证明：每个嵌入$f$都定义了变量重命名$\mathcal{L}\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }f\left( {X}^{ + }\right)$（对所有$X \subseteq  \mathbf{X}$成立），反之亦然。

Fix an embedding $f : \mathbf{L} \rightarrow  {\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$ ,and an instance ${D}^{\prime }$ for ${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$ . Proposition 3.6. If $f : \mathbf{L} \rightarrow  {\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$ is a lattice embedding, then for any instance ${D}^{\prime }$ for ${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime },{h}_{D} = {h}_{{D}^{\prime }} \circ  f$ ,where $D\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }$ ${f}^{-1}\left( {D}^{\prime }\right)$ . If $f : \left( {\mathbf{L},\mathbf{R}}\right)  \rightarrow  \left( {{\mathbf{L}}^{\prime },{\mathbf{R}}^{\prime }}\right)$ is a query embedding, and ${h}^{\prime } : {\mathbf{L}}^{\prime } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$ is materializable,then $h\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{h}^{\prime } \circ  f$ is also materializable.

命题3.6。若$f : \mathbf{L} \rightarrow  {\mathbf{L}}^{\prime }$是格嵌入，则对于${\mathbf{L}}^{\prime },{h}_{D} = {h}_{{D}^{\prime }} \circ  f$的任意实例${D}^{\prime }$（其中$D\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }$${f}^{-1}\left( {D}^{\prime }\right)$），当$f : \left( {\mathbf{L},\mathbf{R}}\right)  \rightarrow  \left( {{\mathbf{L}}^{\prime },{\mathbf{R}}^{\prime }}\right)$是查询嵌入且${h}^{\prime } : {\mathbf{L}}^{\prime } \rightarrow  {\mathbb{R}}^{ + }$可物化时，$h\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{h}^{\prime } \circ  f$亦可物化。

Definition 3.7. A quasi-product database instance for $\mathbf{L}$ is an instance of the form ${f}^{-1}\left( {D}^{\prime }\right)$ where $f$ embeds $\mathbf{L}$ into a Boolean algebra $A$ ,and ${D}^{\prime }$ is a product instance for $A$ .

定义3.7。$\mathbf{L}$的拟积数据库实例是形如${f}^{-1}\left( {D}^{\prime }\right)$的实例，其中$f$将$\mathbf{L}$嵌入布尔代数$A$，且${D}^{\prime }$是$A$的积实例。

In absence of fd's, the worst-case output size reaches the AGM-bound on some product instance. In Theorem 4.5 we characterize queries whose worst-case output size is the GLVV-bound, achieved on a quasi-product database instance

在无函数依赖的情况下，最坏情况输出大小在某些积实例上达到AGM界。定理4.5中，我们刻画了最坏情况输出大小为GLVV界的查询，该界在拟积数据库实例上达成。

### 3.5 Inequalities

### 3.5 不等式

In information theory, an information inequality is defined by a vector of real numbers ${\left( {w}_{X}\right) }_{X \subseteq  \mathbf{X}}$ such that the inequality $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{X \subset  \mathbf{X}}}{w}_{X}H\left( X\right)  \geq  0$ holds for any entropic function $H$ . Fix a query(L,R). An output-inequality is given by a vector of non-negative real numbers ${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }$ such that the inequality

在信息论中，信息不等式由实数向量${\left( {w}_{X}\right) }_{X \subseteq  \mathbf{X}}$定义，使得不等式$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{X \subset  \mathbf{X}}}{w}_{X}H\left( X\right)  \geq  0$对任意熵函数$H$成立。给定查询(L,R)，输出不等式由非负实数向量${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }$给出，满足不等式

$$
\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{w}_{j}h\left( {R}_{j}\right)  \geq  h\left( \widehat{1}\right)  \tag{5}
$$

holds for all polymatroids $h$ on the lattice $\mathbf{L}$ . For example, in a Boolean algebra output inequalities correspond to Shearer’s lemma: $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{w}_{j}h\left( {R}_{j}\right)  \geq  h\left( \mathbf{X}\right)$ iff ${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ is a fractional edge cover of the hypergraph with nodes $\{ x \mid  x \in  \mathbf{X}\}$ and hyperedges $\left\{  {{R}_{j} \mid  j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }\right\}$ . Any output inequality gives us immediately an upper bound on the output size of a query,because (5) implies $h\left( \widehat{1}\right)  \leq  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{w}_{j}{n}_{j}$ for any feasible solution $h$ of the LLP with log-cardinalities ${\left( {n}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ .

该性质适用于格$\mathbf{L}$上的所有多拟阵$h$。例如，在布尔代数中，输出不等式对应于希勒引理：当且仅当${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$是节点集为$\{ x \mid  x \in  \mathbf{X}\}$、超边集为$\left\{  {{R}_{j} \mid  j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }\right\}$的超图的一个分数边覆盖时，$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{w}_{j}h\left( {R}_{j}\right)  \geq  h\left( \mathbf{X}\right)$成立。任何输出不等式都能立即给出查询输出大小的上界，因为对于具有对数基数${\left( {n}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$的LLP可行解$h$，(5)式意味着$h\left( \widehat{1}\right)  \leq  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{w}_{j}{n}_{j}$。

An output size upper bound is best if it is minimized, which is precisely the objective of the dual-LLP, which is defined over non-negative variables ${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ and ${\left( {s}_{X,Y}\right) }_{X \nleq  Y}$ , corresponding to the input ${R}_{j}$ and incomparable pairs of lattice elements (thus ${s}_{X,Y}$ is the same variable as ${s}_{Y,X}$ ). The definition of the dual-LLP and the proof of the following lemma can be found in the full version [2].

当输出尺寸上界最小时即为最优，这正是对偶LLP的目标。该问题定义在非负变量${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$和${\left( {s}_{X,Y}\right) }_{X \nleq  Y}$上，分别对应输入${R}_{j}$及格的不可比元素对（因此${s}_{X,Y}$与${s}_{Y,X}$是同一变量）。对偶LLP的定义及下述引理的证明详见完整版[2]。

Lemma 3.8. Let $M$ denote the matrix of sub-modular inequalities (first inequality of the LLP (4)). Given a nonnegative vector ${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }$ ,define the vector ${\left( {c}_{X}\right) }_{X \in  \mathbf{L}}$ by setting ${c}_{\widehat{1}}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }1,{c}_{{R}_{j}}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = } - {w}_{j}$ and ${c}_{X}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }0$ otherwise. Then,the following statements are equivalent

引理3.8. 设$M$表示子模不等式矩阵（LLP(4)的第一个不等式）。给定非负向量${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }$，通过设定${c}_{\widehat{1}}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }1,{c}_{{R}_{j}}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = } - {w}_{j}$（否则为${c}_{X}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }0$）定义向量${\left( {c}_{X}\right) }_{X \in  \mathbf{L}}$，则下列陈述等价：

(i) (5) holds for all polymatroids $h$

(i) 对于所有多拟阵$h$，(5)式成立

(ii) (5) holds for all non-negative sub-modular functions $h$

(ii) 对于所有非负子模函数$h$，(5)式成立

(iii) there exists $s \geq  0$ such that ${c}^{T} \leq  {s}^{T}M$ . Equivalently, $w$ is part of a feasible solution(s,w)of the dual-LLP.

(iii) 存在$s \geq  0$使得${c}^{T} \leq  {s}^{T}M$成立。等价地说，$w$是对偶LLP可行解(s,w)的组成部分。

Furthermore,if $\left( {{s}^{ * },{w}^{ * }}\right)$ and ${h}^{ * }$ are dual- and primal-optimal solutions,then ${h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{w}_{j}^{ * }{n}_{j}$ .

此外，若$\left( {{s}^{ * },{w}^{ * }}\right)$与${h}^{ * }$分别为对偶最优解和原始最优解，则${h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{w}_{j}^{ * }{n}_{j}$。

## 4. NORMAL LATTICES

## 4. 正规格

This section shows that an $\mathbf{L}$ -polymatroid $h$ can be materialized as a quasi-product database instance iff it satisfies all output inequalities given by fractional edge covers of a certain hypergraph; in that case we call $h$ normal. Normal polymatroids are the largest class of polymatroids that preserve the elegant properties of the AGM bound: upper bound given in terms of a fractional edge cover, and lower bound given by a (quasi-) product database. We then extend normality to lattices, which we call normal if the lattice's optimal polymatroid ${h}^{ * }$ is normal. We show that every feasible solution to LLP on a normal lattice is materializable, and thus the GLVV-bound is tight on a normal lattice.

本节证明：当且仅当<b1></b0>多拟阵满足特定超图分数边覆盖给出的所有输出不等式时，才能将其物化为拟积数据库实例，此时我们称$h$为正规多拟阵。正规多拟阵是保持AGM界优良特性的最大多拟阵类——上界由分数边覆盖给出，下界由（拟）积数据库给出。随后将正规性推广至格，若格的最优多拟阵${h}^{ * }$是正规的，则称该格为正规格。我们证明正规格上LLP的每个可行解均可物化，因此GLVV界在正规格上是紧致的。

### 4.1 Normal polymatroids

### 4.1 正规多拟阵

Recall the Möbius inversion formula [22] in a lattice $\mathbf{L}$ :

回顾格$\mathbf{L}$中的莫比乌斯反演公式[22]：

$$
h\left( X\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Y : X \preccurlyeq  Y}}g\left( Y\right) \;\text{ iff }\;g\left( X\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Y : X \preccurlyeq  Y}}\mu \left( {X,Y}\right) h\left( Y\right) 
$$

(6)

where $\mu \left( {X,Y}\right)$ is the Möbius function on $\mathbf{L}$ [22]. In information theory,when $h$ is an entropy and $\mathbf{L}$ a Boolean algebra, the quantity $- g\left( X\right)$ is exactly the (multivariate) conditional mutual information $I\left( {\widehat{1} - X \mid  X}\right)$ ,which we abbreviate CMI. There is a simple sufficient condition on $g$ which implies that $h$ ,defined by Eq.(6) is a polymatroid. (See Appendix A or the full version [2] for missing proofs.)

其中$\mu \left( {X,Y}\right)$是定义在$\mathbf{L}$上的莫比乌斯函数(Möbius function)[22]。在信息论中，当$h$表示熵且$\mathbf{L}$为布尔代数时，量$- g\left( X\right)$正是(多元)条件互信息<条件互信息>(multivariate conditional mutual information)$I\left( {\widehat{1} - X \mid  X}\right)$，我们简称为CMI。存在一个关于$g$的简单充分条件，可确保由公式(6)定义的$h$是拟阵多面体(polymatroid)。（缺失证明参见附录A或完整版[2]）

Lemma 4.1. Let $g$ be any function s.t. $g\left( Z\right)  \leq  0$ for $Z \prec  \widehat{1}$ , and $g\left( \widehat{1}\right)  =  - \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Z \prec  \widehat{1}}}g\left( Z\right)$ . Then the function $h$ defined by Eq. (6) is a polymatroid. Furthermore,if $\mathbf{L}$ is distributive, then $h$ is a modular polymatroid,i.e. $h\left( X\right)  + h\left( Y\right)  = h(X \vee$ $Y) + h\left( {X \land  Y}\right)$ for all $X,Y \in  \mathbf{L}$ .

引理4.1. 设$g$为满足$g\left( Z\right)  \leq  0$（当$Z \prec  \widehat{1}$时）及$g\left( \widehat{1}\right)  =  - \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{Z \prec  \widehat{1}}}g\left( Z\right)$的任意函数。则由公式(6)定义的函数$h$构成拟阵多面体。此外，若$\mathbf{L}$具有分配性，则$h$为模拟阵多面体(modular polymatroid)，即对所有$X,Y \in  \mathbf{L}$满足$h\left( X\right)  + h\left( Y\right)  = h(X \vee$$Y) + h\left( {X \land  Y}\right)$。

Any function $h$ satisfying the property stated in Lemma 4.1 is called a normal submodular function. If, furthermore, $g\left( Z\right)  = 0$ for all $Z \prec  \widehat{1}$ other than the co-atoms,then we say that $h$ is strictly normal. The function $h$ in our running example Fig. 1 is strictly normal because it is defined by the CMI $g$ shown in the figure. The function $h$ on the left of Fig. 6 is not normal,because its CMI satisfies $g\left( \widehat{0}\right)  > 0$ .

满足引理4.1所述性质的函数$h$称为正规子模函数(normal submodular function)。若进一步对除余原子(co-atoms)外的所有$Z \prec  \widehat{1}$满足$g\left( Z\right)  = 0$，则称$h$为严格正规函数。图1示例中的函数$h$因由图中所示CMI$g$定义，故为严格正规函数。图6左侧的$h$函数因CMI满足$g\left( \widehat{0}\right)  > 0$而非正规。

Normal polymatroids are precisely non-negative linear combinations of "step functions". For every $Z \in  \mathbf{L}$ ,the step function ${h}_{Z}$ at $Z$ is defined by ${h}_{Z}\left( X\right)  = 1$ if $X \npreceq  Z$ ,and 0 otherwise. Every step function is normal, because its Möbius inverse is ${g}_{Z}\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = 1,{g}_{Z}\left( Z\right)  =  - 1$ ,and ${g}_{Z}\left( X\right)  = 0$ otherwise. Any non-negative linear combination of step functions is normal. Conversely,if $h$ is a normal polymatroid,then denoting ${a}_{Z} =  - g\left( Z\right)$ for all $Z \neq  \widehat{1}$ we have ${a}_{Z} \geq  0$ (since $h$ is normal) and $g = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{Z}{a}_{Z}{g}_{Z}$ ,implying $h = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{Z}{a}_{Z}{h}_{Z}$ .

正规拟阵多面体本质上是"阶梯函数"的非负线性组合。对于每个$Z \in  \mathbf{L}$，定义$Z$处的阶梯函数${h}_{Z}$为：若$X \npreceq  Z$则${h}_{Z}\left( X\right)  = 1$，否则为0。每个阶梯函数都是正规的，因为其莫比乌斯逆为${g}_{Z}\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = 1,{g}_{Z}\left( Z\right)  =  - 1$（否则为${g}_{Z}\left( X\right)  = 0$）。阶梯函数的任意非负线性组合均为正规。反之，若$h$是正规拟阵多面体，则对所有$Z \neq  \widehat{1}$令${a}_{Z} =  - g\left( Z\right)$，可得${a}_{Z} \geq  0$（因$h$正规性）且$g = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{Z}{a}_{Z}{g}_{Z}$，故有$h = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{Z}{a}_{Z}{h}_{Z}$。

There are a couple of interesting properties of normal polymatroids, both of which allow us to prove lower bounds for ${h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right)$ . The first property is a generalization of the fact that the AGM-bound is tight.

正规拟阵多面体具有若干重要性质，均可用于推导${h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right)$的下界。首个性质是AGM界紧性(AGM-bound tightness)的推广。

Lemma 4.2. Let $h$ be an integral,non-negative,submodular function $h$ on a lattice $\mathbf{L}$ . Then $h$ is normal iff it is the entropy function of a quasi-product instance (Definition 3.7).

引理4.2. 设格$\mathbf{L}$上的函数$h$为整值、非负、子模函数，则$h$是正规的当且仅当其为拟积实例(quasi-product instance)的熵函数（定义3.7）。

The second property is a generalization of AGM-bound. To state the second property, we need:

第二个性质是AGM界的推广。阐述该性质需要以下前提：

Definition 4.3. Let(L,R)be a query given in lattice representation. The co-atomic hypergraph ${H}_{\mathrm{{co}}} = \left( {{V}_{\mathrm{{co}}},{E}_{\mathrm{{co}}}}\right)$ is defined as follows. The nodes ${V}_{\mathrm{{co}}}$ are the co-atoms of $\mathbf{L}$ ,and ${E}_{\mathrm{{co}}} = \left\{  {{e}_{1},\ldots ,{e}_{m}}\right\}$ with ${e}_{j} = \left\{  {Z \mid  Z \in  {V}_{\mathrm{{co}}},{R}_{j} \npreceq  Z,{R}_{j} \in  \mathbf{R}}\right\}$ . In other words,each relation ${R}_{j}$ defines a hyperedge ${e}_{j}$ consisting of those nodes that do not contain the variables of ${R}_{j}$ . A simple illustration of a co-atomic hypergraph is in Fig.2.

定义4.3 设(L,R)为格表示法给出的查询。共原子超图${H}_{\mathrm{{co}}} = \left( {{V}_{\mathrm{{co}}},{E}_{\mathrm{{co}}}}\right)$定义如下：节点${V}_{\mathrm{{co}}}$是$\mathbf{L}$的共原子，且满足${E}_{\mathrm{{co}}} = \left\{  {{e}_{1},\ldots ,{e}_{m}}\right\}$与${e}_{j} = \left\{  {Z \mid  Z \in  {V}_{\mathrm{{co}}},{R}_{j} \npreceq  Z,{R}_{j} \in  \mathbf{R}}\right\}$的关系。换言之，每个关系${R}_{j}$定义了一个超边${e}_{j}$，该超边由不包含${R}_{j}$变量的节点组成。图2展示了共原子超图的简单示例。

Lemma 4.4. Inequality (5) holds for all normal polyma-troids $h$ iff it holds for all strictly normal polymatroids iff ${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ is a fractional edge cover of ${H}_{co}$ .

引理4.4 不等式(5)对所有正规多拟阵$h$成立，当且仅当其对所有严格正规多拟阵成立，当且仅当${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$是${H}_{co}$的分数边覆盖。

The co-atomic hypergraph is the natural concept to capture Shearer's lemma in a general lattice. In fact, every vector ${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ for which the output inequality (5) holds is a fractional edge cover of the co-atomic hypergraph. One may wonder whether the output inequalities could also be described by the atomic hypergraph, defined in a similar way, however the latter does not seem to lead to any interesting properties,except for a Boolean algebra ${2}^{\mathbf{X}}$ where the atomic and co-atomic hypergraphs are isomorphic via $x \mapsto  \mathbf{X} - \{ x\}$ .

共原子超图是捕捉一般格中希勒引理的自然概念。实际上，所有使输出不等式(5)成立的向量${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$都是共原子超图的分数边覆盖。有人可能质疑输出不等式是否也能用类似方式定义的原子超图来描述，但后者除布尔代数${2}^{\mathbf{X}}$外似乎不具备有趣性质——在布尔代数中，原子超图与共原子超图通过$x \mapsto  \mathbf{X} - \{ x\}$同构。

### 4.2 Normal Lattices

### 4.2 正规格

A normal lattice is a lattice where, at optimality, the poly-matroid is normal. This is captured by the following, which is the main result of this section. (See the full version [2] for a proof.)

正规格是指在该格的最优状态下，多拟阵呈现正规性质的格结构。这由以下定理精确刻画，该定理是本小节的核心成果（证明详见完整版[2]）。

Theorem 4.5. Let(L,R)be a query in lattice representation. The following are equivalent:

定理4.5 设(L,R)为格表示法中的查询，下列陈述等价：

1. For every non-negative,submodular function $h$ there exists a normal polymatroid ${h}^{\prime }$ s.t. ${h}^{\prime }\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = h\left( \widehat{1}\right)$ and ${h}^{\prime }\left( {R}_{j}\right)  \leq  h\left( {R}_{j}\right)$ for all ${R}_{j} \in  \mathbf{R}$ .

1. 对每个非负次模函数$h$，存在正规多拟阵${h}^{\prime }$使得${h}^{\prime }\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = h\left( \widehat{1}\right)$且${h}^{\prime }\left( {R}_{j}\right)  \leq  h\left( {R}_{j}\right)$对所有${R}_{j} \in  \mathbf{R}$成立。

2. The previous property holds for ${h}^{\prime }$ strictly normal.

2. 上述性质对严格正规的${h}^{\prime }$成立。

3. Output inequality (5) holds for all non-negative, sub-modular functions $h$ iff ${\left\{  {w}_{j}\right\}  }_{j = 1}^{m}$ is a fractional edge cover of the co-atomic hypergraph.

3. 输出不等式(5)对所有非负次模函数$h$成立，当且仅当${\left\{  {w}_{j}\right\}  }_{j = 1}^{m}$是共原子超图的分数边覆盖。

4. Every non-negative submodular function $h$ on $\mathbf{L}$ has a materialization that is a quasi-product database instance.

4. $\mathbf{L}$上每个非负次模函数$h$都具有可实现为拟积数据库实例的具体化。

We call $\mathbf{L}$ a normal lattice w.r.t. $\mathbf{R}$ if it satisfies any of these conditions, and a normal lattice if it is normal w.r.t. all $\mathbf{R}$ . Normal lattices appear to be a new concept. It is decidable whether $\mathbf{L}$ is normal w.r.t. $\mathbf{R}$ using a brute-force procedure. In a normal lattice the GLVV-bound is tight (by item 4); we show [2] that every distributive lattice is normal, and thus the GLVV-bound is tight on those lattices.

若格$\mathbf{L}$满足上述任一条件，则称其关于$\mathbf{R}$是正规格；若关于所有$\mathbf{R}$都正规，则简称为正规格。正规格似乎是一个新概念。通过穷举法可判定$\mathbf{L}$是否关于$\mathbf{R}$正规。在正规格中GLVV界是紧的（由条件4可得）；我们证明[2]所有分配格都是正规格，因此GLVV界在这些格上具有紧性。

Example 4.6. The lattice $\mathbf{L}$ in Fig.1 is normal w.r.t. inputs ${xy},{yz},{zu}$ ,which follows by exhaustively proving all inequalities defined by the fractional edge covers of the co-atomic hypergraph, shown in Fig. 2. For example, the edge cover $\left( {1/2,1/2,1/2}\right)$ corresponds to the inequality $h\left( {xy}\right)  + h\left( {yz}\right)  +$ $h\left( {zu}\right)  \geq  {2h}\left( \widehat{1}\right)$ ,which can be proven by $h\left( {xy}\right)  + h\left( {yz}\right)  \geq$ $h\left( \widehat{1}\right)  + h\left( y\right)$ and $h\left( y\right)  + h\left( {zu}\right)  \geq  h\left( \widehat{1}\right)  + h\left( 0\right)$ . In fact, $\mathbf{L}$ is normal w.r.t. any inputs. Notice that $\mathbf{L}$ is not distributive.

In the full version [2] we prove a necessary condition for normality, which we conjecture to be sufficient. Gottlob et al. [12] define a coloring of a query $Q$ with variables $\mathbf{X}$ to be a function $\mathcal{L} : \mathbf{X} \rightarrow  {2}^{{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }}$ such that $\mathcal{L}\left( \mathbf{X}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$ ,and for any FD $X \rightarrow  Y,\mathcal{L}\left( Y\right)  \subseteq  \mathcal{L}\left( X\right)$ ,where,for each set $X$ , $\mathcal{L}\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( X\right)$ . Then the color number of $\mathcal{L}$ is defined as $C\left( \mathcal{L}\right)  = \left| {\mathcal{L}\left( \mathbf{X}\right) }\right| /\mathop{\max }\limits_{j}\left| {\mathcal{L}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right|$ and the color number of $Q$ is $\mathop{\max }\limits_{\mathcal{L}}C\left( \mathcal{L}\right)$ . They prove two results: if the functional dependencies are restricted to simple keys then $\left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq  {\left( \mathop{\max }\limits_{j}\left| {R}_{j}^{D}\right| \right) }^{C\left( Q\right) }$ ,and moreover this bound is essentially tight, even for general functional dependencies.

完整版本[2]中我们证明了正规性的必要条件，并推测该条件亦为充分条件。Gottlob等人[12]将查询$Q$（含变量$\mathbf{X}$）的着色定义为函数$\mathcal{L} : \mathbf{X} \rightarrow  {2}^{{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }}$，其满足$\mathcal{L}\left( \mathbf{X}\right)  \neq  \varnothing$，且对于任意函数依赖$X \rightarrow  Y,\mathcal{L}\left( Y\right)  \subseteq  \mathcal{L}\left( X\right)$（其中每个集合$X$对应$\mathcal{L}\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( X\right)$）。此时$\mathcal{L}$的色数定义为$C\left( \mathcal{L}\right)  = \left| {\mathcal{L}\left( \mathbf{X}\right) }\right| /\mathop{\max }\limits_{j}\left| {\mathcal{L}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right|$，而$Q$的色数为$\mathop{\max }\limits_{\mathcal{L}}C\left( \mathcal{L}\right)$。他们证明了两项结论：若函数依赖限定为简单键，则$\left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq  {\left( \mathop{\max }\limits_{j}\left| {R}_{j}^{D}\right| \right) }^{C\left( Q\right) }$；且此界限本质上具有紧致性，即便对于一般函数依赖亦然。

Colorings correspond one-to-one to integral, normal poly-matroids,via $h\left( x\right)  = \left| {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right) }\right|$ . To see this,in one direction let $\mathcal{L} : \mathbf{X} \rightarrow  {2}^{{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }}$ be a coloring and define $h\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }$ $\left| {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right) }\right|$ . The function $f\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right)$ is an embedding $f : \mathbf{L} \rightarrow  {2}^{{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }}$ into a Boolean algebra (we assume w.l.o.g. that $f\left( \mathbf{X}\right)  = {\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }$ ,otherwise we redefine ${\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }$ ),and we have $h = {h}^{\prime } \circ  f$ where ${h}^{\prime }\left( Y\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| Y\right|  :$ since ${h}^{\prime }$ is a strictly normal polymatroid Lemma A. 1 says that $h$ is also normal. In the other direction,if $h$ is an integral,normal polyma-troid, then its canonical embedding (Definition A.2) defines a coloring $\mathcal{L}$ s.t. $h\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right) }\right|$ . Thus,colorings are essentially normal polymatroids. This implies the two results in [12] as follows. If FD consists only of simple keys, then the lattice $\mathbf{L}$ is distributive (Prop. 3.2),hence it is normal. Consequently,at optimality ${h}^{ * }$ can be assumed to be a normal polymatroid (Theorem 4.5 item 1), equivalently a coloring. The second result, tightness, follows from our Lemma 4.2, since any normal polymatroid (coloring) is the entropy of a quasi-product database instance.

着色与整型正规多拟阵通过$h\left( x\right)  = \left| {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right) }\right|$形成一一对应关系。为理解这一点，一方面设$\mathcal{L} : \mathbf{X} \rightarrow  {2}^{{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }}$为着色并定义$h\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }$$\left| {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right) }\right|$。函数$f\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right)$是将$f : \mathbf{L} \rightarrow  {2}^{{\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }}$嵌入布尔代数的映射（不妨设$f\left( \mathbf{X}\right)  = {\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }$，否则重新定义${\mathbf{X}}^{\prime }$），且有$h = {h}^{\prime } \circ  f$其中${h}^{\prime }\left( Y\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| Y\right|  :$因为${h}^{\prime }$是严格正规多拟阵。引理A.1表明$h$也是正规的。另一方面，若$h$是整型正规多拟阵，则其典范嵌入（定义A.2）定义了满足$h\left( X\right) \overset{\text{ def }}{ = }\left| {\mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{x \in  X}}\mathcal{L}\left( x\right) }\right|$的着色$\mathcal{L}$。因此，着色本质上是正规多拟阵。由此可推出文献[12]中的两个结论：当FD仅含简单键时，格$\mathbf{L}$是分配的（命题3.2），故其正规；因此在最优情形下，${h}^{ * }$可视为正规多拟阵（定理4.5第1项），等价于着色。第二个结论（紧致性）由引理4.2得出，因为任何正规多拟阵（着色）都是拟积数据库实例的熵。

## 5. PROOF SEQUENCE TO ALGORITHM

## 5. 从证明序列到算法

We aim to design an algorithm that, given an input query $Q$ and database instance $D$ ,computes ${Q}^{D}$ in time $\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{h * \left( \widehat{1}\right) }\right)$ , where ${h}^{ * }$ is the optimal solution to the LLP (4). From Lemma 3.8,if $\left( {{s}^{ * },{w}^{ * }}\right)$ is dual-optimal then ${h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{w}_{j}^{ * }{n}_{j}$ and the output inequality (5) holds with ${w}_{j} = {w}_{j}^{ * }$ ,for all polymatroids. Our main algorithmic theme is to turn this process "inside-out": find a "proof" of inequality (5) consisting of a sequence of steps transforming the left-hand-side into the right-hand-side, then interpret these steps as algorithmic steps whose runtime does not exceed what the symbolic step allows.

我们的目标是设计一种算法，在给定输入查询$Q$和数据库实例$D$时，能在$\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{h * \left( \widehat{1}\right) }\right)$时间内计算出${Q}^{D}$，其中${h}^{ * }$是LLP问题(4)的最优解。根据引理3.8，若$\left( {{s}^{ * },{w}^{ * }}\right)$对偶最优，则${h}^{ * }\left( \widehat{1}\right)  = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{w}_{j}^{ * }{n}_{j}$且输出不等式(5)对所有多拟阵成立且${w}_{j} = {w}_{j}^{ * }$。我们的核心算法思想是将此过程"内外反转"：寻找将不等式(5)左端转化为右端的证明步骤序列，然后将这些步骤解释为算法步骤，其运行时间不超过符号步骤所允许的复杂度。

We show that three different proof techniques for (5) lead to three different bounds and algorithms. The first two are known techniques for proving Shearer's lemma (i.e. the Boolean algebra case) that we adapt to lattices. The bounds are tight and the corresponding algorithms are worst-case optimal for some classes of lattices (such as distributive lattices, which include the Boolean algebra and the lattices of queries with simple fd’s, subsuming results from [12, 18,23]). However, for a generic lattice the bounds these techniques can prove are not $h\left( \widehat{1}\right)  \leq  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{w}_{j}^{ * }h\left( {R}_{j}\right)$ ,and thus the corresponding algorithms' runtimes in general can be worse than $\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{h * \left( \widehat{1}\right) }\right)$ . An advantage of these two algorithms is that there is only one log-factor hidden in the $\widetilde{O}$ . Due to space limitations, the second bound and its corresponding algorithm are only described in the full version [2].

我们证明针对(5)式的三种不同证明技术会导出三种不同的界和算法。前两种是已知证明Shearer引理（即布尔代数情形）的技术，我们将其适配到格结构。这些界是紧致的，对应算法在特定格类（如分配格，包含布尔代数及具有简单函数依赖的查询格，涵盖[12,18,23]的结果）上是最坏情况最优的。但对于一般格结构，这些技术能证明的界并非$h\left( \widehat{1}\right)  \leq  \mathop{\sum }\limits_{j}{w}_{j}^{ * }h\left( {R}_{j}\right)$，因此对应算法的运行时可能劣于$\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{h * \left( \widehat{1}\right) }\right)$。这两种算法的优势在于$\widetilde{O}$中仅隐含一个对数因子。由于篇幅限制，第二界及其对应算法仅在完整版[2]中描述。

The last proof technique is our own, designed specifically to achieve the optimal LLP bound in an arbitrary lattice; this leads to an algorithm called CSMA running in the stated time of $\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{h * \left( \widehat{1}\right) }\right)$ ,where $\widetilde{O}$ hides a polylogarithmic factor. This algorithm needs to regularize the data, somewhat similar in spirit to the uniformization step in [17] (see [2]).

最后一种证明技术是我们独创的，专为在任意格中达到最优LLP界而设计；由此产生的CSMA算法运行时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {2}^{h * \left( \widehat{1}\right) }\right)$，其中$\widetilde{O}$隐含多对数因子。该算法需对数据进行正则化处理，其思想与文献[17]中的均匀化步骤类似（参见[2]）。

### 5.1 Chain Bound and Chain Algorithm (CA)

### 5.1 链界与链算法(CA)

Our first proof sequence adapts Jaikumar Radhakrishnan's proof of Shearer's lemma [20] to lattices, which is based on an arbitrary but fixed order of the random variables. We observe that a variable ordering corresponds to a maximal chain in the Boolean algebra. This idea allows us to generalize Radhakrishnan's proof to general lattices. Fix a chain $\mathbf{C} : \widehat{0} = {C}_{0} \prec  {C}_{1} \prec  \cdots  \prec  {C}_{k} = \widehat{1}$ in $\mathbf{L}$ . For $X \in  \mathbf{L}$ ,we say that $X$ covers $i$ if $X \land  {C}_{i} \neq  X \land  {C}_{i - 1}$ . Intuitively, $X$ covers $i$ if it contains some variable in ${C}_{i}$ that does not appear in ${C}_{i - 1}$ . Fix a query represented by(L,R),where $\mathbf{R} = \left( {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}}\right)$ .

首个证明序列将Jaikumar Radhakrishnan证明Shearer引理的方法[20]推广到格结构，其基于随机变量的任意但固定排序。我们观察到变量排序对应布尔代数中的极大链。这一思想使我们能将Radhakrishnan的证明推广到一般格。固定$\mathbf{L}$中的链$\mathbf{C} : \widehat{0} = {C}_{0} \prec  {C}_{1} \prec  \cdots  \prec  {C}_{k} = \widehat{1}$。对于$X \in  \mathbf{L}$，当$X \land  {C}_{i} \neq  X \land  {C}_{i - 1}$时称$X$覆盖$i$。直观上，若$X$包含${C}_{i}$中某些${C}_{i - 1}$未出现的变量，则称其覆盖$i$。固定由(L,R)表示的查询，其中$\mathbf{R} = \left( {{R}_{1},\ldots ,{R}_{m}}\right)$。

Definition 5.1. The chain hypergraph associated with a chain $\mathbf{C}$ is ${H}_{\mathbf{C}} = \left( {\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  ,\left\{  {{e}_{j} \mid  j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }\right\}  }\right)$ ,where the hyperedge ${e}_{j}$ contains all nodes $i$ such that ${R}_{j}$ covers $i$ .

定义5.1. 与链$\mathbf{C}$关联的链超图是${H}_{\mathbf{C}} = \left( {\left\lbrack  k\right\rbrack  ,\left\{  {{e}_{j} \mid  j \in  \left\lbrack  m\right\rbrack  }\right\}  }\right)$，其中超边${e}_{j}$包含所有满足${R}_{j}$覆盖$i$的节点$i$。

For that reason, we relax the maximality requirement on the chain, as follows. We say that the chain $\mathbf{C}$ is good for ${R}_{j}$ if:

布尔代数中所有极大链具有相同的超图，这与查询超图及共原子超图一致。但在一般格结构中这些超图可能不同，且某些输出不等式(5)只能从非极大链导出。因此我们放宽链的极大性要求如下：称链$\mathbf{C}$对${R}_{j}$是好的，若满足：

$$

The Chain Algorithm. In the proof above of the chain bound, the main idea is to take mixtures of conditional entropies (i.e. differences of two entropies), climbing up the chain. This strategy corresponds combinatorially to conditional search. From this proof, we derive Algorithm 1 that computes a query $Q$ in time bounded by any fractional edge cover of the chain hypergraph. The algorithm assumes a fixed,good chain $\mathbf{C}$ ,where every node $i$ is covered ${}^{4}$ . It preprocesses input relations by indexing them in an attribute order consistent with the chain. Then, it starts by expanding the query, as explained at the end of Sec. 2. Thus far it takes $\widetilde{O}\left( N\right)$ -time. Next,the algorithm computes inductively

链式算法。在上述链式边界证明中，核心思想是通过混合条件熵（即两个熵的差值）沿链式结构递推。该策略在组合逻辑上对应条件搜索。基于此证明，我们推导出算法1，该算法能在由链式超图的任意分数边覆盖所限定的时间内计算查询$Q$。算法假定存在一个固定优质链$\mathbf{C}$，其中每个节点$i$都被${}^{4}$覆盖。算法首先按照与链式结构一致的属性顺序对输入关系建立索引进行预处理，随后如第2章末尾所述展开查询——此阶段耗时$\widetilde{O}\left( N\right)$。接着，算法通过归纳计算

$$
{Q}_{i}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{\left( { \boxtimes  }_{j : {R}_{j} \land  {C}_{i} \neq  \widehat{0}}{\Pi }_{{R}_{j} \land  {C}_{i}}\left( {R}_{j}\right) \right) }^{ + },
$$

for $i = 0,1,\ldots ,k$ ,where ${Q}_{i}$ is an intermediate relation with attributes ${C}_{i}$ . Initially ${Q}_{0}$ consists of just the empty tuple. Evidently,when $i = k,{Q}_{k}$ is the output $Q$ .

对于$i = 0,1,\ldots ,k$，其中${Q}_{i}$是包含属性${C}_{i}$的中间关系。初始时${Q}_{0}$仅含空元组。显然当$i = k,{Q}_{k}$为输出时$Q$。

To compute ${Q}_{i}$ ,let ${R}_{j}$ be some relation such that $i \in  {e}_{j}$ , in other words ${R}_{j}$ has some new variable that occurs in ${C}_{i}$ but not in ${C}_{i - 1}$ . Note that there exists at least one such ${R}_{j}$ , because $i$ is covered. Define

为计算${Q}_{i}$，设${R}_{j}$为满足$i \in  {e}_{j}$的关系，即${R}_{j}$包含出现在${C}_{i}$但未在${C}_{i - 1}$中的新变量。注意由于$i$被覆盖，至少存在一个这样的${R}_{j}$。定义

$$
{T}_{ij} = {Q}_{i - 1} \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\left( {R}_{j}\right) ,
$$

whose attributes are ${X}_{ij}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{C}_{i - 1} \cup  \left( {{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\right)$ ; and,by Eq.(7), its closure is ${X}_{ij}^{ + } = {C}_{i - 1} \vee  \left( {{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\right)  = {C}_{i}$ . Consider ${T}_{ij}$ ’s expansion ${T}_{ij}^{ + }$ (see Sec. 2): it has the same size as ${T}_{ij}$ and has attributes ${X}_{ij}^{ + } = {C}_{i}$ . Therefore,

其属性为${X}_{ij}\overset{\text{ def }}{ = }{C}_{i - 1} \cup  \left( {{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\right)$；根据式(7)，其闭包为${X}_{ij}^{ + } = {C}_{i - 1} \vee  \left( {{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\right)  = {C}_{i}$。考察${T}_{ij}$的扩展${T}_{ij}^{ + }$（见第2章）：其规模与${T}_{ij}$相同且具有属性${X}_{ij}^{ + } = {C}_{i}$。因此，

$$
{Q}_{i} = \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j : i \in  {e}_{j}}}{T}_{ij}^{ + } = \mathop{\bigcap }\limits_{{j : i \in  {e}_{j}}}{\left( {Q}_{i - 1} \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\left( {R}_{j}\right) \right) }^{ + }.
$$

However,we do not want to compute all the ${T}_{ij}^{ + }$ and then compute the intersection to obtain ${Q}_{i}$ ,because this naïve strategy will push the runtime over the budget. In order to stay within the time budget, the algorithm computes this intersection differently: it iterates over all tuples $t \in  {Q}_{i - 1}$ ,and for each tuple computes the intersection $T$ in line 6 in time $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathop{\min }\limits_{{j : i \in  {e}_{j}}}\left( \left| {t \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{R}_{j} \land  {C}_{i}}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right| \right) }\right)$ . This can be accomplished by first computing ${j}_{ * } = {\operatorname{argmin}}_{j : i \in  {e}_{j}}\left( \left| {t \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{R}_{j} \land  {C}_{i}}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right| \right)$ and tentatively setting $T = {\left( t \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{R}_{{j}_{ * }} \land  {C}_{i}}\left( {R}_{{j}_{ * }}\right) \right) }^{ + }$ . Then,the algorithm removes from $T$ any tuple ${t}^{\prime }$ that is not in the intersection defined in line 6. A tuple ${t}^{\prime } \in  T$ is not removed from $T$ only if the following holds: for every $j \neq  {j}_{ * }$ s.t. ${R}_{j}$ covers $i$ ,we have ${\Pi }_{{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\left( {t}^{\prime }\right)  \in  {\Pi }_{{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\left( {R}_{j}\right)$ and ${\left( t \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\left( {t}^{\prime }\right) \right) }^{ + } = {t}^{\prime }$ . Note the crucial fact that the relation ${R}_{{j}_{ * }}$ that is used to iterate over may depend on the tuple $t \in  {Q}_{i - 1}$ . Due to the pre-processing step where every input relation is indexed with an attribute order consistent with the chain, ${j}_{ * }$ can easily be computed in logarithmic time in data complexity. The following is proved in Appendix B.

然而，我们并不希望先计算所有${T}_{ij}^{ + }$再求交集来获得${Q}_{i}$，因为这种朴素策略会导致运行时间超出预算。为了将时间控制在预算范围内，该算法采用不同方式计算交集：遍历所有元组$t \in  {Q}_{i - 1}$，并在第6行以$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathop{\min }\limits_{{j : i \in  {e}_{j}}}\left( \left| {t \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{R}_{j} \land  {C}_{i}}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right| \right) }\right)$时间复杂度为每个元组计算交集$T$。具体实现时，首先计算${j}_{ * } = {\operatorname{argmin}}_{j : i \in  {e}_{j}}\left( \left| {t \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{R}_{j} \land  {C}_{i}}\left( {R}_{j}\right) }\right| \right)$并暂设$T = {\left( t \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{R}_{{j}_{ * }} \land  {C}_{i}}\left( {R}_{{j}_{ * }}\right) \right) }^{ + }$，然后从$T$中移除所有不符合第6行定义的交集条件的元组${t}^{\prime }$。仅当满足以下条件时，元组${t}^{\prime } \in  T$才会保留在$T$中：对于每个满足${R}_{j}$覆盖$i$的$j \neq  {j}_{ * }$，都有${\Pi }_{{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\left( {t}^{\prime }\right)  \in  {\Pi }_{{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\left( {R}_{j}\right)$和${\left( t \boxtimes  {\Pi }_{{C}_{i} \land  {R}_{j}}\left( {t}^{\prime }\right) \right) }^{ + } = {t}^{\prime }$。需注意关键点：用于迭代的关系${R}_{{j}_{ * }}$可能依赖于元组$t \in  {Q}_{i - 1}$。由于预处理阶段已将所有输入关系按照与链一致的属性顺序建立索引，${j}_{ * }$可在数据复杂度对数时间内完成计算。附录B给出了相关证明。

Theorem 5.7. Assume the chain $\mathbf{C}$ is good for $\mathbf{R}$ ,and every node $i$ is covered (i.e. no isolated vertices). Then,for any fractional edge cover of the chain hypergraph, ${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$ , the time taken by the Chain Algorithm to compute $Q$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( {N + \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{N}_{j}^{{w}_{j}}}\right)$ ,where $\widetilde{O}$ hides a logarithmic factor needed for index lookup or binary search, and a small polynomial factor in query complexity.

定理5.7. 假设链$\mathbf{C}$对$\mathbf{R}$是良构的，且每个节点$i$均被覆盖（即不存在孤立顶点）。那么对于链超图的任意分数边覆盖${\left( {w}_{j}\right) }_{j = 1}^{m}$，链算法计算$Q$的时间复杂度为$\widetilde{O}\left( {N + \mathop{\prod }\limits_{{j = 1}}^{m}{N}_{j}^{{w}_{j}}}\right)$，其中$\widetilde{O}$隐含了索引查找或二分查找所需的对数因子，以及查询复杂度中的小多项式因子。

我们给出若干示例。

Example 5.8. Continue with Example 5.5. The variable order corresponding to the chain $\widehat{0} \prec  y \prec  {yz} \prec  \widehat{1}$ is $y,z,\left( {xu}\right)$ , where $x,u$ can be arranged in any order. The Chain Algorithm computes three intermediate relations:

示例5.8. 续示例5.5。与链$\widehat{0} \prec  y \prec  {yz} \prec  \widehat{1}$对应的变量顺序为$y,z,\left( {xu}\right)$，其中$x,u$可任意排列。链算法计算三个中间关系：

$$
{Q}_{1}\left( y\right)  = {\Pi }_{y}\left( {R\left( {xy}\right) }\right)  \cap  {\Pi }_{y}\left( {S\left( {yz}\right) }\right) 
$$

$$
{Q}_{2}\left( {yz}\right)  = {Q}_{1}\left( y\right)  \boxtimes  S\left( {yz}\right) 
$$

$$
{Q}_{3}\left( {yzxu}\right)  = {\left( {Q}_{2}\left( yz\right)  \boxtimes  R\left( xy\right) \right) }^{ + } \cap  {\left( {Q}_{2}\left( yz\right)  \boxtimes  T\left( zu\right) \right) }^{ + }
$$

The first two steps are straightforward. In the third, the join ${Q}_{2}\left( {yz}\right)  \boxtimes  R\left( {xy}\right)$ results in a relation with attributes ${xyz}$ ,which needs to be expanded with $u$ (e.g. by computing the user-defined function $u = f\left( {x,z}\right)$ ),and similarly for the second join. However, the algorithm does not compute the joins first then intersect, instead it iterates over tuples $t \in  {Q}_{2}\left( {yz}\right)$ and computes an intersection on a per-tuple basis using the less expensive option. In particular, for each $t = \left( {y,z}\right)  \in  {Q}_{2}$ ,it compares $\left| {t \boxtimes  R\left( {x,y}\right) }\right|$ and $\left| {t \bowtie  T\left( {z,u}\right) }\right|$ (which can be done in logarithmic time given that $R$ was indexed with attribute order(y,x)and $T$ with order(z,u)). Suppose $\left| {t \boxtimes  R\left( {x,y}\right) }\right|  \leq  \left| {t \boxtimes  T\left( {z,u}\right) }\right|$ ,then for each $\left( {y,z,x}\right)  \in  t \boxtimes  R\left( {x,y}\right)$ the algorithm uses the ${xz} \rightarrow  u$ FD to obtain the tuple(y,z,x,u). The next task is to verify that this tuple is indeed in the intersection as defined in line 6. This is done with two verifications: we make sure that $\left( {z,u}\right)  \in  T$ ,and that ${yu} \rightarrow  x$ is indeed satisfied. ${}^{5}$

前两步较为直接。第三步中，连接操作${Q}_{2}\left( {yz}\right)  \boxtimes  R\left( {xy}\right)$会生成包含属性${xyz}$的关系，需要通过$u$进行扩展（例如通过计算用户自定义函数$u = f\left( {x,z}\right)$），第二个连接操作同理。但该算法并非先计算连接再求交集，而是迭代遍历元组$t \in  {Q}_{2}\left( {yz}\right)$，采用成本较低的方式逐条计算交集。具体而言，对于每个$t = \left( {y,z}\right)  \in  {Q}_{2}$，它会比较$\left| {t \boxtimes  R\left( {x,y}\right) }\right|$和$\left| {t \bowtie  T\left( {z,u}\right) }\right|$（由于$R$已按属性order(y,x)建立索引，$T$按order(z,u)建立索引，可在对数时间内完成）。假设$\left| {t \boxtimes  R\left( {x,y}\right) }\right|  \leq  \left| {t \boxtimes  T\left( {z,u}\right) }\right|$，则算法会利用${xz} \rightarrow  u$函数依赖获取元组(y,z,x,u)。后续任务是验证该元组确实符合第6行定义的交集条件，通过双重验证实现：确保$\left( {z,u}\right)  \in  T$成立，且${yu} \rightarrow  x$确实满足。${}^{5}$

On this chain the algorithm runs in optimal time $O\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$ . We note that all previously proposed known worst-case optimal join algorithms for queries without FD’s $\left\lbrack  {{18},{19},{23}}\right\rbrack$ require $\Omega \left( {N}^{2}\right)$ to compute the previous query on this instance: $R = S = T = \{ \left( {1,i}\right)  \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  {N/2}\right\rbrack  \}  \cup  \{ \left( {i,1}\right)  \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  {N/2}\right\rbrack  \}$ . For example,LFTJ with variable order $y,z,x,u$ computes queries ${Q}_{1}\left( y\right) ,{Q}_{2}\left( {yz}\right) ,{Q}_{3}\left( {xyz}\right) ,{Q}_{4}\left( {xyzu}\right)$ ,where $\left| {Q}_{3}\right|  = {N}^{2}$ . Note, however, that not every maximal chain gives an optimal bound: for example the chain $\widehat{0} \prec  x \prec  {xu} \prec  {xyu} \prec  {xyzu} =$ 1 has hyperedges: ${e}_{R} = \{ x,{xyu}\} ,{e}_{S} = \{ {xyu},{xyzu}\} ,{e}_{T} =$ $\{ {xu},{xyzu}\}$ (isomorphic to the atomic hypergraph in Fig.2), and the optimal fractional edge covering number ${\rho }^{ * } = 2$ , hence the chain bound is $\left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq  {N}^{2}$ ,which is sub-optimal.

该算法在此链上以最优时间$O\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$运行。值得注意的是，此前提出的所有已知无函数依赖$\left\lbrack  {{18},{19},{23}}\right\rbrack$查询的最坏情况最优连接算法，在此实例上计算前序查询$R = S = T = \{ \left( {1,i}\right)  \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  {N/2}\right\rbrack  \}  \cup  \{ \left( {i,1}\right)  \mid  i \in  \left\lbrack  {N/2}\right\rbrack  \}$都需要$\Omega \left( {N}^{2}\right)$时间。例如采用变量顺序$y,z,x,u$的LFTJ算法计算查询${Q}_{1}\left( y\right) ,{Q}_{2}\left( {yz}\right) ,{Q}_{3}\left( {xyz}\right) ,{Q}_{4}\left( {xyzu}\right)$时，其中$\left| {Q}_{3}\right|  = {N}^{2}$。但需注意并非所有极大链都能产生最优边界：例如链$\widehat{0} \prec  x \prec  {xu} \prec  {xyu} \prec  {xyzu} =$1包含超边${e}_{R} = \{ x,{xyu}\} ,{e}_{S} = \{ {xyu},{xyzu}\} ,{e}_{T} =$$\{ {xu},{xyzu}\}$（与图2中的原子超图同构），其最优分数边覆盖数为${\rho }^{ * } = 2$，因此链边界为$\left| {Q}^{D}\right|  \leq  {N}^{2}$，属于次优解。

Our first goal in this section is to develop a set of rules that can prove any valid output inequality, including the one shown above in Example 5.10. Our suggested proof strategy involves the following three basic rules:

- CD-rule: for $X \prec  Y,h\left( Y\right)  \rightarrow  h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  + h\left( X\right)$ .

- CD规则：适用于$X \prec  Y,h\left( Y\right)  \rightarrow  h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  + h\left( X\right)$

- CC-rule: for $X \prec  Y,h\left( {Y \mid  X}\right)  + h\left( X\right)  \rightarrow  h\left( Y\right)$ .

## 6. CONCLUSIONS

## 6. 结论

We studied ways to prove worst-case output size bounds, and algorithms meeting the bounds for join queries with functional dependencies. A main aim was to design an algorithm running within time bounded by the entropy-based linear program proposed by Gottlob et al. [12]. For this purpose we developed new, lattice theoretic techniques, of independent interest. We described several classes of lattices, and several ways to prove upper or lower bounds on the worst-case query output, summarized in Fig. 5. On the algorithmic side, we devised the novel idea of turning a proof of an inequality into an algorithm. Three proof techniques lead to three different algorithms with increasing complexity. Our main algorithm does meet GLVV bound, up to a poly-log factor. The algorithm manages to solve a stronger problem, where input relations have prescribed maximum degree bounds, of which functional dependencies and cardinality bounds are special cases.

我们研究了最坏情况下输出规模界限的证明方法，以及满足函数依赖连接查询界限的算法。主要目标是设计在Gottlob等人[12]提出的基于熵的线性规划时间界限内运行的算法。为此我们开发了具有独立价值的新格论技术。描述了多类格结构，以及证明查询输出最坏情况下界或上界的多种方法（图5汇总）。算法方面，我们创新性地将不等式证明转化为算法。三种证明技术衍生出复杂度递增的三种算法。主算法在多项式对数因子内达到GLVV界，并能解决输入关系带有预设最大度数约束的更强问题——函数依赖和基数约束都是其特例。
