## Abstract

## 摘要

We study a class of aggregate-join queries with multiple aggregation operators evaluated over annotated relations. We show that straightforward extensions of standard multiway join algorithms and generalized hypertree decompositions (GHDs) provide best-known runtime guarantees. In contrast, prior work uses bespoke algorithms and data structures and does not match these guarantees. We extend the standard techniques by providing a complete characterization of (1) the set of orderings equivalent to a given ordering and (2) the set of GHDs valid with respect to the given ordering, i.e., GHDs that correctly answer a given aggregate-join query when provided to (simple variants of) standard join algorithms. We show by example that previous approaches are incomplete. The key technical consequence of our characterizations is a decomposition of a valid GHD into a set of (smaller) unconstrained GHDs, i.e., into a set of GHDs of sub-queries without aggregations. Since this decomposition is comprised of unconstrained GHDs, we are able to connect to the wide literature on GHDs for join query processing, thereby obtaining improved runtime bounds, MapReduce variants, and an efficient method to find approximately optimal GHDs.

我们研究了一类在注解关系上执行多聚合操作的聚合-连接查询。研究表明，标准多路连接算法与广义超树分解(GHD)的直接扩展可提供最优运行时保证。相比之下，现有研究采用定制算法和数据结构，却未能达到同等保证水平。我们通过以下两个方面的完整特征描述扩展了标准技术：(1)与给定排序等价的排序集合；(2)符合给定排序的有效GHD集合，即当应用于标准连接算法(的简单变体)时能正确回答聚合-连接查询的GHD。通过示例证明先前方法存在不完整性。我们特征描述的关键技术成果是将有效GHD分解为一组(更小)无约束GHD，即分解为不含聚合操作的子查询GHD集合。由于该分解由无约束GHD构成，我们可以衔接GHD在连接查询处理中的广泛研究成果，从而获得改进的运行时边界、MapReduce变体及近似最优GHD的高效查找方法。

## 1. INTRODUCTION

## 1. 引言

Generalized hypertree decompositions (GHDs), introduced by Gottlob et al. $\left\lbrack  {9,{10}}\right\rbrack$ and further developed by Grohe and Marx [12], provide a means for performing early projection in join processing, which can result in dramatically faster runtimes. In this work, we extend GHDs to handle queries that include aggregations, which allows us to capture both SQL-aggregate processing and message passing problems. Motivated by our own database engine based on GHDs [1, $\left. {{23},{27}}\right\rbrack$ ,we seek to more deeply understand the space of optimization for aggregate-join queries.

由Gottlob等人$\left\lbrack  {9,{10}}\right\rbrack$提出并经Grohe和Marx[12]发展的广义超树分解(GHD)，为连接处理中的早期投影提供了技术手段，可显著提升运行时性能。本研究将GHD扩展至支持包含聚合操作的查询，使其既能处理SQL聚合运算也能解决消息传递问题。基于我们自主研发的GHD数据库引擎[1,$\left. {{23},{27}}\right\rbrack$]，我们试图更深入地探索聚合-连接查询的优化空间。

We build upon work by Green, Karvounarakis, and Tannen [11] on annotated relations to define our notion of aggregation. These annotations provide a general definition of aggregation, allowing us to represent a wide-ranging set of problems as aggregate-join queries. Our queries, which we call AJAR (Aggregations and Joins over Annotated Relations) queries, contain semiring quantifiers that "sum over" or "marginalize out" values. We formally define AJAR queries in Section 3, but they are easy to illustrate by example:

我们在Green、Karvounarakis和Tannen[11]关于注解关系的研究基础上定义了聚合概念。这些注解提供了聚合的通用定义，使我们能将各类问题表示为聚合-连接查询。这类被我们称为AJAR（基于注解关系的聚合与连接）的查询包含"求和"或"边缘化"值的半环量词。第3节将正式定义AJAR查询，但通过示例可直观理解：

AJAR queries capture both classical SQL-style project-join queries and newer data processing problems like probabilistic inference via message passing on graphical models [15]. In fact, Aji and McEliece proposed the "Marginalize a Product Function" (MPF) problem [4], which is a special case of an AJAR query, and showed how the problem and its solution capture a number of classic problems and algorithms, including fast Hadamard transforms, Viterbi's algorithm, forward-backward algorithm, FFT, and probabilistic inference in Bayesian networks. These algorithms are fundamental to various fields; for example the forward-backward algorithm over conditional random fields forms the basis for state of the art solutions to named entity recognition, part of speech tagging, noun phrase segmentation, and other problems in NLP [26]. We are motivated by the wide applicability of queries over annotated relations; annotated relations may provide a framework for combining classical query processing, linear algebra, and statistical inference in a single data processing system.

AJAR查询既涵盖经典的SQL式投影-连接查询，也包含通过图模型消息传递进行概率推断等新型数据处理问题[15]。实际上，Aji和McEliece提出的"边缘化乘积函数"(MPF)问题[4]正是AJAR查询的特例，他们论证了该问题及其解法如何囊括快速哈达玛变换、维特比算法、前向-后向算法、快速傅里叶变换以及贝叶斯网络概率推断等经典问题和算法。这些算法是多个领域的基础——例如基于条件随机场的前向-后向算法，构成了命名实体识别、词性标注、名词短语分割等自然语言处理前沿解决方案的基石[26]。标注关系查询的广泛适用性激励着我们的研究：标注关系可能为整合经典查询处理、线性代数和统计推断提供统一的数据处理框架。

We consider a generalization of MPF with multiple aggregation operators. We represent an aggregate-join query as a join and an aggregation ordering, which specifies both the ordering and the aggregation of each attribute. Our class of queries directly follows from the work of Abo Khamis, Ngo, and Rudra [16], who investigated the "Functional Aggregate Query" (FAQ) problem. In addition to MPF, FAQ is a generalization of Chen and Dalmau's QCQ problem [7], in which the only aggregates are logical quantifiers (AND and OR).

我们研究带多重聚合算子的MPF广义形式。将聚合-连接查询表示为连接运算与聚合序——后者同时规定属性排序方式和聚合操作。我们的查询类别直接继承自Abo Khamis、Ngo和Rudra关于"函数聚合查询"(FAQ)问题的研究[16]。除MPF外，FAQ也是Chen与Dalmau提出的QCQ问题的推广形式[7]，其中仅含逻辑量词(AND与OR)这类聚合操作。

The key technical challenge in these problems is characterizing the permissible aggregation orders to answer the query. A query consists of body $Q$ and a string of attribute-operator pairs $\alpha$ ,which we call an aggregation ordering. The challenge arises because different aggregation operators (e.g., $\sum$ and max) cannot freely commute. We show that attribute-operator pairs can commute for only two reasons: (1) their operators commute or (2) their attributes are "independent" in the query,e.g.,in query $\mathop{\max }\limits_{B}\mathop{\max }\limits_{A}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{C}R\left( {A,B}\right) ,S\left( {B,C}\right)$ , the aggregations involving $A$ and $C$ can commute - even though max and $\sum$ do not commute as operators,the query body renders them independent given $B$ . These two conditions for commuting generate equivalence classes among aggregation orderings.

这类问题的核心技术挑战在于界定可执行聚合序的合法条件。查询由主体$Q$和属性-算子对序列$\alpha$（即聚合序）构成。挑战源于不同聚合算子（如$\sum$与max）无法自由交换。我们证明属性-算子对仅因以下两种情况可交换：(1)算子本身可交换；(2)属性在查询中"独立"——例如查询$\mathop{\max }\limits_{B}\mathop{\max }\limits_{A}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{C}R\left( {A,B}\right) ,S\left( {B,C}\right)$中，涉及$A$和$C$的聚合可交换，尽管max与$\sum$作为算子不可交换，但查询主体在给定$B$时使它们相互独立。这两种交换条件形成了聚合序的等价类。

Given the ordering $\alpha$ ,we wish to identify which orderings are equivalent to $\alpha$ and which GHDs are compatible with an equivalent ordering. Chen and Dalmau give a complete characterization of which variable orders are equivalent for QCQ via a procedure. In the same spirit, we provide such a characterization for both equivalent orderings and compatible GHDs. We describe these characterizations in turn.

给定序$\alpha$时，我们需要识别哪些序与之等价，以及哪些广义超树分解(GHD)与等价序兼容。Chen和Dalmau通过特定程序完整刻画了QCQ中变量序的等价性。基于相同思路，我们同时给出了等价序与兼容GHD的完整刻画。下文将依次阐述这些特征。

- A Complete Characterization of Equivalent Orders: Given an order $\alpha$ and two attribute-operator pairs $x,y \in  \alpha$ ,we describe a set of constraints of the form "in any order, $x$ must appear after $y$ ." Our constraints are sound and complete,i.e.,a string $\beta$ satisfies these constraints if and only if it is equivalent to $\alpha$ . In contrast, previous approaches have incomplete characterizations.

- 等价序的完整刻画：给定序$\alpha$和两个属性-算子对$x,y \in  \alpha$，我们描述一组形式为"在任何序中，$x$必须出现在$y$之后"的约束条件。这些约束具有完备性——字符串$\beta$满足约束当且仅当它与$\alpha$等价。相较之下，现有方法均存在刻画不完整的问题。

- A Complete Characterization of GHDs Compatible with any Equivalent Order. Given an order $\alpha$ and a query hypergraph $Q$ ,we call a GHD ’valid’ if it is compatible with any ordering equivalent to $\alpha$ . We give a succinct characterization for all valid GHDs. We then describe a decomposition of the query $\left( {Q,\alpha }\right)$ into a series of characteristic hypergraphs (without attached aggregation orderings). GHDs for these hypergraphs can be combined into a valid GHD for the original query. We show that for any "node-monotone" ${}^{1}$ width function, there is a GHD with optimal width $w$ that can be constructed with this decomposition. Treewidth, fractional hypertreewidth, and submodular width are all node-monotone.

- 兼容任意等价序的广义超树分解(GHD)完备性表征。给定序$\alpha$和查询超图$Q$，若某GHD能与$\alpha$的所有等价序兼容，则称其"有效"。我们给出了所有有效GHD的简明表征方法，并提出将查询$\left( {Q,\alpha }\right)$分解为一系列特征超图（不含聚合序）的策略。这些特征超图的GHD可组合成原查询的有效GHD。我们证明：对于任何"节点单调"${}^{1}$宽度函数，都能通过该分解构造出具有最优宽度$w$的GHD。树宽、分数超树宽及次模宽均满足节点单调性。

Conceptually, the latter result is important for tying our work to existing GHD literature; the result reduces our problem to constructing standard GHDs. Pragmatically, it lets us apply existing GHD results to our characteristic hypergraphs and obtain the following results for free:

从概念上讲，后一项成果对衔接本研究与现有GHD文献至关重要——它将问题简化为构建标准GHD。实用层面而言，该成果使我们能将现有GHD研究成果应用于特征超图，并免费获得以下推论：

- Based on Grohe and Marx [12], we are able to describe our runtime in terms of classical metrics like fractional hypertreewidth. In turn, we can use tighter notions of width to upper bound the runtime like Marx's sub-modular width [18] or Joglekar's efficiently computable variant [14].

- 基于Grohe和Marx[12]的研究，我们可用分数超树宽等经典指标描述运行时复杂度。进而可采用更紧凑的宽度指标（如Marx的次模宽度[18]或Joglekar的可高效计算变体[14]）来确定运行时上限。

- Based on Afrati et al. [3], who bound the communication costs of join processing in terms of a "width" parameter for GHDs, we can develop efficient MapRe-duce algorithms for solving AJAR queries.

- 基于Afrati等学者[3]提出的GHD"宽度"参数与连接处理通信成本关系研究，我们可开发高效的MapReduce算法求解AJAR查询。

- Based on Marx's approximation [17] for GHDs, we can find approximately optimal GHDs for the popular fractional hypertreewidth measure in polynomial time

- 借鉴Marx[17]的GHD近似理论，我们能在多项式时间内为流行的分数超树宽度量找到近似最优GHD。

We get the above results essentially for free from forging this connection to GHDs. We view this simple link as a strength of our approach.

通过建立与GHD理论的这种关联，我们实质上免费获得了上述成果。这种简洁的关联正是本方法的优势所在。

Finally, we discuss an extension to handle "product aggregations" that allows us to aggregate away an attribute before we join the relations containing the attribute when the aggregation operator is the multiplication operator of the semiring. FAQ was the first to observe that this special case can improve certain types of logical queries. This opens up a new space of equivalent orderings and valid GHDs; mirroring the above results, we give a simple test and a complete characterization of the valid GHDs for queries that include this aggregation. This extension allows us to recover the results on QCQ presented by Chen and Dalmau. Moreover, we obtain similar improvements in runtime as previous work.

最后我们讨论了处理"乘积聚合"的扩展方案：当聚合运算符为半环乘法算子时，可在连接包含该属性的关系前先行聚合。FAQ最早发现这种特例能优化特定逻辑查询。这开辟了新的等价序与有效GHD空间——我们通过镜像前述成果，为包含此类聚合的查询提供了简易测试方法与有效GHD的完备表征。该扩展使我们能复现Chen与Dalmau提出的QCQ结论，并在运行时获得与既有研究相当的改进。

We also present our algorithm for answering AJAR queries. Section 4 provides a succinct characterization of all orderings that are equivalent to a given ordering. Section 5 discusses how to connect our work with recent research on GHDs, explaining how to construct valid optimal query plans and how to further improve and parallelize our results. In Section 6, we discuss how to incorporate product aggregations.

## 2. RELATED WORK

## 2. 相关工作

Join Algorithms. The Yannakakis algorithm, introduced in 1981,guarantees a runtime of $O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ for $\alpha$ -acyclic join queries [29]. Modern multiway algorithms can process any join query and have worst-case optimal runtime. In particular, Atserias, Grohe, and Marx [5] derived a tight bound on the worst-case size of a join query given the input size and structure. Ngo et al. [20] presented the first algorithm to achieve this runtime bound, i.e., the first worst-case optimal algorithm. Soon after, Veldhuizen presented Leapfrog Triejoin, a very simple worst-case optimal algorithm that had been implemented in LogicBlox's commercial database system [28]. Ngo et al. [21] later presented the simplified and unified algorithm GenericJoin (GJ) that captured both of the previous worst-case optimal algorithms.

连接算法。Yannakakis算法(1981年提出)保证$O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间复杂度处理$\alpha$-无环连接查询[29]。现代多路连接算法可处理任意查询且具有最坏情况最优运行时。Atserias、Grohe和Marx[5]首次根据输入规模与结构推导出连接查询最坏情况规模的紧确界。Ngo等[20]提出首个达到该运行时界的算法（即首个最坏情况最优算法）。随后Veldhuizen提出Leapfrog Triejoin——这个极其简洁的最坏情况最优算法已应用于LogicBlox商业数据库系统[28]。Ngo等[21]后来提出统一简化算法GenericJoin(GJ)，涵盖前述两种最坏情况最优算法。

GHDs. First introduced by Gottlob, Leone, and Scar-cello [10], hypertree decompositions and the associated hyper-tree width generalize the concept of tree decompositions [25]. Conceptually, the decompositions capture a hypergraph's cyclicity, allowing them to facilitate the selective use of GJ and Yannakakis in the standard hybrid join algorithm GHD-Join. There are deep connections between variable orderings and GHDs [16], which we leverage extensively. Grohe and Marx [12] introduced the idea of fractional hypertree width over GHDs, which bounds the runtime of GHDJoin by $\widetilde{O}\left( {I{N}^{w} + {OUT}}\right) (\widetilde{O}$ hides poly-logarithmic factors) for $w$ defined to be the minimum fractional hypertree width among all GHDs.

广义超树分解(GHDs)。由Gottlob、Leone和Scarcello首次提出[10]，超树分解及其关联的超树宽度推广了树分解的概念[25]。从概念上讲，这种分解能捕捉超图的环性特征，使其能在标准混合连接算法GHD-Join中灵活选择使用GJ或Yannakakis方法。变量序与GHD之间存在着深刻联系[16]，我们对此进行了充分运用。Grohe和Marx提出了基于GHD的分数超树宽度概念[12]，该指标通过$\widetilde{O}\left( {I{N}^{w} + {OUT}}\right) (\widetilde{O}$隐藏多对数因子）来限定GHDJoin的运行时间上界，其中$w$定义为所有GHD中最小的分数超树宽度。

Semirings and Aggregations. Green, Karvounarakis, and Tannen developed the idea of annotations over a semiring [11]. Our notation for the annotations is superficially different from theirs, solely for notational convenience. We delve into more detail in Section 3. This also has been used as a mechanism to capture aggregation in probabilistic databases [24].

半环与聚合运算。Green、Karvounarakis和Tannen提出了基于半环的标注理论[11]。我们的标注表示法在形式上与其略有差异，这仅为记法便利。第3节将详细探讨。该理论还被用作概率数据库中捕获聚合运算的机制[24]。

MPF. Aji and McEliece [4] defined the "Marginalize a Product Function" (MPF) problem, which is equivalent to the space of AJAR queries with only one aggregation operator. They showed that MPF generalizes a wide variety of important algorithms and problems, which also implies that AJAR queries are quite general. They also provided a message passing algorithm to solve MPF, which has since been refined [15]. We further improve runtimes over the state of the art.

There is a standard modification to Yannakakis to handle aggregations [29], but the classic analysis provides only a $O\left( {\mathrm{{IN}} \cdot  \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ bound. Abo Khamis, Ngo, and Rudra present the "Functional Aggregate Query" (FAQ) problem [16], which is equivalent to AJAR. The FAQ/AJAR problems arose out of discussions between Ngo, Rudra, and Ré at PODS12 about how to extend the worst-case result to queries using aggregation and message passing via Green et al.'s semiring formulation. We originally worked jointly on the problem, but we developed substantially different approaches, and decided to split our work. We argue that the AJAR approach is simpler and yields better runtime guarantees. We also describe new complete characterizations as described above. These completeness results let us connect more easily to existing literature. The algorithm described here has been implemented in the related database engine EmptyHeaded ${\left\lbrack  1\right\rbrack  }^{2}$ . This engine has run motif finding, pagerank, and single-source shortest path queries, faster than previous high-level approaches that take datalog-like queries as input.

A primary application of multiple aggregation operators is quantified conjunctive queries (QCQ) and the counting variant, which can be expressed as AJAR queries over the semiring $\left( {\vee , \land  }\right)$ with aggregations involving both operators. Here, we follow FAQ's idea to formulate this as a query with product aggregation. Chen and Dalmau [7] completely characterized the space of tractable QCQ by defining a notion of width that relies on variable orderings. Chen and Dalmau's width definition includes a complete characterization of the permissible variable orderings for a QCQ instance. Their characterization is similar in spirit to the partial ordering we define in Section 4 that characterizes the space of valid GHDs for an AJAR query. However, their results are focused on tractability rather than the optimal runtime exponents; our characterization extends theirs and has improved runtime bounds.

GHDs can be interpreted as query plans for joins. Given a GHD, we first join the attributes in each bag using worst case optimal algorithms $\left\lbrack  {{20},{28}}\right\rbrack$ to get one intermediate relation per bag. ## 4. CHARACTERIZING EQUIVALENT OR- DERINGS

## 4. 等价顺序的特征刻画

We discussed an algorithm to solve AJAR queries with a seemingly optimal (or near-optimal) runtime of $\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{w}^{ * }} + }\right.$ OUT). However,note that ${w}^{ * }$ is optimal width over an extremely restrictive class of GHDs. In this section we expand the class of permissible GHDs by identifying alternative aggregation orders that produce the same output as $\alpha$ over any database instance; some of these new orders may allow for faster runtimes, e.g., some orders may allow early aggregation.

我们讨论了以看似最优（或接近最优）运行时$\widetilde{O}\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{w}^{ * }} + }\right.$ OUT)求解AJAR查询的算法。但需注意${w}^{ * }$是极端受限GHD类上的最优宽度。本节通过识别能产生与$\alpha$在任何数据库实例上相同输出的替代聚合顺序，扩展了允许的GHD类别；某些新顺序可能实现更快运行速度，例如允许早期聚合的顺序。

We note that our current specifications of PREC and DNC are mutually recursive. To prove the soundness and completeness of ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ ,we now state two key lemmas illustrating properties of ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ . ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$的可靠性与完备性。为证明${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$的可靠性与完备性，现阐述两个关键引理说明其性质。此处主要提供证明概要，完整证明见技术报告[13]。

LEMMA 24. Suppose we are given a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ and an aggregation ordering $\alpha$ . Suppose $\left( {A, \oplus  }\right) ,\left( {B,{ \oplus  }^{\prime }}\right)  \in  \alpha$ for differing operators $\oplus   \neq  { \oplus  }^{\prime }$ . Then,for any path $P$ in $\mathcal{H}$ between $A$ and $B$ ,there must exist some attribute in the path $C \in  P$ such that $C{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }A$ or $C{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$ .

引理24：给定超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$与聚合序$\alpha$，若对不同运算符$\oplus   \neq  { \oplus  }^{\prime }$有$\left( {A, \oplus  }\right) ,\left( {B,{ \oplus  }^{\prime }}\right)  \in  \alpha$，则在$\mathcal{H}$中连接$A$与$B$的任何路径$P$上，必存在某个属性$C \in  P$满足$C{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }A$或$C{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$。

Note that the $C$ in the statement above can be either $A$ or $B$ . Lemma 24 is only non-trivial if $A$ and $B$ are incomparable. Intuitively the lemma states that attributes incomparable under ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ with differing operators must be separated in $\mathcal{H}$ by their combined predecessors in ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ .

注意上述陈述中的$C$可以是$A$或$B$。仅当$A$与$B$不可比较时，引理24才具有非平凡性。直观而言，该引理表明：在${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$下不可比较且具有不同运算符的属性，必须通过它们在${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$中的共同前驱在$\mathcal{H}$中被分隔开。

Proof. (Sketch) We proceed by induction on the length of the path $P$ . We let $C$ be the attribute between $A$ and $B$ that appears earliest in $\alpha$ and assume without loss of generality that $A$ and $C$ have differing operators. By the inductive hypothesis,there must exist a $D$ on the path from $A$ to $C$ such that either $D{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }A$ or $D{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C$ . In the former case, our proof is done. In the latter case we reason that since $C$ is defined to be the attribute that appears earliest in $\alpha$ between $A$ and $B,D{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C$ implies $D = A$ . We then prove by induction that $A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }X$ for all $X$ on the path between $C$ and $B$ ,which then implies that $A$ and $B$ cannot be incomparable.

证明。（概要）我们对路径$P$的长度进行归纳。设$C$为$A$与$B$之间在$\alpha$中出现最早的属性，且不失一般性地假定$A$与$C$具有不同运算符。根据归纳假设，在$A$到$C$的路径上必然存在$D$使得$D{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }A$或$D{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C$成立。前者情形下证明已完成。后者情形中，由于$C$被定义为$A$与$B,D{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C$之间在$\alpha$中出现最早的属性，可推出$D = A$。继而通过归纳法证明：对于$C$到$B$路径上所有$X$都有$A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }X$，这意味着$A$与$B$不可能不可比较。

This lemma allows us to prove the soundness of ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ .

$\bullet   \oplus   = { \oplus  }^{\prime }$

LEMMA 26. Given a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$ and an aggregation ordering $\alpha$ ,suppose $A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$ for some $A,B \in  V\left( \alpha \right)$ . Then there must exist a path $P$ from $A$ to $B$ such that for every $C \in  P,C \neq  A$ we have $A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C$ .

引理26. 给定超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}}\right)$和聚合序$\alpha$，若存在$A,B \in  V\left( \alpha \right)$使得$A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$，则必然存在从$A$到$B$的路径$P$，且对于每个$C \in  P,C \neq  A$都满足$A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C$。

Proof. (Sketch) We proceed by induction on the recursive definition of ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ . Lemma 18 implies $A$ and $B$ share an edge. Lemma 20 implies the existence of a $C$ such that either $A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C,\left( {B,C}\right)  \in  \mathcal{E}$ or $B{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C,\left( {A,C}\right)  \in  \mathcal{E}$ . Either way we inductively construct a path between $C$ and the attribute it doesn't share an edge with. Finally, Lemma 21 implies the existence of a $C$ such that $A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$ ,which lets us inductively construct paths from $A$ to $C$ and $C$ to $B$ .

证明.（概要）根据${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$的递归定义进行归纳。引理18表明$A$与$B$共享边。引理20保证存在$C$使得$A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C,\left( {B,C}\right)  \in  \mathcal{E}$或$B{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C,\left( {A,C}\right)  \in  \mathcal{E}$成立。无论哪种情况，我们都能归纳构造$C$与其无共享边的属性间的路径。最后，引理21确保存在满足$A{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }C{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$的$C$，从而归纳构造从$A$到$C$及$C$到$B$的路径。

LEMMA 27. (Completeness of ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ ) If $\beta$ is not a linear extension of ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ ,then $\alpha  ≢ \beta$ .

引理27.（${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$的完备性）若$\beta$不是${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$的线性扩展，则$\alpha  ≢ \beta$。

Proof. (Sketch) We first construct a particular ordering ${\alpha }^{\prime } \equiv  \alpha$ that allows us to find two attribute-ordering pairs $\left( {A, \oplus  }\right) ,\left( {B,{ \oplus  }^{\prime }}\right)  \in  {\alpha }^{\prime }$ such that $B{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }A, \oplus   \neq  { \oplus  }^{\prime }$ ,and $A$ precedes $B$ in $\beta$ . Since $\oplus   \neq  { \oplus  }^{\prime }$ ,there must exist $x,y \in  \mathbb{K}$ such that $x \oplus  y \neq  x{ \oplus  }^{\prime }y$ . Our proof constructs an instance $\widehat{I}$ such that ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }\left( \widehat{I}\right)$ returns one tuple with annotation $x{ \oplus  }^{\prime }y$ and ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\beta }\left( \widehat{I}\right)$ returns one tuple with annotation $x \oplus  y$ .

证明.（概要）首先构造特定序${\alpha }^{\prime } \equiv  \alpha$，可找到两个属性-序对$\left( {A, \oplus  }\right) ,\left( {B,{ \oplus  }^{\prime }}\right)  \in  {\alpha }^{\prime }$满足$B{ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }A, \oplus   \neq  { \oplus  }^{\prime }$，且$A$在$\beta$中先于$B$。由于$\oplus   \neq  { \oplus  }^{\prime }$，必存在$x,y \in  \mathbb{K}$使$x \oplus  y \neq  x{ \oplus  }^{\prime }y$成立。通过构造实例$\widehat{I}$，使得${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }\left( \widehat{I}\right)$返回带标注$x{ \oplus  }^{\prime }y$的元组，而${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\beta }\left( \widehat{I}\right)$返回带标注$x \oplus  y$的元组。

Our construction relies on Lemma 26, which implies the existence of a path $P$ from $A$ to $B$ such that every attribute in our path comes after $B$ in ${\alpha }^{\prime }$ . Additionally,by construction, we can guarantee that every attribute in our path comes after $A$ in $\beta$ . We then finish by constructing an instance so that only the outermost aggregation of the path $P$ changes the relation; since the outermost aggregations of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ have different operators, we get different annotations in the output.

该构造依赖引理26，保证存在从$A$到$B$的路径$P$，且路径中所有属性在${\alpha }^{\prime }$中位于$B$之后。此外通过构造可确保路径属性在$\beta$中位于$A$之后。最终构造实例使仅路径$P$的最外层聚合改变关系，由于$\alpha$与$\beta$的最外层聚合算子不同，输出得到相异标注。

## 5. DECOMPOSING VALID GHDS

## 5. 有效GHDS分解

We express our AJAR algorithm directly in terms of GHDs, rather than in terms of aggregation orderings. As such, our goal is the characterization of GHDs that are compatible with at least one equivalent ordering, i.e., the GHDs that can be used to answer an AJAR query. We call a GHD valid if it is compatible with at least one equivalent ordering. We first give a simple characterization of valid GHDs. Then we demonstrate a way to reduce the problem of finding a minimum-width valid GHD to multiple subproblems on unconstrained GHDs (Section 5.1). This decomposition of the problem gets us three things:

我们直接基于广义超树分解(GHDs)而非聚合顺序来表达AJAR算法。因此，我们的目标是刻画那些至少与一个等价顺序兼容的GHDs特性，即能够用于回答AJAR查询的GHDs。若某GHD至少与一个等价顺序兼容，则称其为有效GHD。我们首先给出有效GHD的简明特征，随后展示将寻找最小宽度有效GHD的问题转化为多个无约束GHD子问题的方法（第5.1节）。这种问题分解为我们带来三项优势：

- We can speed up our brute force search for an optimal valid GHD. We can also find approximately optimal valid GHDs in polynomial time using Marx's GHD approximation algorithm [17] (Section 5.2).

- 可加速最优有效GHD的暴力搜索。利用Marx的GHD近似算法[17]，还能在多项式时间内找到近似最优的有效GHD（第5.2节）。

- We can apply existing MapReduce join algorithms that utilize GHDs [3], obtaining efficient parallel algorithms for solving AJAR queries (Section 5.4).

- 可应用现有基于GHD的MapReduce连接算法[3]，获得求解AJAR查询的高效并行算法（第5.4节）。

- We can apply improved join algorithms $\left\lbrack  {{14},{18}}\right\rbrack$ to further reduce our runtime exponent (Section 5.3).

- 可应用改进的连接算法$\left\lbrack  {{14},{18}}\right\rbrack$进一步降低运行时指数（第5.3节）。

### 5.1 Valid and Decomposable GHDs

### 5.1 有效与可分解GHD

We can easily characterize valid GHDs by combining the definition of compatible GHDs with Theorem 15.

通过结合兼容GHD的定义与定理15，我们可以轻松刻画有效GHD的特性。

THEOREM 28. For an AJAR query ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha },{aGHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ is valid if and only if for every pair of attributes $A,B$ such that ${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( A\right)$ is an ancestor of ${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( B\right) ,B{ \nless  }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }A$ .

定理28. 对于AJAR查询${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha },{aGHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$，当且仅当对于每对属性$A,B$满足${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( A\right)$是${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( B\right) ,B{ \nless  }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }A$的祖先时，该GHD有效。

Theorem 28 gives us a criterion specifying which GHDs can act as query plans. We now consider the problem of finding a minimum width valid GHD for any AJAR query. We call a GHD optimal if it has the minimum width possible for valid GHDs. We show how to reduce the problem of finding an optimal valid GHD into smaller problems of finding ordinary optimal GHDs. This unlocks a trove of powerful GHD results and makes them applicable to our problem.

定理28给出了判定哪些GHD可作为查询计划的准则。现在我们考虑为任意AJAR查询寻找最小宽度有效GHD的问题。若某有效GHD具有可能的最小宽度，则称其为最优GHD。我们展示了如何将寻找最优有效GHD的问题转化为寻找普通最优GHD的若干子问题，由此解锁大量强大的GHD研究成果并使其适用于我们的问题。

Given an AJAR query ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ ,suppose we have a subset of the nodes $V \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$ . Define ${\mathcal{E}}_{V}$ to be $\{ E \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  E \cap  V \neq  \varnothing \}$ , i.e.,the set of edges that intersect with $V$ . As before, ${\alpha }_{V}$ denotes the aggregation ordering restricted to the nodes in $V$ . Additionally define ${V}^{O}$ to be $\left\{  {v \in  V\mid \forall w \in  V,w{ \nless  }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }v}\right\}$ , i.e.,the nodes in $V$ that have no predecessors in $V$ according to the partial ordering ${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ . Note that ${\alpha }_{V \smallsetminus  {V}^{O}}$ is then ${\alpha }_{V}$ with all the nodes in ${V}^{O}$ removed (this makes the nodes in ${V}^{O}$ output attributes). Finally,for a GHD $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ ,we will use $\chi \left( \mathcal{T}\right)$ to denote ${ \cup  }_{t \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}\chi \left( t\right)$ .

给定AJAR查询${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$，假设存在节点子集$V \subseteq  \mathcal{V}$。定义${\mathcal{E}}_{V}$为$\{ E \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  E \cap  V \neq  \varnothing \}$，即与$V$相交的边集合。如前所述，${\alpha }_{V}$表示限于$V$节点内的聚合顺序。另定义${V}^{O}$为$\left\{  {v \in  V\mid \forall w \in  V,w{ \nless  }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }v}\right\}$，即根据偏序${ < }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$在$V$内无前驱节点的节点集合。注意此时${\alpha }_{V \smallsetminus  {V}^{O}}$即为移除${V}^{O}$中所有节点后的${\alpha }_{V}$（这将使${V}^{O}$中的节点成为输出属性）。最后，对于GHD$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$，我们将用$\chi \left( \mathcal{T}\right)$表示${ \cup  }_{t \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}\chi \left( t\right)$。

DEFINITION 29. Given an AJAR query ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ ,we say a ${GHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ is decomposable if:

定义29. 给定AJAR查询${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$，若满足以下条件则称${GHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$可分解：

- There exists a rooted subtree ${\mathcal{T}}_{0}$ of $\mathcal{T}$ such that $\chi \left( {\mathcal{T}}_{0}\right)  =$ $\mathcal{V}\left( {-\alpha }\right)$ (i.e.,output attributes).

- 存在$\mathcal{T}$的根子树${\mathcal{T}}_{0}$使得$\chi \left( {\mathcal{T}}_{0}\right)  =$$\mathcal{V}\left( {-\alpha }\right)$（即输出属性）。

- For each connected component $C$ of $\mathcal{H} \smallsetminus  {V}_{-\alpha }$ ,there is exactly one subtree ${\mathcal{T}}_{C} \in  \mathcal{T} \smallsetminus  {\mathcal{T}}_{0}$ such that ${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$ is a decomposable GHD of ${Q}_{\left( {{ \cup  }_{E \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{C}}E,{\mathcal{E}}_{C}}\right) ,{\alpha }_{C \smallsetminus  C}O}$ .

对于$\mathcal{H} \smallsetminus  {V}_{-\alpha }$的每个连通分量$C$，恰好存在一个子树${\mathcal{T}}_{C} \in  \mathcal{T} \smallsetminus  {\mathcal{T}}_{0}$，使得${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$成为${Q}_{\left( {{ \cup  }_{E \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{C}}E,{\mathcal{E}}_{C}}\right) ,{\alpha }_{C \smallsetminus  C}O}$的可分解广义超树分解（GHD）。

We start by connecting this idea of decomposable GHDs to valid GHDs. We only give proof sketches here; see our technical report for the full proofs.

我们首先将可分解GHD的概念与有效GHD建立关联。此处仅提供证明概要，完整证明请参阅我们的技术报告。

THEOREM 30. Every decomposable GHD is valid.

定理30. 每个可分解的广义超树分解（GHD）都是有效的。

Proof. (Sketch) Suppose the AJAR query is ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ . We need to show for any $A,B$ such that ${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( A\right)$ is an ancestor of ${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( B\right) ,A{ \nless  }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$ . We use induction on $\left| \alpha \right|$ . If $\left| \alpha \right|  = 0$ , all GHDs are valid and decomposable. For $\left| \alpha \right|  > 0,{\mathcal{T}}_{0}$ ensures that the output attributes are above non-output attributes. If $A$ and $B$ are non-output attributes and ${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( A\right)$ is an ancestor of ${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( B\right)$ ,then both are in some ${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$ . By the inductive hypothesis, ${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$ is valid with respect to ${Q}_{\left( {{ \cup  }_{E \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{C}}E,{\mathcal{E}}_{C}}\right) ,{\alpha }_{C \smallsetminus  {C}^{O}}}$ . By inspecting the partial order created by this subgraph, we conclude that $A{ \nless  }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$ as desired.

证明。（概要）假设AJAR查询为${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$。需证明对于任意满足$A,B$且${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( A\right)$是${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( B\right) ,A{ \nless  }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$祖先节点的情形。我们对$\left| \alpha \right|$进行归纳。若$\left| \alpha \right|  = 0$，则所有GHD均有效且可分解。当$\left| \alpha \right|  > 0,{\mathcal{T}}_{0}$时，确保输出属性位于非输出属性之上。若$A$与$B$为非输出属性且${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( A\right)$是${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( B\right)$的祖先节点，则二者必存在于某个${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$中。根据归纳假设，${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$相对于${Q}_{\left( {{ \cup  }_{E \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{C}}E,{\mathcal{E}}_{C}}\right) ,{\alpha }_{C \smallsetminus  {C}^{O}}}$有效。通过分析该子图生成的偏序关系，可得出符合预期的$A{ \nless  }_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }B$。

Not every valid GHD is decomposable. However, every valid GHD can be transformed into a corresponding decomposable GHD using some simple transformations. Each bag of the resulting decomposable GHD is a subset of one of the bags of the original GHD. Thus the fhw of the decomposable GHD is at most the fhw of the original valid GHD. In fact, we can make a more general claim, using a notion of node-monotone functions, defined next.

并非所有有效GHD都可分解。但通过简单变换，任何有效GHD均可转化为对应的可分解GHD。所得可分解GHD的每个包都是原GHD某包的子集，因此其fhw至多等于原有效GHD的fhw。实际上，借助下文定义的节点单调函数概念，我们可以提出更普遍的论断。

Definition 31. Given a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} = \left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{H}},{\mathcal{E}}_{\mathcal{H}}}\right)$ ,we define a function to be node-monotone if it is a function $\gamma  : {2}^{{\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{H}}} \rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$ such that $\forall A \subseteq  B \subseteq  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{H}} : \gamma \left( A\right)  \leq  \gamma \left( B\right)$ . Given any node-monotone function $\gamma$ ,we define the $\gamma$ -width of a ${GHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ over $\mathcal{H}$ as $\mathop{\max }\limits_{{v \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}}\gamma \left( {\chi \left( v\right) }\right)$ .

定义31。给定超图$\mathcal{H} = \left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{H}},{\mathcal{E}}_{\mathcal{H}}}\right)$，若函数$\gamma  : {2}^{{\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{H}}} \rightarrow  \mathbb{R}$满足$\forall A \subseteq  B \subseteq  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{H}} : \gamma \left( A\right)  \leq  \gamma \left( B\right)$，则称该函数为节点单调函数。对于任意节点单调函数$\gamma$，定义${GHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$在$\mathcal{H}$上的$\gamma$-宽度为$\mathop{\max }\limits_{{v \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}}}\gamma \left( {\chi \left( v\right) }\right)$。

Many standard notions of widths can be expressed as $\gamma$ -widths for a suitably chosen $\gamma$ . Specifically:

许多标准宽度概念均可表示为适当选择$\gamma$后的$\gamma$-宽度。具体而言：

Proposition 1. Suppose we are given a hypergraph $\mathcal{H} =$ $\left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{H}},{\mathcal{E}}_{\mathcal{H}}}\right)$ and database instance $I$ on $\mathcal{H}$ . Then for each of following notions of width: (i) Treewidth (ii) Generalized Hypertree Width (iii) Fractional Hypertree Width (iv) Sub-modular Width,there exists a node-monotone function $\gamma$ such that $\gamma$ -width equals the given notion of width.

命题1。给定超图$\mathcal{H} =$$\left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{H}},{\mathcal{E}}_{\mathcal{H}}}\right)$及$\mathcal{H}$上的数据库实例$I$。则下列宽度概念：(i)树宽(ii)广义超树宽(iii)分数超树宽(iv)次模宽，均存在对应的节点单调函数$\gamma$，使得$\gamma$-宽度等于给定宽度概念。

As a simple example,tree-width can be expressed as $\gamma$ - width for $\gamma \left( A\right)  = \left| A\right|  - 1$ . We can now relate valid and decomposable GHDs with respect to their $\gamma$ -widths.

简单示例：树宽可表示为$\gamma \left( A\right)  = \left| A\right|  - 1$的$\gamma$-宽度。至此可建立有效GHD与可分解GHD在其$\gamma$-宽度上的关联。

THEOREM 32. For every valid ${GHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ ,there exists a decomposable ${GHD}\left( {{\mathcal{T}}^{\prime },{\chi }^{\prime }}\right)$ such that for all node-monotone functions $\gamma$ ,the $\gamma$ -width of $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}^{\prime },{\chi }^{\prime }}\right)$ is no larger than the $\gamma$ -width of $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ .

定理32. 对于每个有效的${GHD}\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$，存在一个可分解的${GHD}\left( {{\mathcal{T}}^{\prime },{\chi }^{\prime }}\right)$，使得对于所有节点单调函数$\gamma$，$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}^{\prime },{\chi }^{\prime }}\right)$的$\gamma$-宽度不大于$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$的$\gamma$-宽度。

Proof. (Sketch) Suppose the AJAR query is ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ . We transform the given GHD $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ into $\left( {{\mathcal{T}}^{\prime },{\chi }^{\prime }}\right)$ such that for each ${v}^{\prime } \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}^{\prime }$ ,there exists a $v \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}$ such that ${\chi }^{\prime }\left( {v}^{\prime }\right)  \subseteq  \chi \left( v\right)$ . The result then follows from the node-monotonicity of $\gamma$ and the definition of $\gamma$ -width. Any transformation of a GHD that ensures that all new bags are subsets of old bags, is called width-preserving.

证明.（概要）假设AJAR查询为${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$。我们将给定的GHD$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$转换为$\left( {{\mathcal{T}}^{\prime },{\chi }^{\prime }}\right)$，使得对于每个${v}^{\prime } \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}^{\prime }$，存在一个$v \in  {\mathcal{V}}_{\mathcal{T}}$满足${\chi }^{\prime }\left( {v}^{\prime }\right)  \subseteq  \chi \left( v\right)$。根据$\gamma$的节点单调性及$\gamma$-宽度的定义可推出结论。任何保证新生成包均为旧包子集的GHD转换，均称为保宽转换。

We then transform the GHD $\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$ to satisfy the following properties (we describe the corresponding width-preserving transformations):

随后我们将GHD$\left( {\mathcal{T},\chi }\right)$转换为满足以下特性（描述相应的保宽转换过程）：

- Every $t \in  \mathcal{T}$ is ${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( A\right)$ for exactly one attribute $A$ .

- 每个$t \in  \mathcal{T}$恰好对应一个属性$A$的${TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( A\right)$。

We can remove every node $t \in  \mathcal{T}$ that is the top node for no attributes. For a node $t$ that is the top node for multiple attributes,we create a new node ${t}^{\prime }$ and set it to be the parent of $t$ such that $\chi \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)  \subseteq  \chi \left( t\right)$ . We specify $\chi \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)$ such that $t$ is the top node for one fewer attribute than before. We can repeat this process until our condition is met.

可移除所有非任何属性顶节点的节点$t \in  \mathcal{T}$。对于作为多个属性顶节点的$t$，我们创建新节点${t}^{\prime }$并将其设为$t$的父节点，使得$\chi \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)  \subseteq  \chi \left( t\right)$。通过设定$\chi \left( {t}^{\prime }\right)$使$t$作为顶节点对应的属性数比之前减少一个。重复此过程直至满足条件。

- For any node $t \in  \mathcal{T}$ and the subtree ${\mathcal{T}}_{t}$ rooted at $t$ , the attributes $\left\{  {v \in  \mathcal{V} \mid  {TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( v\right)  \in  {\mathcal{T}}_{t}}\right\}$ form a connected subgraph of $\mathcal{H}$ .

- 对于任意节点$t \in  \mathcal{T}$及其以$t$为根的子树${\mathcal{T}}_{t}$，属性$\left\{  {v \in  \mathcal{V} \mid  {TO}{P}_{\mathcal{T}}\left( v\right)  \in  {\mathcal{T}}_{t}}\right\}$构成$\mathcal{H}$的连通子图。

We transform the GHD to ensure this property in a bottom-up manner. In particular, we only consider a node $t \in  \mathcal{T}$ after we know its children all satisfy the property. Then if any child's subtree is not connected to $t$ in $\mathcal{H}$ ,we can safely connect the child to $t$ ’s parent. This process ensures that $t$ satisfies our property.

采用自底向上方式转换GHD以确保此特性。特别地，仅当确认某节点$t \in  \mathcal{T}$的所有子节点均满足该特性时才处理该节点。若某子节点的子树在$\mathcal{H}$中未与$t$连通，则可安全将该子节点连接到$t$的父节点。此过程确保$t$满足特性。

We can show, by induction, any valid GHD that satisfies these two properties is decomposable. Intuitively, the first transformation ensures the subtree ${\mathcal{T}}_{0}$ exists as desired. The second transformation ensures that each of the ${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$ ’s exists and satisfies the requisite properties.

通过归纳法可证，任何满足这两个特性的有效GHD都是可分解的。直观而言，第一次转换确保子树${\mathcal{T}}_{0}$按需存在；第二次转换保证每个${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$存在且满足必要特性。

This theorem lets us restrict our search to the smaller space of decomposable GHDs (instead of all valid GHDs) when looking for the optimal valid GHD. Moreover, the space of decomposable GHDs is simpler; it can be factored into smaller spaces of unconstrained GHDs, as we show next. We present the definition of characteristic hypergraphs, which are intuitively the set of hypergraphs that specify the factors, i.e., the unconstrained GHDs.

该定理使我们在寻找最优有效GHD时，可将搜索范围限制在更小的可分解GHD空间（而非所有有效GHD）。此外，可分解GHD空间更简单——如后文所示，它可分解为无约束GHD的较小空间。接下来我们将给出特征超图的定义，直观上这是指定因子（即无约束GHD）的超图集合。

Our goal is two-fold: (1) to be able to split a decomposable GHD into component GHDs of the characteristic hypergraphs and (2) to be able to take arbitrary GHDs of the characteristic hypergraphs and connect them to create a decomposable GHD of the original AJAR problem. The definition of decomposable GHDs decomposes a GHD into a series of sub-trees ${\mathcal{T}}_{0},\ldots ,{\mathcal{T}}_{k}$ . The definition specifies that the subtrees ${\mathcal{T}}_{1},\ldots ,{\mathcal{T}}_{k}$ must be decomposable GHDs of (smaller) AJAR problems. Additionally,it is simple to show ${\mathcal{T}}_{0}$ is a GHD of the hypergraph $\left( {V\left( {-\alpha }\right) ,\{ E \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  E \subseteq  V\left( {-\alpha }\right) \} }\right)$ . If we apply this decomposition recursively to the subtrees ${\mathcal{T}}_{1},\ldots ,{\mathcal{T}}_{k}$ ,we can divide any decomposable GHD into a series of (unrestricted) GHDs of particular hypergraphs. This provides the basis of our definition of the characteristic hy-pergraphs; we define a hypergraph ${\mathcal{H}}_{0}$ that specifies the hypergraph corresponding to ${\mathcal{T}}_{0}$ and then recurse on the smaller AJAR queries specified in Definition 29.

我们的目标有双重性：(1) 能够将可分解的广义超树分解(GHD)拆分为特征超图的组件GHD；(2) 能够获取特征超图的任意GHD并通过连接它们构建原始AJAR问题的可分解GHD。可分解GHD的定义将GHD分解为一系列子树${\mathcal{T}}_{0},\ldots ,{\mathcal{T}}_{k}$。该定义明确规定这些子树${\mathcal{T}}_{1},\ldots ,{\mathcal{T}}_{k}$必须是（更小规模）AJAR问题的可分解GHD。此外，容易证明${\mathcal{T}}_{0}$是超图$\left( {V\left( {-\alpha }\right) ,\{ E \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  E \subseteq  V\left( {-\alpha }\right) \} }\right)$的GHD。若我们对子树${\mathcal{T}}_{1},\ldots ,{\mathcal{T}}_{k}$递归应用此分解，就能将任何可分解GHD划分为特定超图的（无约束）GHD序列。这为我们定义特征超图奠定了基础——我们定义一个超图${\mathcal{H}}_{0}$来指定与${\mathcal{T}}_{0}$对应的超图，然后根据定义29中规定的更小型AJAR查询进行递归处理。

However, if we are given arbitrary GHDs of the hyper-graphs as defined thus far, we may not be able to stitch them together while preserving the running intersection property of GHDs. To ensure this stitching is possible, we need the characteristic hypergraphs to contain additional edges that we can use to guarantee the running intersection property. Intuitively the edges we add will be the intersections of the adjacent subtrees in our decomposition; for example, for any connected component $C$ of $\mathcal{H} \smallsetminus  V\left( {-\alpha }\right) ,{\mathcal{T}}_{0}$ and ${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$ are adjacent,and we will add the edge $\chi \left( {\mathcal{T}}_{0}\right)  \cap  \chi \left( {\mathcal{T}}_{C}\right)$ to the corresponding hypergraphs. We can use these 'intersection edges' to connect particular nodes in the adjacent subtrees.

然而，若仅使用目前定义的特征超图的任意GHD，我们可能无法在保持GHD运行交集性质的前提下将它们拼接起来。为确保这种拼接可行，需要特征超图包含额外的边集，用以保证运行交集性质。直观上，这些新增边将是我们分解过程中相邻子树的交集；例如，对于$\mathcal{H} \smallsetminus  V\left( {-\alpha }\right) ,{\mathcal{T}}_{0}$的任何连通分量$C$，当${\mathcal{T}}_{C}$与之相邻时，我们会将边$\chi \left( {\mathcal{T}}_{0}\right)  \cap  \chi \left( {\mathcal{T}}_{C}\right)$加入对应超图。通过这些"交集边"，可以连接相邻子树中的特定节点。

DEFINITION 33. Given an AJAR problem ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ ,suppose ${C}_{1},\ldots ,{C}_{k}$ are the connected components of $\mathcal{H} \smallsetminus  {\mathcal{V}}_{-\alpha }$ . Define a function $H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)$ that maps AJAR queries to a set of hypergraphs as follows:

定义33. 给定AJAR问题${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$，设${C}_{1},\ldots ,{C}_{k}$是$\mathcal{H} \smallsetminus  {\mathcal{V}}_{-\alpha }$的连通分量。定义映射函数$H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)$将AJAR查询映射到超图集合如下：

$$
\text{-}{C}_{i}^{ + } = \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{E \in  {\mathcal{E}}_{{C}_{i}}}}E\text{for all}1 \leq  i \leq  k
$$

- ${\mathcal{H}}_{0} = \left( {{\mathcal{V}}_{-\alpha },\left\{  {F \in  \mathcal{E} \mid  F \subseteq  {\mathcal{V}}_{-\alpha }}\right\}   \cup  \left\{  {{\mathcal{V}}_{-\alpha } \cap  {C}_{i}^{ + } \mid  1 \leq  i \leq  k}\right\}  }\right)$

$$
\text{-}{\mathcal{H}}_{i}^{ + } = \left( {{C}_{i}^{ + },{\mathcal{E}}_{{C}_{i}} \cup  \left\{  {{\mathcal{V}}_{-\alpha } \cap  {C}_{i}^{ + }}\right\}  }\right) 
$$

$$
\text{-}H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)  = \left\{  {\mathcal{H}}_{0}\right\}   \cup  \mathop{\bigcup }\limits_{{1 \leq  i \leq  k}}H\left( {{\mathcal{H}}_{i}^{ + },{\alpha }_{{C}_{i} \smallsetminus  {C}_{i}^{O}}}\right) 
$$

The hypergraphs in the set $H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)$ are defined to be the characteristic hypergraphs.

该集合$H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)$中的超图被定义为特征超图。

Note that the definition of characteristic hypergraphs depends only on $\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)$ ,and not on a specific GHD or the instance. Now we state a key result that lets us reduce the problem of searching for an optimal valid GHD over $\mathcal{H}$ to that of searching for (not necessarily valid) optimal GHDs over characteristic hypergraphs. Each decomposable GHD corresponds to a GHD over each characteristic hyper-graph; conversely, a combination of GHDs for characteristic hypergraphs gives us a decomposable GHD for $\mathcal{H}$ . Formally:

需注意特征超图的定义仅依赖于$\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)$，而与特定GHD或实例无关。现在我们陈述关键结论：通过将$\mathcal{H}$上寻找最优有效GHD的问题，转化为在特征超图上寻找（不必有效）最优GHD的问题。每个可分解GHD都对应着各特征超图上的GHD；反之，特征超图GHD的组合将生成$\mathcal{H}$的可分解GHD。形式化表述如下：

THEOREM 34. For an AJAR query ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ ,suppose ${\mathcal{H}}_{0},\ldots$ , ${\mathcal{H}}_{k}$ are the characteristic hypergraphs $H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)$ . Then GHDs ${G}_{0},{G}_{1},\ldots ,{G}_{k}$ of ${\mathcal{H}}_{0},\ldots ,{\mathcal{H}}_{k}$ can be connected to form a decomposable GHD G for ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ . Conversely,any decomposable GHD $G$ of ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ can be partitioned into GHDs ${G}_{0},{G}_{1},\ldots ,{G}_{k}$ of the characteristic hypergraphs ${\mathcal{H}}_{0},\ldots ,{\mathcal{H}}_{k}$ . Moreover,in both of these cases, $\gamma$ -width $\left( G\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{i}\gamma$ -width $\left( {G}_{i}\right)$ .

定理34. 对于AJAR查询${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$，假设${\mathcal{H}}_{0},\ldots$和${\mathcal{H}}_{k}$是其特征超图$H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right)$。则${\mathcal{H}}_{0},\ldots ,{\mathcal{H}}_{k}$的GHDs${G}_{0},{G}_{1},\ldots ,{G}_{k}$可连接构成${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$的可分解GHD G。反之，${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$的任意可分解GHD$G$可划分为特征超图${\mathcal{H}}_{0},\ldots ,{\mathcal{H}}_{k}$的GHDs${G}_{0},{G}_{1},\ldots ,{G}_{k}$。此外，这两种情况下都满足$\gamma$-宽度$\left( G\right)  = \mathop{\max }\limits_{i}\gamma$-宽度$\left( {G}_{i}\right)$。

The proof is provided in the technical report, but it is a straightforward application of definitions.

证明详见技术报告，本质上是定义的直接应用。

COROLLARY 35. Given an optimal GHD for each characteristic hypergraph of an AJAR query ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ ,we can construct an optimal valid GHD. The width of the optimal valid GHD equals the maximum optimal-GHD-width over its characteristic hypergraphs.

推论35. 给定AJAR查询${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$各特征超图的最优GHD，可构造出最优有效GHD。该最优有效GHD的宽度等于其特征超图的最优GHD宽度的最大值。

This reduces the problem of finding the optimal valid GHD to smaller problems of finding optimal GHDs. Then we present several applications of our decomposition.

这将寻找最优有效GHD的问题简化为若干寻找最优GHD的子问题。随后我们将展示该分解方法的若干应用。

### 5.2 寻找最优有效GHD

### 5.3 Tighter Runtime Exponents

### 5.3 更严格的运行时指数

Marx [18] introduced the notion of submodular width(sw) that is tighter than fhw, and showed that a join query can be answered in time ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{O\left( {sw}\right) }$ . The $O$ in the exponent is because Marx's algorithm requires expensive preprocessing that takes ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{2 \times  {sw}}$ time. After the pre-processing,the join can be performed in time ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{sw}$ . Despite the $O$ in the exponent, this algorithm can be very valuable because there are families of hypergraphs that have unbounded fhw but bounded ${sw}$ . We can apply Marx’s algorithm to the characteristic hypergraphs, potentially improving our runtime. Marx also showed that joins on a family of hypergraphs are fixed parameter tractable if any only if the submodular width of the hypergraph family is bounded [18]. Moreover, adaptive width [19] (applicable only when relations are expressed as truth tables) is unbounded for a hypergraph family if and only if submodular width is unbounded. Corollary 35 gets us an analogous tractability result for AJAR queries.

Marx[18]提出了比fhw更严格的子模宽度(sw)概念，并证明连接查询可在${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{O\left( {sw}\right) }$时间内解答。指数中的$O$源于Marx算法需要耗时${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{2 \times  {sw}}$的昂贵预处理。预处理后，连接操作可在${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{sw}$时间内完成。尽管指数含$O$，该算法极具价值——存在某些超图族具有无界fhw但有界${sw}$。我们可将Marx算法应用于特征超图，可能改善运行时。Marx还证明：超图族的连接问题具有固定参数可追踪性，当且仅当该族的子模宽度有界[18]。此外，适应性宽度[19]（仅适用于真值表表示的关系）对超图族无界当且仅当子模宽度无界。推论35使我们得到AJAR查询的类似可追踪性结果。

THEOREM 40. We can answer an AJAR query ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ in time $O\left( {{\operatorname{IN}}^{O\left( {\mathop{\max }\limits_{{{\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } \in  H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right) }}\left( {\operatorname{sw}\left( {\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }\right) }\right) }\right) } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ .

定理40. 我们可在$O\left( {{\operatorname{IN}}^{O\left( {\mathop{\max }\limits_{{{\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } \in  H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right) }}\left( {\operatorname{sw}\left( {\mathcal{H}}^{\prime }\right) }\right) }\right) } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间内解答AJAR查询${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$。

Recent work [14] uses degree information to more tightly bound the output size of a query. The bound in the reference, called the DBP bound, has a tighter exponent than the AGM bound, while requiring only linear preprocessing to obtain. The authors also provide algorithms whose runtime matches the DBP bound. We can define DBP-width ${dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right)$ such that ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{dbpw }\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) }$ is the maximum value of the DBP bound over all bags of GHD $T$ . We then use the improved algorithm in place of GJ in AggroGHDJoin. This lets us get tighter results "for free",reducing our runtime to ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{dbpw }}$ instead of ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhw }}$ . Formally:

近期研究[14]利用度数信息更严格地限定查询输出规模。文献中称为DBP界的约束，其指数比AGM界更严格，且仅需线性预处理即可获得。作者还提供了与DBP界匹配的运行时算法。我们可定义DBP宽度${dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right)$，使得${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{dbpw }\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) }$是GHD$T$所有包上DBP界的最大值。随后在AggroGHDJoin中用改进算法替代GJ，从而"免费"获得更严格结果，将运行时从${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{fhw }}$降至${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{dbpw }}$。形式化表述：

THEOREM 41. Given an AJAR query ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ and a valid ${GHD}$ for $\mathcal{H}$ ,we can answer the query in time $O\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) } + }\right.$ OUT). Equivalently, we can answer the query in time $O\left( {{\operatorname{IN}}^{\max }{\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } \in  H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right) \overset{{dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) }{ \rightarrow  } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$ .

定理41. 给定AJAR查询${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$和针对$\mathcal{H}$的有效${GHD}$，我们可在$O\left( {{\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) } + }\right.$ OUT)时间内解答查询。等价地，可在$O\left( {{\operatorname{IN}}^{\max }{\mathcal{H}}^{\prime } \in  H\left( {\mathcal{H},\alpha }\right) \overset{{dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) }{ \rightarrow  } + \mathrm{{OUT}}}\right)$时间内完成查询。

### 5.4 MapReduce and Parallel Processing

### 5.4 MapReduce与并行处理

The GYM algorithm [3] uses GHDs to efficiently process joins in a MapReduce setting. GYM makes use of the GHD structure to parallelize different parts of the join. Given a GHD of depth $d$ ,and width $w$ ,with $n$ attributes,GYM can perform a join in a MapReduce setting in $O\left( {d + \log \left( n\right) }\right)$ rounds at a communication cost of ${M}^{-1}{\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w} + \mathrm{{OUT}}\right) }^{2}$ where $M$ is the memory per processor on the MapReduce cluster. Combining this with the degree-based MapReduce algorithm [14] gives us the following result:

GYM算法[3]利用广义超树分解(GHDs)在MapReduce环境中高效处理连接操作。该算法通过GHD结构实现连接操作不同部分的并行化。给定一个深度为$d$、宽度为$w$且包含$n$个属性的GHD时，GYM可在$O\left( {d + \log \left( n\right) }\right)$轮MapReduce中完成连接操作，通信开销为${M}^{-1}{\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{w} + \mathrm{{OUT}}\right) }^{2}$（其中$M$表示MapReduce集群中每个处理器的内存容量）。结合基于度数的MapReduce算法[14]，我们得到以下结论：

THEOREM 42. Given an optimal valid ${GHD}\left( {{\mathcal{T}}^{ * },\chi }\right)$ of depth $d$ ,and DBP-width dbpw,we can answer an AJAR query with Communication Cost equal to $O\left( {{M}^{-1}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) }\right. }\right.$ + OUT ${)}^{2}$ ) in $d + \log \left( n\right)$ MapReduce rounds,where $n$ is the number of attributes and $M$ is the available memory per processor.

定理42. 给定深度为$d$的最优有效${GHD}\left( {{\mathcal{T}}^{ * },\chi }\right)$及DBP宽度dbpw时，我们可在$d + \log \left( n\right)$轮MapReduce中以$O\left( {{M}^{-1}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) }\right. }\right.$ + 输出${)}^{2}$的通信代价回答AJAR查询，其中$n$为属性数量，$M$表示每个处理器的可用内存。

A GHD can have depth up to $O\left( n\right)$ ,in which case the algorithm can take a very large number of MapReduce rounds $\left( {O\left( n\right) }\right)$ . To address this,the GYM paper uses the ’Log-GTA’ algorithm to reduce the depth of any given GHD to $\log \left( n\right)$ while at most tripling its width. This lets it process joins in $\log \left( n\right)$ MapReduce rounds at a cost of ${M}^{-1}{\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{3w} + \mathrm{{OUT}}\right) }^{2}$ .

GHD的最大深度可达$O\left( n\right)$，此时算法可能需要$\left( {O\left( n\right) }\right)$轮MapReduce。为解决此问题，GYM论文采用'Log-GTA'算法将任意GHD深度缩减至$\log \left( n\right)$，同时至多使其宽度增加三倍。这使得算法能在$\log \left( n\right)$轮MapReduce中以${M}^{-1}{\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{3w} + \mathrm{{OUT}}\right) }^{2}$的代价处理连接操作。

Log-GTA involves some shuffling of the attributes in the GHD bags, so naively applying it to a valid GHD could make the GHD invalid (see technical report for an example). But our decomposition lets us apply Log-GTA to the GHD of each characteristic hypergraph, and then stitch the short GHDs together. Our decomposition is recursive in nature; let ${d}^{\prime }$ be the maximum recursive depth of the decomposition for a given ${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$ . Then the depth of the shortened GHD of each characteristic hypergraph is $O\left( {\log \left( n\right) }\right)$ ,and so the depth of the valid GHD obtained by stitching them together is $O\left( {{d}^{\prime }\log \left( n\right) }\right)$ . This gives us the result:

Log-GTA算法涉及GHD包中属性的重新排列，若直接应用于有效GHD可能导致其失效（示例见技术报告）。但我们的分解方法允许对每个特征超图的GHD单独应用Log-GTA，再将缩短后的GHD拼接。该分解本质上是递归的——设${d}^{\prime }$为给定${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$时分解的最大递归深度，则每个特征超图的缩短GHD深度为$O\left( {\log \left( n\right) }\right)$，拼接后所得有效GHD的总深度为$O\left( {{d}^{\prime }\log \left( n\right) }\right)$。由此可得：

THEOREM 43. If dbpw is the DBP width of an AJAR query, we can answer that query with Communication Cost equal to $O\left( {{M}^{-1}{\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{3 \times  \operatorname{dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) } + \mathrm{{OUT}}\right) }^{2}}\right)$ in ${d}^{\prime }\log \left( n\right)$ MapRe-duce rounds,where $n$ is the number of attributes and $M$ is the available memory per processor.

定理43. 若dbpw是AJAR查询的DBP宽度，我们可在${d}^{\prime }\log \left( n\right)$轮MapReduce中以$O\left( {{M}^{-1}{\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{3 \times  \operatorname{dbpw}\left( {\mathcal{T},\mathcal{H}}\right) } + \mathrm{{OUT}}\right) }^{2}}\right)$的通信代价回答该查询，其中$n$为属性数量，$M$表示每个处理器的可用内存。

${d}^{\prime }$ can vary from $O\left( 1\right)$ to $O\left( n\right)$ depending on the query. The star query from example 37 has ${d}^{\prime } = 2$ ,which lets us process it in $\log \left( n\right)$ MapReduce rounds. Any query that only has a single type of aggregation will have ${d}^{\prime } = 2$ as well. ## 7. CONCLUSION

## 7. 结论

We investigate solutions to and the structure of AJAR queries: aggregate-join queries with multiple aggregators over annotated relations. We start by providing a simple algorithm based on a variant of GHDJoin that operates with a limited set of GHDs. We proceed to investigate the class of equivalent orderings to expand the set of GHDs that we can give our algorithm. To that end, we develop a partial ordering that fully characterizes equivalent orderings. We then develop a characterization of the corresponding valid GHDs, describing how they can be decomposed into ordinary, unrestricted GHDs. This reduction connects us to a trove of parallel work on GHDs. We finish by extending our work to handle product aggregations.

我们研究了AJAR查询的解决方案与结构特征——即在标注关系上执行多聚合器连接的查询。首先基于GHDJoin变体提出简单算法，该算法仅使用有限GHD集合。随后通过研究等价排序类来扩展算法适用的GHD集合。为此，我们建立了完全表征等价排序的偏序关系，进而描述了对应有效GHD的特征及其分解为普通无约束GHD的方法。这种归约使我们得以借鉴大量关于GHD的并行研究成果。最后将工作延伸至处理乘积聚合运算。

In the classic analysis of Yannakakis for join queries (without projection), the runtime of the semi-join portion is bounded by $O\left( \mathrm{{IN}}\right)$ and the bottom-up join is bounded by $O\left( \mathrm{{OUT}}\right)$ . Adding projection (or aggregation) leaves the semi-join portion is unchanged, but the projection reduces the size of the output, thereby making the OUT bound harder to achieve. 示例62. 设$n$为偶数，考虑一个AJAR查询${Q}_{\mathcal{H},\alpha }$，其中$\mathcal{H} = \left( {\left\{  {{A}_{i} \mid  1 \leq  i \leq  n}\right\}  ,\left\{  \left\{  {{A}_{i},{A}_{i + 1}}\right\}  \right\}  }\right.$$1 \leq  i \leq  n - 1\}  \cup  \left\{  \left\{  {{A}_{n},{A}_{1}}\right\}  \right\}$，且$\alpha$为空集（即该查询仅为连接操作）。假设每个属性取值$1,2,3,\ldots 2 \times  \lfloor \sqrt{N}\rfloor$。当每个关系$\left\{  {{A}_{i},{A}_{i + 1}}\right\}$（对于$1 \leq  i \leq  n - 1$）连接相同奇偶性的数值，而关系$\left\{  {{A}_{n},{A}_{1}}\right\}$连接相反奇偶性的数值时，每个关系的大小为$N$，且$\operatorname{IN} = O\left( N\right)$（$n$为常数），此时连接结果为空集。存在一个包含包$\left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{2},{A}_{3}}\right\}  ,\left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{3},{A}_{4}}\right\}  ,\ldots \left\{  {{A}_{1},{A}_{n - 1},{A}_{n}}\right\}$的GHD，该GHD与空序${com}$相容，其fhw值为2，因此${w}^{ * } = 2$。本算法运行时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{2}\right)$。InsideOut将计算由$n - 1$个关系连接组成的中间结果，其规模为${N}^{\left( {n - 1}\right) /2}$，故InsideOut的运行时间至少为$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\left( {n - 1}\right) /2}\right)$。

The FAQ paper does discuss, at a very high-level and without proofs, changes to InsideOut that will allow its runtime to be output-sensitive [16, Section 10.2]. It's most general and useful change involves building a GHD for the output variables and running a message passing algorithm between the bags, which exactly describes GHDJoin. Implementing this change would make InsideOut completely equivalent to AggroGHDJoin. We note that the FAQ paper frames these changes as decisions in how to represent the output, whereas we present the optimization in an algorithmic context, independent of any other storage optimizations.

FAQ论文确实在高度概括且未提供证明的情况下，讨论了使InsideOut运行时间具有输出敏感性的修改方案[16, Section 10.2]。其最通用且有效的修改涉及为输出变量构建GHD，并在各包间运行消息传递算法——这正是GHDJoin的工作原理。实施此修改将使InsideOut完全等同于AggroGHDJoin。需注意的是，FAQ论文将这些修改表述为输出表示方式的决策，而我们将该优化置于算法层面进行阐述，独立于其他存储优化方案。

### B.3 Section 5

### B.3 第5节

In Section 5, we define a decomposition that relates the width of a valid GHD to the widths of a series of ordinary GHDs. Variable orderings (as used by FAQ) are not as readily suited as GHDs are for decompositions. FAQ does have its own version of a decomposition, but the difficulties that arise when using variable orderings are exemplified in the way FAQ switches between GHDs and variable orderings in proofs [16]. In addition, the FAQ decomposition incurs some overhead costs when combining the sub-orderings to build the overall ordering, precluding a result like Corollary 35 that is required for our extensions. We briefly describe this overhead cost, and then provide an example of the runtime difference it can cause.

在第5节中，我们定义了一种分解方法，将有效GHD的宽度与一系列普通GHD的宽度相关联。变量排序（如FAQ所用）不如GHD适合分解操作。FAQ虽有其自身的分解版本，但使用变量排序时产生的困难体现在FAQ在证明过程中频繁切换GHD与变量排序的做法中[16]。此外，FAQ分解在组合子排序以构建整体排序时会产生额外开销，这使得我们扩展所需的关键推论（如推论35）无法成立。我们将简要说明这种开销成本，并通过实例展示其导致的运行时差异。

Output Addition. The FAQ paper uses a decomposition of the problem that is not width-preserving. It removes the set of output attributes $V\left( {-\alpha }\right)$ and decomposes the rest of the hypergraph into smaller hypergraphs. Then it constructs a regular Variable-Ordering/GHD for each hypergraph. Then it adds all output attributes $V\left( {-\alpha }\right)$ into each bag of each of the GHDs, and then stitches the GHDs together. This output addition to the bags of the GHDs leads to a potentially $2 \times$ increase in width compared to our method which stitches the GHDs together without changing their width. As a result, FAQ's decomposition incurs higher runtime costs in each application of the decomposition, as we see in the next three subsections.

输出属性叠加。FAQ论文采用的分解方法无法保持宽度不变。该方法先移除输出属性集$V\left( {-\alpha }\right)$，将剩余超图分解为更小的超图，然后为每个子超图构建常规变量排序/GHD，再将所有输出属性$V\left( {-\alpha }\right)$重新加入每个GHD的所有包中，最后拼接这些GHD。这种对GHD包的输出属性叠加操作，相比我们保持原始宽度拼接GHD的方法，可能导致$2 \times$的宽度增加。正如下文三个小节所示，FAQ分解在每次应用时都会产生更高的运行时成本。

EXAMPLE 64. Consider the query $\mathop{\sum }\limits_{B}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{C}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{D}R\left( {A,B}\right)$ $S\left( {B,C}\right) T\left( {C,D}\right) U\left( {D,A}\right)$ . Suppose $\left| A\right|  = \sqrt{N},\left| B\right|  = 2 =$ $\left| D\right| ,\left| C\right|  = N$ ,and all of the pairwise relations are constructed as complete cross products of the attributes' values. Our decomposition will result in the chain GHD A-ABD-BCD, while the FAQ decomposition will result in the GHD A - ${ABC} - {ACD}$ . The runtimes of both FAQ and GHDJoin using the former ${GHD}$ is $\widetilde{O}\left( N\right)$ ,whereas the runtimes using the latter GHD are $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$ . As such,the FAQ decomposition will perform asymptotically worse than our decomposition.

示例64. 考虑查询$\mathop{\sum }\limits_{B}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{C}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{D}R\left( {A,B}\right)$$S\left( {B,C}\right) T\left( {C,D}\right) U\left( {D,A}\right)$。假设$\left| A\right|  = \sqrt{N},\left| B\right|  = 2 =$$\left| D\right| ,\left| C\right|  = N$，且所有成对关系都是属性值的完全笛卡尔积。我们的分解将生成链式GHD A-ABD-BCD，而FAQ分解将生成GHD A-${ABC} - {ACD}$。使用前者${GHD}$时，FAQ和GHDJoin的运行时均为$\widetilde{O}\left( N\right)$，而使用后者GHD时运行时为$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{3/2}\right)$。由此可见，FAQ分解的渐近性能将逊于我们的分解方法。

More generally,consider a query ${Q}_{n}$ with relations ${R}_{i}\left( {{A}_{i},B}\right)$ , ${S}_{i}\left( {B,{C}_{i}}\right) ,{T}_{i}\left( {{C}_{i},D}\right) ,{U}_{i}\left( {D,{A}_{i}}\right)$ for $1 \leq  i \leq  n$ . Like before, all $\left| {A}_{i}\right|$ ’s are $\sqrt{N}$ ,all $\left| {C}_{i}\right|$ ’s are $N$ ,and $\left| B\right|  = \left| D\right|  = 2$ . And the aggregation ordering only has the + operator, on $B,D$ and all ${C}_{i}$ ’s. Our decomposition gives the chain ${A}_{1}\ldots {A}_{n}$ $- {A}_{1}\ldots {A}_{n}{BD} - {C}_{1}\ldots {C}_{n}{BD}$ . This results in a runtime of $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{n}\right)$ . FAQ’s decomposition gives ${A}_{1}\ldots {A}_{n}B{C}_{1}\ldots {C}_{n}$ $- {A}_{1}\ldots {A}_{n}D{C}_{1}\ldots {C}_{n}$ . This decomposition,and its corresponding ordering,give a runtime of $\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{{3n}/2}\right)$ . Thus the difference between runtime exponents caused by FAQ's decomposition and our decomposition can be arbitrarily high.

更一般地，考虑一个查询${Q}_{n}$，其涉及关系${R}_{i}\left( {{A}_{i},B}\right)$、${S}_{i}\left( {B,{C}_{i}}\right) ,{T}_{i}\left( {{C}_{i},D}\right) ,{U}_{i}\left( {D,{A}_{i}}\right)$对于$1 \leq  i \leq  n$。如前所述，所有$\left| {A}_{i}\right|$都是$\sqrt{N}$，所有$\left| {C}_{i}\right|$都是$N$，且$\left| B\right|  = \left| D\right|  = 2$。聚合排序仅对$B,D$和所有${C}_{i}$使用+运算符。我们的分解得到链式结构${A}_{1}\ldots {A}_{n}$$- {A}_{1}\ldots {A}_{n}{BD} - {C}_{1}\ldots {C}_{n}{BD}$，其运行时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{n}\right)$。FAQ的分解结果为${A}_{1}\ldots {A}_{n}B{C}_{1}\ldots {C}_{n}$$- {A}_{1}\ldots {A}_{n}D{C}_{1}\ldots {C}_{n}$，该分解及其对应排序产生的运行时间为$\widetilde{O}\left( {N}^{{3n}/2}\right)$。因此，FAQ分解与我们的分解所导致的运行时指数差异可以无限大。

The runtime of InsideOut on a query involving idempotent product aggregations is given by $\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{faqw }}\right)$ ,where the faqw depends on the ordering, and the presence of product aggregations. Our algorithm for handling product aggregations recovers the runtime of FAQ. Formally,

InsideOut算法在处理涉及幂等乘积聚合(idempotent product aggregations)的查询时，其运行时间由$\widetilde{O}\left( {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{faqw }}\right)$给出，其中faqw因子取决于排序方式和乘积聚合的存在性。我们处理乘积聚合的算法恢复了FAQ(常见问题)的运行时间。形式化表述如下：

THEOREM 66. For any AJAR query involving idempotent product aggregations, ${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{w}^{ * }} + \mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  2 \cdot  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{faqw }}$ .

定理66. 对于任何包含幂等乘积聚合的AJAR查询，${\mathrm{{IN}}}^{{w}^{ * }} + \mathrm{{OUT}} \leq  2 \cdot  {\mathrm{{IN}}}^{\text{faqw }}$。

The proof is in the technical report. By applying ideas from the FAQ paper to our setting, we can also recover the FAQ runtime on $\# {QCQ}$ . Our order equivalence characterization for product aggregations is both sound and complete; FAQ's characterization algorithm is sound but not complete. We also have a width-preserving decomposition for queries with product aggregates. This lets us get tighter runtime exponents in terms of submodular and DBP-widths (Theorems 40, 41) and efficient MapReduce Algorithms (Theorems ${42},{43})$ .

证明详见技术报告。通过将FAQ论文中的思想应用于我们的场景，我们还能在$\# {QCQ}$上恢复FAQ的运行时间。针对乘积聚合的序等价特征描述既完备又可靠；而FAQ的特征描述算法仅可靠但不完备。我们还为含乘积聚合的查询提供了保持宽度的分解方法，这使得我们能基于次模性和DBP宽度获得更精确的运行时间指数（定理40、41），以及高效的MapReduce算法（定理${42},{43})$）。
